    Let 1 <= p < infinity and let Omega be a bounded connected open subset of R^n with Lipschitz boundary. Poincare-Wirtinger form: there is a constant C depending only on p and Omega such that ||u - u_Omega||_{L^p(Omega)} <= C ||grad u||_{L^p(Omega)} for every u in W^{1,p}(Omega), where u_Omega denotes the average of u over Omega. Zero-boundary form: for u in W_0^{1,p}(Omega), with Omega merely bounded in one direction, ||u||_{L^p(Omega)} <= C ||grad u||_{L^p(Omega)}, so the gradient term alone is an equivalent norm on W_0^{1,p}(Omega). The inequality says a Sobolev function is controlled by its gradient once its mean (or boundary value) is pinned; it is the engine behind coercivity of variational problems, spectral gaps for the Laplacian, and compactness arguments.
