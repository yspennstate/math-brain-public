    The Riemann zeta function is zeta(s) = sum over n >= 1 of n^{-s}, absolutely convergent for Re(s) > 1, extended by analytic continuation to a meromorphic function on C with a single simple pole at s = 1 of residue 1. It satisfies the functional equation relating zeta(s) and zeta(1 - s) through the completed function xi(s) = pi^{-s/2} Gamma(s/2) zeta(s), which is symmetric under s -> 1 - s. Its zeros in the critical strip 0 < Re(s) < 1 govern the distribution of primes; the Riemann hypothesis places them all on Re(s) = 1/2.
