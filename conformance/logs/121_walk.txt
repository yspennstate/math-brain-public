    [Lemma 3 of arXiv:2506.16259, 'Two-dimensional Rademacher walk'] Let $d_1,d_2,d_3\dots$ be a sequence of distinct positive integers, and let $T_k=d_1\eps_1+\dots+d_k\eps_k$ where $\eps_i$ are i.i.d.\ Rademacher. Then for every integer $\displaystyle m\ge \max_{1\le i\le k}d_i$ and every $M\in\Z$ we have $$ \P(T_k-M\equiv 0\pmod m)\le \frac{C \log k}{k} $$ for some universal constant $C>0$.
