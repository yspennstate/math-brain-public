    Publication record. The limiting spectral distributions of random symmetric Toeplitz and Hankel matrices (Bryc, Dembo and Jiang 2006; Hammond and Miller 2005) have moments that are sums of volumes of rational polytopes indexed by pair partitions. Y. Shmalo, Exact moments of the Toeplitz and Hankel limiting spectral distributions (2026) computes them in exact rational arithmetic through order 14: for orders 2, 4, ..., 14 the Toeplitz moments are 1, 8/3, 11, 908/15, 415, 23840/7, 325719/10 and the Hankel moments 1, 2, 11/2, 281/15, 2717/36, 1052/3, 1331087/720. The values at orders 10 to 14 are new; at order 8 the Toeplitz value differs from the 964/15 in Hammond and Miller, with the configuration count behind it in Section 5 of the paper. The paper also proves that a pairing's polytope volume is 1 exactly when the pairing is non-crossing and at most 2/3 otherwise, in both ensembles. Code, data and paper: https://github.com/yspennstate/toeplitz-hankel-exact-moments (commit 2e0eafce8576); ResearchGate record 406782500. Not refereed.
