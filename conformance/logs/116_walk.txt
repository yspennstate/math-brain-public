[card] Smith Normal Form over a Principal Ideal Domain  (id: claim_e6f9d98b0259988ef273dd00e336994eb13c56c3ef42022838b20396950ceba5; claim)
    Let R be a principal ideal domain (for instance Z or a field's polynomial ring k[x]) and A an m x n matrix over R. Then there are invertible matrices P (m x m) and Q (n x n) over R such that P A Q = D is diagonal in the sense that its only nonzero entries are d_1, ..., d_r in positions (1,1), ..., (r,r), with each d_i dividing d_{i+1}. The integer r is the rank of A, and the d_i (the invariant factors) are unique up to multiplication by units: d_1 ... d_k equals the greatest common divisor of all k x k minors of A (the k-th determinantal divisor), which invertible transformations do not change. Consequences: the structure theorem for finitely generated modules over a PID (every such module is R^{n-r} direct sum of R/(d_i)), in particular the classification of finitely generated abelian groups and the rational canonical form of a matrix over a field. R a principal ideal domain (the Bezout step needs gcds to be linear combinations; over a general integral domain the form need not exist) matrices are rectangular, entries in R
