Set \[ H_0 := 1 + \frac{H-1}{q} \in \Bigl(1 - \frac{1}{2q},\, 1\Bigr), \qquad c(H,q) := \sqrt{\frac{H(2H-1)}{q!\,\beta^q\!\left(H_0 - \tfrac{1}{2},\, 2 - 2H_0\right)}}, \] where $\beta$ denotes the Beta function. The \emph{Hermite process of order $q$ and self-similarity parameter $H$} is the stochastic process $Z^{(q,H)} = \{Z_t^{(q,H)}\}_{t \geq 0}$ defined by \begin{equation} Z_t^{(q,H)} = I_q\!\left(L_t^{H,q}\right), \qquad L_t^{H,q}(\xi_1,\ldots,\xi_q) := c(H,q) \int_0^t \prod_{j=1}^q \bigl(s - \xi_j\bigr)_+^{H_0 - 3/2}\, ds, \end{equation} with the convention $\theta_+^\alpha := \theta^\alpha \mathbf{1}_{\theta > 0}$.
