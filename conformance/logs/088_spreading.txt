    Publication record. Y. Shmalo, Fourier entropy of threshold functions and their compositions (arXiv:2610.16069, October 2026) proves the Fourier entropy-influence inequality of Friedgut and Kalai (the spectral entropy of f is at most a constant times its total influence) for every linear threshold function f, with arbitrary real weights and arbitrary threshold; the arXiv abstract gives a constant below 8.937. The argument also gives logarithmic bounds for Boolean combinations of regular functions and for unions with bounded variable overlap, and, under arbitrary product measures, the strengthened inequality needed for read-once composition for symmetric thresholds. The unrestricted FEI conjecture and the product-biased FEI+ for arbitrary weighted thresholds remain open. Paper: https://github.com/yspennstate/fourier-entropy-threshold-functions (commit d8eb4ceb7010). Not refereed.
