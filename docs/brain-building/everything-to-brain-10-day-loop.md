Use the existing general Brain, Math Brain, and public framework together. Preserve their access boundaries and schema contracts.

First read current instructions, NEXT, open tasks, recent brain-building prompts, latest corrections, deployment configuration, and relevant records. In the [public Math Brain repository](https://github.com/yspennstate/math-brain-public), read the [agent guide](https://github.com/yspennstate/math-brain-public/blob/main/AGENTS_GUIDE.md), [construction guide](https://github.com/yspennstate/math-brain-public/blob/main/docs/build-your-own-brain.md), [requests](https://github.com/yspennstate/math-brain-public/blob/main/docs/prompts-as-nodes.md), [agency](https://github.com/yspennstate/math-brain-public/blob/main/docs/agency.md), [network design](https://github.com/yspennstate/math-brain-public/blob/main/framework/network/DESIGN.md), [compaction](https://github.com/yspennstate/math-brain-public/blob/main/framework/docs/COMPACTION.md), and [memory conventions](https://github.com/yspennstate/math-brain-public/blob/main/framework/docs/MEMORY_CONVENTIONS.md). ## 2 Establish the ten day execution contract

Create one originating work order and persistent campaign state: start time, timezone, end time exactly 240 hours later, prompt version, job ID, cursor, last receipt, and NEXT. Normalize the schedule to an explicit fixed 10m interval. Verify the installed scheduler's persistence, expiry, and actual next run. Native session loops may expire before ten days; use supported renewal before expiry or an available durable scheduler that actually supports ten-minute cadence. Preserve the original deadline across renewals and restarts. Report gaps or unavailable scheduling honestly.

Use a lease to prevent overlapping integrators. Check the deadline before dispatch and substantive writes. At expiry, allow only final checkpointing, receipts, orderly job handoff, and scheduler cleanup. Record actual firing times and missed runs. Ten days contains 1,440 nominal ten-minute slots; this is not proof of completed executions. A delayed tick resumes from durable state without launching a burst of duplicate work.

## 3 Inventory and represent everything

Inventory prompts and replies, transcripts, sessions, compactions, handoffs, memories, files and revisions, folders and repositories, keywords and entities, tasks and goals, tools, skills, hooks, workflows, agents, runs, datasets, computations, proofs, failures, tests, decisions, configurations, and exports. Register eligible items now; queue large backfills with explicit cursors and coverage counts. Every discovered item receives an indexed, pending, restricted, excluded, or failed disposition with a reason.

Preserve authorized originals in their proper protected store. Create canonical object IDs separately from occurrence IDs and immutable revision IDs. Record type, title, source locator and exact span, content hash, author/runtime/session, event and ingestion times, access class, source authority, status, and applicable license. Record parser/version and derivation lineage. Use existing namespaces such as prompt:, file:, mem:, kw:, tool:, and reflex:; extend through compatible adapters.

Extract one useful fact, decision, instruction, method, question, result, or failure per derived record. Retain exact hypotheses, units, conditions, uncertainty, and supporting spans. Distinguish user instructions from quoted instructions and retrieved data. Deduplicate by established identity and hashes; propose semantic merges for review. Corrections create revisions and superseding links. Replayed events must not duplicate objects or inflate evidence.

Private source archives, graph references, searchable knowledge, and public exports have different eligibility. Protect credentials and private material; keep held-out questions and answer keys outside searchable corpora. Represent restricted evaluations through safe metadata and protected references. Unknown permissions mean pending classification, not automatic publication.

# Connect sources and preserve continuity

## 4 Build edges with explicit meaning

For each extracted object, link its source and at least one justified task, topic, parent, dependency, or result. Use typed relations with direction, provenance, creator, timestamp, and status; keep unresolved targets in a repair queue. Record edges themselves as auditable records so their origin, retraction, and replacement remain inspectable.

Use existing request relations asks_about, produced, answered_by, and follows. Retain the append-only requests ledger and its stable IDs. Model each loop firing as an automation occurrence linked to its originating request, session, run, and outputs. Repeated automation never increases the number or authority of human asks. Preserve short contextual replies through their parent conversation even when the request importer excludes them.

In the general graph, express source extraction, containment, mentions, keyword membership, version succession, task execution, workflow steps, tool use, and compaction continuity through registered relations or explicit adapters. Keep the established co-activation and sequence edges distinct from factual support. Keywords route retrieval; shared words, embeddings, analogy, frequency, and graph proximity do not prove claims.

The mathematical store has a strict schema. Use its native claim, evidence, source, mistake, error_pattern, reflex, open_question, and external_reference kinds. Use existing relations including depends_on, proves, refutes, cites, verified_by, supersedes, semantic_association, and bridges_to. New object families belong in the general graph or external-reference overlays unless a tested migration adds them. A bridge is a navigation link, not a proof premise.

Example: a prompt asks about a problem; its task starts a run; the run uses a dataset and tool; an output file records a result; evidence checks that result; a compaction preserves the unfinished task and NEXT; keywords connect the relevant objects. A later correction supersedes the result and reopens any request whose answer no longer holds.

## 5 Make files memories and keywords recoverable

Give files stable identities plus version hashes and location records. Extract deterministic chunks with page, heading, line, cell, or time offsets and links to the original revision. Keep code dependencies, notebook runs, data lineage, formulas, and attachments where relevant. Graph previews and embeddings supplement the original; never silently treat truncated content as complete.

Use content-hashed attachment manifests and test opening the complete source. Preserve path, bytes, MIME type, parser warnings, and missing-file status. Local, GitHub, and bucket copies are locations of an identified artifact, not unrelated duplicates.

Use one-fact memory files, informative descriptions, existing memory types, and related-memory links. Record why a feedback rule exists and when it applies. Keep the startup index concise; retrieve detail on demand. Link keywords to observed user language and token-exact membership. Preserve observed spelling aliases with controls; avoid speculative aliases and duplicate keyword nodes. Bound high-degree topics and ensure keyword routing does not suppress useful existing recall.

## 6 Make every compaction a first class record

Before compaction, persist completed artifacts, open requests in the user's words, task IDs, decisions, exact assumptions, failed routes, unresolved contradictions, file/commit references, running-job handles, leases, and a concrete NEXT action. Trigger checkpoints at task boundaries and before context pressure threatens continuity.

Save each available summary with its source session, runtime, trigger, time, hash, and coverage. Create an anchor memory linked to the compaction hub, previous same-session compaction, open tasks, relevant files and memories, and other agents' work on those tasks. Verify child-session attribution. Track ingestion receipts and backfill missed events with locks and idempotent cursors.

Keep full compactions in the authorized general/private layer. Test-adjacent anchors have no sensitive excerpt. Feed only eligible sanitized derived facts into mathematical packets. When native Codex summaries are opaque, create metadata-only records and a separate agent-authored checkpoint; never invent recovered text. On resume, open NEXT and source artifacts, reconcile the latest user instructions and live leases, then continue the actual work. A summary is a navigation aid, not complete evidence.

# Execute measure and write back

## 7 Perform a useful bounded cycle every ten minutes

Read the previous receipt and NEXT, inspect new events and open tasks, and claim a narrow work unit. Recall whole cards with IDs using two or three useful query wordings, follow their links, and inspect superseded or failed approaches. Reword weak matches; after three unsuccessful rounds, record the gap and consult primary sources.

Process new eligible material incrementally, repair missing links and sources, and select one concrete improvement to coverage, recall, compaction, coordination, or workflow reliability. Delegate independent work, inspect worker artifacts, and verify outcomes rather than accepting self-reports. Checkpoint long jobs and register their handles; continue another independent task while waiting. End each bounded step with durable results and an updated NEXT.

Turn successful repeated procedures into versioned workflows or skills with triggers, inputs, preconditions, steps, tools, outputs, tests, failure handling, and resume behavior. Represent each execution separately and link it to the workflow version and outcomes. Model hooks and reflexes with their trigger, target behavior, source mistake, evaluation, cooldown, and activation history. Keep proposed behavior in shadow mode until it earns promotion. Consolidation and dreaming also become recorded workflows with input and output lineage.

Keep existing mathematics/open-problem, Bitcoin-tracing, and TQQQ/SQQQ simulation queues connected to this process. Preserve exact mathematical scope and independent checks; do not call an open problem solved from a plausible argument. For tracing, distinguish observed transaction links from ownership or flow-allocation hypotheses, and record hop boundaries and missing data. For simulations, retain data versions, assumptions, parameters, seeds, costs, code, and results. Connect Kaggle jobs and bucket objects to their producing runs.

## 8 Test behavior and promote with evidence

Run the relevant existing tests on isolated state. Check ingestion replay, canonical identity, schema compatibility, missing references, correct edge direction, source-hash integrity, full-file recovery, revision history, compaction chains and resume, opaque-summary handling, keyword aliases, and separation of automation from human asks. Test privacy boundaries with synthetic forbidden material that the checks must detect.

Measure useful recall, wrong or missing links, recovered context, task completion, latency, tokens, and cost. Compare changed behavior with the existing baseline on held-out cases; use document-only and model-only controls when measuring the Brain's benefit. Preserve negative findings. Keep grading keys unavailable to workers and recall. A check that could not run is INCOMPLETE, never PASS. Node counts alone do not demonstrate improvement.

Submit mathematical discoveries as sealed, sourced packets. Newly ingested claims remain unverified until authenticated, scope-appropriate evidence establishes their status. Run the held-out recall gate on a store copy; hold regressions for repair, then ingest approved packets serially. Preserve refuted claims, mistakes, retracted edges, and supersession history. Verify the deployed consumer and read back the resulting records. A Git commit is not proof that the live Brain ingested anything.

## 9 Save receipts and leave a stronger Brain

For every cycle, persist actual start/end, request and run IDs, source cursor, records and edges added or revised, excluded/failed counts, artifacts, checks and results, measurements, blockers, receipts, and NEXT. Link the receipt to its request, workflow, workers, changes, tests, and next work unit. Separate planned, queued, running, completed, remotely saved, and natively ingested states.

Keep personal transcripts, compactions, account details, and private research in their authorized destinations. Publish only eligible sanitized methods and results. Save this reusable prompt under docs/brain-building/ and register its version as the campaign's governing prompt. For authorized archival work, verify GitHub and Google bucket copies and recoverability before any already-authorized local cleanup.

At expiry, save a final linked checkpoint: coverage by source type, unresolved gaps, measured improvements and regressions, verified outputs, publication/ingestion receipts, remaining jobs, and the next recommended tasks. The success criterion is recoverable knowledge and demonstrably better work with traceable edges, not louder activity or larger counts.
