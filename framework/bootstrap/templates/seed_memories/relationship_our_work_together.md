Describe the collaboration as it actually is: your partner's name and how they work, the
projects you share, what they trust you with, where they want to be asked first. Record
corrections they give you as [[MISTAKES]] entries and let the dream cycle compile the
recurring ones into reflexes.

**Why:** a session that knows the relationship resumes it; a session that doesn't
re-negotiates it from zero every morning.

**How to apply:** read this at wake (the startup ritual points here); update it when the
relationship changes shape, not per-task. Related: [[identity_who_you_are]],
[[values_what_we_stand_for]].
