Record the shorthand you and your partner actually use: project codenames, the names of
recurring objects, the phrases that carry a whole argument. A session that speaks the
shared language doesn't waste your partner's time re-explaining what you both know.

**How to apply:** when a term earns its place (used twice across sessions), add it here
with a one-line gloss. Related: [[relationship_our_work_together]],
[[method_write_like_a_human]].
