# What changed on 2026-09-02, and why

A day's work on the reflex hook, the judge and the store, in the order it happened. Each item names the
measurement behind it; the mechanisms are in `NOTES_ARE_HINTS.md`, the bank measurements in
`MEASURING_A_NOTES_ARM.md`.

## The reflex hook fires less and says how sure it is

- The per-session backoff state used to be one shared file that every concurrent session read and rewrote;
  a reader that hit a half-written file fell back to an empty state, so counts kept collapsing and a reflex
  re-fired about five times per session-reflex pair instead of twice. Now one small file per session,
  replaced atomically (`instinct_prime.py`).
- At most two reflexes per action on every path; once on entering a territory, then after 30 minutes, 2
  hours, 8 hours. Command-hazard scars stay silent on a write of prose. Everything a gate silences is
  logged with its reason, and the nightly judge samples those too, so each gate's cost is measured.
- Every note carries a hint on how far to trust it: a reflex says how often a judge found it on-domain, an
  association its status from its own frontmatter, a math note what kind of thing it is. The header says a
  note can point right or wrong and is to be weighed, not obeyed.
- Precision comes from the judged log (`reflex_precision.py`), per reflex and, since today, per firing
  path. A recognition cue judged off-domain on most fires is silenced; a danger scar is only ranked down.
- The graph path (a fire because the action is graph-near a scar) was priming itself: forty fires judged
  by hand, two on-domain, thirty-nine impossible without priming. Closed in three steps: a prime must be a
  hot conscious deposit; the gut deposits nothing into the buffer it reads; a memory cited as the source by
  many reflexes primes none of them. The disconfirming test on the live model still passes: a deliberate
  recall of a scar's own memory primes its reflex.
- Eleven triggers were rewritten to their referents with positive and negative controls on the text the
  hook actually matches (the JSON-escaped tool input). The pattern behind most of them: a trigger that
  fires on most multi-line commands is detecting the encoding, not the referent (`replay_text_form.py`
  measures a whole trigger set in both forms before anyone changes the form).
- The cap of two has a measured cost on reports, so the block ends with one line naming what the cap held;
  and a rendered note is cut at a sentence end near 600 characters with a pointer to the memory.
- Fires per hook event on this machine: 1.59 the day before, 1.28 after the order, 1.02 since the graph
  fixes; per session-reflex pair 2.39, 2.01, 1.00.

## The nightly judge

- Sheets carry `fired_at` and `path`; `--since` draws a sheet from a given time so a rewrite is judged on
  fires after it landed. The suppressed-case builder takes any reason the hook logs (a fixed list hung it
  once); the transcript scan is one pass per session with a time window. Two tripwires: the regression gate
  builds the agenda under a budget and fails loudly, and the dream builds the section in a child with a
  visible skip line. Tests: `test_reflex_precision_paths.py`, `test_judge_reasons.py`,
  `graph_fire/test_graph_priming.py`.

## The research bank and the store

- Four notes arms measured on the same frozen sample, one look each: embedding-ranked notes, framed notes,
  one note instead of three, and BM25 notes on the grown store. None helped the solver; framing did not
  repair the harm a note does when it makes a wrong use of a result look legitimate. The store grew by the
  encyclopedia leads, the open-licensed literature statements and twelve hand-authored concept nodes with
  their hypothesis lists (name coverage of the bank's needs 0.64 to 0.84); the hypothesis list served as a
  checklist is the next pre-registered arm.

## Not done, by name

- Log rotation for the fire logs: a tool exists (`rotate_logs.py`, dry run only) but is not applied, because
  every session appends at any moment and a rewrite could lose a row.
- The app test-suite needs a Linux or Mac run (it imports fcntl); the framework tests above pass anywhere.
