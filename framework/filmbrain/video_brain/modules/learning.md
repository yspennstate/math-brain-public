# Grow a useful film-making memory

Retain concrete failures, evidence and corrective mechanisms; graph size alone says little about usefulness.

## Store each lesson with its evidence and limits

For a useful outcome record the observed failure, cause hypothesis, correction, tested result and the circumstance where it applies. Use owner-stated for preferences, verified only for checked observations, and provisional for craft hypotheses.

A metric, an attractive clip or an agent's confidence is not universal evidence. The next reader needs the uncertainty that belongs to the lesson.

**Check:** Every card has provenance and a check; measured claims point to a run. No generated conclusion promotes itself to verified.

## Fire a small relevant warning at the point of risk

Route a brief when there is clear video-making intent; choose the relevant domain cards. Before a final delivery, surface the current-artifact review and recording-identity scars. Do not inject the full handbook into unrelated work.

Too much generic warning text trains agents to ignore it. Recall is a hint to compare with the actual task, not authority to override a direct instruction.

## Distinguish written, retrieved, used and verified

Track documented, retrievable, fired, acted_on and verified independently. A graph import proves storage; a query proves one retrieval; a delivery manifest can prove check execution; only actual viewed/listened outcomes support quality improvement.

Node count, a green dashboard or sending a mesh message cannot prove another agent read or followed the guidance.
