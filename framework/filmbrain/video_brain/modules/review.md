# Review the pictures, words and final video repeatedly

A view of source code or sampled thumbnails cannot stand in for watching and listening to the current cut.

## Give every slide and every cut multiple real passes

Use separate passes for factual/mathematical content, full-resolution visuals, audio-only explanation and combined playback. Repeat after corrections. A generic project needs at least two visual and two audio passes per explanatory unit and two full audiovisual passes per final cut.

Those are minimum implementation defaults, not a reduction of a stronger project order. The ReLU order requested over ten watches and over one hundred audits; its own brief must retain and track those obligations.

**Check:** Receipts identify pass number, reviewer, actual method, scope, current artifact hash, evidence and result. No missing or stale receipt is treated as a pass.

## Look at every rendered slide at useful resolution

Inspect composition, label collisions, clipping, line breaks, source images, plots and all symbols on every slide. Use contact sheets for navigation, then native-resolution frames for text and mathematical details.

Sampling a few slides cannot establish every-slide coverage. A 420-pixel thumbnail previously concealed missing arrow glyphs.

**Check:** The review ledger covers every expected slide ID and records the actual rendered artifact, not just the source LaTeX or Python.

## Audit the recording independently from the board

Listen without using the slide as a crutch. Check pronunciation, natural phrasing, pauses, numbers, omitted premises and ambiguous formula scope. Re-derive important quantities through another path.

A slide containing the right step can falsely reassure a reviewer while the spoken instruction gives the wrong constant.

**Check:** Audio receipts name the current recording hash and specific observations. Numeric waveform metrics do not count as listening.

## Watch the final encoded video in its entirety

Review every moment of the actual exported file, including beginnings, transitions, end cards, credits and chapter boundaries. Record watched time intervals for each full pass so coverage can be checked for gaps.

Scene-level passes do not catch wrong concatenation order, late audio drift, missing chapters or an older file copied to Downloads.

**Check:** Each full pass covers from zero to measured duration with no unreviewed interval; every receipt is tied to the final export hash.

**Evidence:** CH16684, CH16717, CX3572.

## Try to break the load-bearing claims

Re-derive numerical claims by an independent method, check historical assertions against sources, and look for the strongest counterexample or alternative reading. Separate an illustration from evidence and a heuristic from a theorem.

Multiple identical checks can repeat one blind spot. A correct script does not prove a corresponding plot, caption or spoken number is correct.

**Check:** Each important claim has evidence and an explicit second check; unresolved discrepancies block the affected final artifact.

**Evidence:** CH16681, CH16717, CH16753.

## Invalidate reviews when their artifact changes

Bind a review to the SHA-256 of the artifact actually inspected. Changing narration invalidates audio and combined-cut reviews; changing a picture invalidates visual and combined-cut reviews. Recheck the affected unit and every dependent final cut.

Copying a green receipt into the next revision is not a repeat audit. Distinct pass IDs alone are insufficient if they refer to the same review event.

**Check:** The checker rejects stale hashes, repeated event IDs, missing evidence, incomplete intervals and source-only methods.
