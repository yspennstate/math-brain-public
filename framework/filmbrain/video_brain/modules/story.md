# Build a documentary people want to follow

Connect people, places, decisions and consequences using varied, accurate, nonrepeating assets.

## Choose realism by viewing comparable scenes

Compare candidate generators or treatments using the same scene brief, output size and duration. Watch motion, anatomy, faces, object permanence, equipment and camera stability. Prefer a verified historical film or faithful photographic treatment when synthetic motion fails.

A nicer still does not establish better video. Do not hide defects with grain or label generated reconstructions as archival footage.
