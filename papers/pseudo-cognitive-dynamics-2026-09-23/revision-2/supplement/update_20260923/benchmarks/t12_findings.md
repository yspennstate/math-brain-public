## Main finding

T12 reports that adding trigger-based recall pins makes the standing film-orders hub appear within the top five on all 24 source-designated held-out film prompts, versus none before, and all 10 reused T11 tasks, versus five before. These are retrieval endpoints. The source explicitly says that the agent-level comparison of pinned-hub delivery against hourly-block delivery had **not yet run**. T12 therefore must not be counted as another solver benchmark, another successful behavior trial, or proof of equivalent instruction compliance. [T12 result](https://github.com/<private-repo>/blob/9c754d786b3a0ea4943da0585d1490c13cd9ef99/12_cognitive_architecture/brain_improvement/campaign_2026-09-23_round6/tests/t12/RESULT.md)

The T12 directory contains six files: the result narrative, three Python scripts, and two candidate configurations. It contains no `compare_arms.json`. The result explicitly says that this result file and the held-out prompts remain in the originating session's scratchpad. The percentages below are independently calculated from reported aggregate counts; the underlying per-query outcomes could not be independently reconstructed. ## Trigger audit on the prompt ledger

| Proxy topic class | Prompts | Trigger fires | Does not fire | Fire proportion |

| Filed under films | 1,213 | 765 | 448 | 63.0668% |
| Filed under other topics | 6,787 | 65 | 6,722 | 0.9577% |
| Total | 8,000 | 830 | 7,170 | 10.3750% |

Of the 830 fired prompts, 765/830 = **92.1687%** were filed under films. This is the film-topic share among triggers, not independently adjudicated semantic precision. Likewise, 765/1,213 is sensitivity against the automatic film-topic label, and 65/6,787 is the trigger rate in the other-topic class. The source states that nearly all 65 other-topic triggers were misfiled film prompts, without giving an exact adjudicated count. Therefore neither an actual false-positive rate nor corrected semantic precision can be computed. The labeler is `owner_prompts_hook.topic_of`; the script filters for owner-origin, nonempty prompts from a local cache. Its source does not itself enforce an 8,000-row limit, so the reported 8,000 is an observed cache size in the result narrative. ## Policy-route regression and landed implementation

The canonical-policy regression reportedly showed three failing positive probes, whose targeted route sat at rank 2 before the change. After the policy-score lift, the report records **10/10 positives at rank 1** and **31/31 negatives quiet**, on both staged and live code. Here “quiet” means that neither canonical twin is in the top eight; it does not mean retrieval returned nothing. The regression script also includes **two accepted collision cases** that are reported but cannot fail the run, for **43 declared probes** in total. Its safeguards reject nonzero subprocess exits and empty parses for negative controls. [T12 result](https://github.com/<private-repo>/blob/9c754d786b3a0ea4943da0585d1490c13cd9ef99/12_cognitive_architecture/brain_improvement/campaign_2026-09-23_round6/tests/t12/RESULT.md), [Regression script](https://github.com/<private-repo>/blob/9c754d786b3a0ea4943da0585d1490c13cd9ef99/12_cognitive_architecture/dreaming/recall_policy_regression.py)

The pinned `dreaming/recall_probe_log.json` is **not round-6 evidence**: all its 35 rows are dated **2026-08-17**, and its expected count is 35. It cannot substantiate the newer 10-positive/31-negative round-6 result. No round-6 regression row log was available among the inspected T12 files. [Older probe log](https://github.com/<private-repo>/blob/9c754d786b3a0ea4943da0585d1490c13cd9ef99/12_cognitive_architecture/dreaming/recall_probe_log.json)

The landing note describes two changes, and both are present in the pinned production files:

- Normal memories can carry trigger groups; a complete matching group promotes that node as a policy seed. Normal ranking remains available outside the trigger condition, and `BRAIN_RECALL_PINS=0` disables this mechanism.
- Policy seeds are lifted again after multiplicative name/question-coverage boosts, preventing ordinary BM25 hits from overtaking an intended policy route at that stage.

The pinned film configuration contains **35 two-token trigger groups, 54 alias keys, and no blocked phrases**. The GitHub-cost configuration contains **15 trigger groups, 23 alias keys, and no blocked phrases**. The trigger groups and aliases in both committed production policy entries match their respective candidate files. These are implementation checks, not behavioral outcomes. [Landing note](https://github.com/<private-repo>/blob/9c754d786b3a0ea4943da0585d1490c13cd9ef99/12_cognitive_architecture/brain_improvement/campaign_2026-09-23_round6/LANDED.md), [Pinned recall implementation](https://github.com/<private-repo>/blob/9c754d786b3a0ea4943da0585d1490c13cd9ef99/12_cognitive_architecture/dreaming/brain.py), [Pinned policy](https://github.com/<private-repo>/blob/9c754d786b3a0ea4943da0585d1490c13cd9ef99/12_cognitive_architecture/dreaming/recall_policy.json), [Film candidate](https://github.com/<private-repo>/blob/9c754d786b3a0ea4943da0585d1490c13cd9ef99/12_cognitive_architecture/brain_improvement/campaign_2026-09-23_round6/tests/t12/pin_candidate.json), [GitHub-cost candidate](https://github.com/<private-repo>/blob/9c754d786b3a0ea4943da0585d1490c13cd9ef99/12_cognitive_architecture/brain_improvement/campaign_2026-09-23_round6/tests/t12/pin_push_candidate.json)

## Controls and material limitations

1. **The comparison bundles two changes.** `make_stage.py` stages both normal-node pins and the post-boost policy lift from local live files. The comparison does not isolate each component's independent effect. It runs live then staged for each query, with retrieval refresh disabled and shared live data paths. The order is fixed, not randomized. No model, token, cost, or solver result is produced by this harness. [Stage builder](https://github.com/<private-repo>/blob/9c754d786b3a0ea4943da0585d1490c13cd9ef99/12_cognitive_architecture/brain_improvement/campaign_2026-09-23_round6/tests/t12/make_stage.py), [Comparison harness](https://github.com/<private-repo>/blob/9c754d786b3a0ea4943da0585d1490c13cd9ef99/12_cognitive_architecture/brain_improvement/campaign_2026-09-23_round6/tests/t12/compare_arms.py)

2. **The saved endpoint is narrower than full output identity.** The harness parses at most the first eight retrieved node identifiers and records their ranks, elapsed seconds, and subprocess return code. It does not save exact stdout or enforce a successful return code before storing a row. The report's top-five hub counts and list-invariance counts require downstream analysis that is absent from the committed result files. The audit therefore preserves these as reported counts, not independently verified stdout-level identity. [Comparison harness](https://github.com/<private-repo>/blob/9c754d786b3a0ea4943da0585d1490c13cd9ef99/12_cognitive_architecture/brain_improvement/campaign_2026-09-23_round6/tests/t12/compare_arms.py)

3. **The 20 silent controls are conditional controls.** They are sampled after filtering out every prompt whose pin trigger fires. Their unchanged output supports invariance on intentionally silent controls; the 20/20 result is not a random-sample estimate of off-domain trigger specificity. The ledger audit supplies the broader topic-conditioned rate instead. [Comparison harness](https://github.com/<private-repo>/blob/9c754d786b3a0ea4943da0585d1490c13cd9ef99/12_cognitive_architecture/brain_improvement/campaign_2026-09-23_round6/tests/t12/compare_arms.py), [Trigger measurement script](https://github.com/<private-repo>/blob/9c754d786b3a0ea4943da0585d1490c13cd9ef99/12_cognitive_architecture/brain_improvement/campaign_2026-09-23_round6/tests/t12/pin_measure.py)

4. **Held-out status has a limited scope.** T12 loads the held-out film file directly from the T11 directory, while `pin_measure.py` can print missed prompts for tuning. The sources do not establish that this set was withheld from pin development. Treat “held-out” as the source's designation relative to the earlier task set, not proof of a fresh unseen test after trigger selection. Original queries are whitespace-normalized and truncated to 500 characters for comparison; only an 80-character prefix is written into the result row. [Comparison harness](https://github.com/<private-repo>/blob/9c754d786b3a0ea4943da0585d1490c13cd9ef99/12_cognitive_architecture/brain_improvement/campaign_2026-09-23_round6/tests/t12/compare_arms.py), [Trigger measurement script](https://github.com/<private-repo>/blob/9c754d786b3a0ea4943da0585d1490c13cd9ef99/12_cognitive_architecture/brain_improvement/campaign_2026-09-23_round6/tests/t12/pin_measure.py)

5. **There are two reporting discrepancies.** LANDED broadly says that the rest of each list is unchanged, while RESULT specifies 23/24 held-out lists and 10/10 T11 lists; use the more specific **23/24** held-out figure. Also, the measurement script's docstring refers to 16 held-out film prompts, but RESULT and the 62-query accounting indicate 24. The private held-out file is unavailable to resolve whether the docstring is simply stale. [Landing note](https://github.com/<private-repo>/blob/9c754d786b3a0ea4943da0585d1490c13cd9ef99/12_cognitive_architecture/brain_improvement/campaign_2026-09-23_round6/LANDED.md), [T12 result](https://github.com/<private-repo>/blob/9c754d786b3a0ea4943da0585d1490c13cd9ef99/12_cognitive_architecture/brain_improvement/campaign_2026-09-23_round6/tests/t12/RESULT.md), [Trigger measurement script](https://github.com/<private-repo>/blob/9c754d786b3a0ea4943da0585d1490c13cd9ef99/12_cognitive_architecture/brain_improvement/campaign_2026-09-23_round6/tests/t12/pin_measure.py)

6. **Historical reproduction needs additional artifacts.** The stage builder reads mutable owner-local live files; the comparison needs the local memory store, prompt cache, and uncommitted prompt sets. The pinned scripts and current landed implementation alone do not reconstruct the exact pre-change baseline or verify the historical per-query results. No new experiment was run in this audit. [Stage builder](https://github.com/<private-repo>/blob/9c754d786b3a0ea4943da0585d1490c13cd9ef99/12_cognitive_architecture/brain_improvement/campaign_2026-09-23_round6/tests/t12/make_stage.py), [Comparison harness](https://github.com/<private-repo>/blob/9c754d786b3a0ea4943da0585d1490c13cd9ef99/12_cognitive_architecture/brain_improvement/campaign_2026-09-23_round6/tests/t12/compare_arms.py)

## Suggested manuscript treatment

A defensible summary is: “A subsequent retrieval-level follow-up (T12) reported that trigger-based pinning raised the film-orders hub's top-five presence from 0/24 to 24/24 on source-designated held-out film prompts and from 5/10 to 10/10 on reused T11 tasks. Twenty selected non-triggering controls retained their result lists. The follow-up did not test whether agents receiving the pinned hub matched hourly-block instruction compliance, and its per-query result file was not included in the pinned repository.”

Keep these findings in a retrieval/engineering section or a clearly labeled follow-up row. Do not add the 62 retrieval probes to solver-trial denominators. Do not substitute the 34/34 hub-retrieval count for T11's instruction-adherence result.
