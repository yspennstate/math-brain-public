Six more are kept here with their working files:

- `p0211-polymer-relaxation-2026-10-12/`: the linear-polymer chain paper with its code, computations, results and review.
- `pseudo-cognitive-dynamics-2026-09-23/`: *Mathematics of pseudo-cognitive dynamics: persistent memory, error learning, and verifiable mathematical research in AI systems*, on memories of the kind this repository holds, with its second revision.
- `public/crossing-cores-rmt/`: *Crossing cores, Gorenstein moment polytopes, and sharp volume gaps for random Toeplitz and Hankel laws*, with the exact rational verification package and the section-profiles paper.
- `public/ising-perceptron-capacity-work/`: working notes and the reverification for the Ising perceptron capacity paper.
- `public/ising-perceptron-margin-work/`: working files for the Ising perceptron at general margin.
- `public/three-problems-in-kernel-methods/`: *Three problems in kernel methods*, proofs and finite-size measurements.
