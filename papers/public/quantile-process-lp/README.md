# Convergence of the quantile process in L^p(0,1)

Paper: *A necessary and sufficient condition for convergence of the quantile process in L^p(0,1)*, by Yitzchak Shmalo.

For 1 ≤ p < ∞, the empirical quantile process √n(Q_n − Q) converges in distribution in L^p(0,1) if and only if the quantile function Q is locally absolutely continuous on (0,1) and ∫ q(t)^p [t(1−t)]^{p/2} dt < ∞, where q = Q'. The limit is −qB, with B a Brownian bridge. No density is assumed, and atoms are allowed. The case p = 1 is due to Beare and Kaji (arXiv:2502.01254). For p > 1 this answers Problem 2 in Section 7 of the survey of del Barrio, González-Sanz, Loubes and Rodríguez-Vítores (arXiv:2505.19104).
