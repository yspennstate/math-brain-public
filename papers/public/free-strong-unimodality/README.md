# Free strong unimodality and a conjecture of Hasebe and Ueda

Yitzchak Shmalo, Einstein Institute of Mathematics, The Hebrew University of Jerusalem

A probability measure μ on the real line is freely strongly unimodal if μ ⊞ ν is unimodal for every unimodal probability measure ν. The paper proves that only point masses have this property. This answers Problem 3.11 of T. Hasebe and Y. Ueda, *Large time unimodality for classical and free Brownian motions with initial distributions*, ALEA Lat. Am. J. Probab. Math. Stat. 15 (2018), 353–374. The witnesses do not depend on μ: if μ is not a point mass, then μ ⊞ (δ₀/100 + 99 U[0, L]/100) is not unimodal for all large L.

The paper also shows that the classical half of Conjecture 5.5 of the same article fails for one-sided stable kernels. If 0 < α < 1 and S_t is the law at time t of a positive strictly α-stable process, then for every nonnegative Borel function f there is a probability measure μ with ∫ f dμ < ∞ such that μ ∗ S_t is unimodal for no t > 0. In particular, finite moments of every order do not give unimodality in large time.
