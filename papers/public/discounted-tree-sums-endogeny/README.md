# Non-endogenous fixed points of the discounted tree sum equation: the regular case

Yitzchak Shmalo

Aldous and Bandyopadhyay (A survey of max-type recursive distributional equations, Ann. Appl. Probab. 15 (2005),
Conjecture 34) conjectured that the invariant measures mu_a, a > 0, that their Corollary 33 builds for the
discounted tree sum equation X = eta + max_i xi_i X_i from a nonzero solution Y of Y = max_i xi_i Y_i are never
endogenous.

The paper shows that the conditional essential supremum M of Y given the innovations is an endogenous solution of
the homogeneous equation on [0, infinity], and that mu_a is not endogenous as soon as M is infinite with positive
probability. So the conjecture, for all nonzero Y at once, is equivalent to the homogeneous equation having no
endogenous solution on [0, infinity) other than the point mass at 0. When the weights have a characteristic
exponent (with the L log L condition if there are infinitely many positive weights), the disintegration theorem of
Alsmeyer, Biggins and Meiners (Ann. Probab. 40 (2012)) shows that M is infinite on the survival event of an
endogenous fixed point of the smoothing transform, and the conjecture holds. Examples 37 and 38 of the survey are
covered. Weights without a characteristic exponent remain open.
