Fits use all available sizes, including small graphs, and log median L1 versus log n.
Bootstrap: 1000 independent resamples of the 40 percolation realizations at each fixed host.
Intervals measure conditional Monte Carlo variability only. They exclude host randomness,
systematic finite-size bias, and uncertainty in any asymptotic power-law model.
The near-regular constructions have very small proven exponents; these simulations
cannot distinguish their eventual polynomial behavior from slow polylogarithmic growth.
Each planted-hub host is fixed for its 40 repeats; seeds and adjacency hashes are in hosts.json.
