# Low-intensity Voronoi limits of correlated point processes in the hyperbolic plane

> Y. Shmalo, *Low-intensity Voronoi limits of correlated point processes in the hyperbolic plane*, November 2026.

D'Achille and Khezeli asked whether a random ideal Voronoi diagram other than the ideal Poisson–Voronoi tessellation can arise as the low-intensity limit of the Voronoi tessellations of a point process, and named the α-hyperbolic ensemble, as α → 0, as a candidate. The paper shows that this ensemble gives the ideal Poisson–Voronoi tessellation again. More generally, the same limit arises whenever the k-point correlation functions are bounded by C^k λ^k and ρ_k/λ^k tends to one uniformly on configurations whose points are at mutual distance at least 2 log(1/λ) − L, for every fixed k and L. Among families satisfying the bound, a different limit therefore needs, for some k, a relative discrepancy of the k-point correlation that does not vanish at distances 2 log(1/λ) + O(1).
