# Major roads and span length in scale-invariant random spatial networks

> Y. Shmalo, *Major roads and span length in scale-invariant random spatial networks*, November 2026.

The paper studies two of Aldous's open problems on scale-invariant random spatial networks. For his binary hierarchy model, almost surely the set of major roads contains every road line above a fixed height. These lines form a connected grid, so the major roads have exactly one unbounded connected component, which answers Open Problem 7 of Aldous for this model. Open Problem 9 asks under what extra assumptions the subnetwork spanning k uniform points in a square of area k has expected length asymptotic to ℓk. In every such network this holds exactly when the part of the span far from the square has expected length o(k), and it holds when the expected length of one route outside the disc of radius t about an endpoint is o(t^{-3}). The condition is met in the binary hierarchy model, with no extra assumption, and in the planar Poisson line network when its speed exponent exceeds 5.

The paper is `paper/main.tex`, compiled to `paper/main.pdf`. No result depends on computation.
