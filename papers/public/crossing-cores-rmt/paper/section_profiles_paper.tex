\title[Section profiles for Toeplitz and Hankel moment polytopes]
{Section profiles, concatenation, and slab hierarchies for random
Toeplitz and Hankel moment polytopes}

\author{Yitzchak Shmalo}
\address{Einstein Institute of Mathematics, The Hebrew University of
Jerusalem, Givat Ram, Jerusalem, Israel}

\subjclass[2020]{Primary 60B20; Secondary 05A18, 52B20, 46L54}
\keywords{Random Toeplitz matrices, random Hankel matrices, limiting
spectral distribution, pair partitions, lattice polytopes, box splines,
unimodality}

\begin{abstract}
The moment polytopes of the limiting spectral laws $\gamma_T$ and
$\gamma_H$ of random symmetric Toeplitz and Hankel matrices are
studied through their sections by the initial coordinate.  The
resulting section profile is multiplicative under concatenation of
double-occurrence words, invariant under ear insertion at every
position, and flattens to a constant under a nesting pair.  These three
rules give an exact calculus: volumes of concatenations are computed by
integrating products of profiles, insertions into a gap are computed
against an explicit gap marginal, and the volume of a fully crossing
$r$-pair word is $2/(r+1)$ in closed form.  Consequences include
supermultiplicativity of pairing volumes and of the primitive core
masses, an exact cluster equation exhibiting the crossing correction to
the Catalan equation, and closed-form extremal families: the reduced
Toeplitz cores of at least five pairs attain $4/9$ exactly on one
dihedral orbit for each size, and their parity-mixing counterparts of at
least seven pairs attain $1/4$.  All the slab constants appearing in
this circle of problems -- $2/3$, $1/2$, $2/5$, $1/3$ and the
conjectural $2/7$ -- are shown to be the single family $2/(j+2)$, with
the exceptional system of the fully crossing word attaining each value.
Finally, exact moment data through order twenty-two gives a four-moment
certificate that $\gamma_H$ is not unimodal, shows no obstruction to the
unimodality of $\gamma_T$, and supports a conjectural description of
$\gamma_T$ as a scale mixture of semicircle laws.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

Let $(a_j)_{j\ge0}$ be independent, identically distributed real random
variables with mean zero and unit variance, and let $T_n$ and $H_n$ be
the symmetric Toeplitz and Hankel matrices they generate.  The empirical
spectral distributions of $n^{-1/2}T_n$ and $n^{-1/2}H_n$ converge
almost surely to deterministic symmetric laws $\gamma_T$ and $\gamma_H$
\cite{BDJ,HM}, whose even moments are sums of volumes of
pairing-indexed polytopes: for a pair partition $\pi$ of
$\{0,\dots,2k-1\}$ one forms
\[
\mathcal P_T(\pi)=\bigl\{(x_0,t)\in\mathbb R^{k+1}:
0\le x_0+S_\ell(t)\le1,\ 0\le\ell<2k\bigr\},
\]
with $S_\ell$ the signed partial sums of the increments attached to the
pairs, and $m_{2k}(\gamma_T)=\sum_\pi p_T(\pi)$ with
$p_T(\pi)=\operatorname{vol}\mathcal P_T(\pi)$; the Hankel law is the
same sum restricted to parity-mixing pairings, with alternating forms.
No closed-form identification of either law is known.

The companion analysis in \cite{Shmalo} exposes a stable combinatorial
skeleton behind these sums: repeated deletion of adjacent pairs
(``ears'') terminates in a canonical crossing core, volumes depend only
on the core, and the resulting Catalan transform is exactly invertible.
That analysis works with the volumes themselves.  The present paper
works one level lower, with the function
\[
f_\pi(x)\;=\;\operatorname{vol}_k\bigl\{t:\ 0\le x+S_\ell(t)\le1,\
0\le\ell<2k\bigr\},\qquad x\in[0,1],
\]
the volume of the section of $\mathcal P_T(\pi)$ at initial coordinate
$x$, so that $p_T(\pi)=\int_0^1f_\pi$.  Sections are finer than
volumes: they are not invariant under rotation of the cyclic word, since
rotating moves the base point.  What they gain is a calculus.
Section~2 proves that profiles multiply under concatenation, are
unchanged by inserting an ear at any position, and collapse to a
constant when a word is nested inside a single pair, and derives an
exact formula for insertion into a gap.  Section~3 computes the profile
of the fully crossing word in closed form.  These tools then do
concrete work: supermultiplicativity of volumes and of the primitive
core masses (Section~4), an exact cluster equation for the profile
generating function (Section~5), extremal families realising $4/9$ and
$1/4$ (Section~6), and the identification of every slab constant in the
subject as one family (Section~7).  Section~8 turns to the limiting laws
themselves, where exact moments through order twenty-two give a short
certificate of non-unimodality for $\gamma_H$ and evidence for a
semicircular-mixture description of $\gamma_T$.

Throughout, references to numbered results of the form ``Theorem 4.1'',
``Lemma 8.1'' and so on are to \cite{Shmalo}.  All computations
described as exact were carried out in rational arithmetic; the
verification scripts are available with the source package.
