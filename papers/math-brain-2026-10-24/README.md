# The math brain

A shared memory for mathematical agents: what it stores, how it is searched, and what it does and does not change in controlled tests. `math_brain.pdf` is the paper; `main.tex` and `sections/` build it with `latexmk -pdf main.tex`. `scripts/mcnemar.py` recomputes the exact paired p values.

The newest test, on 1,000 items in three arms (the brain, the research repositories without it, and the model alone), is Section 9.2 of the paper and the first section of `../../TESTS.md`.
