# Access and reuse

This repository is public. Everyone may read and download it without payment, registration, institutional affiliation, or individual permission.

The project's original code, documentation and project-owned data are released under the MIT licence in `LICENSE`, copyright 2026 Yitzchak Shmalo. MIT permits use, copying, modification, distribution and commercial reuse when its copyright and licence notice is retained. This includes original project code copied into `framework/`; third-party components retain their own notices.

The original Math Brain paper, including its original text, figures and tables, is additionally offered under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Its [licence notice](papers/math-brain-2026-10-24/LICENSE.md) permits sharing, adaptation and commercial reuse with attribution, a licence link and an indication of changes.

## Review boundary

Public availability is not a representation that every third-party quotation has received a source-by-source rights review. Long quotations require that review and, where needed, shortening, permission or removal. A citation alone does not grant republication rights. The MIT and CC BY grants above cover only rights the author can grant; they do not override third-party licences or notices.

Private evaluation banks, answer keys, personal communications and private working-repository histories are not licensed for release by this notice. Historical benchmark markers in code describe the status of benchmark data; they do not make the source code proprietary. Paper and dataset integrity checks are documented in [the release review](papers/math-brain-2026-10-24/RELEASE_REVIEW.md).
