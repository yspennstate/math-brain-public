Check the licence of every music cue before picture lock, not after.

**Why:** on the bakery short a cue's licence excluded festival screening; it was found after lock, and two scenes were re-cut to a new cue's length.
**How to apply:** the lock checklist has a "rights cleared" line per cue; a cue without it blocks the lock. See [[reference_music_library_terms_2026_03_14]].
