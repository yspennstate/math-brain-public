End every report with one line naming the request it answers, in the director's words, with the time it was asked.

**Why:** with four agents reporting in the same hour, a report that assumed its context could not be matched to its request.
**How to apply:** last line, e.g. "(This was your 14:10 ask: cut the harbour film to twelve minutes.)" Related: [[partner_the_director_prefers_short_answers_2026_03_02]].
