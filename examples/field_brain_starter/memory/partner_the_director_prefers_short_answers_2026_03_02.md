The director reads every report on a phone, usually between takes. A report that opens with background gets skimmed
and its result is missed. Put the outcome in the first sentence, then the one number that matters, then details.

**Why:** two reports in February buried a missed delivery date under a list of finished tasks; it was found a day late.
**How to apply:** first line = what happened; never open with "I looked at". See [[feedback_say_which_request_a_report_answers_2026_03_09]].
