A recalled memory is a lead. It may be superseded, out of date or about a different film. Read its status and
supersession line, check it against what is in front of you, then decide.

**Why:** an agent applied the 18-minute plan ([[project_harbour_film_runs_eighteen_minutes_2026_02_20]]) after it had been superseded.
