## FEI for bounded ascending paths

and both full audits. If E is EITHER Boolean label class and every upward
cube-edge path in E has at most k VERTICES, then

H(f)<=4 cot^2(pi/[2(k+1)]) I(f).

This covers arbitrary k-Sperner families and strictly more general
families with disconnected skipped-rank chains. For edge-free supports,
H<=4I and4 is optimal from singleton dimensions tending to infinity.
Arbitrary internal labels in a closed positive-weight band also qualify
with k=floor(width/(2 minweight))+1; zero weights with arbitrary labels
are excluded. No strong I-Var or composition claim follows.
