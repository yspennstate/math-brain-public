# Source and scope check: rank-one weighted `K_{2,3}` apex stability

Checked on 2026-10-03 against the primary sources below.

## Current surrounding literature

- Grimmett and Winkler, *Negative association in uniform forests and connected
  graphs* (2004), formulate and computationally investigate forest edge
  negative association.
- Erickson, *Sums of squares and negative correlation for spanning forests of
  series parallel graphs* (2012), proves Rayleigh inequalities for the
  series-parallel class.  `K_{2,3}` is a natural nontrivial theta backbone,
  while the apex construction used here is not simply an invocation of that
  theorem.
- Huang, *On Negative Correlation of Arboreal Gas on Some Graphs* (2024),
  explicitly describes general arboreal-gas negative correlation as open and
  proves specific-graph/parameter results.
- Çiçeksiz and Ravichandran, arXiv:2610.00549v1 (submitted 2026-09-30), prove
  general high-fugacity results and several complete-graph results, while
  explicitly retaining the arbitrary-graph/arbitrary-weight conjectures.

## What was and was not found

Searches of the above papers and targeted arXiv queries did not locate this
specific five-parameter real-stability statement for the apex-spoke polynomial
of rank-one weighted `K_{2,3}`.  That is a limited prior-art check, not a
novelty guarantee.  No claim of publication priority is made.

## Exact scope

**Proved here:** real stability of the five-variable apex-spoke forest
polynomial for weights `(pa,pb,pc;qa,qb,qc)`, with all five parameters
positive; consequently, strong Rayleigh negative dependence for those five
spoke indicators under external fields and admissible spoke conditioning.

**Not proved:** real stability for six arbitrary edge weights, negative
association of every backbone-edge family, or the full forest negative
association conjecture.
