# Source and scope: arbitrary weighted `K_{2,3}` apex stability

Checked on 2026-10-03 against the primary forest-negative-correlation sources
already archived in this campaign: Grimmett--Winkler (2004), Erickson (2012),
Huang (2024), and Çiçeksiz--Ravichandran arXiv:2610.00549v1 (2026-09-30).
Those sources retain arbitrary-graph/arbitrary-weight forest negative
correlation as open while proving other graph classes or regimes.

Targeted searches did not locate this precise arbitrary-weight `K_{2,3}`
apex-spoke real-stability theorem.  This is a bounded search, not a priority
or novelty guarantee, and there has been no external specialist review.

Proved here: real stability for all six positive weights on the unsubdivided
`K_{2,3}` backbone, and consequent strong-Rayleigh properties of the five
apex spokes.  Not proved: the general forest conjecture, arbitrary theta
subdivisions, or negative association involving all backbone edges.
