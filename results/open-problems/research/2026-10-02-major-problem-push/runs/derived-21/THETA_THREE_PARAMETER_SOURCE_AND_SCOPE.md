# Source and scope: three-parameter theta apex stability

Checked 3 October 2026.

The multiaffine Rayleigh criterion and strong-Rayleigh consequences are
credited to Borcea--Branden--Liggett, arXiv:0707.2340. Huang,
arXiv:2311.00965, and Ciceksiz--Ravichandran, arXiv:2610.00549, are credited
for the broader forest-correlation problem, reductions, identities, and
current established regimes.

Targeted primary-source searches combining `K_{2,3}`, `theta graph`, `apex
forest polynomial`, `real stable`, and `strongly Rayleigh` located no source
stating the theorem proved here. This limited search does not establish
historical novelty or priority; no external expert review has occurred.

Exact proved scope: weights `(pr,p,p;qr,q,q)` with `p,q,r>0`, independent
apex-spoke variables, the resulting spoke strong-Rayleigh law, and the
proportional-row marked-pair corollary.

Not proved: six arbitrary edge activities, three unrelated arm multipliers,
theta subdivisions, series-parallel closure, backbone cylinder conditioning,
nonproportional rows, joint negative association involving backbone or marked
edges, or the full forest negative-correlation conjecture.
