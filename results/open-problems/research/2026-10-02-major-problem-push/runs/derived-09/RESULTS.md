## Completed forest theorem

The entire vector of terminal-matching indicators over a weighted
three-vertex core with rank-one spokes is negatively associated, including
after conditioning any other matching bits and applying arbitrary positive
matching fields. Terminal and matching counts are unbounded. Row factors
and core activities may be nonnegative; the three column factors are
positive. The result concerns matching indicators only.

the complete ant and root analytic audits, and both standalone exact
verifiers in that folder. The self-contained support-at-most-two NA lemma,
literal independent Bernoulli OR representation, parameter inequalities,
zero endpoints, conditioning closure and fields are all proved. No general
NA closure or real-stability premise is assumed.

Root independently rederived the entire argument, read the final proof and
both full checkers, and reran them. Author checks cover four full matching
laws, 432 conditionings and 10,714 exact increasing-event inequalities;
the independent six-edge checker covers all 729 conditionings and 86,634

strict pairwise predecessor retains integrated matching variance and

## Completed restricted Sidorenko theorems

Read the [full cycle-surplus proof](../../../../proofnet24/C30-sidorenko/continuations/2026-10-02-restricted-hosts/continuation_20261002_klein4_cycle_surplus_proof.md)
and [completion/source record](../../../../proofnet24/C30-sidorenko/continuations/2026-10-02-restricted-hosts/RESTRICTED_ML10_COMPLETION_AND_CHECKS.md).
For every equal-measure, equal-diagonal regular symmetric four-state host,
write s for its mean and r_i for the three centered eigenvalues. Then

t(ML10,W) >= s^15+5s^11 sum r_i^4+(8165/567)s^9 sum r_i^6.

The coefficient 5 is sharp in an infinitesimal one-mode direction. The
sixth-order coefficient is the optimal value of the displayed
coefficientwise ordered-gap certificate only, not the global optimum.
The value 15 is refuted by an explicit strictly positive rational host.
Direct host assignment, 16-state transfer, and binary cycle-space flow
enumerations agree exactly; full positive coefficient certificates and
independent generators/audits are archived. Root read and reran them.

The separate Schur-convexity proof also establishes
t >= s^15+1024 sum_(i<j)|p_i-p_j|^15. The rank-one quantitative companion
has t >= 1+5lambda^4+2lambda^6 on its stated normalized kernels. Its
qualitative t>=1 conclusion is already implied by known two-spin Bethe
theorems; the full prior-art file proves that implication. No new
qualitative rank-one resolution is claimed. Original historical source
checkpoint and associated search artifacts are retained for continuity,
explicitly subordinate to the later proofs and corrected source record.
