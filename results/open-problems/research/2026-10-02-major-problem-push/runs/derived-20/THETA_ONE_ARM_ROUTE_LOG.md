# Route log: breaking the theta arm symmetry

## Route A: exact Rayleigh discriminants — success

Starting from the side-uniform theorem, one internal arm was given a common
multiplier `r`. Endpoint symmetry and the swap of the two ordinary arms leave
five pair orbits. Choosing the quadratic variable carefully makes every
remaining discriminant a shifted binary quadratic form. Five exact positive-
definite certificates prove global real stability.

## Route B: simultaneous side and arm asymmetry — unresolved

The larger three-parameter family with weights
`pr,p,p,qr,q,q` was expanded exactly. Several pair orbits still factor into
positive binary forms, but the special--ordinary internal pair leaves a
genuine bivariate quartic after the obvious square factors. No SOS or
negative exact witness was obtained, so no claim is made.

## Route C: two-terminal determinant gluing — unresolved

The cactus one-terminal normalization has a universal scalar Gram block.
For two terminals the off-diagonal Gram entry depends on the network. The
existing Schur gluing therefore does not prove parallel closure, and no
determinantal representation was assumed.

## Route D: six-weight countersearch — evidence only

Checkpoint 19's deterministic search tested 36,000,000 real Rayleigh
evaluations for unrelated positive weights and found no negative value.
This motivated extension attempts but is not used in the proof.
