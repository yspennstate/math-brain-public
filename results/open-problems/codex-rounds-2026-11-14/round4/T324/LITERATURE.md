# Brief literature update

The initial search was limited to five minutes. It found no later resolution of the full problem after the supplied reports. This does not establish publication priority for the special case in PROOF.md.

The following pages were opened, rather than inferred from search snippets:

- Giuseppe Bruno, Federico Pasqualotto, Andrea Agazzi, *Emergence of meta-stable clustering in mean-field transformer models*, [arXiv:2410.23228](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.23228), v2, 16 April 2025. Its abstract describes perturbation of uniform initialization and a structured metastable manifold selected by an instability index.
- Borjan Geshkovski, Cyril Letrouit, Yury Polyanskiy, Philippe Rigollet, *A mathematical perspective on Transformers*, [arXiv:2312.10794](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.10794), v5, 21 August 2025. This is the framework and survey cited in the question.
- Giuseppe Bruno, Federico Pasqualotto, Andrea Agazzi, *A multiscale analysis of mean-field transformers in the moderate interaction regime*, [arXiv:2509.25040](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.25040), 29 September 2025. Also opened its [HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.25040v1). The paper studies joint token-number and inverse-temperature scaling and describes limiting dynamics in several phases. The earlier workers already inspected its slow-phase theorem and recorded the pre-collapse limitation; this session did not repeat their full PDF audit.
- Sayan Banerjee, *Metastability and Sharp Propagation-of-Chaos Thresholds for Mean-Field Interacting Diffusions on the Circle*, [arXiv:2610.08755](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08755), v2, 14 October 2026. Its abstract concerns empirical-measure metastability on exponential scales in particle number, subject to assumptions on critical rotation orbits and free-energy minima. It also discusses noisy transformer applications. The earlier workers record why its conditional transformer consequences do not settle the stated normalized-attention problem.

The new proof is self-contained and uses no theorem from these papers. It addresses a deterministic two-group concentration estimate suggested by the round 3 report. No SOLVED_ELSEWHERE.md is warranted by this short update.
