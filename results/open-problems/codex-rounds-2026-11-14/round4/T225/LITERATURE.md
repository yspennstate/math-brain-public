Opened primary sources:

1. Guillaume Wang and Lénaïc Chizat,
   [Local convergence of mean-field Langevin dynamics: from gradient flows
   to linearly monotone games](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.11999),
   arXiv:2602.11999v1, submitted 12 February 2026. The abstract states local
   exponential convergence in chi-square under stationary Poincare and
   monotonicity assumptions. This does not supply a global low-temperature
   risk-time guarantee from Gaussian initialization.

2. Zong Shang, Tomoya Wakayama, Guillaume Lecué and Taiji Suzuki,
   [The Geometry of Statistical Feature Learning in Mean-Field Langevin
   Dynamics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.31429), arXiv:2606.31429v2,
   revised 30 July 2026. The abstract concerns spherical MFLD and
   low-temperature stationary concentration in Gaussian index models.
   Stationary geometry alone does not decide the posed runtime question.

3. Alireza Mousavi-Hosseini, Denny Wu and Murat A. Erdogdu,
   [Learning Multi-Index Models with Neural Networks via Mean-Field
   Langevin Dynamics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.07254),
   arXiv:2408.07254v2, revised 27 March 2025. The abstract describes
   computational dependence on effective dimension and conditional
   polynomial convergence with parameters on a compact manifold of
   positive Ricci curvature. These statements do not cover the general
   unbounded two-layer parameterization in the prompt.
