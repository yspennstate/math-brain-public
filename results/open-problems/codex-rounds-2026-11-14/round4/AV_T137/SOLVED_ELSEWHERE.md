# Separate full-row proof claim

Dmitry Panchenko, *The Mézard-Parisi formula*, 29 October 2026, [arXiv:2610.39545v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.39545). Independently opened the [full text, Section 2 and Theorem 2.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.39545v1#S2).

The theorem claims the unrestricted-depth Mézard–Parisi pressure equality for even-K diluted spin glasses with either requested coupling law, and positive-temperature random K-SAT for every K >= 2. Thus it includes odd-K SAT, which the matching OpenAI candidate excludes. It is stated for the original pressure, with the auxiliary perturbation removed in Section 13. These are claims of the paper; proof correctness and peer review are not established here.

The pressure uses counting measure, so no additive log 2 is missing. Its Poisson clause convention matches our Bernoulli-edge Viana–Bray limit at density lambda = c/2, by the independently proved bound in [PROOF.md](PROOF.md). No finite-RSB restriction is imposed on the pressure formula.
