# Verify: has open problem T132 been settled by the OpenAI math release?

Our row: Metric scaling limits of decorated random planar maps
Statement: Random planar maps weighted by a critical FK model with 0 ≤ q < 4 (q = 0 is the map decorated by a uniform spanning tree, q = 1 the uniform map, q = 2 the critical Ising map), with distances rescaled by n^{−1/d_γ+o(1)}, converge in the Gromov–Hausdorff–Prokhorov topology to the γ-Liouville quantum gravity metric sphere, where q = 2 + 2cos(πγ²/2) (γ = √2, √(8/3), √3 in the three cases).
Where it stands (our note, Nov 2026, written before we knew of the release): Uniform maps converge to the Brownian map (Le Gall 2013; Miermont 2013), identified with √(8/3)-LQG (Miller–Sheffield). The γ-LQG metric exists and is unique for every γ ∈ (0,2) (Ding–Dubédat–Dunlap–Falconet 2020; Gwynne–Miller 2021). For decorated maps only distance-exponent bounds are known (Ding–Gwynne, Comm. Math. Phys. 374 (2020); Gwynne–Holden–Sun, arXiv:1711.00723); for spanning-tree-decorated maps a directed distance scales as n^{1/3} (arXiv:2609.22514), which is not the graph distance. Open for every γ ≠ √(8/3).
