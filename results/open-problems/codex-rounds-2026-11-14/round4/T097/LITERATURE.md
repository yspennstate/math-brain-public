# Opened sources

The brief update search found no resolution of the lattice critical-exponent
problem. This records the sources actually opened; it is not a claim of
exhaustive coverage or novelty of the capacity argument.

1. Stephen Muirhead, *Percolation of strongly correlated Gaussian fields II.
   Sharpness of the phase transition*,
   [arXiv:2206.10724v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2206.10724v2).
   Theorems 1.2 and 1.10 supply the lattice density and subcritical-decay
   inputs. Equation (1.8) confirms the SRW Green normalization. The density
   conclusion in dimension three uses powers q>4, with the endpoint excluded.

2. Pierre-François Rodriguez, Jialu Shi, Wen Zhang, *On the one-arm probability
   for the metric graph Gaussian free field in high dimensions*,
   [arXiv:2611.05357](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05357), submitted
   3 November 2026. Its abstract concerns cable-system sign clusters in the
   high-dimensional regime, not critical lattice excursions in dimension three.

3. Alexander Drewitz, Alexis Prévost, Pierre-François Rodriguez, *Fractal
   dimension of critical Gaussian free field sign clusters*,
   [arXiv:2610.03703](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03703), submitted
   2 October 2026. The abstract concerns metric-graph clusters and their
   scaling limits; it does not resolve lattice excursions at h_*>0.

4. Zhenhao Cai, Jian Ding, *One-arm Probabilities for Metric Graph Gaussian
   Free Fields below and at the Critical Dimension*,
   [arXiv:2406.02397v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.02397), revised
   12 July 2024. Its one-arm estimates concern metric-graph connectivity.
   No such estimate is transferred to lattice connectivity here.

The last targeted source read only checked Muirhead's covariance
normalization; it did not extend the brief update search.
