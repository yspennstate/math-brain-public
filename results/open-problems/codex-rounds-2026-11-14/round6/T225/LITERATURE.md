# Bounded literature update

Checked within five minutes of starting on 15 November 2026.
Queries are recorded in LOG.md. No whole-problem solution was found;
this limited check does not establish absence or novelty.

1. Zong Shang, Tomoya Wakayama, Guillaume Lecué, Taiji Suzuki,
   *The Geometry of Statistical Feature Learning in Mean-Field Langevin
   Dynamics*, arXiv:2606.31429v2, 30 July 2026.
   Opened the [arXiv page](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.31429) and
   [full HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.31429v2).
   It studies spherical stationary geometry and statistical feature
   learning. Its stationary concentration statements do not themselves
   give the stated general Euclidean low-temperature learning time.

2. Guillaume Wang, Lénaïc Chizat, *Local convergence of mean-field
   Langevin dynamics: from gradient flows to linearly monotone games*,
   arXiv:2602.11999.
   Opened the [arXiv page](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.11999) and
   [full HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.11999v1).
   Local rates depend on a stationary Poincare inequality and a
   monotonicity assumption. This does not remove the global
   low-temperature risk-time question.

3. Guillaume Wang, Alireza Mousavi-Hosseini, Lénaïc Chizat,
   *Mean-Field Langevin Dynamics for Signed Measures via a Bilevel
   Approach*, arXiv:2406.17054v3, 1 January 2025.
   Opened the [arXiv page](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.17054).
   It concerns a bilevel reduction and includes local polynomial
   dependence for a single neuron. This changes the dynamics from the
   vanilla lifted Euclidean parameterization considered here.

4. Alireza Mousavi-Hosseini, Denny Wu, Murat A. Erdogdu,
   *Learning Multi-Index Models with Neural Networks via Mean-Field
   Langevin Dynamics*, arXiv:2408.07254v2, 27 March 2025.
   Opened the [arXiv page](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.07254).
   Polynomial guarantees require additional compact manifold and
   curvature assumptions. The abstract distinguishes these from the
   Euclidean computational bounds.

The analytic result in this folder is self-contained and does not use
theorems from these papers. No claim of novelty over other local
kernel expansions is made.
