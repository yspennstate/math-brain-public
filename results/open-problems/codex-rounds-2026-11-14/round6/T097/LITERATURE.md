# Opened sources

The brief web search found no resolution of the lattice exponent problem.
This is a scope check, not a novelty determination.

- Stephen Muirhead, *Percolation of strongly correlated Gaussian fields II.
  Sharpness of the phase transition*,
  [arXiv:2206.10724v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2206.10724v2).
  Read Theorems 1.2 and 1.10 and covariance (1.8). Used only for the
  existing lattice density and subcritical inputs stated in PROOF.md.
- Alexander Drewitz, Alexis Prévost and Pierre-François Rodriguez,
  *Fractal dimension of critical Gaussian free field sign clusters*,
  [arXiv:2610.03703](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03703).
  Opened the abstract: metric-graph sign clusters, not lattice excursions
  at their positive critical level.
- Zhenhao Cai and Jian Ding, *One-arm Probabilities for Metric Graph
  Gaussian Free Fields below and at the Critical Dimension*,
  [arXiv:2406.02397](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.02397).
  Opened the abstract: the model is explicitly the metric graph.
- Pierre-François Rodriguez, Jialu Shi and Wen Zhang, *On the one-arm
  probability for the metric graph Gaussian free field in high dimensions*,
  [arXiv:2611.05357](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05357).
  Opened the abstract: cable-system clusters in the high-dimensional regime.

The new deterministic Gaussian arguments and the constructed events do
not use cable-system results. Authors above are copied from the opened
paper or arXiv page. No other paper is cited as a source for a theorem.
