The check ran approximately 11:28–11:30 Asia/Tbilisi. No resolution of the requested nonlinear joint limit was verified. This is not an exhaustive absence claim. The following papers were actually opened through the web tool; scopes below are limited to what was inspected.

* Jeffrey Pennington, Samuel S. Schoenholz, Surya Ganguli, [Resurrecting the sigmoid in deep learning through dynamical isometry: theory and practice](https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.04735), arXiv:1711.04735. Original free-probability calculation; arXiv page opened.
* Benoit Collins, Tomohiro Hayase, [Asymptotic Freeness of Layerwise Jacobians Caused by Invariance of Multilayer Perceptron: The Haar Orthogonal Case](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.13466), arXiv:2103.13466. Opened the arXiv page and [full journal article](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00220-022-04441-7). The finite family of layers and width-limit cutoff argument do not supply the accumulated growing-depth estimates sought here.
* Leonid Pastur, [Eigenvalue Distribution of Large Random Matrices Arising in Deep Neural Networks: Orthogonal Case](https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.04543), arXiv:2201.04543. ArXiv page opened; treats infinite-width dependent orthogonal Jacobians. This orthogonal paper has one author.
* Boris Hanin, Tianze Jiang, [Global Universality of Singular Values in Products of Many Large Random Matrices](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.07872), arXiv:2503.07872. ArXiv page opened. Any-relative-rate joint theorem for independent matrices with i.i.d. entries and a normalized singular-value statistic; it does not establish the nonlinear dependent orthogonal case.
* Benjamin Dadoun, Soufiane Hayou, Hanan Salam, Mohamed El Amine Seddik, Pierre Youssef, [On the Stability of the Jacobian Matrix in Deep Neural Networks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.08764), arXiv:2506.08764, version 3 dated 17 June 2026. Current arXiv page opened. No new version beyond the version already checked in the preceding round was listed. This session does not claim to have re-read the full text.
