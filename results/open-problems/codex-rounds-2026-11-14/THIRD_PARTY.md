# Third-party material in this folder

The folders whose names begin with `AV_` (for example `round3/AV_T032/`, `results/mini1/briefs/AV_T054/`) check
whether a row of our problem list was settled by the mathematics release of OpenAI, github.com/openai/math
(manuscripts and Lean proofs dated September to October 2026). To check a manuscript, the worker copied its source
files into the folder: the `sources/` subfolders, the `source-*.tex` and `*-main.tex` files, and the Lean modules
under `sources/lean/`.

Those copied files are the work of OpenAI and are redistributed here under the Apache License, Version 2.0, a copy
of which is in `LICENSE-openai-math.txt`. Apart from the replacement of local machine paths described in `README.md`, they
are unchanged. The upstream repository is the authoritative version; read it there for the current text and for any
corrections made after 15 November 2026.

Everything else in this folder (briefs, logs, proofs, scripts, reports and vetting notes) was written by our workers
and coordinating agent. The papers those files cite are cited by reference only; their texts are not included.
