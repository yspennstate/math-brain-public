Rigorous infinite-width spectral law with derivative-dependent Haar weights and Gaussian biases; it does not state a joint growing-depth result in the abstract.

Joint limits at any relative rate for independent matrices with i.i.d. entries, distinct from dependent nonlinear orthogonal layers.
