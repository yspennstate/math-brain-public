# Bounded literature update

Searched within the first five minutes on 15 November 2026. The queries
are recorded in LOG.md. No whole-problem solution was found in this
limited check. This is not evidence that no such solution exists.

1. Alireza Mousavi-Hosseini, Denny Wu, Murat A. Erdogdu,
   *Learning Multi-Index Models with Neural Networks via Mean-Field
   Langevin Dynamics*, arXiv:2408.07254v2 (27 March 2025).
   Opened the [arXiv page](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.07254) and
   [full HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2408.07254v2).
   Its abstract distinguishes Euclidean computational guarantees that
   can depend exponentially on effective dimension from polynomial
   guarantees under additional compact positive-curvature manifold
   assumptions. It does not supply the requested general Euclidean
   fixed-prior low-temperature guarantee.

2. Zong Shang, Tomoya Wakayama, Guillaume Lecué, Taiji Suzuki,
   *The Geometry of Statistical Feature Learning in Mean-Field Langevin
   Dynamics*, arXiv:2606.31429v2 (30 July 2026).
   Opened the [arXiv page](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.31429) and
   [full HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.31429v2).
   Its low-temperature claims concern spherical stationary geometry,
   concentration near hidden indices, and statistical consequences.
   Those results do not by themselves give the requested nonlinear
   Euclidean learning time.

3. Anming Gu, Juno Kim, *Mirror Mean-Field Langevin Dynamics*,
   arXiv:2505.02621v2 (18 May 2026).
   Opened the [arXiv page](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.02621).
   The abstract introduces mirror dynamics on constrained convex
   domains and obtains convergence via a uniform LSI. A different
   dynamics/domain and a uniform-LSI hypothesis do not settle this
   question's small-temperature risk complexity.

An attempted OpenReview PDF fetch returned a browser verification page;
it was not read and is not used as a source. No claim of novelty is made
for the initial-kernel or generator calculations in this folder.
