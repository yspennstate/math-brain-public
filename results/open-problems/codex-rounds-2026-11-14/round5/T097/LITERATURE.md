# Opened sources

Brief web check: 15 November 2026, completed within five minutes.
No later solution of the lattice exponent problem was found. This is a
limited search, not a proof that no such paper exists. No novelty search
for the interpolation lemma is claimed.

- Stephen Muirhead, *Percolation of strongly correlated Gaussian fields II.
  Sharpness of the phase transition*,
  [arXiv:2206.10724v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2206.10724v2).
  Opened the full HTML, Theorems 1.2 and 1.10 and the SRW normalization.
  This is the external percolation input in PROOF.md section 7.
- Hugo Duminil-Copin, Subhajit Goswami, Pierre-François Rodriguez,
  Franco Severo, *Equality of critical parameters for percolation of
  Gaussian free field level-sets*,
  [arXiv:2002.07735](https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.07735).
  Opened the arXiv page. It states equality of the three critical
  parameters; its abstract does not claim exact lattice exponents.
- Alexander Drewitz, Alexis Prévost, Pierre-François Rodriguez,
  *Fractal dimension of critical Gaussian free field sign clusters*,
  [arXiv:2610.03703](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03703).
  Opened the arXiv page; the model is the metric graph field.
- Pierre-François Rodriguez, Jialu Shi, Wen Zhang,
  *On the one-arm probability for the metric graph Gaussian free field in
  high dimensions*,
  [arXiv:2611.05357](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05357).
  Opened the arXiv page; its high-dimensional cable-system result does
  not settle the present lattice question.

The new capacity argument is proved directly in PROOF.md. It does not
depend on a cited lattice-to-continuum theorem or cable connectivity.
