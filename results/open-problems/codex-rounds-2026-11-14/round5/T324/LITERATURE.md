# Literature check, 15 November 2026

The search was limited to the first five minutes. It found no complete later resolution of the stated problem; this does not establish priority for the special case proved here.

The following arXiv pages were actually opened:

- Giuseppe Bruno, Federico Pasqualotto and Andrea Agazzi, *Emergence of meta-stable clustering in mean-field transformer models*, [arXiv:2410.23228](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.23228), revised 16 April 2025. This is the source paper.
- Borjan Geshkovski, Cyril Letrouit, Yury Polyanskiy and Philippe Rigollet, *A mathematical perspective on Transformers*, [arXiv:2312.10794](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.10794), revised 21 August 2025. This is the other source in the brief.
- Shi Chen, Zhengjiang Lin, Yury Polyanskiy and Philippe Rigollet, *Quantitative Clustering in Mean-Field Transformer Models*, [arXiv:2504.14697](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.14697), revised 8 May 2026. The abstract states quantitative long-time synchronization for regular mean-field initializations; it does not state the requested generic cluster count and noise-dependent sequential merging law.
- Giuseppe Bruno, Federico Pasqualotto and Andrea Agazzi, *A multiscale analysis of mean-field transformers in the moderate interaction regime*, [arXiv:2509.25040](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.25040), 29 September 2025. Its HTML was also opened. It describes alignment, heat and pairing phases, including a finite-dimensional limiting pairing dynamics on exponential time scales. This was already considered by the earlier sessions. No new revision was listed. It is not a complete resolution of the noisy question in this brief.
- Philippe Rigollet, *The Mean-Field Dynamics of Transformers*, [arXiv:2512.01868](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.01868), revised 30 January 2026. An overview including exact rates for an equiangular reduction.
- Qucheng Gao, Zuyi Yang and Xiao Chen, *Clustered Attractor Manifolds and Dynamical Condensation in Self-Attention*, [arXiv:2608.08922](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.08922), 9 August 2026. The abstract concerns the thermodynamic limit and clustered attractor manifolds. No result from this paper is used below; the full theorem statements were not audited.
- Sayan Banerjee, *Metastability and Sharp Propagation-of-Chaos Thresholds for Mean-Field Interacting Diffusions on the Circle*, [arXiv:2610.08755](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08755), revised 14 October 2026. Its abstract assumes finitely many critical rotation orbits and distinct minimum energies and gives exponential scales in particle number. Its application to noisy transformer interactions does not by itself verify the different large-beta normalized-attention problem posed here.

The proof in PROOF.md is self-contained and uses none of these papers as a theorem input.
