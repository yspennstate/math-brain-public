# Later result and remaining scope

Yi Han, *Weak uniqueness for the stochastic heat equation with nondegenerate
diffusion: the full Hölder regime*, [arXiv:2611.07310v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07310),
submitted 5 November 2026.

[Theorems 1.1 and 1.2, page 3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.07310), establish
uniqueness in law for every positive Hölder exponent: bounded diffusion permits
arbitrary deterministic L2 initial data; unbounded diffusion permits bounded
deterministic initial data. Proposition 8.1 gives the latter drift-free result.

This settles the exponent question for continuous initial data on the torus.
It does not settle the complete initial-data class in the original
[Theorem 1.1 of arXiv:2608.01279](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.01279v1):
unbounded diffusion together with arbitrary L2 initial data at exponents
0<beta<=2/3. The new paper explicitly leaves the initial-data extension open;
the older theorem already handles beta>2/3. Work below concerns this residual
case.

The theorem statements and restrictions were checked in the full text; the
121-page proof and accompanying formalization were not independently verified.
