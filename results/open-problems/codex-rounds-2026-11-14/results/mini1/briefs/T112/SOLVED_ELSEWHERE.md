# Literature status checked 14 November 2026

No resolution of the whole conjecture was found in the bounded search.

Max Aires, Swee Hong Chan, Igor Pak and Greta Panova,
*Breaking the Infinite Barrier in the 1/3–2/3 Conjecture*,
[arXiv:2609.30888v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30888), revised 28 October
2026, proves a positive absolute improvement over the
Brightwell–Felsner–Trotter constant. Theorem 1.2 does not reach 1/3.
The full [HTML paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.30888v2) was opened.

Anish Gupta, *Balance Constants, Majority Cycles, and the Gold Partition
Conjecture through Fourteen Elements*,
[arXiv:2607.23926v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.23926), reports exhaustive
verification through fourteen elements. Its arXiv page was opened. This
is a finite result and predates the status supplied in the brief.

A later lead is the [Zenodo record 10.5281/zenodo.23933091](https://zenodo.org/records/23933091),
by Kevin Fathi, published 3 November 2026. The record claims new
bounded-degree cases but explicitly says that general width three and
the unrestricted conjecture remain open. The record was opened; repeated
attempts to open the linked PDF failed. Its proof claims are therefore
unverified here, and the paper is not used in the computation or its
justification. The index identifier aiXiv:2611.00391 is not an arXiv ID.

The search was bounded and does not establish that no other advance exists.
