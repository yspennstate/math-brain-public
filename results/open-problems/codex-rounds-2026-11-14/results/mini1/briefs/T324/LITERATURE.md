# Literature checked on 14 November 2026

The search found no resolution dated after the supplied November 2026 status. The following earlier results restrict what can be claimed as new. This was a short search, not a comprehensive novelty review.

- Giuseppe Bruno, Federico Pasqualotto, Andrea Agazzi, [*Emergence of meta-stable clustering in mean-field transformer models*, arXiv:2410.23228](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.23228). Opened the arXiv abstract and version history.
- Giuseppe Bruno, Federico Pasqualotto, Andrea Agazzi, [*A multiscale analysis of mean-field transformers in the moderate interaction regime*, arXiv:2509.25040](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.25040), 29 September 2025. Read the PDF's Propositions 3.9 and 3.11, Appendix C and discussion. Proposition 3.11 covers closest-pair motion from equal-weight atomic data before collapse, under a state-dependent exponential time change. This provides relevant deterministic partial results; it does not establish the full requested statement.
- Borjan Geshkovski, Hugo Koubbi, Yury Polyanskiy, Philippe Rigollet, [*Dynamic metastability in the self-attention model*, arXiv:2410.06833](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.06833), 9 October 2024. Opened the abstract and PDF. It establishes exponentially long deterministic multicluster persistence for suitable separated initial configurations and develops a slow-motion interpretation. It predates the status cutoff.
- Philippe Rigollet, [*The Mean-Field Dynamics of Transformers*, arXiv:2512.01868](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.01868). Opened the arXiv page and full HTML; checked Sections 3.2, 5 and 7. This gives the normalized SA equation used here and reviews deterministic metastability. It describes quantitative noisy-transformer dynamics as largely open.
- Sayan Banerjee, [*Metastability and Sharp Propagation-of-Chaos Thresholds for Mean-Field Interacting Diffusions on the Circle*, arXiv:2610.08755v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08755v2), 14 October 2026. Read the abstract, model, and Section 3.3.1 in the PDF. The general theory gives exponential-in-particle-number thresholds under free-energy landscape assumptions. Its transformer application uses unnormalized attention and remains conditional on verifying critical-orbit finiteness, with further conditions for a sharp common threshold. Its attention parameter and inverse noise parameter are distinct. This does not directly settle normalized-attention cluster merging as beta tends to infinity.
- [*Quantifying Concentration Phenomena of Mean-Field Transformers in the Low-Temperature Regime*, arXiv:2605.10931](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.10931). Opened the abstract page only; no theorem from it is used here.
- Borjan Geshkovski, Cyril Letrouit, Yury Polyanskiy, Philippe Rigollet, [*A mathematical perspective on Transformers*, arXiv:2312.10794](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.10794). Opened the arXiv page and version history for the source supplied in the task.

PROOF.md gives a self-contained special case. Its independent-noise joint-scaling theorem was not identified in this short check, but publication priority is not asserted.
