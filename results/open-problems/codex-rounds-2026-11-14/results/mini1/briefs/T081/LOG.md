## 2. Mathematical result written

Created PROOF.md. The model has a hard core only within each cube and equal attraction `-j` within a cube and between face-neighboring cubes. A uniform occupation bound proves stability. Direct shift probabilities give the averaged hard-core pair potential as a sum of tent products.

For two copies of `{0, (3L/2)e1}` separated by `ML e2`, `M >= 3`, set `q = exp(beta*j)`. Their isolated averaged weights equal `(1+q)/2`, but their joint averaged weight is `(1+q^2)/2`. The excess over the product is exactly `(q-1)^2/4`, independent of `M`. Translating the second pair by `(L/2)e1` instead gives joint weight `q`. These are exact algebraic formulas, still to be checked by a separate interval-enumeration program.

The proof also checks open-neighborhood persistence (so the witness is not only a measure-zero configuration), and derives the extensive discrepancy from `n` distant congruent pairs in two ways. No phase-coexistence claim is made.
