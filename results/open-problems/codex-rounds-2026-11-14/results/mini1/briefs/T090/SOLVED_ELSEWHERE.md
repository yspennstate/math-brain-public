## Exact scope

Theorem 1.2 (PDF p. 6) treats Gaussian directed polymers on the square lattice with critical tuning

\[
 e^{\beta_N^2}-1=\frac{1}{R_N}\left(1+\frac{\vartheta}{\log N}\right),
 \qquad R_N=\sum_{n=1}^N\mathrm P(S_{2n}=0)
 =\frac{\log N}{\pi}+O(1),
\]

for fixed real \(\vartheta\). Centered log partition fields converge in law in every \(H^{-a}_{\mathrm{loc}}(\mathbb R^2)\), \(a>0\), jointly for finitely many positive times and bounded continuous initial conditions. Centered regularized logarithms of the critical SHF converge in probability to the same process.

Theorem 1.6 (PDF p. 8) states, for flat initial data,

\[
 t>0,\quad\vartheta\in\mathbb R,\quad
 0\ne\psi\in C_c^\infty(\mathbb R^2),\quad\psi\ge0
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 \mathscr H_t^\vartheta(\psi)\text{ is non-Gaussian}.
\]

## Boundary of the result

Here \(\beta_N\to0\). Neither the fixed-coupling growth exponent nor the roughness exponent in T090 follows. The theorem concerns random distributions, not point heights. Remark 1.3 (PDF p. 7) only proposes the noise-mollified KPZ extension and omits its adaptations.
