# Substantial advance elsewhere: theorem announcement

The full T133 statement is not established by the material checked here. A recent announcement substantially advances the status described in the brief.

Alexey Bufetov, *Limit shape for domain-wall six-vertex model*, [arXiv:2609.20576v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20576v1), submitted 17 September 2026, 11 pages. Checked 14 November 2026. [Full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.20576v1); [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.20576v1).

## Exact scope asserted

- Theorem 3.1: all positive weights with Delta < -1 and arbitrary r = a/b > 0. It specifies the liquid region between an outer curve and an inner antiferroelectric boundary, and the limiting height in the other regions.
- Theorem 3.2: all positive weights with -1 < Delta <= 1/2 and arbitrary r > 0.
- Section 1.3 defines the conclusion as uniform convergence in probability of the rescaled height function. Section 4.2 identifies the outer curves with the previously predicted curves and gives their envelope parameterization in equations (4.3)–(4.6).
- Remark 3.3 excludes 1/2 < Delta < 1 and Delta = -1 from these theorems.

## What is actually proved in the document inspected?

The abstract explicitly calls the note an announcement. Sections 1–4 define the formulas and state the theorems; Section 5 gives history and a short description of the proof strategy. A complete proof of Theorems 3.1–3.2 is not supplied there. No independent verification of those theorems is claimed here.

Consequently, record this as a substantial announced advance, rather than a complete verified solution. Also distinguish the asserted height limit from a stronger statement excluding every isolated nonfrozen defect outside the curve: uniform height convergence alone does not imply that exclusion.

## Correction to the supplied status

This announcement predates the brief's claimed November 2026 check, but postdates its cited arctic-curve theorem. The blanket description of all remaining parameters as open omits it. Locate a full proof or a later version before upgrading the announced parameter ranges to a checked theorem. The interval 1/2 < Delta < 1 remains outside this announcement.
