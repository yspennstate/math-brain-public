# Partial advances elsewhere; the whole problem is not settled by these results

Literature search: 14 November 2026, 17:24–17:32 UTC, under ten minutes. This file records substantial related results beyond the two references in the brief. No whole-problem solution was found. The session continued with the special case in PROOF.md.

Only papers whose arXiv pages or full papers were opened are listed. The descriptions below identify the stated results and their scope; the cited proofs were not independently audited in this session.

| Paper and opened source | What it establishes or states | What remains outside it |

| Zhengmian Hu, Heng Huang, *On the Random Conjugate Kernel and Neural Tangent Kernel*, ICML 2021. [PMLR paper](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v139/hu21b/hu21b.pdf). An arXiv identifier was not located in the allotted search. | Theorems 2–4 give initialization laws for the conjugate kernel and individual layer contributions to diagonal NTKs, including lognormal limits. | The paragraph before Corollary 2 explicitly leaves the joint correlations unresolved and bounds moments of the summed NTK. It does not give the full training-time process. |
| Mariia Seleznova, Gitta Kutyniok, *Neural Tangent Kernel Beyond the Infinite-Width Limit: Effects of Depth and Initialization*, [arXiv:2202.00553](https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.00553); [opened ICML paper](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v162/seleznova22a/seleznova22a.pdf). | Initialization dispersion formulas and phase-dependent conclusions, together with first-update analysis. | A closed joint-limit law of the whole NTK trajectory is not supplied. |
| Mufan Bill Li, Mihai Nica, Daniel M. Roy, *The Neural Covariance SDE: Shaped Infinite Depth-and-Width Networks at Initialization*, [arXiv:2206.02768](https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.02768). | An SDE for random activation covariance at initialization, with depth-dependent activation shaping. | This is a covariance result at initialization, rather than the trained NTK law for ordinary ReLU networks. |
| Blake Bordelon, Lorenzo Noci, Mufan Bill Li, Boris Hanin, Cengiz Pehlevan, *Depthwise Hyperparameter Transfer in Residual Networks: Dynamics and Scaling Limit*, [arXiv:2309.16620](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.16620). | A feature-learning description for residual branches scaled by inverse square root of depth, in combination with the muP parameterization. | Different architecture and training scaling from the plain-network question. |
| Zihan Yao, Ruoyu Wu, Tianxiang Gao, *Feature Learning Dynamics in Infinite-Depth Neural Networks*, [arXiv:2512.21075v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.21075v3); [opened full HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.21075v3). | The authors state conditional convergence to a forward–backward feature-dynamics system for one-layer residual blocks under depth-muP, subject to nondegeneracy assumptions. | It concerns residual networks with depth scaling; it does not settle the joint plain ReLU NTK problem in the brief. |
| Andrea Combette, Nelly Pustelnik, Antoine Venaille, *Correlation flow governs learning at criticality*, [arXiv:2608.08350](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.08350). | A relation between the NTK and output correlation in the sequential infinite-width then infinite-depth limit. | The sequential limit does not determine the stochastic proportional-depth limit. |

One correction to the starting summary is already present in the original source: Boris Hanin and Mihai Nica, [arXiv:1909.05989](https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.05989), analyze the mean first SGD update as well as initialization fluctuations. That remains weaker than a law for the training trajectory. Jiaoyang Huang and Horng-Tzer Yau, [arXiv:1909.08156](https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.08156), supply the hierarchy referred to in the brief.

No result here licenses identifying the distribution of a sum of layer kernels with a layer's marginal distribution, or inferring a stochastic training process from a second moment or a mean first update.
