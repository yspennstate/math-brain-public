# Substantial advance elsewhere; T075 is not solved

Samuel Kittle and Constantin Kogler, *On $L^p$ density of self-similar measures and Bernoulli convolutions*, [arXiv:2611.17277v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17277v1), submitted 12 November 2026.

[Theorem 1.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.17277v1) says that a self-similar probability measure generated by finitely many contracting similarities of $\mathbb R^d$ has a density in some $L^p$, $p>1$, whenever it is absolutely continuous. No algebraicity hypothesis is imposed.

For Bernoulli convolutions this identifies absolute continuity with existence of an $L^{1+\epsilon}$ density for some parameter-dependent $\epsilon>0$. It rules out an absolutely continuous Bernoulli convolution whose density fails every such higher-integrability condition. It does not rule out singular full-dimensional measures.

This is the substantial advance triggering step 1's stopping branch. The required filename must not be read as a claim that T075 was solved. The source is a new preprint; its correctness and any formalization have not been independently certified here. The paper's additional explicit continuity criteria concern algebraic parameters.
