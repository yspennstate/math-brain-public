# Literature check, 14 November 2026

The bounded web search did not locate a theorem resolving the stated nonlinear joint depth-width problem. It did locate joint-limit spectral theorems for linear products, so the broader claim that all joint spectral limits are open would be incorrect. No priority claim is made for the elementary finite-width calculation in PROOF.md.

1. Jeffrey Pennington, Samuel S. Schoenholz, Surya Ganguli, *Resurrecting the sigmoid in deep learning through dynamical isometry: theory and practice*, [arXiv:1711.04735](https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.04735). Opened the arXiv page and PDF. Section 2.5.2 predicts critical orthogonal hard-tanh squared-singular-value variance L(1-p)/p. At fixed p < 1 this grows with L; approaching empirical isometry as L grows requires tuning p towards 1. This distinction is necessary when stating an infinite-depth isometry claim.

2. Benoît Collins, Tomohiro Hayase, *Asymptotic Freeness of Layerwise Jacobians Caused by Invariance of Multilayer Perceptron: The Haar Orthogonal Case*, [arXiv:2103.13466](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.13466). Opened page and PDF (v4, 3 February 2022). The paper establishes the orthogonal freeness justification. Section 5.1 explicitly describes first taking the wide limit; Corollary 5.1 gives the layerwise S-transform formula. It does not provide a uniform growing-depth error estimate.

3. Leonid Pastur, *Eigenvalue Distribution of Large Random Matrices Arising in Deep Neural Networks: Orthogonal Case*, [arXiv:2201.04543](https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.04543). Opened arXiv page. Its abstract describes the justification for dependent derivative gates and Haar orthogonal weights in the infinite-width limit. This orthogonal paper is by Pastur alone.

4. L. Pastur, V. Slavin, *On Random Matrices Arising in Deep Neural Networks: General I.I.D. Case*, [arXiv:2011.11439](https://arxiv.org/abs/2011.11439). Opened arXiv page. The abstract extends the infinite-width justification from Gaussian to general i.i.d. entries with zero mean and finite fourth moment. Authors here are given exactly as on that arXiv page. No joint-depth theorem was identified from the abstract.

5. Boris Hanin, Mihai Nica, *Products of Many Large Random Matrices and Gradients in Deep Neural Networks*, [arXiv:1812.05994](https://arxiv.org/abs/1812.05994). Opened arXiv page. The abstract gives a quantitative joint-limit Gaussian theorem for the logarithm of the norm of a product applied to one fixed vector, with a ReLU-gradient application. This is not a full empirical spectral theorem for sigmoid networks.

6. Boris Hanin, Grigoris Paouris, *Non-asymptotic Results for Singular Values of Gaussian Matrix Products*, [arXiv:2005.08899](https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.08899). Opened page and PDF. Joint n,N growth is treated for independent Gaussian factors; the global empirical measure of s_i^(2/N) converges to the uniform distribution on [0,1]. This is a linear-product result on a depth-root scale.

7. Boris Hanin, Tianze Jiang, *Global Universality of Singular Values in Products of Many Large Random Matrices*, [arXiv:2503.07872](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.07872). Opened page and PDF. Theorem 1 treats independent n by n factors whose entries, multiplied by sqrt(n), are i.i.d. from a fixed mean-zero, unit-variance law with finite fourth moment and bounded density. It gives quantitative Kolmogorov distance control of the empirical measure of s_i^(2/N) to the uniform law, with n,N growing at any relative rate. This is substantial progress for linear i.i.d.-entry products, but does not cover D_l O_l or derivative-dependent gates, nor the unrooted spectrum near 1 in dynamical isometry.

8. Alex Massucco, Davide Murari, Carola-Bibiane Schönlieb, *Deep Network Trainability via Persistent Subspace Orthogonality*, [arXiv:2508.02882](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.02882). Opened current arXiv page (v2, 11 February 2026). The current title differs from the v1 title returned by initial search. The abstract describes designed network architectures with orthogonal Jacobians and a subspace relaxation. This is a different initialization/architecture problem.

9. Nathanaël Haas, François Gatine, Augustin M Cosse, Zied Bouraoui, *Why Deep Jacobian Spectra Separate: Depth-Induced Scaling and Singular-Vector Alignment*, [PMLR 306, 2026](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/haas26a.html). Opened the proceedings page and abstract; PDF fetches failed. The abstract treats a fixed-gates model, Lyapunov exponents, finite-depth corrections, and singular-vector alignment. It does not assert the joint nonlinear freeness/isometry theorem asked for here. No theorem-level conclusion about that paper beyond the abstract is claimed.
