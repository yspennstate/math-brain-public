# Substantial advance found; exact constant not determined

Viktor Harangi, *Improved Replica Bounds for the Independence Ratio of Random
Regular Graphs*, Journal of Statistical Physics **190**, article 60 (2023).
[arXiv:2204.01353v4](https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.01353v4).
[Published paper](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10955-022-03062-7).

Theorem 2.2 and Corollary 2.3 provide rigorous replica upper bounds. For the
same limiting constant as in T119, Tables 1 and 2 report

\[
\alpha_3\leq 0.45078521.
\]

Section 1.2 supplies explicit four-atom parameters giving
\(\alpha_3<0.450789952\). Even this weaker bound lies below the quoted
one-step value 0.45086, which cannot be the exact answer at degree 3.

This is a substantial improvement over the supplied upper bound, not a
determination of the constant. The stopping instruction applies to advances
as well as solutions; the required filename should be read accordingly.
