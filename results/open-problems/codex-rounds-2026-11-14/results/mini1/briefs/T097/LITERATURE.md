# Literature check, 14 November 2026

No resolution of the lattice critical-exponent problem was found in the
time-limited search. This is a search result, not a claim of exhaustive coverage.
Every reference below was opened on arXiv; full text was read where indicated.

1. Hugo Duminil-Copin, Subhajit Goswami, Pierre-François Rodriguez, Franco
   Severo, *Equality of critical parameters for percolation of Gaussian free
   field level-sets*, [arXiv:2002.07735](https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.07735).
   Opened abstract and PDF. This is the lattice sharpness result in the prompt.

2. Stephen Muirhead, *Percolation of strongly correlated Gaussian fields II.
   Sharpness of the phase transition*,
   [arXiv:2206.10724v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2206.10724v2), Ann. Probab. 52
   (2024), 838–881. Full HTML, Theorems 1.2 and 1.10, read. On Z^3, for every
   q > 4 there is c_q > 0 with theta(h_* - t) >= c_q t^q for small t > 0.
   The paper uses the increasing parameter ell = -h, so ell_c = -h_*.
   Consequently beta <= 4 if beta exists; existence remains open.

3. Vivek Dewan, Stephen Muirhead, *Upper bounds on the one-arm exponent for
   dependent percolation models*,
   [arXiv:2102.12123v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2102.12123v2).
   Full HTML opened. Its stated finite-range and rapid-decay Gaussian bounds
   are not being applied directly to the massless Z^3 GFF.

4. Zhenhao Cai, Jian Ding, *One-arm Probabilities for Metric Graph Gaussian
   Free Fields below and at the Critical Dimension*,
   [arXiv:2406.02397](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.02397).
   Abstract and PDF opened. The cable one-arm probability is of order
   R^(-(d-2)/2) for 3 <= d <= 5, as stated in its abstract's theorem sentence.
   One displayed conjecture in the abstract's web rendering drops the `+1` and
   must not be copied as the theorem.

5. Zhenhao Cai, Jian Ding, *Scaling Limit of Critical Loop Soup Clusters in
   Three, Four, and Five Dimensions*,
   [arXiv:2609.08702](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.08702).
   Abstract read: scaling limits for critical metric-graph clusters, not
   critical lattice excursion clusters.

6. Alexander Drewitz, Alexis Prévost, Pierre-François Rodriguez, *Fractal
   dimension of critical Gaussian free field sign clusters*,
   [arXiv:2610.03703](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03703).
   Abstract read: metric-graph clusters and their scaling limits.

7. Pierre-François Rodriguez, Jialu Shi, Wen Zhang, *On the one-arm probability
   for the metric graph Gaussian free field in high dimensions*,
   [arXiv:2611.05357](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05357).
   Abstract read: cable-system bounds on transient weighted graphs in the
   high-dimensional and borderline regimes. On Z^d these are d > 6 and d = 6.
   This November update does not settle the lattice problem in dimension three.

PROOF.md uses only item 2 as an external quantitative theorem. The finite-set
comparison and its conditional consequences are proved directly there.
