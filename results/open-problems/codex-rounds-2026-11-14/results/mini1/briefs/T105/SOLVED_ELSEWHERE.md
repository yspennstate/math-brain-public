# Substantial partial advance; T105 remains open

This file records the “substantially advanced” branch of instruction 1. Its filename does not mean that the stated flat 2-torus conjecture has been solved.

Wooyeon Kim, Jens Marklof, Matthew Welsh, *Values of ternary quadratic forms at integers and the Berry-Tabor conjecture for 3-tori*, [arXiv:2601.03209v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.03209v2), uploaded 5 November 2026; arXiv lists it as forthcoming in Inventiones Mathematicae.

[Theorem 1.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.03209v2) establishes Poisson pair correlation for the unfolded Dirichlet spectrum of a three-dimensional box when every ratio of squared edge lengths is κ-Diophantine, with κ sufficiently close to 1. Here κ-Diophantine means a lower bound |x−r/q| ≥ Cq^(−κ−1) for all integer r and positive integer q, with C>0 depending on x. Unfolding is ξ = c₃λ^(3/2), where c₃ = ℓ₁ℓ₂ℓ₃/(6π²). The paper gives the example (ℓ₁,ℓ₂,ℓ₃) = (1,2^(1/3),2^(−1/3)).

This is an omitted 2026 extension to individual three-dimensional boxes. It does not establish all correlations or nearest-neighbour spacing for a flat 2-torus. It predates the brief’s unspecified November check date; it is additional to the cited coverage, not established here to postdate that check.

The abstract, HTML theorem and PDF theorem agree on dimension, boundary conditions and statistic. This session did not independently audit the full proof.

Two corrections to the supplied background are supported by the opened [published triple-correlation paper](https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/42560), introduction §1.2:

- Its authors are Christoph Aistleitner, Valentin Blomer, Maksym Radziwiłł; the publication is *J. Eur. Math. Soc.* **26** (2024), 41–74, [arXiv:1809.07881](https://arxiv.org/abs/1809.07881).
- VanderKam’s higher-correlation results concern higher-dimensional tori. They should not be cited as almost-everywhere higher-correlation theorems for flat 2-tori.
