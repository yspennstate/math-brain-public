OpenAI's “Strict convexity and differentiability of the planar exponential first-passage limit shape” (September 24, 2026), Theorem 1.1, claims this special case; it does not claim all continuous laws or dimensions d >= 3.
The Gamma extension claims differentiability, not strict convexity; the other two candidates concern absence of bigeodesics and univalent-function integral means.
