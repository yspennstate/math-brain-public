# Literature check: partial advances, no verified solution of T087

Checked 14 November 2026, in a web-search pass lasting less than ten minutes. None of the opened sources settles existence and values of the critical exponents for nearest-neighbour bond percolation on Z^3. These 2026 developments concern other regimes; they do not by themselves supersede the supplied November 2026 status of T087.

- M. Cabezas, D. Croydon, A. Fribergh, N. Kawamoto, *Range convergence and sharp one-arm asymptotics for the critical percolation cluster in high dimensions*, **arXiv:2607.24561**, submitted 27 July 2026. The opened arXiv abstract states that in the high-dimensional regime they prove convergence to super-Brownian occupation measure and the sharp limit r^2 P(0↔∂B_r)→θ_1 in (0,∞). This refines one-arm asymptotics in that regime, not in dimension three. The precise high-dimensional model hypotheses were not inspected here. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24561

- Caio Alves, Rangel Baldasso, Carlos Gustavo Moreira, Augusto Teixeira, *Percolation on hierarchical lattices*, **arXiv:2606.11503**, submitted 9 June 2026. The opened abstract states existence of several exponents and scaling relations for hierarchical graph constructions under stated hypotheses. Those graphs are not the nearest-neighbour cubic lattice. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.11503

- Tom Hutchcroft, *Dimension dependence of critical phenomena in long-range percolation*, **arXiv:2510.03951**, ICM 2026 contribution. The full text describes nearest-neighbour d=3,4,5 hyperscaling results as conditional and surveys progress in long-range models. https://arxiv.org/html/2510.03951v1
