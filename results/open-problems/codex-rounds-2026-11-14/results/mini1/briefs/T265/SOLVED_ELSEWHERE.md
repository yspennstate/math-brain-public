# Literature check: the whole problem is not verified as settled

Checked 14 November 2026; web search completed in under five minutes.

- **Ichiro Hashimoto**, *Directional Convergence, Benign Overfitting of Gradient Descent in leaky ReLU two-layer Neural Networks*, [arXiv:2505.16204v3](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.16204v3), revised 10 February 2026, ICLR 2026. Theorem 4.8 gives a positive-margin limit with a linear decision boundary. Its model is `X=Y mu+Z`, without separate label flips. The probabilistic result's Assumption 6.1(a) requires `tr(Sigma)>=C n ||Sigma||_F`; (b) requires `tr(Sigma)>=C n ||Sigma||`. For nonzero covariance, `tr(Sigma)<=sqrt(d)||Sigma||_F` and `tr(Sigma)<=d||Sigma||` respectively imply `d>=C^2 n^2` and `d>=Cn`. Thus these sufficient conditions exclude `n/d -> infinity`. This does not delimit the deterministic theorem's full scope.

- **Kedar Karhadkar, Erin George, Michael Murray, Guido Montúfar, Deanna Needell**, *Benign overfitting in leaky ReLU networks with moderate input dimension*, [arXiv:2403.06903](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.06903), NeurIPS 2024. Gives hinge-loss GD results with `d=Omega(n)` for an orthogonal signal/noise model, improving older quadratic dimension requirements. It does not provide `n/d -> infinity`.

- **Junhyung Park, Patrick Bloebaum, Shiva Prasad Kasiviswanathan** (author names as on the arXiv page), *A Classical View on Benign Overfitting: The Role of Sample Size*, [arXiv:2505.11621v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.11621v1), 16 May 2025. Claims approximate regression interpolation and generalization for gradient flow. Its arXiv page says it subsumes their [arXiv:2410.06191](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.06191), *Benign Overfitting for Regression with Trained Two-Layer ReLU Networks*. A specific defect prevents treating this as verification of a solution: Lemma 17(2) claims `lambda_min(H)>=n/(5d)` although its unit-sphere kernel has diagonal `1/2`, hence `lambda_min(H)<=1/2`. These contradict when `n>5d/2`. Its proof also assigns a positive smallest eigenvalue to the rank-deficient `n x n` matrix `XX^T` when `n>d`. This diagnoses a local error; it does not show that every conclusion is false or unrepairable. The same issue occurs in the older paper's Appendix D.2.

- **Guy Kornowski, Gilad Yehudai, Ohad Shamir**, *From Tempered to Benign Overfitting in ReLU Neural Networks*, [arXiv:2305.15141v3](https://arxiv.org/html/2305.15141v3), revised 21 March 2024. Provides low-dimensional negative and high-dimensional positive results under implicit-bias assumptions. Its displayed Theorem 4.3 has a dimension requirement much larger than sample size, so does not resolve the target regime.

- **Moritz Haas, David Holzmüller, Ulrike von Luxburg, Ingo Steinwart**, *Mind the spikes: Benign overfitting of kernels and neural networks in fixed dimension*, [arXiv:2305.14077v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.14077), revised 6 November 2024. Establishes fixed-dimension obstructions and positive results using spiky kernels or modified activations. These do not give the requested GD result for ordinary ReLU networks in growing dimension with `n/d -> infinity`.

No verified later result settling the entire stated problem was found. This is a bounded search, not a completeness claim. The session's separate mathematical result is in PROOF.md.
