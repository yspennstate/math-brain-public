The filename follows the requested convention for a substantial related
advance. None of these results proves a fixed chaotic billiard's GOE spacing

Julien Moy, *Smooth spectral statistics of random perturbations of Schrödinger
operators near Anosov energy levels*, [arXiv:2610.09869](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09869),
submitted 7 October 2026. Theorem 1.4 of the
[opened full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.09869v1) gives Gaussian limits
for centered smooth counts, for perturbations in sets whose probability tends
to one. The scale is mesoscopic, with smoothing width at least of order
h/|log h| in the theorem. It does not reach the unit-mean-level-spacing scale
or fix one unperturbed operator.
