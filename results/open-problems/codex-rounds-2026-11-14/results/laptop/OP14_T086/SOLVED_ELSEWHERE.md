# T086: complete result located

Dmitriy Bilyk, Sebastian Neumayer, Tino Ullrich, *The small ball problem for the Brownian sheet: sharp bounds via entropy numbers in L_infinity*, arXiv:2611.09495v1 [math.FA], submitted 6 November 2026.

[arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09495v1) · [full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.09495v1) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.09495v1)

Theorem 1.1, equation (1.3), on PDF page 2, gives

\[
-\log\mathbb P\{\|B\|_\infty\leq\varepsilon\}
\asymp_d \varepsilon^{-2}\bigl(\log(1/\varepsilon)\bigr)^{2d-1},
\qquad d\geq2,\quad\varepsilon\downarrow0.
\]

Equations (1.1)–(1.2) define the supremum norm on the unit cube and the ordinary Brownian-sheet covariance. This covers the WHOLE problem T086, including every fixed d >= 3; it gives order up to constants, not an asymptotic constant.

Verification: the theorem was read in both the HTML and extracted PDF. Section 4 applies equation (4.1) with r = 1 and beta = d - 1/2, giving epsilon power 1/(1 - 1/2) = 2 and logarithmic power (d - 1/2)/(1 - 1/2) = 2d - 1.
