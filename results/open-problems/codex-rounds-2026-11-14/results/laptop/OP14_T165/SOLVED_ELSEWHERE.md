# Substantial advance elsewhere; the full problem remains open

Curtis Heyda, *Construction of positive recurrent semimartingale reflecting Brownian motions with divergent fluid paths using predator–prey models*, Queueing Systems **110**, article 48 (11 October 2026).

DOI and opened full text: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11134-026-10006-5

ArXiv identifier: none located; no identifier is asserted.

Theorem 2.2 gives a four-dimensional positive recurrent example with a divergent linear fluid path. Theorems 2.4–2.5 give five-dimensional examples, including an identity-covariance example. Section 5, Theorem 5.1, together with Theorem 2.3, gives fixed drift and reflection matrix for which changing a nonsingular covariance matrix changes positive recurrence. Thus dimension four already requires covariance-sensitive conditions.

This corrects the supplied status, although the paper predates its stated November 2026 check. It does not classify general completely-S reflection matrices in dimensions four and above. The present session therefore continues on a finite algebraic piece; no claim is made that the whole problem was solved elsewhere.

The other opened primary references were Maury Bramson, J. G. Dai and J. M. Harrison, *Positive recurrence of reflecting Brownian motion in three dimensions*, arXiv:1009.5746, https://arxiv.org/abs/1009.5746; and Maury Bramson, *A Positive Recurrent Reflecting Brownian Motion with Divergent Fluid Path*, arXiv:1010.1751, https://arxiv.org/abs/1010.1751.
