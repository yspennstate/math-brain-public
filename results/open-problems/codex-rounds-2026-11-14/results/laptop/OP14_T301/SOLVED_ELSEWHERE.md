# Substantial related advance; NOT a solution of the whole T301 specification

Reference: Itay Safran, *Every Layer Counts: An Exponential L2 Depth Hierarchy for ReLU Networks*, [arXiv:2608.23877v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23877v2), revised 30 August 2026. The [full paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.23877) was opened and Sections 3--5 checked.

Theorem 3.1 gives an exponential approximation hierarchy for adjacent fixed depths, including 4 versus 3, with polynomial target width and unrestricted shallow parameters. Its distribution is an engineered union of cubes at exponential distance from the origin. Theorem 4.1 gives an exact depth-4 versus depth-3 separation on the unit cube. Neither statement verifies the complete polynomial-weight, natural-distribution, constant-error claim requested here. Individual target parameter bounds were not checked.

This paper predates the supplied November 2026 status, so it corrects an incomplete baseline rather than constituting a post-baseline discovery. No qualifying later full solution was found in the bounded search. Work continued on the regular-distribution question; PROOF.md contains this session's separate checkable lemma.
