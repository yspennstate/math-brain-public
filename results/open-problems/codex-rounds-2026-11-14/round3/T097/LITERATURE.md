# Sources opened, 15 November 2026

The brief search found no resolution of the critical lattice exponent
problem after the earlier report. This is not an exhaustive literature claim.

1. Stephen Muirhead, *Percolation of strongly correlated Gaussian fields II.
   Sharpness of the phase transition*,
   [arXiv:2206.10724v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2206.10724v2).
   Full HTML opened; Theorems 1.2 and 1.10 checked. These supply the density
   and subcritical arm bounds stated in PROOF.md section 5.

2. Pierre-François Rodriguez, Jialu Shi, Wen Zhang, *On the one-arm
   probability for the metric graph Gaussian free field in high dimensions*,
   [arXiv:2611.05357](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05357).
   Abstract opened. It treats cable sign clusters in high dimensions and
   the borderline regime, rather than critical lattice excursions on Z^3.

3. Alexander Drewitz, Alexis Prévost, Pierre-François Rodriguez, *Fractal
   dimension of critical Gaussian free field sign clusters*,
   [arXiv:2610.03703](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03703).
   Abstract opened. It concerns metric-graph clusters and their scaling
   limits. It does not transfer those conclusions to the lattice critical
   level h_*>0.

Only item 1 is used as an external quantitative input. The capacity
optimization, rational estimates and crossover coefficient are derived
in PROOF.md; novelty relative to all published work is not established.
