# Literature check, 15 November 2026

The short update search found no whole-problem solution. It ran within the
first five minutes of the session. This is a limited search rather than a
claim that no such theorem exists. All pages cited here were opened through
the web tool in this session.

- Alireza Mousavi-Hosseini, Denny Wu, Murat A. Erdogdu,
  *Learning Multi-Index Models with Neural Networks via Mean-Field Langevin
  Dynamics*, [arXiv:2408.07254](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.07254).
  The arXiv page still lists v2, 27 March 2025. Its abstract describes
  polynomial convergence under assumptions on a compact positively curved
  parameter manifold; it does not assert the requested general Euclidean
  fixed-teacher result. This is the same version read by the earlier worker.

- Zong Shang, Tomoya Wakayama, Guillaume Lecué, Taiji Suzuki,
  *The Geometry of Statistical Feature Learning in Mean-Field Langevin
  Dynamics*, [arXiv:2606.31429](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.31429).
  The page still lists v2, 30 July 2026. Its abstract treats spherical
  stationary feature geometry and statistical recovery, rather than a new
  global polynomial low-temperature risk-time guarantee. This is also the
  same version as in the earlier report.

- Guillaume Wang, Lénaïc Chizat,
  *Local convergence of mean-field Langevin dynamics: from gradient flows
  to linearly monotone games*,
  [arXiv:2602.11999](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.11999).
  The opened abstract concerns sharp local rates under a stationary Poincare
  inequality. It does not settle the global small-temperature learning time.

- Jing An, Lihan Wang,
  *Local Algebraic Stability of Wasserstein Gradient Descent-Ascent and
  Mean Field Langevin Dynamics without Spectral Gap*,
  [arXiv:2610.35528](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35528).
  The opened abstract concerns local stability without a spectral gap.
  It does not give the global Gaussian-prior risk-time result requested here.

Only the arXiv abstract pages were opened in this update check; the present
proof does not invoke a theorem from any of these papers. No novelty claim
over the general functional-inequality literature is made.
