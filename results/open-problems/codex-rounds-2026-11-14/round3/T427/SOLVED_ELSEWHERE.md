# Existing preprint result rechecked

Yunzong Xu, *Random Matching Between Distinct Distributions in Two Dimensions*, [arXiv:2610.40509v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40509v1), 29 October 2026. Its arXiv record still lists only v1.

[Corollary 2](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.40509v1#S5.SS1) states, for uniform equal-mass semidiscrete matching on the unit square,

\[
n\mathbb E W_2^2(\mu_n,\lambda)
=\frac{\log n}{4\pi}+\beta_{\rm sd}+h_Q
+O(n^{-1/120}\log(en)).
\]

The universal equal-mass constant beta_sd is not evaluated. The earlier report already found this theorem. This session confirmed its normalization and evaluated the separate deterministic quantity h_Q in PROOF.md. The full preprint proof has not been independently certified.

No new literature resolution beyond the earlier report was found in the bounded search. In particular, Martin Huesmann and Arthur Wachtel, *Asymptotic transportation cost of the optimal matching subject to different boundary conditions*, [arXiv:2611.17066v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17066v1), concerns other thermodynamic asymptotics; its high-resolution theorem in the introduction assumes dimension at least three.
