## 5. Method #14 — find the group acting: exact equal-residue stabilizer quotient

Mark the two coordinates in the relation and partition the other twelve coordinates into equal-residue blocks modulo the current modulus `Q`, with multiplicities

\[
m_1,\ldots,m_s,
\qquad \sum_jm_j=12.
\]

The stabilizer

\[
G_w=S_{m_1}\times\cdots\times S_{m_s}
\]

acts on the relative lift fiber.

### Theorem R19-LR6

For a lift multiplier `c`, the number of `G_w`-orbits among the relation-compatible lifts is exactly

\[
\boxed{
 c\prod_{j=1}^s {m_j+c-1\choose c-1}.
}
\]

The raw relation-compatible fiber has size `c^13`.

### Proof mechanism

Write a lift as `w_i'=w_i+Qq_i`, with `q_i mod c`.  If

`w_2-a w_1=Qh`,

the lifted relation is

\[
q_2\equiv a q_1-h\pmod c.
\]

Thus the marked pair contributes `c` choices.  In an unmarked block of size `m_j`, an orbit under coordinate permutation is determined exactly by the histogram of the `c` lift digits.  There are `C(m_j+c-1,c-1)` such histograms.  Different current residue blocks cannot merge after lifting because equality modulo `cQ` would imply equality modulo `Q`.  Properness is coordinate-symmetric and the relation does not involve the unmarked labels.  Therefore one representative per displayed orbit is a complete search.

The artifact also gives the exact aggregate generating function for a level-one covering support of size `r`:

\[
\boxed{

=c[z^{12}](1-z)^{-2c}
\big((1-z)^{-c}-1\big)^{r-2}.

\]

This counts canonical multiplier-`c` branches across all multiplicity patterns over that fixed support.

The witness proof is exact: multiplication by a unit is compensated by multiplying the grid-time residue by the inverse unit.  The gcd clause is unchanged because the unit is coprime to the lift level.

### Theorem R19-LR9 — prime-lift gauge reduction

If `r` is a **first occurrence**, `r` does not divide `Q`.  By CRT, `K_{Q,r}` is `F_r^*`.  On the `r` lift digits of any chosen coordinate it has exactly two orbit types: the unique lift whose new coordinate is `0 mod r`, and all `r-1` nonzero lifts.  Thus a complete exact search may use **two gauge cases instead of `r` digit cases**, while applying the same unit to the full tuple.

If `r` is a **repeated occurrence**, `r|Q`.  For an improper current seed there is necessarily a coordinate not divisible by `r`; otherwise all but at most one coordinates would share divisor `r` with the lift level and the subset-gcd clause would already declare the seed proper.  For a chosen nondivisible coordinate, the units

\[
b_s=1+Qs,\qquad s\in\mathbb F_r,

act freely and transitively on its `r` lift digits.  Every orbit therefore has a unique representative with that coordinate's lift digit fixed to zero.  A repeated prime lift gets an **exact additional factor-`r` quotient**.

This reduction is separate from the relation's `r^14 -> r^13` saving and from equal-residue permutation stabilizers.
