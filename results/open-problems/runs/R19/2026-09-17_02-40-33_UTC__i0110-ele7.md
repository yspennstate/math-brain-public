## R19-I0110-C010 — proxy domination implies the frozen ELE extraction clause

Let `X_n` have `N_n` states. Let `pi_n,q_n` be probabilities and `s_n` a subprobability. Put

\[
\delta_n=\|q_n-\pi_n\|_{TV},
\qquad
\zeta_n=1-s_n(X_n).
\]

Assume

\[
\delta_n,\varepsilon_n,\zeta_n\to0
\]

and

\[
s_n(x)\le(1+\varepsilon_n)q_n(x)+\frac{\varepsilon_n}{N_n}.
\tag{5.1}
\]

Then there is `G_n subset X_n` such that `r_n=s_n 1_{G_n}` has mass `m_n->1` and satisfies the exact ELE defect (3.1).

### Proof

Take

\[
a_n=\sqrt{\delta_n}+N_n^{-1},
\qquad
b_n=\sqrt{\varepsilon_n}+N_n^{-1},
\]

and define

\[
B_n=\{x:|q_n(x)-\pi_n(x)|>a_n\pi_n(x)\},
\]

\[
C_n=\{x:\pi_n(x)<b_n/N_n\},
\qquad
G_n=X_n\setminus(B_n\cup C_n).
\]

Because `sum |q-pi|=2 delta`,

\[
\pi_n(B_n)\le\frac{2\delta_n}{a_n},
\qquad
q_n(B_n)\le\frac{2\delta_n}{a_n}+2\delta_n,
\]

hence by (5.1)

\[
s_n(B_n)=o(1).
\tag{5.2}
\]

Independently of any lower bound on individual stationary atoms,

\[
\pi_n(C_n)\le b_n,
\qquad
q_n(C_n)\le b_n+2\delta_n,
\]

so

\[
s_n(C_n)=o(1).
\tag{5.3}
\]

Thus

\[
m_n=r_n(X_n)\to1.
\tag{5.4}
\]

On `G_n`,

\[
q_n(x)\le(1+a_n)\pi_n(x),
\qquad
\frac{\varepsilon_n}{N_n}
\le\frac{\varepsilon_n}{b_n}\pi_n(x).
\]

Therefore

\[
r_n(x)\le(1+c_n)\pi_n(x),
\]

where

\[
c_n=(1+\varepsilon_n)(1+a_n)
 +\frac{\varepsilon_n}{b_n}-1\to0.
\tag{5.5}
\]

Writing `f_n=r_n/pi_n`, we have `0<=f_n<=1+c_n` and `E_pi f_n=m_n`. Hence

\[
\begin{aligned}
D_n
&=\mathbb E_{\pi_n}(f_n-m_n)^2\\
&=\mathbb E_{\pi_n}f_n^2-m_n^2\\
&\le(1+c_n)m_n-m_n^2\\
&=m_n(1+c_n-m_n)\\
&\le c_n+(1-m_n)\to0.
\end{aligned}
\]

`QED`
