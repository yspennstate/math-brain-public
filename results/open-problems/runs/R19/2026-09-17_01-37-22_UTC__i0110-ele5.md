# 3. Method #1 — CHANGE THE CATEGORY: from graph geometry to source coding + extraction

The preceding route kept trying to phrase the common mechanism as a geometric graph property: expansion, tree-likeness, local-product constants, or curvature. The nine source models defeat any literal version of those: `[21]` is nonreversible/directed, `[52]` has growing degree and many short commutator cycles, and `[13]/[62]` may have a decaying macroscopic bottleneck.

The change of category is to treat mixing as a **source-coding / extraction problem**. Before the entropic threshold, the walk is represented by an auxiliary path/lift law whose typical part has too few effective outputs to cover stationary mass. After the threshold, the same model-generated lift can be truncated to a high-mass typical part whose projected collision defect is small.

This suggests a graph/model property that does not mention total variation closeness in its hypotheses.

## Definition — Entropic Lift-Extraction (ELE)

For a deterministic sequence of finite Markov chains with stationary laws `pi_n`, fix the source-relevant initialization and write `p_{n,t}` for the law at time `t`. An **ELE certificate** consists of times

\[
t_n^-<t_n^+,
\qquad \frac{t_n^+}{t_n^-}\to1,
\tag{3.1}
\]

and, at each side of the window, a model-generated auxiliary law `q_n^\pm` on a space `Omega_n^\pm`, a structurally specified projection

\[
\Phi_n^\pm:\Omega_n^\pm\to X_n,
\]

and a typical set `E_n^\pm`. “Structurally specified” means generated by the model architecture—cover/local-limit exploration, quasi-tree, coordinate-count lattice, backbone/stopping construction, or another explicit lift—not selected retrospectively after inspecting total-variation distance.

The lower-side requirements are

\[
\|p_{n,t_n^-}-(\Phi_n^-)_\#q_n^-\|_{TV}\le\eta_n^-,
\tag{3.2}
\]

\[
q_n^-(E_n^-)\ge1-\varepsilon_n^-,
\qquad
\pi_n(\Phi_n^-(E_n^-))\le\varepsilon_n^-,
\tag{3.3}
\]

with `epsilon_n^-,eta_n^- -> 0`.

For the upper side, require

\[
\|p_{n,t_n^+}-(\Phi_n^+)_\#q_n^+\|_{TV}\le\eta_n^+,
\qquad
m_n:=q_n^+(E_n^+)\ge1-\varepsilon_n^+,
\tag{3.4}
\]

and define the retained pushed-forward subprobability

\[
r_n(x)=\sum_{\omega\in E_n^+:\Phi_n^+(\omega)=x}q_n^+(\omega).
\tag{3.5}
\]

The extraction requirement is

\[
\boxed{
D_n:=\sum_x\frac{(r_n(x)-m_n\pi_n(x))^2}{\pi_n(x)}\to0.
}
\tag{3.6}
\]

This deliberately uses **smooth/truncated collision** rather than raw H2. The previous R19 run proved that raw H2 can reach its threshold strictly later than Shannon entropy even in the binary Abelian test case; ELE charges the discarded atypical mass explicitly instead of letting it dominate the collision statistic.

# 4. R19-I0110-C008 — ELE deterministically triggers cutoff

**STATUS: PROVED.** This is the deterministic half required by Salez’s formulation, conditional only on the explicit ELE clauses above.

Let

\[
\widehat p_n^-=(\Phi_n^-)_\#q_n^-,
\qquad A_n=\Phi_n^-(E_n^-).
\]

Every retained lift point maps into `A_n`, so

\[
\widehat p_n^-(A_n)\ge1-\varepsilon_n^-.
\]

Using (3.2),

\[
p_{n,t_n^-}(A_n)\ge1-\varepsilon_n^--\eta_n^-.
\]

Together with (3.3),

\[
\boxed{
\|p_{n,t_n^-}-\pi_n\|_{TV}
\ge1-2\varepsilon_n^--\eta_n^-\to1.
}
\tag{4.1}
\]

For the upper side put

\[
\widehat p_n^+=(\Phi_n^+)_\#q_n^+,
\qquad s_n=\widehat p_n^+-r_n.
\]

Then `s_n` is a nonnegative subprobability of mass `1-m_n`, and

\[
\widehat p_n^+-\pi_n
=(r_n-m_n\pi_n)+(s_n-(1-m_n)\pi_n).
\tag{4.2}
\]

Two nonnegative measures with the same total mass `a` have TV distance at most `a`, hence

\[
\|s_n-(1-m_n)\pi_n\|_{TV}\le1-m_n\le\varepsilon_n^+.
\tag{4.3}
\]

Weighted Cauchy--Schwarz gives

\[
\begin{aligned}
\|r_n-m_n\pi_n\|_{TV}
&=\frac12\sum_x|r_n(x)-m_n\pi_n(x)|\\
&\le\frac12
\left(\sum_x\pi_n(x)\right)^{1/2}
\left(\sum_x\frac{(r_n(x)-m_n\pi_n(x))^2}{\pi_n(x)}\right)^{1/2}\\
&=\frac12\sqrt{D_n}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.4}
\]

Therefore

\[
\boxed{
\|p_{n,t_n^+}-\pi_n\|_{TV}
\le\eta_n^++\varepsilon_n^++\frac12\sqrt{D_n}\to0.
}
\tag{4.5}
\]

Equations (4.1), (4.5), monotonicity of TV distance under the semigroup, and `t_n^+/t_n^- -> 1` prove cutoff. `QED`

# 5. Method #10 — DERANDOMIZE: annealed extraction defects imply a quenched structural certificate

Salez’s source asks for a property that the random graphs satisfy asymptotically almost surely, then a deterministic implication. Several source proofs first establish L2/collision control averaged over the random environment. The following elementary step cleanly separates those quantifiers.

## R19-I0110-C009 — annealed-to-quenched ELE

Let `Z_n` be the random graph/environment and suppose the ELE data are measurable in `Z_n`. Define the nonnegative total defect

\[
S_n(Z_n)=
\varepsilon_n^-(Z_n)+\eta_n^-(Z_n)+
\varepsilon_n^+(Z_n)+\eta_n^+(Z_n)+D_n(Z_n).
\tag{5.1}
\]

Assume

\[
\mathbb E S_n\to0.
\tag{5.2}
\]

Let `a_n=E S_n` and, when `a_n>0`, set `delta_n=sqrt(a_n)` (take `delta_n=0` when `a_n=0`). Markov’s inequality gives

\[
\mathbb P(S_n>\delta_n)
\le\frac{a_n}{\delta_n}
=\sqrt{a_n}\to0.
\tag{5.3}
\]

On the complementary event each nonnegative ELE defect is at most `delta_n`, so C008 applies to that **realized deterministic chain**. Thus annealed vanishing of the aggregate defect yields the source-style high-probability deterministic certificate. `QED`

This is an asymptotically-high-probability conclusion. I do **not** silently promote it to pathwise eventual almost sure validity. If a source supplies summable failure probabilities, Borel--Cantelli gives that stronger form.
