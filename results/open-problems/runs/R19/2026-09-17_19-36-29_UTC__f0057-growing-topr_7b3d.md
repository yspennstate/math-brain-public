## 4. What failed in the preceding direct-conditioning route

The preceding R19 proof fixed a rare-cloud intensity `D`, proved the conditioned and iid clouds close in TV, then sent `D->infinity` after `n->infinity`. That is enough for every fixed finite vector but does not directly control a vector whose dimension grows with `n`.

Method #20 identified the exact obstruction. If a revealed cloud has `q` points and all relevant values are below the source cutoff `U_n`, then revealing the cloud can shift the remaining-sum target by order

\[
qU_n.
\]

The source local CLT still sees a central target whenever

\[
qU_n=o(\sqrt n).
\]

Thus the cloud does not need to stay bounded; it only needs to grow below this central-window scale.

## 5. New theorem: the number of jointly TV-coupled top ranks may diverge

The full proof is in

`artifacts/R19/2026-09-17_19-36-29_UTC__f0057_growing_topr_7b3d.md`

created at commit

`f6e728767d4d0cfa008bb2b29b90c04c6417cbdf`

and read back at that exact commit; blob

`f7fe3d99b7dbfa472d563521c1d64df8221e0129`.

Let `U_n` be the cutoff supplied by the source proof, so

\[
U_n=o(\sqrt n),\qquad
P(Y_{(1)}>U_n)=o(1),\qquad
nP(Z_{n,1}>U_n)=o(1).
\]

For **every** integer sequence `r_n` with

\[
r_n\to\infty,
\qquad
r_nU_n=o(\sqrt n),
\]

the new theorem proves

\[
\boxed{
 d_{TV}\!\left(
 (Y_{(1)},\ldots,Y_{(r_n)}),
 (Z_{n,(1)},\ldots,Z_{n,(r_n)})
 \right)\to0.
}
\]

The same result holds in the source's tuned `xi^(n)` regime. Since there the source gives `U_n=O(log n)`, this includes every

\[
r_n=o(\sqrt n/\log n).
\]

This is strictly stronger than the finite-dimensional joint statement: the dimension of the TV comparison itself diverges.

### Mechanism

Set `A_n=sqrt(n)/U_n`. From `r_n=o(A_n)` choose a growing cloud budget `D_n` with

\[
r_n=o(D_n),\qquad D_n=o(A_n).
\]

Define the adaptive tail threshold

\[
h_n=\min\{h:nP(Z_{n,1}\ge h)\le D_n\}
\]

and the cloud band `T_n=[h_n,U_n]`.

The complement law `p_n^c=p_n(. | Z\notin T_n)` removes probability at most `D_n/n`. Its mean changes by at most `O(D_nU_n/n)`, its second moment by `o(1)`, it remains uniformly square-integrable, and its lattice span is unchanged because `h_n->infinity` leaves a fixed finite span-generating subset of the source support untouched.

For every cloud configuration with at most `2D_n` points, the total revealed value is `o(sqrt n)`. Janson's triangular local CLT therefore gives uniformly

\[
\frac{P(S_n=m\mid\eta_n=\nu)}{P(S_n=m)}=1+o(1).
\]

A sequential contradiction argument supplies the required uniformity even though the cloud cardinality bound grows: any violating sequence would itself form a triangular array satisfying the same convergence, UI, span and `o(sqrt n)` centering hypotheses, contradicting Remark 13.2.

Bayes plus the iid binomial tail `P(|eta_n|>2D_n)=o(1)` then yields

\[
d_{TV}(L(\eta_n\mid S_n=m),L(\eta_n))\to0.
\]

Finally, minimality of `h_n` gives more than `D_n` expected observations in `[h_n-1,U_n]`. For the conditioned allocation, the already-source-proved uniform one- and two-coordinate local ratios imply

\[
E C_n^Y=(1+o(1))\Lambda_n,
\qquad
E(C_n^Y)_2=(1+o(1))\Lambda_n^2,
\]

with `Lambda_n>D_n-o(1)`. Hence `C_n^Y/Lambda_n -> 1` in probability. Because `r_n=o(D_n)`, both conditioned and iid samples have at least `r_n` observations at or above `h_n-1` w.h.p. The top `r_n` vector is therefore a deterministic function of the cloud, padding missing cloud positions with the single boundary value `h_n-1`; this handles arbitrary ties. TV contraction finishes the proof.
