## 3. Idea before code: keep the exact relation through every lift

The preceding R19 theorem used

`J_a(14,p) = J(14,p) intersect H_a(p)`

where `H_a(p)` is the level-one relation hyperplane. That still asks the lift engine to eliminate **all** improper lifts of a base point, including lifts that could never come from an actual counterexample satisfying the Beck--Everett integer relation.

An actual counterexample satisfies the relation as an equality in `Z`, hence its reductions satisfy it modulo **every** lifted modulus. The correct gate is therefore relative to the relation at all levels.

This changes the search dimension by one at every lift stage, not just at level one.

## 4. Relative eventual properness

Let `m=(m_1,...,m_k)` be primitive and nonzero and let `p` be prime. Put

`H_m(p)={v in (F_p^*)^k : m.v=0 mod p}`.

For `v in H_m(p)` and positive integer `l`, call a lift `w` modulo `lp` **m-compatible** if

1. `w=v mod p`, and
2. `m.w=0 mod lp`.

Call `v` **m-relatively eventually proper** if some `l` makes every m-compatible lift `(k,p,l)`-proper in the Sungkawichai--Trakulthongchai sense. Let `J_m^rel(k,p)` be the m-compatible residue classes that are not relatively eventually proper.

Since global eventual properness implies relative eventual properness,

`J_m^rel(k,p) subseteq J(k,p) intersect H_m(p)`.

Thus `J_m^rel(k,p)=empty` is a strictly weaker sufficient gate condition than the relation-sliced global gate used last run.

## 5. Lemma R19-LR3 — an actual counterexample cannot leave the relation-relative bad set

**Lemma.** Assume `k>=3` and `LRC(k-1)`. Let `u=(u_1,...,u_k)` be a primitive integer counterexample to `LRC(k)` satisfying the exact relation `m.u=0`. Let `p` divide none of the coordinates. Then

`u mod p in J_m^rel(k,p)`.

**Proof.** Suppose instead that `u mod p` is m-relatively eventually proper. Then for some level `l`, every m-compatible lift is proper. Reduce the actual integer vector `u` modulo `lp`. It is m-compatible because `m.u=0` as an integer equality.

If this lift is proper by the witness clause, the corresponding time in `(lp)^(-1) Z` is already a lonely witness for the actual tuple `u`, contradiction.

If it is proper by the gcd clause, then for some `i`

`d=gcd(l,u_1,...,omit u_i,...,u_k)>1`.

Primitivity implies `d` does not divide `u_i`. Put `g=gcd(d,u_i)` and `q=d/g>=2`. Apply `LRC(k-1)` to the remaining integer speeds `u_j/d`. Choose `s` with

`||s u_j/d|| >= 1/k`, `j!=i`.

For integer `r`, put `t=(s+r)/d`. The other `k-1` fractional parts are unchanged. As `r` varies modulo `d`, the exceptional coordinate traverses a translate of a `q`-point equally spaced grid. If `q=2`, one point has distance at least `1/4` from the nearest integer; if `q>=3`, one point has distance at least `1/3`. Since `1/4>=1/(k+1)` for `k>=3`, some `r` makes every coordinate at least `1/(k+1)` from the integers, again contradicting that `u` is a counterexample. QED.

## 6. Theorem R19-LR4 — relative relation gates suffice for the product contradiction

Let `A_14` be the 17,574 nontrivial primitive harmful absolute-coefficient shapes already certified in the preceding R19 run. Independent coordinate sign changes preserve lonely-runner distances, so a Beck--Everett relation may be written `a.u=0` with `a` one of these positive shapes without changing the absolute speed product.

**Theorem.** Assume `LRC(13)`. Suppose that for every `a in A_14` there is a finite set `P_a` of primes `p>31` satisfying

1. `J_a^rel(14,p)=empty` for every `p in P_a`, and
2. `product_{p in P_a} p >= B_14`, with

   `B_14=(105^13/14)^14=105^182/14^14`.

Then `LRC(14)` holds.

**Proof.** Suppose a primitive counterexample exists. Beck--Everett gives one shape `a in A_14`. Fix `p in P_a`. If `p` divided no coordinate of the counterexample, Lemma R19-LR3 would place its residue in `J_a^rel(14,p)`, contradicting emptiness. Therefore every `p in P_a` divides the absolute speed product, so

`|u_1...u_14| >= product_{p in P_a} p >= B_14`.

But the Sungkawichai--Trakulthongchai finite-checking theorem, with the now-proved `LRC(13)` as induction input, gives the strict upper bound `|u_1...u_14|<B_14` for a primitive counterexample. Contradiction. QED.

This improves the preceding R19 criterion without changing any source quantifier: a gate need eliminate only relation-preserving branches that an actual counterexample could occupy.

## 7. Proposition R19-LR5 — exact factor-l reduction at every lift level

**Proposition.** Let `m in Z^k` be primitive, let `v in H_m(p)`, and let `l>=1`. The number of m-compatible lifts of `v` from modulus `p` to modulus `lp` is exactly

`l^(k-1)`.

The unrestricted lift fiber has size `l^k`; hence enforcing the relation gives an exact factor-`l` reduction at every lift stage.

**Proof.** Choose integer representatives of `v`. Every lift is uniquely `w=v+p q` with `q in (Z/lZ)^k`. Since `m.v=0 mod p`, write `m.v=p c`. The lifted relation is

`m.q=-c mod l`.

The homomorphism

`phi_m:(Z/lZ)^k -> Z/lZ`, `q -> m.q`

is surjective because `gcd(m_1,...,m_k)=1`: Bezout gives an integer combination of the coefficients equal to one and hence a preimage of `1` modulo `l`. Thus `|ker phi_m|=l^(k-1)`, and every fiber has exactly that cardinality. QED.

For `k=14`, the relation-compatible raw lift fiber is therefore exactly `l^13` instead of `l^14`. In a factorized lift pipeline each binary stage saves another factor `2`, and a terminal factor-`7` stage saves another factor `7`. This is a much stronger computational gain than imposing the relation only at level one.

## 8. Method #8 — discretize the first three support-two gates

There are 106 primitive support-two harmful shapes `(a,b)` with `a+b` odd, `a+b<=31`, and `gcd(a,b)=1`.

For the relation `a u_i=b u_j`, fold independent signs and use the global unit action to normalize the first marked speed to folded class `1`. The second marked speed is forced to

`r=[a b^(-1)] in F_p^*/{+/-1}`.

Write `n=(p-1)/2`. Before any witness filtering, a relation-marked normalized tuple has twelve unmarked folded coordinates, giving at most

`C(n+11,12)`

multisets. The corresponding normalized unrestricted marked family has thirteen remaining coordinates, giving

`C(n+12,13)`.

Hence the exact pre-cover candidate ratio is

`C(n+12,13)/C(n+11,12)=(n+12)/13=(p+23)/26`.

So the raw signed-space factor `p-1` from the hyperplane is largely consumed by the unit/sign quotient at small primes.

For `p=37,41,43`, `floor((p-1)/15)=2`. Every folded bad-time set is a multiplicative translate of `{1,2}`. Therefore level-one improper supports become an exact finite edge-cover problem in the graph on `F_p^*/{+/-1}` with edges `{x,2x}`.

For folded speed class `v`, let `D_p(v)` be its bad-time set. Let `C_{p,r}(s)` count size-`s` supports containing `{1,r}` whose bad-time sets cover all folded times. If a covering support has size `s`, the twelve unmarked coordinates must use all `s-2` non-marked support classes at least once, while the two marked classes may absorb arbitrary extra multiplicity. Stars-and-bars gives exactly `C(13,s-1)` marked tuples over that support. Hence

`N_p(r)=sum_{s=2}^{14} C_{p,r}(s) C(13,s-1)`.
