## 5. Method #18 — relation hyperplanes instead of unrestricted residue space

For `k=14`, Beck--Everett's concrete bound gives a harmful integer relation `m.u=0` with `||m||_1<=31`.

Because `sum m_i` is odd, `||m||_1` is odd. Divide by the gcd of the coefficients; that gcd is necessarily odd, so harmful parity survives. LRC is invariant under coordinate permutation and under changing the sign of any individual speed. Thus, after applying the corresponding sign to the relation coefficient, a primitive harmful relation is represented by its positive absolute coefficients

`a=(a_1,...,a_s)`, `2<=s<=14`,

with odd sum at most 31 and gcd one.

So the relation **shapes** are exactly primitive integer partitions of odd integers at most 31 into 2 through 14 parts.

The exact checker counts `17,575` such primitive shapes if support one is allowed. The sole support-one shape cannot satisfy `a u_i=0` with a nonzero speed. Therefore the counterexample search reduces to

`17,574`

nontrivial relation shapes up to permutation and the independent speed-sign symmetry.

Exact support counts are

`2:106, 3:437, 4:994, 5:1623, 6:2067, 7:2276, 8:2212, 9:2016, 10:1715, 11:1424, 12:1129, 13:894, 14:681`.

For a fixed shape `a`, pad by zeros to length 14 and, for a prime `p>31`, define

`H_a(p) = {v in (F_p^*)^14 : a.v=0 mod p}`,

`J_a(14,p) = J(14,p) intersect H_a(p)`.

Since a nonzero coefficient of `a` is invertible modulo such `p`, arbitrary choices of thirteen coordinates determine the remaining coordinate uniquely. Hence

`|H_a(p)| <= (p-1)^13`,

whereas the unrestricted signed residue space has `(p-1)^14` points. A fixed relation slice therefore gives at least a factor-`p-1` reduction before the usual sign/unit/permutation optimizations.

## 6. Local theorem: one eventually proper residue already excludes a counterexample

### Lemma R19-LR1

Assume `LRC(k-1)` with `k>=3`. Let `u=(u_1,...,u_k)` be a primitive integer counterexample to `LRC(k)`. Let `p` be prime and suppose `p` divides no coordinate. Then the residue `v=u mod p` is not eventually `(k,p)`-proper.

### Proof

Suppose `v` is eventually proper. Then some level `l` has no improper lift of `v`. Reduce the actual integer tuple `u` modulo `lp`; this is one of the lifts.

If it is proper via the witness clause, some `t in (lp)^(-1)Z` satisfies

`||t u_i|| >= 1/(k+1)`

for all coordinates because congruence modulo `lp` preserves those grid-time fractional parts. This is already a contradiction.

Suppose instead the lift is proper by the gcd clause. Then for some `i`,

`d = gcd(l,u_1,...,omit u_i,...,u_k) > 1`.

The divisibility transfers from the residue lift to the actual integers since `d|l|lp`. Primitivity implies `d` does not divide `u_i`. Put `g=gcd(d,u_i)` and `q=d/g>=2`.

Apply `LRC(k-1)` to the other `k-1` integer speeds `u_j/d`. There is a real `s` with

`||s u_j/d|| >= 1/k`, `j!=i`.

Set `t=(s+r)/d` with integer `r`. The other coordinates retain the same fractional parts because `r u_j/d` is integral. As `r` varies modulo `d`, the exceptional coordinate runs through a translate of the `q` equally spaced points on the circle. If `q=2`, one of the pair has distance at least `1/4` from the nearest integer. If `q>=3`, one grid point lies within `1/(2q)` of `1/2`, giving distance at least `1/2-1/(2q)>=1/3`. Hence some `r` gives

`||t u_i|| >= 1/4 >= 1/(k+1)`.

At that same time all other coordinates have distance at least `1/k>1/(k+1)`. This is a lonely witness, contradiction. Therefore `v` cannot be eventually proper. QED.

This is the localized form of the mechanism behind the published global prime-divisibility lemma. It means a gate computation may legally ignore residue classes that cannot match the extra structure already forced on a counterexample.

## 7. Method #27 — swap the gate quantifiers

Let `A_14` denote the 17,574 nontrivial relation shapes above. For a shape `a`, allow its own set `P_a` of easy primes rather than insisting that one global prime set close every residue class.

### Theorem R19-LR2 — relation-indexed prime-product criterion

Assume `LRC(13)`. Suppose that for every `a in A_14` there exists a finite set `P_a` of primes `p>31` satisfying

1. `J_a(14,p)=empty` for every `p in P_a`;
2. `product_{p in P_a} p >= B_14`, where

`B_14 = (105^13/14)^14 = 105^182 / 14^14`.

Then `LRC(14)` holds.

### Proof

Assume a counterexample exists and divide its positive speeds by their common gcd. A counterexample cannot be rescued by this scaling, so take it primitive. Beck--Everett supplies a harmful relation of `l1` norm at most 31. Normalize its gcd, coordinate order, and coefficient signs as above. Equivalently, keep the original absolute speeds for every product estimate and use an independently sign-changed speed representative for the relation equation. This yields some fixed `a in A_14`.

Fix `p in P_a`. If `p` divided none of the original speeds, the sign-changed residue vector would lie in `H_a(p)`. By `J_a(14,p)=empty`, that residue is eventually proper. Lemma R19-LR1 then rules out the counterexample. Therefore **every** `p in P_a` divides the absolute speed product.

Consequently

`|u_1...u_14| >= product_{p in P_a} p >= B_14`.

But the finite-checking product bound of Sungkawichai--Trakulthongchai, with `LRC(13)` now supplied by Allikvere, gives for every primitive counterexample

`|u_1...u_14| < B_14`.

Contradiction. QED.

The standard gate criterion has the order

`exists P, for every p in P, close all residue classes`.

The new criterion has the weaker and computationally more flexible order

`for every short relation shape a, exists P_a, for every p in P_a, close only the a-hyperplane slice`.

An exact integer comparison shows that if one naively used the first primes above 31 for every shape, the first 140 such primes, ending at 877, cross `B_14`; the first 139 do not. That is only a scale estimate. The point is to pick `P_a` adaptively and abandon primes that are difficult for a specific relation shape.
