## 4. Why deleting these words is rigorous

Let a saturated canonical collation of a physical string pair have order

\[
\ell=\operatorname{LCS}(P).
\]

Suppose a contiguous block of `r` designated minimal-match factors generated a physical substring pair with LCS at least `r+1`. Replace those `r` designated matches by the longer local common subsequence while retaining every designated match before and after the block. Because the block is contiguous in the collation, the surrounding match order is preserved. The result is a common subsequence of the full pair of length at least `\ell+1`, contradiction.

Therefore no saturated covering LCS collation can contain any locally deficient block. The new `507,296` six-factor words are actual physical words with exact offsets `|l|<=4`; no tail truncation is involved in their deficiency claim. The inherited length-`<=5` component uses the previously proved cap theorem. Deleting the union therefore preserves at least one covering collation for every physical pair.
