### Family A: unit zigzags

\[
\boxed{(c,s),(c,-s),(\bar c,-s).}
\tag{C035.1}

## 4. #19 — make the obstruction into a 17-state exact DFA

The new language can be recognized by 17 nonrejecting memory states:

- `N`: neutral;
- four `H(c,s)` states: the previous label has terminal bit `c`, sign `s`, and `|l|>=2`;
