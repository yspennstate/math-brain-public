# 6. Method 5 — ADD A PARAMETER AND DIFFERENTIATE: replace z-grid search by Perron sensitivity

The direct metric movement above comes from a better exact cone certificate near `z=0.32089625`. The more useful new route is to stop treating `z` as a blind scalar grid.

Write, for a fixed history operator,
\[
F(z)=\frac{2a(z)}{b(z)},\qquad
a(z)=-\log(2z),\qquad
b(z)=-\log\rho(z),
\]
where `rho(z)=rho(A_8(z))`.
On an interval where a single irreducible block supplies a simple Perron root, the Perron sensitivity formula is
\[
\rho'(z)=\frac{w^T A_8'(z)v}{w^Tv}
\]
for positive left/right Perron vectors. Differentiating `F` gives
\[
F'(z)=\frac{2}{b(z)^2}
\left[-\frac{b(z)}z+a(z)\frac{\rho'(z)}{\rho(z)}\right].
\]
Thus an interior stationary point must satisfy the dimensionless elasticity identity
\[
\boxed{
 z\frac{\rho'(z)}{\rho(z)}
 =\frac{b(z)}{a(z)}
 =\frac{2}{F(z)}.
}
\tag{6.1}
\]
At the present bound the right side is approximately `2.42048940835`.

This has a useful operator interpretation. For non-boundary transition length `|l|=r<7`, the weight is `z^{r+2}`, so its logarithmic elasticity is exactly `r+2`. The history-boundary tail has weight `z^9/(1-z)`, with elasticity
\[
9+\frac{z}{1-z}.
\]
Under the Perron edge measure, `z rho'(z)/rho(z)` is the corresponding weighted average of these local elasticities. Therefore the true z-optimization can be reframed as balancing one exact edge-statistic against `2/F`, rather than searching decimal z values.
