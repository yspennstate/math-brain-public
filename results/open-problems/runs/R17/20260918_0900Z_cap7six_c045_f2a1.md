## 4. Why deleting the complete local language is rigorous

Let a saturated collation of a physical pair have order
\[
\ell=\operatorname{LCS}(P).
\]
Suppose a contiguous block of `r` designated minimal-match factors generates a local pair with a common subsequence of length at least `r+1`. Replace the `r` designated matches inside that block by the longer local common subsequence and retain every designated match before and after the block. Contiguity preserves the surrounding order, yielding a common subsequence of the full pair of length at least `\ell+1`, contradiction.

Hence no saturated covering LCS collation can contain a locally deficient factor block. Removing every minimal deficient block of length at most six therefore preserves at least one covering collation for every physical pair.

For the tail symbols, the cap lemma supplies the finite reduction. A length-`r+1` local witness uses at most `r+1` symbols from any one homogeneous run. If a run is longer, move the witness positions monotonically into the first `r+1` positions of that identical-symbol run. Thus truncating every longer run to `r+1` preserves deficiency; conversely, every truncated pair is a subsequence of the original pair. For `r=6`, cap `7` is exact. This is why the full 30-symbol dictionary above covers the canonical `\pm7` geometric tails without replacing the true dependent-string model.
