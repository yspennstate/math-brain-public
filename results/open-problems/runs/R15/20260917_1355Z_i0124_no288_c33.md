## 3. Exact incidence moments for a hypothetical 288-cap

Assume for contradiction that \(C\subset AG(7,3)\) is a 288-cap.

There are

\[
\frac{3^7-1}{3-1}=1093
\]

hyperplane directions, hence 3279 affine hyperplanes. Write \(h_K=|C\cap K|\).

A point lies in 1093 affine hyperplanes, a pair in 364, and—because a cap has no collinear triple—every triple of cap points is affinely independent and lies in 121 affine hyperplanes. Therefore

\[
\sum_K h_K=1093\cdot288=314784,
\]

\[
\sum_K\binom{h_K}{2}
=364\binom{288}{2}=15043392,
\]

\[
\sum_K\binom{h_K}{3}
=121\binom{288}{3}=476732256.
\]

Centering at the directional mean 96, put \(x_K=h_K-96\). The exact centered moments are

\[
D_2:=\sum_Kx_K^2=182304,
\qquad
D_3:=\sum_Kx_K^3=-2584224.
\]

These agree with the preceding R15 calculation.

## 9. One anchor supports at most 15 large slices

Fix an anchor \(m\). Let \(S_m\subset PG(6,3)\) be the set of directions \([v]\) for which both \(m+v\) and \(m-v\) lie in \(C\).

Then \(S_m\) is a projective cap: if three of its directions were projectively collinear, signs could be chosen so that the corresponding three affine cap points sum to \(3m=0\), giving a forbidden collinear triple in \(C\).

The published projective-cap bound is

\[
|S_m|\le136
\]

for caps in \(PG(6,3)\). Also the exact maximum in \(PG(4,3)\) is 20.

Suppose \(s\) distinct 111/112 hyperplane slices have the same anchor \(m\). Each contributes at least 55 points of \(S_m\) lying in a projective hyperplane of \(PG(6,3)\). Two distinct such projective hyperplanes intersect in \(PG(4,3)\), so the corresponding two 55-point subsets intersect in at most 20 points.

If \(n_x\) is the number of those \(s\) subsets containing \(x\in S_m\), then

\[
\sum_x n_x\ge55s,
\]

and

\[
\sum_x\binom{n_x}{2}
\le20\binom{s}{2}.
\]

Cauchy gives

\[
\frac{(55s)^2}{|S_m|}
\le
\sum_xn_x^2
=
\sum_xn_x+2\sum_x\binom{n_x}{2}
\le
55s+40\binom{s}{2}.
\]

Using \(|S_m|\le136\),

\[
|S_m|
\ge
\frac{55^2s}{55+20(s-1)}.
\]

For \(s=16\) the right-hand side exceeds 136 (the exact integer lower bound is 137). Thus

\[
\boxed{s\le15.}
