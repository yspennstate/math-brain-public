## 5. Method #14 — FIND THE GROUP ACTING: Towsner identifies the exact quotient issue

The decisive current-scope complication is Henry Towsner, *Limits of Sequences of Markov Chains*, arXiv:1404.3815v3 / EJP 20 (2015).

Towsner studies essentially the same density-array invariant for pseudofinite Markov chains. His paper explicitly states that **an arbitrary density array does not correspond to a unique arbitrary pseudofinite Markov chain**. He introduces the quotient/refinement by *types*, with two extra properties:

- `twin-free`: at most one point realizes each type;
- `saturated`: every wide type is realized.

Lemma 7.3 constructs from any pseudofinite chain a twin-free saturated quotient via a measure-preserving map preserving all transition densities. Theorem 7.5 then proves that two twin-free saturated pseudofinite Markov chains with the same density array are measure-preservingly isomorphic (up to null sets, with the minimal sigma-algebras).

This is exactly the hidden group/quotient structure the literal source statement leaves implicit: the array invariant naturally identifies a canonical **type quotient**, not necessarily every unreduced presentation.

Primary current paper: `https://arxiv.org/pdf/1404.3815`, especially Lemma 7.3 and Theorem 7.5.

Outcome: I0074 cannot honestly be marked simply OPEN from the old note, nor simply KNOWN from Towsner, without reconciling Aldous's unspecified `t downarrow 0` condition and his literal bijective process-isomorphism convention with Towsner's twin-free/saturated pseudofinite quotient class.
