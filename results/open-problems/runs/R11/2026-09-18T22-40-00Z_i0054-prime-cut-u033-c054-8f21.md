## 2. Move #12 — the conserved object is the exact pair-normalized split measure

The previous runs produced many model-specific routes. The common object is simpler.

For each `N`, let `S_N` be a label-exchangeable random subset of `[N]`, with
\[
\pi_N(m)=\Pr(|S_N|=m),\qquad 1\le m\le N-1.
\tag{2.1}
\]
Conditional on `|S_N|=m`, exchangeability makes `S_N` uniform among the `m`-subsets.

Use `S_N` as a binary cut: a current block `C` is replaced by
`C cap S_N` and `C minus S_N` when both visible sides are nonempty. For a specified visible split `A | A^c` with `|A|=i`, `|A^c|=j`, its one-proposal probability is exactly
\[
P_N(i,j)
=\sum_{m=1}^{N-1}\pi_N(m)
\frac{(m)_{\underline i}(N-m)_{\underline j}
      +(m)_{\underline j}(N-m)_{\underline i}}
     {(N)_{\underline{i+j}}}.
\tag{2.2}
\]

Normalize by the probability that two specified labels separate:
\[
\lambda_N:=P_N(1,1)^{-1}.
\tag{2.3}
\]
Since
\[
P_N(1,1)
=2\sum_m\pi_N(m)\frac{m(N-m)}{N(N-1)},
\]
define the symmetric probability measure
\[
\boxed{
\rho_N
=\lambda_N\sum_{m=1}^{N-1}\pi_N(m)
  \frac{m(N-m)}{N(N-1)}
  \bigl(\delta_{m/N}+\delta_{1-m/N}\bigr).
}
\tag{2.4}
\]
Its total mass is exactly one, not asymptotically one.

This is the invariant that survives all model-specific descriptions: it is the pair-normalized symmetrized law of the cut proportion, weighted by the chance that the cut actually separates a pair.
