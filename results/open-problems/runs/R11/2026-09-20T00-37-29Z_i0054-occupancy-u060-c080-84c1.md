# 4. General fresh exchangeable-partition proposal model

For each `N`, let `Xi_N` be an exchangeable random partition of `[N]`. A **fresh independent copy** is sampled at every proposal event on every active clade. Put

\[
\boxed{
 c_N:=\Pr(1\sim_{\Xi_N}2).
}
\tag{4.1}
\]

At harmonic-grid mark

\[
x_s=\frac{s}{N},\qquad s=1,\dots,N-1,
\]

each nonsingleton clade has an independent proposal clock of rate

\[
\boxed{
\lambda_{N,s}
=\frac{N}{2s(N-s)}
=\frac{1}{2N x_s(1-x_s)}.
}
\tag{4.2}
\]

When the clock rings:

1. sample a fresh copy of `Xi_N`;
2. independently color every `Xi_N` block red with probability `x_s` and blue otherwise;
3. restrict those colors to the labels currently in the active clade;
4. if both colors occur, split the clade into the two color classes; otherwise do nothing.

Every child receives fresh independent clocks and future partition/color samples.

The total proposal rate of a clade is exactly

\[
\sum_{s=1}^{N-1}\lambda_{N,s}=H_{N-1}.
\tag{4.3}
\]

# 5. U060 — sharp pair-collision criterion and quantitative growing-`k` bound

## Theorem U060

For the fresh exchangeable-partition harmonic-grid hierarchy above, the following are equivalent as `N->infinity`:

1. every fixed finite visible restriction has generator converging to the CTCS generator;
2. the two-label restriction converges to CTCS;
3. `c_N -> 0`.

More quantitatively, for a specified unordered nontrivial split `A|A^c` of an `m`-label visible block,

\[
\boxed{
\left|q_N^{\Xi}(A\mid A^c)-q_N^{\mathrm{iid}}(A\mid A^c)\right|
\le
\binom m2 c_N\frac{N-1}{N}.
}
\tag{5.1}
\]

For two labels there is the exact identity

\[
\boxed{
q_N^{\Xi}(1\mid1)
=
\frac{N-1}{N}(1-c_N).
}
\tag{5.2}
\]

If `|A|=i`, `|A^c|=j`, `i+j=m`, then the iid harmonic-grid comparator obeys

\[
\boxed{
\left|q_N^{\mathrm{iid}}(i,j)-B(i,j)\right|
\le \frac{m-1}{N}.
}
\tag{5.3}
\]

Hence, uniformly over every specified split on every block of size at most `k`,

\[
\boxed{
\delta_{N,k}
:=
\max_{m\le k}\max_{A}
\left|q_N^{\Xi}(A\mid A^c)-B(|A|,m-|A|)\right|
\le
\binom k2 c_N+\frac{k-1}{N}.
}
\tag{5.4}
\]

For the complete restriction to `[k]`, on every finite horizon `[0,T]`,

\[
\boxed{
\left\|
\mathcal L(\Pi_N^{[k]}|_{[0,T]})
-
\mathcal L(\Pi_{\rm CTCS}^{[k]}|_{[0,T]})
\right\|_{\rm TV}
\le
\min\left\{1,
T(2^{k-1}-1)\delta_{N,k}
\right\}.
}
\tag{5.5}
\]

In particular, if `k=k_N` grows with `N` and

\[
2^{k_N}\left(k_N^2c_N+\frac{k_N}{N}\right)\to0,
\tag{5.6}
\]

then the **whole `[k_N]` partition path** converges in total variation on every finite time horizon to the corresponding CTCS restriction.

### Proof of (5.1)

Fix a visible set `V` of size `m` and a specified split `A|A^c`. Let `C` be the event that at least one pair of visible labels lies in the same block of the fresh `Xi_N` sample. Exchangeability and the union bound give

\[
\Pr(C)
\le
\binom m2 c_N.
\tag{5.7}
\]

Conditional on `C^c`, all visible labels lie in distinct `Xi_N` blocks, and those blocks receive independent Bernoulli(`x`) colors. Therefore the visible color vector is **exactly iid Bernoulli(`x`)** on `C^c`.

On `C`, a specified nontrivial support can occur only if no `Xi_N` block crosses the prescribed split boundary and at least one dependence block lies on each side. If the split side `A` occupies `r_A>=1` dependence blocks and `A^c` occupies `r_B>=1`, then conditional on that partition the unordered split probability is

\[
x^{r_A}(1-x)^{r_B}+(1-x)^{r_A}x^{r_B}
\le 2x(1-x).
\tag{5.8}
\]

The iid specified-split probability is itself at most `2x(1-x)`. Since the partition collision event is independent of the future block colors,

\[
\left|p_N^{\Xi}(A\mid A^c;x)-p_N^{\mathrm{iid}}(A\mid A^c;x)\right|
\le
2x(1-x)\Pr(C).
\tag{5.9}
\]

Multiplying by (4.2) cancels the singular beta-grid factor:

\[
\lambda_{N,s}\,2x_s(1-x_s)=\frac1N.
\]

Summing over the `N-1` marks and using (5.7) proves (5.1).

### Proof of exact pair identity (5.2)

Two visible labels are separated at a mark `x` exactly when they occupy distinct `Xi_N` blocks and the two blocks receive opposite colors. Thus

\[
\Pr(\text{pair separates at }x)
=(1-c_N)2x(1-x).
\]

Multiplying by the clock and summing gives

\[
q_N^{\Xi}(1\mid1)
=(1-c_N)\sum_{s=1}^{N-1}\frac1N
=rac{N-1}{N}(1-c_N).
\]

CTCS has pair rate `B(1,1)=1`; therefore pair convergence forces `c_N->0`. Together with (5.1) and the iid comparator limit below, this proves the equivalence of (1)--(3).

### Proof of the iid Riemann bound (5.3)

For `i,j>=1`, `m=i+j`, symmetry of the harmonic grid gives

\[
q_N^{\mathrm{iid}}(i,j)
=
\frac1N\sum_{s=1}^{N-1}
\left(\frac{s}{N}\right)^{i-1}
\left(1-\frac{s}{N}\right)^{j-1}.
\tag{5.10}
\]

Let

\[
f(x)=x^{i-1}(1-x)^{j-1}.
\]

Since

\[
|f'(x)|
\le(i-1)+(j-1)=m-2
\qquad(0\le x\le1),
\]

the left Riemann sum including `s=0` differs from `int_0^1 f(x)dx` by at most `(m-2)/N`. Removing the `s=0` term costs at most `1/N`. Hence

\[
\left|q_N^{\mathrm{iid}}(i,j)-\int_0^1x^{i-1}(1-x)^{j-1}dx\right|
\le\frac{m-1}{N},
\]

and the integral is

\[
B(i,j)=\frac{(i-1)!(j-1)!}{(i+j-1)!}.
\]

This proves (5.3) and then (5.4).

### Proof of path-TV bound (5.5)

The projection to `[k]` is itself a finite-state CTMC. Indeed, every visible block lies inside one full clade; a proposal redraws a fresh partition and colors independently of the hidden labels and past proposals. A hidden-only split can move the visible carrier to a child but cannot change its future visible hazard law. Thus the visible process is an exact lumping.

A block of size `b` has `2^{b-1}-1` possible specified unordered nontrivial binary splits. For any partition of `[k]`, the sum of this quantity over its current blocks is at most

\[
2^{k-1}-1.
\tag{5.11}
\]

Couple the two finite CTMCs by common clocks of rate `min(q_N,q)` for each visible transition and independent residual clocks of rate `|q_N-q|`. Their first disagreement rate in any state is therefore at most

\[
(2^{k-1}-1)\delta_{N,k}.
\]

Hence over `[0,T]`

\[
P(\text{disagreement by }T)
\le
1-e^{-T(2^{k-1}-1)\delta_{N,k}}
\le
T(2^{k-1}-1)\delta_{N,k},
\]

which proves (5.5). Fixed-`k` whole-path TV convergence follows immediately when `c_N->0`. `J_1` convergence follows a fortiori, and consistency of the restrictions yields the summable projective CTCS(infinity) limit.

This completes U060. `square`

# 6. C080 — iid occupancy boxes

For each `N>=2`, set

\[
\boxed{
M_N:=\max\{2,\lfloor\sqrt N\rfloor\}.
}
\tag{6.1}
\]

At every proposal on every active clade:

1. each label in the clade independently chooses one of the `M_N` boxes uniformly;
2. each box is independently colored red with probability `x_s=s/N`, blue otherwise;
3. every label inherits the color of its chosen box;
4. if both colors occur, split the clade into the two color classes.

Assignments and colors are redrawn independently at every proposal and in every descendant clade.

This induces a fresh exchangeable occupancy partition. Two labels collide exactly when they choose the same box, so

\[
\boxed{c_N=\frac1{M_N}.}
\tag{6.2}
\]

Hence `c_N->0`, and U060 gives the full fixed-restriction CTCS(infinity) limit.

The finite hierarchy is nondegenerate. For `M_N>=2`, every distinct pair has positive separation hazard: at every interior mark, they choose distinct boxes with probability `1-1/M_N` and those boxes have opposite colors with probability `2x(1-x)`. Every nonsingleton clade therefore has positive exit rate. Each proper split increases the number of clades; after at most `N-1` proper splits the singleton partition is reached almost surely. Thus C080 defines a genuine finite rooted binary hierarchy with continuous edge lengths.

# 7. Move #25 in exact form — the occupancy split law counted twice

Fix `M`, mark `x`, and a visible specified unordered split with side sizes `i,j`.

Let `R` be the number of red boxes. Since box colors are iid Bernoulli(`x`),

\[
R\sim\operatorname{Bin}(M,x).
\]

Conditional on the entire box-color vector, each label independently chooses a uniform box. Thus each label is independently red with conditional probability

\[
P=\frac RM.
\]

Therefore the exact specified split probability is

\[
\boxed{
 p_M(i,j;x)
=
E\left[P^i(1-P)^j+P^j(1-P)^i\right]
}
\tag{7.1}
\]

or explicitly

\[
\boxed{
 p_M(i,j;x)
=
\sum_{r=0}^M
\binom Mr x^r(1-x)^{M-r}
\left[
\left(\frac rM\right)^i
\left(1-\frac rM\right)^j
+
\left(\frac rM\right)^j
\left(1-\frac rM\right)^i
\right].
}
\tag{7.2}
\]

Direct enumeration of all label-to-box assignments and all box-color vectors gives the same rational quantity. The exact checker verifies this equality exhaustively for small `M,N,m`.

For `i=j=1`, (7.1) gives

\[
2E[P(1-P)]
=2x(1-x)\left(1-\frac1M\right),
\]

which independently reproduces the pair formula (5.2) with `c_N=1/M`.
