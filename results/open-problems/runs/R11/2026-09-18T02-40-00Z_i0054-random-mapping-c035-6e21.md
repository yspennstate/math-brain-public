## 3. Move #1 — change category: permutations to random mappings

Let `f:[N]->[N]` be a uniformly random mapping. Its functional digraph has weakly connected components, each consisting of one directed cycle with rooted in-trees attached.

Let `c_m` be the number of connected mappings on a fixed `m`-set. Counting first by directed cycle length `r` gives

\[
c_m=\sum_{r=1}^{m-1}\frac{m!}{(m-r)!}m^{m-r-1}+(m-1)!.
\tag{3.1}
\]

Equivalently, if `T(z)` is the rooted labeled-tree EGF,

\[
T=ze^T,
\tag{3.2}
\]

then a connected mapping is a directed cycle of rooted trees, so

\[
\boxed{C(z):=\sum_{m\ge1}c_m\frac{z^m}{m!}=-\log(1-T(z)).}
\tag{3.3}
\]

Mappings are sets of connected components, hence mappings with exactly three components have EGF `C(z)^3/3!`. Denote their exact count by

\[
A_{N,3}=N![z^N]\frac{C(z)^3}{6}.
\tag{3.4}
\]

C035 will repeatedly sample a uniform mapping conditioned on exactly three weak components.

## 4. Move #25 — exact visible-partition count by the exponential formula

Fix `b` distinguished visible labels and a **specified** visible set partition

\[
\pi=\{B_1,\dots,B_q\},\qquad |B_a|=n_a,\quad q\le3,\quad \sum_an_a=b.
\]

The number of ambient `N`-mappings with exactly three components whose component partition restricted to these visible labels is exactly `pi` is

\[
\boxed{
A_N(\pi)
=(N-b)![z^{N-b}]
\left(\prod_{a=1}^q C^{(n_a)}(z)\right)
\frac{C(z)^{3-q}}{(3-q)!}.
}
\tag{4.1}
\]

**Proof.** A connected component that is required to contain a prescribed block of `n_a` distinguished labels is marked by `C^{(n_a)}`: its coefficient of `z^r/r!` counts connected mappings on those `n_a` fixed labels plus `r` ordinary labels. Distinct visible blocks in `pi` must lie in distinct connected components, so multiply these `q` marked connected-component classes. The remaining `3-q` components contain no visible label and form an unordered SET, contributing `C^{3-q}/(3-q)!`. Distributing the `N-b` remaining labels through the labeled product multiplies the coefficient by `(N-b)!`. This counts every desired ambient mapping once. QED.

Thus the exact conditional probability is

\[
\boxed{p_N(\pi)=\frac{A_N(\pi)}{A_{N,3}}.}
\tag{4.2}
\]

## 5. Singularity calculation: the beta kernel is the binary shadow

Put

\[
\rho=e^{-1},\qquad x=1-z/\rho,\qquad L=\log(1/x).
\]

From `z=T e^{-T}`, writing `T=1-u`,

\[
\frac z\rho=(1-u)e^u=1-\frac{u^2}{2}+O(u^3),
\]

hence in a standard dented neighborhood of `rho`,

\[
u=\sqrt{2x}+O(x),
\]

and therefore

\[
\boxed{C(z)=\frac12L-\frac12\log2+O(\sqrt x).}
\tag{5.1}
\]

For every fixed integer `r>=1`, termwise differentiation in the same Delta-domain gives

\[
\boxed{
C^{(r)}(z)=\frac{(r-1)!}{2\rho^r}x^{-r}\{1+O(\sqrt x)\}.
}
\tag{5.2}
\]

We use the standard logarithmic transfer rule

\[
[z^n]x^{-b}L^m
\sim
\rho^{-n}\frac{n^{b-1}}{(b-1)!}(\log n)^m
\qquad(b\ge1,\ m\ge0),
\tag{5.3}
\]

which follows by differentiating the usual transfer formula for `x^{-alpha}` with respect to `alpha`. Also

\[
[z^N]L^3
=\frac{3}{N}\left(H_{N-1}^2-H_{N-1}^{(2)}\right)
\sim \frac{3(\log N)^2}{N}.
\tag{5.4}
\]

Consequently (5.1) and (5.4) give

\[
[z^N]\frac{C(z)^3}{6}
\sim
\frac{\rho^{-N}(\log N)^2}{16N}.
\tag{5.5}
\]

### Two visible components

For a specified visible binary split of sizes `i,j`, `b=i+j`, equations (4.1), (5.1)--(5.5) yield

\[
\boxed{
p_N^{(2)}(i,j)
\sim \frac{2}{\log N}\frac{(i-1)!(j-1)!}{(b-1)!}
=\frac{2B(i,j)}{\log N}.
}
\tag{5.6}
\]

Indeed, the numerator series is `C^{(i)}C^{(j)}C`; its leading singular term is

\[
\frac{(i-1)!(j-1)!}{8\rho^b}x^{-b}L,
\]

and `(N-b)!/N!~N^{-b}` supplies exactly the additional `1/N` needed to compare with (5.5).

### Three visible components

For a specified ternary visible partition of sizes `i,j,l`, `b=i+j+l`, the numerator has no remaining factor `C`, and the same transfer gives

\[
\boxed{
p_N^{(3)}(i,j,\ell)
\sim \frac{2}{(\log N)^2}
\frac{(i-1)!(j-1)!(\ell-1)!}{(b-1)!}.
}
\tag{5.7}
\]

There can be no visible split into four or more components because the ambient proposal has exactly three components.

The key point is that the beta kernel `B(i,j)` appears automatically as the first binary shadow of a conditioned random-mapping component law; it was not inserted into the finite model as a split distribution.

## 6. C035 — conditioned-three-component random-mapping hierarchy

For every `N>=3`, let

\[
p_{11,N}:=p_N^{(2)}(1,1),
\qquad
\lambda_N:=p_{11,N}^{-1},
\qquad
\delta_N=N^{-2},
\qquad
a_N=\delta_N\lambda_N.
\tag{6.1}
\]

The normalization is exact: the two-label particular split has accelerated rate exactly one for every finite `N`.

The attempt probability is always legitimate. Conditional on any three positive ambient component sizes `s_1+s_2+s_3=N`, the probability two fixed labels lie in different components equals

\[
\frac{2(s_1s_2+s_1s_3+s_2s_3)}{N(N-1)}.
\]

This is minimized at `(N-2,1,1)`, so

\[
p_{11,N}\ge\frac{4N-6}{N(N-1)}>\frac1N,
\]

hence `lambda_N<N` and `0<a_N<1/N<1`. From (5.6),

\[
\lambda_N\sim\frac12\log N.
\tag{6.2}
\]

Now run the following discrete fragmentation of `[N]`. At every raw step, independently for every current nonsingleton block `B`:

1. attempt with probability `a_N`;
2. on success sample a fresh uniform mapping `f:[N]->[N]`, conditioned to have exactly three weakly connected functional-digraph components;
3. replace `B` by its nonempty intersections with those three ambient components;
4. if only one intersection is nonempty, the attempt is invisible/no-change;
5. children are first processed at the next raw step.

One raw step has physical duration `delta_N=N^{-2}`.

At the full root `[N]`, every successful attempt produces **exactly three nonempty children**. The hierarchy completes almost surely: for any two distinct labels there is a positive-mass three-component mapping separating them (take three loops and attach every other vertex to one of them), so every nonsingleton carrier has a positive chance of proper refinement on each independent attempt. Every proper refinement increases the number of blocks, and at most `N-1` such increases are possible before all blocks are singletons.

## 7. U014 — exact ambient-component restriction/lumping theorem

### Theorem U014

Fix `N` and any visible subset `S`. Conditional on the complete visible history of C035, every current visible block evolves exactly as follows: independently attempt with probability `a_N`; on success sample the restriction to that visible block of a fresh ambient uniform `N`-mapping conditioned on exactly three components; refine by its nonempty visible component intersections. The visible transition law is independent of the sizes and histories of hidden carrier blocks.

### Proof

At every raw time a visible block `C` lies in one unique current full carrier `B`. The attempt probability depends only on global `N`, not on `|B|`. Every successful attempt samples a completely fresh ambient conditioned mapping, independently of `B` and of the past.

If the proposal properly splits `B` but all labels of `C` happen to lie in the same ambient component, the visible partition does not change and the whole visible block passes into one unique child carrier. At the next raw step that new carrier again receives the same attempt probability and, on success, a fresh independent ambient conditioned mapping. Thus an invisible hidden refinement leaves no state observable from `C`.

If two or three ambient components meet `C`, their nonempty intersections are exactly the visible children. Distinct visible blocks lie in distinct current full carriers and receive independent attempt/proposal pairs. Induction over raw steps proves the statement. QED.

This is the random-mapping analogue of C034/U013, but its proposal law and finite shape law are genuinely different.
