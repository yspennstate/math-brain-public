## 3. The source specified-split generator

For later comparison, let a visible block have `m=i+j` labels and fix one **unordered specified** split `A|B` with `|A|=i`, `|B|=j`.

In the source unordered CTCS, a size-`m` clade splits at rate `h_{m-1}`. Its oriented split-size law is

\[
q(m,i)=\frac{m}{2h_{m-1}i(m-i)}.
\]

Given the oriented size, the corresponding subset is uniform. Therefore the rate of the specified unordered split is

\[
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_{\rm CTCS}(A|B)
&=h_{m-1}\left[
\frac{q(m,i)}{\binom mi}+\frac{q(m,j)}{\binom mj}
\right]\\
&=\frac{m}{ij\binom mi}
=\frac{(i-1)!(j-1)!}{(m-1)!}\\
&=:B(i,j)
=\int_0^1x^{i-1}(1-x)^{j-1}\,dx.
\end{aligned}
\tag{3.1}
\]

Thus convergence of every specified binary split rate to `B(i,j)` is exactly entrywise convergence of the finite-restriction off-diagonal generator.

## 5. U049 — uniform shifted-inversive discrepancy

The key theorem turns the classical inversive-pseudorandom mechanism into a CTCS split-rate estimate.

### Lemma 5.1 — exponential sums

Fix `m` and distinct offsets `a_1,...,a_m in F_p`. For an integer vector `h=(h_1,...,h_m)` whose coordinates are reduced modulo `p` and are not all zero, put

\[
S_p(h;a)
:=\sum_{t\in\mathbb F_p}
 e_p\!\left(\sum_{r=1}^m h_r\iota_p(t+a_r)\right),
\qquad e_p(y)=e^{2\pi i y/p}.
\tag{5.1}
\]

There is a constant `C_m`, independent of `p`, the distinct offsets, and the nonzero frequency vector, such that

\[
\boxed{|S_p(h;a)|\le C_m\sqrt p+C_m.}
\tag{5.2}
\]

#### Proof

Remove the at most `m` exceptional translations `t=-a_r`. On the remaining field points the phase is the rational function

\[
R_h(X)=\sum_{r=1}^m\frac{h_r}{X+a_r}.
\tag{5.3}
\]

Because the poles `-a_r` are distinct, the residue at `-a_r` is `h_r`. Hence if `h` is nonzero modulo `p`, `R_h` is nonconstant and has at least one simple pole. In particular it is not an Artin--Schreier degeneracy `G^p-G+c`, whose pole orders (when present) are divisible by `p`. The classical Weil bound for additive-character sums of a rational function therefore gives an `O_m(sqrt(p))` bound on the nonexceptional sum. Restoring at most `m` omitted unit-modulus terms changes the absolute value by at most `m`, absorbed into `C_m`. `square`

### Lemma 5.2 — fixed-dimensional star discrepancy

Define the `p` points

\[
X_t(a)=\left(
\frac{\iota_p(t+a_1)}p,\ldots,
\frac{\iota_p(t+a_m)}p
\right)\in[0,1)^m,
\qquad t\in\mathbb F_p.
\tag{5.4}
\]

Let `D_p^{(m)}(a)` be their star discrepancy. Uniformly over all distinct offset `m`-tuples,

\[
\boxed{
D_p^{(m)}(a)
\le C'_m p^{-1/2}(\log p)^m.
}
\tag{5.5}
\]

#### Proof

Apply the `m`-dimensional Erdős--Turán--Koksma inequality with truncation

\[
H=\lfloor(p-1)/2\rfloor.
\]

Every nonzero frequency vector with `||h||_infinity<=H` is nonzero modulo `p`, so Lemma 5.1 applies. The ETK weight sum satisfies

\[
\sum_{0<\|h\|_\infty\le H}
\prod_{r=1}^m\max(1,|h_r|)^{-1}
=O_m((\log H)^m).
\]

Dividing the exponential-sum bound by the number `p` of points and adding the `O_m(1/H)` truncation term yields (5.5). `square`

A general axis-parallel half-open box is a signed union of at most `2^m` anchored boxes, so its normalized counting discrepancy is at most `2^mD_p^{(m)}(a)`.

## 6. U049 — split-rate convergence

Fix distinct visible offsets indexed by the disjoint nonempty sets `A,B`, with

\[
i=|A|,\quad j=|B|,\quad m=i+j.
\]

For a threshold `u`, let `x=u/p`, and let `C_{A,B}(u)` be the number of translations `t` for which every coordinate in `A` is red and every coordinate in `B` is blue. By Lemma 5.2 and the general-box inclusion--exclusion bound,

\[
\left|
C_{A,B}(u)-p x^i(1-x)^j
\right|
\le 2^m pD_p^{(m)}.
\tag{6.1}
\]

The same bound holds after interchanging `A,B`. Hence the exact C070 rate of the specified **unordered** split is

\[
Q_p(A|B)
=\sum_{u=1}^{p-1}
\frac{C_{A,B}(u)+C_{B,A}(u)}{2u(p-u)}.
\tag{6.2}
\]

The volume term in (6.2) equals

\[
I_p(i,j)
=\frac1{2p}\sum_{u=1}^{p-1}
\left[
 x^{i-1}(1-x)^{j-1}
+x^{j-1}(1-x)^{i-1}
\right].
\tag{6.3}
\]

Since the bracket is a fixed polynomial on `[0,1]`, the Riemann sum gives

\[
I_p(i,j)=B(i,j)+O_m(p^{-1}).
\tag{6.4}
\]

For the discrepancy error, use

\[
\sum_{u=1}^{p-1}\frac1{u(p-u)}
=\frac2pH_{p-1}.
\tag{6.5}
\]

The two oriented count errors in (6.2) therefore give

\[
\begin{aligned}
|Q_p(A|B)-I_p(i,j)|
&\le
2^m pD_p^{(m)}
\sum_{u=1}^{p-1}\frac1{u(p-u)}\\
&=2^{m+1}D_p^{(m)}H_{p-1}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{6.6}
\]

Combining (5.5), (6.4), and (6.6), uniformly over all distinct visible offsets of fixed cardinality `m`,

\[
\boxed{
|Q_p(A|B)-B(i,j)|
\le
C''_m p^{-1/2}(\log p)^{m+1}+O_m(p^{-1})
\longrightarrow0.
}
\tag{6.7}
\]

This is **U049**. It is an explicit derandomization of the existential C069 split-test mechanism: there is no random ranking and no search for a good permutation.

## 7. Whole-process convergence and validity as a finite tree model

### 7.1 Exact hidden-label lumpability

Fix `k`. A visible block is contained in a unique full carrier clade. For every specified visible cut `A|B`, the aggregate hazard generated by (4.2)--(4.3) depends only on the visible absolute labels and the fixed deterministic masks, not on which hidden labels happen to share the carrier.

If a proposal splits hidden labels while all visible labels remain on one side, the visible partition does not change. The unique child carrying those visible labels receives a fresh independent memoryless family of the same clocks. Thus any two full states having the same visible partition have identical rates into every distinct visible partition class.

Therefore the projection to `[k]` is **strongly lumpable** and is itself a finite-state CTMC.

### 7.2 Generator convergence

For every fixed `k`, all visible split tests involve at most `k` distinct offsets. Since `p_N>=N -> infinity`, U049 gives uniform convergence of every off-diagonal specified split rate to its CTCS value. Because the finite partition space `P_k` has only finitely many one-block binary refinements,

\[
\delta_{N,k}:=
\max_{\pi\in\mathcal P_k}
\|Q_{N,k}(\pi,\cdot)-Q_k^{\rm CTCS}(\pi,\cdot)\|_1
\longrightarrow0.
\tag{7.1}
\]

Exit rates are consequently uniformly bounded for fixed `k`.

### 7.3 Bounded-interval path total variation

Choose a common uniformization rate `Lambda_k` larger than all sufficiently large-`N` exit rates and the CTCS exit rates. Uniformize both chains with the same rate-`Lambda_k` Poisson clock and maximally couple the discrete transition row at every ring. At one ring the mismatch probability is at most

\[
\frac{\delta_{N,k}}{2\Lambda_k}.
\]

The expected number of rings by time `T` is `Lambda_kT`, so a union bound gives

\[
\boxed{
\left\|
\mathcal L(\Pi_{N,070}^{[k]}|_{[0,T]})
-
\mathcal L(\Pi_{\rm CTCS}^{[k]}|_{[0,T]})
\right\|_{\rm TV}
\le \frac{T\delta_{N,k}}2
\longrightarrow0.
}
\tag{7.2}
\]

Path total variation is stronger than Skorokhod `J1` convergence.

### 7.4 Projective CTCS(infinity) convergence

Use the frozen projective path metric

\[
d(\omega,\omega')
=\sum_{k\ge1}2^{-k}
\bigl(1\wedge d_{J_1,k}(\omega^{[k]},\omega'^{[k]})\bigr).
\tag{7.3}
\]

For every finite `K`, the joint first `K` coordinate restrictions are deterministic projections of the `[K]` path. Equation (7.2) for `[K]` therefore gives their joint convergence to the corresponding consistent CTCS restrictions. Tightness in the countable product follows by the standard finite-head / summable-tail argument, and the source exchangeable-partition consistency identifies the unique projective limit. Hence

\[
\boxed{
\Pi_{N,070}\Longrightarrow\Pi_{\operatorname{CTCS}(\infty)}
}
\tag{7.4}
\]

in the frozen summable finite-restriction topology.

### 7.5 Every finite model actually resolves into a tree

The map `iota_p` is a permutation of `F_p`. For distinct leaf labels `a != b` and any translation `t`, the two ranks `iota_p(t+a)` and `iota_p(t+b)` are distinct. Choosing a threshold strictly between those ranks gives a positive-rate proposal that separates the pair. Therefore every nonsingleton clade has positive total rate of a nontrivial split.

The process only refines partitions and has at most `N-1` nontrivial splitting events before all leaves are singletons. Thus there is no explosion and absorption at the singleton partition occurs almost surely. C070 is a genuine finite discrete hierarchy/tree with the continuous-time embedding above.
