# 5. Harmonic active/inactive decomposition

For `N>=2`, let

\[
\lambda_{N,s}=\frac{N}{2s(N-s)}
=\frac12\left(\frac1s+\frac1{N-s}\right),
\qquad 1\le s\le N-1.
\tag{5.1}
\]

The total harmonic rate is exactly

\[
\boxed{
\sum_{s=1}^{N-1}\lambda_{N,s}=H_{N-1}.
}
\tag{5.2}
\]

Fix the compact-logit active interval

\[
I=[1/2,4/5)
\tag{5.3}
\]

and write

\[
A_N=\{s:s/N\in I\},
\quad
W_N=\sum_{s\in A_N}\lambda_{N,s},
\quad
R_N=\sum_{s\notin A_N}\lambda_{N,s}.
\tag{5.4}
\]

Since `I` stays away from the endpoints,

\[
W_N\longrightarrow
\int_{1/2}^{4/5}\frac{dx}{2x(1-x)}
=\log2,
\tag{5.5}
\]

while

\[
R_N=H_{N-1}-W_N\sim\log N.
\tag{5.6}
\]

Conditional on an inactive harmonic mark, define

\[
\pi_{N,s}=\frac{\lambda_{N,s}}{R_N},
\qquad s\notin A_N.
\tag{5.7}
\]

If

\[
m_s=\min\{s,N-s\},
\]

then

\[
\boxed{
\pi_{N,s}\le\frac1{R_Nm_s}.
}
\tag{5.8}
\]

Indeed `lambda_{N,s}<=1/m_s`.

# 6. U076(A): exact dual lower bound for every deterministic hash

Let `d>=2`, let `h` be any deterministic map from the inactive mark set into `Z/dZ`, and let

\[
q_j=\sum_{s:h(s)=j}\pi_{N,s}.
\tag{6.1}
\]

Put

\[
\boxed{
E_N(d)=\sum_s\left(\pi_{N,s}-\frac1d\right)_+.
}
\tag{6.2}
\]

### Lemma 6.1

For every deterministic `h`,

\[
\boxed{
\|q-U_d\|_{\rm TV}\ge E_N(d).
}
\tag{6.3}
\]

### Proof

For one output bin `j`, suppose it contains `r` source atoms larger than `1/d`. Then

\[
q_j-\frac1d
\ge
\sum_{\substack{s:h(s)=j\\\pi_{N,s}>1/d}}
\left(\pi_{N,s}-\frac1d\right)
\tag{6.4}
\]

because the difference between the left side and the displayed sum is `(r-1)/d` plus the mass of all non-oversized atoms in the bin. If `r=0` the right side is zero. Taking positive parts and summing over bins yields (6.3), since

\[
\|q-U_d\|_{\rm TV}
=\sum_j\left(q_j-\frac1d\right)_+.
\tag{6.5}
\]

This is the exact dual obstruction used below.

# 7. U076(B): a matching deterministic upper construction

Let

\[
\delta_N=\frac1{d\sqrt{R_N}}
\tag{7.1}
\]

and call a source atom **heavy** when `pi_{N,s}>delta_N`.

By (5.8), heaviness implies

\[
m_s<\frac d{\sqrt{R_N}}.
\]

There are therefore at most

\[
M_N\le2\left\lceil\frac d{\sqrt{R_N}}\right\rceil
\tag{7.2}
\]

heavy atoms. For sufficiently large `N`, `M_N<d`.

Assign every heavy atom to its own output bin. Let their masses be `p_1,...,p_M`, and give these bins target masses exactly `p_i`. Give each of the remaining `e=d-M` bins the common target

\[
t=\frac{1-\sum_{i=1}^M p_i}{e}.
\tag{7.3}
\]

This target vector has total mass one. Its TV distance from uniform is at most

\[
E_N(d)+\frac{M_N}{d}.
\tag{7.4}
\]

To see this, the oversized heavy atoms contribute exactly their excess `E_N(d)`; every heavy atom at or below `1/d` contributes a deficit at most `1/d`, and the equal residual targets balance the signed total.

Every remaining source atom has mass at most `delta_N`. Order these tail atoms arbitrarily, let `S_r` be their cumulative mass, and for `j=1,...,e-1` cut after the largest index with

\[
S_r\le jt.
\]

The cumulative undershoot at every cut lies in `[-delta_N,0]`. Hence every residual-bin mass differs from `t` by at most `delta_N`, including the final bin. Therefore the extra TV error from integral packing is at most

\[
\frac{e\delta_N}{2}\le\frac1{2\sqrt{R_N}}.
\tag{7.5}
\]

Combining (7.2)--(7.5) gives, for all large `N`, a deterministic hash with

\[
\boxed{
\|h_N(\pi_N)-U_d\|_{\rm TV}
\le
E_N(d)+\frac{5}{2\sqrt{R_N}}+\frac2d.
}
\tag{7.6}
\]

The constants are deliberately not optimized.

Now bound `E_N(d)`. If `d<=R_N`, (5.8) already implies `E_N(d)=0`. If `d>R_N`, an oversized atom must have

\[
m_s<\frac d{R_N}.
\]

At most two indices have a given `m_s`, so

\[
E_N(d)
\le
\frac2{R_N}
H_{\lceil d/R_N\rceil}
\le
\frac2{R_N}\left(1+\log\left(1+\frac d{R_N}\right)\right).
\tag{7.7}
\]

Consequently,

\[
\boxed{
d_N\to\infty,\qquad \log d_N=o(R_N)
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\exists h_N:\ \|h_N(\pi_N)-U_{d_N}\|_{\rm TV}\to0.
}
\tag{7.8}
\]

For a completely deterministic definition of C092, among all finite maps `h` choose the lexicographically last TV-minimizer. The construction above proves that its error is no larger than (7.6). At `N=4` this convention maps the unique inactive atom to phase `1`, which is used in the exact witness.

# 8. U076(C): the logarithmic-scale obstruction is real

The dual bound is not cosmetic. Let

\[
d_N=\lfloor N^\alpha\rfloor,
\qquad 0<\alpha\le1.
\]

Set

\[
M_N=\left\lfloor\frac{d_N}{4R_N}\right\rfloor.
\tag{8.1}
\]

For all large `N`, every `m<=M_N` gives two inactive edge marks `s=m` and `s=N-m`. Moreover

\[
\pi_{N,m},\pi_{N,N-m}
\ge\frac1{2R_Nm}
\ge\frac2{d_N}.
\tag{8.2}
\]

Thus all these atoms are oversized, and

\[
E_N(d_N)
\ge
\frac1{R_N}H_{M_N}-\frac{2M_N}{d_N}.
\tag{8.3}
\]

Since `R_N~log N`, `log M_N=alpha log N-o(log N)` and `2M_N/d_N=O(1/R_N)`, so

\[
\boxed{
\liminf_{N\to\infty}E_N(d_N)\ge\alpha.
}
\tag{8.4}
\]

By Lemma 6.1, every deterministic one-mark hash has the same lower bound. For `alpha>1`, an output of a deterministic function of at most `N-1` source values has at most `N-1` nonzero bins, so

\[
\|h_N(\pi_N)-U_{d_N}\|_{\rm TV}
\ge1-\frac{N-1}{d_N}\to1.
\tag{8.5}

Hence one inactive harmonic mark can deterministically generate an asymptotically uniform `N^{o(1)}`-size phase alphabet, but not a polynomial-size one. This is the #11 obstruction.

\boxed{d_N=2^{\max\{1,r_N\}}.}
\tag{8.6}

Then `d_N->infinity` and

# 9. C092 — the shadow-harmonic-refresh hierarchy

Let

\[
L=\log4,
\quad
\sigma(y)=\frac{e^y}{1+e^y},
\quad
\ell(x)=\log\frac{x}{1-x},
\quad
\alpha_{N,s}=\frac{\ell(s/N)}L.
\tag{9.1}
\]

Each current nonsingleton clade carries a phase

\[
J\in\mathbb Z/d_N\mathbb Z.
\]

The root starts at `J=0`.

Each clade has two independent finite clock families.

### Ordinary tree-proposal clocks

For every `1<=s<=N-1`, an ordinary clock rings at rate `lambda_{N,s}`.

- If `s notin A_N`, color each label iid Bernoulli(`s/N`).
- If `s in A_N`, color each label iid Bernoulli
  \[
  p_{N,s,J}
  =
  \sigma\left(
  L\left\{b_N\alpha_{N,s}+\frac J{d_N}\right\}
  \right).
  \tag{9.2}
  \]

If both colors occur, split the clade into its two color classes. Otherwise leave the clade unchanged. A split does **not** alter the phase: both children inherit the current `J`.

### Shadow refresh clocks

For every inactive `s notin A_N`, an independent shadow clock rings at the same rate `lambda_{N,s}`. A shadow ring makes no coloring and no tree change. It only sets

\[
\boxed{J\leftarrow h_N(s).}
\tag{9.3}
\]

Children receive fresh independent ordinary and shadow clocks.

The branch count `b_N` is arbitrary in the theorem. For the concrete class C092 use

\[
b_N=1\quad(N\le4),
\qquad
b_N=2^{\,2^{N-4}-1}\quad(N\ge5).
\tag{9.4}
\]

The finite model is exchangeable in leaf labels. Its extra shadow events are genuinely present finite dynamics but are invisible in the partition coordinate.

## 9.1 Exact restriction consistency

Fix `[k]`. Conditional on a mark and phase, ordinary colors are iid across labels, so restriction preserves the same coloring law. If a full split is invisible on `[k]`, exactly one child carries the visible block and inherits the same phase `J`; fresh clocks have the same law by Poisson memorylessness. Shadow rings do not inspect labels and merely update the visible carrier phase by (9.3).

Thus the finite restrictions of the augmented `(partition, block phases)` process are exactly compatible. In particular, hidden labels do not change the phase law seen by the visible restriction.

## 9.2 Coupling to fresh uniform dither

On one visible-carrier lineage, active ordinary proposals occur at total rate `W_N`, while shadow refreshes occur at total rate `R_N`. Inactive ordinary proposals may happen in between; crucially they do not use `J`, so their colors and split outcomes do not bias the current phase. If they split a carrier, the child or children simply inherit that phase and restart independent clocks.

After an active ordinary proposal, the phase may be biased by the event just observed, because active colors depend on `J`. Before the next active ordinary proposal, however, the competition between an active clock and a shadow refresh has probabilities

\[
\boxed{
q_N:=\Pr(\text{active arrives before any shadow})
=\frac{W_N}{W_N+R_N}
=\frac{W_N}{H_{N-1}}
=O((\log N)^{-1}).
}
\tag{9.5}
\]

\[
\nu_N=h_N(\pi_N),
\qquad
\|\nu_N-U_{d_N}\|_{\rm TV}=:\epsilon_N\to0.
\tag{9.6}

\[
q_N+\epsilon_N.
\tag{9.7}

-
