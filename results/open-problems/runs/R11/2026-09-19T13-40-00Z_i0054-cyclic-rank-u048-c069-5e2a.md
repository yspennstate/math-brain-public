## 3. U048 — a deterministic cyclic-translation CTCS compiler

### 3.1 Construction from one frozen ranking

Put the ambient labels at the cyclic positions

\[
\mathbb Z/N\mathbb Z=\{0,1,\ldots,N-1\}.
\]

Let `rho` be a permutation of the ranks `1,...,N` on these positions. For every threshold

\[
u=1,\ldots,N-1
\]

and cyclic shift `t in Z/NZ`, assign every current nonsingleton clade an independent Poisson proposal clock of rate

\[
\boxed{c_{u,t}=\frac1{2u(N-u)}.}
\tag{3.1}
\]

When `(u,t)` rings, ambient label `ell` is colored red iff

\[
\rho(t+\ell\pmod N)\le u,
\tag{3.2}
\]

and the current clade is split by red/blue whenever both colors occur. Descendants receive fresh independent clock families but reuse the same frozen ranking `rho`.

For disjoint nonempty specified visible sets `A,B`, write

\[
Q_\rho(A\mid B)
\]

for the aggregate rate of proposals that separate exactly `A` from `B` on `A union B`, with the two colors regarded as unordered. Put

\[
i=|A|,\qquad j=|B|,\qquad m=i+j.
\]

### 3.2 Exact random-ranking mean

Let `rho` now be uniform on `S_N`. For a fixed threshold `u` and shift `t`, the ranks seen at the `m` visible positions are a sample without replacement. Therefore

\[
\Pr\{A\text{ red},B\text{ blue}\}
=\frac{(u)_i(N-u)_j}{(N)_m},
\tag{3.3}
\]

and the reversed coloring has probability `(u)_j(N-u)_i/(N)_m`. Summing the `N` cyclic shifts and the clock weights gives

\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathbb E Q_\rho(A\mid B)
&=\frac{N}{2(N)_m}
\sum_{u=1}^{N-1}
\Bigl[(u-1)_{i-1}(N-u-1)_{j-1}\\
&\hspace{38mm}+(u-1)_{j-1}(N-u-1)_{i-1}\Bigr].
\end{aligned}
\tag{3.4}
\]

The falling-factorial Vandermonde convolution is

\[
\sum_{u=1}^{N-1}
(u-1)_{i-1}(N-u-1)_{j-1}
=(i-1)!(j-1)!\binom{N-1}{m-1}.
\tag{3.5}
\]

The two terms in (3.4) have the same sum, and `(N)_m=N(N-1)_{m-1}`. Hence

\[
\boxed{
\mathbb E Q_\rho(A\mid B)
=B(i,j)
=\frac{(i-1)!(j-1)!}{(i+j-1)!}
}
\tag{3.6}
\]

**exactly for every `N>=m`**. These are exactly the specified binary split rates of CTCS.

### 3.3 Transposition Lipschitz bound

Suppose `rho'` is obtained from `rho` by swapping the ranks at two positions `y,z`. For a fixed threshold `u`, the threshold sets differ only when `u` lies between the two swapped ranks. At such a threshold the split indicator for `A|B` can change only when one of the two changed cyclic positions coincides with one of the `m` translated visible labels. There are at most `2m` affected shifts. Thus

\[
|\Delta_u Q|
\le\frac{2m}{2u(N-u)}
=\frac{m}{u(N-u)}.
\tag{3.7}
\]

Summing over all thresholds, which only enlarges the bound, yields

\[
\boxed{
|Q_\rho(A\mid B)-Q_{\rho'}(A\mid B)|
\le L_{N,m}:=\frac{2mH_{N-1}}{N}.
}
\tag{3.8}
\]

### 3.4 Concentration on permutations

Reveal the random permutation one position at a time and consider the Doob martingale of `Q_rho(A|B)`. If two possible values of the next revealed rank are `a,b`, couple the corresponding uniform completions by swapping `a` and `b`. The completed permutations differ by one transposition, so (3.8) implies that the two conditional expectations differ by at most `L_{N,m}`. Consequently every martingale increment has absolute value at most `L_{N,m}`. Azuma--Hoeffding gives

\[
\boxed{
\Pr\left\{
|Q_\rho(A\mid B)-B(i,j)|>\varepsilon
\right\}
\le
2\exp\left\{-\frac{\varepsilon^2N}
{8m^2H_{N-1}^2}\right\}.
}
\tag{3.9}
\]

No independence between different split tests is required.

### 3.5 Probabilistic method and a deterministic sequence

Set

\[
K_N=\max\left\{2,
\left\lfloor\frac{N^{1/8}}{\log(N+1)}\right\rfloor
\right\},
\qquad
\varepsilon_N=N^{-1/8}.
\tag{3.10}
\]

There are at most `3^{K_N}` ordered choices of disjoint nonempty `A,B subset [K_N]`, hence fewer unordered split tests. Using `m<=K_N`, (3.9), and the union bound,

\[
\Pr\left\{
\max_{A,B\subset[K_N]}
|Q_\rho(A\mid B)-B(|A|,|B|)|>\varepsilon_N
\right\}
\le
2\,3^{K_N}
\exp\left\{-\frac{\varepsilon_N^2N}
{8K_N^2H_{N-1}^2}\right\}.
\tag{3.11}
\]

Now `K_N=O(N^{1/8}/log N)`, `H_{N-1}=O(log N)`, and `eps_N^2 N=N^{3/4}`. Therefore the negative exponent in (3.11) is `-Theta(N^{1/2})`, whereas `K_N log 3=o(N^{1/2})`. The right-hand side tends to zero.

Thus for all sufficiently large `N` at least one ranking satisfies all the tests simultaneously. Define `rho_N` to be the **lexicographically first** such ranking; this is a finite deterministic search rule. Define the finitely many smaller `N` arbitrarily, except take

\[
\rho_4=(1,2,3,4)
\tag{3.12}
\]

for the explicit finite witness below. Since `K_N->infinity` and `eps_N->0`, every fixed split test converges to its CTCS beta rate.

This proves the finite-dimensional generator part of U048.

## 4. Why this refutes the proposed exact-strength necessity

At threshold `u=1`, **every** translated mask has exactly one red ambient label. Therefore for any two distinct coordinates there is zero frequency of the red-red pattern in this orbit. An exact strength-two Bernoulli design of density `1/N` would assign that pattern positive frequency `1/N^2` (or its exact finite-design analogue). Hence the translation orbit is not even an exact strength-two orthogonal array at `u=1`.

Nevertheless Sections 3.2--3.5 show that the weighted split-rate tests converge simultaneously on every fixed restriction. Thus the condition `K_N -> infinity` of U047 is sufficient but emphatically **not necessary**: microscopic exact independence can fail at the lowest nontrivial strength while the tree-level CTCS generator still emerges.

## 5. Whole-process convergence and finite-tree validity

### 5.1 Exact hidden-label lumpability

Fix `k`. A visible block is contained in one unique full carrier block. The rate at which the carrier produces a specified nontrivial cut `A|B` of its visible labels is exactly `Q_{rho_N}(A|B)` and depends only on those visible labels and the fixed global ranking, not on which hidden labels share the carrier. If a full proposal splits hidden labels but leaves all visible labels of the carrier on one side, the visible partition is unchanged; the unique child containing the visible labels receives a fresh memoryless copy of the same clock family.

Hence full states with the same visible partition have identical rates into every distinct visible partition class. The projection to `[k]` is therefore strongly lumpable and is itself a finite-state CTMC.

### 5.2 Generator and path convergence

For all sufficiently large `N`, `k<=K_N`. Every off-diagonal visible split rate then differs from the CTCS rate by at most `eps_N`. A `k`-label partition has only finitely many possible one-block binary refinements; a crude bound is `k 2^{k-1}`. Therefore

\[
\max_{\pi\in\mathcal P_k}
\|Q_{N,k}(\pi,\cdot)-Q_k^{\rm CTCS}(\pi,\cdot)\|_1
\le C_k\varepsilon_N
\longrightarrow0
\tag{5.1}
\]

for a constant `C_k` depending only on `k`. Exit rates are consequently uniformly bounded for fixed `k`.

Choose a common uniformization rate `Lambda_k` larger than all large-`N` exit rates and the CTCS exit rates. Couple the two uniformized jump chains maximally at each common Poisson clock ring. If `delta_N` is the maximum row `l1` generator discrepancy in (5.1), then the mismatch probability at one ring is at most `delta_N/(2 Lambda_k)`. The expected number of rings by time `T` is `Lambda_k T`, hence

\[
\boxed{
\left\|
\mathcal L(\Pi_{N,069}^{[k]}|_{[0,T]})
-
\mathcal L(\Pi_{\rm CTCS}^{[k]}|_{[0,T]})
\right\|_{\rm TV}
\le\frac{T\delta_N}{2}
\longrightarrow0.
}
\tag{5.2}
\]

Path total variation is stronger than the required Skorokhod `J1` convergence.

For the infinite object, all restrictions `[j]`, `j<=K`, are deterministic projections of the `[K]` path, so their joint law converges whenever the `[K]` path law does. In the frozen summable projective metric, the tail beyond `K` is deterministically summable. First send `N->infinity`, then `K->infinity`. Thus

\[
\boxed{
\Pi_{N,069}\Longrightarrow\Pi_{\rm CTCS(\infty)}.
}
\tag{5.3}
\]

### 5.3 The finite hierarchy really terminates

The total raw proposal-clock rate of one full block is finite:

\[
\sum_{u=1}^{N-1}\sum_{t=0}^{N-1}\frac1{2u(N-u)}
=H_{N-1}.
\tag{5.4}
\]

For any two distinct labels `a,b` in a nonsingleton block, at every shift their two cyclic positions are distinct and therefore carry distinct ranks. Choose a threshold strictly between those two ranks. The corresponding positive-rate proposal separates the pair. Hence every nonsingleton state has positive total nontrivial exit rate. Every nontrivial jump increases the number of blocks, so at most `N-1` such jumps occur before the singleton partition. The process is nonexplosive and reaches the singleton partition almost surely.

This completes U048 and proves C069's full frozen limit.
