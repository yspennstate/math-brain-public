## 3. The source specified-split generator

Fix a visible clade of size `m=i+j` and a specified **unordered** split `A|B`, with `|A|=i`, `|B|=j`.

In CTCS a size-`m` clade splits at rate `h_{m-1}` and has oriented split-size law

\[
q(m,i)=\frac{m}{2h_{m-1}i(m-i)}.
\]

There are `binom(m,i)` choices of a specified oriented `i`-set. Summing the two orientations of the same unordered split gives

\[
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_{\rm CTCS}(A|B)
&=h_{m-1}\left(\frac{q(m,i)}{\binom mi}+\frac{q(m,j)}{\binom mj}\right)\\
&=\frac{m}{ij\binom mi}\\
&=\frac{(i-1)!(j-1)!}{(m-1)!}\\
&=:B(i,j)\\
&=\int_0^1 x^{i-1}(1-x)^{j-1}\,dx.
\end{aligned}
\tag{3.1}
\]

Thus convergence of all specified unordered binary split rates to `B(i,j)` is exactly entrywise convergence of the off-diagonal generator on every fixed partition state.

## 4. B24 first: the sharp fixed-degree Fourier obstruction

Let `d>=1` be fixed and consider the monomial map

\[
\phi_{p,d}(x)=x^d\quad\text{on }\mathbb F_p.
\]

Take distinct offsets `a_1,...,a_m` and a Fourier vector `h=(h_1,...,h_m)`. The translation orbit has additive-character phase

\[
P_h(t)=\sum_{j=1}^m h_j(t+a_j)^d.
\tag{4.1}
\]

Assume `p>d`. If `P_h` is constant, then the coefficients of `t^d,t^{d-1},...,t` vanish. For `0<=r<=d-1`, the coefficient of `t^{d-r}` is

\[
\binom dr\sum_{j=1}^m h_j a_j^r.
\tag{4.2}
\]

Since `d<p`, every displayed binomial coefficient is nonzero mod `p`.

### Proposition U051(a) — sharp polynomial Fourier rank

For distinct offsets:

1. if `m<=d`, then `P_h` constant implies `h=0`;
2. at `m=d+1`, nonzero constant phases exist already on consecutive offsets `a_j=j`, `j=0,...,d`.

#### Proof

For `m<=d`, equations (4.2) for `r=0,...,m-1` form the square Vandermonde system

\[
\left(a_j^r\right)_{0\le r<m,1\le j\le m}h=0.
\]

Distinct offsets make this matrix invertible over `F_p`, so `h=0`.

For `m=d+1`, take

\[
h_j=(-1)^{d-j}\binom dj,\qquad j=0,...,d.
\tag{4.3}
\]

The `d`th finite-difference identity gives exactly

\[
\sum_{j=0}^d(-1)^{d-j}\binom dj(t+j)^d=d!.
\tag{4.4}
\]

Because `p>d`, `d!` is nonzero mod `p`. Thus a nontrivial Fourier mode is perfectly constant. `square`

This is the extremal obstruction promised by #24: degree `d` supports full Fourier nondegeneracy through dimension `d`, and fails immediately in dimension `d+1`. In particular, **fixed degree is the wrong deformation** if one wants one construction whose finite restrictions work for every fixed `k`.

I do **not** claim (4.4) alone proves a CTCS split-rate failure for every fixed-degree model; it proves the sharper statement relevant to the Fourier-equidistribution compiler: full translation-orbit equidistribution cannot hold beyond dimension `d`.

## 5. B6: let the polynomial degree grow slowly

For each `N>=2`, define

\[
d_N:=\text{the least odd prime }d\ge \max\{3,\lceil\log(N+2)\rceil\}.
\tag{5.1}
\]

By Bertrand's postulate,

\[
d_N=O(\log N).
\tag{5.2}
\]

Now let `p_N` be the least prime satisfying

\[
p_N\ge\max\{N,d_N+1\},
\qquad
p_N\equiv2\pmod{d_N}.
\tag{5.3}
\]

Dirichlet's theorem gives existence because `d_N` is an odd prime. Since

\[
p_N-1\equiv1\pmod{d_N},
\]

we have

\[
\gcd(d_N,p_N-1)=1.
\tag{5.4}
\]

Therefore

\[
\phi_N(x):=x^{d_N}
\tag{5.5}
\]

is a permutation of `F_{p_N}`. Also `p_N>=N` implies `p_N->infinity`, while (5.2) gives

\[
d_N=O(\log N)=O(\log p_N).
\tag{5.6}
\]

The key point is simultaneous:

- `d_N -> infinity`, so every fixed visible dimension `m` eventually satisfies `m<=d_N`;
- `d_N` grows slowly enough that the Weil discrepancy loss still vanishes.

## 6. B11: dual Fourier discrepancy estimate

Fix `m` and distinct ambient offsets `a_1,...,a_m in F_{p_N}`. For large `N`, `d_N>=m`. Consider the `p_N` points

\[
X_t=\left(
\frac{(t+a_1)^{d_N}\bmod p_N}{p_N},\ldots,
\frac{(t+a_m)^{d_N}\bmod p_N}{p_N}
\right),
\qquad t\in F_{p_N}.
\tag{6.1}
\]

Let `h` be a nonzero integer Fourier vector with

\[
\|h\|_\infty\le (p_N-1)/2.
\]

Modulo `p_N`, `h` is still nonzero. By U051(a), the polynomial

\[
P_h(t)=\sum_{j=1}^m h_j(t+a_j)^{d_N}
\tag{6.2}
\]

is nonconstant. Its degree is at most `d_N<p_N`. The classical Weil bound for complete additive-character sums therefore gives

\[
\left|
\sum_{t\in F_{p_N}}e_{p_N}(P_h(t))
\right|
\le (d_N-1)\sqrt{p_N}.
\tag{6.3}
\]

Apply `m`-dimensional Erdos--Turan--Koksma with truncation `H=floor((p_N-1)/2)`. The harmonic Fourier weight sum is `O_m((log p_N)^m)`, so the star discrepancy of (6.1) satisfies

\[
\boxed{
D_N^{(m)}
=O_m\!\left(
\frac{d_N(\log p_N)^m}{\sqrt{p_N}}+\frac1{p_N}
\right)
=O_m\!\left(
\frac{(\log p_N)^{m+1}}{\sqrt{p_N}}
\right).
}
\tag{6.4}
\]

In particular `D_N^{(m)} -> 0` for every fixed `m`.

This proves the first half of **U051**: a slowly growing power-permutation translation orbit is asymptotically equidistributed on every fixed visible dimension, despite the sharp fixed-degree obstruction of Section 4.

## 7. C072 — the growing-power threshold hierarchy

\tag{7.1}

When `(u,t)` rings, color ambient label `\ell` red iff the standard representative in `{0,...,p_N-1}` of

\[
(t+\ell)^{d_N}\pmod{p_N}

is `<u`. Split the current clade by red/blue if both colors occur; otherwise the proposal is null. Descendant clades receive fresh independent clocks with the same deterministic ambient masks.

### 7.1 Finite-tree validity

The total proposal rate per active clade is finite and exact:

\[
\begin{aligned}
\sum_{u=1}^{p_N-1}\sum_{t\in F_{p_N}}
\frac1{2u(p_N-u)}
&=\frac{p_N}{2}\sum_{u=1}^{p_N-1}\frac1{u(p_N-u)}\\
&=H_{p_N-1}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{7.3}
\]

For distinct labels `a!=b`, injectivity of `x^{d_N}` implies that for every translation `t`, the two ranks `(t+a)^{d_N}` and `(t+b)^{d_N}` are distinct. ## 8. Split-rate convergence

Let `A|B` be a specified unordered split of one visible block, with `|A|=i`, `|B|=j`, `m=i+j`. Put `p=p_N`, `d=d_N`, and `x=u/p`.

Let `C_{A,B}(u)` be the number of translations `t` for which all coordinates in `A` have rank `<u` and all coordinates in `B` have rank `>=u`. The corresponding box is a signed union of at most `2^m` anchored boxes, so (6.4) gives uniformly in `u`

\[
C_{A,B}(u)
=p x^i(1-x)^j+O_m(pD_N^{(m)}),
\tag{8.1}
\]

and similarly with `A,B` reversed.

The exact split rate is

\[
Q_N(A|B)
=\sum_{u=1}^{p-1}
\frac{C_{A,B}(u)+C_{B,A}(u)}{2u(p-u)}.
\tag{8.2}
\]

The volume contribution equals

\[
\frac1{2p}\sum_{u=1}^{p-1}
\left[
 x^{i-1}(1-x)^{j-1}
+x^{j-1}(1-x)^{i-1}
\right]
=B(i,j)+O_m(p^{-1}),
\tag{8.3}
\]

by a fixed-polynomial Riemann sum. For the discrepancy term use

\[
\sum_{u=1}^{p-1}\frac1{u(p-u)}
=\frac{2H_{p-1}}p.
\tag{8.4}
\]

Thus

\[
\boxed{
|Q_N(A|B)-B(i,j)|
\le
C_m D_N^{(m)}H_{p_N-1}+O_m(p_N^{-1})
=O_m\!\left(
\frac{(\log p_N)^{m+2}}{\sqrt{p_N}}
\right)
\to0.
}
\tag{8.5}
\]

(The displayed logarithmic exponent is deliberately loose by one harmless power; only convergence is needed.)

This completes the CTCS-specific part of **U051**.
