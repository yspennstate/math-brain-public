## 3. Method #10 — DERANDOMIZE: the CTCS split-vector energy collapses to `O(K 2^K)`

Define

`L_p(r)=H_r-H_{p-1-r}`, `0 <= r < p`,

with `H_0=0`. Fix one translation `t` and one visible subset `V` of size `m`. Write its threshold ranks in increasing order as

`x_1 < x_2 < ... < x_m`,

and set `y_r=L_p(x_r)`.

### Lemma U055.1 — exact adjacent-gap energy identity

For the coordinates corresponding to unordered nontrivial cuts of this fixed `V`, exactly the `m-1` cuts between consecutive threshold ranks can have nonzero contribution under the translation `t`, and

`sum_{c a cut of V} g_t(c)^2
 = (1/4) sum_{r=1}^{m-1} (y_{r+1}-y_r)^2`.

**Proof.** A threshold sweep through the ordered ranks can only separate the lower `r` points from the upper `m-r` points, for `r=1,...,m-1`; every other unordered cut of `V` has zero contribution for this translation. For the cut at gap `r`, summing its threshold rates over `x_r < u <= x_{r+1}` gives

`sum_{u=x_r+1}^{x_{r+1}} p/[2u(p-u)]`.

Using

`p/[u(p-u)] = 1/u + 1/(p-u)`,

the sum equals

`(1/2)[H_{x_{r+1}}-H_{x_r}+H_{p-1-x_r}-H_{p-1-x_{r+1}}]`

`=(1/2)[L_p(x_{r+1})-L_p(x_r)]`.

Squaring and summing over the `m-1` gaps proves the identity. `QED`

Since `(a-b)^2 <= 2a^2+2b^2`,

`sum_{r=1}^{m-1}(y_{r+1}-y_r)^2 <= 4 sum_{r=1}^m y_r^2`.

Therefore

`sum_{c a cut of V} g_t(c)^2 <= sum_{v in V} L_p(phi(t+v))^2`.      (3.1)

U054 proved the uniform harmonic second-moment estimate

`M_p := (1/p)sum_{x in F_p} L_p(x)^2 < 8`.                          (3.2)

Average (3.1) over all translations. For each fixed visible label `v`, the map `t -> phi(t+v)` is a permutation of `F_p`, so its average squared harmonic score is exactly `M_p`. Summing over every visible subset of `[K]` of size at least two yields

`(1/p) sum_t ||g_t||_2^2
 < 8 sum_{m=2}^K m binom(K,m)`

`= 8 K(2^{K-1}-1)`.                                                (3.3)

This is the key improvement: although the number of represented split coordinates is

`D_K=(3^K-2^{K+1}+1)/2`,

the **total squared energy** of the full vector is only `O(K2^K)`.

Center the vectors:

`z_t=g_t-mu`.

The usual variance identity gives

`(1/p)sum_t ||z_t||_2^2
 = (1/p)sum_t ||g_t||_2^2 - ||mu||_2^2
 < 8K(2^{K-1}-1)`.                                                (3.4)

## 4. Explicit conditional-expectation selection

Fix support size `1 <= L <= p`. We seek an equal-weight `L`-subset of translations.

Suppose a partial selected set is `A`, with `r=|A|`; let `R` be the remaining translation set, `n=|R|`, and let `s=L-r` be the number still to choose. Put

`S_A=sum_{t in A} z_t`,

`Q_R=sum_{t in R} ||z_t||_2^2`.

Because the entire centered population sums to zero,

`sum_{t in R} z_t = -S_A`.

If the completion is a uniformly random `s`-subset `U` of `R`, then the exact conditional expectation of the final **sum** error is

`F(A):=E[||S_A + sum_{t in U} z_t||_2^2 | A]`

`= ((n-s)(n-s-1)/(n(n-1))) ||S_A||_2^2`

`  + (s(n-s)/(n(n-1))) Q_R`,                                      (4.1)

for `0<s<n`, with the evident terminal conventions for `s=0` or `s=n`.

### Lemma U055.2 — formula (4.1)

**Proof.** For uniform sampling without replacement,

`E sum_{t in U} z_t = (s/n) sum_{t in R} z_t = -(s/n)S_A`.

Moreover

`E ||sum_{t in U}z_t||^2`

`= (s/n)Q_R + [s(s-1)/(n(n-1))](||sum_{R}z_t||^2-Q_R)`

`= (s/n)Q_R + [s(s-1)/(n(n-1))](||S_A||^2-Q_R)`.

Expanding `||S_A+sum_Uz_t||^2` and collecting coefficients gives precisely (4.1), because

`1-2s/n+s(s-1)/(n(n-1))`

`=((n-s)(n-s-1))/(n(n-1))`.

`QED`

### Deterministic algorithm

Start with `A=empty`. At each stage, for every remaining candidate `t`, compute

`F(A union {t})`

for the corresponding uniformly random completion, and choose the **least** translation among the minimizers. By the law of total expectation, the average of these one-step conditional expectations is the current `F(A)`, so at least one choice does not increase `F`. Hence the deterministic rule maintains

`F(A_next) <= F(A)`.

At the start, `S_A=0`, `n=p`, `s=L`, so

`F(empty) = [L(p-L)/(p(p-1))] sum_t ||z_t||_2^2`.

At termination `F(S)=||sum_{t in S}z_t||_2^2`. Dividing by `L^2` and using (3.4), we obtain the explicit deterministic guarantee

`|| (1/L)sum_{t in S}g_t - mu ||_2^2`

`<= [(p-L)/(L(p-1))] * (1/p)sum_t ||z_t||_2^2`

`< 8K(2^{K-1}-1)/L`.                                              (4.2)

In particular every coordinate satisfies

`max_c |q_S(c)-q_full(c)|`

`< sqrt(8K(2^{K-1}-1)/L)`.                                       (4.3)

This is an actual finite deterministic algorithm rather than an existential lexicographic scan over all `L`-subsets. A direct implementation is polynomial in `p`, `L`, and the represented coordinate dimension. I make no complexity-optimality claim.

The exact checker independently brute-forces all completions in several small `(p,d,K,L)` instances and confirms formula (4.1) exactly with `fractions.Fraction` arithmetic.
