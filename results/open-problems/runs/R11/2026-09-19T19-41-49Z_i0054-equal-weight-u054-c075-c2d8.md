## 3. Method #9 — randomize the retained translation set

Fix one N and write `p=p_N`, `phi=phi_N`. Fix a visible set `V` and a specified unordered nontrivial split `A|B` of `V`. For one translation `t`, define its rescaled specified-split contribution

`g_t(A|B) := sum_{u=1}^{p-1} p/[2u(p-u)] * 1{threshold (t,u) induces A|B on V}`.

Then the full C072 specified-split rate is

`q_full(A|B)=(1/p) sum_t g_t(A|B)`.

The issue is that `g_t` is not uniformly bounded in p near endpoint ranks, so a naive Hoeffding argument would carry an avoidable logarithmic envelope. The useful quantity is its **second moment**, controlled by any one cross-pair.

### Lemma U054.1 — exact pair-separation identity

Let `a in A`, `b in B`. Put

`x = representative(phi(t+a))`, `y = representative(phi(t+b))`,

with `x,y in {0,...,p-1}`, and define

`L_p(r)=H_r-H_{p-1-r}`, with `H_0=0`.

Let `r_t(a,b)` be the rescaled sum of all thresholds that separate a and b:

`r_t(a,b)=sum_{u=min(x,y)+1}^{max(x,y)} p/[2u(p-u)]`.

Then

`r_t(a,b) = (1/2)|L_p(y)-L_p(x)|`.

**Proof.** Assume `x<y`; the other order is symmetric. Since

`p/[u(p-u)] = 1/u + 1/(p-u)`,

we have

`2r_t(a,b) = sum_{u=x+1}^y [1/u+1/(p-u)]`

`= (H_y-H_x) + (H_{p-1-x}-H_{p-1-y})`

`= L_p(y)-L_p(x)`.

The displayed quantity is positive for `x<y`, proving the absolute-value form. `QED`

Every threshold realizing the full split `A|B` separates the chosen pair a,b. Therefore

`0 <= g_t(A|B) <= r_t(a,b)`.

### Lemma U054.2 — a universal second-moment bound

For every prime p, every permutation phi of `F_p`, every distinct ambient a,b, and every specified split coordinate,

`(1/p) sum_t g_t(A|B)^2 < 8`.

**Proof.** From Lemma U054.1 and `(alpha-beta)^2 <= 2alpha^2+2beta^2`,

`r_t(a,b)^2 <= (L_p(x)^2+L_p(y)^2)/2`.

As t ranges over `F_p`, both `phi(t+a)` and `phi(t+b)` range bijectively over `F_p`. Hence

`(1/p)sum_t r_t(a,b)^2 <= M_p := (1/p)sum_{r=0}^{p-1} L_p(r)^2`.

It remains to bound `M_p`. First, `L_p(p-1-r)=-L_p(r)`. The two endpoints contribute

`2H_{p-1}^2/p < 4`,

because Cauchy--Schwarz gives

`H_n^2 <= n sum_{j=1}^n j^{-2} < 2n`.

For `1 <= r <= (p-1)/2`,

`|L_p(r)| = H_{p-1-r}-H_r`

`= sum_{j=r+1}^{p-1-r} 1/j <= log(p/r)`.

By symmetry, the interior contribution to `M_p` is at most

`(2/p) sum_{r=1}^{floor((p-1)/2)} log^2(p/r)`.

The function `f(x)=log^2(1/x)` is positive and decreasing on `(0,1]`, so the right Riemann sum is dominated by its integral:

`(1/p)sum_{r=1}^{floor((p-1)/2)} f(r/p) <= integral_0^1 f(x) dx = 2`.

Therefore the interior contribution is at most 4, and `M_p<8`. Since `g_t <= r_t`, the same bound holds for `g_t^2`. `QED`

This is the point that makes arbitrary slowly diverging **equal-weight** support possible.

### Lemma U054.3 — equal-weight finite-population approximation

Let S be a uniformly random L-element subset of `F_p`, `1 <= L < p`. Then for every specified split coordinate c,

`E[(1/L)sum_{t in S} g_t(c)] = q_full(c)`

and

`Var[(1/L)sum_{t in S}g_t(c)] <= 8/L`.

**Proof.** Unbiasedness is symmetry of simple random sampling. For population mean `mu` and population variance

`sigma^2=(1/p)sum_t (g_t-mu)^2`,

the exact without-replacement formula is

`Var(sample mean)=(p-L)/(L(p-1))*sigma^2`.

By Lemma U054.2, `sigma^2 <= (1/p)sum_t g_t^2 <8`. `QED`

## 4. Simultaneous approximation of the entire finite visible generator

For cutoff K, include one coordinate for every specified unordered nontrivial split of every visible subset of `[K]`. The exact number of coordinates is

`D_K = sum_{m=2}^K binom(K,m)(2^{m-1}-1)`

`= (3^K-2^{K+1}+1)/2`.

For a random equal-weight L-subset S, Chebyshev and a union bound give

`P(max_c |q_S(c)-q_full(c)| > eps) <= 8 D_K/(L eps^2)`.

Now let `B_N -> infinity` be **any** integer support budget and set

`ell_N=min(B_N,p_N)`, `eps_N=ell_N^{-1/8}`.

Define `K_N` to be the largest `k>=1` (capped by N if desired) such that

`16 D_k <= ell_N^{3/4}`,

using `D_1=0`. Since `ell_N -> infinity`, we have `K_N -> infinity`.

If `ell_N<p_N`, then

`8D_{K_N}/(ell_N eps_N^2)`

`=8D_{K_N}/ell_N^{3/4} <= 1/2`.

Hence at least one `ell_N`-subset has simultaneous error at most `eps_N` on **every** generator coordinate supported inside `[K_N]`. If `ell_N=p_N`, use all translations, for zero error.

This proves existence without tuned weights.

## 5. Method #19 — make the selection deterministic

Among all `ell_N`-subsets satisfying the preceding simultaneous inequalities, choose the lexicographically first. This is a finite deterministic algorithm: enumerate subsets in lexicographic order and compute the finitely many rational split rates. No efficiency claim is made.

For each retained translation `t` and each threshold `u`, run an independent clock at rate

`c_N(t,u)=p_N/[2 ell_N u(p_N-u)]`.

All retained translations therefore carry **exactly the same weight**. The total proposal intensity per active clade is unchanged:

`sum_{t in S_N}sum_{u=1}^{p_N-1} p_N/[2ell_N u(p_N-u)]`

`= H_{p_N-1}`.

Because `phi_N` is a permutation, for any two distinct ambient labels a,b and any fixed retained translation t, the ranks `phi_N(t+a)` and `phi_N(t+b)` are distinct; some threshold lies strictly between them. Thus every pair is separated by a positive-rate proposal and the recursion produces a finite rooted binary tree almost surely.

## 6. U054 — sharp equal-weight support theorem

**Theorem U054.** Within the frozen power-permutation threshold-translation architecture, equal translation weights do not change the sharp support condition from U053:

`L_N -> infinity`

is sufficient, and bounded support along a subsequence is impossible, for convergence of every fixed restriction to the CTCS generator. More precisely, for every integer budget `B_N -> infinity` the deterministic equal-weight construction above gives, for every fixed k and finite T,

`|| Law(Pi_N^[k]|_[0,T]) - Law(Pi_CTCS^[k]|_[0,T]) ||_TV -> 0`.

Hence `Pi_N^[k] => Pi_CTCS^[k]` in `J_1` for every fixed k, and the consistent full process converges to CTCS(infinity) in R11's frozen summable projective finite-restriction topology.

**Proof.** Fix k. Since `K_N -> infinity`, eventually `k <= K_N`. For every specified split coordinate on every subset of `[k]`, the selected equal-weight generator differs from the full C072 generator by at most `eps_N ->0`. C072's corresponding generator coordinates converge to the CTCS beta-split rates

`B(i,j)=(i-1)!(j-1)!/(i+j-1)!`.

Therefore the selected generators converge entrywise to the finite CTCS generator.

For a state partition of `[k]`, visible-changing transitions are obtained by splitting one current visible block. The number of possible off-diagonal splits is at most `2^{k-1}-1`. Thus the row `l_1` distance between selected and C072 generators is at most

`2(2^{k-1}-1) eps_N`,

where the factor two includes the diagonal. Hidden labels lump out exactly, for the same reason as in C072: a visible-changing hazard is determined solely by the ambient mask restricted to the visible labels; hidden-only splits leave the visible state unchanged and transfer its visible block to a descendant with fresh clocks of the same law.

On the finite partition state space, entrywise generator convergence implies convergence of the CTMC path law on `[0,T]`; a common-clock uniformization gives total-variation convergence. This is stronger than `J_1` convergence. Consistency of the finite restrictions then yields convergence in the fixed summable projective topology.

For necessity, suppose at most L translations are retained along an infinite subsequence, for fixed L. One translation gives a total order of any k visible labels; sweeping the threshold realizes at most `k-1` unordered nontrivial cuts. Hence L translations can give positive rate to at most `L(k-1)` of the `2^{k-1}-1` possible unordered splits. Choose fixed k with

`2^{k-1}-1 > L(k-1)`.

At least one specified split has rate zero along that subsequence, while CTCS gives every specified `i|(k-i)` split the positive rate `B(i,k-i)`. Generator convergence is impossible. `QED`

Thus the `sqrt(p)` barrier from C073 was only an artifact of an equal-weight **interval** support and incomplete exponential-sum completion, and U053's tuned Caratheodory weights are unnecessary for the sharp support threshold.
