# 4. U061 — direct whole-path coupling without the `2^k` penalty

Retain the U060 fresh-exchangeable-dependence setup. At every harmonic-grid mark

\[
x_s=s/N,\qquad 1\le s\le N-1,
\]

a nonsingleton active clade has a proposal clock of rate

\[
\lambda_{N,s}=\frac{N}{2s(N-s)}=\frac{1}{2N x_s(1-x_s)}.
\]

A fresh exchangeable random partition `Xi_N` of `[N]` is sampled, its blocks are colored independently Bernoulli(`x_s`), and the active clade is split according to those inherited colors if both colors occur. Put

\[
c_N:=\Pr(1\sim_{\Xi_N}2).
\]

## Theorem U061

For every `k<=N` and `T<infinity`, let `Pi_N^Xi[k]` be the `[k]` restriction of this hierarchy and `Pi_CTCS[k]` the exact CTCS restriction. Then

\[
\boxed{
\left\|
\mathcal L(\Pi_N^{\Xi,[k]}|_{[0,T]})-
\mathcal L(\Pi_{\rm CTCS}^{[k]}|_{[0,T]})
\right\|_{\rm TV}
\le
\min\left\{1,
T\left[
\binom{k}{2}c_N\frac{N-1}{N}
+\frac{2(k-1)H_{k-1}}{N}
\right]
\right\}.
}
\tag{4.1}
\]

Consequently any sequence `k_N` satisfying

\[
\boxed{
k_N^2c_N+\frac{k_N\log(k_N+1)}{N}\to0}
\tag{4.2}
\]

has whole-path total-variation convergence on every finite horizon.

### Proof, part A: fresh dependence versus iid harmonic-grid colors

Fix a currently active visible block `B` of size `m` and one mark `x=x_s`. Couple the dependence coloring and an iid Bernoulli(`x`) coloring as follows. After sampling `Xi_N|_B`, choose one representative from each restricted dependence block. Give each representative an independent Bernoulli(`x`) color. The dependence model copies that color to every label in the same dependence block. For the iid comparator, keep each representative's color and independently resample Bernoulli(`x`) colors for every nonrepresentative.

The comparator colors are genuinely iid. If a restricted dependence block has size `r`, the probability that at least one of its `r-1` nonrepresentatives differs from the representative is at most

\[
2x(1-x)(r-1).
\]

Since `r-1 <= C(r,2)`, summing over dependence blocks and taking expectation gives

\[
\Pr(\text{color vectors disagree})
\le
2x(1-x)\binom m2 c_N.
\tag{4.3}
\]

Multiplication by the proposal rate cancels the singular harmonic factor:

\[
\lambda_{N,s}\Pr(\text{disagreement})
\le
\frac{1}{N}\binom m2c_N.
\tag{4.4}
\]

Summing the `N-1` marks gives a disagreement hazard at most

\[
\binom m2c_N\frac{N-1}{N}.
\tag{4.5}
\]

Before the first disagreement, the two visible partition processes have the same current blocks. If their block sizes are `m_1,m_2,...`, then

\[
\sum_b\binom{m_b}{2}\le\binom{k}{2}.
\]

Therefore the probability of any dependence-vs-iid path disagreement before time `T` is at most

\[
T\binom{k}{2}c_N\frac{N-1}{N}.
\tag{4.6}
\]

### Proof, part B: iid harmonic grid versus exact CTCS

For an m-label active block and a nonempty proper **oriented** red set `A` of size `i`, write `j=m-i`. The iid harmonic-grid oriented jump rate is

\[
r_N(A)=\frac1{2N}\sum_{s=1}^{N-1}
\left(\frac{s}{N}\right)^{i-1}
\left(1-\frac{s}{N}\right)^{j-1}.
\tag{4.7}
\]

The exact CTCS oriented rate is

\[
r(A)=\frac12B(i,j).
\tag{4.8}
\]

Indeed the source has total m-block split rate `H_{m-1}` and size law

\[
q(m,i)=\frac{m}{2H_{m-1}i(m-i)};
\]

summing the two left/right orientations and choosing the specified labels uniformly gives the unordered specified rate `B(i,j)`, hence half of this for one color orientation.

Put

\[
h_{i,j}(x)=x^{i-1}(1-x)^{j-1}.
\]

For any bounded-variation `h`, the right rectangle rule obeys

\[
\left|\frac1N\sum_{s=1}^{N}h(s/N)-\int_0^1h(x)\,dx\right|
\le\frac{\operatorname{Var}(h)}N.
\]

Removing the `s=N` endpoint and using the unimodality/monotonicity of `h_{i,j}` yields uniformly

\[
\left|\frac1N\sum_{s=1}^{N-1}h_{i,j}(s/N)-B(i,j)\right|
\le\frac{2\sup h_{i,j}}N.
\tag{4.9}
\]

Also

\[
\binom{m-2}{i-1}h_{i,j}(x)\le1
\]

because the left side is a binomial probability mass. Therefore

\[
\sup h_{i,j}\le\binom{m-2}{i-1}^{-1}.
\tag{4.10}
\]

Using (4.7)--(4.10), the total absolute difference of the **oriented** jump measures from one m-block is bounded by

\[
\begin{aligned}
\sum_{\varnothing\ne A\subsetneq[m]}|r_N(A)-r(A)|
&\le
\frac1N\sum_{i=1}^{m-1}
\frac{\binom mi}{\binom{m-2}{i-1}}\\
&=\frac{m(m-1)}N\sum_{i=1}^{m-1}\frac1{i(m-i)}\\
&=\boxed{\frac{2(m-1)H_{m-1}}N}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.11}
\]

Pushing oriented colorings to unordered binary splits cannot increase total variation. Across all current visible blocks,

\[
\sum_b(m_b-1)H_{m_b-1}
\le (k-1)H_{k-1},
\]

so the iid-grid versus CTCS path disagreement hazard is at most

\[
\frac{2(k-1)H_{k-1}}N.
\tag{4.12}
\]

Couple both stages until their first disagreement and apply the union bound (equivalently, add the mismatch intensities). Equations (4.6) and (4.12) prove (4.1). Since `H_k=O(log(k+1))`, (4.2) follows.

### Consequence for the source topology

For every fixed `k`, if `c_N->0`, the right side of (4.1) tends to zero. Thus the complete partition-valued `[k]` path converges in total variation on every finite horizon. This is stronger than `J_1` convergence on the finite-state path space. Since CTCS(infinity) is the consistent projective family of these finite restrictions, this establishes R11's frozen projective finite-restriction convergence criterion. No weaker root-split or one-time marginal is substituted.

# 5. C081 — fresh perfect/near-perfect matching dependence

For `N>=3`, define `Xi_N` at every proposal as follows:

- if `N` is even, draw a uniformly random perfect matching of `[N]` and let its edges be the dependence blocks;
- if `N` is odd, draw a uniformly random near-perfect matching, with one uniformly random singleton, and let the matched edges plus singleton be the dependence blocks.

At mark `x_s=s/N`, independently color every dependence block Bernoulli(`x_s`); active-clade labels inherit those colors and split if both colors occur. A fresh matching and fresh colors are sampled at every proposal and on every child. For the harmless exceptional size `N=2`, use the iid harmonic-grid rule so the finite hierarchy always separates.

This is a discrete finite combinatorial mechanism: a random 1-factor/near-1-factor and a finite harmonic mark drive each binary split. Exponential proposal clocks provide the continuous-time embedding needed to compare to CTCS.

## Collision probability

By `S_N` symmetry, for even `N` a fixed label is matched to each of the other `N-1` labels equally often, hence

\[
\boxed{c_N=\frac1{N-1}.}
\tag{5.1}
\]

For odd `N`, the expected number of 2-blocks is `(N-1)/2`; dividing the expected number of colliding unordered pairs by `C(N,2)` gives

\[
\boxed{c_N=\frac1N.}
\tag{5.2}
\]

Thus `c_N=O(1/N)`.

Applying U061,

\[
\left\|

\right\|_{\rm TV}

T\left[O\left(\frac{k^2}{N}\right)
+\frac{2(k-1)H_{k-1}}N
\right]
\right\}.
\tag{5.3}
\]

Hence for every fixed `k` the complete genealogy converges to CTCS, and more strongly

\[
\boxed{k_N=o(\sqrt N)\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\text{whole `[k_N]` path-TV convergence on every finite horizon}.}
\tag{5.4}
\]

Therefore

\[
\boxed{\Pi_{N,081}\Rightarrow\Pi_{\operatorname{CTCS}(\infty)}}

in the frozen projective finite-restriction sense.
