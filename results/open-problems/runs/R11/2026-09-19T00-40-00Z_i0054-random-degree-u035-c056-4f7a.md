## 2. Move #2 — attack the negation: pairwise independence is not enough

C055 used polynomial dimension `d_N -> infinity`, which gave an exact deterministic local-independence window growing to infinity. The first negation to test is whether one can freeze the polynomial dimension at `d=2` and still get CTCS because the harmonic threshold law has the correct pair tangent.

Take an affine hash over `F_q`,

\[
P(x)=A+Bx,
\qquad A,B\stackrel{iid}{\sim}\operatorname{Unif}(F_q),
\tag{2.1}
\]

and use exactly C055's threshold law

\[
\Pr(U=u)=\frac{1}{W_q}\frac1{u(q-u)},
\qquad
W_q=\sum_{v=1}^{q-1}\frac1{v(q-v)}=\frac{2H_{q-1}}q.
\tag{2.2}
\]

Set

\[
S=\{x:\operatorname{rep}P(x)<U\}.
\tag{2.3}
\]

Any two distinct evaluations are independent uniform field elements, so the pair law and pair normalization are exactly the same as C055:

\[
P_N(1,1)=\frac{2(q-1)}{q^2W_q},
\qquad
\lambda_N=P_N(1,1)^{-1}=\frac{q}{q-1}H_{q-1}.
\tag{2.4}
\]

This makes `d=2` a plausible counterexample to the belief that the deterministic `K_N->infinity` hypothesis of U034 is necessary. It fails, and the first failure occurs at four visible labels.

### 2.1 Every three-label binary split is fooled exactly

For three membership bits `z_0,z_1,z_2`, the unordered split `{0}|{1,2}` has indicator

\[
z_0(1-z_1)(1-z_2)+(1-z_0)z_1z_2
=z_0-z_0z_1-z_0z_2+z_1z_2.
\tag{2.5}
\]

The cubic term cancels. Therefore its expectation is determined entirely by one- and two-point moments. Pairwise independence reproduces the fully independent paintbox split probability **exactly**. By symmetry the same is true for every nontrivial binary split of three labels.

Thus no test on one, two, or three labels can disprove CTCS for this affine-hash candidate.

### 2.2 Four labels detect the obstruction

Consider four consecutive labels `0,1,2,3`. In the large-field limit, conditional on threshold fraction `t`, the affine evaluations converge to the torus progression

\[
(Y_0,Y_1,Y_2,Y_3)
=(a,a+b,a+2b,a+3b)\pmod1,
\tag{2.6}
\]

with `(a,b)` uniform on the two-torus. Let `p(t)` be the probability that the threshold cut realizes the specified balanced split `{0,1}|{2,3}`, either orientation. Exact polygon decomposition on the torus gives

\[
\boxed{
p(t)=
\begin{cases}
 t^2, & 0\le t\le 1/3,\\
 -2t^2+2t-1/3, & 1/3\le t\le 2/3,\\
 (1-t)^2, & 2/3\le t\le1.
\end{cases}}
\tag{2.7}
\]

Here is a direct derivation. For one orientation `Y_0,Y_1<t\le Y_2,Y_3` and `t\le1/2`, only the floor patterns `(0,0,0,0)` and `(0,1,1,2)` contribute, with equal area. For `0\le t\le1/3`, each contributing cell is the triangle with vertices

\[
(t,0),\quad(0,t),\quad(0,t/2),
\]

of area `t^2/4`. For `1/3\le t\le1/2`, each is the polygon with vertices

\[
(t,0),\quad((3t-1)/2,(1-t)/2),\quad(0,1/3),\quad(0,t/2),
\]

of area `-t^2/2+t/2-1/12`. Multiplying by the two floor cells and two orientations gives (2.7) on `[0,1/2]`; complement symmetry gives the second half.

Under the harmonic threshold law, the pair-normalized limiting balanced rate is

\[
L
=\frac12\int_0^1\frac{p(t)}{t(1-t)}\,dt
=\int_0^{1/2}\frac{p(t)}{t(1-t)}\,dt.
\tag{2.8}
\]

Using (2.7),

\[
\begin{aligned}
L
&=\int_0^{1/3}\frac{t}{1-t}\,dt
 +\int_{1/3}^{1/2}\left(2-\frac1{3t(1-t)}\right)dt\\
&=\boxed{\log3-\frac43\log2}\\
&=0.174416\ldots.
\end{aligned}
\tag{2.9}
\]

CTCS instead requires

\[
B(2,2)=\frac16=0.166666\ldots.
\tag{2.10}
\]

The inequality is rigorous without decimal numerics:

\[
6L-1=\log(729/256)-1>0,
\tag{2.11}
\]

because

\[
\frac{729}{256}>\frac{11}{4}>e.
\]

For the last inequality, `e=1+1+1/2+\sum_{n\ge3}1/n!<5/2+1/4=11/4`.

So the fixed affine/pairwise-independent model is **not** CTCS. This is a useful obstruction, not movement in the class-count metric by itself.

## U035 — typical-order limited-independence CTCS criterion

For each `N`, let `(D_N,X_N)` satisfy:

1. `D_N` is integer-valued with `D_N>=2`;
2. `D_N` and `X_N in (0,1)` are independent;
3. conditional on `(D_N=d,X_N=x)`, the membership indicators of **any specified set of at most `d` labels** are exactly iid Bernoulli(`x`);
4. `D_N -> infinity` in probability.

No global exchangeability is assumed, and there need not exist any deterministic sequence `K_N->infinity` for which the unconditional proposal is exactly `K_N`-wise paintbox.

Let

\[
p_N:=2E[X_N(1-X_N)],\qquad \lambda_N=p_N^{-1},
\tag{4.1}
\]

and define the pair-normalized paintbox measure

\[
\rho_N
=\lambda_NE\left[
X_N(1-X_N)(\delta_{X_N}+\delta_{1-X_N})
\right].
\tag{4.2}
\]

Assume

\[
\rho_N\Rightarrow dx.
\tag{4.3}
\]

Then every fixed specified binary split `A|B`, with `|A|=i, |B|=j`, satisfies

\[
\boxed{
\lambda_NP_N(A|B)\longrightarrow B(i,j).
}
\tag{4.4}
\]

### Proof of the rate criterion

Put `k=i+j` and let `G_N(i,j)` be the exact normalized paintbox rate,

\[
G_N(i,j)
:=\int_0^1x^{i-1}(1-x)^{j-1}\rho_N(dx).
\tag{4.5}
\]

On `{D_N>=k}`, the specified split law is exactly the paintbox law, so its normalized rate is `G_N(i,j)`.

On `{D_N<k}`, choose any fixed cross pair `(a,b) in A x B`. The split event implies that this pair is separated. Because `D_N>=2`, that pair is still exactly Bernoulli-paintbox distributed conditional on every possible degree. Independence of `D_N` and `X_N` makes its separation probability exactly `p_N`, so

\[
0\le \lambda_NP_N(A|B\mid D_N=d)\le1
\qquad(d<k).
\tag{4.6}
\]

The normalized paintbox target `G_N(i,j)` also lies in `[0,1]`, by the same cross-pair domination. Consequently

\[
\boxed{
\left|\lambda_NP_N(A|B)-G_N(i,j)\right|
\le \Pr(D_N<k).
}
\tag{4.7}
\]

The right side tends to zero, and (4.3) gives `G_N(i,j)->B(i,j)`. This proves (4.4).

### Process-level compiler

Use physical mesh

\[
\delta_N=\min\{N^{-2},\lambda_N^{-3}\}
\tag{4.8}
\]

and let every current nonsingleton block independently attempt a fresh proposal with probability

\[
a_N=\delta_N\lambda_N.
\tag{4.9}
\]

At each attempt draw a fresh independent environment `(D_N,X_N,S_N)` and split the block by intersection with `S_N` and its complement.

Fix `k`. The finite visible partition state space `P_k` has finitely many possible specified split transitions. Equation (4.7), uniformly over those finitely many label patterns, gives convergence of every one-block transition rate to its CTCS beta rate. Fresh proposals mean hidden labels leave no residual environment in the visible state. Two visible blocks changing in the same raw step has probability `O_k(a_N^2)`, and after division by the mesh its contribution is

\[
O_k(\delta_N\lambda_N^2).
\tag{4.10}
\]

For `lambda_N>=1`,

\[
\delta_N\lambda_N^2
=\min\{\lambda_N^2/N^2,1/\lambda_N\}
\le N^{-2/3}.
\tag{4.11}
\]

Thus the finite-state transition matrices satisfy

\[
K_N^{[k]}=I+\delta_NQ_{CTCS}^{[k]}+o_k(\delta_N).
\tag{4.12}
\]

Finite-state Euler/uniformization convergence gives

\[
\Pi_N^{[k]}\Longrightarrow\Pi_{CTCS}^{[k]}
\quad\text{in }D([0,T],P_k),\ J_1,
\tag{4.13}
\]

for every fixed `k,T`. The first `K` projective coordinates are all determined by the `[K]` path, while the tail of the summable projective metric is at most `\sum_{k>K}2^{-k}`. Therefore

\[
\boxed{
\Pi_N\Longrightarrow\Pi_{CTCS(\infty)}

\tag{4.14}

in the frozen projective path topology.

\tag{5.2}

\tag{5.3}

\tag{5.4}

S_N=\{x\in[N]:\operatorname{rep}P(x)<U\}.
\tag{5.5}
\]

Conditional on `D_N=d`, any `d` distinct evaluations are exactly independent uniform field elements by the Vandermonde evaluation map. Thus U035 applies with `X_N=U/q`.

\tag{5.10}

\[
\boxed{\lambda_NP_N(i,j)\to B(i,j).}
\tag{5.11}

\delta_N\lambda_N^2

\tag{5.12}

Hence for every fixed restriction,

\[
\boxed{
\Pi_{N,056}^{[k]}\Longrightarrow\Pi_{CTCS}^{[k]}

\tag{5.13}
