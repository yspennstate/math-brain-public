## 4. Metric theorem

### R04-FM-C1 — diagonal-conditioned Wegner–Minami count bound

**PROVED (by reduction to published spectral-averaging/Minami inequalities).** Assume
\[
2W+1\le N.
\]
Then
\[
\boxed{
\mathbb E X_N^2
\le
B_{c,N}:=
2\pi(2W+1)+\sqrt{2\pi(2W+1)},
\qquad W=\lfloor c\sqrt N\rfloor.
}
\tag{R04.1}
\]
Equivalently,
\[
\boxed{
\mathbb E X_N^2
\le
2\pi(2\lfloor c\sqrt N\rfloor+1)
+
\sqrt{2\pi(2\lfloor c\sqrt N\rfloor+1)}.
}
\tag{R04.2}
\]
For fixed `c`, this is
\[
B_{c,N}=4\pi c\sqrt N+O_c(N^{1/4}+1).
\]

### Full proof

Condition on the sigma-field generated by **all off-diagonal upper-triangular variables**. Since those variables are independent of the diagonal variables, conditionally
\[
H_N=A+\sum_{i=1}^N\omega_i|e_i\rangle\langle e_i|,
\tag{R04.3}
\]
where `A` is an arbitrary deterministic self-adjoint matrix (it contains the entire frozen band-dependent off-diagonal background) and the remaining diagonal variables are independent with
\[
\omega_i\sim N(0,v),\qquad v=(2W+1)^{-1}.
\]
Their common density is
\[
\rho_v(x)=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi v}}e^{-x^2/(2v)},
\]
so
\[
\rho_*:=\|\rho_v\|_\infty
=\frac1{\sqrt{2\pi v}}
=\sqrt{\frac{2W+1}{2\pi}}.
\tag{R04.4}
\]

Combes–Germinet–Klein, *J. Stat. Phys.* 135 (2009), 201–216, Eq. (2.2), gives rank-one spectral averaging with
\[
Q_\mu(s)=\|\rho\|_\infty s
\]
for bounded density. Their generalized Anderson form permits arbitrary deterministic self-adjoint `H_0`; geometry of `A` is irrelevant after conditioning. Summing the rank-one estimate gives the Wegner bound (their Eq. (2.4)):
\[
\mathbb E[X_N\mid A]
\le N\rho_*|I_N|.
\tag{R04.5}
\]

The same paper records the Minami factorial-moment estimate as Eq. (1.4):
\[
\mathbb E[X_N(X_N-1)\mid A]
\le (N\rho_*|I_N|)^2.
\tag{R04.6}
\]
This is the bounded-density constant-one version; I checked the open-access PDF directly, including the definition of `Q_mu` and Eq. (1.4), rather than using the older `pi rho_*` form quoted as Eq. (1.3).

Since
\[
X_N^2=X_N+X_N(X_N-1),
\]
(R04.5)–(R04.6) imply
\[
\mathbb E[X_N^2\mid A]
\le a_N+a_N^2,
\qquad
a_N:=N\rho_*|I_N|.
\tag{R04.7}
\]
Now `|I_N|=2\pi/N`, so
\[
a_N
=N\sqrt{\frac{2W+1}{2\pi}}\frac{2\pi}{N}
=\sqrt{2\pi(2W+1)}
\tag{R04.8}
\]
and
\[
a_N^2=2\pi(2W+1).
\]
Substitution into (R04.7), followed by expectation over the frozen off-diagonal background, proves (R04.1)–(R04.2). QED.

Authoritative estimate source checked directly:
`https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10955-009-9731-3`, PDF Eq. (1.4), (2.2), (2.4).

### Scope

This is a **uniform finite-N bound** over all off-diagonal backgrounds, hence it fully preserves the exact band dependence by conditioning rather than replacing the matrix by a mean-field ensemble. But it grows like `sqrt(N)`, so it does **not** prove O(1) microscopic count tightness and does not identify any limiting point process.

## 5. Exact two-site block resolvent reduction

The same conditioning exposes an exact block Schur complement behind the two-site Minami step. Fix `Lambda={i,j}` and condition on all variables except the two diagonal coordinates `omega_i,omega_j`. Write
\[
H-z=
\begin{pmatrix}
V_\Lambda+B_{\Lambda\Lambda}^{(0)}-zI_2 & C\\
C^* & H_{\Lambda^c\Lambda^c}-z
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
V_\Lambda=\operatorname{diag}(\omega_i,\omega_j),
\tag{R04.9}
\]
where every matrix except `V_Lambda` is frozen and retains the true band sparsity/profile. Whenever `Im z>0`, the lower-right block is invertible, and the exact Schur formula is
\[
\boxed{
G_{\Lambda\Lambda}(z)
=
\left[
V_\Lambda+B_{\Lambda\Lambda}^{(0)}-zI_2
-C(H_{\Lambda^c\Lambda^c}-z)^{-1}C^*
\right]^{-1}.
}
\tag{R04.10}
\]
Equivalently, with
\[
A_\Lambda(z)
:=zI_2-B_{\Lambda\Lambda}^{(0)}
+C(H_{\Lambda^c\Lambda^c}-z)^{-1}C^*,
\]
we have
\[
G_{\Lambda\Lambda}(z)=[V_\Lambda-A_\Lambda(z)]^{-1}.
\tag{R04.11}
\]

This is an exact block-resolvent reduction at every N. It is **not** yet a transfer-matrix scaling limit. At `W^2/N=Theta(1)`, the 2025 supercritical local laws do not provide the missing critical microscopic two-point estimate needed to replace the `sqrt(N)` bound by O(1).
