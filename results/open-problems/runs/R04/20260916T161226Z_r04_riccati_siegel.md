## 4. R04-FM-C5 — exact symplectic transfer and inverse-free Riccati/Siegel recursion

**Status: PROVED on the exact divisible `W`-block reduction.**

Retain the exact blocking from the previous report on a divisible subsequence `N=K W`, `K>=3`. For block vectors `u_j in R^W`,

`B_{j-1}^T u_{j-1} + D_j u_j + B_j u_{j+1} = z u_j`,

where `D_j` is a full real-symmetric `W x W` Gaussian block and `B_j` is lower triangular with `(B_j)_{pq} != 0` iff `q<=p`; every independent nonzero coordinate has target variance `v=(2W+1)^(-1)`. The pairs `(D_j,B_j)` are iid over `j`, including the circular seam.

Almost surely `B_j` is invertible. Define

`M_j(z) = [[B_j^{-1}(zI-D_j), -B_j^{-1}], [B_j^T, 0]]`

and `J=[[0,-I],[I,0]]`. Then

`M_j(z)^T J M_j(z)=J`.

**Proof.** Put `A=zI-D_j`, `P=B_j^{-1}A`, `Q=-B_j^{-1}`, `R=B_j^T`. Direct block multiplication gives

`M^T J M = [[-P^T R + R^T P, R^T Q],[-Q^T R,0]]`.

Since `A^T=A`, `P^T R=A^T B_j^{-T}B_j^T=A` and `R^T P=B_j B_j^{-1}A=A`; also `R^T Q=-I` and `-Q^T R=I`. Hence the matrix equals `J`. For real `z`, `M_j(z)` is therefore real symplectic. QED.

Now let `R_0=R_0^T` be a symmetric graph coordinate and assume `zI-D_j-R_0` invertible. Acting on the Lagrangian graph `[I;R_0]`, the top and bottom blocks are

`T=B_j^{-1}(zI-D_j-R_0)` and `U=B_j^T`.

Therefore the image graph is represented by the exact Möbius/Riccati map

`Phi_{j,z}(R_0)=U T^{-1}=B_j^T (zI-D_j-R_0)^(-1) B_j`.

This is precisely the one-step Schur-complement recursion, and **all factors `B_j^{-1}` cancel from the projective update**.

For `z=E+i eta`, `eta>0`, and complex symmetric `R_0` with `Im R_0<=0`, set `A=zI-D_j-R_0`. Then `Im A=eta I-Im R_0>=eta I`, so `A` is invertible and

`Im A^{-1} = - A^{-*} (Im A) A^{-1}`.

Consequently

`-Im Phi_{j,z}(R_0)=B_j^T A^{-*}(eta I-Im R_0)A^{-1}B_j >=0`.

Thus the lower Siegel half-space is invariant. The dissipativity inequality `||A^{-1}||<=eta^{-1}` also gives

`||Phi_{j,z}(R_0)|| <= eta^{-1} ||B_j||^2`.

**Meaning.** The previous report showed that raw `B_j^{-1}` has `W^(3/2)` inverse spikes with nonvanishing probability. `E[B X B^T]=v diag(X_11, sum_{a<=2}X_aa, ..., sum_{a<=W}X_aa)`.

## 6. R04-FM-C7 — exact structured singular-value moments of the triangular coupling block

`E ||c_q||^4 = v^2(n_q^2+2n_q)`
