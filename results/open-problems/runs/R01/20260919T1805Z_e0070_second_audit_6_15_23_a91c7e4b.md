## 5. Independent reconstruction of the construction

Take

\[
G=\operatorname{Sym}_{\rm fin}(\mathbb N_0),
\qquad H=\operatorname{Stab}_G(0),
\qquad \tau_n=(0\ n)\quad(n\ge1),
\]

and choose any strictly positive probability vector `(p_n)`, in particular the explicit source witness

\[
p_n=2^{-n},\qquad \mu(\tau_n)=p_n.
\]

All source-side structural obligations check directly.

**Countability.** Every finitary permutation is supported on some finite initial segment, so `G` is a countable union of finite symmetric groups.

**Measured-group semigroup generation.** For `i != j`,

\[
(0\ i)(0\ j)(0\ i)=(i\ j).
\]

Hence star transpositions generate every finite transposition and therefore every finitary permutation. Because each star transposition is an involution, group generation is already semigroup generation.

**Symmetry.** Every support element is an involution, so `\check\mu=\mu` exactly.

**Infinite-index quotient.** The map

\[
G/H\longrightarrow\mathbb N_0,\qquad gH\longmapsto g(0)
\]

is well-defined and bijective. If `g(0)=g'(0)`, then `g^{-1}g'` fixes zero and lies in `H`. Thus `[G:H]=infinity`.

This settles every group/quotient interface before the harmonic argument begins.

## 6. Independent reconstruction of the quotient operator

Under `G/H ≅ N_0`, left multiplication by `tau_n` acts exactly as the transposition `(0 n)` on the label. Therefore the reflected left convolution operator is the infinite-star kernel

\[
(Pu)(0)=\sum_{n\ge1}p_nu(n),
\]

and, for every `k>=1`,

\[
(Pu)(k)=p_ku(0)+(1-p_k)u(k).
\]

The quotient orientation is important: `G/H` is represented by cosets `gH`, and left multiplication sends `gH` to `tau_n gH`; the label becomes `tau_n(g(0))`. No left/right coset reversal is present.

For a source-valid quasiharmonic function the pullback to `G` is `mu`-integrable. At the basepoint this is exactly

\[
\sum_{n\ge1}p_n|u(n)|<\infty.
\]

At a leaf only the two values `u(0),u(k)` occur, so no additional infinite-tail convergence issue is hidden there.

## 7. Main proof — arbitrary defect is impossible

Suppose

\[
Pu=u+c
\]

for an arbitrary complex constant `c`. At leaf `k>=1`,

\[
p_ku(0)+(1-p_k)u(k)=u(k)+c,
\]

hence

\[
\boxed{u(k)=u(0)-\frac{c}{p_k}}.
\]

If `c != 0`, then

\[
p_k|u(k)|=|p_ku(0)-c|.
\]

Since every positive summable sequence has `p_k -> 0`, these summands converge to `|c|>0`. They therefore cannot be summable, contradicting source integrability at the basepoint. Hence

\[
\boxed{c=0}.
\]

The same leaf identity now gives `u(k)=u(0)` for every `k>=1`; thus `u` is constant. Ordinary harmonicity is included as the special case `c=0`.

This is the full source quantifier: no boundedness, Lipschitzness, finite support of `mu`, or hidden normalization of `u(0)` has been inserted.
