\[

\]

\[

\]

The R01 formula is

\[

\inf_{R:\,BR=I_{\mathcal H_0}}
\sup_{0\ne b\in\mathcal H_0}
\frac{(Rb)^TC^{-1}(Rb)}{b^T\Pi^{-1}b}.

\tag{4.1}
\]

Define the weighted divergence

\[

\tag{4.2}
\]

For a demand `b in H_0`, put `y=Pi^{-1/2}b`, and write a physical flow as `theta=C^{1/2}x`. Then

\[

\]

while

\[

\qquad
b^T\Pi^{-1}b=\|y\|_2^2.
\tag{4.3}

\[

\tag{4.4}

because every right inverse must send a minimum left singular vector to a solution of norm at least `1/sigma_min`, while the Moore--Penrose inverse attains equality.

Now

\[

=\Pi^{-1/2}BCB^T\Pi^{-1/2}

\tag{4.5}
\]

Reversibility makes `Pi^{1/2} P Pi^{-1/2}` symmetric and similar to `P`. Hence on `sqrt(pi)^perp` the eigenvalues of `DD^T` are

\[

\tag{4.6}
