## 4. Method #18 — SAY IT IN ANOTHER LANGUAGE: translate CL-A into a closure obligation instead of a slogan

`https://randomstrasse101.math.ethz.ch/posts/clique-low-degree/`

The first open box on that page is labeled `Open Problem 35` despite the post title's inconsistent numbering. Its source statement asks, in substance:

> Find conditions on distributions `P_n,Q_n` under which bounded low-degree advantage through degree `(log n)^{1+Omega(1)}` rigorously implies that polynomials of some degree cannot distinguish `P_n` from `Q_n`.

The post itself records that the older Hopkins-style broad conjectural picture has counterexamples and cites Hsieh--Kane--Kothari--Li--Mohanty--Tiegel (2026) as a partial rigorous salvage for vector/matrix settings.

## 6. Method #30 — ONE SCALE / ONE DIRECTION AT A TIME: isolate CL-C exactly, then classify the lane honestly

### Source

The final open box on the same Randomstrasse post asks for a multivariate polynomial

`f : {0,1}^{binom(n,2)} -> R^n`

of degree `O(log n)` that finds a clique of size `log_2 n` in `G(n,1/2)`, with output semantics similar to Definition 1.1 of Alexander Wein's low-degree optimization framework: coordinates above the inclusion threshold identify clique vertices, coordinates below the exclusion threshold identify nonmembers, and only a small number of specified mistakes are allowed.

The source explicitly says that a polynomial-time greedy algorithm finds a clique of size about `log_2 n`, but it is **not clear** whether this algorithm can be represented by a polynomial of degree `O(log n)` or replaced by an equivalent polynomial with similar performance.

Wein's primary paper, *Optimal Low-Degree Hardness of Maximum Independent Set* (Mathematical Statistics and Learning 4 (2022), arXiv:2010.06563), supplies the low-degree algorithm/output formalism cited by the source. The Randomstrasse page itself, updated/current in 2026, still presents the matching low-degree achievability direction as open. Fresh searches for `degree O(log n) polynomial`, `log_2 n clique`, and low-degree greedy-clique representations did not surface a later primary resolution.

CL-C is much more concrete than CL-A, but concreteness is not the same as CLOSABILITY. The source target is an asymptotic algorithmic construction simultaneously requiring:
