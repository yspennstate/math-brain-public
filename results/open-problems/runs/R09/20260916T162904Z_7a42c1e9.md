## 3. R09-C180: composition-averaged completion of the radius-4 failure certificate

## 5. R09-C181: sharper uniform passage to criticality

STATUS: PROVED. This is an analytic theorem; the finite constant arithmetic is separately checked exactly.

Let R_L^{(1)}={0 connects to S_L} and rho_p^{(1)}(L)=P_p(R_L^{(1)}). Since reaching the cube boundary implies reaching l1-distance L,

P_p(A_L)<=rho_p^{(1)}(L).

R_L^{(1)} depends only on the l1 ball, whose exact vertex count is

V_L=(4L^3+6L^2+8L+3)/3.

For any increasing event A of N Bernoulli-s SITE coordinates let a(s)=P_s(A), and I_i(s)=P_s(i pivotal). Put g_i=(X_i-s)/sqrt(s(1-s)). The g_i are orthonormal and monotonicity gives

Cov(1_A,X_i)=s(1-s) I_i(s).

Bessel therefore yields

s(1-s) sum_i I_i(s)^2 <= a(s)(1-a(s)).

Russo plus Cauchy--Schwarz gives

a'(s)<=sqrt(N a(s)(1-a(s))/(s(1-s))).

For Psi(x)=arcsin sqrt(x),

Psi(a(p))-Psi(a(q)) <= sqrt(N)[Psi(p)-Psi(q)].

Apply this to R_L^{(1)} with q=p_c-delta and p=p_c:

Psi(P_{p_c}(A_L))
<=Psi(rho_{p_c-delta}^{(1)}(L))
  +sqrt(V_L)[Psi(p_c)-Psi(p_c-delta)].

The current rigorous literature gives

p_c^site(Z^3)>p_0:=0.2522=1261/5000

(Georgakopoulos--Panagiotis, CPC 2023), while Campanino--Russo gives p_c<1/2. On (0,1/2], Psi'(x)=1/(2 sqrt(x(1-x))) is decreasing. For fixed delta>0, p -> Psi(p)-Psi(p-delta) is therefore decreasing wherever defined. Thus, for 0<delta<p_0,

Psi(p_c)-Psi(p_c-delta)
<=Psi(p_0)-Psi(p_0-delta).

This gives the exact sharpened passage inequality

Psi(P_{p_c}(A_L))
<=Psi(rho_{p_c-delta}^{(1)}(L))
 +sqrt(V_L)[Psi(p_0)-Psi(p_0-delta)].

For the explicit range 0<delta<=0.001, p_0-delta>=0.2512=157/625. Since Psi' decreases,

Psi(p_0)-Psi(p_0-delta)
<=1.153 delta.

The exact checker verifies this without floating point through

4*(157/625)*(468/625)*(1153/1000)^2
=24419913921/24414062500 > 1.

Hence the fully explicit finite-volume passage bound is

arcsin sqrt(P_{p_c}(A_L))
<=arcsin sqrt(rho_{p_c-delta}^{(1)}(L))
  +1.153 sqrt(V_L) delta,

for every integer L>=1 and 0<delta<=0.001.

The asymptotically optimal coefficient supplied by p_0 is

1/(2 sqrt(p_0(1-p_0)))=1.1513430210...,

but the exact 1.153 bound above avoids reliance on decimal floating arithmetic.

Consequently the same exponent-level sufficient condition remains

rho_{p_c-delta_L}^{(1)}(L)->0
and
delta_L=o(L^{-3/2}),

which implies P_{p_c}(A_L)->0 and hence theta(p_c)=0. This run improves the constant, not the exponent. Fixed-p subcritical exponential decay still does not supply the required moving-window estimate.

A run-local exact 12-step self-avoiding-walk checker also certifies the weaker independent lower bound p_c>=0.203449919 by c_12 p^12<1, but the published p_c>0.2522 bound supersedes it for C181. It is retained only as a self-check artifact.
