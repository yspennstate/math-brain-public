## 6. R09-PASSAGE — square-root total-influence bound and the missing uniform passage

**Status: PROVED compiler; elementary level-one/Bessel argument, no novelty claim.**

Let A be any increasing Boolean event determined by N independent Bernoulli(s) SITE variables X_1,...,X_N. Put

\[
a(s)=\mathbb P_s(A),
\qquad
I_i(s)=\mathbb P_s(i\text{ is pivotal}).
\]

For

\[
g_i=\frac{X_i-s}{\sqrt{s(1-s)}},
\]

the g_i are orthonormal in L^2 because the coordinates are independent. Monotonicity gives the exact covariance identity

\[
\operatorname{Cov}(1_A,X_i)=s(1-s)I_i(s).
\tag{PASS.1}
\]

Bessel's inequality applied to 1_A-a(s) therefore yields

\[
\boxed{
s(1-s)\sum_{i=1}^N I_i(s)^2
\le a(s)(1-a(s)).
}
\tag{PASS.2}
\]

Russo's SITE formula and Cauchy--Schwarz give

\[
a'(s)=\sum_iI_i(s)
\le
\sqrt{\frac{N\,a(s)(1-a(s))}{s(1-s)}}.
\tag{PASS.3}
\]

Let Psi(x)=arcsin(sqrt(x)). Since

\[
\Psi'(x)=\frac1{2\sqrt{x(1-x)}},
\]

integration of PASS.3 gives, for 0<q<p<1,

\[
\boxed{
\Psi(a(p))-\Psi(a(q))
\le
\sqrt N\,[\Psi(p)-\Psi(q)].
}
\tag{PASS.4}
\]

Apply this to A=A_L, N=N_L=(2L+1)^3, q=p_c-delta, p=p_c.
The elementary self-avoiding-path union bound gives p_c>=1/5: the number of n-edge self-avoiding paths from 0 is at most 6*5^{n-1}, so for p<1/5 their all-open probability tends to zero after a union bound. Campanino--Russo gives p_c<1/2.

Hence, for 0<delta<=1/10, every s in [p_c-delta,p_c] lies in [1/10,1/2]. On this interval

\[
\Psi'(s)=\frac1{2\sqrt{s(1-s)}}\le\frac53.
\]

Therefore

\[
\boxed{
\arcsin\sqrt{\alpha_{p_c}(L)}
\le
\arcsin\sqrt{\alpha_{p_c-\delta}(L)}
+\frac53(2L+1)^{3/2}\delta.
}
\tag{PASS.5}
\]

This is the missing uniform passage compiler in a strictly stronger form than the previous run's crude common-uniform estimate

\[
\alpha_{p_c}(L)
\le \alpha_{p_c-\delta}(L)+(2L+1)^3\delta.
\]

In particular, a sufficient moving-window statement is now

\[
\boxed{
\alpha_{p_c-\delta_L}(L)\to0,
\qquad
L^{3/2}\delta_L\to0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\alpha_{p_c}(L)\to0.
}
\tag{PASS.6}
\]

The previous crude coupling required the much narrower condition delta_L=o(L^{-3}). PASS.6 widens the rigorous passage window to delta_L=o(L^{-3/2}).

This still does not close the problem: fixed-p<p_c exponential decay does not, by itself, provide the required moving-window estimate as p approaches p_c on this scale.
