# 6. Move 25 — COUNT THE SAME THING TWICE: exact strict-Pick energy identity

The easiest place for a universal arbitrary-law proof to fail would be `eta_c=0` for some non-Dirac law. The preceding reports used Cauchy--Schwarz plus the inverse-transform definition to rule this out. I rewrote that step as one exact double-integral identity.

Let `z=x+iy in C+`, let `X,Y` be iid with law `mu`, and write

\[

\qquad G_\mu(z)=\mathbb E f_z(X),
\qquad F_\mu(z)=\frac1{G_\mu(z)}.
\]

Since

\[
-\Im G_\mu(z)=y\,\mathbb E|f_z(X)|^2,
\]

we have exactly

\[
\Im F_\mu(z)-y
=
\frac{y}{|G_\mu(z)|^2}
\left(\mathbb E|f_z(X)|^2-|\mathbb Ef_z(X)|^2\right)
\]

and, counting the variance a second way,

\[
\boxed{
\Im F_\mu(z)-\Im z
=
\frac{\Im z}{2|G_\mu(z)|^2}
\mathbb E\left|f_z(X)-f_z(Y)\right|^2.
}

The right side is zero iff `f_z(X)` is almost surely constant. Since `t -> 1/(z-t)` is injective on `R`, equality occurs iff `mu` is Dirac. Hence for every non-Dirac law

\Im\phi_\mu(z)=\Im(w-z)<0.
