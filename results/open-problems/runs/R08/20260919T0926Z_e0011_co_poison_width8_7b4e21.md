## 4. B3 — exact all-CO poisoning probability

The obstruction is not merely qualitative. It has a short exact recurrence.

Let `S` be a finite set of currently vacant sites in an all-CO environment and let `e(S)` be the number of nearest-neighbour edges induced by `S`. At the source anchor `p=1/2`, while no O has yet appeared:

- each vacant site has an effective CO-fill clock of rate `1/2`;
- each vacant-vacant edge has an effective O2 clock of rate `1/4`.

Let `q(S)` be the probability that **all vacancies are filled by CO before the first effective O2 adsorption**. First-effective-event conditioning gives

\[
q(\varnothing)=1,
\]

and for nonempty `S`,

\[
\boxed{
q(S)=\frac{2}{2|S|+e(S)}\sum_{v\in S}q(S\setminus\{v\}).
}
\tag{4.1}
\]

Indeed the total effective rate is

\[
\frac{|S|}{2}+\frac{e(S)}4=\frac{2|S|+e(S)}4,
\]

and a specified CO fill therefore occurs next with probability `2/(2|S|+e(S))`; an O2 event contributes zero to the poisoning event.

For the actual current `3 x 2` entrance rectangle, exact rational dynamic programming gives

\[
\boxed{q_3=\frac{79066}{329175}\approx0.24019442545758335.}
\tag{4.2}
\]

Hence for this admissible worst boundary the eventual target-hitting probability of the proposed unconditioned full-hazard local process is at most

\[
\boxed{
1-q_3=\frac{250109}{329175}\approx0.7598055745424166
<\frac{63}{64}.
}
\tag{4.3}
\]

This is far stronger than saying that a particular finite word is unlikely: **even infinite waiting cannot get the current width-three robust local stage above the percolation-scale threshold against this legal adversarial boundary**, because about 24% of the mass can fall directly into the all-CO trap.
