### Current literature found

- `"Open Problem 4.3" "Critical percolation threshold on preferential attachment models"`;

- `"Bernoulli preferential attachment" percolation threshold`.

Rajat Subhra Hazra, Remco van der Hofstad, and Rounak Ray, **Percolation on preferential attachment models**, arXiv:2312.14085 (2023; listed in 2026 as Electronic Journal of Probability, DOI `10.1214/26-EJP1557`), proves for the fixed-outdegree affine PA models with `m >= 2`, `delta > -m` that

`pi_c = delta / [2( m(m+delta) + sqrt(m(m-1)(m+delta)(m+1+delta)) )]` for `delta>0`,

and `pi_c=0` for `delta<=0`, together with the giant-component phase transition. Their introduction explicitly situates this against the Bernoulli/conditionally-independent preferential-attachment literature and notes that the independent-edge `delta=0` case had already been studied.

The older conditionally-independent-edge model itself is not unknown: Dereich--Mörters, **Random networks with sublinear preferential attachment: The giant component**, *Annals of Probability* 41 (2013), gives necessary-and-sufficient giant-component criteria and percolation robustness, explicit for linear attachment; Eckhoff--Mörters--Ortgiese (2018) studies its near-critical regime. Thus the phrase `conditionally independent edges` points to a mature separate model, not an unstudied finite comparison.

### What is and is not resolved
The first clause of the source problem -- locating and proving the phase transition for the standard fixed-outdegree PA model -- is now decisively answered by Hazra--van der Hofstad--Ray.

The bounded search did **not** establish a single canonical all-parameter normalization under which the source's second sentence, `Are the results identical ...?`, has a one-line yes/no theorem in the literature. The two model classes use different parameterizations (fixed outdegree versus random outdegree/affine attachment rule), and the source's comparison clause is qualitative unless a parameter matching is fixed. I therefore do not turn a plausible model comparison into an invented theorem.

### Disposition
`I0048 = MAIN_CLAUSE_KNOWN / COMPARISON_SCOPE_REQUIRES_EXACT_NORMALIZATION / NOT_READY`.

No claim is created. A future worker could only admit a residual after freezing the exact parameter matching and identifying a still-open invariant (threshold, critical order, giant size, etc.) that the source actually intended.
