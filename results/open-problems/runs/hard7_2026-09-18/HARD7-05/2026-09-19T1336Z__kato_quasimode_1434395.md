## 5. Exact degree-two Rayleigh quotient

\tag{5.1}

\[

\tag{5.2}

\[
G=\begin{pmatrix}

\tag{5.3}

## 6. Exact residual without numerical quadrature

To evaluate

\[

\tag{6.1}

\[
a=\frac r s,\qquad q(y)=\Phi(ay),\qquad p(y)=\phi(ay).
\tag{6.2}
\]

The conditional truncated moments are

\[
J_0=q,
\quad J_1=ryq+sp,
\quad J_2=(r^2y^2+s^2)q+rsy\,p.
\tag{6.3}
\]

Hence every entry of `N_{ij}=<e_i,K^2e_j>` reduces to the three scalar families

\[
U_m=\int_0^\infty y^m\phi(y)q(y)^2\,dy,
\quad
V_m=\int_0^\infty y^m\phi(y)q(y)p(y)\,dy,
\quad
W_m=\int_0^\infty y^m\phi(y)p(y)^2\,dy.
\tag{6.4}

For `U_0`, the classical trivariate centered-Gaussian orthant formula, with correlations `(r,r,r^2)`, gives

\[
U_0=\frac18+\frac{2\arcsin r+\arcsin(r^2)}{4\pi}.
\tag{6.5}

\[
\mu=p\lambda_1+(1-p)m.
\tag{8.1}

The residual variance satisfies

\[

\ge p(1-p)(\lambda_1-m)^2.
\tag{8.2}

\[
\mu-m=p(\lambda_1-m)\ge \mu-\beta
