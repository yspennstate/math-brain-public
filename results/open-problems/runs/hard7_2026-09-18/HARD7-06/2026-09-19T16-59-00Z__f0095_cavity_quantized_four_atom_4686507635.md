### Proof

The exact checker committed with this report conditions on total Poisson degree `n` and enumerates all four-cell multinomial count vectors through `n=28` with integer weights. For every enumerated state the quantity `S(N)` is a positive rational at most one.

For each vertex logarithm, write a rational ratio in `(0,1]`, scale it by an exact power of two into `[1/2,1]`, and use

\[
\log z=2\operatorname{atanh}\frac{z-1}{z+1}
\]

with a finite rational series. Because the transformed atanh argument is nonpositive, truncating its alternating-by-sign-free odd series on the negative side gives an explicit **upper** bound; the `log 2` scaling correction uses a separate rigorous rational lower bound. All bounds are then rounded outward onto a `2^{-128}` dyadic lattice.

The omitted Poisson terms `n>28` need no numerical tail estimate for the vertex contribution: since `S(N)<=1`, every omitted `log S(N)` term is nonpositive. Dropping the entire tail therefore raises the vertex expectation and is one-sided in the required direction.

The retained Poisson polynomial is negative. The checker upper-bounds `e^{d_*}` by a positive Taylor polynomial plus a geometric remainder, so its reciprocal is a rigorous lower bound on `e^{-d_*}`.
