## 5. #18 SAY IT IN ANOTHER LANGUAGE: derive the scalar limit as a one-dimensional large-deviation problem

This route eliminates the questionable max/expectation interchange from the source.

`x -> -c sqrt(alpha x)`

is continuous and bounded above. The standard Laplace principle therefore gives

`lim_n c I_per,n`

`= sup_{x>=0}{ -c sqrt(alpha x) - alpha I_Y(x) }`.

Now use the exact convex representation

`-c sqrt(alpha x)`

`= sup_{gamma>0}{ -c gamma - c alpha x/(4 gamma) }`.

`lim_n c I_per,n`

`sup_x{ -t x-I_Y(x)} = log E exp(-tY)` for `t>=0`.
