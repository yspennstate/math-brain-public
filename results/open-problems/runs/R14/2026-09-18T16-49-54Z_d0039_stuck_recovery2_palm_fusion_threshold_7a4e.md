## 5. Method #25 — count the same collision twice: an exact threshold match

The most useful new route-level observation is a cancellation in the **two-marked-root Palm bookkeeping**. It is algebraically exact up to the remaining characteristic-polynomial moment asymptotic, and it gives the same threshold as the independent branching calculation.

In a beta-4 log gas, mark two distinct eigenvalues `x,y` and tilt each by `|D_N'|^{-2t}`. At density level:

- the original marked-pair Vandermonde contributes `|x-y|^4`;
- the two derivative tilts contribute `|x-y|^{-4t}`;
- hence the explicit pair factor is

\[
|x-y|^{4-4t};
\]

- each remaining particle sees insertion power

\[
p=4-2t
\]

at each marked point.

Kivimae's GSE normalization sends a characteristic-polynomial power `p` to GMC parameter `p/sqrt(2)`, while the reference Gaussian field has covariance `-(1/2)log|x-y|`. Therefore the corresponding normalized two-point characteristic-polynomial singularity is expected to be

\[

Combining it with the exact Palm pair factor gives the striking cancellation

\[
(4-4t)-\frac{(4-2t)^2}{4}=-t^2,

\[
\boxed{|x-y|^{-t^2}.}
