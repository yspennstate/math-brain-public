## R14-C188 — exact three-variable reduction of complete 2-sfl stationarity

**STATUS: PROVED, conditional only on the source complete-sfl setup and the stated interior domain.**

Define the reduced objective

`Ftilde_alpha(p,q,gamma)=F(p,q,c(p,q),gamma,gamma_p(p,q);alpha)`.

Then every interior zero of

`grad_{p,q,gamma} Ftilde_alpha=0`

is a zero of **all five** partial derivatives

`F_p=F_q=F_c=F_gamma=F_gamma_p=0`.

### Proof

Put

`r=sqrt(p/q)`.

The source relations imply

`gamma_p=(1/2)((1-q)/(1-p))r`,

`c=r/(1-p)-1/[r(1-q)]`.

Therefore

`D=2 gamma_p-c(1-q)=1/r=sqrt(q/p)`.

Consequently the source `q`- and `gamma_p`-stationarity relations

`p=q/D^2`,

`1=(1-q)/(2 gamma_p D)+q/D^2`

hold identically on the reduced surface. In particular, on that surface

`F_q=0`, `F_gamma_p=0`.

Now fix `p` and write

`s=sqrt(q/p)`.

Then

`c=1/[s(1-p)]-s/(1-q)`

and `s_q=s/(2q)`. Hence

`c_q = -1/[2 q s(1-p)] - s/[2q(1-q)] - s/(1-q)^2 <0`

throughout `0<q<p<1`.

By the chain rule,

`partial_q Ftilde = F_q + F_c c_q + F_gamma_p (gamma_p)_q = F_c c_q`.

Thus `partial_q Ftilde=0` forces `F_c=0` because `c_q` is strictly nonzero. Next,

`partial_p Ftilde = F_p + F_c c_p + F_gamma_p (gamma_p)_p = F_p`,

so reduced `p`-stationarity gives `F_p=0`. Finally

`partial_gamma Ftilde=F_gamma`.

Therefore a reduced three-variable stationary point gives the full five-variable source stationary point. QED.

# 6. Move #27 — SWAP THE QUANTIFIERS: why a naive fixed-direction net proof cannot certify UNSAT

The convex-hull form suggests proving

`h_K(v)>1 for every ||v||=1`

by first controlling one fixed direction and then netting the sphere. This direct route has a clean entropy obstruction.

On the high-probability event

`R_N:=max_a ||g_a|| <= C sqrt(N)`

for a fixed sufficiently large `C`, the support function is `C sqrt(N)`-Lipschitz on the sphere. If we prove a fixed positive slack `h_K(v)>1+s` on a net and want to retain `h_K>1` everywhere, the net radius must be

`epsilon <= s/(2C sqrt(N))`.

Any such Euclidean sphere net has cardinality of order

`(c/epsilon)^{N-1}=exp((1/2+o(1))N log N)`.

For a fixed unit `v`, however,

`P(h_K(v)<=1+s)=Phi(1+s)^M=exp[-c_s N+o(N)]`

when `M=alpha N`, with only an order-`N` exponent.

Thus a plain union bound over a Lipschitz net compares `Theta(N log N)` entropy against only `Theta(N)` fixed-direction decay and cannot close.
