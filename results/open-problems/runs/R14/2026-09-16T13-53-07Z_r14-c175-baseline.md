## R14-C175 — first rigorous repository-certified bracket at \(\kappa=-1\)

Let

### SAT endpoint

At \(\kappa=0\), the classical Wendel/Cover threshold is \(2\): for every fixed
\[
\alpha<2,
\]

\[
\boxed{
\alpha<2
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\Pr(\mathrm{SAT}\text{ at }\kappa=-1)\to1.
}
\tag{C175.1}
\]

\[

\]

### UNSAT endpoint

Stojnic's Theorem 2 defines
\[
f_{\rm gar}(\kappa)
=
\int_{-\kappa}^\infty
(z+\kappa)^2\phi(z)\,dz
\]
and, for \(\kappa<0\), yields overwhelming-probability infeasibility whenever
\[
\alpha>\frac1{f_{\rm gar}(\kappa)}.
\]
At \(\kappa=-1\),
\[
f_{\rm gar}(-1)
=
\int_1^\infty(z-1)^2\phi(z)\,dz.

Gaussian integration by parts gives
\[
\int_1^\infty z\phi(z)\,dz=\phi(1),
\]
\[
\int_1^\infty z^2\phi(z)\,dz

\bar\Phi(1)+\phi(1).
\]
Therefore
\[
\boxed{
f_{\rm gar}(-1)=2\bar\Phi(1)-\phi(1).}
\tag{C175.2}

\boxed{
