## 3. #9 RANDOMIZE — exact conditional-zero lemma for every finite resampling

Fix a source-admissible iid continuous law `F`, write

`P_F = F^{E(Z^2)}`,

and let `N_x(omega)` be the number of one-sided FPP geodesic rays starting from `x`. Assume the exact-four counterhypothesis

`P_F(N_0=4)=1`.                                                    (H4)

By translation invariance and a countable intersection,

`P_F(N_x=4 for every x in Z^2)=1`.                                 (H4-all)

Let `A` be **any finite set of edges**, let `F_{A^c}` be the sigma-field generated by all weights outside `A`, and define

`E_x^5 := {N_x >= 5}`.

Since `(H4-all)` gives `P_F(E_x^5)=0`, nonnegativity of conditional expectation gives

`P_F(E_x^5 | F_{A^c}) = 0` almost surely.                           (R14.1)

Because the edge law is a product law, the regular conditional law of the weights on `A` given the exterior is exactly `F^{\otimes A}`. Therefore, for `P_F`-almost every exterior configuration `xi`,

`F^{\otimes A}{ eta : N_x(xi union eta) >= 5 } = 0`.               (R14.2)

This is the key exact obstruction. Under the exact-four hypothesis, the conditional probability that **any finite annulus resampling creates a persistent fifth ray is not merely nonuniform in scale; it is exactly zero at every fixed finite scale for almost every exterior**.

The same statement holds for any finite-energy local resampling kernel `K_A(xi,d eta)` that is absolutely continuous with respect to `F^{\otimes A}` for almost every exterior: `(R14.2)` implies

`K_A(xi, {eta:N_x(xi union eta)>=5})=0` almost surely.              (R14.3)

So a finite local surgery with an absolutely continuous change of the source weights cannot be the missing iid mechanism. If a proposed proof claims that a positive-probability finite pattern *forces* a persistent fifth tail under `(H4)`, `(R14.2)` pinpoints the contradiction: the forcing statement itself is already false on almost every exterior.

### Proof

Only Fubini/conditional expectation is used. Set

`q_A(xi)=F^{\otimes A}{eta:N_x(xi union eta)>=5}`.

Then `q_A>=0` and, by product disintegration,

`int q_A(xi) dF^{\otimes A^c}(xi) = P_F(E_x^5)=0`.

Hence `q_A=0` almost surely, proving `(R14.2)`. Absolute continuity gives `(R14.3)`. No finite computation is involved.

This is deliberately labelled a **self-check / route obstruction**, not a new literature theorem: mathematically it is an elementary consequence of the exact-four counterhypothesis plus product disintegration.

## 4. #15 BREAK THE SYMMETRY — one neighboring gap cannot be locally populated

Do not average over the four rotations. Under `(H4-all)`, fix one vertex `x` and one ordered neighboring pair of its four rays, say the pair bounding the first counterclockwise gap. A “persistent separator” for that gap means a fifth geodesic ray from `x` which is eventually strictly between the two selected rays in planar order.

Such a separator is a subset of `E_x^5`. Therefore `(R14.2)` immediately gives the one-gap statement:

> **One-gap finite-resampling no-go.** For every finite annulus `A`, for almost every exterior configuration, an independent or absolutely-continuous resampling of the weights in `A` has conditional probability zero of creating a persistent separator in that fixed neighboring gap.

This distinguishes the obstruction from the harmless operation of creating a **finite branch**. With continuous weights, two genuine geodesic rays from the same root cannot split and later rejoin (otherwise the split/rejoin vertices would have two distinct finite geodesics), so a true fifth ray would already be persistent. The difficulty is exactly that no finite pattern can force the local branch to remain globally geodesic forever.

This also explains why Jacquet's finite-pattern theorem cannot by itself solve the special case. It supplies abundant occurrences of prescribed local configurations along long geodesics, but `(R14.2)` says that, under `(H4)`, no finite positive-measure local configuration can have the extra global implication “there is now a fifth ray”. Some new infinite-scale ingredient is necessary.

## 5. #25 COUNT THE SAME THING TWICE — the scale-uniform annulus plan collapses at Fubini

Recovery 1 proposed finding a scale-uniform lower bound on the conditional probability that a separated-annulus resampling creates a persistent third tail between a fixed neighboring pair. The double count above decides that proposal exactly under the counterhypothesis:

`E[q_A(outside)] = P_F(N_x>=5) = 0`.

Thus `q_A(outside)=0` almost surely for every fixed finite annulus `A`. There is no positive constant `p`, no scale-dependent `p_A>0`, and in fact no positive conditional mass at all to bootstrap with a conditional Borel--Cantelli argument **when the full exterior is fixed**.

The same conclusion survives any finite number of absolutely-continuous surgeries: if the final law `Q` satisfies `Q << P_F`, then `Q(N_x>=5)=0`. Purely replacing the weights in any deterministic collection of disjoint annuli by fresh independent `F`-samples is even stronger: the resulting field still has exactly law `P_F`, whether finitely or infinitely many coordinates are refreshed.

There is a useful quantitative infinite-scale corollary. Partition a deterministic edge set into disjoint finite blocks `A_n`, let `P_n=F^{\otimes A_n}`, and tilt independently to mutually absolutely continuous block laws `Q_n`. Kakutani's product-measure dichotomy gives

`sum_n H^2(P_n,Q_n) < infinity  ==>  Q := tensor_n Q_n tensor P_rest  ~  P_F`,

for either standard equivalent convention of squared Hellinger distance. Consequently

`Q(N_x>=5)=0`.                                                     (R14.4)

Therefore any block-product change of measure that really forces a fifth ray must cross the **singularity threshold**: in this setup it must have divergent total Hellinger cost (or contain a singular block). This is not a proof that exact-four is possible. It identifies the exact point at which an infinite-annulus change-of-measure argument would have to become genuinely new.

## 7. What a proof of the exact-four special case must now do

This second recovery narrows the missing mechanism further. A proof ruling out `(H4)` cannot rely only on any of the following:

1. one finite-energy surgery;
2. any finite collection of such surgeries;
3. fresh independent resampling of deterministic annuli;
4. a finite positive-measure local pattern, even one occurring linearly often along geodesics;
5. an equivalent (`Q ~ P_F`) block-product change of measure.

A successful iid argument must instead extract a persistent fifth tail **from the original product environment itself through an infinite-scale property**, or use an infinite-scale change of measure that is singular and then somehow transfer a contradiction back to `P_F`. The latter transfer is exactly the missing step; equivalence is unavailable once the Kakutani cost diverges.

A potentially useful handoff idea is a Cameron--Martin/Kakutani-style near-singular annular tilt: use many tiny biases whose cumulative passage-time advantage diverges while monitoring Hellinger cost. The calculation above shows the sharp constraint. If the biases stay square-summable in block Hellinger cost, they cannot change the exact-four a.s. event; if they cross into singularity, one needs a separate comparison theorem to return to the iid law.
