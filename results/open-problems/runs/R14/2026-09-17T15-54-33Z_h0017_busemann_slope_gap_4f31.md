## 5. Method #20 — identify exactly why the algorithm fails

Let

Because `C_*(F)` is closed in the compact circle, choose `rho in C_*` with normal direction `phi` satisfying

### Proof: upper bound

Assume

`P_F(G^{theta,alpha}) > 0`.

Intersect the survival event with the full-measure events from source Proposition 2 and Ahlberg--Hoffman Theorem 2.5/12.2. Proposition 2 supplies a single infinite geodesic `g=(v_k)` all of whose vertices are infected by type 2. Hence for every initial source point

`B_g(0,x) <= 0`.                                      (A)

`alpha > pi/2-r_F(theta)`,

`alpha_c^F(theta) <= pi/2-r_F(theta)`.
