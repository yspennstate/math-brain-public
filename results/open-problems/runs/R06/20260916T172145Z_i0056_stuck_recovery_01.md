### Method #14 — FIND THE GROUP ACTING

The `[4,5,4,5]` graph has a much stronger description than an arbitrary mixed-face transitive tiling: it is the rectification/medial graph of the regular `{5,4}` tessellation. Thus a vertex of `T` can be viewed as an edge of the regular base graph `H_{4,5}`. A medial edge records a corner between consecutive base edges.

This is promising because the base `{5,4}` tessellation and its dual are regular, and their exact edge constants are known. It changes the remaining optimization from an arbitrary mixed-face vertex-set problem into an edge-subset problem on a regular primal/dual pair.

However, transitivity alone does **not** collapse the finite boundary optimization to one scalar state. For a finite edge set of the base graph, the medial cut depends on the cyclic incidence pattern at every partially occupied primal vertex (and dually at faces), not only on the cardinality of the selected edge set. A two-orbit/local-state description exists in principle, but the checked literature/growth recurrences do not supply a sharp lower-bound certificate for all finite regions.

**Result of #14:** a new recovery representation was identified (`T` as a medial graph of a regular tessellation), but it has not yet been converted into an exact Cheeger certificate. No theorem is claimed in this stuck run.

### Method #20 — FIND WHY THE ALGORITHM FAILS

Three obvious algorithms fail for different reasons.

1. **Multiplicity-two closure.** It converts edge expansion into vertex expansion but does not compute edge expansion. For `d=4` it needs the still-unproved input `Phi_E(T)>=1`.
2. **Breadth-first balls / growth generating functions.** Exact sphere counts can give explicit finite ratios and asymptotic candidate upper bounds, but a growth recurrence does not prove balls are isoperimetric minimizers. A smaller sampled ratio is not a rigorous lower bound.
3. **Whole-face Euler/curvature count.** The local face sequence alternates squares and pentagons. Counting only average local curvature loses information carried by separating cycles, holes, and the cyclic pattern of selected base edges. The regular/co-regular scalar dual identity no longer closes because the dual has multiple vertex degrees/orbits.

The precise algorithmic obstruction is therefore **optimality**, not enumeration: generating candidate regions is easy compared with proving that every finite region has no smaller boundary ratio.

**Result of #20:** the missing ingredient is a sharp multi-type lower-bound certificate, not another recurrence or sampled construction.
