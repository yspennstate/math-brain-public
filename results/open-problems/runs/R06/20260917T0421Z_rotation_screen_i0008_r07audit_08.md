### Audit of (iii)

The key argument is also sound, but the cleanest null-set bookkeeping is slightly more explicit than the owner report.

Choose `0<epsilon<1`. Seam pinching gives a deterministic `delta0>0` with

`[ell(u),r(u)] subset [-epsilon sqrt(u), epsilon sqrt(u)]`

`B^(n)_t = X_{S_n+t}-X_{S_n}`

`|B^(n)_{T_n-h}-B^(n)_{T_n}| <= epsilon sqrt(h)`

for all `0<h<=1/k`.
