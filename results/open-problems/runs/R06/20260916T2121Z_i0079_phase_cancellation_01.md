## 5. Complete proof of T1

Fix `R>=1`. A boundary point of `T_d` is an infinite ray from the root. Its Busemann function on the radius-`R` tree ball is completely determined by the first `R` ray edges. There are
\[
D_R=dq^{R-1}
\]
such prefixes, and every depth-`R` boundary cylinder has harmonic mass `1/D_R`.

For the `k`-fold product there are therefore
\[
M_R=D_R^k
\tag{5.1}
\]
product signatures
\[
\sigma=(\sigma_1,\ldots,\sigma_k),
\]
all with `beta^k`-mass `1/M_R`. If `h_sigma` denotes the corresponding product Busemann restriction, then every boundary tuple with signature `sigma` has the same `h_sigma(v)` for every
\[
v\in B_R(o).
\]

### 5.1 Collapse each signature to its only potentially winning atom

Under `mu_c`, atoms of a fixed signature `sigma`, delay `m in Z`, and mark `u in [0,1]` form a PPP with intensity
\[
\frac{c}{M_R}q^m\,du.
\tag{5.2}
\]

Within one signature all atoms have the same `h_sigma` on `B_R(o)`. Hence among atoms of that signature only the lexicographically smallest pair
\[
(Z_\sigma,U_\sigma)
\]
in `(delay,mark)` can win anywhere in `B_R(o)`. All larger-delay atoms are worse everywhere; at the same delay all larger marks lose every tie everywhere.

Different signatures are restrictions of the PPP to disjoint boundary cylinders, so these first pairs are independent over `sigma`.

### 5.2 Exact exponential transform

Order the state space `Z x [0,1]` lexicographically. The cumulative intensity of points preceding `(m,u)` is
\[
\begin{aligned}
\Lambda_c(m,u)
&=
\frac c{M_R}\sum_{j<m}q^j
+
\frac c{M_R}q^m u\\
&=
\frac{c}{M_R(q-1)}q^m\bigl(1+(q-1)u\bigr).
\end{aligned}
\tag{5.3}
\]

Define
\[
A_c:=\frac{c}{M_R(q-1)}
\]
and
\[
E_\sigma
:=
A_c q^{Z_\sigma}\bigl(1+(q-1)U_\sigma\bigr).
\tag{5.4}
\]

By the standard cumulative-intensity transform for the first point of a Poisson process,
\[
E_\sigma\stackrel{\rm iid}{\sim}{\rm Exp}(1).
\tag{5.5}
\]
In particular the joint law of `(E_sigma)` is exactly independent of `c`.

Conversely, the intervals
\[
[A_cq^m,A_cq^{m+1})
\]
partition `(0,infinity)`, so `E_sigma` determines the unique `Z_sigma`, and within that interval it determines `U_sigma`.

### 5.3 Winner comparison loses the phase exactly

At `v in B_R(o)`, signature `sigma` competes with lexicographic key
\[
\bigl(Z_\sigma+h_\sigma(v),\,U_\sigma\bigr).
\tag{5.6}
\]

For any integer `r` and `u in [0,1)`,
\[
q^r\bigl(1+(q-1)u\bigr)\in[q^r,q^{r+1}).
\]
Thus ordering the lexicographic pair `(r,u)` is **exactly equivalent** to ordering the positive real number
\[
q^r(1+(q-1)u).
\tag{5.7}
\]

Using (5.4), the winner at `v` is therefore
\[
\boxed{
\sigma_*(v)
=
\operatorname*{argmin}_{\sigma}
q^{h_\sigma(v)}E_\sigma.
}
\tag{5.8}
\]

The common multiplicative factor `A_c` has cancelled. Since the `E_sigma` are iid continuous `Exp(1)`, ties in (5.8) have probability zero. Hence the **entire joint winner map**
\[
(\sigma_*(v))_{v\in B_R(o)}
\]
has a law independent of `c`.

The equivalence relation on `B_R(o)` is
\[
v\sim w
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\sigma_*(v)=\sigma_*(w),
\tag{5.9}
\]
so every finite equivalence-relation cylinder has a `c`-independent law.

Because `R` was arbitrary, all finite-dimensional partition laws are independent of `c`, and cylinder laws determine the probability measure on the countable equivalence-relation configuration space. This proves T1. `QED`

## 7. Method #2 — attack nonuniqueness first: the apparent phase obstruction disappears

The first planned refutation route was to exploit the subsequential phase
\[
c=\theta(0).
\]
That route fails decisively for the partition law.

The reason is not an asymptotic approximation: within each finite boundary signature, the entire discrete-delay/continuous-mark PPP is converted exactly to one `Exp(1)` variable. Changing `c` merely changes the common coordinate chart `(Z,U)<->E`; the winner rule after the transform is (5.8), which has no phase.

This also explains why phase information may survive in a richer **nuclei/enriched IPVT** while disappearing after passing to the partition IRE. The later D'Achille--Khezeli work on graph IPVTs explicitly distinguishes phase behavior of richer vertex-measured objects from independence statements after forgetting some structure. There is therefore no contradiction between phase-sensitive enriched data and T1's phase-free equivalence relation.

## 8. Method #5 — add the phase parameter: exact scale invariance replaces differentiation

Rather than differentiate a cylinder probability in `c`, formula (5.3) exhibits the correct coordinate:
\[

The transformed variable has `Exp(1)` law for **every** `c`, so the derivative of every finite winner-cylinder probability with respect to `c` is zero wherever one chooses to formulate it.

## 11. Adversarial checks / possible failure modes

I explicitly checked the following potential defects.

**Different atoms with the same finite signature.**  
They have identical Busemann restrictions on `B_R`; the smallest `(delay,mark)` dominates every other such atom at every vertex, so collapsing the signature is exact.

**Infinitely many negative delays.**  
Although delays range over all integers, the total intensity below any fixed level is finite and tends to zero at `-infinity`; the lexicographic first atom exists almost surely.

**Phase-dependent integer rounding.**  
The mark coordinate exactly fills each multiplicative interval `[q^m,q^{m+1})`, so no rounding residue survives in the winner comparison.

**Different product signatures having equal keys.**  
The `E_sigma` variables are independent continuous exponentials, so equality at any fixed vertex has probability zero; a finite union handles every vertex of a finite ball.

**Passing from edge-encoding convergence to IRE cylinders.**  
The Appendix-B finite restricted-function/mark-order proof records enough information to determine winner identities on the finite ball, so the extension to finite equivalence cylinders is direct. This should nevertheless be an explicit item for independent auditors.

**Tie-rule scope.**  
T1 is proved for iid continuous labels attached to nuclei. It is not claimed here for every possible equivariant tie rule allowed abstractly by the source.
