### `R05-I0030-C1` — Arc MNHD

**PROVED.** Let `G=(V,E)` be any finite, simple, connected arc-transitive graph with `|V|=n>=2` and degree `d`. Let
\[
P=A/d,
\qquad
H_t=e^{t(P-I)}
\]
be rate-one continuous-time simple random walk. Then for every oriented edge `(x,y)`,
\[
 r_{x,y}(t):=\frac{H_t(x,y)}{H_t(x,x)}
\]
is **strictly increasing** on `[0,\infty)`, with
\[
r_{x,y}(0)=0,
\qquad
\lim_{t\to\infty}r_{x,y}(t)=1.
\]
In particular
\[
0\le H_t(x,y)\le H_t(x,x)
\]
for every edge and every `t>=0`, and the ratio has the time-monotonicity of Proposition 7.29(ii) at distance one.

This is an intermediate geometric statement in exactly the source's arc-transitive class. Bundled with Corollary 7.3 for all pairs, it is strictly stronger information than Corollary 7.3 alone. It does **not** assert all-pairs MNHD on arc-transitive graphs.

## 4. Full proof of `R05-I0030-C1`

Arc-transitivity implies vertex-transitivity and regularity. Fix an arc `(x,y)`. By invariance of the heat semigroup under graph automorphisms, there are scalar functions
\[
a(t)=H_t(x,x)
\]
(independent of `x`) and
\[
b(t)=H_t(u,v)\quad (u\sim v)
\]
(independent of the oriented edge).

The Kolmogorov equation gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
a'(t)
&=(H_t(P-I))_{xx}\\
&=\frac1d\sum_{z\sim x}H_t(x,z)-H_t(x,x)\\
&=b(t)-a(t).
\end{aligned}
\]
Therefore
\[
\boxed{\frac{b(t)}{a(t)}=1+\frac{a'(t)}{a(t)}=1+(\log a(t))'.}
\tag{1}
\]

It remains to prove that `log a(t)` is strictly convex. Because `P` is real symmetric, `L=I-P` is positive semidefinite and the spectral theorem gives a positive probability measure `mu_x` on its spectrum such that
\[
a(t)=\langle\delta_x,e^{-tL}\delta_x\rangle
=\int e^{-\lambda t}\,d\mu_x(\lambda).
\]
Then
\[
\begin{aligned}
a(t)a''(t)-a'(t)^2
&=\frac12\iint
(\lambda-\nu)^2e^{-(\lambda+\nu)t}
\,d\mu_x(\lambda)d\mu_x(\nu)\\
&\ge0.
\end{aligned}
\]
Hence
\[
(\log a(t))''\ge0,
\]
and by (1), `r'_{x,y}(t)>=0`.

The inequality is strict for every finite `t`: connectivity makes eigenvalue `0` simple, its spectral weight at `delta_x` is `1/n>0`, while the remaining spectral weight is `1-1/n>0` and lies on positive eigenvalues. Thus the double integral above is strictly positive.

Finally, `H_0=I`, so `a(0)=1`, `b(0)=0`, and `r(0)=0`. Irreducibility gives
\[
H_t(x,x),H_t(x,y)\longrightarrow 1/n,
\]
so `r(t)->1`. This proves the claim. QED.

### Scope note

The proof actually needs only that the heat kernel be constant on the diagonal and constant over arcs (equivalently, the relevant one-walk regularity); arc-transitivity is the source-matching sufficient hypothesis. I do not broaden the frozen source claim in the metric.
