## 2. Frozen source statement remains unchanged

Aldous-Fill Proposition 7.29 states for continuous-time random walk on a distance-regular graph that, writing
\[
P_v(X_t=w)=q(t,d(v,w)),
\]
(i) `d -> q(t,d)` is non-increasing for fixed `t`, and
(ii) `q(t,d)/q(t,0)` is non-decreasing in `t` for fixed `d`.
They then ask in Open Problem 7.30:

> Does random walk on an arc-transitive graph have some monotonicity property stronger than that of Corollary 7.3?

The frozen R05 statement, fixed before finite metric extension in the preceding run, is:

### `R05-I0030-C1` — Arc MNHD

Let `G=(V,E)` be a finite simple connected arc-transitive graph, let `P=A/d`, and let
\[
H_t=e^{t(P-I)}
\]
be rate-one continuous-time simple random walk. For every arc `x~y`,
\[
\boxed{
r_{xy}(t):=\frac{H_t(x,y)}{H_t(x,x)}
}
\]
is strictly increasing on `[0,infinity)`, with `r_{xy}(0)=0` and `r_{xy}(t)->1`.

The proof is unchanged and is not re-derived as a new theorem this run. Its core exact identity is
\[
\frac{H_t(x,y)}{H_t(x,x)}=1+(\log H_t(x,x))',
\qquad x\sim y,
\]
obtained from arc-transitivity and the Kolmogorov equation, followed by strict log-convexity of the return probability as a Laplace transform of the positive Laplacian spectral measure. Full proof: `runs/R05/20260916T131432Z_i0030_edge_mnhd.md`.
