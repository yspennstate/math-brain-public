### Method #28 — replace all times by the late-time regime

This produces a clean theorem independent of the source-adjacent proof.

**R05-I0030-EVENTUAL-MNHD-01 — PROVED.**  Let `G` be a finite connected walk-regular graph. For every two distinct vertices `x!=y`,

\[
t\longmapsto \frac{H_t(x,y)}{H_t(x,x)}
\]

is strictly increasing for all sufficiently large `t`. More precisely, let

\[
L=I-P=\sum_{j=0}^m \lambda_jE_j,\qquad
0=\lambda_0<\lambda_1<\cdots<\lambda_m.
\]

Walk-regularity makes every diagonal entry of `E_j` equal to

\[
\alpha_j=\frac{\operatorname{rank}E_j}{|V|}>0.
\]

For fixed `x!=y`, put `beta_j=(E_j)_{xy}` and

\[
\delta_j:=\alpha_j-\beta_j.
\]

Because `E_j` is an orthogonal projector and its `x,x` and `y,y` entries are both `alpha_j`,

\[
2\delta_j=(e_x-e_y)^TE_j(e_x-e_y)\ge0.
\]

Also `delta_0=0`, while not every `delta_j` can vanish because `sum_j E_j=I` and `x!=y`. Let `k>=1` be the first index with `delta_k>0`. Then

\[
H_t(x,x)-H_t(x,y)
=\sum_j\delta_j e^{-\lambda_jt}
=\delta_ke^{-\lambda_kt}(1+o(1)),
\]

whereas `H_t(x,x)=|V|^{-1}+o(1)`. Therefore

\[
1-\frac{H_t(x,y)}{H_t(x,x)}
=|V|\,\delta_ke^{-\lambda_kt}(1+o(1)),
\]

and differentiation of this finite exponential sum gives

\[
\boxed{
\frac{d}{dt}\frac{H_t(x,y)}{H_t(x,x)}
=|V|\,\lambda_k\delta_ke^{-\lambda_kt}(1+o(1))>0
}
\]

for all sufficiently large `t`.

Thus every finite vertex-transitive graph (hence every arc-transitive graph) has **eventual all-pairs** normalized heat monotonicity, even though Regev–Shinkar show that full all-time MNHD can fail for a finite vertex-transitive Cayley graph.
