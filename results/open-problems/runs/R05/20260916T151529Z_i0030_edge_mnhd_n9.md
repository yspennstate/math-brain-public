\[

### Independent derivation of CLOSURE-01

Let `G` be infinite simple `d`-regular, with edge and outer-vertex isoperimetric constants `h_E,h_V`, and suppose

\[
h_E\ge d-3.
\]

Always `h_V<=h_E`. Given finite nonempty `K_0`, repeatedly add any outside vertex having `r>=2` neighbors in the current set. If `e_j,b_j` are the edge and outer-vertex boundary sizes, then exactly

\[
e_{j+1}=e_j+d-2r\le e_j+d-4,
\]

and

\[
b_{j+1}\le b_j-1+(d-r)\le b_j+d-3.
\]

After `m` additions,

\[
h_E(|K_0|+m)\le e_m\le d|K_0|+m(d-4).
\]

Since `h_E-d+4>=1`, this bounds `m`; hence the process terminates. At termination every outside boundary vertex has exactly one neighbor inside, so `b_m=e_m`. Reversing the vertex-boundary estimate,

\[
\begin{aligned}
b_0
&\ge b_m-(d-3)m\\
&=e_m-(d-3)m\\
&\ge h_E(|K_0|+m)-(d-3)m\\
&\ge h_E|K_0|.
\end{aligned}
\]

Taking the infimum gives `h_V>=h_E`, hence `h_V=h_E`. I find no gap in the theorem.

### Independent check of the `f>=5` corollary

Oh's published sharp edge formula for regular/co-regular plane tessellations is

\[
h_E=(d-2)\sqrt{1-\frac4{(d-2)(f-2)}}.
\]

For `d>=4`, `f>=5`,

\[
\begin{aligned}
h_E^2-(d-3)^2
&=2d-5-\frac{4(d-2)}{f-2}\\
&\ge \frac{2d-7}{3}>0.
\end{aligned}
\]

For `d=3`, the admissibility `1/d+1/f<=1/2` forces `f>=6`, and `h_E>=0=d-3`. Thus the hypothesis of CLOSURE-01 holds throughout the stated `f>=5` branch. The mathematical implication is correct at this scope.
