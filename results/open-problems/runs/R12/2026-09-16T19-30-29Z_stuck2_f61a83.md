## 11. Nearest solved problems and the missing ingredient

Two neighboring solved mechanisms explain the gap.

1. **Ordinary Paley theta / spectral square-root bound.** Additive characters exactly diagonalize the vertex adjacency/Seidel algebra, whose spectral data close in a tiny association algebra. Degree four does not close in such a constant-dimensional algebra: after additive Fourier reduction, a nonzero block retains `Theta(p)` internal dimensions and an elliptic/Legendre character channel.

2. **Random-graph degree-four SOS / planted-clique graph-matrix analysis.** The random setting has trace/moment mechanisms that exploit edge independence or random cancellation. Kunisky--Yu explicitly show that the analogous pseudocalibration graph-matrix norm estimate does not simply transfer to Paley because deterministic algebraic structure creates large modes. Paley replaces independence by exact character sums, and the previous run found a genuine `p`-scale Mellin component.

The closest Paley positive result, Kunisky's one-localization empirical spectral analysis, controls fixed moments using character sums; it does not provide the growing-moment/extreme-eigenvalue control needed here. The missing ingredient is therefore not “another Weil bound” but an exact use of the union-consistency quotient before the extreme norm is taken.
