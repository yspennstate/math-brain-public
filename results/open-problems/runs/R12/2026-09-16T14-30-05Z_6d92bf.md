## 3. Retained exact SOS normalization

Let

\[
\mathcal J=\{\varnothing\}\cup V(G)\cup E(G).
\]

A degree-four clique pseudoexpectation is represented by squarefree moments \(y_U\), \(|U|\le4\), with nonclique moments zero, \(y_\varnothing=1\), and moment matrix

\[
M(y)_{S,T}=y_{S\cup T}\qquad(S,T\in\mathcal J)
\]

when \(S\cup T\) is a clique, zero otherwise. The primal objective is

\[
U_4(G)=\max \sum_{v}y_v\quad\text{subject to }M(y)\succeq0.
\]

The dual retained from run 1 is

\[
\min Z_{\varnothing,\varnothing}
\]

subject to \(Z\succeq0\),

\[
\langle A_{\{i\}},Z\rangle=-1,
\qquad
\langle A_U,Z\rangle=0\quad(2\le |U|\le4),
\]

where \((A_U)_{S,T}=1_{S\cup T=U}\). In particular

\[
2Z_{\varnothing,i}+Z_{ii}=-1.
\]

No alternative normalization is introduced in this run.

## 4. R12-C04 — exact degree-four certificate for K4-free graphs

**STATUS: PROVED. No novelty claim is made.**

Let

\[
S=\sum_{v\in V(G)}x_v
\]

in the Boolean clique quotient \(x_v^2=x_v\), \(x_ux_v=0\) on nonedges. The exact scalar polynomial identity

\[
\boxed{
3-S
=
\frac16[S(S-3)]^2
+\frac13(S-3)^2
-\frac16S(S-1)(S-2)(S-3)
}
\tag{C04.1}
\]

holds identically.

In the clique quotient,

\[
S(S-1)(S-2)(S-3)
=24\sum_{K\in\mathcal K_4(G)}x_K.
\tag{C04.2}
\]

Therefore, if \(G\) is K4-free, the final term vanishes and (C04.1) is literally a sum of squares of polynomials of degree at most two. Every feasible degree-four pseudoexpectation consequently satisfies

\[
\widetilde{\mathbb E}S\le3.
\]

If \(G\) contains a triangle, evaluation at that triangle is an actual feasible clique distribution with objective 3, hence

\[
\boxed{U_4(G)=3\quad\text{for every K4-free graph containing a triangle}.}
\tag{C04.3}
\]

The exact checker enumerates Paley cliques and certifies

\[
U_4(G_{13})=U_4(G_{17})=3.
\]

Specifically, \(G_{13}\) has 26 triangles and no K4; \(G_{17}\) has 68 triangles and no K4.

For a general graph, taking the pseudoexpectation of C04.1 gives the exact useful inequality

\[
\boxed{
\widetilde{\mathbb E}S
\le
3+4\sum_{K\in\mathcal K_4(G)}y_K.
}
\tag{C04.4}
\]

The aggregate K4 moment need not consist of individually nonnegative terms; (C04.4) is an algebraic reduction, not a probability interpretation.

## 5. R12-C05 — edge-compatibility theta compiler

**STATUS: PROVED. No literature-novelty claim is made.**

This is the first reduction in the R12 work that uses the global edge-indexed PSD block rather than discarding it.

Define the **edge-compatibility graph** \(H_2(G)\):

- vertices of \(H_2(G)\) are edges \(e\in E(G)\);
- two distinct graph-edges \(e,f\) are adjacent in \(H_2(G)\) iff \(e\cup f\) is a clique of \(G\).

Let \(X\) be the principal block of a feasible degree-four moment matrix indexed by edges of \(G\). Then

\[
X_{e,e}=y_e,
\qquad
X_{e,f}=0\quad\text{whenever }e,f\text{ are nonadjacent in }H_2(G),
\]

and \(X\succeq0\). Put

\[
P_2=\sum_{e\in E(G)}y_e=\operatorname{Tr}X.
\]

If \(P_2>0\), the normalized matrix \(X/P_2\) is feasible for the standard theta primal of \(\vartheta(\overline{H_2(G)})\). Hence, with

\[
\Theta_2(G)=\vartheta(\overline{H_2(G)}),
\]

we have

\[
\mathbf1^TX\mathbf1\le \Theta_2(G)P_2.
\tag{C05.1}
\]

On the other hand, for the degree-two polynomial

\[
E_2=\sum_{e\in E(G)}x_e,
\]

pseudo-Cauchy gives

\[
P_2^2=(\widetilde{\mathbb E}E_2)^2
\le \widetilde{\mathbb E}E_2^2
=\mathbf1^TX\mathbf1.
\tag{C05.2}
\]

Combining C05.1--C05.2 yields

\[
P_2\le\Theta_2(G).
\tag{C05.3}
\]

Finally, the exact clique-algebra identity

\[
S^2=S+2E_2
\tag{C05.4}
\]

and pseudo-Cauchy for \(S\) give, with \(T=\widetilde{\mathbb E}S\),

\[
T^2\le T+2P_2\le T+2\Theta_2(G).
\]

Therefore

\[
\boxed{
U_4(G)
\le
\frac{1+\sqrt{1+8\vartheta(\overline{H_2(G)})}}{2}.
}
\tag{C05.5}
\]

This isolates a concrete sufficient condition for the Paley source conjecture:

\[
\vartheta(\overline{H_2(G_p)})
=O(p^{1-2\varepsilon})
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
U_4(p)=O(p^{1/2-\varepsilon}).
\tag{C05.6}
\]

C05.5 is not yet an exponent improvement: no sublinear-in-\(p\) uniform upper bound for the auxiliary theta quantity has been proved here. It is also not R20's one-localization graph; its vertices are **all Paley edges** and it records simultaneous degree-four compatibility.

For reference, the checker also verifies the exact quotient identities

\[
S E_2=2E_2+3E_3,
\qquad
E_2^2=E_2+6E_3+6E_4,
\qquad
(S)_4=24E_4
\]

on sample Paley graphs.

## 6. R12-C06 — affine symmetry reduces PSD to three Fourier block types

**STATUS: PROVED.**

The full degree-four moment matrix has dimension

\[
1+p+\frac{p(p-1)}4,
\]

so a uniform analytic dual needs more than naive matrix inspection. Affine Paley symmetry yields an exact reduction.

Average any feasible pseudoexpectation over

\[
x\mapsto ax+b,
\qquad b\in\mathbb F_p,
\quad a\in Q:=\{\text{nonzero squares}\}.
\]

This preserves feasibility and objective. Parameterize every undirected Paley edge uniquely as

\[
e_{x,[h]}=\{x-h,x+h\},
\]

where \(x\in\mathbb F_p\) is the midpoint and

\[
[h]\in R_p:=\{h\ne0:\chi(2h)=1\}/\{\pm1\}.
\]

The type set has

\[
|R_p|=r:=\frac{p-1}{4}.
\]

Translations act by

\[
e_{x,[h]}\mapsto e_{x+b,[h]},
\]

so Fourier transform in the midpoint coordinate decomposes the vertex and edge index spaces by additive frequency \(\xi\in\mathbb F_p\). The empty-set index lives only in frequency zero. Consequently the symmetrized moment matrix is unitarily equivalent to

- one \(\xi=0\) block of dimension \(r+2\): empty + vertex + \(r\) edge types;
- for every \(\xi\ne0\), one block of dimension \(r+1\): vertex + \(r\) edge types.

Square multipliers act by

\[
(\xi,[h])\mapsto(a^{-1}\xi,[ah]),
\qquad a\in Q.
\]

There are exactly two \(Q\)-orbits on nonzero frequencies: the square and nonsquare classes. Thus all nonzero-frequency blocks fall into only two unitary equivalence classes. We obtain:

\[
\boxed{
M\succeq0
\iff
M_0\succeq0,\quad M_Q\succeq0,\quad M_N\succeq0,
}
\tag{C06.1}
\]

where

\[
\dim M_0=(p-1)/4+2,
\qquad
\dim M_Q=\dim M_N=(p-1)/4+1.
\]

This is an exact reduction of the PSD part of the affine-symmetrized degree-four problem from \(\Theta(p^2)\) dimension to three block **types** of \(\Theta(p)\) dimension. It does not collapse the triangle/K4 moment orbit variables to constants; those remain the nontrivial arithmetic content.

The checker verifies the edge parameterization and affine action exactly at \(p=13,17,29,41\). It also records the first orbit-complexity transition:

| p | triangle affine-orbit sizes | K4 affine-orbit sizes |
|---:|---|---|
| 13 | 26 | none |
| 17 | 68 | none |
| 29 | 406 | 203 |
| 41 | 410, 820 | 205, 820 |

The p=41 split shows why a one-parameter `all triangles/all K4s equal` ansatz cannot be assumed uniformly.
