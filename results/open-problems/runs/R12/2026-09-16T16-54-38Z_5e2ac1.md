## 3. The three new-method attacks

### Method #5 — add parameters and optimize them

Instead of another adjacency/Hoffman certificate, I introduced the first two
entrywise character-polynomial directions that **vanish identically on every
required theta entry**. This gives an explicit two-parameter affine family
of valid dual matrices.

Result: the family is rigorously too expensive. Its best possible spectral
objective is bounded below by `p/3+O(1)`.

### Method #18 — rewrite the graph problem as incidence/character algebra

I rewrote the edge-compatibility geometry through the unsigned vertex-edge
incidence matrix and Paley Seidel matrix. The apparently large edge-edge
matrices then compress to the three-dimensional Paley association algebra
`span{I,J,Q}`, allowing an exact calculation.

Result: this exposes the three dangerous representation sectors (constant,
`+sqrt(p)`, `-sqrt(p)`) and makes the obstruction algebraic rather than
numerical.

### Method #24 — find the extremal object

Rayleigh lower bounds, rather than optimizing finite SDPs prime by prime.

Result: a positive convex combination cancels both free parameters and leaves
the exact unavoidable value

\frac p3+O(1).
\]
So this natural route cannot produce `o(p)` auxiliary theta.

These attacks do not move the frozen R12 exponent. They eliminate a

## 4. R12-C08 — exact incidence/character dual family

**STATUS: PROVED.** No literature-novelty claim is made.

Let `G=G_p`. Write `Q` for its Paley Seidel matrix,
\[
Q_{uv}=\chi(u-v),\qquad Q_{uu}=0,
\]
and let `N` be the unsigned edge-vertex incidence matrix of `G`. On the edge
set put
\[
C=NQN^T,
\qquad
L=NN^T-2I,
\]
so `L` is the ordinary line-graph adjacency matrix.

For an edge pair:
- on the diagonal, `(C,L)=(2,0)`;
- for two distinct overlapping edges whose union is a triangle,
  `(C,L)=(3,1)`;
- for two disjoint edges whose union is a `K_4`, `(C,L)=(4,0)`.

Consequently the two edge matrices
\[
K_1=C+L+2I-4J,
\tag{C08.1}
\]
\[
K_2=C^{\circ2}-7C-2I+12J
\tag{C08.2}
\]
vanish on the diagonal and on every edge of `H_2(G_p)`. Hence, in the
standard theta dual convention retained in C05,
\[
\boxed{B(t,s)=J+tK_1+sK_2}
\tag{C08.3}
\]
is a valid dual matrix for
`theta(overline(H_2(G_p)))` for every real `t,s`.

The first identity has an especially useful form:
\[
K_1=N(Q+I)N^T-4J.
\tag{C08.4}
\]

### Exact compression of the second direction

Put
\[
d=\frac{p-1}2,\qquad
A=\frac{J-I+Q}2,
\qquad
D=N^TN=dI+A
=
\frac{p-2}2I+\frac12J+\frac12Q.
\]
The Paley identities
\[
QJ=0,\qquad Q^2=pI-J
\]
give
\[
N^TCN=DQD
=
\frac{p(p-2)}2I
-\frac{p-2}2J
+\frac{p^2-3p+4}4Q.
\tag{C08.5}
\]

The only nontrivial ingredient is
\[
S:=N^T(C^{\circ2})N.
\]
I give the full count because this is the part that must not be inferred from
finite primes.

The Paley graph has strongly regular parameters
\[
d=\frac{p-1}2,\qquad
\lambda=\frac{p-5}4,\qquad
\mu=\frac{p-1}4.
\]
For `u=v`, summing over the two stars gives
\[
S_{uu}
=
4d+9d\lambda+d(d-1-\lambda)
=
\frac{5(p-1)(p-3)}4.
\tag{C08.6}
\]

For `u != v`, put `q=A_uv` and
`c_q=lambda` if `q=1`, `c_q=mu` if `q=0`. Write the two incident edges as
`e={u,a}`, `f={v,b}`, and define
\[
\alpha=A_{ub},\quad
\beta=A_{av},\quad
\gamma=A_{ab},
\]
\[
X=q+\alpha+\beta+\gamma,
\qquad
h=1_{\{a=v\}}+1_{\{b=u\}}+1_{\{a=b\}}.
\]
Then
\[
C_{ef}=2X-4+h.
\tag{C08.7}
\]

The required sums over
`a in N(u), b in N(v)` are:
\[
\sum\alpha=\sum\beta=dc_q,
\qquad
\sum\gamma=g_q:=(A^3)_{uv},
\]
\[
\sum\alpha\beta=c_q^2,
\qquad
\sum\alpha\gamma
=
\sum\beta\gamma
=c_q\lambda,
\]
\[
\sum h=2qd+c_q,
\qquad
\sum h^2=2qd+c_q+2q,
\]
\[
\sum Xh=q(4d+2\lambda)+c_q(q+2).
\tag{C08.8}
\]
Since
\[
A^2=(d-\mu)I+(\lambda-\mu)A+\mu J,
\]
one obtains
\[
g_0=\frac{(p-3)(p-1)}8,
\qquad
g_1=\frac{p^2-2p+9}8.
\tag{C08.9}
\]
Expanding
\[
C_{ef}^2
=
4X^2-16X+16+4Xh-8h+h^2
\]
and substituting C08.8--C08.9 gives
\[
S_{uv}
=
\begin{cases}
\dfrac{4p^2-23p+55}4,&u\sim v,\\[2mm]
\dfrac{(p-1)(4p-7)}4,&u\not\sim v.
\end{cases}
\tag{C08.10}
\]
Together with C08.6 this is exactly
\[
S=
\frac{p^2-3p-16}4I
+
\frac{4p^2-17p+31}4J
-
\frac{3(p-4)}2Q.
\tag{C08.11}
\]

Now use
\[
N^TK_2N
=
S-7DQD-2D+12d^2J
\]
to obtain the closed form
\[
\boxed{
N^TK_2N
=
\frac{p-1}4
\left[
-(13p-8)I
+(16p-11)J
-(7p-8)Q
\right].
}
\tag{C08.12}
\]

This is the exact all-prime compression used below.

## 5. R12-X06 — the two-parameter character-polynomial dual is intrinsically linear

**STATUS: PROVED RESTRICTED-FAMILY OBSTRUCTION.** It is not a lower bound on
the unrestricted auxiliary theta number.

Let
\[
n_H=|E(G_p)|=\frac{p(p-1)}4.
\]
For the constant edge vector, C08 gives the Rayleigh/eigenvalue
\[
c_0(t,s)
=
n_H-(p-1)^2t+(4p^2-6p+2)s.
\tag{X06.1}
\]

For a nonconstant Paley eigenvector `x` with
\[
Qx=\sigma\sqrt p\,x,
\qquad \sigma\in\{+1,-1\},
\]
put `y=Nx`. Since
\[
Dx=d_\sigma x,\qquad
d_\sigma=\frac{p-2+\sigma\sqrt p}2,
\]
C08.4 gives the exact `K_1` Rayleigh value
\[
\kappa_\sigma
=
(1+\sigma\sqrt p)d_\sigma.
\tag{X06.2}
\]
C08.12 gives the exact `K_2` Rayleigh value
\[
\mu_\sigma
=
\frac{p-1}{4d_\sigma}
\left[
-(13p-8)-(7p-8)\sigma\sqrt p
\right].
\tag{X06.3}
\]
Therefore every matrix in the valid dual family C08.3 obeys
\[
\lambda_{\max}(B(t,s))
\ge
\max\{c_0(t,s),\
t\kappa_++s\mu_+,\
t\kappa_-+s\mu_-\}.
\tag{X06.4}
\]

This lower envelope can be optimized **exactly**. Put
\[
r=\sqrt p,
\qquad
\Delta=3p^2-16p+11.
\]
Define
\[
a_0=
\frac{2(2p^2-11p+8)}{p\Delta},
\]
\[
a_+
=
\frac{(p-1)(r+2)(3r^3-4r^2-9r+8)}{2p\Delta},
\]
\[
a_-
=
\frac{(p-1)(r-2)(3r^3+4r^2-9r-8)}{2p\Delta}.
\tag{X06.5}
\]
For every admissible `p>=5`, all three coefficients are positive:
`Delta>0` and `2p^2-11p+8>0` already at `p=5` and then increase;
the `a_+` cubic is increasing for `r>=sqrt(5)` and equals
`6(sqrt(5)-2)>0` at the endpoint; the `a_-` cubic is already positive at
`r=2` and increasing. Direct algebra gives
\[
a_0+a_++a_-=1,
\]
\[
a_0[-(p-1)^2]+a_+\kappa_++a_-\kappa_-=0,
\]
\[
a_0(4p^2-6p+2)+a_+\mu_++a_-\mu_-=0.
\tag{X06.6}
\]
Taking this positive convex combination of the three quantities in X06.4
cancels **both** free parameters:
\[
\boxed{
\lambda_{\max}(B(t,s))
\ge
z_p:=
\frac{(p-1)(2p^2-11p+8)}{2(3p^2-16p+11)}
=
\frac p3+O(1).
}
\tag{X06.7}
\]
For every admissible `p>=13`,
\[
z_p\ge \frac p4,
\]
because
\[
z_p-\frac p4
=
\frac{p^3-10p^2+27p-16}
{4(3p^2-16p+11)}>0.
\]

Moreover the three affine lower bounds meet at
\[
t_*=
\frac{7p^2-35p+24}
{4(3p^2-16p+11)},
\qquad
s_*=
\frac{(p-1)(p-4)}
{4(3p^2-16p+11)},
\tag{X06.8}
\]
where each one equals `z_p`. Thus X06.7 is the exact minimax value of this
three-Rayleigh lower envelope.
