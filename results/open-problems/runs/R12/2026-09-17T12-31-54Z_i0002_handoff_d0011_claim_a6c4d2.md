## 3. Method #25 — count the same thing twice: the nonreversible dissipation is exactly SRW Dirichlet energy

Counting measure is invariant because every vertex also has exactly two incoming directed edges, one horizontal and one vertical. Hence the counting-measure adjoint is

\[
(L_\omega^* f)(x,y)

Therefore the additive symmetric part is **environment-independent**:

\[

The left side is the dissipation of the full nonreversible Manhattan walk; the right side is exactly the Dirichlet energy of continuous-time simple random walk with rate `1/2` along each of the four undirected nearest-neighbor edges. This is the useful double count.

## 4. Method #29 — add the boundary at infinity: exact killed-box theorem

### Theorem C22 — uniform quenched confined-return bound

For every environment `omega`, every integer `R>=0`, and every `t>=0`,

\[
\boxed{

\le
\exp\!\left[-2\left(1-\cos\frac{\pi}{2R+2}\right)t\right]
\le
\exp\!\left[-\frac{t}{(R+1)^2}\right].
}
\tag{C22}
\]

#### Proof

Let `u_t=e^{tL_{omega,R}}u`. From (3.4), now with zero extension outside `B_R`,

\[
\frac{d}{dt}\|u_t\|_2^2
=2\operatorname{Re}\langle u_t,L_{\omega,R}u_t\rangle
=2\langle u_t,S_Ru_t\rangle.
\tag{4.1}
\]

The exact first Dirichlet eigenvalue of `-S_R` on the `(2R+1) x (2R+1)` square is

\[
\lambda_R
=2\left(1-\cos\frac{\pi}{2R+2}\right).
\tag{4.2}

Indeed, in one coordinate the killed operator is

\[
\frac12[f(j+1)+f(j-1)-2f(j)],

whose first Dirichlet eigenvalue on `2R+1` sites is

\[
1-\cos\frac{\pi}{2R+2};
\]

the two coordinate contributions add. Therefore the Dirichlet Poincare inequality gives

\[
\frac{d}{dt}\|u_t\|_2^2
\le -2\lambda_R\|u_t\|_2^2,

hence

\[
\|e^{tL_{\omega,R}}\|_{2\to2}\le e^{-\lambda_R t}.
\tag{4.3}

The killed diagonal kernel is nonnegative and equals the confined-return probability, so

\[
\mathbb P_0^\omega(X_t=0,\tau_R>t)
