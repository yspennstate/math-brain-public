# 3. Method #14 — the full D4 action and the exact two-parameter reduction

It is useful to introduce both horizontal and vertical persistence parameters at once. For iid fair horizontal line signs `H_y` and vertical line signs `V_x`, and `a,b in [-1,1]`, define the discrete-time kernel

\[
\begin{aligned}
(P_{a,b}^{H,V}f)(x,y)
&=\frac14\sum_{d=\pm1}(1+d a H_y)f(x+d,y)\\
&\quad+\frac14\sum_{d=\pm1}(1+d b V_x)f(x,y+d).
\end{aligned}
\tag{3.1}
\]

Thus `P_{1,1}` is exactly the embedded source walk, while `P_{1,0}` has statically oriented horizontal rows and fresh symmetric vertical steps. Put

\[
F_n(a,b)=\mathbb E\big[(P_{a,b}^{H,V})^n(0,0)\big].
\tag{3.2}
\]

### C10 — exact D4 symmetry

For every `n`,

\[
\boxed{
F_n(a,b)=F_n(-a,b)=F_n(a,-b)=F_n(b,a).
}
\tag{C10}
\]

Consequently there is a symmetric polynomial `Q_n` such that

\[
\boxed{F_n(a,b)=Q_n(a^2,b^2),\qquad Q_n(u,v)=Q_n(v,u).}
\tag{3.3}
\]

#### Proof

Flipping all horizontal signs `H -> -H` preserves the environment law and sends `a -> -a`; flipping all vertical signs does the same for `b`. A quarter-turn of the lattice exchanges horizontal rows and vertical columns and exchanges the two persistence parameters, up to a sign absorbed by the two global sign flips. Since each `n`-step transition probability is polynomial in `a,b`, separate evenness gives the polynomial representation in `a^2,b^2`. QED.

This is the useful group quotient: the source-versus-partial comparison is the one-variable edge

\[
q\longmapsto F_n(1,q)=Q_n(1,q^2),
\qquad q\in[0,1].
\]

The group action by itself does **not** give a sign, but it says that the natural variable is `q^2`, not `q`.

# 6. Method #11 — dualize by parity sectors: the exact Schur-complement compiler

Fix spatial frequency `p` and `lambda>0`, and use the source notation

\[

Let `J_H` flip all horizontal line signs and `J_V` flip all vertical line signs. Then

- `H` commutes with both `J_H,J_V`;
- `A_H` flips horizontal parity and preserves vertical parity;
- `A_V` preserves horizontal parity and flips vertical parity.

Thus

\[
\mathcal H
=\mathcal H_{00}\oplus\mathcal H_{10}\oplus\mathcal H_{01}\oplus\mathcal H_{11},
\]

where the indices record the two sign parities, is the natural quotient. The constant vector `1` lies in `H_00`.

Group total parity as

\[
\mathcal H_E=\mathcal H_{00}\oplus\mathcal H_{11},
\qquad
\mathcal H_O=\mathcal H_{10}\oplus\mathcal H_{01}.

\[

\begin{pmatrix}
0&C_q\\
-C_q^*&0
\end{pmatrix}

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{Re}\langle1,(H-A_q)^{-1}1\rangle

\left\langle1,
\big(H_E+C_qH_O^{-1}C_q^*\big)^{-1}1
\right\rangle.

\tag{C12}

This is the positive self-adjoint form hidden inside the nonreversible resolvent.

Now split the even space once more as `00 + 11`. Because `A_H` and `A_V` use different parity edges, the positive penalty in C12 has the exact structural form
