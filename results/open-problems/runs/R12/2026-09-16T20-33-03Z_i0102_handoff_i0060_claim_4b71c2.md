# R12-C11 — the full quartic character tensor is exactly S3-invariant

**STATUS: PROVED.**  
**Scope:** an exact algebraic identity for every odd prime. It is a route theorem, **not** a source-level exponent improvement and **not** a refutation of Conjecture 28.

Let `chi` be the quadratic character on `F_p`, extended by `chi(0)=0`. Define

\[
\begin{aligned}
T_p(\xi,\eta,\zeta)
:=\sum_{s\in\mathbb F_p}\chi\Bigl(&
(s-\tfrac{\xi+\eta-\zeta}{2})
(s+\tfrac{\zeta}{2})\\
&\cdot(s-\tfrac{\eta}{2})
(s-\tfrac{\xi}{2})
\Bigr).
\end{aligned}
\tag{C11.1}
\]

This is the quartic character sum appearing in C09 after the additive Fourier change of variables. Then

\[
\boxed{
T_p(\xi,\eta,\zeta)
=
T_p(\sigma(\xi,\eta,\zeta))
\quad\text{for every }\sigma\in S_3.
}
\tag{C11.2}
\]

### Proof

It suffices to check the adjacent transpositions `(xi eta)` and `(eta zeta)`.

Multiply every root in C11.1 by `2`; since `2` is invertible, the doubled-root multiset is

\[
R(\xi,\eta,\zeta)
=
\{\xi+\eta-\zeta,\ -\zeta,\ \eta,\ \xi\}.
\tag{C11.3}
\]

Under `xi <-> eta`,

\[
R(\eta,\xi,\zeta)
=
\{\eta+\xi-\zeta,-\zeta,\xi,\eta\}
=
R(\xi,\eta,\zeta)
\]

as multisets. Therefore the character sum is unchanged.

Under `eta <-> zeta`,

\[
R(\xi,\zeta,\eta)
=
\{\xi+\zeta-\eta,-\eta,\zeta,\xi\}.
\]

Put `d=zeta-eta`. Translating every element of C11.3 by `d` gives

\[
\begin{aligned}
R(\xi,\eta,\zeta)+d
&=
\{\xi,-\eta,\zeta,\xi+\zeta-\eta\}\\
&=R(\xi,\zeta,\eta)
\end{aligned}
\]

as multisets. Thus the actual roots in C11.1 are translated by `d/2`. The substitution

\[
s\longmapsto s+d/2
\]

permutes `F_p`, so the complete character sum is unchanged. The two transpositions generate `S_3`, proving C11.2. `QED`
