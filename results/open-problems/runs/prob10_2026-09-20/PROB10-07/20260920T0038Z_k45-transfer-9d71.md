\[
\Pr(e,f\in F)\le \Pr(e\in F)\Pr(f\in F).
\tag{2.1}

## 4. Transfer lemma

Fix `m in {4,5}` and let the left terminals be `[m]={0,...,m-1}`. A state is a set partition `pi` of `[m]` recording which terminals are already connected by the processed part of the forest.

When a new right vertex arrives, choose a spoke subset `S subseteq [m]`. Because the previously chosen edges form a forest, adding the new star creates a cycle **iff** two elements of `S` already lie in the same block of `pi`. Thus `S` is legal exactly when it meets each block of `pi` in at most one point. When legal, the new partition is obtained by merging precisely the blocks hit by `S`.

Let `M_m` be the partition-state transfer matrix whose `(pi,rho)` entry is the number of legal `S` producing `rho`. Empty `S` and the `m` singleton sets preserve every partition, so every diagonal entry is `m+1`. Every other legal transition strictly coarsens the partition. Therefore

\[
M_m=(m+1)I+N_m,
\qquad N_m^m=0.
\tag{4.1}
\]

Let `v` be the row vector supported on the discrete partition and `1` the all-ones column vector. Let `E_0` be the marked transfer retaining only legal subsets containing terminal `0`, `E_1` the corresponding matrix for terminal `1`, and `E_{01}` the matrix retaining legal subsets containing both.

By edge transitivity and right-vertex symmetry, write `Z` for the total forest count, `E` for the count containing one fixed edge, and `R,L,D` for counts containing respectively a pair sharing the same right vertex, sharing the same left vertex but different right vertices, or vertex-disjoint. Then

\[
Z=vM_m^n\mathbf1,
\quad E=vE_0M_m^{n-1}\mathbf1,
\quad R=vE_{01}M_m^{n-1}\mathbf1,
\tag{4.2}
\]

and for `n>=2`,

\[
L=vE_0E_0M_m^{n-2}\mathbf1,
\qquad D=vE_0E_1M_m^{n-2}\mathbf1.
\tag{4.3}
\]

By (4.1), for every integer `k>=0`,

\[
M_m^k=\sum_{j=0}^{m-1}\binom{k}{j}(m+1)^{k-j}N_m^j.
\tag{4.4}
\]

Thus all five marked counts are explicit polynomials in `n` times `(m+1)^n`. The finite coefficient extraction from (4.2)-(4.4) is reconstructed from the combinatorial definition by the exact checker described below.

## 7. Conversion from counts to probabilities

For any edge orbit, `P(e)=E/Z` and `P(e,f)=Q/Z`, where `Q` is `R`, `L`, or `D`. Hence

\[
\Pr(e)\Pr(f)-\Pr(e,f)=\frac{E^2-ZQ}{Z^2}=\frac{\Delta}{Z^2}.
\]

Sections 5-6 therefore establish the exact Grimmett–Winkler pairwise statement in the original uniform-all-forests model. No spanning-tree, fixed-component, or random-minimum-spanning-tree measure enters the proof.
