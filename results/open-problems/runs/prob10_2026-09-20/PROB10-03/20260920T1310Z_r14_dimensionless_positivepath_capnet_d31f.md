## 6. Attack C — exact dual cap-covering reformulation, and why the naive epsilon-net proof is vacuous

This attack is materially different from the lifted variational compiler and from the preceding one-facet polytope first moment.

Write `sigma=sqrt(N)u`. SAT is equivalent to existence of `u in S^{N-1}` such that

`g_a dot u >= -1` for every `a<=M`.

Equivalently, UNSAT means that the random violation caps

`C_a={u in S^{N-1}: g_a dot u < -1}`

cover the sphere.

A tempting route is a deterministic epsilon-net plus a union bound. The following obstruction shows that the standard version cannot prove a constant-alpha UNSAT bound.

### Proposition R14-T03-CAP-NET-OBSTRUCTION

Fix `eta>0` and `M=floor(alpha N)` with fixed `alpha`. On the event

`L_N:=max_{a<=M} ||g_a|| <= 2sqrt(N)`,                       (6.1)

if the instance is SAT, then an epsilon-net with

`epsilon=eta/(2sqrt(N))`                                    (6.2)

contains a point `v` satisfying

`g_a dot v >= -1-eta` for every `a`.                         (6.3)

Any standard volumetric net can be taken with size at most

`|Net| <= (1+2/epsilon)^N=(1+4sqrt(N)/eta)^N`.               (6.4)

For each fixed `v`,

`P(g_a dot v >= -1-eta for all a)=Phi(1+eta)^M`.             (6.5)

Therefore the direct net union bound has logarithm

`N log(1+4sqrt(N)/eta)+M log Phi(1+eta)`

`=(1/2)N log N+O(N)`,                                       (6.6)

which tends to `+infinity` for every fixed `alpha,eta`. Hence this standard fixed-slack epsilon-net union bound is asymptotically vacuous at every constant density `alpha`.

#### Proof

If `u` is feasible and `||u-v||<=epsilon`, then on (6.1)

`g_a dot v >= g_a dot u-||g_a|| epsilon >= -1-eta`,

proving (6.3). The usual volumetric covering estimate gives (6.4). For fixed unit `v`, every `g_a dot v` is independent `N(0,1)`, giving (6.5). Substituting (6.2) yields (6.6). Finally, standard Gaussian norm concentration and `M=O(N)` imply `P(L_N>2sqrt(N))->0`, so (6.1) is a high-probability event. QED.

This does **not** rule out a sophisticated covering proof. It rules out only the naive deterministic-net/Lipschitz/union-bound route. Any useful geometric dual attack at constant density must exploit correlations, chaining, intrinsic volumes, or many-facet structure rather than finer bookkeeping of the same net.
