## 7. Rigorous fourth-trace bound by Gaussian four-cycle HGR

The trace is handled without importing the old `r=7/8` trace cap.

Under the unrestricted four-step AR cycle, normalized cycle vertices `(X_0,...,X_3)` have adjacent and opposite correlations

\[
a=\frac{r+r^3}{1+r^4},\qquad
b=\frac{2r^2}{1+r^4}.
\tag{7.1}
\]

Put

\[
A_i=\frac{X_i+X_{i+1}}{\sqrt{2(1+r)}},
\qquad
V_i=\sqrt{s}A_i+\sqrt{1-s}E_i.
\tag{7.2}
\]

A direct covariance calculation gives

\[
v=\frac{1+a}{1+r},\qquad
 g=\frac{1+b+2a}{2(1+r)},\qquad
 h=\frac{a+b}{1+r},
\tag{7.3}
\]

and hence

\[
\rho_{\rm adj}=\frac{sg}{sv+1-s},\qquad
\rho_{\rm opp}=\frac{sh}{sv+1-s}.
\tag{7.4}
\]

The four-step Hermite trace of the unrestricted AR kernel is `1/(1-r^4)`. Conditioning on the cycle vertices and adjoining the independent `E_i` therefore gives the exact identity

\[
\operatorname{tr}(K^4)
=\frac{1}{1-r^4}
\Pr(V_0<0,V_1<0,V_2<0,V_3<0).
\tag{7.5}
\]

Write `S_i=sgn(V_i)` and

\[
q_{\rm adj}=\frac2\pi\arcsin\rho_{\rm adj},\qquad
q_{\rm opp}=\frac2\pi\arcsin\rho_{\rm opp}.
\tag{7.6}
\]

Expanding the four indicators gives

\[
p_4:=\Pr(V_i<0\ \forall i)
=\frac{1+4q_{\rm adj}+2q_{\rm opp}+m_4}{16},
\quad
m_4=\mathbb E S_0S_1S_2S_3.
\tag{7.7}
\]

Now group the opposite pairs and set

\[
A=S_0S_2,\qquad B=S_1S_3.

Both have mean `q_opp` and variance `1-q_opp^2`. For the jointly Gaussian blocks `(V_0,V_2)` and `(V_1,V_3)`, the cross-covariance is rank one in symmetric/antisymmetric coordinates. The nonzero canonical correlation is exactly

\[
