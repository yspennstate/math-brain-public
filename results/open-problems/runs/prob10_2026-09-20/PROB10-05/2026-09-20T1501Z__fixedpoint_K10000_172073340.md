## 5. New mathematics: outward fixed-point enclosure through K=10000

The prior checker used exact `Fraction` arithmetic for `K=1000`. Extending that literally causes numerator/denominator growth of tens of thousands of bits and becomes unnecessarily expensive. The key observation is that every operation in (4.9)--(4.11) is monotone on the positive cone. Therefore a fixed-denominator outward interval recurrence remains rigorous.

Let `S=10^60`. For any positive rational `x`, define

\[
\lfloor x\rfloor_S=\frac{\lfloor Sx\rfloor}{S},
\qquad
\lceil x\rceil_S=\frac{\lceil Sx\rceil}{S}.
\tag{5.1}
\]

Initialize exact intervals

\[
P_1\in[1,1],\qquad D_1\in[1/2,1/2],\qquad q^2\in[q^2,q^2].
\]

Inductively, if `P_j in [P_j^-,P_j^+]`, `D_j in[D_j^-,D_j^+]`, and `q^{2j} in[Q_j^-,Q_j^+]`, set

\[

\quad
P_{j+1}^+=
\left\lceil 1+\frac{2j}{2j-1}a_0P_j^+\right\rceil_S,
\tag{5.2}
\]

\[
D_{j+1}^-=
\left\lfloor D_j^-\frac{(2j-1)^2}{(2j+2)(2j+1)}\right\rfloor_S,
\quad
D_{j+1}^+=
\left\lceil D_j^+\frac{(2j-1)^2}{(2j+2)(2j+1)}\right\rceil_S,
\tag{5.3}
\]

and similarly

\[
Q_{j+1}^- = \lfloor Q_j^-q^2\rfloor_S,
\qquad
Q_{j+1}^+ = \lceil Q_j^+q^2\rceil_S.
\tag{5.4}
\]
