## 5. Attack 2 — signed two-mode continuous covariance comparator

To attack the unchanged upper endpoint continuously, consider the broader two-exponential family

\[
C(t)=a e^{-\lambda|t|}+(1-a)e^{-\mu|t|},
\qquad 0<\lambda<\mu,
\tag{5.1}
\]

allowing a signed coefficient. Its spectral density, up to a positive common constant, is

\[
\frac{a\lambda}{\lambda^2+\omega^2}
+\frac{(1-a)\mu}{\mu^2+\omega^2}.
\tag{5.2}
\]

After multiplying by the positive denominator, nonnegativity for every `omega` is equivalent to the two endpoint conditions

\[
\lambda+a(\mu-\lambda)\ge0,
\qquad
\mu-a(\mu-\lambda)\ge0.
\tag{5.3}
\]

Hence the exact positive-definiteness range is

\[
\boxed{-\frac{\lambda}{\mu-\lambda}\le a\le
\frac{\mu}{\mu-\lambda}.}
\tag{5.4}
\]

This confirms that signed weights genuinely enlarge the positive-definite class; the earlier positive-mixture tail obstruction was not the whole story.

However the exact Lamperti tail is

\[

-\frac13e^{-5t/4}+O(e^{-7t/4}).
\tag{5.5}
\]

If `lambda<1/4` and `a>0`, then `C(t)/R(t)->+infinity`, so `C<=R` eventually is impossible. Escaping this requires `a<=0`, which is allowed by (5.4), but then the resulting two-dimensional Gaussian Markov/state-space observation has no exact scalar persistence exponent available to this run. ## 6. Attack 3 — continuous interpolation via local regularity/Rice: obstruction located

A reverse grid-to-continuum implication would be needed to turn a discrete persistence estimate into a source-valid upper bound. The local Lamperti covariance is much smoother than an OU covariance, which makes a fixed-mesh unconditional correction tempting but insufficient.

Differentiate (2.1) at zero:

\[
X'(0)=\sqrt{\frac52}\left(-\frac54 I(1)+B_{1/4}(1)\right).
\tag{6.1}
\]

Direct covariance integration gives

\[
\operatorname{Var}I(1)=\frac25,
\qquad
\operatorname{Cov}(I(1),B_{1/4}(1))=\frac12.
\tag{6.2}
\]

Therefore

\[
\operatorname{Var}X'(0)=\frac52\left(
\frac{25}{16}\frac25+1-\frac52\frac12\right)
=\frac{15}{16},
\tag{6.3}
\]

and stationarity implies

\[
R(t)=1-\frac{15}{32}t^2+o(t^2).
\tag{6.4}
\]

The standard Rice intensity of zero upcrossings is consequently

\[
\nu_0^+=\frac{1}{2\pi}\sqrt{-R''(0)}
=\frac{\sqrt{15}}{8\pi}>0.
\tag{6.5}
\]

Thus an unconditional crossing-count/union-bound correction over a horizon of Lamperti length `L` is naturally of order `L`, not exponentially small relative to the persistence probability. This does **not** prove that reverse interpolation is impossible; it proves that the naive fixed-mesh Rice/union route cannot preserve the persistence exponent. A successful reverse interpolation must instead control bridge crossings **conditional on long grid survival**, or establish a uniform spectral-radius approximation as the mesh tends to zero. No discrete-to-continuous upper bound is promoted here.
