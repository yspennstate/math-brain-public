1. **One-variable cavity (run42 / C298).** Exposing one variable gives an exact finite-`n` comparison, but a fixed positive multiplicative loss in satisfiability probability is paid per exposed variable. Iterating it over `Theta(n)` variables destroys threshold-scale information.

A formula violating one clause and a satisfiable formula both have normalized ground-state defect tending to zero, so ordinary thermodynamic compactness erases the distinction needed for (BLOCK).

### (c) What a proof must contain that none of these routes contains

A successful route needs a **boundary-sensitive, model-specific quantity** that simultaneously has:

- enough interpolation/subadditivity stability to compare different system sizes;

## 3. The three thinking moves

### #11 DUALIZE — move from solutions/boosters to exact UNSAT certificates

The previous route tracked the geometry of the solution set and asked how a booster kills it. Dualizing asks instead: **what witness certifies that the solution set is empty, and can its occurrence be controlled with a summable tail?**

For 2-SAT the answer is an implication-cycle/SCC certificate.
