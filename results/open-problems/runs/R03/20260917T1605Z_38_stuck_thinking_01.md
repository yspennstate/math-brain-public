## 7. (e) Nearest solved problem and the ingredient it has that R03 lacks

The nearest solved primary result is Jain--Sah--Sawhney's sharp theorem for iid entries from a finite-support distribution that is **not uniform on its support**.

Their proof has exactly one kind of resource the uniform Rademacher endpoint lacks: a strict distributional entropy/norm gap.
