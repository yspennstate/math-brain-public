## 5. Method #20 — FIND WHY THE ALGORITHM FAILS: the sparse equal-triple cover cannot be an I0095 lower-witness engine

For a fixed sign matrix `A` and threshold `d`, define for each row `a_i`

\[
B_i(d)=\{[x]\in\{\pm1\}^n/\{\pm1\}: |a_i\cdot x|\ge d\}.
\]

Then

\[
\operatorname{disc}(A)\ge d
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\bigcup_i B_i(d)=\{\pm1\}^n/\{\pm1\}.
\tag{5.1}
\]

This is exactly the old R02 covering-code dictionary in set-cover form.
