#### #17 LOOK FOR YOUR NUMBERS SOMEWHERE ELSE — hypercube edge isoperimetry / stability

For `F subset {0,1}^n`, let `k_i` be the number of internal cube edges of `F` in coordinate direction `i`; these are exactly the double-fiber counts from the previous report. The weak edge-isoperimetric inequality

\[
|\partial F|\ge |F|\log_2\frac{2^n}{|F|}

\[
\sum_i k_i\le \frac{|F|}{2}\log_2|F|.

Equality is attained by subcubes.
