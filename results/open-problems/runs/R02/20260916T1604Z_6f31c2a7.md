## 5. What a successful proof must contain that none of these routes contains

The product obstruction identifies the missing kind of information more sharply than another covariance norm estimate would.

A successful argument must have a **transfer mechanism from the localized/conditional problem back to the original Dirichlet form which tolerates a small number of high-covariance conditional directions**. In particular it must pass the following consistency test:

- for a product log-concave prior, coordinatewise posterior variances may develop extreme spikes on the `1/log n` scale (`C004`);
- nevertheless the original Poincare constant is dimension-free by one-dimensional control plus tensorization;
- therefore the transfer cannot declare failure solely from `max_i Var(X_i | localization data)` or `||A_t||_op`.

What is absent from every R02 covariance route so far is exactly the analogue of tensorization/conditional-variance decomposition for a **dependent** log-concave measure: a way to charge a high localized direction only according to the amount of Dirichlet/variance mass of the test function actually carried in that direction, while controlling interactions with the remaining coordinates.

This is deliberately stated as a requirement on a proof architecture, not as a new private conjecture or theorem. R02 remains attached to the outside KLS source.

## 7. Nearest solved analogue and the ingredient it has

The nearest solved analogue for the obstruction actually observed in C004 is the **product-measure case**.

Bobkov--Houdre, *Isoperimetric constants for product probability measures*, Ann. Probab. 25 (1997), 184--205, prove dimension-free isoperimetric bounds for product probability measures and derive analytic inequalities including Poincare-type consequences. For variance-normalized one-dimensional log-concave factors, the relevant one-dimensional constants are universal; tensorization then gives a dimension-free product Poincare constant.

The key ingredient is exact independence: variance/Dirichlet energy decomposes coordinatewise and tensorizes. A rare coordinate with a large conditional posterior variance does not force the original global Poincare constant to be large.

An unconditional log-concave law has coordinate-reflection symmetry but generally **not independence**, so this exact tensorization step is precisely what is missing. This is the closest solved mechanism to the failure exposed by C004.

For comparison, the spherically symmetric log-concave class is also known to satisfy KLS (Bobkov's theorem, sharpened by Bonnefont--Joulin--Ma, JFA 2016); its proof uses radial/angular decomposition and an effectively one-dimensional radial problem. Again, a symmetry-driven decomposition is present there and absent in the general class.
