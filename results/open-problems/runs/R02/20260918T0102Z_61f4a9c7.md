## 4. New route before code

### Method #12 — FIND WHAT IS CONSERVED: vertex splitting preserves cyclomatic number

Let `H` be a connected simple graph with minimum degree at least three and cyclomatic number

`beta(H)=|E(H)|-|V(H)|+1=7`.

Write `v=|V(H)|`, `m=|E(H)|`. Then `m=v+6`, while minimum degree at least three gives

`3v <= 2m = 2v+12`,

hence `v<=12`. Moreover

`sum_x (deg(x)-3) = 2m-3v = 12-v`.

If `v<12`, some vertex `x` has degree at least four. Partition its distinct neighbors into two nonempty groups `A,B` with `|A|,|B|>=2`. Replace `x` by two new vertices `x_A,x_B`, attach the neighbors in `A` to `x_A`, the neighbors in `B` to `x_B`, and add the new edge `x_A x_B`.

This operation:

1. preserves simplicity, because the old neighbor set is partitioned and `x_A,x_B` are new;
2. preserves connectedness, because the two replacement vertices are joined;
3. preserves minimum degree at least three, since `deg(x_A)=|A|+1>=3` and similarly for `x_B`;
4. adds one vertex and one edge, hence preserves `beta=m-v+1` exactly.

Repeat until the graph has 12 vertices. At `v=12`, `m=18` and the degree sum is 36. Since every degree is at least three, every degree is exactly three. Thus the final graph `C` is a connected simple cubic graph on 12 vertices.

Reversing each split contracts the newly inserted split edge and recovers the preceding graph exactly. Therefore:

> **Cubic-completion lemma.** Every connected simple graph `H` with `delta(H)>=3` and `beta(H)=7` is a contraction minor of a connected simple cubic graph on 12 vertices.

This is the conservation mechanism: fixed cyclomatic number plus minimum degree drives every core to a cubic extremal object without changing `beta`.

### Method #3 — PROVE SOMETHING STRONGER: certify the cubic extremizers in the weighted/minor-closed class

Unit p-NC alone is not obviously contraction-closed at graph level. The correct stronger target is the **independence-correlated** graphic matroid property. Cocks's fixed-graph theorem upgrades a graph satisfying the unit uniform-forest conjecture to an independence-correlated graphic matroid. Semple--Welsh Proposition 3.5 then gives minor closure.

So it suffices to prove the unit conjecture on every connected simple cubic graph on 12 vertices. Cocks upgrades each endpoint graph to the weighted matroid statement, and minor closure transfers that stronger property to every contraction minor produced by the cubic-completion lemma.

### Method #18 — SAY IT IN ANOTHER LANGUAGE: graphic-matroid independence polynomial

For a graph `G`, let

`I_G(y)=sum_{F forest} prod_{e in F} y_e`.

For distinct edges `e,f`, independence correlation is the positive-weight Rayleigh inequality

`Delta_ef(y)=I_e(y) I_f(y)-I(y) I_ef(y) >= 0` for every `y>0`.

Semple--Welsh explicitly proves this class is closed under matroid minors. For completeness, contraction can also be seen directly from multiaffinity: write

`I=A+y_g B`,

where `B` is the contraction polynomial. For `e,f != g`, `Delta_ef(I)` is a polynomial in `y_g` whose leading coefficient is `Delta_ef(B)`. If the full polynomial is nonnegative for every positive `y_g`, that leading coefficient cannot be negative. Deletion is the `y_g -> 0+` limit. This is exactly the closure needed after the cubic lift.

## 6. The theorem obtained

**Theorem (P0001 handoff, PARTIAL relative to Grimmett Conjecture 4.2).** Let `G` be a finite simple graph. If every nontrivial 2-connected block `B` of `G` satisfies

`beta(B)=|E(B)|-|V(B)|+1 <= 7`,

then the uniform probability measure on forests of `G` has pairwise nonpositive edge correlations.

### Proof

**Step 1: reduce to blocks.** A cycle is contained in a 2-connected block, and block edge sets are disjoint. Hence a global edge set is a forest iff its restriction to every block is a forest. The uniform forest law factors over blocks; edges in distinct blocks are independent. It suffices to prove p-NC on one nontrivial block.

**Step 2: simplify each block.** Apply Huang's simplification equivalence as in the preceding R02 run: remove pivotal edges, suppress degree-two vertices, and simplify parallel edges until reaching nontrivial connected simple terminal cores of minimum degree at least three. These operations do not increase cyclomatic number, and the source p-NC property is transferred by Huang's theorem.

**Step 3: cores with `beta<=6`.** These are already covered by the previous R02 theorem/certificate, which established `r_block>=6`.

**Step 4: a core with `beta=7`.** By the cubic-completion lemma in §4, it is a contraction minor of some connected simple cubic graph `C` on 12 vertices.

**Step 5: certify `C` and strengthen.** The exact certificate in §5 verifies the unit uniform-forest p-NC inequality for every one of the 85 connected simple cubic 12-vertex graphs. By Cocks's fixed-graph theorem, `M(C)` is therefore independence correlated for all positive edge weights.

**Step 6: contract back.** Independence-correlated matroids are closed under minors by Semple--Welsh Proposition 3.5. Hence the graphic matroid of the terminal core is independence correlated, and in particular its unit uniform-forest law is p-NC.

**Step 7: lift through simplification and blocks.** Huang's equivalence lifts p-NC back to the original block, and block factorization lifts it to `G`.

QED.
