\[

\[
\max_i |\langle a_i,x\rangle|
=n-2\min_{c\in C_A}d_H(x,c),
\]

hence

\[
\operatorname{disc}(A)=n-2R(C_A).
\]

That identity is inherited history, not a new claim of this run.

The new way to organize the **asymptotic boundary** is to define

\[
\rho_n:=\min\{R(C): C=-C,\ |C|\le 2n,\ C\subset\{\pm1\}^n\},

\[
Z_n:=\frac{n/2-\rho_n}{\sqrt n}.
\]

Then the source quantity becomes exactly

\[
C^*=2\sup_n Z_n.
\]

Thus the relevant “point at infinity” is not fixed covering radius and not positive-rate coding. It is the **zero-rate central-shell regime**
