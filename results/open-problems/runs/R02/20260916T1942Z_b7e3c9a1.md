### `R02-AUDIT-R03-C253-04 = PASS` — claim scoped

For `A in G_m`, let `rho=rank_{F_p}(A)` and `d=m-rho`. The row space contains zero and all `m` distinct nonzero binary rows, hence at least `m+1` cube vectors. Projecting a `rho`-dimensional row space to pivot coordinates is injective, and every binary vector projects into `{0,1}^rho`, so

\[
m+1\le 2^\rho,
\qquad
\rho\ge\lceil\log_2(m+1)\rceil,
\]

which gives the stated

\[
d\le d_{\max}(m)=m-\lceil\log_2(m+1)\rceil.
\]

For `d>=2`, with Gaussian binomial coefficients

\[
Z_1(d)={d\brack1}_p=\frac{p^d-1}{p-1},
\]

\[
Z_2(d)={d\brack2}_p,
\]

direct algebra gives

\[
\frac{Z_1(d)-1}{Z_2(d)}
=\frac{p(p^2-1)}{p^d-1},
\]

which decreases in `d`. Choosing

\[
c_{p,m}=\frac{p(p^2-1)}{p^{d_{\max}(m)}-1}
\]

therefore yields

\[
\mathbf1_{\{d\ge1\}}\le Z_1(d)-c_{p,m}Z_2(d)
\]

for the whole permitted range, with equality at `d=0,1`. Summing over matrices gives C253. The argument is correct.
