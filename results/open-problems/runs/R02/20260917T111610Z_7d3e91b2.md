## 5. Method #5 — add union depth as a parameter; exact multi-letter route lemma

I rewrote the entropy obstruction at `r` whole-set unions rather than at one OR of two copies. This produces a clean exact inequality and, more importantly, identifies where a stronger proof would have to enter.

Let `X` be uniform on a finite union-closed family `F` with `m=|F|`; write

\[
p_i=\Pr(i\in X).
\]

Take `r>=2` independent copies `X^(1),...,X^(r)` and set

\[
Z_r=X^{(1)}\cup\cdots\cup X^{(r)},
\qquad
q_i(r)=\Pr(i\in Z_r)=1-(1-p_i)^r.
\]

Let `h_2` be binary entropy and define total correlation

\[
TC(W)=\sum_i H(W_i)-H(W)
      =D\!\left(P_W\middle\|\prod_i P_{W_i}\right).
\]

### Route lemma (elementary; no novelty claim)

For every finite union-closed `F` and every integer `r>=2`,

\[
\boxed{
(r-1)\log_2 m
\le
\sum_i\left[r h_2(p_i)-h_2\!\left(1-(1-p_i)^r\right)\right].
}
\tag{R02-UC-r}
\]

### Full proof

For each coordinate `i`, group the `r` input bits into

\[
V_i=(X_i^{(1)},\ldots,X_i^{(r)}).
\]

The joint vector `(V_i)_i` is just a reordering of `r` independent copies of `X`. Hence

\[
TC(V)=r\sum_i h_2(p_i)-rH(X)
     =r\left(\sum_i h_2(p_i)-\log_2m\right).
\]

Coordinatewise OR is a product of local deterministic channels `V_i -> Z_{r,i}`. Relative-entropy data processing therefore gives

\[
TC(Z_r)\le TC(V).
\]

On the other hand, union closure implies `Z_r in F`, so its entropy is at most the entropy of the uniform distribution on `F`:

\[
H(Z_r)\le\log_2m.
\]

Since `Z_{r,i}` has Bernoulli parameter `q_i(r)`, this yields

\[
TC(Z_r)
=
\sum_i h_2(q_i(r))-H(Z_r)
\ge
\sum_i h_2(q_i(r))-\log_2m.
\]

Combining the last two displays and rearranging proves `(R02-UC-r)`.

This is an elementary data-processing consequence and may well be folklore; I make **no novelty claim** for it.

### What the parameter experiment says

The lawful parameter is the number of **whole sets** being unioned. A tempting continuous interpolation that independently turns coordinates of the second set on and off does not stay inside `F`; union closure protects whole-set union, not coordinatewise thinning. That is the first precise obstruction to simply differentiating a continuous OR strength.

At the one-coordinate level, the normalized bound

\[
\psi_r(p)=\frac{r h_2(p)-h_2(1-(1-p)^r)}{r-1}
\]

did not beat `r=2` in a numerical scan for `2<=r<=10` and `0<p<=0.3828852549667978`. This scan is **heuristic only**, not a registered theorem. It reinforces the failure diagnosis: adding more iid whole-set unions without exploiting extra structure does not obviously escape the two-copy/single-letter barrier.

The genuinely new target suggested by `(R02-UC-r)` is not a larger `r`; it is a **strict, union-structure-sensitive contraction estimate for total correlation** (or an analogous multi-information functional) that is unavailable for arbitrary product channels. Any such estimate must use the fact that `X` is uniform on a union-closed support and, under a hypothetical counterexample, every marginal is below `1/2`.
