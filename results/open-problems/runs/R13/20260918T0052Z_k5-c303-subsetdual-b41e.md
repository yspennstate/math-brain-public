### #11 — DUALIZE

For an oriented witness block `B`, a scaled active row `r`, and every nonempty `U\subseteq B`, the individual inequalities imply

\[
r\cdot t>1\quad(t\in B)
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
r\cdot u_U>|U|,
\quad u_U:=\sum_{t\in U}t.
\]

For any positive diagonal `\Lambda=diag(\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_5)` with `\sum_j\lambda_j=1`, weighted Cauchy therefore gives

\[
r^T\Lambda r>
\frac{|U|^2}{u_U^T\Lambda^{-1}u_U}.

Define the subset-dual block functional

\[
\psi_\Lambda(B)
:=
\max_{\varnothing\ne U\subseteq B}
\frac{|U|^2}{u_U^T\Lambda^{-1}u_U}.
\tag{2.1}

\[
r^T\Lambda r>\psi_\Lambda(B).
\tag{2.2}
\]

## 3. Main theorem

### R13-C41

### Proof

Set

\[
T=\sqrt{1000/303},\qquad c=T^{-2}=303/1000.
\]

Assume for contradiction that an admissible five-column matrix `A` has `disc(A)>T`. Scale every row by `T`, writing `r_\alpha=a_\alpha/T`. For each coordinate `j`, the unit-column condition gives

\[
E_j:=\sum_\alpha r_{\alpha j}^2\le c,
\qquad
\sum_{j=1}^5E_j\le5c=\frac{303}{200}.
\tag{3.1}
\]

Let

\[
S_5=\{t\in\{\pm1\}^5:t_1=1\},
\]

the 16 projective sign classes. For every class choose one row witnessing badness; choose the representative orientation so its chosen row has `r_i\cdot t>1`. Assign the class to that row and discard unused rows. This produces `q` active rows and a partition

\[
S_5=B_1\sqcup\cdots\sqcup B_q.
\]

Write `k_i=|B_i|`. We exclude every possible `q`.

### Case 1: `q<=3`

Apply the retained partial-rounding lemma to the active `q`-row submatrix. Its five column vectors lie in the Euclidean unit ball of `R^q`. Choose an extreme point of

\[
\{x\in[-1,1]^5:\sum_jx_jw_j=0\}.
\]

At most `q` coordinates are fractional. Independently rounding only those coordinates with their prescribed expectations makes the cross terms vanish and yields expected squared Euclidean error at most `q`. Hence some Boolean signing satisfies

\[
\|A_{\rm active}\varepsilon\|_\infty
\le\|A_{\rm active}\varepsilon\|_2
\le\sqrt q\le\sqrt3<T,
\]

because `3<1000/303`. This contradicts the witness assignment.

### Case 2: `q>=6`

For `k` distinct projective five-cube classes let

\[
M_k=\max\left\|\sum_{s=1}^k t_s\right\|_2^2,
\]

allowing independent orientations. The retained exact table is

\[
(M_1,\ldots,M_{16})
=(5,16,29,48,61,80,101,128,133,144,157,176,189,208,229,256).
\tag{3.2}
\]

For row `i`, summing its `k_i` witness inequalities and applying ordinary Cauchy gives

\[
\|r_i\|_2^2>\frac{k_i^2}{M_{k_i}}.
\]

The exact partition scan gives

\[
\min_{q\ge6,\ \sum_i k_i=16}
\sum_i\frac{k_i^2}{M_{k_i}}
=\frac{49}{30},
\tag{3.3}
\]

attained at `(1,1,1,1,4,8)`. But

\[
\frac{49}{30}-5c
=\frac{49}{30}-\frac{303}{200}
=\frac{71}{600}>0,
\]

contradicting (3.1).

### Case 3: `q=5`

Exact total-energy pruning leaves precisely six sorted block-size profiles:

\[
(1,1,1,5,8),\ (1,1,2,4,8),\ (1,2,2,3,8),\
(1,3,4,4,4),\ (2,2,2,2,8),\ (2,2,4,4,4).
\tag{3.4}
\]

For each block orient its members as above and put `v_i=\sum_{t\in B_i}t`. For a positive diagonal `\Lambda` with trace one, weighted Cauchy gives

\[
r_i^T\Lambda r_i>
\frac{k_i^2}{v_i^T\Lambda^{-1}v_i}.
\tag{3.5}
\]

The exact checker enumerates **50,820** genuine oriented five-row configurations after safe norm pruning. For every one, a positive rational diagonal weight with denominator 10 already certifies

\[
\sum_{i=1}^5
\frac{k_i^2}{v_i^T\Lambda^{-1}v_i}
\ge c.
\tag{3.6}
\]

Summing (3.5) contradicts

\[
\sum_i r_i^T\Lambda r_i
=\sum_j\lambda_jE_j\le c.
\]

Thus `q=5` is impossible.
