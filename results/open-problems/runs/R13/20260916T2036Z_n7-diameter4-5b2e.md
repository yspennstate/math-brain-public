## 5. Method #3 — stronger diameter-four convex-mean theorem

The central new lemma does not mention matrices.

### R13-C13 — diameter-four downset convex mean

**PROVED, with a finite exact core.**

Let `F` be a nonempty downset in `2^[7]` with Hamming diameter at most four, and put `b=|F|`. Then there exists a probability distribution on `F` such that, if `T` is sampled from it and

\[
s_T\in\{\pm1\}^7,\qquad
(s_T)_j=
\begin{cases}
-1,&j\in T,\\
+1,&j\notin T,
\end{cases}
\]

then

\[
c:=\mathbb E s_T
\quad\text{satisfies}\quad
\boxed{\|c\|_2^2\le \frac{48}{b}.}
\tag{5.1}
\]

#### 5.1 Reduction to maximal members

Let `M` be the antichain of maximal members of `F`, `h=|M|`, and

\[
d_j:=|\{M\in\mathcal M:j\in M\}|.
\]

Choose a maximal member uniformly. Conditional on the chosen maximal member containing coordinate `j`, retain `j` independently with probability

\[
\theta_j=
\min\left(1,\frac{h}{2d_j}\right)
\]

when `d_j>0`; if `d_j=0`, the coordinate is never present. The resulting random set is a subset of the chosen maximal member, hence belongs to `F` because `F` is a downset.

Its inclusion marginal is

\[
x_j=\min\left(\frac{d_j}{h},\frac12\right),
\]

and therefore

\[
c_j=1-2x_j
=\frac{(h-2d_j)_+}{h}.
\]

Thus C13 reduces exactly to the finite inequality

\[
\boxed{
b\sum_{j=1}^7(h-2d_j)_+^2\le48h^2.
}
\tag{5.2}
\]

#### 5.2 Exact classification of the maximal antichain

A useful characterization removes the continuous geometry completely.

**Claim.** For maximal members `A,B` of a diameter-four downset,

\[
|A\cup B|\le4.
\tag{5.3}
\]

Indeed, if `|A union B|>=5`, partition the common part `A intersect B` between two disjoint subsets while putting `A\B` in the first and `B\A` in the second. This produces `X subset A`, `Y subset B` with

\[
|X\triangle Y|=|A\cup B|\ge5,
\]

and downset closure gives `X,Y in F`, contradiction.

Conversely, if every pair of maximal members has union size at most four, then any two members `X subset A`, `Y subset B` satisfy

\[
X\triangle Y\subset A\cup B,
\]

so the whole downset has diameter at most four.

Also every maximal member has size at most four because `emptyset in F`.

This leaves exactly the following ranks.

- **Rank 0:** `F={emptyset}`.
- **Rank 1:** any collection of singleton maxima.
- **Rank 2:** maximal 2-sets are the edges of an arbitrary simple graph on seven vertices; a singleton can additionally be maximal iff its vertex is isolated.
- **Rank 4:** if a maximal 4-set `Q` exists, every other maximal member has union at most four with `Q`, hence is contained in `Q`, contradicting maximality. Therefore `M={Q}` and `F=2^Q`.
- **Rank 3:** the triple maxima are pairwise 2-intersecting. Such a family is either a **star** (all triples contain one fixed pair) or a **top** (all triples lie inside one fixed 4-set).

For the last assertion, take two distinct triples `A,B`; they share a pair `P` and have union `Q` of size four. Any further triple meeting both in at least two points either contains `P` or lies inside `Q`. If one triple fails to contain `P`, then a triple containing `P` and a point outside `Q` would meet it in only one point, impossible. Hence the entire family is of star or top type.

Once the triple maxima are fixed, a pair is eligible to be an additional maximal member iff it is not contained in a triple maximum and its union with every triple maximum has size at most four. Any subset of eligible pairs may occur, because two 2-sets automatically have union size at most four. The optional singleton maxima are exactly an arbitrary subset of coordinates uncovered by the chosen triples/pairs.

Therefore (5.2) has a **finite exact exhaustive core**.

#### 5.3 Exact finite verification of (5.2)

The checker uses only integer inequalities.

- Rank 0: direct.
- Rank 1: seven orbit cases.
- Rank 2: every graph on seven labeled vertices plus every subset of its isolated vertices as singleton maxima — `2,350,601` nonempty exact configurations.
- Rank 3: all `756` distinct star/top triple cliques, every compatible pair-maxima subset, and every optional uncovered singleton-maxima subset — `291,991` exact configurations.
- Rank 4: the unique type `F=2^Q`.

For rank 2 the worst checked ratio `(left side)/(48h^2)` is

\[
\frac{4655}{5808}<1.
\]

For rank 3 it is

\[
\frac{15}{16}<1.
\]

Rank 4 attains equality:

\[
b=16,\quad h=1,\quad
\sum_j(h-2d_j)_+^2=3,
\quad 16\cdot3=48.
\]

Thus (5.2), and therefore C13, hold for every diameter-four downset.

This is the finite core checked by `artifacts/R13/20260916T2036Z_n7-diameter4-5b2e_checker.py` and its archived output.

## 6. Method #8 — discretize an arbitrary row into a diameter-four downset

Now return to an arbitrary real `m x 7` matrix `A` with column Euclidean norms at most one.

Fix one row `r`. Absorb its coordinate signs into the Boolean coloring and write

\[
a_j=|r_j|.
\]

Because each column has Euclidean norm at most one,

\[
0\le a_j\le1.
\tag{6.1}
\]

For `T subset [7]`, define `s_T` as above and the positive bad family

\[
\mathcal F(r)=\{T\subset[7]:a\cdot s_T>t_7\}.
\tag{6.2}
\]

Every bad antipodal Boolean sign class has exactly one positive orientation in `F(r)`, hence

\[
b(r)=|\mathcal F(r)|.
\tag{6.3}
\]

The family is a downset.

If `T,U in F(r)`, then

\[
a\cdot(s_T+s_U)>2t_7=\sqrt{21}.
\]

On coordinates in `T triangle U` the signs cancel; on all remaining coordinates the contribution is at most `2a_j<=2`. Consequently

\[
a\cdot(s_T+s_U)\le2(7-|T\triangle U|).
\]

If `|T triangle U|>=5`, the right side is at most four, while `sqrt(21)>4`, impossible. Therefore

\[
\boxed{\operatorname{diam}_H\mathcal F(r)\le4.}
\tag{6.4}
\]

Apply C13 to `F(r)` when it is nonempty. Its convex mean `c` obeys

\[
\|c\|_2^2\le\frac{48}{b(r)}.
\]

Every point in the support has `a dot s_T>t_7`, so

\[
a\cdot c>t_7.
\]

By Cauchy,

\[
t_7^2<\|a\|_2^2\|c\|_2^2\le\|r\|_2^2\frac{48}{b(r)}.
\]

Since `t_7^2=21/4`,

\[
\boxed{b(r)<\frac{64}{7}\|r\|_2^2.}
\tag{6.5}
\]

This is exactly the boundary coefficient identified by X04.

Sum (6.5) over all rows with at least one bad class:

\[
\sum_i b(r_i)<\frac{64}{7}\sum_i\|r_i\|_2^2
=\frac{64}{7}\sum_{j=1}^7\|A_{\cdot j}\|_2^2\le64.
\tag{6.6}
\]

The left side is an integer, so it is at most `63`. But there are exactly

\[
2^{7-1}=64
\]

antipodal Boolean sign classes. If every class were bad in at least one row, the incidence count would be at least `64`, contradicting (6.6). Therefore some antipodal class is bad in no row. Any representative `epsilon` of that class satisfies

\[
\boxed{\|A\varepsilon\|_\infty\le\frac{\sqrt{21}}2.}
\]

This proves R13-C12 for **every row dimension `m`**.
