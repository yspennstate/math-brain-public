## Parametric threshold lemma

Let `A=1000`, fix `epsilon in (0,1)`, integer `M>=1`, and `lambda>1`, and write

\[
\theta=M\varepsilon,\quad q=22^{-\theta},\quad
K_1=\frac{1+C_0/A}{1-\varepsilon},\quad C_0=D_0=\sec(\pi/8).
\]

Assume

\[
\frac{2^\varepsilon}{\lambda}\le q^2,
\tag{4}
\]

\[

\tag{5}
\]

and

\[
\frac{2^{15}\lambda^2c_GK_1^2(5/2)^{2(1-\varepsilon)}}{M}\le q^5.
\tag{6}

\[
D_T\le A T^{-\varepsilon}\qquad(T\ge1).
\tag{7}
\]

### Proof

Set `E_s=22^(Ms)`. Prove simultaneously

\[
D_k\le Ak^{-\varepsilon}\quad(1\le k\le E_s),
\qquad
D_{E_s}\le qA E_s^{-\varepsilon}.
\tag{8}
\]

The base `s=0` is `D_1<=qA`. Assume (8) through `E_{s-1}` and put `T_0=E_{s-1}`, `K=E_s=22^M T_0`. For every `T_0<=k<=K`, monotonicity gives

\[
D_k\le D_{T_0}\le qAT_0^{-\varepsilon}=AK^{-\varepsilon}\le Ak^{-\varepsilon},
\tag{9}
\]

so the ordinary inductive bound is already valid throughout the block.

From (1) and the previous endpoint bound,

\[
\sum_{k\ge T_0}G_k\le c_GD_{2T_0-1}\le c_GqAT_0^{-\varepsilon}.
\tag{10}
\]

For `T_j=22^jT_0`, `0<=j<M`, the bands `[T_j,21T_j]` are disjoint. Hence some selected `T=T_j` obeys

\[
\sum_{k=T}^{21T}G_k\le\frac{c_GqA}{M}T_0^{-\varepsilon}.
\tag{11}
\]

Also, for every integer `R>=1`,

\[
\sum_{i=0}^{R}D_i
\le C_0+A\sum_{i=1}^{R}i^{-\varepsilon}
\le AK_1R^{1-\varepsilon}.
\tag{12}
\]

If source case (3a) holds, first suppose `D_{4T}>0` and put

\[
R_a=\frac{D_{\lceil(T+1)/2\rceil}}{D_{4T}}.
\]

If `R_a>lambda`, then `4T<=K` and

\[
D_K\le D_{4T}<\lambda^{-1}D_{\lceil(T+1)/2\rceil}
\le \lambda^{-1}A2^\varepsilon T_0^{-\varepsilon}
\le q^2AT_0^{-\varepsilon}=qAK^{-\varepsilon}

by (4). If `D_{4T}=0`, the endpoint is trivial. If `R_a<=lambda`, then (3a), (11), (12), and `T>=T_0` imply

\[
\begin{aligned}
D_K^2&\le D_{10T}^2\\
&\le\frac{256\sqrt2\lambda}{T}(AK_1T^{1-\varepsilon})
\left(\frac{c_GqA}{M}T_0^{-\varepsilon}\right)\\

\end{aligned}
\]

where (5) was used.

by (6). Thus the strong endpoint estimate closes in every branch, and induction proves (7). `square`
