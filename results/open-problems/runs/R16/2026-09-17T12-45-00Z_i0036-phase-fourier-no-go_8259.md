## 3. Setup: the 48 winding phases

## 4. Method #3 — prove something stronger: all nontrivial modes still miss the positive mass

The preceding R16 obstruction used only the single parafermionic sector. The stronger statement is that **even exact knowledge of all 47 nontrivial Fourier modes is insufficient, by itself, to upper-bound the positive mass**.

### Theorem R16-I0036-C06(a) — nontrivial-Fourier data cannot bound total mass

For every `Z>=0` and every `c>=0`, let

\[
Z^{(c)}=Z+c\mathbf 1,
\qquad \mathbf1=(1,\dots,1).
\]

Then for every `k != 0`,

\[
\widehat{Z^{(c)}}(k)=\widehat Z(k),
\]

whereas

\[
\widehat{Z^{(c)}}(0)=\widehat Z(0)+48c.
\]

Consequently there is no finite-valued functional `Phi` of the 47 nontrivial Fourier coefficients alone such that

\[
\sum_{r=0}^{47}Z_r
\le
\Phi\bigl((\widehat Z(k))_{k=1}^{47}\bigr)
\tag{4.1}
\]

for every nonnegative phase profile `Z`.

### Proof

For `k != 0`, the geometric sum is

\[
\sum_{r=0}^{47}\omega^{kr}=0.
\]

Hence adding `c` to every phase coordinate changes no nontrivial Fourier coefficient. It changes the trivial coefficient by exactly `48c`. If (4.1) held, fix one `Z` and let `c -> infinity`; the right side stays fixed and the left side diverges. `QED`

This is deliberately stronger than the previous one-sector cancellation: no finite collection of additional nonzero parafermionic characters can solve the information deficit, even if one takes all of them.

## 5. Method #23 — assume the opposite: a phase-only Perron certificate exists

The natural positive-transfer idea was to hope that the nontrivial winding sectors determine enough of a strip transfer operator to control its Perron root and hence the positive critical partition function.

There is a matrix-level no-go example.

Let `J` be the `48 x 48` all-ones matrix and, for `a>0`, let

\[
M_a=aJ.
\]

`M_a` is nonnegative and commutes with the cyclic phase shift. On the trivial character,

\[
M_a\mathbf1=48a\,\mathbf1,
\]

so

\[
\rho(M_a)=48a.
\]

On every nontrivial Fourier character `v_k=(1,omega^k,...,omega^{47k})`,

\[
Jv_k=0.
\]

Therefore **all 47 nontrivial Fourier blocks of `M_a` are identically zero for every `a`**, while the positive Perron eigenvalue ranges over `(0,infinity)`.

### Theorem R16-I0036-C06(b) — phase-only Perron no-go

Across nonnegative cyclic 48-phase transfer matrices, no bound on the Perron root can be a function only of the nontrivial Fourier blocks. In particular, adding more nonzero winding characters to the parafermionic sector cannot, without an additional `k=0` normalization or a structural relation coupling `k=0` to `k!=0`, produce a universal positive Collatz--Wielandt certificate.

The conclusion is route-scoped. It does **not** say that the actual SAW transfer matrix contains an arbitrary `aJ` component, nor that geometry-aware identities cannot couple the trivial and nontrivial sectors. It says that phase/Fourier algebra alone cannot perform that coupling.
