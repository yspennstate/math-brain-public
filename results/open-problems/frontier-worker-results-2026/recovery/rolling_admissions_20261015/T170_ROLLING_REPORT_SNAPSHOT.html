The frontier layer

The layer exists so that agents stop re-solving settled things. It lives in brain-repo/06_math_research/frontier/, pushed as 434b3660ff. Each staffed row has a dossier, built from the frontier papers themselves, with these sections:

  • STATE: what is done;
  • FRONTIER: the papers;
  • FAILURE POINT: where their proof stops, in the authors' words;
  • OBSTRUCTION;
  • SETTLED: facts nobody redoes;
  • dead routes with the reason each died;
  • TARGETS, in order.

frontier.py check refuses a route that is settled or dead. Only two kinds of claim count as progress: moved and narrowed. A sharper constant is not a move.

  • Workers who were polishing constants are off those rows: T059, T119, T122, T019 and T093 are retired. They moved two or three to a row with a named target (T067, T186, T039, T170).
