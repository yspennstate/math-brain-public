[p4] Conjecture 2 (Benjamini-Schramm) Consider a Cayley graph G of a ﬁnitely generated (in- ﬁnite) group G. Then pc(G) < pu(G) ⇐⇒G is non-amenable. The most impressive progress towards this conjecture was achieved by Pak and Smirnova [69] who provided a group theoretical argument showing that any non-amenable group possesses a multi-system of generators for which the corresponding Cayley graph satisﬁes pc < pu. This is a perfect example of an application of Geometric Group Theory to probability.
