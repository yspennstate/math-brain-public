   \le CKL\sqrt{n\log\!\left(e+\frac{b^2|Q|}{n}\right)} .
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Let $E_Q:\mathcal F\to\bigoplus_{q\in Q}M_b(\C)$ be the evaluation map.  Since
$Q$ is $L$-norming, $E_Q$ is injective on $\mathcal F$.  Hence the map
\[
   \widetilde\Pi(E_QF)=\Pi(F),
   \qquad F\in\spanop\{F_1,\ldots,F_n\},
\]
is well-defined and satisfies
\[
   \norm{\widetilde\Pi(E_QF)}_{op}
   \le \norm{F}_{L^\infty}
   \le L\norm{E_QF}_{\oplus_{q\in Q}M_b} .
\]
Set $Y_i=E_QF_i$.  Then $\norm{Y_i}\le K$.  Apply
\cref{thm:block-spencer} to $Y_i/K$ and reconstruct with $\widetilde\Pi$.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Fixed-level block Toeplitz and Hankel]\label{thm:block-toeplitz}
Fix integers $s\ge1$ and $b\ge1$.  There is a constant $C_{s,b}$ such that every
family of $s$-level $b\times b$ block Toeplitz contractions
$A_1,\ldots,A_n\in M_D(\C)$ satisfies
\[
   \discop(A_1,\ldots,A_n)
   \le C_{s,b}\sqrt{n\log\!\left(e+\frac Dn\right)} .
\]
The same estimate holds for the corresponding fixed-level block Hankel class.
Thus, for fixed $s,b$ and $D\le n$, these classes satisfy the Matrix Spencer
bound $O_{s,b}(\sqrt n)$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Carlsson--Perfekt's multivariable Nehari theorem gives, for fixed level $s$, a
bounded extension of finite multilevel Toeplitz data to an essentially bounded
matrix-valued multiplier, with constants depending only on $s$; see
\cite{CarlssonPerfekt2021}.  For fixed block size $b$, this gives an
$M_b(\C)$-valued multiplier with loss depending only on $s$ and $b$.  Convolving
with a product de la Vall\'ee Poussin kernel preserves the finite Toeplitz
coefficients and gives coordinate degrees $O(d_j)$.  A product Bernstein grid
with $O_s(\prod_j d_j)=O_{s,b}(D)$ points is norming.  Applying
\cref{thm:sampled} gives the displayed estimate.  The Hankel case follows by
multiplication with the tensor-product reversal matrix.
\end{proof}
