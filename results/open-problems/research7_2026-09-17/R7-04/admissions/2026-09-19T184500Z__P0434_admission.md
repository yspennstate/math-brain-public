## Source hypotheses and quantifiers

The target concerns a stable M/G/1 queue: Poisson arrivals of rate `lambda`, i.i.d. service requirements `X`, load `rho=lambda E[X] < 1`, individual job sizes unknown to the scheduler but the service distribution and attained service (age) known. Gittins uses the source rank

`r_G(a)=inf_{b>a} [integral_a^b Fbar(t) dt]/[Fbar(a)-Fbar(b)]`,

while SERPT uses `r_S(a)=E[X-a | X>a]` and M-SERPT uses the nondecreasing envelope `r_MS(a)=max_{0<=u<=a} r_S(u)`. Lower rank has priority, with the source SOAP tie convention. The 2020 paper states its regularity assumptions for the displayed continuous proof and says the results extend to discrete distributions; the source lower-bound example itself is discrete.

Exact closure criterion adopted by R7-04: prove

`sup_{rho<1, X admissible} E[T_MS]/E[T_G] = 2`,

or rigorously refute it by an admissible stable distribution/load with ratio strictly greater than 2. Restricted distribution classes, asymptotic heavy-traffic statements, simulations, or improvements that leave a gap are PARTIAL only.
