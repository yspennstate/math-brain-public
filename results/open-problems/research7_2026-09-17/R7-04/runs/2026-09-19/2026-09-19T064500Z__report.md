## 6. B10 — derandomize the stationary law into finite tableau coefficients

The Fredholm formula is compact, but the source asks for a fairly explicit stationary distribution. This run derives a second, positive description whose coefficients are finite integer counts.

Let

\[
1=b_1<b_2<\cdots<b_k=n
\]

and, for `lambda |- n` with `lambda_1=k`, let

\[
A_\lambda(b)
=
\#\{Q\in\operatorname{SYT}(\lambda):Q(1,j)=b_j,1\le j\le k\}.
\tag{6.1}
\]

A discrete Plancherel-growth path ending at `lambda |- n` is a standard Young tableau. Its probability is exactly

\[
\prod_{m=1}^n \frac{f^{\lambda^{(m)}}}{m f^{\lambda^{(m-1)}}}
=
\frac{f^\lambda}{n!}.
\tag{6.2}
\]

At the first time the first row reaches length `k`, the endpoint has first-row length exactly `k`, and the labels in that first row are exactly the successive first-row hitting labels. Through the already proved RSK bridge those are the stabilized patience-pile labels `B(j)`. Therefore

\[
\boxed{
q_k(b_2,\ldots,b_k)
:=\Pr\{B(j)=b_j,2\le j\le k\}
=
\frac1{n!}
\sum_{\substack{\lambda\vdash n\\\lambda_1=k}}
 f^\lambda A_\lambda(b).
}
\tag{6.3}
\]

This is a finite exact formula for every coefficient.

Conditional on `B`, the source representation consists of independent rate-one Poisson arrival spacings. With `b_0=x_0=0`, the all-`k` stationary density therefore has the positive expansion

\[
\boxed{

=e^{-x_k}
\sum_{1=b_1<\cdots<b_k}
q_k(b_2,\ldots,b_k)
\prod_{j=1}^k
\frac{(x_j-x_{j-1})^{b_j-b_{j-1}-1}}
{(b_j-b_{j-1}-1)!}.
}
\tag{6.4}
\]

Every coefficient in this series is now reduced to finite standard-tableau enumeration by (6.3).
