## B5/B13: deform to `Unif{1,2,c}` and find the exact bifurcations

### First nontrivial cell: `9/2<c<=6`

\[
r_0=5/2,\qquad c-2>m,\qquad c/2\le m.

\[
\boxed{c-9/2}

\[
\beta=1-\lambda\mathbb E\min\{X,2\}=1-5\lambda/3,

T_G(1)=1+\frac{61\lambda}{24\beta},

T_G(2)=2+\frac{61\lambda}{24\beta}.

\[
T_{MS}(1)=1+\frac{3\lambda}{2\beta},

\[

\[
\boxed{
