## 3. Section-I(a): the exact blocking statement

The preceding run removed arbitrary rank-function geometry from the first unresolved finite class. After scaling the smallest support point to one, write a generic three-point law as

\[
\Pr\{X=1\}=p,\qquad

with

\[
p,q,r,u,v>0,\qquad 0<\rho<1,
\qquad \lambda=\rho/\mathbb E X.
\]

The previous B3 reduction proves that the Gittins lower envelope has at most eight combinatorial rank types. Let `T_3` denote those feasible types, and let `C_tau` be the exact parameter cell defined by the corresponding switch-order and phase-nonemptiness inequalities. For each type, the published SOAP response-time theorem turns the already-known piecewise ranks into exact functions `E[T_G^tau(theta)]` and `E[T_MS(theta)]`, where `theta=(p,q,u,v,rho)`.

The current finite-support blocker is exactly

\[
\boxed{
\min_{\tau\in\mathcal T_3}

F_\tau(\theta)\ \ge 0,
\qquad
F_\tau(\theta):=
2\,\mathbb E[T_G^\tau(\theta)]

\tag{3.1}

A feasible point with `F_tau(theta)<0` would be a genuine source-valid ratio `E[T_MS]/E[T_G]>2` and therefore refute Conjecture 8.1 and move the frozen lower endpoint. ## 5. Section-I(c): the ingredient no route so far contains

A successful proof route now needs a **cell-uniform SOAP charging/positivity lemma**.

Concretely, it must compare, at finite load and without asymptotic disposal of terms,

1. the squared old-work/recycle contribution of Gittins;
2. the new-work denominators produced by each worst-future-rank phase;
3. the tagged-job residence integral; and
4. the corresponding monotone-envelope penalties of M-SERPT,

in a way that remains valid when a parameter crosses a Gittins rank-type boundary.

What all previous routes lack is a sign mechanism for the **interaction of multiple rank valleys**. The two-point charge controls one valley. The source family has enough algebraic structure to combine its valleys. A generic three-point law has neither simplification.

A proof of (3.1) therefore needs one of the following equivalent kinds of ingredient, none currently established:

- a decomposition of `F_tau` into nonnegative local charges that glue across all feasible cells;
- a monotone deformation showing every three-point law is no worse than a canonical boundary family while respecting rank-type changes; or
- a uniform semialgebraic positivity principle derived from the queueing structure, not an unsupported finite grid.

The same conceptual issue would have to survive a passage from finite support to general distributions before the source conjecture could close.
