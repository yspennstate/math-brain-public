`e^{-2s}V_n <= Var_{n,s}(Y_n) <= e^{2s}V_n`.  (6.1)

The lower bound follows by applying the lower density bound to every `a` before taking the infimum. For the upper bound, evaluate the tilted variance at the zero-field mean `a=0` and use the upper density bound.

`psi_n''(u)=Var_{n,u}(Y_n)`, `psi_n(0)=psi_n'(0)=0`,

`psi_n(s)=integral_0^s (s-u)psi_n''(u)du`.

`a_-(s)V_n <= psi_n(s) <= a_+(s)V_n`,  (6.2)
