## B3 — prove something stronger: classify every three-point rank geometry

Instead of treating only the source's special distribution, I first solved the rank geometry for an arbitrary three-point service law

\[
\Pr\{X=a\}=p,\quad \Pr\{X=b\}=q,\quad \Pr\{X=c\}=r,
\qquad 0<a<b<c,\quad p+q+r=1.
\]

Write `d1=b-a`, `d2=c-b`, and `m=E X=a+(q+r)d1+r d2`. Because the tail of a discrete three-point law is constant between support points, the Gittins stopping optimization needs only the remaining support points. Therefore for `0<=u<a`,

\[
r_G(u)=\min\left\{
\frac{a-u}{p},
\frac{(a-u)+(q+r)(b-a)}{p+q},
m-u
\right\}.
\tag{B3.1}
\]

For `a<=u<b`,

\[
r_G(u)=\min\left\{
\frac{(q+r)(b-u)}q,
(b-u)+\frac{r(c-b)}{q+r}
\right\},
\tag{B3.2}
\]

and for `b<=u<c`,

\[
r_G(u)=c-u.
\tag{B3.3}
\]

On the first interval the three affine slopes are `-1/p`, `-1/(p+q)`, and `-1`, in that strict order. Hence as age increases the lower envelope can only move in the order `A3 -> A2 -> A1`, with phases skipped but never reversed; it ends in `A1`. There are at most four first-interval types. On the second interval the first line is steeper and tends to zero, so there are at most two types. Thus:

\[
\boxed{\text{Every three-point service law has at most eight Gittins combinatorial rank types.}}
\tag{B3.4}
\]

All switch locations are explicit. In terms of `x=a-u`,

\[
A_1=A_2\iff x=\frac{p(q+r)d_1}{q},
\]

\[
A_2=A_3\iff x=(p+q)d_2-(q+r)d_1,
\]

\[
A_1=A_3\iff x=\frac{p((q+r)d_1+r d_2)}{q+r},
\]

and on the second interval, with `y=b-u`,

\[
B_1=B_2\iff y=\frac{q d_2}{q+r}.
\]

SERPT is piecewise decreasing between atoms, so its increasing envelope is even simpler. M-SERPT is exactly the three-level feedback rank

\[
r_{MS}(u)=M_0\;(0\le u<a),\qquad
M_1\;(a\le u<b),\qquad
M_2\;(b\le u<c),
\]

with

\[
M_0=m,
\quad
M_1=\max\left\{m,d_1+\frac{rd_2}{q+r}\right\},
\quad
M_2=\max\{M_1,d_2\}.
\tag{B3.5}
\]

This is a stronger finite reduction than the preceding two-point result: continuous Gittins optimization is eliminated for the entire three-point class. A remaining all-three-point factor-two proof, if true, can be organized by finitely many rank types rather than arbitrary functions.

## B12 — find what is conserved: exact r-work accounting for the source family

I then specialized to the exact Section 7 source family
