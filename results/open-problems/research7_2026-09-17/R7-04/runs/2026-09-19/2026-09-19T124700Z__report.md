The bounded-service light-traffic expansion was derived and showed that changing the IID service distribution only produces a positive mixture of the same deterministic triangular covariance mechanism at second order.

## 6. What a successful proof or refutation must contain that the existing routes do not

The missing ingredient is a way to control **nonnormal cross-mode interference**, not another spectral bound.

Every existing route controls one of:

- individual eigenvalues/modes;
- reversible or near-reversible generators;
- three-state quotients;
- endpoint modulation scales;
- polyhedral feasibility of `Q`;
- finite boundary samples.

None controls how nonorthogonal right/left eigendirections enter

`Sym_pi(Q Psi(Q))`.

A proof of second-order monotonicity needs an order-compatible matrix inequality that forces

\[
\operatorname{Sym}_\pi(Q\Psi(Q))\preceq0

through the genuinely nonnormal interior of the doubly-monotone cone. A refutation instead needs one exact source-admissible generator for which the top centered eigenvalue is positive, followed by the already available light-traffic remainder conversion to a positive workload margin.

This is why more complex eigenvalues, more covariance rebounds, or more floating cone samples are not the missing ingredient.

## 8. Nearest solved analogue and what it has that P0290 lacks

The source-nearest literature theorem is Bäuerle--Rolski (1998), *A monotonicity result for the workload in Markov-modulated queues*, cited by Leskelä. It proves workload monotonicity when the **intensity chain itself** is doubly stochastically monotone. Leskelä's accepted manuscript explicitly records this as a principal positive case and then poses Problem 2 precisely because an arbitrary deterministic image `f(X)` need not inherit the required order structure.

The missing feature is therefore concrete: the solved theorem compares the intensity processes through stochastic/supermodular order. In P0290, `X` has the order property but an arbitrary deterministic `f` can destroy it. R7-04's exact four-state Boolean image already demonstrates that the source's sufficient supermodular-order route need not survive deterministic imaging.

At the second-order linear-algebra level, the nearest solved analogue is the reversible case. Self-adjointness supplies an orthogonal spectral decomposition, so the triangular derivative is a positive sum of scalar negative mode contributions. `Q_1` is nonnormal; the scalar mode signs remain negative, but orthogonality is gone, leaving cross-mode interference. That is exactly the structural ingredient absent from the solved analogue.
