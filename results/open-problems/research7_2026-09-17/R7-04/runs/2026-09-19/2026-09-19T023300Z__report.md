## 4. Method B4 — fully explicit two-coordinate Bessel form

\[

Using

\[
\frac{I_1(2\sqrt u)}{\sqrt u}
=\sum_{i\ge0}\frac{u^i}{i!(i+1)!},
\qquad
\frac{I_2(2\sqrt u)}{u}
=\sum_{i\ge0}\frac{u^i}{i!(i+2)!},
\]

we obtain the exact Bessel simplification

\[
\boxed{
a(u)=e^{-u}\left[
\frac{I_1(2\sqrt u)}{\sqrt u}

\[
