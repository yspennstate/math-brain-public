## 3. B11 — dualize: a correlation-aware certificate hypergraph

### 3.1 Certificates instead of pointwise uncertainty

Fix a realization `H` of the Harris clocks and coins in the finite backward dependency cone of a terminal finite support `S` at time `t`, and fix a deterministic initial configuration `eta` with `eta_x=0` at the anchor. The Harris realization defines a deterministic Boolean map from the relevant time-zero bits to the terminal vector on `S`.

Call a finite set `C` of time-zero sites an **eta-certificate** if every initial configuration `zeta` agreeing with `eta` on `C` produces exactly the same terminal vector on `S` as `eta` under the same `H`.

Let

```text
nu_x = pi(. | eta_x=0).
```

Outside `x`, the initial bits under `nu_x` are independent Bernoulli(`p`) healthy bits. For an eta-certificate `C`, define its matching weight

```text
w_eta(C)
 = product_{y in C \ {x}} pi( zeta_y = eta_y )
 = p^(# healthy sites of eta in C\{x})
   q^(# infected sites of eta in C\{x}).             (3.1)
```

### Proposition 3.1 — disjoint-certificate packing bound

If `C_1,...,C_m` are eta-certificates whose sets `C_j\{x}` are pairwise disjoint, then conditional on `H`,

```text
P_{zeta~nu_x}( terminal_H(zeta) != terminal_H(eta) )
   <= product_j (1-w_eta(C_j))
   <= exp( - sum_j w_eta(C_j) ).                     (3.2)
```

#### Proof

Let `E_j` be the event that `zeta` agrees with `eta` on every site of `C_j`. By the certificate definition, occurrence of any `E_j` forces terminal agreement. Therefore terminal disagreement implies `cap_j E_j^c`.

The anchor always agrees, and the remaining coordinates in the disjoint sets `C_j\{x}` are independent under `nu_x`. Hence the events `E_j` are independent and have probabilities `w_eta(C_j)`. This gives the product bound, and `1-u <= e^{-u}` gives the exponential bound. QED.

Define the **certificate packing mass**

```text
M_H(eta,S,t;x)
 := sup { sum_j w_eta(C_j) :
          C_j eta-certificates,
          C_j\{x} pairwise disjoint }.               (3.3)
```

Then (3.2) gives the exact positive reduction

```text
P_{nu_x}(terminal disagreement | H)
 <= exp[-M_H(eta,S,t;x)].                             (3.4)
```

This is a strict upgrade over the rectangular possible-value process from the preceding run. Logical correlations are retained inside each certificate, while independence is used only after certificates have been made disjoint at time zero.

## 4. B28 — replace worst-background control by the actual product-conditioned law

The preceding certificate bound already uses the actual `nu_x` law rather than a supremum over exterior backgrounds. A second, independent exploitation of the product law comes from a centered-product transform.

Set

```text
p = 1-q,
phi(a) = (a-p)/q,
```

so

```text
phi(1)=1,
phi(0)=-p/q,
E_pi[phi(eta_x)] = 0.
```

For a finite set `A` define

```text
Phi_A(eta) = product_{y in A} phi(eta_y),
Phi_empty = 1.                                       (4.1)
```

The exact stochastic duality generating these functions is proved in Section 5. Assuming that theorem for the moment gives several exact typical-law identities.

### Corollary 4.1 — product initial law

Let `nu_alpha` be the iid initial law with infection probability `alpha`, i.e. `P(eta_y=0)=alpha`. Then

```text
nu_alpha(P_t Phi_A)
 = E_A [ r_alpha ^ |A_t| ],
r_alpha = 1 - alpha/q.                               (4.2)
```

#### Proof

Independence gives

```text
E_{nu_alpha}[Phi_B]
 = ((1-alpha)*1 + alpha*(-p/q))^|B|
 = (1-alpha/q)^|B|.
```

Apply the duality theorem. QED.

Thus, whenever `|1-alpha/q|<1`, an exponential lower tail for linear growth of the dual particle number would immediately give exponential convergence of every centered cylinder polynomial. This does not solve the source, because neither the required all-parameter dual growth/mixing estimate nor arbitrary source-admissible initial laws are disposed of here.

### Corollary 4.2 — equilibrium conditioned on the shared zero

For `nu_x=pi(.|eta_x=0)` and any nonempty finite `A`,

```text
nu_x(P_t Phi_A)
 = -(p/q) P_A( A_t = {x} ).                          (4.3)
```

#### Proof

For every nonempty finite `B`, product centering gives `nu_x(Phi_B)=0` unless `B={x}`. For the singleton `{x}` the value is `-p/q`. The dual never reaches the empty set, so averaging over `A_t` gives (4.3). QED.

The already-known spectral-gap relaxation of `nu_x` therefore implies exponential decay of the dual's probability of being a prescribed singleton. This is a useful consistency check, but singleton probability is not the same as local occupation probability.

### Corollary 4.3 — exact dual form of the single-infection stress test

Let `eta^(x)` have exactly one infection, at `x`, and be healthy elsewhere. Then for every nonempty finite `A`,

```text
(delta_{eta^(x)} P_t Phi_A)
 = 1 - (1/q) P_A( x in A_t )
 = ( q - P_A(x in A_t) ) / q.                       (4.4)
```

Since `pi(Phi_A)=0`, exponential relaxation from the single-infection initial state for all local observables is equivalent, in this basis, to

```text
|P_A(x in A_t) - q| <= C_A exp(-c_A t)               (4.5)
```

for every nonempty finite dual start `A`.

#### Proof

For any nonempty finite `B`,

```text
Phi_B(eta^(x))
 = 1,       x notin B,
 = -p/q,    x in B,
```

which is exactly `1-(1/q) 1_{x in B}`. Average with `B=A_t` using the duality theorem. QED.

This is the cleanest new blocker obtained in the entire R7-04 sequence: the singular single-vacancy FA-1f relaxation problem is **exactly** local one-site ergodicity of a finite-set process with equilibrium occupation density `q`.

## 5. B12 — find what is conserved: exact all-q finite-set duality

### 5.1 The dual process

For a finite nonempty set `A subset Z`, define a process `(A_t)` as follows.

Each occupied site `x in A_t` rings at rate one. At its ring, the membership indicators of `x-1` and `x+1` are independently replaced by fresh Bernoulli(`q`) bits. All other membership indicators are unchanged, and the center `x` itself remains occupied.

Equivalently, if `R_y` means “replace the membership bit of `y` by an independent Bernoulli(`q`) bit”, then the generator is

```text
(Qg)(A)
 = sum_{x in A} ( E[g(R_{x-1} R_{x+1} A)] - g(A) ). (5.1)
```

I will call this the **neighbor-refresh dual** only as a descriptive internal name; no naming/novelty claim is intended.

### Theorem 5.1 — exact FA-1f / neighbor-refresh duality

For every deterministic FA-1f initial configuration `eta`, every finite `A subset Z`, and every `t>=0`,

```text
E_eta[ Phi_A(eta_t) ]
 = E_A[ Phi_{A_t}(eta) ].                            (5.2)
```

#### Proof

The FA-1f generator is

```text
(Lf)(eta)
 = sum_x c_x(eta) (pi_x f(eta)-f(eta)),
c_x(eta)=1-eta_{x-1} eta_{x+1}.                     (5.3)
```

If `x notin A`, resampling `eta_x` does not change `Phi_A`. If `x in A`, centering gives

```text
pi_x Phi_A = 0.
```

Hence

```text
L Phi_A
 = sum_{x in A}
   ( eta_{x-1} eta_{x+1} Phi_A - Phi_A ).            (5.4)
```

The key one-site algebra is stochastic, not signed. For any site `y`,

- if `y notin A`, then

```text
eta_y Phi_A
 = (p + q phi(eta_y)) Phi_A
 = p Phi_A + q Phi_{A union {y}}
 = E[Phi_{R_y A}];                                   (5.5)
```

- if `y in A`, use `eta_y phi(eta_y)=eta_y` to get

```text
eta_y Phi_A
 = (p+q phi(eta_y)) Phi_{A\{y}}
 = p Phi_{A\{y}} + q Phi_A
 = E[Phi_{R_y A}].                                   (5.6)
```

The independent refreshes at `x-1,x+1` therefore satisfy

```text
eta_{x-1} eta_{x+1} Phi_A
 = E[ Phi_{R_{x-1}R_{x+1}A} ].                      (5.7)
```

Substituting (5.7) into (5.4) gives the generator identity

```text
L_eta Phi_A(eta) = Q_A Phi_A(eta).                   (5.8)
```

Starting from finite `A`, the dual remains finite at finite time. In one dimension its occupied set lies inside its running left/right extremes; only an extreme particle can enlarge the running span by one, and each outward enlargement has hazard at most `q`. Thus the span, and hence `|A_t|`, has all finite exponential moments on every finite time interval. The duality function is therefore integrable, and the standard generator-duality argument (or localization followed by monotone stopping times) yields (5.2). QED.

### 5.2 Why the normalization is forced

The transform above is not an arbitrary scaling trick. Let

```text
phi_c(a)=(a-p)/c.
```

For `y notin A`, multiplication by `eta_y` has coefficients `p,c`; for `y in A`, it has coefficients `pq/c,q`. Both pairs are nonnegative probability weights summing to one exactly when

```text
p+c=1,
pq/c+q=1,
```

and both equations force

```text
c=q.                                                 (5.9)
```

Thus `(eta-p)/q` is the unique centered affine normalization that turns multiplication by a healthy-site indicator into an honest Bernoulli membership refresh in the dual.

### Proposition 5.2 — conserved nonemptiness and reversible product law

The dual process preserves nonemptiness. On a finite cycle, Bernoulli(`q`) product measure conditioned on being nonempty is reversible for `Q`.

#### Proof

At every transition the ringing center `x` remains occupied, so a nonempty set cannot become empty.

For reversibility, write the dual configuration as `xi in {0,1}^V`. The update at `x` is enabled by `xi_x=1` and replaces the two neighbor variables by their equilibrium Bernoulli(`q`) law. Conditional on `xi_x=1`, the nonempty conditioning is already satisfied and the neighbor block has exactly the product Bernoulli(`q`) conditional law. Hence each enabled block heat-bath is reversible, and summing over `x` preserves detailed balance. QED.

### Proposition 5.3 — exact Dirichlet-form comparison with BABP on a cycle

Let `D_Q` be the Dirichlet form of the neighbor-refresh dual on a finite cycle, under Bernoulli(`q`) conditioned nonempty. Let `D_B` be the Dirichlet form of BABP written in infection-set variables: each occupied center independently refreshes its left and right neighbors at separate rate-one clocks. Then for every `f`,

```text
D_Q(f) <= D_B(f) <= 2 D_Q(f).                        (5.10)
```

Consequently, under the same finite-volume conventions, the relaxation times and logarithmic-Sobolev constants are comparable within a factor two whenever those constants are defined on the common nonempty component.

#### Proof

Condition on the variables outside the two neighbors of a fixed occupied center `x`. The `Q` contribution is the two-variable product heat-bath variance

```text
Var_{x-1,x+1}(f),
```

whereas the BABP contribution is the sum of the two one-coordinate heat-bath variances. For two independent coordinates, Efron-Stein/tensorization and the elementary bound of each one-coordinate variance by the full two-coordinate variance give

```text
Var_pair(f)
 <= Var_left(f)+Var_right(f)
 <= 2 Var_pair(f),                                    (5.11)
```

after averaging over the pair. Multiplication by the common nonnegative factor `xi_x`, integration under the reversible law, and summation over `x` yield (5.10). QED.
