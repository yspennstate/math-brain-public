## 2. B19 — make the regeneration mechanism an explicit algorithm

The previous run asked for a finite Harris word which erases the local initial condition after a facilitator enters a target interval. Such a word exists in a stronger form: it can finish by producing an **exact equilibrium sample** on the target coordinates.

### Lemma 2.1 — exact finite-interval strong-stationary reset word

Let

```text
I = [a,b] intersect Z,       L = b-a+1 >= 2,
I^- = [a,b-1] intersect Z.
```

Use the standard rate-one independent Poisson clocks and independent Bernoulli refresh coins. Suppose that at the beginning of the construction the configuration contains at least one infection somewhere in `I`.

There is a graphical event `R_I`, depending only on Harris marks inside `I` during a time interval of length

```text
T_L = (3L-2)/L < 3,
```

with probability exactly

```text
r_L(q) = q^(2L-1) * (e L)^(-(3L-2)) > 0,
```

such that, conditional on `R_I`,

1. `eta_{T_L}(b)=0`;
2. the vector `eta_{T_L}|_{I^-}` has **exactly** the product equilibrium law `pi_{I^-}`;
3. that vector is independent of the starting configuration and of all graphical randomness outside `I`.

In particular, if two FA-1f processes with arbitrary exterior states each have at least one infection in `I` at the start and are run with the same Harris marks, then on `R_I` their restrictions to `I^-` coincide at time `T_L` and are distributed exactly as `pi_{I^-}`.

### Proof

Set `delta=1/L`. Split the construction into `3L-2` consecutive windows, each of length `delta`.

#### Phase A: synchronize the whole interval to all zeros

Use the deterministic site word

```text
a, a+1, ..., b, b-1, b-2, ..., a,
```

which has `2L-1` entries. In the window assigned to a site `x`, require:

- exactly one Poisson ring among all sites of `I`;
- that ring is at `x`;
- its refresh coin is `0`.

Let `j` be any initially infected site of `I`. During the first left-to-right sweep, the mark at `j` cannot destroy the infection: if legal it refreshes to `0`, while if illegal the state remains `0`. Inductively, when the scheduled mark reaches `j+1`, its left neighbor is `0`, so the update is legal and makes `j+1=0`; the same argument propagates through `b`. Hence after the first sweep every site in `[j,b]` is zero.

Now run the right-to-left part. Starting from the zero block `[j,b]`, when the schedule reaches `j-1`, its right neighbor `j` is zero, so it is legally refreshed to zero. Induction gives zeros at `j-2,j-3,...,a`. Therefore after Phase A every site of `I` is zero, regardless of the initial states outside `I` and regardless of where the initial zero in `I` was located.

#### Phase B: sample equilibrium from left to right while keeping a zero guard

Use the site word

```text
a, a+1, ..., b-1,
```

of length `L-1`. In each corresponding window require exactly one ring among sites of `I`, at the prescribed site, but impose **no condition on its Bernoulli coin**.

At the start of Phase B all sites in `I` are zero. When `a` rings, `a+1` is still zero, so the update is legal. When `a+1` rings, `a+2` is still zero, and so on. Finally `b-1` is legal because the guard site `b` has not been updated in Phase B and remains zero. Thus every scheduled Phase-B mark is legal. Each final state on `I^-` is exactly its own independent Bernoulli refresh coin. No site of `I^-` rings again later in the construction. Hence the terminal vector on `I^-` is exactly `pi_{I^-}`, independent of the initial configuration and outside randomness, while `b` remains zero.

#### Probability

In a window of length `delta=1/L`, the probability that the total rate-`L` clock process in `I` has exactly one ring and that the ring belongs to one prescribed site is

```text
exp(-L delta) * delta = e^(-1)/L.
```

For each of the `2L-1` Phase-A windows one also pays the zero-coin probability `q`; Phase-B coins are unrestricted. By independence of disjoint time windows,

```text
P(R_I)
 = (q/(eL))^(2L-1) * (1/(eL))^(L-1)
 = q^(2L-1) (eL)^(-(3L-2)).
```

This proves the lemma. `QED`

### Interpretation

This is stronger than a mere coalescence word: once one vacancy is present in a fixed interval, there is a completely explicit finite Harris event that erases all local initial information and outputs an **exact equilibrium draw** on an arbitrary fixed interior target, with one zero guard left over.

**B19 outcome:** complete local strong-stationary regeneration lemma. It is source-relevant but does not move `E_FAexp`, because the probability of this one reset event is only a fixed positive number rather than tending to one with time.

## 3. B20 — find exactly why the local reset does not already prove Conjecture 2

The first temptation is to repeat Lemma 2.1 in fixed-length time blocks and declare failure whenever the chosen fixed interval `I` contains no infection at the beginning of a block. That architecture cannot by itself establish convergence to `pi`.

### Lemma 3.1 — fixed-window vacancy-gate obstruction

For any fixed finite interval `I` of length `L`, under equilibrium

```text
pi(I contains no infection) = p^L > 0.
```

Consequently, any coupling estimate whose error contains the raw term

```text
P_eta(I contains no infection at the trial time)
```

and which requires this term to vanish as `t -> infinity` is structurally incapable of proving convergence to equilibrium for fixed `I`: even at equilibrium the corresponding probability is the positive constant `p^L`.

This is a proof-architecture obstruction, not a counterexample to Conjecture 2. It says the reset mechanism must do at least one of the following:

1. **import a facilitator from outside `I` during the final coupling attempt** rather than require one already inside at the attempt start;
2. let the window scale with time while keeping regeneration cost under control; or
3. use a regeneration event triggered by a moving infection/front rather than a deterministic fixed-box vacancy gate.

Option 2 is unattractive for the explicit word above because `r_L(q)` decays faster than exponentially in `L` through the rigid clock ordering. Thus the meaningful direction is to combine transport of a vacancy with a fixed-size terminal reset.

This identifies a more precise missing mechanism than the previous report's generic phrase “interior forgetting”: **transport + exact local stationaryization must be amplified to high probability without paying global box entropy.**

**B20 outcome:** the exterior does not invalidate the reset word; the actual failure is probabilistic amplification. A fixed local reset conditioned on a pre-existing local infection leaves a nonvanishing equilibrium-sized failure mode.

## 4. B30 — one direction at a time: an exponential-distance import-and-reset certificate

The fixed-window obstruction suggests importing a vacancy from one side and only then applying the strong-stationary reset. There is an explicit one-sided construction whose cost is merely exponential in the distance, not superexponential.

### Lemma 4.1 — one-sided zero transport with exponential distance cost

Assume `eta_x=0` and let `a>x`, `d=a-x`. There is a Harris event using time `d/2` with probability

```text
s_d(q) = (q/(2e))^d
```

on which `eta_{d/2}(a)=0`, regardless of all other initial states and all graphical marks not explicitly constrained by the event.

### Proof

For `k=0,...,d-1`, use a successive time window of length `1/2`. In window `k`, require

- no clock ring at the current anchor `x+k`;
- exactly one clock ring at `x+k+1`;
- the refresh coin at that ring is `0`.

No restrictions are imposed elsewhere. Inductively the anchor `x+k` is zero at the start of its window and stays zero because its clock does not ring. Therefore the unique ring at `x+k+1` is legal and makes that site zero. The next window uses `x+k+1` as the new anchor.

For a length-`delta` window the probability is

```text
exp(-delta) * [delta exp(-delta)] * q
 = q delta exp(-2 delta).
```

This expression is maximized at `delta=1/2`, where it equals `q/(2e)`. Independent Poisson increments on disjoint windows give `(q/(2e))^d`. `QED`

The reflected construction handles an infection to the right of the target.

### Proposition 4.2 — local equilibrium minorization with explicit spatial cost

Let `I=[a,b]`, `L=b-a+1>=2`, and suppose the initial configuration has an infection at `x<a`. Set `d=a-x` and

```text
tau_d = d/2 + T_L,
w_d(q) = (q/(2e))^d * r_L(q).
```

Then for every cylinder event `A` depending only on `I^-=[a,b-1]`,

```text
P_eta( eta_{tau_d}|_{I^-} in A ) >= w_d(q) * pi_{I^-}(A).      (4.1)
```

Moreover the contribution realizing the right-hand side has `eta_{tau_d}(b)=0`.

### Proof

First realize the one-sided transport event of Lemma 4.1, producing a zero at `a`. On the subsequent disjoint time interval realize the reset event `R_I` of Lemma 2.1. Conditional on the concatenated event, the final interior is an exact `pi_{I^-}` sample. The two graphical events use disjoint time increments, hence their probabilities multiply. Therefore

```text
P_eta(A and concatenated event)
 = (q/(2e))^d r_L(q) pi_{I^-}(A),
```

which gives (4.1). `QED`

### Why this matters

The spatial dependence is now exactly of the type the previous run was seeking:

```text
w_d(q) = r_L(q) exp[- beta(q) d],

beta(q) = log(2e/q).
```

Thus **transport to a fixed target plus exact equilibrium resampling costs only exponentially in the initial distance to a facilitator**. This is a rigorous local Doeblin/minorization certificate and matches the acceptable `exp(+bR)` spatial scale in the desired deterministic estimate H(a,b).

But the direction of the estimate is not yet enough: (4.1) gives a positive equilibrium component of weight `w_d`, whereas H(a,b) requires the *non-equilibrium remainder* to become exponentially small in time. The missing theorem is therefore an amplification/renewal statement producing linearly many effective import-reset opportunities, or an equivalent high-probability coupling, once time exceeds the initial-distance scale.

**B30 outcome:** complete exponential-distance import-and-reset minorization; the gluing obstruction has been reduced to high-probability amplification rather than construction of a local exact reset.

## 5. What is now proved versus what remains open

### Proved in R7-04 so far

1. The source conjecture includes every deterministic initial configuration containing at least one infection.
2. The deterministic estimate H(a,b) implies the exact source conjecture with explicit rate `a*kappa/(b+kappa)`.
3. Once a fixed interval contains one infection, an explicit finite Harris word maps its interior to an **exact product-equilibrium sample** independent of initial/exterior state.
4. A vacancy at distance `d` can be imported to a target by an explicit controlled Harris path with probability `(q/(2e))^d` in time `d/2`.
5. Combining 3 and 4 gives a local equilibrium minorization with precisely exponential spatial cost.

### Still missing

For every fixed `q>0`, turn the positive one-shot reset weight into exponentially high coupling probability on the time scale `t`, uniformly over deterministic initial states except for the allowed `exp(bR)` cost. Concretely, one needs a theorem of the following flavor:

```text
P_eta(no successful local import-reset opportunity by time t)
    <= C exp(-a t + b R(eta)).
```

The lemmas above show that, **once such opportunities occur with a linear-rate/exponential-tail renewal structure**, the local stationaryization itself is no longer an obstacle. Establishing that renewal structure for all `q>0` is the genuine open step and is consistent with the source's diagnosis of the low-density non-stationary difficulty.

No full-source proof or counterexample is claimed.

## 7. Rejected shortcuts / exact failure reasons

- **“The reset word solves the conjecture.”** False: its success probability is a fixed positive constant, not `1-O(e^{-ct})`.
- **“Require a zero in a fixed box at the last trial.”** Insufficient: at equilibrium the no-zero event in a fixed length-`L` box has probability `p^L>0`.
- **“Make the reset box linearly large.”** The explicit rigid word then pays a severe `L^{-(3L)}`-type ordering cost and does not combine with an `e^{-ct}` error estimate.
- **“Source ballistic spreading gives repeated target visits.”** Not established by the quoted diameter/hitting estimates. Spread of extreme infections is not a local recurrence or local regeneration theorem.
- **“Minorization weight equals mixing rate.”** False. A one-shot lower bound `P >= w pi` only yields useful contraction if it can be iterated under a valid renewal/coupling structure.
