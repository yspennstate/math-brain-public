## 3. B2 — attack the negation first

### 3.1 The source hypothesis already contains every deterministic infected configuration

For a deterministic configuration `eta`, define the nearest-infection radius

```text
R(eta) := inf{|x| : eta_x = 0},
```

with `R(eta)=infinity` for the all-healthy configuration.

If `R(eta)<infinity`, then for `nu=delta_eta`,

```text
delta_eta(no infected vertices in [-ell,ell]) = 0
```

for every `ell >= R(eta)`. Consequently `delta_eta` satisfies the source's exponential-gap assumption for **every** `kappa>0` once `ell` is sufficiently large.

Hence Conjecture 2 contains, as special cases, exponential local relaxation from **every deterministic configuration having at least one infection**. In particular it contains the configuration with exactly one infection at the origin.

This is stronger than a positive-density formulation and is an important counterexample screen: an eventual counterexample does not need a complicated random initial law. A single deterministic infected configuration would refute the source.

The all-healthy absorbing state is not an admissible counterexample because its no-infection probability is identically one and violates the source hypothesis.

### 3.2 No counterexample found from the sparse extreme

The single-infection state is the sparsest admissible deterministic start, but the source's Section 6 proves ballistic-scale spreading statements from precisely this kind of start. Those statements do not prove local equilibrium, but they remove the simplest proposed failure mechanism, namely that facilitation never reaches the observation window.

**B2 outcome:** no refutation. The sparse extreme instead identifies the right proof obligation: after facilitation arrives, prove exponential *forgetting* of the initial condition for every `q>0`.

## 4. B18 — translate the source hypothesis into a deterministic influence bound

The prior attempt used a qualitative finite-volume relaxation time. The sharper formulation is to charge explicitly for the distance to the nearest initial infection.

For a bounded local observable `f`, write

```text
Delta_f(t,eta) := | E_eta[f(eta(t))] - pi(f) |.
```

The following deterministic estimate is sufficient for the full source conjecture:

> **Nearest-infection influence estimate H(a,b).** For fixed `q>0` and local `f`, there exist finite constants `A>=1`, `a>0`, `b>=0` such that for every deterministic `eta` with `R(eta)<infinity`,
> 
> ```text
> Delta_f(t,eta) <= A ||f||_infty exp(-a t + b R(eta))
> ```
> 
> for all `t>=0`.

The point is that the dependence on the entire initial configuration has been reduced to one scalar, `R(eta)`, with at most exponential cost. No independence, stationarity, density, or mixing assumption on the initial law is inserted.

The next section proves exactly that H(a,b) closes Conjecture 2 and gives the resulting exponential rate.

### 4.1 Why the naive finite-box proof can lose the all-q regime

A standard finite-volume route has two competing linear scales.

1. To isolate an infinite-volume local observable from the boundary up to time `t` by a finite-speed argument, one takes a box radius `r` larger than a propagation speed times `t`, schematically
   
   ```text
   r > v_FS t.
   ```

2. A worst-state spectral-gap/L2 estimate in a box of radius `r` has the form
   
   ```text
   TV <= const * exp(-gamma(q) t + h(q) r),
   ```
   
   because the point-mass-to-equilibrium density contributes an entropy/minimum-mass factor exponential in the volume. For Bernoulli product equilibrium one naturally pays `h(q)` on the order of `log(1/min(q,1-q))` per unit length.

With `r=theta t`, this two-step proof requires simultaneously

```text
theta > v_FS
and
theta < gamma(q)/h(q).
```

The source's high-`q` threshold is therefore not mysterious from this viewpoint: a crude global-box argument can simply have no admissible `theta` when `q` is small. Uniform positivity of the spectral gap for each fixed `q` is not by itself enough to make these two inequalities compatible.

This is not a counterexample to the conjecture; it is an exact diagnosis of why a global finite-box mixing proof can stop at a `q` threshold. To reach every `q>0`, the proof should avoid paying global equilibrium entropy for an `O(t)` box and instead obtain **local interior forgetting behind the infection front**.

**B18 outcome:** the full source is reduced to a quantitative local-influence estimate H(a,b), and the old global-box route is shown to have a concrete two-speed obstruction.

## 5. B25 — exact exponential-tail gluing lemma

Here is the new complete lemma of this run.

### Lemma (nearest-infection exponential gluing)

Fix `q>0` and a bounded local observable `f`. Suppose H(a,b) holds for some `A>=1`, `a>0`, `b>=0`:

```text
|E_eta f(eta(t)) - pi(f)|
    <= A ||f||_infty exp(-a t + b R(eta))
```

for every deterministic configuration with at least one infection.

Let `nu` be **any** initial law satisfying, for some `C<infinity`, `kappa>0` and all sufficiently large integers `r`,

```text
nu(R > r) <= C exp(-kappa r).
```

Then there is `C_f<infinity` such that, for all sufficiently large `t`,

```text
|E_nu f(eta(t)) - pi(f)|
    <= C_f exp(-c t),

c = a kappa / (b + kappa) > 0.
```

Thus H(a,b) for every `q>0` and local `f` proves the exact source Conjecture 2.

### Proof

For any integer `r>=0`, linearity in the initial law and the triangle inequality give

```text
|E_nu f(eta(t)) - pi(f)|
 <= integral Delta_f(t,eta) nu(deta).
```

Split the integral according to `{R<=r}` and `{R>r}`. On `{R<=r}`, H(a,b) gives

```text
Delta_f(t,eta)
 <= A ||f||_infty exp(-a t + b R(eta))
 <= A ||f||_infty exp(-a t + b r).
```

On `{R>r}`, the trivial bounded-observable estimate gives

```text
Delta_f(t,eta) <= 2 ||f||_infty.
```

Therefore

```text
|E_nu f(eta(t)) - pi(f)|
 <= A ||f||_infty exp(-a t + b r)
    + 2 C ||f||_infty exp(-kappa r).        (1)
```

Choose

```text
theta := a/(b+kappa),
r := floor(theta t).
```

The time exponent of the first term is

```text
-a + b theta
 = -a + ab/(b+kappa)
 = -a kappa/(b+kappa),
```

while the second has exponent

```text
-kappa theta = -a kappa/(b+kappa).
```

The floor changes only the multiplicative constant (by at most an `exp(kappa)` factor in the second term). Hence (1) is bounded by

```text
C_f exp[- a kappa t/(b+kappa)]
```

for all sufficiently large `t`. This proves the lemma. `QED`

### What this improves over D0027-A001

The earlier attempt asked vaguely for a subexponential finite-volume relaxation time. The lemma identifies the **exact quantitative interface** between dynamics and the source initial-law assumption:

- dynamics must pay time at rate `a`;
- distance to the first facilitator may cost at most `b R`;
- the source supplies tail rate `kappa`;
- the optimally balanced output rate is `a kappa/(b+kappa)`.

No stronger property of `nu` is used. This is the right gluing target for subsequent runs.

**B25 outcome:** complete source-valid reduction lemma, but not metric movement because H(a,b) remains unproved.

## 6. New obstruction and simplest next lemma

The exact remaining dynamic statement is now:

> For every `q>0` and every finite-support local `f`, prove constants `A_f(q), a_f(q)>0, b_f(q)<infinity` such that
> 
> ```text
> Delta_f(t,eta)
> <= A_f(q) ||f||_infty
>    exp[-a_f(q)t + b_f(q) R(eta)]
> ```
> 
> uniformly over **all deterministic initial configurations with at least one infection**.

The source's ballistic excursion estimates suggest that reaching the support of `f` can be charged by `R(eta)`. The genuinely missing part is a regeneration/coupling statement saying that the local region forgets all remaining background information exponentially after facilitation has penetrated it.

A useful next formulation is a graphical **interior-regeneration event**. In the Harris construction, seek an event inside a forward cone from one initial infection such that:

1. the cone reaches the support of `f` with failure `<= exp(-a t + bR)`;
2. after reaching it, a finite sequence of legal rings and refresh coins cuts the value of `f(eta_t)` off from all pre-cone initial states;
3. the required event can be repeated in disjoint time slabs with a success probability bounded below for each fixed `q>0`.

A geometric trial structure of this type would produce H(a,b) directly and would avoid the global-box entropy penalty diagnosed above.
