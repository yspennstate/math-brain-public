## 4. First substantive correction: the source process is attractive

The recovered prior intake attempt `E0010-A001` correctly warned that density estimates alone do not prove uniqueness, but it also claimed the sexual-reproduction process is not attractive because births require two occupied neighbours. That obstruction is false.

Use the common Harris construction. Each death mark sets one coordinate to zero, hence preserves order. A birth mark attached to an ordered pair of distinct neighbours applies the Boolean update

`eta_x <- eta_x OR (eta_y AND eta_z)`,

which is increasing in all coordinates. A stirring mark applies the same coordinate swap to both coupled configurations, also preserving order. Therefore the process is attractive.

This is not source closure, but it restores the monotone-coupling/second-class-particle route that the old attempt had incorrectly discarded.

## 5. B2 — attack the negation: exact stationary disagreement equation

Assume, toward the negation of uniqueness, that there is a second positive stationary law `mu != bar_nu`. Couple `eta<=xi` stationarily with lower marginal `mu` and upper marginal `bar_nu`. Put

`D_x=xi_x-eta_x in {0,1}`, `q_x=E D_x`.

For the ordered-pair birth polynomial

`A_x(zeta)=[1/(n(n-1))] sum_{y!=z in N_x} zeta_y zeta_z`,

the generator calculation for `D_x` is exact:

`0 = epsilon^{-2} sum_{w in S_x}(q_w-q_x) - q_x`

`    + beta E[(1-xi_x)(A_x(xi)-A_x(eta)) - D_x A_x(eta)].`  (5.1)

The pair difference has the exact parent-disagreement decomposition

`A_x(xi)-A_x(eta)`

`= [1/(n(n-1))] sum_{y!=z} [D_y xi_z + eta_y D_z].`  (5.2)

Thus disagreements have no spontaneous source. They are transported by stirring, killed by death marks, can be created at a common vacant site only by a birth whose selected parent pair contains an already-existing disagreement, and can be killed when the lower process gives birth at a disagreement site.

Outcome: the negation is converted into existence of a nonzero stationary second-class-particle field satisfying an exact balance law.

## 6. B18 — translate the theorem: stable-root killing versus a local factorization defect

For `beta>4`, set

`s=sqrt(1-4/beta)`, `rho=(1+s)/2`.

Then `rho` is the stable positive root of `f(u)=-u+beta u^2(1-u)` and

`f'(rho)=-2s/(1-s)`.

Define the strict contraction margin

`lambda_beta=-f'(rho)=2s/(1-s)>0`

and its normalized form

`kappa_beta=lambda_beta/beta=s(1+s)/2`.

At the source threshold `beta=4.5`, `s=1/3`, so `lambda_beta=1` and `kappa_beta=2/9`.

Let `bar q_x=n^{-1}sum_{y in N_x}q_y` and define the exact signed relative-equilibrium remainder

`R_x = [1/(n(n-1))] sum_{y!=z} {`

` E[(1-xi_x)D_y xi_z]-(1-rho)rho q_y`

`+E[(1-xi_x)eta_y D_z]-(1-rho)rho q_z`

`} - [E[D_x A_x(eta)]-rho^2 q_x].`

Adding and subtracting those mean-field products in (5.1) yields the exact translated equation

`0 = epsilon^{-2} sum_{w in S_x}(q_w-q_x)`

`    - q_x + beta[2rho(1-rho)bar q_x-rho^2 q_x+R_x].`  (6.1)

If a tagged disagreement saw the local background as equilibrium density `rho` with a sufficiently small *relative* correlation error, then `R_x` would be small compared with `q_x`, and the remaining mass term is precisely `f'(rho)q_x=-lambda_beta q_x`.

Outcome: source uniqueness is translated to a fast-stirring relative local-equilibrium theorem for one tagged disagreement.

## 7. B25 — count the same thing twice: a sharp necessary obstruction to nonuniqueness

Let `Q=sup_x q_x>0` in any nonzero stationary disagreement coupling. Choose an approximate-max sequence `x_m` with `q_{x_m}->Q` sufficiently fast that

`epsilon^{-2} d_s (Q-q_{x_m})/q_{x_m}->0`.

Because `bar q_x<=Q` and

`epsilon^{-2}sum_{w in S_x}(q_w-q_x) <= epsilon^{-2}d_s(Q-q_x)`,

rearranging (6.1), dividing by `q_{x_m}`, and passing to `liminf` proves

`liminf_{m->infinity} R_{x_m}/q_{x_m}`

`>= [1+beta rho^2-2beta rho(1-rho)]/beta`

`= -f'(rho)/beta`

`= kappa_beta = s(1+s)/2.`  (7.1)

This is a source-relevant exact obstruction: **if uniqueness fails, near-maximal disagreements must sustain a positive normalized factorization defect at least `kappa_beta`, even under arbitrarily fast stirring.**

The pair identity in (5.2) is the double count: every excess successful upper birth is counted through the generator at the child site and again by charging it to the disagreement in one of the two ordered parent positions.

Outcome: new exact quantitative reduction, still PARTIAL.

## 8. Exact blocker

An ordinary hydrodynamic or propagation-of-chaos estimate of the form

`R_x=o_epsilon(1)`

is insufficient. A hypothetical family of distinct stationary phases could have disagreement density `Q_SR(beta,epsilon)->0`; then an absolute local-equilibrium error can dominate the disagreement signal.

The missing theorem must be **relative/Palm**:

for every fixed `beta>4.5`, uniformly over stationary ordered basic couplings and approximate-maximal disagreement sites, prove

`R_x/q_x < kappa_beta`

for all sufficiently small `epsilon` (preferably with a strict margin), even when `q_x` itself tends to zero.

That lemma, combined with (7.1), would force `Q_SR=0` and close the source theorem.
