## 3. B11 — dualize: the exact raw reproduction threshold

Let `Q=sup_x q_x>0` under a hypothetical nonzero stationary disagreement coupling. For fixed `epsilon`, choose approximate maximizers `x_m` so rapidly that

\[
\varepsilon^{-2}d_s\frac{Q-q_{x_m}}{q_{x_m}}\to0.
\]

Since every stirring neighbor has `q_w<=Q`,

\[
\varepsilon^{-2}\sum_{w\sim_s x_m}(q_w-q_{x_m})
\le \varepsilon^{-2}d_s(Q-q_{x_m}).
\]

Equation (2.1) then implies

\[
\beta\frac{\Gamma_{x_m}}{q_{x_m}}
=1-
\frac{\varepsilon^{-2}\sum_{w\sim_s x_m}(q_w-q_{x_m})}{q_{x_m}}
\ge 1-o(1).
\]

Hence any failure of uniqueness necessarily satisfies the exact threshold

\[
\boxed{\liminf_m\frac{\Gamma_{x_m}}{q_{x_m}}\ge\frac1\beta.}
\tag{3.1}
\]

This is equivalent to, but cleaner than, the previous stable-root remainder inequality. If

\[
s=\sqrt{1-4/\beta},\qquad \rho_+=(1+s)/2,
\qquad \kappa_\beta=s(1+s)/2,
\]

and `g(u)=2u-3u^2`, then direct algebra gives

\[
g(\rho_+)+\kappa_\beta=1/\beta.
\]

Thus the source uniqueness problem has become: show that sufficiently fast stirring makes the **per-tag disagreement reproduction number** strictly less than `1/beta` at approximate-maximal disagreement sites.

### Exact Palm dual

For `q_y>0`, define

\[
\widehat{\mathbb E}_y[F]=\frac{\mathbb E[D_yF]}{q_y}.
\]

Using (2.3) term by term gives, with no approximation,

\[
\boxed{
\frac{\Gamma_x}{q_x}
=\frac1{n(n-1)}\sum_{y\ne z}
\left[
\frac{q_y}{q_x}\widehat{\mathbb E}_y[(1-\xi_x)\xi_z]
+\frac{q_z}{q_x}\widehat{\mathbb E}_z[(1-\xi_x)\eta_y]
-\widehat{\mathbb E}_x[\eta_y\eta_z]
\right].}
\tag{3.2}
\]

Indeed the three terms are simply

`E[(1-xi_x)D_y xi_z]=q_y Ehat_y[(1-xi_x)xi_z]`,

`E[(1-xi_x)eta_y D_z]=q_z Ehat_z[(1-xi_x)eta_y]`,

and

`E[D_x eta_y eta_z]=q_x Ehat_x[eta_y eta_z]`.

At an approximate maximum the `q_y/q_x` ratios are asymptotically at most one. The difficult object is therefore a **single tagged-disagreement Palm law**, not an arbitrary relative factorization of all local correlations. This is a real narrowing of the previous blocker.

## 4. B6 — deform to a solvable point: exact fully mixed color sectors

The attempted solvable endpoint is pure fast stirring in a finite block, where conditional on conserved color counts the interchange process is uniform over permutations.

Classify each site in an ordered coupling by three colors:

- common one: `(eta,xi)=(1,1)`, count `A`;
- common zero: `(eta,xi)=(0,0)`, count `C`;
- discrepancy: `(eta,xi)=(0,1)`, count `K`;

with `A+C+K=M` and `K>0`.

Conditional on these counts, suppose the colors are uniformly permuted over an `M`-site block. For distinct `x,y,z`, conditioning on one discrepancy gives the exact hypergeometric identities

\[
\widehat P_y(C_x,\xi_z=1)
=\frac{C}{M-1}\frac{A+K-1}{M-2},
\tag{4.1}
\]

\[
\widehat P_z(C_x,\eta_y=1)
=\frac{C}{M-1}\frac{A}{M-2},
\tag{4.2}
\]

and

\[
\widehat E_x[\eta_y\eta_z]
=\frac{A}{M-1}\frac{A-1}{M-2}.
\tag{4.3}
\]

Every site has discrepancy probability `K/M`, so the `q`-ratios in (3.2) equal one. The exact normalized reproduction bracket becomes

\[
\boxed{B_M(A,K)=
\frac{C(2A+K-1)-A(A-1)}{(M-1)(M-2)}.}
\tag{4.4}
\]

Write `a=A/M`, `d=K/M`, `c=1-a-d`. Then

\[
\psi(a,d):=c(2a+d)-a^2
=2a-3a^2+d(1-3a)-d^2,
\tag{4.5}
\]

and exact expansion of (4.4) yields

\[
B_M=\frac{M^2\psi(a,d)+M(a-c)}{(M-1)(M-2)}.
\tag{4.6}
\]

Because `|a-c|<=1` and `|psi|<=1` on the color simplex,

\[
|B_M-\psi(a,d)|
\le\frac{4M-2}{(M-1)(M-2)}
\le\frac6{M-2}.
\tag{4.7}
\]

The crucial sign appears directly in (4.5): for `a>=1/3`,

\[
\psi(a,d)\le g(a):=2a-3a^2,
\tag{4.8}
\]

because `d(1-3a)-d^2<=0`. Thus, once the lower/common-one density is above `1/3`, the presence of additional discrepancy mass itself makes disagreement reproduction smaller, not larger.

Define the upper solution of `g(a)=1/beta` by

\[
r_\beta=\sqrt{1-3/\beta},\qquad
a_+(\beta)=\frac{1+r_\beta}{3}.
\tag{4.9}
\]

For `beta>4.5`, `a_+>1/3`. If `a>=a_++delta`, then since `g'(a_+)=-2r_beta` and `g` is quadratic,

\[
g(a)\le\frac1\beta-2r_\beta\delta-3\delta^2.
\tag{4.10}
\]

Combining (4.7), (4.8), and (4.10) proves the exact restricted theorem

\[
\boxed{
B_M(A,K)
\le\frac1\beta-igl(2r_\beta\delta+3\delta^2\bigr)+\frac6{M-2}}
\tag{4.11}
\]

whenever `A/M>=a_+(beta)+delta`. Therefore if

\[
\frac6{M-2}<2r_\beta\delta+3\delta^2,
\tag{4.12}
\]

then `B_M<1/beta`, contradicting the necessary nonuniqueness threshold (3.1) in the exactly mixed sector.

### What B6 changed conceptually

The previous run appeared to require factorization all the way around the stable mean-field density

\[
\rho_+(\beta)=\frac{1+\sqrt{1-4/\beta}}2.
\]

That is too strong. The new exact threshold is `a_+(beta)`, and one checks `a_+(beta)<rho_+(beta)` throughout the source range. A tagged-disagreement proof only needs a **strict lower-density floor above `a_+` plus tagged color mixing**; it does not need to show that every local correlation factors to exactly `rho_+`, and it does not need a lower bound on the global disagreement density.

There is also a failed B6 route that should not be repeated. Simply taking stirring to infinity and appealing to uniqueness of the mean-field positive equilibrium cannot work: for `beta>4`, `f(u)=-u+beta u^2(1-u)` has two positive roots `rho_-` and `rho_+`, the first unstable and the second stable. The solvable endpoint is the **high-density upper-phase tagged sector**, not the set of all positive mean-field equilibria.

B6 outcome: **exact restricted theorem and a discarded naive deformation route; source still open**.

## 5. B30 — localize and identify the gluing failure

The exact fully mixed theorem is useful only if it can be approached on a mesoscopic block without dividing an absolute mixing error by the possibly tiny disagreement density.

Take a `d`-dimensional block of side `L`, with `M` of order `L^d`. For nearest-neighbor stirring at rate `epsilon^{-2}`, the interchange mixing time has diffusive scale

\[
t_{mix}=O(\varepsilon^2L^2\log M)
\tag{5.1}
\]

up to constants depending on the block geometry. The total graphical reaction rate in the block is `O((1+beta)M)`, so the expected number of reaction marks during one mixing window is

\[
O(\varepsilon^2L^{d+2}\log L).
\tag{5.2}
\]

If

\[
L=\varepsilon^{-\alpha},\qquad 0<\alpha<2/(d+2),
\tag{5.3}
\]

then `L->infinity`, `t_mix->0`, and (5.2) tends to zero. Hence there is a nonempty scale window in which the finite-size term `6/(M-2)` vanishes and pure interchange has enough time to mix before a typical whole-block reaction.

This calculation does **not** finish the proof. Ordinary total-variation mixing gives an absolute error. If `Q_SR(beta,epsilon)` itself tends to zero, dividing that absolute error by the tag probability recreates the old `1/q_x` failure. The gluing theorem must carry the discrepancy tag throughout.

A further exact hazard count makes the tagged route more plausible. In the translation-symmetric `n`-neighbor ordered-pair model, a tagged discrepancy sees:

- its own death mark at rate `1`;
- a lower birth at its current site, killing it, at rate at most `beta`;
- birth marks at possible child sites for which it is one of the two ordered parents, at total rate exactly `2 beta`.

The last identity is because there are `n` possible children, `2(n-1)` ordered pairs containing the tag per child, and each ordered pair has rate `beta/[n(n-1)]`. Thus directly tag-involving reaction marks have total rate at most

\[
1+3\beta,
\tag{5.4}
\]

independent of `epsilon` and independent of the global disagreement density. This does not control all environmental reactions, so it is not a Palm replacement proof; it does identify a route to a **relative tagged-interchange averaging estimate without a `1/q` loss**.

B30 outcome: **nonempty mesoscopic scale and exact gluing obstruction identified; no source closure**.
