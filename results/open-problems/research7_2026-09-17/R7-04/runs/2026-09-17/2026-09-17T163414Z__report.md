## 3. Exact blocking formula: anchored disagreement extinction

The cleanest way to expose that bridge is to use the `L^2` theorem already proved rather than trying to reset directly to `pi`.

Fix a deterministic configuration `eta` with at least one infected site, and choose one infected anchor `x`, ideally a nearest infection to the support of `f`. Define

```text
nu_x := pi( . | eta_x = 0 ).
```

Because `pi(eta_x=0)=q`,

```text
d nu_x/d pi = 1_{eta_x=0}/q,
||d nu_x/d pi - 1||_2 = sqrt((1-q)/q).
```

Therefore the already-proved spectral-gap estimate gives, for every local `f`,

```text
| nu_x(P_t f) - pi(f) |
 <= exp[-gamma(q)t]
    sqrt((1-q)/q) sqrt(Var_pi(f)).                   (3.1)
```

Now couple two FA-1f processes with the **same Harris clocks and refresh coins**:

- process A starts from the deterministic `eta`;
- process B starts from a random `zeta ~ nu_x`.

Both processes start with a common infected anchor at `x`. For the support `S=supp(f)`, let

```text
D_{S,t}^{x}
  := { eta_t|_S != zeta_t|_S }
```

under this basic graphical coupling. The coupling inequality gives exactly

```text
| delta_eta(P_t f) - nu_x(P_t f) |
 <= 2 ||f||_infty P(D_{S,t}^{x}).                    (3.2)
```

Combining (3.1) and (3.2) yields the blocking formula

```text
Delta_f(t,eta)
 <= 2 ||f||_infty P(D_{S,t}^{x})
    + exp[-gamma(q)t]
      sqrt((1-q)/q) sqrt(Var_pi(f)).                  (3.3)
```

Thus the entire singular-start problem is reduced to one graphical question:

> **Anchored disagreement extinction.** Prove constants `A,a,b>0` (depending on `q` and the finite support `S`) such that, whenever `eta_x=0`,
> 
> ```text
> P(D_{S,t}^{x}) <= A exp[-a t + b dist(x,S)] .        (3.4)
> ```

If (3.4) holds, choose `x` as the nearest initial infection. Then `dist(x,S) <= R(eta)+O_f(1)`, and (3.3) supplies the deterministic `H(a,b)` estimate from run 1, which in turn supplies the full source Conjecture 2 by the proved gluing lemma.

Equation (3.4), not radial speed or one-shot reset probability, is the exact current blocker.

### Why this formulation is sharper than the previous reset language

The prior local reset word gives a positive probability of agreement in a window, but **agreement in a finite window is not an absorbing state** under the basic coupling: a disagreement just outside the window can later change whether a boundary clock ring is legal, thereby reintroducing disagreement into the target. Repeating positive-probability resets therefore does not by itself imply exponential extinction.

The anchored formulation asks directly for a decay estimate on the only event that matters at the observation time. In backward-Harris language, `D_{S,t}^{x}` is the event that some unresolved legality/refresh dependency from `S x {t}` reaches initial data without being cut by a common-facilitator coalescence event. The missing theorem is therefore an exponential tail for a backward disagreement-ancestor cluster, or an equivalent persistent shielding theorem.

## 5. Missing ingredient

The missing ingredient is a **persistent common-facilitator shielding lemma**, equivalently a backward-ancestor extinction theorem.

A sufficient form is exactly (3.4). A still more structural version would say that in the basic coupling of two processes sharing an infected anchor at `x`, the backward dependency cluster of a finite terminal set `S` has an exponentially decaying probability of connecting to time zero at a location not already coupled, with a cost at most exponential in `dist(x,S)`.

The crucial word is **persistent**. A one-time local coalescence is insufficient because different exterior states can later create different constraint legality. The proof must either:

- create a common infected shield that survives long enough to prevent exterior disagreement from re-entering; or
- work backward and show that every possible legality-dependency path is cut with uniformly positive density.

This is the precise point where non-attractiveness matters: more vacancies can make an update legal and thereby create, rather than only remove, disagreement. Any valid argument has to control the branching of legality dependencies, not just the motion of one tagged vacancy.
