## 4. Exact blocking formula

The newest exact pair-law reduction defines, for an independent set `I`,

`b(empty)=1`,

`b(I)=|N[I]|^{-1} sum_{x in I} b(I\{x})`.

Fix the highest peak `r` and put `U_r=V\N[r]`. For a non-neighbour `v`, the positive actual pair weight is represented by the alternating sum

`C_n(r,v)
 = sum_{I independent, I subset U_r, v in I}
     (-1)^(|I|-1) b(I)`.

The exact ordered-pair count is

`W_n(r,v)=(n^2-1)! C_n(r,v)`,

so the conditioned distance expectation is exactly

`E[rho_n/n | two peaks]
 = [sum_{r,v} d(r,v) C_n(r,v)]
   / [n sum_{r,v} C_n(r,v)]`.                         (BLOCK-1)

Thus the source-level block is not “find another exact formula.” It is the missing inequality

`limsup_n [sum d(r,v) C_n(r,v)]
          / [n sum C_n(r,v)] > 0`,                    (BLOCK-2)

or its negation if the source is true.

There is a useful sharpening of what actually has to be proved. A source-valid NO does **not** require pointwise estimates on every `C_n(r,v)`, radial monotonicity, convergence of the whole pair law, or even macroscopic separation with probability tending to one. It is enough to find constants `epsilon, eta>0` and infinitely many `n` such that

`sum_{d(r,v) >= epsilon n} C_n(r,v)
 >= eta sum_{r,v} C_n(r,v)`.                          (BLOCK-3)

Then `D_* >= epsilon eta >0`. This is strictly weaker than the earlier sufficient target that forced near-pair probability to vanish. The diagnosis therefore narrows the recovery target from a full large-deviation theorem to a coarse **aggregate annular-mass lower bound along a subsequence**.
