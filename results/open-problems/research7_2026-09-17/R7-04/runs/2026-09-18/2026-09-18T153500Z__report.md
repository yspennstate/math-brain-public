## 5. Direct later theorem: a large part of the literal problem is already solved

### 5.1 Jorritsma–Komjáthy 2020

Joost Jorritsma and Júlia Komjáthy, *Weighted distances in scale-free preferential attachment models*, Random Structures & Algorithms 57 (2020), 823–859, DOI `10.1002/rsa.20947` / arXiv:1907.01907, studies exactly first-passage percolation on three preferential-attachment models with nonnegative i.i.d. edge weights in the power-law regime `tau in (2,3)`.

Their theorem determines the typical weighted-distance asymptotics in both edge-weight universality classes:

- explosive weights: convergence in distribution to a sum of two independent explosion times;
- conservative weights: divergence with an explicit first-order main term, plus tight fluctuations under a mild extra hypothesis;
- the typical hopcount is analyzed as well.

Thus this is a direct theorem on the source observable, not merely an analogy.

### 5.2 Jorritsma–Komjáthy 2022

The same authors' *Distance evolutions in growing preferential attachment graphs*, Annals of Applied Probability 32 (2022), 4356–4397, DOI `10.1214/22-AAP1789` / arXiv:2003.08856, further treats graph-distance and weighted-distance evolution for preferential attachment with `tau in (2,3)`, again with nonnegative i.i.d. edge weights.

This later-author follow-up confirms a developed solution theory in that parameter regime rather than a single isolated restricted calculation.
