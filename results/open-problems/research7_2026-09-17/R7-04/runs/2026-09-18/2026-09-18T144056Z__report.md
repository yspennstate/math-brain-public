## 2. Substantive result A — C0089 is positively RESOLVED_IN_LITERATURE_2025

The strongest new source-status result is for `C0089`, Heydenreich--van der Hofstad Open Problem 16.7.

Primary source: Markus Heydenreich and Remco van der Hofstad, *Progress in High-Dimensional Percolation and Random Graphs* (2017 author version), Open Problem 16.7. The source asks for a quenched invariance principle for simple random walk on the random-interlacement set and on an infinite component of the vacant set of random interlacements.

Primary source PDF:

`https://www.mathematik.uni-muenchen.de/~heyden/survey-high-d-percolation.pdf`

The repository's prior campaign status was deliberately cautious: PRS 2016 gave the random-interlacement-set QIP at every level and the vacant-set QIP under structural/local-uniqueness hypotheses; the campaign suspected that later sharpness should extend the vacant-set result throughout `u<u_*`, but had not verified the full hypothesis match.

The exact later theorem is now located:

A. Goswami, P.-F. Rodriguez, V. Shulzhenko, *Strong local uniqueness for the vacant set of random interlacements*, arXiv:2503.14497 (2025),

`https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.14497`.

Its Corollary 1.2 states that for **every** `u in (0,u_*)`, for almost every vacant-set environment, the rescaled simple random walk on the infinite vacant cluster converges to Brownian motion with covariance `sigma^2 I`, `sigma^2>0`. The paper explicitly says this is an immediate consequence of its new strong-local-uniqueness theorem together with PRS Theorem 1.1, and explicitly identifies the previously outstanding PRS structural condition as supplied by the new theorem.

The companion ingredients are:

- E. B. Procaccia, R. Rosenthal, A. Sapozhnikov, *Quenched invariance principle for simple random walk on clusters in correlated percolation models*, PTRF 166 (2016), arXiv:1310.4764, `https://arxiv.org/abs/1310.4764`;
- H. Duminil-Copin, S. Goswami, P.-F. Rodriguez, F. Severo, A. Teixeira, *Phase transition for the vacant set of random walk and random interlacements*, arXiv:2308.07919, `https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.07919`.
