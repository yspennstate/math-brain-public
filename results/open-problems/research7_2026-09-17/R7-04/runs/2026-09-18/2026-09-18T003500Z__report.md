## 5. Exact blocking formula — the obstruction is branch selection, not mixing

The fully mixed color-sector calculation from the second research run gives, in the large-block limit, the normalized disagreement reproduction bracket

\[
\psi(a,d)=2a-3a^2+d(1-3a)-d^2,
\tag{5.1}
\]

where `a` is the common-one fraction and `d` the discrepancy fraction. Nonuniqueness requires, asymptotically,

\[
\psi(a,d)\ge \frac1\beta.
\tag{5.2}
\]

The previous plan treated the missing labeled-stirring merger as the principal bridge. The present diagnosis shows that this cannot be the whole difficulty: **even if perfect tagged exchangeability and perfect `q`-flattening are granted for free, (5.1) retains an uncontrolled scalar branch parameter `a`.**

Take the cleanest limit `d=0`. Then

\[
g(a):=\psi(a,0)=2a-3a^2.
\tag{5.3}
\]

The dangerous interval is exactly

\[
\boxed{
a_-(\beta)\le a\le a_+(\beta),\qquad
 a_\pm(\beta)=\frac{1\pm\sqrt{1-3/\beta}}3,
}
\tag{5.4}
\]

because `g(a)>=1/beta` precisely between the two roots.

Now let

\[
\rho_\pm(\beta)=\frac{1\pm\sqrt{1-4/\beta}}2
\]

be the two nonzero mean-field equilibria. They satisfy

\[
\frac1\beta=\rho_\pm(1-\rho_\pm).
\tag{5.5}
\]

At the **unstable** root `rho_-<1/2`,

\[
\begin{aligned}
g(\rho_-)-\frac1\beta
&=2\rho_- -3\rho_-^2-\rho_-(1-\rho_-)\\
&=\boxed{\rho_-(1-2\rho_-)}>0.
\end{aligned}
\tag{5.6}
\]

So the unstable mean-field branch lies **strictly inside the reproduction-allowed region**. In contrast,

\[
g(\rho_+)-\frac1\beta=\rho_+(1-2\rho_+)<0,
\tag{5.7}
\]

so the stable high branch contracts discrepancies.

This is the exact blocker: the existing fast-stirring machinery can make the Palm environment well mixed, but a perfectly mixed environment near `rho_-` still satisfies the necessary nonuniqueness reproduction inequality with positive slack. **The proof needs a dynamical/stationary mechanism that selects the high branch and excludes the unstable branch.**

### Upper-phase density alone also cannot close the gap

Set the upper occupancy fraction

\[
u=a+d.
\]

Then (5.1) rewrites exactly as

\[
\psi(a,u-a)=-a^2-au+a-u^2+u.
\tag{5.8}
\]

For fixed `u>1/2`, this concave quadratic is maximized at

\[
a_*=(1-u)/2,
\]

with

\[
\max_a\psi(a,u-a)=\frac{(1-u)(3u+1)}4.
\tag{5.9}
\]

Even if one grants the strongest currently available upper-density heuristic, namely `u=rho_+`, (5.5) gives

\[
\max_a\psi(a,\rho_+-a)-\frac1\beta
=\boxed{\frac{(1-\rho_+)^2}{4}}>0.
\tag{5.10}
\]

Therefore convergence of the **upper invariant density** to `rho_+` does not by itself imply disagreement contraction. One must control the lower/common-one component under the discrepancy Palm law, not merely the upper marginal.
