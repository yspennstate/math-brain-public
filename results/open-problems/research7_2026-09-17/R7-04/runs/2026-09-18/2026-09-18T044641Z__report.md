## B2 — attack the negation: a sharp universal upper obstruction

Define `alpha_*` as the unique root in `(0,1)` of

`-alpha_* log(1-alpha_*) = 1`.

Equivalently,

`alpha_* = 1-exp(-1/alpha_*)`,

and numerically

`alpha_* = 0.7407536433...`.

### Theorem

For every `alpha>alpha_*`, Conjecture 7.9 fails with that value of `alpha`. Therefore

`alpha_dom <= alpha_*`.

### Proof

For large `N`, put

`p_N=1/(alpha N)`

and let `nu_N` be the uniform law on the `N` singleton subsets of `S_N={1,...,N}`. For every singleton `{i}`,

`nu_N(H_{ {i} })=1/N=alpha p_N`,

and every higher-order principal cylinder has zero mass. Thus `nu_N` is `alpha p_N`-spread.

For the up-class

`A_N={K:K != emptyset}`,

an `alpha`-thinning of a singleton is nonempty with probability exactly `alpha`, so

`W_alpha(nu_N)(A_N)=alpha`.

But

`mu_{p_N}(A_N)=1-(1-1/(alpha N))^N -> 1-exp(-1/alpha)`.

If `alpha>alpha_*`, then

`-alpha log(1-alpha)>1`,

which is equivalent to

`alpha>1-exp(-1/alpha)`.

Hence the domination fails for all sufficiently large `N`. `□`

This does not refute the existential conjecture, but it rigorously excludes every possible universal constant above `0.7407536433...`.

## B18 — translate through the 2026 Chen Li coupling theorem

Let

`kappa=W_alpha(nu)`

with `nu` `alpha p`-spread. Then for every `I`,

`kappa(H_I)=alpha^{|I|}nu(H_I)<=(alpha^2 p)^{|I|}`.

Thus `kappa` is `q`-spread with

`q=alpha^2 p`.

Chen Li, *On p-Spread Measures*, arXiv:2609.08967v1 (posted 2026-09-08), proves in Theorem 3.3 that every `q`-spread `kappa` admits a coupling

`X~kappa`, `R_1~mu_q`, `R_2~mu_q`, `X⊆R_1 union R_2` a.s.

The theorem does not require `R_1,R_2` to be independent. This run isolates an exact source-closing strengthening:

> **Independent two-cover lemma for noisy spread laws.** For some universal `alpha_0>0`, if `nu` is `alpha_0 p`-spread and `kappa=W_{alpha_0}(nu)`, then the Li cover can be chosen with *independent* `R_1,R_2~mu_{alpha_0^2 p}`.

If this holds for any `alpha_0<=1/sqrt(2)`, then

`R_1 union R_2 ~ mu_s`,

with

`s=2 alpha_0^2 p-alpha_0^4 p^2 <= p`,

so

`W_{alpha_0}(nu) <=_st mu_s <=_st mu_p`,

which closes F0106 positively.

The independence strengthening is false for arbitrary `q`-spread laws: if `kappa` is uniform on `N` singletons with `q=1/N`, it is always nonempty, whereas the union of two independent `mu_q` sets is nonempty with probability tending only to `1-e^{-2}`. Therefore any successful strengthening must use the special **alpha-thinning representation**, not spreadness alone. This is the exact gluing obstruction exposed by B18.

## B25 — a complete sharp theorem for disjoint minimal witnesses

Let an up-class have pairwise disjoint minimal witnesses

`B_1,...,B_m`,

with arbitrary sizes `k_j=|B_j|`, so

`A=union_j H_{B_j}`.

### Theorem — sharp disjoint-witness domination

For every `0<alpha<=alpha_*`, every `0<p<1`, and every `alpha p`-spread `nu`,

`W_alpha(nu)(A) <= mu_p(A)`.

The constant `alpha_*` is sharp for this whole class, already when all `B_j` are singletons.

### Proof

Sample `J~nu` and then its independent `alpha`-thinning `K`. Put

`c_j=alpha^{k_j}`,
`x_j=p^{k_j}`,
`Z_j=1{B_j⊆J}`,
`lambda_j=-log(1-c_j)`.

Conditional on `J`, survival of different witnesses is independent because the `B_j` are disjoint. Therefore

`P(K notin A | J)
 = product_j (1-c_j)^{Z_j}
 = exp(-sum_j lambda_j Z_j)`.

Since `exp(-y)` is convex, Jensen gives

`P(K notin A)
 >= exp(-sum_j lambda_j E Z_j)`.

Spreadness gives

`E Z_j=nu(H_{B_j}) <= (alpha p)^{k_j}=c_j x_j`.

The scalar function

`g(c)=-c log(1-c)`

is strictly increasing on `(0,1)`, and `g(alpha_*)=1`. Since

`c_j=alpha^{k_j}<=alpha<=alpha_*`,

we have

`lambda_j c_j<=1`.

Thus

`P(K notin A)>=exp(-sum_j x_j)`.

Finally, `1-x<=e^{-x}` implies

`exp(-sum_j x_j)>=product_j(1-x_j)`.

Under `mu_p`, the disjoint witness events are independent, so

`mu_p(A^c)=product_j(1-p^{k_j})=product_j(1-x_j)`.

Hence

`W_alpha(nu)(A^c)>=mu_p(A^c)`,

which is equivalent to the desired domination. `□`

Talagrand's source explicitly handled the special case `A={X:X != emptyset}` with a sufficient `alpha<=1/2` estimate. The theorem above pushes that family to its exact sharp constant `alpha_*` and simultaneously handles all up-classes whose minimal witnesses are pairwise disjoint, of arbitrary sizes. No publication novelty claim is made here; that requires a dedicated prior-art audit.
