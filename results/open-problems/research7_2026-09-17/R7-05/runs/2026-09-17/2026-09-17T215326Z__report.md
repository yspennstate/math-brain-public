## 4. New status correction for D0091

This was the most important admission result of the run because the repository's September 8 intake record and at least one newer neighboring screen understate the later literature.

### Exact outside source

Leonardo T. Rolla, **“Activated Random Walks on Z^d,” Probability Surveys 17 (2020), 478–544**, DOI `10.1214/19-PS339`. Immediately after Theorem 2.14, the source states:

> Show that the critical density zeta_c is continuous and strictly increasing in the sleep rate lambda.

Theorem 2.14 itself only supplies non-decreasing monotonicity.

Primary source landing page:
`https://www.emis.de/journals/PS/ps/viewarticle%2Bid339.html`

### Later-author/current literature check

Lorenzo Taggi, **“Essential enhancements in Abelian networks: continuity and uniform strict monotonicity,” Annals of Probability (2023)**, arXiv `2003.00932`, proves on every vertex-transitive graph that the ARW critical curve `mu_c(lambda)` is continuous for every positive lambda and gives the uniform one-sided slope bound

```text
limsup_{delta -> 0} [mu_c(lambda+delta)-mu_c(lambda)]/delta
    <= 1/[lambda(1+lambda)].
```

Crucially, the same paper then states as **Open Question 1**: prove that `mu_c(lambda)` is strictly increasing in lambda. Thus D0091 is not wholly open in its 2020 form and not wholly resolved either: its continuity clause is **KNOWN**, while its strict-increase clause remains an explicit later-source open question.

Fresh bounded 2025–2026 ARW checks also recovered Kaufman–Meisel on asymptotics of the critical density (arXiv `2512.00720`), Junge–Kaufman–Meisel on the mean-field limit and complete graph, Hoffman–Richey–Son on one-dimensional local density, and Forien–Hoffman–Johnson–Meisel–Richey–Rolla on one-dimensional explosivity. None of these bounded checks supplied the missing global strict-increase theorem. This is negative evidence only, not a worldwide openness certificate.

### Admission consequence

The source-faithful residual is therefore

```text
For all relevant positive lambda_1 < lambda_2,
prove zeta_c(lambda_1) < zeta_c(lambda_2).
```

That is a global theorem about a critical curve of an infinite interacting-particle system. A derivative at one lambda, a finite torus computation, endpoint asymptotics, or a restricted graph class does not close the source residual. It remains **HARD / NOT CLOSABLE FOR R7-05**. No claim was created.

This correction should be preserved for future coordinator reconciliation, but this worker did not edit shared ledgers.
