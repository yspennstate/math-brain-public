## New result 2 — exact replica-overlap posterior theorem

Let `N_z(e)` be departure counts of the observed path and `M_z(e)` departure counts of a translated virtual length-`n` path. For the one-site law `mu`, set

`Phi(A)=int product_e p(e)^{A(e)} mu(dp)`.

The exact conditional virtual-path likelihood given the observed path is

`prod_z Phi(N_z+M_z)/Phi(N_z)`,

whereas the fresh annealed likelihood is `prod_z Phi(M_z)`.

Uniform ellipticity gives, at every shared site,

`[Phi(N+M)/Phi(N)] / Phi(M)
 <= kappa^{-min(|N|,|M|)}`.

Therefore, with

`I(gamma,eta)=sum_z min(|N_z|,|M_z|)`, 

one has the exact useful bound

`P(eta | gamma) <= kappa^{-I(gamma,eta)} P_fresh(eta)`.

Consequently

`E[Z(tau_{X_t}omega)|gamma]
 <= E_0[exp(-(alpha/n)Y_n.u) kappa^{-I(gamma,Y)}]`.

This replaces the artificial charge for all nearby old sites by a charge only for actual old-path/virtual-future intersections.

## Why the LLN alone does not finish (SW)

The ballistic strong law gives the correct sign of proportional-window increments, but not the quantitative rate needed for the polynomial source condition. For fixed `alpha` the window transform remains of constant order `exp(-alpha v.u)` on macroscopic windows. Taking `alpha` of order `log L` would formally create a polynomial gain, but then one needs a deviation estimate strong enough to justify the growing exponential weight. Likewise the LLN only gives `P(tau_L>A L)->0`, not its `L^{-(d-1)}` rate.

This isolates the missing estimate much more sharply than the old neighbourhood-recycling term.
