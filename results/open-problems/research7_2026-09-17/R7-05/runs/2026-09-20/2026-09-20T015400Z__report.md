## New theorem 1 — `d-1` initial-radius moment is enough

Let the deterministic limiting velocity be `v`, with `v.ell>0`. Shrink to an open neighborhood `U` of `ell` such that `v.u>0` for all `u in U`. Directional transience therefore holds for every such `u`.

Let `tau_1^u` be the first standard regeneration time and define

`R_u=max(0,-inf_{0<=n<=tau_1^u} X_n.u)`.

### Theorem

If

`E_0[R_u^{d-1}]<infinity`                                    (1)

for every `u` in one open neighborhood `U` of `ell`, then `(mathcal P^{d-1})|ell` holds. In the i.i.d. uniformly elliptic source setting this implies `(T)|ell`, and hence `(P)_M|ell` for every standard effective `M>=15d+5`.

### Proof

Fix `u in U` and `b>0`. A hit of `-bL` before `+L` must occur before `tau_1^u`: if regeneration occurs below `+L`, no subsequent path point can drop below the positive regeneration record; if the regeneration record is already at or beyond `+L`, then `T_L^u<=tau_1^u`.

Therefore

`P_0[\tilde T_{-bL}^u<T_L^u] <= P_0[R_u>=bL]`.               (2)

Put `p=d-1`. Finite `p`-moment gives the strict little-o tail

`t^p P(R_u>=t)
 <= E[R_u^p 1_{R_u>=t}] -> 0`.                               (3)

Thus

`L^p P_0[\tilde T_{-bL}^u<T_L^u]
 <= b^{-p}(bL)^p P_0[R_u>=bL] -> 0`.                         (4)

The same open set `U` works for every fixed `b`, which is precisely `(mathcal P^{d-1})|ell`. Guerra 2020 Theorem 1.9 then gives `(T)|ell` in the i.i.d. case; Guerra--Ramírez gives the standard source `(P)_M` conclusion. QED.

### Dimension-two consequence

For `d=2`, the entire source implication follows once one proves only

`E_0[R_u]<infinity`                                           (5)

for every direction `u` in one neighborhood of `ell`.

A stronger but simpler sufficient condition is `E_0[tau_1^u]<infinity`, since `R_u<=tau_1^u`. This replaces the earlier apparent need for very high regeneration moments by a **first-moment problem in two dimensions**.

## New theorem 2 — occupation-time reduction

Define

`N_u=sum_{n>=0} 1_{ {X_n.u<=0} }`.

Positive speed gives `N_u<infinity` almost surely but gives no moment estimate by itself.

For every nearest-neighbor path,

`R_u <= 1+N_u`.                                               (6)

To see this, if the path reaches depth at least `r`, take the last nonnegative-projection time before that hit. The subsequent segment remains negative until the hit, and because a step changes projection by at most one, it contains at least `floor(r)` nonpositive visits.

Hence

`E[N_u^{d-1}]<infinity`                                       (7)

on a neighborhood of directions is another sufficient condition for the full source conclusion.

In `d=2`, Tonelli gives the concrete criterion

`E[N_u]=sum_{n>=0}P_0(X_n.u<=0)<infinity`.                    (8)

So a summable annealed slowdown-to-the-wrong-halfspace estimate in nearby directions would already solve P0487 in dimension two. Positive speed itself only gives decay of the summands to zero, not summability.

## New theorem 3 — geometric failed-attempt compression

The classical regeneration construction makes successive record attempts until one succeeds; the number of failed attempts has geometric tail. The global initial backward radius can be reduced to the depth of one generic failed attempt as follows.

Let `K` be the first successful attempt and suppose

`P(K>=j)<=q^j`, `q<1`.                                       (9)

On failed attempt `j`, let `B_j` be the maximum downward displacement from that attempt's record level. Suppose for a deterministic nonincreasing `f`

`P(B_j>=r, j<K | H_j) <= f(r) 1_{ {K>=j} }`                 (10)

where `H_j` is the attempt-start history.

Then

`P(R_u>=r)<=f(r)/(1-q)`.                                      (11)

Indeed a global hit below `-r` must occur during some failed segment, and because every attempt starts at a nonnegative record, that segment then has downward displacement at least `r`. Union bound plus (9)--(10) gives the geometric series.

Consequently the full P0487 implication follows if the i.i.d. spatial model yields, for every nearby `u`,

`r^{d-1} f_u(r) -> 0`.                                       (12)

This is weaker than requiring a finite `(d-1)`-moment and is now the sharpest identified local target.

## Decisive obstruction / noncircularity

The geometric number of attempts is not the missing input. Nor is the successful conditioned regeneration law enough. Positive-speed regeneration formulas concern a law conditioned on no backtracking after a successful record. The radius `R_u` is created by the **failed** attempts, precisely the trajectories removed by that conditioning.

Moreover, a failed attempt can descend into already explored lower territory. That environment is no longer fresh conditional on the attempt history. Therefore replacing (10) by an unconditional origin tail using i.i.d. independence would be invalid. This is the exact place where the finite-state/periodic Perron mechanism and naive renewal arguments stop.

The spatial i.i.d. structure must be used to prove that a very deep failed excursion necessarily encounters enough genuinely fresh material, or else that repeated recycling inside the explored region has a sufficiently small tail.
