## 6. B6 — deform from the regular point and obtain a quantitative gap

The full derivation is in the artifact

Here is the mathematical core.

Let `D*` be the size-biased degree law and

`nu = E[D*-1] > 1`, `beta_q = atanh(1/nu)`.

For the later paper's i.i.d.-degree tilt, put

`t(beta)=(1/2) log cosh(beta)`

and write `E_t` for exponential tilting of `D*` by `exp(tD*)`. The forward-degree mean of the tilted law is exactly

`h(t)=E_t[D*-1]`.

Differentiating the exponential family gives

`h'(t)=Var_t(D*)`,

hence the exact identity

`nu(q(beta))-nu = integral_0^{t(beta)} Var_s(D*) ds`.   (6.1)

If `D*` has bounded nondegenerate support `[a,b]`, width `W=b-a` and variance `V`, then

`dP_s/dP_0 >= exp(-sW)`,

so

`Var_s(D*) >= exp(-sW)V`.

Thus

`nu(q(beta))-nu >= (V/W)(1-exp(-W t(beta))).`   (6.2)

Let `bar beta` be the later paper's unique comparison root

`bar beta = atanh(1/nu(q(bar beta)))`,

for which the proved theorem gives `beta_c^an,D <= bar beta`. Since `nu(q(bar beta))<=b-1`, define

`beta_0 = atanh(1/(b-1))`,

`t_0 = (1/2)log cosh(beta_0) = -(1/4)log(1-1/(b-1)^2)`.

Then monotonicity and the derivative of `atanh(1/x)` give

`beta_q-beta_c^an,D`

`>= beta_q-bar beta`

`>= [V / (W((b-1)^2-1))] [1-exp(-W t_0)] > 0`.   (6.3)

This is a complete explicit lower bound under bounded nondegenerate size-biased support. It uses only the later paper's proved inequality `beta_c^an,D <= bar beta`; it does not assume the paper's unproved conjecture that equality holds.

For a two-positive-degree law `D in {r,s}`, `r<s`, if

`alpha = r P(D=r)/E[D]`,

then `V=alpha(1-alpha)(s-r)^2`, and (6.3) becomes

`beta_q-beta_c^an,D >= alpha(1-alpha)(s-r)(1-exp(-(s-r)t_0))/((s-1)^2-1)`.
