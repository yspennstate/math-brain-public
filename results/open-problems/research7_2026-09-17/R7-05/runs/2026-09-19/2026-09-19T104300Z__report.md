## G. Nearest solved analogue and what it has that P0336 lacks

The nearest **selection** analogue found in the bounded literature review is:

Luigi De Pascale, Jean Louet, Filippo Santambrogio, *The Monge problem with vanishing gradient penalization: Vortices and asymptotic profile*, J. Math. Pures Appl. 106 (2016), 237–279, arXiv:1407.7022.

That work also starts from a nonunique Euclidean/Monge optimal-transport problem and asks which optimizer is selected after a vanishing perturbation. Their perturbation is explicit:

`J_epsilon(T) = J_0(T) + epsilon * integral |DT|^2`,

**What their proof has that P0336 lacks:** a deterministic external regularizer with a known sign, known scale, and compactness/coercivity structure. P0336 has no explicit `epsilon R(T)`.
