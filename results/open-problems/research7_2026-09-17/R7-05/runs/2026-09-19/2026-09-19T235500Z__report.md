## 4. New proof I — wrong-way slabs are completely localized to the first regeneration block

Fix a unit direction `u` with positive limiting velocity component. Let `tau_1^u` be the first standard regeneration time in direction `u`, and let

`R_u=max(0,-inf_{0<=n<=tau_1^u} X_n.u)`

be the pre-regeneration backward radius.

For every `b,L>0`,

`{H_{bL}^{-u}<H_L^u} subset {R_u>=bL} subset {tau_1^u>bL}`.    (4.1)

The first inclusion is tautological once the second statement below is proved: if the first regeneration occurred before the wrong-way hit, then its positive record level and no-backtracking property would prevent all later visits to `X.u<=-bL`. Hence any wrong-way hit occurs before `tau_1^u`. Since a nearest-neighbor step changes the unit-direction projection by at most one, reaching `-bL` also requires at least `bL` steps.

This yields a complete source-valid sufficient criterion.

### Theorem 4.1

If, for some `M>0` and every `u` in a neighborhood of `ell`,

`E_0[R_u^M]<infinity`,                                         (4.2)

then `(P*)_M|ell` holds.

Indeed,

`L^M P_0(H_{bL}^{-u}<H_L^u)
 <= b^{-M} E_0[R_u^M 1_{R_u>=bL}] -> 0`.

Thus a moment of the *spatial backtracking depth* is enough; a full moment of the first regeneration time is stronger than necessary. In particular, a moment exponent above the Berger--Drewitz--Ramírez threshold gives `(P*)_M`, then `(T')`, and hence `(P)_M` at the standard threshold by the established equivalences.

The P0487 bottleneck has therefore been reduced to a concrete high-moment question about a single initial regeneration block:

`positive velocity => E_0[R_u^M]<infinity for u near ell and some M>15d+5`. (4.3)

## 5. New proof II — a decisive obstruction to regeneration-only arguments

I constructed an exact bounded-step regenerative process showing that positive speed plus excellent *later* regeneration blocks does not control the initial wrong-way tail.

Choose `B>=2` with

`P(B=n)=c/[n^2(log(n+1))^2]`.

Then `E[B]<infinity`, but `E[B^{1+eps}]=infinity` for every `eps>0`, and

`P(B>=L) asymp 1/[L(log L)^2]`.

Starting at zero, move left deterministically for `B` steps, then right for `B+1` steps to reach `+1`, then move right forever. The first regeneration time is `2B+1`; every post-first regeneration duration is exactly one. Hence the asymptotic speed is exactly `+1`, yet

`P(H_L^-<H_L^+)=P(B>=L) asymp 1/[L(log L)^2]`.

Therefore `(P*)_M` fails for every `M>1` despite bounded steps, deterministic positive speed and perfect post-first regeneration tails.

This is **not** an i.i.d. RWRE and does not refute P0487. It proves that the missing implication (4.3) cannot be obtained from abstract regenerative renewal structure plus finite mean later blocks. Any successful proof must use the i.i.d. spatial environment and uniform ellipticity to control the initial trap/backtracking block.

## 6. New proof III — bounded correctors can be weakened to `L^p` / inverse-`L^q` correctors on finite boxes

Let

`(K_theta h)(omega)=sum_e omega(0,e)e^{-theta e.u}h(tau_e omega)`

and assume `h>0` with

`K_theta h<=h` almost surely.                                  (6.1)

Suppose for some `s,q>0`,

`E[h^s]<infinity`,  `E[h^{-q}]<infinity`.                     (6.2)

For any polynomial-volume box `B_L` with `|B_L|<=CL^D` and a back face lying at `X.u<=-cL`, let `A_L^-` be the back-face exit event. For arbitrary `a,b>0`, define the good environment event by

`h(omega)<=L^a`

and

`h(tau_x omega)>=L^{-b}` for every lattice site in the one-layer enlargement of `B_L`.

Stationarity, Markov's inequality and a union bound give

`P(G_L^c)<=E[h^s]L^{-as}+C E[h^{-q}]L^{D-bq}`.                (6.3)

On `G_L`,

`M_n=e^{-theta X_n.u}h(tau_{X_n}omega)`

is a nonnegative quenched supermartingale. Optional stopping at box exit yields

`P_{0,omega}(A_L^-)<=L^{a+b}e^{-theta cL}`.                   (6.4)

Therefore

`P_0(A_L^-)
 <= E[h^s]L^{-as}+C E[h^{-q}]L^{D-bq}+L^{a+b}e^{-theta cL}`.   (6.5)

For any prescribed polynomial exponent `M`, choose `as>M` and `bq>D+M`; then

`P_0(A_L^-)=o(L^{-M})`.                                       (6.6)

So *any* positive and negative moments of a tilted corrector are enough for arbitrarily high polynomial **back-face** decay on polynomial-volume boxes. Uniform boundedness is unnecessary.

Scope limitation: `(P)_M` also counts lateral exits. A one-directional tilt does not penalize a nearly transverse lateral exit. Thus a direct finite-box corrector route still needs a separate lateral-confinement estimate. The regeneration-radius route avoids this issue by first proving `(P*)_M`.
