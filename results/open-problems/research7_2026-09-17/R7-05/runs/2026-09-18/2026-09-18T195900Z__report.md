## 4. New target of the B6 deformation

The preceding finite-state theorem used the Poisson equation

\[
(I-K)c=\beta(\bar\delta_q\mathbf1-\delta^q)
\]

only to construct a bounded correction to the negative-power Lamperti function. That suggests a sharper hypothesis than finite-state Markov dependence.

Let the positive stack environment be a stationary ergodic sequence

\[
\eta_x=(p_{x,1},p_{x,2},\ldots),
\qquad p_{x,i}\ge1/2,
\]

with mean total drift

\[
\bar\delta
=E\sum_{i\ge1}(2p_{0,i}-1)>1.
\]

Fix `0<beta<bar(delta)-1`. Choose a finite prefix `m_0` and radial weakening `t<1` so that the finite-depth baseline

\[
q_{x,i}
=
\begin{cases}
1/2+t(p_{x,i}-1/2),&i\le m_0,\\
1/2,&i>m_0
\end{cases}
\]

has mean drift

\[
\bar\delta_q>1+\beta.
\]

Its centered site drift is

\[
Y_n=\delta_n^q-\bar\delta_q.
\]

The new hypothesis is the bounded projective condition

\[
\boxed{
\sum_{r\ge0}
\|E[Y_r\mid\mathcal F_0]\|_\infty<\infty,
}
\tag{4.1}
\]

where `F_n=sigma(eta_j:j<=n)`.

Summably phi-mixing stack processes satisfy (4.1), because for bounded centered `Y_r`,

\[
\|E[Y_r\mid\mathcal F_0]\|_\infty
\le2\|Y_0\|_\infty\phi(r).
\]

Thus the class is genuinely infinite-memory and strictly broader than finite-state Markov chains.

## 5. B6 — bounded Gordin corrector replaces the phase Poisson equation

Under (4.1), define

\[
G_n
=
\sum_{r\ge0}E[Y_{n+r}\mid\mathcal F_n].
\]

This converges in `L^infinity`, uniformly in `n`, and satisfies

\[
E[G_{n+1}\mid\mathcal F_n]=G_n-Y_n.
\]

Set

\[
c_n=-\beta G_n.
\]

Then

\[
\boxed{
E[c_{n+1}\mid\mathcal F_n]
=c_n+\beta(\delta_n^q-\bar\delta_q).
}
\tag{5.1}
\]

For the baseline forward branching process, when `k>=m_0`, the exact finite-depth decomposition gives

\[
V_{n+1}=G_1+\cdots+G_k+Z_n
\]

conditionally on the environment, where the `G_i` are fair geometric success counts and `Z_n` is a bounded Bernoulli sum of mean `delta_n^q`. Hence, uniformly over histories,

\[
E[\Delta_n]=\delta_n^q,
\qquad
E[\Delta_n^2]=2k+O(1),
\qquad
E|\Delta_n|^3=O(k^{3/2}),
\]

with an exponential tail outside `|Delta_n|<=k/2`.

Choose `a` large enough and define

\[
F_n(k)
=(k+a)^{-\beta}
\left(1+\frac{c_n}{k+a}\right).
\]

The factor in parentheses stays between `1/2` and `3/2`. Taylor expansion plus (5.1) yields the exact leading drift

\[
\boxed{
E[F_{n+1}(V_{n+1})\mid\mathcal H_n]-F_n(V_n)
=
\beta(\beta+1-\bar\delta_q)
(k+a)^{-\beta-1}
+o(k^{-\beta-1}).
}
\tag{5.2}
\]

The local random drift `delta_n^q` cancels completely. Since `bar(delta)_q>1+beta`, the coefficient is negative, so for a deterministic `L` the process is a nonnegative supermartingale while the population exceeds `L`.

This is the main B6 success: the finite-state Poisson corrector is not essential. A bounded projective/Gordin corrector is enough.

## 6. B28 — control only histories that actually occur

Let `sigma` be any finite stopping time of the joint environment/cookie filtration and suppose `V_sigma=k>L`. Optional stopping gives

\[
\boxed{
P_q(\exists n\ge\sigma:V_n\le L\mid\mathcal H_\sigma)
\le
3\left(\frac{L+a}{k+a}\right)^\beta.
}
\tag{6.1}
\]

Since extinction implies a visit to `<=L`,

\[
\boxed{
P_q(\text{eventual extinction}\mid\mathcal H_\sigma,V_\sigma=k)
\le C_\beta(1+k)^{-\beta}.
}
\tag{6.2}
\]

The quantifier change is crucial. The previous obstruction came from trying to control every deterministic environment after an adaptive high-population exit. Equation (6.2) controls the **actual admissible environmental history**, and it remains valid at the exit stopping time itself. Thus no uniform-integrability estimate for an exit-biased environmental density is needed inside the projective class.

## 7. B30 — exact one-scale gluing

For population `1<=k<K`, the censored next outcome in

\[
\{0,1,\ldots,K-1,H\}
\]

is determined after at most

\[
k+K-1\le2K-2
\]

cookie tosses. Therefore, if `M>=2K-2`, the full stack and its neutral-tail truncation have identical stopped dynamics below population `K`.

Let

\[
\tau_K=\inf\{n:V_n=0\text{ or }V_n\ge K\}.
\]

Both the full process and every truncation `M>=m_0` dominate the common weakened baseline `q`. At the common high-population exit, (6.2) applies **conditional on the exit-biased history**, so both continuation survival probabilities lie in

\[
[1-C_\beta K^{-\beta},1].
\]

On zero exit both are zero. Hence

\[
\boxed{
0\le h-h_M\le C_\beta K^{-\beta}
\qquad(M\ge2K-2).
}
\tag{7.1}
\]

With

\[
K_M=\left\lfloor\frac M2\right\rfloor+1,
\]

this becomes

\[
\boxed{
0\le h-h_M\le C_\beta M^{-\beta}.
}
\tag{7.2}
\]

The established forward/backward identity then transfers the same estimate to the walk:

\[
\boxed{
0\le
p_{\rm esc}^{(\infty)}-p_{\rm esc}^{(M)}
\le C_\beta M^{-\beta},
\qquad
p_{\rm esc}^{(M)}\uparrow p_{\rm esc}^{(\infty)}.
}
\tag{7.3}
\]

This proves infinite-depth truncation continuity for every stationary positive environment whose selected finite-prefix drift satisfies (4.1), in particular every summably phi-mixing stationary stack process. Cookies of strength one are allowed for the continuity theorem.
