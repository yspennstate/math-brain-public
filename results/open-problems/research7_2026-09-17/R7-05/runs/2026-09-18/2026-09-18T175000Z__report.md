## 4. Exact blocking formula

The previous run proved the local fact that finite population cannot see arbitrarily deep cookies. I now write the surviving global obstruction in a form that makes the dependence problem explicit.

Let `eta` denote the spatial environment beginning at the first site used by the forward branching process. For a positive environment define

\[
h(\eta,k)=P_{\eta,k}(V_n>0\text{ for all }n\ge0)
\]

for the full stack, and let `h_M(eta,k)` be the same survival probability after all cookies deeper than `M` are replaced by neutral cookies. By monotonicity,

\[
0\le h_M(\eta,k)\le h(\eta,k)\le1.
\]

Put

\[
\Delta_M(\eta,k)=h(\eta,k)-h_M(\eta,k).
\]

For a population cutoff `K`, let

\[
\tau_K=\inf\{n\ge0:V_n=0\text{ or }V_n\ge K\}.
\]

If

\[
M\ge2K-2,
\tag{4.1}
\]

then the full and truncated processes have **exactly the same stopped kernel below `K`** under the common-uniform coupling: any one-site transition that starts below `K` and is censored at `K` uses at most `2K-2` cookie tosses. Therefore optional stopping of the bounded harmonic survival functions gives the exact identity

\[
\boxed{
\Delta_M(\eta,1)
=
E_{\eta,1}\!\left[
\Delta_M(\theta^{\tau_K}\eta,V_{\tau_K});
V_{\tau_K}\ge K
\right].
}
\tag{4.2}
\]

No approximation occurs in (4.2). The entire depth-truncation error has been moved to the **environment seen at the high-population exit**.

The already-proved forward/backward identity gives, in the right-transient positive case,

\[
p_{\rm esc}^{(\infty)}-p_{\rm esc}^{(M)}
=
E\left[p_{0,1}(\omega)\,
\Delta_M(\theta\omega,1)
\right].
\tag{4.3}
\]

Choose

\[
K_M=\lfloor M/2\rfloor+1,
\]

so (4.1) holds. Combining (4.2) and (4.3), the remaining continuity problem is exactly an exit-weighted boundary defect. In particular, since `Delta_M <= 1-h_M`, it would suffice to prove

\[
\boxed{
E\!\left[
 p_{0,1}(\omega)
 E_{\theta\omega,1}\!\left[
 (1-h_M(\theta^{\tau_{K_M}}(\theta\omega),V_{\tau_{K_M}}));
 V_{\tau_{K_M}}\ge K_M
 \right]
\right]
\longrightarrow0.
}
\tag{4.4}
\]

Equation (4.4), or an equivalent direct control of (4.2), is the **exact blocking obligation**. The issue is not finite-cookie algebra and not local truncation visibility anymore.

## 6. B27 — swap the quantifiers: uniform quenched safety is stronger than the source needs

The previous report deliberately wrote the too-strong candidate

\[
\inf_{\eta}\inf_{j\ge K}h(\eta,j)\to1.
\tag{6.1}
\]

Arbitrary stationary-ergodic environments can have long atypically weak stretches, so an essential-infimum or all-environment version of (6.1) is not a plausible general route.

But (4.2) shows that the source does **not** require (6.1). It only requires safety under the environment distribution selected by the high-boundary exit. Define the weighted exit functional

\[
\Gamma_K(F)
=
E\!\left[
 p_{0,1}(\omega)
 E_{\theta\omega,1}\!\left[
 F(\theta^{\tau_K}(\theta\omega),V_{\tau_K});
 V_{\tau_K}\ge K
 \right]
\right].
\tag{6.2}
\]

Then the exact continuity target is the **annealed exit-biased statement**

\[
\boxed{
\Gamma_{K_M}(\Delta_M)\to0,
}
\tag{6.3}
\]

and the sufficient form (4.4) is

\[
\Gamma_{K_M}(1-h_M)\to0.
\tag{6.4}
\]

This is a materially weaker and more source-matched quantifier order than uniform quenched safety. Any next proof should target (6.3)/(6.4), not return to (6.1).

## 7. B14 — use the shift action: the obstacle is uniform integrability of an induced/Palm law, not absolute continuity

Let `theta` be the spatial shift on the stationary environment space. Conditional on the event that the forward process reaches the high boundary, (6.2) induces a probability law `nu_K` for the shifted environment seen at exit. For any fixed `K`, `nu_K` is absolutely continuous with respect to the stationary environment law `P`: if `P(A)=0`, then stationarity gives `P(theta^{-n}A)=0` for every deterministic integer `n`, and decomposing over the countable events `{tau_K=n}` makes the weighted exit numerator of `A` zero.

This fixed-`K` absolute continuity is **not enough**. The densities can become increasingly concentrated as `K` grows. A familiar stopping-shift phenomenon is that choosing the first occurrence of a rare local pattern produces an induced law heavily biased toward that pattern even though every fixed shifted law is absolutely continuous with respect to the stationary measure.

Thus the missing mechanism can be stated sharply:

> control the family of exit-induced environment laws strongly enough that stationary high-population survival estimates remain valid after the adaptive random shift `theta^{tau_K}`.

A sufficient route would be a uniform-integrability or `L^q`, `q>1`, bound for the Radon-Nikodym densities of the relevant exit laws together with an `L^p(P)` high-state extinction bound. An equivalent direct route is an estimate of (6.4) without naming densities. No such estimate has been proved under arbitrary `(SE)+(POS_1)` in the repository.

This explains why ordinary stationarity/Birkhoff ergodicity does not automatically close the proof: those tools control deterministic shifts or long deterministic averages, whereas `tau_K` is an adaptive environment-dependent spatial index.
