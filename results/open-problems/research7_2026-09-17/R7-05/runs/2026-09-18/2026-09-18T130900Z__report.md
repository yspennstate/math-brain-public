## 4. B1 — exact category change: high states are a critical GWI

Let a fresh bounded stack be

\[
(p_1,\ldots,p_M,1/2,1/2,\ldots),
\qquad
\alpha_j=2p_j-1,
\qquad
\delta=\mathbb E\sum_{j=1}^M\alpha_j.
\]

Let `S_k` be the number of successes before the `k`-th failure and

\[
T(z)=\frac1{2-z},
\]

the pgf of the fair geometric number of successes before one fair failure.

If `K` is the number of successes among the first `M` cookie tosses, then for every `k>=M`

\[
S_k\overset d=K+\sum_{r=1}^{k-M+K}G_r,
\]

with `G_r` i.i.d. fair geometric. Hence, conditional on the stack,

\[
\mathbb E[z^{S_k}\mid(p_1,\ldots,p_M)]
=T(z)^k\prod_{j=1}^M\big((1-\alpha_j)+\alpha_j z\big).
\]

After annealing, define

\[
\boxed{
B(z)=\mathbb E\prod_{j=1}^M\big((1-\alpha_j)+\alpha_j z\big).
}
\]

Then

\[
\boxed{\mathbb E[z^{S_k}]=T(z)^kB(z),\qquad k\ge M.}
\]

For nonnegative cookies `p_j>=1/2`, each `alpha_j in [0,1]`. Conditional on the stack, let independent `J_j~Bernoulli(alpha_j)` and put `I=sum J_j`. Then `B` is the pgf of `I` and

\[
\boxed{S_k\overset d=\sum_{r=1}^kG_r+I,\qquad k\ge M.}
\]

Thus **the entire high-state ERW forward kernel is exactly a critical Galton–Watson process with fair-geometric offspring and explicit immigration law `I`**. Furthermore

\[
\boxed{E I=B'(1)=\delta,}
\]

so the familiar drift parameter is literally the auxiliary immigration mean.

This is a theorem-sized identity, not a fit or simulation. The complete derivation is in the new proof artifact.
