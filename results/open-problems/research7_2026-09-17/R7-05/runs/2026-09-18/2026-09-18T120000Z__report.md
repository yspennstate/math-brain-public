## 4. B11 — exact forward/backward Siegmund duality

At one fresh site let `F_m` be failures before the `m`-th success and `S_k` successes before the `k`-th failure. Let `P` be the backward-chain kernel

\[
Z_{n+1}=F_{n+1,Z_n+1},
\]

and `Q` the forward-chain kernel

\[
V_{n+1}=S_{n+1,V_n},
\qquad 0\text{ absorbing}.
\]

For the order kernel `H(i,k)=1_{i<k}`, the coin sequence gives pathwise

\[
\{F_{i+1}<k\}=\{S_k\ge i+1\},
\]

hence

\[
\boxed{PH=HQ^{\mathsf T}.}
\tag{4.1}
\]

Let `pi` be the positive-recurrent stationary law of the backward chain for `delta>1`, and define

\[
s(k)=\sum_{i<k}\pi_i.
\]

Stationarity and (4.1) give `s=Qs`. Setting `q=1-s`, the process `q(V_n)` is a bounded martingale. Under `(WEL)`, for every finite `K`,

\[
\min_{1\le j\le K}Q(j,0)>0,
\]

so on nonextinction the forward chain cannot visit `{1,...,K}` infinitely often; therefore `V_n->infinity` on survival. Since `q(0)=1` and `q(k)->0`, martingale convergence yields

\[
\boxed{
q(k)=P_k(V\text{ eventually hits }0),
\qquad
s(k)=P_k(V\text{ survives}).
}
\tag{4.2}
\]

Consequently the **entire** backward stationary law is recovered as increments of the forward survival scale:

\[
\boxed{\pi_i=s(i+1)-s(i),\qquad i\ge0.}
\tag{4.3}
\]

In particular

\[
\pi_0=s(1),
\qquad
\pi_1=s(2)-s(1),
\qquad
p_{\rm esc}=E[p_1]s(1).
\tag{4.4}
\]

This is the main structural advance of the run. It is proved directly above; publication novelty is not asserted.
