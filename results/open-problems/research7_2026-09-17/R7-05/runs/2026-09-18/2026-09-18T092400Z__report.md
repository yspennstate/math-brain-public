## 4. Method B16 — exact small case: solve the three-cookie stationary balance as far as it actually closes

Consider an i.i.d. site stack

\[
(p_1,p_2,p_3,1/2,1/2,\ldots),\qquad 0<p_j<1,
\]

with

\[
\delta=\mathbb E[(2p_1-1)+(2p_2-1)+(2p_3-1)]>1.
\]

For a fresh site let `F_m` be the number of failures before its `m`-th success, and let the backward chain be

\[
Z_{n+1}=F_{Z_n+1}.
\]

Write its stationary PGF as

\[
G(s)=\sum_{i\ge0}\pi_i s^i.
\]

Let `K` be the number of successes among the first three cookie tosses and let `a_k=P(K=k)`. For every `i>=2`, after the first three trials there are `3-K` fixed failures and `i+1-K` successes still required from fair Bernoulli trials. Thus

\[
\mathbb E[s^{F_{i+1}}]
=\sum_{k=0}^3 a_k s^{3-k}(2-s)^{-(i+1-k)}.
\]

Set

\[
T(s)=\frac1{2-s},
\quad
Q(s)=\sum_{k=0}^3a_ks^{3-k}(2-s)^k,
\quad
A(s)=Q(s)T(s),
\]

and

\[
P_0(s)=\mathbb E[s^{F_1}],\qquad P_1(s)=\mathbb E[s^{F_2}].
\]

Summing the stationary transition equation gives the exact depth-three functional equation

\[
\boxed{
G(s)=A(s)G(T(s))
+\pi_0(P_0(s)-A(s))
+\pi_1(P_1(s)-A(s)T(s)).
}
\tag{4.1}
\]

### 4.1 Finite-mean-free Abelian step

Put `x=1-s` and `t=T(s)=1/(1+x)`. Then

\[
t-s=\frac{x^2}{1+x},\qquad 1-t=\frac{x}{1+x}.
\]

For every integer `i>=1`,

\[
0\le t^i-s^i\le i t^{i-1}(t-s),
\]

and

\[
i t^{i-1}\le 1+t+\cdots+t^{i-1}\le\frac1{1-t}.
\]

Therefore

\[
0\le\frac{t^i-s^i}{x}\le1.
\]

For every fixed `i` the ratio tends to zero as `s\uparrow1`; dominated convergence under the probability law `pi` proves

\[
\boxed{
\frac{G(T(s))-G(s)}{1-s}\longrightarrow0.
}
\tag{4.2}
\]

This is the key technical point: no assumption `E_pi Z<infinity` is required.

### 4.2 Exact first stationary balance

Let

\[
m_0=\mathbb E F_1,\qquad m_1=\mathbb E F_2.
\]

Since after the third cookie the coin is fair, both are finite. Also

\[
P_0(s)=1-m_0(1-s)+o(1-s),
\quad
P_1(s)=1-m_1(1-s)+o(1-s).
\]

Writing `s=1-x`,

\[
Q(1-x)=\mathbb E[(1-x)^{3-K}(1+x)^K]
=1+\delta x+O(x^2),
\]

because the coefficient of `x` is `E(2K-3)=delta`. Hence

\[
A(1-x)=1+(\delta-1)x+O(x^2).
\]

Divide (4.1) by `1-s`, use (4.2), and let `s\uparrow1`. The result is the exact identity

\[
\boxed{
C_0\pi_0+C_1\pi_1=\delta-1,
}
\tag{4.3}
\]

where

\[
C_0=m_0+\delta-1,
\qquad
C_1=m_1+\delta-2.
\]

For a fixed triple and `q_j=1-p_j`, direct first-three-trial enumeration gives

\[
m_0=q_1p_2+2q_1q_2p_3+4q_1q_2q_3,
\]

\[
m_1=p_1q_2p_3+q_1p_2p_3
+3(p_1q_2q_3+q_1p_2q_3+q_1q_2p_3)
+5q_1q_2q_3.
\]

Exact symbolic simplification yields, after annealing over the random site stack,

\[
\boxed{C_1=\mathbb E[p_1p_2(2p_3-1)]}
\tag{4.4}
\]

and

\[
\boxed{
C_0=
\mathbb E[p_1(2p_2-1)+(2p_3-1)(p_1+p_2-p_1p_2)].
}
\tag{4.5}
\]

### 4.3 A genuinely three-cookie closed family

If

\[
\boxed{C_1=\mathbb E[p_1p_2(2p_3-1)]=0,}
\tag{4.6}
\]

then (4.3) determines `pi_0` and (3.1) gives

\[
\boxed{
 p_{\rm esc}
 =
 \mathbb E[p_1]
 \frac{\delta-1}{
 \mathbb E[p_1(2p_2-1)+(2p_3-1)(p_1+p_2-p_1p_2)]}.
}
\tag{4.7}
\]

This is not merely the old depth-two case: (4.6) can hold by annealed cancellation while the third cookie is nonneutral almost surely.

For example, take the site stack independently to be, with probability `1/2` each,

\[
(9/10,9/10,7/10)
\quad\text{or}\quad
(9/10,9/10,3/10).
\]

Then exact arithmetic gives

\[
\delta=8/5,
\quad C_0=18/25,
\quad C_1=0,
\quad \mathbb E[p_1]=9/10,
\]

so

\[
\boxed{\pi_0=5/6,\qquad p_{\rm esc}=3/4.}
\]

The third cookie is `7/10` or `3/10`, never `1/2`.

**B16 outcome:** substantial PARTIAL progress but no metric movement: the first stationary balance is now exact throughout the right-transient three-cookie regime, and it closes a nontrivial codimension-one family, but a general law still contains the unknown `pi_1`.

## 5. Method B24 — extremize the missing stationary mass

For nonnegative three-cookie stacks,

\[
p_1,p_2,p_3\ge1/2\quad\text{a.s.},
\]

we have `C_1>=0`. Moreover

\[
\begin{aligned}
C_0-C_1
=\mathbb E[&p_1(2p_2-1)\\
&+(2p_3-1)(p_1(1-p_2)+p_2(1-p_1))]
\ge0.
\end{aligned}
\]

Using only (4.3), `pi_0,pi_1>=0` and `pi_0+pi_1<=1` gives

\[
\boxed{
\pi_0\le\frac{\delta-1}{C_0}
}
\tag{5.1}
\]

and, if `C_0>C_1`,

\[
\boxed{
\pi_0\ge
\max\left\{0,\frac{\delta-1-C_1}{C_0-C_1}\right\}.
}
\tag{5.2}
\]

These are extremal for the **reduced constraint system** consisting of (4.3) and the simplex constraints. I do not claim every endpoint is realized by an actual ERW stationary law.

For the deterministic stack

\[
p_1=p_2=p_3=4/5,

exact arithmetic gives

\[
\delta=9/5,
\quad C_0=132/125,
\quad C_1=48/125,
\]

hence

\[
13/21\le\pi_0\le25/33

and therefore

\[
\boxed{52/105\le p_{\rm esc}\le20/33.}
\tag{5.3}
\]

## 6. Method B18 — translate to a rational difference equation and identify the gluing failure

The Möbius iterate in (4.1) becomes much simpler after the change of variable

\[
u=\frac1{1-s},\qquad s=1-\frac1u,
\qquad g(u)=G(1-1/u).
\]

Indeed,

\[
T(1-1/u)=1-\frac1{u+1}.
\]

Define the monic cubic

\[
R(u)=\sum_{k=0}^3 a_k(u-1)^{3-k}(u+1)^k.
\]

Then

\[
A(1-1/u)=\frac{R(u)}{u^2(u+1)},
\]

so (4.1) becomes

\[
\boxed{
g(u+1)=\frac{u^2(u+1)}{R(u)}g(u)+\pi_0d_0(u)+\pi_1d_1(u),}
\tag{6.1}
\]

with explicit rational functions `d_0,d_1`.

The coefficient of `u^2` in `R` is exactly `delta`. If

\[
R(u)=\prod_{j=1}^3(u-r_j),
\]

then

\[
\sum_jr_j=-\delta.
\]

The homogeneous equation attached to (6.1),

\[
h(u+1)=\frac{u^2(u+1)}{R(u)}h(u),
\]

has the exact meromorphic solution

\[
\boxed{
H(u)=
\frac{\Gamma(u)^2\Gamma(u+1)}
{\Gamma(u-r_1)\Gamma(u-r_2)\Gamma(u-r_3)}.
}
\tag{6.2}
\]

Gamma recurrence verifies (6.2) directly. Stirling's ratio formula and `sum r_j=-delta` give

\[
\boxed{H(u)\asymp u^{1-\delta}.}
\tag{6.3}
\]

Thus in the transient regime `delta>1` the nonzero homogeneous mode decays at infinity. Multiplying `H` by a 1-periodic meromorphic factor gives the general homogeneous freedom at this level.

This pins down the PGF gluing failure precisely: the physical tail condition

\[
g(u)\to1\qquad(u\to\infty)
\]

does **not** eliminate a homogeneous contribution that already decays like `u^(1-delta)`. At depth two, the first Abelian balance determines the lone exceptional stationary atom; at depth three, the additional atom `pi_1` couples to a genuine decaying mode.

**B18 outcome:** the failure of the naive depth-three closure is now structural rather than a failed algebraic manipulation. A second independent bounded/renewal identity is required to select the physical amplitude.
