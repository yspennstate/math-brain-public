## Immediate mathematical representation

For finite `E`, ranks `r=dim H1`, `s=dim H2`, and orthonormal column matrices `U,V`, the projection DPP masses are

`p_A = |det U_A|^2`, `q_B = |det V_B|^2`, `R_C = |det [U V]_C|^2`

for `|A|=r`, `|B|=s`, `|C|=r+s`. A desired coupling is exactly a nonnegative array `w(A,B)` on disjoint pairs satisfying

- `sum_B w(A,B)=p_A`,
- `sum_A w(A,B)=q_B`,
- `sum_{A disjoint B, A union B=C} w(A,B)=R_C`.

This is the finite exact core. The first research iteration will attack infeasibility before attempting a positive construction, and any computational search will be followed by an exact Farkas/minor certificate before being treated as mathematics.
