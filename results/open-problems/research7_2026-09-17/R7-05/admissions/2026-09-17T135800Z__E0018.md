## Exact outside source

Florent Malrieu, *Some simple but challenging Markov processes*, Annales de la Faculte des Sciences de Toulouse 24(4) (2015), 857–883, DOI `10.5802/afst.1468`; arXiv:1412.7516. Section 4.1, **Open question 3 (Oscillations of the Lyapunov exponent)**, printed p. 871 / source PDF p. 15.

The surrounding model is the two-state Markov-switched planar linear ODE

`dX_t/dt = A_{I_t} X_t`,

where `I_t in {0,1}` switches `0 <-> 1` at the common constant jump rate `r>0`; equivalently the generator is

`L_r f(x,i) = (A_i x) . grad_x f(x,i) + r ( f(x,1-i)-f(x,i) )`.

The source question asks whether one can choose two real `2 x 2` matrices `A_0,A_1` such that the set of jump rates `r` associated with unstable processes is the union of several intervals. In the section, instability is governed by the sign of the top radial/Lyapunov exponent. The question itself does not add an extra matrix-family restriction beyond the two-matrix planar switched model; any proposed answer must use the source's symmetric two-state switching-rate convention and actual almost-sure growth/decay criterion, not a deterministic switching controller, discrete-time product, mean-square criterion, or higher-dimensional construction.

Source URL: `https://doi.org/10.5802/afst.1468`.
