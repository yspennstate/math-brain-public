## Outside source and exact locator

Elena Kosygina and Martin P. W. Zerner, *Excited random walks: results, methods, open problems*, arXiv:1204.1895 (2012); published in *Bulletin of the Institute of Mathematics, Academia Sinica (New Series)* 8(1) (2013), 105–157. Primary-source PDF: `https://www.math.uni-tuebingen.de/user/zerner/pub/KosyginaZerner.pdf`. Exact locator: Problem 3.12, printed p. 13 (PDF page 13 in the source's numbering), immediately following Theorem 3.10.

Problem 3.12 asks: in the transient one-dimensional case, compute

\[
p_{\rm esc}:=P_0[\forall n\ge1:X_n\ne0],
\]

the probability that the excited random walk never returns to its starting point. The surrounding Theorem 3.10 treats recurrence/transience under either (3.9) `(SE)+(POS_1)` with the stated nondegeneracy, or (3.10) `(IID)+(BD)+(WEL)`; under the latter, transience is rightward for `delta>1` and leftward for `delta<-1`. The source itself points to Zerner 2005, Theorem 18 as a solvable special case.
