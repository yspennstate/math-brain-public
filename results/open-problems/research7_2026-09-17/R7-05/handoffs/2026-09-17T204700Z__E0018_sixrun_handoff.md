## Final-run B5/B13/B25 result: exact Laplace-ratio reduction

Normalize the nonproportional co-rotating elliptic pair as above and choose the origin of `x` at an even point of the cosine profile. Then `u(x)` is even and `A`-periodic. Put

`y(x)=ell(u(x))`,

`M = integral_0^A u(x) dx`,

`A_u(s)=integral_0^A u(x-s)y(x) dx`,

`C_u(s)=integral_0^A u(x)u(x-s) dx`.

Define the two nonnegative chord functions

`D(s)=A_u(0)-A_u(s)`,

`E(s)=M-C_u(s)`.

Evenness gives `A_u(s)=A_u(-s)`, hence

`D(s) = 1/2 integral_0^A [u(x)-u(x-s)][y(x)-y(x-s)] dx >= 0`.

Also

`E(s)=integral_0^A u(x)[1-u(x-s)] dx > 0`,

and the useful double-count identity

`E(s)=E_0 + 1/2 integral_0^A [u(x)-u(x-s)]^2 dx`,

where

`E_0=integral_0^A u(x)(1-u(x)) dx >0`.

Let

`h_r(s)=r e^{-rs}/(1-e^{-rA})`, `0<=s<A`.

Because `q_r=h_r*u`, the entropy formula collapses exactly to

`F(r)/J(r) = (r/2) E_{h_r}[D(s)]`,

while stationary normalization gives

`J(r)^{-1}=2 E_{h_r}[E(s)]`.

The normalization factor of `h_r` cancels. Therefore the co-rotating elliptic traceless Lyapunov exponent has the exact one-dimensional formula

`F(r) = (r/4) [ integral_0^A e^{-rs} D(s) ds ] / [ integral_0^A e^{-rs} E(s) ds ].`

This is the sharpest reduction produced on the all-Hurwitz contextual branch: the rate-shape problem is a ratio of two Laplace transforms of explicit nonnegative geometric chord energies of a two-parameter realizable profile.

### Exact derivative criterion

Let

`L_D(r)=integral_0^A e^{-rs}D(s)ds`,

`L_E(r)=integral_0^A e^{-rs}E(s)ds`,

and let `mu_D(r),mu_E(r)` be the means of `s` under the corresponding normalized tilted densities. Then

`F'(r)/F(r) = 1/r - mu_D(r) + mu_E(r)`.

Equivalently, with

`chi(r)=r[mu_D(r)-mu_E(r)]`,

one has

`sign F'(r)=sign(1-chi(r))`.

Moreover

`mu_D'(r)=-Var_D(r)`, `mu_E'(r)=-Var_E(r)`,

so

`chi'(r)=mu_D-mu_E + r(Var_E-Var_D)`.

The desired all-Hurwitz one-band theorem in this co-rotating elliptic regime would follow from a one-crossing theorem for `chi(r)=1`; a sufficient route is monotonicity of `chi`, but the displayed variance identity shows exactly why monotonicity does not follow merely from positivity.

### Endpoint asymptotics

For nonconstant realizable `u`, put

`I = integral_0^A u_x(x)^2/[u(x)(1-u(x))] dx >0`.

Then as `s->0`,

`D(s) = (I/2)s^2 + O(s^4)`,

`E(s) = E_0 + (1/2)||u_x||_2^2 s^2 + O(s^4)`.

Consequently

`F(r) ~ I/(4E_0 r)` as `r->infinity`,

while

`F(r) ~ (r/4) [ integral_0^A D(s)ds ]/[ integral_0^A E(s)ds ]` as `r->0`.

Thus `F` rises linearly from zero and decays like `1/r`; at least one finite maximum exists. In terms of `chi`,

`chi(r)->0` as `r->0`,

`chi(r)->2` as `r->infinity`.

A genuine two-band level set in this regime must therefore be generated by non-monotone internal shape, equivalently by repeated crossings of `chi=1`.

The low-rate constants can also be written exactly:

`integral_0^A D(s)ds = A integral_0^A u(x)y(x)dx - (integral_0^A u)(integral_0^A y)`,

`integral_0^A E(s)ds = M(A-M)`.
