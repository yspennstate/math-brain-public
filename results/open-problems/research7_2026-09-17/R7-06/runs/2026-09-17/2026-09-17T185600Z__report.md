## 5. B25 — exact 6+2 coset decomposition into two honeycombs

Choose a triangular-lattice basis `e1,e2` satisfying

`M := {m e1+n e2 : m == n (mod 3)}`.                               (5.1)

Equivalently, `M` is generated by

`b1=e1+e2`, `b2=-e1+2e2`.

`|b1|^2=|b2|^2=3`, `<b1,b2>=3/2`.

Hence `M` is itself a triangular lattice, rotated and scaled by `sqrt(3)` relative to `Lambda`. The determinant of the coordinate matrix of `b1,b2` in the `e1,e2` basis is `3`, so

`Lambda\3Lambda = (Lambda\M) disjoint_union (M\3Lambda)`.          (5.2)
