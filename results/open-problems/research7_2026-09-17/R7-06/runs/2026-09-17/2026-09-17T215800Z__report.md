## 3. Exact full-milestone blocker

The current obstruction should be stated at the level of the full nine-point milestone, not at the level of another rigid-pair slice.

Let

`Lambda=A2`, `Lambda'=(1/3)Lambda`, `G=Lambda'/Lambda`, `|G|=9`,

and let `g_1,...,g_9` be the subgroup configuration `G`. For the periodized Gaussian

`V_a(x)=sum_{lambda in Lambda} exp(-a|x+lambda|^2)`,

let `phi_a` be a feature map with

`<phi_a(x),phi_a(y)>=V_a(x-y)`.

For an arbitrary labelled competing configuration `X={x_1,...,x_9}`, put

`u_i := phi_a(x_i)-phi_a(g_i)`.

The recovered exact identity is

`boxed{
2[U_a(X)-U_a(G)]
 = ||sum_{i=1}^9 u_i||^2
   -2 sum_{i=1}^9 D_{Lambda',a}(x_i-g_i),
}`                                                            (3.1)

where

`D_{Gamma,a}(z)=F_{a,Gamma}(0)-F_{a,Gamma}(z)`.

Because every `g_i` lies in `Lambda'`, the second term is actually independent of which subgroup labels are assigned:

`D_{Lambda',a}(x_i-g_i)=D_{Lambda',a}(x_i)` modulo `Lambda'`.

Thus the nine-point Gaussian milestone is exactly the assertion

`boxed{
||sum_{i=1}^9 u_i||^2
 >= 2 sum_{i=1}^9 D_{Lambda',a}(x_i)
\qquad\text{for every }a>0\text{ and every nine-point }X.

`boxed{L_a(X)>=C_a(X).}`                                         (3.4)
