## 6. Method B28 — TYPICAL versus exceptional half-completion

\[

\tag{9}

\[
PC+CD=0,
\tag{10}

\[
C^TC+D^2=2rI.
\tag{11}

Let

\[
K_P=2rI-P^2.

Every exact completion obeys

\[
\boxed{CC^T=K_P}.
\tag{12}

\[
\boxed{\det K_P=(\det C)^2}
\tag{13}

\[
\boxed{D=-C^{-1}PC}.
\tag{14}

Therefore `D` is similar to `-P`, so `Spec(D)=-Spec(P)` and

\[
\boxed{\operatorname{tr}H=0}.
\tag{15}
