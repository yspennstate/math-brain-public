## B27 — swap the quantifiers: quotient a fixed spectral fiber all the way to colored two-graphs

For off-diagonal signs `s_ij=M_ij` define

\[
\tau_{ijk}=s_{ij}s_{jk}s_{ki}.
\]

Diagonal sign conjugation leaves all diagonal entries `d_i=M_ii` and all `tau_ijk` invariant.

I proved the converse exactly: two labeled symmetric sign matrices with the same diagonal vector and the same triangle products are diagonally switching-equivalent. Fix vertex 1, set `epsilon_1=1` and

\[
\epsilon_i=N_{1i}/M_{1i}.
\]

Equality of the triangle products on `{1,i,j}` then gives

\[
N_{ij}=\epsilon_i\epsilon_jM_{ij}.
\]

Thus the diagonal colors together with the set of negative triples form a complete switching invariant. The underlying equivalence between a Seidel switching class and a two-graph is classical; I am not claiming that mechanism as new.

Let `K_n(lambda)` be the number of isomorphism classes of these **colored two-graphs** in the exact spectrum-`lambda` fiber. Equivalently, it is the number of signed-permutation conjugacy classes in that fiber.

Permutation orbits in the switching quotient have size between `1` and `n!`, hence

\[
\boxed{
K_n(\lambda)\le F_n(\lambda)\le n!K_n(\lambda).
}
\]

Together with the already-proved `|S_n|=exp(O(n))`, this yields the exact source-equivalent formulation

\[
\boxed{
\text{Vu 9.4}
\iff
\max_{\lambda\in S_n}K_n(\lambda)=2^{o(n^2)}.
}
\]

This is stronger localization than the previous run: the only possible quadratic entropy is now the number of genuinely non-isomorphic colored two-graphs sharing one integral spectrum. Labeling and diagonal switching account for only `o(n^2)` bits.

**B27 outcome:** a lossless source-equivalent quotient theorem; source not closed.

## B18 — move to signed-graph/star-complement language, then identify the exact failure of simple-spectrum machinery

The earlier mod-2 theorem forces all integral eigenvalues even for even `n`, and exactly one odd eigenvalue for odd `n`.

This gives a new exact obstruction.

### Proposition

An integral-spectrum symmetric sign matrix cannot have simple spectrum for even `n>=4` or odd `n>=7`. In particular, every F0011 target matrix with `n>=6` has characteristic discriminant zero.

For `n=2m`, `2m` distinct even integers have square sum at least

\[
8\sum_{k=1}^{m-1}k^2+4m^2
=
\frac{8m^3+4m}{3}.
\]

But `tr(M^2)=n^2=4m^2`; comparison forces `(2m-1)(m-1)<=0`, hence `m<=1`.

For `n=2m+1`, simple integral spectrum would require `2m` distinct even integers plus one odd integer. Its square sum is at least

\[
\frac{8m^3+4m}{3}+1.
\]

Comparing with `(2m+1)^2` forces `(2m+1)(m-2)<=0`, hence `m<=2`.

This matters because Alexander Van Werde's August 2026 cospectral-mate framework uses discriminant/Krylov methods in the nonzero-discriminant/simple-spectrum regime. F0011 asymptotically lies exactly on the complementary zero-discriminant boundary, so that newest machinery cannot simply be imported as a black box.

I also quantified what a one-eigenvalue star-complement reconstruction can save. If eigenvalue `theta` has multiplicity `m`, choose a principal star complement `C` of order `n-m` with `theta` absent and write

\[
M=\begin{pmatrix}A&B\\B^T&C\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Schur complementation forces

\[
A-\theta I=B(C-\theta I)^{-1}B^T.
\]

Thus `B,C` determine `A`. But the spectral-square bound guarantees only `m=Omega(n^{1/3})` in general. Encoding arbitrary signs in `B,C` then uses

\[
\frac{n(n+1)-m(m+1)}2
\]

bits: only an `Omega(n^{2/3})` saving, far short of the quadratic saving required. A successful star-complement route must couple many eigenvalues or exploit the colored-two-graph quotient globally.

**B18 outcome:** exact repeated-spectrum/zero-discriminant theorem plus a quantitative explanation of why a naive one-eigenvalue star-complement count cannot close Vu 9.4.

### B24 boundary family: symmetric square-order Hadamards

A symmetric Hadamard matrix `H` of square order `n=q^2` satisfies

\[
H^2=nI=q^2I,
\]

so its eigenvalues are `+q` and `-q`: it is automatically an F0011 success.

Therefore Vu 9.4 would imply that labeled symmetric Hadamard matrices of square order number only `2^{o(n^2)}` along those orders. Their spectrum has only `n+1` possible multiplicity patterns, so a quadratic-entropy symmetric-Hadamard family would already create a quadratic-entropy **single fixed spectral fiber**.

This is a useful boundary obstruction. Ferber–Jain–Zhao proved the first nontrivial general Hadamard-count upper bound and explicitly conjecture `H(n)=2^{O(n log n)}`; even `2^{o(n^2)}` for all Hadamards is beyond their theorem. The symmetric square-order subclass is narrower, so I do not equate the two problems. A fresh bounded check found Valtonen's September 2026 symmetric-Hadamard classification through order 32 but no asymptotic subquadratic count for this subclass.

**B24 outcome:** exact small-case support for the quotient route and a structured family showing why a uniform fixed-fiber theorem is genuinely deep.
