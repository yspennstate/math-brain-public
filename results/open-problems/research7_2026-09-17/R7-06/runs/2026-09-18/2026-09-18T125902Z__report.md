## 5. B5 — add a uniform mean penalty and differentiate

For deterministic scenery `z`, introduce the penalized recursion

\[
u_0^{(r)}=0,
\qquad
u_{k+1}^{(r)}=(z-r+Pu_k^{(r)})_+,
\qquad r\ge0.
\tag{5.1}
\]

Its Bellman form is

\[
\boxed{
 u_n^{(r)}(x)

 \sum_{j=0}^{\tau-1}(z(X_j)-r)
 \right].
}
\tag{5.2}
\]

Thus `r -> u_n^(r)(x)` is decreasing and convex. Resolve Bellman ties by stopping. The resulting optimal stopping time is

\[
\tau_n^{(r)}
=
\inf\{0\le j\le n:
 j=n\text{ or }F_{n-1-j}^{(r)}(X_j)\le0\},
\tag{5.3}
\]

and an induction proves that it minimizes expected duration among all optimal rules.

Let `K_{n,r}` be the dynamically killed Green kernel from the preceding run and let

\[
L_n^{(r)}(x)=\sum_yK_{n,r}(x,y).
\]

The kernel survives exactly until `tau_n^(r)`, so

\[
\boxed{
L_n^{(r)}(x)=\mathbb E_x\tau_n^{(r)}.
}
\tag{5.4}
\]

The key derivative identity is

\[
\boxed{
-\partial_{r+}u_n^{(r)}(x)

\tag{5.5}

\[
\boxed{
 u_j^{z-\eta}=u_j^z-G_j\eta
\quad(0\le j\le n),
}
\tag{7.3}
\]

\[
\boxed{
F_j^z-F_j^{z-\eta}=G_{j+1}\eta
\quad(0\le j<n).
}
\tag{7.4}
\]

The proof is a one-line induction after the key complementarity identity

\[
(a-b)_+=a_+-b
\quad\text{whenever }0\le b\le a_+.

\[
F_j^{z-\eta}=F_j^z-G_{j+1}\eta,
\]

and (7.2) says exactly that the subtraction stays below `(F_j^z)_+=u_{j+1}^z`, allowing the positive part to pass through linearly.

Thus the cone

\[
\mathcal C_n(z)

\{\eta\ge0:G_j\eta\le u_j^z\text{ for all }j\le n\}
\tag{7.5}
