## 5. Method B6 — deform from the solved noncritical regimes

For a general stretched-exponential exponent `gamma_0>1`, let

\[
q(\gamma_0)=\frac{\gamma_0}{\gamma_0-1}.
\]

The radial Green-power sum has exponent

\[
\beta(\gamma_0)
=d-(d-2)q(\gamma_0)
=
\frac{2\gamma_0-d}{\gamma_0-1}.
\]

Thus

\[
\sum_{1\le |y|\le R}G(0,y)^{q(\gamma_0)}
\asymp
\sum_{r\le R}r^{-1+\beta(\gamma_0)}.
\]

For `gamma_0<d/2`, `beta<0` and the sum is bounded; for `gamma_0>d/2`, `beta>0` and the divergence is power-law. At the endpoint,

\[
\beta(d/2)=0,
\]

and

\[
\lim_{\beta\to0}\frac{R^\beta-1}{\beta}=\log R.
\]

So the endpoint logarithm is exactly the continuous limit of the source's noncritical Green-power mechanism. It is not an artifact of splitting the proof into separate cases.

## 6. Method B24 — identify and solve the extremal negative profile

For positive weights `a_i`, `gamma>1`, and conjugate `q=gamma/(gamma-1)`, Holder gives

\[
\sum_i a_i t_i\le
\left(\sum_i a_i^q\right)^{1/q}
\left(\sum_i t_i^\gamma\right)^{1/\gamma}.
\]

Therefore the minimum Weibull cost subject to a prescribed weighted deficit

\[
\sum_i a_i t_i\ge s
\]

is exactly

\[
\boxed{
\inf \sum_i t_i^\gamma
=
\frac{s^\gamma}{(\sum_i a_i^q)^{\gamma-1}},
}
\tag{6.1}
\]

with optimizer

\[
t_i=
\frac{s a_i^{q-1}}{\sum_j a_j^q}.
\tag{6.2}
\]

At `gamma=d/2`, `(q-1)gamma=q`, which makes the probabilistic cost of the optimal profile exactly match the critical Green `q`-energy.

**B24 outcome:** the extremal object is explicit; the optimal fluctuation is not a single deep site but a multiscale profile proportional to `g_m^(q-1)`.

## 7. Method B30 — localize into shells and prove the lower bound

Choose

\[
R=\left\lfloor
\eta\sqrt{\frac{s}{\log(e+s)}}
\right\rfloor
\]

with fixed small `eta`, and take the annulus

\[
A=\{y:r_0\le|y|\le R\}.
\]

The condition `m>=K s/log(e+s)` guarantees, after fixing `K`, that `A` lies inside the diffusive region where

\[
c|y|^{2-d}\le g_m(0,y)\le C|y|^{2-d}.
\]

Set `a_y=g_m(0,y)` and

\[
Q_A=\sum_{y\in A}a_y^q.
\]

Because `q(d-2)=d`,

\[
\boxed{Q_A\asymp\log R\asymp\log s.}
\tag{7.1}
\]

Define the negative thresholds

\[
t_y=\frac{2s a_y^{q-1}}{Q_A}.
\]

Then

\[
\sum_{y\in A}a_y t_y=2s.
\]

The outermost threshold obeys

\[
t_y\ge c\frac{s}{R^2\log R}\ge c'/\eta^2,
\]

so choosing `eta` sufficiently small puts every `t_y` inside the assumed regular lower-tail regime. Independence gives

\[
\mathbb P(\zeta(y)\le-t_y\text{ for all }y\in A)
\ge
c_-^{|A|}\exp\left(-C\sum_{y\in A}t_y^\gamma\right).
\]

Now `(q-1)gamma=q`, so

\[
\sum_{y\in A}t_y^\gamma
=
\frac{(2s)^\gamma}{Q_A^{\gamma-1}}
\le C\Phi(s).
\]

Also

\[
|A|\le CR^d
\le C\frac{s^\gamma}{(\log(e+s))^\gamma}
\le C\Phi(s),
\]

so the fixed prefactor cost from `c_-^{|A|}` is lower order at the same exponential scale. Hence the forced annular deficit has probability at least `exp(-C Phi(s))` and contributes at most `-2s`.

The complementary sum

\[
T_A=\sum_{y\notin A}g_m(0,y)\zeta(y)
\]

is independent, mean zero, and has uniformly bounded variance because `sum_y G(0,y)^2<infinity` for `d>=5`. Therefore `P(T_A<=s)>=1/2` for all large `s`, and on the intersection the full Green average satisfies `S_m<=-s`. This proves the lower half of (4.1).

The shell mechanism is even clearer dyadically. On

\[
A_j=\{2^j\le|y|<2^{j+1}\},
\]

criticality gives

\[
\sum_{y\in A_j}g_m(0,y)^q\asymp1.
\]

There are `Theta(log s)` active shells before the optimizer's threshold falls below the regular-tail scale; each shell pays approximately `(s/J)^gamma`, so the total cost is

\[
J(s/J)^\gamma
=
\frac{s^\gamma}{J^{\gamma-1}}
\asymp\Phi(s).
\]

**B30 outcome:** the logarithm is a real gluing effect across `Theta(log s)` spatial scales.
