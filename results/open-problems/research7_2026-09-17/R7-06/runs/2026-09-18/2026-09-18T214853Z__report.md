## 4. Method B5 — ratio parameter removes the self-consistent unknown globally

Put

\[
\beta=\alpha/2,\qquad r=|z|^2,\qquad Y(r)=y_*(r)^2,
\]

and let `S,S'` be independent positive beta-stable variables normalized by

\[
E e^{-tS}=e^{-\Gamma(1-\beta)t^\beta}.
\]

The source scalar equation is

\[
1=E(r/S+Y(r)S')^{-\beta}.
\tag{4.1}
\]

The key new parameter is

\[
a=r/Y(r).
\]

Define

\[
F(a)=E(S'+a/S)^{-\beta}.
\]

Then (4.1) factors, with no approximation, into

\[
1=Y^{-\beta}F(a),
\]

so

\[
\boxed{Y(a)=F(a)^{1/\beta}},\qquad
\boxed{r(a)=aF(a)^{1/\beta}}.
\tag{4.2}
\]

This is the main structural movement of the run: the fixed point `Y(r)` has disappeared from the right-hand side.

For every integer `n>=0`, the stable negative moments give

\[
\boxed{
F^{(n)}(0)=(-1)^n(\beta)_n m_n m_{\beta+n}},
\qquad
m_q=\frac{\Gamma(1+q/\beta)}
{\Gamma(1+q)\Gamma(1-\beta)^{q/\beta}}.
\tag{4.3}
\]

Moreover

\[
r'(a)=F(a)^{1/\beta}
\left(1+\frac{aF'(a)}{\beta F(a)}\right)>0,
\tag{4.4}
\]

because pointwise

\[
0<\frac{a/S}{S'+a/S}<1.
\]

The beta-stable tail also gives `F(a)>=C a^{-beta} log a` for large `a`, hence `r(a)->infinity`.  Thus `a -> r(a)` is a global increasing smooth bijection of `[0,infinity)`.

With `T=SS'`, define

\[
K(a)=E(a+T)^{-1},\qquad
J(a)=E\frac{T}{(a+T)^2}=(aK(a))'.
\]

Substitution into the BCC density identity yields

\[
\boxed{
\pi\widetilde g_\alpha(r(a))=\frac{J(a)}{r'(a)}.
}
\tag{4.5}
\]

If `M(r)=mu_alpha{|z|^2<=r}`, then

\[
\boxed{M(r(a))=aK(a).}
\tag{4.6}
\]

Equations (4.2), (4.5), and (4.6) already give the complete law as a monotone one-parameter curve with no recursive or self-consistent unknown.

## 5. Method B18 — Mellin language makes F and K explicit contour transforms

For `0<Re(s)<beta`, Beta integration and the stable fractional moments give

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal M F(s)
=\Gamma(1-\beta)^{-1+2s/\beta}
\frac{\Gamma(s)\Gamma(1-s/\beta)^2}
{\Gamma(1+\beta)\Gamma(1-s)}.
}
\tag{5.1}
\]

Hence `F` is the Mellin-Barnes inverse of the right-hand side on any vertical line in that strip.

For `0<Re(s)<1`, the product variable `T=SS'` gives

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal M K(s)
=
\frac{\Gamma(s)\Gamma((1-s)/\beta)^2}
{\beta^2\Gamma(1-s)\Gamma(1-\beta)^{2(1-s)/\beta}}.
}
\tag{5.2}
\]

Again this has a standard Mellin-Barnes inversion.  The Gamma-factor pattern is of generalized Wright/Fox-H type.  Thus the full radial parameterization can be written with explicit one-dimensional contour transforms rather than an unknown fixed point.

At `alpha=1` (`beta=1/2`) the parameter `F` collapses further to ordinary named special functions.  With `z=pi/(2sqrt(a))`,

\[
\boxed{
F(a)=\frac\pi{2\sqrt a}
\left[\mathbf H_0(z)-Y_0(z)\right],
}
\tag{5.3}
\]

where `mathbf H_0` is the Struve function and `Y_0` the Bessel function of the second kind.  Therefore

\[
\boxed{
r(a)=\left\{\frac\pi2[\mathbf H_0(z)-Y_0(z)]\right\}^2.
}
\tag{5.4}
\]

So the special `alpha=1` finite-radius fixed point is genuinely eliminated in standard special functions.

\[
\boxed{
