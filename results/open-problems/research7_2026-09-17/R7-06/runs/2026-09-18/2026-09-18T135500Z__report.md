## 3. Exact blocking formula

The previous three ordinary runs reduce the slowly varying endpoint question to one coupled obstacle event.

For deterministic scenery `z`, let

\[
u_0^z=0,\qquad u_{j+1}^z=(z+Pu_j^z)_+,
\]

and let

\[
G_j\eta=\sum_{i=0}^{j-1}P^i\eta.
\]

For a critical Green-shaped negative profile `eta_R`, the exact transparency theorem from the preceding run says that **reflection loses nothing** whenever

\[
\boxed{
G_j\eta_R\le u_j^\zeta
\qquad\text{pointwise for every source-relevant }1\le j\le n.
}
\tag{3.1}
\]

On (3.1),

\[
u_j^{\zeta-\eta_R}=u_j^\zeta-G_j\eta_R
\]

and the finite-amplitude response is the full raw Green response. The critical Green optimizer then has Weibull cost

\[
\asymp
\frac{h^{d/2}}{(\log h)^{d/2-1}}.
\]

Conversely, the exact killed-kernel sensitivity formula gives

\[
F_n^\zeta(0)-F_n^{\zeta-\eta}(0)
=
\int_0^1\sum_y\widetilde K_{n,\zeta-s\eta}(0,y)\eta(y)\,ds,
\tag{3.2}
\]

and its critical `q=d/(d-2)` energy is controlled by the averaged effective lifetime. If reflection typically forces that lifetime to `O(1)` along source-relevant critical deformations, then the critical logarithmic gain disappears and one expects pure `h^{d/2}` cost.

Therefore the precise unresolved dichotomy is:

\[
\boxed{
\text{At the critical tilted profile, what is the source-probability cost of the event }
\{G_j\eta_R\le u_j^\zeta\ \forall j\le n\}?
}
\tag{3.3}
\]

A proof of a pure `(log t)^{2/d}` order must show that source-relevant near-optimal profiles are clipped strongly enough, at the correct exponential scale, before they can remain inside the transparency cone. A proof that the current `log log` correction is genuine must instead exhibit source-relevant near-optimizers that remain in or sufficiently close to the cone at cost

\[
\exp\left[-O\left(
\frac{h^{d/2}}{(\log h)^{d/2-1}}
\right)\right].
\]

That is the exact blocker. No earlier route decides (3.3).
