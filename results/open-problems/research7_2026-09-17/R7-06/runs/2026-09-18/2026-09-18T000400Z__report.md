## 5. B5 — the Gaussian parameter is exactly a heat-flow time

Let the physical periodized Gaussian be

`F_a^phys(u)=sum_{v in A2} exp(-a |u+v|^2)`.

In dimension two one summand satisfies

`Delta_u exp(-a|u+v|^2)=(-4a+4a^2|u+v|^2)exp(-a|u+v|^2)`.

Because

`partial_a exp(-a|u+v|^2)=-|u+v|^2 exp(-a|u+v|^2)`,

`boxed{ partial_a F_a^phys = -(1/(4a^2)) Delta_u F_a^phys - F_a^phys/a. }`    (5.1)

Under the Hardin–Tenpas change of variables

`x=cos(2 pi u_1)`, `y=cos(2 pi u_2/sqrt(3))`,

`Delta_u = 4 pi^2 L`,

where

`L=(1-x^2)partial_xx-x partial_x`

`  +(1/3)[(1-y^2)partial_yy-y partial_y]`.

Hence the transformed Gaussian satisfies

`partial_a F_a = -(pi^2/a^2) L F_a - F_a/a`.             (5.2)

Normalize by

`tau=pi^2/a`, `U(tau,x,y)=a F_a(x,y)`.

The scalar term cancels exactly and (5.2) becomes

`boxed{ partial_tau U = L U. }`                           (5.3)

Multiplication by the positive scalar `a` does not change minorant feasibility, so the entire all-Gaussian problem may be parameterized by this exact forward heat flow.
