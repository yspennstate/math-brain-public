## 5. B11 — dualize: Herglotz spectral obstruction

### Theorem 5.1

For every fixed `p`, the sequence `tau_|n|(p)` is positive definite on `Z`.  Hence there is a symmetric probability measure `mu_p` on the unit circle such that

`tau_n(p) = integral cos(n theta) d mu_p(theta)`.

### Proof

For a fixed percolation configuration and axial vertices `v_i=i e_1`, define

`K(i,j)=1_{v_i <-> v_j}`.

Connectivity partitions the selected vertices into open clusters, so for every finitely supported scalar family `(c_i)`,

`sum_{i,j} c_i conjugate(c_j) K(i,j) = sum_C |sum_{i:v_i in C} c_i|^2 >= 0`.

Thus every sample kernel is PSD.  Expectation preserves PSD, and translation invariance makes the expected kernel Toeplitz with coefficient `tau_|i-j|`.  Herglotz gives the measure; `tau_0=1` gives unit mass and real/even coefficients give symmetry.  QED.

Therefore

`tau_1-tau_2 = integral (cos theta-cos 2theta) dmu`

`= integral 2 sin(3theta/2) sin(theta/2) dmu`.

The integrand is nonnegative on `|theta|<=2pi/3` and negative beyond that arc.  Thus a first-step counterexample requires high-frequency spectral mass to dominate exactly:

`integral_{|theta|>2pi/3}(cos 2theta-cos theta)dmu > integral_{|theta|<2pi/3}(cos theta-cos 2theta)dmu`.

This spectral condition is necessary, but PSD alone is insufficient.  For any `0<r<=1/2`,

`mu=(1-r)delta_0+r delta_pi`

has all Fourier coefficients nonnegative, yet `hat mu(2)=1 > 1-2r=hat mu(1)`.  So any successful dual proof must inject planar/percolative structure beyond positive definiteness.
