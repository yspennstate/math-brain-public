## 4. Exact blocking formula

The simplest unresolved source-valid milestone is already the first comparison

\[
\tau_1(p)\ge \tau_2(p)\qquad\text{for every }0<p<1.
\tag{4.1}
\]

Let `x=0`, `y=e_1`, `z=2e_1`, and delete `xy,yz`, obtaining the slit graph `H_p` with all remaining bonds at parameter `p`. Write

- `a_p = P_{H_p}(xy|z)`,
- `b_p = P_{H_p}(xz|y)`,
- `s_p = P_{H_p}(x|y|z)`,
- `q=1-p`.

The first ordinary run proved the exact identity

\[
\boxed{
\tau_1(p)-\tau_2(p)
=q\,F(p),
\qquad
F(p)=a_p-qb_p+ps_p.
}
\tag{4.2}
\]

Equivalently, with

\[
\Delta_{H_p}=a_p-b_p,
\qquad
D_{H_p}=b_p+s_p,
\]

\[
\boxed{F(p)=\Delta_{H_p}+pD_{H_p}.}
\tag{4.3}
\]

Thus **`M_axis>=1` is equivalent to `F(p)>=0` for every `p in (0,1)`**.

The punctured-star reduction gives a more diagnostic form. Delete all four edges incident to `y`; let the six `D_4` orbit masses of the noncrossing partition of the four neighbours be

\[
\theta_p=(\sigma_p,\alpha_p,\beta_p,\gamma_p,\delta_p,\epsilon_p).
\]

Then

\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
F(p)={}&p\sigma_p
+2p(3-2p)\alpha_p
+(2p-1)\beta_p\\
&+2p(1-p)(2-p)\gamma_p\\
&+(2p^3-7p^2+8p-2)\delta_p
-(1-p)^3\epsilon_p.
\end{aligned}}
\tag{4.4}
\]

This is the exact present blocker. The coefficient of `epsilon_p` is always negative away from `p=1`, while the coefficient of `beta_p` is negative for `p<1/2`; the `delta_p` coefficient changes sign at the unique real root in `(0,1)`, approximately

\[
p_\delta=0.342701893861624\ldots .
\]

At `p=1/2`, the previous run chain obtained an additional self-dual linear relation among the six orbit masses and FKG then proves the strict certificate

\[
\tau_1(1/2)-\tau_2(1/2)\ge {13\over4096}>0.
\tag{4.5}
\]

Away from `p=1/2`, planar duality no longer closes on the same law: it sends the primal `p` orbit law to a dual `(1-p)` orbit law. That is the precise point where the critical proof stops gluing.
