### B2 — attack the negation: low-boundary clusters are already exponentially negligible

For a finite connected site animal `A` containing the origin, write `k=|A|`, `m=|partial A|`. Exactly

`P_p(C(0)=A)=p^k(1-p)^m`.

For `0<p<q<1`, define

`r_*(p,q)=log(q/p)/log((1-p)/(1-q))`.

If `r<r_*(p,q)` and

`kappa=log(q/p)-r log((1-p)/(1-q))>0`, then summing the exact likelihood ratio of the same cluster under `p` and `q` proves

`P_p(n<=|C(0)|<infinity, |partial C(0)|<=r|C(0)|) <= exp(-kappa n)`.

Moreover

`lim_{q downarrow p} r_*(p,q)=(1-p)/p`.

Hence at hypothetical critical percolation `theta(p_c)>0`, Cerf's Theorem 1.2 forces the non-stretched-exponential tail to be carried entirely by clusters with

`|partial C|/|C| > r`

for every fixed `r<(1-p_c)/p_c`, up to an exponentially small error. Compact/Wulff-like droplets are therefore **not** the remaining obstruction; the slow tail must be highly exposed/ramified.

### B14 — translation symmetry: exact mass-transport conversion to closed boundary witnesses

Let `N_n(y)` be the number of distinct finite open clusters of size at least `n` adjacent to a closed site `y` (`N_n(y)=0` for open `y`). A translation-covariant mass transport in which every cluster vertex sends `1/|C|` to every external boundary site gives the exact identity

`E_p[(|partial C(0)|/|C(0)|) 1_{n<=|C(0)|<infinity}]`
`=E_p[1_{0 closed}N_n(0)]`.

Combining with B2 yields

`E_p[1_{0 closed}N_n(0)] >= r(T_p(n)-e^{-kappa n})`.

Because `N_n(0)<=2d`, if

`Q_p(n)=P_p(0 closed and adjacent to at least one finite cluster of size >=n)`, then

`Q_p(n) >= [r/(2d)](T_p(n)-e^{-kappa n})`.

Thus any hypothetical slow critical volume tail creates a comparable density of **closed boundary witnesses**. The unresolved gluing problem is sharper now: prove that enough of these witnesses are gateways/pivotal toward the infinite cluster rather than remaining screened by further finite/closed layers.

### B16 — exact finite cases and finite local obstruction

A second exact reduction uses

`B_R={||x||_infinity<=R}`,

`H_R={no infinite open cluster intersects B_R}`,

and the finite event `U_R` that `B_{2R}` contains two distinct open components each touching `B_R` and `partial B_{2R}`. Since `|B_{2R}|=(4R+1)^d`,

`T_p((4R+1)^d+1) <= P_p(H_R)+P_p(U_R)`.

Also

`P_p(H_R)=lim_{L->infinity}P_p(B_R not connected to partial B_L)`

and

`P_p(U_R)<=|B_R|^2 a_2(R)`,

where `a_2(R)` is the worst finite two-component crossing probability for a pair of starting sites in `B_R`. Therefore stretched-exponential bounds on the hole term and the finite two-component term would close the source in that dimension; under `theta(p_c)>0` plus Cerf's theorem, at least one must fail every such bound along a sequence of scales.
