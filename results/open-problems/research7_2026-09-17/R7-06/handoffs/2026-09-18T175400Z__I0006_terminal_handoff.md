`E log(1+min{Y,R}) = (log 2) sum_{k=0}^{K-1} P(Y >= 2^k) + O(1)`

`chi_{n,w_R}(lambda) = kappa_5 (log 2) sum_{k=0}^{K-1} P(|X_tau| >= 2^k) + O(1)`.

`S_{n,R}(lambda) := K^(-1) sum_{k=0}^{K-1} P(|X_tau| >= 2^k)`.
