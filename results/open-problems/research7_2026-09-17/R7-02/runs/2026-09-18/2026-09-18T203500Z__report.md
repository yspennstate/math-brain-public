# Method B27 — SWAP THE QUANTIFIERS

The previous report front-loaded Bernoulliized deterministic successes and obtained first-cycle domination. This run proves a stronger **single-stack simultaneous coupling**.

For one deterministic positive stack set

\[
d_i=2p_i-1,
\qquad
A_i\sim\mathrm{Bernoulli}(d_i),
\qquad
C_i\sim\mathrm{Bernoulli}(1/2),
\]

independently, and define

\[
B_i=A_i+(1-A_i)C_i.
\]

Conditionally on the stack, the `B_i` are exactly the original independent cookie tosses because

\[
P(B_i=1\mid p)=d_i+(1-d_i)/2=p_i.
\]

Let

\[
N=\sum_i A_i.
\]

Move all deterministic-success marks to the front while keeping the fair `C_i` with `A_i=0` in their original order. Conditional on the marks, that retained subsequence is iid fair and independent of `N`. If `G_r` is the number of failures before the `r`-th success in this retained fair sequence, then on this **one** realization

\[
\boxed{
F_m^{\rm orig}\ge G_{(m-N)_+}
\quad\text{for every }m\ge1
}
\tag{2}
\]

simultaneously. The proof is by adjacent transpositions: moving one deterministic success left can only increase the cumulative-success count of every prefix, hence can only move every `m`-th success weakly left and decrease its preceding failure count.

Applying (2) independently at every generation and using monotonicity of `r\mapsto G_n(r)` gives a global induction. If

\[
V_{n+1}=F^{\rm orig,n}_{V_n+1}
\]

is the original Bernoulliized backward chain and

\[
Z_{n+1}=G_n((Z_n+1-N_n)_+)
\]

is the front-loaded scalar chain, both started at zero, then

\[
\boxed{V_n\ge Z_n\quad\forall n\ge0}
\tag{3}
\]

pathwise. Therefore, for first positive return times and first-cycle total populations,

\[
\boxed{
\sigma_V\ge\sigma_Z,
\qquad
A_V\ge A_Z.
}
\tag{4}
\]

This is stronger than an annealed comparison at a fixed success level and removes all need to construct an invariant law for the original infinite-stack chain.

**B27 outcome: successful.** The quantifiers are now in the strongest useful order: one stackwise coupling controls all success levels, and the generation induction is global rather than only asserted distributionally.
