# Method B18 — translate speed exactly into a one-cycle total-progeny problem

Define the first positive return to zero and total progeny of the backward chain by

\[
\sigma^V:=\inf\{j\ge1:V_j=0\},
\qquad
A^V:=\sum_{j=0}^{\sigma^V-1}V_j.
\tag{3}
\]

For a right-transient iid environment, the source's backward-jump bookkeeping gives

\[
\sigma^V\stackrel d=X_{\tau_2}-X_{\tau_1}.
\tag{4}
\]

The second identity can be derived directly without any depth assumption. During a nearest-neighbor regeneration block, if the net displacement is `r` and the number of left jumps is `L`, then every left jump must be compensated by one additional right jump, so the elapsed time is exactly

\[
r+2L.
\]

The backward process records exactly these left jumps site-by-site; their total is `A^V`. Hence

\[
A^V\stackrel d=
\frac{\tau_2-\tau_1-(X_{\tau_2}-X_{\tau_1})}{2}
\tag{5}
\]

and therefore

\[
\tau_2-\tau_1\stackrel d=\sigma^V+2A^V.
\tag{6}
\]

Lemma 4.5 gives `E sigma^V<infinity`. Substituting (4)-(6) into Theorem 4.6 yields the exact source-valid criterion

\[
\boxed{
 v=\frac{E\sigma^V}{E\sigma^V+2EA^V}
}
\tag{7}
\]

with an infinite denominator allowed. Consequently, throughout the right-transient positive-cookie source branch,

\[
\boxed{v=0\iff EA^V=\infty.}
\tag{8}
\]

This translation is stronger proof engineering than the previous stationary-law bridge: to prove zero speed in the original unbounded environment it is enough to force divergence of **one killed life-cycle reward**.

**B18 outcome: successful.** It removes the need to establish or compare invariant distributions for the original un-front-loaded infinite-stack chain.

## Bernoulliization and pathwise life-cycle domination

For completeness, the cumulative Bernoulliization is stated at the exact hypotheses used now. Write

\[
d_i:=2p_i-1\in[0,1]
\]

for the drift of the `i`-th cookie in a random positive stack. Conditional on that stack, mark cookie `i` independently with

\[
A_i\sim\operatorname{Bernoulli}(d_i).
\]

A marked cookie is replaced by a deterministic success and an unmarked cookie by a fair coin. Its conditional success probability is

\[
d_i+(1-d_i)/2=p_i,
\]

so the auxiliary independent coin sequence has exactly the original conditional law. Put

\[
N:=\sum_{i\ge1}A_i.
\tag{9}
\]

At finite drift, Tonelli gives `EN=delta`, hence `N<infinity` almost surely. The left `(WEL)` event has positive probability and has `p_i<1` for every `i`; on this event all `d_i<1`, while `sum_i d_i<infinity` almost surely. Therefore

\[
\prod_i(1-d_i)>0,
\]

the conditional probability that all marks vanish. Hence

\[
\boxed{P(N=0)>0.}
\tag{10}
\]

Now move every deterministic-success mark to the front while preserving the order and values of all fair outcomes. For every required success count `m`, this can only reduce the number of failures before the `m`-th success:

\[
F_m^{front}\le F_m^{orig}
\quad\text{simultaneously for all }m.
\tag{11}
\]

After front-loading, the backward chain becomes the scalar random-emigration chain

\[
Z_{n+1}=
\sum_{j=1}^{(Z_n+1-N_n)_+}\xi_{n,j},
\tag{12}
\]

where `N_n` are iid copies of `N` and the `xi` are iid geometric random variables on the nonnegative integers with `P(xi=k)=2^{-(k+1)}` and mean one.

Using the same site randomness, (11) and monotonicity of `m -> F_m` inductively give

\[
V_n^{orig}\ge Z_n^{front}
\]

for every generation before the front-loaded chain returns to zero. Consequently their first life cycles satisfy pathwise

\[
\boxed{
\sigma^{orig}\ge\sigma^{front},
\qquad
A^{orig}\ge A^{front}.
}
\tag{13}
\]

This is the central bridge repair of the current run. It bypasses all stationary-law questions for the original infinite-stack backward chain.
