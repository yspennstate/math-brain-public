### B7 — take the established limit

Set `X_n=W_3(n)` and

\[

Because

\[
p_3=\frac{X_n}{t_n}r_n,

conditional Borel–Cantelli first gives `X_n->infinity`. For `L_n=log X_n`,

\[
\mathbb E[\Delta L_n\mid\mathcal F_n]

\sim\frac1{t_n}.
\]

The martingale error has predictable quadratic variation `O(log n)` and bounded increments, so Freedman plus Borel–Cantelli gives an `o(log n)` error. Therefore

\[
\boxed{\frac{\log W_3(n)}{\log n}\to1.}
\]

This already proves that the normalized bridge cannot decay at any fixed polynomial rate.

### B18 — translate quadratic normalized drift to reciprocal coordinates

Define

\[
Y_n:=\frac{t_n}{X_n}=\frac1{w_3(n)}.
\]

If the next ant does not reinforce edge 3,

\[
\Delta Y_n=\frac1{X_n};
\]

if it does,

\[
\Delta Y_n=-\frac{t_n-X_n}{X_n(X_n+1)}.
\]

The exact conditional drift simplifies to

\[
\boxed{
 b_n:=\mathbb E[\Delta Y_n\mid\mathcal F_n]
 =\frac{1-d_n^2/w_3}{t_nQ_n}
 +\frac{r_n(1-w_3)}{X_n(X_n+1)}.
}
\]

The first term is `(1+o(1))/(q t_n)`. The second term is summable because the B7 exponent-one result implies, for example, `X_n>=n^{3/4}` eventually. Hence

\[
\sum_{k<n}b_k=\frac1q\log n+o(\log n).
\]

### B3 — prove the exact stronger asymptotic

\[

\[
\mathbb E[(\Delta Y_n)^2\mid\mathcal F_n]
\le\frac1{X_n^2}
