## 2. Important correction: the old twelve-variable restriction checker had the wrong mean normalization

Before building on the twelve-variable degree-five theorem, I re-audited its exact Newton/Hamming arithmetic. Let `b_k` be the number of `+1` values on Hamming layer `k` of an `N`-variable Boolean function, and write its degree-five symmetrized positive-set profile as

`b_k = sum_{j=0}^5 u_j C(N-j,k-j)`.

Then

`sum_k b_k = sum_j u_j 2^(N-j)`.

Since `mu=E f = 2^(1-N) sum_k b_k -1`, the correct identity is

`mu = 2u_0 + u_1 + u_2/2 + u_3/4 + u_4/8 + u_5/16 -1`,

or

`16 mu = 32u_0+16u_1+8u_2+4u_3+2u_4+u_5-16`.              (2.1)

The historical checker `p0169_degree5_12var_boundary_impossibility.py` instead used `sum_j u_j/2^j -1`, which is off by a factor of two in the positive-set contribution. The constant `+1` function is an immediate sanity test: `u=(1,0,...,0)` must have mean `+1`, but the old formula returns zero.

Therefore the old checker claim “zero necessary profiles at true `(mean,L)=(+/-3/16,33/16)`” is withdrawn as evidence. Corrected enumeration actually leaves **two** necessary profiles for each sign. I did not overwrite the historical artifact; the repair is immutable and explicit.

The signed singleton formula itself is correct:

`L = -u_1-u_2-(3/4)u_3-(1/2)u_4-(5/16)u_5`,              (2.2)

so

`16L=-16u_1-16u_2-12u_3-8u_4-5u_5`.
