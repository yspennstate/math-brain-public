## Exact outside source

Elena Kosygina and Martin P. W. Zerner, **“Excited random walks: results, methods, open problems”**, arXiv:1204.1895 (original version 9 Apr 2012); published in *Bulletin of the Institute of Mathematics, Academia Sinica (N.S.)* 8 (2013), 105–157. Primary author PDF: `https://www.math.uni-tuebingen.de/user/zerner/pub/KosyginaZerner.pdf`. Source locator: **Problem 5.3, printed p. 20** (PDF page 20 in the author copy), immediately after Theorem 5.2.

The source defines, for one-dimensional nearest-neighbour excited random walk, `(IID)` to mean that site cookie stacks are i.i.d.; `(WEL)` to mean weak ellipticity; `(POS1)` to mean every cookie has nonnegative drift to the right, i.e. `omega(0,1,i) >= 1/2` almost surely for every `i`; and `(BD)` to mean there is a deterministic finite `M` after which all cookies are placebo cookies. The drift parameter is

`delta = E[ sum_{i>=1} (2 omega(0,1,i)-1) ] in [0,+infinity]`

under `(POS1)`. The source's Theorem 4.1 gives a deterministic speed `v in [0,1]` under `(SE)+(POS1)`, hence in particular under `(IID)+(POS1)`. Theorem 5.2 proves under `(IID)+(BD)+(WEL)` that `v=0` for `delta in [-2,2]` and `v>0` for `delta>2` (with the symmetric negative-drift clause). Problem 5.3 asks verbatim in mathematical scope: **can `(BD)` in Theorem 5.2 be replaced by `(POS1)`?** Under `(POS1)` this becomes the complete criterion

1. if `delta <= 2`, prove `v=0`;
2. if `delta > 2` (including `delta=+infinity`), prove `v>0`.

These two clauses, with `(IID)+(WEL)+(POS1)` retained, are the full source closure criterion for this target.
