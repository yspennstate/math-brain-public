Statement:

> For every `epsilon > 0` there exist `d_0, gamma > 0` such that, if `G` has average degree `d >= d_0` and `p >= (1+epsilon)/d`, then `G_p` contains a cycle of length at least `gamma d` asymptotically almost surely as `|V(G)| -> infinity`.
