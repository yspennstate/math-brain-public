`S_j=supp(mu_j)` and `A_i=supp(rho_i^R)`.

`boxed: b in S_j, r in A_i  ==>  F_b(r) in S_i`,

`F_b(r)=(b+r)/(1+b+r)`.

### Proof

Take arbitrary open neighborhoods `U` of `b` and `V` of `r`. By support membership,

`mu_j(U)>0`, `rho_i^R(V)>0`.

Consequently the source parent law assigns positive measure to configurations for which the distinguished child conductance lies in `U` and the sibling sum lies in `V`. On those configurations the parent conductance is exactly
