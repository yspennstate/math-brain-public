## 5. Cumulative mathematical results worth preserving

### 5.1 Exact source refutation

For every `n>=1`, equation (2.1) holds. In even dimension `n=2m`, the signed-shear union has exactly

`N_{2m}=2^{2m^2}3^m`

matrices. In odd dimension `n=2m+1`, the rectangular-shear family has exactly

`N_{2m+1}=2^{2m^2+2m+1}3^m`.

### 5.2 Rational/Gaussian lattice flag reduction

Every integer matrix whose spectrum lies in `Z[i]` can be triangularized by a Gaussian-unimodular change of basis, hence preserves a complete primitive `Z[i]` lattice flag. For a fixed rational complete flag, the sign-hypercube intersection of its Borel algebra is at most `2^{n(n+1)/2}`. One explicit flag already contains `2^{n^2/2+Theta(n)}` sign matrices, so the fixed-flag exponent `1/2` is sharp.

### 5.4 Nilpotent-fiber recovery

For square-zero sign matrices of rank `r`,

`#{A in {+/-1}^{n x n}: A^2=0, rank A=r}
 <= binom(n,r)^2 2^{2r(n-r)}`,

which yields the sharp quadratic half-exponent upper bound for the full square-zero stratum.

For cube-zero matrices with `rank A=r` and `rank A^2=s`, the later rank-core argument removed a previous logarithmic loss and gave

`#{A: rank A=r, rank A^2=s}
 <= binom(n,r)^2 binom(r,s)^2 2^{2nr-2r^2+2rs-s^2}`.

Consequently the half exponent is established for all cube-zero families with `rank(A^2)=o(n)`. The remaining balanced linear-second-rank regime requires genuinely arithmetic sign-cube sparsity beyond algebraic dimension.

### 5.5 Finite-field splitting entropy

For a uniform matrix in `M_n(F_q)`, the probability its characteristic polynomial splits completely over `F_q` is, up to absolute constant factors uniform in `n,q`,

`q^{-n} binom(n+q-1,n)`.

This showed that generic complete splitting at one fixed prime carries only linear information, while large primes can carry `n log n` bits.

### 5.6 Archimedean single-prime reduction

For `E_n`, Schur/Frobenius gives every Gaussian-integer eigenvalue in

`D_n={z in Z[i]: |z|<=n}`,

so only `2^{O(n log n)}` admissible characteristic polynomials occur. For a split prime `p>4n^2`, reduction is injective on `D_n`.

Writing

`C_{n,p}=P(chi_{M_n}=chi_{M_n'} mod p)`, 

one obtains

`P(E_n) <= sqrt(B_n C_{n,p})`, `B_n=2^{O(n log n)}`.

Thus a single-prime collision estimate

`C_{n,p_n} <= 2^{-n^2+o(n^2)}`

would prove the corrected exponent `u_GI=1/2`.

### 5.7 Terminal B11 Fourier identity

For the characteristic coefficient vector `c(M) in F_p^n`, with

`Phi_xi=E e_p(<xi,c(M)>)`,

Parseval gives exactly

`C_{n,p}=p^{-n} sum_xi |Phi_xi|^2`.

Conditioned on all entries outside one row,

`<xi,c(M)>=b+sum_j a_j x_j`,

and the row conditional Fourier factor is exactly

`product_j |cos(2 pi a_j/p)|`.

This identifies the inverse-Littlewood--Offord interface, but no multistage theorem was found that accumulates the contraction across `Theta(n)` dependent rows.

### 5.8 Terminal Schur--Krylov reconstruction

For

`M=[[a,r^T],[c,B]]`,

`chi_M(t)=(t-a)chi_B(t)-r^T adj(tI-B)c`.

The linear map

`c -> r^T adj(tI-B)c`

has rank

`dim span{r,B^T r,...,(B^T)^{n-2}r}`.

Hence a cyclic `r` determines `c` uniquely **after** `B,r,a,chi_M` are given. This is an enriched-invariant theorem only; summing over the revealed `B,r,a` loses almost all entropy and does not control a bare characteristic-polynomial fiber.

### 5.9 Universal diagonal-switching fibers

For every sign matrix `M`, the similarity orbit

`{DMD: D=diag(+/-1)}`

`C_{n,p} >= 2^{-n^2+n-1}`.

This is subquadratic relative to the desired collision exponent and hence is compatible with an exponent-1 collision theorem after quotienting/tolerating trivial switching.

`|S_n|<=2^{(sigma+o(1))n^2}`

## 6. Exact final blocker

The terminal mathematical blocker is a **quadratic-rate cospectral-fiber theorem for sign matrices**, tailored to the `2^{o(n^2)}` Gaussian-integral admissible spectra.

Two equivalent useful forms are:

1. prove the growing-prime collision estimate `C_{n,p_n}<=2^{-n^2+o(n^2)}`; or
2. prove the structured-exception criterion above with `max(sigma,tau)<1` (movement) or `<=1/2` (sharp correction).

The one-row Fourier identity and Schur--Krylov injectivity do not presently close either form.

Importantly, Vu's same survey states Conjecture 11.2 (“Finger Print”), asking qualitatively that almost all sign matrices be determined by their spectrum up to trivial permutations. The F0012 corrected-exponent route requires a much stronger rare-tail quantitative version: a mere `1-o(1)` fingerprint theorem permits an exceptional set vastly larger than `2^{n^2/2+o(n^2)}`.
