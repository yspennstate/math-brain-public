## B5 — add the AP density parameter and solve the entropy optimization

For `Q>6`, coprime to `6`, let `S^{AP}_{Q,N}(A)` count surjective modular null rows taking values `-u,0,u`, for a unit `u`, with exactly `N` entries of each nonzero sign and `Z=n-2N` zeros. Divide by the free unit-scaling action:

\[
U^{AP}_{Q,N}(A):=S^{AP}_{Q,N}(A)/\varphi(Q).
\]

The preceding exact contingency theorem gives

\[
M_{n,N}:=\mathbb E_{\rm conf}U^{AP}_{Q,N}(A)
=\frac12\frac{n!}{N!^2Z!}\frac1{(3n-1)!!}
\sum_{x\in\mathcal X_{n,N}}T_{n,N,x},
\]

where

\[
T_{n,N,x}=
\binom Nx^2\binom Z{N+2x}6^{3N}(N+2x)!^2(N-x)!((N-x-1)!!)^2(D-1)!!,
\]

`D=3n-8N-4x`, and feasibility is `0<=x<=N`, `N+2x<=Z`, `N-x` even.

Put `alpha=N/n`, `xi=x/n`. Uniform Stirling expansion gives the exact exponential rate

\[
\begin{aligned}
R(\alpha,\xi)
={}&\alpha\log(\alpha+2\xi)-4\alpha\log(3-8\alpha-4\xi)
+3\alpha\log(1-3\alpha-2\xi)+3\alpha\log6\\
&-2\xi\log\xi+2\xi\log(\alpha+2\xi)
-2\xi\log(3-8\alpha-4\xi)+2\xi\log(1-3\alpha-2\xi)\\
&+\frac32\log(3-8\alpha-4\xi)-\log(1-3\alpha-2\xi)-\frac32\log3.
\end{aligned}
\]

The key derivative is

\[
\partial_\xi R
=2\log\frac{(\alpha+2\xi)(1-3\alpha-2\xi)}{\xi(3-8\alpha-4\xi)}.
\]

The numerator-minus-denominator inside the logarithm collapses to

\[
\alpha(1-3\alpha)-\xi.
\]

Hence the unique maximizer is

\[
\boxed{\xi_*(\alpha)=\alpha(1-3\alpha).}
\]

Substitution gives

\[
\boxed{
R_*(\alpha)=
\alpha\log(8\alpha)+(2-4\alpha)\log(1-2\alpha)+(3\alpha-1)\log(1-3\alpha).
}
\]

Moreover

\[
R_*'(\alpha)=\log\frac{8\alpha(1-3\alpha)^3}{(1-2\alpha)^4},
\qquad
R_*''(\alpha)=-\frac{6\alpha-1}{\alpha(2\alpha-1)(3\alpha-1)}.
\]

The derivative has its maximum at `alpha=1/6`, where it equals `log(27/32)<0`. Therefore

\[
R_*(\alpha)<0\qquad(0<\alpha\le1/3).
\]

A stronger envelope follows by differentiating the difference from `alpha log alpha +(3 log2-1)alpha`:

\[
\boxed{R_*(\alpha)\le\alpha\log(8\alpha/e).}
\]

Consequently, for one absolute `C`,

\[
\boxed{
M_{n,N}\le n^C\left(\frac{8N}{en}\right)^N.
}
\]

**Outcome:** every positive-density AP profile is exponentially negligible, and the same bound reaches into the mesoscopic regime. The balanced `(2/3)^n` law from the previous run is the endpoint `R_*(1/3)=log(2/3)` of this single formula.
