\[

\[
K((a,b),(c,d)):=e_p(ad+bc).
\tag{K}
\]

\[

=\frac{p^{-n}}{(3n-1)!!}
\sum_{t\in\mathbb F_p^n}
\operatorname{Haf}(K^{(t,y)}_{\rm blowup}).
\tag{FH}
\]

The candidate kernel has unusually rigid Fourier algebra:

\[
KK^*=p^2I,
\qquad
K^2=p^2R,
\qquad
(Rf)(a,b)=f(-a,-b).
\tag{D}

Equivalently, `K/p` is unitary and its fourth power is the identity. There is also a purely formal Wick rewriting: if `W_K` denotes the combinatorial Wick functional with covariance kernel `K`, then `(FH)` should become

\[

\prod_{i=1}^n\left(\sum_{a\in\mathbb F_p} Z_{(a,y_i)}^3\right)

No positive Gaussian measure is asserted: `K` is complex, so `W_K` is an algebraic pairing functional. The point is structural. `(FH)` / `(W)` keeps every undirected matching and every Fourier phase **before** absolute values are taken, while `(D)` exposes an exactly diagonalizable `p^2`-dimensional transform.
