## B20 — the pointwise growing-modulus milestone is false

The obstruction is a family of two-valued, full-affine-span test rows.

### General deterministic classification

Let `G` be `d`-regular, `Q>d`, `gcd(d,Q)=1`, and

\[
y_i=\alpha+u1_{\{i\in S\}},
\]

with `u` a unit modulo `Q` and both parts nonempty. If `j(v)` is the number of neighbour-stubs of `v` landing in `S`, then

\[
(Ay)_v=d\alpha+j(v)u.
\]

Because `u` is a unit and `Q>d`, the residues corresponding to `j=0,...,d` are distinct. Hence `Ay=0` forces one fixed `k in {1,...,d-1}` with `j(v)=k` for every vertex and

\[
d\alpha+ku=0\pmod Q.
\]

Double-counting directed incidences into `S` gives

\[
kn=d|S|,
\qquad |S|=\frac{k}{d}n.
\]

Conversely these conditions imply `Ay=0`, and summing the coordinates gives zero because `d` is invertible modulo `Q`. Thus every two-level modular null row is an equitable cut of this form.

### Cubic normal form

For `d=3`, after choosing the smaller class,

\[
|S|=n/3,
\qquad
y_i=\begin{cases}2u/3,&i\in S,\\-u/3,&i\notin S,\end{cases}
\]

with `u` a unit, and the null-vector equation is exactly

\[
\boxed{\text{every vertex has exactly one neighbour in }S.}
\tag{3}
\]

The two values differ by the unit `u`, so these rows really have full affine span and are surjective cyclic cokernel maps.

### Exact fixed-row probability

For a fixed `S` with `|S|=n/3` in the cubic configuration model, let `E_S` denote (3). Necessarily `n` is a multiple of six. Counting pairings gives exactly

\[
\Pr_{\rm conf}(E_S)
=
\frac{3^n (n/3-1)!!\,(2n/3)!\,(4n/3-1)!!}{(3n-1)!!}.
\tag{4}
\]

Writing `n=6m`, this becomes

\[
3^{6m}2^{4m}\frac{(2m)!(8m)!(9m)!}{m!(18m)!},
\]

so Stirling gives

\[
\Pr_{\rm conf}(E_S)
\sim4\sqrt{\frac{\pi n}{6}}\left(\frac49\right)^n.
\tag{5}
\]

Conditioned on `E_S`, the `S-S` matching and the cross edges are automatically simple; only the residual degree-two configuration on the complement can create loops or parallel edges. Its simplicity probability tends `e^{-3/4}`, while the ordinary cubic configuration simplicity probability tends `e^{-2}`. Therefore under the uniform simple cubic graph law,

\[
\boxed{
\Pr_{\rm simple}(E_S)
\sim4e^{5/4}\sqrt{\frac{\pi n}{6}}\left(\frac49\right)^n.
}
\tag{6}
\]

For the corresponding full-affine row `y`, its null event is exactly `E_S`. Hence

\[
\rho_Q(n)\ge Q^{n-1}\Theta(\sqrt n(4/9)^n).
\tag{7}
\]

If `Q=n^{\theta+o(1)}` for any fixed `theta>0`, then

\[
\log\rho_Q(n)=\theta n\log n+n\log(4/9)+o(n\log n),
\]

which is enormous rather than `n^{o(1)}`. Thus the previous pointwise growing-modulus milestone is definitively false. The failure mechanism is precise: full affine span does not imply a flat residue profile.

## B28 — the bad pointwise stratum is harmless after averaging

The pointwise failure does not destroy the exact cokernel-surjection inequality (1). It says that its expectation cannot be controlled by a worst-row supremum.

Let `S_Q^(2)(A)` count surjective null rows using exactly two residue values. The cubic classification above parametrizes every such contributing row uniquely by

* a canonical minority class `S` of size `n/3`; and
* a unit difference `u`, of which there are `phi(Q)`.

Therefore

\[
\mathbb E S_Q^{(2)}(A)
=
\varphi(Q)\binom n{n/3}\Pr_{\rm simple}(E_S).
\tag{8}
\]

Using

\[
\binom n{n/3}
\sim\frac{3}{2\sqrt{\pi n}}e^{nH(1/3)},
\qquad H(1/3)=\log3-\frac23\log2,
\]

and (6), one gets the explicit asymptotic

\[
\boxed{
\mathbb E S_Q^{(2)}(A)
\sim e^{5/4}\sqrt6\,\varphi(Q)
\left(\frac{2^{4/3}}3\right)^n.
}
\tag{9}
\]

Since

\[
\frac{2^{4/3}}3=0.839947\ldots<1,
\]

the entire exceptional two-level family is exponentially negligible for every polynomially growing `Q`, indeed whenever `phi(Q)=e^{o(n)}`. This is the first exact profile-stratified estimate for the new growing-modulus route.
