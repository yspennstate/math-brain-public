## 2. Inherited setup

Let `K=K_H` be the harmonic-descendant kernel on surviving rooted environments and `mu` its canonical stationary ergodic law. Let

`I_n = -log theta_T(X_n)`

be the information of a depth-`n` harmonic cylinder, sampled under harmonic measure in the fixed tree `T`. For every `r>=1` define the conditional cumulant

`kappa_{r,n}(T) = Cum_r( I_n | T )`.

Thus

`kappa_{1,n}=m_n`, `kappa_{2,n}=V_n`.

The preceding runs prove:

1. every finite moment of the electrical edge information
   `j(T,u)=-log H_T(u)`;
2. full-state two-spine mixing: for centered `f in L^{2+eta}(mu)`,
   `||K^n f||_2 <= C e^{-cn}`;
3. the Shannon corrector
   `m_n = n d_H + G + epsilon_{1,n}`;
4. the variance corrector
   `V_n = n sigma_H^2 + J_2 + epsilon_{2,n}`;
5. `G` and `J_2` have moments of every finite order, after the interpolation strengthening below.

The deterministic regular no-thinning model remains the unique case `sigma_H^2=0` under the inherited hypotheses.

## 3. Strengthening used in the induction: full-state mixing in every finite L^p

The previous report stated the key estimate in `L^2`. For an observable `f` having moments of every finite order, it immediately upgrades to every finite `L^p`.

Fix `p>2`, choose `q>p`, and let `theta in (0,1)` satisfy

`1/p = theta/2 + (1-theta)/q`.

Stationarity makes `K` a contraction on `L^q(mu)`, while the two-spine theorem gives exponential decay in `L^2` for centered `f`. Interpolation yields

`||K^n f||_p <= ||K^n f||_2^theta ||K^n f||_q^{1-theta}`
`             <= C_{p,f} exp(-c_{p,f} n)`.

For `1<=p<=2`, the `L^2` estimate implies the same conclusion. Therefore

`boxed{ ||K^n f||_p <= C_{p,f} e^{-c_{p,f}n} }`  (3.1)

for every finite `p`, whenever `E_mu f=0` and `f` has all finite moments.

Applying the same interpolation to the variance-corrector proof upgrades the inherited statements to

`G,J_2 in intersection_{p<infinity} L^p(mu)`,

`||epsilon_{1,n}||_p + ||epsilon_{2,n}||_p <= C_p e^{-c_p n}`  (3.2)

for every finite `p`.

This all-`L^p` strengthening is what allows cumulant polynomials of arbitrary fixed order to be closed without introducing `2^r` spines.

## 4. Exact law-of-total-cumulance recursion

Condition on the first harmonic child `U` of `T`. Write

`Y_{1,n}(T,U)=j(T,U)+kappa_{1,n}(T_U)`,

and for `a>=2`,

`Y_{a,n}(T,U)=kappa_{a,n}(T_U)`.

Given `(T,U)`, the future depth-`n` information in `T_U` is independent of the first edge and has conditional cumulants `Y_{a,n}` (with the first cumulant shifted by `j`).

The law of total cumulance gives, for every fixed `r>=1`,

`kappa_{r,n+1}(T) = K kappa_{r,n}(T) + S_{r,n}(T)`,  (4.1)

where `S_{1,n}(T)=E_{U~H_T} j(T,U)`, and for `r>=2`,

`S_{r,n}(T)`
` = sum_{pi in Partitions([r]), |pi|>=2}`
`     Cum_{U~H_T}( Y_{|B_1|,n}(T,U), ..., Y_{|B_|pi||,n}(T,U) ).`  (4.2)

The partition with one block is exactly `K kappa_{r,n}` and has been separated in (4.1).

For `r=2`, (4.2) reduces to the ordinary law-of-total-variance increment. For `r=3`, it reduces to the familiar total-third-cumulance identity used explicitly in Section 6.

## 5. Main induction: an O(1) random corrector for every fixed conditional cumulant

### Theorem 5.1 — all-fixed-orders quenched cumulant correctors

For every fixed integer `r>=1` there exist a deterministic constant `gamma_r`, a random corrector

`J_r in intersection_{p<infinity} L^p(mu)`,

and, for every finite `p`, constants `C_{r,p},c_{r,p}>0` such that

`boxed{ || kappa_{r,n} - n gamma_r - J_r ||_p <= C_{r,p} e^{-c_{r,p} n}. }`  (5.1)

The same error converges to zero almost surely. Hence (5.1) holds `mu`-a.s. and, by equivalence of `mu` with the original source law conditioned on survival, source-a.s. on survival.

#### Proof

The cases `r=1,2` are the inherited Shannon and variance correctors, strengthened to all finite `L^p` by Section 3.

Assume the theorem for all orders `<r`. Thus for each `a<r`,

`kappa_{a,n}=n gamma_a+J_a+epsilon_{a,n}`,  (5.2)

with `J_a` having all finite moments and `epsilon_{a,n}` exponentially small in every finite `L^p`.

Insert (5.2) into the variables `Y_{a,n}` in (4.2). Every term in `S_{r,n}` is an outer joint cumulant over the first-child variable `U` of order at least two, because `|pi|>=2`. Joint cumulants of order at least two are invariant under adding deterministic constants to any argument. Hence all terms `n gamma_a` disappear identically from `S_{r,n}`.

Define the limiting child observables

`A_1(T,U)=j(T,U)+J_1(T_U)` with `J_1=G`,

`A_a(T,U)=J_a(T_U)` for `2<=a<r`,

and define

`S_{r,infty}(T)`
` = sum_{pi in Partitions([r]), |pi|>=2}`
`     Cum_{U~H_T}( A_{|B_1|}(T,U), ..., A_{|B_|pi||}(T,U) ).`  (5.3)

A finite-order joint cumulant is a finite polynomial in joint moments. All `A_a` have moments of every finite order. Multilinearity of cumulants, Holder, and the exponentially small errors in (5.2) therefore give, for every finite `p`,

`S_{r,infty} in L^p(mu)`,

`||S_{r,n}-S_{r,infty}||_p <= C_{r,p} e^{-c_{r,p} n}`.  (5.4)

Put

`gamma_r = E_mu S_{r,infty}`,

`Delta_{r,n}=S_{r,n}-S_{r,infty}`,

`a_{r,n}=E_mu Delta_{r,n}`.

Iterating (4.1) from `kappa_{r,0}=0` gives

`kappa_{r,n}`
` = sum_{s=0}^{n-1} K^s S_{r,n-1-s}`.  (5.5)

Subtract `n gamma_r` and split as

`sum_{s=0}^{n-1} K^s(S_{r,infty}-gamma_r)`
` + sum_{m=0}^{n-1} K^{n-1-m} Delta_{r,m}`.  (5.6)

The first term converges exponentially in every finite `L^p` by (3.1) to

`J_r^(0)=sum_{s>=0} K^s(S_{r,infty}-gamma_r)`.  (5.7)

For the second term, decompose `Delta_{r,m}=a_{r,m}+(Delta_{r,m}-a_{r,m})`. The scalar series

`C_r=sum_{m>=0} a_{r,m}`

converges absolutely and exponentially fast by (5.4). The centered convolution is exponentially small because (3.1) supplies decay in `n-1-m` while (5.4) supplies decay in `m`; summing the convolution gives `O(n e^{-c n})`, hence `O(e^{-c' n})` after reducing the exponent.

Thus

`J_r=J_r^(0)+C_r`  (5.8)

has all finite moments and satisfies (5.1). Exponential `L^2` convergence implies almost-sure convergence by Borel--Cantelli. This closes the induction. `square`

### Important structural point

No new `2^r`-spine expansion is required. Two spines are used once, upstream, to prove full-state mixing. The entire hierarchy of conditional cumulants then closes by one-step total-cumulance recursion plus the stationary mixing theorem.

## 6. The conditional third cumulant closes explicitly

Write

`C_n(T)=kappa_{3,n}(T)`.

The exact law of total third cumulance is

`C_{n+1}`
` = K C_n`
`   + Cum_{3,U}( j(T,U)+m_n(T_U) )`
`   + 3 Cov_U( j(T,U)+m_n(T_U), V_n(T_U) )`.  (6.1)

Using

`m_n = n d_H + G + o_{L^p}(1)`,

`V_n = n sigma_H^2 + J_2 + o_{L^p}(1)`,

the deterministic linear terms disappear from the child cumulant/covariance. Put

`A(T,U)=j(T,U)+G(T_U)`.

Then the limiting increment is

`S_{3,infty}(T)`
` = Cum_{3,U}(A(T,U))`
`   + 3 Cov_U(A(T,U),J_2(T_U)).`  (6.2)

Therefore

`boxed{ gamma_3 = E_mu[ Cum_{3,U}(A) + 3 Cov_U(A,J_2(T_U)) ]. }`  (6.3)

and there exists `J_3` with all finite moments such that

`boxed{ C_n(T)=n gamma_3+J_3(T)+O_{L^p}(e^{-c_p n}) }`  (6.4)

for every finite `p`, and source-a.s. on survival.

This is the precise third-cumulant theorem proposed as the next target in the previous report.

## 7. Annealed cumulant rates and the quenched Renyi jet

Condition now only on the environment `T` under `mu`. The law of total cumulance at order `r` writes the unconditional cumulant of `I_n` as a sum over partitions. The one-block term is

`E_mu kappa_{r,n}=n gamma_r+O(1)`.

Every partition with at least two blocks is an outer joint cumulant of lower conditional cumulants `kappa_{a,n}`. By Theorem 5.1 each lower cumulant is `n gamma_a+J_a+o(1)`, and deterministic constants disappear from an outer cumulant of order at least two. Since all `J_a` have all finite moments, every such remaining term is `O(1)`. Hence

`boxed{ Cum_r(I_n under stationary annealed law)=n gamma_r+O(1) }`  (7.1)

for every fixed `r`.

Now define, for a fixed surviving tree,

`Z_n(q;T)=sum_{|v|=n} theta_T(v)^q`

and

`p_n(q,T)=(1/n) log Z_n(q;T)`.

Since

`Z_n(q;T)=E_H^T exp(-(q-1)I_n)`,

finite-level differentiation at `q=1` gives exactly

`p_n^{(r)}(1,T)=((-1)^r/n) kappa_{r,n}(T)`.  (7.2)

Theorem 5.1 therefore yields, simultaneously for each fixed `r`,

`boxed{ p_n^{(r)}(1,T) -> (-1)^r gamma_r }`  (7.3)

for source-a.e. surviving tree. More precisely,

`p_n^{(r)}(1,T)=(-1)^r gamma_r + (-1)^r J_r(T)/n + o(1/n)`  (7.4)

almost surely.

In particular,

`gamma_1=d_H`,

`gamma_2=sigma_H^2`,

and for every nonregular source `gamma_2>0` by the inherited variance-rigidity theorem.

Equation (7.3) is an **all-orders quenched cumulant jet at the Shannon point**. It does not assert a nonzero radius of convergence in `q-1`, nor convergence of `p_n(q,T)` at any fixed `q != 1`.

### Why this is a real method change

The previous next step suggested attacking the third cumulant and warned about an eight-spine expansion. The present argument avoids that hierarchy entirely. The only multi-ray input is the already established two-spine theorem used to obtain full-state mixing. After that, total-cumulance recursion generates every fixed order algebraically.

### Remaining obstruction

The gap between an all-orders pointwise jet and a genuine quenched pressure neighborhood is now quantitative rather than structural. To pass from (7.3) to a nonzero `q`-interval, one needs **uniform growth control in the cumulant order**, for example a factorial bound of the form

`|gamma_r| + ||J_r||_2 <= C^r r!`  (8.1)

with a corresponding uniform bound on the finite-`n` errors. Such a bound would give a positive radius for the conditional cumulant series and a route to a locally analytic quenched pressure. The induction above proves each fixed order but does not control the combinatorial growth of the partition constants as `r->infinity`.

The next strongest attack is therefore to majorize the total-cumulance recursion by an exponential generating function and prove a uniform factorial estimate such as (8.1), using: (i) the all-orders endpoint moments already available, (ii) the full-state spectral decay, and (iii) Bell-number / partition-function bounds in exponential generating form. A successful majorant would be a direct local quenched multifractal theorem near `q=1`; failure would isolate the first genuine analyticity obstruction.
