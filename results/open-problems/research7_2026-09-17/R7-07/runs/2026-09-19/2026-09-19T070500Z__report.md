\tag{4.1}

\[

### 4.1 Why generic algebraic codimension is provably the wrong scale

There is a useful dimension sanity check. The balanced rank-pair stratum can be parameterized by:

1. a flag `X subset Y subset R^{3m}` with `dim X=m`, `dim Y=2m`, costing `3m^2` continuous dimensions;
2. an invertible map from `R^{3m}/X` to `Y`, costing `4m^2` dimensions.

So the rank-pair stratum has dimension `7m^2`, matching the binary exposure count in (4.5) at leading order.

The nilpotent orbit of Jordan type `(3^m)` has centralizer dimension `3m^2`, hence orbit dimension

\[
9m^2-3m^2=6m^2.
\tag{4.9}
\]

Thus imposing `A^3=0` inside the rank-pair stratum has ordinary algebraic codimension only

\[
7m^2-6m^2=m^2.
\tag{4.10}
\]

That is exactly the number of scalar equations in (4.8). Even a perfect generic-codimension argument would therefore reduce `7m^2` only to `6m^2`, i.e. exponent `2n^2/3`, still above the needed `n^2/2` by

\[
6m^2-\frac92m^2=\frac32m^2=\frac16n^2.
\tag{4.11}
\]

So the missing theorem cannot be a Zariski-dimension count. It must prove **arithmetic sparsity of the sign cube inside the nilpotent orbit**, beyond ordinary algebraic codimension.

## 7. Missing ingredient selected for the final recovery: a many-prime splitting entropy theorem

For every rational prime

\[
p\equiv1\pmod4,
\]
choose `i_p in F_p` with `i_p^2=-1`. Reduction defines a ring homomorphism

\[
\mathbf Z[i]\to\mathbf F_p,
\qquad
a+bi\mapsto a+b i_p.
\tag{7.1}
\]

For a sign matrix `A`, let

\[
S_p(A)
=
\{\chi_A(x)\text{ splits completely into linear factors over }\mathbf F_p\}.
\tag{7.2}
\]

If `A in E_n`, then over `Z[i]`

\[
\chi_A(x)=\prod_{j=1}^n(x-\lambda_j),
\qquad
\lambda_j\in\mathbf Z[i].
\]

Reducing every `lambda_j` by (7.1) proves the exact inclusion

\[
\boxed{
E_n\subseteq S_p
\quad\text{for every }p\equiv1\pmod4.
}
\tag{7.3}
\]

Consequently, for every finite prime set `P_n` consisting of primes `1 mod 4`,

\[
\boxed{
\Pr(E_n)
\le
\Pr\Big(\bigcap_{p\in P_n}S_p\Big).
}
\tag{7.4}
\]

This is the source-level reduction the final recovery will use.

A theorem of the form

\[
\boxed{
\Pr\Big(\bigcap_{p\in P_n}S_p\Big)
\le
2^{-(\delta+o(1))n^2}
}
\tag{7.5}
\]

for **any explicit constant `delta>0`** would immediately move the frozen metric to

\[
\delta\le u_{GI}\le\frac12.
\tag{7.6}
\]

Unlike the nilpotent-fiber work, (7.5) is therefore directly source-metric-moving.

### 7.1 Calibration in the ideal random-polynomial model

This is only a calibration, not a claim about Bernoulli-matrix characteristic polynomials.

Among all monic degree-`n` polynomials over `F_p`, exactly

\[
\binom{p+n-1}{n}
\tag{7.7}
\]

split completely, because such a polynomial is uniquely a multiset of `n` roots from the `p` field elements. Hence the random-monic-polynomial complete-splitting probability is exactly

\[
\frac{\binom{p+n-1}{n}}{p^n}.
\tag{7.8}
\]

Two regimes show the required scale.

- For fixed `p`, (7.8) is `p^{-n} n^{p-1+O(1)}`, costing only `Theta(n)` bits. One would need linearly many genuinely entropy-contributing primes.
- For `p` much larger than `n^2`, (7.8) is `(1+o(1))/n!`, costing

\[
\log_2 n!
=n\log_2 n-(\log_2 e)n+O(\log n)
\tag{7.9}
\]

bits per idealized independent test. Then on the order of `n/log n` entropy-independent primes would already accumulate `Theta(n^2)` bits.

The phrase **entropy-independent** is the entire difficulty. The same `n^2` Rademacher bits define the matrix modulo every prime, so CRT does not make the splitting events independent. Establishing enough joint entropy across a growing prime set is precisely the missing theorem.

## 8. Nearest solved analogue and why it does not already give (7.5)

The closest same-ensemble result recovered is Eberhard's 2022 characteristic-polynomial theorem. Conditionally on ERH it proves that an iid sign/integer-entry matrix has irreducible characteristic polynomial and Galois group containing `A_n` with probability tending to one. Its modular-factorization viewpoint is exactly the right category for (7.4): factorization patterns modulo primes expose cycle types in the Galois group.

But qualitative `1-o(1)` irreducibility is much weaker than the present target. F0012 needs an exceptional-event estimate on the scale

\[
2^{-\Theta(n^2)}.
\]

A proof that merely produces one or finitely many incompatible modular factorization patterns with high probability cannot supply that rate. The final recovery needs a **quantitative many-prime entropy accumulation statement** for the much more rigid event “completely split at every selected `p`.”

Hansen--Schmutz provides the neighboring finite-field benchmark that a uniform random matrix characteristic polynomial is random-like away from small factors. Ferber--Jain--Luh--Samotij shows how inverse Littlewood--Offord information can be converted into counting bounds rather than only structural classifications. Kwan--Sauermann shows that robust polynomial dependence can be decoupled to high-dimensional linear anti-concentration. These are methodological analogues, not imported solutions.
