## 5. B18 — strong lumpability is exactly a hidden Markov tree extension

For `c_AB>0`, define a fine-label transition distribution on child block `B` by

\[
R_i^B(j):=\frac{a_{ij}}{c_{AB}},
\qquad i\in C_A,\ j\in C_B.
\tag{5.1}
\]

### Theorem 5.1 — exact hidden-Markov representation

Under (4.3), the complete fine-type source tree is generated exactly by:

1. generating the quotient block MGW tree, where a potential child of a block-`A` parent is a failed block-`B` child with probability `c_AB` and absent with probability `1-s_A`, `s_A=sum_B c_AB`;
2. choosing the root fine type inside its block according to the source root mixture;
3. conditional on the quotient tree and fine parent type `i`, assigning every actual block-`B` child a fine type independently according to `R_i^B`.

#### Proof

For one potential child of fine parent `i in C_A`, source probabilities are

\[
P(\text{failed fine type }j)=a_{ij},
\qquad
P(\text{absent})=1-\sum_ja_{ij}.
\]

Summing over `j in C_B` gives

\[
P(\text{failed block }B)=c_{AB},
\]

which depends only on parent block by (4.3). Conditional on block `B`, Bayes gives

\[
P(j\mid B,i)=a_{ij}/c_{AB}=R_i^B(j).
\]

Thus one source categorical draw factors exactly into a block/absence draw followed by a fine label inside the chosen block. Potential-child draws are independent, so the factorization holds jointly conditional on `nu`. Source reproduction primitives are independent across vertices. Induction over generations gives the complete tree factorization. `square`

Therefore, conditional on the quotient tree, the fine types form a finite-state tree-indexed Markov decoration and never feed back into quotient reproduction.
