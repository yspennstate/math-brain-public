## 2. B2 — attack the negation: the sibling denominator vanishes at a genuine fresh-edge regeneration

R14 showed that a naive type-only chain obtained by directly selecting the harmonic child fails: the selection probability contains the child's conductance divided by a random sibling-conductance sum. I attacked that obstruction directly at the fresh-edge event R14 proposed for handoff.

Let `P_j` be the law of the unrevealed forward subtree below a fresh child of type `j`, in deferred-revelation form. After the walk first crosses the fresh parent-child edge, let

`B(T) = P(never cross that edge backwards | forward subtree T)`

and

`b_j = E_{P_j} B(T)`.

For the successful no-return event `R`, Bayes gives exactly

`P(T in A, R | type=j) = integral_A B(T) P_j(dT)`

and therefore

`Q_j(dT) := P(T in dT | R, type=j) = [B(T)/b_j] P_j(dT)`.

This has **no sibling denominator**. Once the walk has crossed the chosen edge, the parent's siblings cannot be visited without first crossing that edge backwards; that backwards crossing is precisely failure of the regeneration event. Thus the obstruction that invalidates direct harmonic-child selection is absent at the fresh-edge Palm event.

If `B>0` `P_j`-a.s. on the surviving/transient type class, then `Q_j~P_j`.

This is an exact symbolic lemma, not a numerical heuristic. It discharges the algebraic content of R14 obligation C2 conditional on a valid C1 regeneration construction.

## 3. B21 — import the Palm/spine measure change: block-start logarithmic integrability is automatic

The same exact reset density gives a quantitative integrability estimate. Since

`dQ_j/dP_j = B/b_j`, 

we have

`E_{Q_j}[-log B] = (1/b_j) E_{P_j}[B(-log B)]`.

The elementary maximum

`sup_{0<x<=1} x(-log x) = 1/e`

gives

`E_{Q_j}[-log B] <= 1/(e b_j)`.

Therefore

`E_{Q_j}[ |log(dQ_j/dP_j)| ] <= 1/(e b_j) + |log b_j| < infinity`.

So the regeneration **block-start** measure already has the logarithmic density control needed for the change-of-measure side of a PF-KL argument. This does not yet establish the corresponding integrability at all intermediate environments inside a regeneration block.

## 4. B1 — change category: conditional Markov-renewal theorem and invariant tower

Assume a source-matching infinite sequence of fresh-edge regenerations C1 has been constructed. Let `J_k` be the regeneration type, `T_k` the forward subtree, `H_k` the number of exit-ray/harmonic-descendant levels to the next regeneration, and `E_{k,r}`, `0<=r<H_k`, the one-level environments inside the block.

The reset lemma and the branching plus strong-Markov properties imply:

- conditional on `J_k=j`, `T_k~Q_j`;
- `T_k` is independent of the pre-regeneration past given `J_k`;
- the next block law depends on the past only through `J_k`.

Hence the regeneration types form a finite-state Markov chain with some kernel

`K_ij = P(J_{k+1}=j | J_k=i)`.

If `K` is irreducible, `pi K=pi`, and the mean exit-level roof

`Hbar = sum_i pi_i E_{Q_i} H`

is finite, then the occupation measure

`mu(A) = [sum_i pi_i E_{Q_i}[sum_{r=0}^{H-1} 1{E_r in A}]] / Hbar`

is invariant under the one-level harmonic-descendant shift.

The proof is a one-line telescoping identity for bounded `f`:

`int(f o S-f)dmu`

`= Hbar^{-1} E_pi[sum_{r=0}^{H-1}(f(E_{r+1})-f(E_r))]`

`= Hbar^{-1} E_pi[f(E_H)-f(E_0)] = 0`,

because under the stationary regeneration chain the endpoint of a block is the start of the next block and has the same law as `E_0`.

Moreover, because the occupation sum contains the block-start term,

`mu(A) >= Hbar^{-1} sum_i pi_i Q_i(A)`.

If every surviving source type has positive source weight, every `pi_i>0`, and `Q_i~P_i`, then the source mixture `Pbar=sum_i q_iP_i` satisfies

`Pbar << mu`.

The reverse implication `mu<<Pbar` is not promoted: it requires absolute-continuity control of all within-block environments.
