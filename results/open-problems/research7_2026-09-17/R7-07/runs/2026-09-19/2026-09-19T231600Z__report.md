\[

Fix `delta in (0,1)` and call `x` delta-good when `Delta_x>=delta`. Let `g_delta(n)` be the minimum number of delta-good branch ancestors along any positive-backbone root-to-level-`n` path.

Normalizing the common-pair transition at each branch gives the exact Feynman--Kac representation

\[
C_n:=P_T(\tau>n)=E_r\prod_{x\text{ before level }n}(1-\Delta_x).

Therefore

\[
\boxed{C_n\le(1-\delta)^{g_\delta(n)}.}
