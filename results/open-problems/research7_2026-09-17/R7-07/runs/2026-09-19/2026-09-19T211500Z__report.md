## 6. B5 — add a Rényi exponent and differentiate

For `-1<=epsilon<=1`, define

\[
Z_\epsilon(p):=\sum_u p_u^{1-\epsilon}.
\tag{6.1}
\]

### Theorem 6.1 — exact Rényi interpolation

\[
\boxed{
P_2V^{1+\epsilon}
=(V-1)^{1+\epsilon}Z_\epsilon(p).
}
\tag{6.2}
\]

Proof:

\[
\begin{aligned}
P_2V^{1+\epsilon}
&=\sum_u\frac{B_u^2}{S^2}B_u^{-1-\epsilon}\\
&=S^{-2}\sum_uB_u^{1-\epsilon}\\
&=S^{-1-\epsilon}\sum_up_u^{1-\epsilon}\\
&=(V-1)^{1+\epsilon}Z_\epsilon(p).
\end{aligned}
\]

For `epsilon<=0`, `Z_epsilon<=1`; for `epsilon>=0`, `Z_epsilon<=N^epsilon`. Therefore

\[
P_2V^{1+\epsilon}\le(V-1)^{1+\epsilon}
\quad(-1\le\epsilon\le0),
\tag{6.3}
\]

and

\[
P_2V^{1+\epsilon}\le N^\epsilon(V-1)^{1+\epsilon}
\quad(0\le\epsilon\le1).
\tag{6.4}
\]

The endpoints are exactly the two earlier identities (4.3).

Let

\[
H(p)=-\sum_up_u\log p_u.
\]

Differentiating at `epsilon=0` gives the exact critical-exponent drift

\[
\boxed{
P_2(V\log V)
=(V-1)[\log(V-1)+H(p)].
}
\tag{6.5}
\]

Thus the superlinear obstruction has an exact information-theoretic form: the pipe case has zero child entropy, while a genuine branch injects `H(p)>0`.
