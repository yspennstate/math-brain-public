## 4. B18 — exact Doob-transform calculation on the regular tree

The source's proposed mechanisms can be compared mathematically rather than treated as synonymous prose.

Let `T_d`, `d>=3`, be the infinite d-regular tree and fix a food vertex `F`. For simple random walk, if `r=dist(x,F)`, the hitting probability of `F` is

`h(x)=(d-1)^(-r)`.

Indeed, the radial chain moves inward with probability `1/d` and outward with probability `(d-1)/d`; the unique bounded harmonic solution satisfying `h(0)=1` and vanishing at infinity is `(d-1)^(-r)`.

Conditioning the walk to hit `F` gives the Doob transform

`P^h(x,y)=P(x,y)h(y)/h(x)`.

Hence at every `r>=1`,

`P^h(r -> r-1)=(d-1)/d`,

while each of the `d-1` outward neighbors has probability

`1/[d(d-1)]`,

for total outward probability `1/d`.

So on the regular tree the source's “conditioned to hit the food” proposal is exactly one radial-drift model, namely inward probability `(d-1)/d`. A generic drift with inward probability `q` agrees with it if and only if `q=(d-1)/d`. Thus “weak drift” and “conditioning” describe different model families except at one parameter value; OP4 does not identify a canonical drift parameter.

Outcome B18: **source ambiguity is mathematically substantive, not just editorial wording.**

## 5. B27 — a source-suggested branch can collapse to a deterministic theorem

The quantifier issue becomes sharper on a tree. Consider the two path-reinforcing update rules described in the source section before the open-problem list: reinforcing the reversed loop-erased path, or reinforcing a shortest N–F path inside the visited subgraph.

### Lemma

Let `T` be any tree with fixed nest `N` and fixed food vertex `F`. For every finite walk from `N` to `F`:

1. chronological loop erasure is the unique simple path `[N,F]`;
2. every connected visited subgraph containing `N,F` has `[N,F]` as its unique shortest `N-F` path.

Therefore, under either path-update rule, every ant that reaches `F` reinforces exactly the same geodesic `[N,F]`, regardless of its excursions.

If each update adds one to the selected path, then after `k` ants

`w_e(k)=w_e(0)+k` for `e in[N,F]`,

and

`w_e(k)=w_e(0)` otherwise.

This proof is elementary but complete for that branch. No novelty is claimed.
