### Proof

For a type-`i` parent and increment `g`, the probability that a potential child both has type `ig` and transmits is

`q_{ig} p_{i,ig} = m^{-1} r(g)`.

`P(g | transmission) = [m^{-1}r(g)]/t = r(g)/R = alpha(g)`,

`p_{ig,igh}=r((ig)^{-1}igh)=r(h)`,
