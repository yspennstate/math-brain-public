## 4. B20 — adversarially enumerate every remaining interface failure mode

The explicit construction remains

\[
G=\operatorname{Sym}_{\rm fin}(\mathbb N_0),
\qquad

\tau_n=(0\ n),

Let `u:G/H -> C` and let

\[
\widetilde u(g)=u(gH)
\tag{4.1}
\]

be its right-`H`-invariant pullback.

### 4.1 Left versus right convolution

The source's left operator uses `check(mu)`. Each `tau_n` is an involution, hence

\[
\check\mu=\mu.
\tag{4.2}
\]

So no reflection mismatch remains.

### 4.2 Quotient orientation

The map

\[
\Psi:G/H\to\mathbb N_0,
\qquad
\Psi(gH)=g(0),
\tag{4.3}
\]

is well-defined, bijective, and equivariant for left multiplication. Indeed, `gH=g'H` iff `g^{-1}g'` fixes `0`, iff `g(0)=g'(0)`. Thus the quotient convention used in the proof is the source-compatible homogeneous-space convention.

Moreover left convolution preserves right-`H`-invariance:

\[
\widetilde u(sgh)=u(sghH)=u(sgH)=\widetilde u(sg).
\tag{4.4}
\]

Hence the group equation descends unambiguously to `G/H`.

### 4.3 Exact quotient operator

Under (4.3), left multiplication by `tau_n` acts as the transposition `(0 n)`. Therefore

\[
(Pu)(0)=\sum_{n\ge1}p_nu(n),
\tag{4.5}
\]

and for `k>=1`,

\[
(Pu)(k)=p_ku(0)+(1-p_k)u(k).
\tag{4.6}
\]

The source equation (2.1) becomes exactly

\[
Pu=u+\ell
\tag{4.7}
\]

for an arbitrary constant `ell`.

At the basepoint the correct identity is

\[
\ell=\sum_{n\ge1}p_nu(n)-u(0),
\tag{4.8}
\]

not the stronger normalization mistakenly asserted in the historical addendum.

### 4.4 Integrability at every quotient state

Source `mu`-integrability of the pullback at the identity is

\[
\sum_{n\ge1}p_n|u(n)|<\infty.
\tag{4.9}
\]

This is precisely the absolute convergence used in the proof. At any leaf `k`, the absolute convolution is automatically finite:

\[
\sum_{n\ge1}p_n|u(\tau_n k)|
=

\tag{4.10}
\]

Thus there is no hidden conditional-convergence or undefined-row issue.

### 4.5 Semigroup generation

Every `tau_n` lies in `supp(mu)` and is an involution. For distinct positive `i,j`,

\tau_i\tau_j\tau_i=(i\ j).
\tag{4.11}

Together with the star transpositions themselves, this generates every transposition of `N_0`, hence every finitary permutation. Because the generators are involutions, group generation and semigroup generation coincide here. The exact measured-group hypothesis is satisfied.

## 5. Full corrected source proof

Assume `u` is source-valid and obeys (4.7). At leaf `k`, equation (4.6) gives

\[
p_ku(0)+(1-p_k)u(k)=u(k)+\ell.
\]

Therefore

\[
p_k\bigl(u(0)-u(k)\bigr)=\ell,
\qquad
u(k)=u(0)-\frac{\ell}{p_k}.
\tag{5.1}
\]

If `ell != 0`, then

\[
p_k|u(k)|=|p_ku(0)-\ell|.
\tag{5.2}
\]

Since `(p_k)` is positive and summable, `p_k -> 0`, and hence

\[
p_k|u(k)|\longrightarrow|\ell|>0.
\tag{5.3}
\]

The terms of the absolutely convergent series (4.9) would then fail even to tend to zero, a contradiction. Thus

\[
\boxed{\ell=0}.
\tag{5.4}
\]

Returning to (5.1),

\[
\boxed{u(k)=u(0)\quad\forall k\ge1},
\tag{5.5}
\]

so `u` is constant. Nonzero constants are allowed, as they must be; Problem 2 excludes only **nonconstant** functions.

This is a complete proof candidate for the literal source statement, not merely a normalized subcase.
