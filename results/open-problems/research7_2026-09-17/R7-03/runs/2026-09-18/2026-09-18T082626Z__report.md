### Exact source scope

Let `d >= 3`. Consider the nearest-neighbor random walk in random environment on `Z^d` under the source assumptions `(E)` and `(IID)`: the site environments are independent and identically distributed and elliptic, so every allowed nearest-neighbor transition has positive probability almost surely. The question asks, **for every such environment law**, whether the resulting RWRE is transient, i.e. whether it visits each fixed site only finitely often almost surely. This is ordinary transience, not transience in one prescribed direction and not merely positive speed.

The source itself records only special cases such as Dirichlet environments and balanced environments. The archived Codex record P0485 contains one correct but restricted homogeneous calculation: for a deterministic translation-invariant elliptic environment, nonzero mean gives transience by the strong law; zero mean yields a Fourier return bound `P(X_n=0) = O(n^{-d/2})`, hence summable returns for `d>=3`. That argument does not extend to spatially varying iid environments because there is no single increment characteristic function whose nth power is the transition law.
