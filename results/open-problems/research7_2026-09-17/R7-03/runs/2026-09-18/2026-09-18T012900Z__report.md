## 2. Exact source and retained source scope

Outside source:

The source asks for a countable measured group `(G,mu)` and an infinite-index subgroup `H<G` such that `G/H` admits **no nonconstant left (quasi-)mu-harmonic functions whatsoever**.  The source definition of measured group requires `supp(mu)` to generate `G` as a semigroup, and the discussion immediately before the problem includes unbounded harmonic functions, so bounded Liouville is insufficient.

The admitted construction retained from the preceding run is

\[
G=\operatorname{Sym}_{\mathrm{fin}}(\mathbb N_0),
\qquad
H=\operatorname{Stab}_G(0),
\qquad
\mu((0\ n))=p_n>0,
\qquad
\sum_{n\ge1}p_n=1.
\]

One explicit choice is `p_n=2^{-n}`.  The quotient identification `gH -> g(0)` gives `G/H ~= N_0`, and because every star transposition is an involution, `check(mu)=mu`.  The exact quotient operator is

\[
(Pu)(0)=\sum_{n\ge1}p_nu(n),
\tag{2.1}
\]

\[
(Pu)(k)=p_ku(0)+(1-p_k)u(k),\qquad k\ge1.
\tag{2.2}
\]

Source validity at the center gives the genuine complex-Lebesgue integrability condition

\[
\sum_{n\ge1}p_n|u(n)|<\infty.
\tag{2.3}
\]

The preceding proof solved `Pu=u+c` by the leaf equations.  This run finds a second, conservation-based proof and identifies why the construction is robust over the entire admissible star-support family.

## 3. B6 — finite-star deformation

Define

\[
S_N=\sum_{n=1}^Np_n,
\qquad
r_{n,N}=\frac{p_n}{S_N}.
\]

On

\[
G_N=\operatorname{Sym}(\{0,1,\ldots,N\}),
\qquad
H_N=\operatorname{Stab}_{G_N}(0),
\]

put `mu_N((0 n))=r_{n,N}`.  Then `G_N/H_N={0,...,N}` and its transition matrix `P_N` is symmetric.  Hence it is doubly stochastic and for every complex `u`,

\[
\sum_{x=0}^N(P_Nu)(x)=\sum_{x=0}^Nu(x).
\tag{3.1}
\]

If

\[
P_Nu=u+c,
\]

summing over all `N+1` states gives

\[
(N+1)c=0,
\]

so `c=0`.  Since all `r_{n,N}>0`, the star is irreducible; (2.2) in finite form then forces every harmonic `u` to be constant.

Thus each finite quotient has exact constant-defect rigidity for the simplest possible reason: total mass is conserved.  The direct-limit geometry is

\[
G=\bigcup_NG_N,
\qquad
H=\bigcup_NH_N,
\]

and `r_{n,N}->p_n` for each fixed `n`.  The source-relevant change at the limit is that `[G:H]` becomes infinite.  A mere limit argument would be insufficient, however, because mass can escape to infinity; B11 identifies the exact escape term and proves it vanishes under the source hypothesis.

**B6 outcome: SUCCESS.**  The source construction is a deformation of completely rigid finite stars, and the only gluing issue is a tail boundary flux.

## 4. B11 — dualize and compute the exact boundary flux

Let

\[
q_N=\sum_{n>N}p_n=1-S_N.
\]

For every source-integrable `u`, direct summation of (2.1)-(2.2) gives

\[
\begin{aligned}
\sum_{x=0}^N\bigl((Pu)(x)-u(x)\bigr)
&=\left(\sum_{n\ge1}p_nu(n)-u(0)\right)
  +\sum_{k=1}^Np_k(u(0)-u(k))\\
&=\sum_{n>N}p_nu(n)-q_Nu(0)\\
&=\boxed{\sum_{n>N}p_n(u(n)-u(0)).}
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.1}
\]

The right side tends to zero because

\[
\sum_{n>N}p_n|u(n)|\to0
\quad\text{and}\quad
q_N|u(0)|\to0.
\tag{4.2}
\]

Equivalently, with `chi_N=1_{\{0,...,N\}}`, symmetry of the kernel gives the dual identity

\[
\langle\chi_N,(P-I)u\rangle
=
\langle(P-I)\chi_N,u\rangle,
\tag{4.3}
\]

where

\[
(P-I)\chi_N(0)=-q_N,
\qquad
(P-I)\chi_N(n)=p_n\quad(n>N),
\]

and the dual defect is zero on `1,...,N`.  So (4.1) is literally the flux through the boundary of the finite star.

Now suppose

\[
Pu=u+c.
\tag{4.4}
\]

Then (4.1) becomes the exact identity

\[
\boxed{
(N+1)c
=
\sum_{n>N}p_n(u(n)-u(0)).
}
\tag{4.5}
\]

The right side tends to zero by (4.2), whereas the left side can do so only if

\[
\boxed{c=0.}
\]

With `c=0`, every leaf equation gives

\[
p_k(u(0)-u(k))=0,
\]

hence `u(k)=u(0)` for every `k` because `p_k>0`.  This is a second complete proof that every source-integrable constant-defect solution is constant.  It does not first solve the leaves and then inspect their growth; it kills the defect globally by conservation and only then uses one local equation.

There is also a quantitative comparison with the first proof.  If one formally ignores integrability and solves the leaf equations for a nonzero defect,

\[
u(n)=u(0)-\frac{c}{p_n},
\]

then for every `N`,

\[
\sum_{n=1}^Np_n|u(n)|
=\sum_{n=1}^N|p_nu(0)-c|
\ge N|c|-S_N|u(0)|
\ge N|c|-|u(0)|.
\tag{4.6}
\]

Therefore

\[
\boxed{
\liminf_{N\to\infty}
\frac1N\sum_{n=1}^Np_n|u(n)|
\ge |c|.
}
\tag{4.7}
\]

A nonzero defect is not merely nonintegrable; its truncated center `L1(mu)` cost diverges at least linearly.

**B11 outcome: SUCCESS.**  The exact obstruction is a dual finite-volume conservation law plus a vanishing tail flux.

## 5. B24 — the entire admissible star-support simplex works

The previous construction allowed arbitrary positive weights.  This run shows that positivity is exactly the source admissibility condition within the star-support class.

Let `mu` be any probability measure supported on

\[
\mathcal S=\{\tau_n=(0\ n):n\ge1\}
\]

and write `p_n=mu(tau_n)`.

**Claim.**

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{supp}(\mu)\text{ semigroup-generates }G
\iff
p_n>0\text{ for every }n.
}
\tag{5.1}
\]

If all `p_n>0`, then every `tau_n` is in the support and

\[
\tau_i\tau_j\tau_i=(i\ j),
\]

so all finitary transpositions, hence all of `G`, lie in the generated semigroup.  Conversely, if `p_m=0`, every supported `tau_n` fixes `m`; therefore every semigroup word in the support fixes `m`, whereas `G` contains `tau_m`, which moves `m`.  So generation fails.

Combining (5.1) with the dual proof above yields:

> **Star-family theorem.** Every probability measure supported on the star transpositions that satisfies Björklund's measured-group generation hypothesis automatically gives, with `H=Stab_G(0)`, a quotient having no nonconstant source-valid left harmonic or quasi-harmonic functions.

Thus the successful example is not a tuned point such as `p_n=2^{-n}`.  It is the **entire admissible interior** of the infinite star-support simplex; the excluded boundary faces are exactly the faces where the measured-group hypothesis itself fails.

**B24 outcome: SUCCESS.**  The source construction is robust over the complete admissible star-support family.

## 6. Exact source normalization and noncircularity

For `u:G/H->C`, let `tilde u(g)=u(gH)`.  The literal source-normalized left quasi-harmonic equation is

\[
\int_G\widetilde u(sg)\,d\check\mu(s)
=
\widetilde u(g)+
\int_G\widetilde u(s)\,d\check\mu(s).
\tag{6.1}
\]

Our measure is symmetric, so this is `Pu=u+c` with

\[
c=\int_G\widetilde u(s)\,d\mu(s)=\sum_{n\ge1}p_nu(n).
\]

The B11 argument applies directly.  Taking `g=e` in (6.1) also forces `u(H)=0`; hence every source-normalized quasi-harmonic solution is the zero function.  Ordinary harmonic functions are the `c=0` case and are constant.
