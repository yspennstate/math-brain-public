## Method B18 — say it in another language: unvisited-set contraction

Idea first: replace the cover time by the number of unvisited vertices

`U_t = sum_v 1_{T_v>t}`,

and let `h=t_hit`.

For every vertex `v`, time `t`, and integer `k>=0`, a complete strong-Markov/Markov-inequality argument gives

`P(T_v>t+2kh | F_t) <= 2^{-k} 1_{T_v>t}`.

Indeed, from every current state `x`, `E_x T_v <= h`, hence `P_x(T_v>2h)<=1/2`; iterate in `2h` blocks. Summing over vertices yields the exact universal contraction

`E[U_{t+2kh} | F_t] <= 2^{-k} U_t`,

so if `m(t)=E U_t`,

`m(t+2kh) <= 2^{-k}m(t)`.

This shows that one-point survival of late vertices already contracts at the source's desired hitting-time scale. The missing issue is therefore correlation among simultaneously late vertices, not the lifetime of a single target.

A second exact lemma reconciles starting conventions: for any starts `x,y`,

`|E_x tau_cov - E_y tau_cov| <= h`.

Proof: hit `y` first from `x`, at cost at most `h`, and then dominate the remaining cover time by a fresh walk started at `y`; reverse `x,y`. Thus every start-specific mean lies within one `t_hit` of the source's worst-start `t_cov`.

## Method B11 — dualize to factorial moments; exact conditional bridge theorem

The exact identities

`P(tau_cov>t)=P(U_t>0) <= E U_t`

and

`E[U_t^2]=E U_t + sum_{u != v}P(T_u>t,T_v>t)`

show that the unresolved object is the pair-survival/covariance term.

I proved the following complete implication. Suppose a universal `C0` satisfies

`Var(U_t) <= C0 E U_t`

for every time up to the first `t_*` at which `1<E U_{t_*}<=2`. Such a `t_*` exists because one step discovers at most one vertex. Then the block contraction gives

`P(tau_cov>t_*+2kh) <= 2^{1-k}`.

Backward application gives `E U_{t_*-2kh} > 2^k`; Chebyshev plus the assumed variance bound gives

`P(tau_cov<=t_*-2kh) <= C0 2^{-k}`.

Therefore `tau_cov` has universal two-sided exponential tails at `t_hit` scale, `Var(tau_cov)=O(t_hit^2)`, and after the start-centering lemma the same scale transfers to the source center.

This is a **conditional theorem**, not source closure: the universal variance hypothesis is unproved and is likely too naive on bottleneck graphs where late vertices survive in clusters. The next route must test and, if necessary, quotient those clusters rather than assume Poisson-like raw vertices.
