## 4. Fresh literature correction

A fresh dated literature pass found the missing later result:

**Yi Han (2025), _Simplicity of singular value spectrum of random matrices and two-point quantitative invertibility_, arXiv:2502.13819, v2 dated 2025-03-01.**

Han’s Theorem 1.7 applies to iid real entries with mean zero, variance one and all moments bounded. For every fixed `epsilon>0`, there is `c'>0` such that, with probability `1-O(n^{-c'})`,

```text
N_R(A_n) >= sqrt(2n/pi)*(1-epsilon) + O(n^{1/2-c'}).
```

The theorem explicitly cites Vu Conjecture 11.3 and states that it proves the difficult `C sqrt(n)` lower bound for independent `+-1` entries. Rademacher entries satisfy the theorem’s hypotheses.

Han’s route uses Mohammed Osman, *Bulk Universality for Real Matrices with Independent and Identically Distributed Entries*, EJP 30 (2025), arXiv:2402.04071, thereby removing the fourth-moment-matching obstruction that blocked the older Tao–Vu transfer to Rademacher entries.

## 5. B2 — attack the negation / exact resolution of Problem 11.4

Specialize Han’s theorem to Rademacher entries and fix `epsilon=1/2`. For some `c0,c'>0`,

```text
P(N_R(M_n) >= c0 sqrt(n)) = 1-O(n^{-c'}).
```

For sufficiently large `n`, `c0 sqrt(n)>=3`; therefore

```text
P(N_R(M_n)>=2)=1-O(n^{-c'}).
```

Hence **Vu Problem 11.4 is KNOWN / RESOLVED_IN_LITERATURE_2025**.

The parity identity sharpens the statement: for odd `n`, `N_R` is odd, so the same event eventually forces at least three real eigenvalues; for even `n`, it forces at least two. The old `1/2-o(1)` determinant-sign bound is thus obsolete as the best source-level first-step result.
