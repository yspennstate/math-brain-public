\[

Then every source-valid left `mu`-quasiharmonic function on `G/H` is constant.

### Proof

Write the value at the base point as `a` and the value at `x` as `b_x`, with defect `c`. At `x`, the only support element changing the state is `s_x`; hence

\[
p_xa+(1-p_x)b_x=b_x+c,

\[
p_x(a-b_x)=c.
\tag{5.1}
\]

At the base point, existence of the source convolution requires `sum_x p_x b_x` to exist. Equation (5.1) implies

\[

Thus `c!=0` violates the term test. Hence `c=0`, and (5.1) gives `b_x=a` for all `x`. ∎
