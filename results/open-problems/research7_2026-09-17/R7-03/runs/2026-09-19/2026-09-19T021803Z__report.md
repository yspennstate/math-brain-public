## (e) Nearest solved analogue and what technique it has that C0117 lacks

The nearest clean solved fluctuation theorem located in the dated literature check is Nathanael Berestycki, Jonathan Hermon, and Lucas Teyssier, *On the universality of fluctuations for the cover time*, arXiv:2202.02255, current manuscript dated **March 22, 2026**. For bounded-degree vertex-transitive graphs satisfying

Their companion paper Berestycki–Hermon–Teyssier, *Stationary hitting times on vertex-transitive graphs*, arXiv:2601.03864, dated **January 7, 2026**, develops the refined Aldous–Brown estimates used in that program. Source: `https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.03864`.
