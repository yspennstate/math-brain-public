## 4. B25 — count the fixed-complement mean a second way

The fixed-complement theorem from the previous run says that for fixed integer `r>=1`, with `k=n-1-r`,

`H_n=k-D_n => H_r`,

with first-two-moment convergence, and

`A_r ~ Gamma(r,1)`,

`H_r | A_r=a ~ Poisson(Lambda_r(a))`,

where

`Lambda_r(a)=e^{-a} sum_{ell=0}^{r-1} (r-ell) a^ell/ell!`.

The earlier proof evaluated

`mu_r=E[H_r]=E[Lambda_r(A_r)]`

by expanding the polynomial/incomplete-gamma formula. This run gives a genuinely different derivation that checks the same quantity.

Let `A_r,G_r` be iid `Gamma(r,1)`. Since

`p_r(y)=P(G_r>y)`

and

`Lambda_r(a)=int_a^infinity p_r(y) dy`,

Fubini gives

`Lambda_r(a)=E[(G_r-a)_+]`.

Therefore

`mu_r = E[(G_r-A_r)_+] = (1/2) E|G_r-A_r|`,

by exchangeability of `A_r,G_r`.

Now set

`S=A_r+G_r`, `B=A_r/(A_r+G_r)`.

Beta–gamma algebra gives independent

`S ~ Gamma(2r,1)`, `B ~ Beta(r,r)`.

Since

`(G_r-A_r)_+ = S(1-2B)_+`,
