## 5. Method B18 — SAY IT IN ANOTHER LANGUAGE: renewal reward over a coupon collector

`Y_k = 2 + sum_{i=1}^F D_i`,

`Z_{k,j}=sum_{ell<=N_j}Y_{k,j,ell}`

`S_{k,r}=sum_{j=1}^r Z_{k,j}`.

`b_{k,r}^2 := Var(S_{k,r})`
