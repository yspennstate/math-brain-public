## 3. B18 — replace the comparison graph by the exact threshold representation

## 4. B25 — exact one-shared-edge covariance localization

For two selected neighbors `i!=j`, their rejection indicators share exactly one non-root edge `(i,j)`, with score `W`. Conditional on `(X,W)`, the two indicators are independent because all their remaining edge sets are disjoint. Therefore their conditional covariance is the variance, over `W`, of a one-edge influence function.

Condition on root-edge score `u`. Removing the shared edge leaves `Bin(n-3,u)` competitors below `u`; changing `W` can affect rejection only at the boundary count `k-1`. Integrating that influence over the root-edge score gives the exact oscillation bound

`osc(p_X) <= (1/X) ∫_0^1 C(n-3,k-1)u^(k-1)(1-u)^(n-k-2)du
          = 1/((n-2)X)`.

Thus

`Cov(R_i,R_j|X) <= 1/[4(n-2)^2 X^2]`.

On the high-probability event `X>=k/(2n)`, summing this over all pairs costs only `O(1)`, rather than the old `O(k^5/n^2)` comparison error.
