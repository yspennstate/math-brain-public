## New theorem: arbitrary 2D active gauge block

This run proves a full-process finite-DPP theorem that strictly generalizes the inherited single swapped-eigenpair lemma.

Let `D` be a diagonal unitary involution and let

\[
\ell^2(E)=W\oplus V,\qquad \dim V=2,
\]

be an orthogonal `D`-invariant decomposition, with one `+1` and one `-1` direction on `V`. Suppose

\[
Q=Q_W\oplus K,\qquad [Q_W,D|_W]=0,
\]

where `K` is an arbitrary positive `2x2` contraction. Put

\[
H=K\#(DKD),\qquad \widetilde Q=Q_W\oplus H.
\]

Then

\[
\boxed{\mathbf P^{\widetilde Q}\preccurlyeq\mathbf P^Q},
\qquad
\boxed{\det\widetilde Q=\det Q}.
\]

The proof is event-level. Conditional on a retained spectral subset `R` of the commuting block `Q_W`, the rank-one active-sector event functional has the form

\[
h(u)=h_0+\langle u,Mu\rangle,
\]

with `M>=0` and `[M,D]=0`. For an arbitrary active kernel `K`, the complete conditional event probability is exactly

\[
\Phi_A(K)=h_0+\operatorname{tr}(MK)+c_A\det K.
\]

The midpoint `H` commutes with `D`, preserves determinant, and satisfies

\[
H\le \frac{K+DKD}{2}.
\]

In the `D`-eigenbasis both `M` and `H` are diagonal, so

\[
\Phi_A(K)-\Phi_A(H)
=m_+(k_{++}-h_+)+m_-(k_{--}-h_-)\ge0.
\]

Averaging over `R` proves stochastic domination for every increasing event. Full details are in the artifact.

## Exact failure of the naive sequential `S_3` route

Arbitrary `S_3` has more than one active sector, so the theorem cannot simply be applied to one block while ignoring the other. This is a genuine obstruction, not just a missing proof.

Take

\[
a_0=5/8,\quad z_0=3/8,\quad
a_1=1/4,\quad a_2=1/2,\quad z=(1+i)/10.
\]

These blocks define a positive contraction: `Q_0` has eigenvalues `1,1/4`, while

\[
\det Q_1=21/200>0,\qquad \det(I-Q_1)=71/200>0,
\]

and likewise for `Q_2`.

Now symmetrize **only** the quotient block,

\[
Q_0\mapsto \widehat Q_0=\frac12I_2,
\]

leaving `Q_1,Q_2` unchanged. This preserves the determinant. Let `A` be the invariant increasing event generated by the left translates of `{e,s_1,s_2}`, with `s_1,s_2` distinct transpositions. Exact determinantal inclusion--exclusion gives

\[
\mathbf P^Q(A)=\frac{26291-908\sqrt3}{96000},
\]

but

\[
\mathbf P^{\widehat Q}(A)=\frac{7973+160\sqrt3}{32000},
\]

so

\[
\boxed{
\mathbf P^Q(A)-\mathbf P^{\widehat Q}(A)
=\frac{593-347\sqrt3}{24000}<0.
}
\]

Thus `P^{Qhat} <=st P^Q` is false. The sequential quotient-first proof route is **REFUTED**. This does not refute the simultaneous midpoint `Q#(DQD)`, because the standard sector was left unsymmetrized in `Qhat`.
