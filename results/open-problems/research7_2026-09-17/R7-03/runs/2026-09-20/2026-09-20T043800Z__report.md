## New structural theorem: index-two constant-coset lift

Let `H normal Gamma` have index two, with quotient character `epsilon:Gamma->{+/-1}` and diagonal gauge `D`. Suppose the full lower Lyons theorem is known on `H`. Let `Q` be a `Gamma`-equivariant positive contraction whose Hermitian convolution kernel `q` is constant on the complementary coset:

\[
q(g)=\tau\qquad(g\in\Gamma\setminus H).
\]

No centrality assumption is placed on `q|_H`.

The complement-coset convolution operator is rank two. Indeed, if `t notin H`,

\[
\sum_{g\in tH}R_g=R_t\sum_{h\in H}R_h,
\]

and the normalized `H`-average is the projection onto the two-dimensional right-`H`-invariant space spanned by the trivial and quotient-sign vectors `nu_+,nu_-`. Hence the complementary-coset term vanishes on their orthogonal complement and has eigenvalues `+tau|H|` and `-tau|H|` on that pair.

The `H`-supported part commutes with `D` and has the same eigenvalue `kappa` on `nu_+,nu_-`. Thus the only gauge-noncommuting spectral pair has eigenvalues

\[
a=\kappa+\tau|H|,\qquad b=\kappa-\tau|H|.
\]

Apply the inherited full-process single-pair theorem and replace `a,b` by `sqrt(ab),sqrt(ab)`. The resulting contraction `Q_tilde` satisfies

\[
\mathbf P^{Q_{\rm tilde}}\preccurlyeq\mathbf P^Q,
\qquad
\det Q_{\rm tilde}=\det Q,
\]

and now commutes with `D`. Therefore its convolution kernel is supported in `H`, so it factors over the two right cosets into identical `H`-DPPs `P^L`. Determinant preservation gives

\[
\operatorname{FK}_\Gamma(Q)=\operatorname{FK}_H(L).
\]

Tensoring the two subgroup couplings yields

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{Ber}(\operatorname{FK}_\Gamma(Q))^{\otimes\Gamma}
\preccurlyeq \mathbf P^Q.
}
\]

Applying the same result to `I-Q` and complementing configurations gives the sharp upper bound. Full-set and empty-set events force optimality. Thus this is a full all-increasing-event theorem for this entire index-two kernel class, conditional only on the already-solved subgroup theorem.

## New noncentral `S_3` class

Take `Gamma=S_3` and `H=A_3=C_3`. The prior R7-03 work proved the full sharp theorem on `C_3`, so the lift is unconditional here. Consequently every `S_3`-equivariant positive contraction whose convolution kernel is constant on the three transpositions satisfies the complete sharp two-sided Lyons bounds, while its values on the two nontrivial three-cycles may be arbitrary complex conjugates.

This strictly enlarges the previous central `S_3` theorem: centrality would additionally force the two three-cycle values to coincide and be real.

The artifact gives an exact noncentral interior example. Start from `C_3` Fourier eigenvalues `(1/2,1/4,3/4)`, whose nontrivial cycle coefficients are `-i sqrt(3)/12` and `+i sqrt(3)/12`, and take common transposition coefficient `tau=1/24`. The quotient eigenvalues are `5/8` and `3/8`, while the standard-sector eigenvalues remain `1/4` and `3/4`; hence `0<Q<I`, the kernel is genuinely noncentral, and the theorem applies to the whole class containing this example.

## Exact obstruction to the next step

For a attempted single-pair smoothing with retained event coefficients `h00,h10,h01,h11` and `g=sqrt(ab)`, the exact difference without gauge invariance of the complement is

\[
F(a,b)-F(g,g)
=(a-g)(h_{10}-h_{00})+(b-g)(h_{01}-h_{00}).
\]

When `a>b`, the second coefficient is negative. The earlier theorem worked because gauge invariance forced `h10=h01`; monotonicity alone does not determine the sign in a general standard sector. This explains why arbitrary `S_3` requires a genuinely simultaneous symmetrization rather than sequential one-pair averaging. This is a proof obstruction, not a counterexample to the midpoint conjecture.
