## 2. Inherited precise bottleneck

## 3. First attack — exact obstruction to naive global pair smoothing

The most direct globalization would repeatedly replace two coordinates `(a,b)` by `(sqrt(ab),sqrt(ab))`. This preserves the product and converges toward the scalar spectrum. To prove (2.1) this way, every such averaging step would need to weakly increase `F`.

That monotonicity is false.

Take

\[
\lambda=
\left(\frac1{16},\frac1{16},\frac1{16},\frac1{16},\frac14,1\right)
\]

and geometrically average the adjacent frequency coordinates `3,4`:

\[
\widetilde\lambda=
\left(\frac1{16},\frac1{16},\frac1{16},\frac18,\frac18,1\right).
\]

Both products equal `2^{-18}`, so both have the same fixed geometric mean `p=1/8`. Exact evaluation from the already-proved projection-mixture table gives

\[
F(\lambda)=\frac{218025}{262144},
\qquad
F(\widetilde\lambda)=\frac{217065}{262144},
\]

hence

\[
\boxed{F(\widetilde\lambda)-F(\lambda)=-\frac{15}{4096}<0.}
\tag{3.1}
\]

The scalar comparator remains larger:

\[
F(1/8,\ldots,1/8)=\frac{120393}{131072}
=\frac{240786}{262144},
\]

so (3.1) is **not** a counterexample to Lyons’s inequality. It is an exact obstruction to the proposed proof mechanism: local Hessian positivity cannot be upgraded to a global theorem by unrestricted pairwise geometric averaging. This run therefore changed method rather than producing another local expansion.

## 4. New theorem — subgroup-supported kernels lift the conjecture to a broader ambient group

The new structural criterion is the main source-level progress of this run.

### Theorem (subgroup-support lifting)

Let `Gamma` be a countable sofic group, let `H <= Gamma`, and let `Q` be a `Gamma`-equivariant positive contraction on `ell^2(Gamma)`. Write

\[
Q(x,y)=q(x^{-1}y).
\]

Assume

\[
q(g)=0\quad(g\notin H).
\tag{4.1}
\]

Let `Q_H` be the restriction to `ell^2(H)`. If Lyons 5.7 holds for `Q_H` on `H`, then Lyons 5.7 holds for `Q` on the full ambient group `Gamma`. Moreover

\[
\boxed{
FK_\Gamma(Q)=FK_H(Q_H),
\qquad
FK_\Gamma(I-Q)=FK_H(I-Q_H).
}
\tag{4.2}
\]

#### Proof

Condition (4.1) makes every right-coset space `ell^2(xH)` a reducing subspace. The unitary `delta_h -> delta_{xh}` identifies the restriction on `xH` with `Q_H`, because

\[
Q(xh_1,xh_2)=q(h_1^{-1}h_2).
\]

Thus `Q` is a direct sum of identical copies of `Q_H` over right cosets.

For finite `A subset Gamma`, decomposing `A` over right cosets gives a block diagonal principal matrix and therefore

\[
\det(Q\upharpoonright A)
=\prod_{C\in\Gamma/H}
\det(Q\upharpoonright (A\cap C)).
\]

The determinantal inclusion probabilities factor, so uniqueness of the discrete DPP yields the exact product decomposition

\[
\boxed{
P^Q=\bigotimes_{xH\in\Gamma/H}P^{Q_H}.
}
\tag{4.3}
\]

The identity coset is reducing. Functional calculus therefore gives the same spectral measure at `delta_e` for `Q` and `Q_H`, hence

\[
\operatorname{tr}_\Gamma\log|Q|
=\operatorname{tr}_H\log|Q_H|,
\]

including the `-infinity` case by spectral integration/regularization. This proves the first equality in (4.2); the same argument for `I-Q` proves the second.

Finally take monotone couplings on `H` witnessing

\[
Ber(a)^{\otimes H}\preccurlyeq P^{Q_H}
\preccurlyeq Ber(b)^{\otimes H},
\]

with

\[
a=FK_H(Q_H),\qquad b=1-FK_H(I-Q_H).
\]

Take these couplings independently on all right cosets. Their products are monotone couplings of (4.3) with `Ber(a)^{otimes Gamma}` and `Ber(b)^{otimes Gamma}`. Using (4.2) gives exactly the source densities on `Gamma`. QED.

If `H` is finite, sharpness also lifts. Restrict any competing product domination on `Gamma` back to the identity coset. The all-occupied event forces a lower density `c` to satisfy `c^{|H|} <= det Q_H = FK_H(Q_H)^{|H|}`, while the nonempty event applied to the upper domination forces `c >= 1-FK_H(I-Q_H)`.

## 5. Genuine source-class enlargement

This theorem is not merely another finite example.

Lyons’s Theorem 5.2 already proves the sharp product bounds for every translation-invariant determinantal process on `Z^d`. Therefore:

> If a sofic group `Gamma` contains a subgroup `H isomorphic to Z^d`, then every `Gamma`-equivariant positive contraction whose convolution kernel is supported in `H` satisfies the full sharp two-sided Conjecture 5.7 on the ambient group `Gamma`.

The ambient group may be infinite, nonabelian, and the subgroup may have infinite index. This is a genuinely broader source class obtained by a structural lifting criterion rather than by finite-volume checking.

Combining the same theorem with the cumulative R7-03 finite-group candidate proofs (orders at most four and `Z/5Z`) also lifts those results to subgroup-supported kernels on arbitrary sofic ambient groups. That corollary is labeled **CANDIDATE FULL for that structural class**, because the earlier finite proofs have not yet undergone the independent review required for an established full claim.
