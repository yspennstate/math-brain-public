## New proof: all invariant increasing events obey reflection monotonicity

\[

\[
\mathbf P^Q(E)=H_E(\lambda)=\mathbb E_\lambda h_E(J),

where `J` selects each Fourier mode independently with probability `lambda_j` and `P_J` is the corresponding Fourier projection.

Both `P^Q` and the product comparator are translation invariant. Averaging an arbitrary increasing test function over the group preserves monotonicity and expectation, and every invariant isotone function on the finite orbit poset is a positive combination of invariant upper-set indicators. ## Globalization and theorem obtained

Use the two reflections

\[
\rho_0(i)=-i,\qquad \rho_1(i)=1-i\pmod6.
\]

In log-spectrum coordinates, each geometric reflection symmetrization is an orthogonal projection. Alternating the two projections converges to their common fixed space. Since `rho_1 rho_0` is one-step rotation, the common fixed space is the constant vectors; preservation of the log sum makes the limit `(p,...,p)`.

Every invariant increasing event probability is nonincreasing along this symmetrization sequence. By continuity,

\[
\mathbf P^Q(E)\ge \mathbf P^{pI}(E)
\]

for every invariant increasing `E`, and the averaging argument upgrades this to every increasing event. Therefore

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{Ber}(\operatorname{FK}(Q))^{\otimes6}
\preccurlyeq \mathbf P^Q
}
\]

for every `Z/6Z`-equivariant positive contraction.

Applying the same lower theorem to `I-Q` and complementing configurations gives

\[
\boxed{
\mathbf P^Q
\preccurlyeq
\operatorname{Ber}(1-\operatorname{FK}(I-Q))^{\otimes6}.
}
\]

The full-set and empty-set probabilities force the two constants to be sharp. Thus this is a **candidate full proof of Conjecture 5.7 on the entire finite group `Z/6Z`**, not merely an event family.
