## New theorem 1: single diagonal-gauge eigenpair symmetrization

Let `D` be a diagonal unitary involution on a finite ground set, and suppose a positive contraction has an orthogonal decomposition

\[
Q=Q_W\oplus aP_u\oplus bP_v,
\]

where `W` is `D`-invariant, `[Q_W,D|_W]=0`, and `Du=v`, `Dv=u`. Put `g=sqrt(ab)` and

\[
\widetilde Q=Q_W\oplus gP_u\oplus gP_v.
\]

Then the run proves the full-process order

\[
\boxed{\mathbf P^{\widetilde Q}\preccurlyeq\mathbf P^Q}
\]

for every increasing event, together with `det(tilde Q)=det Q`.

The proof is not a principal-minor surrogate. Diagonalize `Q_W` jointly with `D`, condition the DPP spectral mixture on an arbitrary retained subset `R` of those common eigenvectors, and set

\[
h_{00}=P^{P_R}(A),\quad h_{10}=P^{P_R+P_u}(A),\quad
h_{01}=P^{P_R+P_v}(A),\quad h_{11}=P^{P_R+P_u+P_v}(A).
\]

Projection-kernel monotonicity gives `h00<=h10,h01<=h11`; diagonal-gauge equivalence gives `h10=h01=:h1`. Therefore, conditionally on `R`,

\[
F(a,b)-F(g,g)
=(a+b-2\sqrt{ab})(h_1-h_{00})
=(\sqrt a-\sqrt b)^2(h_1-h_{00})\ge0.
\]

Averaging over `R` proves stochastic domination of the entire process. This is the main new lemma of the run.

## New theorem 2: central odd generalized-dihedral lifting

\[

Consequently, a full Lyons theorem on `A` lifts to the full sharp two-sided theorem for every central kernel on `Dih(A)`.
