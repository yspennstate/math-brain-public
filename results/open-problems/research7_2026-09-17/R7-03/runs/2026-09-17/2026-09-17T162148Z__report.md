## 4. (a) Exact blocking formula

The first research run proved, for a hypothetical order-19 counterexample with minimum outdegree eight, the exact slack identity

`z = h + 2E + L + C`,

where all five quantities are nonnegative integers:

- `z = #{x : q_x=0}`;
- `h` counts vertices of outdegree at least ten;
- `E` is excess outdegree above ten on those vertices;
- `L` is total source-side excess over the lower bounds for `|U(v)|`;
- `C` is total target-capacity slack in `|P(x)| <= 2q_x-1`.

The third research run proved the entire equality branch `z=0` impossible. Thus the first remaining slice is `z=1`, and the blocking equation becomes

```text
                 1 = h + 2E + L + C.                 (BLOCK)
```

Purely arithmetically, with no new graph-theoretic claim, `(BLOCK)` leaves exactly three one-defect types:

`E=0` and `(h,L,C)` is one of

`(1,0,0)`, `(0,1,0)`, `(0,0,1)`.

The global double count is therefore exhausted: on `z=1` it does not distinguish the surviving configurations, because the single unit of discrepancy can live as one high-degree defect, one source defect, or one target-capacity defect.
