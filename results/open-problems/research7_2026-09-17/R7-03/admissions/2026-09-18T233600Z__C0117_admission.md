## Exact outside source

Jian Ding, James R. Lee, and Yuval Peres, *Cover times, blanket times, and majorizing measures*, **Annals of Mathematics 175 (2012), 1409–1471**, DOI `10.4007/annals.2012.175.3.8`.

Primary source page: https://annals.math.princeton.edu/2012/175-3/p08

Exact locator: **Section 5, Question 5.2, printed page 1466 (PDF page 58 / zero-indexed image page 57 in the official PDF).**

Source setting: `G=(V,E)` is a finite connected graph; `tau_cov` is the first time simple random walk has visited every vertex; `t_cov(G)=max_v E_v tau_cov`; `t_hit` is the maximal expected hitting time. Immediately before Question 5.2 the authors recall Aldous's qualitative concentration when `t_cov(G_n)/t_hit(G_n) -> infinity`.

Exact source question, paraphrased without changing quantifiers: determine whether the standard deviation of `tau_cov` is universally bounded by a constant multiple of `t_hit`, and whether `(tau_cov-t_cov)/t_hit` has an exponentially decaying tail with a universal constant rate. The source's own start/centering convention controls; this worker will not silently replace it by a stationary-start, transitive, reversible-chain, or continuous-time-only statement.
