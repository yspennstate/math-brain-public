## Stronger local lemma from the second recovery

Indeed capacity equality at `x` makes `P(x)` a regular tournament: if `q=q_x`, then `|P(x)|=2q-1`, every source has all `9-q` vertices of `T(x)\P(x)` as outneighbors, and therefore each source has internal outdegree `q-1`. Every vertex of a regular tournament is a king, so the second target `y` cannot lie in `P(x)`. It also cannot lie in `T(x)\P(x)`, because `p` points directly to every such vertex. Hence `y` lies in `N^-(x)`, so `y->x`. Reversing the roles of `x,y` gives `x->y`, impossible in an oriented graph.
