## 2. B2 — attack the negation directly on the group

Assume for contradiction that there is a nonconstant right-`H`-invariant source-valid left `mu`-quasiharmonic function `Phi:G->C`, with defect `c`, so

\[
\sum_{n\ge1}p_n\Phi(\tau_n g)=\Phi(g)+c
\tag{2}
\]

for every `g`, with the source convolution defined/integrable.

Because `Phi` is right-`H`-invariant, its value depends only on `g(0)`.  Put

\[
a:=\Phi(e),\qquad b_k:=\Phi(g)\quad\text{for any }g\text{ with }g(0)=k.
\]

This is well-defined: `g(0)=g'(0)` iff `g^{-1}g' in H`, hence `g'H=gH`.

Now fix `k>=1` and choose `g` with `g(0)=k`.  Left multiplication by `tau_k` sends `g(0)` to `0`, whereas every `tau_n`, `n!=k`, fixes the point `k`.  Therefore (2) is exactly

\[
p_k a+(1-p_k)b_k=b_k+c,
\]

so

\[
p_k(a-b_k)=c,
\qquad
b_k=a-\frac{c}{p_k}.
\tag{3}
\]

At `g=e`, equation (2) requires the series

\[
\sum_{k\ge1}p_k b_k
\tag{4}
\]

to exist (indeed the source hypothesis is stronger: `mu`-integrability).  But by (3),

\[
p_kb_k=p_ka-c\longrightarrow-c.
\]

If `c!=0`, the terms of (4) do not tend to zero, impossible.  Thus `c=0`, and then (3) gives `b_k=a` for all `k`.  Since every right coset has one of these labels, `Phi` is constant.

This is a complete group-level proof.  It uses neither the quotient Markov-chain notation nor any external theorem.

**B2 outcome:** the supposed counterexample dies directly in the source group equation; no coset-orientation or quotient-converse assumption is needed.

## 3. B14 — isolate the action-theoretic mechanism

The proof above is not tied to the numerical labels on `N_0`.  The actual mechanism is the following source-valid abstract lemma.

### Lemma (transitive star-action obstruction)

Let a countable group `G` act transitively on a countably infinite set `X`; fix `o in X` and let `H=Stab_G(o)`.  Suppose there is a family of involutions `{s_x:x in X\{o}}` such that

1. `s_x(o)=x` and `s_x(x)=o`;
2. `s_x(y)=y` for every `y notin {o,x}`;
3. the `s_x` semigroup-generate `G`;
4. `mu(s_x)=p_x>0` with `sum_x p_x=1`.

Then every source-valid left `mu`-quasiharmonic function on `G/H` is constant.

### Proof

Write its value at `o` as `a` and at `x` as `b_x`; let the quasiharmonic defect be `c`.  At `x`, only `s_x` changes the state, hence

\[
p_xa+(1-p_x)b_x=b_x+c,
\]

so `p_x(a-b_x)=c` for every `x`.  At the center `o`, the left convolution is the series `sum_x p_xb_x`.  If `c!=0`, its terms are `p_xa-c -> -c`, so the series cannot exist.  Hence `c=0`, and then every `b_x=a`. ∎

For the admitted example take `X=N_0`, `o=0`, and `s_k=(0 k)`.  Thus the construction depends only on the star Schreier action plus positive edge weights; the coordinate labeling and the special choice `2^{-k}` are inessential.

**B14 outcome:** the proof is now invariantly formulated in the language of a transitive group action and stabilizer, which is exactly the source's homogeneous-space setting.

## 4. B12 — conserved edge flux and why convolution powers add nothing

Define the oriented edge flux from the center to leaf `k` by

\[
J_k:=p_k\bigl(a-b_k\bigr).
\]

The leaf quasiharmonic equation says

\[
\boxed{J_k=c\quad\text{for every }k.}
\tag{5}
\]

Thus the defect is a conserved identical flux along every ray of an infinite-degree star.  The center equation would have to sum the opposite fluxes:

\[
(Pu)(0)-u(0)
=\sum_{k\ge1}p_k(b_k-a)
=-\sum_{k\ge1}J_k.
\tag{6}
\]

If `c!=0`, every summand in (6) equals `-c`, so the center flux is not even conditionally summable.  This is the exact structural obstruction: **a nonzero constant Poisson forcing would require the same nonzero current through infinitely many star rays, producing infinite total center flux.**

The tempting convolution-power identity is valid whenever the successive sums exist:

\[
Pu=u+c\quad\Longrightarrow\quad P^m u=u+mc.
\tag{7}
\]

For this example it yields no independent contradiction because a nonzero `c` already makes the first center convolution undefined.  In particular, attempting optional stopping at the null-recurrent return time is unnecessary and would introduce avoidable integrability questions.  The one-step conserved-flux identity (5)-(6) is the sharp argument.

**B12 outcome:** the exact conserved quantity is the ray current `J_k`; it explains why the first-step source domain, rather than a delicate recurrence theorem, kills every nonzero defect.
