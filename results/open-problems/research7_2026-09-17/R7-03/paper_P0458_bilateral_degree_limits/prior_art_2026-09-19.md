## 3. Fixed-k prior art: Erlang weighted tree

M. Moharrami, V. Subramanian, M. Liu, and R. Sundaresan, **The Erlang weighted tree, a new branching process**, *Random Structures & Algorithms* 64 (2024), 537–624, DOI `10.1002/rsa.21180` (first published online 23 October 2023).

The paper proves that the La–Kabkab finite graph model has the Erlang weighted tree as a local weak limit and, in Theorem 5.1, characterizes the root-degree distribution. Specializing the potential degree distribution to a point mass at a fixed integer `kappa` gives the fixed-`kappa` degree law relevant to `LK(n,kappa)`.

This package therefore does **not** claim fixed finite `k` as new. It uses that theorem only to complete the regime classification. Because the finite-graph root degree is bounded by `kappa`, local weak convergence also gives convergence of its first two moments, so standardization is legitimate whenever the limiting variance is nonzero.

## 6. Mechanism comparison

The source's main technical difficulty is dependence among bilateral edge-retention events. The new mechanism is more specific than a generic dependency-graph approximation.

### Interior regime

Condition on the root's `(k+1)`st order statistic `X`. Two root-retention indicators then share exactly one non-root random edge. Conditioning on that one edge makes them independent. The resulting pair covariance is controlled by the exact beta integral

`int_0^1 C(n-3,k-1) u^(k-1)(1-u)^(n-k-2) du = 1/(n-2)`.

This reduces the full dependence problem to a one-edge influence bound and shows that conditional Bernoulli noise is negligible at the natural scale `s_n=sqrt(k(n-k)/n)`.

### Fixed-complement regime

For `r=n-1-k` fixed, the correct coordinates are the endpoint point-process variables `Y=n(1-U)`. On every bounded window, the finitely many candidate neighbors share only finitely many mutual edges, and each mutual edge affects a mark with probability `O(1/n)`. This produces asymptotically independent marks on a rate-one Poisson point process and hence the Gamma-mixed-Poisson law.

The EWT fixed-`k` analysis is related in spirit through Poisson/Erlang structure, but its asymptotic regime and branching-process mechanism are different from both of these growing-`k` arguments.
