## Outside source and exact target

Primary outside source: Mor Harchol-Balter, *Open problems in queueing theory inspired by datacenter computing*, Queueing Systems 97 (2021), 3–37, DOI 10.1007/s11134-020-09684-6, Section 5 (“Limited fork–join model for parallel jobs”), printed p. 18 / PDF page 16 in the archived rendering, paragraph beginning “While good progress has been made…”. Public source URL: `https://www.pdl.cmu.edu/PDL-FTP/CloudComputing/QUESTA21.pdf`.

The paragraph credits Wang et al. for asymptotic independence when `k(n)=o(n^(1/4))` and asks how much larger `k` can be while asymptotic independence still holds.

The precise model inherited from the cited theorem is the limited fork–join model of Weina Wang, Mor Harchol-Balter, Haotian Jiang, Alan Scheller-Wolf, and R. Srikant, *Delay Asymptotics and Bounds for Multi-Task Parallel Jobs*, Queueing Systems 91 (2019), 207–239 / Performance 2018 version, arXiv:1710.00296:

1. There are `n` FCFS unit-speed single-server queues.
2. Jobs arrive as a Poisson process of rate `Lambda(n)=n*lambda/k(n)`.
3. Every job consists of `k(n) <= n` tasks and chooses `k(n)` **distinct** servers uniformly at random, independently from job to job, sending one task to each chosen queue.
4. Task service times are iid with common cdf `G`, mean `m1=1/mu`, finite second moment `m2<infinity`, and are independent of routing and arrivals.
5. Each individual queue therefore has Poisson task rate `lambda` and load `rho=lambda*m1=lambda/mu<1`.
6. The source notion of asymptotic independence is convergence of the joint steady-state workload distribution of any `k(n)` queues to the product of its one-queue M/G/1 stationary marginals; this implies the corresponding task-delay independence.

**Source-level closure criterion.** Determine the maximal growth scale of `k(n)` for which this `k(n)`-dimensional stationary product approximation holds under the above source hypotheses, with both a sufficient theorem and a source-valid obstruction/necessity statement. A theorem only for exponential service, a fixed number of queues, finite time, or a stricter invented model is PARTIAL.
