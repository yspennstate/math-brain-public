The source first recalls the network RSP model introduced in Aletti–Crimaldi–Ghiglietti: for `N>=2`, binary actions and a nonnegative interaction matrix `A` with `A^T 1 = 1`, assumed irreducible, the coordinate actions are conditionally independent given the past and have conditional means `A^T Z_n`. Open Problem 16 adds a forcing vector `p in [0,1]^N` and constant reinforcement/freezing parameters `r_r,r_f`:

`Z_{n+1} = (1-r_r-r_f) Z_n + r_r X_{n+1} + r_f p`,  
`E[X_{n+1}|F_n] = A^T Z_n`.

The source asks (i) for the convergence/nonconvergence behavior at fixed parameters and (ii) whether, in the regime `r_f ~ c r_r^2`, `r_r -> 0`, the discrete-time process can be rescaled to a proper continuous-time limit. The source heuristic writes a singular interacting-Wright–Fisher expression whose meaning/asymptotics are explicitly said to be unknown.

For the inherited conditional-independence hypothesis, see the source model's cited parent paper Aletti–Crimaldi–Ghiglietti, *Synchronization of reinforced stochastic processes with a network-based interaction*, Ann. Appl. Probab. 27 (2017), where the binary coordinate actions are explicitly conditionally independent given the past.
