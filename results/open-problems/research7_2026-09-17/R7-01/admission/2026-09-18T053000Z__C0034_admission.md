## Exact outside source

Source locator: **Question 2.8**, p. 6 of the author preprint / p. 142 of the published proceedings.

For a denumerable set `E`, let `P^H` denote the determinantal probability measure associated with the orthogonal projection onto a closed subspace `H <= l^2(E)`. If

`H = H_1 ⊕ H_2`

orthogonally, Question 2.8 asks whether there exists a coupling `(X_1,X_2)` of `P^{H_1}` and `P^{H_2}` whose union `X_1 ∪ X_2` has law `P^H`. The source notes the full-space case `H=l^2(E)` and numerical evidence. In the finite-rank finite-ground-set case cardinalities imply any source-valid union coupling is automatically disjoint almost surely.
