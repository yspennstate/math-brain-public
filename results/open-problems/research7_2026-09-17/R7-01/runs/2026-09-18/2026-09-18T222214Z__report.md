## Substantive source result 2 — C0160's residual is a scope mismatch

Exact source family recovered: Häggström--Jonasson, *Uniqueness and non-uniqueness in percolation theory*, Probability Surveys 3 (2006), 289--344. The C0160 row itself encodes iid Bernoulli bond percolation and acknowledges that uniqueness monotonicity is proved on transitive graphs, then attempts to preserve a residual by moving to quasi-transitive and general invariant dependent settings.

The quasi-transitive Bernoulli part is already standard theorem-level prior art. Hutchcroft's 2019 GAFA paper explicitly recalls that Häggström--Peres--Schonmann / Schonmann proved that if `G` is quasi-transitive then `G[p]` has a unique infinite cluster almost surely for every `p > p_u`. Tang's 2018 work further identifies `p_u` for any quasi-transitive graph with the connectivity-decay threshold.

Thus the literal Bernoulli uniqueness-monotonicity source scope is known. Moving to a general invariant dependent family changes both the object and required hypotheses/coupling and cannot silently keep the same canonical source ID open.
