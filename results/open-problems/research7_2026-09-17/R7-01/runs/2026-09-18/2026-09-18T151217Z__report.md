## 3. Exact blocking formula

The cumulative proof has already isolated the Perron slow coordinate. Put

`epsilon=r_r`, `f_epsilon=r_f`, `Y_n=pi^T Z_n`,
`P=I-1 pi^T`, `W_n=PZ_n`,
`B=(I-Q)|_{pi^Tw=0}`,

where `pi^TQ=pi^T`. On the transverse hyperplane the exact recursion is

`W_{n+1}=T_epsilon W_n+f_epsilon Pp+epsilon Pxi_{n+1}`,

`T_epsilon=I-epsilon B-f_epsilon I`.

Iteration gives the exact decomposition

`W_n
 =T_epsilon^n W_0
 +f_epsilon sum_{j=0}^{n-1}T_epsilon^jPp
 +epsilon sum_{k=1}^n T_epsilon^(n-k)Pxi_k`.                 (3.1)

At the critical scaling `f_epsilon=O(epsilon^2)`, the second term is uniformly `O(epsilon)`, and the third term is `o_P(1)` uniformly for `n <= T/epsilon^2` by the already-proved stable-kernel martingale estimate. The only order-one term near slow time zero is therefore

`T_epsilon^n W_0`.                                                   (3.2)

On slow time `t=epsilon^2 n`, for the initial layer this behaves as

`T_epsilon^floor(t/epsilon^2) W_0
   ~ exp[-(t/epsilon)B] W_0`.                                       (3.3)

For every fixed `t>0`, (3.3) tends to zero; at `t=0` it equals `W_0`. Hence, if `W_0^epsilon -> w_0 != 0`, there is no right-continuous fixed limiting transverse path on `[0,T]` that both retains the actual initial state and is zero at every positive time. In particular, no Skorokhod `D([0,T])` limit can hide this boundary layer: admissible time changes fix zero, and a candidate path with value `w_0` at zero and value zero immediately afterward is not right-continuous.

This is the exact blocker to compressing all cumulative work into one unqualified vector-valued slow-time convergence statement. It is **not** a failure of the scalar diffusion theorem. The scalar coordinate already satisfies

`Y^epsilon => Y`,

`dY_t=c(pbar-Y_t)dt+sqrt(kappa Y_t(1-Y_t))dB_t`,

`kappa=sum_j pi_j^2`,

and the vector converges to `1Y` on every `[delta,T]`, `delta>0`, or on `[0,T]` when the initial transverse component vanishes. The remaining mathematical issue is the source-facing formulation for arbitrary nonsynchronized starts.

`t_epsilon=epsilon^2 n_epsilon

The transverse second-moment estimate from the cumulative proof gives

`E||W_(n_epsilon)||^2

At the same matching time the slow coordinate has barely moved. From
