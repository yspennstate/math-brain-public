## 3. Method B14 — canonical gauge-invariant signed coupling for every finite decomposition

### Idea

Instead of searching directly for a nonnegative transport table, first construct a canonical **signed** table whose three marginals are already exactly correct, and isolate the entire source difficulty as a positivity correction inside the kernel of the marginal map.

Let `E` be finite, `H=H1 \oplus H2`, and let `r1=dim H1`, `r2=dim H2`.  Choose orthonormal row matrices `V1,V2` spanning `H1,H2`, and let

`W=[V1;V2]`.

For `|A|=r1`, `|B|=r2`, `A cap B=empty`, and `T=A union B`, set

`p_A=det V1[:,A]`,

`q_B=det V2[:,B]`,

`r_T=det W[:,T]`.

Let `eps(A,B)` be the block-Laplace sign that moves the increasing list of `A` before the increasing list of `B` inside `T`.  Define

`s_{A,B}=eps(A,B) p_A q_B conjugate(r_T)`.                      (3.1)

### Theorem 3.1

The complex array `s` has exactly the desired marginals:

`sum_{B disjoint A} s_{A,B}=|p_A|^2`,                          (3.2)

`sum_{A disjoint B} s_{A,B}=|q_B|^2`,                          (3.3)

and for every `T`,

`sum_{A disjoint B, A union B=T}s_{A,B}=|r_T|^2`.              (3.4)

Hence

`x^0_{A,B}=Re s_{A,B}`

is a real signed solution of the full finite coupling equations.

### Proof

Block Laplace expansion gives

`r_T=sum_{A union B=T}eps(A,B)p_A q_B`.

Multiplication by `conjugate(r_T)` proves (3.4).

For (3.2), identify the unit decomposable exterior vectors of `H1,H2` with

`p in wedge^{r1} C^E`, `q in wedge^{r2} C^E`.

Because `H1` and `H2` are orthogonal, exterior multiplication by the unit vector `q` is an isometry on `wedge^{r1}H1`.  Its adjoint contraction sends `p wedge q` back to `p`.  In coordinates,

`conjugate(p_A)=sum_{B disjoint A}eps(A,B)q_B conjugate(r_{A union B})`.

Multiplying by `p_A` gives (3.2).  Interchanging the two blocks gives (3.3).  Taking real parts preserves all three real marginals.  QED.

### Symmetry quotient

The signed table (3.1) is intrinsic.  A basis change `V1 -> U1V1`, `V2 -> U2V2` multiplies `p_A,q_B,r_T` by `det U1`, `det U2`, and `(det U1)(det U2)` respectively, which cancel in (3.1).  Coordinate phases cancel because every coordinate in `T=A union B` appears once in the numerator and once conjugated in `r_T`; coordinate permutations merely relabel atoms with the Laplace sign.

If `M` denotes the fixed marginal-incidence map, finite C0034 is therefore exactly the cone-shift problem

`find k in ker M such that x^0+k >= 0`.                         (3.5)

This is a real reduction of the obstruction: all marginal equalities are solved canonically, and only destructive Pluecker interference/positivity remains.

Outcome B14: **new universal structural theorem, but not universal nonnegativity.**

## 4. Method B16 — exact strictly positive coupling at the first source-unknown ambient size

### Idea

`H1=span(rows 0,1)`,

`H2=span(rows 2,3)`,

`H3=span(rows 4,5)`.

Then `C^6=H1 \oplus H2 \oplus H3`, all three subspaces have rank two, and each associated rank-two projection DPP has the same edge law

`w_{ij}=(1/36)|omega^j-omega^i|^2`.

`1/20, 1/20, 3/20, 7/10, 1/20`,                               (4.2)

`1/2, 3/2, 1`.

`1/12, 1/4, 1/3`,

`sum_{A contains i}a_A + sum_{B contains i}b_B

The six equations (5.2) have one dependence modulo the two total-mass equations, so they contribute five further independent relations.  Thus the seven displayed relations account for the complete codimension `45-38=7` of `im M`.

`diag(P_{H1})+diag(P_{H2})=diag(P_H)`.

`Mx0=b0`, `min_j x0_j=1/720`.

`||R delta||_infinity < 1/720`,

and therefore `x>0` coordinatewise.
