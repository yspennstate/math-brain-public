## 3. Exact outside source recovered

Authors: **Antonio Agresti and Mark Veraar**.

Published source: **Nonlinear SPDEs and Maximal Regularity: An Extended Survey**, *Nonlinear Differential Equations and Applications NoDEA* **32**, article 123 (2025), published online **1 September 2025**, DOI `10.1007/s00030-025-01090-2`.

Preprint: arXiv `2501.18561`, first submitted **30 January 2025**.

Exact source locator: **Problem 7, “Reaction-diffusion with triangular structure”**, referring to system **(8.14)**. The published Springer full text places the problem in the final open-problems section; the repository's canonical preprint locator is `Problem7,PDF93;system8.14`.

System (8.14) is the componentwise reaction--diffusion SPDE

\[
\mathrm d u_i-\nu_i\Delta u_i\,\mathrm dt
=f_i(\cdot,u)\,\mathrm dt
+\sum_{n\ge1}\left[(b_{n,i}\cdot\nabla)u_i+g_{n,i}(\cdot,u)\right]\,\mathrm dW^n,
\qquad u_i(0)=u_{0,i}.
\]

The source notes that the components are coupled through `f` and `g`, while the leading diffusion/transport operators are componentwise.

## 4. Full source hypotheses and the material scope correction

Problem 7 asks for global well-posedness for `O=T^d` or `R^d`, `ell>=2`, transport coefficients `(b_{n,i})_{n>=1} in C^gamma(O;ell^2)` for some `gamma>0`, and a fixed polynomial exponent `h>1`, assuming the following structural conditions:

1. nonnegative initial data;
2. each `g_i:R^ell -> ell^2` globally Lipschitz and vanishing when its own coordinate is zero on the positive cone;
3. each `f_i` nonnegative on that boundary face;
4. polynomial local Lipschitz growth of order `h` for `f_i`;
5. linear mass control `sum_j f_j(u) <= K_0 + K_1 sum_j u_j`;
6. an invertible nonnegative lower-triangular matrix `R` and vector `r` satisfying the componentwise triangular estimate `R f(y) <= [1+sum_i y_i]r` for every nonnegative `y`.
