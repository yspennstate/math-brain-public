## 3. The exact remaining blocking formula

Write

\[

The exact one-step decomposition is

\[

\tag{3.1}
\]

where `xi` is a bounded martingale difference and its conditional covariance is order one under the inherited conditional-Bernoulli hypothesis.

Now do **not** prejudge the macroscopic clock. Let `m_\varepsilon` be the number of discrete steps per unit macroscopic time for an arbitrary scalar rescaling. The three source-relevant characteristic scales are

\[
D_\varepsilon:=m_\varepsilon\varepsilon
\quad\text{(network drift)},
\]

\[
F_\varepsilon:=m_\varepsilon f_\varepsilon
\sim c\,m_\varepsilon\varepsilon^2
\quad\text{(forcing drift)},
\]

and

\[
V_\varepsilon:=m_\varepsilon\varepsilon^2
\quad\text{(martingale quadratic variation)}.
\]

The exact scale identity is

\[
\boxed{
F_\varepsilon\sim cV_\varepsilon,
\qquad
\frac{V_\varepsilon}{D_\varepsilon}=\varepsilon\longrightarrow0.
}
\tag{3.2}
\]

This is the final source-interpretation blocker.

A single scalar clock cannot keep **both** the network interaction and the stochastic quadratic variation at finite nonzero strength:

- if `D_epsilon -> d in (0,infinity)`, then `V_epsilon=epsilon D_epsilon ->0`, so the martingale diffusion disappears and the forcing also disappears;
