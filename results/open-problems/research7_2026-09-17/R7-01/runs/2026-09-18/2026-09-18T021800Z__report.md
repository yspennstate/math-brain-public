> Is there a universal constant C such that for any n symmetric matrices H_1,...,H_n in R^{n x n} with ||H_k|| <= 1 there exist signs eps_1,...,eps_n in {-1,+1} with ||sum_k eps_k H_k|| <= C sqrt(n)?

This matches the source hypotheses and conclusion, not merely a low-rank or polylogarithmic-rank subcase. Therefore E0050 is **KNOWN / RESOLVED IN CURRENT LITERATURE** for admission purposes and must not be claimed by R7-01.
