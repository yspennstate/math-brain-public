## 5. B30 target selection: a balanced-cut colored facet orbit

Fix a balanced cut

`[6]=X sqcup Y`, `|X|=|Y|=3`,

and let `T_X` be the six edges internal to the two sides. For a color `r`, define

`t_r(X) = sum_{e in T_X} x^r_e`.

For every deterministic colored perfect matching and every permutation `(r,s,t)` of the colors,

` t_r(X) <= t_s(X)+t_t(X). `                                           (FACET)

The proof at a vertex is elementary but structurally useful. A perfect matching crosses a `3+3` cut in either one or three edges, hence uses either two or zero internal edges. If singled color `r` is internal, exactly one other color is internal, and the inequality is tight. If `r` is crossing, the other two colors are either both crossing or both internal, giving slack zero or two.

## 6. B11 — dualize the facet to one block-projection inequality

Specialize `(FACET)` to the Pluecker masses of an arbitrary orthogonal `2+2+2` flag. It suffices by symmetry to prove

` t_1 <= t_2+t_3. `

Let `P_X` be the coordinate projection onto the three coordinates in `X`. Express it in the orthonormal basis obtained by concatenating bases of `H1,H2,H3`; call the resulting rank-three projection `Q`. Write `Q` in `2 x 2` blocks `Q_rs` and set

`J=2Q-I_6`, `A_r=J_rr=2Q_rr-I_2`, `a_r=tr A_r`,

`h_rs=||J_rs||_F^2=4||Q_rs||_F^2`.

Because `J^2=I`, the trace of its `(r,r)` block gives

`tr(A_r^2)+h_rs+h_rt=2`.                                           (6.1)

Since `tr J=0`,

`a_1+a_2+a_3=0`.                                                     (6.2)

Cauchy--Binet identifies the same-side Pluecker mass as

`t_r = det(Q_rr)+det(I_2-Q_rr) = (1+det A_r)/2`.                    (6.3)

For a `2 x 2` matrix, `2 det A_r = (tr A_r)^2-tr(A_r^2)`. Combining this with (6.1) gives

`4 t_r = a_r^2+h_rs+h_rt`.                                          (6.4)

Hence, using (6.2),

`4(t_2+t_3-t_1)=2(h_23-a_2a_3)`.                                   (6.5)

Put `q_r=tr Q_rr`. Then `a_r=2(q_r-1)` and `h_23=4||Q_23||_F^2`, so

` t_2+t_3-t_1 = 2( ||Q_23||_F^2 - (q_2-1)(q_3-1) ). `              (6.6)

This is the exact B11 dual form of the colored facet.

`(N_2-1)(N_3-1) <= 0` pointwise.                                    (7.1)
