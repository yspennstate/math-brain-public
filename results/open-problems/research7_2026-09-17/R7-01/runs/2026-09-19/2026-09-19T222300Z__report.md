The source asks:

> For the 3-symbol Potts model, find the values for which the reconstruction problem is solvable on the `b`-ary tree.

The symmetric `q`-state channel is

\[

\qquad
M_{ij}=\varepsilon\quad(i\ne j),

\lambda_2=1-q\varepsilon.

A root state is broadcast independently down every edge through `M`. Reconstruction is solvable when the total-variation distance between level-`n` configurations conditional on two distinct root states does not tend to zero. For F0119 one fixes `q=3` and seeks the full exact region in `(b,\varepsilon)` or equivalently `(b,\lambda_2)`.

\[
