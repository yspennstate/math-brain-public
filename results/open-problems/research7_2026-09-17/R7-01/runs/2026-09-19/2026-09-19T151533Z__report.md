## 4. New mathematical diagnosis: the literal printed formula is impossible

The key point was missed by the existing intake attempt.

Let `A_p` be the local event that the origin is open and all six nearest neighbors in the triangular lattice are closed. Then `C={0}` on `A_p`, so `|C|^{-1}=1`. By independence,

`P_p(A_p)=p(1-p)^6`.

Therefore

`κ(p)=E_p(|C|^{-1}) >= p(1-p)^6`.

Consequently

`liminf_{p -> 1/2} κ(p) >= (1/2)(1/2)^6 = 1/128 > 0`.

But the source's literal exponent is

`2+α = 2-2/3 = 4/3`,

so

`|1/2-p|^{2+α+o(1)} = |1/2-p|^{4/3+o(1)} -> 0`.

Thus the literal displayed statement cannot hold for `κ(p)` itself.

This is an exact local certificate, not a numerical experiment and not an unproved asymptotic lemma.
