## 4. Substantive source/current reconciliation

### 4.1 MLE branch

Emmanuel Abbe, João M. Pereira and Amit Singer, *Estimation in the group action channel*, arXiv:1801.04366 / ISIT 2018, treats a compact-group observation model that explicitly contains one-dimensional cyclic multireference alignment.

Its Theorem II.2 proves that the marginalized MLE converges in probability to the true signal orbit when

`N / sigma^(2 d_bar) -> infinity`,

where `d_bar` is the moment-order cutoff separating the true orbit from alternatives.

For fixed finite `L` and fixed finite `sigma`, this yields MLE consistency as `N -> infinity` in the identifiable MRA model. It therefore proves the MLE alternative written directly into Open Problem 10.4(1).

### 4.2 Sample-complexity branch

Amelia Perry, Jonathan Weed, Afonso S. Bandeira, Philippe Rigollet and Amit Singer, *The Sample Complexity of Multireference Alignment*, SIAM J. Math. Data Sci. 1(3) (2019), arXiv:1707.00943, proves that for generic MRA the low-SNR sample complexity is proportional to `SNR^(-3)`. Under the standard fixed-signal normalization, `SNR ~ sigma^(-2)`, hence the generic low-SNR scale is `sigma^6`.

This matches the source's second question at the intended asymptotic sample-complexity level. It is also later work coauthored by the original problem poser.
