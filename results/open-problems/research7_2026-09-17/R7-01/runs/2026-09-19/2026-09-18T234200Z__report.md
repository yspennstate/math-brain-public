## 6. New theorem: exact cubic threshold for every service law with a positive atom

Let `P_{n,k}` be the stationary joint workload law of the `k` tagged queues and `pi_lambda` the one-queue M/G/1 stationary workload law. Assume, in addition to the source hypotheses, that

\[
q:=P(X=x)>0
\]

for some `x>0`.

Then

\[
\boxed{
 d_{TV}(P_{n,k},\pi_\lambda^{\otimes k})\to0
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 k^3/n\to0.
}
\]

The sufficiency direction is the preceding full-class theorem. The new work is necessity.

### 6.1 Multi-hit and singleton rates

For one parent routing set `S`, put `N=|S cap K|`. Let

\[
p_n=P(N\ge2),
\qquad
R_n=\frac{n\lambda}{k}p_n.
\]

The multi-hit parents form a Poisson process of rate `R_n`.

For a fixed tagged queue,

\[
a_{n,k}=\frac{\binom{n-k}{k-1}}{\binom{n-1}{k-1}},
\qquad
\lambda_e=\lambda a_{n,k}
\]

is the rate of the independent stream of parent jobs whose tagged intersection is exactly that one queue.

### 6.2 Association removes the old low-load restriction

Wang et al. prove the steady-state workload vector is associated. The indicators `1_{W_i=0}` and `1_{W_j=0}` are decreasing, so their negatives are increasing and association yields

\[
P(W_i=0,W_j=0)
\ge P(W_i=0)P(W_j=0)
=(1-\rho)^2.
\]

Because the multi-hit stream is a state-independent Poisson thinning, PASTA gives the same bound immediately before a multi-hit parent arrival.

This is the key improvement over the preceding deterministic argument, which used only `P(both idle)>=1-2rho` and therefore required `rho<1/2` in its normalized deterministic example.

### 6.3 The independent product has no positive diagonal atoms

The Pollaczek–Khinchine geometric-sum representation gives

\[
W\overset d=\sum_{r=1}^{J}Y_r,
\]

where `P(J=0)=1-rho` and the equilibrium-service law of `Y_r` has distribution function

\[
F_e(t)=\frac1{m_1}\int_0^t\bar G(s)\,ds.
\]

Thus the positive part of `pi_lambda` is absolutely continuous even when `G` itself is atomic. Therefore

\[
\pi_\lambda^{\otimes k}
\{\exists i<j:\ 0<W_i=W_j\}=0.
\]

### 6.4 A recent multi-hit job creates positive diagonal mass

Let `U` be the age of the most recent multi-hit parent. Conditional on `R_n>0`,

\[
P(U\in du)=R_ne^{-R_nu}\,du.
\]

At this job choose uniformly one pair among the tagged queues it hit. With probability at least `(1-rho)^2` the pair was idle just before the arrival. With probability `q^2` the two new service requirements both equal `x`. By definition there is no later multi-hit parent. Conditional on `U=u`, the probability of no later singleton arrival to either member of the pair is `e^{-2lambda_e u}`.

Therefore for every `0<epsilon<x`, on an event of probability at least

\[
(1-\rho)^2q^2
\int_0^\epsilon
R_ne^{-(R_n+2\lambda_e)u}\,du,
\]

the two queues both have workload exactly `x-u>0` at time zero. Hence

\[
\boxed{
 d_{TV}(P_{n,k},\pi_\lambda^{\otimes k})
\ge
(1-\rho)^2q^2
\frac{R_n}{R_n+2\lambda_e}
\left(1-e^{-(R_n+2\lambda_e)\epsilon}\right).
}
\]

Thus TV convergence forces `R_n->0`.

### 6.5 `R_n->0` forces the cubic condition

The preceding artifact's hypergeometric factorial-moment argument is source-valid and noncircular. For

\[
Y=(N)_2,
\]

one has

\[
E(N)_r=\frac{(k)_r^2}{(n)_r},\qquad r=2,3,4,
\]

and

\[
Y^2=(N)_4+4(N)_3+2(N)_2.
\]

Paley–Zygmund yields

\[
p_n\ge\frac1{128}\min\left(\frac{k^4}{n^2},1\right).
\]

Consequently

\[
R_n\ge
\frac{\lambda}{128}\frac nk
\min\left(\frac{k^4}{n^2},1\right).
\]

If `k^2/n<=1`, this is at least `lambda k^3/(128n)`. If `k^2/n>=1`, it is at least `lambda/128`. Therefore `R_n->0` implies `k^3/n->0`, completing necessity.

## 7. New critical-window asymptotics

Put

\[
Z=\binom N2.
\]

Then

\[
EZ=\frac{(k)_2^2}{2(n)_2}.
\]

Since `p_n=P(Z>=1)`,

\[
EZ-p_n=E[(Z-1)1_{Z\ge1}].
\]

For every integer `N>=2`,

\[
\binom N2-1\le2\binom N3,
\]

so

\[
0\le EZ-p_n
\le\frac{(k)_3^2}{3(n)_3}.
\]

Hence

\[
0\le1-\frac{p_n}{EZ}
\le\frac23\frac{(k-2)^2}{n-2}.
\]

If

\[
\frac{k^3}{n}\to\tau\in(0,\infty),
\]

then `k^2/n->0`, and therefore

\[
p_n=\frac{k^4}{2n^2}(1+o(1)),
\qquad
R_n\to\frac{\lambda\tau}{2},
\qquad
\lambda_e\to\lambda.
\]

Substitution into the diagonal-event bound gives the explicit critical separation

\[
\boxed{
\liminf d_{TV}(P_{n,k},\pi_\lambda^{\otimes k})
\ge
(1-\rho)^2q^2
\frac{\tau}{\tau+4}
\left(1-e^{-\lambda(\tau/2+2)\epsilon}\right)>0
}
\]

for every `0<epsilon<x`.

So the cubic threshold is not only necessary for atomic service; exactly at `k ~ c n^(1/3)` the model retains an explicit positive amount of dependence in total variation.
