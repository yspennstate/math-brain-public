Numbered locator: Section 3, Open problems, item (13), printed PDF p. 28.

The literal endpoint is:

> “Prove a distributional limit theorem in any of the examples discussed in this paper.”

This is an existential request over examples, not a universal demand over every model and every cost law.

The source's own Section 2.4 defines the random assignment problem by

`C_n = min_{pi in S_n} sum_i a_{i,pi(i)}`

Therefore `Uniform[0,1]` random assignment is unambiguously one of the examples discussed in the source, even though Chatterjee's separate lower-bound Theorem 2.9 assumes a cost law in `P^+(1)`.
