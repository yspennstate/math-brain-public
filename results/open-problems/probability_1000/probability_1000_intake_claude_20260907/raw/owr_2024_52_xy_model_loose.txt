The mini-workshop featured talks by almost all participants except organizers as well as an open problem session. The talks consisted of: a) three mini-courses of four lectures each and b) hour long seminars from the remaining participants. The mini-courses covered random currents for the XY model, random walk representations and triviality in the XY and Ising models, and connections between the spherical model and the Gaussian Free Field. The remaining seminars spanned a di- verse range of topics, such as novel probabilistic approaches to the BKT tran- sition in 2d, recent progress on high-dimensional spin systems and ﬁnite-size eﬀects, and novel geometric representations for correlations in spin systems.
