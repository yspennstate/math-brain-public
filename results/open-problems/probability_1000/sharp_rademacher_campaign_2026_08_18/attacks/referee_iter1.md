## 2. Lane ENTROPY (`attack_entropy.md`) — verdict: ALIVE WITH REPAIRS; central program currently blocked at a currency mismatch and an unproved band-coverage assumption

### What survives referee scrutiny (verified by me)

1. **Lemma NK (a)/(b) is correct.** I checked the proof: the ≤2^{dim V} cube-point bound for a subspace (coordinate functionals restrict to a spanning set of V*, valid over ℝ and odd 𝔽_p), the union bound in (a), the Markov + binomial-tail count in (b). The star calibration is exact (my recomputation: D ≤ (6−log₂46) + log₂(46/8) = 3, attained). This is a real, self-contained, field-uniform lemma.
2. **Referee-added consistency check the memo did not run, which it passes:** NK does not collide with the 2.409 floor. On the floor witness (product of t NAE triples) the selected residual b+c is active with probability exactly 1/3 per circuit (my count on the 6-point fibre), so NK(c)'s small-activity hypothesis (constant 2 < 2.409) does not apply there; NK's general form charges log₂(3/2) + 0.415 = 1 per circuit and reproduces d = t exactly. NK is calibrated on both dossier extremes and on the floor family. No conflict with S10's constant-one disproof either: §5a's certificate is an upper bound ⌈n/3⌉+1 ≥ n/3, consistent with S10's d_cb ≥ n/3.
3. **§4 one-dominant-batch sliver: derivation sound.** All inputs verified (κ = max_s a_s/c_s p.38; c_s ≥ max{β, ½log₂(a_s+1)−1} pp.31, S1 line 507; Σc_s ≤ ℓ p.31 / ≤ L p.39; convexity p.32; threshold p.36/(32)). The feedback step (a_{s*} ≳ log n·log log n, hence the dominant charge consumes the whole threshold budget) checks out.
4. **§7 quadric-rank necessary condition: proof correct** ((h·x)² = 1 forces M_h constant; the r(1+d)+d² count is right). This is the cheapest genuine refutation tool the campaign owns: any fibre with rank₂(A) ≫ r·ℓ(A) kills the unrooted form (1) outright.

### Objections

**E1 — Currency mismatch, and "which telescopes" is an assertion, not a proof. This is the central defect.** The rooted target (3) and the standing bounded-congestion theorem (p.39) are charged in the *conditional* defect L_{Q|W}. Lemma NK charges the *support defect* of whatever fibre it is applied to. For a rooted block, ℓ(block fibre) = [w − H(W)] + L_{Q|W} (chain rule; uniform measure), so an NK-paid bound overcharges by the boundary-entropy surplus w − H(W) — which is the *parent's* ledger money. In the memo's own Gap-3 bookkeeping the boundary near-kernel is "paid by NK against the parent's marginal defect, **which telescopes**" — that final clause is asserted nowhere-proved, and it is exactly the sibling-multiplicity failure mode: k children of one parent each drawing the same parent marginal defect pay it k times unless a coordination lemma exists. Consequently the memo's claim that NK + cluster lemma "would give the quotient clause (i) with a linear coefficient… a genuine widening of the bounded-congestion theorem" is **not established even conditionally**: as stated it widens the κ-dependence while *worsening* the budget variable from L_{Q|W} to ℓ_B ≥ L_{Q|W}. On the star the memo's own numbers show the charge is 3.58·L. Repair path (not done in the memo): a per-W-slice NK with max-over-slices defect, or an explicit sibling-coordination lemma — which is the same open bookkeeping the telescope reconstruction (problem\telescope_reconstruction_draft1.md) is trying to pin. The lane has silently re-derived the campaign's known hard joint and parked it under "which telescopes".

**E2 — NK's proven coverage band is plausibly empty for the residual class.** NK pays near-vanishing residuals (clause (c)) or the joint-vanishing log-probability (general (a)). On residuals with activity Θ(1) the charge is Θ(1) per dimension *independent of defect* — there NK is not a mechanism but a restatement: "span ≤ C·defect" ⟺ "joint vanishing probability ≥ 2^{−C·defect}", an equivalent-strength simultaneous small-ball statement. The class's rows are polylogarithmically dense (S1 p.66), and the memo's own §5b shows density pushes pure-boundary residual activity → 1; nothing anywhere shows class blocks carry *any* near-vanishing residual mass. The memo's strongest-case item 1 names the medium band as the danger; I extend it: the *active* band is the likelier home of the class, and there the program has no coverage argument at all. (Constructive flip side, worth keeping: NK converts clause (i) into a joint-anticoncentration statement — a legitimately new formulation that probabilistic tools might attack. But as written it is a reformulation, not a reduction.)

**E3 — The named next step (cluster forcing-mass lemma) has no proof route as proposed.** Its second inequality Σ_{a≥2} π_a ≤ C₁ℓ is claimed to follow because difference masses "telescope into the chain rule". They do not: an active difference (q_a − q₁)(x) = ±2 fixes the *values* of two dual rows, not any coordinate of x, so the §1 forcing-mass mechanism (which counts coordinate determinations) does not apply. The inequality is not refuted — it holds numerically on the star (my check: Σπ ≈ 8/9 vs ℓ = 1.245) — but its status is CONJECTURE with no identified toolkit, not "looks provable by the NK machinery". The memo's next step is therefore mislabeled as bounded.

**E4 — §5a's connector claim is overstated.** The solved rooted cycle's certificate is connector-*transverse*: R ∩ span{A} = 0, "zero boundary surcharge" (p.49, read verbatim). The slice-rooted family's certificate has R ∋ Σ_{i≥2}u_i, a nonzero *pure-boundary* direction, so it satisfies absorption only if the parent selects h₀ and pays exactly that residual — the S14 absorbed-alternative pattern (p.50: "cost becomes zero once the two boundary residuals have already been paid by ancestors"), i.e. conditional compatibility, strictly weaker than p.49's. "Connector-compatible in the same sense as E4" is wrong as stated; densifying the root *creates* a parent-paid obligation the s = 1 case does not have. Also the general-(s,n) certificate is verified only at seven small cases (s ≤ 5, n ≤ 7); the construction looks provable in ~10 lines — write the general proof or scope the consequence to "seven exact data points".

**E5 — §4's sliver is genuinely a sliver.** The one-dominant-batch geometry holds for ℓ ≤ log₂ log n − O(1) only. The admissible interval reaches ℓ ≲ rj/n, which at the class ceiling is polynomial in n/log²n; there the bound gives up to ℓ/τ₀ dense batches — thousands, not one. The memo's scoping sentence is technically honest but the framing ("the missing theorem only ever has to face one dominant dense direction per block in this window") invites over-generalization. Follow-on work must also respect the negative ledger's ban on "concluding a small residual from a single dense matching batch" (p.52): §4 locates geometry; it prices nothing.

**E6 — Conjecture B is honestly self-assessed** (indirect value, provably insufficient in the core regime, possible author's-pet bias — their words). No objection beyond agreeing with their own falsifier plan (exhaustive r = 6 over full-closed fibres). Low campaign priority.

### Entropy lane bottom line

The advertised *program* — NK + forcing-mass + cluster lemma ⇒ linear quotient clause — is blocked three ways (E1 currency/sibling, E2 band coverage, E3 unproved second half), and every one of the three lands on ground the campaign already knew was the hard part. **T1 — The interface conditional cannot be discharged from inside the dossier, and the memo's next step oversells the upgrade.** (25) is introduced in S1 as "the **existing** ternary basis-mass encoding" (line 461) — its derivation lives in the earlier packet tree, which is not reproduced in the dossier. And even a proved interface lemma upgrades W2/W3 only to "consistent with the displayed bookkeeping of an encoding whose consuming moment argument is unwritten" — the dossier itself says the transfer "must then be written explicitly and audited" (p.53). The ladder's lower rungs stay conditional on an unwritten proof's shape until Layer III is drafted. Risk 1 in the memo says this correctly; the next-step paragraph ("upgrades… from [CONDITIONAL] to dossier-grade bookkeeping") quietly forgets it.

**T2 — W1-rooted rests on the destroyed telescope.** "No per-level compounding appears in the dossier's description" — the description is one sentence (p.8/S6 p.113) about a file nobody can read. The reconstruction draft (T1a–c, filtration bookkeeping) supports a per-block γ_n passing through additively, but that draft is explicitly unrefereed and the campaign's own STATE forbids building on T1c until it is. Until then only W1-component stands on (25) alone — and a component-level certificate d_cb ≤ γ_n·ℓ with no tree assembly is a materially *stronger* per-object demand than the rooted blockwise statement, not obviously easier than W0.

**T3 — The crosspolytope retirement is oversold: the exclusion is component-scale only.** The 4.2 argument (basis-mass forces j > n − o(n) > k) operates on the crosspolytope as a *full component*. It does not exclude a log-scale crosspolytope **embedded as the dense batch of an admissible component**: r' ≈ c·log n·log log n crosspolytope-configured rows contribute marginal defect ≈ r' − log₂2r' ≤ ℓ (support defect is superadditive over coordinate marginals), which is admissible for high-ℓ slices (ℓ can reach ~rj/n ≫ r'), and such a batch has κ ≈ r'/log r' ≈ c·log n — *exactly the class's congestion signature*. So the crosspolytope mechanism plausibly persists inside admissible components; only its global-scale incarnation dies. The memo's closing half-sentence ("its warning still binds proof techniques") is right; the campaign-level advice ("set the crosspolytope aside as a certificate-side pathology") is dangerous and should not be adopted. Corollary worth keeping (referee synthesis, heuristic label): the class threat model **bifurcates in ℓ** — in the threshold window (ℓ ≤ log₂ log n) a crosspolytope-shaped dominant batch is *impossible* (its own marginal defect ≈ a_{s*} ≫ window ℓ), so there the entropy lane's slice-germ model is the right local picture (a slice form's charge ½log₂ s is exactly the two-point central-binomial scale, i.e. the germ is the charge-optimal dense batch); above the window, embedded-crosspolytope batches become admissible and certificates plausibly fail there, making counting (W3) the only visible route for that regime. Neither memo states this split; it reconciles them.

**T4 — The κ ≳ log n premise is the strong horn of a disjunction the dossier itself hedges.** S1 p.66 asserts the conjunction ("beyond (26) **and** κ ≳ log n in every useful basis"); the synthesis p.53 states "**either** has congestion at least of order log n… **or fails the common-lift condition**." The weaker synthesis form matters: a class block with bounded κ but common-lift failure already has quotient coefficient O(1) — for it, W1's coefficient relaxation buys *nothing*; the open content is absorption, which is binary and untouched by any coefficient. So the memo's headline ("the demanded absolute constant is a full log-factor stronger than the moment bookkeeping consumes") is right for the quotient clause on the high-κ horn and silent on the actually-hard clause. The campaign should not read W1 as "the problem got a log factor easier"; it should read it as "the *quotient* clause got a log factor easier, absorption is exactly as hard as yesterday, and on count routes absorption is bypassed only if the counting conditional (T1) holds."
