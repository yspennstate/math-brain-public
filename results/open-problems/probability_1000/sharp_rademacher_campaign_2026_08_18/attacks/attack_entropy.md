## 1. Where the exponential bridge loses to linear, in entropy terms

**[PROVED HERE, elementary]** Setting of p.33-34: after compression every row is $h_a(x)=q_a(x_C)+\lambda_a x_{j(a)}$, at most one private coordinate $j(a)\notin C$. On the fibre, $x_{j(a)}$ is *forced* exactly when $q_a(X_C)\ne 0$ and *free* exactly when $q_a(X_C)=0$ (evaluate the two sign options; this is the dossier's own free/forced dichotomy, p.34). Write $p_a=\Pr_A(q_a(X_C)\ne0)$ and, for an outside coordinate $j$, $p_j=\Pr_A(X_j\text{ determined by }X_C)$. By the entropy chain rule (order the core first, then the outside coordinates):
$$\ell \;\ge\; \ell_{\mathrm{ent}}(A_C)\;+\;\sum_{j\notin C}\bigl(1-H(X_j\mid X_C)\bigr)\;\ge\;\ell_{\mathrm{ent}}(A_C)+\sum_{j\notin C} p_j,$$
and $p_a\le p_{j(a)}$. So the **total forcing mass of all outside coordinates is at most $\ell$**, and each selected residual $q_a$ is a linear form on the core that vanishes on the fibre except on an event of probability $p_a$, with the $p_j$-masses summing to $\le\ell$. The dossier's never-free identity $|N_0|\le\ell$ (p.34; S6 tag (28) p.120) is the special case "$p_j=1$ exactly". The bridge then pays $\operatorname{span}(C)$; the linear target needs $\dim\operatorname{span}\{q_a\}=O(\ell)$. That is the loss, localized. What removes it is the next lemma.

## 2. Near-kernel dimension lemma (robust never-free identity)

**[PROVED HERE]** **Lemma NK.** Let $K=\mathbb R$ or $\mathbb F_p$, $p$ odd. Let $B\subseteq\{\pm1\}^m$ with $|B|=2^{m-\ell_B}$ (support defect $\ell_B$), and let $q_1,\dots,q_D$ be linearly independent forms over $K$ with exception masses $p_i=\Pr_{x\in B}(q_i(x)\ne0)$.

(a) If $P=\sum_i p_i<1$, then
$$D\;\le\;\ell_B+\log_2\frac1{1-P}.$$
More generally $D\le \ell_B+\log_2(1/\Pr_B(\text{all }q_i=0))$ whenever the joint vanishing event is nonempty.

(b) In general, with $P=\sum_i p_i$:
$$D\;\le\;\ell_B+2P\Bigl(1+\log_2\frac{eD}{2P}\Bigr)+\log_2(2P+1)+1 .$$

(c) Threshold corollary: if every $p_i\le \varepsilon$ with $2\varepsilon(1+\log_2(e/2\varepsilon))\,(1+\log_2 D)\le 1/2$-type smallness (any constant making the middle term $\le D/2$), then $D\le 2(\ell_B+O(1))$: *forms that vanish outside individually-small exception events span at most $\approx 2\ell_B$ dimensions.*

*Proof.* (a) $V=\bigcap_i\ker q_i$ has dimension $m-D$. A linear subspace $V\le K^m$ contains at most $2^{\dim V}$ points of $\{\pm1\}^m$: coordinate functionals span $V^*$, so some $m-D$ coordinates project injectively from $V$, and a cube point in $V$ is determined by those signs (valid over $\mathbb R$ and over odd $\mathbb F_p$ since $+1\ne-1$). By the union bound $|B\cap V|\ge|B|(1-P)$, so $2^{m-\ell_B}(1-P)\le 2^{m-D}$. (b) Let $\mathrm{viol}(x)=\{i:q_i(x)\ne0\}$; $\mathbb E|\mathrm{viol}|=P$, so at least $|B|/2$ points have $|\mathrm{viol}|\le 2P$ (Markov). For fixed $S$, $\{x:\mathrm{viol}(x)\subseteq S\}\subseteq\bigcap_{i\notin S}\ker q_i$, a subspace of dim $m-D+|S|$, hence $\le 2^{m-D+|S|}$ cube points. Summing over $|S|\le\lfloor 2P\rfloor$ and using $\binom{D}{t}\le(eD/t)^t$ gives the bound. (c) Substitute. $\square$

**[CALIBRATION — this lemma is exactly sharp on the dossier's own hard examples.]**
- *NAE star* (p.42; S9 pp.153-154): boundary marginal $A_W\subseteq\{\pm1\}^6$, $|A_W|=46$ (I recomputed: $27+27-8$), the three pure-boundary residuals $x_i+z_i$ vanish jointly on exactly the $8$ all-opposite configurations. Lemma NK(a), general form: $D\le \ell_B+\log_2(1/\Pr_B(V))=(6-\log_2 46)+\log_2(46/8)=6-\log_2 8=3$. True span: $D=3$. **Saturated exactly** (the star realizes $|B\cap V|=2^{m-D}$ with equality).
- *Crosspolytope core* (p.43): the forms $c_z$ vanish on $A_r$ off mass $p_z=1/r$; a basis of $r$ of them has $P=1$, and NK(b) gives $D\le \ell+2\log_2 r+O(1)=r+\log_2 r+O(1)$ against the truth $D=r$, $\ell=r-\log_2(2r)$. **Tight to $O(\log r)$.**

Interpretation: Lemma NK is a mechanism that *spends unused global defect* — the constructive instruction of the negative ledger (p.52: "spend unused entropy after basis exchange") and of S6 §7.3 (p.127: "charge additional exchanges or residual directions to the remaining entropy defect... or prove a new quotient-class aggregation theorem"). It charges the *joint log-probability* $\log_2(1/\Pr(\text{all residuals vanish}))$, a global quantity, instead of per-row atom charges — precisely what the crosspolytope showed per-row charges cannot do (p.43). It is field-uniform over $\mathbb R$ and odd $\mathbb F_p$, as the moment argument requires (p.28; S6 p.113).

## 3. What Lemma NK does and does not give for the rooted target

**[PROVED HERE, assembly]** Splitting the block's selected residuals by exception mass gives, for any threshold $\theta\in(0,1)$:
$$\dim\operatorname{span}\{q_a\} \;\le\; \underbrace{2\ell_B+O(1)}_{\text{rows with }p_a\le\theta_{\mathrm{NK}}\ \text{(NK(c))}}\;+\;\underbrace{\#\{j: p_j\ge\theta\}}_{\le\, \ell/\theta\ \text{(forcing mass)}}\;\cdot\;(\text{rows per such }j),$$
and the honest gaps are visible in the last factor and in what "$q_a$" omits:

- **Gap 1 (cluster multiplicity).** Many rows can share one private coordinate. **[PROVED HERE, trivial but load-bearing]**: two rows with the same private coordinate and sign have $h_a-h_{a'}=q_a-q_{a'}$ *ternary* on the whole fibre ($\{0,\pm2\}$-valued pointwise, since both rows are $\pm1$-valued). **[PROVED HERE, sketch]**: a form ternary on the *whole cube* has support $\le2$ (case analysis on $2c\in\{0,\pm2\}\pm$ differences; the support-3 case is impossible). So cluster differences are either support-$\le2$ moves ($q_a=q_{a'}\pm e_i\pm e_j$) or genuinely fibre-constrained ternary forms. In the first case the pair event $\{u_i=u_j=\sigma\}$ forces the value of $h_{a'}\cdot x$, so each such row carries conditional forcing mass $\Pr(u_i+u_j=2\sigma)$, and these masses again sum into $\ell$; in the extreme (all pairs present) the fibre collapses onto $\{u_i\ \text{all equal}\}$ and pays $\approx|C|$ defect. **[CONJECTURE, named "cluster forcing-mass lemma"]**: the residual span of a same-coordinate cluster is at most $C\cdot(\text{its total conditional forcing mass})+O(1)$. This is the first missing lemma; it looks provable by the NK machinery applied to difference forms, and nothing in the negative ledger (p.52) blocks it.
- **Gap 2 (core-only rows).** Rows supported inside $C$ recurse to the fibre $A_C$: dimension drops from $r$ to $m=|C|\le9\cdot4^\ell$ (p.33) with defect still $\le\ell$ — a massive first-step shrink after which the recursion stalls. The irreducible quantitative heart, in clean coordinates: **fibres on $m$ coordinates with defect $\ell\approx\tfrac12\log_2 m$, for which the target demands $d_{cb}=O(\log m)$ while the bridge gives $O(m)$.** Note the crosspolytope lives at $\ell\approx m$, far from this regime; no known counterexample lives in it (see §6-7).
- **Gap 3 (mostly-active boundary residuals).** Pure-boundary residuals with $\Pr(\ne0)\approx1$ are invisible to NK and cannot be defect-paid at all — the dossier's one-private-vertex interval says exactly this (p.50: cost zero only once ancestors have paid), and the dense stars of §5 show it survives at dense scale. NK therefore *reframes* absorption rather than proving it: **propose $R_<\supseteq$ (the boundary marginal's near-kernel at the appropriate exception threshold), so that clause (ii) splits into (near-vanishing part: paid by NK against the parent's marginal defect, which telescopes) + (mostly-active part: must be ancestor-paid — the true content of absorption).** On the star this bookkeeping reproduces $D=3$ exactly (§2 calibration) with the $\log_2(1/\Pr)$ term equal to $2.52=3.58\,L$; note $\log_2(27/8)/L = 2.494$ on the joint fibre, just above the universal constant floor $2.409$ (S6 tag (5)) — the constant battleground is real and this is where it lives.

**Honest status:** Lemma NK + forcing-mass + cluster lemma (if proved) would give the *quotient clause* (i) with a linear coefficient for blocks whose non-near-vanishing structure is bounded — a genuine widening of the bounded-congestion theorem (p.39), because NK does not see $\kappa$ at all. It does **not** touch clause (ii) beyond the reframing of Gap 3. No claim that the rooted theorem is proved. **[UNKNOWN]** whether Gaps 1-3 close.

## 4. Endpoint sliver: the survivor has one dominant batch

**[PROVED HERE, from audited dossier inequalities only]** Inputs: $c_s\ge\max\{\beta,\tfrac12\log_2(a_s+1)-1\}$ and $\sum_s c_s\le\ell$ (or $L_{Q|W}$) (p.31, p.39; S1 p.65 line 507); survivor congestion $\kappa\ge c_0\log n$ in every useful basis in the endpoint range (p.53; S1 lines 505-506 p.65); threshold $\ell\ge\tfrac12\log_2\log n+\tfrac12\log_2\log\log n-O(1)$ (S6 tag (32) pp.121-122).

In *any* batching of a survivor block: some batch has $a_s/c_s\ge c_0\log n$, hence $a_s\ge\beta c_0\log n$, hence (feeding back through $c_s\ge\tfrac12\log_2(a_s+1)-1$) $a_s\ge(\tfrac{c_0}2-o(1))\log n\log_2\log n$ — the dossier's own $\Omega(\log n\log\log n)$ density (S1 p.66). Call a batch *dense* if $a_s\ge\beta c_0\log n$; every dense batch has $c_s\ge\tfrac12\log_2\log n-O(1)=:\tau_0$. Therefore:

- **#dense batches $\le \ell/\tau_0$; in the window $\ell<2\tau_0$, i.e. for every survivor with $\ell\le\log_2\log n-O(1)$ (which includes the whole threshold neighbourhood), every batching has exactly ONE dense batch.**
- The dominant batch's charge is $\ge\tfrac12(\log_2\log n+\log_2\log\log n)-O(1)$, i.e. it consumes the entire threshold budget; with $\Delta:=\ell-(\text{threshold})$, the remaining batches have total charge $\le\Delta+O(1)$, so by the convexity step of p.32, their total exposure is $\sum_{s\ne s^*}a_s\le\tfrac92\,4^{\Delta+O(1)}$ — **$O(1)$ at the threshold**.
- Consequently the maximum matching of the two-shadow graph satisfies $N\le a_{s^*}+O(4^\Delta)$: **essentially the whole matching lies inside one witness row's support**, and the vertex cover $C$ has $|C|\le 2N=\Theta(4^\ell)$ with the dominant row's support covering a constant fraction.

So in the threshold window the residual class is forced into an extreme "one dominant dense witness + sparse remainder" two-shadow geometry — the shape the crosspolytope warns about (p.53: the remainder may retain essentially all of $\ell$ even with one witness), but now *derived as necessary*, not just possible, and pinned to the narrow support band $a_{s^*}\in[\beta c_0\log n,\ \tfrac92\cdot4^\ell]$ with $4^\ell=\Theta(\log n\log\log n)$. This sharpens where to look: the missing theorem only ever has to face one dominant dense direction per block in this window (for $\ell$ above the window, at most $\ell/\tau_0$ of them). Quantifier care: "every useful junction basis" (p.53) means this holds in every basis the proof would use; a basis-free statement is **[UNKNOWN]**.

## 5. Two new exact test families (both verified by exact enumeration)

### 5a. Slice-rooted NAE cycles: a dense root does not break the cycle certificate

Define, for odd $s\ge1$ and $n\ge4$:
$$A(s,n)=\Bigl\{(u,b)\in\{\pm1\}^{s+n}:\ \textstyle\sum_i u_i\in\{\pm1\},\ \ \sum_i u_i+b_j+b_{j+1}\in\{\pm1\}\ \forall j\ \text{cyclic}\Bigr\}.$$
At $s=1$ this *is* the solved rooted NAE cycle (p.44); for $s\ge3$ the root coordinate is replaced by a dense slice form $h_0=\sum u_i$ of support $s$ — a minimal model of "dense witness coupled to a cyclic block".

**[VERIFIED-COMPUTATIONALLY, exact integer enumeration, cases $(s,n)\in\{(1,4),(3,4),(3,5),(3,6),(5,4),(5,6),(3,7)\}$]**
- $|A(s,n)|=2\binom{s}{(s+1)/2}L_n$ ($L_n$ Lucas), matching all seven enumerations exactly; so $\ell(A(s,n))=\ell_{\text{cycle}}(n)+\bigl[s-1-\log_2\binom{s}{(s+1)/2}\bigr]$, i.e. cycle defect plus $\approx\tfrac12\log_2 s$.
- The projective full dual is *exactly* $\{e_i\}\cup\{h_0\}\cup\{h_j=h_0+e_{b_j}+e_{b_{j+1}}\}$ — no cross forms appear (0 extra, 0 missing, all cases).
- The NAE optimal selector basis transplants: basis $\{h_0,u_2,\dots,u_s\}\cup\{\text{gap forms }B_v,C_v\ \text{per p.47}\}$ is a coordinate basis inside the full dual, and with residual space $R=\operatorname{span}\{h_0+e_{b_v}:v\in U\}\oplus\operatorname{span}\{\sum_{i\ge2}u_i\}$ every full-dual form has a signed selector with residual in $R$: **$d_{cb}(A(s,n))\le\lceil n/3\rceil+1$**, verified selector-by-selector in all cases ($\dim R=\lceil n/3\rceil+1$ for $s\ge3$; the control case $s=1$ reproduces $\lceil n/3\rceil$ exactly, matching p.46-47). Whether the $+1$ is necessary is **[UNKNOWN]** (no lower-bound run).
- Connector shape: the private parts $e_{b_v}$ of the cycle residuals are distinct coordinates, so as in the solved cycle (p.49) no combination becomes pure-boundary except the explicit generator $\sum_{i\ge2}u_i=h_0-u_1$; and $h_0$ is a *boundary-only form*, which the dossier's rules assign to the parent (p.8; S6 p.113) — the parent's own selection of $h_0$ pays exactly that direction. So the family is connector-compatible in the same sense as E4.

Consequence for the residual class: **a dominant dense witness attached to a cyclic private structure is not, by itself, the obstruction** — the certificate, the activation-style pay structure, and absorption all survive densification of the root. Combined with §4 (one dominant dense batch is forced), the surviving obstruction must lie in *how the sparse remainder couples through the dense row*, not in the dense row itself. This kills a natural pessimism and narrows the hunt to incoherent couplings (next family).

## 6. Bipolar counting conjecture: the full dual is entropy-bounded

**[CONJECTURE B]** For every spanning $A\subseteq\{\pm1\}^r$ (antipodal or not), with $\widetilde D(A)=\{h:h\cdot x\in\{\pm1\}\ \forall x\in A\}$ counted with signs:
$$|A|\cdot|\widetilde D(A)|\;\le\;2r\cdot 2^{r}\qquad\Longleftrightarrow\qquad |\widetilde D(A)|\le 2r\cdot2^{\ell(A)} .$$

Status and evidence:
- **[VERIFIED-COMPUTATIONALLY, exhaustive over the antipodal case]**: all spanning *antipodal* fibres for $r\le5$ (5 / 151 / 60,879 sets at $r=3/4/5$; exact integer frame arithmetic), zero violations; **equality cases are exactly the frames ($A=\pm\{p_1..p_r\}$ independent, includes the Walsh crosspolytope and NAE-3) and the full cube** — 3,009 equality cases at $r=5$, all frames + cube. Random/structured sampling at $r=6,7$ (7,460 sets incl. frame-plus-$k$-points families): zero violations, max ratio $1.0$ attained only by frames. The non-antipodal version (which the matrix equivalence below actually requires) is tested only through these reductions, not exhaustively — a scoped caveat.
- **[PROVED HERE]** Equivalences: (i) B is equivalent to the sign-matrix statement "*every $\pm1$ matrix of rank $r$ with pairwise-distinct rows and columns has $(\#\text{rows})(\#\text{cols})\le 2r\cdot2^r$*" (project rows to $r$ independent columns; columns become sign-valued forms on the projected point set). (ii) B is equivalent to a *defect duality*: fixing a frame $P\subseteq A$, $|\widetilde D(A)|=2^{r-\ell^*}$ where $\ell^*$ is the defect of the dual system $\{w_q=P^{-1}x_q:q\}$, and B says $\ell^*\ge\log_2(|A|/2r)$.
- **[PROVED HERE]** Extremal endpoint: $|\widetilde D(A)|=2^r$ $\iff$ $A$ is a frame (if every sign assignment on a frame extends, then every $x\in A$ has frame coordinates $c$ with $v\cdot c\in\{\pm1\}$ for all $v\in\{\pm1\}^r$, forcing $c=\pm e_i$; this generalizes the closure computation of S6 tag (39) p.125). Also closure-monotone: $D(\operatorname{cl}A)=D(A)$, so full-closed fibres are the tight case.
- Proof attempts that fail, recorded so the next lane does not repeat them: coordinate-splitting induction leaks a constant per level (the $\le2$-preimage pinning is exact only against nondegenerate opposite sides — both tight chains, frames and cubes, saturate the *amortized* potential $(d+1)2^{d+1}$ through opposite (size, multiplicity) splits, so a correct proof needs an amortized/entropy potential, not per-step bounds); the affine wall is the same one the dossier hits (conditioning makes targets affine, S6 p.114) and homogenization returns to dimension $r$.

Role, stated honestly: the trivial **excess-dual bound** $d_{cb}(A)\le\#\{\text{noncoordinate projective classes}\}$ (standard coordinates are a coordinate basis; assign arbitrarily) is *known to the packet and known to be useless alone* — S6 p.122 item 6 says exactly this for the crosspolytope. B does **not** rescue it: in the core regime $\ell\approx\tfrac12\log_2 m$, B permits $|\widetilde D|\approx 2m\sqrt m$, far above $C\ell$. B's value is different: it is the exact *ledger of unused defect* — every extra dual class beyond $2r$ certifies defect (B is the sharp form of "the unused global defect must also be allocated", p.43), it is tight precisely at the two known extremal mechanisms, and its dual-fibre form (ii) turns the dossier's own compression machinery loose on $\widetilde D$ itself (the dual system is again a sign-form system, so Theorem 1 of S6 applies to it — an unexplored self-duality).

## 7. Quadric-rank necessary condition (a refutation tool)

**[PROVED HERE]** For $h\in D(A)$, $(h\cdot x)^2=1$ on $A$ and $x_i^2=1$ give: the multilinear quadric $M_h=\sum_{i<j}h_ih_j\,x_ix_j$ is *constant* on $A$; $M_h=0$ iff $h$ is a signed coordinate; $M_h$ determines $h$ up to sign. If $d_{cb}(A)=d$ with basis $\{f_i\}$ and residual space $R$, then writing $h=\sigma f+\rho$, $\rho\in R$:
$$\operatorname{rank}_2(A):=\dim\operatorname{span}\{M_h:h\in D(A)\}\;\le\; r(1+d)+d^2 .$$
So **if the conjectured $d_{cb}\le C\ell$ is true, every spanning full-closed antipodal fibre satisfies $\operatorname{rank}_2(A)\le r(1+C\ell)+C^2\ell^2$.** The crosspolytope has $\operatorname{rank}_2\ge\binom r2+1-r$ against the bound $\approx2r^2$ — consistent with factor-4 room; i.e. the necessary condition survives its closest known call. **Refutation recipe:** any construction with $\operatorname{rank}_2(A)\gg r\,\ell(A)$ *disproves the unrooted target (1) outright* (and hence forces the campaign onto weaker forms). This is the cheapest structured counterexample hunt I can name: search fibres with many independent row-quadrics per unit defect; by §6's ledger such a fibre must push $|\widetilde D|$ toward $r2^\ell$ while keeping the quadrics independent — frames do the first but their quadrics are far from independent... the tension is exactly where a counterexample would have to live. **[UNKNOWN]** whether one exists; my $r\le5$ exhaustive data contains none (all fibres there satisfy $\operatorname{rank}_2\le r(1+d)+d^2$ vacuously since B held).

## 8. Pairwise clustering of dense rows

Consequence if proved: **every two rows of support $\gg2^\ell$ have a combination of support $O(2^\ell)$; hence all dense rows are sparse perturbations of a single germ, $h=\lambda_h h_1+(\text{support }O(2^\ell))$** — "the dense part of the full dual is rank one modulo a sparse mantle". Consistency checks: the crosspolytope's mutually unclustered dense rows have $\tfrac12\log_2(ss')=\log_2 r\ll\ell\approx r$, so the claim is silent there, as it must be; the slice-rooted cycles of §5a *instantiate* the conclusion exactly ($h_j=h_0+$ support-2). ## Strongest case this is wrong

1. **The route may only relocate the difficulty, not reduce it.** Lemma NK pays *near-vanishing* residuals; the survivor class could arrange all its residual mass in the medium band ($p_a$ of order $1/\log$ to $1/2$), where NK(b)'s $2P\log D$ term is exactly as expensive as the structures it must pay, and the "cluster forcing-mass lemma" is unproved. 3. **The sliver argument (§4) assumes the survivor's batching charges obey the audited inequalities in the basis the proof actually uses.** "Every useful junction basis" (p.53) is not a formal quantifier; if the eventual proof needs a basis outside the class where $\kappa\gtrsim\log n$ was derived, the one-dominant-batch geometry could evaporate, and with it my claim that §5a's shape is the canonical one.
