## Band IV: the marginal-entropy bound (01:5x — proved, and the vacuity frontier made exact)

**Proposition (marginal-entropy bound).** For independent ternary forms d_1,…,d_D on a fibre B with defect ℓ_B and masses p_a:
  D ≤ ℓ_B + Σ_a (h(p_a) + p_a),   where h is binary entropy.
*Proof.* Let pattern(x) = (sign(d_a(x)))_a ∈ {0,+,−}^D. Given the pattern, x lies in a coset of ⋂ker d_a, so H(X|pattern) ≤ m − D (cube points in a coset of a codim-D subspace ≤ 2^{m−D}, as in NK(a)). Hence H(pattern) ≥ H(X) − H(X|pattern) ≥ (m−ℓ_B) − (m−D) = D − ℓ_B. On the other side, subadditivity: H(pattern) ≤ Σ_a H(sign(d_a)) = Σ_a (h(p_a) + p_a) (ternary marginal with masses (1−p_a, p_a/2, p_a/2), the ± split at worst even). ∎

Placement: strictly better than NK(b) in the many-small-masses regime (e.g. 100 forms at p = 0.05, ℓ_B = 30: NK(b) ≈ 77, this ≈ 45; NO log factor), and it is field-agnostic and structure-free. But h(p)+p ≥ 1 exactly for p ∈ (≈0.227, 1], so in the medium band EVERY dimension's marginal carries ≥ 1 bit and the bound is vacuous there — as is NK(b) (coefficient ≥ 1 at p ≈ ½) and the coset/binomial count (same reason). **The vacuity frontier is now exact: every counting/entropy route pays ≥ 1 bit per dimension precisely when p_a ∈ (≈0.227, 0.773), so the open band of this note is provably not closable by pattern-counting alone.** What remains is whether FIBRE STRUCTURE — the d_a being differences of ±1-valued rows sharing a private part, not arbitrary ternary forms — forbids medium-band masses from carrying D ≫ ℓ_B.
