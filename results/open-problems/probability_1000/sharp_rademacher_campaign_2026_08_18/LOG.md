## 20:3x — a new formula, read off the obstructions rather than fitted

k=6 squeeze finished: (2,2,1)=3, (2,1,1,1)=3, (1,1,1,1,1)=3, chain=3, all exact. The
(1,1,1,1,1) case and the chain share a profile and agree, which is the same-profile invariance
check the k=5 run could not provide (there the floor dominated every profile tested).

With (3,2)=4 and star=k-1, a better formula falls out - and this one is READ OFF the two
obstructions rather than fitted to the table. Let h = the number of coordinates lying in two or
more triangles. Since sum_j (n_j - 1) = 3k - r = k-1 on any tree, the local star obstructions
always total k-1, and discounting one shared triangle per additional hub leaves k-h:

    d_cb = max(ceil(k/2), k - h)

It matches every exact value in hand (20+), and absorbs the two families that killed its
predecessor: disjoint triples are the h=0 case (k-0 = m, the proved value, where the old formula
said ceil(m/2) and was wrong by a factor growing without bound), and (3,2) is k-h = 4.
