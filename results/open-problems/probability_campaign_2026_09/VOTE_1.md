VOTE: Hasebe-Ueda (ALEA 2018) Problem 3.3: the optimal C with mu boxplus S(0,t) unimodal for all t >= C D_mu^2

RUNNER-UP: Agresti-Veraar (2025) Problems 4, 5, critical Allen-Cahn

Justification. All three referees, working independently and with adversarial intent, re-derived every step of the Hasebe-Ueda argument and found it CLEAN: the level-set reduction, the pair weights, the closed forms Phi and Psi, Lemma A and Lemma B, the Winkler two-atom extreme-point step, and the equality case. It is the only unpublished result with two independent proofs (Choquet route and the Cauchy-profile moment route), a symbolic check, a 1.2e8-triple scan, an end-to-end density computation that puts the extremal law on the bound to five places, and a sharp answer with a rigidity statement. Its load-bearing core is four real-polynomial inequalities on a box, which is exactly the shape a proof assistant certifies outright (nlinarith/polyrith), so the Lean verification can cover the mathematics rather than an arithmetic fragment. Prior-art searches (two, nothing found) and the citing literature repeating the bound 4 put the risk low. Importance is honest rather than large, but the brief says a finished, slightly less important result beats an unfinished important one, and the competitors fail that test: Janson 20.2's part (b), the case Thevenin emphasised, rests on a sketch whose growth condition Reviewer A showed is wrong as stated; the 18.x group carries a defective 18.40; Aldous-Janson hinges on an unverified range of summation; the RRT layers note is two citations plus one identification with a peeling-literature risk. Agresti-Veraar is the closest complete rival (manuscript, partial Lean, 2025 problem), but its core is stochastic calculus, not formalisable arithmetic.

Biggest risk: the whole argument runs through the source's Lemma 3.1 (unimodality iff every level set of xi_R has at most two points), which no reviewer read from the paper itself, only validated by reproducing Example 2.1 numerically; it must be re-read from the PDF before submission, and the printed "2 <= C" should be mentioned once, neutrally, with D the diameter.
