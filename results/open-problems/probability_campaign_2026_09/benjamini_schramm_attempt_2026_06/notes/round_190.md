# Round 190

Round 190 turns the half-logarithmic obstruction into a direct exclusion
criterion.

When \(\alpha_\omega=\log_\Delta\omega>0\), the criterion says that if, for
every obstruction constant \(\epsilon\), one can exclude expanded gate-annulus
data with
\[
  2^j>
  \left(\frac{1}{2\alpha_\omega}-\delta\right)
  \log_\Delta\bar\chi_p
\]
for some \(\delta>0\), then
\[
  \mathcal R_p^*/\bar\chi_p\to0.
\]
Under the quasi-transitive mean-field susceptibility input this implies
\[
  p_c(G)<p_{2\to2}(G).
\]

When \(\alpha_\omega=0\), the analogous criterion excludes expanded gate data
beyond some finite multiple of \(\log_\Delta\bar\chi_p\).

This is now the clean residual geometric target produced by the
anchor-count route: a counterexample must generate half-logarithmic expanded
gate-annulus data, so proving that such data cannot exist would close the
finite-set obstruction.
