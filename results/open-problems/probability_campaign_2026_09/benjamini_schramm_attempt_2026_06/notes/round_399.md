# Round 399

Round 399 splits the sixteenth confined bridge profiles from Round 398 into
inward-free bridge profiles plus a new bridge-return spread family.

Round 398 left
\[
  P^{\rm pre,br,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch}_{i,j,h}
\]
with confined bridge scale
\[
  K_i^{\rm gap,br,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch}
\]
and finite height range
\[
  1\le h\le
  B_i^{\rm br,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch}.
\]
Applying the confined-bridge dichotomy gives, for each \(i,j,h\),
\[
  P^{\rm pre,br,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch}_{i,j,h}
  \le
  P^{\rm pre,br,free,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch}_{i,j,h}
  +
  R^{\rm spr,br,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch}_{i,j,h}.
\]
The new inward-free and bridge-return profiles use the doubled threshold
\[
  2K_i^{\rm gap,br,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch},
\]
so their profile superscripts carry sixteen \({\rm recbr}\) markers while
the inherited gap scale and height budget carry fifteen.

Substitution into the Round 398 finite ladder replaces the confined bridge
sum under the unchanged coefficient
\[
  D_i^{\rm rec,br,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch}
\]
by two finite sums over the same height range: the sixteenth inward-free
bridge profiles and the sixteenth bridge-return spread profiles.  The
adjacent sixteenth return-spread family, fifteenth bridge-return spread
family, adjacent fifteenth return-spread family, older retained spread
families, long-run profiles, enlarged spread-return profiles, and exact-level

The radial-tail and subdivision criteria now use the sixteenth inward-free
bridge and sixteenth bridge-return profile hypotheses.  The next internal
move is to absorb the inward-free bridge profiles into a prefix-record family
while retaining the new bridge-return spread family, paralleling the previous
inward-free bridge absorption.
