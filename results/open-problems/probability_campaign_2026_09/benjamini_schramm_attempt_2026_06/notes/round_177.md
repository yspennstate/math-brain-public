# Round 177

Round 177 packages the Round 176 dichotomy into a usable criterion.

The new capped gate-annular operator criterion says that the finite-set
obstruction disappears if, for the relevant obstruction parameters, two things
can be ruled out near criticality:

1. expanded gate-annulus obstruction data with
\[
  2^j>U\log_\Delta\bar\chi_p;
\]
2. capped gate intervals \(2^j\le U\log_\Delta\bar\chi_p\) whose restricted
two-point operator satisfies the Round 176 lower bound
\[
  \|T_p^{I_{j,p}^{\rm gate}}\|_{2\to2}
  \ge
  C_{\epsilon,\eta,J,U,C_{\rm vol}}
  (\log_\Delta\bar\chi_p)^{-6\eta}
  \bar\chi_p^{\beta(\theta,U,\omega)}.
\]

The important correction is that the large-scale branch is not merely the
existence of a large integer \(j\); it is the existence of a large \(j\) that
carries the full expanded gate-annulus obstruction data.  This keeps the
criterion non-vacuous and aligned with the Round 175 audit.

Under quasi-transitivity and the mean-field susceptibility input, this
criterion again implies \(p_c(G)<p_{2\to2}(G)\).
