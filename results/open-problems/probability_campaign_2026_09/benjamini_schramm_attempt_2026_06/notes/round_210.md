# Round 210

Round 210 records a bipartite simplification of the radial-defect program.

If \(G\) is bipartite, then for any root \(x\), adjacent vertices have
opposite parity of distance from \(x\).  Since adjacent distances differ by at
most one, no edge can join two vertices in the same sphere around \(x\).
Therefore every path satisfies
\[
  H_x(\gamma)=0.
\]

Consequently
\[
  \mathfrak M_{n,b,h}^{\rm rad,saw,sph}=0
  \qquad(h\ge1),
\]
and the same-sphere tail from Round 209 vanishes.

For bipartite graphs, the remaining radial tail target is only the inward
tail, together with the finite-excess hypothesis.
