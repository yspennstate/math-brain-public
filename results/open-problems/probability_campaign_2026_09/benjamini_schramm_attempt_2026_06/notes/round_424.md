# Round 424 nineteenth inward-free bridges absorb into records

Round 424 absorbs the nineteenth inward-free bridge profiles left by Round
423 into prefix-record profiles and a long-run term, retaining the new
bridge-return spread family.

Round 423 left, for every finite \(i,j,h\) with
\[
  1\le h\le B_i^{\rm br,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch},
\]
the inward-free bridge contribution
\[
  P^{\rm pre,br,free,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch}_{i,j,h}
\]
under coefficient
\[
  D_i^{\rm rec,br,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch}.
\]
The child profiles are evaluated at the doubled fixed-gap scale
\[
  2K_i^{\rm gap,br,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch}.
\]

Define the prefix-record profile at this doubled scale by
\[
  P^{\rm pre,rec,br,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch}_{i,j}
  =
  \mathfrak P^{G\to H,{\rm rad,tan,pre,out,rec,short}}_
  {Q,\lambda_i,\sigma_i,\beta_i,j,
   2K_i^{\rm gap,br,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch},L_i}.
\]
For each finite \(i,j,h\), the inward-free bridge chain/long-run alternative
gives
\[
  P^{\rm pre,br,free,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch}_{i,j,h}
  \le
  P^{\rm pre,stair,loc,chain}_{i,j,h+1}
  +
  W_{\beta_i,p}T^{\rm long}_{i,j}.
\]
Since \(h+1\ge2\), the locally short staircase-chain term is absorbed by the
prefix-record profile, yielding
\[
  P^{\rm pre,br,free,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch}_{i,j,h}
  \le
  P^{\rm pre,rec,br,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch}_{i,j}
  +
  W_{\beta_i,p}T^{\rm long}_{i,j}.
\]

Substitution into the Round 423 finite ladder replaces the finite
inward-free bridge sum under coefficient
\[
  D_i^{\rm rec,br,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch}
\]
by a prefix-record sum with new coefficient
\[
  D_i^{\rm rec,br,recbr,\ldots,recbr,recbr,ch}
  =
  D_i^{\rm rec,br,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch}
  B_i^{\rm br,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch}.
\]
The long-run coefficient is updated by
\[
  A_i^{\rm long,br,recbr,\ldots,recbr,recbr,ch}
  =
  A_i^{\rm long,br,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch}
  +
  D_i^{\rm rec,br,recbr,\ldots,recbr,recbr,ch}.
\]

The nineteenth bridge-return spread family from Round 423, the earlier
nineteenth bridge-return spread family from Round 417, the adjacent
nineteenth return-spread family from Round 419, the eighteenth retained
spread families, older retained families, enlarged spread-return profiles,
exact-level return terms, and all finite cutoffs carry forward.

The radial-tail and subdivision criteria now use the new prefix-record
formulation with one additional bridge-record marker.  The next unresolved
issue is to split these prefix-record profiles into persistent-prefix
profiles plus adjacent return-spread profiles.
