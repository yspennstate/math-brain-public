# Round 208

Round 208 splits the radial long-excess tail into two geometric tails.

From Round 207, excess is
\[
  k=2b+h,
\]
where \(b\) is the number of inward radial steps and \(h\) is the number of
same-sphere steps.  Let
\[
  K_{\rho,p}=\lfloor\rho\log_\Delta\bar\chi_p\rfloor.
\]
If \(2b+h>K_{\rho,p}\), then either
\[
  b>K_{\rho,p}/4
\]
or
\[
  h>K_{\rho,p}/2.
\]

The Round 204 long-excess profile is therefore bounded by an inward-tail
profile
\[
  \mathfrak I_{q,\rho}^{\rm rad,saw}(s,p)
\]
and a same-sphere-tail profile
\[
  \mathfrak H_{q,\rho}^{\rm rad,saw}(s,p).
\]

If the finite-excess hypothesis from Round 204 holds, and both radial tails
have exponent below the Round 203 margin, then the two-replica obstruction
closes.

This does not solve the problem, but it makes the long-excess target less
monolithic: it separates radial backtracking from tangential wandering along
distance spheres.
