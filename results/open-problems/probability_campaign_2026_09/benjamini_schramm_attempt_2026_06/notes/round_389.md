Round 389 splits the fourteenth prefix-record profiles from Round 388 into
persistent-prefix profiles and the next adjacent return-spread family.

Round 388 left the prefix-record profile
\[
  P^{\rm pre,rec,br,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch}_{i,j}.
\]

\[
  K=
  2K_i^{\rm gap,br,recbr,recbr,recbr,recbr,recbr,recbr,recbr,recbr,recbr,recbr,recbr,recbr,recbr,ch}

\[
  P^{\rm pre,rec,br,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch}_{i,j}

  +
  R^{\rm spr,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch}_{i,j}
  +
  R^{\rm spr,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch}_{i,j+1}.
\]
The persistent-prefix threshold is \(2K\), and the return-spread threshold is
\(4K\).  Thus the persistent profile carries fourteen \({\rm recbr}\)
markers and the new adjacent return-spread family carries fifteen.

contribution under the unchanged coefficient
