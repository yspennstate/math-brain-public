# Round 266

Round 266 wires the finite-stage chain ladder into the later tail and
subdivision criteria.

The new radial tail criterion starts from the staircase-chain/bridge radial
tail criterion and replaces the raw \(q=3,4\) locally short chain hypotheses
by the finite-stage residuals:
\[
  P_{i,j,q,\mathbf a}^{\rm pre,stair,loc,nchain,sh}
  \qquad(\mathbf a\in\mathcal A_n(q))
\]
and
\[
  P_{i,j,q,r,\mathbf a,h}^{\rm pre,stair,loc,nchainbr,sh}
  \qquad(1\le r\le n,\ 
    \mathbf a\in\mathcal A_{r-1}(q),\
    a_{r-1}<h\le B_{i,r}^{\rm ch}).
\]
For each fixed \(n\), all of these families are finite, so the same exponent
transfer through the far-tail ladder applies.

The new subdivision criterion then combines this finite-stage radial tail
criterion with the finite-defect bin assumptions, giving the usual conditional
conclusion \(p_c(G)<p_{2\to2}(G)\).
