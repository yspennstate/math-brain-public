# Round 223

Round 223 adds the final packaged subdivision criterion and refreshes the
open-status evidence.

The new corollary says that the abstract subdivision criterion can be checked
using the shifted original radial profiles from Round 222.  In short, if the
non-profile hypotheses of the one-parameter bipartite inward criterion hold on
\(H=\operatorname{Sub}(G)\), and if the exponent hypotheses hold for
\[
  \mathfrak F_{Q,\rho}^{G\to H,{\rm rad}},
  \qquad
  \mathfrak L_{Q,\rho}^{G\to H,{\rm rad}},
  \qquad Q=q^2,
\]
then
\[
  p_c(G)<p_{2\to2}(G).
\]

The web refresh located special-case confirmations, not a general proof:

- Hutchcroft, "Non-uniqueness and mean-field criticality for percolation on
  nonunimodular transitive graphs", verifies the nonunimodular case of the
  Benjamini-Schramm conjecture.
- Hutchcroft, "Percolation on hyperbolic graphs", verifies the conjecture for
  nonamenable Gromov-hyperbolic quasi-transitive graphs.
- Hutchcroft, "The \(L^2\) boundedness condition in nonamenable percolation",
  states the \(L^2\) boundedness condition as a conjectural route that implies
  the older nonuniqueness conjecture.

Thus the current target remains a general-case route, not a solved theorem.
