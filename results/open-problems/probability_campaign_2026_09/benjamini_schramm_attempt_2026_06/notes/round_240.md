# Round 240

Round 240 removes the upper-shifted exterior-return family from the current
endpoint.

For the slab
\[
  A_{j,p}=[jW_{\beta,p},(j+1)W_{\beta,p}),
\]

\[
  u+1\in A_{j,p}\cap\mathbb N
  \quad\text{or}\quad
  u+1\in A_{j+1,p}\cap\mathbb N .
\]
Thus
\[
  \mathfrak X_{j,K}^{\rm ret,up}
  \le
  \mathfrak X_{j,K}^{\rm ret}
  +
  \mathfrak X_{j+1,K}^{\rm ret}.
\]

  3X_{i,j}^{\times}+U_{i,j}^{\times}

  4B_{i,j}^{\times}+B_{i,j+1}^{\times},
\]

price is one extra boundary slab \(j=J_{\sigma_i,\beta_i}\), which is a finite
non-asymptotic addition.

near-tail bins, a terminal far tail, same-sphere spread, long tangential
