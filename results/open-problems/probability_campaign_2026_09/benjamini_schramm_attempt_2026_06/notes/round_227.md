# Round 227

Round 227 records that overshoot forces deeper radial defect.

If a path starts at \(x\), ends at radius \(r\), and reaches maximum radius
\[
  M_x(\gamma)=\max_t d(x,v_t),
\]
then after the last visit to the maximum radius it must make at least
\[
  M_x(\gamma)-r
\]
inward steps.  Hence
\[
  I_x(\gamma)\ge M_x(\gamma)-r,
  \qquad
  2I_x(\gamma)+H_x(\gamma)\ge2(M_x(\gamma)-r).
\]

Therefore the overshoot profile from Round 226 obeys
\[
  \mathfrak O_{Q,\lambda,\sigma}^{G\to H,{\rm rad}}
  \le
  \mathfrak Y_{Q,\max\{\lambda,2\sigma\}}^{G\to H,{\rm rad}}.
\]
If \(\sigma>\lambda/2\), overshoot is absorbed into a strictly deeper far-tail
cutoff.  The remaining work can therefore focus on heavy-slab estimates, plus
an iterated or terminal control of progressively deeper far tails.
