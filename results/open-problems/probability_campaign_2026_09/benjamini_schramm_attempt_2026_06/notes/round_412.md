# Round 412

Round 412 absorbs the eighteenth inward-free bridge profiles from Round 411
into prefix-record profiles and updates the long-run coefficient.

Round 411 left the finite family
\[
  P^{\rm pre,br,free,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch}_{i,j,h},
  \qquad
  1\le h\le B_i^{\rm br,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch},
\]
at the doubled fixed-gap scale
\[
  2K_i^{\rm gap,br,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch}.
\]
Define
\[
  P^{\rm pre,rec,br,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch}_{i,j}
  =
  \mathfrak P^{G\to H,{\rm rad,tan,pre,out,rec,short}}_
  {Q,\lambda_i,\sigma_i,\beta_i,j,
   2K_i^{\rm gap,br,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch},L_i}.
\]

For each finite \(i,j,h\), the inward-free bridge chain/long-run reduction
gives
\[
  P^{\rm pre,br,free,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch}_{i,j,h}
  \le
  P^{\rm pre,rec,br,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch}_{i,j}
  +
  W_{\beta_i,p}T_{i,j}^{\rm long}.
\]
The first term follows by bounding the locally short staircase-chain profile
of length \(h+1\) by the prefix-record profile.

Substitution into the Round 411 finite ladder replaces the finite inward-free
bridge sum, under the source coefficient
\[
  D_i^{\rm rec,br,recbr,\ldots,ch},
\]
by the prefix-record sum with the new fixed coefficient
\[
  D_i^{\rm rec,br,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch}
  =
  D_i^{\rm rec,br,recbr,\ldots,ch}
  B_i^{\rm br,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch}.
\]
The long-run coefficient is updated by
\[
  A_i^{\rm long,br,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch}
  =
  A_i^{\rm long,br,recbr,\ldots,ch}
  +
  D_i^{\rm rec,br,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch}.
\]
