# Round 394

Round 394 absorbs the fifteenth inward-free bridge profiles from Round 393
into prefix-record profiles, retaining the fifteenth bridge-return spread
family.

Round 393 left the inward-free bridge family
\[
  P^{\rm pre,br,free,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch}_{i,j,h}
\]
over the finite range
\[
  1\le h\le B_i^{\rm br,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch},
\]
with cutoff
\[
  2K_i^{\rm gap,br,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch}.
\]
The inward-free bridge chain/long-run alternative gives, for each \(i,j,h\),
\[
  P^{\rm pre,br,free,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch}_{i,j,h}
  \le
  \mathfrak P^{\rm rad,tan,pre,out,per,stair,loc,chain,short}_{i,j,h+1}
  +
  W_{\beta_i,p}T_{i,j}^{\rm long}.
\]
Since \(h+1\ge2\), the locally short chain-to-record lemma bounds the chain
term by
\[
  P^{\rm pre,rec,br,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch}_{i,j}.
\]

Thus the inward-free bridge contribution is replaced by a prefix-record
contribution with fixed coefficient
\[
  D_i^{\rm rec,br,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch}
  =
  D_{i,\mathrm{old}}^{\rm rec,br,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch}
  B_i^{\rm br,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch},
\]
where the new coefficient has fourteen \({\rm recbr}\) markers, the old
coefficient has thirteen, and the height budget has fourteen.  The same new
coefficient is added to the long-run coefficient
\[
  A_i^{\rm long,br,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch}.
\]

The finite-ladder, radial-tail, and subdivision criteria now use the fifteenth
prefix-record profiles in place of the fifteenth inward-free bridge profiles.
The fifteenth bridge-return spread family, adjacent fifteenth return-spread
family, fourteenth bridge-return and adjacent return-spread families, older
retained spread families, enlarged spread-return profiles, and exact-level
return profiles are retained.

No external literature refresh was performed in this round; the targeted
post-Round 382 source check remains the latest external status recorded by
this project.
