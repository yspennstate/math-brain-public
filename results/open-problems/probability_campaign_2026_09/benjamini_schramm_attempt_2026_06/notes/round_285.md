# Round 285

Round 285 is a refreshed external open-status check plus a short internal
target note.

External check, 2026-06-09:

- I found no announced general proof of the Benjamini--Schramm
  non-uniqueness conjecture for all nonamenable quasi-transitive graphs, and
  no announced proof of the stronger project target
  \(p_c(G)<p_{2\to2}(G)\) in that generality.
- Hermon--Hutchcroft's Inventiones article remains a direct status anchor:
  it explicitly says the question of non-uniqueness of the infinite cluster
  remains completely open at this level of generality.
  Source: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00222-020-01011-3
- Hutchcroft's \(L^2\)-boundedness paper remains the operator-threshold
  anchor for this project: it states that the \(L^2\)-boundedness conjecture
  would imply the older nontrivial non-uniqueness phase conjecture.
  Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/1904.05804
- The newer visible papers found in this refresh are special-case or
  adjacent progress, not a general solution.  Examples include subgroup
  relativization at \(p_u\), tree-product/lamplighter threshold geometry,
  weighted-amenability/nonunimodular variants, and a 2026 planar-site
  Benjamini--Schramm variant.
  Sources:
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.12283,
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.15895,
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.02560,
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.09958
