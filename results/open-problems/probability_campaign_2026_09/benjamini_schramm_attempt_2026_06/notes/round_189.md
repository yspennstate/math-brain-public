# Round 189

Round 189 rewrites the optimized anchor-count escape in the clean residual
scale form.

With
\[
  \alpha_\omega=\log_\Delta\omega,
\]
the expanded gate-annulus obstruction can be forced beyond every scale
\[
  2^j>
  \left(\frac{1}{2\alpha_\omega}-\delta\right)
  \log_\Delta\bar\chi_p
  \qquad(\delta>0)
\]
when \(\alpha_\omega>0\).  If \(\alpha_\omega=0\), it can be forced beyond
every finite multiple of \(\log_\Delta\bar\chi_p\).

This is just the optimized form of the Round 186 anchor-count inequality, but
it is the right residual formulation: any counterexample branch that survives
the finite-set reductions must be genuinely half-logarithmic in the
volume-growth scale.  Capped data below that scale cannot persist near
criticality.
