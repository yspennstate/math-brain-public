the finite-ladder level.

lengths \(4,\ldots,8\), represented as \(\ell_{q,h}=q+h\) for

  q\in\{3,4\},\qquad 1\le h\le q.
\]

\[
  E_i=
  \left\lceil
    \frac{16K_i^\sharp\beta_i}
         {\theta_{\lambda_i,\sigma_i,\beta_i}}
  \right\rceil+9,

  K_i^\flat=2K_i^\sharp E_i.

\[
  \text{longer chains of length }\ell_{q,h}+a

\[
  \quad(\ell_{q,h}<a\le E_i).
