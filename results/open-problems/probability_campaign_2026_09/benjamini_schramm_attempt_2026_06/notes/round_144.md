# Round 144 log

Added `cor:endpoint-anchor-annular-mass-dichotomy`.

The Round 143 endpoint-anchor alternative already gives a lower bound on
`sum_i tau_p(y_i,z_i)` in the logarithmic regime `2^j <= B log_Delta chiBar_p`.
Round 144 records the exact distance localization of this mass.  Since
`d(y_i,a_i) in (2^j-r_p,2^{j+1}+r_p]` and `d(a_i,z_i)=r_p`, every diagonal
endpoint-anchor pair satisfies

```tex
d(y_i,z_i) in (2^j-2r_p,2^{j+1}+2r_p].
```

Thus the same lower bound lives in a controlled annular interval rather than
only on unlocalized endpoint-anchor pairs.
