# Round 328

Round 328 absorbs the bridge-record-record-record-record inward-free bridge
family from Round 327 into a deeper prefix-record profile, at the cost of
another finite long-run contribution.

Round 327 left the inward-free bridge-record-record-record-record family
\[
  P^{\rm pre,br,free,recbr,recbr,recbr,recbr,ch}_{i,j,h}
  =
  P^{\rm pre,out,per,bridge,free}_{j,
    2K_i^{\rm gap,br,recbr,recbr,recbr,ch},L_i,h}.
\]
Applying the inward-free-bridge chain-or-long-run reduction with
\[
  K=2K_i^{\rm gap,br,recbr,recbr,recbr,ch}
\]
gives
\[
  P^{\rm pre,br,free,recbr,recbr,recbr,recbr,ch}_{i,j,h}
  \le
  P^{\rm pre,out,per,stair,loc,chain}_{j,
    2K_i^{\rm gap,br,recbr,recbr,recbr,ch},L_i,h+1}
  +
  W_{\beta_i,p}T^{\rm long}_{i,j}.
\]
Since \(h+1\ge2\), the locally short staircase-chain-to-prefix-record
reduction bounds the chain term by
\[
  P^{\rm pre,rec,br,recbr,recbr,recbr,recbr,ch}_{i,j}
  =
  P^{\rm pre,out,rec}_{j,
    2K_i^{\rm gap,br,recbr,recbr,recbr,ch},L_i}.
\]
Thus
\[
  P^{\rm pre,br,free,recbr,recbr,recbr,recbr,ch}_{i,j,h}
  \le
  P^{\rm pre,rec,br,recbr,recbr,recbr,recbr,ch}_{i,j}
  +
  W_{\beta_i,p}T^{\rm long}_{i,j}.
\]

Substitution into the Round 327 ladder replaces the inward-free bridge sum
with the new prefix-record profile under coefficient
\[
  D_i^{\rm rec,br,recbr,recbr,recbr,ch}
  =
  D_i^{\rm rec,br,recbr,recbr,ch}
  B_i^{\rm br,recbr,recbr,recbr,ch}.
\]
It also adds the same coefficient to the long-run term, giving
\[
  A_i^{\rm long,br,recbr,recbr,recbr,ch}
  =
  A_i^{\rm long,br,recbr,recbr,ch}
  +D_i^{\rm rec,br,recbr,recbr,recbr,ch}.
\]
All bridge-record-record-record-record bridge-return, adjacent return-spread,
earlier bridge-return, staircase/local bridge, enlarged spread-return, and
exact-level return terms are retained.
