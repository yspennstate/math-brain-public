# Round 209

Round 209 sharpens the Round 208 tail split.

Let
\[
  K_{\rho,p}=\lfloor\rho\log_\Delta\bar\chi_p\rfloor.
\]
If \(2b+h>K_{\rho,p}\), then either
\[
  b>K_{\rho,p}/4
\]
or else
\[
  b\le K_{\rho,p}/4,\qquad h>K_{\rho,p}/2.
\]

So the same-sphere tail does not need to include all inward counts.  It is
enough to control
\[
  \sum_{0\le b\le K_{\rho,p}/4,\ h>K_{\rho,p}/2}
  q^{2b+h}\mathfrak M_{n,b,h}^{\rm rad,saw,sph}.
\]
The high-inward part is already charged to the inward tail.

This makes the tangential target cleaner: prove many same-sphere steps are
rare under a low-inward constraint.
