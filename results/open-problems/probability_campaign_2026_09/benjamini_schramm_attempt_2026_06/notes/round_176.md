# Round 176

Round 176 moves back upstream from the vacuous pointwise-sup criteria and
keeps the mass-bearing expanded gate-annulus obstruction.

The new mass-to-operator lemma says that if finite sets \(A,Y\) carry
annular two-point mass
\[
  M=\sum_{a\in A}\sum_{\substack{y\in Y\\d(y,a)\in I}}\tau_p(y,a),
\]
then
\[
  \|T_p^I\|_{2\to2}\ge \frac{M}{\sqrt{|A||Y|}}.
\]

Applying this to the volume-sensitive expanded gate-annulus obstruction gives
a structured dichotomy.  With \(\alpha_\omega=\log_\Delta\omega\), for any
logarithmic cap \(U>B\), a counterexample forces either
\[
  2^j>U\log_\Delta\bar\chi_p
\]
or a gate-annular operator lower bound
\[
  \|T_p^{I_{j,p}^{\rm gate}}\|_{2\to2}
  \ge
  C_{\epsilon,\eta,J,U,C_{\rm vol}}
  (\log_\Delta\bar\chi_p)^{-6\eta}
  \bar\chi_p^{\beta(\theta,U,\omega)},
\]
where
\[
  \beta(\theta,U,\omega)
  =
  2-4\theta-8\theta\alpha_\omega
  -3U\alpha_\omega-\frac32\theta\alpha_\omega .
\]

This is non-vacuous in exactly the way the pointwise-sup route was not: the
lower bound comes from simultaneous root-to-gate and \(K\)-to-gate mass over a
structured family, not from the trivial open geodesic between one worst pair.
The remaining route is now split into controlling super-logarithmic gate
scales or ruling out this restricted gate-annular operator mass.
