Lemma 7.19 matches exactly. Example 7.14: title "A Cayley graph where E_v T_w is not the same for all edges", generators (1,0), (-1,0), (0,1); the book computes n^{-1} E_00 T_01 -> 3/(1+sqrt3) (rung) and n^{-1} E_00 T_10 -> 3 sqrt3/(2(1+sqrt3)) (ring); the book does not say its tau* and tau_2 are of order n^2 (true, lambda_2 = (2 cos(2 pi/m) + 1)/3, but the paper's claim, not the book's), and the inference "not arc-transitive" is made at the opening of Section 7.2. The book's printed definition of arc-transitivity is garbled (gamma(v1) = w1, gamma(v2) = w2); the paper uses the standard Biggs/Godsil-Royle definition, which is what Lemma 7.19 needs. tau*, tau_0, tau_2: (4.1), (4.6), (3.38)-(3.39), second largest eigenvalue, unchanged by continuizing; the paper is faithful.
