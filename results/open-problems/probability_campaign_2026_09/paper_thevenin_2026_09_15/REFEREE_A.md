## What was verified and found correct

- **Lemma 2.1.** The Lukasiewicz characterisation; the cycle lemma (one admissible rotation among the n rotations of a sequence with sum n-1; rotations distinct because a proper period d would make the sum divisible by n/d >= 2); P(|T|=n) = P(C_n)/n; the multiset identity (rotation invariance of the weight and of the multiset). The remark P(C_n) >= pi_1^(n-1) pi_0 > 0 is correct.
- **Lemma 2.2.** The inversion formula (2.3) (the integrand is E e^{it(S_r-k_r)} e^{is(...)}, and S_r-k_r is an integer, so the t-integral over [-pi,pi] picks out S_r=k_r); the substitution t=v/sqrt N; W_r has mean 0 and E W_r^2 -> v^2 sigma^2 + 2 v s gamma + s^2 b (needs E X_r -> mu, E X_r^2 -> E X^2, q_r -> q, all given by convergence in distribution on the integers plus uniform integrability of X_r^2); the second-order expansion with the min(|x|^3/6, x^2) remainder and the K-then-N order of limits; the domination 1-|phi_r|^2 >= 2 P(X_r=0) P(X_r=1) (1-cos u), u = t + s/sqrt N (the centering cancels in |phi|^2; only the pairs (0,1),(1,0) are kept, all discarded terms being non-negative); 1-cos u >= 2u^2/pi^2 on |u| <= pi (Jordan); the treatment of pi-delta <= |t| <= pi (|u| in [pi-2delta, pi+delta], cos u <= cos(pi-2delta), so the piece is O(sqrt N e^{-c^2 N (1+cos 2 delta)})); the Gaussian integral with beta = ia + gamma s, beta^2 = -a^2 + 2 i a gamma s + gamma^2 s^2; the resulting mean gamma a/sigma^2 and variance b - gamma^2/sigma^2 (the bivariate-Gaussian regression of the count on the sum, as it should be); Levy continuity after division by the value at s=0; the Cauchy--Schwarz criterion.
- **Sign of the mean.** With a = -epsilon (the larger atom L^+ leaves a smaller sum for the others), the conditional mean is -epsilon a_*: given L^+ the count of ones is low. The numerics confirm this at n=256 (47 = 64-17 given 208; 79 = 64+15 given 176) and at n=2^32.
- **Bernstein (2.5).** Matches Boucheron--Lugosi--Massart Theorem 2.10 (only the upper bound Z_i <= M is needed; centred bounded variables satisfy it).
- **Proposition 3.1.** (a) The invariant d_r >= 5/8 + 2 n_{r+1}^{-3}: (5/8+2x)(1-2x) = 5/8 + 3x/4 - 4x^2 >= 5/8 + 2x^4 for x <= 2^{-24}, and n_{r+2}^{-3} = x^4; base case d_0 = 3/4; H_{r+1} = H_r n_r^3 - 2 H_r; sqrt(n_r) = n_{r-1}^2; n_r/2 < L_r^- < L_r^+ < n_r. ## Findings

1. **Proposition 3.1(d), lower bound on the atoms** (Section 3.1, proof of (d)). Severity: gap (minor). The inequality (5/8)^h n_r^{h-4} <= p_r (L_r^-)^h requires L_r^- >= (5/8) n_r, i.e. d_{r-1} >= 5/8 + n_r^{-1/2}. This does not follow from the bound 5/8 < d_{r-1} of part (a) (the invariant gives only d_{r-1} >= 5/8 + 2 n_r^{-3}, which is smaller than the needed 5/8 + n_r^{-1/2}); it follows from d_{r-1} >= d_infty > 11/16, established two sentences earlier, since then L_r^- > (11/16 - 1/16) n_r = (5/8) n_r. The text does not say which bound is being used. Fix: insert "since d_{r-1} >= d_infty > 11/16, L_r^- > (5/8) n_r, so".

2. **Proposition 3.1(a), strictness of d_infty > 5/8** (statement of (a) and its proof). Severity: gap (cosmetic). The proof of (a) shows d_r >= 5/8 + 2 n_{r+1}^{-3} for every r, which in the limit gives only d_infty >= 5/8; the strict inequality asserted in (a) is actually proved in (d) (d_infty > 11/16). Fix: in (a) write "d_r decreases to a limit d_infty >= 5/8 (in fact d_infty > 11/16, see (d))", or move the 11/16 bound into (a).

3. **Discussion after Theorem 1.1, "within 0.02 sqrt(n_r) ... with probability tending to one"** (paragraph following (1.6)). Severity: overclaim (minor, but the paper is otherwise exact). For a fixed window the limiting probability is 1 - 2 Phi(-0.02/sqrt(v_*)) = 1 - 1.5 x 10^{-6}, not 1; at n = 2^32 the exact mass within 0.02 sqrt n of n/4 +- sqrt n is 0.99981. Fix: "with probability tending to a limit exceeding 1 - 2 x 10^{-6}", or "within c sqrt(n_r) for any fixed c > 0 with probability tending to one" (this is true, since a_* is fixed and the spike width is fixed; the specific 0.02 is what breaks the sentence).

4. **Introduction, last paragraph, and the Lean file: "integrality of the atoms ... proved in Lean 4"** (Section 1, paragraph "The paper is organised as follows", and Section 3.1). Severity: overclaim (minor). `DegreeCounts.lean` certifies integrality only for the first two stages (`stage_one`, `stage_two`); the general statement H_{r+1} = H_r n_r^3 - 2 H_r in Z and sqrt(n_r) = n_{r-1}^2 is not formalised (the file's own header says "the first two stages"). The other listed items (bounds on d_r, rare mass, exclusion of a second large coordinate, centering identity, Bernstein exponent, mean increments, first-stage critical atoms) are formalised in the generality the paper uses. Fix: either add the one-line induction on `dd k * nn (k+1)` being an integer to the Lean file, or write "the integrality of the atoms of the first two stages" in the paper. (The current file builds: CI run 34970928788 completed successfully with axiom reports for all 17 theorems; note that the file on disk was edited at 15:47 and that run started at 15:48, so the build log in the repository must be the one for the shipped file.)

5. **Lemma 2.2, remainder bound in the pointwise limit** (proof, paragraph "Pointwise limit"). Severity: typo/gap (presentation). The parenthesis "bound the remainder by |W_r|^3/6 <= K W_r^2/6 there and by W_r^2 on the complement" drops the N-scaling: the remainder of e^{ix} at x = W_r/sqrt N is at most min(|W_r|^3/(6 N^{3/2}), W_r^2/N), so N times the remainder is at most K E W_r^2/(6 sqrt N) + E[W_r^2; |W_r| > K], which is what tends to 0 as N -> infinity then K -> infinity. The argument is right; the sentence as written is not the bound used. Fix: write the two terms with their powers of N.

6. **Lemma 2.1, distinctness of the rotations** (proof). Severity: typo. "the n rotations are distinct because sum(x_i - 1) = -1 is not divisible by any period" should read: if the sequence had a period d < n, d | n, then the sum would be n/d times the sum over one period, hence divisible by n/d >= 2, which -1 is not.

7. **Theorem 1.1 and 1.2, the symbol xi** (statements). Severity: typo (notation). In Section 1, xi is introduced as a random variable with law mu; the theorems then write nu = E xi and E xi^h for the law pi without saying so. Fix: "let xi have law pi" in the theorem statements, or "where xi ~ pi".

8. **Section 3.4, D_r >= n/3** (first sentence). Severity: typo (trivial). mu_r < 5/12 gives D_r = n - 1 - n mu_r > 7n/12 - 1, which is >= n/3 only for n >= 4; true for every n_r but worth a word, or replace 5/12 by the actual bound (mu_r < 5/12 gives D_r > (7/12) n - 1).

9. **Section 5.1, "they contribute less than e^{-9 x 10^6} of the total"** (n = 2^32 paragraph). Severity: none (consistency note). This figure uses M = 208 (the true maximum of A_2), giving exponent 9.896 x 10^6; the paper's general bound with M_r = n^{1/4} = 256 gives 8.10 x 10^6 and the stated n^{3/4}/24 gives 6.99 x 10^5. All three are valid bounds and the conclusion is unaffected; the text could say that the sharper constant M = 208 was used.

10. **Lemma 2.2, hypotheses versus use** (statement). Severity: none (remark). The lemma is applied in Section 3 with two different target sequences k_r^+ and k_r^- on the same rows; this is two applications of the lemma and is fine. The lemma also silently assumes P(S_r = k_r) > 0 for the conditional law to make sense; (2.4) guarantees it for large r, which is all that is used.

## Statement versus proof

Every clause of Theorem 1.1 is proved: (i) by Proposition 3.1(a),(b),(d) and the remark after Lemma 2.1 (the atoms L_r^{+-} are distinct across stages by (a) and (c), so pi is well defined, and pi_0 + pi_1 < 1); (ii) by Sections 3.2-3.5, with sigma^2 = Var xi > 0, gamma = q(1-nu), a_* = gamma/sigma^2, v_* = b - gamma^2/sigma^2 > 0 and the mixture with means -+ a_* (given L^{+-}); (iii) by the three-type decomposition. The mixture's characteristic function e^{-v_* s^2/2} cos(a_* s) and its zero at pi/(2 a_*) are correct, so the non-Gaussianity along the full sequence n follows. The numbers quoted in the theorem and in Section 5.1 are consistent with the theory to the digits shown, and the finite-size deviations at n = 2^32 (mixture weights, conditional means and variances) have the sign and size predicted by the O(1/sqrt n) terms of the centering identity and the Edgeworth correction, which are odd in the displacement and cancel in P(|T| = n_2). The quoted conjecture matches Thevenin's text verbatim. Every constant is defined before use (C_sigma in (3.6), t_r, z_r, u_r, mu_r, q_r, M_r, D_r in Section 3.2-3.4, b and c in Lemma 2.2), with the notational point of finding 7. Nothing load-bearing is asserted without proof; findings 1 and 2 are the only places where the justification given does not by itself yield the stated inequality.

## Verdict

Theorem 1.1 and the two lemmas it rests on are correct as stated; the proofs are complete up to the minor gaps in findings 1 and 2, which are repaired by one clause each. Recommend acceptance after the small revisions in findings 1-8.
