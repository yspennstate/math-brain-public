VOTE: Hasebe-Ueda (ALEA 2018) Problem 3.3: the optimal C with mu boxplus S(0,t) unimodal for all t >= C D_mu^2

RUNNER-UP: Agresti-Veraar (2025) Problems 4, 5, critical Allen-Cahn

JUSTIFICATION. I distrust results that satisfy the words of a question rather than its intent, and that test cuts against the nominally more important candidate. Janson 20.2 / Thevenin's conjecture is refuted by a lacunary two-rare-atom law that every specialist would call a missing regularity hypothesis (Janson's own Examples 18.36-37 are in that spirit); the critical infinite-variance case, the one people actually care about, is a sketch whose growth condition Reviewer A showed is wrong as written; and there is no Lean-shaped core. Its "importance 4" is importance of the poser, not of the answer.

Hasebe-Ueda 3.3 is the opposite case. The authors asked for the optimal constant; the answer is the constant (1, from their own 4), the extremiser is identified, rigidity is proved, and Reviewer C confirms it "answers exactly the question as posed". Three referees re-derived both proofs independently; an end-to-end mode count that bypasses the criterion sits on the bound only at the extremal law. The load-bearing part (Lemmas A and B, real-polynomial inequalities on a box, plus the two-atom Choquet reduction) is what a proof assistant certifies in full, not an arithmetic fragment, and numerics are natural. Two literature searches found nothing. Modest audience, but a final, sharp, machine-checkable answer to a printed open problem beats a half-finished refutation of a broader one.

Agresti-Veraar is the honest runner-up: a genuine answer on the exact model the authors named, prominent posers, complete Ito reduction; but its Lean core covers only algebra and the Theorem 6.4 scope sentence is unverified.

BIGGEST RISK: the entire argument runs through the source's Lemma 3.1 (unimodality iff every level set of xi_R has at most two points), which no reviewer read from the paper itself; if its wording differs from the memo's quotation, the proof must be rebuilt from Biane's density description directly.
