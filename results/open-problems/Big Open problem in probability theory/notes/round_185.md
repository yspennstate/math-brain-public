# Round 185

Round 185 records the exact feasibility inequality for the remaining capped
support-profile branch.

With
\[
  \alpha_\omega=\log_\Delta\omega,
\]
define
\[
  \delta(\theta,U,\omega)
  =
  2-6\theta-15\theta\alpha_\omega-6U\alpha_\omega .
\]
If the expanded gate-annulus data are still capped by \(2^j\le U
\log_\Delta\bar\chi_p\), then the support-profile argument forces
\[
  G_{\epsilon,\eta,J,U,C_{\rm vol}}\,
  L^{-9\eta}\bar\chi_p^{\delta(\theta,U,\omega)}
  \le 1,
  \qquad
  L=\log_\Delta\bar\chi_p,
\]
where
\[
  G_{\epsilon,\eta,J,U,C_{\rm vol}}
  =
  \frac{1}{4096C_{\rm vol}^{11}}
  \left[\frac{\epsilon}{2}(1-2^{-\eta/2})^2\right]^9
  2^{9\eta J}U^{-9\eta}.
\]

Thus any capped branch occurring arbitrarily close to criticality must have
\[
  \delta(\theta,U,\omega)\le0.
\]
Equivalently, for fixed \(U\), the current method cannot allow capped data
unless
\[
  6U\alpha_\omega
  \ge
  2-6\theta-15\theta\alpha_\omega .
\]

This is the quantitative saturation form of Rounds 183--184.  It identifies
the precise exponent budget that a remaining counterexample branch must obey.
