# Round 395

Round 395 splits the fifteenth prefix-record profiles from Round 394 into
persistent-prefix profiles and a new adjacent return-spread family.

Round 394 left the prefix-record profile
\[
  P^{\rm pre,rec,br,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch}_{i,j}.
\]
Applying the short prefix-record persistent/returning split with
\[
  K=
  2K_i^{\rm gap,br,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch}
\]
gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
  P^{\rm pre,rec,br,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch}_{i,j}
  \le&\;
  P^{\rm pre,per,br,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch}_{i,j}
\\
  &+
  R^{\rm spr,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch}_{i,j}
  +
  R^{\rm spr,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch}_{i,j+1}.
\end{aligned}
\]
The persistent-prefix threshold is \(2K\), and the return-spread threshold is
\(4K\).  Thus the persistent profile carries fifteen \({\rm recbr}\)
markers and the new adjacent return-spread family carries sixteen.

Substitution into the Round 394 finite ladder replaces the prefix-record
contribution under the unchanged coefficient
\[
  D_i^{\rm rec,br,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch}
\]
by the persistent-prefix sum plus the two adjacent sixteenth return-spread
sums.  The fifteenth bridge-return spread family, adjacent fifteenth
return-spread family, fourteenth bridge-return and adjacent return-spread
families, older retained spread families, and the long-run coefficient are
unchanged.

The radial-tail and subdivision criteria now use the persistent-prefix
profiles and sixteenth adjacent return-spread family in place of the fifteenth
prefix-record profiles.  The next internal move is to convert the
persistent-prefix profiles into bounded-gap prefix-pair profiles, paralleling
the previous bounded-gap reduction.

No external literature refresh was performed in this round; the targeted
post-Round 382 source check remains the latest external status recorded by
this project.
