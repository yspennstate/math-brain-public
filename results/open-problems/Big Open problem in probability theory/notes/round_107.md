# Round 107 log

## Goal

Show how the older critical shell-summability route sits inside the new
far-leg framework.

## Result

Added `prop:critical-shell-gives-far-leg`.

If
\[
  \sum_{n\ge0}\tau_{p_c}^{\max}(n)\|S_n^G\|_{2\to2}<\infty,
\]
then uniformly in \(p<p_c\),
\[
  \|T_p^{>R}\|_{2\to2}
  \le
  \sum_{n>R}\tau_{p_c}^{\max}(n)\|S_n^G\|_{2\to2}\to0.
\]
Consequently
\[
  \mathcal R_p^*/\bar\chi_p\to0.
\]

With quasi-transitivity and the mean-field susceptibility input, this again
implies \(p_c<p_{2\to2}\).

## Interpretation

Critical shell summability is stronger than the normalized annular estimates
now targeted by the far-leg program.  This proposition makes the relationship
explicit and keeps the older route integrated with the newer obstruction
language.
