# Round 219

Round 219 records the weight match in the subdivision comparison.

If \(H=\operatorname{Sub}(G)\) and the subdivision inward criterion uses
parameter \(q\), set
\[
  Q=q^2.
\]
For old-root even spheres, the lifted-path identity gives
\[
  B=2i+h,
\]
where \(B\) is the inward count in \(H\) and \((i,h)\) are the original
inward/horizontal radial counts in \(G\).  Therefore
\[
  q^{2B}=Q^{2i+h}.
\]
This is exactly the original two-coordinate radial-defect weight.

For old-root odd spheres there may be one final inward half-step, so
\[
  B=2i+h \quad\text{or}\quad B=2i+h+1,
\]
and still \(q^{2B}\le Q^{2i+h}\).  For midpoint roots, deleting the first
half-edge preserves the old-root inward count or lowers it by one, again
causing no adverse \(q\)-power.  The remaining losses are constants and
bounded radius shifts.
