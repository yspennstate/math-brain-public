# Round 423 nineteenth confined bridges split inward-free or returning

Round 423 splits the nineteenth confined bridge profiles left by Round 422
into inward-free bridge profiles plus bridge-return spread profiles.

Round 422 left, for every finite \(i,j,h\) with
\[
  1\le h\le B_i^{\rm br,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch},
\]
the confined bridge family
\[
  P^{\rm pre,br,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch}_{i,j,h}
\]
under coefficient
\[
  D_i^{\rm rec,br,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch}.
\]

At the doubled fixed-gap scale
\[
  2K_i^{\rm gap,br,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch},
\]
define the inward-free bridge profiles
\[
  P^{\rm pre,br,free,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch}_{i,j,h}
\]
and the bridge-return spread profiles
\[
  R^{\rm spr,br,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch}_{i,j,h}.
\]
The bridge-return spread radius is
\[
  2K_i^{\rm gap,br,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch}\max\{h-1,1\}.
\]

The confined-bridge inward-free-or-returning dichotomy gives, for every
finite \(i,j,h\),
\[
  P^{\rm pre,br,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch}_{i,j,h}
  \le
  P^{\rm pre,br,free,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch}_{i,j,h}
  +
  R^{\rm spr,br,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch}_{i,j,h}.
\]

Substitution into the Round 422 finite ladder replaces
\[
  D_i^{\rm rec,br,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch}
  \sum_h P^{\rm pre,br,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch}_{i,j,h}
\]
by
\[
  D_i^{\rm rec,br,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch}
  \sum_h
  \left(
    P^{\rm pre,br,free,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch}_{i,j,h}
    +
    R^{\rm spr,br,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch}_{i,j,h}
  \right).
\]
The coefficient, finite \(h\)-range, and long-run coefficient are unchanged.

The adjacent nineteenth return-spread family from Round 419, the earlier
nineteenth bridge-return spread family from Round 417, the eighteenth
retained spread families, older retained families, enlarged spread-return
profiles, exact-level return terms, and all finite cutoffs carry forward.

The radial-tail and subdivision criteria now use the nineteenth inward-free
bridge and new bridge-return spread formulation.  The next unresolved issue
is to absorb the inward-free bridge profiles into prefix-record profiles and
update the long-run coefficient.
