# Round 364

Round 364 absorbs the tenth inward-free bridge family from Round 363 into a
deeper prefix-record profile plus a long-run coefficient update.

Round 363 left the inward-free bridge family
\[
  P^{\rm pre,br,free,recbr,recbr,recbr,recbr,recbr,recbr,recbr,recbr,recbr,recbr,ch}_{i,j,h}
\]
at cutoff
\[
  2K_i^{\rm gap,br,recbr,recbr,recbr,recbr,recbr,recbr,recbr,recbr,recbr,ch}.
\]
Applying the inward-free-bridge chain/long-run alternative and then the
locally short staircase-chain prefix-record spread bound gives
\[
  P^{\rm pre,br,free,recbr,recbr,recbr,recbr,recbr,recbr,recbr,recbr,recbr,recbr,ch}_{i,j,h}
  \le
  P^{\rm pre,rec,br,recbr,recbr,recbr,recbr,recbr,recbr,recbr,recbr,recbr,recbr,ch}_{i,j}
  +
  W_{\beta_i,p}T_{i,j}^{\rm long}.
\]

The finite \(h\)-range has size
\[
  B_i^{\rm br,recbr,recbr,recbr,recbr,recbr,recbr,recbr,recbr,recbr,ch},
\]
so the finite ladder replaces
\[
  D_i^{\rm rec,br,recbr,recbr,recbr,recbr,recbr,recbr,recbr,recbr,ch}
\]
by
\[
  D_i^{\rm rec,br,recbr,recbr,recbr,recbr,recbr,recbr,recbr,recbr,recbr,ch}
  =
  D_i^{\rm rec,br,recbr,recbr,recbr,recbr,recbr,recbr,recbr,recbr,ch}
  B_i^{\rm br,recbr,recbr,recbr,recbr,recbr,recbr,recbr,recbr,recbr,ch},
\]
and increments the long-run coefficient by this new fixed coefficient:
\[
  A_i^{\rm long,br,recbr,recbr,recbr,recbr,recbr,recbr,recbr,recbr,recbr,ch}
  =
  A_i^{\rm long,br,recbr,recbr,recbr,recbr,recbr,recbr,recbr,recbr,ch}
  +
  D_i^{\rm rec,br,recbr,recbr,recbr,recbr,recbr,recbr,recbr,recbr,recbr,ch}.

The tenth bridge-return spread family from Round 363, the adjacent tenth
return-spread family, the ninth bridge-return family, older return-spread and
bridge-return families, and exact-level return profiles are retained.

profile and the updated fixed long-run coefficient.  The next internal move
is to split the tenth prefix-record family into a persistent-prefix family
plus adjacent return-spread profiles, paralleling Round 359.

No external literature refresh was performed in this round; the Round 339
source-checked status remains the latest external status.
