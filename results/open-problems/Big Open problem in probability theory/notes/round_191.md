# Round 191

Round 191 splits the residual half-logarithmic obstruction into a more
localized density alternative.

Fix \(\alpha_\omega=\log_\Delta\omega>0\) and \(\lambda>0\), and put
\[
  U_\lambda=\frac{1}{2\alpha_\omega}+\lambda .
\]
If the finite-set obstruction persists, then for every admissible parameter
choice the expanded gate-annulus data can be chosen so that either
\[
  2^j>U_\lambda\log_\Delta\bar\chi_p,
\]
or, after capping the scale by \(U_\lambda\log_\Delta\bar\chi_p\), the selected
anchors
\[
  A=\{a_z:z\in Z^\sharp\}
\]
occupy a polynomially large fraction of their containing root ball:
\[
  \frac{|A|}{|B(x,2^{j+1}+r_p)|}
  \ge
  D_{\epsilon,\eta,J,\lambda,C_{\rm vol},\omega}
  L^{-3\eta}
  \bar\chi_p^{-2\lambda\alpha_\omega-2\theta-5\theta\alpha_\omega},
  \qquad
  L=\log_\Delta\bar\chi_p .
\]

Thus excluding only the half-log scale is not the only possible closing
route.  It is also enough to exclude super-half-log expanded gate-annulus data
and this near-half-log anchor-density alternative.  The latter is now a
concrete saturation target: near the optimized anchor-count barrier, any
counterexample must place many selected gates inside almost the full
available volume of the corresponding root ball.
