# Round 392

Round 392 projects the fifteenth fixed-gap prefix-pair profiles from
Round 391 to confined bridge profiles.

Round 391 left the finite family
\[
  P^{\rm pre,gap,br,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch}_{i,j,h}
\]
with fixed-gap scale
\[
  K_i^{\rm gap,br,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch}
\]
and height range
\[
  1\le h\le
  B_i^{\rm br,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch}.
\]
The fixed-gap-to-confined-bridge projection gives, for each \(i,j,h\),
\[
  P^{\rm pre,gap,br,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch}_{i,j,h}
  \le
  P^{\rm pre,br,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch}_{i,j,h}.
\]

Substitution into the Round 391 finite ladder replaces the fixed-gap
prefix-pair contribution under the unchanged coefficient
\[
  D_i^{\rm rec,br,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch}
\]
by the finite confined-bridge sum.  The height range, fixed-gap scale,
adjacent fifteenth return-spread family, fourteenth bridge-return and adjacent
return-spread families, older retained spread families, and long-run
coefficient are unchanged.

The radial-tail and subdivision criteria now use the fifteenth confined bridge
profiles.  The next internal move is to split these confined bridge profiles
into inward-free bridge profiles plus the next bridge-return spread family,
paralleling the previous confined-bridge split.

No external literature refresh was performed in this round; the targeted
post-Round 382 source check remains the latest external status recorded by
this project.
