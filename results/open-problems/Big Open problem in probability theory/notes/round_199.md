# Round 199

Round 199 specializes the remote exponent criterion from Round 198 to a
clean half-to-full logarithmic window.

Choose
\[
  \lambda=s,\qquad
  \theta=s,\qquad
  B=\frac{1}{2\alpha_\omega}-2s,
  \qquad
  0<s<\frac{1}{6\alpha_\omega}.
\]
Then the admissible remote window becomes
\[
  \left(\frac{1}{2\alpha_\omega}-3s\right)L<n\le
  \left(\frac{1}{\alpha_\omega}+3s\right)L,
  \qquad
  L=\log_\Delta\bar\chi_p,
\]
and the obstruction exponent becomes
\[
  (2+7\alpha_\omega)s.
\]
Thus, after excluding expanded gate-annulus data above
\[
  \left(\frac{1}{2\alpha_\omega}+s\right)L,
\]
it is enough to prove that the spherical-average profile supremum on this
half-to-full logarithmic window has strict exponent smaller than
\(-(2+7\alpha_\omega)s\).
