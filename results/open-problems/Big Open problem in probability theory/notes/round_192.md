# Round 192

Round 192 replaces the near-half-log anchor-density alternative by an
intrinsic root-ball mass alternative.

In the capped branch from Round 191, the selected anchors satisfy
\[
  A=\{a_z:z\in Z^\sharp\}\subseteq B(x,2^{j+1}+r_p)
\]
and they carry root mass
\[
  \sum_{a\in A}\tau_p(x,a)\ge \kappa_j^{\rm vol}(p).
\]
Therefore the same near-half-log cap forces
\[
  \frac{1}{|B(x,2^{j+1}+r_p)|}
  \sum_{a\in B(x,2^{j+1}+r_p)}\tau_p(x,a)
  \ge
  D_{\epsilon,\eta,J,\lambda,C_{\rm vol},\omega}
  L^{-3\eta}
  \bar\chi_p^{-2\lambda\alpha_\omega-2\theta-5\theta\alpha_\omega}.
\]

This is a cleaner residual condition.  It no longer mentions the selected
anchor set in the closing branch: either the expanded gate-annulus obstruction
escapes above
\[
  \left(\frac{1}{2\alpha_\omega}+\lambda\right)
  \log_\Delta\bar\chi_p,
\]
or an ordinary metric ball of radius \(2^{j+1}+r_p\) has unusually large
average root two-point mass.  A proof that excludes both phenomena near
criticality would force \(\mathcal R_p^*/\bar\chi_p\to0\), and hence the
\(L^2\) gap under the usual quasi-transitive mean-field input.
