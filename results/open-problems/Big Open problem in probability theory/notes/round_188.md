# Round 188

Round 188 packages the anchor-count feasibility inequality as a direct
large-scale criterion.

For fixed obstruction parameters, define
\[
  \rho(\theta,U,\omega)
  =
  1-2\theta-5\theta\alpha_\omega-2U\alpha_\omega,
  \qquad
  \alpha_\omega=\log_\Delta\omega .
\]
When \(\rho>0\), the capped anchor-count alternative from Round 186 is
impossible near criticality.  Therefore a finite-set counterexample must
produce expanded gate-annulus data satisfying
\[
  2^j>U\log_\Delta\bar\chi_p .
\]

The new criterion says: if, for every obstruction constant \(\epsilon\), one
can exclude such large expanded gate-annulus data for some admissible
parameters with \(\rho>0\), then
\[
  \mathcal R_p^*/\bar\chi_p\to0.
\]
Under the quasi-transitive mean-field susceptibility input, this implies
\[
  p_c(G)<p_{2\to2}(G).
\]

This is the cleanest use of the anchor-count correction: in the positive
\(\rho\) range, there is no remaining capped operator branch to estimate.  The
only surviving obstruction is genuinely large-scale expanded gate-annulus
data.
