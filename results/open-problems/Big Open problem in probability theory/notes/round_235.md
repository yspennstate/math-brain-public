subdivision criterion.

  \mathfrak L_{Q,\rho}^{G\to H,{\rm rad}}

\[
  p_c(G)<p_{2\to2}(G)

four fully localized profile families:
same-sphere spread, exact-level same-sphere congestion, spread exterior
