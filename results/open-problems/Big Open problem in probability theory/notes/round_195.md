# Round 195

Round 195 inserts an exact-sphere average between the annular-average branch
and the pointwise diagnostic.

If the super-half-log alternative fails, Round 193 gives a lower bound on the
average of \(\tau_p(x,\cdot)\) over the expanded root annulus
\[
  I_{j,p}^{\rm gate}=(2^j-r_p,2^{j+1}+r_p].
\]
This annulus is a disjoint union of exact distance spheres around \(x\).  Hence
at least one radius
\[
  n\in I_{j,p}^{\rm gate}\cap\mathbb N
\]
has
\[
  \frac{\sum_{a:\ d(x,a)=n}\tau_p(x,a)}
       {|\{a:\ d(x,a)=n\}|}
  \ge
  D_{\epsilon,\eta,J,\lambda,C_{\rm vol},\omega}
  L^{-3\eta}
  \bar\chi_p^{-2\lambda\alpha_\omega-2\theta-5\theta\alpha_\omega}.
\]

This keeps averaged information while localizing the residual branch to a
single graph distance.  It is stronger than the pointwise diagnostic from
Round 194 and more local than the annular-average target from Round 193.
