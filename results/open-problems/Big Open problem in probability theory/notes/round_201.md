# Round 201

Round 201 rewrites the half-to-full spherical-profile target in terms of
excess over the open-geodesic baseline.

For
\[
  c_s=\frac{1}{2\alpha_\omega}-3s
\]
define
\[
  \mathfrak E_p^{\rm sph}(s)
  =
  \sup
  \frac{\bar\tau_p^{\rm sph}(n)}{p^n}
\]
over integers \(n\) satisfying
\[
  c_sL<n\le
  \left(\frac{1}{\alpha_\omega}+3s\right)L,
  \qquad L=\log_\Delta\bar\chi_p.
\]
Since \(p^n\le p^{c_sL}\) on this window, a strict bound
\[
  \limsup_{p\uparrow p_c}
  \frac{\log_\Delta\mathfrak E_p^{\rm sph}(s)}
       {\log_\Delta\bar\chi_p}
  <
  -c_s\log_\Delta p_c-(2+7\alpha_\omega)s
\]
implies the Round 199 profile exponent condition.  Thus the residual target
can be read as controlling the polynomial overhead beyond geodesic
connections, rather than forcing the profile below the geodesic scale itself.
