# Round 422 nineteenth fixed-gap profiles project to confined bridges

Round 422 projects the nineteenth fixed-gap prefix-pair profiles left by
Round 421 to confined bridge profiles at the same marker depth.

Round 421 left, for every finite \(i,j\), the fixed-gap contribution
\[
  \sum_{h=1}^{B_i^{\rm br,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch}}
  P^{\rm pre,gap,br,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch}_{i,j,h}
\]
under coefficient
\[
  D_i^{\rm rec,br,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch}.
\]
The fixed-gap scale has already absorbed the bounded-successor budget and is
relabeled as
\[
  K_i^{\rm gap,br,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch}.
\]

For the same finite \(h\)-range, define the confined bridge profile
\[
  P^{\rm pre,br,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch}_{i,j,h}
  =
  \mathfrak P^{G\to H,{\rm rad,tan,pre,out,per,bridge,short}}_
  {Q,\lambda_i,\sigma_i,\beta_i,j,
   K_i^{\rm gap,br,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch},L_i,h}.
\]
The fixed-gap-to-confined-bridge reduction gives, for every finite \(i,j,h\),
\[
  P^{\rm pre,gap,br,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch}_{i,j,h}
  \le
  P^{\rm pre,br,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch}_{i,j,h}.
\]

Substitution into the Round 421 finite ladder replaces
\[
  D_i^{\rm rec,br,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch}
  \sum_{h=1}^{B_i^{\rm br,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch}}
  P^{\rm pre,gap,br,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch}_{i,j,h}
\]
by
\[
  D_i^{\rm rec,br,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch}
  \sum_{h=1}^{B_i^{\rm br,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch}}
  P^{\rm pre,br,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch}_{i,j,h}.
\]
The coefficient
\[
  D_i^{\rm rec,br,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch}
\]
is unchanged, the finite \(h\)-range is unchanged, and the long-run
coefficient
\[
  A_i^{\rm long,br,recbr,\ldots,recbr,ch}
\]
is unchanged from Round 418.

The adjacent nineteenth return-spread family from Round 419, the nineteenth
bridge-return spread family from Round 417, the eighteenth retained spread
families, older retained families, enlarged spread-return profiles,
exact-level return terms, and all finite cutoffs carry forward.

The radial-tail and subdivision criteria now use the nineteenth confined
bridge formulation.  The next unresolved issue is to split these confined
bridge profiles into inward-free bridge profiles plus bridge-return spread
profiles, paralleling the previous confined-bridge dichotomies.
