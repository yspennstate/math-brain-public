## 5. (b) Routes tried and the precise reason each failed to close the target

This is the nearest successful historical method, but its known seeds are **5-chromatic**, i.e. 4-color UNSAT and 5-color SAT. Prior HARD8-08 work reconstructed published six-chromatic two-distance scaffolds, most notably the exact 33-vertex `{1,sqrt(3)}` graph of De Neve et al., and proved a general substitution principle: a pure unit-distance gadget whose terminals are separated by `sqrt(3)` and forced different in every 5-coloring would convert that scaffold into a pure unit-distance non-5-colorable graph.

The obstruction is not the scaffold. The missing object is the **five-color forced-different pure unit-distance terminal gadget**. The two-distance graph itself is outside the source target.

Thus the bottleneck is not a general-purpose SAT encoding, a lack of small six-chromatic abstract graphs, or a lack of exact distance scaffolds. It is a missing one-distance geometry carrying a certified five-color contradiction.
