\[

Consider the natural **near-skew** subclass: a block contains `0`, omits a small set `O` of pairs, and chooses exactly one orbit from every remaining pair. Write the orientation of a nonomitted pair as `s_j in {+1,-1}`, where `+1` selects `C_j` and `-1` selects `C_{j+29}`.

Let the pinned Gauss periods over `F_617` be `eta_r`, and for a frequency orbit `C_c` define

\[
F(c)=1+\sum_{C_j\subset X}\eta_{c+j}.
\]

Then

\[
F(c)+F(c+29)=2+\sum_{j\notin O}(\eta_{c+j}+\eta_{c+j+29}).
\]

The 58 periods sum to `-1` in `F_617`, so

\[
\boxed{S_O(c):=F(c)+F(c+29)=1-\sum_{j\in O}(\eta_{c+j}+\eta_{c+j+29}).}

Subtracting gives

\[
\boxed{D_s(c):=F(c)-F(c+29)=\sum_{j\notin O}s_j(\eta_{c+j}-\eta_{c+j+29}).}

\[
\boxed{4F(c)F(c+29)=S_O(c)^2-D_s(c)^2.}
