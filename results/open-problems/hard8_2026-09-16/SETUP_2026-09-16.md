## HARD8-01: Černý conjecture

**Full target.** Let `(Q, Sigma, delta)` be a complete deterministic finite automaton with `n >= 2` states and a finite nonempty alphabet. If there is any word sending every state to one state, prove that there is one of length at most `(n-1)^2`, or give a rigorously certified counterexample.

All states, automata and finite alphabets are in scope. Binary, circular, Eulerian and one-cluster classes must not be substituted for the general statement. A proposed counterexample requires an exact automaton, a synchronization witness, and a certified shortest reset length greater than the conjectured bound. Failure of a search heuristic is not a counterexample.

**Initial research directions.** Subset compression versus extension; non-greedy potentials that control accumulated compression costs; transition-semigroup rank; exact small-instance experiments to expose missing invariants. Weaker asymptotic bounds or new subclasses are partial progress.

**Primary source.** Mikhail V. Volkov, *List of Results on the Černý Conjecture and Reset Thresholds for Synchronizing Automata*, https://arxiv.org/html/2508.15655v3. Its living survey explicitly records the general conjecture as neither confirmed nor refuted as of August 24, 2026. Check subsequent literature before making a novelty claim.

## HARD8-02: graceful tree conjecture

**Full target.** Every finite tree `T` on `n` vertices admits a bijection `phi: V(T) -> {0,...,n-1}` such that the edge differences `|phi(u)-phi(v)|`, over all edges, are exactly `{1,...,n-1}`.

Every tree and every size are in scope. Almost-graceful, range-relaxed, bounded-degree, almost-all-tree and tree-decomposition statements are not the full target. An exact tree plus a complete nonlabelability certificate is required for a refutation; a solver timeout does not suffice.

**Initial research directions.** Rainbow spanning embeddings into difference-colored complete graphs; robust leaf-extension invariants; absorption and repair of almost-graceful labelings; decomposition with exact compatibility conditions; checkable constraint certificates.

**Primary source.** Shoham Letzter, Alexey Pokrovskiy and Ella Williams, *On the gracesize of trees*, version dated July 31, 2026, https://arxiv.org/html/2511.11331v2. Conjecture 1.1 is the full target; their asymptotic gracesize theorem is a partial result rather than an exact graceful-labeling theorem.

## HARD8-03: deterministic log-rank conjecture

**Full target.** Find universal constants `C,c > 0` such that every finite total Boolean communication matrix `M in {0,1}^{X x Y}` satisfies

`D(M) <= C [log_2(rank_R(M)+1)]^c`,

or refute existence of such constants. Here `D` is worst-case deterministic two-party communication complexity, with protocol/output conventions stated explicitly. The `+1` handles zero and small rank.

The target uses total Boolean matrices and real rank. Approximate rank, sign rank and randomized communication are different quantities. A refutation requires an asymptotic family exceeding every fixed power of log rank, not an isolated hard matrix. A universal bound polynomial in rank is still weaker than this target.

**Initial research directions.** Monochromatic rectangles with quantitative recursion; hereditary low-rank structure; discrepancy; exact rank certificates; lifting and equivalent formulations.

**Primary sources.** *The Log-Rank Conjecture: New Equivalent Formulations*, ECCC TR25-141, including its 2026 revision: https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2025/141/. Benny Sudakov and István Tomon, *Matrix discrepancy and the log-rank conjecture*, https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.18524. Verify any more recent bounds before crediting an improvement.

## HARD8-04: Hadamard existence conjecture

**Full target.** For every positive integer `m`, prove existence of a matrix `H in {+1,-1}^{4m x 4m}` with `H H^T = 4m I`, or rigorously prove nonexistence at an admissible order.

This is the all-orders existence conjecture, not Sylvester discrepancy, the stronger circulant/skew variants, complex Hadamard matrices, or a finite-order challenge. Validate a construction in exact integer arithmetic. A solver timeout cannot establish nonexistence. Finite tables and infinite subfamilies remain partial unless they cover every admissible order.

**Initial research directions.** Complementary sequences, difference families, block constructions, orthogonal designs, composition with exact order coverage, and algebraic nonexistence certificates.

**Primary problem-setting source.** Epoch AI, https://epoch.ai/frontiermath/open-problems/hadamard, accessed September 16, 2026. Its update reports a finite-order-668 solution while its general-conjecture description continues to identify the all-orders problem as open. Do not reuse stale text calling order 668 the smallest unknown case. Inspect the original construction papers and verify subsequent developments.

## HARD8-05: Erdős–Straus conjecture

**Full target.** For every integer `n >= 2`, there exist positive integers `x,y,z`, with repetitions allowed, satisfying

`4/n = 1/x + 1/y + 1/z`.

Cover all integers; include the elementary `n=2` case. Density-one results, finitely many checked integers, residue subclasses and tame-only solutions are not the full result. A refutation needs a rigorously complete finite search reduction and a certificate excluding every admissible triple, not merely an unsuccessful bounded search.

**Initial research directions.** Verify prime reductions; study divisor factorizations, modular coverings and parametric identities with exact symbolic certificates; characterize uncovered classes without assuming a finite congruence cover must suffice.

**Primary source.** Xiaoping Xu, *Congruence Classes of Supporting the Erdös-Straus Conjecture I: Tame Solutions*, May 22, 2026, https://arxiv.org/html/2605.23601v1. The paper explicitly identifies the full conjecture as open and studies restricted families. Check every reused identity and hypothesis, and inspect newer literature.

## HARD8-06: Lehmer's Mahler-measure gap conjecture

**Full target.** For a nonzero integer polynomial `P(x)=a product_j(x-alpha_j)`, let

`M(P)=|a| product_j max(1,|alpha_j|)`.

Prove existence of an absolute `epsilon>0` such that `M(P)>1` implies `M(P)>=1+epsilon`, uniformly over all degrees, or construct a certified sequence with `1<M(P_k)->1`.

Account for monomials, cyclotomic factors, reducibility and nonmonic polynomials. The stronger assertion identifying the exact smallest measure with Lehmer's degree-ten example is not required. This is not Lehmer's totient problem or the nonvanishing conjecture for Ramanujan tau, and not merely an elliptic version. Bounded-degree searches, special families and degree-dependent positive gaps are partial.

**Initial research directions.** Finite-field counting reformulations, resultants, reciprocal algebraic units, and attempts to eliminate degree dependence from known lower-bound methods.

**Primary source.** Emmanuel Breuillard and Péter P. Varjú, *On the Lehmer conjecture and counting in finite fields*, Discrete Analysis 2019:5, https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.13363, DOI 10.19086/da.8306. Contemporary context checked in the May 5, 2026 Max Planck Institute/Bonn seminar listing *Mahler Measure and Numerical Semigroups*: https://math-events.uni-bonn.de/event/1274/?print=1. These are starting points, not evidence that claimed new proofs are valid; critically inspect later literature.

## HARD8-07: classical Littlewood conjecture

**Full target.** For every pair of real numbers `alpha,beta`,

`liminf_{n -> infinity} n ||n alpha|| ||n beta|| = 0`,

where `||t||` is distance to the nearest integer. Include rational cases and retain all universal quantifiers. A refutation requires an exactly specified pair with a proved positive eventual lower bound.

Distinguish the classical simultaneous Diophantine conjecture from the trigonometric L1 theorem/inverse problem, p-adic and mixed variants, or stronger uniform variants. A purported refutation of a different variant does not resolve this one. Almost-everywhere validity, dimension-zero exceptions, individual pairs, and fixed positive numerical upper bounds are partial. This is separate from the existing Furstenberg x2/x3 task, though checked techniques may transfer.

**Initial research directions.** Continued-fraction resonances, diagonal flows and lattice formulations, entropy-zero obstructions, and exact constructions with certifiable estimates.

**Primary sources.** Yann Bugeaud, *Around the Littlewood conjecture in Diophantine approximation*, https://pmb.centre-mersenne.org/articles/10.5802/pmb.1/. Tapani Matala-Aho, Topi Törmä and Matti Vapa, *Numerical Upper Bounds in the Littlewood Conjecture*, Experimental Mathematics, April 2, 2026, DOI 10.1080/10586458.2026.2615945; institutional record: https://research.aalto.fi/en/publications/numerical-upper-bounds-in-the-littlewood-conjecture/. Check subsequent original papers and the precise hypotheses of all variant results.

## HARD8-08: Hadwiger–Nelson plane coloring

**Full target.** Determine the exact chromatic number of the graph with vertex set all points of `R^2`, where edges join pairs at Euclidean distance exactly one. Colorings are arbitrary; measurable and fractional versions are different problems.

The starting established bounds are `5 <= chi <= 7`, to be rechecked against current primary literature. A full solution requires matching exact lower and upper bounds. Off-diagonal six-colorings in which the sixth color avoids a distance different from one are not ordinary unit-distance six-colorings. A finite lower-bound graph must have a certified planar unit-distance embedding, not just a high abstract chromatic number. A coloring must cover every point and handle all boundaries rigorously; sampled-point checks are insufficient. New five-chromatic examples or a one-sided improvement without a matching bound are partial. This is not the graph-minor Hadwiger conjecture.

**Initial research directions.** Exact algebraic unit-distance graphs, independently checkable noncolorability certificates, geometric composition, periodic/aperiodic tilings and exact boundary conventions. Use numerical optimization only to propose objects that are subsequently certified.

**Primary sources.** Aubrey D. N. J. de Grey, *The chromatic number of the plane is at least 5*, https://arxiv.org/abs/1804.02385. Konrad Mundinger, Sebastian Pokutta, Christoph Spiegel and Max Zimmer, *Extending the Continuum of Six-Colorings*, arXiv:2404.05509; authors' explanation: https://www.pokutta.com/blog/research/2024/07/28/hadwiger-nelson.html. Recent primary search result: Jan Kristian Haugland, *A Moser-spindle-free 5-chromatic unit distance graph on 2131 vertices in the plane*, arXiv:2608.04542, August 5, 2026. The latest result is a five-chromatic construction, not a matching determination of the full plane's chromatic number.
