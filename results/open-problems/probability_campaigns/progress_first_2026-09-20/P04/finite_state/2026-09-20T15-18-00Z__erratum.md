# P04 / D0038 — proof clarification for Theorem 2.1 in the 15:02 report

Date: 2026-09-20 UTC.

This is a narrow correctness clarification to the immutable report

`probability_campaigns/progress_first_2026-09-20/P04/finite_state/2026-09-20T15-02-00Z__report.md`

(committed as `ea745300c5b9aeb3b2e946350d63d7e1dc1b83ef`). No mathematical claim or status changes.

In the proof of Theorem 2.1, after choosing the infinite subsequence of pad indices on which the exact regular equivariance (2.1) holds, **absorb every nonmasking pad coordinate into the auxiliary variable `Z` and relabel the masking subsequence as `P_1,P_2,...`**. This is legitimate because the original pads are independent and the masking subsequence remains iid uniform and independent of the enlarged `Z`.

With that harmless reduction, the increasing sigma-fields

\[
\mathcal F_r:=\sigma(Z,P_1,\ldots,P_r)
\]

really do increase to the full source sigma-field with respect to which `Y` is measurable. For every `r`, choose a masking index `n>r`. Exact regular masking by `P_n` gives

\[
\mathcal L(Y\mid \sigma(Z,P_j:j\ne n))=\mathrm{Unif}(A),
\]

and since `F_r` is contained in that conditioning sigma-field,

\[
\mathbb E[1_{\{Y=a\}}\mid\mathcal F_r]=1/|A|
\qquad(a\in A).
\]

Martingale convergence now yields

\[
1_{\{Y=a\}}=1/|A|\quad\text{a.s.},
\]

which is impossible for `|A|>=2`.

Thus Theorem 2.1 stands as stated; the original report merely omitted the explicit absorption/relabeling step needed to make the final martingale filtration generate the whole source.
