## 2. Pairwise independence of the three overlap distances

Let `B_1,B_2,B_3` be independent uniform `m`-subsets of `[2m-1]`, and put
\[
D_{ij}=m-|B_i\cap B_j|.
\]

### Lemma P08-TRIPLE-DISTANCE-PAIRWISE-INDEPENDENCE
The variables `D_12,D_13,D_23` are pairwise independent.

**Proof.** Conditional on `B_1`, the sets `B_2,B_3` remain independent, while the conditional law of `D(B_1,B_j)` depends only on `|B_1|=m` and therefore is deterministic. Hence, for bounded `f,g`,
\[
\mathbb E[f(D_{12})g(D_{13})\mid B_1]
=\mathbb Ef(D)\,\mathbb Eg(D).
\]
Average over `B_1`. The other two pairs follow by conditioning on `B_2` or `B_3`. \(\square\)

Passing from iid sampling to an ordered distinct triple changes total variation by at most `3/L_m`. Since `g_m(d)1_{d>=4}` is uniformly bounded,
\[
\mathbb E_{\rm distinct}g_m(D_{12})g_m(D_{13})
=(\mathbb Eg_m(D))^2+O(L_m^{-1})
=m^{-4}+O(m^{-5}).
\tag{2.1}
\]
Therefore pair-overlap kernels have no algebraic covariance at order `m^-3`. Also
\[
\mathbb E_{\rm distinct}\sum_{i<j}g_m(D_{ij})
=3m^{-2}+33m^{-3}+300m^{-4}+O(m^{-9/2}).
\tag{2.2}
\]
The `33` is only the explicit-kernel contribution because the exact pair theorem still has an `O(m^-3)` analytic remainder.

## 3. Exact connected-triple identity

Fix distinct `B_1,B_2,B_3`. Define singleton Venn blocks
\[
U_i=B_i\setminus(B_j\cup B_k),\qquad d_i=|U_i|,
\tag{3.1}
\]
where `{i,j,k}={1,2,3}`. Let `F` be the sigma-field generated by all Gaussian rows that occur in at least two of the three sets. Put
\[
q_i=\Pr(E_{B_i}\mid F),\qquad p_m=\Pr(E_B),\qquad X_i=q_i/p_m-1.
\]
The singleton Gaussian row blocks are disjoint and independent conditional on `F`; hence the events are conditionally independent and
\[
\Pr(E_1E_2E_3)=\mathbb E(q_1q_2q_3),\qquad
\Pr(E_iE_j)=\mathbb E(q_iq_j).
\]
Thus the normalized connected kernel
\[
\mathfrak K(B_1,B_2,B_3)
:=\frac{\Pr(E_1E_2E_3)}{p_m^3}
-\sum_{i<j}\frac{\Pr(E_iE_j)}{p_m^2}+2
\]
satisfies the exact identity
\[
\boxed{\mathfrak K(B_1,B_2,B_3)=\mathbb E(X_1X_2X_3).}
\tag{3.2}
\]
No independence among the overlapping minors is assumed.

## 4. Transverse triples have connected correction `O(m^-3)`

### Theorem P08-TRANSVERSE-TRIPLE-CUMULANT
Fix `theta in (0,1/3)` and fixed `lambda>0`. If
\[
\theta m\le d_i\le(1-\theta)m,\qquad i=1,2,3,
\tag{4.1}
\]
then
\[
\boxed{|\mathfrak K(B_1,B_2,B_3)|\le C_{\theta,\lambda}m^{-3}.}
\tag{4.2}
\]
Equivalently,
\[
\frac{\Pr(E_1E_2E_3)}{p_m^3}
=1+\sum_{i<j}\left(\frac{\Pr(E_iE_j)}{p_m^2}-1\right)
+O_{\theta,\lambda}(m^{-3}).
\tag{4.3}
\]

**Proof.** Given `F`, the matrix in event `i` is a fixed `(m-d_i)x m` Gaussian shared block plus `d_i` fresh Gaussian rows. The inherited conditional block transfer and one-square hard-edge estimate give, for arbitrary fixed `J`,
\[
\frac{q_i}{p_m}
=\frac{\mathcal L_{d_i}}{\mathbb E\mathcal L_{d_i}}+O_{L^3}(m^{-J}).
\tag{4.4}
\]
Apply (1.1) with `q=3`. Under (4.1),
\[
\|X_i\|_3\le C_{\theta,\lambda}m^{-1}.
\]
The `X_i` need not be independent. Holder and (3.2) yield
\[
|\mathfrak K|\le\prod_i\|X_i\|_3\le C_{\theta,\lambda}m^{-3}.
\]
\(\square\)

This is a direct order-three proof; the full saturated atlas is not needed on the transverse sector.

## 5. Transverse Venn geometry is overwhelmingly typical

### Lemma P08-TRIPLE-SINGLETON-CONCENTRATION
For independent uniform `m`-subsets,
\[
\Pr\{\exists i:\ d_i\notin[m/16,2m/3]\}\le Ce^{-cm}.
\tag{5.1}
\]
The same holds for ordered distinct triples with an additional `O(L_m^{-1})` term.

**Proof.** Fix `i=1` and let `D_{12}=|B_1\setminus B_2|`. Its hypergeometric mean is `m(m-1)/(2m-1)=m/2+O(1)`, so sampling-without-replacement Hoeffding gives
\[
\Pr\{D_{12}\notin[m/3,2m/3]\}\le2e^{-c_1m}.
\]
Conditional on `B_1,B_2` and `D_{12}=d`,
\[
d_1=|(B_1\setminus B_2)\cap B_3^c|
\]
is hypergeometric with mean
\[
d\frac{m-1}{2m-1}.
\]
On `d>=m/3` this is at least `m/7` for large `m`, so a second hypergeometric Chernoff bound gives
\[
\Pr\{d_1<m/16\mid D_{12}\in[m/3,2m/3]\}\le e^{-c_2m}.
\]
Also `d_1<=D_{12}<=2m/3`. Union bound over `i`. \(\square\)

Thus almost every triple by count lies in the sector of Theorem P08-TRANSVERSE-TRIPLE-CUMULANT. This count statement alone cannot discard endpoint triples from the factorial moment, because a normalized conditional hard-edge probability may become exponentially large when fresh rows disappear.

## 6. Uniform `L^3` environment integrability for every `d>=3`

Let `C` be `(m-d)x m` standard Gaussian and
\[
Y_d=\frac{\mathcal L_d(C)}{\mathbb E\mathcal L_d(C)}.
\]

### Lemma P08-ENV-L3-DEFICIENCY-THREE
There is a universal `C_3<infinity` such that for every `m>d>=3`,
\[
\boxed{\mathbb E Y_d^3\le C_3.}
\tag{6.1}
\]

**Proof.** Jensen in the independent `Z` variable gives
\[
\mathcal L_d(C)^3
\le\mathbb E_Z(1+Z^T(CC^T)^{-1}Z)^{3/2}.
\]
After averaging over `C`, Hotelling's identity writes
\[
1+Z^T(CC^T)^{-1}Z=B^{-1},\qquad
B\sim\mathrm{Beta}\left(\frac{d+1}{2},\frac{m-d}{2}\right).
\]
For `d>=3`,
\[
\mathbb EB^{-3/2}
=\frac{\Gamma((d-2)/2)\Gamma((m+1)/2)}
{\Gamma((d+1)/2)\Gamma((m-2)/2)}.
\tag{6.2}
\]
The exact mean is
\[
\mathbb E\mathcal L_d
=\frac{\Gamma((m+1)/2)\Gamma(d/2)}
{\Gamma(m/2)\Gamma((d+1)/2)}.
\tag{6.3}
\]
Hence
\[
\mathbb EY_d^3
\le
\frac{\Gamma((d-2)/2)\Gamma((d+1)/2)^2}{\Gamma(d/2)^3}
\frac{\Gamma(m/2)^3}{\Gamma((m+1)/2)^2\Gamma((m-2)/2)}.
\tag{6.4}
\]
Standard Gautschi/Wendel gamma-ratio inequalities bound both factors uniformly for integers `d>=3,m>d`; therefore (6.1) holds. \(\square\)

This shows the Gaussian environment itself has no third-moment blowup anywhere once the fresh deficiency reaches three. The remaining difficulty for `3<=d=o(m)` is quantitative transfer from the true conditional hard-edge probability to this environment, not integrability of the environment.
