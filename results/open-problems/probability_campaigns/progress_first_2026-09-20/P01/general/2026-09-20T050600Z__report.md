# 1. The precise new obligation inherited from P01-A

P01-A proved that, on a terminal-stabilized finite-support ray, factor-two slack increases strictly between M-SERPT record contacts and can drop only at the contacts. For one generic atomic contact it obtained

\[
J=\frac{\lambda p}{2A_uA_cA_b}
\left[A_b(v_c+2LM)-v_b(A_c-\lambda Lq)\right]\ge0,
\tag{1.1}
\]

where `u<c<b` lie on one old M-SERPT plateau of level `L`,

\[
p=P(u<X\le c),\qquad q=P(c<X\le b),\qquad
M=E[X1_{\{c<X\le b\}}],
\]

and `A_x=1-lambda E[X wedge x]`, `v_x=E[(X wedge x)^2]`.

The finite-support bottleneck was to pay one or many such downward sawtooth jumps from a prefix/rank credit. The previous P01-B reports had already exposed the exact residence slack

\[
\mathcal S_V=D_1+2D_2+D_3
\tag{1.2}
\]

on one M-SERPT valley, with

\[
D_1=
\left(\frac{S(u)}{A_u}-\frac{S(b)}{A_b}\right)
\left(\frac{\tau_b}{A_b}-\frac{\tau_u}{A_u}\right),
\tag{1.3}
\]

\[
D_2=\frac{S(b)\tau_b(A_u-A_b)}{A_uA_b^2},
\tag{1.4}
\]

\[
D_3=\int_u^b
\left(\frac{S(x)}{A(x)}-\frac{S(b)}{A_b}\right)
\left(1-\frac{A(x)}{A_u}\right)dx.
\tag{1.5}
\]

The new result is that **`D1+D3` alone pays every contact refinement of that valley, in aggregate**.

# 2. First synthesis: atomic jump = smooth split charge + endpoint overshoot charge

Write

\[
S(x)=P(X>x),\qquad N(x)=E[(X-x)^+],\qquad
\alpha=1-\rho.
\]

The universal identity

\[
A(x)=\alpha+\lambda N(x)
\tag{2.1}
\]

will be used repeatedly.

On the old plateau let

\[
g(x)=LS(x)-N(x)\ge0\qquad (u<x<b),
\tag{2.2}
\]

and let the post-boundary record overshoot be

\[
O=N(b)-LS(b)\ge0.
\tag{2.3}
\]

Thus `O=0` is the smooth/contact-saturated case and `O>0` is the atomic record jump isolated by P01-A.

At the exact internal contact `c`, `N(c)=LS(c)`, and at `u`, `N(u)=LS(u)`. Hence

\[
A_u-A_c=\lambda Lp.
\tag{2.4}
\]

Also

\[
A_c-A_b=\lambda(Lq-O),
\qquad
A_c-\lambda Lq=A_b-\lambda O.
\tag{2.5}
\]

P01-A's zero-load identity is

\[
\frac12(v_c+2LM-v_b)
=\int_c^b g(x)dx+bO.
\tag{2.6}
\]

Substituting `(2.5)` and `(2.6)` into `(1.1)` gives the sharper exact decomposition

\[
\boxed{
J=
\frac{A_u-A_c}{LA_uA_c}
\left[
\int_c^b g(x)dx
+O\left(b+\frac{\tau_b}{A_b}\right)
\right].
}
\tag{2.7}
\]

Put

\[
Y(x)=\frac1{A(x)},\qquad T(x)=\frac{\tau(x)}{A(x)},
\qquad B(x)=x+T(x).
\]

Then `(2.7)` is simply

\[
\boxed{
J=\frac{Y_c-Y_u}{L}
\left[I_c+O B_b\right],
\qquad
I_c:=\int_c^b g(x)dx.
}
\tag{2.8}
\]

The first term is exactly the smooth rank-tie split charge from the previous P01-B report. P01-A's atomic theorem adds only the explicit endpoint surcharge `O B_b`.

# 3. A useful convexity identity hidden in the SOAP primitives

Define

\[
P(x):=A(x)x+\tau(x)=A(x)B(x).
\tag{3.1}
\]

Since

\[
A'(x)=-\lambda S(x),\qquad
\tau'(x)=\lambda xS(x),
\]

we have the exact cancellation

\[
\boxed{P'(x)=A(x)>0.}
\tag{3.2}
\]

Moreover

\[
Y'(x)=\frac{\lambda S(x)}{A(x)^2},
\qquad
T'(x)=\frac{\lambda S(x)}{A(x)^2}P(x),
\]

so

\[
\boxed{\frac{dT}{dY}=P(x),}
\tag{3.3}
\]

and `T` is a strictly convex function of the load coordinate `Y=1/A` wherever `S>0` because `P` strictly increases. The proof below only needs the exact primitive identity generated by `(3.2)`.

# 4. Decompose the source's `D3` credit by the SERPT deficit

From `(2.1)` and `g=LS-N`, for interior valley ages

\[
\frac{S(x)}{A(x)}
=\frac1{\lambda L}\left(1-\frac{\alpha}{A(x)}\right)
+\frac{g(x)}{LA(x)}.
\tag{4.1}
\]

At the terminal boundary, `N(b)=LS(b)+O`, hence

\[
\frac{S(b)}{A_b}
=\frac1{\lambda L}\left(1-\frac{\alpha}{A_b}\right)
-\frac{O}{LA_b}.
\tag{4.2}
\]

Subtracting gives the exact nonnegative decomposition

\[
\boxed{
\frac{S(x)}{A(x)}-\frac{S(b)}{A_b}
=
\frac{\alpha}{\lambda L}(Y_b-Y(x))
+\frac{g(x)}{LA(x)}
+\frac{O}{LA_b}.
}
\tag{4.3}
\]

Because

\[
1-\frac{A(x)}{A_u}=A(x)(Y(x)-Y_u),
\]

the `g`-part of `D3` is

\[
D_{3,g}=\frac1L\int_u^b g(x)(Y(x)-Y_u)dx.
\tag{4.4}
\]

For `x>=c`, `Y(x)-Y_u>=Y_c-Y_u`, so

\[
\boxed{
D_{3,g}
\ge
\frac{Y_c-Y_u}{L}\int_c^b g(x)dx.
}
\tag{4.5}
\]

Thus `D3` already pays the entire smooth part of the contact jump `(2.8)`.

# 5. The overshoot surcharge is paid exactly by the `O` pieces of `D1+D3`

At `u` we have `g(u)=0`. Evaluating `(4.3)` at `u` gives

\[
\frac{S(u)}{A_u}-\frac{S(b)}{A_b}
=
\frac{\alpha}{\lambda L}(Y_b-Y_u)
+\frac{O}{LA_b}.
\tag{5.1}
\]

Therefore the `O`-part of `D1` is

\[
D_{1,O}=\frac{O}{LA_b}(T_b-T_u).
\tag{5.2}
\]

The `O`-part of `D3` is

\[
D_{3,O}=\frac{O}{LA_b}
\int_u^b\left(1-\frac{A(x)}{A_u}\right)dx.
\tag{5.3}
\]

The bracket obtained by adding `(5.2)` and `(5.3)` has an exact primitive. Using `P'=A`,

\[
\begin{aligned}
&T_b-T_u+
\int_u^b\left(1-\frac{A(x)}{A_u}\right)dx\\
&=T_b-T_u+(b-u)-\frac{P_b-P_u}{A_u}\\
&=B_b-B_u-\frac{P_b-P_u}{A_u}\\
&=P_b(Y_b-Y_u).
\end{aligned}
\tag{5.4}
\]

Consequently

\[
\boxed{
D_{1,O}+D_{3,O}
=\frac{O B_b}{L}(Y_b-Y_u).
}
\tag{5.5}
\]

Since `u<c<b` and `Y` is nondecreasing,

\[
D_{1,O}+D_{3,O}
\ge
\frac{O B_b}{L}(Y_c-Y_u),
\tag{5.6}
\]

which is exactly the atomic overshoot part of `(2.8)`.

Combining `(4.5)` and `(5.6)` proves

\[
\boxed{J\le D_1+D_3.}
\tag{5.7}
\]

No `D2` credit is needed.

# 6. Main new theorem: all contacts in one valley are paid in aggregate

Let

\[
u=c_0<c_1<\cdots<c_k<b
\]

be any finite ordered set of exact contacts on the same old plateau level `L`. Refine the plateau successively at these contacts. Let `J_i` be the response jump from the `i`th refinement. The same derivation as `(2.8)` gives

\[
J_i=\frac{Y_i-Y_{i-1}}L
\left[I_i+OB_b\right],
\qquad
I_i=\int_{c_i}^b g(x)dx.
\tag{6.1}
\]

For the smooth pieces, Tonelli/summation gives

\[
\sum_{i=1}^k\frac{Y_i-Y_{i-1}}L I_i
=
\frac1L\int_u^b g(x)
\sum_{i:c_i\le x}(Y_i-Y_{i-1})dx.
\tag{6.2}
\]

The inner sum is the last contact load coordinate reached before `x`, minus `Y_u`, hence it is at most `Y(x)-Y_u`. Therefore

\[
\sum_{i=1}^k\frac{Y_i-Y_{i-1}}L I_i
\le D_{3,g}.
\tag{6.3}
\]

The overshoot pieces telescope exactly:

\[
\sum_{i=1}^k\frac{Y_i-Y_{i-1}}L OB_b
=
\frac{OB_b}{L}(Y_k-Y_u)
\le
\frac{OB_b}{L}(Y_b-Y_u)
=D_{1,O}+D_{3,O}.
\tag{6.4}
\]

Hence

\[
\boxed{
\sum_{i=1}^k J_i\le D_1+D_3.
}
\tag{ACP}
\]

By monotone convergence the same bound holds for a countable contact refinement whenever the source quantities are finite.

This is the aggregate record-chain inequality requested by the latest P01-A report: the individual downward sawtooth jumps need not be paid one by one from `F`; the whole contact chain is charged to one pre-existing rank-valley credit.
