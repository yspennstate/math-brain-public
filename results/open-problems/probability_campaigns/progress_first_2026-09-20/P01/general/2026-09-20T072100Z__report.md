## 2. Exact central-plateau correction hidden in source equation (5.10)

Fix a Gittins valley `V=(u,v]`. Following the source proof of Lemma 5.6, define the auxiliary decreasing function

\[
H(x):=1-\rho+\rho\frac{S(x)}{S(u)}.
\]

Thus

\[
H(u)=1,\qquad H(v)=g.
\]

The source chooses `x_* in [u,v]` such that

\[
H(x_*)=a=\frac{A(v)}{A(u)}.
\]

Let

\[
y_*=y_{MS}(x_*),\qquad z_*=z_{MS}(x_*),
\]

and abbreviate

\[
Y:=H(y_*),\qquad Z:=H(z_*).
\]

Because `u<=y_*<=x_*<=z_*<=v` and `H` is decreasing,

\[
\boxed{1\ge Y\ge a\ge Z\ge g.}
\tag{2.1}
\]

Before the source replaces the internal M-SERPT geometry by an AM-GM extremum, its equation (5.10) gives

\[
q_V\le\frac{1}{1-g}
\left(
\frac aY-a+1-\frac ZY+\frac Za-\frac ga
\right),
\tag{2.2}
\]

where `q_V` is the waiting ratio after the source has already replaced `tau(z_MS(x))` by `tau(v)`.

The bracket has the exact factorization

\[
\frac aY-a+1-\frac ZY+\frac Za-\frac ga
=2-a-\frac ga-
\left(1-\frac aY\right)\left(1-\frac Za\right).
\tag{2.3}
\]

Since

\[
\frac{2-a-g/a}{1-g}=1+\eta_V,
\]

define the **central M-SERPT plateau credit**

\[
\boxed{
\chi_V:=
\frac{\left(1-a/Y\right)\left(1-Z/a\right)}{1-g}\ge0.
}
\tag{2.4}
\]

Then the source proof actually yields the stronger inequality

\[
\boxed{q_V\le 1+\eta_V-\chi_V.}
\tag{2.5}
\]

Moreover `(2.1)` gives `chi_V<=eta_V`: each factor in the numerator of `chi_V` is bounded by the corresponding endpoint factor in

\[
(1-a)(1-g/a)=(1-g)\eta_V.
\]

Set

\[
\boxed{\varepsilon_V:=\eta_V-\chi_V\ge0.}
\tag{2.6}
\]

The key structural identity is

\[
\boxed{
(1-g)\varepsilon_V
=
\frac{(1-Y)(a-g)}{Y}
+
\frac{(Y-a)(Z-g)}{aY}.
}
\tag{2.7}
\]

Every term is nonnegative. Thus the residual waiting penalty is carried only by the **outer gaps** between the Gittins valley and the central M-SERPT plateau. The central plateau itself is not a cost; it is a credit that the published proof throws away.

In particular,

\[
\boxed{\varepsilon_V=0\quad\text{if }y_*=u\text{ and }z_*=v.}
\tag{2.8}
\]

This is exactly the geometry in the aligned one-record theorem proved by P01-A, explaining from the general source proof why no positive waiting-ratio penalty remains there.

## 4. Global sharpened waiting theorem

Every Gittins hill age is an M-SERPT hill age, so on Gittins hill sizes the two Proposition-4.7 work-set expressions have no positive M-SERPT excess. Sum `(3.2)` over Gittins valleys and use monotone convergence for a countable decomposition. Define

\varepsilon_V\underline Q_{G,V},
\qquad
\mathcal C_\tau:=\sum_{V\in\mathcal V_G}C_{\tau,V}.
\]

Then

\[
\boxed{

\le
