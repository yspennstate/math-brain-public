## 3. The sixth-order form is globally nonnegative

For a three-state centered vector, complex magnetization gives

\[
A=\frac23|m|^2,\qquad C_x=\frac29\operatorname{Re}(m^3),
\]

and therefore

\[
|C_x|\le\frac{A^{3/2}}{\sqrt6},\qquad
|C_y|\le\frac{B^{3/2}}{\sqrt6}.
\]

Dropping the positive `27(C_x^2+C_y^2)/80` term and taking the worst sign in the cross term gives

\[
H_6\ge
\frac{81}{320}(A^3+B^3)
+\frac3{32}AB(A+B)
-\frac9{16}A^{3/2}B^{3/2}.
\]

If `A,B>0`, put `t=sqrt(A/B)` and divide by `B^3/320`. The right side becomes

\[
P(t)=81(t^6+1)+30(t^4+t^2)-180t^3.
\]

After division by `t^3` and setting `s=t+t^{-1}>=2`,

\[
\frac{P(t)}{t^3}=81s^3-213s-180.
\]

At `s=2` this equals `42`, and its derivative `243s^2-213` is positive for every `s>=2`. Hence

\[
\boxed{H_6\ge0}
\]

for all `x,y`.

## 5. A useful below-KS corollary by channel factorization

For any `0<=lambda<=lambda_*`, let

\[
\mu=\lambda/\lambda_*=\sqrt2\,\lambda.
\]

The Potts channel semigroup gives `T_lambda=T_{lambda_*}T_mu`, hence exactly

\[
K_\lambda(p,q)=K_*(T_\mu p,T_\mu q).
\]

If

\[
\mu\max(|m|,|n|)\le1/10,
\]

then the theorem above and the global edge inequality `Psi(T_mu p)<=mu^2 Psi(p)` imply

\[
\boxed{
K_\lambda(p,q)
\le
\frac{\mu^2}{2}(\Psi(p)+\Psi(q))
-0.0161\mu^4(|m|^4+|n|^4).
}
\]

Thus every below-KS parameter has a rigorous full-dimensional small-message contraction region. This still does **not** prove global non-reconstruction, because an arbitrary posterior law need not enter or stay in this ball.

A distributional corollary is nevertheless useful: no nontrivial finite-support or general symmetric fixed posterior law whose support lies entirely inside this ball can solve the density-evolution fixed-point equation. Any discontinuous reconstruction fixed law below KS must carry positive mass at finite amplitude outside the proved ball.
