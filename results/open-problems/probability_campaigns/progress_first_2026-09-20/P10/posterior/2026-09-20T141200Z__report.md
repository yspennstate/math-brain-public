\[
r=|m|,\qquad s=|n|,\qquad \rho=\max(r,s),\qquad S_4=r^4+s^4.

## 3. New radial-ratio remainder bound

By symmetry exchange `p,q`, assume `r>=s` and write

\[
s=t r,\qquad 0\le t\le1.
\]

The previous radius-0.132 proof bounded each degree-7-and-higher tail after replacing `S_4` by `rho^4`. That loses almost a factor two in the hardest balanced case `t=1`. Here I normalize exactly by

\[
r^3S_4=r^7(1+t^4).
\]

For the endpoint entropy tail define

\[
e(v)=\frac{32/21}{1-2v}
+\frac{(64/21)2^{-7/2}}{1-\sqrt2\,v}.
\]

Since `(1/2)B_7(v)+B_7(v/sqrt2)=e(v)v^7`, its normalized two-child contribution at an outer radius `R` is

\[
C_E(R,t)=\frac{e(R)+t^7e(tR)}{1+t^4}.
\]

For the KL fusion interaction, after retaining the `c^2,c^3` terms,

\[
C_D(R,t)=\frac{R t^4}{12(1-R^2t)(1+t^4)}.
\]

For the polynomial fusion terms put

\[
z=R^2t,\qquad \ell=\frac{1+t}{\sqrt2},\qquad q=\frac t2.
\]

The denominator tails of `(1+c)^{-2}` are

\[
C_{21}=\frac{3-2z}{(1-z)^2},\qquad
C_{20}=\frac{2-z}{(1-z)^2},
\]

and the cubic contribution is bounded by

\[
C_{\rm cub}(R,t)=\frac1{3(1+t^4)}\left[
\ell^3t^2C_{21}
+3R\ell^2qt^2C_{21}
+3\ell q^2tC_{20}
+Rq^3tC_{20}

\]

Likewise for `(1+c)^{-3}` let

\[
C_{31}=\frac{6-8z+3z^2}{(1-z)^3},\qquad

Then

\[
C_{\rm quart}(R,t)=\frac{2}{9(1+t^4)}\left[
R\ell^4t^2C_{31}
+4\ell^3qtC_{30}
+6R\ell^2q^2tC_{30}
+\frac{4\ell q^3+Rq^4}{(1-z)^3}

Every term is obtained directly by expanding

\[
\frac13\frac{\Re(L+Q)^3}{(1+c)^2}
-\frac29\frac{|L+Q|^4}{(1+c)^3},
\]

where `|L|<=r(1+t)/sqrt2`, `|Q|<=r^2t/2`, `|c|<=r^2t`, and retaining all monomials through total degree six. Therefore

\[
|R_{\ge7}|\le
C_{\rm tail}(R,t)\,r^3S_4,
\quad
C_{\rm tail}=C_E+C_D+C_{\rm cub}+C_{\rm quart}.

All positive coefficients above are nondecreasing in the outer radius `R` on the range used here.
