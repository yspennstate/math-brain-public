**Triangle inflation.** This is correct.
- Filling a triangle that K meets in s ∈ {1,2} vertices changes the edge boundary by at most (3−s)(1−s) ≤ 0.
- The r missing vertices were already in ∂_V K and leave it, while at most r new vertices can enter.
- Filling one triangle never touches another triangle, so filling all touched triangles is well defined.
- For a full-triangle set K⁺, both boundaries are in bijection with ∂_E^H S. Each outside incidence (w,e) is adjacent to K⁺ only through its edge e.
