# Referee report on I0043 (Schramm, Problem 7.2): which compact sets SLE_κ hits

## Scope

The submission answers only the boundary case K ⊂ ℝ. It does not treat interior sets K ⊂ ℍ. It labels itself "partial", which is accurate.

On the boundary it does not give a criterion that is both necessary and sufficient. The necessary condition H^α(K) > 0 is not matched by any sufficient condition for fractal sets. The submission says so in its stated gap.

## Line-by-line check

**G (sandwich {T_a<T_b} ⊂ {hit [a,b]} ⊂ {T_a′<T_b′}).** The argument is sound.
- The borderline case T_b′ = τ is handled correctly. γ(τ) ∈ [a,b] lies left of b′, so the contact at or beyond b′ must occur at some time s < τ.
- It relies on the standard trace/hull facts: existence and continuity of the trace (Rohde–Schramm), and that the collision time equals swallowing. These are standard and are stated as given.

**D (exact swallowing probability).** I re-derived it.
- Z = X/D in the clock dv = dt/D² has drift 2/z + 2/(z+1) and diffusion coefficient √κ. ✓
- Scale density is [z(z+1)]^(−4/κ). It is integrable at 0 because β < 1, and at ∞ because 2β > 1, i.e. κ < 8. ✓
- The substitution u = y/(1+y) gives u^(−β)(1−u)^(2β−2), so α = 2β−1 = 8/κ−1, and z = a/(b−a) maps to u = a/b. ✓
- The clock arguments hold. Reaching 0 forces ∫dv/Z < ∞ by the nonnegative drift, so D stays positive, which is exactly T_a < T_b. The bound v ≥ t/(b−a)² excludes infinite physical time.
- The Green-function bound for finite exit time is correct; S(y)m(y) = O(y) near 0.

**I (consequences).**
- At κ = 6 the density is symmetric, so P = 1/2. This agrees with Cardy's formula at cross-ratio 1/2, an independent check.
- The constant 1/(αB(1−β,α)) = Γ(β)/(Γ(1−β)Γ(1+α)) uses 1−β+α = β. ✓
- Numerically C₆ ≈ 0.566, which matches the reported 0.0566 for [1, 1.001].
- The limits as κ → 4 and κ → 8 are correct.
- The point-avoidance argument is correct.

**S (κ ≤ 4).** For κ ≤ 4 the scale function h(x) = ∫x^(−4/κ) diverges at 0, including log at κ = 4. So no positive real point is ever swallowed or touched. With G and a countable dense set this gives γ ∩ ℝ = {0} a.s. This is correct and is the known Rohde–Schramm result.

**H (H^α-null sets are polar).**
- The covering intervals have length ≤ δ/2 and meet K, so their left endpoints are ≥ δ/2 and r ≥ 1/2.
- Then u^(−β) ≤ 2^β, and 1−r ≤ ℓ/a ≤ 2ℓ/δ. The constant 2^(β+α)/(αB) δ^(−α) is right.
- The union bound is valid, and the hypothesis 0 ∉ K is used.

**C (Cantor counterexample).**
- 2ⁿL_n^α = 1/(n+1) → 0, so H^α(K) = 0. ✓
- The child-to-parent ratio is at most 2^(−1/α) < 1/2 because α < 1. ✓
- Gaps at level n are ≥ (1−2r)L_{n−1}, so an interval of length ℓ < L_{n−1} meets a bounded number of level-n intervals.
- Since 2^(−n) ≤ A_s L_n^s with A_s finite, the mass distribution principle gives dim_H K ≥ s for every s < α.
- K is a deterministic compact set with dim_H K = α that is avoided a.s. So it is a genuine counterexample to "dim_H K ≥ 8/κ−1 is sufficient". It does not, and does not claim to, refute the problem.

**No errors found.** Constants, exponents and hypotheses check out.

## Literature

- The interval swallowing and hitting formula is classical. It appears in Rohde–Schramm (2005) and in Lawler's book (2005, the SLE_κ swallowing-order computation), and is consistent with Cardy/Dubédat for κ = 6.
- The κ ≤ 4 classification is Rohde–Schramm (2005).
- The boundary dimension 2−8/κ of γ ∩ ℝ is Alberts–Sheffield (2008).
- The H^α-null obstruction and the Cantor example are routine consequences of that literature.
- I know of no paper giving a full capacity-type iff criterion for boundary compact sets, or any criterion for interior sets. I hold that view with moderate confidence.
