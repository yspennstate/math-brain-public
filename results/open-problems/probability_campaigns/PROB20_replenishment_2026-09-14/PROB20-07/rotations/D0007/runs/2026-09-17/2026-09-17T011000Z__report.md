# B27 — defect-extraction lemma

## Lemma

Let `F` be symmetric about zero. Let `n>=m` be odd integers. Suppose `H` is a symmetric `m`-CM law with center zero and, as measures,

\[
\frac mn H\le F.
\]

Define

\[
G:=\frac{F-(m/n)H}{1-m/n}.
\]

Then `G` is a symmetric probability law and `F` is `n`-CM.

## Proof

The domination makes the numerator a nonnegative measure of total mass `1-m/n`; hence `G` is a probability law. Symmetry of `F` and `H` gives symmetry of `G`.

Take an `m`-complete mix `(H_1,...,H_m)` of `H` with sum zero. Since `n-m` is even, take `(n-m)/2` variables `Y_j~G` and use the pairs `(Y_j,-Y_j)`. Concatenating the `m`-block with all symmetric pairs gives `n` random variables whose total sum is zero. The first `m` coordinates have law `H` and the remaining `n-m` have law `G`.

Now apply an independent uniformly random permutation of the `n` coordinates. The total sum remains zero, while every coordinate has marginal

\[
\frac mn H+\frac{n-m}{n}G=F.
\]

Therefore `F` is `n`-CM. `QED`

This lemma is the main quantifier swap of B27.

# B24 — an extremal odd shell block

Set

\[
r=20000,\qquad m=2r+1=40001,
\]

and choose the exact rational parameters

\[
\delta=\frac1{40003},\qquad
 a=\frac{40000}{40003},\qquad
 b=\frac{40002}{40003}.
\]

They satisfy

\[
(r+1)a=rb,
\]

because

\[
20001\cdot40000=20000\cdot40002.
\]

Define the symmetric four-atom law `H_0` by

\[
\Pr(H_0=\pm a)=\frac{r+1}{2m},\qquad
\Pr(H_0=\pm b)=\frac r{2m}.
\]

### `H_0` is `m`-CM

Start from the deterministic zero-sum `m`-vector consisting of `r+1` copies of `+a` and `r` copies of `-b`. Its sum is zero by `(r+1)a=rb`. With probability `1/2`, replace the entire vector by its negative, and then uniformly permute its coordinates. Every coordinate then has precisely the four-atom law `H_0`, and the sum is always zero. Hence `H_0` is `m`-CM.

### A self-contained smooth `m`-CM noise

Let `U_delta` be uniform on `[-delta,delta]`. For completeness, uniform laws are `3`-CM by the following elementary construction. Let `T` be uniform on `[0,1/2]` and set

\[
A=T,\qquad B=T+\frac12,\qquad C=1-2T.
\]

Then `A+B+C=3/2`. After an independent uniform permutation, each coordinate is uniform on `[0,1]`: `A` supplies density `2` on the left half, `B` density `2` on the right half, and `C` density `1` on the whole interval, so their one-third mixture has density `1`. Centering and scaling gives a 3-complete mix of `U_delta` with sum zero. A symmetric pair is a 2-complete mix of `U_delta`. Since

\[
40001=3+2\cdot19999,
\]

concatenating one 3-block with 19999 symmetric pairs gives an `m`-complete mix of `U_delta`.

Take independent `m`-complete mixes of `H_0` and `U_delta` and add them coordinatewise. Their convolution

\[
H:=H_0*U_\delta
\]

is therefore symmetric and `m`-CM.

The two positive support intervals of `H` are

\[
I_a=[a-\delta,a+\delta]
 =\left[\frac{39999}{40003},\frac{40001}{40003}\right],
\]

\[
I_b=[b-\delta,b+\delta]
 =\left[\frac{40001}{40003},1\right].
\]

They touch at one endpoint and otherwise do not overlap. The negative intervals are their reflections.
