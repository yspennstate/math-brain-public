## Route B — differentiate the Laplace factor in `w`

**What succeeded.** All explicit `w`-dependence of Mo's displayed integrand was isolated into `exp(-wT/2)`, and `∂_w,∂_w^2` convert into `T,T^2` moments.

**Why it stopped.** Moving the `w^2` term onto the contour variable by integration by parts produces a nontrivial finite real-axis boundary/jump contribution. Also Mo's printed scalar `C` is not tracked in a way that licenses silently treating it as `w`-independent. A pointwise interior residual is only a sufficient strengthening; cancellation may be global across contour pieces.

## Route C — scalar contour residual / total-`T` derivative

**What succeeded.** The PDE was reduced, under a fixed normalization, to an adjoint contour residual and an explicit boundary term. A stronger sufficient interior identity was written for the determinant/Airy/Painlevé amplitude.

**Why it stopped.** The residual was not shown to vanish and the real-axis gluing was not closed. “It is a total derivative” is not enough because the Laplace weight changes the adjoint operator and because the contour has a finite meeting point with branch data.

## Route D — finite 4x4 variational `T`-flow
