\[

shows that the same Fisher metric can expand over two iterates at an explicit state/tangent direction. Therefore neither raw one-step nor raw two-step Fisher contraction can be the missing universal proof.

**Total variation / product coupling.** The later runs sharpened invariant-set localization, ultimately forcing every hypothetical non-singleton weak-endpoint omega-limit set into TV diameter `<1/2`. The scalar TV inequality is nevertheless locally expansive in the worst case near the diagonal at the `1/12` endpoint, so localization alone does not imply collapse.

**Fixed-point spectral analysis.** The project proved uniqueness of the fixed point at weak floor `1/12` and closed the complex tangent-spectrum obstruction; under the strict floor the unique fixed point is locally attracting. Local attraction in a two-dimensional nonlinear dynamical system does not exclude a remote period-`>=3` orbit or an aperiodic recurrent set.

**Period-two classification.** Nontrivial period two is completely excluded at the weak endpoint. This is not enough: continuous maps of a two-dimensional simplex can have higher-period or aperiodic recurrence without period two in the restricted operator family unless an additional structural theorem is proved.

**Invariant-measure information inequality.** The entropy/redundancy bound constrains every invariant measure but degenerates for measures concentrating near a point; it did not force a non-Dirac invariant measure to disappear.

**Endpoint Lyapunov / neutral triangle / robust tube.** Exact polynomial Lyapunov functions prove global regularity of the two real-neutral endpoint normal forms, their barycentric rays, the full aligned neutral triangle, and a positive-thickness tube away from the two neutral vertices. These are real advances in parameter-space exclusion, but they do not cover all admissible tensors. The robust tube radius collapses precisely near the neutral vertices, while a generic perturbation also shifts the fixed point and changes the nonlinear direction, so the exact radial/triangle identities do not extend verbatim.

## (c) What a successful proof must contain that no tried route yet contains
