## Post-write mathematical review

I rechecked the argument most vulnerable to a scale-direction error: Theorem 2.1 in the main report. If `i(j)` is the last record below `U_j` and the cap-scaled gap were bounded, then

\[
h_{i(j)}-h_j\le C/U_j\le C/R_{i(j)}.
\]

Iterating `j_{s+1}=i(j_s)` while the record scale remains above `X_j=h_j^{-1/2}` gives geometric scale decay and therefore

\[
\sum_s R_{j_{s+1}}^{-1}\le 2R_{j_L}^{-1}.
\]

This implies

\[
A_{j_L}=R_{j_L}h_{j_L}\le X_jh_j+2C=\sqrt{h_j}+2C,
\]

while `j_L->infinity`, contradicting `A_i->infinity`. The inequality directions are correct.

I also rechecked the exact support-sensitive factorization in the addendum. With

\[
d_j(k)=-\log(c_0q_k)-kh_j-\Gamma_j,
\]

one has identically

\[
Q(L_j,U_j)
=(c_0\nu)^{-1}e^{-\Gamma_j-h_jL_j}
\sum_{L_j<k\le U_j}e^{-d_j(k)}e^{-h_j(k-L_j)}.
\]

Thus the sign in

\[
\Gamma_j+h_jL_j
=\beta_j+\log Z_j-\log(c_0\nu)
\]

is correct.

Finally, the explicit sparse law in the addendum was checked against the structural hypotheses used for the diagnostic: it is a probability law, has finite positive mean, span one because `0,1` are in support, and generating radius one because along `R_j=2^{2^j}` its positive coefficients have `R_j`-th roots tending to one. It is not claimed to solve or refute Problem 18.39; it only disproves the proposed purely record-theoretic strengthening `Gamma_j >> log(1/h_j)`.
