- On the good block-type event, the first fixed number of successful Bernoulli block values are independent of the indicator sigma-field and of the unselected remainder; hence the decomposition `S_n=aN+Bin(N,1/2)+R_n` is legitimate after conditioning on the good event.
- Conditioning on the good event changes the canonical mean by at most `O(n U_n exp(-c n))`, and the conditional variance is at most a fixed multiple of `n sigma_n^2`.
- The binomial displacement is `O(sqrt(n(1+sigma_n^2)))`; since `sigma_n^2<=C U_n=o(n)`, it lies in the central linear binomial window needed for Stirling's lower bound.
- Averaging the binomial lower bound over the Chebyshev-good residual event is why the exact-mean lower bound loses only the universal `n^{-1/2}` factor, not an extra residual pigeonhole factor.
