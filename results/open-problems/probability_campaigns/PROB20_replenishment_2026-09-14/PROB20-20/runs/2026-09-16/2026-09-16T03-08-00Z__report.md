## Correction

In Section 2 of the `02-55-00Z` report, the conditions

\[
K_n\to\infty,
\qquad
K_n=o(n),
\qquad
\epsilon_n/J_{K_n}\to0
\]

are not, by themselves, sufficient to conclude from

\[
\Pr(\text{bad residual tail event}\mid S_N=M)
\le
\exp\{-N(J_{K_n}-\epsilon_n)\}
\]

that the right-hand side tends to zero. One must also ensure

\[
\boxed{nJ_{K_n}\to\infty.}
\tag{C.1}
\]

This is available by the same block-diagonal construction because, for every fixed sufficiently large integer `j`, the bounded-variable Cramer rate `J_j` is a fixed strictly positive number.

Choose increasing block thresholds `N_j` so large that for every `n>=N_j`, simultaneously

\[
\epsilon_n\le\frac{J_j}{j},
\qquad
nJ_j\ge j,
\qquad
n\ge j^2.
\tag{C.2}
\]

Set

\[
K_n=j
\qquad(N_j\le n<N_{j+1}).
\tag{C.3}
\]

Then

\[
K_n\to\infty,
\qquad
K_n=o(n),
\qquad
\frac{\epsilon_n}{J_{K_n}}\le\frac1{K_n}\to0,
\qquad
nJ_{K_n}\ge K_n\to\infty.
\tag{C.4}
\]

For every residual state in the compact family of the theorem, `N>=n/2`, so eventually

\[
N(J_{K_n}-\epsilon_n)
\ge
\frac n2J_{K_n}\left(1-\frac1{K_n}\right)
\to\infty.
\tag{C.5}
\]

Thus the uniform bad-event bound indeed tends to zero, and the uniform lower bound

\[
\alpha_{N,M}^{(K_n)}\ge a_0+o(1)
\]

is retained.
