## Post-write self-review

I rechecked the two points most vulnerable to the previously identified change-of-measure mistake.

First, the process in Sections 2–7 of the main report is the **ordinary** sequential size-biased permutation. Conditional on an initial microcanonical allocation, the next box is chosen proportional to its occupancy; after marginalizing the allocation, the residual state transition is exactly `K_{N,M}`. No step conditions the selected box to exceed `L_n`, so the initial-allocation marginal is not tilted.

Second, the cloud-hit lower bound is used only after conditioning on the initial allocation being in the amplified-cloud event. It enters through a pathwise conditional probability bound and is then averaged. The only loss from the complement is at most `(cn+1) P(G_n^c)`, and the diagonal amplified-cloud exponent plus `b_n >= (1/2) log n-O(1)` gives `n P(G_n^c)->0`. Thus no uncontrolled factor of `H_n^{amp}` is multiplied by a merely `o(1)` exceptional probability: once `(cn+1)P(G_n^c)<1/2` and `E tau_c>=1`, the expected number of cloud hits is at least a fixed fraction of `E tau_c` before the KL factor is inserted.

A minor support edge case is also harmless: if `Q(L_n,m_n)=0`, then no admissible exact-sum allocation can contain a box in `(L_n,m_n]`. Since the amplified-cloud event has probability tending to one and requires positive mass above `L_n`, this case is excluded for all sufficiently large admissible `n`.

No correction to the main mathematical statements was found in this post-write review.
