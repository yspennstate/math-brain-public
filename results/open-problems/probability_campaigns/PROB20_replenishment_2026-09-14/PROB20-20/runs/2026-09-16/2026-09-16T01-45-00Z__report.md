## Correction

Section 6 of the `01-31-10Z` report uses

\[
\frac{m_n-x_n}{n}\ge \nu+e^{-s_n}
\]

for

\[
x_n=(\delta-2e^{-s_n})n,
\qquad 1\ll s_n\ll R_n.
\]

Plain convergence `m_n/n -> lambda` by itself is not enough to compare its unspecified error with the moving quantity `e^{-s_n}`. The block-diagonal construction in Section 3 must explicitly absorb the admissible-centering convergence rate.

For the block `R_n=j`, in addition to all conditions already imposed there, enlarge `N_j` so that for every `n>=N_j`,

\[
\boxed{
\left|\frac{m_n}{n}-\lambda\right|\le e^{-8j}.
}
\tag{C.1}
\]

This is legitimate because `m_n/n -> lambda` and `j` is fixed while `N_j` is chosen. It is compatible with every other diagonal threshold because only finitely many fixed-`j` eventual conditions are being combined.

Thus on the actual block `N_j<=n<N_{j+1}` with `R_n=j`,

\[
\left|\frac{m_n}{n}-\lambda\right|\le e^{-8R_n}.
\tag{C.2}
\]

Since `s_n=o(R_n)`,

\[
e^{-8R_n}=o(e^{-s_n}).
\]

Therefore

\[
\begin{aligned}
\frac{m_n-x_n}{n}
&=
\frac{m_n}{n}-(\lambda-\nu-2e^{-s_n})\\
&\ge
\nu+2e^{-s_n}-e^{-8R_n}\\
&\ge
\nu+e^{-s_n}
\end{aligned}
\]

for all large `n`, exactly as required for the nonnegative density-premium step in the stopped Palm KL telescope.
