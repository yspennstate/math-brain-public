## 3. B14 — find the group acting: remove a hidden “which mean?” ambiguity

The exact source model is defined on the complete graph with iid continuous edge weights and the same bilateral `k`-preference rule at every vertex. The full symmetric group on vertex labels preserves the joint law. Therefore

\[
D_1,\ldots,D_n
\]

are identically distributed, even though they are highly dependent. In particular, there is a single deterministic number

\[
\mu_n=\mathbb E[D_v]

independent of the choice of `v`, and it also equals the expectation of the degree of a uniformly sampled vertex.

This matters for source fit: the proof is not centering each vertex at a different conditional/local expectation and is not replacing the source's mean degree by a random empirical mean. The theorem is centered at the common source-model mean.

The symmetry observation also confirms that the expectation bridge from one fixed vertex is valid for every vertex simultaneously once the same global event `A_n` has been established. No additional exchangeability or independence step is missing.
