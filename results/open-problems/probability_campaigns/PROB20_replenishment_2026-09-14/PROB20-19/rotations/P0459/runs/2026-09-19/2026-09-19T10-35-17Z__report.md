## 1. Pinned theorem; no proof recycling

The current candidate remains the theorem proved in the 08:33 report. Let `D_v` be the degree of vertex `v` in `LK(n,k(n))` and let `mu_n=E[D_v]`. If

\[
1\le k(n)\le n-1,\qquad \frac{k(n)}{\log n}\to\infty,
\]

then

\[
\boxed{\frac{\max_{1\le v\le n}|D_v-\mu_n|}{\mu_n}\xrightarrow{\mathbb P}0.}
\]

With `delta_n=sqrt(6 k log n)`, the historical threshold/Chernoff comparison supplies, for all sufficiently large `n` (in particular once `k>2 delta_n`, equivalently eventually under `k/log n -> infinity`), a global event `A_n` with

\[
P(A_n)\ge 1-2/n^2
\]

on which every source-model degree lies in `[k-2 delta_n,k]`. The new bridge already proved is

\[
\mu_n\ge (k-2\delta_n)(1-2/n^2),
\]

hence on `A_n`

\[
\frac{\max_v|D_v-\mu_n|}{\mu_n}
\le
\frac{2\delta_n+2k/n^2}{(k-2\delta_n)(1-2/n^2)}\to0.
\]

This run does not repackage the threshold proof and creates no checker.

## 2. B2 — attack the negation first: search for a hidden defect or counter-regime

I attacked the four interfaces identified by the gate prompt rather than adding a new lemma.

### 2.1 Event range / positivity

A possible hidden defect would be using the threshold event before `k-2 delta_n` is positive. Here

\[
k>2\sqrt{6k\log n}
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
k>24\log n.
\]

The theorem assumes `k/log n -> infinity`, so this holds for all sufficiently large `n`. The asymptotic theorem therefore does not rely on an invalid finite-`n` range. The phrase “probability at least `1-2/n^2`” is to be read for sufficiently large `n` in the proved asymptotic regime, not uniformly over arbitrary `k`.

### 2.2 Threshold-subgraph implication

On the global threshold event every threshold degree is at most `k`. Thus an edge under threshold is among the `k` lightest incident edges at each endpoint and is present in `LK(n,k)`. Therefore the threshold graph is a subgraph of `LK(n,k)` on that event. Continuity of the iid edge weights removes ties almost surely. No independence across vertex threshold degrees is required.

### 2.3 Expectation bridge

From `D_v>=k-2 delta_n` on `A_n` and `D_v>=0` always,

\[
E[D_v]\ge (k-2\delta_n)P(A_n).
\]

This is a pointwise lower bound integrated over the event; it does not assume independence between `D_v` and `A_n`. Together with the deterministic `D_v<=k`, it gives the required deterministic mean deficit.

### 2.4 Counterexample search at logarithmic scale

The earlier negative lane remains valid for the **task-frozen simultaneous relative formulation**: at `k=floor(t log n)`, `0<t<1`, the source's isolated-vertex result makes `max_v |D_v-mu_n|/mu_n >=1` with high probability once `mu_n>0`.
