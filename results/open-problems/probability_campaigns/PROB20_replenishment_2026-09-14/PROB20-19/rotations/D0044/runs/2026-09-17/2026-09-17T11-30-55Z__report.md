## 6. B18 — say the remaining problem in the language of stability of an inverse map

Instead of asking for a closed-form Hamiltonian as a nonlinear function of all decimated points, treat the source demand as a continuity/stability problem for a sequence of finite inverse maps.

For each `N`, define schematically

\[
\mathcal D_{k,N}(T^-_{kN},W_N^+)
:=
\operatorname{Rec}_N\!\left(
\frac1kD_k(\operatorname{spec}T^-_{kN}),W_N^+
\right).
\tag{6.1}
\]

A sufficient source-valid bulk construction would be a coupling for which

\[
T^-_{kN}\to T^-\sim\mathrm{Sine}_{2/k}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\mathcal D_{k,N}(T^-_{kN},W_N^+)\to
\mathcal D_k(T^-,G)\sim\mathrm{Sine}_{2k},
\tag{6.2}

and the limiting spectrum is the anchored compressed every-kth spectrum of `T^-` pathwise.

This formulation isolates the needed estimates: local spectral-data convergence is not enough unless `Rec_N` is stable in a topology controlling the normalized canonical-system coefficients, Weyl functions, or resolvents. **B18 outcome:** the question has been moved from an abstract inverse-existence problem to a concrete stability-of-finite-reconstruction problem. This is a diagnosis/reformulation, not a new theorem.

## 7. B19 — make the next proof attempt an algorithm
