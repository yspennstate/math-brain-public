## 2. Dated source and literature check — 2026-09-17

### 2.1 Exact source wording and source intent

Virág's Question 6 is exactly:

> In Forrester [21] it was shown that deleting all but every kth eigenvalue of many finite beta=2/k ensembles gives the corresponding beta=2k ensemble. Can the limiting operators (bulk or edge) be coupled explicitly in this way?

The surrounding review matters for interpreting “operator.” Early in the article Virág describes the objective method as taking a limit of the **entire object**, rather than only a statistic, so that matrix structure is preserved. The same review also emphasizes that a Jacobi/operator representative encodes the spectral measure, and in the finite Jacobi setting the eigenvalues plus norming weights form bijective spectral data. Therefore inverse-spectral reconstruction is not alien to the source's operator viewpoint. At the same time, the wording “coupled explicitly” leaves open whether a globally reconstructed operator counts, or whether the intended standard is a direct coupling of coefficient/driving paths.

### 2.2 Original finite decimation input

Peter Forrester, arXiv:0711.1914 / CMP 285 (2009), Theorem 2, gives for `k=r+1` the circular relation

\[
D_k\!\left(CE^0_{2/k,kN}\right)=CE^0_{2k,N},
\]

with the angles ordered relative to the deterministic cut at zero. This is the exact finite identity used in the bulk construction.

### 2.3 Sine operator and spectral weights

Benedek Valkó and Bálint Virág, arXiv:1710.06988 / Ann. Probab. 48 (2020), construct the finite circular and `Sine_beta` operators and give a direct coupling with Hilbert--Schmidt resolvent convergence. The standard `Sine_beta` canonical path starts from the hyperbolic point `i` at the regular left endpoint, so its coefficient matrix has the canonical left normalization `R(0)=I/2` and `det R=1/4`.

Valkó--Virág, arXiv:2207.10626 / SPA 163 (2023), Proposition 3, prove that the right spectral weights of `tau_beta` are iid Gamma with shape `beta/2`, mean 2, and independent of the full eigenvalue sequence. Their Corollary 40 identifies these in law with the left spectral measure of standard `Sine_beta`. This is the mark-independence input used by the deterministic bulk lift.

### 2.4 Inverse uniqueness

Eckhardt--Kostenko--Teschl, arXiv:1305.3100v2 (2014), Theorem 4.1 and Corollary 4.6, show that equal scalar spectral measures for zero-potential weighted Dirac systems determine the system up to a reparameterization and constant `SL(2,R)` gauge, under a bounded-type condition. Their Section 4 and Corollary 5.2 show that the bounded-type condition is automatic when the left endpoint is limit-circle/regular because the corresponding entire functions are Cartwright. In the Sine normalization, equal `det R=1/4` forces the reparameterization slope to be one; the common left normalization and boundary line remove the remaining constant gauge up to the operator-trivial sign. Thus the previously flagged endpoint/bounded-type issue is not the remaining blocker.

### 2.5 Later operator-level coupling literature

The closest later operator-coupling literature found in this run is:

- Vincent Painchaud and Elliot Paquette, arXiv:2502.10305 (2025), *Operator level soft edge to bulk transition in beta-ensembles via canonical systems*. They prove coefficient-matrix convergence and couple the Brownian paths driving the operators, then deduce Weyl/spectral convergence.
- Vincent Painchaud, arXiv:2510.06120 (2025), *Operator level hard edge to bulk transition in beta-ensembles via canonical systems*. Again the proof couples the driving Brownian motions and first proves canonical-system coefficient convergence.
- Joseph Najnudel and Bálint Virág, arXiv:1904.00848 / 2021, *The bead process for beta ensembles*, uses spectral measures/Gamma weights and rank-one perturbations to build an explicit Markov transition at fixed beta. It is conceptually close but is not the `2/k -> 2k` decimation relation.

A fresh bounded search for `Sine beta decimation operator`, `every kth eigenvalue Sine beta operator`, the exact Question-6 wording, and later citations recovered the finite Forrester relation and the modern Sine/canonical-system literature but **no later paper explicitly claiming a solution of Virág Question 6**. This is bounded negative evidence only. **Publication novelty remains UNVERIFIED.**

## 6. B27 — swap the quantifiers: two precise meanings of “explicit coupling”

The ambiguity can be made precise by separating two statements.

### Weak/operator-spectral explicitness

There exists a specified deterministic measurable map `F_k` on normalized source operators such that

\[
T_-\sim\mathtt{Sine}_{2/k}
\implies F_k(T_-)\sim\mathtt{Sine}_{2k},

and the target spectrum is the scaled every-`k`th source spectrum pathwise.

This is exactly what (3.1) supplies.

There exists a direct coupling or transform of the driving canonical-system coefficient paths (or Brownian inputs) that produces the target operator while preserving the same spectral decimation relation, without reconstructing the entire operator globally from its complete spectral measure.

No proof of this stronger statement is currently on file. The naive phase/time-change subclass is impossible. The finite exact marked lift does not automatically converge to such a local continuum map.

## 7. B29 — add the endpoint and remove a false blocker

One possible way the inverse-spectral construction could have failed would be a hidden singular-endpoint normalization problem. Re-reading the primary Sine operator definition rules this out.

The standard `Sine_beta` path is

\[
\widetilde{\mathcal B}(t)=\mathcal B\!\left(-\frac4\beta\log(1-t)\right),\qquad t\in[0,1),
\]

with hyperbolic Brownian motion started at `i`. Hence the left endpoint is regular and starts at `i`, giving the canonical matrix `R(0)=I/2`; the singular behavior is at `t\uparrow1`. The `tau_beta` representation reverses the interval, which is why its regular endpoint appears at the opposite side. Valkó--Virág Corollary 40 swaps the corresponding left/right spectral measures in law.

Thus the earlier use of the **left** standard-Sine spectral measure together with the regular-left EKT inverse theory is the correct orientation. The Cartwright/bounded-type condition is available at this regular/limit-circle endpoint. No new hidden endpoint parameter remains to be supplied.

**B29 outcome:** endpoint compactification/orientation does not create a new mathematical gap. This further isolates (3.4) as the actual blocker.
