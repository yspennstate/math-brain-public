# 6. (a) Exact blocking statement

The first unresolved gluing problem can be written without any vague reference to a general orthogonal matrix.

Let

\[
\Lambda=\operatorname{diag}(\lambda_1,\lambda_2,\lambda_3,\lambda_4),\qquad \lambda_i>0,
\]

and define

\[
A=\cos\alpha\,Y_1+\sin\alpha\,Y_2,
\qquad
B=-\sin\alpha\,Y_1+\cos\alpha\,Y_2,
\]

followed by

\[
C_\beta=\cos\beta\,B+\sin\beta\,Y_3,
\qquad
D_\beta=-\sin\beta\,B+\cos\beta\,Y_3.
\]

The exact unresolved inequality is

\[
\boxed{
\Delta(\alpha,\beta;\Lambda)
:=\mathbb E\,\operatorname{Top}_2(A^2,C_\beta^2,D_\beta^2,Y_4^2)
-\mathbb E\,\operatorname{Top}_2(Y_1^2,Y_2^2,Y_3^2,Y_4^2)
\le0
}
\tag{6.1}
\]

for the parameter region not already covered by the task-local safe theorems.

Conditioning on `A`, the exact residual variance of `B` is

\[
v_\alpha
=\operatorname{Var}(B\mid A)
=\frac{\lambda_1\lambda_2}
       {\lambda_1\cos^2\alpha+\lambda_2\sin^2\alpha}.
\tag{6.2}
\]

The shifted-pair rearrangement theorem from the previous run proves the second rotation safe when `v_alpha >= lambda_3`. The first genuinely unresolved two-Givens region is therefore

\[
\boxed{\lambda_3>v_\alpha.}
\tag{6.3}
\]

In that regime the conditional pair has the exact form

\[
B\mid A=a\sim N(\mu(a),v_\alpha),
\qquad
Y_3\sim N(0,\lambda_3),
\qquad
v_\alpha<\lambda_3,
\tag{6.4}
\]

and the folded angular density used by the prior rearrangement proof is no longer monotone in the required direction. The problem is not to repeat a derivative calculation; it is to determine whether the loss from the first rotation is always large enough to dominate any recovery available to the second rotation after averaging over `A` and the selection threshold contributed by `Y_4`.
