### Proof

=\frac1{F'(r)}-\frac{F''(r)}{2F'(r)^2}z+O(z^2).

\[

\qquad

\tag{4.3}

\tag{4.4}

\tag{4.5}

Using (4.3) in (4.4),
\[
-F'(r)^2R(r)
=F'(r)I'(r)+\frac{F''(r)}{2F'(r)}
=c_{m,r}.

Since `kappa_{m,r}=1/F'(r)`, this is exactly (4.2). QED.
