# 4. B1 — change category: exact CRT cofactor selector and its order barrier

The prompt called the product ring “semisimple.” A precision correction is useful: because a clean component may be `p^f` with `f>1`,

\qquad

\tag{4.2}

## Theorem 4.1 — one global unit represents all local quotient units

In `A`:

1. for `Z\ne Y`,
   \[
   c_Zc_Y\equiv0\pmod W;
   \tag{4.3}
   \]
2. for every `Z`,
   \[
   C\equiv c_Z\pmod Z;
   \tag{4.4}
   \]
3. `C` is a unit modulo `W`;
4. the single global unit
   \[
   \mathcal T:=UC\in A^\times
   \tag{4.5}
   \]
   has `Z`-coordinate exactly
   \[
   \mathcal T\bmod Z=T_Z=t/Z.
   \tag{4.6}
   \]

### Proof
For distinct `Z,Y`, pairwise coprimality gives
\[
c_Zc_Y=\frac{W^2}{ZY}=W\frac{W}{ZY},
\]
which proves (4.3). Modulo a fixed `Z`, every `c_Y` with `Y\ne Z` contains the factor `Z`, while `c_Z=W/Z` is coprime to `Z`. Hence (4.4) holds and every coordinate of `C` is a unit; CRT then gives `C\in A^\times`. Equation (3.3) gives `U\in A^\times`, and (3.4) proves (4.6). ∎

This is the exact global form of the previously unused coupling. It pays the small cofactor `U` only once.

\sum_Z T_Z=U\sum_Z c_Z,
\qquad
\prod_ZT_Z=U^{s}W^{s-1},
\tag{4.7}
\]
where `s` is the number of clean components. Thus the coordinate product contains exactly the obvious `W^{s-1}` and no unexpected extra rough divisibility.

### Proof

## Lemma 7.1 — inverse series through cubic order

In the `p`-adic localization of a clean component `Z=p^f`, if

\tag{7.2}

### Proof
Taylor expansion gives
\[
F_k(r+ZQ)=aZQ+bZ^2Q^2+cZ^3Q^3+O(Z^4).
\]
After division by `Z`, substitute
\[
Q=\kappa T+ZA T^2+Z^2BT^3+O(Z^3).

The constant coefficient gives `a\kappa=1`; the coefficient of `ZT^2` gives `aA+b\kappa^2=0`, hence `A=\alpha`; and the coefficient of `Z^2T^3` gives

aB+2b\kappa\alpha+c\kappa^3=0,

so `B=2b^2\kappa^5-c\kappa^4=\beta`. ∎

There is also a closed harmonic form. Set

\beta=\frac{3s_1^2+s_2}{2}\,\kappa^3.

\tag{7.5}

\tag{7.8}

\[
G_I(\mathbf r):=\Lambda^3\Gamma_I(\mathbf r)\in\mathbb Z,
\tag{7.9}
\]
and hence the completely integral congruence is

\boxed{
\Lambda q_I(Z)\equiv T_Z^3G_I(\mathbf r(Z))\pmod Z.

\tag{7.10}

### Proof
From (7.2), after dividing by `\kappa`,
