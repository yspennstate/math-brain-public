## 3. B4 — prove something weaker completely: exact reflection-kite normal form for Q0

The first goal was not another generic lemma. It was to make the single body `Q0` as explicit as possible.

Define the invertible linear map

`(x,y) = M(u,v)`,  with  `M = [[1,3],[-1,1]]`,

so

`(u,v) = M^{-1}(x,y) = ((x-3y)/4,(x+y)/4)`.

Its determinant is `4`. The four vertices of `Q0` map to

`(2,-3), (5,0), (2,3), (-7,0)`.

Thus `Q0` is linearly equivalent to the reflection-symmetric kite

`K = conv{(-7,0),(2,-3),(5,0),(2,3)}`.

Because complete mixability is preserved by invertible linear maps, `Unif(Q0)` is vector `3`-CM if and only if `Unif(K)` is vector `3`-CM.

Write

`h(u) = (u+7)/3` for `-7 <= u <= 2`,

`h(u) = 5-u` for `2 <= u <= 5`.

Then

`K = { (u,v): -7 <= u <= 5, |v| <= h(u) }`.

The area of `K` is `36`, and therefore for `(U,V) ~ Unif(K)`,

`f_U(u) = h(u)/18`,

while

`V | U=u ~ Unif[-h(u),h(u)]`.

The marginal `f_U` is exactly the triangular density on `[-7,5]` with mode `2` and mean `0`:

`f_U(u)=(u+7)/54` on `[-7,2]`, and `f_U(u)=(5-u)/18` on `[2,5]`.

This gives a sharp one-dimensional lifting formulation. If one can construct a scalar complete mix `(U_1,U_2,U_3)` of this triangular marginal such that

`U_1+U_2+U_3=0`

and, almost surely,

`2 max_i h(U_i) <= h(U_1)+h(U_2)+h(U_3)`,

then `Q0` is vector `3`-CM. Indeed, conditional on `(U_1,U_2,U_3)`, the three centered uniform intervals `[-h(U_i),h(U_i)]` satisfy the length inequality, so Wang–Wang Theorem 3.1 supplies a zero-sum joint mix of the three conditional vertical marginals. A standard measurable-selection argument for compact coupling sets gives a measurable conditional kernel. Combining the horizontal and vertical coordinates yields a vector zero-sum coupling with all three marginals `Unif(K)`, and applying `M` returns a coupling of `Unif(Q0)`.

This is a **sufficient reduction**, not an equivalence: a genuine vector complete mix is allowed to correlate the conditional horizontal/vertical fibers nonlocally, which becomes important below.

Outcome of B4: `Q0` is reduced to an explicit triangular scalar complete-mix problem with one additional polygon-width constraint. This is a proper special-case reduction and does not move `C_proj`.

## 4. B12 — find what is conserved: hidden reflection and barycentric coordinates

The kite reflection `v -> -v` transports back to the non-obvious linear involution

`T = [[-1/2,-3/2],[-1/2,1/2]]`

on the original `Q0`. Exact arithmetic gives `T^2=I`, and `T` permutes the four vertices of `Q0`. In the original coordinates the fixed line is `x+y=0`; it splits `Q0` into two equal-area triangles.

That symmetry gives a convenient exact sampling representation for `Unif(K)`. Let

`(A,B,C) ~ Dirichlet(1,1,1)`

and let `eps` be an independent fair sign. Then

`U = -7 A + 5 B + 2 C`,

`V = 3 eps C`

is uniform on `K`: `eps=+1` gives the upper triangle and `eps=-1` the reflected lower triangle, each of area `18`.

Several useful identities follow immediately.

First, `C` has density

`f_C(c)=2(1-c)`, `0<=c<=1`.

Second, conditional on `C=c`, the Dirichlet law makes the remaining barycentric coordinate uniform on the base segment, so

`U | C=c ~ Unif[-7+9c, 5-3c]`

with conditional mean

`E[U|C=c] = -1+3c`.

Third, the scalar triangular marginal has a very explicit complete mix. By cyclically permuting the exchangeable Dirichlet coordinates, define

`U_1=-7A+5B+2C`,

`U_2=-7B+5C+2A`,

`U_3=-7C+5A+2B`.

Each `U_i` has the target triangular marginal and

`U_1+U_2+U_3=0`

identically.

So the scalar part is not the obstruction. The obstruction is whether one can synchronize that scalar mix with the conditional vertical intervals while preserving the full two-dimensional marginals.

Outcome of B12: `Q0` has more structure than its original asymmetric coordinates suggest. The source-faithful obstruction is now a synchronization problem inside one explicit Dirichlet/reflection model, not an arbitrary planar transport problem.

## 5. B19 — make it an algorithm, then locate the exact failure

### 5.1 The canonical scalar algorithm fails the width test exactly

The cyclic Dirichlet construction above is the first natural algorithm: produce a zero-sum scalar triple, then conditionally mix the vertical intervals.

It fails on a positive-measure region. An exact rational witness is

`A=4/5`, `B=C=1/10`.

Then the cyclic scalar mix is

`(U_1,U_2,U_3)=(-49/10, 7/5, 7/2)`

and the corresponding half-widths are

`(h(U_1),h(U_2),h(U_3))=(7/10,14/5,3/2)`.

But

`14/5 > 7/10 + 3/2 = 11/5`,

so the three conditional vertical uniforms cannot have a zero-sum joint mix at that fiber. This is not a numerical observation: it is exact rational arithmetic, archived by the checker below.

### 5.2 A stronger no-go theorem for every slice-local construction

The failure is not specific to the cyclic scalar coupling. Consider any attempted vector complete mix of three copies of `Unif(K)` and write each point in the exact barycentric form

`(U_i,V_i)`, with `V_i=3 eps_i C_i`.

Call the construction **fiber-local** if, after conditioning on the full latent fiber data

`(C_1,eps_1,C_2,eps_2,C_3,eps_3)`,

each `U_i` still has its correct one-point conditional law

`Unif[-7+9C_i, 5-3C_i]`.

No such fiber-local vector complete mix exists.

**Proof.** If `U_1+U_2+U_3=0` almost surely, then conditioning on the full latent fiber data and using the fiber-local assumption gives

`0 = sum_i E[U_i | all fiber data] = sum_i (-1+3C_i)`.

Hence

`C_1+C_2+C_3=1` almost surely.

The vertical zero-sum condition gives

`eps_1 C_1 + eps_2 C_2 + eps_3 C_3 = 0`.

The `C_i` are nonnegative and sum to `1`, so the total `C`-mass with sign `+` and sign `-` must each equal `1/2`. With three signed coordinates, one of the two sign classes contains a single coordinate; therefore at least one `C_i` must equal `1/2` almost surely. But every marginal `C_i` has the continuous `Beta(1,2)` density `2(1-c)`, so

`P(C_i=1/2)=0`

for each `i`, and therefore the union over `i=1,2,3` also has probability zero. Contradiction. QED.

This isolates the genuine mechanism needed to solve `Q0`: any successful complete mix must be **nonlocal across fibers**. Conditional on the other points' fibers it must distort an individual horizontal slice law and restore the correct uniform slice law only after averaging over the other fibers. That is exactly the kind of gluing obstruction the retired D0005 track saw from the quadrilateral hinge calculation, now proved in a particularly clean form for this fixed `Q0`.
