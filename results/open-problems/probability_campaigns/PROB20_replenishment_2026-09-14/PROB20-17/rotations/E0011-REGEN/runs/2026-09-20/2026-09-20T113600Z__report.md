## 2. One straight phase-boundary cell

Work on a straight portion of a canonical phase boundary. For two adjacent columns `i=0,1`, write

- `u_i=(i,1)` for the interior site,
- `v_i=(i,0)` for the boundary site,
- `w_i=(i,-1)` for the exterior site.

The clean local phase state is

`u_0=u_1=O`,  `v_0=v_1=0`,  `w_0=w_1=CO`,

with the tangential boundary neighbors of `v_0,v_1` vacant, the next interior neighbors O, and the next exterior neighbors CO. These are exactly the local conditions present on a straight side of the canonical annular seed used in the preceding reports.

Define three legal source operations whenever their stated preconditions hold.

- `Z`: one O2 adsorption on the boundary edge `{v_0,v_1}`.
- `C_i`: one CO adsorption at `v_i` while `u_i=O`.
- `R_i`: one O2 adsorption on the radial edge `{u_i,v_i}` after `C_i` has occurred and before the boundary vacancy `v_i` has been filled.

All reactions in the words below are forced; no favorable reaction tie is hidden.

## 3. Column service identity

### Lemma 3.1 — a first CO defect has a one-mark productive service

Start from one clean column

`u_i=O, v_i=0, w_i=CO`.

After `C_i`, the arriving CO at `v_i` has exactly one adjacent O, namely `u_i`; it reacts immediately with that O. Hence

`u_i=0, v_i=0, w_i=CO`.

Now fire `R_i`, the O2 clock on `{u_i,v_i}`. The O deposited at `u_i` sees no adjacent CO and survives. The O deposited at `v_i` has exactly one adjacent CO, namely `w_i`, and reacts with it. Therefore

`C_i R_i : (O,0,CO) -> (O,0,0)`.                    (3.1)

In particular:

1. the original boundary site `v_i` is vacant before, during, and after the two-mark service;
2. the lost interior O shield is restored exactly;
3. the exterior CO is converted to vacancy.

So `R_i` is not a damaging “defect-site O2” event. It is an exact service event which converts a first CO defect into useful outward vacancy mass while preserving the boundary anchor.

### Proof

The statement follows from the forced nearest-neighbor reaction geometry just described. At the `R_i` mark the newly deposited O at `u_i` has O or vacancy neighbors tangentially/inward and the co-deposited O at `v_i`; it has no CO neighbor. At `v_i`, the only CO neighbor is `w_i`. QED.

## 4. Main exact identity: two CO defects plus two services equal one clean zipper

### Theorem 4.1 — defect-service diamond

From the clean two-column phase state,

`Z`

and

`C_0, C_1, R_0, R_1`

have **exactly the same terminal local state**, provided each `R_i` occurs after its own `C_i`. The four defect/service marks may otherwise be interleaved arbitrarily. The common terminal state is

`u_0=u_1=O`,
`v_0=v_1=0`,
`w_0=w_1=0`.                                      (4.1)

Equivalently, at the level of the exact source transition semigroup on this guarded cell,

`Z  ==  (C_0 R_0)(C_1 R_1)`,                       (4.2)

where the two column pairs commute and the right side allows every linear extension of `C_i<R_i`.

### Proof

For the direct zipper `Z`, O2 is deposited on `{v_0,v_1}`. At each endpoint `v_i`, the outward site `w_i` is the unique adjacent CO; the interior site `u_i` is O and the tangential sites are vacancies. Thus each deposited O annihilates `w_i`, leaving `v_i,w_i` vacant and leaving `u_i` unchanged O. This gives (4.1).

For the defect-service route, apply Lemma 3.1 independently in columns 0 and 1. `C_i R_i` maps exactly `(O,0,CO)` to `(O,0,0)`. Operations in column 0 do not alter any site consulted by the corresponding reactions in column 1 except for tangential sites which remain vacancy/O and never become opposite-type reaction partners. Hence the two column services commute. Every interleaving respecting `C_i<R_i` therefore gives (4.1). QED.

### Geometric interpretation

The common terminal state contains the outward vacancy detour

`v_0 -- w_0 -- w_1 -- v_1`.

Thus the two-defect/two-service word lands on **the same outward annular ear** as the clean zipper. This is stronger than merely repairing the defects back to the old boundary: the interference has been recycled into forward annular work.

Moreover, throughout the four-mark word the original edge `{v_0,v_1}` remains vacant. Therefore the pre-existing vacancy separator itself is never destroyed by this service word, even though the strict O collar is temporarily punctured after a `C_i` mark.

## 5. The two service marks are source-count optimal after the two CO defects

Let

`Q=N_CO-N_O`.

A successful CO source arrival changes `Q` by `+1`, an O2 source arrival changes `Q` by `-2`, and instantaneous reactions preserve `Q`.

The direct zipper terminal state differs from the clean state by `Delta Q=-2`, because it is reached by one O2 source mark. After the two first CO defects `C_0,C_1`, the accumulated source charge is `+2`. Therefore any continuation from the double-defect state to the zipper terminal state must supply source charge

`-4`.

If the continuation uses `A_CO` additional CO marks and `A_O2` additional O2 marks, then

`A_CO - 2 A_O2 = -4`.                             (5.1)

Hence necessarily

`A_O2 >= 2`.                                      (5.2)

The word `R_0,R_1` uses exactly two O2 marks and no additional CO marks. Therefore it is **source-count optimal**.

This matters for the service queue: the repair is not paying a gratuitous long prescribed word. Once an adjacent pair of first CO defects exists, two useful service marks are the minimum allowed by the exact source charge, and they produce forward progress rather than mere reset.

## 6. Tensorization over a boundary matching

Take a straight side with boundary vertices

`v_1,...,v_{2k}`

and corresponding interior/exterior columns `u_j,w_j`. Pair the vertices into the matching

`e_r={v_{2r-1},v_{2r}}`,  `1<=r<=k`.

For each cell `e_r`, choose independently one of two deterministic source routes:

1. the direct zipper `Z_r` on `e_r`; or
2. the four-mark service route `C_{2r-1}, C_{2r}, R_{2r-1}, R_{2r}` with only the two local precedence constraints `C_j<R_j`.

### Theorem 6.1 — mixed direct/service sweep

For any subset of matching cells completed by either route, and in any global interleaving consistent with the local service precedence constraints, the terminal configuration on every completed cell is exactly the same as if its direct zipper `Z_r` alone had been used.

Consequently:

- the completed cells can be spliced into the same outward-detour vacancy circuit used in the monotone annular partial-sweep theorem;
- the terminal phase geometry depends only on **which cells completed**, not on whether completion came from a designated O2 zipper or from two interfering CO marks followed by service;
- throughout all service words, every old boundary vertex belonging to a service cell remains vacant, so the original vacancy separator survives until the cell is compiled back into the exact ear state.

### Proof

Theorem 4.1 gives equality of the two route maps cell by cell. Distinct matching cells use disjoint triples of vertical column sites. A source operation in one cell cannot change the forced reaction partner used in another cell. Therefore the cell maps commute. Replacing every completed service cell by its equal direct-zipper map reduces the final configuration to the already-proved direct partial-sweep geometry. QED.

### Word multiplicity

A serviced cell has four marks with two independent precedence constraints `C_0<R_0` and `C_1<R_1`. Thus it already has

`4!/(2*2)=6`

legal internal interleavings leading to the same exact ear. Across disjoint cells these interleavings can themselves be arbitrarily interwoven. The important point is structural, not combinatorial bookkeeping: the source-success set contains an extensive family of interference histories, not a single prescribed word.

## 7. Uniform positive cylinder probability for the service route

The exact identity also gives a source-valid finite-horizon probability that is uniform in the perimeter.

Fix one service cell and a finite guard containing its two vertical columns and one interaction halo. Let `Gamma<infinity` upper-bound the total primitive source-clock rate in that guard during the four-mark path. Divide a time interval of length `tau` into four equal pieces and require, for example,

1. `C_0` in the first quarter,
2. `C_1` in the second quarter,
3. `R_0` in the third quarter,
4. `R_1` in the fourth quarter,

with no other guarded source mark during the full interval.

At `p=1/2` the two CO rates are `1/2` and the two radial O2 rates are `1/4`. Therefore

`P(service ear in one guarded cell by tau)`
` >= (tau/4)^4 (1/2)^2 (1/4)^2 exp(-Gamma tau)`
` = tau^4 exp(-Gamma tau) / 16384`.                  (7.1)

This is a fixed positive constant for every fixed `tau>0`, independent of the annular perimeter.

If `N` service-cell guards are chosen pairwise disjoint, their four-mark cylinder events use disjoint Harris labels and are independent. Thus the number of completed service ears stochastically dominates

`Bin(N, c_srv(tau))`,

with

`c_srv(tau)=tau^4 exp(-Gamma tau)/16384 >0`,          (7.2)

on these guarded cylinders. In particular, a linear bank contains linearly many such productive interference histories with exponentially small binomial lower tail **if the surrounding annular topology is protected separately**.

The final qualification is important: this is not yet a complete regenerative block estimate, because cells whose guarded cylinder fails may undergo other source histories. The inherited logarithmic-belt topology theorem is the natural place to absorb those local failures; I do not silently count failed cells as harmless.

## 8. Exact change to the service-versus-defect state space

The previous annular analyses conservatively treated source activity through an interior vacancy defect as fatal or outside the recurrent calculus. Theorem 4.1 shows that this loses a whole productive channel.

For a straight boundary column, introduce three local labels:

- `A` = active clean column `(O,0,CO)`;
- `U` = unmatched upper defect `(0,0,CO)` created by a first boundary CO hit;
- `R` = serviced/retired column `(O,0,0)`.

Then the exact source transitions include

`A --CO--> U`,
`U --radial O2--> R`.                              (8.1)

For two adjacent columns,

`(A,A) --direct boundary O2--> EAR`,

while also

`(A,A) --CO,CO--> (U,U) --two radial O2--> EAR`,    (8.2)

and the two routes have the identical `EAR` endpoint.

Therefore the correct finite-state queue is not “number of CO defects, each of which is bad.” It is a queue of **unmatched** `U` columns, together with already serviced outward-vacancy columns. Adjacent serviced pairs compile exactly into annular progress. The truly dangerous events are now narrower: repeated/base-filling hits before service, defect cascades which leave this three-label closure, and interactions between serviced spurs and later sweep marks.

This is the concrete reduction achieved in this run.
