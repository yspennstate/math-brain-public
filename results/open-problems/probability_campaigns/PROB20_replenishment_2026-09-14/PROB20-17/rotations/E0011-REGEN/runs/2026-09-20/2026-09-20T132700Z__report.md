## 4. Conditional source-count optimality for all nine routes

Let

`Q=N_CO-N_O`.

A CO source mark changes `Q` by `+1`, an O2 source mark changes it by `-2`, and instantaneous reactions preserve it.

From the EAR `E` to the translated target `T`, the only net local occupation change is that `v_0,v_1` become O. Hence

`Delta Q(E,T)=-2`.                                        (4.1)

The direct terminal mark `F` realizes this in one source arrival, which is trivially optimal.

Now condition on a specified first nonterminal mark `d`.

### Proposition 4.1 — CO-first routes are conditionally optimal

If `d` is one of the four CO labels, the first mark contributes source charge `+1`, while the total target charge is `-2`. The continuation must therefore contribute charge `-3`.

If the continuation has `x` CO marks and `y` O2 marks,

`x-2y=-3`.                                                (4.2)

The smallest nonnegative solution has `y=2,x=1`, so every continuation has length

`x+y >= 3`.                                               (4.3)

The inherited two-mark repair followed by `F` uses exactly three continuation marks, one CO and two O2, and attains the lower bound.

### Proposition 4.2 — O2-first routes are conditionally optimal

If `d` is one of the five nonterminal O2 labels, the first mark already contributes source charge `-2`, equal to the total charge displacement (4.1). Since `d` is nonterminal, the post-`d` state is not `T`, so a nonempty continuation with net source charge zero is required.

No one-mark or two-mark source word has charge zero:

- one mark has charge `+1` or `-2`;
- two marks have possible charges `+2,-1,-4`.

The shortest nonempty zero-charge source word therefore has length at least three, necessarily two CO marks and one O2 mark. The inherited two-mark repair followed by `F` has exactly this composition and attains length three.

### Corollary 4.3

For **each of the nine possible first nonterminal core labels**, the four-mark route (3.2) is globally shortest among exact source continuations conditioned on that first label and target `T`.

Thus the complete first-order terminal catalogue is not padded by avoidable repair depth.
