## 2. Source clocks at the anchor

Work at `p=1/2`, hence `q=1`.

- A CO source clock at a vacant site has rate `1/2`.
- An O2 source clock on a nearest-neighbor vacant edge has rate `1/4`.
- A source occurrence can make at most two previously vacant adsorption sites occupied.
- Instantaneous unlike-neighbor reactions delete occupied particles; they never create occupation at a site that was not an adsorption endpoint.

All constructions below use only these exact source clocks. Favorable reaction choices are not needed in the phase-interface lemmas because the relevant deposited O atoms have a unique adjacent CO partner.

## 3. Main local simplification: one O2 mark already creates the exact packet

Consider a straight portion of the outer boundary of an all-vacant belt. Let

`v_0 - v_1`

be two adjacent boundary vacancies. Let `w_0,w_1` be their axial outward neighbors, and assume the canonical phase collar has

`w_0=w_1=CO`.

All other nearest neighbors of `v_0,v_1` in the controlled local picture are vacant: the tangential boundary neighbors are vacant and the inward neighbors lie in the vacant belt.

### Lemma 3.1 — one-mark V2 birth

A single O2 adsorption on the vacant edge `{v_0,v_1}` produces the exact all-vacant plaquette

`{v_0,v_1,w_0,w_1}`.

#### Proof

The O atom deposited at `v_i` has exactly one adjacent CO, namely `w_i`. It therefore reacts with `w_i`. The reaction removes both particles and leaves both sites vacant. This occurs independently at the two adsorption endpoints. Hence after the one O2 source occurrence all four sites are vacant. QED.

### Two immediate consequences

1. The same one mark already gives **two site-disjoint vacancy-edge seeds**: the old edge `{v_0,v_1}` and the new outward edge `{w_0,w_1}`.
2. The three-mark packet cylinder in the newest P02-B report is valid but not minimal. Its first mark already achieves the V2 endpoint that Section 5 of that report needed. The later two marks are useful only if one specifically wants a longer zipper excursion.

This matters probabilistically because the clean packet probability is first-order, not third-order, in a short time window.

## 4. Correct one-mark guarded packet probability

Fix a finite guard around one selected boundary edge, large enough that if no other primitive source clock in the guard rings, the four-site conclusion of Lemma 3.1 remains unchanged until readout. Let `Gamma_1<infinity` be the total rate of all **other** guarded primitive clocks. It is universal and independent of the perimeter.

For a time window `[0,t]`, require:

- the designated O2 clock, rate `1/4`, rings exactly once in `[0,t]`;
- no other guarded source clock rings in `[0,t]`.

Then the exact clean-cylinder probability is

`z_1(t) = (t/4) exp[-(Gamma_1+1/4)t] > 0`.                              (4.1)

Compare this with the corrected three-mark lower bound from P02-B,

`z_3(t)=t^3 exp[-Gamma_* t]/864`.

The qualitative conclusion of the P02-B reservoir theorem survives, but the one-mark event is parametrically stronger and needs a smaller local guard.

### Corollary 4.1 — strengthened fixed-time logarithmic-belt packet reservoir

Use P02-B's logarithmic belt

`w_n = 2 ceil(beta log n)`

and its fixed small time `tau>0` for which topology loss and causal leakage are `o(1)`. On each of two separated forward banks, choose `M_n>=c_pack n` boundary edges with disjoint one-mark guards.

Let `X_i` count clean one-mark V2 births in bank `i`. Then

`X_i >=_st Bin(M_n,z_1(tau))`.

Therefore, with

`rho_1 := c_pack z_1(tau)/2 > 0`,

`P(X_i < rho_1 n) <= exp(-c_1 n)`

for a fixed `c_1>0`. Intersecting with the inherited logarithmic-belt survival and causal-insulation events gives

`P(two forward banks each contain >=rho_1 n exact V2 packets by time tau) = 1-o(1)`.   (4.2)

Thus the packet-reservoir conclusion needs only one source mark per successful cell. No second-stage exact checker is needed for this strengthening.

This still does **not** give a recurrent macroscopic child bank by itself; Section 8 returns to that point.

## 5. Three vacant layers, not five, are enough for arbitrary fixed local margin

The previous P02-A theorem needed width five because its useful local zipper event required two source marks. Lemma 3.1 changes the exponent balance.

Let

`A_3 = R^{+3} \ R`

be an initially all-vacant three-layer rectangular belt, with the canonical exterior CO collar on the straight outer boundary. Let `L` be its outer perimeter.

### Lemma 5.1 — two-occurrence topology barrier for width three

If at some time the three-layer belt contains no nearest-neighbor vacant circuit separating `R` from the exterior, then at least **two** source occurrences have occurred whose adsorption supports form a connected cluster in the finite-range matching geometry.

#### Proof

By planar site duality, absence of a nearest-neighbor vacant separating circuit implies an occupied `*`-connected radial crossing of the three initially vacant layers. Such a crossing contains at least three sites that were initially vacant.

One source occurrence can turn at most two previously vacant adsorption endpoints occupied, and reactions only delete particles. Hence one source occurrence cannot account for all three occupied crossing sites. At least two source occurrences are necessary. Consecutive crossing sites are `*`-neighbors, so the source labels responsible for their most recent vacancy-to-occupation transitions have supports at uniformly bounded distance; after discarding repetitions they form a connected finite-range cluster. QED.

As in the preceding five-layer proof, the bounded degree of the source-label graph now gives a quadratic small-time estimate.

### Lemma 5.2 — width-three topology loss is `O(L t^2)`

There is a universal `C_3<infinity` such that for all sufficiently small `t`,

`P(F_3(t)) <= C_3 L t^2 exp(C_3 t)`.                                   (5.1)

The bound includes both distinct adjacent clock labels and the possibility that one local label rings twice. The number of possible roots is `O(L)` and every relevant source rate is at most `1/2`.

### One-mark progress in two banks

Choose two separated forward banks, each containing at least `c_2 L` guarded edges as in Section 4. Let `G_1(t)` be the event that each bank contains at least one clean one-mark V2 birth by time `t`.

Using disjoint guards and (4.1),

`P(G_1(t)) >= [1-exp(-c_2 L z_1(t))]^2`.                               (5.2)

Set

`t_L = theta/L`,                                                       (5.3)

where `theta>0` is fixed. Then

`L z_1(theta/L) -> theta/4`,

up to the fixed packing factor, and hence

`liminf_{L->infinity} P(G_1(t_L))
 >= [1-exp(-c_2 theta/4)]^2
 =: s_1(theta) > 0`.                                                    (5.4)

On the other hand, (5.1) gives

`P(F_3(t_L)) = O_theta(1/L)`.                                          (5.5)

### Exterior causal leakage is lower order

The inherited causal-cone estimate at `p=1/2` has the form

`eta_L(t) <= C L sum_{r>=N} (38t)^r/r!`.

Choose any fixed buffer requiring `N=3` causal source steps to enter the read region. At `t=t_L=theta/L`,

`eta_L(t_L)=O_theta(L^{-2})`.                                          (5.6)

Thus arbitrary stable exterior data cost less than the already vanishing topology-loss term.

### Theorem 5.3 — minimal-width arbitrary-coefficient local margin

Let `a_L` be the exact-source probability of one clean V2 packet birth in each forward bank before topology loss or causal leakage, and let

`f_L := P(F_3(t_L)) + eta_L(t_L)`.

Then for every fixed `kappa<infinity`,

`a_L - kappa f_L > 0`                                                   (5.7)

for all sufficiently large `L`. In particular,

`a_L - 63 f_L > 0`                                                      (5.8)

for all sufficiently large `L`.

#### Proof

From (5.4), `a_L` has strictly positive liminf after subtracting `f_L`. From (5.5)--(5.6), `f_L=O(L^{-1})`. Hence

`a_L-kappa f_L >= P(G_1(t_L))-(kappa+1)f_L`

is positive for all sufficiently large `L`. QED.

### Minimality of width three for this exponent mechanism

A width-two vacant belt can lose its separating circuit after a **single** O2 occurrence occupying two radial sites. Therefore at the one-mark progress scale `t=theta/L`, both useful progress and width-two topology loss can be first-order in `L t`, so no exponent separation is available from this argument.

Thus width three is the first redundant width for which one-mark packet progress is first order while genuine topology loss is second order. The previous width-five theorem was optimal for a two-mark useful event; the new one-mark observation shifts the minimal redundancy from five layers to three.

## 6. Perfect-matching strip lift: coherent one-layer translation in `m/2` marks

The one-mark lemma also has a coherent version.

Let `m=2r` be even. Consider a straight phase-interface segment

`v_1,...,v_m`

of vacancies. Its outward neighbors

`w_1,...,w_m`

are all CO. Assume a one-site tangential guard and the inward neighbors are vacant, so each `v_i` has `w_i` as its unique adjacent CO when the designated event occurs.

Use the perfect matching

`{v_1,v_2}, {v_3,v_4}, ..., {v_{m-1},v_m}`.

### Lemma 6.1 — exact matching lift

Fire one O2 source occurrence on every matched edge, in any order, and no other relevant source occurrence. After the `r=m/2` marks, the entire `2 x m` strip

`{v_1,...,v_m,w_1,...,w_m}`

is vacant.

#### Proof

Each matched O2 mark is supported on two base vacancies and sees two distinct outward CO particles. By Lemma 3.1 it clears exactly those two outward COs and restores the two adsorption endpoints to vacancy. Different matching edges are site-disjoint and clear disjoint CO pairs, so the operations commute. After all `m/2` marks every `w_i` is vacant and the original base row remains vacant. QED.

### Lemma 6.2 — `m/2` source marks are optimal for this exact lift

Any exact source trajectory that starts with the `m` outward sites occupied by CO and ends with all of them vacant, with no pre-existing adjacent O resource used from outside the protected phase cell, requires at least `m/2` O2 source arrivals.

#### Proof

Only an O atom can annihilate a target CO. Every O2 source arrival creates at most two O atoms, and each deposited O can remove at most one target CO. Hence one O2 source occurrence can remove at most two of the `m` target CO particles. At least `m/2` O2 source arrivals are therefore necessary. Lemma 6.1 attains this bound. QED.

Equivalently, in the protected exact transformation the source charge identity gives `Delta Q=-m=A_CO-2A_O2`, so `A_O2>=m/2`; equality is attained with `A_CO=0`.

### Clean-cylinder lower bound

Let the total rate of all other primitive clocks in the `O(m)` guard be at most `gamma m`. Requiring each of the `m/2` designated O2 clocks to ring exactly once in `[0,t]` and all other guarded clocks not to ring gives

`p_m(t)
 >= (t/4)^(m/2) exp[-(1/8 + gamma)m t]`.                               (6.1)

Thus coherent length-`m` strip translation has an explicit `exp[-Theta(m)]` clean-cylinder lower bound for fixed `t`.

### Mesoscopic amplification consequence

A bank of macroscopic length `L` contains at least `cL/m` disjoint guarded length-`m` cells. If

`m = 2 floor(alpha log L / 2)`

and

`alpha < 1 / I(t)`,

where

`I(t):=(1/2) log(4/t) + (1/8+gamma)t`,

then from (6.1)

`p_m(t)=L^{-alpha I(t)+o(1)}`

and

`(cL/m) p_m(t) -> infinity`.

Hence each of two separated banks contains, with probability tending to one, at least one **coherent translated vacancy strip of length `Theta(log L)`** after one layer of outward motion.

This is not yet same-macroscopic-type bank closure, but it is a stronger spatial object than an isolated packet and shows that the local phase geometry supports coherent logarithmic segments, not only isolated V2 births.

## 7. Arbitrary-exterior vacancy-edge transport: the bank can move a finite distance without a phase collar

The preceding lift uses the favorable phase collar. The older P02-A edge-carving lemma gives a complementary arbitrary-exterior transport statement.

Recall the exact structural fact already proved in the `2026-09-20T035500Z` report: from a vacant nearest-neighbor edge `{x,y}` and an arbitrary stable state at a neighboring target site `z`, one can extend the vacancy path through `z` in at most two legal source arrivals while keeping a vacant edge at the advancing tip.

### Lemma 7.1 — uniform exponential lower bound for prescribed edge transport

There is a universal constant `chi in (0,1)` and a universal finite time per lattice step `tau_0` such that the following holds at `p=1/2`.

Let

`x_0,x_1,...,x_{d+1}`

be any prescribed self-avoiding lattice path and suppose `{x_0,x_1}` is vacant initially. The stable exterior is arbitrary. There is a protected adaptive protocol, using only a moving fixed-radius guard, which by time `d tau_0` leaves the terminal edge `{x_d,x_{d+1}}` vacant with probability at least

`chi^d`.                                                               (7.1)

#### Proof

At one extension step, the exact structural lemma needs either zero, one, or two requested source arrivals depending on whether the next target is vacant, O, or CO. Every requested source clock has rate at least `1/4`. Whenever a reaction choice is required, the designated opposite particle has conditional choice probability at least `1/4`, because there are at most four nearest neighbors.

Fix a small constant subinterval length `delta>0`. Around the current vacant edge and next target, take a fixed-radius guard containing every primitive clock whose source occurrence could change that three-site extension before the requested mark(s) complete. The total guard rate is bounded by a universal constant `G`, independent of `d` and of the exterior state.

For a one-arrival extension, the event that the designated clock is the first relevant guarded clock in its subinterval and that the designated reaction choice occurs has probability at least a universal `c_1>0`. For a two-arrival extension, use two consecutive subintervals and obtain probability at least `c_1^2`. Thus one lattice extension succeeds with conditional probability at least

`chi := c_1^2 > 0`

uniformly over every stable exterior state encountered.

The protocol immediately advances its guarded workspace to the new terminal edge. Earlier path sites need not remain protected: the next structural step only uses the current vacant edge, and the extension lemma is uniform over the rest of the stable configuration. Applying the strong Markov property step by step gives the product lower bound `chi^d`. Taking `tau_0=2delta` (or any larger fixed slot accommodating the two-mark case) proves the claim. QED.

### Corollary 7.2 — quantitative transport of an edge bank

Suppose a forward bank contains `N_L>=rho L` pairwise separated vacancy edges, and choose pairwise disjoint parallel transport corridors of length `d`. Running Lemma 7.1 in those corridors gives a transported edge count `Y_L` satisfying

`Y_L >=_st Bin(N_L,chi^d)`.                                             (7.2)

Hence for fixed `d`,

`P(Y_L < (rho chi^d/2)L) <= exp[-c(d,rho)L]`.                           (7.3)

So a linear vacancy-edge bank can be moved any **fixed finite distance** through arbitrary stable chemistry while retaining a linear number of edge seeds with exponentially high probability.

More generally, for

`d_L = floor(alpha log L)`

with `alpha < 1/|log chi|`, the expected number of transported edges diverges polynomially:

`E Y_L >= rho L^(1-alpha|log chi|+o(1))`,                               (7.4)

and standard Chernoff bounds give a corresponding polynomial-size transported family with high probability.

This is a genuine source-valid spatial-advance lemma. It does not use a pre-existing CO corridor and it does not stop at a code-only reachability statement.

### Why `M_reg` is still not promoted

The historical metric requires one finite recurrent seed class whose SUCCESS endpoint supplies the forward seed(s) needed for the next identical block trial and whose nonterminal outcomes RETRY to that same class.

The fixed-time logarithmic annulus produces a **linear bank** of V2/edge packets. Corollary 7.2 transports such a bank, but the crude arbitrary-exterior lower bound multiplies its density by `chi^d<1` over each fixed-distance transport stage. Repeating only that lower bound would therefore contract the guaranteed bank density geometrically. Conversely, the one-mark annular source that replenishes the bank is not yet reconstructed from an arbitrary packet bank.

Thus the precise remaining closure statement is now:

> Find a source-valid recurrent macroscopic packet type for which one coarse step restores a **noncontracting** bank of the same type in each required outgoing slot, or prove a multitype comparison whose reproduction matrix has spectral radius greater than one.

This is stronger and more specific than the previous phrase "bank-to-bank spatial advance": finite-distance transport is now proved. The missing ingredient is **noncontracting recurrent mass**, not motion.

## 9. Failed route that now has a precise reason

A tempting idea is to iterate the arbitrary-exterior edge-carving lower bound from Corollary 7.2. It does not close the block recursion by itself. If a bank starts with density `rho`, the proved one-step lower bound after distance `d` is only `rho chi^d/2`, and applying the same estimate again contracts the certified density by another fixed factor.

This does **not** prove that the actual dynamics is subcritical; it proves only that a prescribed rare-word transport certificate is the wrong final tool. A closure proof has to exploit one of the following genuinely different mechanisms:

- typical parallel interface dynamics whose effective reproduction factor is at least one;
- a multitype packet in which a high-density phase-bank type and a sparse arbitrary-exterior edge type regenerate each other with supercritical spectral radius;
- or a repair/replenishment mechanism that converts the transported edge family back into the logarithmic-belt packet reservoir without exponentially rare all-site coordination.

The next run should work on that reproduction factor directly rather than produce a longer carving word.
