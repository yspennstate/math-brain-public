## 2. Source dynamics used

At `p=1/2`, `q=1`. I use only source-model events:

- a CO arrival at a vacant site has rate `1/2`; if an adjacent O exists, instantaneous reaction removes the incoming CO and one such O, leaving both sites vacant; otherwise CO survives;
- an O2 arrival on a nearest-neighbour vacant edge has rate `1/4`; each deposited O either survives or instantly removes an adjacent CO.

Every construction below is a finite adaptive sequence of legal source events in the exact unstirred model.

I inherit the proved **vacancy bulldozer lemma** from the preceding P02-A report: if two adjacent columns of height `n` are vacant, then an arbitrary stable `m x n` rectangle immediately ahead of them can be cleared to vacancies while restoring the two-column workspace, using at most `4mn` legal source arrivals. The reflected/rotated versions hold by lattice symmetry.

## 3. Main new theorem: the `2 x 2` vacancy square is already self-replicating

Let

\[
S_0=\{0,1\}\times\{0,1\}
\]

and suppose every site of `S_0` is vacant. Make **no assumption** on the stable configuration outside `S_0`.

Define the two disjoint translated child squares

\[
S_- = \{-1,0\}\times\{0,1\},
\qquad
S_+ = \{1,2\}\times\{0,1\}.
\]

### Theorem 3.1 — four-site vacancy seed gives two exact children in at most 16 arrivals

For every stable exterior configuration, there is an adaptive legal source-event sequence of length at most 16 after which every site of `S_- union S_+` is vacant. Equivalently, one exact `2 x 2` vacancy seed produces two disjoint translated exact copies of itself.

### Proof

The parent square consists of two adjacent vacant columns, `x=0` and `x=1`, each of height two.

First apply the vacancy bulldozer with `m=1,n=2` to the target column

\[
\{2\}\times\{0,1\}.
\]

The workspace is the two parent columns. The target column may contain arbitrary stable chemistry and may have arbitrary stable chemistry on its exterior side. The bulldozer clears it in at most

\[
4mn=4\cdot1\cdot2=8
\]

source arrivals and finishes with columns `0,1,2` vacant on these two rows.

Now reflect the same construction and clear

\[
\{-1\}\times\{0,1\}
\]

using the still-vacant parent columns `0,1` as the two-column workspace. Again at most 8 arrivals suffice and the workspace is restored.

At the end, the entire `4 x 2` rectangle

\[
\{-1,0,1,2\}\times\{0,1\}
\]

is vacant. Its left two columns are exactly `S_-` and its right two columns are exactly `S_+`; these child seeds are site-disjoint. Total source arrivals are at most `8+8=16`. QED.

### Corollary 3.2 — uniform positive finite-horizon two-child probability

There are constants `tau_sq<infinity` and `s_sq>0`, depending only on a fixed finite interaction halo around the four columns, such that uniformly over every stable exterior state, a process started with `S_0` vacant produces both exact child squares by time `tau_sq` with probability at least `s_sq`.

#### Proof

The policy above has at most 16 adaptive steps and reads only a fixed finite halo. At every step the desired legal source clock has rate bounded below by a positive constant at `p=1/2`, the total rate of relevant competing clocks in that finite halo is bounded above, and any reaction-choice auxiliary has at most four alternatives. Divide a fixed finite horizon into 16 short subintervals, require the policy's desired mark/reaction choice in the next interval, and suppress all other relevant marks there. The conditional probability of each step is uniformly positive over the finite local state space. Multiplying the 16 lower bounds gives `s_sq>0`. QED.

### Why this is a real simplification

The preceding report's explicit mixed template required a `12 x 8` box, a `4 x 4` O core, a 28-site CO circuit, four vacancy launch columns and at most 840 arrivals for two-child reproduction. The exact same source-valid reachability obligation is now met by **four vacant sites and at most 16 arrivals**, a factor `840/16=52.5` reduction in worst-case event depth, with no builder stage and no prescribed CO/O chemistry in the seed.

This does not by itself make the block event high probability. It changes what the probability calculation has to protect: a four-site vacancy seed rather than a 96-site mixed template.

## 4. A second new theorem: vacancy-seed density already forces CO/O coexistence in a stationary law

A possible concern with a vacancy-only block seed is that it does not explicitly carry either chemical species. The next proposition removes that concern.

For a finite set `A`, write `V(A)` for the event that every site of `A` is vacant.

### Proposition 4.1 — stationary vacancy square implies both adsorbates occur with positive density

Fix any source parameter `p in (0,1)` with `q/2=1-p`, so both CO and O2 source rates are strictly positive. Let `mu` be a translation-invariant stationary law of the exact infinite-reaction catalyst process. If

\[
\mu\big(V(\{0,1\}^2)\big)>0,
\]

then

\[
\mu(\eta(0)=1)>0
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
\mu(\eta(0)=2)>0.
\]

Thus a stationary law with positive `2 x 2` vacancy-seed density is a genuine non-poisoned CO/O coexistence law.

### Proof

#### Step 1: a `2 x 2` vacancy square reaches a `4 x 4` vacancy square with uniformly positive probability

Starting from the `2 x 2` vacancy square, Theorem 3.1 clears one adjacent column on each side, leaving a `4 x 2` all-vacant rectangle, in at most 16 arrivals.

Rotate the bulldozer by 90 degrees. The two vacant rows of this `4 x 2` rectangle are a two-row workspace of length four. Clear one adjacent row above and one adjacent row below. Each one-row target costs at most

\[
4\cdot1\cdot4=16
\]

arrivals. Hence in at most

\[
16+16+16=48
\]

legal source arrivals the process reaches a `4 x 4` all-vacant square, uniformly over arbitrary stable exterior data.

As in Corollary 3.2, because the policy uses a fixed finite halo and at most 48 source marks, there are fixed `t_0<infinity` and `c_0>0` such that for every configuration `eta` containing the initial `2 x 2` vacancy square,

\[
P_{t_0}(\eta,V_4)\ge c_0,
\]

where `V_4` denotes the corresponding `4 x 4` all-vacant event.

Stationarity gives

\[
\mu(V_4)
=\int P_{t_0}(\eta,V_4)\,d\mu(\eta)
\ge c_0\,\mu(V_2)>0.
\]

#### Step 2: from `V_4`, a CO can survive with positive probability

Choose an interior site `z` of the `4 x 4` square, so all four nearest neighbours of `z` also lie in the vacant square. On `V_4`, trigger a CO arrival at `z` and suppress all other source marks that can affect `z` for a short fixed interval afterward. At the arrival time there is no adjacent O, so the CO survives. Because `p>0` and only finitely many clocks need be controlled, this event has conditional probability at least some `c_1>0`, uniformly over the exterior.

Using stationarity once more,

\[
\mu(\eta(z)=1)\ge c_1\mu(V_4)>0.
\]

Translation invariance gives the same positive one-site CO density at the origin.

#### Step 3: from `V_4`, O can survive with positive probability

Choose an interior nearest-neighbour edge `e={z,z'}` whose endpoints and all their nearest neighbours lie in the `4 x 4` square. Trigger the O2 clock on `e` and suppress other relevant marks for a short fixed interval. On `V_4` there is no adjacent CO, so both deposited O atoms survive. The O2 edge rate is

\[
q/4=(1-p)/2>0.
\]

Thus this event has uniform conditional probability `c_2>0`, and stationarity gives

\[
\mu(\eta(z)=2)\ge c_2\mu(V_4)>0.
\]

Again translation invariance transfers this to the origin. QED.

## 7. What is still missing

The principal obstruction is now much narrower:

\[
\boxed{\text{Construct a finite protected trial on a tiny vacancy seed class with }a-\kappa_*f>0.}
\]

The pure prescribed-word probability from Corollary 3.2 is far too small to settle this. Ordinary histories must overwhelmingly be accepted as RETRY rather than FATAL. The right next object is therefore not a larger builder but a **small topological vacancy Markov class** around `V_2`/short paths/circuits, using the exact charge-Euler identity to classify repairs and genuine exits.

A promising next attack, not claimed here as proved, is to put an O-rich collar around a small connected vacancy cluster and analyze only topology-changing effective events: O2 deletion of a vacancy edge can split the cluster into two child components, while CO/O reactions can grow or repair the vacancy set. The needed theorem is a state-uniform SUCCESS/FATAL ratio, not another exact rare word.
