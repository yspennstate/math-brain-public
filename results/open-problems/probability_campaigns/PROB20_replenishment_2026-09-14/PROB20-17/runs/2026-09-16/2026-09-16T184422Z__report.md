## 4. B15 + B24: exact log-scale reformulation of the `m=n` obstruction

This was a **thinking/route-diagnosis calculation**, not metric movement. It explains why another brute-force scale sweep is not a useful seventh run.

Fix nonzero PSD “shapes” `B_1,...,B_n` and vary only positive scales

\[
A_i=s_i B_i,\qquad s_i>0.
\]

For a word `w=(w_1,...,w_n) in [n]^n`, define

\[
c_w:=\|B_{w_1}\cdots B_{w_n}\|_{\rm op}\ge0
\]

and let `m_i(w)` be the number of occurrences of symbol `i` in `w`. Thus

\[
\sum_{i=1}^n m_i(w)=n.
\]

Define the fixed-shape without-replacement average

\[
W(B):=\frac1{n!}\sum_{\sigma\in S_n}
\|B_{\sigma(1)}\cdots B_{\sigma(n)}\|.
\]

Every without-replacement word uses every index exactly once, hence

\[
\operatorname{WOR}_n(s_1B_1,\ldots,s_nB_n)
=\Big(\prod_i s_i\Big)W(B).
\tag{4.1}
\]

The with-replacement average is

\[
\operatorname{WR}_n(s_1B_1,\ldots,s_nB_n)
=\frac1{n^n}\sum_{w\in[n]^n}
 c_w\prod_i s_i^{m_i(w)}.
\tag{4.2}
\]

Put

\[
P_B(s):=\sum_w c_w\prod_i s_i^{m_i(w)}.
\]

If `W(B)=0`, the source ratio is zero for every positive scale vector, so there is nothing to optimize. Assume `W(B)>0`. Then

\[
Q_B(s):=\frac{\operatorname{WOR}_n}{\operatorname{WR}_n}
=\frac{n^n W(B)\prod_i s_i}{P_B(s)}.
\tag{4.3}
\]

Set `x_i=log s_i`. Then

\[
F_B(x):=\log Q_B(e^x)
=\log(n^nW(B))+\sum_i x_i
-\log\sum_w c_w e^{m(w)\cdot x}.
\tag{4.4}
\]

The last term is a log-sum-exp. Therefore **`F_B` is concave in the log-scales**. It is also invariant under `x -> x+t 1`, exactly expressing common scalar homogeneity.

For

\[
p_x(w):=
\frac{c_w e^{m(w)\cdot x}}
{\sum_u c_u e^{m(u)\cdot x}},
\]

direct differentiation gives the exact identities

\[
\frac{\partial F_B}{\partial x_i}
=1-\mathbb E_x[m_i(w)],
\tag{4.5}
\]

and

\[
\nabla^2F_B(x)
=-\operatorname{Cov}_x(m(w))\preceq0.
\tag{4.6}
\]

Hence any positive stationary relative-scale vector is automatically a **global** maximizer for those fixed shapes, and stationarity is equivalent to the multiplicity-balance equations

\[
\boxed{\mathbb E_x[m_i(w)]=1\quad\text{for every }i.}
\tag{4.7}
\]

If the multiplicity covariance is positive definite on `1^\perp`, the maximizer is unique modulo common scale.

An equivalent capacity formulation is

\[
\operatorname{Cap}(P_B)
:=\inf_{s_i>0}\frac{P_B(s)}{\prod_i s_i},
\tag{4.8}
\]

so that

\[
\boxed{
\sup_{s_i>0} Q_B(s)
=\frac{n^nW(B)}{\operatorname{Cap}(P_B)}.
}
\tag{4.9}
\]

Thus for fixed PSD shapes the entire arbitrary-scale `m=n` source inequality is equivalent to

\[
\boxed{\operatorname{Cap}(P_B)\ge n^n W(B).}
\tag{4.10}
\]

This changes what a future attack should optimize: **scales can be eliminated by a finite-dimensional convex/log-capacity problem; the genuinely hard variables are the PSD shapes.** No stability or real-stability property of `P_B` is asserted here.

## 5. B26: the scale boundary cannot contain a fixed-shape `m=n` counterexample maximizer

Again this is route diagnosis for the handoff, not source progress.

Work on the gauge `1^\perp`, so `sum_i x_i=0`. Rewriting (4.3),

\[
Q_B(e^x)
=\frac{n^nW(B)}{
\sum_w c_w e^{(m(w)-\mathbf 1)\cdot x}}.
\tag{5.1}
\]

Because every `B_i` is nonzero PSD,

\[
c_{(i,i,\ldots,i)}=\|B_i^n\|_{\rm op}>0.
\]

For any nonzero direction `y in 1^\perp`, choose an index `i` with `y_i>0`. The constant word `(i,...,i)` contributes an exponent

\[
(ne_i-\mathbf1)\cdot y=ny_i>0.
\]

Therefore along the relative-scale ray `x+ty`, the denominator in (5.1) grows at least exponentially and

\[
Q_B(e^{x+ty})\longrightarrow0
\qquad(t\to+\infty).
\tag{5.2}
\]

Compactness of the unit sphere in `1^\perp`, together with the finitely many positive constant-word coefficients, makes this coercive uniformly in direction. Hence the fixed-shape scale maximum in (4.9) is attained at an **interior** relative-scale point.

If some `B_i=0`, every without-replacement product contains that zero matrix, so `WOR=0` immediately.

**Consequence for B26/B15:** sending one or more fixed PSD shapes to an extreme relative scale cannot create a hidden boundary maximizer with ratio above one. The remaining counterexample search has to change the shapes/eigenbases themselves, not merely drive their scalar weights to zero or infinity.
