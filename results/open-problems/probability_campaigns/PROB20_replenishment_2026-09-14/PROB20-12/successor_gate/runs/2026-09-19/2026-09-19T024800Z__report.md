\[
W(n,q)\le U(n,1-q)

1. **Bernoulli-width identification/asymptotic:** match the two-orientation edge-midpoint reachability model exactly, or to `o(n sqrt(q))`, with a solved strict/weak Bernoulli Hammersley/LPP model; and
