## 2. B10 — derandomize globally, not block by block

The previous two E0092 reports recovered and repaired a block-Toeplitz construction. This run found that the blocks are not mathematically necessary if the source only asks for a deterministic explicit family and does not demand polynomial construction time.

The clean route is to derandomize a **single `n x 2n` Toeplitz matrix** directly by conditional expectation. This removes the product-chain allocation argument, the `b_n/log n -> infinity` bookkeeping, and the mixed `b,b+1` partition entirely.

### 2.1 The exact shell potential

Fix `n` and put `N=2n`, `L=3n-1`. For a Toeplitz seed `s in F_2^L`, let `M_s` be the `n x 2n` binary Toeplitz matrix

`(M_s)_{r,c} = s_{c-r+n-1}`.

For `w in {0,...,2n}`, let `S_w` be the Hamming sphere, `C_w=binom(2n,w)`, and define

`Q_w(M) = 2^n P(MZ=MZ') - 1`,

where `Z,Z'` are independent uniform points of `S_w`.

As proved in the recovered memo and previous runs, the Toeplitz family is 2-universal: for every nonzero `y in F_2^{2n}`,

`P_s(M_s y=0)=2^{-n}`.

Therefore

`E_s Q_w(M_s) = (2^n-1)/C_w`.                                             (2.1)

Use exactly the old simultaneous-shell threshold

`eta_w = 2(2n+1) 2^n / C_w`                                               (2.2)

and define the nonnegative potential

`Phi(s) = sum_{w=0}^{2n} Q_w(M_s)/eta_w`.                                  (2.3)

By (2.1),

`E_s Phi(s)
 = (2n+1) * [(2^n-1)/(2(2n+1)2^n)]
 = (1-2^{-n})/2
 < 1/2`.                                                                    (2.4)

Because every summand in (2.3) is nonnegative, **any seed with `Phi(s)<1` automatically satisfies `Q_w(M_s)<eta_w` for every shell simultaneously.**

### 2.2 Exact conditional-expectation algorithm

Order the `L=3n-1` seed bits. At a partial prefix `p`, define

`Psi(p) = E[Phi(S) | S extends p]`.

At the next bit choose `0` if `Psi(p0) <= Psi(p1)`, otherwise choose `1`. Since

`Psi(p) = [Psi(p0)+Psi(p1)]/2`,

this choice never increases the conditional expectation. At the final seed `s_n`,

`Phi(s_n) <= E Phi < 1/2 < 1`; hence

`Q_w(M_{s_n}) < eta_w` for all `w`.                                        (2.5)

This is already a deterministic epsilon-independent family. It is not an existence statement with an unspecified good seed.

The only remaining issue for an actual algorithm is exact computability of each `Psi(p)`. That also has a finite exact formula.

For `y in F_2^{2n}` of weight `t`, the number of ordered pairs `(z,z') in S_w^2` satisfying `z+z'=y` is zero unless `t` is even, and otherwise is

`A_{w,t} = binom(t,t/2) binom(2n-t, w-t/2)`,                               (2.6)

with the convention that an invalid binomial coefficient is zero. Indeed, on `supp(y)` exactly `t/2` coordinates of `z` must be one in order that `z` and `z+y` have equal weight; off `supp(y)` there must be `w-t/2` ones.

For fixed `y`, the vector `M_s y` is a linear function of the Toeplitz seed. Under a prefix `p`, write this affine system as

`B_y p + R_y u = 0`,                                                       (2.7)

where `u` are the unfixed seed bits. Then

`P(M_S y=0 | p) = 0`

if `B_y p` is not in the image of `R_y`, and otherwise

`P(M_S y=0 | p) = 2^{-rank(R_y)}`.                                         (2.8)

Consequently every conditional shell energy is the exact rational number

`E[Q_w(M_S)|p]
 = (2^n/C_w^2) sum_{y in F_2^{2n}} A_{w,|y|} P(M_Sy=0|p) - 1`.              (2.9)

Equations (2.6)-(2.9) give a completely deterministic finite algorithm using only binomial coefficients and Gaussian elimination over `F_2`. A naive implementation costs `2^{O(n)} poly(n)` exact operations, but **efficiency is not needed for the literal source question**. The method of conditional expectations itself is standard/folklore; I make no novelty claim for that mechanism.

### 2.3 Direct source-level mixing theorem for the global matrix

Let `M_n := M_{s_n}` be the conditionally derandomized matrix and let its `2n` columns be the generator list `S_n`.

Let `Z_k` be nearest-neighbor walk on `F_2^{2n}` started at zero, flipping one uniformly chosen coordinate per step. Then the Wilson walk generated by the columns of `M_n` is exactly

`X_k = M_n Z_k`.                                                           (2.10)

Conditional on `W_k=wt(Z_k)=w`, `Z_k` is uniform on `S_w`. For shell law `f_w=L(M_n Z | Z uniform on S_w)`, the exact collision/chi-square inequality is

`TV(f_w,U_n) <= (1/2) sqrt(Q_w(M_n))`.                                     (2.11)

Fix `epsilon in (0,1]`. Put

`alpha = H_2^{-1}(1/2)`,
`t_* = log(1/(1-2alpha))`,
`alpha_e = [1-(1-2alpha)^(1+epsilon)]/2`,
`delta = (alpha_e-alpha)/2 > 0`.                                           (2.12)

At

`k_0 = ceil((1+epsilon)n t_*)`,

the `2n`-cube weight has expectation

`E W_{k_0} = n[1-(1-1/n)^{k_0}]
            >= n[1-exp(-k_0/n)]
            >= 2n alpha_e`.                                                (2.13)

As in the preceding report, changing one selected coordinate in the `k_0` driving choices changes final weight by at most two. Since `t_*<log 2`, `k_0=O(n)`. McDiarmid therefore gives, for a fixed constant `c_1(epsilon)>0`,

`P(W_{k_0} notin [2n(alpha+delta), 2n(1-alpha-delta)])
 <= 2 exp(-c_1(epsilon)n)`.                                                (2.14)

For every shell in that central interval, binary-entropy monotonicity and the mean-value theorem give a constant `c_2(epsilon)>0` such that

`C_w >= 2^{n+2c_2 n}/(2n+1)`.                                             (2.15)

Combining (2.2), (2.5), and (2.15),

`Q_w(M_n)
 <= 2(2n+1)^2 2^{-2c_2 n}`,                                               (2.16)

hence by (2.11),

`TV(f_w,U_n) <= (2n+1) 2^{-c_2 n}/sqrt(2)`.                                (2.17)

Mix over `W_{k_0}` and use (2.14):

`TV(L(X_{k_0}),U_n)
 <= 2 exp(-c_1 n) + (2n+1)2^{-c_2 n}/sqrt(2)
 -> 0`.                                                                     (2.18)

Total variation to stationarity contracts under further convolution, so the same upper bound applies at every `k>=k_0`. Therefore, for every fixed `epsilon>0`, for all sufficiently large `n`,

`TV(L(X_k),U_n) < epsilon`

for every integer

`k > (1+epsilon)n t_* = (1+epsilon)T_W(n,2n)`.                             (2.19)

Finally, eventual ergodicity follows from the same estimate. If the columns failed to span `F_2^n`, every time law would be supported on a subgroup of size at most `2^{n-1}` and have TV at least `1/2`. If the irreducible walk had period two, word parity would define a nonzero linear functional taking value one on every generator, so each fixed-time law would lie in one half-space and again have TV at least `1/2`. Equation (2.18) is eventually below `1/2`, excluding both obstructions.

Thus the single global conditional-expectation Toeplitz family gives a **candidate complete mathematical answer to the admitted source target**, without the block-product construction. The metric nevertheless remains 0 because the fleet closure protocol requires more than the worker's own proof.

## 3. B11 — dualize: this is exactly fixed-sphere code smoothing

The previous reports used `Q_w(M)` operationally. In coding language its meaning is exact and reveals substantial prior-art overlap.

Assume `M` has full row rank and set

`C = ker M <= F_2^{2n}`.

Then `C` is a rate-1/2 binary linear code and `C^perp = row(M)`. Let `u_C` be uniform on `C`, `u_w` uniform on the Hamming sphere `S_w`, and `u_full` uniform on `F_2^{2n}`. The quotient law of `u_w` modulo `C` is exactly the law of `MZ` for `Z~u_w`, and

`TV(L(MZ),U_n) = TV(u_C * u_w, u_full)`.                                   (3.1)

For a character indexed by `a in F_2^n`,

`E[(-1)^{a.MZ}]
 = K_w(|M^T a|)/C_w`,                                                      (3.2)

where `K_w` is the binary Krawtchouk polynomial in the standard sphere-Fourier normalization. Parseval gives the exact identity

`Q_w(M)
 = sum_{a != 0} [K_w(|M^T a|)/C_w]^2`.                                    (3.3)

Equivalently, if `A_t(C^perp)` is the dual weight distribution,

`Q_w(M)
 = sum_{t=1}^{2n} A_t(C^perp) [K_w(t)/C_w]^2`.                             (3.4)

This is the Renyi-2 / chi-square smoothing energy of fixed-sphere noise on the quotient.

This places E0092 directly inside the framework of Debris-Alazard--Ducas--Resch--Tillich (2022/2023): they study code smoothing with uniform Hamming-sphere noise and show the optimal random-code threshold. At rate `R=1/2`, their Gilbert-Varshamov smoothing radius is

`omega_GV(1/2)=H_2^{-1}(1/2)=alpha`,                                      (3.5)

exactly Wilson's entropy point. Wilson's time constant appears because the `2n`-cube walk reaches relative weight `alpha` when

`[1-exp(-k/n)]/2 = alpha`,

namely

`k/n = log(1/(1-2alpha)) = t_*`.                                           (3.6)

So the analytic threshold is not a new coding phenomenon. It is the dynamic realization of the known rate-1/2 fixed-sphere smoothing threshold.

The exact distinction from the inspected smoothing literature is:

1. Debris-Alazard et al. provide the random/average-code threshold and general worst-case smoothing bounds, not an inspected deterministic `N=2n` generator family answering Diaconis's explicit-set question.
2. Pathegama--Barg advertise explicit families for Bernoulli and **ball** noise; the inspected statements do not give the fixed-sphere family needed here. Ball/Bernoulli smoothing does not imply shell-by-shell smoothing because a radial mixture can be close to uniform while individual shells are not.
3. The global Toeplitz argument above is therefore best viewed as a deterministic derandomization of the known random fixed-sphere smoothing mechanism, followed by Wilson's cube-weight dynamics. The Fourier/Krawtchouk identity itself is prior art/standard and must not be sold as new.

A possible source-interpretation risk also becomes clearer here. If “explicit” were allowed to mean merely “lexicographically first object known to exist by a probabilistic proof,” Diaconis's question would be close to vacuous. The B10 conditional-expectation procedure is materially stronger: it gives a direct deterministic construction algorithm based on exact local choices and a monotone pessimistic estimator, not merely an existential search. It is still exponential-time at full dimension, so publication claims should use “deterministically constructible” rather than “efficient” unless the algorithm is further improved.
