## 4. B25 — COUNT THE SAME THING TWICE

The package now gives the shell energy in two independently normalized forms.

For uniform `Z,Z'` on the Hamming shell `S_w`, with `C_w=binom(2n,w)`, define

\[
Q_w(M)=2^n\Pr(MZ=MZ')-1.
\]

If `p_{M,w}` is the law of `MZ`, collision counting gives

\[
Q_w(M)=2^n\sum_x p_{M,w}(x)^2-1
