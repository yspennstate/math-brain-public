## 3. B20 — try to break the algorithm

I attacked the construction at the four points most likely to invalidate a source-level answer: the block search, the one-block sharp constant, the product-chain time allocation, and ergodicity/periodicity.

### 3.1 Exact projection and shell reduction

Let `M` be the `n x N` binary matrix whose columns are the generators `V_i`. Let `Z_k` be the nearest-neighbor walk on `F_2^N`, starting at zero, that flips a uniformly chosen coordinate at each step. Then pathwise

`X_k = M Z_k`.

By permutation symmetry of the cube walk, conditional on `W_k=wt(Z_k)=w`, `Z_k` is uniform on the Hamming sphere `S_w`. Therefore the projected walk is a mixture of the shell-image laws.

For `z,z'` independent and uniform on `S_w`, define

`Q_w(M) = 2^n P(Mz=Mz') - 1`.

If `f_w` is the probability mass function of `Mz`, Cauchy--Schwarz gives

`TV(M nu_w,U_n) <= (1/2) sqrt(Q_w(M))`.

The Lean file `U_E0092.lean` certifies exactly this finite inequality. Convexity of total variation then gives, for every weight window `[w_0,w_1]`,

`TV(L(X_k),U_n) <= P(W_k notin [w_0,w_1]) + (1/2) max_{w_0<=w<=w_1} sqrt(Q_w(M)).`   (R)

This reduction survived the audit. The existing `check_identity.py` independently compares the direct collision count with the dependency-code formula and checks the full shell mixture on a small instance.

### 3.2 Toeplitz two-universality and deterministic existence

For a block of dimension `b`, length `2b`, use a `b x 2b` Toeplitz matrix

`(M_t)_{r,c}=t_{c-r+b-1}`

with seed `t in F_2^{3b-1}`. For every nonzero `y in F_2^{2b}`, let `c_0` be its first nonzero coordinate. Looking at seed coordinates `t_{c_0-r+b-1}`, `r=0,...,b-1`, produces a triangular `b x b` coefficient matrix with diagonal one. Hence the linear map `t -> M_t y` is onto `F_2^b`. Thus

`P_t(M_t y=0)=2^{-b}` for every `y!=0`.

Consequently

`E_t Q_w(M_t) = (2^b-1)/C(2b,w)`.

Set

`eta_w = 2(2b+1)2^b / C(2b,w)`.

Markov's inequality gives

`P_t(Q_w>eta_w) < 1/[2(2b+1)]`.

Union bounding the `2b+1` possible values of `w` shows that more than half the Toeplitz seeds satisfy `Q_w<=eta_w` simultaneously for every `w`. Therefore the instruction

> enumerate `t in F_2^{3b-1}` lexicographically and take the first seed satisfying all `Q_w<=eta_w`

is a total deterministic algorithm for every block size `b`.

#### Exact-arithmetic repair of the old floating-point description

No floating-point comparison is needed in the definition. For each shell let

`c_{w,x} = #{z in F_2^{2b}: wt(z)=w, M_t z=x}`.

Then

`Q_w = 2^b [sum_x c_{w,x}^2] / C(2b,w)^2 - 1`.

The acceptance condition `Q_w<=eta_w` is **exactly** the integer inequality

`2^b sum_x c_{w,x}^2 - C(2b,w)^2 <= 2(2b+1)2^b C(2b,w)`   (A_w)

for every `w=0,...,2b`.

Thus the family can be defined without numerical tolerances: enumerate finite binary seeds and test finitely many integer inequalities `(A_w)`. The existing `block_search.py` is an implementation prototype, not the mathematical definition.

### 3.3 One-block sharp mixing estimate

Let `N_b=2b`, and let `W_K` be the cube weight after `K` steps. Then exactly

`E W_K = b(1-(1-1/b)^K)`.

Changing one of the `K` chosen coordinates changes `W_K` by at most two, so McDiarmid gives

`P(|W_K-EW_K|>=s) <= 2 exp(-s^2/(2K))`.

For `theta in (0,1]`, define

`alpha_theta = [1-(1-2alpha_*)^(1+theta)]/2`,

`delta_theta = (alpha_theta-alpha_*)/2 > 0`.

If `K >= (1+theta)b t_*`, then `E W_K >= 2b alpha_theta`. Put

`w_0=ceil[2b(alpha_*+delta_theta)]`.

For large enough `b`, the two weight-tail probabilities outside `[w_0,2b-w_0]` are at most `2 exp(-delta_theta^2 b/4)`.

Inside the window, entropy monotonicity and the elementary chord bound used in the memo give

`H(w/(2b)) >= H(alpha_*+delta_theta) >= 1/2 + 2.4 delta_theta`,

while `C(2b,w) >= 2^{2b H(w/(2b))}/(2b+1)`. Therefore a good Toeplitz block obeys

`Q_w <= 2(2b+1)^2 2^{-4.8 delta_theta b}`.

Putting this into `(R)` yields

`TV_b(K) <= 2 exp(-delta_theta^2 b/4) + (2b+1)2^{-2.4 delta_theta b}`

for every `K >= (1+theta)b t_*`. In particular, for fixed `theta>0` this is exponentially small in `b`. The prior numerical checker tested the assembled shell bound against the exact finite chain; the present run's contribution is not another numerical certificate but the adversarial use of this estimate below.

### 3.4 Product decomposition — no time-allocation failure found

Partition the `n` coordinates into block sizes `b_j in {b,b+1}`, `sum_j b_j=n`, and put the `2b_j` columns of the deterministic good block `M_{b_j}` into that coordinate block. There are exactly

`sum_j 2b_j = 2n`

global generators.

If the global walk runs for `k` steps, let `K_j` count steps whose chosen generator belongs to block `j`. Since block `j` has `2b_j` of the `2n` generators,

`K_j ~ Bin(k,b_j/n)` marginally.

Conditional on the whole count vector `(K_j)`, the final states of the disjoint blocks are independent and block `j` has exactly the law of its local walk after `K_j` steps. Hence

`TV(L(X_k),U_n) <= sum_j E[TV_{b_j}(K_j)].`   (P)

The old `check_product.py` checks this inequality exactly on a small three-block example; structurally, `(P)` follows from conditional product form plus `TV(product mu_j, product nu_j) <= sum TV(mu_j,nu_j)`.

Take global `k >= (1+epsilon)n t_*` and set `theta=epsilon/2`. Then

`E K_j >= (1+epsilon)t_* b_j`.

A Chernoff bound gives

`P(K_j < (1+epsilon/2)t_* b_j) <= exp[-epsilon^2 t_* b_j/32]`.

Therefore `(P)` plus the one-block estimate is small after summing over `L<=n/b` blocks, provided `b` is a sufficiently large multiple of `epsilon^{-2} log n` (the actual memo uses the stronger `b >= 8 delta(epsilon/2)^{-2} log n`). I rechecked the allocation direction: the mean has the required `(1+epsilon)` margin and the local threshold uses only `(1+epsilon/2)`, so there is no hidden loss that pushes the constant above Wilson's `t_*`.

### 3.5 Ergodicity and periodicity — a real omitted obligation, but it repairs cleanly

The old theorem says the construction is ergodic but does not isolate a proof. This was the most serious candidate gap in B20.

For a random walk on `F_2^b`, failure to span `F_2^b` means every time-`K` law is supported on a subgroup of size at most `2^{b-1}`. Therefore its total variation distance from uniform is at least `1/2` for every `K`.

Period two has an equally sharp character obstruction: a walk on an elementary abelian 2-group is bipartite iff there is a nonzero linear functional `ell` with

`ell(V_i)=1` for every generator `V_i`.

Then after exactly `K` steps the chain is supported on the affine half-space `ell(x)=K mod 2`, again forcing `TV>=1/2` forever.

But for every fixed `theta>0`, the good-block estimate above tends to zero exponentially in `b`; hence for all sufficiently large `b` it produces some finite `K` with `TV_b(K)<1/2`. This is impossible under either rank deficiency or period two. Thus every sufficiently large good block is both irreducible and aperiodic.

The direct-sum global generator set is then irreducible because each coordinate block is spanned. It is aperiodic as well: a hypothetical global functional equal to one on **all** global generators would restrict on each coordinate block to a functional equal to one on all generators of that block, contradicting local aperiodicity. Hence the eventual global family is ergodic in the exact source sense.

This repairs a genuine presentation gap without changing the construction.

## 4. B27 — quantifier audit: one family before every epsilon

The fixed-`epsilon_0` construction in the old memo is not enough for the admitted target. The key question is whether the proposed growing block size really produces

`there exists (S_n)_n  such that  for every fixed epsilon>0 ...`

rather than

`for every epsilon>0 there exists (S_n^(epsilon))_n ...`.

It does.

Define once and for all, independently of epsilon,

`b(n) = max(2, ceil(log n * log log n))`

for sufficiently large `n` (and patch finitely many smaller `n` by any fixed deterministic exactly-`2n` list). Whenever `n >= b(n)(b(n)+1)`, set `L=floor(n/b(n))` and partition `n` into `L` blocks of sizes `b(n)` and `b(n)+1`; for each of those two dimensions, use the lexicographically first Toeplitz seed passing the exact integer tests `(A_w)` for all shell weights. This specifies `S_n` without mentioning epsilon.

Now fix **afterward** an arbitrary `epsilon>0`. Put `theta=epsilon/2` and `delta=delta_theta>0`. Both are constants independent of `n`. The proof in Section 3 needs only

`b(n) >= [8/delta^2] log n`

and a finite collection of weaker large-`n` inequalities. But

`b(n)/log n ~ log log n -> infinity`.

Hence for this fixed epsilon all those conditions hold for all sufficiently large `n`. The global product estimate then gives, in fact,

`TV(L(X_k),U_n) <= 4/n`

for every integer `k >= (1+epsilon)n t_*` once `n` is large enough. Since `4/n<epsilon` eventually, the admitted source conclusion follows.

The quantifier swap therefore survives: the deterministic family is chosen first, and the threshold `n_0(epsilon)` is the only quantity allowed to depend on epsilon.

A finite-`n` patch is harmless because the source theorem itself is asymptotic. To make the family literally defined for every positive integer `n`, one may use any deterministic spanning list for the finitely many values where the block partition is not invoked; no claim about sharp mixing is needed for those finitely many indices.

## 5. B18 — dependency-code / universal-hash translation

The earlier E0092-A001 phrased the task as requiring an explicit code with a random-like dual-distance profile. The exact reduction is both cleaner and weaker.

Let

`D=ker M <= F_2^{2n}`

be the dependency code. If `B_u=#{y in D: wt(y)=u}`, then for shell radius `w`,

`Q_w(M)=2^n [1/C(2n,w) + sum_{u>=1} B_u p_w(u)/C(2n,u)] - 1`,

where for even `u`

`p_w(u)= C(u,u/2) C(2n-u,w-u/2) C(2n,u)/C(2n,w)^2`,

and `p_w(u)=0` for odd `u`.

Thus Wilson's relevant property is not a large minimum distance by itself. It is an `L^2` smoothing / collision property of the image of each relevant Hamming sphere: in the shell window encountered just after the cutoff threshold, `Q_w` must tend to zero.

The Toeplitz ensemble is exactly a 2-universal hash family at the level needed for the expectation of this collision statistic. Rather than solve the much harder global explicit-code pseudorandomness problem, the construction derandomizes **small blocks** by exhaustive deterministic search, where two-universality proves that a good seed exists. Growing the block size slightly faster than `log n` makes the union of local errors negligible while preserving the global Wilson constant.

This language also explains why the 2023 smoothing-code literature is adjacent but not automatically a published solution of Diaconis's question: Bernoulli-noise or ball-noise smoothing capacity is not by itself the fixed-sphere collision bound at the precise `alpha_*` window needed for Wilson's sharp threshold. The block search directly targets the required shell statistic.

### Theorem (candidate answer to Diaconis Question 1)

For each sufficiently large `n`, let `b=b(n)=max(2,ceil(log n log log n))`. Partition the `n` output coordinates into `L=floor(n/b)` blocks of sizes `b` and `b+1`. For each occurring dimension `d in {b,b+1}`, let `T_d` be the lexicographically first `d x 2d` binary Toeplitz matrix whose shell counts satisfy the exact integer inequalities

`2^d sum_x c_{w,x}^2 - C(2d,w)^2 <= 2(2d+1)2^d C(2d,w)`

for every `w=0,...,2d`.

Embed the `2d` columns of `T_d` in the corresponding coordinate block and concatenate over blocks, obtaining exactly `2n` vectors `S_n` in `F_2^n`. Patch the finitely many remaining small `n` deterministically.

Then for every fixed `epsilon>0`, for all sufficiently large `n`, the walk driven uniformly by `S_n` is ergodic and for every integer

`k >= (1+epsilon)n t_* = (1+epsilon)T_W(n,2n)`

one has

`TV(L(X_k),U_n) <= 4/n < epsilon`.

### Proof dependencies now checked in this run

1. Toeplitz 2-universality implies positive density of block seeds satisfying all shell inequalities.
2. Lexicographic finite search therefore defines each block deterministically and exactly.
3. The shell collision inequality `(R)` converts those inequalities into an exponentially small local mixing error at `(1+theta)b t_*`.
4. Multinomial block-time concentration and the conditional product bound preserve the global Wilson constant.
5. `b(n)/log n -> infinity` verifies the correct `exists family / for every fixed epsilon` order.
6. Eventual local TV `<1/2` rules out both rank deficiency and bipartite period two, proving eventual ergodicity.
7. Convolution contraction of total variation against the uniform stationary law justifies monotonicity for all later times.

No closed-form formula for the Toeplitz seed is used or needed by the admitted source wording. The deterministic construction has quasi-polynomial worst-case time `n^{O(log log n)}`; computational efficiency is not part of Diaconis's question.

## 7. Failed attacks / what did *not* break

- **Rank-deficient block attack:** failed. Rank deficiency would force TV at least `1/2` forever, contradicting the proved local shell bound for large blocks.
- **Period-two attack:** failed for the same reason, via the character criterion `ell(V_i)=1` for all generators.
- **Product-chain time-allocation attack:** failed. Each block receives the correct mean `(1+epsilon)t_* b_j`, and the epsilon/2 slack has exponentially small Chernoff failure.
- **Quantifier attack:** failed. `b(n)=log n log log n` is fixed before epsilon and eventually dominates every epsilon-dependent constant times `log n`.
- **Floating-point explicitness attack:** failed after replacing the implementation test by the exact integer condition `(A_w)`.
- **Fixed bounded block size:** genuinely fails as a route to the sharp global result; the per-block error cannot beat the growing number of blocks. This explains why `b(n)->infinity` is essential.
- **Minimum-distance/GV shortcut:** not sufficient. The relevant statistic is shell collision/smoothing, not merely dual minimum distance.
