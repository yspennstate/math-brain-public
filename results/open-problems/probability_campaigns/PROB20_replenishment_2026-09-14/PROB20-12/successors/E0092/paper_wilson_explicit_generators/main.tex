\title{A Deterministic Toeplitz Construction for Wilson's Random Walks on $\F_2^n$}
\author{Internal source-level proof package for E0092}

\begin{abstract}
Persi Diaconis asked whether the ``almost all choices'' assertion in item 2 of David B. Wilson's theorem on random walks generated by $N$ vectors in $\F_2^n$ can be replaced by an explicit choice, for example when $N=2n$.  This note gives a deterministic finite construction of a labeled list of exactly $2n$ generators.  The construction is independent of the accuracy parameter $\varepsilon$: it fixes the $3n-1$ bits of one $n\times 2n$ binary Toeplitz matrix by the method of conditional expectations applied to simultaneous fixed-Hamming-sphere collision energies.  The resulting family has the same asymptotic upper mixing threshold as Wilson's random family.  The proof also gives all-later-times control and deduces eventual irreducibility and aperiodicity rather than assuming them.

This folder is a closure candidate, not a fleet closure certificate.  The fixed-sphere Fourier/Krawtchouk smoothing mechanism and the rate-$1/2$ entropy threshold are prior art; the source-specific point isolated here is the deterministic derandomization and its assembly with Wilson's dynamics.  Independent P26 and second-track audits, and final source/prior-art adjudication of the word ``explicit'', are still required before the repository metric may move.
\end{abstract}

\section{The source question and the exact target}

Let $V_1,\dots,V_N\in\F_2^n$.  Starting from $0$, at each step choose $I$ uniformly from $\{1,\dots,N\}$ and add $V_I$.  Write $X_k$ for the position at time $k$ and $U_n$ for uniform measure on $\F_2^n$.

Wilson's item-2 threshold, in the notation used by Diaconis, is
\[
 T_W(n,N)=\frac N2\log\!\left(\frac{1}{1-2H_2^{-1}(n/N)}\right),
\]
where $H_2(x)=-x\log_2x-(1-x)\log_2(1-x)$ and $H_2^{-1}$ denotes the inverse on $[0,1/2]$.  For $N=2n$, set
\[
 \alpha:=H_2^{-1}(1/2),\qquad
 t_*:=\log\frac{1}{1-2\alpha},
\]
so that $T_W(n,2n)=nt_*$ (numerically $t_*\approx0.24853$).

Diaconis's Question 1 asks whether an explicit generator choice can replace Wilson's almost-all choice, with $N=2n$ suggested as the model case.  The literal source does not add a polynomial-time or closed-form requirement.  Accordingly, the theorem below uses ``explicit'' in the precise sense of one deterministic finite algorithm whose input is $n$ only.  The algorithm is exponential-time in its naive implementation.  Whether the source authors intended a stronger computational notion is a separate source-interpretation question and is not hidden here.

\begin{theorem}[Candidate source-sufficient theorem]\label{thm:main}
There is one deterministic finite algorithm $A$ such that, on input $n$, $A$ outputs a labeled list
\[
 S_n=(V_1^{(n)},\dots,V_{2n}^{(n)})\in(\F_2^n)^{2n}
\]
with no dependence on $\varepsilon$, and the following holds.  For every fixed $\varepsilon>0$ there is $n_0(\varepsilon)$ such that, for every $n\ge n_0(\varepsilon)$,
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item the walk generated by $S_n$ is irreducible and aperiodic;
\item for every integer $k>(1+\varepsilon)T_W(n,2n)$,
\[
 \TV(\mathcal L(X_k),U_n)<\varepsilon.
\]
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

The list is labeled because the transition chooses one of the $2n$ indices uniformly; duplicate columns, if any, carry their multiplicity.  Eventual ergodicity proved below in fact forces the necessary spanning property.

\section{The Toeplitz family and shell collision energy}

Put $N=2n$.  A binary $n\times N$ Toeplitz matrix is encoded by
\[
 s=(s_q)_{q=-(n-1)}^{N-1}\in\F_2^{3n-1},
 \qquad M(s)_{r,c}=s_{c-r},
\]
for $0\le r\le n-1$ and $0\le c\le N-1$.
For $0\le w\le N$, let
\[
 S_w=\{z\in\F_2^N:\wt(z)=w\},\qquad C_w=|S_w|=\binom Nw.
\]
If $Z,Z'$ are independent uniform points of $S_w$, define
\begin{equation}\label{eq:Qdef}
 Q_w(M):=2^n\Pp(MZ=MZ')-1.
\end{equation}
If $p_{M,w}$ is the law of $MZ$ on $\F_2^n$, then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:Qcollision}
 Q_w(M)=2^n\sum_{x\in\F_2^n}p_{M,w}(x)^2-1
       =2^n\sum_x\bigl(p_{M,w}(x)-2^{-n}\bigr)^2\ge0.
\end{equation}
Consequently Cauchy--Schwarz gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:TVQ}
 \TV(p_{M,w},U_n)\le\frac12\sqrt{Q_w(M)}.
\end{equation}
This inequality is also the small algebraic core formalized in the inherited Lean file listed in the README.

\subsection{Toeplitz universality}

\begin{lemma}[Exact two-universality]\label{lem:universal}
For every nonzero $y\in\F_2^N$, if $s$ is uniform on $\F_2^{3n-1}$, then $M(s)y$ is uniform on $\F_2^n$.  In particular
\[
 \Pp_s(M(s)y=0)=2^{-n}.
\]
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Let $c_*=\max\{c:y_c=1\}$.  For row $r$,
\[
 (M(s)y)_r=s_{c_*-r}+\sum_{c<c_*}y_c s_{c-r}.
\]
Consider the $n$ seed variables $x_r=s_{c_*-r}$, $0\le r<n$.  If another selected variable $x_{r'}$ appears in row $r$ through a term with $c<c_*$, then
$c-r=c_*-r'$, hence $c-c_*=r-r'<0$ and therefore $r<r'$.  Thus the coefficient matrix of $(x_0,\dots,x_{n-1})$ in the $n$ outputs is upper triangular with every diagonal entry equal to one.  The map from the full seed to $M(s)y$ has rank $n$, so the output is uniform.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Expected shell energy]\label{lem:EQ}
For every $0\le w\le N$,
\[
 \E_s Q_w(M(s))=\frac{2^n-1}{C_w}.
\]
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
For independent uniform $Z,Z'\in S_w$, the event $Z=Z'$ has probability $1/C_w$.  On $Z\ne Z'$, the difference $Y=Z+Z'$ is nonzero, and Lemma~\ref{lem:universal} gives conditional collision probability $2^{-n}$.  Hence
\[
 \E_s\Pp(M(s)Z=M(s)Z')
 =\frac1{C_w}+\left(1-\frac1{C_w}\right)2^{-n}.
\]
Insert this into \eqref{eq:Qdef}.
\end{proof}

\section{A canonical deterministic seed}

Define
\begin{equation}\label{eq:eta}
 \eta_w:=\frac{2(N+1)2^n}{C_w}
\end{equation}
and the nonnegative potential
\begin{equation}\label{eq:Phi}
 \Phi(s):=\sum_{w=0}^N\frac{Q_w(M(s))}{\eta_w}.
\end{equation}
By Lemma~\ref{lem:EQ},
\begin{equation}\label{eq:EPhi}
 \E_s\Phi(s)
 =(N+1)\frac{2^n-1}{2(N+1)2^n}
 =\frac{1-2^{-n}}2<\frac12.
\end{equation}

We now specify $A$ without any hidden choices.  Fix the seed bits in the order
\[
 s_{-(n-1)},s_{-(n-2)},\dots,s_{-1},s_0,s_1,\dots,s_{N-1}.
\]
For a fixed prefix $p$, let
\[
 \Psi(p):=\E[\Phi(S)\mid S\text{ extends }p],
\]
where the unfixed seed bits remain independent fair bits.  At the next bit, choose $0$ if $\Psi(p0)\le\Psi(p1)$ and choose $1$ otherwise.  Ties therefore always go to $0$.

To make this an actual finite algorithm rather than a formal conditional-expectation slogan, we give a closed exact formula for $\Psi(p)$.

\begin{lemma}[Exact conditional expectation]\label{lem:conditional}
Fix $y\in\F_2^N$ with $t=\wt(y)$.  The number of ordered pairs $(z,z')\in S_w^2$ satisfying $z+z'=y$ is zero when $t$ is odd, and when $t$ is even it is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:Awt}
 A_{w,t}=\binom{t}{t/2}\binom{N-t}{w-t/2},
\end{equation}
with the convention that an invalid binomial coefficient is zero.

For a seed prefix $p$, write the affine system $M(S)y=0$ in the unfixed seed vector $u$ as
\[
 R_yu=b_y(p).
\]
Then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:affprob}
 \Pp(M(S)y=0\mid p)=
 \begin{cases}
 0,&b_y(p)\notin\operatorname{im}R_y,\\
 2^{-\operatorname{rank}R_y},&b_y(p)\in\operatorname{im}R_y.
 \end{cases}
\end{equation}
Consequently
\begin{equation}\label{eq:condQ}
 \E[Q_w(M(S))\mid p]
 =\frac{2^n}{C_w^2}\sum_{y\in\F_2^N}A_{w,\wt(y)}
   \Pp(M(S)y=0\mid p)-1.
\end{equation}
All quantities in \eqref{eq:condQ} are exact rationals computable by finite sums, binomial coefficients, and Gaussian elimination over $\F_2$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
On $\operatorname{supp}(y)$ the vectors $z$ and $z'=z+y$ are complementary.  If $z$ has $j$ ones there, then $z'$ has $t-j$; off the support they agree.  Equality of their weights forces $j=t/2$, proving \eqref{eq:Awt}.  With a prefix fixed, $M(S)y=0$ is an affine linear system in the remaining uniform bits.  An inconsistent system has probability zero; a consistent system of rank $r$ has exactly a $2^{-r}$ fraction of solutions.  Summing the collision indicator over the difference $y=z+z'$ gives \eqref{eq:condQ}.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Deterministic simultaneous shell control]\label{prop:det}
The bit-by-bit algorithm above terminates after exactly $3n-1$ decisions and outputs a unique seed $s_n$.  Its matrix $M_n:=M(s_n)$ satisfies
\begin{equation}\label{eq:good}
 Q_w(M_n)<\eta_w\qquad(0\le w\le N).
\end{equation}
The family $(M_n)_{n\ge1}$ is deterministic and independent of $\varepsilon$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
At every prefix,
\[
 \Psi(p)=\frac{\Psi(p0)+\Psi(p1)}2,
\]
so the tie-broken minimizing child has conditional expectation at most that of its parent.  Therefore the final seed satisfies
\[
 \Phi(s_n)\le\E\Phi<\frac12.
\]
Each summand $Q_w/\eta_w$ is nonnegative.  In particular $Q_w/\eta_w<1$ for every $w$, proving \eqref{eq:good}.  Lemma~\ref{lem:conditional} shows that every comparison made by the algorithm is an exact finite rational comparison.
\end{proof}

\section{The same shell energy counted twice: collision and Fourier forms}

This section is both a normalization check and a prior-art boundary.  The collision form above is convenient for the Toeplitz conditional-expectation construction.  The Fourier form below is the standard fixed-sphere smoothing language.

Let
\[
 K_w(t)=\sum_{j=0}^w(-1)^j\binom tj\binom{N-t}{w-j}
\]
be the binary Krawtchouk polynomial.  For $a\in\F_2^n$,
\[
 \widehat p_{M,w}(a)
 :=\E_{Z\sim S_w}(-1)^{a\cdot MZ}
 =\frac{K_w(\wt(M^Ta))}{C_w}.
\]
Parseval on $\F_2^n$ gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:fourierQ}
 Q_w(M)
 =\sum_{a\in\F_2^n\setminus\{0\}}
   \left(\frac{K_w(\wt(M^Ta))}{C_w}\right)^2.
\end{equation}
Indeed the right side is $2^n\sum_x(p_{M,w}(x)-2^{-n})^2$, which is \eqref{eq:Qcollision}.

If $M$ has full row rank and $C=\ker M$, then $C^\perp=\operatorname{row}(M)$ and \eqref{eq:fourierQ} becomes
\[
 Q_w(M)=\sum_{t=1}^N A_t(C^\perp)\left(\frac{K_w(t)}{C_w}\right)^2,
\]
where $A_t(C^\perp)$ is the dual weight distribution.  Thus $Q_w$ is exactly the chi-square/Renyi-2 fixed-Hamming-sphere smoothing energy of the quotient.  This Fourier/Krawtchouk mechanism and its random-code entropy threshold are not claimed as new here; they belong to the modern smoothing-code literature, including Debris-Alazard--Ducas--Resch--Tillich.  Pathegama--Barg give explicit smoothing families for Bernoulli and ball noise in the statements inspected for this project, which does not by itself supply the shell-by-shell deterministic Wilson family needed here.

\section{From the deterministic shells to Wilson's sharp time}

Let the $N=2n$ columns of $M_n$ be $V_1^{(n)},\dots,V_N^{(n)}$.  Let $Z_k$ be nearest-neighbor walk on $\F_2^N$ started at zero, obtained by flipping one uniformly chosen coordinate at every step.  Then exactly
\begin{equation}\label{eq:projection}
 X_k=M_nZ_k.
\end{equation}
Conditional on $W_k:=\wt(Z_k)=w$, symmetry makes $Z_k$ uniform on $S_w$.

Fix $\varepsilon>0$.  Define
\[
 \alpha_\varepsilon
 :=\frac{1-(1-2\alpha)^{1+\varepsilon}}2>\alpha,
 \qquad
 \delta:=\frac{\alpha_\varepsilon-\alpha}{2}>0.
\]
Let
\begin{equation}\label{eq:k0}
 k_0=\left\lfloor(1+\varepsilon)nt_*\right\rfloor+1,
\end{equation}
which is the smallest integer strictly larger than the desired threshold.
For every coordinate of the $N$-cube, parity after $k$ updates is one with probability
$[1-(1-2/N)^k]/2=[1-(1-1/n)^k]/2$.  Hence
\begin{align}
 \frac{\E W_{k_0}}N
 &=\frac{1-(1-1/n)^{k_0}}2\\
 &\ge\frac{1-e^{-k_0/n}}2
 >\frac{1-e^{-(1+\varepsilon)t_*}}2
 =\alpha_\varepsilon.
\end{align}
Also $\E W_{k_0}/N\le1/2$.

Changing one coordinate choice in the driving sequence of length $k_0$ changes the final parity vector in at most two coordinates and therefore changes $W_{k_0}$ by at most two.  Set
\[
 \gamma:=\min\left\{\delta,\frac12-\alpha-\delta\right\}>0.
\]
McDiarmid's bounded-differences inequality yields a constant $c_1(\varepsilon)>0$ such that, for all sufficiently large $n$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:weightconc}
 \Pp\!\left(\frac{W_{k_0}}N\notin
 [\alpha+\delta,1-\alpha-\delta]\right)
 \le 2e^{-c_1(\varepsilon)n}.
\end{equation}

For a central shell $w$ in this interval, put $r=w/N$.  The standard type-class lower bound gives
\[
 C_w=\binom Nw\ge\frac{2^{NH_2(r)}}{N+1}.
\]
By symmetry and monotonicity of $H_2$ on $[0,1/2]$,
\[
 H_2(r)\ge H_2(\alpha+\delta)=\frac12+\kappa,
 \qquad \kappa=\kappa(\varepsilon)>0.
\]
Since $N=2n$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:Ccentral}
 C_w\ge\frac{2^{n+2\kappa n}}{2n+1}.
\end{equation}
Proposition~\ref{prop:det}, \eqref{eq:eta}, and \eqref{eq:Ccentral} imply
\begin{equation}\label{eq:Qcentral}
 Q_w(M_n)
 <2(2n+1)^2 2^{-2\kappa n}.
\end{equation}
Therefore, by \eqref{eq:TVQ}, uniformly on all central shells,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:TVcentral}
 \TV(\mathcal L(M_nZ\mid Z\in S_w),U_n)
 \le\frac{2n+1}{\sqrt2}\,2^{-\kappa n}.
\end{equation}

Mixing over the random weight and using convexity of total variation,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:mixk0}
 \TV(\mathcal L(X_{k_0}),U_n)
 \le 2e^{-c_1(\varepsilon)n}
 +\frac{2n+1}{\sqrt2}\,2^{-\kappa n}
 \longrightarrow0.
\end{equation}
Thus for all sufficiently large $n$, the right side is smaller than $\varepsilon$ and also smaller than $1/2$.

\section{All later times and eventual ergodicity}

Let $\mu_n$ be the one-step increment law, uniform on the labeled column list.  Uniform measure on $\F_2^n$ is stationary under convolution with any probability measure, and convolution is a contraction in total variation.  Thus, for $j\ge0$,
\[
 \TV(\mathcal L(X_{k_0+j}),U_n)
 =\TV(\mathcal L(X_{k_0})*\mu_n^{*j},U_n*\mu_n^{*j})
 \le\TV(\mathcal L(X_{k_0}),U_n).
\]
Together with \eqref{eq:k0} and \eqref{eq:mixk0}, this proves the required bound for every integer
$k>(1+\varepsilon)T_W(n,2n)$.

It remains to justify that ergodicity was not smuggled in.  If the columns of $M_n$ fail to span $\F_2^n$, then every time law is supported on a subgroup of size at most $2^{n-1}$, so its total-variation distance from $U_n$ is at least $1/2$.  This contradicts \eqref{eq:mixk0} for all sufficiently large $n$.  Hence the walk is eventually irreducible.

Once irreducible, its period divides two because repeating any generator gives a return in two steps.  If the period were two, all points reachable at a fixed time would lie in one of the two cosets of the even-word subgroup, again a set of size $2^{n-1}$, forcing total variation at least $1/2$.  Equation \eqref{eq:mixk0} again contradicts this.  The walk is therefore eventually aperiodic.  This completes the proof of Theorem~\ref{thm:main}.

\section{What is known, what is candidate-new, and what remains uncertified}

The proof deliberately separates three layers.
\begin{enumerate}
\item The collision-to-TV inequality, Fourier/Krawtchouk representation, and fixed-sphere smoothing viewpoint are standard harmonic-analysis/coding ingredients.  In particular, Debris-Alazard--Ducas--Resch--Tillich study spherically uniform noise and obtain the optimal random-code smoothing threshold; at rate $1/2$ this is the entropy point $H_2^{-1}(1/2)$ appearing above.
\item The method of conditional expectations is standard.  The source-specific construction here is the choice of the simultaneous shell potential \eqref{eq:Phi} over the two-universal Toeplitz family, with exact rational conditional expectations, producing one epsilon-independent labeled $2n$-generator list.
\item The final assembly with the $2n$-cube weight process turns the fixed-sphere bounds into Wilson's exact threshold, handles every later time by convolution contraction, and derives eventual ergodicity from the same TV estimate.
\end{enumerate}

No publication-novelty claim is made by this package.  As of the dated search recorded in the accompanying README/run report, no later source was located that explicitly states the exact deterministic $N=2n$ Wilson-threshold result, but search misses are not novelty certificates.  Likewise, Diaconis's printed question contains no efficiency qualifier, yet an independent source-level audit must still decide whether this deterministic finite algorithm is acceptable under the intended meaning of ``explicit.''
