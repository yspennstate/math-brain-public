## 3. B15 — break the symmetry: compute the exact pair variance of the source Cox law

The previous report knew the abstract correct quantity

\[
v(a)=\mathbb E_a\!\left[\sum_{i\ne j}h(A_i)h(A_j)\right].
\]

Here it can be evaluated exactly for the source's strength-only Cox construction rather than left as an abstract second moment.

Use the **formal child-thinning interpretation encoded by Definition 4.1 and Theorem 5.1**. Let `N` be the potential non-root forward-child count, independent of strength, and put

\[
m:=\mathbb E N=\bar\nu,
\qquad
\beta:=\mathbb E[N(N-1)]<\infty.
\]

Given parent strength `a`, draw potential child strengths `Y_1,...,Y_N` iid from `Gamma(a,dy)`. A potential child of strength `y` is retained as an offspring with probability `p(y)`, independently conditional on the proposed strengths. Define the subprobability kernel

\[
R_a(dy):=p(y)\Gamma(a,dy).
\]

Then the source mean operator is exactly

\[
Kf(a)=m\,R_af.
\tag{3.1}
\]

At criticality let `Kh=h`. Equation (3.1) gives

\[
R_ah=\frac{h(a)}{m}.
\tag{3.2}
\]

Now condition first on `N`. Distinct potential children are iid and their Bernoulli thinning variables are conditionally independent, so

\[
\begin{aligned}
v(a)
&=\mathbb E_a\!\left[\sum_{i\ne j}B_iB_jh(Y_i)h(Y_j)\right]\\
&=\mathbb E[N(N-1)]\,(R_ah)^2\\
&=\boxed{\frac{\beta}{m^2}h(a)^2}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{3.3}
\]

This is exact; no discretisation, Gaussian approximation, or stationarity replacement is used.

Let `pi` be the left Perron probability and normalize `h` by `pi(h)=1`, so the h-spine stationary law is

\[
\varpi_*(da)=h(a)\pi(da).
\]

The Foutel-Rodier / regeneration genealogical variance is therefore

\[
\boxed{
\Sigma_{\rm FR}^2
=\pi(v)
=\frac{\beta}{m^2}\pi(h^2)
=\frac{\beta}{m^2}\int h(a)\,\varpi_*(da).
}
\tag{3.4}
\]

Hence, in the standard unit-variance Aldous-CRT convention used throughout the preceding H0011 calculations, the intrinsic graph-distance coefficient is

\[
\boxed{
\Sigma_{\rm intr}^2
=\frac{\beta}{4m^2}\int h(a)\,\varpi_*(da).
}
\tag{3.5}
\]

For the UGW forward count `N=D^*-1`, when the corresponding derivatives exist,

\[
m=\frac{g''(1)}{g'(1)},
\qquad
\beta=\frac{g'''(1)}{g'(1)},
\]

so (3.5) becomes

\[
\boxed{
\Sigma_{\rm intr}^2
=\frac{g'(1)g'''(1)}{4g''(1)^2}
\int h(a)\,\varpi_*(da).
}
\tag{3.6}
\]

The source's exponential-moment assumption makes the displayed factorial moments finite. In a larger `D x R` formulation where the potential count depends on type, (3.3) must be replaced by the corresponding type-conditioned factorial moment; this report does not silently extend (3.6) beyond the strength-only `\bar\nu` model actually written in Definition 4.1.

**B15 outcome:** success. Breaking the scalar symmetry produces a closed-form source-specific Perron pair variance. This is the cleanest normalization formula recovered on H0011 and is a direct handoff target for a future proof.

## 4. B23 — assume the proposer’s scalar reduction is wrong and identify the exact missing identity

The source Corollary 6.2 says the ordinary offspring count, averaged over the stationary spine strength `varpi_*`, is mean one because `rho(K)=1`. For the formal Cox law above, that implication is not automatic.

Let

\[
q(a):=R_a1=\int p(y)\Gamma(a,dy).
\]

The ordinary number of retained children `C_a` has conditional mean

\[
\mathbb E[C_a]=m q(a).
\tag{4.1}
\]

Because `pi` is the left Perron **probability** and `pi K=pi`, testing against `1` gives the true scalar critical identity

\[
1=\pi(1)=\pi(K1)=m\int q(a)\,\pi(da).
\tag{4.2}
\]

But the source averages over the h-spine stationary law `varpi_*=h pi`, which instead gives

\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathbb E_{\varpi_*}C
&=m\int q(a)h(a)\,\pi(da)\\
&=1+m\,\operatorname{Cov}_{\pi}(h,q),
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.3}
\]

using `pi(h)=1` and (4.2). Thus

\[
\boxed{
\mathbb E_{\varpi_*}C=1
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\operatorname{Cov}_{\pi}(h,q)=0.
}
\tag{4.4}
\]

Neither Theorem 5.1 nor Theorem 6.1 states this covariance-zero identity. The exact finite two-type witness from the preceding report shows that it fails in a perfectly critical irreducible multitype process. Equation (4.4) is the continuous-type version of that diagnosis.

The same issue appears in the variance. Conditional on `a`,

\[
\mathbb E[C_a(C_a-1)]=\beta q(a)^2,
\]

whereas the genealogical variance controlling CRT geometry is (3.4), involving `h(a)^2`, not `q(a)^2`. Therefore an ordinary stationaryly mixed count variance `sigma_off^2` cannot replace `pi(v)` merely from Perron criticality.

There is a plausible **exact OU route to a strict mismatch**: if the two-barrier transmission function and the OU inheritance kernel make both `q(a)` and the positive Perron function `h(a)` strictly increasing, then Chebyshev's covariance inequality under the full-support left Perron law gives `Cov_pi(h,q)>0`, and (4.3) would show the source's claimed stationary-spine count is actually supercritical. The paper does state that the Gaussian forced-response mean increases with `a`, but this run did not recover a source statement proving the needed monotonicity of the exact `p` in the intended child-variable convention. I therefore record this as the sharp next lemma, not as a completed refutation.

**B23 outcome:** success as a diagnosis, not source closure. The precise missing identity is covariance zero in (4.4); future work should prove it from the exact cascade model or, more likely, prove a strict sign and replace the scalar reduction by the Perron/regeneration route.

The actual two-barrier OU transmission mechanism has nondegenerate Gaussian noise and two competing finite boundaries; for an interior starting configuration its hitting probability is expected to lie strictly between zero and one. Therefore I cannot honestly promote the deterministic-ray construction to a source-valid counterexample.
