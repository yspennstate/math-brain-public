## 4. Human-scale proof that the degree-five fibre has exactly two monomials

The exact certificate also enumerates the fibre by a complete meet-in-the-middle search, but this particular fibre admits a small direct proof.

For each color, equation (3.1) prescribes five distinct edges. Label the five edges for color 0 by

`a0=03, a1=23, a2=24, a3=13, a4=25`;

for color 1 by

`b0=15, b1=05, b2=03, b3=24, b4=14`;

and for color 2 by

`c0=24, c1=14, c2=15, c3=05, c4=03`.

A deterministic state in this fibre must choose one prescribed edge of each color and the three chosen edges must partition all six sites. Checking complements gives exactly the following ten compatible triples:

`(a0,b0,c0)`, `(a0,b3,c2)`,

`(a1,b1,c1)`, `(a1,b4,c3)`,

`(a2,b0,c4)`, `(a2,b2,c2)`,

`(a3,b1,c0)`, `(a3,b3,c3)`,

`(a4,b2,c1)`, `(a4,b4,c4)`.

So for every `ai` there are exactly two choices. Let `xi in {0,1}` indicate choosing the second triple in the corresponding displayed pair.

For a degree-five monomial in the fibre, each of the five color-1 edges `b0,...,b4` must be used exactly once. Looking at the ten triples, this gives:

- `b0`: exactly one of `x0=0`, `x2=0`, hence `x0+x2=1`;
- `b1`: exactly one of `x1=0`, `x3=0`, hence `x1+x3=1`;
- `b2`: exactly one of `x2=1`, `x4=0`, hence `x2=x4`;
- `b3`: exactly one of `x0=1`, `x3=1`, hence `x0+x3=1`;
- `b4`: exactly one of `x1=1`, `x4=1`, hence `x1+x4=1`.

Solving these binary equations gives only

`(x0,x1,x2,x3,x4)=(0,0,1,1,1)`

or

`(1,1,0,0,0)`.

For each of these two choices the five `c`-indices are automatically a permutation of `0,...,4`. They are exactly the monomials `P` and `N` above.

Therefore the complete `A`-fibre of this degree-five marginal vector contains exactly two monomials.

### Theorem 4.1 — an indispensable quintic

The quintic binomial associated to `(P,N)` is indispensable in `I_A`.

Indeed, its fibre consists of exactly two relatively-prime monomials. Any path generated by lower-degree binomials would require a third monomial in the same degree-five fibre (multiplying a lower-degree binomial by a common factor), but none exists. Equivalently, neither endpoint admits any cubic or quartic move inside this fibre.

Hence

`I_A` is **not generated in degrees <=4**.                              (4.1)

In particular the 135 cubics plus the 630 indispensable quartics from the predecessor do **not** form a Markov basis.

This is an exact obstruction, not a random-fibre heuristic.

## 7. New exact symmetry character of `K/L3`

Because `S6 x S3` preserves both `K` and `L3`, it acts on

`K/L3 ~= Z/3Z`.

The automorphism group of `Z/3Z` has two elements, so this action is a sign character.

### Theorem 7.1

For `(sigma,tau) in S6 x S3`, the action on `K/L3` is

`[z] -> sgn(sigma) sgn(tau) [z]`.                              (7.1)

#### Proof

Use the predecessor's cube generator built from the three ground pairs

`01, 23, 45`

assigned the three color pairs

`01, 02, 12`,

and orient each pair by one bit. The cube move is the alternating parity sum over the eight orientations.

Swapping the two endpoints `0,1` of the first ground pair flips exactly one orientation bit, so it sends the cube move to its negative **exactly**, not just modulo `L3`. Therefore one ground transposition acts by `-1` on the nonzero quotient. All transpositions of `S6` are conjugate, so the restriction to `S6` is its ordinary sign character.

Now swap colors 0 and 1. The color pair `01` remains `01` but its orientation flips; the color pairs `02` and `12` are exchanged. Swapping the two ground-pair blocks `23` and `45` is the even vertex permutation `(2 4)(3 5)` and restores those two assignments. Thus the combination of this even ground permutation with the color transposition again sends the cube parity sum to its negative. Since the even ground permutation acts trivially on the quotient, the color transposition acts by `-1`. All color transpositions are conjugate, so the restriction to `S3` is also its sign character.

The two group factors commute, yielding (7.1). QED.

The accompanying exact certificate independently checks all five adjacent vertex transpositions and both adjacent color transpositions; each sends the normalized cube class to `2=-1 mod 3`.

## 8. What this changes in the constructive strategy

The discrete repair program has now encountered two successive exact obstructions:

1. Cubics span the real kernel but miss an index-three integer class.
2. Adding all indispensable quartics repairs the lattice issue but still does **not** give a Markov basis; a degree-five move is indispensable.

Therefore a proof of the determinantal union coupling should not assume that a bounded list of the previously found local parity/cube moves connects all nonnegative fibres. At minimum, the move set must include the new quintic orbit.

This does not invalidate the strictly positive Fourier coupling or its certified open feasible neighborhood from the predecessor. It only blocks a proposed route for extending that local solution globally by cubic/quartic fibre navigation.
