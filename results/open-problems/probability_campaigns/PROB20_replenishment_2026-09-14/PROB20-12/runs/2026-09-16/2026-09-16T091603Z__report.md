## 5. NEW exterior lemma: four-space wedge contraction with an annihilating sector

The following is the analytic core of this run.

### Lemma 5.1 — four-space wedge contraction

Let `V` be a complex Hilbert space, let `C<=V` have dimension four, and let `xi in wedge^2 C`. Then for every `gamma in wedge^2 V`,

`|| xi wedge gamma || <= ||xi|| ||gamma||`.                     (5.1)

#### Proof

Write the orthogonal decomposition

`V = O direct-sum C`.

Decompose `gamma` by the number of `O` factors:

`gamma = gamma_CC + gamma_OC + gamma_OO`,

with

`gamma_CC in wedge^2 C`,

`gamma_OC in O wedge C`,

`gamma_OO in wedge^2 O`.

The three outputs

`xi wedge gamma_CC`, `xi wedge gamma_OC`, `xi wedge gamma_OO`

live in mutually orthogonal exterior summands with respectively 0, 1, and 2 `O` factors.

For the `CC` term, `wedge^4 C` is one-dimensional, and the wedge pairing of two two-forms on a four-dimensional Hilbert space is the Hodge pairing. Hence Cauchy-Schwarz gives

`||xi wedge gamma_CC|| <= ||xi|| ||gamma_CC||`.

For the `OC` term, choose an orthonormal basis `(o_a)` of `O` and write

`gamma_OC = sum_a o_a wedge v_a`, `v_a in C`.

The outputs for different `a` are orthogonal, while exterior multiplication by a vector has operator norm equal to that vector norm. Thus

`||xi wedge gamma_OC||^2`

`= sum_a ||v_a wedge xi||^2`

`<= ||xi||^2 sum_a ||v_a||^2`

`= ||xi||^2 ||gamma_OC||^2`.

For the `OO` term, factor an orthonormal basis wedge in `O`; exterior multiplication by a form entirely supported in `O` is an isometry on `wedge^2 C` up to the scalar coefficient. Therefore

`||xi wedge gamma_OO|| <= ||xi|| ||gamma_OO||`.

Adding the three orthogonal squared norms proves (5.1). QED.

### Lemma 5.2 — annihilating-sector gain

Let `alpha` be a unit decomposable two-form spanning a two-plane `W<=V`, let `K=W^perp`, and suppose `D,S <= wedge^2 C` satisfy

`D wedge S = {0}`.

Put

`s=||P_S alpha||^2`.

Then every `beta in wedge^2 K` satisfies

`||P_D beta||^2 <= (1-s)||beta||^2`.                            (5.2)

#### Proof

For `xi in D`, (4.6)-type annihilation gives

`xi wedge alpha = xi wedge (I-P_S)alpha`.

By Lemma 5.1,

`||xi wedge alpha||`

`<= ||xi|| ||(I-P_S)alpha||`

`= sqrt(1-s)||xi||`.                                            (5.3)

Because `alpha` is a unit simple form spanning `W`, exterior multiplication by `alpha` annihilates the `W wedge K` and `wedge^2 W` components and is an isometry on `wedge^2 K`. Consequently

`||xi wedge alpha||=||P_(wedge^2 K) xi||`.                     (5.4)

Thus the operator

`P_(wedge^2 K)|_D : D -> wedge^2 K`

has norm at most `sqrt(1-s)`. Its Hilbert-space adjoint is

`P_D|_(wedge^2 K)`,

which has the same operator norm. This proves (5.2). QED.

The gain over the trivial contraction is exactly the squared mass of `alpha` in a sector that wedges to zero with every source vector in `D`.

## 7. NEW shared-capacity contraction theorem

### Lemma 7.1

`||M||_op <= 1`.                                                (7.1)

#### Proof

Take a unit vector `lambda=(lambda_0,lambda_1) in C^2`. Compute the norm of the adjoint row functional `M^*lambda`.

The two private `E` source blocks are orthogonal. On either one, the coefficient functional is wedge pairing with the complementary `S=star_C(E)` component of `alpha_2`. Therefore their combined squared contribution is

`(|lambda_0|^2+|lambda_1|^2) ||P_S alpha_2||^2 = s`.           (7.2)

The shared `F` source block sees the linear combination

`beta = conjugate(lambda_0) alpha_1 + conjugate(lambda_1) alpha_0`

(up to an irrelevant convention-dependent conjugation). Since `alpha_0,alpha_1` are orthonormal unit vectors in `wedge^2(H2^perp)` and `lambda` is unit,

`||beta||=1`, `beta in wedge^2(H2^perp)`.                      (7.3)

Because `star_C(D)=F`, the wedge coefficient functional on `F` has norm exactly

`||P_D beta||`.                                                 (7.4)

Hence

`||M^*lambda||^2 = s + ||P_D beta||^2`.                        (7.5)

Apply Lemma 5.2 with `alpha=alpha_2`, `K=H2^perp`, and the concrete `D,S` of (4.2)-(4.3). Since `D wedge S=0`,

`||P_D beta||^2 <= 1-s`.                                       (7.6)

Therefore

`||M^*lambda||^2 <= 1`                                         (7.7)

for every unit `lambda`. Thus `||M^*||<=1` and `||M||<=1`. QED.

This is the exact mechanism that lets the two target fibres share the color-2 capacity rather than paying it twice.
