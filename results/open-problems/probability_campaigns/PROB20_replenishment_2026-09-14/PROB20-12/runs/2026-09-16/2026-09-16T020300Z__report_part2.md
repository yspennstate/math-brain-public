## 1. Singleton-block edge case

There is one harmless edge case worth stating explicitly. In the main report, Lemma 4.1 says that every zero-sum block representation `V_b` is assigned to a unique owner color. This is literally true when `|B_b|>=2`. When `|B_b|=1`, however,

`V_b={0}`,

so there is no nonzero representation to assign and no owner is canonically determined by the projections.

For a singleton block, choose an owner **arbitrarily** for the bookkeeping in Theorem 6.1. The lifting rule is unchanged and independent of this choice in substance: `B_b\{Z_b}=emptyset`, so if the quotient color differs from the bookkeeping owner, the owner receives the empty set and the quotient color receives the unique point; if they agree, that color receives the unique point. The one-color sampling formula is exactly the quotient DPP law on that coordinate.

Thus the theorem as a coupling equivalence holds for all nonempty finite blocks. If one wants the representation statement in Lemma 4.1 without a bookkeeping convention, phrase it as: every **nonzero** `V_b` is wholly assigned to a unique color, while singleton blocks have `V_b=0` and may be assigned arbitrarily for the lifting construction.

Also, the opening paragraph of the main report says the exact rational certificate is described in Section 7; the actual diagnostic is Section 8. This is a numbering typo only.
