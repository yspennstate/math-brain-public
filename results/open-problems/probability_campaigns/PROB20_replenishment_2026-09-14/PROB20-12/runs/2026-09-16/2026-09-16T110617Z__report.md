## 8. Determinantal status of the two new families

The two new families are **exact combinatorial facets of the colored-perfect-matching marginal polytope**. That is not yet the same as proving that every orthogonal rank-(2,2,2) determinantal triple satisfies them.

A bounded exploratory search sampled 30,000 complex Haar unitaries and then locally optimized the two representative objectives on `U(6)`. It found no violation:

- for (4.1), the best random value was about `-0.0749` relative to the bound `0`, and local optimization approached `-2.9e-14`;
- for (6.1), the best random value was about `0.95997` against bound `1`, and local optimization approached `1` to numerical precision.

These are **not** proofs, certificates, or independent verification. They only suggest that analytic validity is plausible and that the facets are worth attacking rather than immediately treating them as counterexamples.

If either family can be violated by an exact orthogonal triple, that would give a finite counterexample to Lyons Question 2.8. If both are proved determinantal-valid, they should be added to the validity ledger before continuing catalogue completion.
