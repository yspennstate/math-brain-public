## 3. Mathematical idea before code: an equality foliation for the 1080 facet (B12)

The preceding run isolated an exact equality point `Q_*` for a canonical member of the newly discovered 1080 combinatorial-facet orbit in the six-site rank-(2,2,2) core. The facet functional in edge-probability notation is

\[

-\mu_2(02)-\mu_2(03)-\mu_2(12)-\mu_2(13)-\mu_2(23)-\mu_2(25)-\mu_2(34).

The previous local expansion had Hessian rank six, a nine-dimensional Hessian kernel, vanishing cubic on that kernel, and a nonpositive quartic form. Before attempting still higher formal expansion, I looked for a conserved geometric reason for part of that flatness.

### Proposition 3.1 — exact all-orders equality foliation

Let

\[
S=\{0,1,3,5\},\qquad T=\{2,4\}.
\]

Suppose an orthogonal rank-(2,2,2) decomposition of `R^6` or `C^6` satisfies

\[
H_0\oplus H_1=\operatorname{span}\{e_i:i\in S\},
\qquad
H_2=\operatorname{span}\{e_2,e_4\}.
\]

Then

\[
\boxed{F_{1080}=0.}
\]

#### Proof

Because `H_2` is exactly the coordinate two-plane on `{2,4}`, its projection DPP is deterministic:

\[
X_2=\{2,4\}\quad\text{a.s.}
\]

None of the color-2 negative edges in the canonical facet is `24`, so the entire color-2 contribution is zero.

Both `H_0` and `H_1` are contained in the four-dimensional coordinate space on `S`. Therefore any rank-two DPP edge using coordinate 2 or 4 has zero probability. In particular

\[
\mu_0(45)=0,\qquad \mu_1(23)=\mu_1(24)=0.
\]

Thus the facet reduces to

\[
F_{1080}=\mu_0(01)-\mu_1(35).
\]

Inside the four-dimensional space `ell^2(S)`, the two rank-two spaces are orthogonal complements:

\[
H_1=H_0^\perp\cap \ell^2(S).
\]

For projection DPPs in a finite full coordinate space, orthogonal-complement duality gives

\[
X_{H_1}=S\setminus X_{H_0}
\]

in the canonical complement coupling, hence for every two-set `A subset S`,

\[
\mathbf P^{H_1}(A)=\mathbf P^{H_0}(S\setminus A).
\]

Taking `A={3,5}` yields

\[
\mu_1(35)=\mu_0(01).
\]

Therefore `F_{1080}=0`, as claimed. `square`

This is not a universal proof of the 1080 facet. It identifies a positive-dimensional **exact equality manifold**, explaining why some tangent directions at `Q_*` are flat to every order rather than merely through quartic order.

Its leading principal minors are exactly

all strictly positive. By Sylvester's criterion `Q_1` is positive definite. Hence

\[
\boxed{[t^6]F_{1080}\le0}

on Branch I, with strict negativity whenever `d r != 0` and `(a,b,c)!=(0,0,0)`.

When `r=0` this branch lies in the equality-foliation tangent directions identified above, so complete flatness there is geometrically expected rather than evidence of a hidden positive escape.

### Branch II

Take

\[

Again the coefficients through order five vanish identically. The exact sixth-order coefficient is

\[

with

\[

&+15030b^2e^2-2890bde^2+289d^2e^2.
\end{aligned}
\]

Regard `P_2` as a quadratic in `d`:

\[
P_2=A d^2+B d+C,
\]

where

\[
A=625b^2+289e^2,
\]

\[
B=-2b(3125b^2-1968be+1445e^2),
\]

\[
C=b^2(57241b^2-19680be+15030e^2).

The exact identity

\[

=20b^2(5202000b^4+2606425b^2e^2+451129e^4)

Hence `C>=0`. If `A>0`, the discriminant of `P_2` as a quadratic in `d` is nonpositive and its leading coefficient is positive, so `P_2>=0`. If `A=0`, then `b=e=0` and `P_2=0` directly. Therefore

\[
\boxed{[t^6]F_{1080}\le0}

throughout Branch II as well.
