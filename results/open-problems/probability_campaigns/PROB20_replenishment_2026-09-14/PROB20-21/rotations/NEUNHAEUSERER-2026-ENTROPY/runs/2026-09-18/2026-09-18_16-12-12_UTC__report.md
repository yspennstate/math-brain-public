# 2. B25 — exact Fisher-loss identity for the two-dominant Jensen-Shannon term

The preceding run reduced a dangerous boundary family to a smaller law `Z` plus two top weights `M,M+r`, with `M>sum weights(Z)`. If `P=P_p` is the law of `Z`, let `Q` be its translate by `r`, and let

\[
M_y=\frac{P_y+Q_y}{2}.
\]

Equivalently `M` is the law of `Z+rJ` with `J~Bernoulli(1/2)` independent of `Z`. Define the Jensen-Shannon excess

\[
\Delta(p)=H(M)-H(P)=I(J;Z+rJ).
\]

The previous two-dominant entropy decomposition is

\[
H(Y)=H(Z)+H(\operatorname{Bin}(2,p))+2p(1-p)\Delta(p).
\]

This run identifies exactly what part of `Delta''` is automatically favorable.

For any differentiable finite probability path `R(p)`, write its Fisher information

\[
I(R)=\sum_y \frac{(R_y')^2}{R_y}.
\]

On points where both translated components are present define

\[
\theta_y=\frac{P_y}{P_y+Q_y},\qquad
s_P(y)=\frac{P_y'}{P_y},\qquad
s_Q(y)=\frac{Q_y'}{Q_y}.
\]

Because `Q` is a fixed translate of `P`, `I(Q)=I(P)`. The mixture score is

\[
s_M(y)=\theta_y s_P(y)+(1-\theta_y)s_Q(y).
\]

Applying the conditional-variance identity to the latent fair label gives the exact Fisher loss

\[
\boxed{
I(P)-I(M)
=
\sum_y M_y\,\theta_y(1-\theta_y)
\bigl(s_P(y)-s_Q(y)\bigr)^2
\ge0.
}
\]

This is not merely data processing as a slogan: it is the exact amount of score energy destroyed when the top-bit label is hidden.

Now use

\[
H(R)''=-\sum_y R_y''\log R_y-I(R).
\]

Since translation preserves entropy and Fisher information,

\[
\boxed{
\Delta''
=
\underbrace{
\frac12\sum_y P_y''\log P_y
+\frac12\sum_y Q_y''\log Q_y
-\sum_y M_y''\log M_y
}_{A_{\rm acc}}
+
\underbrace{I(P)-I(M)}_{L_{\rm Fisher}\ge0}.
}
\]

Thus the Fisher part of the Jensen-Shannon curvature can never cause failure. **Any failure of the two-dominant compensation inequality must come from the signed acceleration term `A_acc`, and must overcome an explicit nonnegative Fisher-loss reserve.** This isolates the remaining obstruction much more sharply than the undifferentiated `Delta''` formula.

For a weighted iid Bernoulli subsystem of `m` bits, the inherited score identity from the earlier conditional-rank reduction gives

\[
s_P(y)=\frac{\mu_y-mp}{D},\qquad D=p(1-p),
\]

where `mu_y=E[K|Z=y]` and `K` is Boolean rank. Therefore

\[
\boxed{
L_{\rm Fisher}
=
\frac1{D^2}
\sum_y M_y\theta_y(1-\theta_y)
(\mu_y-\mu_{y-r})^2.
}
\]

This is the requested double count: the same term is simultaneously Fisher information lost by merging the two translated channels and squared separation of conditional Boolean-rank means across the overlap edges. The missing theorem is now an allocation bound for `A_acc` against this rank-separation energy plus the `2/D` binomial reserve.
