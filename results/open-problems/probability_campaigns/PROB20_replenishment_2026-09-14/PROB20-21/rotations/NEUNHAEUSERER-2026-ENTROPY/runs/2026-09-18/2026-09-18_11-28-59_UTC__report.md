# 4. B6 — exact insertion/deformation structure: generic weights are trivial, collision walls are isolated spikes

Let the old weighted Bernoulli sum have positive weights `w_1,...,w_n`, support `S`, and output masses

`Q_s(p) = P(Y_n=s)`, `s in S`.

Insert one new positive weight `d` with an independent `B~Bernoulli(p)`. Then

`Y_{n+1}=Y_n+dB`

has masses

`R_y(p) = (1-p)Q_y(p) + p Q_{y-d}(p)`,                 (4.1)

where `Q_x=0` for `x not in S`.

Define the positive difference set

`Delta^+(S) = {t-s>0 : s,t in S}`.

### Proposition 4.1 — every noncolliding insertion is automatic

If `d not in Delta^+(S)`, then `S` and `S+d` are disjoint. Therefore `B` is determined by the new output and no new merging occurs. Hence

`H_{n+1}(p)=H_n(p)+h(p)`,                               (4.2)

where `h` is binary entropy. Consequently

`H_{n+1}''(p)=H_n''(p)-1/[p(1-p)]<0`                    (4.3)

whenever the old law is concave.

So an induction step is nontrivial **only** when the newly inserted weight equals an exact positive difference of two old support values.

This also changes the meaning of the proposed B6 deformation. For fixed old weights, entropy as a function of the new real weight `d` is not a smooth quantity to be followed continuously: outside the finite set `Delta^+(S)` the two support copies are disjoint and the entropy is exactly the same value `H_n+h`; at a collision wall `d in Delta^+(S)` support atoms coincide and entropy drops. Thus the weight deformation is piecewise constant with isolated downward collision spikes. A continuity argument across a wall is invalid. The mathematically useful version of B6 is to analyze the exact wall itself.

**B6 outcome: SUCCESSFUL REDUCTION / FAILED CONTINUITY ROUTE.** Generic insertion is solved for free; only exact difference walls matter.

# 5. B19 — the collision walls are one-dimensional chain channels

For a fixed colliding shift `d`, form the directed graph on old support `S` with an edge

`s -> s+d`

whenever both endpoints lie in `S`.

Every vertex has indegree at most one and outdegree at most one, and a directed cycle is impossible because every edge strictly increases the real support coordinate. Therefore every connected component is a finite arithmetic-progression chain

`s_0, s_1=s_0+d, ..., s_L=s_0+Ld`.                    (5.1)

On such a chain, writing `q_j=Q_{s_j}(p)`, the new masses are exactly

`r_0=(1-p)q_0`,

`r_j=(1-p)q_j + p q_{j-1}`,  `1<=j<=L`,

`r_{L+1}=p q_L`.                                        (5.2)

Thus insertion is not an arbitrary coarse-graining. It is a disjoint union of tridiagonal two-parent merge channels on one-dimensional chains.

There is an exact entropy identity. The pre-merge joint variable `(Y_n,B)` has entropy `H_n+h`. Given the new output `Y_{n+1}`, knowing `B` recovers `Y_n` exactly. Hence

`H_{n+1}=H_n+h-C_d`,                                    (5.3)

where

`C_d = H(B | Y_{n+1})`.                                 (5.4)

Equivalently, for every overlapped output `y`, set

`x_y=(1-p)Q_y`, `z_y=pQ_{y-d}`,

and define the binary merge loss

`ell(x,z)=(x+z)log(x+z)-x log x-z log z`.                (5.5)

Then

`C_d(p)=sum_{y in S intersect (S+d)} ell(x_y,z_y)`.      (5.6)

Differentiating (5.3), with `D=p(1-p)` and `F_n=-H_n''`, gives the exact induction recurrence

`F_{n+1}=F_n + 1/D + C_d''`.                            (5.7)

Therefore a universal proof can be reduced to a local chain inequality, but that inequality must use the old-law curvature reserve `F_n`; the next section proves that the much simpler bound `C_d'' >= -1/D` is false.

**B19 outcome: SUCCESS.** The recursion is now an explicit finite algorithm: compute old collision classes, form `Delta^+(S)`, discard all noncolliding `d`, decompose each surviving shift into arithmetic chains, and analyze the chain losses (5.5)-(5.7).

# 6. B13 — exact local curvature identity and a rigorous obstruction to naive monotonicity

For one binary merge with positive parent masses `x(p),z(p)`, write

`ell=(x+z)log(x+z)-x log x-z log z`.

A direct differentiation gives

`ell''
 = -x'' log[x/(x+z)]
   - z'' log[z/(x+z)]
   - xz/(x+z) * (x'/x-z'/z)^2`.                         (6.1)

The last term is exactly the Fisher-information loss under the merge and is nonpositive. Thus the raw curvature certificate `F=-H''` is not automatically monotone under collision merging.

A tempting induction would be to prove that every one-bit entropy increment

`I_d(p)=H_{n+1}(p)-H_n(p)=h(p)-C_d(p)`                  (6.2)

is concave. If that were true, old concavity would induct immediately. It is false in the smallest nontrivial colliding example.

Take old weights `(1,2)` and insert `d=1`, giving new weights `(1,1,2)`. The old subset sums are collision-free, so

`H_(1,2)=2h`.

For `(1,1,2)`, the two same-rank pair merges contribute total loss `2p(1-p)log 2`, and the cross-rank merge contributes `p(1-p)h(p)`. Hence

`H_(1,1,2)=3h-2p(1-p)log2-p(1-p)h`,                    (6.3)

and therefore

`I(p)=h-2p(1-p)log2-p(1-p)h`.                           (6.4)

Exact symbolic differentiation at `p=1/3` gives

`I''(1/3)=log 36 - 7/2`.                                (6.5)

This number is rigorously positive without floating point. Indeed

`e < 8/3 + (1/24) sum_{j>=0} 5^{-j} = 87/32 < 11/4`,

and exact rational arithmetic gives

`(11/4)^7 = 19487171/16384 < 1296 = 36^2`.

Therefore `e^7<36^2`, hence `7<2 log36`, proving (6.5) is positive.

So the one-step entropy increment is **locally convex** at `p=1/3` even though both the old and new total entropies are concave. Equivalently,

`C_1''(1/3) < -1/[p(1-p)]`.

# 8. What the next proof must contain

The insertion-chain reduction identifies the precise missing mechanism.

For every old positive-weight law and every colliding shift `d`, a proof must control

`F_n + 1/D + C_d'' >= 0`                                (8.1)

using the fact that `C_d` is assembled from the arithmetic-chain channel (5.2), not from arbitrary binary merges. Section 6 proves that `C_d'' >= -1/D` is too strong and false. Therefore the old curvature `F_n` must participate quantitatively.

The most promising next formulation is to introduce a second Bernoulli parameter `u` only for the newly inserted bit:

`Y(p,u)=Y_n(p)+d B(u)`.

For fixed old law `Q(p)`, entropy is concave in `u` because the new mass vector is affine in `u`. The source step is the diagonal `u=p`; its second derivative is

`d^2/dp^2 H(p,p)=H_pp + 2H_pu + H_uu`.                  (8.2)

Here `H_uu<=0` is automatic. The problem becomes a quantitative bound on the mixed term `H_pu` by the old curvature term `H_pp`. This is a genuinely different route from asking the one-bit increment itself to be concave.
