Idea: view every ambiguous output `y` as an edge between the two possible parents `Z=y` and `Z=y-1`, and pay every negative piece of `C_1''` with the Fisher information lost when those parent states are merged.

The fiber at sum 6 has subset-cardinality multiset `[3,1]`, so conditional rank mean/variance are `(2,1)`. The fiber at sum 7 has multiset `[3,3,3]`, so they are `(3,0)`. The anchor collision at output 7 merges `(B_0=1,Z=6)` with `(B_0=0,Z=7)`. Using the exact score formula from earlier reports,

The nearest fully solved result remains the **Shepp-Olkin entropy concavity theorem**, proved by Hillion and Johnson, *A proof of the Shepp-Olkin entropy concavity conjecture*, arXiv:1503.01570. Their method exploits the one-dimensional count-convolution structure and sign/log-concavity properties of derivatives of the count pmf.
