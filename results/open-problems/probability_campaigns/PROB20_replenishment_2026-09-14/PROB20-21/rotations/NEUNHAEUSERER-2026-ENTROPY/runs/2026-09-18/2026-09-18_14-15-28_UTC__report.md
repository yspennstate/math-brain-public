# 5. B4 — prove something weaker completely: a separated-block theorem at arbitrary depth

The two source-proved extremes are `all weights equal` (binomial) and `all subset sums distinct` (non-overlap). There is a larger elementary class interpolating between them.

### Theorem 5.1 — separated equal-weight blocks

Partition the positive weights into blocks

\[
\underbrace{c_1,\ldots,c_1}_{m_1},
\underbrace{c_2,\ldots,c_2}_{m_2},\ldots,
\underbrace{c_r,\ldots,c_r}_{m_r},
\]

with `0<c_1<...<c_r`, and assume the mixed-radix separation inequalities

\[
c_j>\sum_{\ell<j}m_\ell c_\ell
\qquad (j=2,\ldots,r).
\tag{5.1}
\]

Then the entropy of the weighted iid Bernoulli sum is concave on `[0,1]` for every choice of positive block multiplicities.

### Proof

Let

\[
K_j=\sum_{i\text{ in block }j}B_i\sim {\rm Bin}(m_j,p).
\]

The variables `K_1,...,K_r` are independent. The weighted output is

\[
Y=\sum_{j=1}^r c_jK_j.
\]

I claim that the map `(K_1,...,K_r) -> Y` is injective. If two count vectors differ, let `j` be their largest differing block. Then

\[
|(K_j-K_j')c_j|\ge c_j
>\sum_{\ell<j}m_\ell c_\ell
\ge\left|\sum_{\ell<j}(K_\ell-K_\ell')c_\ell\right|,
\]

so the lower blocks cannot cancel the discrepancy in block `j`. Hence the count vector is recoverable from `Y`.

Entropy is therefore preserved by this injective relabeling:

\[
H(Y)=H(K_1,\ldots,K_r)=\sum_{j=1}^r H({\rm Bin}(m_j,p)).
\tag{5.2}
\]

Each summand is concave by the ordinary binomial/Shepp–Olkin theorem, so their sum is concave. End of proof.

This gives an infinite all-depth family with genuine within-block collisions. It is PARTIAL relative to the universal source and is not claimed novel.

# 7. B15 — break symmetry: an exact Fisher-reserve extremal theorem

The transport identity suggests trying to minimize `I_out` while keeping enough collisions to make the signed rhombus sum positive. The exact score identity from the earlier report gives

\[
I_{\rm out}
=\frac{\operatorname{Var}(\mathbb E[K\mid Y])}{D^2},
\qquad K=\sum_iB_i,\quad D=p(1-p).
\tag{7.1}
\]

Let

\[
W=\sum_iw_i,\qquad Q=\sum_iw_i^2.
\]

Because `Y` is measurable with respect to itself,

\[
\operatorname{Cov}(\mathbb E[K\mid Y],Y)=\operatorname{Cov}(K,Y).
\]

Independence gives

\[
\operatorname{Cov}(K,Y)=D W,
\qquad
\operatorname{Var}(Y)=D Q.
\tag{7.2}
\]

Cauchy–Schwarz therefore yields

\[
\operatorname{Var}(\mathbb E[K\mid Y])
\ge\frac{D^2W^2}{DQ}
=D\frac{W^2}{Q},
\]

hence the exact arbitrary-depth lower bound

\[
\boxed{
I_{\rm out}(p)\ge\frac{W^2}{D Q}.
}
\tag{7.3}
\]

Now let `M=max_i w_i`. If `M>W/2`, then `M>W-M`, so the two support intervals with the maximal bit equal to zero or one are disjoint. The output `Y` therefore recovers that Bernoulli bit and the residual weighted sum, giving exactly

\[
H_n(p)=h(p)+H_{n-1}(p).
\tag{7.4}
\]

Such an insertion cannot be the first failing depth if depth `n-1` is already concave. Thus every **minimal-depth** source counterexample must satisfy

\[
M\le W/2.
\tag{7.5}
\]

But `w_i^2<=Mw_i`, so

\[
Q\le MW\le W^2/2.
\]

Combining with (7.3), every minimal counterexample must obey

\[
\boxed{
I_{\rm out}(p)\ge\frac{2}{p(1-p)}.
}
\tag{7.6}
\]

For positive weights at depth at least three the inequality is in fact strict, though the constant `2` is the sharp infimum: two comparable dominant weights plus an arbitrarily small positive remainder make `W^2/Q` approach `2`.

By (4.1), a counterexample would therefore need

\[
R_w(p)>\frac1{p(1-p)}.
\tag{7.7}
\]

This is a concrete compensation threshold, not merely a qualitative statement that Fisher information is positive.

**B15 outcome: SUCCESS — exact extremal constraint.** Breaking the symmetric weight regime identifies the only way to make Fisher reserve nearly minimal: two comparable dominant weights with the remaining total mass small. But a single dominating weight is automatically safe, so the extremal search is squeezed against a collision boundary rather than toward arbitrary sparsity.

# 8. B23 — assume the conjecture is false: exact two-dominant counterexample architecture

The sharp regime suggested by (7.6) is two large comparable weights plus a smaller collision subsystem. This can be reduced exactly.

Let `Z` be the weighted Bernoulli sum from a smaller positive-weight subsystem with total deterministic weight

\[
S=\sum_{i=1}^m u_i.
\]

Add two weights

\[
M,\qquad M+r,
\]

with `M>S` and `r>=0`. Let `A,B~Bernoulli(p)` be their bits. The output is

\[
Y=Z+MA+(M+r)B.
\tag{8.1}
\]

The bands with `A+B=0`, `A+B=1`, and `A+B=2` are disjoint because `M>S`. Therefore the top-bit count

\[
T=A+B\sim {\rm Bin}(2,p)
\]

is recoverable from `Y`.

Conditioned on `T=0` or `2`, the residual output is just a translate of `Z`. Conditioned on `T=1`, the identity of the selected large bit is a fair Bernoulli variable `J~Bernoulli(1/2)`, independent of `Z`, so the residual law is `Z+rJ`. Consequently

\[
\boxed{
H(Y)=H({\rm Bin}(2,p))+H(Z)+2p(1-p)\Delta_r(p),
}
\tag{8.2}
\]

where

\[
\Delta_r(p)=H(Z+rJ)-H(Z).
\tag{8.3}
\]

This is an exact identity.

It exposes both safe extremes and the only dangerous window:

- If `r=0`, then `Delta_r=0`; the two large equal weights contribute an independent binomial block and the construction is safe whenever `Z` is safe.
- If `r>S`, the shifted copies `Z` and `Z+r` are disjoint, so `Delta_r=log 2`, and (8.2) reduces to `H(Z)+2h(p)`, again safe.
- Only `0<r<=S` can create cross-rank collisions between the two one-large-bit bands.

The same threshold has an extremal meaning: the larger top weight dominates the rest exactly when

\[
M+r>M+S
\iff r>S.
\]

Thus the regime in which the universal dominance argument stops working is **exactly** the regime in which the two middle bands can overlap.

This gives a concrete false-conjecture architecture: any near-Fisher-minimizing counterexample should look, after scaling/projectivization, like two large comparable weights whose difference `r` is represented by a subset-sum difference of a much smaller subsystem. The entire dangerous curvature is then concentrated into the Jensen–Shannon-type excess `2D Delta_r(p)` in (8.2). Proving the source in this extremal regime is reduced to controlling that one term; refuting it requires finding a small subsystem and shift for which its curvature overwhelms the already-negative `H(Bin(2,p))+H(Z)` reserve.
