## 3. Frozen enumeration convention: the source family \(S_5\)

The source compression theorem gives a much smaller exhaustive family than all \(2^{32}\) truth tables.

For every coordinate pair \(i<j\), with all other coordinates fixed, a pairwise-compressed zero-set has a section that is an initial lexicographic segment in
\[
00<01<10<11.
\]
Equivalently, membership in a section obeys
\[
01\Rightarrow 00,\qquad 10\Rightarrow 01,\qquad 11\Rightarrow 10.
\tag{3.1}
\]
Taking all such implications over all ten coordinate pairs defines a finite poset on the 32 vertices of the five-cube; the compressed sets are exactly its order ideals.

The main C++ checker enumerates those ideals exactly and obtains **119**, agreeing with Courtade–Kumar Table I. An independent Python enumerator, written separately from the interval checker, also obtains 119 and directly rechecks every pair-section. Its sorted mask list has SHA-256
\[
\texttt{3deb4806a8975055ee9bff0ff4c91918f403c4940bc512c89a9fe06644f46cf7}.
\]

Among the 119 compressed sets:

- 2 are constant;
- 1 is a dictator in the frozen orientation;
- 116 are nontrivial nondictators;
- 6 of those are balanced and 110 biased.

Our masks can be viewed as zero-sets or as indicator-one sets: output complementation preserves mutual information, so the interval check is equivalent.

By Courtade–Kumar Theorem 2 and Corollary 1, for every five-bit Boolean function and every fixed \(\alpha\), repeated pair-compression preserves output cardinality and never decreases the mutual information, terminating in this 119-element family. Therefore proving (1.1) for all 119 functions for every \(\alpha\in[0,1/2]\) proves it for **all \(2^{32}\) five-bit truth tables**.

## 4. Exact posterior transition polynomials

Use natural logarithms; signs are unchanged when converting to bits. For a compressed set \(B\subseteq\{0,1\}^5\), let
\[
p=\frac{|B|}{32}.
\]
For each noisy output \(y\), define
\[
q_y(\alpha)=\Pr(X\in B\mid Y=y).
\]
Because the BSC is doubly stochastic and \(X\) is uniform,
\[
q_y(\alpha)=\sum_{x\in B}\alpha^{d(x,y)}(1-\alpha)^{5-d(x,y)}.
\tag{4.1}
\]
If
\[
c_{y,d}=|\{x\in B:d(x,y)=d\}|,
\]
then, with \(B_{d,5}(\alpha)=\binom5d\alpha^d(1-\alpha)^{5-d}\),
\[
\boxed{q_y(\alpha)=\sum_{d=0}^{5}\frac{c_{y,d}}{\binom5d}B_{d,5}(\alpha).}
\tag{4.2}
\]
Thus every posterior is an exact degree-five Bernstein polynomial with rational coefficients.

Let
\[
h(t)=-t\ln t-(1-t)\ln(1-t).
\]
The target gap is
\[
\boxed{D_B(\alpha)=\ln2-h(\alpha)-h(p)+\frac1{32}\sum_y h(q_y(\alpha)).}
\tag{4.3}
\]
Equation (1.1) is exactly \(D_B(\alpha)\ge0\).

Constants are trivial, dictators are equality for every \(\alpha\), and the endpoints \(\alpha=0,1/2\) are immediate. It remains to certify the 116 nontrivial compressed representatives on the open interval.

## 6. Low-noise interval \([0,1/8192]\)

### 6.1 Biased functions

After output complementation, every nonconstant biased five-bit function has
\[
p\le\frac{15}{32}.
\]
Hence
\[
I(B;Y)\le H(B)=h(p)\le h(15/32).
\]
Capacity decreases in \(\alpha\), so on \(0\le\alpha\le1/8192\),
\[
\ln2-h(\alpha)\ge \ln2-h(1/8192).
\]
Directed MPFR rounding certifies
\[
\boxed{\ln2-h(1/8192)-h(15/32)>0.00073237068691549994>0.}
\tag{6.1}
\]

### 6.2 Balanced nondictators

For balanced \(B\),
\[
D_B(\alpha)=H(B(X)\mid Y)-h(\alpha).
\]
For each \(y\), let \(s_y\in\{0,\dots,5\}\) be its local sensitivity and put
\[
t=\alpha(1-\alpha)^4.
\]
If \(e_y\) is posterior error relative to \(B(y)\), every opposite-valued Hamming neighbor contributes \(t\), so
\[
e_y\ge s_yt.
\tag{6.2}
\]
Also
\[
e_y\le1-(1-\alpha)^5<\tfrac12
\]
throughout this low-noise interval. Since \(h\) is increasing on \([0,1/2]\) and \(h(x)\ge x\ln(1/x)\),
\[
H(B\mid Y)\ge \frac1{32}\sum_y h(s_yt)\ge t\left[\bar s\ln\frac1t-\overline{s\ln s}\right].
\tag{6.3}
\]
As \(s_y\le5\),
\[
\overline{s\ln s}\le \bar s\ln5.
\]
The exact enumeration proves that among the six balanced compressed nondictators,
\[
\boxed{\bar s\ge\frac{11}{8},}
\tag{6.4}
\]
with equality at mask `0x15555557`. Therefore
\[
H(B\mid Y)\ge \frac{11}{8}t\ln\frac1{5t}.
\tag{6.5}
\]
Using
\[
h(\alpha)\le\alpha\left(\ln\frac1\alpha+1\right)
\]
and \(c=(1-\alpha)^4\ge c_0=(8191/8192)^4\), after division by \(\alpha>0\) the gap is bounded below by
\[
\left(\frac{11}{8}c_0-1\right)\ln8192-\frac{11}{8}\ln5-1.
\tag{6.6}
\]
Directed MPFR rounding certifies
\[
\boxed{\left(\frac{11}{8}c_0-1\right)\ln8192-\frac{11}{8}\ln5-1>0.16006667575037495>0.}
\tag{6.7}
\]

Thus every compressed representative is certified on \([0,1/8192]\).

## 7. High-noise interval \([63/128,1/2]\)

Switch to the \(\{-1,1\}\)-valued representation \(f=1-2B\) and put
\[
\rho=1-2\alpha,\qquad 0\le\rho\le\rho_0:=\frac1{64}.
\]
Write
\[
f=\sum_S\widehat f(S)\chi_S,\qquad \mu=\widehat f(\varnothing),\qquad V=1-\mu^2,
\]
and define
\[
W_1=\sum_{|S|=1}\widehat f(S)^2,\qquad \Delta=V-W_1,\qquad L_1=\sum_{S\ne\varnothing}|\widehat f(S)|.
\]
For every nonconstant nondictator in the frozen family, exact rational enumeration gives
\[
\boxed{\Delta\ge\frac{13}{128}>0,}
\tag{7.1}
\]
with the minimum at mask `0x1`.

Let \(g=T_\rho f=\mu+z\) and
\[
\psi(x)=\ln2-h\!\left(\frac{1+x}{2}\right).
\]
Then
\[
I_{\rm nat}(f(X);Y)=\mathbb E\psi(\mu+z)-\psi(\mu).
\tag{7.2}
\]
Moreover
\[
\psi''(x)=\frac1{1-x^2},\qquad \psi'''(x)=\frac{2x}{(1-x^2)^2}.
\]
For each representative, define
\[
R=|\mu|+\rho_0L_1.
\]
The exact scan verifies \(R<1\). Thus
\[
M_3:=\sup_{|x|\le R}|\psi'''(x)|=\frac{2R}{(1-R^2)^2}.
\]
By Parseval,
\[
\mathbb Ez^2=\sum_{S\ne\varnothing}\rho^{2|S|}\widehat f(S)^2\le \rho^2W_1+\rho^4\Delta=\rho^2[V-(1-\rho^2)\Delta].
\tag{7.3}
\]
Also
\[
\|z\|_\infty\le\rho L_1,\qquad \mathbb E|z|^3\le \|z\|_\infty\mathbb Ez^2\le \rho^3L_1V.
\tag{7.4}
\]
Taylor's theorem at \(\mu\), using \(\mathbb Ez=0\), yields
\[
I_{\rm nat}\le\frac{\mathbb Ez^2}{2V}+\frac{M_3}{6}\mathbb E|z|^3.
\tag{7.5}
\]
The BSC capacity satisfies
\[
\psi(\rho)=\sum_{m\ge1}\frac{\rho^{2m}}{2m(2m-1)}\ge\frac{\rho^2}{2}.
\tag{7.6}
\]
Combining (7.3)--(7.6),
\[
\frac{\psi(\rho)-I_{\rm nat}}{\rho^2}\ge\frac{(1-\rho^2)\Delta}{2V}-\frac{M_3L_1V\rho}{6}.
\tag{7.7}
\]
The right side is bounded uniformly below on \(0\le\rho\le\rho_0\) by replacing \(\rho\) and \(M_3\) with their endpoint upper bounds. Exact rational evaluation over all 116 representatives gives the minimum
\[
\boxed{\frac{16423678341965}{332604682539008}=0.049378975114214824\ldots>0,}
\tag{7.8}
\]
attained by mask `0x15555555`.

Therefore the full high-noise interval is certified.

## 8. Middle interval \([1/8192,63/128]\)

This is the interval-computation core. It is not a noise grid.

For every posterior Bernstein polynomial (4.2), exact de Casteljau subdivision restricts it to a rational subinterval \([a,b]\). On that subinterval the minimum and maximum Bernstein coefficients enclose the exact posterior range:
\[
q_y([a,b])\subseteq[\underline q_y,\overline q_y].
\]
Binary entropy is concave and symmetric around \(1/2\), so its minimum over this range occurs at the endpoint farther from \(1/2\). Therefore a rigorous lower bound for the gap on the entire interval is
\[
\underline D_B([a,b])=\underline{\ln2}-\overline{h(b)}-\overline{h(p)}+\frac1{32}\sum_y\underline{\min\{h(\underline q_y),h(\overline q_y)\}}.
\tag{8.1}
\]
Every logarithm is evaluated with 256-bit MPFR outward rounding.

If (8.1) is positive, the whole interval is certified. Otherwise the checker bisects exactly at the rational midpoint and repeats. It halts only after every leaf interval has a strictly positive directed lower bound.

Across all 116 nontrivial compressed functions:

- subdivision nodes: **17,782**;
- certified leaves: **8,949**;
- maximum depth: **14**;
- uncertified leaves: **0**;
- smallest strictly positive interval lower bound:
  \[
  \boxed{5.1083634992863697\times10^{-8}\ {\rm nats},}
  \]
  with worst representative mask `0x15555557`.

Thus the full middle continuum is certified.
