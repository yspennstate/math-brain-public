# 2. Exact posterior collapse to four tail classes

Write

\[
a=\alpha,\qquad b=1-a.
\]

For the tail observation `Y'=(Y_1,...,Y_6)`, let `K` be its Hamming weight. Uniform-prior BSC reversibility gives

\[
S_k:=\Pr(V=\mathbf1\mid Y',K=k)=b^k a^{6-k},
\]
\[
T_k:=\Pr(V=\mathbf0\mid Y',K=k)=a^k b^{6-k}.
\tag{2.1}
\]

Conditioned on `Y_0=0`,

\[
\Pr(U=1\mid Y_0=0)=b,
\]

whereas conditioned on `Y_0=1`, that posterior is `a`. Using (1.1), the two conditional output posteriors are therefore

\[
\boxed{
q_{+,k}=b(1-S_k)+aT_k,
}
\tag{2.2}
\]

and

\[
\boxed{
q_{-,k}=a(1-S_k)+bT_k.
}
\tag{2.3}
\]

The function is balanced. Since `Y_0` is uniform and independent of the tail and `K~Bin(6,1/2)`, its Courtade–Kumar defect in natural logarithms is

\[
\mathcal D_f(a)
=H(f\mid Y)-h(a)
=2^{-6}\sum_{k=0}^6\binom6k\Phi_k(a),
\tag{2.4}
\]

where

\[
\boxed{
\Phi_k(a)
:=\frac{h(q_{+,k})+h(q_{-,k})}{2}-h(a).
}
\tag{2.5}
\]

Now

\[
S_{6-k}=T_k,
\]

and direct substitution gives

\[
q_{+,6-k}=1-q_{-,k},\qquad
q_{-,6-k}=1-q_{+,k}.
\]

Binary entropy is complement-symmetric, hence

\[
\boxed{\Phi_{6-k}(a)=\Phi_k(a).}
\tag{2.6}
\]

Thus the seven-dimensional posterior problem has exactly four inequivalent tail classes `k=0,1,2,3`.
