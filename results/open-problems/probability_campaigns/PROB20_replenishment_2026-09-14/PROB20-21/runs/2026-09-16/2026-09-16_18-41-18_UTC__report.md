# 2. B5 — differentiate the **full-vector** information defect

This section introduces a new analytic variable rather than returning to the previous B11 moment expansion.

Use `{-1,+1}` coding and natural logarithms. Define

\[
\psi(t)=\log 2-h\!\left(\frac{1+t}{2}\right),
\qquad
\mu=\mathbb E f,
\qquad
g_\rho=T_\rho f.
\]

Then exactly

\[
I(f(X);Y)=\mathbb E\psi(g_\rho(Y))-\psi(\mu),
\]

and the Courtade–Kumar defect is

\[
\mathcal D_f(\rho)
:=\psi(\rho)+\psi(\mu)-\mathbb E\psi(g_\rho).
\tag{2.1}
\]

Thus the source conjecture is `D_f(rho)>=0` for every Boolean `f` and every `0<=rho<=1`.

Let the Boolean number operator be

\[
N\chi_S=|S|\chi_S.
\]

Because

\[
\partial_\rho T_\rho f=\frac1\rho N T_\rho f
\]

for `rho>0`, and

\[
\psi'(t)=\operatorname{atanh}(t),
\]

we obtain the exact derivative identity

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal D_f'(\rho)
=
\operatorname{atanh}\rho
-
\frac1\rho\,
\mathbb E\!\left[
\operatorname{atanh}(g_\rho)\,N g_\rho
\right].
}
\tag{2.2}
\]

This still concerns the original **full vector-output** mutual information; no coordinate-wise surrogate has been substituted.

## 2.1 Exact edge/Dirichlet form

Take the cube generator

\[
L u(x)=\frac12\sum_{i=1}^n[u(x^{\oplus i})-u(x)],
\]

so `N=-L`. Uniform-cube integration by parts gives, for any scalar `phi`,

\[
\mathbb E[\phi(g)Ng]
=
\frac14\sum_{i=1}^n
\mathbb E[(\phi(g(Y))-\phi(g(Y^{\oplus i})))(g(Y)-g(Y^{\oplus i}))].
\tag{2.3}
\]

For a fixed coordinate write

\[
g(y)=a_i(y_{-i})+b_i(y_{-i})y_i.
\]

Substituting `phi=atanh` into (2.2)–(2.3) yields

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal D_f'(\rho)
=
\operatorname{atanh}\rho
-
\frac1{2\rho}\sum_i
\mathbb E_{Y_{-i}}
\left[
b_i\bigl(
\operatorname{atanh}(a_i+b_i)-
\operatorname{atanh}(a_i-b_i)
\bigr)\right].
}
\tag{2.4}
\]

Equivalently,

\[
\operatorname{atanh}(a+b)-\operatorname{atanh}(a-b)
=2b\int_0^1\frac{ds}{1-(a-b+2bs)^2},
\]

so

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal D_f'(\rho)
=
\operatorname{atanh}\rho
-
\frac1\rho\sum_i
\mathbb E_{Y_{-i}}
\left[
b_i^2\int_0^1
\frac{ds}{1-(a_i-b_i+2b_i s)^2}
\right].
}
\tag{2.5}
\]

This identity converts the full information problem into a comparison between the one-dimensional dictator edge energy and a nonlinear Dirichlet energy of the noisy Boolean function.

**Status:** PROVED by differentiation and exact cube integration by parts.

# 3. B20 — why the obvious derivative algorithm fails

The most tempting use of (2.5) would be to prove

\[

for every Boolean function, because `D_f(0)=0`. This is false even for the exact nearest `n=7` compressed competitor to dictatorship.

\[

\qquad \varepsilon=2^{1-n}=\frac1{64}.
\tag{3.1}
\]

Its noisy posterior is

\[
T_\rho f(y)
=
\rho y_1-arepsilon\prod_{j=1}^7(1+\rho y_j).
\tag{3.2}

If

\[
R_k=(1+\rho)^k(1-\rho)^{6-k},

\[
g_+(k)=\rho-\varepsilon(1+\rho)R_k,

 g_-(k)=-\rho-\varepsilon(1-\rho)R_k,

## 4.1 Low-noise theorem: `alpha in [0,2^-17]`

### Biased Boolean functions

The data-processing/trivial entropy bound gives

\[
I(f(X);Y)\le H(f(X))=h(p).
\]

Therefore the Courtade–Kumar defect satisfies

\[
[\log2-h(\alpha)]-I(f(X);Y)
\ge
\log2-h(\alpha)-h(63/128).
\tag{4.1}
\]

Binary entropy is increasing on `[0,1/2]`. Exact rational logarithm enclosures at `alpha_0` give

\[
\boxed{
\log2-h(\alpha_0)-h(63/128)
>2.4544928363351194\times10^{-5}\ \text{nats}.
}
\tag{4.2}
\]

Hence every biased seven-bit Boolean function is certified for the full interval `[0,alpha_0]`.

### Balanced nondictators

Let `d_y` be the number of sensitive cube edges incident to `y`. Condition on `Y=y`, and set

\[
e_y=\Pr(f(X)\ne f(y)\mid Y=y).
\]

For `alpha<=alpha_0`,

\[
e_y\le\Pr(X\ne y\mid Y=y)\le7\alpha<\frac12.
\]

The disjoint event "exactly one coordinate flips, it is a sensitive edge, and the other six coordinates do not flip" gives

\[
e_y\ge d_y\alpha(1-\alpha)^6.
\tag{4.3}
\]

Put

\[
c=(1-\alpha)^6,
\qquad L=\log(1/\alpha).
\]

Since binary entropy is increasing on `[0,1/2]` and

\[
h(z)\ge z\log(1/z),
\]

we obtain

\[
H(f(X)\mid Y)
=\mathbb E_y h(e_y)
\ge
\alpha c\left[
\operatorname{AS}(f)L-
\mathbb E_y(d_y\log d_y)
\right].
\tag{4.4}
\]

Here `AS(f)=E d_y` is average sensitivity, with the convention `0 log 0=0`. Also

\[
h(\alpha)\le\alpha(L+1).
\]

For balanced `f`, the Courtade–Kumar defect is `H(f|Y)-h(alpha)`, so

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal D_f
\ge
\alpha\left[
(c\operatorname{AS}(f)-1)L
-c\mathbb E(d_y\log d_y)-1
\right].
}
\tag{4.5}
\]

Let

\[
c_0=(1-2^{-17})^6,
\qquad L_0=17\log2.
\]

For `alpha<=alpha_0`, `c>=c_0` and `L>=L_0`. Moreover `L_0>\log7`, hence

\[
\operatorname{AS}(f)L-\mathbb E(d_y\log d_y)
\ge \operatorname{AS}(f)(L-\log7)>0,

so the bracket in (4.5) is increasing in `c`. The exact enumeration also verifies `c_0 AS(f)-1>0`, so it is increasing in `L`. It therefore suffices to check `(c_0,L_0)`.

The exact finite computation covers all `1006` balanced compressed nondictators and gives

## 4.2 High-noise theorem: `alpha in [67/135,1/2]`

This subsection re-derives the general endpoint lemma used by the previous `n=6` certificate, so the `n=7` application does not rely on an unchecked dimensional substitution.

Use `{-1,+1}` coding. Let

\[
\mu=\mathbb Ef,
\quad V=1-\mu^2,
\quad W_1=\sum_i\widehat f(\{i\})^2,
\quad \Delta=V-W_1,
\]

and write

\[
g=T_\rho f=\mu+r.
\]

There are

\[
M=2^n-1
\]

nonconstant Walsh characters. Cauchy–Schwarz gives

\[
\|r\|_\infty
\le
q:=\rho\sqrt{MV},
\tag{4.8}
\]

while Parseval gives

\[
\mathbb E r^2
\le
\rho^2W_1+\rho^4\Delta.
\tag{4.9}
\]

Taylor's formula for `psi`, using

\[
\psi''(t)=\frac1{1-t^2},
\]

gives, whenever `|mu|+q<1`,

\[
\mathbb E\psi(\mu+r)-\psi(\mu)
\le
\frac{\mathbb E r^2}{2[1-(|\mu|+q)^2]}.
\tag{4.10}
\]

Also the positive Taylor series for `psi` gives

\[
\psi(\rho)\ge\frac{\rho^2}{2}.
\tag{4.11}
\]

Combining (4.8)–(4.11), a sufficient condition for the Courtade–Kumar defect to be nonnegative is

\[
(1-\rho^2)\Delta
\ge
2|\mu|q+q^2.
\tag{4.12}
\]

Indeed, (4.12) itself implies `|mu|+q<1` strictly when strict, because `Delta<=V=1-mu^2`.

Define

\[
L(\rho)=(1-\rho^2)\Delta-M\rho^2V.
\]

Since `q=rho sqrt(MV)`, it is enough that

\[
\boxed{L(\rho)>0}
\tag{4.13}
\]

and

\[
\boxed{L(\rho)^2-4\mu^2\rho^2MV>0.}
\tag{4.14}
\]

Both left-hand margins become weaker as `rho` increases: `L` decreases with `rho`, while the subtracted term in (4.14) increases. Hence endpoint verification at `rho_0` certifies all `0<=rho<=rho_0`.

For `n=7`, take

\[
M=127,
\qquad
\rho_0=\frac1{135},
\qquad
\alpha_0^{\mathrm{high}}=\frac{1-\rho_0}{2}=\frac{67}{135}.
\]

The exact `S_7` checker verifies every nonconstant nondictator and obtains

\[
\boxed{\min\Delta=\frac{15}{512},}
\tag{4.15}
\]

\[
\boxed{
\min L(1/135)=\frac{111121}{4976640}>0,
}
\tag{4.16}
\]

with the latter attained at the one-point dictator perturbation mask

`15555555555555555555555555555555`,

and

\[
\boxed{
\min\left[L^2-4\mu^2\rho_0^2MV\right]
=
\frac{45393431101}{5572562780160000}>0,
}
\tag{4.17}
\]

attained by the singleton mask

`00000000000000000000000000000001`.

Thus **every seven-bit Boolean function is certified for**

\boxed{\frac{67}{135}\le\alpha\le\frac12.}
