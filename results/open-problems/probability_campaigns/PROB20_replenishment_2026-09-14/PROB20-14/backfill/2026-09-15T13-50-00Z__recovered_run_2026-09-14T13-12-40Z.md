N=\sum_{j=0}^{k-1}Z_j,\qquad L=Z_k,\qquad m=n-N.

\tag{B2}

## Stable-domain partial theorem
If the critical offspring law lies in the domain of attraction of a nondegenerate stable law (including the infinite-variance normal domain), then the lattice local limit theorem supplies a central lower bound and uniform local upper bound at the natural norming scale. Substituting these in (B1) gives \(W_n\lesssim L\) whenever \(m\ge\delta n\). Since \(\mathbb EZ_k^q<\infty\), this gives the required UI contribution on that region. If \(m<\delta n\), completion forces an order-\(n\) generation followed within the fixed number of levels by a factor-two downward jump; a fixed negative tilt gives probability \(e^{-cn}\), which stays negligible after conditioning. Hence UI holds for every fixed \(k\) throughout stable/normal domains of attraction. This is a partial theorem only; it does not settle arbitrary infinite-variance laws.
