## 2. Fast exact coarse formula used for K=5

`artifacts/2026-09-15T165500Z__k4_coarse_audit.py`

For K=5, the same exact quantity can be evaluated more efficiently by starting from the all-far baseline and adding corrections only for triples containing a close edge.

If there are `a` even and `b=12-a` odd centers, the all-far baseline is

\[
S_{1,E}=67a+232b,\qquad S_{1,O}=232a+67b,
\]

\[
S_{2,E}=40\binom b2+10ab,\qquad
S_{2,O}=40\binom a2+10ab,
\]

and the exact all-far triple-max contributions are

\[
S_{3,E}=a\binom b2+4\binom b3,
\qquad
S_{3,O}=4\binom a3+\binom a2b.
\]

These formulas use the exact far-triple maxima

```text
EEE -> (0,4)
EEO -> (0,1)
EOO -> (1,0)
OOO -> (4,0)
```

on `(even half, odd half)`. Every close edge then contributes its exact pair correction, and every active triple containing at least one close edge contributes the exact difference between its parity/distance triple maximum and the far baseline. Triples containing a close edge plus an isolated center are aggregated by the number and parity of isolates.

As a regression test, this fast formula was applied to all eleven archived K=4 graph shapes and reproduced **every** raw/canonical count of the direct audit exactly, including the total `486 raw / 74 canonical`.
