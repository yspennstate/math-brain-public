For each parity half `H`, any cover satisfies

\[

\]

where `d_H` is the sum of exact triple-max deficits and

\[
E_H=\sum_{x:m(x)>0}\binom{m(x)-1}{3}\ge0

is the higher-multiplicity Bonferroni excess. Therefore when `U=(1024,1024)`, a genuine cover must have

\[

Equivalently, every triple must attain its exact parity-half maximum, and no quotient point may lie in four of the chosen folded balls. This condition is monotone under adding centers: once a partial configuration acquires a positive triple deficit or a nonempty fourfold intersection, it cannot extend to a zero-slack cover.
