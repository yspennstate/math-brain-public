## 2. Exact invariant used

Work in the 2048-point antipodal quotient

\[
G=\mathbb F_2^{12}/\langle\mathbf 1\rangle
\]

with folded distance

\[
\delta(x,y)=\min\{\operatorname{wt}(x+y),12-\operatorname{wt}(x+y)\}.
\]

For either 1024-point parity half `H`, let `U_H` be the third-Bonferroni value obtained by replacing every triple intersection by the exact maximum allowed by its center-parity/distance orbit. Every genuine cover satisfies

\[
d_H+E_H=U_H-1024,
\]

where

- `d_H` is the sum of exact deficits of actual triple intersections from those orbitwise maxima;
- `E_H` is the higher-multiplicity Bonferroni excess
  \[
  E_H=\sum_{x:m(x)>0}\binom{m(x)-1}{3}\ge0.
  \]

Both terms are monotone under adding centers. Therefore if a partial configuration already has `d_H+E_H > U_H-1024` on either half, it cannot extend to a cover.

The new certificate recomputes all folded radius-3 balls and all exact triple-intersection maxima directly. No floating point, MILP/SAT timeout, heuristic failure, or unverified solver status is used.
