## 2. Exact setup and monotone obstruction

Work in the antipodal quotient

\[
G=\mathbb F_2^{12}/\langle\mathbf1\rangle,
\qquad |G|=2048,
\]

represented by 11-bit words with folded distance

\[
\delta(x,y)=\min\{\operatorname{wt}(x+y),12-\operatorname{wt}(x+y)\}.
\]

A candidate discrepancy-6 witness is exactly a set of 12 quotient centers whose folded radius-3 balls cover all 2048 points.

For either 1024-point parity half `H`, let `U_H` be the third-Bonferroni expression obtained by replacing each triple intersection by the exact maximum permitted by its center parity/distance orbit. For a concrete center set let

- `d_H` be the sum of the actual deficits from those triple maxima;
- `E_H` be the higher-multiplicity Bonferroni excess.

The exact identity is

\[
E_H=\sum_{x:m(x)>0}\binom{m(x)-1}{3}\ge0,
\]

and for a full cover

\[
\boxed{d_H+E_H=U_H-1024.}
\]

Both `d_H` and `E_H` are monotone under adding centers. Hence a partial configuration is impossible as soon as its exact `d_H+E_H` exceeds the final half-budget `U_H-1024`.

The new certificate independently recomputes the 2048 folded balls and every realizable parity/distance triple-intersection maximum using exact bitsets. It uses no floating point and no optimization-solver status.
