## 2. Exact invariant and finite-search rule

For a parity half `H`, let `U_H` be the third-Bonferroni value obtained by replacing every triple intersection by the exact maximum allowed by its parity/distance orbit. Every genuine cover satisfies

\[
U_H-d_H=1024+E_H,
\]

where `d_H` is the sum of exact triple-intersection deficits and

\[
E_H=\sum_{x:m(x)>0}\binom{m(x)-1}{3}\ge0
\]

is the higher-multiplicity Bonferroni excess. Hence

\[
\boxed{d_H+E_H=U_H-1024.}
\]

Both `d_H` and `E_H` are monotone under adding centers. Thus every partial configuration in a genuine cover must stay within the two exact parity-half slack budgets.
