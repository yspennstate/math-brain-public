\[
f-\binom f2+\binom f3=1+\binom{f-1}{3}\qquad(f\ge1)
\]
gives
\[
B_3=|\cup B_i|+H,
\qquad
H=\sum_{z:f(z)>0}\binom{f(z)-1}{3}.
\]

\[
