#### Step 1. Exact iid-top-six finite information game

Temporarily sample each of ranks `1,...,6` independently with probability `p`. For hidden preliminary order `pi` and top-six sample mask `M`, assign prior mass

`F(pi) p^{|M|}(1-p)^{6-|M|}`.

There are `22*2^6=1408` positive hidden `(pi,M)` states before observational quotienting. All unsampled distinguished ranks arrive before the common decreasing tail. Until winning becomes impossible, the information needed by the Bellman recursion consists exactly of the compatible hidden-state set, the distinguished-arrival step, and whether the single query has been used.

At each distinguished arrival, the algorithm sees only its ordinal position among all elements observed so far. A legal query partitions the compatible hidden states by the number of unseen elements better than the current record. The exact recursion compares query-now with wait-for-next-arrival, and after each arrival compares select-now with reject-and-continue. Thus delayed query timing is unrestricted. The compiled exact graph has `933` Bellman nodes.

As in the preceding top-five proof, if no distinguished rank has yet been observed, detailed common-tail query information is independent of the hidden top-order variable conditional on the empty distinguished sample mask.
