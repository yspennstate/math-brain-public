# B19 — make it an algorithm: exact full-Gram facial structure

\[

written in cell notation. An `N=25` source-exceedance candidate consists of these 16 one-edge squares plus nine two-edge squares pairing 18 of the 20 complementary cells; the two unpaired complement cells are the holes.

Write `z_ij=x_i y_j` and let `u` be the vector of all 36 cell monomials. For any displayed polynomial `P` in this layer, every full Gram representation has

\[
P=u^\top Q u,\qquad Q\succeq0.
\]

The two hole diagonal coefficients are zero. PSD therefore forces the entire two corresponding rows/columns of `Q` to vanish. Every occupied cell has diagonal entry one.

Now take two distinct occupied cells `a,b`.

- If `a,b` share a row or a column, the product `z_a z_b` has no different cell-pair realization, so `Q_ab` is fixed by the polynomial coefficient.
- If they lie in distinct rows and columns, they are one diagonal of a unique `2x2` rectangle. If the other diagonal is `c,d`, the only coefficient freedom is

\[
Q_{ab}+Q_{cd}=\varepsilon_{ab}+\varepsilon_{cd},
\]

where `epsilon` records whether the cell-pair is one of the nine displayed two-edges.
- If the rectangle contains a hole, one diagonal Gram entry is already zero, so the surviving diagonal entry is fixed.
- If all four rectangle cells are occupied, that rectangle contributes exactly one free opposite-diagonal transfer parameter.

Because unordered distinct-row/distinct-column cell pairs partition into these rectangle-diagonal pairs, these parameters are independent. Hence the entire affine Gram-fibre dimension depends only on the two holes:

\[
\boxed{
\dim \mathcal A(P)=
\begin{cases}
180,&\text{holes in a common row or column},\\
176,&\text{holes in distinct rows and distinct columns}.
\end{cases}}
\]

Proof of the counts: there are `C(6,2)^2=225` rectangles. Each hole lies in 25. Two holes in a common row/column lie together in 5 rectangles, whereas two holes in distinct rows and columns lie together in exactly one. Inclusion-exclusion gives `225-25-25+5=180` and `225-25-25+1=176`.
