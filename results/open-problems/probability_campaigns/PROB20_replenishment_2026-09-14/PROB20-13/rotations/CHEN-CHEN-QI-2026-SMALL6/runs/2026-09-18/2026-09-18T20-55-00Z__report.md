# New lemma: nonzero displayed-range Gram kernel is a sound reducibility certificate

This is the main mathematical mechanism of the run.

Let the displayed doubly-simple decomposition have `N=|E_1|+|E_2|` selected squares. For each selected square `e`, let `b_e` be its coefficient vector in the `mn` cell-monomial basis: `b_e` is one cell basis vector for a 1-edge and the sum of the two half-cell basis vectors for a 2-edge. Simplicity makes the supports of the `b_e` disjoint, hence the `b_e` are linearly independent.

The displayed form is

\[
P=\sum_{e=1}^N (b_e^T z)^2.
\]

Consider a real symmetric `N x N` matrix `H` with zero diagonal such that

\[
\sum_{e,f=1}^N H_{ef}(b_e^T z)(b_f^T z)\equiv 0. \tag{*}
\]

Equivalently, `H` is a nonzero coefficient-preserving perturbation in the Gram space spanned by the displayed squares.

**Lemma.** If a nonzero `H` satisfying (*) exists, then the displayed doubly-simple form is reducible: `SOS(P) <= N-1`.

**Proof.** Since `H` is symmetric, nonzero, and has zero diagonal, `tr(H)=0`. A nonzero symmetric positive-semidefinite or negative-semidefinite matrix cannot have zero trace, so `H` has at least one positive and at least one negative eigenvalue. Let `lambda_min(H)<0` and put

\[
t_*=-1/\lambda_{\min}(H)>0.
\]

Then

\[
K:=I_N+t_* H\succeq 0
\]

and `K` is singular. By (*), replacing `I_N` by `K` leaves the represented polynomial unchanged:

\[
\sum_{e,f}K_{ef}(b_e^Tz)(b_f^Tz)=P(z).
\]

Factor `K=C^T C`. The rows of `C` give an SOS representation of `P` by `rank(K)` linear combinations of the displayed bilinear forms `b_e^T z`. Because `K` is singular, `rank(K)<=N-1`. Hence `SOS(P)<=N-1`, so the displayed `N`-square decomposition is reducible. QED.

Two cautions are essential.

- A **nonzero** displayed-range kernel is a rigorous reducibility certificate by the lemma.
- A **zero** displayed-range kernel is only a screening result. It does *not* prove irreducibility, because a shorter Gram representation need not lie in this displayed-square range. This is exactly why the invalidated 19:05 checker cannot be resurrected.
