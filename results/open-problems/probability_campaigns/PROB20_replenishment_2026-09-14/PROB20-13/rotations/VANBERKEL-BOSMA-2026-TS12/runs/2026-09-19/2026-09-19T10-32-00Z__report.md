## 6. B11 — DUALIZE: from strict triples to a fixed-anchor pair-sum state

### 6.1 Exact algebraic change of language

Let the characteristic formal series of a strict 3-sumfree sequence be

`C(x) = sum_{s in S} x^s`.

The generating series whose coefficient at `x^n` counts **unordered triples of pairwise-distinct sequence values** summing to `n` is the elementary-symmetric Newton identity

`T(x) = ( C(x)^3 - 3 C(x) C(x^2) + 2 C(x^3) ) / 6`.

Indeed, `C(x)^3` counts ordered triples, the subtraction removes the three ways in which two coordinates coincide, and the final term restores the all-three-equal contribution with the standard Newton coefficient; division by 6 passes from the six orderings of a distinct triple to an unordered triple. Thus the greedy exclusion rule is exactly the support condition `[x^n]T(x)>0` evaluated against already accepted values.

This identity by itself is not finite-state, but it suggests asking whether the support of `T` for this special row is already generated by triples touching a fixed prefix. If so, the dynamic obstruction drops from a general three-body state to a union of fixed shifts of a pair-sum state.
