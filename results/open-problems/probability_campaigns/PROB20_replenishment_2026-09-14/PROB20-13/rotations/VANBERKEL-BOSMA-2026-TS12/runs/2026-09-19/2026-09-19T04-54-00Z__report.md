## 4. B18/B30: exact first-gap lemma

The source’s Theorem 19 proves periodicity when, with `d=h-g`,

`f+g >= d+1`.

All twelve unresolved cases lie on the other side. Set

`q := f+g`,  
`delta := d+1-q > 0`,  
`A := 2q+h`.

The source’s Step 1 and the part of Step 2 that does not use `q>=d+1` establish the following exact prefix:

- below `A`, the sequence elements after the prescribed `f,g` are the full interval `[h,h+q-1]`;
- the full interval `[q+h,A-1]` is excluded by the three-sum representations `f+g+u` with `u in [h,h+q-1]`.

The condition `q>=d+1` in Theorem 19 is exactly what makes the next forbidden-sum family start no later than `A`; when it fails, the uncovered interval is

`G = [A, A+delta-1]`.

### Lemma (first uncovered-gap dynamics)

Assume `0<delta<=2q`. In the greedy strict 3-sumfree sequence `S_{f,g,h}`, the membership pattern on `G` is exactly

`G ∩ S = [A, A+min(delta,q)-1]`.

Equivalently:

- if `delta<=q`, **every** point of the first uncovered gap enters the sequence;
- if `q<delta<=2q`, exactly the first `q` points enter and the remaining `delta-q` points are excluded.

### Proof

Before `A`, the only sequence elements are `f`, `g`, and the interval

`I=[h,h+q-1]`.

Classify sums of three distinct elements from this prefix.

1. Sums using both `f` and `g` and one `u in I` are at most

`f+g+(h+q-1)=A-1`.

2. The smallest sum using `f` and two distinct elements of `I` is

`f+h+(h+1)`.

Using `h=g+d` and `delta=d+1-q`,

`f+2h+1 = A+delta`.

So this and all larger prefix triples lie strictly after `G`.

3. Triples using `g` and two elements of `I`, or three elements of `I`, are still larger.

Therefore **no prefix triple represents any integer of `G`**.

Now scan `G` greedily. Suppose `0<=j<min(delta,q)`. Any representation of `A+j` using a previously inserted gap element has value at least

`A + f + g = A+q > A+j`,

because `f,g` are the two smallest distinct earlier sequence elements. Two gap elements are even larger. Hence `A+j` is unrepresentable and enters `S`.

If instead `q<=j<delta`, the hypothesis `delta<=2q` gives `0<=j-q<q`. The previous paragraph has already placed `A+(j-q)` in `S`, and

`A+j = (A+j-q)+f+g`

is a sum of three distinct earlier sequence elements. Hence `A+j` is excluded.

This proves the claimed pattern. ∎

This is a theorem-sized local result, but it does **not** establish ultimate periodicity for any source case and therefore does not move `R_TS12`.
