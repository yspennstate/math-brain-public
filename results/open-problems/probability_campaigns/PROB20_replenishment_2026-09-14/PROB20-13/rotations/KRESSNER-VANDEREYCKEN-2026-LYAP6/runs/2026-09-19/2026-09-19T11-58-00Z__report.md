## 1. Exact outside source and target

Outside source: Daniel Kressner and Bart Vandereycken (2026), *A counterexample to the symmetric-maximizer conjecture for Lyapunov operators*, arXiv:2608.20875v1, submitted 2026-08-21; see the abstract, §1, and the concluding discussion of the unique unresolved order.

For `A in R^{6 x 6}`, define

\[
\mathcal L_A(X)=AX+XA^T,
\]

with the operator norm induced by the Frobenius norm. Let `S_6` and `K_6` be the symmetric and skew-symmetric subspaces. The exact surviving question is

\[
\boxed{\quad \|\mathcal L_A|_{K_6}\|\le \|\mathcal L_A|_{S_6}\|\quad\text{for every }A\in\mathbb R^{6\times6}?\quad}
\]

Equivalently: in order six, is the full Frobenius-induced norm of `L_A` always attained on a symmetric matrix?

The 2026 source gives an exact integer/rational counterexample in order seven and extends it by direct sum to every `n>=7`; it explicitly leaves `n=6` open. Chen--Tian (2015) prove the conjecture for `n<=5`. Therefore order six is the only surviving dimension. This assignment does **not** reopen the refuted all-orders conjecture and does **not** weaken the target to a special matrix class.

### Exact finite theorem

**Proposition.** None of these 49 row/column deletions is an order-six counterexample. In fact, for every `(r,c)`,

\[
\|\mathcal L_{A_{r,c}}|_{K_6}\|^2
<
\|\mathcal L_{A_{r,c}}|_{S_6}\|^2.
\]

**Proof.** Use the natural integer coordinate bases

- `S_6`: `E_ii` and `E_ij+E_ji`, with Gram matrix `D_S=diag(I_6,2I_15)`;
- `K_6`: `E_ij-E_ji`, with Gram matrix `D_K=2I_15`.

If `B_U` is the integer coordinate matrix of `L_A` on one of these invariant subspaces, then

\[
\|\mathcal L_A|_U\|^2
=
\max_{x\ne0}\frac{x^T B_U^T D_U B_U x}{x^T D_U x}.
\]

For each of the 49 matrices the exact certificate supplies an integer separator `tau` and an integer symmetric-coordinate vector `v` such that

\[
\tau D_K-B_K^TD_KB_K\succ0
\]

and

\[
v^TB_S^TD_SB_Sv>\tau\,v^TD_Sv.
\]

The first inequality gives `||L_A|K_6||^2<tau`; the second gives `||L_A|S_6||^2>tau`. Combining them proves the strict symmetric domination for that deletion. The checker performs fraction-exact `LDL^T` elimination for every skew upper certificate and exact integer arithmetic for every symmetric lower witness. All 49 pass. QED.

## 6. B11 — DUALIZE / SELF-ADJOINT SPECTRAL FORM

The order-six problem admits two exact reductions that should be used before any further brute-force search.

### 6.1 Orthogonal-similarity and scale reduction

For an orthogonal `Q`, set

\[
A'=Q^TAQ,\qquad \Phi_Q(X)=Q^TXQ.
\]

Then `Phi_Q` is a Frobenius isometry preserving both `S_6` and `K_6`, and

\[
\mathcal L_{A'}(\Phi_Q(X))
=Q^T(AX+XA^T)Q
=\Phi_Q(\mathcal L_A(X)).
\]

Hence both restricted norms, and therefore their difference, are invariant under orthogonal similarity. Also `L_{cA}=cL_A`, so every nonzero candidate can be normalized by `||A||_F=1`.

Consequently, a universal proof or a counterexample search may assume without loss of generality that `A` is a normalized **real Schur form**, i.e. real quasi-upper-triangular under orthogonal similarity. This removes the irrelevant orthogonal-conjugacy directions rather than letting optimization repeatedly slide along them.

### 6.2 Self-adjoint dual operator

With the Frobenius inner product,

\[
\mathcal L_A^*=\mathcal L_{A^T}.
\]

Therefore the squared singular-value problem is the top-eigenvalue problem for the positive self-adjoint operator

\[
H_A:=\mathcal L_A^*\mathcal L_A,
\]

and direct expansion gives

\[
\boxed{
H_A(X)=A^TA X+A^T X A^T+A X A+X A^TA.
}
\]

Both `S_6` and `K_6` are invariant, so the exact source question is equivalently

\[
\boxed{
\lambda_{\max}(H_A|_{K_6})
\le
\lambda_{\max}(H_A|_{S_6})
\quad\text{for every real }A\in\mathbb R^{6\times6}.
}
\]

The two matrices have dimensions 15 and 21 and entries quadratic in the entries of `A`. In real Schur coordinates this turns the positive direction into a concrete semialgebraic spectral-domination problem. No domination map or global PSD certificate was found in this first run, so this is a rigorous reduction, not a solution.
