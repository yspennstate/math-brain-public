## Method B3 — prove something stronger: enforce exact fixed-cardinality consistency before optimizing

The strongest new route in this run comes directly from a constraint that Bachoc and Vallentin explicitly record but omit from their variable-cardinality SDP.

For a code `C` of cardinality `N`, write its normalized three-point distribution as

\[
x(u,v,t)=\frac1N\#\{(c,c',c'')\in C^3:
 c\cdot c'=u,\ c\cdot c''=v,\ c'\cdot c''=t\}.
\]

Then

\[
\sum_u x(u,u,1)=N,
\]

and, for every occurring inner product `t`, there is the exact marginal-consistency identity

\[
\boxed{\sum_{u,v}x(u,v,t)=N\,x(t,t,1).}\tag{MC}
\]

Bachoc–Vallentin Remark 4.1 points out these additional equalities. In the ordinary upper-bound optimization `N` is itself a variable, so `(MC)` is bilinear in the relaxation variables and is not an SDP constraint of the standard formulation. But the present metric-moving question is not “maximize N”; it is the fixed feasibility question **does N=44 exist?** Once `N=44` is fixed, `(MC)` becomes linear:

\[
\boxed{\sum_{u,v}x(u,v,t)=44\,x(t,t,1).}\tag{MC44}
\]

Thus the source-level `N=44` exclusion problem admits a valid strengthening of the usual three-point relaxation that is unavailable when cardinality is optimized simultaneously.

Because a general spherical code can have continuously varying inner products, the computationally useful finite-degree form is a moment identity. For every polynomial test function `q`, any genuine 44-code satisfies

\[
\boxed{
\sum_{u,v,t}q(t)x(u,v,t)=44\sum_t q(t)x(t,t,1).
}\tag{M_q}
\]

In particular one may impose `(M_q)` for Gegenbauer polynomials `q=G_0^{(5)},\ldots,G_r^{(5)}` or another finite polynomial basis. Each such constraint is linear and exact for a genuine code. Therefore:

> **Fixed-N consistency program (research specification, not a theorem).** Take a degree-truncated Bachoc–Vallentin primal feasibility problem for `n=5`, `s=1/2`, fix the cardinality to `N=44`, retain all ordinary positivity/support constraints, and add `(M_q)` for a growing finite polynomial basis. If one finite level is proved infeasible with an exact/rational or interval-certified dual certificate, then `A(5,1/2)\le43`.

This is materially different from merely increasing the degree of the published cardinality-maximizing three-point SDP: it restores exact pair/triple marginal information that the standard relaxation deliberately drops.

### Dual interpretation

Let `mu_3` denote the triple-distance measure and `mu_2` the pair-distance measure. For a genuine fixed-`N` code, `(MC)` is the measure identity

\[
(\pi_t)_\#\mu_3=N\mu_2.
\]

At `N=44`, a polynomial multiplier `q(t)` may therefore be introduced freely in the dual through

\[
\int q(t)\,d\mu_3-44\int q(t)\,d\mu_2=0.
\]

This couples the pair and triple dual inequalities. It is exactly the kind of missing global consistency that the previous two-point/Musin correction could not encode. The immediate analytic/numerical objective for a future reassignment is to search for a low-degree `q` and the usual matrix-polynomial multipliers giving a strict dual separation of the fixed-44 feasible set, then rationalize/interval-certify only if such a margin is found.

No claim is made here that this strengthened fixed-44 program is infeasible, or that the idea is novel. A bounded search found the identity in the original Bachoc–Vallentin paper itself and did not locate a source proving that this particular fixed-cardinality strengthening resolves dimension five; a negative search is not a novelty certificate.

## Method B12 — conserved quantity: pair/triple marginal mass

The invariant that was missing from the preceding attacks is not merely total triple mass

\[
\sum_{u,v}x(u,v,t)=N x(t,t,1).

The earlier `A_4` structural route controlled a special geometric section. The max-volume route controlled conditioning/rank nondegeneracy. The Musin tail route controlled a one-anchor scalar tail. This diagnoses a concrete relaxation gap to attack rather than merely saying that “higher-point information is needed.” The fixed-cardinality quantifier turns that gap into linear information.
