## 3. Direct weak duality from the source normalization

The new lower bound needs only a one-line metric weak dual, so I reproduce it rather than depending circularly on an earlier product-demand equivalence.

Let `c_e=pi_i P_ij` for a physical undirected edge `e={i,j}`. For any nonnegative edge lengths `ell_e` with

\[
\sum_e c_e\ell_e\le1,
\tag{3.1}
\]

let `d_ell` be the induced shortest-path metric. Then every Aldous--Fill flow family obeys

\[
\boxed{
\psi({\bf f})\ge
\sum_{a,b}\pi_a\pi_b d_\ell(a,b).
}
\tag{3.2}
\]

**Proof.** Fix a source `a` and put `phi(v)=-d_ell(a,v)`. Then `|phi(u)-phi(v)|<=ell_e` on every edge. Discrete summation by parts with `div f^(a)=delta_a-pi` gives

\[
\sum_b\pi_b d_\ell(a,b)
\le
\sum_e\ell_e|f_e^{(a)}|.
\]

Multiply by `pi_a`, sum over `a`, use

\[
\sum_a\pi_a|f_e^{(a)}|\le\psi({\bf f})c_e,
\]

and then (3.1). This proves (3.2). QED.

Thus any feasible metric family gives a rigorous lower bound on `F=inf_f psi(f)` without any path-decomposition convention.

## 4. The explicit base `H_k=J_2(2k,k)`

Fix `k>=2`. Let `H_k` have as vertices the `k`-dimensional subspaces of `F_2^{2k}`, with two vertices adjacent iff their intersection has dimension `k-1`.

Write

\[
A:= [k]_2=2^k-1.
\]

### 4.1 Simplicity, degree, distance

The graph is finite and simple by definition. For a fixed `k`-space `U`, choose its `(k-1)`-dimensional intersection with a neighbor in `A` ways. In the quotient by that hyperplane, there are `[k+1]_2-1=2A` one-dimensional extensions other than `U`; each adjacent vertex is counted once. Hence

\[
\boxed{d:=\deg(H_k)=2A^2.}
\tag{4.1}
\]

For two `k`-spaces `U,V`, each adjacency step can change the intersection dimension with `V` by at most one, while a basis-replacement path attains this lower bound. Therefore

\[
\boxed{d_{H_k}(U,V)=k-\dim(U\cap V),}
\tag{4.2}
\]

so `H_k` is connected and has diameter `k`.

### 4.2 Exact second adjacency eigenvalue

Specializing the standard Grassmann spectrum (2.1) to `q=2,n=2k` gives

\[
\theta_j
=2^{j+1}(2^{k-j}-1)^2-(2^j-1),
\qquad 0\le j\le k.
\tag{4.3}
\]

At `j=0`, (4.3) gives the degree `d=2A^2`. At `j=1`,

\[
\boxed{\theta_1=A^2-2A.}
\tag{4.4}
\]

For every `j>=2`, direct subtraction yields

\[
\theta_1-\theta_j
=(2^{j-1}-1)(2^{2k-j+1}-2)\ge0.
\tag{4.5}
\]

Hence `theta_1` is the second-largest adjacency eigenvalue.

## 5. Cone spectrum and exact relaxation time

Let

\[
G_k:=K_1\vee H_k
\]

and denote its hub by `h`. If `N=|V(H_k)|`, then every base vertex has degree `d+1`, the hub has degree `N`, and

\[
2|E(G_k)|=N(d+2).
\]

Thus

\[
\pi_h=\frac1{d+2},
\qquad
\pi(V(H_k))=\frac{d+1}{d+2},
\tag{5.1}
\]

and, because of the hub,

\[
\boxed{D(G_k)=2.}
\tag{5.2}
\]

Every base adjacency eigenvector orthogonal to constants extends by zero at `h` and becomes a random-walk eigenvector of the cone with eigenvalue `theta/(d+1)`. The two-dimensional hub/base-constant block contributes eigenvalues `1` and `-1/(d+1)`. Since `theta_1>0`,

\[
\lambda_2(P_{G_k})=rac{A^2-2A}{2A^2+1}.
\tag{5.3}
\]

Therefore

\[
1-\lambda_2
=rac{(A+1)^2}{2A^2+1}
\]

and

\[
\boxed{
\tau_{\rm rel}(G_k)
=rac{2A^2+1}{(A+1)^2}<2.
}
\tag{5.4}
\]

This exact formula is one place where the new family differs sharply from Hamming/Johnson-type candidates whose relaxation time grows with their intrinsic diameter.

## 6. Mean base distance: an elementary exact lower bound

Let `U,V` be independent uniform vertices of `H_k`, and put

\[
J:=\dim(U\cap V).
\]

By (4.2),

\[
\mathbb E d_{H_k}(U,V)=k-\mathbb EJ.
\tag{6.1}
\]

Let `Z` be the number of one-dimensional subspaces contained in `U cap V`. Over `F_2`, `Z=2^J-1>=J`. There are

\[
[2k]_2=2^{2k}-1=A(A+2)
\]

lines in `F_2^{2k}`. A fixed line belongs to a uniform random `k`-space with probability

\[
\frac{[k]_2}{[2k]_2}=rac1{A+2}.
\]

Hence

\[
\mathbb EZ
=A(A+2)\frac1{(A+2)^2}
=\frac{A}{A+2}<1.
\tag{6.2}
\]

Since `J<=Z`,

\[
\boxed{
\mathbb E d_{H_k}(U,V)
\ge k-\frac{A}{A+2}>k-1.
}
\tag{6.3}
\]

No asymptotic estimate is used.

## 7. Exact feasible dual metric on the cone

Give every spoke the length

\[
x:=\frac{k(d+2)}{d+k}
\]

and every base edge the length

\[
y:=\frac{2(d+2)}{d+k}.
\tag{7.1}
\]

Then

\[
2x=ky
\tag{7.2}
\]

and

\[
x+\frac d2y=d+2.
\tag{7.3}
\]

Because every physical cone edge has conductance `1/[N(d+2)]`, (7.3) is exactly the dual budget condition

\[
\sum_ec_e\ell_e=1.
\]

For base vertices `u,v`, (4.2) gives `d_H(u,v)<=k`; a base geodesic costs `y d_H(u,v)`, while any route through the hub costs at least `2x=ky`. Therefore

\[
\boxed{
d_\ell(u,v)=y\,d_{H_k}(u,v).
}
\tag{7.4}
\]

Discarding the nonnegative hub/base contribution from (3.2), and using (5.1), (6.3), and (7.4), gives

\[
\begin{aligned}
F(G_k)
&\ge
\left(\frac{d+1}{d+2}\right)^2
\frac{2(d+2)}{d+k}
\left(k-\frac{A}{A+2}\right)\\
&=
\frac{2(d+1)^2}{(d+2)(d+k)}
\left(k-\frac{A}{A+2}\right).
\end{aligned}
\tag{7.5}
\]

## 8. Unbounded source ratio

Divide (7.5) by `tau_rel D`, using `D=2` and (5.4). We obtain

\[
\frac{F(G_k)}{\tau_{\rm rel}(G_k)D(G_k)}
\ge
Q_k\left(k-\frac{A}{A+2}\right),
\tag{8.1}
\]

where

\[
Q_k:=
\frac{(d+1)(A+1)^2}{(d+2)(d+k)}.
\tag{8.2}
\]

Now

\[
2(d+1)(A+1)^2-(d+2)(d+k)
=2\left[A^2(4A-k+1)+2A-k+1\right].
\tag{8.3}
\]

For `k>=2`, `A=2^k-1` gives `4A-k+1>0` and `2A-k+1>0`. Hence

\[
\boxed{Q_k>\frac12.}
\tag{8.4}
\]

Combining (6.3), (8.1) and (8.4),

\[
\boxed{
\frac{F(G_k)}{\tau_{\rm rel}(G_k)D(G_k)}
>\frac{k-1}{2}.
}
\tag{8.5}
\]

Since `k` is arbitrary,

\[
\boxed{
\sup_G\frac{F(G)}{\tau_{\rm rel}(G)D(G)}=\infty.
}
\tag{8.6}
\]

Thus the source-level diameter replacement in Aldous--Fill Open Problem 4.49(ii) is false, conditional only on the standard published Grassmann spectrum formula used in (4.3). Unlike C31's proof, this family is explicit for every integer `k>=2` and needs no Ramanujan-existence theorem or large-order selection.

For a concrete exact specialization, `k=128` gives the simple certificate

\[
\frac{F(G_{128})}{\tau_{\rm rel}(G_{128})D(G_{128})}>\frac{127}{2}=63.5.
\]

The executed exact-arithmetic self-check for this run also checks the stronger rational lower bound from (8.1), not only the simplified `127/2` certificate.
