## 4. Independent recheck of the full source-level theorem

I now rederive the C31 theorem from the source normalization. This is a self-check by PROB20-13; it does not replace either independent audit.

### 4.1 Weak metric duality directly from source flows

For a physical unoriented edge `e={u,v}` write

\[
c_e:=\pi_uP_{uv}=\pi_vP_{vu}.
\]

Let nonnegative edge lengths `ell_e` satisfy

\[
\sum_e c_e\ell_e\le1,
\tag{4.1}
\]

and let `d_ell` be the induced shortest-path metric. For any feasible source flow `f^(a)` with divergence `delta_a-pi`, define

\[
\phi_a(v):=-d_\ell(a,v).
\]

Then `|phi_a(u)-phi_a(v)|<=ell_e` on every edge. Discrete summation by parts gives

\[
\sum_b\pi_b d_\ell(a,b)
\le\sum_e\ell_e|f_e^{(a)}|.
\]

Multiply by `pi_a`, sum over `a`, and use the definition of `psi(f)`:

\[
\sum_a\pi_a|f_e^{(a)}|\le\psi(f)c_e.
\]

Therefore

\[
\sum_{a,b}\pi_a\pi_b d_\ell(a,b)
\le\psi(f)\sum_ec_e\ell_e
\le\psi(f).
\]

Taking the infimum over feasible flow families yields the weak dual lower bound

\[
\boxed{F(G)\ge \mathbb E_{\pi\otimes\pi}d_\ell(X,Y).}
\tag{4.2}
\]

No exact LP-duality theorem is needed for the refutation.

### 4.2 Ramanujan base and the cone

Fix an integer `s>=4`, and set

\[
d=s^2,\qquad r=s.
\]

By the MSS theorem choose a connected simple `d`-regular bipartite Ramanujan graph `H` of sufficiently large order `n`. Define

\[
B(d,r):=1+d\sum_{j=0}^{r-2}(d-1)^j.
\]

Because the MSS family has unbounded orders, choose a member satisfying

\[
n\ge2B(d,r).
\tag{4.3}
\]

For independent uniform vertices `U,V` of `H`, at most `B(d,r)` vertices lie at distance `<r` from any fixed `U`. Hence

\[
\Pr(d_H(U,V)\ge r)\ge\frac12
\]

and

\[
\boxed{\mathbb E\min\{d_H(U,V),r\}\ge\frac r2.}
\tag{4.4}
\]

Now form the cone

\[
G=K_1\vee H
\]

with hub `h`. Then

\[
|E(G)|=\frac{n(d+2)}2,
\qquad
D(G)=2,
\]

and simple random walk has

\[
\pi_h=\frac1{d+2},
\qquad
\pi_v=\frac{d+1}{n(d+2)}\quad(v\in H).
\tag{4.5}
\]

Every physical edge has the same conductance

\[
c_e=\frac1{n(d+2)}.
\tag{4.6}
\]

### 4.3 Cone spectrum and relaxation time

If `theta` is a base adjacency eigenvalue with eigenvector orthogonal to constants, extending that eigenvector by zero at the hub gives a cone random-walk eigenvalue

\[
\frac{\theta}{d+1}.
\]

The two-dimensional hub/base-constant subspace contributes eigenvalues

\[
1,\qquad -\frac1{d+1}.
\]

Since `H` is Ramanujan,

\[
|\theta|\le2\sqrt{d-1}
\]

for every nontrivial adjacency eigenvalue. Therefore

\[
\lambda_2(P_G)\le\frac{2\sqrt{d-1}}{d+1}
<\frac{2s}{s^2+1}.
\]

Thus

\[
\boxed{
\frac1{\tau_{\rm rel}(G)}
=1-\lambda_2(P_G)
>\frac{(s-1)^2}{s^2+1}.
}
\tag{4.7}
\]

### 4.4 Exact feasible metric

Give every spoke the length

\[
x:=\frac{r(d+2)}{d+r}
\]

and every base edge the length

\[
y:=\frac{2(d+2)}{d+r}.
\]

Then

\[
2x=ry
\tag{4.8}
\]

and, using (4.6),

\[
\sum_ec_e\ell_e
=\frac{nx+(nd/2)y}{n(d+2)}=1.
\tag{4.9}
\]

So the metric is feasible for (4.2). Its distances are

\[
d_\ell(h,v)=x,
\qquad
 d_\ell(u,v)=y\min\{d_H(u,v),r\}\quad(u,v\in H).
\tag{4.10}
\]

Indeed, a hub-using base-to-base path pays at least `2x=ry`, while a hub-free path is a base path and costs at least `y d_H(u,v)`.

### 4.5 Exact lower bound on F

The total stationary mass on the base is

\[
\alpha=\frac{d+1}{d+2}.
\]

From (4.2), (4.4), and (4.10), including both ordered hub/base directions,

\[
\begin{aligned}
F(G)
&\ge
2\frac1{d+2}\alpha x
+\alpha^2y\,\mathbb E\min\{d_H(U,V),r\}\\
&\ge
\boxed{
\frac{r(d+1)(d+3)}{(d+2)(d+r)}
}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.11}
\]

### 4.6 Divergence of F/(tau_rel D)

Using `D=2`, (4.7), (4.11), `d=s^2`, and `r=s`,

\[
\frac{F(G)}{\tau_{\rm rel}(G)D(G)}
>
L_s
:=
\frac{(s-1)^2(s^2+3)}{2(s+1)(s^2+2)}.
\tag{4.12}
\]

The exact identity

\[
L_s-\frac{s-3}{2}
=
\frac{5s^2-2s+9}{2(s+1)(s^2+2)}
>0
\tag{4.13}
\]

shows

\[
\boxed{
\frac{F(G)}{\tau_{\rm rel}(G)D(G)}
>\frac{s-3}{2}\to\infty.
}
\]

Hence there is no universal finite constant `C` such that

\[
F(G)\le C\tau_{\rm rel}(G)D(G)
\]

for all finite connected simple unweighted graphs. This is exactly a negative answer to Aldous--Fill Open Problem 4.49(ii).

## 5. MSS hypotheses and simplicity/connectedness check

The published MSS theorem supplies infinite families of `d`-regular bipartite Ramanujan graphs for every `d>2`. The standard construction is by iterated 2-lifts from a simple bipartite seed. A 2-lift of a simple graph is simple. Moreover, if a 2-lift of a connected `d`-regular graph were disconnected into two copies, the new spectrum would contain another eigenvalue `d`; the MSS-selected new eigenvalues are bounded by `2 sqrt(d-1)<d` for `d>2`. Thus the selected lifts can be taken connected, and their orders double, which is enough to satisfy (4.3) for every fixed `s`.

This dependency was separately checked by both P26 and C30 in their PASS audits.
