## 1. Inherited displacement-group certificate

Fix a ten-bin block with global mesh `h=1/n` and rank interval

`B=[a,a+10h]`.

Use rotated coordinates

`U=X+Y-1`, `V=X-Y`.

Conditional on the source band, set

`t=(F_U(U)-a)/(10h)`,
`Q(t)=F_U^{-1}(a+10h t)`,
`A(t)=1-|Q(t)|`,
`z=V/A(t)`.

Then `t~Unif[0,1]` and `z~Unif[-1,1]` independently.

For a local permutation `sigma in S_10`, group branches with common displacement `d_j=j-sigma(j)`. The archived displacement-group construction gives

`Delta = -10 h^2 [ H_B B_sigma + int_0^1 Q(t) F_sigma(t) dt ]`,

where

`H_B=int_0^1 A(t)dt`,
`G_B=E|Q(T)-Q(T')|`.

If

`A_sigma(s)=int_0^s F_sigma(t)dt`,
`C_sigma=sup_{0<s<1} A_sigma(s)/(2s(1-s))`,

the archived Stieltjes argument yields

`int Q F_sigma >= -C_sigma G_B`.

Hence

`B_sigma/C_sigma >= G_B/H_B`                                         (1.1)

is sufficient.

For a finite permutation and a rational threshold `rho`, the assertion

`B_sigma/C_sigma >= rho`                                             (1.2)

is checked exactly by finitely many rational quadratic inequalities. No floating-point optimization enters (1.2).

## 4. Exact universal two-sample endpoint certificate

For iid

`P=(A,B), Q=(A',B') ~ pi`,

the crossed point `Z=(A,B')` has law `lambda⊗lambda`. Choosing `P` or `Q` according to the smaller squared coordinate displacement gives

`W_2^2(lambda⊗lambda,pi) <= 1/6 - J(pi)/2`,

`J(pi)=E |(A-A')^2-(B-B')^2|`.

## 6. Exact domino score formulas

Write

`E_L Q`, `E_L(tQ)`, `E_L A`

for conditional moments on `t in [0,1/2]`, and similarly `E_H`. Let full-band moments be `E Q`, `E(tQ)`, `E A`.

For branch `j`, put

`s_j=j+sigma(j)`, `d_j=j-sigma(j)`.

For a low horizontal domino with vertical-slab mean `zbar`, the normalized cross score is

`s_j E_L Q + 4 E_L(tQ) + d_j zbar E_L A`.                           (6.1)

For a high horizontal domino it is

`(s_j-2) E_H Q + 4 E_H(tQ) + d_j zbar E_H A`.                       (6.2)

For a vertical domino it is

`s_j E Q + 2 E(tQ) + d_j zbar E A`.                                 (6.3)

The diagonal reference criterion is

`(1/10) sum_j score_j >= 20 E(tQ)`.                                 (6.4)

For a horizontal certificate define integer totals

`CSL=sum_low s_j`,
`CSH=sum_high (s_j-2)`,

and write the summed vertical correlations as

`sum_low d_j zbar = ZL/10`,
`sum_high d_j zbar = ZH/10`.

Then the exact margin in (6.4) is

`M_H = (CSL/10) E_L Q + (CSH/10) E_H Q`
`      + (ZL/100) E_L A + (ZH/100) E_H A`
`      - 8[E_L(tQ)+E_H(tQ)]`.                                       (6.5)

For a vertical certificate let

`sum_j d_j zbar = Z/10`.

Since `sum_j s_j=90`, its exact margin is

`M_V = 9 E Q + (Z/100) E A - 18 E(tQ)`.                              (6.6)

Thus every numerical assignment candidate reduces to a small tuple of integers and finitely many exact triangular-quantile moments.
