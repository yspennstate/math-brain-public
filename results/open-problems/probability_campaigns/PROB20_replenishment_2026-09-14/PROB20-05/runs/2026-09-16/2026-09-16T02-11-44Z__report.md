## 1. Inherited equal-slab block transport

Fix an `m`-bin block with global mesh `h=1/n` and conditional triangular-quantile moments

`G_B = E |Q(T)-Q(T')|`,

`H_B = E [1-|Q(T)|]`.

For a local permutation `sigma`, write

`d_j = j-sigma(j)`.

Sort the displacement multiset and pair it increasingly with the equal `z`-slab means

`mu_l=(2l+1-m)/m`.

The archived equal-slab transport gives the exact cost change from the diagonal-optimal transport

`Delta/h^2 = [m(m-1)/2] G_B - D(sigma) H_B`,                 (1.1)

where

`D(sigma) = max_beta sum_j d_j mu_{beta(j)}`.

For a sorted zero-sum displacement vector one has the useful identity

`D(sigma) = (1/m) sum_{i<j} |d_i-d_j|`.                    (1.2)

Hence

`D(sigma) >= [m(m-1)/2] (G_B/H_B)`                         (1.3)

is sufficient.

## 2. New general combinatorial lemma for indecomposable permutations

Call `sigma in S_m` indecomposable when no proper initial interval is invariant:

`{0,...,c-1}` is not mapped to itself for every `1<=c<=m-1`.

> **Lemma.** Every indecomposable `sigma in S_m` satisfies
>
> `D(sigma) >= m-1`.                                        (2.1)

### Proof

For every cut `c in {1,...,m-1}`, indecomposability implies at least one right-to-left crossing

`j>=c`, `sigma(j)<c`.

Let

`S_+ = sum_j (d_j)_+`.

Each positive displacement `d_j=j-sigma(j)` counts exactly the cuts crossed by the edge from `j` to `sigma(j)`. Therefore

`S_+ = sum_{c=1}^{m-1} N_c`,

where `N_c` is the number of right-to-left crossings of cut `c`. Since every `N_c>=1`,

`S_+ >= m-1`.                                               (2.2)

Also `sum_j d_j=0`, so

`sum_j |d_j| = 2 S_+`.                                      (2.3)

Let `X` be uniform on the displacement multiset and `X'` an independent copy. Since `E X=0`, conditional Jensen gives

`E|X-X'| >= E|X-E X'| = E|X|`.                              (2.4)

By (1.2),

`D(sigma) = (m/2) E|X-X'|`
`          >= (m/2) E|X|`
`          = (1/2) sum_j |d_j|`
`          = S_+`
`          >= m-1`.

This proves (2.1). QED.

Combining (2.1) with (1.3) gives an all-`m` corollary:

> **Indecomposable short-ratio criterion.** If
>
> `G_B/H_B <= 2/m`,                                         (2.5)
>
> then every indecomposable `m`-bin positive shuffle is covered by the explicit equal-slab transport.

For `m=11`, the universal indecomposable displacement floor is therefore

`D(sigma)>=10`,                                             (2.6)

and the generic target ratio is `2/11`.

## 5. New odd-block two-row domino transport

The ten-bin checkpoint used even two-row domino tilings. For eleven bins, a two-row tiling can contain one vertical domino and five horizontal dominoes in each half.

Use the independent source coordinates `(t,z)` on the block, with

`t~Unif[0,1]`, `z~Unif[-1,1]` independently.

Split `t` into

`L=[0,1/2]`, `H=[1/2,1]`,

and split `z` into eleven equal slabs labeled `0,...,10`, whose means are

`z_q=(2q-10)/11`.

Choose one slab `v` for a vertical domino. Pair the other ten slabs in the low half and independently in the high half. A low horizontal domino uses target branch rank

`r=2t`,

a high horizontal domino uses

`r=2t-1`,

and the vertical domino uses

`r=t`.

Each domino has source mass `1/11`, and in all three cases the induced target rank is exactly uniform on `[0,1]`. Assigning the eleven dominoes bijectively to the eleven target branches therefore gives an exact admissible transport.

A concrete finite library is obtained by using the nine matchings in a cyclic one-factorization of `K_10` on the nonvertical slabs. There are

`11*9*9 = 891`                                             (5.1)

such mixed tilings.

### 5.1 Scale-free formula on a one-sided block

For a left one-sided eleven-bin block starting at index `k`,

`Q(t)=-1+c f_k(t)`,

`A(t)=c f_k(t)`,

where

`f_k(t)=sqrt(k+11t)` and `c=sqrt(2/n)>0`.                    (5.2)

The constant `-1` cancels exactly from the transport-margin comparison, so the sign is independent of the ambient resolution `n`.

For branch `j`, put

`s_j=j+sigma(j)`, `d_j=j-sigma(j)`.

For a selected mixed tiling define

`CSL = sum_{low branches} s_j`,

`CSH = sum_{high branches} (s_j-2)`,

`sV = s_j` for the branch assigned to the vertical domino.

If a low horizontal domino uses slabs `q1,q2`, its `z`-mean is `(q1+q2-10)/11`; hence define the integer

`ZL = sum_low d_j (q1+q2-10)`,

and similarly

`ZH = sum_high d_j (q1+q2-10)`.

For the vertical domino on slab `v`, define

`ZV=d_j(2v-10)`.

After multiplying the cross-score margin by the positive common scale, the exact sign is the sign of

`T_k = (CSL+ZL/11) E_L f_k`
`    + (CSH+ZH/11) E_H f_k`
`    + (sV +ZV/11) E f_k`
`    - 200 E[t f_k]`.                                      (5.3)

Thus a floating assignment routine may be used only to *locate* a combinatorial tiling/assignment; once the six integers

`(CSL,CSH,sV,ZL,ZH,ZV)`

are recorded, positivity of (5.3) is an exact radical statement.
