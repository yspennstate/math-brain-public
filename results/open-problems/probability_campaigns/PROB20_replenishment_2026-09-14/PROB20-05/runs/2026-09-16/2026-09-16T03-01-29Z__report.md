## 1. Endpoint triangular coordinates and the inherited equal-slab certificate

`B=[0,11/n]`.

For `n>=22`, this lies in `[0,1/2]`, so the triangular source quantile has the one-sided form

`Q(t) = -1 + sqrt(22/n) sqrt(t)`,

and

`A(t)=1-|Q(t)| = sqrt(22/n) sqrt(t)`.

As in the archived block construction, the normalized coordinates

`t in [0,1]`, `z in [-1,1]`

are independent and uniform.

For a local permutation `sigma in S_11`, put

`d_j=j-sigma(j)`.

Let

`S_D(sigma)=sum_{i<j}|d_i-d_j|`,

`D(sigma)=S_D/11`.

The equal-slab block identity therefore certifies every permutation with

## 2. Endpoint specialization of the odd two-row domino transport

Use the mixed domino construction from the preceding checkpoint. Split `t` into

`L=[0,1/2]`, `H=[1/2,1]`,

and split `z` into eleven equal slabs `q=0,...,10`, with slab means

`z_q=(2q-10)/11`.

A legal odd domino tiling consists of one vertical slab crossing both `t` halves, five horizontal dominoes in the low half, and five horizontal dominoes in the high half. A low horizontal domino uses target rank `r=2t`; a high horizontal domino uses `r=2t-1`; the vertical domino uses `r=t`. Each domino has exactly mass `1/11` and sends its source rank to a uniform target branch parameter, so any bijection between the eleven dominoes and the eleven target branches is an admissible transport.

For a branch `j`, define

`s_j=j+sigma(j)`, `d_j=j-sigma(j)`.

For a chosen domino assignment define the six integers

`CSL = sum_low s_j`,

`CSH = sum_high (s_j-2)`,

`sV = s_j` for the vertical branch,

`ZL = sum_low d_j (q_1+q_2-10)`,

`ZH = sum_high d_j (q_1+q_2-10)`,

`ZV = d_j(2q_V-10)`.

For the endpoint function `f_0(t)=sqrt(11t)`, the exact conditional moments are

`E_L f_0 = sqrt(22)/3`,

`E_H f_0 = sqrt(11)(4-sqrt(2))/3`,

`E f_0 = 2 sqrt(11)/3`,

`E[t f_0] = 2 sqrt(11)/5`.

The preceding general mixed-domino margin therefore simplifies dramatically. Put

`A = 11 CSL + ZL`,

`B = 11 CSH + ZH`,

`C = 11 sV + ZV`.

Then the exact transport-improvement margin is a positive common factor times

`M = sqrt(2)(A-B) + 4B + 2C - 2640`.                         (2.1)

More precisely, the preceding checkpoint's scaled margin `T_0` satisfies

`T_0 = sqrt(11) M / 33`.                                    (2.2)

Thus `M>=0` is a rigorous sufficient endpoint certificate, and `M>0` gives a strict improvement over the diagonal benchmark on the block.

Crucially, (2.1) lies in `Z[sqrt(2)]`, so its sign can be checked exactly using one integer square comparison. If `M=Y+X sqrt(2)` and `X,Y` have opposite signs, compare `Y^2` with `2X^2`; no floating-point sign decision is needed.

## 3. A deterministic domino rule closes 2,949,943 of the 2,970,828 slab residuals

A single deterministic legal tiling/assignment rule is surprisingly effective.

Choose the central slab `q=5` as the vertical domino. Pair the ten remaining slabs adjacently in each horizontal half:

`(0,1), (2,3), (4,6), (7,8), (9,10)`.

Their `z`-mean numerators are

`-9,-5,0,5,9`.

For a residual permutation:

1. sort the eleven branches increasingly by `s_j=j+sigma(j)` (breaking ties by `d_j` and then `j`);
2. assign the lowest five branches to the low half;
3. assign the median branch to the vertical domino;
4. assign the highest five branches to the high half;
5. within each half, sort the five displacements `d_j` increasingly and pair them increasingly with `-9,-5,0,5,9`.

This is a fully explicit admissible transport. Exhaustive exact enumeration of all `2,970,828` equal-slab residual indecomposables gives

- deterministic-domino exact successes: `2,949,943`;
- remaining residuals: `20,885`.

Every success was checked by the exact sign rule for (2.1), not by a floating threshold. The smallest strictly positive deterministic margin encountered is

`536 - 379 sqrt(2) > 0`,                                    (3.1)

and the exact square gap is

`536^2 - 2*379^2 = 14 > 0`.                                 (3.2)

Therefore the fixed rule alone closes more than 99.29% of the already difficult slab-residual class.

This rule is not universal: the remaining 20,885 are genuine failures of this particular fixed assignment. They are not counterexamples to the conjecture.
