## 2. Independent triangular coordinates and the odd two-row domino transport

Let `U=X+Y-1`, let `p=F_U(U)` be its triangular rank, and restrict to the source-rank band corresponding to the eleven consecutive cells beginning at global cell `k`. Put

`t=(p-k/15)/(11/15)`.

Then `t~Unif[0,1]`. Conditional on `U=Q(t)`, put

`z=V/(1-|Q(t)|)`, `V=X-Y`,

so that `z~Unif[-1,1]` independently of `t`. Write

`A(t)=1-|Q(t)|`.

For local branch `j`, define

`s_j=j+sigma(j)`, `d_j=j-sigma(j)`.

Split `t` into lower and upper halves and split `z` into eleven equal slabs. A legal odd-domino source partition contains one vertical slab spanning both halves and five horizontal pairs in each half. Every domino has source mass exactly `1/11`. Use target branch parameter `r=t` on the vertical domino, `r=2t` on a lower domino, and `r=2t-1` on an upper domino. Thus every branch receives exactly mass `1/11` with a uniform branch parameter. This is an explicit transport, not a finite-Birkhoff extremality assumption.

For a branch-to-domino assignment define the six integer coefficients

`(CSL,CSH,sV,ZL,ZH,ZV)`

by summing `s_j` on lower branches, `s_j-2` on upper branches, the vertical `s_j`, and the products of displacements `d_j` with the corresponding integer `z`-mean numerators. Since each slab mean has denominator `11`, the exact transport-improvement margin over the diagonal-Brenier reference is
