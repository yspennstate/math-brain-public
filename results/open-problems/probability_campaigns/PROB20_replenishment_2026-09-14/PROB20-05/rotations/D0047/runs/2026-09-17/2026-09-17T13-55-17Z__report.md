## 4. B25 — count the same compatibility twice: the moving Lax pole is apparent

\tag{4.1}

\tag{4.2}

The apparent difficulty is that `b_+` and `b_1` have a moving real pole at `x=Q`. Put

\[

\[
b_+=-\frac{1+(Q+\xi)^2}{\xi},
\qquad
b_1=-\frac{J_0+rQ}{2}+\frac{N}{2\xi},

\[

\boxed{

\tag{4.3}
