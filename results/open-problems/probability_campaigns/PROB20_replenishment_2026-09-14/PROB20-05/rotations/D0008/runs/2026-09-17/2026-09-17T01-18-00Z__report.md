## 3. The exact statement that blocks

The recovered D0008 reduction makes the `n=3` obstacle concrete. After affine normalization and the archived layer/barycentric reduction, it suffices to solve every two-step law

\[
P_{r,s}
=
\frac{s-1}{s-r}\,\mathrm{Unif}[-1,r]
+
\frac{1-r}{s-r}\,\mathrm{Unif}[-1,s],
\qquad -1<r<1<s\le2,
\tag{3.1}
\]

whose mean is zero.

Thus the **source-matching blocking statement** is

\[
\boxed{
\text{For every }-1<r<1<s\le2,\text{ explicitly construct a probability law }\eta_{r,s}
\text{ on }\{x_1+x_2+x_3=0\}
\text{ whose three coordinate marginals are all }P_{r,s}.
}
\tag{3.2}
\]

Solving (3.2) uniformly, with a measurable/explicit dependence on `(r,s)` adequate for the archived barycentric mixing step, would solve the full decreasing condition-(b) class at `n=3`; reflection gives the increasing class. That would move `n_explicit` from `2` to `3`.

One **sufficient architecture**, not a necessary characterization, is the fan-kernel inverse problem from the previous report. A fan

\[
(-u,Y,u-Y),\qquad Y\sim\mathrm{Unif}[u-v,v],
\]

after coordinate permutation has one-coordinate law

\[
K_{u,v}
=\frac13\delta_{-u}
+\frac23\mathrm{Unif}[u-v,v].
\]

A source-general fan solution would therefore require an explicit nonnegative probability measure `nu_{r,s}` satisfying

\[
\boxed{
P_{r,s}
=\int K_{u,v}\,\nu_{r,s}(du,dv).
}
\tag{3.3}
\]

Equivalently, on the absolutely continuous portion,

\[
p_{r,s}(x)
=
\frac13\int\delta_{-u}(dx)\,\nu_{r,s}(du,dv)
+
\frac23\int
\frac{\mathbf 1_{\{u-v\le x\le v\}}}{2v-u}
\,\nu_{r,s}(du,dv)\,dx.
\tag{3.4}
\]

Failure of (3.3) would refute only this **architecture**, not Wang's Open Problem 8.

## 4. Full route inventory and precise failure of each

The following inventory is cumulative from the recovered D0008 reports and the original intake attempt. It is the reason this run does not add another lemma on an exhausted route.

1. **Generic finite-cycle / shuffle shortcut (`D0008-A001`).** A measure-preserving `n`-cycle need not have constant quantile orbit sum. For the proposed rotation `u -> u+1/n mod 1`, the orbit sum for the uniform quantile varies with `u`. Missing ingredient: the constant-sum identity itself.

2. **Layer-cake then completely mix the layer radii.** A decreasing density can be decomposed into uniforms on initial intervals, but the induced radius law need not itself admit the required constant-sum coupling. This transfers the unknown problem one level upward rather than solves it.

3. **One-high / `(n-1)`-low allocation.** The natural decomposition leaves a measurable free-boundary problem: after a high value is chosen, the remaining coordinates must hit an exact residual sum while simultaneously preserving all margins. No explicit source-general allocation rule was obtained.

4. **Universal equal-pair curve `(T,T,-2T)` for `n=3`.** Exact inversion on the centered triangular source law forces the candidate latent density negative on part of its support. Thus that one-dimensional support geometry is not universal.

5. **Finite Wang–Wang peeling plus weak-limit closure.** The finite decompositions certify existence and can be made algorithmic at each mesh, but the published/archived limit step does not select one explicit continuum copula. The D0008 exact-marginal epsilon-mix improves this by keeping target marginals exact at finite scale, yet its sum error is nonzero. Missing ingredient: projective consistency or a contractive exact correction.

6. **Simplex/Dirichlet complete mixes.** Sampling a symmetric Dirichlet point on the constant-sum simplex gives explicit copulas for a genuine continuum of monotone beta marginals in every dimension. This is a strict subclass, not every condition-(b) law.

7. **Coordinatewise quantile deformation of the simplex.** The archived Pexider/Cauchy rigidity argument shows that applying the same scalar measurable map to all coordinates while preserving constant sum on a full-dimensional simplex forces the map to be affine. Therefore arbitrary monotone targets cannot be reached by the obvious quantile deformation.

8. **Fixed-top fan peeling.** If the fan partner endpoint is fixed at `v=s`, the induced density is forced to be constant on a whole tail interval. Generic two-step targets with their jump inside that tail are therefore excluded from this restricted fan specialization.

9. **Atom-local dyadic projective refinement.** Refining each of three zero-sum parent cells independently into left/right children shifts their centers by three signs `±h/4`; the sum of three signs cannot vanish. Coherent refinement must exchange mass across coarse hyperedges, carry compensation state, or use different arity.

10. **Endpoint-aligned one-segment support.** Exact symbolic exhaustion of the natural intervals `[-1,r]`, `[r,s]`, `[-1,s]` leaves only the source curve `r+s=1` and isolated points `(0,2)` and `(1/2,2)`. One line segment with only those target endpoints is too rigid.

11. **Numerical rearrangement / detection procedures.** They can diagnose or approximate mixability but do not provide an exact source-general copula with exact constant sum. Decimal solver output is not the requested result.

12. **Discrete complete-mixability / assignment formulations.** These are valuable finite analogues, but a finite exact matching alone does not solve the continuum explicitness problem; moreover the discrete matrix problem is known to have substantial algorithmic hardness. A continuum extension/gluing theorem is indispensable.

13. **Multimarginal optimal-transport reformulation.** Later work connects some joint mixes to multimarginal OT and extremal negative dependence. This provides language and variational structure, but the sources exposed in this run do not provide an explicit positive right inverse that maps an arbitrary condition-(b) marginal to a constant-sum joint law.

## 5. B2 — attack the negation first

The correct negation target is **not** Wang's source statement, because condition (b) already guarantees existence. The legitimate counterexample-first question is narrower:

> Is the fan architecture (3.3) itself too small to cover all two-step extremes?

A useful dual diagnostic would be a bounded test function `phi` such that, for every admissible fan parameter `(u,v)`,

\[
\Lambda_\phi(u,v)
:=
\frac13\phi(-u)
+
\frac{2}{3(2v-u)}\int_{u-v}^{v}\phi(x)\,dx
\ge0,
\tag{5.1}
\]

while for a target two-step law

\[
\int \phi(x)\,P_{r,s}(dx)<0.
\tag{5.2}
\]

Such a witness would certify failure of the fan cone for that target. It would **not** refute complete mixability; it would tell us to abandon (3.3) and use richer zero-sum hyperedges.

## 6. B8 — discretize only as a diagnostic

The tempting next move is to put `(u,v)` on a rational grid and solve a nonnegative LP for (3.3). That naive discretization has a built-in artifact: a finitely atomic `nu` produces the anchor atoms `delta_{-u}` in `K_{u,v}`, while a generic target `P_{r,s}` is absolutely continuous. Therefore infeasibility of a node-only LP can be caused solely by the discretization of the anchor marginal and cannot be interpreted as failure of the continuum fan architecture.

A useful finite diagnostic would instead have to discretize **mass over parameter cells or a density class for `nu`**, integrate the anchor and interval contributions consistently over output cells, and retain exact rational/symbolic coefficients. Even then:

- feasibility is only evidence for a possible continuum formula unless one proves a refinement/extension theorem;
- infeasibility only refutes the chosen finite-dimensional density class unless accompanied by a genuine continuum dual separator of the form (5.1)–(5.2).

Because this is the mandated thinking-only run, and because a node LP would answer the wrong question, I did **not** create or archive another finite checker. That avoids certificate churn while specifying exactly what a later diagnostic computation would need to mean.

**B8 outcome:** no computation promoted; the naive finite LP is rejected as structurally misleading, and the minimum requirements for a meaningful exact discretization are now explicit.

## 8. What a proof must contain that no recovered route contains

Any successful source-general `n=3` construction now appears to need all of the following simultaneously:

1. **Positive continuum redistribution across more than one hyperedge type.** One line, one fixed fan top, or one atom-local refinement is too rigid.
2. **Exact degree/marginal balance.** Weak convergence, small sum error, or a numerical rearrangement is insufficient.
3. **An explicit positivity mechanism.** The construction must explain why all mixing weights/densities are nonnegative over the full parameter region `-1<r<1<s<=2`.
4. **Exact constant-sum support at every sample.** It cannot be recovered only in expectation or asymptotically.
5. **A gluing/measurable-selection rule across `(r,s)`.** The archived barycentric reduction solves the full monotone class only if the two-step kernels can be selected and mixed explicitly, not merely asserted to exist pointwise.
6. **Compensation across scales or hyperedges.** The dyadic parity obstruction shows that purely local refinement cannot maintain the zero-sum constraint in three coordinates.

In operator language, the missing object is an explicit **positive right inverse** of the degree map from exchangeable probability measures on the plane `x_1+x_2+x_3=0` to the two-step marginal class. Every recovered route either constructs such a right inverse only on a strict subclass or establishes existence without an explicit inverse.

## 10. Nearest solved problem and what it has that this target lacks

The most useful nearby solved construction is the **uniform-interval joint-mixability** problem treated constructively in Wang–Wang (2016). Uniform intervals have affine support geometry: mass can be placed/re-cut in constant-sum blocks while the marginal on each interval remains uniform. That gives an explicit local building block.

By contrast, a monotone condition-(b) density is a continuously weighted nested mixture of such intervals. The 2011/2015 source route proves existence by discretizing those weights, decomposing the discrete law into simple complete mixes, and passing to a limit. What is lost is a coherent, positive, explicit rule telling each infinitesimal layer **which other layers it must pair with** so that all coordinates have the prescribed marginal and every triple sums exactly to the center.

Wang's survey explicitly notes the same structural gap: symmetric cases have clear dependence structures, while the monotone-density results rely on combinatorics/discretization and hide the copula.

Thus the importable ingredient from the solved interval case is not another existence criterion; it is the **re-cutting geometry**. The missing step is a continuum allocation law that glues those explicit interval blocks without freezing the canonical layer labels.
