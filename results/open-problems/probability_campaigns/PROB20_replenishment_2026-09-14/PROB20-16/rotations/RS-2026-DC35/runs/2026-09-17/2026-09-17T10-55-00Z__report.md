## 3. Exact blocker at handoff

The source-complete obligation can now be stated more sharply than at admission. Let

`Omega_D = closure{ [v] : v in D\{0} } subset RP^{r-1}`

inside `V=span D`, after the proved coset reduction. The problem is to characterize the closed projective direction sets `Omega` for which

\[
\boxed{
\forall\; K\subset V\text{ finite},\qquad
\operatorname{conv}_{\Omega}(K)\text{ is semialgebraic}.
}
\tag{3.1}
\]

Equivalently, for ordered finite support `P=(p_1,...,p_N)`, define the Jensen-weight set

\[
\Lambda_D(P,x)
=
\left\{\lambda\in\Delta_{N-1}:
 f(x)\le \sum_{i=1}^N\lambda_i f(p_i)
 \text{ for every real-valued }D\text{-convex }f
\right\}.
\tag{3.2}
\]

Then

\[
x\in\operatorname{conv}_D(P)
\iff
\Lambda_D(P,x)\neq\varnothing.
\tag{3.3}
\]

The campaign has not found a condition on `Omega_D` that is both necessary and sufficient for semialgebraicity of `(3.3)` for **every** finite support. It also has not produced a source-valid non-semialgebraic functional hull whose mechanism could anchor the negative side of such a classification.

The blocker is therefore not one missing algebraic identity. A complete answer needs at least one of the following source-level mechanisms:

1. a finite real-algebraic representation theorem for the restriction/Jensen cone induced by all `D`-convex functions, under a structural condition on `Omega_D`, together with a proof that failure of that condition forces a non-semialgebraic finite hull; or
2. a different exact necessary-and-sufficient invariant of the projective direction geometry, with both directions proved.

No route so far supplies both sides.
