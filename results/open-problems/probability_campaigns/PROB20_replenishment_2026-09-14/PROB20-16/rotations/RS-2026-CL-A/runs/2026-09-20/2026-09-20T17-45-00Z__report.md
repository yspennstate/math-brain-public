## 2. Inherited theorem and the next architectural bottleneck

The 16:44 report proved a strong restricted theorem for a **single** bounded symmetric row-centered graphon component. Conditional on iid latent vertices, edges are independent with mean

`theta_n W_n(sigma_i,sigma_j)`.

For a fixed bounded kernel it proved bounded noisy low-degree advantage through a positive linear fraction `c n`; the proof actually only needs a uniform bound on the ratio

`R_n := ||W_n||_infty / ||T_{W_n}||_op`.

The missing architectural implication is now clear: a general exchangeable law can contain a **shared global latent variable** that chooses among many graphon-like components. Such a variable destroys unconditional edgewise factorization even though each conditional component still has it. Mao has exactly this flavor at an algebraic level: the random alternating form is global and shared by all edges.

The question for this run is therefore not to optimize the previous linear constant. It is:

> Can bounded source LDA expose and control every conditional graphon component when the global latent variable has low enough entropy, thereby preserving the linear-degree noisy conclusion for the mixture?

The answer is yes under a sharp and transparent `exp(o(D))` exposure condition.

## 3. Model: a global discrete mixture of moving graphons

Let `Q_n` be the uniform product law on edge signs `{-1,+1}^{E_n}`, `E_n=binom([n],2)`.

For each `n`, let `J_n` be a finite index set with weights

`p_{n,a}>0`,   `sum_{a in J_n} p_{n,a}=1`.

Conditional on a global latent index `A=a`, define `P_{n,a}` as follows.

- There is a probability space `(S_{n,a},mu_{n,a})`.
- `W_{n,a}:S_{n,a}^2 -> R` is bounded, symmetric, measurable, and row-centered:

  `int W_{n,a}(x,y)dmu_{n,a}(y)=0`

  for almost every `x`.
- Given `A=a`, sample `sigma_1,...,sigma_n iid mu_{n,a}`.
- Conditional on those vertex latents, sample the edge signs independently with

  `E[X_ij | A=a,sigma]=theta_{n,a} W_{n,a}(sigma_i,sigma_j)`,

  with `|theta_{n,a}| ||W_{n,a}||_infty <=1`.

The planted law is the global mixture

`P_n = sum_a p_{n,a} P_{n,a}`.                                  (3.1)

Let

`Lambda_{n,a}=||T_{W_{n,a}}||_op`,
`M_{n,a}=||W_{n,a}||_infty`,
`R_{n,a}=M_{n,a}/Lambda_{n,a}`                                (3.2)

for nonzero components. Zero kernels are exactly null components and may be discarded from all maxima below.

Assume a uniform component-complexity bound

`sup_{n,a} R_{n,a} <= R_* < infinity`.                        (3.3)

Since the base measure is a probability measure,

`||W_{n,a}||_2 <= M_{n,a}`,

so (3.3) also uniformly controls the Hilbert–Schmidt/operator-norm ratio used in the cycle estimates.

Finally define the smallest non-null mixture weight

`p_{min,n}=min_{a: Lambda_{n,a}>0} p_{n,a}`.                  (3.4)

The new structural hypothesis is that there exists an even source degree `ell_n<=D_n` with

`ell_n -> infinity`,   `ell_n=o(n)`,

and

`boxed{ log(1/p_{min,n}) = o(ell_n). }`                       (3.5)

For comparable weights this permits `|J_n|=exp(o(ell_n))` global states. At Kireeva's source scale one may take `ell_n` of polylogarithmic order, so polynomially many or subexponentially-many-in-`D_n` global components are allowed.

## 4. New spectral-exposure lemma

For an even integer `ell>=4`, let `C_ell` be a simple cycle. For a component `a`,

`E_{P_{n,a}} chi_{C_ell}`
` = theta_{n,a}^ell Tr(T_{W_{n,a}}^ell)`.                    (4.1)

Because `ell` is even and `T_W` is self-adjoint compact,

`Tr(T_W^ell)=sum_j lambda_j^ell >= ||T_W||_op^ell=Lambda^ell`. (4.2)

Thus the **mixture** cycle moment has no sign cancellation:

`E_{P_n} chi_{C_ell}`
` = sum_a p_{n,a} theta_{n,a}^ell Tr(T_{W_{n,a}}^ell)`
` >= p_{n,a} (|theta_{n,a}| Lambda_{n,a})^ell`               (4.3)

for every individual component `a`.

There are

`N_ell=(n)_ell/(2 ell)`                                      (4.4)

simple `ell`-cycles. If

`Adv_{<=D_n}(P_n,Q_n) <= A_*`                                (4.5)

and `ell<=D_n`, orthogonality of Walsh characters gives

`A_*^2 >= N_ell [E_{P_n} chi_{C_ell}]^2`.                   (4.6)

Combining (4.3)–(4.6), for every non-null component,

`gamma_{n,a}:=n theta_{n,a}^2 Lambda_{n,a}^2`

satisfies

`boxed{`
`gamma_{n,a}`
` <= [ 2 ell A_*^2 n^ell / ((n)_ell p_{n,a}^2) ]^(1/ell).`
`}`                                                                 (4.7)

This is the key new lemma.

### Consequence under subdegree exposure

Uniformly in `a`,

`(1/ell) log[n^ell/(n)_ell]`
` = (1/ell) sum_{j=0}^{ell-1} -log(1-j/n)`
` = O(ell/n)`                                                (4.8)

when `ell=o(n)`. Also

`log(2 ell A_*^2)/ell ->0`,

and by (3.5)

`2 log(1/p_{n,a})/ell <= 2 log(1/p_{min,n})/ell ->0`.        (4.9)

Therefore

`boxed{ sup_a gamma_{n,a} <= 1+o(1). }`                      (4.10)

The previous single-graphon cycle-pressure argument required one component. Equation (4.10) shows that a low-entropy global mixture cannot hide a supercritical component behind cancellation: even cycle moments are positive and expose every component whose weight is not exponentially small on the source-degree scale.

Note also that only `ell_n=o(n)` is needed for the root asymptotics in (4.8); the stronger inherited condition `ell_n^2/n ->0` is unnecessary here.

## 5. Uniform subcritical component lemma

Fix a noise-retention parameter `rho in (0,1)`.

By (4.10), choose a constant

`beta_* in (rho^2,1)`

such that for all large `n` and all components `a`,

`beta_{n,a}:=n rho^2 theta_{n,a}^2 Lambda_{n,a}^2 <= beta_*`. (5.1)

I now record explicitly the uniform version of the 16:44 diagram theorem.

### Lemma 5.1 (uniform moving-kernel subcritical bound)

Fix `R_*<infinity` and `beta_*<1`. There exist constants

`c=c(R_*,beta_*)>0`,   `C=C(R_*,beta_*)<infinity`             (5.2)

such that for every `n` and every bounded symmetric row-centered kernel `W` with

`||W||_infty/||T_W||_op <= R_*`,                             (5.3)

if

`n rho^2 theta^2 ||T_W||_op^2 <= beta_*`,                   (5.4)

then

`Adv_{<= floor(c n)}(T_rho P_{W,theta},Q_n) <= C`.           (5.5)

#### Proof

The proof is the 16:44 factorial-aware expansion with all constants made uniform.

For a connected edge motif `F`, row-centering kills `t(F,W)` if `F` has a degree-one vertex.

**Cycles.** If `F=C_e`, then

`|t(C_e,W)|=|Tr(T_W^e)|`
` <= ||W||_2^2 Lambda^(e-2)`
` <= M^2 Lambda^(e-2)`
` <= R_*^2 Lambda^e`.                                       (5.6)

Hence the total cycle activity is at most

`(R_*^4/2) sum_{e>=3} beta_*^e/e < infinity`.               (5.7)

**Positive excess.** If `F` is connected, minimum degree at least two, not a cycle, with `e` edges and excess `r=e-v>=1`, degree-two suppression gives

`|t(F,W)| <= B_*^(3r) Lambda^e`,                             (5.8)

where one may take

`B_* = max{1,R_*,R_*^2}`.

The factorial-aware embedded count from the previous report is

`S_{e,r} <= C_1^r e^(3r-1) r^(1-2r)`.                      (5.9)

Thus the fixed-`(e,r)` squared-moment activity is at most

`beta_*^e n^(-r) B_*^(6r) C_1^r e^(3r-1) r^(1-2r)`.        (5.10)

Summing the edge count first using

`sum_{e>=1} beta_*^e e^(3r-1) <= C_{beta_*}^r (3r-1)!`      (5.11)

produces a constant `K=K(R_*,beta_*)` with

`A_r <= r (K r/n)^r`.                                       (5.12)

Choose `c<1/(2K)`. For degree at most `cn`,

`sum_{r<=cn} A_r <= sum_{r>=1} r(Kc)^r < infinity`.         (5.13)

The standard polymer bound over vertex-disjoint connected components then exponentiates the uniformly bounded connected activity, proving (5.5). QED.

Importantly, this lemma permits `W=W_n` to change arbitrarily with `n`; only the scale-free ratio (5.3) and subcriticality (5.4) matter.

## 6. Main theorem: low-entropy global mixtures inherit linear-degree hardness

### Theorem 6.1 (global-mixture linear-degree amplification; PROVED PARTIAL)

Assume the mixture model of Section 3, the uniform complexity bound (3.3), and the exposure condition (3.5). Suppose

`Adv_{<=D_n}(P_n,Q_n) <= A_* < infinity`                     (6.1)

for all sufficiently large `n`, where `D_n` contains even `ell_n -> infinity` with `ell_n=o(n)`.

Then for every fixed retention parameter `rho in (0,1)` there exist constants

`c=c(rho,R_*)>0`,   `C=C(rho,R_*)<infinity`                 (6.2)

such that

`boxed{ Adv_{<= floor(c n)}(T_rho P_n,Q_n) <= C }`           (6.3)

for all sufficiently large `n`.

#### Proof

Section 4 gives

`sup_a n theta_{n,a}^2 Lambda_{n,a}^2 <=1+o(1)`.            (6.4)

Therefore, after fixed noise,

`sup_a n rho^2 theta_{n,a}^2 Lambda_{n,a}^2 <= beta_*<1`    (6.5)

for some fixed `beta_*` and all sufficiently large `n`.

Lemma 5.1 then gives the **same** `c,C` for every conditional component:

`Adv_{<=cn}(T_rho P_{n,a},Q_n) <= C`.                       (6.6)

For every Walsh character `chi_F`, mixture and noise commute:

`E_{T_rho P_n} chi_F`
` = sum_a p_{n,a} E_{T_rho P_{n,a}} chi_F`.                 (6.7)

By Jensen,

`[sum_a p_{n,a} m_{a,F}]^2 <= sum_a p_{n,a} m_{a,F}^2`.     (6.8)

Summing (6.8) over all nonempty `F` with `|F|<=cn` gives

`Adv_{<=cn}(T_rho P_n,Q_n)^2`
` <= sum_a p_{n,a} Adv_{<=cn}(T_rho P_{n,a},Q_n)^2`
` <= C^2`.                                                   (6.9)

This proves (6.3). QED.

## 7. What materially changed

The inherited theorem required a single edgewise-factorized graphon component. The new theorem permits an additional **shared global latent variable** with as many as

`exp(o(ell_n))`

source-visible states, while the graphon and signal strength may change from state to state and from `n` to `n`.

This removes three material restrictions simultaneously:

1. the kernel no longer has to be fixed in `n`;
2. the signal strength may depend on the global state;
3. the planted law need not itself have conditional edge independence after only the iid vertex latents are exposed — a shared discrete global state may couple all edges.

The reason this is still controllable is not a frame inversion. It is positivity of **even spectral traces**. A long even cycle acts as a source-scale detector for every globally selected component. If no component can be hidden behind weight `exp(-Theta(ell_n))`, bounded source LDA forces every component below the same critical spectral scale. Fixed noise then contracts all of them strictly into the subcritical diagram regime, and Jensen recombines them without loss.

At Kireeva's `D_n=(log n)^{1+Omega(1)}` scale, the theorem therefore covers polynomially many global regimes, and more generally any number `exp(o(D_n))` of comparably weighted regimes, while yielding bounded noisy polynomial advantage all the way through degree `c n`.

## 8. Mao calibration: what the counterexample must violate

Mao's construction uses

`K=Theta((log n)^r)`

and samples a uniformly random alternating bilinear form on `F_2^K`. The number of such forms is

`2^{K(K-1)/2}`,

so one form has weight

`p_B = 2^{-K(K-1)/2}`

and therefore

`log(1/p_B)=Theta(K^2)=Theta((log n)^{2r})`.                 (8.1)

Meanwhile Mao's exact low-degree matching scale is

`D_n=Theta((log n)^{r-1})`.                                 (8.2)

Hence

`log(1/p_B)/D_n = Theta((log n)^{r+1}) -> infinity`.         (8.3)

So the new subdegree exposure hypothesis fails by an enormous margin for Mao's explicit global algebraic latent variable. This does **not** claim that conditioning on the form turns Mao's law into the iid-vertex graphon model above; his fixed evaluation vectors plus random vertex permutation have a different finite-population dependence architecture. But it gives a concrete structural calibration: the successful counterexample carries global latent entropy far larger than the degree scale being fooled.

Thus the result identifies an additional axis, beyond row-centering or ordinary pair correlations, on which a corrected low-degree principle can be valid:

> bounded local graphon complexity **and** global latent complexity subexponential in the source degree.

## 9. Reusable positive-trace exposure lemma for P08/P09

The key argument is not graph-specific. Let a discrete global state `A=a` select a self-adjoint compact operator `T_a`, with weights `p_a`. For every integer `m>=1`,

`sum_a p_a Tr(T_a^{2m}) >= p_a ||T_a||_op^{2m}`             (9.1)

for each `a`.

Therefore any bound

`sum_a p_a Tr(T_a^{2m}) <= M_m`                             (9.2)

implies

`boxed{ ||T_a||_op <= (M_m/p_a)^(1/(2m)). }`                (9.3)

If `log(1/p_min)=o(m)`, low-entropy mixing is asymptotically invisible at the `2m`-th-root scale. This is directly reusable in Gaussian-process, covariance, phase-retrieval, and tensor settings whenever the observable contraction is an even positive trace/Schatten moment. It says that a low-entropy global mixture cannot hide a component with a larger operator scale than the observed even-moment scale.

## 10. Failed/superseded routes and exact remaining gap

A natural first route was to average componentwise diagram bounds directly without controlling the worst component. That fails: a very rare supercritical component can have enormous high-degree advantage, and Jensen alone does not prevent it from contaminating the mixture at degrees much larger than the source degree. The even-cycle exposure lemma is the missing coercive step.

A second possible route was to demand that the global state have only polynomial support. That is unnecessarily restrictive. The proof only uses

`log(1/p_min)=o(ell_n)`,

so `exp(o(D_n))` approximately balanced global states are admissible.

The exact remaining source gap is now sharper. Mao lies outside the theorem in at least two visible ways:

1. his global alternating-form entropy is `Theta((log n)^{2r})`, much larger than the fooled degree `Theta((log n)^{r-1})`;
2. conditional on that form, the vertices come from a fixed evaluation set followed by a random permutation, not iid latent sampling from a component graphon.

The next highest-value attack is therefore a **finite-population / sampling-without-replacement extension**. The target is to replace ordinary homomorphism densities by injective homomorphism densities, quantify the degree-one leakage caused by excluding already-used latent points, and prove that for degree `L=o(n)` the correction is controlled by a collision/excess expansion. Such a theorem would move materially closer to Mao's actual permutation-of-evaluation-points architecture. The second route, if that leakage cannot be controlled cleanly, is to prove a converse obstruction showing that any Mao-style rank construction necessarily requires global latent entropy at least `Omega(D log n)` or a comparable high-complexity algebraic state space.
