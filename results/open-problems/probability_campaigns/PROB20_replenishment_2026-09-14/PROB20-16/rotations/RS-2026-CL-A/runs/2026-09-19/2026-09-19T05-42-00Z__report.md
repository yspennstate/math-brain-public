# 3. B25 — COUNT THE SAME THING TWICE: exact posterior/Gram identity and replica-connected cancellation

The newest report identified the exact scalar pressure

## 3.1 Exact count-twice identity

Let `Q` be any null on a finite observation space. For latent states `z`, let positive unnormalized component weights be `w_z(x)`, let

\[
Z_z:=\mathbb E_Q w_z(X),
\]

and fix constants `c_z>0`. Put

\[
C:=\sum_z c_z Z_z,
\qquad
\pi_z^*:=\frac{c_z Z_z}{C},
\qquad
L_z:=\frac{w_z}{Z_z}.
\tag{3.1}
\]

The normalizer-balanced mixture likelihood is

\[
L_*(x)=\sum_z\pi_z^*L_z(x)=\frac{W(x)}{C},
\qquad
W(x):=\sum_z c_z w_z(x).
\tag{3.2}
\]

Counting its second moment by observations gives

\[
1+\chi^2(P_*\|Q)
=\frac{\mathbb E_Q W(X)^2}{C^2}.
\tag{3.3}
\]

Counting the same quantity by latent pairs gives

\[
\boxed{
1+\chi^2(P_*\|Q)
=\sum_{z,z'}\pi_z^*\pi_{z'}^* K(z,z'),
}
\tag{3.4}
\]

where

\[
K(z,z'):=\mathbb E_Q[L_zL_{z'}].
\tag{3.5}
\]

Indeed,

\[
\sum_{z,z'}\pi_z^*\pi_{z'}^*K(z,z')
=C^{-2}\sum_{z,z'}c_zc_{z'}\mathbb E_Q[w_zw_{z'}]
=C^{-2}\mathbb E_QW^2.
\]

Thus the aggregate free-energy and two-copy Gram pictures are not competing bounds; they are exactly the same scalar object.

## 3.2 Exact replica representation after bulk neutrality

Now write

\[
w_z(x)=w_z^{(0)}(x)e^{U_z(x)}
\tag{3.6}
\]

and assume exact baseline neutrality

\[
\sum_z c_z w_z^{(0)}(x)=C_0
\quad\text{for every }x.
\tag{3.7}
\]

Define the baseline posterior

\[
q_x(z)=\frac{c_z w_z^{(0)}(x)}{C_0}
\tag{3.8}
\]

and

\[
M(x):=\mathbb E_{q_x}e^{U_z(x)},
\qquad
F(x):=\log M(x).
\tag{3.9}
\]

Then `W=C_0 M`, so if

\[
Z_1:=\mathbb E_Q M(X),
\qquad
Z_2:=\mathbb E_Q M(X)^2,
\tag{3.10}
\]

we have the exact identity

\[
\boxed{
\Pi_2(P_*\|Q)=\log Z_2-2\log Z_1.
}
\tag{3.11}
\]

Moreover

\[
M(x)^2
=\mathbb E_{q_x\otimes q_x}
\exp\{U_z(x)+U_{z'}(x)\},
\tag{3.12}
\]

so `Z_2` is literally a two-latent-replica partition function while `Z_1` is the one-replica partition function. Equation (3.11) says that the relevant pressure is the **replica-connected free energy**: any contribution to `log Z_r` that is exactly linear in the replica number `r` cancels.

This is the exact cancellation the previous “bulk versus interface” language was missing.

## 3.3 Replica-linear cluster cancellation lemma

Assume `log Z_1` and `log Z_2` admit absolutely convergent connected-cluster expansions on a common physical dependency graph:

\[
\log Z_r=\sum_{\Gamma}\Phi_r(\Gamma),
\qquad r\in\{1,2\}.
\tag{3.13}
\]

Let `I` be an interface/defect set of physical sites. Assume:

1. **bulk replica linearity:**

\[
\boxed{
\Phi_2(\Gamma)=2\Phi_1(\Gamma)
\quad\text{whenever }\operatorname{supp}(\Gamma)\cap I=\varnothing;
}
\tag{3.14}
\]

2. **rooted absolute convergence:** for `r=1,2`,

\[
C_r:=\sup_v\sum_{\Gamma:\,v\in\operatorname{supp}(\Gamma)}
|\Phi_r(\Gamma)|<\infty.
\tag{3.15}
\]

Then (3.11) and (3.14) give

\[
\Pi_2
=\sum_{\Gamma:\,\operatorname{supp}(\Gamma)\cap I\ne\varnothing}
\bigl(\Phi_2(\Gamma)-2\Phi_1(\Gamma)\bigr).
\tag{3.16}
\]

Therefore

\[
\begin{aligned}
0\le\Pi_2
&\le
\sum_{v\in I}
\sum_{\Gamma:\,v\in\operatorname{supp}(\Gamma)}
\left(|\Phi_2(\Gamma)|+2|\Phi_1(\Gamma)|\right)\\
&\le
\boxed{(C_2+2C_1)|I|.}
\end{aligned}
\tag{3.17}
\]

This proves an exact interface-size pressure theorem once replica linearity is available.

### Approximate form

Define the rooted bulk replica mismatch

\[
\rho_n:=
\sup_v
\sum_{\substack{\Gamma:\,v\in\operatorname{supp}(\Gamma),\\
\operatorname{supp}(\Gamma)\cap I=\varnothing}}
|\Phi_2(\Gamma)-2\Phi_1(\Gamma)|.
\tag{3.18}
\]

If the physical graph has `N_n` sites, then the same argument gives

\[
\boxed{
\Pi_2
\le
(C_2+2C_1)|I|+N_n\rho_n.
}
\tag{3.19}
\]

Thus at the source scale it is enough that

\[
|I_n|=o(D_n),
\qquad
N_n\rho_n=o(D_n).
\tag{3.20}
\]

Exact bulk neutrality is the special case `rho_n=0`. Generic high temperature does **not** imply (3.20): an `O(1)` replica mismatch per bulk site accumulates to `Theta(N_n)`, far above polylogarithmic `D_n`.

# 5. B22 — READ THE FAILURES, NOT THE SUCCESSES: what cluster expansion must actually prove

The nearest classical high-temperature tools prove convergence, locality, concentration, mixing or correlation decay. The source-scale problem needs something more specific: **bulk replica linearity**.

## 5.1 Why ordinary high temperature is insufficient

A Kotecký–Preiss or Dobrushin condition can make every connected cluster coefficient small and summable. But if the two-replica coefficient differs from twice the one-replica coefficient by a fixed nonzero amount per site, then

\[
\log Z_2-2\log Z_1=\Theta(n),
\]

which is fatal when `D_n` is only polylogarithmic. The prior interval example from the preceding run and the biased-sign example (4.16) show this obstruction exactly.

Likewise, approximate tensorization or a Poincaré/log-Sobolev inequality controls mixing/concentration but does not force the two-replica free energy to be linear in replica number. De Roeck–Schütz-type quasi-local spectral flow controls how a weak perturbation spreads spatially, but by itself does not cancel a volume free-energy mismatch between `Z_2` and `Z_1^2`.

The missing condition is therefore not “high temperature” in isolation.

## 5.2 A factor-table hypothesis that is sufficient

For `r=1,2`, build the local graphical model representing `Z_r` after introducing `r` latent replicas. Let `I_n` be a set of defect/interface factor nodes. A directly checkable sufficient hypothesis is:

**Replica decoupling outside the interface.** After summing the bounded local latent neutralizing variables, every local factor table supported outside `I_n` in the `r=2` lifted model factorizes as the product of two copies of its `r=1` table, and no remaining compatibility/constraint factor outside `I_n` couples the two replicas.

Equivalently, away from `I_n` the two-replica polymer gas is literally the disjoint union of two one-replica gases. Therefore every connected cluster entirely outside `I_n` lies in exactly one replica, giving (3.14) automatically.

If the resulting one- and two-replica polymer gases satisfy a uniform Kotecký–Preiss/rooted absolute convergence bound, (3.17) gives

\[
\Pi_2\le(C_2+2C_1)|I_n|.
\tag{5.1}
\]

For bounded-arity factor tables this condition is finite/local to verify once the neutralizing latent variables and interface are specified. It is strong, but unlike a black-box `chi^2=o(e^{D_n})` assumption it explains *why* the volume cancels.

## 5.3 Approximate local decoupling

Exact factorization can be weakened. If the bulk two-replica lifted model differs from two one-replica copies by local mismatch factors whose induced connected cluster discrepancy has rooted norm `rho_n`, then (3.19) gives

\[
\Pi_2\le(C_2+2C_1)|I_n|+N_n\rho_n.
\tag{5.2}
\]

At polylogarithmic source degree this forces

\[
N_n\rho_n=o(D_n).
\tag{5.3}
\]

This is the exact failure mode to test in concrete factor models. A merely small **constant** high-temperature mismatch is not enough; the bulk mismatch must vanish at the much finer `D_n/N_n` scale, or cancel exactly by symmetry/quadrature.
