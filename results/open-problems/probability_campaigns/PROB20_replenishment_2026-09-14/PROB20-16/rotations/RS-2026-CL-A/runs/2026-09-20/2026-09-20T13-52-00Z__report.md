## 3. General finite-state graphon model

Fix a finite state space `S` and a full-support probability law `mu` on `S`. Let

`W:S x S -> R`

be symmetric, bounded by `B=max |W|`, and **row-centered**:

`sum_t mu(t) W(s,t)=0` for every `s`.

Let `T_W` be the self-adjoint operator on `L2(mu)`

`(T_W f)(s)=sum_t mu(t)W(s,t)f(t)`,

and write

`Lambda=||T_W||_op>0`.

For each `n`, sample latent labels `sigma_1,...,sigma_n iid mu`. Conditional on `sigma`, sample independent edge signs `X_ij in {-1,+1}` with

`E[X_ij|sigma]=theta_n W(sigma_i,sigma_j)`.

For all sufficiently large `n` assume `|theta_n| B<=1`. Let `P_n` be the resulting graph law and `Q_n=U_{binom(n,2)}` the uniform edge-sign null. This model is `S_n`-invariant.

Let `T_rho`, `0<rho<1`, denote product resampling noise with Walsh retention eigenvalue `rho`; conditional edge means become `rho theta_n W`.

## 4. Cycle pressure still controls the local spectral radius

For any simple edge set `F`,

`E_{P_n} chi_F = theta_n^{|F|} t(F,W)`,

where `t(F,W)` is the usual kernel homomorphism moment. For a simple cycle `C_l`,

`t(C_l,W)=Tr(T_W^l)`.

For even `l`, all eigenvalue powers are nonnegative, hence

`Tr(T_W^l) >= Lambda^l`.

There are `(n)_l/(2l)` unoriented simple `l`-cycles. Since Walsh characters are orthonormal under the uniform null,

`Adv_{Q_n}^{<=D}(P_n,Q_n)^2`

is the squared Euclidean norm of all nonconstant moments through degree `D`. Therefore, for every even `l<=D`,

`A_{n,D}^2 >= [(n)_l/(2l)] * theta_n^{2l} * Lambda^{2l}`.

Exactly as in the rank-one checkpoint, if `sup_n A_{n,D_n}<infinity` and there is an even `l_n<=D_n` with

`l_n -> infinity`,  `l_n^2/n -> 0`,

then

**Lemma 4.1 (finite-state cycle pressure).**

`limsup_n n theta_n^2 Lambda^2 <= 1`.

This part uses only the cycle tower and is valid for every fixed finite-state row-centered kernel.

## 5. The new nonlinear quantity: overlap-entropy ratio

Let `nu=mu otimes mu` on the replica state space `Y=S x S`. Define the replica kernel

`K((a,b),(c,d)) = W(a,c) W(b,d)`.

Row-centering of `W` implies `K nu=0`. For a probability law `pi` on `Y`, put

`Q_K(pi)=sum_{y,y'} pi(y)pi(y')K(y,y')`

`       =sum_{y,y'} (pi-nu)(y)(pi-nu)(y')K(y,y')`.

Define the finite nonlinear overlap constant

`E_ent(W,mu) = sup_{pi != nu} Q_K(pi) / [2 D(pi||nu)]`,

where negative numerators are harmless and the local value at `nu` is interpreted by continuity. Because `Y` is finite and `nu` has full support, this supremum is finite.

A small perturbation in a top eigenfunction direction shows

**Lemma 5.1 (local lower bound).**

`E_ent(W,mu) >= Lambda^2`.

Indeed, if `T_W f=lambda f`, `E_mu f=0`, `||f||_2=1`, then `h(a,b)=f(a)f(b)` is a replica eigenfunction with eigenvalue `lambda^2`. For `pi_epsilon=nu(1+epsilon h)`,

`Q_K(pi_epsilon)=epsilon^2 lambda^2`,

while

`2D(pi_epsilon||nu)=epsilon^2+O(epsilon^3)`.

Thus the ratio tends to `lambda^2`; maximize over `|lambda|=Lambda`.

The ratio

`R(W,mu):=E_ent(W,mu)/Lambda^2 >=1`

measures exactly how much **global replica-overlap pressure** can exceed the local Hessian/spectral radius seen by long cycles.

## 6. Finite-type entropy MGF lemma

The following elementary lemma is the main technical bridge.

**Lemma 6.1.** Let `nu` have full support on a fixed finite set of size `d`. If `hat_nu_n` is the empirical law of `n` iid samples from `nu`, then for every fixed `0<=gamma<1`,

`sup_n E exp[gammma n D(hat_nu_n||nu)] < infinity`.

(`gammma` here is the scalar `gamma`; the extra `m` is only typographical in this sentence.)

**Proof.** Split empirical types into a fixed neighborhood of `nu` and its complement. Outside the neighborhood, compactness gives `D(p||nu)>=d_0>0`; the method-of-types bound `P(hat_nu=p)<=exp[-nD(p||nu)]` and at most `(n+1)^{d-1}` types make the weighted sum exponentially small.

Inside the neighborhood all coordinates satisfy `p_i>=nu_i/2`. Stirling gives uniformly

`P(hat_nu=p) <= C_nu n^{-(d-1)/2} exp[-nD(p||nu)]`.

Strong convexity on that neighborhood gives

`D(p||nu)>=c_nu ||p-nu||_2^2`.

After multiplying by `exp[gamma nD]`, parameterize the first `d-1` counts by their integer deviations from `n nu`. The remaining sum is bounded by

`C n^{-(d-1)/2} sum_{k in Z^{d-1}} exp[-c(1-gamma)||k||^2/n]`,

which is uniformly bounded by comparison with a Gaussian integral. QED.

## 7. Main positive theorem: finite-state overlap-entropy transfer

Two independent latent replicas `sigma,tau` give `Y_i=(sigma_i,tau_i) iid nu`. The exact noisy second moment is

`1+chi^2(T_rho P_n || Q_n)`

`= E_Y prod_{i<j} [1+rho^2 theta_n^2 K(Y_i,Y_j)]`.

Using `1+x<=e^x`,

`1+chi^2 <= E exp[rho^2 theta_n^2 sum_{i<j}K(Y_i,Y_j)]`.

Let `hat_nu_n` be the empirical replica law and put

`c_n=n rho^2 theta_n^2`.

Because `K nu=0`,

`sum_{i<j}K(Y_i,Y_j)`

`= (n^2/2) Q_K(hat_nu_n) - (n/2)sum_y hat_nu_n(y)K(y,y)`.

Since `|K(y,y)|<=B^2`,

`1+chi^2`

`<= exp[c_n B^2/2] * E exp[(c_n n/2) Q_K(hat_nu_n)]`

`<= exp[c_n B^2/2] * E exp[c_n E_ent(W,mu) n D(hat_nu_n||nu)]`.

Cycle pressure gives

`c_n E_ent(W,mu)`

`= rho^2 [n theta_n^2 Lambda^2] * R(W,mu)`

`<= rho^2 R(W,mu) + o(1)`.

Therefore:

### Theorem 7.1 (finite-state overlap-entropy transfer; PROVED PARTIAL)

Assume the finite-state row-centered graphon model above, bounded LDA through degrees `D_n` containing even `l_n->infinity` with `l_n^2/n->0`, and fixed retention `0<rho<1`. If

`rho^2 R(W,mu) < 1`,

then

`sup_n chi^2(T_rho P_n || Q_n) < infinity`

(after discarding finitely many initial `n`). Consequently, for every `0<=phi_n<=1`,

`E_{T_rho P_n} phi_n <= C sqrt(E_{Q_n} phi_n)`,

so no test whatsoever can strongly distinguish the noisy planted law from the uniform null.

This is a genuine finite-state/fixed-rank generalization of the 12:45 theorem. It does not require rank one; it requires that fixed noise beat the **global** overlap-entropy ratio rather than merely the local spectral radius.

### Rank-one recovery

For the old Rademacher rank-one kernel `W(s,t)=st`, the replica kernel depends only on `z=st in {-1,+1}`. If `m=E_pi z`, then `Q_K(pi)=m^2`. Data processing followed by the binary Pinsker inequality gives

`D(pi||nu) >= D(Bern((1+m)/2)||Bern(1/2)) >= m^2/2`.

Hence `E_ent<=1`, while Lemma 5.1 gives `E_ent>=Lambda^2=1`. Therefore

`R=1` exactly,

and Theorem 7.1 recovers the prior “every fixed rho<1” rank-one conclusion.

## 8. Why local cycle control is not enough: the Potts kernel

Now take `S=Z_q`, `mu` uniform, and

`W_q(a,b)=q * 1_{a=b} - 1`.

It is row-centered. On the mean-zero subspace of `L2(mu)`, `T_{W_q}` is the identity, so

`Lambda=1`.

Consider the replica law `pi_diag` uniform on the diagonal `{(a,a):a in Z_q}`. Then

`D(pi_diag||nu)=log q`,

while

`Q_K(pi_diag)=E_{a,b uniform} W_q(a,b)^2 = q-1`.

Therefore

**Proposition 8.1 (nonlinear overlap gap).**

`R(W_q,mu)=E_ent(W_q,mu) >= (q-1)/(2 log q)`.

For `q>=4`, this lower bound already exceeds one. Thus the global overlap pressure can be strictly larger than the local cycle/Hessian threshold. This is the finite-state obstruction that the rank-one calculation cannot see.

## 9. Full low-degree bound for the Potts graphon below the local threshold

The preceding observation is only useful for the source problem if bounded LDA really persists through polylogarithmic degree. It does.

Set

`theta_n^2 = alpha/n`,  with fixed `0<alpha<1`.

For an edge set `F`, orient its edges arbitrarily. The character identity on the finite abelian group `Z_q` gives

`W_q(a,b)=sum_{chi != 1} chi(a-b)`.

Expanding over edges and averaging vertex labels shows:

**Lemma 9.1 (flow moment identity).**

`t(F,W_q)` is the number of nowhere-zero `Z_q`-flows on `F` (equivalently on its dual character group). In particular:

- if `F` has a bridge, `t(F,W_q)=0`;
- if `F` has `v` nonisolated vertices, `e` edges and `c` connected components,

`t(F,W_q) <= q^{e-v+c}`;

- for a simple cycle, `t(C_l,W_q)=q-1`.

Because the null Walsh basis is orthonormal,

`A_{n,D}^2 = sum_{1<=|F|<=D} (alpha/n)^{|F|} t(F,W_q)^2`.

To bound the sum, decompose every nonzero-flow graph into connected bridgeless components. For nonnegative component weights, the polymer partition function is bounded by the exponential of the sum of connected weights.

The connected excess-zero graphs are exactly simple cycles, whose total weight is

`S_cycle <= ((q-1)^2/2) * sum_{l>=3} alpha^l/l`

`= ((q-1)^2/2) * [-log(1-alpha)-alpha-alpha^2/2] < infinity`.

For connected bridgeless graphs with excess

`r=e-v>=1`, minimum degree is at least two. Suppressing maximal degree-two paths leaves a multigraph kernel with at most `2r` branch vertices and at most `3r` kernel edges. A crude encoding gives at most `(6r)^{6r}` kernels. Subdivision lengths summing to `e<=D` contribute at most `D^{3r}` possibilities. Each unlabeled shape has at most `n^v` embeddings, and Lemma 9.1 gives `t^2<=q^{2(r+1)}`. Therefore the total connected excess contribution is at most

`S_excess <= q^2 D sum_{r=1}^D [C_q D^9/n]^r`.

Hence, whenever

`D_n^10/n -> 0`, 

we have `S_excess=o(1)` and therefore

### Theorem 9.2 (Potts bounded-LDA regime; PROVED PARTIAL)

For fixed `q` and fixed `0<alpha<1`, if `theta_n^2=alpha/n` and `D_n^10/n->0`, then

`sup_n Adv_{Q_n}^{<=D_n}(P_n,Q_n) < infinity`.

More explicitly,

`limsup A_{n,D_n}^2`

`<= exp{ ((q-1)^2/2)[-log(1-alpha)-alpha-alpha^2/2] } - 1`.

Every fixed-power polylogarithmic degree, including the source scale `(log n)^{1+Omega(1)}` when interpreted as a fixed polylog power, lies in this regime.

## 10. Decisive all-test obstruction after weak fixed noise

The Potts family shows that Theorem 7.1’s nonlinear condition is not just proof decoration.

Let

`c = alpha rho^2`.

After noise, conditional means are `sqrt(c/n) W_q(sigma_i,sigma_j)`. For a candidate labeling `z in Z_q^n`, define the score

`S_z(X)=sum_{i<j} W_q(z_i,z_j) X_ij`.

Restrict to the near-balanced class

`B_n={z: |N_a(z)-n/q|<=n^{2/3} for every a}`.

Uniformly for `z in B_n`,

`V_z:=sum_{i<j} W_q(z_i,z_j)^2`

`= ((q-1)/2)n^2 + o(n^2)`.

Under the null, weighted-Rademacher Hoeffding gives

`P_Q(S_z >= t) <= exp[-t^2/(2V_z)]`.

Since `|B_n|<=q^n`, for every constant `tau` with

`tau > sqrt((q-1) log q)`,

`Q_n(max_{z in B_n} S_z >= tau n^{3/2}) -> 0`.

Under the noisy planted law, the true latent labeling `sigma` lies in `B_n` with probability `1-o(1)`, and conditional on `sigma`,

`E[S_sigma|sigma]=sqrt(c/n) V_sigma`

`= [sqrt(c)(q-1)/2+o(1)] n^{3/2}`.

Its lower tail around this mean is `exp[-Omega(n)]` by the same bounded-summand estimate. Therefore if

`sqrt(c)(q-1)/2 > sqrt((q-1) log q)`,

i.e.

`c > 4 log q/(q-1)`,

one may choose `tau` strictly between the two coefficients and obtain a strong distinguisher.

For `q>=11`,

`4 log q/(q-1) < 1`.

Choose constants

`4 log q/(q-1) < c < alpha < 1`

and set `rho=sqrt(c/alpha)<1`. Then all of the following hold simultaneously:

1. the pre-noise planted law is `S_n`-invariant with uniform product null;
2. by Theorem 9.2 its LDA stays bounded through every source-scale fixed polylog degree;
3. `rho<1` is a fixed positive resampling/noise level;
4. the exhaustive balanced-label score test strongly distinguishes after noise.

### Corollary 10.1 (all-test strengthening is false for general finite-state graphons)

The rank-one theorem from 12:45 cannot be generalized to all finite-state row-centered graphons by replacing rank one with only “bounded LDA + finite latent state + permutation invariance.” For `q>=11` the Potts family above satisfies bounded polylog-degree LDA but is strongly distinguishable after weak fixed noise by an exhaustive test.

This is **not** a counterexample to Kireeva’s polynomial-degree question or to Mao’s polynomial-time boundary: the score maximization over `q^n` labelings is exponential-time. Its role is sharper. It proves that the remaining source implication is genuinely algorithmic/polynomial and cannot be bypassed by aiming for an unrestricted all-test contiguity theorem over natural finite-state graphons.

## 12. Reusable Gaussian/tensor/correlation lesson

The finite-state proof isolates a general principle useful to P08/P09:

- low-degree cycle/contraction information controls the **local Hessian** of a replica pressure;
- an all-test `chi^2` conclusion needs a **global entropy-versus-overlap inequality**;
- a first-order large-deviation overlap can invalidate the global conclusion even when the local spectral radius is subcritical.

For empirical finite-type replicas, the exact sufficient inequality is

`Q_K(pi) <= 2 kappa D(pi||nu)` for all `pi`,

because the finite-type entropy MGF lemma then controls the whole partition function whenever the effective coupling times `kappa` is below one.

For Gaussian/tensor roles, the analogous target is a sharp sub-Gaussian/log-Sobolev or entropy-energy inequality for the replica overlap, not merely a covariance eigenvalue bound. This preserves mixture cancellations while detecting possible nonlocal overlap states.
