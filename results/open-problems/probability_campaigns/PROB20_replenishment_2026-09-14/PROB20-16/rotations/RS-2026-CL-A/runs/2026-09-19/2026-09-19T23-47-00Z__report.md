## 2. The exact missing implication after the quotient-shell work

The predecessor solved an important geometric problem but left a harder implication:

> how does one turn `Q`-based bounded low-degree advantage into a spectral statement that a natural noise operator can actually damp, without inverting a huge monomial/adapted Gram matrix?

The key observation is that one can insert a **reference chaos** `R` between the source polynomial space and the noise. What is needed from `Q` is only an *upper norm inflation bound* on low-degree polynomials relative to `R`; no global pointwise lower bound `dQ/dR >= m` is needed.

This is materially weaker in direction than the previous quotient-shell theorem. The old lower-density hypothesis controlled every function globally. The new bridge only controls how much `Q` can enlarge the norm of a degree-`d` polynomial, and only for degrees actually used.

# 3. B18 — SAY IT IN ANOTHER LANGUAGE: reference-chaos formulation

Let `R_n` be a reference probability space for which

\[
L^2(R_n)=\bigoplus_{k\ge0}\mathcal H_{n,k},
\qquad \mathcal H_{n,0}=\operatorname{span}\{1\},
\tag{3.1}
\]

is an orthogonal polynomial/chaos decomposition. Write `Pi_{n,k}` for the orthogonal projection onto `H_{n,k}` and

\[
\mathcal P_{n,\le d}=\bigoplus_{k=0}^{d}\mathcal H_{n,k}.
\tag{3.2}
\]

Assume there is a reversible Markov semigroup `T_{n,t}` preserving `R_n` and acting diagonally by

\[
T_{n,t}f=e^{-tk}f
\quad\text{for }f\in\mathcal H_{n,k}.
\tag{3.3}
\]

Examples are independent coordinate refresh on a product Boolean cube and the Ornstein–Uhlenbeck semigroup on Gaussian space.

Let `P_n,Q_n << R_n` with densities

\[
p_n=\frac{dP_n}{dR_n},
\qquad
q_n=\frac{dQ_n}{dR_n},
\qquad
\Delta_n=p_n-q_n.
\tag{3.4}
\]

For `d>=1`, define the **null low-degree norm inflation**

\[
\Lambda_n(d)
:=
\sup_{0\ne f\in\mathcal P_{n,\le d}}
\frac{\|f\|_{L^2(Q_n)}^2}{\|f\|_{L^2(R_n)}^2}.
\tag{3.5}
\]

This is a one-sided comparison. It asks only that `Q_n` not magnify degree-`d` polynomials too much relative to the reference geometry. It does not require any lower comparison, a Riesz basis, or a global density floor.

Define the reference cumulative signal mass

\[
S_n(d)
:=
\sum_{k=1}^{d}\|\Pi_{n,k}\Delta_n\|_{L^2(R_n)}^2
=
\|\Pi_{n,1:d}\Delta_n\|_{L^2(R_n)}^2.
\tag{3.6}
\]

### Lemma 3.1 — source LDA controls reference-chaos mass under one-sided inflation

For every `d`,

\[
\boxed{
S_n(d)
\le
\Lambda_n(d)
\operatorname{Adv}_{Q_n}^{\le d}(P_n,Q_n)^2.
}
\tag{3.7}
\]

### Proof

Let

\[
g=\Pi_{n,1:d}\Delta_n.
\]

If `g=0`, there is nothing to prove. Otherwise `g` is a nonconstant degree-`d` polynomial. Since `Delta_n` has mean zero under `R_n`, orthogonality gives

\[
\mathbb E_{P_n}g-\mathbb E_{Q_n}g
=
\langle\Delta_n,g\rangle_{R_n}
=
\|g\|_{R_n}^2.
\tag{3.8}
\]

Moreover

\[
\operatorname{Var}_{Q_n}(g)
\le
\mathbb E_{Q_n}g^2
\le
\Lambda_n(d)\|g\|_{R_n}^2.
\tag{3.9}
\]

Testing the source advantage with `g` therefore yields

\[
\operatorname{Adv}_{Q_n}^{\le d}(P_n,Q_n)
\ge
\frac{\|g\|_{R_n}}{\sqrt{\Lambda_n(d)}}.
\]

Square and rearrange. `square`

This is the missing functional implication in a basis-free form. It transfers a `Q`-normalized low-degree statement into the exact spectral coordinates seen by the reference noise.

# 4. B18 main theorem — quantitative noisy total-variation implication

Let `D_n -> infinity`. Define

\[
A_n:=\operatorname{Adv}_{Q_n}^{\le D_n}(P_n,Q_n),
\tag{4.1}
\]

\[
\eta_n
:=
\sup_{1\le d\le D_n}
\frac{\log_+\Lambda_n(d)}{d},
\tag{4.2}
\]

and the global reference-`L^2` complexity exponent

\[
B_n:=\|p_n-q_n\|_{L^2(R_n)}^2,
\qquad
\beta_n:=\frac{\log_+B_n}{D_n}.
\tag{4.3}
\]

### Theorem 4.1 — reference-chaos noisy indistinguishability theorem

Assume:

1. **bounded source LDA:** `sup_n A_n < infinity`;
2. **fixed-degree escape:** for every fixed integer `K`,
   \[
   \operatorname{Adv}_{Q_n}^{\le K}(P_n,Q_n)\to0;
   \tag{4.4}
   \]
3. **subcritical null inflation at noise level `t`:**
   \[
   \limsup_n\eta_n<2t;
   \tag{4.5}
   \]
4. **subcritical global reference complexity:**
   \[
   \limsup_n\beta_n<2t.
   \tag{4.6}
   \]

Then, after applying the common reference-noise kernel `T_{n,t}` to both laws,

\[
\boxed{
\|T_{n,t}P_n-T_{n,t}Q_n\|_{\mathrm{TV}}\to0.
}
\tag{4.7}
\]

In particular, no statistic of any computational complexity can strongly distinguish the two noised laws.

If instead

\[
\eta_n\to0,
\qquad
\beta_n\to0,
\tag{4.8}
\]

then `(4.7)` holds for **every fixed `t>0`**.

### Proof

Let

\[
a_{n,k}=\|\Pi_{n,k}\Delta_n\|_{R_n}^2.
\tag{4.9}
\]

By reversibility and `(3.3)`, the noised density difference relative to `R_n` is `T_{n,t}\Delta_n`, and

\[
\|T_{n,t}\Delta_n\|_{R_n}^2
=
\sum_{k\ge1}e^{-2tk}a_{n,k}.
\tag{4.10}
\]

Fix `K`. Split the sum into `k<=K`, `K<k<=D_n`, and `k>D_n`.

**Low fixed degrees.** By Lemma 3.1,

\[
\sum_{k\le K}a_{n,k}
\le
\Lambda_n(K)
\operatorname{Adv}_{Q_n}^{\le K}(P_n,Q_n)^2.
\tag{4.11}
\]

For fixed `K`, `(4.5)` makes `Lambda_n(K)` uniformly bounded for all large `n`, while `(4.4)` sends the advantage to zero. Hence the low piece tends to zero.

**Intermediate source degrees.** Monotonicity of source LDA and Lemma 3.1 give, for every `k<=D_n`,

\[
a_{n,k}
\le S_n(k)
\le
\Lambda_n(k)A_n^2
\le
A_*^2e^{\eta_n k},
\tag{4.12}
\]

where `A_*:=sup_n A_n`. Choose `delta>0` with

\[
\limsup_n\eta_n\le2t-2\delta.
\]

Then for all large `n`,

\[
\sum_{K<k\le D_n}e^{-2tk}a_{n,k}
\le
A_*^2\sum_{k>K}e^{-2\delta k}.
\tag{4.13}
\]

The right side tends to zero as `K->infinity`, uniformly in large `n`.

**Tail above the source cutoff.** By `(4.3)`,

\[
\sum_{k>D_n}e^{-2tk}a_{n,k}
\le
B_ne^{-2tD_n}
=
\exp\{-(2t-\beta_n)D_n\}.
\tag{4.14}
\]

Condition `(4.6)` sends this to zero.

First take `n->infinity`, then `K->infinity`, in `(4.10)`–`(4.14)`. Therefore

\[
\|T_{n,t}\Delta_n\|_{R_n}\to0.
\]

Finally,

\[
\|T_{n,t}P_n-T_{n,t}Q_n\|_{TV}
=
\frac12\|T_{n,t}\Delta_n\|_{L^1(R_n)}
\le
\frac12\|T_{n,t}\Delta_n\|_{L^2(R_n)}
\to0.
\]

`square`

## 4.1 Why condition (4.4) is not cosmetic

The source only says the polylog-degree advantage remains bounded. That alone cannot force **TV convergence to zero** after fixed noise, even in the easiest product model. Take `R_n=Q_n` uniform on a Boolean cube and

\[
p_n(x)=1+\theta x_1,
\qquad 0<|\theta|<1.
\]

Then `Adv^{<=D_n}=|theta|` for every `D_n>=1`, so the source headline condition is bounded, but after fixed coordinate refresh with eigenvalue `e^{-t}` the density difference is `theta e^{-t}x_1`, and

\[
\|T_tP_n-Q_n\|_{TV}
=\frac{|\theta|e^{-t}}2>0.
\]

Thus a theorem whose conclusion is full statistical indistinguishability needs either fixed-degree escape such as `(4.4)` or a weaker conclusion than TV convergence. This is separate from Mao and is a simple necessary calibration of what “bounded” alone can mean.

# 5. B6 — DEFORM TO A POINT YOU CAN SOLVE: Boolean nonproduct nulls without a global density floor

The predecessor's quotient-shell theorem assumed the global pointwise lower bound

\[
\frac{dQ}{dU}\ge m>0.
\]

That is too strong for many sparse Gibbs laws because a global density ratio can be exponentially small in ambient dimension even when every local interaction is weak.

The reference-chaos theorem needs no lower bound. On the Boolean cube with `R=U`, write

\[
q=\frac{dQ}{dU}.
\]

Let `Pi_{<=j}^U` denote uniform Walsh projection. If `f` has degree at most `d`, then `f^2` has degree at most `2d`, hence exactly

\[
\mathbb E_Q f^2
=
\mathbb E_U[qf^2]
=
\mathbb E_U[(\Pi_{\le2d}^Uq)f^2].
\tag{5.1}
\]

Therefore

\[
\boxed{
\Lambda_n(d)
\le
\left\|\Pi_{\le2d}^Uq_n\right\|_{\infty}.
}
\tag{5.2}
\]

No positivity of the truncated polynomial is required for this upper estimate; `(5.1)` is positive because it equals `E_Q f^2`, and the pointwise upper bound is enough.

### Corollary 5.1 — explicit Boolean sufficient conditions

Let `P_n,Q_n` be arbitrary full-support laws on `{−1,+1}^{N_n}`, with densities `p_n,q_n` relative to uniform `U_n`, and let `D_n=(log n)^{1+Omega(1)}`. Suppose:

1. `Adv_{Q_n}^{<=D_n}(P_n,Q_n)=O(1)`;
2. for every fixed `K`, `Adv_{Q_n}^{<=K}(P_n,Q_n)->0`;
3. the low-degree null-density envelope satisfies
   \[
   \sup_{1\le d\le D_n}
   \frac1d
   \log_+\left\|\Pi_{\le2d}^{U_n}q_n\right\|_\infty
   \to0;
   \tag{5.3}
   \]
4. the pair has subexponential reference-`L^2` complexity at the source scale,
   \[
   \log_+\|p_n-q_n\|_{L^2(U_n)}^2=o(D_n).
   \tag{5.4}
   \]

Then for every fixed positive coordinate-refresh noise time `t`,

\[
\boxed{
\|T_tP_n-T_tQ_n\|_{TV}\to0.
}
\tag{5.5}
\]

This theorem requires neither permutation invariance nor a product null. It is a **PROVED PARTIAL** answer to Kireeva: it supplies explicit source-valid conditions on arbitrary Boolean distribution pairs under which the bounded polylog source LDA, together with the necessary fixed-degree escape, gives a rigorous and in fact statistical noisy indistinguishability consequence.

Its scope is transparent. The price for dropping symmetry/product structure is the null-envelope condition `(5.3)` and global pair-complexity condition `(5.4)`. These are not definitions of the desired conclusion: they are unnoised norm-growth conditions that can in principle be checked by Fourier/cluster estimates.

## 5.1 Quantitative single-noise version

The theorem does not require both exponents to vanish when one only asks about one fixed noise level. If

\[
\bar\eta
:=
\limsup_n\sup_{d\le D_n}
\frac{\log_+\|\Pi_{\le2d}q_n\|_\infty}{d},
\]

and

\[
\bar\beta
:=
\limsup_n
\frac{\log_+\|p_n-q_n\|_2^2}{D_n},
\]

then the same proof yields TV convergence for every `t` satisfying

\[
\boxed{2t>\max\{\bar\eta,\bar\beta\}.}
\tag{5.6}
\]

This makes the structural tradeoff explicit: noise energy `2t` must beat both low-degree null-norm inflation and total pair-complexity growth per source cutoff.

# 6. Mao stress test becomes a quantitative complexity lower bound

Mao's counterexample has `Q_n=U_n`. Thus

\[

\qquad
\eta_n=0,
\]

and every fixed-degree advantage is exactly zero for all sufficiently large `n` because the exact marginal matching extends through `D_n->infinity`.

\[
e^{-t_r}=1-\epsilon_r

for the corresponding Fourier attenuation convention. The rank test strongly distinguishes after this noise, so the noised total variation tends to one rather than zero.

\[
\boxed{
\limsup_{n\to\infty}

\log_+\|p_n-1\|_{L^2(U_n)}^2
\ge 2t_r.

\tag{6.1}

In the uniform-null case the left side is

\[
\frac1{D_n}\log\chi^2(P_n\|U_n)
