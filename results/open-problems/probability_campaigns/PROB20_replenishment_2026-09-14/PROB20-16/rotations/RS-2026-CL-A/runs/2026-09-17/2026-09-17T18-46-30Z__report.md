## 3. The second-instantiation model: a dense Rademacher two-community graph mixture

Let

\[
\Omega_n=\{-1,+1\}^{E(K_n)},
\qquad
Q_n=\operatorname{Unif}(\Omega_n),
\]

so `Q_n` is `G(n,1/2)` after identifying an edge bit with `x_{ij}=2A_{ij}-1`.

For a signal parameter `lambda_n>=0`, sample latent labels

\[
\sigma_1,\ldots,\sigma_n\stackrel{\mathrm{iid}}{\sim}\operatorname{Unif}\{\pm1\},
\]

and then, conditionally on `sigma`, sample edges independently with

\[
\mathbb P(x_{ij}=+1\mid\sigma)
=
\frac{1+\mu_n\sigma_i\sigma_j}{2},
\qquad
\mu_n:=\frac{\lambda_n}{\sqrt n}.
\tag{3.1}
\]

For large `n` this is valid whenever `lambda_n=O(1)`. Call the resulting law `P_n`. It is invariant under vertex relabeling but **not** under the full permutation group of the `N=\binom n2` edge coordinates, so it is not merely the fully coordinate-exchangeable Boolean setting of the first Hsieh et al. theorem.

For an edge set `S`, write

\[
\chi_S(x)=\prod_{e\in S}x_e.
\]

The characters form the orthonormal Fourier basis of `L^2(Q_n)`. Since conditional edge means are `mu_n sigma_i sigma_j`,

\[
\begin{aligned}
\widehat L_n(S)
&:=\mathbb E_{P_n}\chi_S(X)\\
&=\mu_n^{|S|}\,
\mathbb E_\sigma
\prod_{\{i,j\}\in S}\sigma_i\sigma_j.
\end{aligned}
\tag{3.2}
\]

The spin expectation is `1` exactly when every vertex has even degree in the graph `([n],S)`, and is `0` otherwise. Hence

\[
\boxed{
\widehat L_n(S)
=
\begin{cases}
\mu_n^{|S|},&S\text{ Eulerian (all degrees even)},\\
0,&\text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}
}
\tag{3.3}
\]

Therefore the source low-degree advantage satisfies the exact identity

\[
\boxed{
\left(\operatorname{Adv}^{\le D}(P_n,Q_n)\right)^2
=
\sum_{\substack{S\neq\varnothing:\,S\text{ Eulerian}\\|S|\le D}}
\left(\frac{\lambda_n^2}{n}\right)^{|S|}.
}
\tag{3.4}
\]

After independent resampling noise with Fourier retention `rho in [0,1]`,

\[
\boxed{
\chi^2(T_\rho P_n\|Q_n)
=
\sum_{\substack{S\neq\varnothing\\S\text{ Eulerian}}}
\left(\frac{\rho^2\lambda_n^2}{n}\right)^{|S|}.
}
\tag{3.5}
\]

These are exact finite-`n` identities, not asymptotic heuristics.

## 4. B7 — TAKE THE LIMIT: the exact primitive-pressure partition function

Define

\[
Z_n(t):=
\sum_{S\text{ Eulerian}}t^{|S|}.
\tag{4.1}
\]

Expanding a spin product gives the classical even-subgraph identity

\[
\boxed{
Z_n(t)
=
\mathbb E_{\sigma}
\prod_{1\le i<j\le n}
(1+t\sigma_i\sigma_j).
}
\tag{4.2}
\]

Indeed, expanding the product and averaging over independent Rademacher spins kills precisely those edge sets having an odd-degree vertex.

Let

\[
M_n=\sum_{i=1}^n\sigma_i,
\qquad
N=\binom n2,
\qquad
a=\operatorname{arctanh}(t).
\]

Since

\[
\sum_{i<j}\sigma_i\sigma_j=\frac{M_n^2-n}{2},
\]

and the counts of `sigma_i sigma_j=+1` and `-1` pairs are obtained by splitting `N` according to this sum, (4.2) becomes

\[
\boxed{
Z_n(t)
=(1-t^2)^{N/2}
\exp\!\left(-\frac{na}{2}\right)
\mathbb E\exp\!\left(\frac{aM_n^2}{2}\right).
}
\tag{4.3}
\]

This reduces the complete high-degree likelihood-ratio second moment to a **one-dimensional magnetization pressure**.

Now put

\[
t=\frac{q}{n},\qquad q\ge0,
\qquad c_n:=n\operatorname{arctanh}(q/n).
\]

For a standard Gaussian `G`,

\[
\exp\!\left(\frac{aM_n^2}{2}\right)
=
\mathbb E_G\exp(\sqrt a\,G M_n).
\]

Using `cosh u<=e^{u^2/2}`,

\[
\mathbb E_\sigma e^{\sqrt a\,G M_n}
=\cosh(\sqrt a\,G)^n
\le e^{naG^2/2}.
\]

Therefore, whenever `c_n<1`,

\[
\mathbb E\exp\!\left(\frac{aM_n^2}{2}\right)
\le
\mathbb E e^{c_nG^2/2}
=(1-c_n)^{-1/2}.
\tag{4.4}
\]

The two prefactors in (4.3) are at most `1`, so

\[
\boxed{
Z_n(q/n)
\le
(1-n\operatorname{arctanh}(q/n))^{-1/2}
\quad\text{whenever }n\operatorname{arctanh}(q/n)<1.
}
\tag{4.5}
\]

Consequently, if `q<=1-delta` eventually for some fixed `delta>0`, then `Z_n(q/n)=O_delta(1)` uniformly.

There is also a sharp limit. If `q_n->q<1`, then `c_n->q`, the sub-Gaussian estimate above gives uniform exponential integrability, and `M_n/sqrt n=>N(0,1)`. Taking limits in (4.3),

\[
\boxed{
Z_n(q_n/n)
\longrightarrow
\frac{\exp(-q/2-q^2/4)}{\sqrt{1-q}}.
}
\tag{4.6}
\]

The expansion begins at order `q^3`, as it must because the smallest nonempty Eulerian simple graph is a triangle. This is the familiar dense-SBM/signed-cycle high-temperature pressure, and its classical nature is acknowledged rather than claimed as new.

## 5. Source-style implication for this model: bounded low degree forces strict subcriticality

The point of the breadth test is not merely to re-prove known contiguity. It is to check whether the **source premise itself** forces the structural regime in which the full tail is bounded.

Assume

\[
D_n\to\infty,
\qquad
D_n=o(\sqrt n),
\tag{5.1}
\]

which in particular contains the source scale `D_n=(log n)^{1+Omega(1)}`. Suppose

\[
\sup_n\operatorname{Adv}^{\le D_n}(P_n,Q_n)<\infty.
\tag{5.2}
\]

For every `3<=k<=D_n`, every simple `k`-cycle is Eulerian, and there are

\[
\frac{(n)_k}{2k}
\]

such cycles. Hence (3.4) gives

\[
\left(\operatorname{Adv}^{\le D_n}\right)^2
\ge
\sum_{k=3}^{D_n}
\frac{(n)_k}{2k\,n^k}\lambda_n^{2k}.
\tag{5.3}
\]

### Proposition 5.1 — bounded low-degree advantage implies a uniform gap below the primitive-cycle threshold

Under (5.1)–(5.2),

\[
\boxed{
\limsup_{n\to\infty}\lambda_n^2<1.
}
\tag{5.4}
\]

**Proof.** Suppose not. Then there is a subsequence `n_r` with `lambda_{n_r}^2 -> lambda_*^2>=1` after passing to a further subsequence (if the parameters are unbounded, the contradiction is only easier). Fix an arbitrary integer `K>=3`. For all sufficiently large `r`, `D_{n_r}>=K`, and for each fixed `k<=K`,

\[
\frac{(n_r)_k}{n_r^k}\to1,
\qquad
\lambda_{n_r}^{2k}\to\lambda_*^{2k}\ge1.
\]

Therefore (5.3) implies

\[
\liminf_{r\to\infty}
\left(\operatorname{Adv}^{\le D_{n_r}}\right)^2
\ge
\sum_{k=3}^{K}\frac1{2k}.
\]

Letting `K->infinity` contradicts the uniform boundedness in (5.2). `□`

Combining Proposition 5.1 with (4.5), choose `delta>0` so that eventually

\[
\lambda_n^2\le1-\delta.
\]

Then even at **rho=1**,

\[
\chi^2(P_n\|Q_n)
=Z_n(\lambda_n^2/n)-1
=O_\delta(1).
\tag{5.5}
\]

Using the already proved overlap inequality

\[
1-\|P_n-Q_n\|_{TV}
\ge
\frac1{2+\chi^2(P_n\|Q_n)},
\tag{5.6}
\]

we obtain a uniform total-variation gap from `1`. Thus:

### Corollary 5.2 — second nontrivial instantiation of the source implication

For the dense Rademacher two-community graph mixture (3.1), if `D_n->infinity`, `D_n=o(sqrt n)`, and the source low-degree advantage remains bounded through degree `D_n`, then **no measurable test strongly distinguishes `P_n` from `Q_n` even before adding noise**. In particular, no polynomial-time or specified polynomial-degree statistic strongly distinguishes after any fixed positive resampling noise.

## 7. B23 — DO WHAT THE PROPOSER DID NOT EXPECT: decomposability is sufficient but not necessary

The previous recovery report proposed **low-interface factorization** as the missing structural bridge. Dense SBM shows why that cannot be the only natural route.

A simple cycle `C_k` is a positive Fourier atom in (3.3). But it has **no proper nonempty positive sub-atom**: every proper nonempty edge subset of a simple cycle has at least two vertices of odd degree, hence its Fourier coefficient vanishes. Therefore a long simple cycle with `k>D` cannot be factorized into nonzero degree-`<=D` Eulerian witnesses.

So the exact hypothesis of the previous low-interface theorem fails on an infinite family of high-degree primitive atoms, even though Corollary 5.2 shows that bounded low-degree advantage forces full statistical hardness in this model.

This yields a concrete correction to the research program:

> The structural condition should not demand that **every** high-degree witness decompose. It should allow a **primitive sector** whose total pressure is controlled directly, plus a **composite sector** controlled by local reassembly.

For the present model the primitive atoms include simple cycles. Their noised mass above degree `D` already obeys, with `q=rho^2 lambda^2<1`,

\[
\begin{aligned}
R_D^{\mathrm{cycle}}
&:=
\sum_{k>D}
\frac{(n)_k}{2k}
\left(\frac{q}{n}\right)^k\\
&\le
\frac12\sum_{k>D}\frac{q^k}{k}\\
&\le
\boxed{
\frac{q^{D+1}}{2(D+1)(1-q)}.
}
\tag{7.1}
\end{aligned}
\]

Thus long indecomposable primitives are harmless below the pressure threshold even though they violate low-interface factorization pointwise.

One can state a general hybrid lemma. Let the high atoms split into primitives `P` and composites `C`. Suppose the total noised primitive mass is `R`, the allowed low base mass is `S`, and every composite factors into at least two ordered base atoms drawn from `P` together with the low base class, with the same factorization/reassembly entropy `Xi` as in the previous report. Put `U=S+R`. If

\[
e^\Xi U<1,
\]

then exactly the same geometric-series proof gives

\[
\boxed{
\sum_{a\in P\cup C}\rho^{2d(a)}\beta(a)
\le
R+U\frac{e^\Xi U}{1-e^\Xi U}.
}
\tag{7.2}
\]

This hybrid statement is intentionally not promoted as the final source condition: in dense SBM, naive cycle-by-cycle reassembly is not sharp enough to reach the whole `q<1` regime, while the exact spin pressure (4.3) does. The lesson is structural, not cosmetic: **primitive pressure and interface factorization are complementary mechanisms.**

## 8. What failed, and why

### 8.1 Failure: low-interface factorization is not necessary

Long simple cycles are indecomposable positive atoms. Therefore “every high witness factors into low witnesses” cannot be a necessary source-level condition.

### 8.2 Failure: geometric reassembly of cycles is quantitatively non-sharp

Every Eulerian graph has an edge-disjoint cycle decomposition, so one might try to sum arbitrary cycle tuples geometrically. But the sum of simple-cycle activities becomes too large before the true dense-SBM threshold `q=1`; the exact magnetization identity retains cancellations/constraints that crude tuple counting discards. This is the same general warning already seen when diagonal leverage lost two-copy cancellation.

### 8.3 Failure: the dense-SBM result is not new

The resulting hardness/contiguity regime is classical. Banerjee's dense planted-partition work and Banerjee–Ma's signed-cycle likelihood-ratio analysis cover the underlying phenomenon. The present derivation is useful as a framework stress test and a compact bridge to primitive pressure, not as a publication claim.
