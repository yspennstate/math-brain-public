## 3. Local spectral envelope for an arbitrary rare event

Let `h:{-1,+1}^w -> {0,1}` and let

`p=E_U h`.

Write its Walsh expansion as

`h=sum_{S subseteq [w]} hhat(S) chi_S`.

### Lemma 3.1 (rare-event weighted Fourier tail)

For every `D>=0` and `rho in (0,1)`,

`boxed{
Tau_{D,rho}(h)
<= sqrt{p(1-p)} [sum_{s>D} binom(w,s) rho^(2s)]^(1/2)
<= sqrt p (1+rho^2)^(w/2).
}`                                                              (3.1)

Consequently, if for some `alpha>log(1+rho^2)` one has

`p <= exp(-alpha w)`,                                          (3.2)

and

`delta=(alpha-log(1+rho^2))/2 >0`,                             (3.3)

then

`boxed{Tau_{D,rho}(h) <= exp(-delta w).}`                      (3.4)

#### Proof

Cauchy--Schwarz on the high-degree Fourier coefficients gives

`Tau_{D,rho}(h)`

`<= [sum_{|S|>D} rho^(2|S|)]^(1/2)
    [sum_{|S|>D} hhat(S)^2]^(1/2)`.

Because `h` is Boolean,

`sum_{S ne empty} hhat(S)^2 = Var_U(h)=p(1-p)`.

Also

`sum_{S subseteq[w]} rho^(2|S|)=(1+rho^2)^w`.

This proves (3.1). Combining (3.1) with (3.2) gives

`sqrt p (1+rho^2)^(w/2)
 <= exp[-(alpha-log(1+rho^2))w/2]`,

which is (3.4). QED.

### Why this is the right local parameter for this route

The proof uses no monotonicity, conjunction structure, symmetry, or formula representation of `h`; only its width and null mass enter. The factor `(1+rho^2)^w` is the full noise-weighted dimension of the Boolean Fourier cube seen by Cauchy--Schwarz. Hence the threshold

`alpha>log(1+rho^2)`                                            (3.5)

is the exact threshold supplied by this representation-free `L2 -> A` estimate. This is not claimed optimal among all possible arguments.

## 4. Main theorem — arbitrary rare local witnesses with read-k overlap

Let independent base bits have law `U_N`. For `j=1,...,m`, let

`h_j:{-1,+1}^{B_j}->{0,1}`

be an arbitrary local witness on block `B_j`, with width

`w_j=|B_j|`, `p_j=E_U h_j`.

Assume the block family is **read-k**:

`max_i #{j:i in B_j} <= k`.                                    (4.1)

Define the union test

`f = 1{exists j: h_j=1}`, `q=E_U f`, `L_q=-log(1-q)`.          (4.2)

### Theorem 4.1 (rare local witness transfer)

Assume

`Adv_U^{<=D}(P,U) <= A`                                        (4.3)

and, for one `alpha>log(1+rho^2)`,

`p_j <= exp(-alpha w_j)` for every `j`.                        (4.4)

Let

`delta=(alpha-log(1+rho^2))/2`.

For every integer `W<=D`,

`boxed{
E_{T_rho P} f
<= k L_q + A k sqrt(L_q)
 + m[ exp(-alpha(W+1))
      + A exp(-alpha(W+1)/2)
      + exp(-delta(W+1)) ].
}`                                                              (4.5)

Consequently, if along a sequence

`A_n=O(1)`, `k_n=O(1)`, `q_n->0`,                              (4.6)

`D_n/log(m_n+2) -> infinity`,                                  (4.7)

and the same fixed `alpha>log(1+rho^2)` works for all local witnesses, then

`boxed{E_{T_rho P_n} f_n -> 0.}`                               (4.8)

Thus at Kireeva's source scale `D_n=(log n)^(1+Omega(1))`, no polynomial-size constant-read union of arbitrary exponentially rare local witnesses satisfying (4.4) can strongly distinguish after any fixed positive product-noise rate.

This strictly generalizes the previous cylinder theorem: a local witness can now be any Boolean function of its block.

### Proof

Split the witnesses at width `W`:

`S={j:w_j<=W}`, `L={j:w_j>W}`,

and let `f_S,f_L` be their unions. Then

`f <= f_S+f_L`.                                                (4.9)

#### Step 1: narrow witnesses

Set

`g_S=sum_{j in S} h_j`, `lambda_S=sum_{j in S} p_j`.          (4.10)

The read-k complement/variance lemma from the 07:52 report applies to arbitrary Boolean functions of independent base coordinates, not merely conjunctions. Therefore

`lambda_S <= k[-log(1-E_U f_S)] <= kL_q`,                     (4.11)

and

`Var_U(g_S) <= k lambda_S <= k^2 L_q`.                        (4.12)

Every `h_j` in `S` is a Boolean function of at most `W` bits, hence has Walsh degree at most `W<=D`. Therefore `g_S` and `T_rho g_S` have degree at most `D`.

Since `f_S<=g_S` and `T_rho` is positivity preserving,

`E_{T_rho P}f_S = E_P T_rho f_S <= E_P T_rho g_S`.

Apply the source LDA bound to `T_rho g_S`, use stationarity of `U` and `L2(U)` contraction of the noise:

`E_{T_rho P}f_S`

`<= lambda_S + A sqrt(Var_U(T_rho g_S))`

`<= lambda_S + A sqrt(Var_U(g_S))`

`<= kL_q + A k sqrt(L_q)`.                                    (4.13)

#### Step 2: wide witnesses

By the union bound and positivity,

`E_{T_rho P}f_L <= sum_{j in L} E_{T_rho P}h_j`.              (4.14)

Apply (2.1) and Lemma 3.1 to each `h_j`. Using (4.4),

`E_{T_rho P}h_j`

`<= p_j + A sqrt(Var_U h_j) + Tau_{D,rho}(h_j)`

`<= exp(-alpha w_j)
   + A exp(-alpha w_j/2)
   + exp(-delta w_j)`.                                         (4.15)

Every `j in L` has `w_j>=W+1`; summing (4.15) gives exactly the wide-witness bracket in (4.5). Adding (4.13) proves (4.5).

#### Step 3: asymptotics

Let

`c_* = min{alpha/2, delta}>0`.

Choose

`W_n = ceil(2 log(m_n+2)/c_*)`.                               (4.16)

Then the entire wide-witness contribution in (4.5) is `o(1)`. Condition (4.7) gives `W_n<=D_n` eventually. The narrow contribution tends to zero by bounded `A_n,k_n` and `q_n->0`. This proves (4.8). QED.

## 6. Fixed finite-alphabet extension

Let the null be uniform on `Omega^N` with `|Omega|=s>=2`, identify `Omega` with `Z_s`, and use the unit-modulus tensor-character basis. Product resampling still multiplies a character of support size `t` by `rho^t`.

For a Boolean local event `h` depending on `w` coordinates and having null mass `p`, Cauchy--Schwarz gives

`Tau_{D,rho}(h)
 <= sqrt p [sum_{t>D} binom(w,t)(s-1)^t rho^(2t)]^(1/2)
 <= sqrt p [1+(s-1)rho^2]^(w/2)`.                             (6.1)

Hence the theorem holds with the rarity threshold

`boxed{alpha>log(1+(s-1)rho^2)}`                              (6.2)

and

`delta_s=(alpha-log(1+(s-1)rho^2))/2`.                        (6.3)

A cylinder witness has `p=s^{-w}`, so `alpha=log s`, and indeed

`log s > log(1+(s-1)rho^2)`

for every fixed `rho<1`. Thus the 07:52 cylinder theorem is recovered as a special case, though the cylinder-specific Fourier calculation there has a sharper decay constant.

For a fixed alphabet, support/tensor degree converts to the source's ordinary polynomial degree with only an alphabet-dependent constant factor under a fixed interpolation/one-hot convention. No claim is made for alphabets growing with `n`.

## 7. Mao obstruction sharpened to arbitrary local witness systems

Orient Mao's successful noised rank-test acceptance indicator `f_n` so that

`q_n=E_U f_n ->0`, `E_{T_rho P_n}f_n ->1`,                   (7.1)

and recall that Mao has

`Adv_U^{<=D_n}(P_n,U)=0`.                                     (7.2)

Suppose `f_n` admitted a representation as the union of `m_n` local Boolean witnesses satisfying:

1. coordinate read number `k_n`;
2. `p_{n,j}<=exp(-alpha w_{n,j})` for one fixed `alpha>log(1+rho^2)`;
3. `log m_n=o(D_n)`.

Take `W=D_n` in (4.5). The wide-witness bracket is `o(1)`. Since `A=0`, strong distinction forces

`boxed{k_n[-log(1-q_n)] >= 1-o(1).}`                          (7.3)

Therefore, as `q_n->0`,

`boxed{k_n q_n >= 1-o(1).}`                                  (7.4)

In particular, Mao's polynomial-time rank test cannot be represented by any subexponential-in-`D_n` **constant-read union of sufficiently rare local certificates**, even when each certificate is an arbitrary Boolean function rather than a conjunction.

This is a source-valid obstruction: it pinpoints one broad notion of local-certifiability that the Mao construction must violate. It does not prove that every natural polynomial-time algorithm has such a representation.

## 8. What was learned from the attempted full arbitrary-block generalization

The desired statement “replace cylinders by arbitrary local block tests with no additional assumption” is false as a route to a general theorem: if one permits a single large block with no spectral or rarity control, that block may encode an arbitrary hard global statistic. ## 10. Old claim, new claim, exact gap

**New proved claim:** conjunction/cylinder structure is removed. The same qualitative conclusion holds for **arbitrary local Boolean witnesses** provided their null probability decays as `exp(-alpha w)` with `alpha>log(1+rho^2)` (or `alpha>log(1+(s-1)rho^2)` over an `s`-ary uniform alphabet).

This is a strict structural generalization because a local witness may now have arbitrary internal Boolean complexity, including parity, threshold, or any other predicate, so long as its null event is sufficiently rare.

**Exact remaining gap:** the exponential-rarity threshold is only a sufficient mechanism for controlling the high-degree `A`-tail of wide witnesses. Many natural local tests have non-rare null probability, and arbitrary polynomial-time computation need not decompose into low-read local witnesses. The source-general problem therefore remains open.

## 11. Gaussian/tensor/correlation handoff

The useful cross-role lemma is the abstract local-tail calculation, not a claim that arbitrary Gaussian indicators satisfy it. For a `w`-dimensional standard Gaussian block and a finite Hermite polynomial

`g=sum_{|beta|<=L} ghat_beta H_beta`

`sum_{D<|beta|<=L} rho^|beta| |ghat_beta|
 <= sigma [sum_{t=D+1}^L binom(w+t-1,t) rho^(2t)]^(1/2)`.     (11.1)

This is the direct Gaussian analogue of the finite Fourier-dimension step.
