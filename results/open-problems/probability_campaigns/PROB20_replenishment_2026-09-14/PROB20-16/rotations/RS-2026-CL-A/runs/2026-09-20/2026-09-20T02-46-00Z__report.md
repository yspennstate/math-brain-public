## 3. Low-degree leverage and exact contamination transfer

Fix one `n` and degree `D`. Let

`V_D^0={f: deg(f)<=D, E_Q f=0}`

with the `L2(Q)` inner product, and let `{phi_1,...,phi_m}` be any orthonormal basis. Define the basis-independent low-degree leverage / reciprocal Christoffel function

`kappa_D(x)=sum_{j=1}^m phi_j(x)^2`

`=sup{ f(x)^2 : f in V_D^0, ||f||_{L2(Q)}=1 }`.

Suppose the planted law decomposes as

`P=(1-eps) P^circ + eps H`, `0<=eps<1`.

### Theorem 3.1 — leverage-trimmed source-LDA transfer

For every `D`,

`Adv_Q^{<=D}(P^circ,Q)`

`<= [ Adv_Q^{<=D}(P,Q) + eps (E_H kappa_D)^(1/2) ]/(1-eps)`.

In particular, if `kappa_D^*=sup_x kappa_D(x)`, then

`Adv_Q^{<=D}(P^circ,Q)`

`<= [Adv_Q^{<=D}(P,Q)+eps sqrt(kappa_D^*)]/(1-eps)`.

### Proof

Take any `f in V_D^0` with `||f||_{L2(Q)}=1`. The mixture identity gives

`(1-eps)(E_{P^circ}f-E_Q f)`

`= (E_P f-E_Q f) - eps(E_H f-E_Q f)`.

Since `E_Q f=0`,

`|(1-eps) E_{P^circ}f| <= Adv_Q^{<=D}(P,Q) + eps |E_H f|`.

Write the coefficient vector of `f` in the orthonormal basis as `a`, `||a||_2=1`, and `Phi(x)=(phi_1(x),...,phi_m(x))`. Then

`|E_H f|=|a dot E_H Phi(X)|`

`<= ||E_H Phi(X)||_2`

`<= E_H ||Phi(X)||_2`

`<= (E_H ||Phi(X)||_2^2)^(1/2)`

`=(E_H kappa_D(X))^(1/2)`.

Taking the supremum over normalized centered `f` proves the theorem. `square`

### Sharpness of the scale

For a point mass `H=delta_x`, the contamination contribution is **exactly**

`sup_{f in V_D^0, ||f||_Q=1} |E_H f| = sqrt(kappa_D(x))`.

Indeed choose the reproducing-kernel direction with coefficient vector proportional to `Phi(x)`. Therefore `eps sqrt(kappa_D)` is not an artifact of the proof: without further information on the contaminant, no universal source-LDA transfer can improve its scale.

This is the precise sense in which a contaminant is “below low-degree resolution.”

## 4. New robust noisy-indistinguishability theorem

The preceding theorem plugs directly into the inherited covariance-pressure framework.

### Theorem 4.1 — covariance-pressure framework with arbitrary bad planted mass removed

Let `D_n->infinity` and fix target noise time `t>0`. Suppose

`P_n=(1-eps_n)P_n^circ + eps_n H_n`, with `eps_n->0`,

and define `kappa_{n,D}` from the source polynomial space under `Q_n` as above. Assume:

1. the **observed/source pair** has bounded LDA through the source window:
   `sup_n Adv_{Q_n}^{<=D_n}(P_n,Q_n)<infinity`;
2. for every fixed `K`, `Adv_{Q_n}^{<=K}(P_n,Q_n)->0`;
3. the contamination is below source-degree resolution:
   `eps_n (E_{H_n} kappa_{n,D_n})^(1/2)->0`;
4. the inherited reference-chaos covariance-pressure hypotheses hold for `Q_n`: fixed-degree weighted covariance blocks are bounded and
   `lim_{K->infinity} limsup_n Theta_n(K,D_n;t)=0`;
5. **only the dominant core** satisfies the smoothed-tail regularity: for some fixed `s in (0,t)`, writing `p_n^circ=dP_n^circ/dR_n`, `q_n=dQ_n/dR_n`,
   `exp(-2(t-s)D_n)||T_{n,s}(p_n^circ-q_n)||_2^2 ->0`.

Then

`||T_{n,t}P_n-T_{n,t}Q_n||_TV ->0`.

### Proof

Because `kappa_{n,K}<=kappa_{n,D_n}` for `K<=D_n`, Theorem 3.1 gives

`sup_n Adv_{Q_n}^{<=D_n}(P_n^circ,Q_n)<infinity`

and, for every fixed `K`,

`Adv_{Q_n}^{<=K}(P_n^circ,Q_n)->0`.

Apply the 01:51 covariance-pressure + smoothed-tail theorem to `(P_n^circ,Q_n)` to obtain

`TV(T_tP_n^circ,T_tQ_n)->0`.

By linearity of the Markov kernel,

`T_tP_n=(1-eps_n)T_tP_n^circ+eps_n T_tH_n`,

and hence

`TV(T_tP_n,T_tQ_n)`

`<= (1-eps_n)TV(T_tP_n^circ,T_tQ_n)+eps_n TV(T_tH_n,T_tQ_n)`

`<= TV(T_tP_n^circ,T_tQ_n)+eps_n ->0`. `square`

All Renyi / `L^2` / smoothed-tail regularity may now fail arbitrarily on `H_n`. Only a `1-o(1)` planted core needs to satisfy the high-degree regularity. The exceptional mass is controlled solely at the **source resolution scale** `eps sqrt(kappa_D)`.

## 5. Uniform Boolean specialization and exact resolution scale

For `Q=U_N=Unif({-1,+1}^N)`, the centered degree-`<=D` Walsh characters are orthonormal. Therefore

`kappa_D(x)=M_{N,D}:=sum_{k=1}^D binom(N,k)`

for **every** point `x`. Hence arbitrary planted contamination transfers whenever

`eps_n sqrt(M_{N,D_n})->0`.

For `1<=D<=N/2`,

`M_{N,D}+1 <= (eN/D)^D`,

`eps_n (eN/D_n)^(D_n/2)->0`.

This scale is sharp against arbitrary point-mass contamination because every point has the same Walsh leverage.

## 6. Strict improvement over whole-law Renyi regularity

The new theorem is not merely a reformulation. There are explicit families for which the whole planted law fails every fixed-order Renyi `o(D)` condition from the 00:42 theorem, while the leverage-trimmed theorem applies.

Let `Q_N=U_N` on the Boolean cube, let `D_N=o(N/log N)`, and put

`M_N=sum_{k=1}^{D_N} binom(N,k)`, `eps_N=M_N^{-1}`.

Let the dominant core be the parity law

`dP_N^circ/dU_N = 1 + chi_[N]`,

i.e. uniform on the half-cube of even parity. Choose a point `x_*` of even parity and define

`P_N=(1-eps_N)P_N^circ+eps_N delta_{x_*}`.

For every `D_N<N`, the parity core has zero Fourier coefficients at all degrees `<=D_N`, while the point mass has coefficient `chi_S(x_*)` on every Walsh character. Therefore exactly

`Adv_{U_N}^{<=D_N}(P_N,U_N)^2`

`=eps_N^2 M_N=M_N^{-1}->0`.

But at the spike point,

`dP_N/dU_N(x_*) >= eps_N 2^N`,

so for every fixed `r>1`,

`E_{U_N}(dP_N/dU_N)^r >= eps_N^r 2^{(r-1)N}`.

Because `log M_N=O(D_N log(eN/D_N))=o(N)`, this implies

`D_r(P_N||U_N) >= N log 2 - o(N)`.

Thus the whole-law fixed-order Renyi complexity is `Theta(N)`, not `o(D_N)`, for **every** fixed `r>1`.

Nevertheless independent refresh at time `t>0` sends the parity-core density to

`1+exp(-tN)chi_[N]`,

so

`TV(T_tP_N^circ,U_N)=0.5 exp(-tN)`.

By mixture contraction,

`TV(T_tP_N,U_N) <= 0.5 exp(-tN)+eps_N ->0`.

This gives an explicit strict separation: the predecessor's whole-law Renyi hypothesis fails maximally, while the leverage-trimmed theorem correctly ignores the vanishing spike.

## 8. Gaussian/tensor transfer: Hermite Christoffel control

The same robustification is useful for P08/P09 when rare Gaussian bad events blow up global `L^r` or `L^2` quantities.

For standard Gaussian `gamma_m`, use normalized probabilists' Hermites

`h_alpha(x)=He_alpha(x)/sqrt(alpha!)`.

The total-degree leverage is

`kappa_{m,D}(x)=sum_{1<=|alpha|<=D} h_alpha(x)^2`.

Classical Mehler gives, for every `rho in (0,1)`,

`sum_{alpha>=0} rho^{|alpha|} h_alpha(x)^2`

`=(1-rho^2)^(-m/2) exp( rho ||x||^2/(1+rho) )`.

Since `rho^{|alpha|}>=rho^D` on `|alpha|<=D`,

`1+kappa_{m,D}(x)`

`<= rho^{-D}(1-rho^2)^(-m/2) exp( rho ||x||^2/(1+rho) )`.

Consequently an exceptional Gaussian component `H` satisfies

`E_H kappa_{m,D}`

`<= rho^{-D}(1-rho^2)^(-m/2) E_H exp(rho||X||^2/(1+rho))`.

This is a direct source-resolution certificate for trimming rare norm/singular-value/bad-environment events before applying the P08/P09 Gaussian arguments. It is only a handoff lemma; RS-2026-CL-A retains source ownership.
