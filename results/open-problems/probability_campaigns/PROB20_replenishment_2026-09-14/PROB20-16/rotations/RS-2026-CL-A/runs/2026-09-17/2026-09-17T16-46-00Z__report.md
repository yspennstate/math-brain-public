## 2. The exact blocker

The metric-moving theorem is mathematically explicit: in a finite graded null space with a symmetry group and grade-contractive reversible noise, if

\[
\mathscr L_{>D}^{G,T}(x)
=
\sum_{j>D}\tau_j^2\kappa_j^G(x),
\]

then bounded low-degree advantage together with bounded planted expectation of this null-defined tail controls post-noise chi-square and hence total variation. The source-validity bottleneck is no longer the inequality itself. It is the absence of a natural structural hypothesis that implies the tail condition for a broad class without simply assuming high-degree smallness.

The blocking obligation is:

\[
\boxed{
\text{Find a recognizable distributional/local hypothesis }\mathsf H(P_n,Q_n,T_n,G_n)
\text{ such that }
\mathsf H\Longrightarrow
\sup_n\mathbb E_{P_n}\mathscr L_{>D_n}^{G_n,T_n}(X)<\infty,
\quad D_n=(\log n)^{1+\Omega(1)},
}
\tag{2.1}
\]

for a genuinely nontrivial class not already subsumed by the known Hsieh or Chen–Li positive regimes, while the same hypothesis fails Mao/Buhai for a structural reason. Until (2.1), or an equivalent two-copy statement, is proved, the framework theorem remains a plausible source-level answer but its breadth/non-tautological significance is the precise point an independent source audit must test.

## 4. B13 — FIND WHAT ONLY INCREASES: why the present monovariants diagnose cliffs but do not prohibit them

The graph case makes this sharper. Under full coordinate exchangeability in the Boolean-vector setting, each exact symmetric degree is effectively one-dimensional. Under vertex relabeling of graph edges, the invariant degree space contains many graph-shape types, with multiplicity growing rapidly with degree. Therefore symmetry itself does not furnish the missing monotone complexity measure.

What would help is an **interface complexity** `I_j` with three properties:

1. every degree-`j+1` contribution can be obtained by extending a degree-`j` object through an interface whose total weighted complexity is controlled by `I_j`;
2. the source low-degree premise controls the base pieces;
3. noise multiplies the cost of each gluing step strongly enough that the accumulated interface complexity contracts.

Chen–Li have precisely such a mechanism in their planted-subgraph setting, implemented with tree decompositions and small separators. CL-A lacks an abstract version stated directly for distribution pairs.

**Outcome of B13:** the existing pressure/leverage/ratio quantities are diagnostics, not the missing structural monovariant. The likely missing object is an interface/reassembly complexity that is preserved or controlled under recursive decomposition.

## 5. B25 — COUNT THE SAME THING TWICE: one-copy leverage versus two-copy cancellation

The most important correction from the post-metric runs is that the diagonal leverage bound can lose enormous cancellation. The exact high-degree energy has a two-copy representation. If `Psi_j` is an orthonormal invariant feature vector and `T_j` is the degree-`j` noise action, then

\[
K_j^T(x,y)=\Psi_j(x)^\top T_j^2\Psi_j(y)
\]

and the noisy chi-square contribution is the two-copy average

\[
\sum_{j\ge1}\mathbb E_{X,Y\stackrel{iid}{\sim}P}K_j^T(X,Y).
\]

By contrast, the leverage route bounds this through a diagonal quantity involving `K_j^T(X,X)`. That replacement is safe but can be extremely loose: even `P=Q` has zero nonconstant likelihood-ratio coefficients and zero chi-square, while the expected diagonal reproducing kernel records the entire invariant-space dimension.

This is the key “count it twice” lesson. There are two honest ways to count the high-degree object:

- **one-copy/diagonal count:** robust, simple, worst-case, but throws away cancellation;
- **two-copy/overlap count:** exact, model-sensitive, and capable of exploiting interface structure.

The previous forced-support overlap formulas are instances of the second count. Chen–Li's isomorphism-triple bookkeeping is another. Mao's code/rank example shows that one cannot expect two-copy cancellation from permutation invariance alone.

**Outcome of B25:** no new theorem claim, but a route is ruled out. Continuing to refine the one-copy leverage envelope is unlikely to settle the source-generalization issue. The next serious attempt should port a decomposition theorem directly into the two-copy kernel/overlap representation.
