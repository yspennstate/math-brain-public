## 1. Exact source and later boundary

Outside source: Anastasia Kireeva, Randomstrasse 101, **“Low-degree hardness of finding the largest clique in Erdős–Rényi graph (problems 36-38)”**, posted 5 June 2026, `https://randomstrasse101.math.ethz.ch/posts/clique-low-degree/`.

The post title says **problems 36–38**, while the first broad conditions box is literally labeled **Open Problem 35**. That box asks for conditions on `(P_n,Q_n)` under which bounded low-degree advantage at

`D=(log n)^(1+Omega(1))`

rigorously implies that no polynomials of an appropriate larger degree can distinguish. The immediately preceding Hopkins formulation is more specialized: fixed `k`; finite alphabet `Omega` (or a real analogue); `Q_n` uniform on `Omega^{binom(n,k)}`; `P_n` invariant under natural `S_n` relabeling; bounded LDLR through the polylogarithmic `D`; and fixed positive independent noise before the proposed algorithmic indistinguishability conclusion.

Mandatory later boundary: Songtao Mao, **“The Polynomial-Time Low-Degree Conjecture is False,”** arXiv:2607.20318, submitted 22 July 2026. For every fixed `r>=3`, Mao gives an `S_n`-invariant graph law with `Q_n=G(n,1/2)` whose marginals on at most

`D_n=Theta((log n)^(r-1))`

edges are exactly uniform, so source LDA is zero through `D_n`, yet after every fixed positive resampling rate a deterministic polynomial-time rank test strongly distinguishes. Therefore product-uniform null + permutation symmetry + exact polylogarithmic moment matching + independent noise are not sufficient.

This report proves a concrete positive theorem for a substantially larger algorithm class than the 05:49 sparse-polynomial/leaf-bounded decision-tree class. It remains **PROVED PARTIAL**, not a full-source characterization.

## 2. Inherited theorem and the precise new gap

The 05:49 report proved, for Boolean uniform product null and retention noise `T_rho`,

`|E_{T_rho P} f-E_U f| <= A sqrt(Var_U f)+Tau_{D,rho}(f)`,      (2.1)

where `A=Adv_U^{<=D}(P,U)` and

`Tau_{D,rho}(f)=sum_{|S|>D} rho^{|S|}|fhat(S)|`.              (2.2)

It then handled arbitrary-degree polynomials with `exp(o(D))` Fourier support and decision trees with `exp(o(D))` leaves. The missing implication was whether one can control a natural polynomial-time representation whose raw Fourier support and decision-tree leaf count can both be enormous.

A polynomial-size **read-once DNF** is such a class: it is evaluable in linear time in its representation, can have exponentially many Fourier monomials, and its exact decision tree can have exponentially many leaves. The point of this run is that its special product-of-complements structure lets fixed resampling noise suppress wide terms while bounded LDA controls narrow terms.

## 3. Boolean theorem

Let `U_N` be uniform on `{-1,+1}^N`. A read-once DNF is

`f(x)= OR_{j=1}^m A_j(x_{B_j})`,                              (3.1)

where the blocks `B_j` are pairwise disjoint and every `A_j` is a conjunction of literals on all coordinates of `B_j`. Write

`w_j=|B_j|`, `p_j=E_U A_j=2^{-w_j}`, and `q=E_U f`.

Let `T_rho`, `0<rho<1`, independently retain each bit with probability `rho` and otherwise redraw it uniformly.

### Theorem 3.1 (robust read-once DNF transfer)

Assume

`Adv_U^{<=D}(P,U) <= A`.                                     (3.2)

Fix any `z` with

`1<z<1/rho`,                                                  (3.3)

and put

`r_z=(1+rho z)/2 < 1`.                                       (3.4)

For every integer `W<=D`, split the terms into

`S={j:w_j<=W}` and `L={j:w_j>W}`.

Then

`E_{T_rho P} f`

`<= [-log(1-q)] + A sqrt[-log(1-q)]`

`   + q + A sqrt(q)`

`   + z^{-(D+1)} exp(m r_z^{W+1}).`                          (3.5)

Consequently, for any sequence with

`A_n=O(1), q_n -> 0, D_n/log(m_n+2) -> infinity`,             (3.6)

after every fixed positive product-noise rate,

`E_{T_rho P_n} f_n -> 0`.                                    (3.7)

Thus no polynomial-size read-once DNF whose null acceptance tends to zero can strongly distinguish the noised planted law from the uniform null when `D_n=(log n)^(1+Omega(1))` and the ambient representation size is polynomial in `n`.

This uses neither permutation invariance nor any high-degree likelihood regularity of `P`.

### Proof

Write `f_S` and `f_L` for the ORs over the two term classes. Since

`f <= f_S+f_L`,                                               (3.8)

it is enough to bound both expectations.

#### Step 1: narrow terms are controlled directly by bounded LDA

Let

`g_S=sum_{j in S} A_j`.                                      (3.9)

Then `f_S<=g_S`, and `T_rho g_S` is a polynomial of degree at most `W<=D`. Since the blocks are disjoint, the summands remain independent under `U` and `T_rho` is an `L2(U)` contraction. Therefore

`E_U g_S = lambda_S := sum_{j in S} p_j`,                    (3.10)

and

`Var_U(T_rho g_S) <= sum_{j in S} Var_U(A_j)`

`<= sum_{j in S} p_j = lambda_S`.                            (3.11)

The low-degree advantage bound gives

`E_{T_rho P} f_S <= E_P T_rho g_S`

`<= lambda_S + A sqrt(lambda_S)`.                            (3.12)

Because the read-once terms are independent under `U`,

`1-q = product_j (1-p_j) <= exp(-sum_j p_j)`,                 (3.13)

hence

`sum_j p_j <= -log(1-q)`.                                    (3.14)

Thus

`E_{T_rho P} f_S`

`<= -log(1-q)+A sqrt[-log(1-q)]`.                            (3.15)

#### Step 2: exact absolute-Fourier generating function for wide terms

For one conjunction `A_j`, every original Fourier coefficient has absolute value `p_j=2^{-w_j}`. After retention noise, a coefficient on a subset of size `s` has absolute value

`p_j rho^s`.                                                  (3.16)

Let

`a_j=T_rho A_j`, `h_L=product_{j in L}(1-a_j)`.              (3.17)

Since the blocks are disjoint,

`T_rho f_L = 1-h_L`.                                         (3.18)

For a formal variable `z>=1`, the absolute Fourier-coefficient generating polynomial of the factor `1-a_j` is exactly

`F_j(z)`

`= (1-p_j) + p_j[(1+rho z)^{w_j}-1]`

`= 1-2p_j+p_j(1+rho z)^{w_j}`                               (3.19)

and therefore

`F_j(z) <= 1 + r_z^{w_j}`.                                  (3.20)

Because the coordinate blocks are disjoint, each global Fourier coefficient has a unique blockwise factorization, so there is no coefficient collision or cancellation in absolute value and

`F_L(z)=product_{j in L} F_j(z)`                             (3.21)

is the exact absolute-coefficient generating polynomial for `h_L`.

Since every `w_j>W`,

`F_L(z) <= exp(sum_{j in L} r_z^{w_j})`

`<= exp(m r_z^{W+1}).`                                      (3.22)

The nonconstant Fourier coefficients of `T_rho f_L` and `h_L` differ only by sign. Therefore Markov/Chernoff extraction of coefficients gives

`Tau_{D,rho}(f_L)`

`= sum_{|S|>D} |widehat{T_rho f_L}(S)|`

`<= z^{-(D+1)} F_L(z)`

`<= z^{-(D+1)} exp(m r_z^{W+1}).`                           (3.23)

#### Step 3: apply the inherited robust Fourier-tail theorem

Since `f_L` is Boolean and `E_U f_L<=q`,

`Var_U(f_L)<=q`.                                             (3.24)

Applying (2.1) to `f_L` and using (3.23),

`E_{T_rho P} f_L`

`<= q+A sqrt(q)+z^{-(D+1)}exp(m r_z^{W+1}).`                (3.25)

Adding (3.15) and (3.25) proves (3.5).

For the asymptotic statement, choose

`W_n = ceil(2 log(m_n+2)/(-log r_z))`.                       (3.26)

Then `m_n r_z^{W_n+1}=o(1)`, while condition (3.6) ensures `W_n<=D_n` eventually. Hence the last term in (3.5) is `exp(o(1)) z^{-D_n}->0`; every other term vanishes because `q_n->0` and `A_n=O(1)`. QED.

## 4. Source-aligned finite-alphabet extension

Let `Omega=Z_q` with fixed `q>=2`, and let the null be uniform on `Omega^N`. A cylinder conjunction fixes every coordinate in a block `B_j` to a prescribed symbol. Its null probability is

`p_j=q^{-w_j}`.                                               (4.1)

In the tensor character basis, the indicator of one prescribed symbol has `q` Fourier coefficients each of absolute value `1/q`; hence after coordinate-retention noise, a width-`w` cylinder has absolute coefficient generating polynomial

`q^{-w}(1+(q-1)rho z)^w`.                                   (4.2)

For the complement factor the analogue of (3.19) is

`1-2q^{-w}+q^{-w}(1+(q-1)rho z)^w`,                          (4.3)

which is at most

`1+r_{q,z}^w`,                                                (4.4)

where

`r_{q,z}=(1+(q-1)rho z)/q < 1` whenever `1<z<1/rho`.         (4.5)

Therefore Theorem 3.1 holds verbatim for read-once cylinder DNFs over every fixed finite uniform alphabet, replacing `r_z` by `r_{q,z}` and interpreting degree as tensor/support degree. Under the standard one-hot encoding this is an ordinary polynomial degree with only a fixed-alphabet constant convention.

This is directly within the finite-uniform-null branch of the source hypotheses.

## 5. Quantitative Mao obstruction

Mao has exact moment matching through `D_n`, so `A_n=0`. Let `f_n` be his successful noised rank-test acceptance indicator, oriented so

`E_U f_n -> 0`, `E_{T_rho P_n} f_n -> 1`.                   (5.1)

Suppose, contrary to the theorem's conclusion, that `f_n` had a read-once DNF representation with `m_n` terms. Taking `W=D_n` in the proof above, the narrow terms contribute `o(1)` because exact moment matching makes their noised expectation equal to their null expectation and `q_n->0`. Hence the wide-term Fourier tail must satisfy

`Tau_{D_n,rho}(f_{n,L}) >= 1-o(1)`.                          (5.2)

But (3.23) gives, for every fixed `z in (1,1/rho)`,

`1-o(1)`

`<= z^{-(D_n+1)} exp(m_n r_z^{D_n+1}).`                     (5.3)

Therefore

`m_n r_z^{D_n+1} >= (D_n+1) log z-o(1)`,                    (5.4)

so

`m_n >= ((D_n+1)log z-o(1)) r_z^{-(D_n+1)}.`                (5.5)

In particular,

`liminf_n log m_n / D_n >= log(2/(1+rho))`.                 (5.6)

The last line follows by taking fixed `z>1` and then sending `z downarrow 1`. Since a genuinely read-once DNF on `N` coordinates has `m<=N`, Mao's graph test (`N=binom(n,2)`) cannot admit such a representation once `D_n >> log n`. This is a structural reason, not merely a restatement that his algorithm is polynomial-time but not spectrally sparse.

## 6. Relation to prior art

Haramaty--Lee--Viola, **“Bounded Independence Plus Noise Fools Products”** (CCC 2017; SIAM J. Comput. 2018), prove that bounded independence plus noise fools general product tests with quantitative parameters. Their theorem is broader in the block function but is an exact bounded-independence statement with a different degree/block-size tradeoff. The argument above is conjunction/cylinder-specific, permits merely **bounded** LDA rather than exact moment matching, and in exchange exploits the exact block Fourier generating function to work whenever `D/log m -> infinity`, even if some conjunction widths greatly exceed `D`.

Hsieh--Kane--Kothari--Li--Mohanty--Tiegel (2026) remain the principal modern low-degree-to-rigorous-consequence comparator. Mao (2026) remains the mandatory negative boundary.

The read-once DNF theorem may be a useful source-directed robustification/specialization of classical pseudorandomness ideas, but **NOVELTY IS UNVERIFIED**. No novelty claim is made here.
