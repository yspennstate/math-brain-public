## Reference-agnostic read-k lemma

Let `X_1,...,X_N` be independent random variables under any product reference law (Bernoulli, finite alphabet, Gaussian, tensor-product coordinates, etc.). Let `Y_j=Y_j(X_{B_j}) in {0,1}` for `j=1,...,m`, and assume every base coordinate appears in at most `k` sets `B_j`.

Put `p_j=E Y_j`, `lambda=sum_j p_j`, `G=sum_j Y_j`, and `q=P(max_j Y_j=1)`. Finner/Shearer gives

`1-q = E product_j(1-Y_j) <= product_j(1-p_j)^(1/k) <= exp(-lambda/k)`,

hence

`lambda <= k[-log(1-q)]`.

Likewise, for `t>=0`,

`E exp(tG) <= product_j [1+p_j(e^(kt)-1)]^(1/k)`

`<= exp((lambda/k)(e^(kt)-1))`.

Comparing second derivatives at zero yields

`Var(G) <= k lambda`.

These estimates use only product structure of the **reference coordinates** and bounded read number; the local functions themselves may be nonlinear.

## Why this may help P08 Gaussian phase retrieval

If a bad-event decomposition for correlated minors can be expressed as local indicators of independent Gaussian entries/columns, with each Gaussian coordinate participating in at most `k` local events, then the total number of local failures has variance at most `k` times its mean and the union intensity obeys the logarithmic bound above. This can replace a naive union bound or a dependency-graph bound that pays block width. The lemma does **not** solve correlated-minor stability; it is useful only after a local event decomposition with controlled read number is identified.

## Why this may help P09 Gaussian tensor bounds

For Gaussian tensors built from independent coordinate blocks, local contraction/bad-net events often overlap because one tensor coordinate participates in several tested contractions. If the overlap can be organized as a read-k family, the same bounds control accumulation of local bad events without assuming the events are independent. This may be paired with a local Hermite/chaos estimate for each event. Again, the target ownership remains P09 and the lemma is only a reusable dependence estimate.
