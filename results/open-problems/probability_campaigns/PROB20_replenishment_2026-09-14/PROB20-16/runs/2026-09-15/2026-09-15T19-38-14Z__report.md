# PROB20-16 — Lyons determinantal entropy concavity
## Run 2026-09-15T19-38-14Z — an elongated convex C082 capsule and quantitative coherent-satellite lift

**SOLUTION STATUS: PARTIAL.**

The unrestricted finite-kernel Lyons conjecture remains open. The exact target is: for every finite measured ground set, every complex Hermitian positive contractions \(Q_0,Q_1\), and every \(0\le t\le1\), prove or refute
\[
H(P_{(1-t)Q_0+tQ_1})\ge (1-t)H(P_{Q_0})+tH(P_{Q_1}),
\]
where \(P_Q(A\subseteq X)=\det Q_A\) and \(H\) is Shannon entropy of the complete random subset law.

This run advances the previous task-local next attack: instead of one small operator-norm ball inside the repository's strong three-site chart C082, it certifies a much longer **convex operator-norm capsule** around an affine scalar-shift chord. The capsule is full nine-real-dimensional, remains wholly inside C082, contains points at operator distance \(3/20\) from the prior center, and therefore is far larger in one direction than the earlier radius-\(25/6201\) ball. The task-local coherent-satellite lifting theorem then produces a correspondingly larger global family with independently moving complex satellite amplitudes.

No universal proof or counterexample is claimed. Historical priority outside the checked sources is not claimed. Self-review is not independent verification.

# 6. Quantitative coherent-satellite lift from the capsule

Apply the previously proved task-local convex-core lifting theorem with
\[
\mathcal C=\mathcal C_\star.
\]
Let \(O_1,\dots,O_m\) be disjoint fixed coherent satellite blocks. For each \(j\), fix a strict Hermitian positive contraction \(C_j\), a core vector \(b_j\in\mathbb C^3\), and a satellite vector \(v_j\in\mathbb C^{O_j}\). Define the full kernel
\[
K(A,z)=
\begin{pmatrix}
A&z_1b_1v_1^*&\cdots&z_mb_mv_m^*\\
\bar z_1v_1b_1^*&C_1&&0\\
\vdots&&\ddots&\\
\bar z_mv_mb_m^*&0&&C_m
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad s_j=|z_j|^2.
\tag{6.1}
\]
For each exact satellite atom \(y_j\), put
\[
Q_{j,y_j}=C_j-P_{O_j\setminus y_j},
\qquad
 d_{j,y_j}=v_j^*Q_{j,y_j}^{-1}v_j,
\tag{6.2}
\]
and
\[
\Delta_j=\max_{y_j}|d_{j,y_j}|.
\tag{6.3}
\]

Let
\[
\mathcal S_\star=
\left\{K_\star+hI:-\frac3{20}\le h\le\frac3{200}\right\}
\tag{6.4}
\]
be the central segment. A simple sufficient robust-domain condition is
\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{dist}_{\mathrm{op}}(A,\mathcal S_\star)
+
\sum_j s_j\Delta_j\|b_j\|_2^2
\le\frac1{1000},
}
\tag{6.5}
\]
together with the exact physical Schur-complement inequalities
\[
A-\sum_j\alpha_js_jb_jb_j^*\succeq0,
\qquad
I-A-\sum_j\beta_js_jb_jb_j^*\succeq0,
\tag{6.6}
\]
where
\[
\alpha_j=v_j^*C_j^{-1}v_j,
\qquad
\beta_j=v_j^*(I-C_j)^{-1}v_j.
\]

The distance to a convex set in a norm is convex, so (6.5)--(6.6) define a convex domain in \((A,s)\). It is coordinatewise downward closed in \(s\). Moreover, choosing a nearest \(K_h\in\mathcal S_\star\), every conditional box vertex
\[
A-\sum_{j\in J}s_jd_{j,y_j}b_jb_j^*
\]
remains within operator distance \(1/1000\) of that same \(K_h\), hence remains in \(\mathcal C_\star\).

Therefore the exact conditional-entropy proof from the previous task-local lifting theorem applies unchanged: arbitrary complex affine interpolation of all satellite amplitudes obeys the full Lyons inequality on this domain.

Because the core curvature constant is still \(\gamma\), the quantitative gap from the previous run also carries over. With endpoint differences
\[
\Delta A=A_1-A_0,\qquad
\Delta s_j=s_j^1-s_j^0,
\]
and
\[
\nu_j=\sum_{y_j}q_j(y_j)d_{j,y_j}^2,
\]
we obtain the core/strength term
\[
\Gamma_t
\ge
\frac\gamma2t(1-t)
\left[
\|\Delta A\|_F^2
+
\sum_j(\Delta s_j)^2\nu_j\|b_j\|_2^4
\right],
\tag{6.7}
\]
before adding the extra nonnegative entropy gain caused by complex chord interpolation lowering \(|z_j|^2\). The same mutual-information/Pinsker argument from the prior report supplies the additional explicit phase-motion term when desired.

# 7. What was proved, what remains open

### New proved scope this run

1. The entire scalar-shift chord \(K_\star+hI\), \(-3/20\le h\le3/200\), lies quantitatively inside C082.
2. The full transverse operator-norm tube of radius \(1/1000\) around that chord lies inside C082.
3. Hence the convex capsule \(\mathcal C_\star\) has uniform full-Hermitian entropy curvature \(-\gamma\).
4. Every pair of endpoints in the capsule satisfies the strong chord inequality (5.2).
5. The task-local coherent-satellite lifting theorem applies to the new capsule via the explicit convex budget (6.5), yielding a larger global complex-amplitude family.

### Previously proved and used noncircularly

- C082 supplies only the pointwise full-Hessian theorem on its nonlinear chart.
- The task-local `2026-09-15T17-48-00Z__report.md` supplies the abstract convex-core satellite lift.
- The task-local `2026-09-15T18-37-00Z__report.md` supplied one smaller convex ball and motivated the present capsule construction.

The present proof does not use the conclusion it is trying to establish: C082 gives local curvature on each chart point, and this run independently proves the whole capsule stays in that chart and is convex before integrating the curvature.

### Still open

- unrestricted three-site determinantal entropy concavity;
- convexification of a substantial fraction of the full nonlinear C082 chart;
- arbitrary finite-dimensional Lyons concavity.

No stationary entropy-rate converse is inferred from these finite-volume results.
