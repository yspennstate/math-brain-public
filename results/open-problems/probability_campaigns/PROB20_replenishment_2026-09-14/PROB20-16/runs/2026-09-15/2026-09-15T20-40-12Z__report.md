# 7. Coherent-satellite lifting enlarges again automatically

The task-local convex-core lifting theorem from the earlier run applies to every convex core set on which kernel entropy is concave. Thus it applies with
\[
\mathcal C=\mathcal P_\star.
\]

Let fixed coherent satellite blocks \(O_j\) have strict kernels \(C_j\), core attachment vectors \(b_j\in\mathbb C^3\), satellite vectors \(v_j\), and independent complex amplitudes \(z_j\), with squared strengths \(s_j=|z_j|^2\). For each exact satellite atom \(y_j\), put
\[
d_{j,y_j}=v_j^*(C_j-P_{O_j\setminus y_j})^{-1}v_j,
\qquad
\Delta_j=\max_{y_j}|d_{j,y_j}|.
\]

Let the central two-dimensional plate be
\[
\mathcal A_\star=
\{K_\star+hI+gD:(h,g)\in\mathcal R\}.
\tag{7.1}
\]
A finite sufficient robust-domain condition is
\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{dist}_{\rm op}(A,\mathcal A_\star)
+
\sum_j s_j\Delta_j\|b_j\|_2^2
\le\frac1{2000},
}
\tag{7.2}
\]
together with the exact physical Schur-complement inequalities
\[
A-\sum_j\alpha_js_jb_jb_j^*\succeq0,
\qquad
I-A-\sum_j\beta_js_jb_jb_j^*\succeq0,
\tag{7.3}
\]
where
\[
\alpha_j=v_j^*C_j^{-1}v_j,
\qquad
\beta_j=v_j^*(I-C_j)^{-1}v_j.
\]

Distance to a convex set in a norm is convex, so (7.2)--(7.3) define a convex domain in the core and squared satellite strengths. It is coordinatewise downward closed in the strengths. Every conditional core box vertex remains within operator distance \(1/2000\) of one common point of \(\mathcal A_\star\), hence lies in \(\mathcal P_\star\). The earlier conditional-entropy proof therefore yields Lyons concavity for arbitrary complex affine motion of all satellite amplitudes on this enlarged domain.

Moreover, the same C082 curvature constant yields the quantitative conditional Jensen term
\[

\ge
\frac\gamma2t(1-t)
\left[
\|A_1-A_0\|_F^2
+
\sum_j(s_j^1-s_j^0)^2\nu_j\|b_j\|_2^4
\right],
\tag{7.4}
\]
where
\[
\nu_j=\sum_{y_j}q_j(y_j)d_{j,y_j}^2.
\]
As in the earlier lifting theorem, complex affine interpolation of \(z_j\) can only lower the intermediate squared strengths below their endpoint convex averages, giving a separate nonnegative mutual-information gain. No converse or stationary entropy-rate statement is inferred.
