\boxed{\delta_\star=\frac{25}{6201}}.
\tag{3.1}
\]
We prove that every Hermitian \(K\) satisfying

\|K-K_\star\|_{\mathrm{op}}\le\delta_\star

\[

\[
L(K)=K(I-K)^{-1}=(I-K)^{-1}-I.

# 5. Quantitative satellite lift from the new ball

We now apply the task-local convex-core lifting theorem with
\[
\mathcal C=\mathcal B_\star.
\]

Let \(O_1,\dots,O_m\) be disjoint fixed coherent satellite blocks. For each \(j\), fix

- a strict Hermitian positive contraction \(0<C_j<I\),
- a core vector \(b_j\in\mathbb C^3\),
- a satellite vector \(v_j\in\mathbb C^{O_j}\).

For complex amplitudes \(z_j\), define
\[
K(A,z)=
\begin{pmatrix}
A&z_1b_1v_1^*&\cdots&z_mb_mv_m^*\\
\bar z_1v_1b_1^*&C_1&&0\\
\vdots&&\ddots&\\
\bar z_mv_mb_m^*&0&&C_m
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
s_j=|z_j|^2.
\tag{5.1}
\]

For a satellite atom \(y_j\subseteq O_j\), put
\[
Q_{j,y_j}=C_j-P_{O_j\setminus y_j},
\qquad
q_j(y_j)=(-1)^{|O_j\setminus y_j|}\det Q_{j,y_j},
\tag{5.2}
\]
and
\[
d_{j,y_j}=v_j^*Q_{j,y_j}^{-1}v_j\in\mathbb R.
\tag{5.3}
\]
Define
\[
\Delta_j=\max_{y_j}|d_{j,y_j}|.
\tag{5.4}
\]

The exact physical squared-strength constraints are
\[
A-\sum_j\alpha_js_jb_jb_j^*\succeq0,
\qquad
I-A-\sum_j\beta_js_jb_jb_j^*\succeq0,
\tag{5.5}
\]
where
\[
\alpha_j=v_j^*C_j^{-1}v_j,
\qquad
\beta_j=v_j^*(I-C_j)^{-1}v_j.
\]

A simple sufficient condition guaranteeing every conditional box vertex lies in \(\mathcal B_\star\) is
\[
\boxed{
\|A-K_\star\|_{\mathrm{op}}
+
\sum_js_j\Delta_j\|b_j\|_2^2
\le\delta_\star.
}
\tag{5.6}
\]
Indeed every conditional box vertex is
\[
M_{y,J}(A,s)
=A-\sum_{j\in J}s_jd_{j,y_j}b_jb_j^*,
\]
and the operator norm of its displacement from \(K_\star\) is at most the left side of (5.6).

The conjunction of (5.5) and (5.6) is convex and coordinatewise downward closed in \(s\). Hence every pair of endpoints satisfying these finite inequalities obeys the full complex-amplitude affine-kernel Lyons inequality.

This gives a new global satellite family centered at a strongly correlated/non-weak-edge three-site core.

# 6. A strong endpoint Jensen gap

The C082 curvature constant propagates through conditioning and gives more than sign.

For each satellite define
\[
\nu_j=\sum_{y_j}q_j(y_j)d_{j,y_j}^2.
\tag{6.1}
\]
If \(v_j\ne0\), then \(\nu_j>0\). Also
\[
\boxed{
\sum_{y_j}q_j(y_j)d_{j,y_j}=0.
}
\tag{6.2}
\]
To see (6.2), perturb the satellite kernel by \(C_j+t v_jv_j^*\). Its exact atom \(y_j\) changes to first order as
\[
q_j(y_j)(1+t d_{j,y_j}),
\]
by the rank-one determinant lemma. Differentiating total mass \(1\) gives (6.2).

Take endpoint data \((A_0,z^0)\), \((A_1,z^1)\) satisfying (5.5)–(5.6), and put
\[
\Delta A=A_1-A_0,
\qquad
s_j^r=|z_j^r|^2,
\qquad
\Delta s_j=s_j^1-s_j^0.
\]
At interpolation time \(t\), let
\[
\bar s_j=(1-t)s_j^0+ts_j^1.
\]
The linear-strength conditional endpoint difference in satellite fiber \(y\) is
\[
R_y=
\Delta A-\sum_j\Delta s_jd_{j,y_j}b_jb_j^*.
\tag{6.3}
\]
Applying the strong core inequality (4.2) in every conditional fiber and averaging gives
\[
F(A_t,\bar s)
-
[(1-t)F(A_0,s^0)+tF(A_1,s^1)]
\ge
\frac\gamma2t(1-t)\mathbb E_y\|R_y\|_F^2.
\tag{6.4}
\]
The satellite atoms are mutually independent. Using (6.2), all linear and cross-satellite terms vanish, so
\[
\boxed{
\mathbb E_y\|R_y\|_F^2
=
\|\Delta A\|_F^2
+
\sum_j(\Delta s_j)^2\nu_j\|b_j\|_2^4.
}
\tag{6.5}
\]
Hence
\[
\boxed{
F(A_t,\bar s)-[(1-t)F_0+tF_1]
\ge
\frac\gamma2t(1-t)
\left[
\|\Delta A\|_F^2+
\sum_j(\Delta s_j)^2\nu_j\|b_j\|_2^4
\right].
}
\tag{6.6}
\]

Now pass from the linearly interpolated squared strengths \(\bar s_j\) to the actual complex affine amplitudes
\[
z_j^t=(1-t)z_j^0+tz_j^1,
\qquad
s_j^t=|z_j^t|^2.
\]
As always,
\[
\bar s_j-s_j^t=t(1-t)|z_j^1-z_j^0|^2.
\tag{6.7}
\]
The strength-monotonicity step from the lifting theorem is nonnegative, so (6.6) already remains a valid lower bound for the actual affine kernel segment.

There is also an explicit extra phase/amplitude term. At the intermediate kernel with strengths \(\bar s\), randomized removal of satellites plus concavity gives
\[
F(A_t,s^t)-F(A_t,\bar s)
\ge
\mathbb E_R I(Y_R;X,Y_{R^c}),
\tag{6.8}
\]
where satellite \(j\) is removed independently with probability
\[
\lambda_j=
\frac{\bar s_j-s_j^t}{\bar s_j}
\]
when \(\bar s_j>0\). The satellite principal marginal is a product, so
\[
I(Y_R;X,Y_{R^c})\ge\sum_{j\in R}I(Y_j;X).
\tag{6.9}
\]
For a core coordinate \(i\) and satellite coordinate \(k\in O_j\), determinantal covariance is
\[
\operatorname{Cov}(1_{i\in X},1_{k\in Y_j})
=-\bar s_j|b_{j,i}|^2|v_{j,k}|^2.
\tag{6.10}
\]
Binary Pinsker gives
\[
I(Y_j;X)
\ge
8\bar s_j^2
\max_i|b_{j,i}|^4
\max_k|v_{j,k}|^4.
\tag{6.11}
\]
Combining (6.7)–(6.11), the full endpoint Jensen gap \(\Gamma_t\) satisfies
\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
\Gamma_t\ge{}&
\frac\gamma2t(1-t)
\left[
\|\Delta A\|_F^2+
\sum_j(\Delta s_j)^2\nu_j\|b_j\|_2^4
\right]\\
&+8t(1-t)
\sum_j
|z_j^1-z_j^0|^2\bar s_j
\max_i|b_{j,i}|^4
\max_k|v_{j,k}|^4.
\end{aligned}
}
\tag{6.12}
\]
Thus the lifted family has an explicit quantitative gap controlling moving cores, changing magnitudes, and pure phase changes.

# 7. Boundary statement

The core ball itself lies strictly inside the positive-contraction domain. Satellite blocks were assumed strict only to write inverse formulas. Closed satellite contractions follow by the same common regularization used in prior task-local reports:
\[
K^\varepsilon=(1-2\varepsilon)K+\varepsilon I.
\]
Finite DPP atom probabilities are polynomial/continuous in kernel entries and \(-p\log p\) is continuous at zero. Therefore any boundary endpoint pair obtained as a limit of admissible strict endpoint pairs inherits the entropy inequality.

The explicit inverse-based quantities \(d_{j,y_j},\Delta_j,\nu_j\) are certificate devices for the strict approximation; the boundary theorem is the continuity statement, not a literal assignment of inverses to singular atom matrices.

# 9. Failed / unresolved route

A tempting stronger statement would be to use the entire C082 parameter box as \(\mathcal C\). That is invalid: C082 explicitly does not assert that its nonlinear odds-coordinate chart is convex in \(K\)-space. Concavity of the Hessian at every point of a nonconvex set does not imply the chord stays in the set.

The operator-ball construction above is the repair: it gives a genuinely convex subset by an explicit resolvent perturbation argument.

Another unresolved direction is to cover a large fraction of the C082 chart by overlapping certified convex balls and prove that arbitrary chords can be subdivided through overlaps. Local overlap alone is insufficient for arbitrary endpoint chords; one needs either direct chord containment or an additional gluing principle.
