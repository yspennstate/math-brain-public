\[

\]
Let \(\Sigma_4=\operatorname{Cov}(Z)\). Let
\[

be their kernel-derivative covectors, and set
\[

=
\operatorname{tr}(MH_\alpha MH_\beta)
-\frac12\operatorname{tr}(MH_\alpha)\operatorname{tr}(MH_\beta).
\tag{1.1}
\]

The preceding checkpoint proved:

- the kernel of the four-feature derivative map is strictly spacelike for the frozen Lorentz form;
- \(C_4\) has inertia \((3+,1-)\);
- the projected Fisher information is \(z^\top\Sigma_4^{-1}z\);
- constrained minimization of the frozen term gives \(\delta z^\top C_4^{-1}z\).

Hence
\[
-D^2H_J[A,A]
\ge
z^\top(\Sigma_4^{-1}+\delta C_4^{-1})z
\]
after minimizing over the feature fiber, with strict positive frozen curvature on the zero-feature kernel. Because \(\Sigma_4^{-1}>0\) and \(C_4^{-1}\) has exactly one negative eigenvalue, the matrix
\[
Q_4:=\Sigma_4^{-1}+\delta C_4^{-1}
\]
has at most one negative eigenvalue. Moreover
\[
\det Q_4
=
\frac{\det(C_4+\delta\Sigma_4)}

Since \(\det\Sigma_4>0\) and \(\det C_4<0\), the strict inequality
\[
\boxed{\det(C_4+\delta\Sigma_4)<0}
