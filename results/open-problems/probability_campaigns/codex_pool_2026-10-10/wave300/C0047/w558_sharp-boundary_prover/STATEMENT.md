# C0047 statement-only packet for the adversary

For every infinite finitely generated group Gamma and every finite symmetric generating set S not containing the identity, let G=Cay(Gamma,S) be the connected, locally finite, undirected simple Cayley graph. Use independent Bernoulli **bond** percolation with a common parameter p on its undirected edges. Define p_c(G)=inf{p: P_p(e belongs to an infinite open cluster)>0} and p_u(G)=inf{p: P_p(exactly one infinite open cluster)=1}. The target is

    p_c(Cay(Gamma,S)) < p_u(Cay(Gamma,S)) iff Gamma is nonamenable.

Both Gamma and S are universally quantified. The unresolved direction of this packet is the strict inequality for each prescribed S of a nonamenable Gamma. An existence theorem for a favorable S, a multiset of generators, a powered generating set, or a special class of groups does not establish that direction. Finite groups are excluded. Bond and site thresholds are distinct quantities and must not be interchanged.

Original source: Benjamini and Schramm (1996), *Percolation beyond Z^d, many questions and a few answers*, Conjecture 6, printed p.76 / PDF p.6: "Assume that the almost transitive graph G has positive Cheeger constant. Then pc(G) < pu(G)." The paper uses site percolation and states at printed p.72 that nonplanar results/questions also extend to bond percolation. The present packet explicitly addresses the bond Cayley subcase, not every almost-transitive graph or the site analogue.
