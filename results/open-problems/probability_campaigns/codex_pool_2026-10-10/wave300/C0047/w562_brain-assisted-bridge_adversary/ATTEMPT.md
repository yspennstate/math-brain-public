Primary target: independent Bernoulli bond percolation on every prescribed simple Cayley graph of every infinite finitely generated nonamenable group has p_c<p_u. The useful brain result is a concrete repair to the stored C0047-A001 spectral route. The returned top six include a generic spectral-radius card but no C0047 theorem/attempt. A bounded target lookup reveals the full attempt's claim p_c<1/rho for normalized simple-random-walk rho. Nonamenability gives 0<rho<1; since p_c<=1 this inequality is automatic. It cannot be the missing case. The primary sufficient criterion has an additional degree factor, p_c*d*rho<1 (1996 Theorem 4). The full assumptions include bounded degree, almost transitivity and a supercritical parameter satisfying p*d*rho<1. Nonamenability supplies only rho<1. On the degree-four tree p_c*d*rho=2sqrt(3)/3>1 despite p_u=1, so even the repaired criterion is not necessary.

Independent of the paired proof, I attacked generating-set transfer. Paths representing a^2 and ab in F(a,b) share their first edge. Their path-open events have marginals p^2 but joint p^3. Both stochastic comparison directions with iid Bernoulli(p^2) fail: the intersection is larger and the union smaller. This is a complete finite counterexample to the route, saved first in independent_obstruction.md. Exact rational enumeration, three parameter choices and disjoint controls are in overlap_measurement.json.

I then derived a restricted valid bridge: route each edge of H through a G-path of length <=L with every G-edge in <=M routes. Give routes distinct independent slots at each G-edge, OR slots to realize iid base probability p, and thin the resulting independent path events to q=(1-(1-p)^(1/M))^L. The product-space coupling and the critical-point limit are written in bridge_derivation.md. It gives p_c(G)<=1-(1-p_c(H)^(1/L))^M. This lemma is a candidate for referee review; no theorem node is promoted. At p=1/2,L=M=2 the repaired q is about 0.085786, not 0.25.

The single hardest missing step in this route is a bound preventing extra base-open edges from merging distinct infinite H-clusters. An existence coupling gives no such bound and no lower bound on p_u(G). This explains why changing generating sets cannot close the fixed-graph claim by marginal estimates alone.
