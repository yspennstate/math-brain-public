# Independent obstruction saved before paired proof

Date: 2026-10-10. Author: w562. No w561 proof has been read.

Fix Bernoulli bond percolation on the Cayley graph of the free group F(a,b), with base generators a, a^-1, b, b^-1. Represent new generators a^2 and ab by length-two base paths. Their paths from the identity are (e,a,a^2) and (e,a,ab). They share the edge {e,a}. If A and B mean that their respective paths are entirely open, then P(A)=P(B)=p^2, but P(A and B)=p^3, not p^4. Moreover P(A|B)=p and P(A|B^c)=p^2/(1+p). Thus the path indicators are neither independent Bernoulli(p^2) nor a stochastic domination of that iid law (the union event has smaller probability than under iid). They also are not stochastically dominated by that iid law (the intersection event has larger probability). These statements hold for 0<p<1. At p=1/2 the probabilities are 1/4, 1/4, 1/8, and 1/16 respectively.

This refutes a route, not the parent conjecture: a theorem giving nonuniqueness after changing the generating set cannot be transferred by simply replacing every new edge with a base path and retaining iid probabilities. The missing premise is a coupling/comparison theorem controlling the full joint law, and a separate argument preventing added base connections from merging infinite clusters. No marginal bound supplies either premise.

The original Benjamini-Schramm Theorem 4 requires supercriticality and rho(G)*k*p<1; rho(G)<1 alone does not provide the necessary interval. The degree-four tree already has p_c=1/3 and rho=sqrt(3)/2, hence rho*k*p_c=2*sqrt(3)/3>1, although p_u=1. This boundary example is a proposed independently checkable derivation, not yet a referee-approved theorem node.
