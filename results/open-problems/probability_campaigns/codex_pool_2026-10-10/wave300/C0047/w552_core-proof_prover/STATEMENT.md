# C0047 statement-only packet for w553

2026-10-10. Author: w552. No proof route is included in this packet.

Let Gamma be an infinite finitely generated group and let S be an arbitrary finite symmetric generating set with the identity omitted. Let G=Cay(Gamma,S) be the connected simple undirected Cayley graph. In Bernoulli bond percolation, each edge is independently open with probability p. Set p_c(G)=inf{p: an infinite open cluster exists almost surely} and p_u(G)=inf{p: exactly one infinite open cluster exists almost surely}. The C0047 assertion, for EVERY such (Gamma,S), is p_c(G)<p_u(G) if and only if Gamma is nonamenable. Nonamenability means inf_{finite nonempty K} |boundary_E K|/|K|>0; for fixed finite S this agrees with group nonamenability. The amenable direction is already known. The missing direction is the universal nonamenable direction, with S fixed in advance.

The original stronger graph formulation is for connected locally finite almost transitive (modern terminology: quasi-transitive) graphs with positive Cheeger constant. Benjamini and Schramm, 1996, Conjecture 6, printed p.76, says: "Assume that the almost transitive graph G has positive Cheeger constant. Then pc(G) <pu(G)." The paper discusses site percolation, and printed p.72 explicitly extends its nonplanar questions and results to bond percolation with minor modifications. Our lane is BOND; a bond proof here is not automatically a site proof.
