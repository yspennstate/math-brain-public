# P0297 statement for independent adversary

Fix gamma in (0,2). Let h be a whole-plane Gaussian free field with unit-circle average fixed to zero, and let D_h be its strong gamma-Liouville quantum gravity metric, defined as the normalized LFPP limit and characterized by length-space, locality, Weyl-scaling, and coordinate-change axioms. The target is an explicit determination, for each gamma, of the deterministic almost-sure Hausdorff dimension d_gamma = dim_H(C,D_h). A constant rescaling of D_h does not change this dimension. No proposed universal formula is part of the target.

Primary statement: Gwynne--Miller, arXiv:1905.00383v3, Problem 7.1 (printed p.80; PDF index 79). Source excerpt: "Compute the exponent d_gamma appearing in (1.5)" (notation transliterated). The same problem identifies xi=gamma/d_gamma and Q=2/gamma+gamma/2. At gamma=sqrt(8/3), d_gamma=4 is established prior work, not a new claim.

Source URL: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1905.00383

Current general openness: supported by the 2026 Holden--Sun ICM survey's statement that general d_gamma is not known; bounded literature/local search is not an exhaustive novelty certification.
