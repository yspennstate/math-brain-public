Let n>=1, L_n=beta_n=n and M_n=2^(n^2). Take M_n distinct singleton paths, each with deterministic zero Gaussian field and positive weight exp(n), and one long path of M_n independent N(0,n) coordinates, each of weight 1/M_n. The covariance is PSD and its maximum diagonal is n. Put S_n(xi)=M_n^(-1) sum_i exp(xi X_i), H_n(xi)=min(n,log S_n(xi)), and F_n(xi)=-(1/n)log(exp[-n^2(1-log 2)]+exp[-n log S_n(xi)]). Then E[H_n(2)-H_n(0)]/n -> 1 whereas E[F_n(2)-F_n(0)]/n -> 1-log 2. Hard-min median exponents converge, beta_n->infinity and maximum variance is O(L_n), but normalized integrated soft-min response has the wrong endpoint limit when uniform smoothing error is omitted. This is a finite Gaussian counterexample, not planar LFPP.

At xi=0 the long-path cost is 1, so H_n(0)=0 and F_n(0)/n tends to zero. At xi=2, E S_n=exp(2n) and Var(S_n)/(E S_n)^2=(exp(4n)-1)/M_n, by independence and the Gaussian exponential moment formula. Chebyshev implies P(S_n(2)<exp(n)) <= (exp(4n)-1)/(M_n*(1-exp(-n))^2) -> 0. Jensen gives log S_n(2)>=2 Xbar with E|2 Xbar|=2 sqrt(2n/(pi M_n)). For 0<c<=1, 0<=c n-E min(c n,log S_n(2)) <= c n P(S_n(2)<exp(n))+2 sqrt(2n/(pi M_n)), so its normalized limit is c. Use c=1 for the hard minimum. The displayed soft minimum differs from min((1-log 2)n,log S_n(2)) by at most log 2/n; use c=1-log 2 to get its limit. The fixed-n Gaussian interpolation identity equals the soft endpoint difference exactly. Here log(M_n+1)/(beta_n L_n)->log 2, explicitly violating the sufficient entropy premise of the valid scale bridge. No network was materialized or sampled.
