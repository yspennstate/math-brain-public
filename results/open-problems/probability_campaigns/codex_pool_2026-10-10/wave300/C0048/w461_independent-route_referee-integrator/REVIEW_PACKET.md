## Hardest load-bearing rederivation

Let μ=free FK and ν=wired FK at fixed p=1-exp(-K), with μ stochastically below ν. For a finite increasing event A with m supporting edges and gap δ=ν(A)-μ(A)>0, take an ordered coupling. The event can change only if a supporting edge changes, so δ≤Σ_e(ν(ω_e)-μ(ω_e)). Translation and lattice symmetry make every nearest-neighbor edge gap Δ_e, yielding Δ_e≥δ/m. Edwards–Sokal then yields a spin nearest-neighbor agreement gap Δ_e/p. For finite boxes, dividing internal bond sums by volume and taking the limit gives subgradients a_f=d e_f, a_w=d e_w of the *same* thermodynamic pressure: the fixed and free boundary log partition functions differ by at most b log q+2dKb, with b/volume→0, and each convex tangent inequality survives the volume limit. The right derivative is at least a_w and the left derivative at most a_f. Therefore P'_+(K)-P'_-(K)≥dδ/(pm). This is a correct conditional deduction. The unproved step is δ>0 at p_c for every missing (d,q), with one fixed finite witness independent of the outer box. Even that energy jump needs a separate bridge to the lecture's magnetization assertion. For the stronger premise wired θ≥η and free θ=0, A_R={0↔∂B_R} gives δ≥η/2 at some R, m=d(2R)(2R+1)^(d-1).
