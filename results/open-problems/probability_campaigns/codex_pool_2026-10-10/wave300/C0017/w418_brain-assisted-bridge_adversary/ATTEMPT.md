Working formulation, deliberately stated as a formulation rather than quoted from the intake: for each bounded simply connected Jordan domain D and four distinct counterclockwise boundary marks a,b,c,d, use consistent inner square-lattice approximations D_delta with approximating marks. Free critical bond crossing probabilities from arc ab to arc cd should converge, along the full mesh sequence delta -> 0, to Cardy's conformal crossing value. Separately, with a,b distinct and Dobrushin primal/dual boundary conditions, the exploration path should converge in distribution modulo increasing reparametrization (uniform curve metric) to chordal SLE_6 from a to b in D. General prime-end/Caratheodory formulations require their own approximation and boundary regularity statements. A crossing sublemma is not a proof of the complete target without the interface-identification input.
