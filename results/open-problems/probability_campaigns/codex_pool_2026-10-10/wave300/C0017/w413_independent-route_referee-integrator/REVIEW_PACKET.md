| w411-C0017-L2 | KEEP, finite special case | Contract deterministic open edges and delete deterministic closed edges. If every optional edge probability is in [ε,1−ε], this exact configuration has probability at least ε^m. Since an all-or-none law gives zero mass to all intermediate partitions, TV is at least ε^m.
