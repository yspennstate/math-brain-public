## Exact statement and status

Drewitz--Ramírez, *Selected Topics in Random Walk in Random Environment*, Open question 2.20, asks whether directional transience of a uniformly elliptic iid nearest-neighbor RWRE in `d>=2` implies ballisticity in that direction. The September 2026 intake says `attempted_un`; it is historical metadata. OpenAI's 6 October 2026 public mathematics collection now claims an affirmative theorem and links a Lean solution module. ## Independent two-site bridge counterexample

This reproduces w450's L4 without relying on the author's arithmetic. Let `U={0,-e1}` and site rows `p+=(9/20,1/20,1/4,1/4)` and `p-=(1/20,9/20,1/4,1/4)` in the order `+e1,-e1,+e2,-e2`, independently with probabilities `51/100` and `49/100`. Uniform ellipticity holds with `kappa=1/20`; raw mean local drift is `(51/100-49/100)*(2/5)=1/125`.

Starting at zero, a return before exit requires the two-step path `0 -> -e1 -> 0`. Thus `G_U(0,0)=1/[1-p_s(-e1)p_t(+e1)]` conditional on types `(s,t)`. The four Green values, in order `(++),(+-),(-+),(--)`, are `400/391,400/399,400/319,400/391`. Multiplying by the type probabilities and the signed drift `+/-2/5` yields weighted numerator `-1004048/59246275`; the occupation denominator is `63724944/59246275`. Their ratio is `-62753/3982809<0`. Command: `python -X utf8 ~/brain_workers/w452/review_checks.py` (PASS, four exact fractions). This is a counterexample only to the auxiliary claim that positive raw mean drift forces positive finite-domain Kalikow drift. Its directional transience is unproved.

For general mixture probability `theta`, write `A=400/391`, `B=400/399`, `C=400/319`. The weighted signed numerator equals `(2/5)[(2theta-1)A+theta(1-theta)(B-C)]`, which expands to `160(31280theta^2+223282theta-127281)/49766871`. Its derivative is positive on `[0,1]`; the sign changes once between `53/100` and `531/1000`. Thus `1/2<theta<theta_star` is the exact interval with positive raw drift and negative two-site Kalikow drift. This is a threshold for this finite-domain bridge alone.

## Peer proof audit

- w450 L0: zero directional drift at every visited site makes the projection a bounded-increment quenched martingale; Azuma plus Borel--Cantelli gives zero directional speed. KEEP.
- w450 L1: first hitting probability `h` and post-departure escape probabilities `q_e` stop before using the transition row at `x`. Conditioning outside `x` is therefore legitimate, and the renewal equation gives `G=h/(p·q)`. KEEP.
- w450 L2: on each outside-`x` configuration, `kappa*S<=p·q<=pmax*S`. Nonnegative local drift is essential for the lower reciprocal bound. The resulting finite-domain lower drift `kappa*m/pmax` follows after averaging with nonnegative `h`. KEEP at finite-domain scope.
- w450 L3: direct division of the two-type numerator by its denominator gives `2b theta/[theta+(1-theta)r]` with `r=1+(b/a)(q_+-q_-)/S`; `r<=1+b/a` proves the bound. The claimed sharpness is for arbitrary nonnegative `q`, not necessarily realizable exit vectors. KEEP scoped.
- w450 L4: exact independent computation above. KEEP as a bridge counterexample. No parent-problem verdict.
- w451 B1: independent increments with mean `2epsilon` imply `X_n/n->2epsilon e1`; skip-free hitting gives `P(T1>n)<=P(X_n.e1<=0)`, which is exponentially summable. Bounded-increment Wald then gives `E T1=1/(2epsilon)`. KEEP.
- w451 B2: at `a=3/4`, `E log rho=-log(3)/2<0`, `E rho=1` and `E(1/p)=2`. The crossing recursion is an equality in extended nonnegative expectations, so `E T1>=2L+1` for every L. With an independent random stream assigned to each level-crossing excursion, durations are a stationary ergodic function of bilateral iid data even though not independent. Truncation and Birkhoff give `T_n/n->infinity`, and inversion plus directional transience gives zero speed. KEEP in dimension one only.
- w451 B3: axial-step selection is independent Bernoulli(1/2), and the chosen axial direction depends only on the shared layer variable, so `X_n.e1=Y_{N_n}`. The lifted walk remains transient but has zero velocity. For distinct sites on a layer, favorable indicators are identical, covariance `3/16`; site iid fails. KEEP as a boundary example, not a C0041 counterexample.
- w451 B4: for the B2 law, the harmonic scale gives `q^-1=1+sum_{k>=0} prod_{j=0}^k rho_j`. Here `E rho^lambda=(3/4)3^-lambda+(1/4)3^lambda<1` exactly when `0<lambda<1`; subadditivity of `t^lambda` yields `E q^-lambda<=1/(1-E rho^lambda)`. At `lambda=1`, Tonelli and `E rho=1` give divergence; monotonicity covers `lambda>1`. The layer lift preserves the same axial survival event but lacks site iid. KEEP as an inverse-moment boundary, not a refutation of the public proof's later stronger estimate.
- w450 L5: the discounted first-return identity uses `q_lambda,e=1-lambda E_{x+e}[lambda^{H_x};H_x<infinity]>=1-lambda`; it still stops before using row `x`. The L2/L3 occupation inequalities therefore hold for each `lambda<1`. Summing over sites is absolutely convergent since `sum_x G_lambda=1/(1-lambda)` and `|d_ell|<=1`. With `a_n=E[X_n dot ell]`, Abel summation gives `((1-lambda)^2/lambda)sum_n lambda^n a_n>=c`. If `X_n/n->v` almost surely with bounded increments, bounded convergence gives `a_n/n->v dot ell`; the weights `((1-lambda)^2/lambda)n lambda^n` sum to one and vanish on finite prefixes, yielding `v dot ell>=c`. KEEP with velocity existence explicit. Fukushima--Ramírez, arXiv:1902.08920, introduction lines 15-22 and 163-184, confirms the standard Kalikow-to-ballisticity implication and deterministic velocity consequence in iid uniformly elliptic RWRE. For `d=2,b=1/5,theta=1/10`, exact constants are `1/425` and `1/43`, factor `425/43`; the author does not claim actual-speed optimality.
