## w447: direct audit of original lemma files

**ER — KEEP, within the finite escape-radius scope.** At every actual block boundary on survival, a path of at most r steps to the complement exists. Quenched conditional probability to follow it is at least kappa^r; conditional tower iteration yields `(1-kappa^r)^m` without multiplying unconditional per-row probabilities. The adaptive set extension requires the set to be measurable when its episode starts and then fixed. It does not license future-event conditioning.

**FV — KEEP, as a necessary trap-size bound only.** At a point of depth r, the nonnegative-coordinate simplex of radius r-1 lies in S and contains `binom(r+d-1,d)>=r^d/d!` sites. Consequently `r^d<=d!|S|`. Combining with ER yields the displayed residence bound. It does not assign an iid probability to a discovered trap.

**CC — KEEP, including a non-stopping tau and future event B.** Independently rederived the load-bearing moment algebra: with `a=kappa^r` and `lambda=a/(4r)`, conditional geometric domination gives `E[e^(lambda D_i)|F_sigma_i,omega] <= a e^(a/4)/(1-(1-a)e^(a/4)) <= 4/3`. Because `e^(lambda sigma_i)` is measurable at sigma_i, tower iteration gives `E[e^(lambda sigma_m)] <= (4/3)^m`. Put `C=2log(4/3)/lambda`; then `P(sigma_m>Cm)<= (3/4)^m`. Pathwise `K_tau<=m` implies `tau<=sigma_m`, even if tau is not a stopping time. For `p=P(B)>0`, one unconditional Chernoff bound followed by one division by p gives `P(tau>Cm|B)<=P(K_tau>m|B)+p^-1(3/4)^m`. Block-tail summation with `L=ceil C` gives `E[tau|B]<=L(E[K_tau|B]+3/p)`. The reverse follows from `K_tau<=tau`. No Markov claim under the future-conditioned law is needed.

**GO — KEEP, solely as a family obstruction.** At zero bias, the transformed increments `(X^1+X^2,X^1-X^2)` are independent fair signs. Thus `P_0(X_2n=0)=[binom(2n,n)/4^n]^2>=1/(4n)`. For a positive bias delta, each 2n-step path has likelihood ratio at least `(1-4delta)^(2n)` relative to zero bias. Taking `delta<=1/(16N)` gives at least half of the zero-bias returns through N and a Green lower bound `1+(1/8)sum_{n=1}^N 1/n`. At each fixed positive delta, exponential tilting gives a return probability bounded by `[1/2+2sqrt(1/16-delta^2)]^n<1`, so the Green mean is finite. This refutes a uniform bound across laws, not C0041.

**Parent verdict — no KEEP.** The missing statement is `E[K_tau|B]<infinity` for the regeneration horizon and episode exit count under the full UE+iid+directional-transience hypotheses. ER/FV/CC/GO do not prove it. The local 2013 source's Remark 3.11 identifies regeneration-time integrability as the velocity gate.

## w446: direct audit of ATTEMPT.md finite-region lemma

**Finite-region visit/time tail — KEEP with its displayed kappa factor.** Let `D` be finite inside `S={0<=x1<=N-1}` and stop at `tau=tau_D∧tau_S`. At each actual visit to a given y before tau, the quenched probability of first stepping to `y+e1` is at least kappa. Starting there, the assumed relative-height crossing probability is at least `a`; the event reaches level `y1+N+1` before dropping back to `y1`, so it exits S before revisiting y. Hence the conditional chance that this is the last visit to y before tau is at least `kappa*a`. Strong Markov iteration at successive visit times gives `P(V_y>=k)<=(1-kappa*a)^(k-1)`; no independence of visits is used. On `tau>t`, exactly `t+1` pre-exit times `0,...,t` lie in D, so pigeonhole gives one site at least `ceil((t+1)/|D|)` visits; the union bound yields w446's stated time tail. A first step that already leaves S only strengthens the last-visit event. The factor kappa cannot be silently omitted: `a_N(y+e1)` is conditional on starting at the neighbor, whereas the walk starts the trial at y. This remains a quenched conditional bound under the strong assumption `a_N(y+e1)>=a` at every site of D; it gives no environment-probability estimate for that assumption and no parent proof.
