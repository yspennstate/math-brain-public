## L01: independent coordinate renewal calculation

Assume d>=2, iid nearest-neighbor site rows, uniform ellipticity, and `P_0(X_n^1 -> +infinity)=1`. Let `D={T_{-1}=infinity}`, `p=P_0(D)>0`, and `Q=P_0(.|D)`. Let `L` be the positive integer height gain of the first true regeneration word under Q, and let `L_1,L_2,...` be successive gains. The source's Proposition 3.5 gives the iid word law under Q; its Corollary 3.6 must not be misread as making the raw initial word identically distributed with these conditioned words.

For `n>=0` set `u_n=P_0(T_n<T_{-1})`. On Q, level n is a true boundary exactly when the walk first reaches n before -1 and thereafter stays at or above n. The prefix uses departure rows with height below n; the continuation uses rows at or above n. IID site rows and the Markov property therefore give raw probability `p u_n`, so `Q(n is a true boundary)=u_n`. Since true-boundary widths are iid, `U(z)=sum_{n>=0}u_n z^n=1/(1-E_Q z^L)` for `0<=z<1`. Directional transience implies `T_n<infinity` on D for every n, and `{T_n<T_{-1}}` decreases to D, hence `u_n -> p`. Abel's theorem gives `(1-z)U(z)->p`. Meanwhile `(1-E_Q z^L)/(1-z)=E_Q sum_{j=0}^{L-1}z^j -> E_Q L` by monotone convergence, allowing infinity. Thus `p=1/E_Q L`, so `E_Q L=1/p<infinity`.

Let `T` be the duration of one conditioned word. At regeneration endpoints, the iid strong law gives spatial gain per word `1/p` and time per word `E_Q T`, allowing `E_Q T=infinity` via truncation. If `E_Q T=infinity`, the coordinate speed along regeneration times is zero, contradicting positive liminf speed. If `E_Q T<infinity`, the word starts and ends at increasing true records, and its coordinate stays between its boundary levels; a time sandwich then yields coordinate speed `(1/p)/E_Q T>0` at all times. The finite raw initial piece does not affect the limit. Therefore coordinate ballisticity is equivalent to `E_Q T<infinity`. This proves the renewal reduction, not the missing integrability.
