The Cheeger convention in this packet is h(mu)=inf_{0<mu(A)<1} mu^+(A)/min(mu(A),1-mu(A)); the target is h(mu)>=c with c>0 universal. In the log-concave setting this is equivalent, up to numerical constants, to the variance-form C_P(mu)<=C.
