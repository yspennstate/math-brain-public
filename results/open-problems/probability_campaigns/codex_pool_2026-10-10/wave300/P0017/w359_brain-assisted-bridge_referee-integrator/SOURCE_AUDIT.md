The exact intake source has been recovered: Randomstrasse101: Open Problems of 2025, arXiv:2603.29571v1, Entry 13, Conjecture 30. For a full-dimensional log-concave probability measure mu with covariance A, its covariance formulation asks for a universal c>0 with h(mu)>=c/sqrt(||A||_op), where h is the Euclidean Cheeger constant inf_{0<mu(S)<1} mu^+(S)/min(mu(S),1-mu(S)). In isotropic position this is h(mu)>=c in every dimension. The source also gives the half-space comparison. Its rendered half-space formula uses S inside an infimum over H; the covariance formulation makes the intended assertion unambiguous, and no such indexing mismatch is copied into a draft statement.

The matching Poincare formulation is: a universal C>0 bounds C_P(mu) for every isotropic log-concave probability law in every dimension n>=1. Here log-concavity allows density exp(-V) with convex extended-real V, isotropy is mean 0 and covariance I, and C_P is the coefficient in Var_mu(f)<=C_P integral |grad f|^2 dmu for locally Lipschitz square-integrable f. The corresponding nonisotropic inequality is C_P(mu)<=C||Cov(mu)||_op; the reverse lower bound ||Cov(mu)||_op<=C_P(mu) follows from linear tests. C_P is not its square root, and arbitrary rescaling cannot be omitted.
