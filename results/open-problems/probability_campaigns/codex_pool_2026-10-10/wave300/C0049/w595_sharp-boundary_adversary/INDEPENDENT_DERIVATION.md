# C0049 independent boundary derivation

Author: w595. Saved 2026-10-10 before reading any w594 proof. Status: proof supplied; independent mathematical check pending w596. This is a conditional identification result and an auxiliary-bridge counterexample, not a solution of C0049. No novelty claim.

## A. Driver identification from stopped parafermionic martingales

Fix a real sigma in (0,1]. Let W be a continuous real adapted process with W_0=0 on a filtered probability space satisfying the usual conditions. Let g_t be its chordal Loewner maps, with partial_t g_t(z)=2/(g_t(z)-W_t). For every R,T>0 let tau=T wedge inf{t:|W_t|>=R}, and s=t wedge tau. Assume that, for every sufficiently large y (a sequence y tending to infinity suffices),

    F_s(iy) = [g'_s(iy)/(g_s(iy)-W_s)]^sigma

is a TRUE complex martingale in t. The branch is normalized at infinity: H_s(z)=z^sigma F_s(z) tends to 1, with the near-one logarithm of z*g'_s(z)/(g_s(z)-W_s). The large imaginary points are not swallowed before tau. No semimartingale property of W is assumed.

Then W is a continuous local martingale with bracket 8t/(1+sigma), hence W=sqrt(8/(1+sigma))*B for a Brownian motion in this filtration.

Proof. For z=iy, uniformly in s<=T and |W_s|<=R, the Loewner ODE gives

    g_s(z)=z+2s/z+O_{R,T}(y^-2),
    g'_s(z)=1-2s/z^2+O_{R,T}(y^-3).

For the deterministic uniform error estimates, Im(g_u(iy))>=sqrt(y^2-4T); thus |g_u(iy)-iy|<=2T/sqrt(y^2-4T). Expand 1/(g_u-W_u) and the integral formula g'_s=exp[-integral_0^s 2/(g_u-W_u)^2 du] in inverse powers of z. It follows that

    H_s(z)=1+sigma*W_s/z
      +[sigma*(sigma+1)*W_s^2/2-4*sigma*s]/z^2
      +O_{R,T,sigma}(y^-3).

The scaled martingales z*(H_s(z)-1)/sigma converge uniformly, with a deterministic error bound tending to zero, to W_s. This passes conditional expectations in L1, so W_{t wedge tau} is a true martingale. Consequently z^2*(H_s(z)-1-sigma*W_s/z) are also true martingales, and their uniform L1 limit is sigma*(sigma+1)*W_s^2/2-4*sigma*s. Hence W_s^2-8s/(1+sigma) is a true martingale. Localizing R,T upward gives the stated bracket, and the continuous-martingale characterization of Brownian motion finishes the proof.

The parameter relation sqrt(q)=2*sin(pi*sigma/2) gives kappa=8/(1+sigma)=4*pi/arccos(-sqrt(q)/2). At q=4, sigma=1, both recovered coefficients remain nonzero: W_s and W_s^2-4s. There is NO loss of this conditional identification at the q=4 endpoint. At sigma=0, F_s is identically 1 for every continuous driver; W=0 is an explicit driver satisfying the observable martingale condition without being sqrt(8)*Brownian motion. This only obstructs extending this identification argument to the excluded q=0 endpoint; it does not refute C0049 or the separate UST result there.

Source context: Duminil-Copin, Divergence of the correlation length for critical planar FK percolation with 1<=q<=4 via parafermionic observables, Proposition 6 and Section 3.2, https://www.unige.ch/~duminil/publi/divergenceFK.pdf. The proof above supplies explicit localization and avoids presuming that a merely continuous limiting driver is a semimartingale.

## B. Bounded martingales may converge uniformly to a non-martingale

On a common probability space take independent exponential times tau_n of rates lambda_n=2^-n. Let the common filtration be generated by all survival indicators 1{tau_n<=t}, with usual augmentation. Define

    X^n_t=exp[-(t wedge tau_n)]*(1+1/lambda_n)^{1{tau_n<=t}}.

Each X^n is a nonnegative cadlag TRUE martingale with X^n_0=1 and deterministic bound 1+2^n. Before its single jump it equals exp(-t); at the jump it multiplies by 1+2^n and is then frozen. The exponential memoryless property, and independence from the other clocks, give the conditional martingale identity. Directly,

    E[X^n_t]=exp[-(1+lambda_n)*t]
      +(1+lambda_n)*integral_0^t exp[-(1+lambda_n)*u] du=1.

On [0,T] it agrees with exp(-t) whenever tau_n>T, whose failure probability is 1-exp(-lambda_n*T)<=T*2^-n. These probabilities are summable. By Borel-Cantelli the entire paths X^n converge uniformly almost surely on every compact time interval to X_t=exp(-t). This deterministic decreasing continuous process is not a martingale, although every approximating martingale is bounded and starts at 1.

For every t>0, E[X^n_t]=1 while E[X_t]=exp(-t)<1. In particular the sequence is not uniformly integrable. This refutes the auxiliary assertion that bounded discrete/cadlag martingales and uniform path convergence suffice to pass a martingale to the limit, when the bounds depend on mesh/n. It is not an FK counterexample and does not refute C0049.

## C. Minimal audit obligations for an FK-to-SLE route

The unproved FK-specific step is to establish the correctly normalized parafermionic limit in the evolving RANDOM slit domains, together with true stopped martingales in the limiting driver filtration. A deterministic-domain observable limit alone must be accompanied by an argument permitting evaluation along those random domains. Bounds such as |F_delta|<=1 become mesh-dependent bounds after multiplication by delta^-sigma; they do not alone give uniform integrability. A valid repair can be uniform local L1/UI bounds, direct common bounded localization, or a separate theorem establishing the limiting martingale property. Uniform integrability is one sufficient repair, not the only necessary formulation.

No statement here assumes that a new discrete Cauchy-Riemann relation is mathematically necessary for C0049. Such a relation would be one possible route, not an exact characterization of every solution route.
