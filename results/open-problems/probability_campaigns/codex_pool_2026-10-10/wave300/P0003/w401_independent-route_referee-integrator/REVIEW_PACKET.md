## Verdicts on peer artifacts

**w399 `w399-P0003-L1`: KEEP as an elementary finite-graph lemma.** For a realized simple prefix η of n edges with tip v, compatible completions have weight `x^n x^{|tail|}` and are precisely vertex-simple tails in `H_n=G\{visited vertices before v}`. Cancelling `x^n` gives the quotient formula. The numerator deletes A as well, unless the prefix already touched A. Thus `M_n=E[1_avoid A | F_n]`, so it is bounded and martingale by the tower property; at the terminal time the empty-tail convention gives the indicator. The denominator is positive for a realized prefix because that prefix has at least one full completion. This neither proves a scaling limit nor applies unchanged after fixed-length conditioning, which also fixes the remaining number of edges.

**w399 `LEMMA_BUFFERED_TRANSFER.md`: KEEP as an abstract conditional lemma.** For compact K,A, `d(K,A)>0` exactly when they are disjoint; `K -> d(K,A)` is 1-Lipschitz in Hausdorff distance. Portmanteau for `{d>0}` gives `P(d(K,A)>0) <= q`; for each positive buffer r_j, closed-set Portmanteau gives `q_j <= P(d(K,A)>=r_j) <= P(d(K,A)>0)`. Let j increase. This requires the stated buffer-stable quotient limits, which are unproved for SAW. w399's own `APPROACH_DISTINCT.md` notes overlap with w405; do not package it as novel independent progress.

**w399 `LEMMA_THREE_PROBE.md`: KEEP as a conditional Loewner lemma.** I rederived its hardest drift calculation independently. Write `h(W+y)=h(W)+a y+b y²/2+c y³/6+...`. Expanding the commuting-map equation `∂_t h=2a²/(h(z)-h(W))-2h'(z)/(z-W)` gives `∂_t h'(W)=b²/(2a)-4c/3`. Itô for a driver `dW=mu dt+dN`, `d<N>=v dt`, then for `M=[h'(W)]^alpha`, yields normalized drift `e=mu u+(v/2-4/3)c+[1+v(alpha-1)]u²/2`, where `u=b/a` and `c=h'''/a`. At the three displayed slits the jet rows are `(-1/2,-3/4,1/4)`, `(1/2,-3/4,1/4)`, `(-3/4,-9/16,9/16)`; direct cofactor expansion gives determinant `9/32`. **w399 `COUNTEREXAMPLES.md`: KEEP as topology/conditioning cautions.** On C6 with opposite endpoints, two three-edge paths each have mass 1/2; avoiding vertices on opposite branches is mutually exclusive while avoiding two vertices on one branch is the same event, so product rules fail in both directions. **w400 `P0003-w400-002`: KEEP as an abstract conditional weak-convergence lemma.** For bounded continuous f, `f 1_E` has discontinuities only on `∂E`, a μ-null set.
