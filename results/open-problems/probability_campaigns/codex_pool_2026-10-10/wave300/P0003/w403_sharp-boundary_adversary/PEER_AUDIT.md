Let omega=exp(i*pi/3), v_j=delta*omega^j and d_j=v_(j+1)-v_j=delta*omega^(j+2). Put h_j=delta/d_j. This gives a six-edge field of modulus one. At v_j the two face-edge midpoint contributions are (d_j/2)h_j=delta/2 and (-d_(j-1)/2)h_(j-1)=-delta/2. The third-edge perturbation is zero. At every other vertex the perturbation is zero on each incident edge. The local relation therefore holds throughout any patch containing the complete interior face. Adding the constant field one preserves the local relation because the three incident displacement vectors sum to zero, and preserves every boundary value because h has interior support. Its sup norm is at most two; it is exactly two in this chosen orientation. A rotated lattice need not attain the upper bound, and no attainment assertion is needed.

Take a midpoint p on a face edge and q on the outward third edge of the same face vertex. Direct calculation gives |p-q|^2=3delta^2/4. The two field values differ by h_j, of modulus one. Both sampling points tend to the face center as delta decreases. Local uniform convergence to a continuous function would force this difference to vanish, which it does not. This verifies the load-bearing step of Proposition 3. The exact-rational check is check_peer_mode.py; it also flips the incoming sign and requires a nonzero residual, so its equation check is sensitive to that error.

For the kernel dimension, orient each internal edge and put J_e=(d_e/2)F(p_e). The field-to-current map is invertible, and the local equations become B J=-b. In a component with n vertices, a spanning tree gives an (n-1)-rank incidence submatrix, while the row sum is zero, so rank n-1. Thus compatibility is the componentwise vanishing of sum b, and the homogeneous complex dimension is |E|-|V|+c. Isolated vertices are included. In the connected planar case Euler's formula gives one degree of freedom per bounded face. Fewer than that number of extra complex linear constraints leave a nonzero kernel. Face circulations form a real basis: cancellation along a shared edge equates adjacent face coefficients, and the exterior face coefficient is zero. The face-basis Gram matrix is invertible over C, so orthogonality to every face circulation kills the kernel.

Boundary stress cases checked in the written derivation: trees have zero kernel, isolated vertices require b_v=0, multiple components require separate compatibility constraints, and bridges cancel in facial circulation vectors. Arbitrary boundary values are not always compatible; w402 explicitly includes the compatibility condition.

The proof does not establish that actual SAW observables realize these fields or satisfy any proposed cycle-fixing conditions. Its counterexample refutes the bridge from the local relation, boundedness and full boundary data to continuous compactness. That narrow conclusion is sound. The displayed sum over primal hexagon edges is a primal circulation; the source's Morera contours run along dual triangular edges, and the two contours must not be identified. This distinction does not affect the kernel or compactness proof.
