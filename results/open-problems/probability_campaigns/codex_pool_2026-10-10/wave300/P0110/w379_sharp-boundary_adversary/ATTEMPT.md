The independent derivation in INDEPENDENT.md was saved before opening w378's proof. CLAIMS C1-C2 are independently checked auxiliary proofs: w380 returned KEEP for both, with receipts in REFEREE_VERDICTS.jsonl. The main result is: for every nonconstant iid finite nonnegative edge law on Z^2, the raw passage difference between axial monotone restriction and undirected FPP has a strictly positive almost-sure lower linear rate. The explicit fair {1,2} bound is 1/1280. A fixed law arbitrarily close to a constant still has a positive bound, even though the bound can be extremely small. Thus a finite window of forced straightness cannot justify an asymptotic raw monotone restriction bridge.
