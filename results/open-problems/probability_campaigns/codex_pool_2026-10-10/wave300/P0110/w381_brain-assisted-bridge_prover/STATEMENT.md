# P0110: statement sent before proof work

2026-10-10. The campaign intake records: exhibit a nondegenerate iid undirected first-passage percolation law with well-defined fluctuation and wandering exponents satisfying chi = 2 xi - 1. The recovered primary source is Auffinger, Damron and Hanson, *50 years of first-passage percolation*, arXiv:1511.03262v2, Question 22, physical PDF page 89 (printed page 88), equation (4.19) on the preceding page. No current openness or solution is asserted.

Work in nearest-neighbor undirected FPP on Z^d, d >= 2, with iid finite nonnegative edge weights, nondegenerate one-edge law, passage time T(x,y) the infimum of sums along finite nearest-neighbor paths. Any chosen law must support the geodesic and moment assumptions needed by the recovered exponent definitions. A directed last-passage model or a point-mass law does not meet this intake statement.

Independent adversary: w382. Referee: w383. This file deliberately contains the statement and scope only, preceding the substantive attempt. Proposed w381 boundary: uniform nonrandom errors in converting a curvature detour bound into a geodesic tail; do not duplicate the other triads' explicit-law computations.

The precise sufficient formulation uses Chatterjee's global thresholds. Let D(0,v) be the maximum distance of all minimizing paths to the segment [0,v]. For every q>chi_a, require some alpha>0 with sup_{v!=0} E exp(alpha |T(0,v)-ET(0,v)|/|v|^q)<infinity; define xi_a analogously with D/|v|^q. For every q<chi_b, require a positive lower bound on Var T(0,v)/|v|^(2q) for all sufficiently large lattice v; define xi_b analogously using E D(0,v)/|v|^q. A solution in this formulation must establish chi_a=chi_b=:chi and xi_a=xi_b=:xi for an actual law. Chatterjee's Theorem 1.1 (Annals 2013, arXiv:1105.4566v3) then gives the relation for nearly-Gamma laws with a moment generating function near zero. Auffinger--Damron, arXiv:1109.0523v2, Theorem 2.2 removes the nearly-Gamma restriction in their stated model. The existence of matching thresholds is the missing hypothesis, not an output of either conditional relation theorem.

Uniform[1,2] on every undirected edge of Z^2 is the concrete candidate for this lane. It is nondegenerate, continuous, has all exponential moments and no atom at the minimum of its support. Uniform laws are in the nearly-Gamma class, as recorded immediately before Chatterjee's Theorem 1.1. This establishes eligibility for the conditional theorem only.
