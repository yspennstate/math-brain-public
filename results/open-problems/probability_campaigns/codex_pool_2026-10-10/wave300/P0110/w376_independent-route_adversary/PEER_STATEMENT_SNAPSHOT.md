Ordinary nearest-neighbor FPP on Z^d, d>=2, has iid nonnegative, nearly-gamma edge weights with a finite moment generating function around zero. T(0,v) is the minimum over self-avoiding nearest-neighbor paths; D(0,v) is the maximal Euclidean distance of the unique geodesic from the line segment [0,v]. Continuity gives uniqueness. For upper exponent thresholds chi_a, xi_a require, at every strictly larger exponent q,r, a uniformly bounded exponential moment of |T-ET|/|v|^q and D/|v|^r. For lower thresholds chi_b, xi_b require, at every strictly smaller q,r, a uniformly positive lower bound for Var(T)/|v|^(2q) and E D/|v|^r for all sufficiently large v. If chi_a=chi_b=chi and xi_a=xi_b=xi, then chi=2xi-1. This is conditional on exponent existence; it does not construct any law with these equalities.
