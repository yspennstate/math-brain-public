Proposed first lemma: if a nonnegative iid edge law has two disjoint support intervals [a_-,a_+] and [b_-,b_+] with positive mass and 0<a_+<b_-, choose n with n*b_->(n+2)*a_+. Set the n horizontal edges from 0 to n*e1 in the upper interval and the n+2 edges on the height-one detour in the lower interval. Independence gives probability p_high^n*p_low^(n+2)>0. The unique coordinate-monotone path has larger cost than the detour. This forbids an exact directed-path reduction even for arbitrarily small bounded disorder. It does not rule out an asymptotic coupling or solve KPZ.
