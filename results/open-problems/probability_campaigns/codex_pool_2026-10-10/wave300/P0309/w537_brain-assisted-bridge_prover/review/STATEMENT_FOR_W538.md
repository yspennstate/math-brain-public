The dated target is positive-temperature crystalline Gibbs phases for three-dimensional Riesz gases. Lewin, arXiv:2202.09240v2, Section VI.2, describes the conjectured 3D BCC/FCC solid phases at sufficiently large beta*rho^(s/3). The source treats short-range s>3 separately from neutralized long-range s<3. The ranking itself specifies no exponent interval or formal definition of a crystal. Current openness: UNKNOWN; no novelty claim.

Our precise necessary subtarget is a translation-breaking Gibbs limit at a fixed finite beta>0, for a specified neutral periodic Riesz Hamiltonian, after removing a bounded periodic field. It is weaker than classifying the BCC/FCC lattice or proving the entire phase diagram.

Boundary: w537 takes the pressure / vanishing periodic-field bridge. The other triads already cover harmonic covariance, matching-to-Fourier bounds, and finite-energy obstructions.

Independent question: For a bounded crystalline Fourier observable M_N with |M_N|<=N and a symmetry mapping M_N to -M_N, does pointwise convergence of p_N(h)=(beta*N)^(-1)*log E exp(beta*h*M_N) near zero determine the double-limit response as h decreases to zero? What extra hypotheses let this response survive a local point-process limit and satisfy the unpinned Gibbs equations?
