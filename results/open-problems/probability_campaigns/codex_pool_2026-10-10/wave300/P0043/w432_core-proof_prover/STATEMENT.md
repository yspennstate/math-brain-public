# P0043 statement sent before the proof attempt

Let G=(V,E) be an infinite connected locally finite quasi-transitive graph (Aut(G) has finitely many vertex orbits). Let P_p be independent Bernoulli bond percolation, retaining every edge with the same probability p. Write C_o for the cluster of a vertex o and p_c(G)=sup{p: P_p(there is an infinite cluster)=0}. Assume p_c(G)<1. The target is P_{p_c}(there is an infinite cluster)=0; equivalently theta_o(p_c)=P_{p_c}(|C_o|=infinity)=0 for every o.

The restriction p_c<1 is necessary: G=Z has p_c=1 and its p=1 cluster is infinite. The dated ranking omits this restriction in its short reason.
