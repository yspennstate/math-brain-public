P0043: critical Bernoulli bond percolation

Let G=(V,E) be an infinite, connected, locally finite, quasi-transitive graph (Aut(G) has finitely many vertex orbits). Give edges independent Bernoulli(p) states. For v in V write theta_v(p)=P_p(v belongs to an infinite open component), and p_c(G)=inf{p:theta_v(p)>0}; connectedness makes the infimum independent of v. Assume p_c(G)<1. The target is P_{p_c}(there exists an infinite open component)=0, equivalently theta_v(p_c)=0 for every v, since V is countable.
