On the undirected nearest-neighbor graph of Z^2, put iid finite nonnegative edge weights with an atomless common law G. For the standard shape-theorem working class also assume E[min(t1,t2,t3,t4)^2] < infinity, where the four samples are independent. Define T(x,y) as the infimum of finite-path weight sums. A bigeodesic is an injective doubly infinite nearest-neighbor sequence (v_i) indexed by the integers, every finite segment of which attains T between its endpoints. The target is P(there exists a bigeodesic anywhere in Z^2)=0.
