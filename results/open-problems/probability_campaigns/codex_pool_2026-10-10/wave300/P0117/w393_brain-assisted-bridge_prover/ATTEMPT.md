The chosen bridge is the distinction between global geodesic events and finite measurable box events. Let H_n say that some full-lattice geodesic between two boundary vertices of B_n passes through 0. Let E_n instead require a shortest path in the graph induced by B_n. H_n need not be measurable in the weights inside B_n. L1 proves that E_n is measurable in precisely those weights, is decreasing, and has the same infinite intersection as the event of a full-lattice bigeodesic through 0. A geodesic in one box is not automatically a global geodesic; global minimization is recovered in the limit by checking each fixed finite competitor after its entire support lies in a large box.

The substantive attempt is L2: expose successively larger boxes and prove extinction from divergent predictable conditional killing hazards. Its exponential nonnegative supermartingale permits dependence between scales. L3 supplies an explicit conditional annular killing construction: make the new outer ring cheap enough to bypass every through-origin path. This handles all boundary pairs simultaneously and uses only fresh ring edges. Unfortunately, its probability lower bounds are summable; this construction does not establish the hypothesis of L2. C1 shows that positive killing at every stage alone can leave limiting survival probability 1/2. Full statements and proofs are in LEMMAS.md.

Comparison with a strong available theorem. Dembin–Elboim–Peled, arXiv:2204.02332v3, Theorem 1.2, assumes absolute continuity, a finite exponential moment, and more than 40 extreme points of the limit shape. It bounds the chance of a specified vertex lying on a geodesic between fixed endpoints by C log^3(D+2)/D^(1/16), where D is the minimum endpoint distance. On the square boundary there are 8n vertices and fewer than 32n^2 unordered endpoint pairs. The resulting union bound has power n^(31/16), which does not vanish. Their common-height endpoint family has a stronger union estimate in Theorem 1.3, but does not contain all candidate endpoint pairs. These global-geodesic estimates also do not automatically apply to box-constrained E_n or after conditioning on every interior edge.
