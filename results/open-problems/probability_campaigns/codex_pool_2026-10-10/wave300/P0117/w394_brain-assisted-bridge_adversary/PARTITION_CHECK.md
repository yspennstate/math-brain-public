Independent derivation (w395 check pending): Fix a positive integer M. Let J_M(k) be a measurable mark indicator at grid site k with a real label L_M(k) whenever marked. All marked labels lie in a fixed compact interval I. Let S(M)<infinity be deterministic and independent of partition index j. Assume the marked center's label differs almost surely from the label of every marked site within distance S(M). Let deterministic finite partitions of I have maximal bin diameter tending to zero. Retain a marked center only when no marked neighbor within this fixed radius has a label in its bin. There are finitely many such neighbors. On a marked outcome, each distinct label pair is eventually separated; the maximum of finitely many required partition indices is finite. Therefore the retained indicator tends pointwise to J_M(k). Bounded convergence gives convergence of its probability to the original mark probability. No independence of marks or labels is required.

If in addition every finite partition yields q_(M,j)<=l W/M with l and W fixed independently of M, then first sending j to infinity at this fixed M yields q_M<=l W/M. A uniform q_M>=p/8>0 then contradicts sufficiently large M. No convergence rate uniform in M is needed in this order of limits.

Boundary counterexample: Let all sites be marked and let their labels be iid uniform on [0,1), so all distinct sites have distinct labels almost surely. Partition [0,1) into k equal bins. With a fixed number 2N of neighbors the center retention probability is (1-1/k)^(2N), which tends to one. If the neighborhood instead grows with the partition, N(k)=k^2, retention is (1-1/k)^(2k^2)<=exp(-2k), tending to zero. Conditional on the center label's bin, the other labels are independent uniform samples, so this product uses actual independence. This is a stationary iid-label counterexample to dropping the fixed-neighborhood premise, not an FPP counterexample or a refutation of the cited preprint.

Verdict on the inspected source passage: PASS CONDITIONAL LIMIT-ORDER CHECK. The passage explicitly freezes M, states that the same S(M) works for every bin of every partition, establishes a finite-neighbor bound, and sends j to infinity before choosing a sufficiently large M. Its form avoids the counterexample above.
