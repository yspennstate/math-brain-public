# P0117 independent-route boundary

Recorded 2026-10-10 before reading any current triad proof packet.

Proposed method: replace direction-by-direction exclusions by a countable, pinned finite-box extension criterion. For a lattice vertex v, consider geodesic segments through v whose two ends exit successively larger boxes. Establish the exact compactness/quantifier statement, then seek a conditional local-modification or annular obstruction that forces the pinned survival probabilities to vanish. The intended exclusion would be simultaneous over random directions and hence would not rely on taking an uncountable union of zero-probability directional events.

Main anticipated difficulty: forcing an annular obstruction uniformly over every possible pair of geodesic endpoints without treating overlapping or adaptively selected annuli as independent. Any such estimate will need a derived filtration or a deterministic obstruction, with its exact hypotheses stated.

This is a route proposal, not a theorem or novelty claim. If a prior attempt already owns this precise route, narrow to its finite-box obstruction/quantifier lemma and an explicit counterexample to an unjustified uniform bound.
