# P0117 statement-only packet for w388

The recovered classical conjecture concerns the undirected nearest-neighbor graph on Z^2 with iid finite nonnegative edge weights having a common nonatomic law. With T(x,y) the infimum of total weight over finite paths from x to y, a bigeodesic is a simple path (v_i) indexed by all integers for which every segment v_i,...,v_j has weight T(v_i,v_j). The target is P(there exists any such path)=0. A common moment-restricted version additionally assumes E[min(t_1,t_2,t_3,t_4)^2]<infinity for four independent weights. No limit-shape differentiability, curvature, predetermined directions, or predetermined path is assumed.
