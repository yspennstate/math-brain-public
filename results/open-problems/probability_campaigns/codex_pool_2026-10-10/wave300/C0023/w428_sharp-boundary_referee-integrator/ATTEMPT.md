## Independent rederivation of the hardest peer steps

For w426's forest lemma, fix o,b in one tree and its unique o-b path P. Every relay a has a unique projection pi(a) onto P (the median of o,a,b). Pick a_star with projection v nearest o. Every o-a path for any relay a passes v; the a_star-b path passes v. Therefore any open o-A path and an open a_star-b path concatenate through v to make an open o-b walk. If H is the former event and K the latter, H and K are increasing, so Harris positive association gives P(o↔b)>=P(H∩K)>=P(H)P(K)>=P(H)min_a P(a↔b). Disconnected components make either P(H)=0 or the minimum zero. On o-a-b the product inequality is equality. The sharp near-one modulus follows by multiplying two probabilities greater than 1-delta, and the same two-edge path rules out delta>1-sqrt(1-epsilon). The only use of independence is Harris positive association, not conditioning on a selected relay.

For w427's exhaustion lemma, define E_n(R) as connection in G_n from o to a vertex at ambient graph distance at least R. Any full-graph occurrence has a finite path witness, contained in some G_n; hence E_G(R)=union_n E_n(R), so P(E_G(R))=sup_n a_n(R). Local finiteness makes the ambient ball of radius R finite, and an origin cluster is infinite exactly when E_G(R) holds for every R. Continuity from above gives theta_G(p)=inf_R sup_n a_n(R). The same argument in each G_n gives theta_Gn(p)=inf_R a_n(R). Consequently separate extinction of every G_n gives sup_n inf_R a_n(R)=0 but need not give the reverse order. For a diagonal proof, choose R_j→infinity and G_nj containing the entire ambient ball of radius R_j; first-hit paths then make E_nj(R_j)=E_G(R_j). A_nj(R_j)→0 is sufficient. Merely growing R for each fixed thin strip is insufficient.

For S_k=Z×{-k,...,k}, each column interface has exactly 2k+1 independently retained horizontal bonds. Its all-closed probability is q=(1-p)^(2k+1); distinct interfaces are disjoint. An arm to x=R avoids R cuts, and likewise to x=-R, giving 2(1-q)^R. Fixed finite k and p<1 imply no infinite cluster; p=1 connects the strip, so p_c=1. At p=99/100 the union Z² percolates: a finite origin cluster has an enclosing closed dual circuit, at most 4m3^(m-1) such circuits of length m>=4, and the union bound is 10557/2352250000<1/100000. Thus this is a counterexample to unrestricted transfer only.

The finite selected-relay and cyclic-witness examples were checked independently by exact enumeration in verify_review.py. The relay substitution is 3/16 against actual 1/8 at edge parameter 1/2; the cyclic product inequality still holds, margin 1/128. Neither refutes probabilistic gluing.
