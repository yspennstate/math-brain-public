## New candidate lemma: a polynomial dependence-range criterion

Let d>=2 and let (X_x)_(x in Z^d) be a {0,1}-valued field. Assume an integer r>=0 and the following setwise independence: whenever two vertex sets have infinity-norm distance greater than r, the sigma-fields generated by their X variables are independent. Assume P(X_x=0)<=delta for every x. Define

    epsilon_r = 64^(-4)/(r+1)^2.

If delta<=epsilon_r, then P(0 belongs to an infinite nearest-neighbour component of good sites)>0. No stationarity, monotonicity, FKG, or independence of adjacent sites is assumed.

Proof candidate: restrict to the coordinate Z^2 plane and put s=r+1. Partition it into disjoint square tiles

    T_z = s z + {0,...,s-1}^2, z in Z^2.

Let Y_z=1 exactly when every X site in T_z is good. A union bound gives P(Y_z=0)<=s^2 delta<=64^(-4). If two coarse index sets have infinity-norm distance greater than 1, their unions of tiles have physical infinity-norm distance at least s+1=r+2>r. Consequently Y is setwise 1-dependent. For any specified m bad coarse sites, one of the four coordinate-parity classes contains at least ceil(m/4) of them, and the variables in that class are jointly independent by successive use of the setwise independence premise. Thus the all-bad probability is at most epsilon^(m/4), with epsilon=64^(-4).

If the coarse origin is good but its good component is finite, the standard square-lattice exterior-boundary contour fact supplies an enclosing circuit of bad sites with star adjacency. There are at most m*8^m candidate circuits of m sites: choose a site on the positive horizontal ray at coordinate 1<=j<=m, then overcount by all star-step sequences. The union bound is at most sum_(m>=1) m*(8 epsilon^(1/4))^m = (1/8)/(1-1/8)^2 = 8/49. Hence the probability that the coarse origin has an infinite good component is at least 1-64^(-4)-8/49>0. Every good tile is fully occupied; adjacent good tiles connect through their faces, so the coarse infinite component yields an infinite X component containing physical origin 0. The same path lies in Z^d. For delta=0 the conclusion is immediate.

Checks requested of w431: setwise independence of tile unions; joint independence in a parity class; the standard exterior contour fact and its count; physical compatibility and the origin assertion; all strict/non-strict inequalities and the r=0 boundary case.
