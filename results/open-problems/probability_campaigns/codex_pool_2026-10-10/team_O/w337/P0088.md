# P0088: couple target marks, then account for adaptive exposure

Label: proof of a coupling lemma and a conditional reduction. Blind audit PENDING. Equality of the two model limits remains unresolved.

For p_i in [0,1], take independent B_i~Bernoulli(p_i) and independent N_i~Poisson(p_i). Keep the older-vertex target i as a mark; counts alone discard it. Total variation here means sup_A |P(A)-Q(A)|, half the l1 distance.

For one coordinate the only positive difference Bernoulli-minus-Poisson occurs at value 1. Indeed exp(-p)>=1-p at zero, and Poisson has additional mass at values >=2. Thus

    d_TV(Bernoulli(p),Poisson(p))=p(1-exp(-p))<=p^2.

Use an optimal coupling independently in each coordinate. A union bound gives

    d_TV(Law((B_i)_i),Law((N_i)_i))
       <= sum_i p_i(1-exp(-p_i)) <= sum_i p_i^2.

Taking any measurable pushforward, including sum_i B_i delta_i or target-age/type marks attached to i, cannot increase total variation. Equivalently the Poisson vector can be sampled by a Poisson outgoing count of mean Lambda=sum_i p_i followed by iid labels with probabilities p_i/Lambda (when Lambda>0). This describes the required MATCHED, frozen-weight Poisson birth kernel, not arbitrary sequential degree-updating attachment.

For a finite-graph exploration in countable exposure and history spaces, exposing at most m conditional birth/neighbor kernels, suppose that whenever the two histories agree, their next marked conditional kernels have total variation at most epsilon_j, uniformly over every matched history at step j. A sequential maximal coupling keeps the histories matched except with probability at most sum_{j=1}^m epsilon_j: condition on agreement up to the preceding step, couple that conditional pair, and union-bound the first failure events. Countability makes the history-indexed maximal couplings measurable. For a random exploration size with P(size>m)<=rho, the bound becomes rho+sum_j epsilon_j. If an additional finite-graph-to-tree comparison has collision probability kappa, add kappa as a separate error.

If max_i p_i<=a and sum_i p_i<=L, the marked one-step error is at most aL. This moves the cached count-only approximation to target-marked kernels. It still does not verify the local limit: actual parent and incoming-neighbor explorations may have different conditional kernels, high-degree early vertices may violate the uniform small-p condition, and sequential updating within a Poisson birth is an extra discrepancy. No assumption about these points is silently made.

A simple exact counterexample shows why matching counts is inadequate. The vectors (B,0) and (0,B), with B~Bernoulli(p), have identical outgoing counts but their target-marked laws have total variation p. In particular p=1/2 gives count distance zero and marked distance 1/2.

Checks: p0088_check.py computes the full marked-vector variation using 1-sum_{binary vectors} min(P_B,P_N), which includes all Poisson nonbinary mass without truncation, and checks the bound at several dimensions and probability regimes. It also verifies the count-versus-mark counterexample exactly.
