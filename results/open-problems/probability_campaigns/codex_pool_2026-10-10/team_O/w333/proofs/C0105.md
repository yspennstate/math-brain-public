## An explicit moment window for time-averaged prime phases

Label: proof; independent audit pending.

Let tau be uniform on [T,2T], T>0, and let A and B be products of at most r prime factors each, all primes <=y, y>=2. For A=B, E exp(-i tau log(A/B))=1. For A!=B its modulus is at most min(1,2/(T |log(A/B)|))<=min(1,2 y^r/T). Independent Haar phases at the primes have the same expectation 1 when A=B and 0 otherwise. Thus for a finite polynomial in these phases and their conjugates with such monomials and coefficient absolute sum M, the time-average versus Haar moment error is at most 2 M y^r/T. This is a sufficient fixed-moment approximation window, not a necessary condition and not a decomposition theorem for log|zeta|.

Proof: Integrate the single exponential exactly over [T,2T]. For distinct positive integers A,B, |log(A/B)|>=|A-B|/max(A,B)>=y^(-r). Unique factorization identifies A=B with matching prime exponents, exactly the Haar resonance condition. Sum the monomial errors against absolute coefficients. A growing moment order or prime cutoff requires a quantitative bound on M y^r/T; failure of this upper bound to vanish proves no failure of independence by itself. Controlling the zeta approximation remainder and spatial increments remains separate.
