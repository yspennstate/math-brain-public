## An exact boundary-occupation functional for synchronous reflection outside a disk

Label: proof; independent audit pending.

Let X,Y solve the synchronous Skorokhod equations in {|x|>=R}, R>0, with the same continuous driving path and inward-to-domain normal X/R or Y/R on the boundary. Write r=|X-Y| and let L_X,L_Y be their boundary regulators. Before r reaches zero, d log r = (1/(2R))(1-(|Y|^2-R^2)/r^2) dL_X + (1/(2R))(1-(|X|^2-R^2)/r^2) dL_Y. For reflected Brownian motions the same identity holds pathwise because the common Brownian increments cancel in X-Y. Thus coalescence at infinity is equivalent to this signed occupation functional tending to minus infinity. No sign or long-time behavior of that functional is proved here.

Proof: The difference X-Y is a continuous finite-variation path. Apply the ordinary chain rule to its squared norm and then its logarithm, localized away from zero. On dL_X support |X|=R and (X-Y).X/R=(r^2+R^2-|Y|^2)/(2R); use the analogous formula for dL_Y. This gives the identity. Integrating it yields log r_t=log r_0 plus the displayed functional, so r_t->0 exactly when that functional tends to -infinity, unless the paths have already met. For R=1 and drive (-t,0), X_0=(1,0) has X_t=(1,0), L_X=t. Starting Y_0=(-2,0) gives r_t=3+t; starting Y_0=(2,0) gives r_t=1-t for 0<=t<1. These deterministic Skorokhod examples confirm both signs; they do not give a positive-probability Brownian asymptotic event.
