## A weighted covariance criterion for power-saving number variance

Label: proof; independent audit pending.

Let (eta_x) be a real, stationary, square-integrable field on Z^d, d>=1, with covariance c(z)=Cov(eta_0,eta_z). Suppose sum_z |c(z)|<infinity, sum_z c(z)=0, and M_alpha=sum_z ||z||_infinity^alpha |c(z)|<infinity for some 0<alpha<=1. For every integer L>=1, Var(sum_(x in {1,...,L}^d) eta_x) <= d M_alpha L^(d-alpha). These assumptions on the infinite-volume covariance are sufficient for a power saving; they are not established here for ARW wake states.

Proof: The variance equals sum_z n_L(z)c(z), where n_L(z)=product_i (L-|z_i|)_+. Subtract L^d sum_z c(z)=0. The overlap deficit satisfies 0<=L^d-n_L(z)<=d L^d min(1,||z||_infinity/L)<=d L^(d-alpha)||z||_infinity^alpha. Absolute summation proves the claim. A dyadic covariance estimate |c(z)|<=C(1+||z||_infinity)^(-d-beta), beta>alpha, supplies M_alpha<infinity by a geometric annulus sum. Finite-torus conservation alone does not supply the infinite-volume zero sum or a uniform weighted tail bound.
