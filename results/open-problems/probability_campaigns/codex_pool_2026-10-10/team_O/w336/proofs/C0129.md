# C0129: exact cookie-prefix moments; log-mean route unsupported

Status: proof written; w346 KEEP pending. The stationary-ergodic recurrence criterion remains unresolved by this round. The cached 2012 review poses Problem 3.11. The prior attempt alternates between a log-mean argument and an averaged-drift claim; neither is a proof of the general criterion.

Fix a cookie stack of length M, with independent Bernoulli success probabilities p_1,...,p_M in its first M trials and probability 1/2 thereafter. Let F_m count failures before the m-th success. For every integer m>=M with m>=1, set s=sum p_i, t=sum p_i(1-p_i), and delta=sum(2*p_i-1). Then

    E[F_m]=m-delta,
    Var(F_m)=2(m-s)+4t.

Proof: sample all M prefix trials and let S be their number of successes. When m>=M, the stopping time processes the whole prefix, except that it may finish precisely at its end when m=M=S; that exception has zero tail and satisfies the same representation. Conditional on S,

    F_m = M-S + NB(m-S,1/2),

where NB(k,1/2) counts failures before k fair-coin successes, with mean k and variance 2k (and NB(0)=0). Conditional expectation is m+M-2S, whose variance is 4t. The total-variance identity gives the formulas. They hold quenched, without any independence between stacks at different sites.

The associated map F_{k+1} has conditional expectation k+1-delta for k+1>=M. Its large-population linear coefficient is exactly 1 at every site. Therefore a nontrivial product of site-dependent offspring means is not supplied by this identity; a standard subcritical/supercritical BPRE log-mean test cannot be used to settle the cookie criterion merely by calling this a BPRE. Additive migration and low-population behavior still matter. This refutes that proposed mechanism, not the possible averaged-drift criterion or every BPRE method.

Boundary check: with M=2 and p_1=p_2=3/4, m=1 is outside the hypothesis. Its actual mean is (1/4)*(3/4*1+1/4*3)=3/8, whereas m-delta=0. The cutoff must stay on the card. No periodic-environment recurrence/transience claim is certified here.

Checks: enumerate prefix outcomes exactly using rational probabilities, check means and variances, and verify the m<M counterexample. These are checks of the local moment lemma, not of the full walk criterion.
