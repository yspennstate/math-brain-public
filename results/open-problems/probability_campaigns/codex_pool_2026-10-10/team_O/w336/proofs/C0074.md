# C0074: constant-order resistance estimates do not give convergence

Status: proof written; w346 KEEP pending. This weighted-path example diagnoses a reduction, not the unweighted IIC itself.

For each n>=2 take the path with vertices 0,...,n, normalized counting measure and distance |i-j|/n. Put conductance c_n on every edge, with c_n=1 for odd n and c_n=2 for even n. These metric-measure spaces converge to the interval with uniform measure. Effective resistance is |i-j|/c_n, so it is uniformly between half and all of graph distance, yet the resistance distance divided by n has no single limit.

Let the continuous-time variable-speed walk have generator L_n h(i)=c_n*sum_{j adjacent i}(h(j)-h(i)), start in its uniform stationary law, and accelerate time by n^2. Its process laws do not have a single scaling limit along all n.

Proof: the nonconstant eigenfunction u_n(i)=cos(pi*(i+1/2)/(n+1)) has eigenvalue -lambda_n, where lambda_n=2*c_n*(1-cos(pi/(n+1))). Its uniform mean is zero and its uniform second moment is 1/2. Thus

    E[u_n(X_0)*u_n(X_(n^2*t))]=(1/2)*exp(-n^2*lambda_n*t).

u_n is uniformly close to the fixed spatial observable cos(pi*i/n). For any fixed t>0, the displayed bounded two-time expectation tends to (1/2)*exp(-pi^2*t) along odd n and (1/2)*exp(-2*pi^2*t) along even n. A common limit of finite-dimensional laws would force the same bounded continuous expectation, a contradiction. Also the endpoint hitting mean from 0 is n(n+1)/(2*c_n), independently confirming the two time constants.

Every graph here is a tree; both volume and resistance orders are controlled. To use a resistance-form convergence theorem for the IIC one must establish actual scaled resistance and measure convergence, matching embedding and time normalization, rather than replace convergence by constant-factor estimates. The example does not assert that the IIC has this alternation, or that its scaling limit fails.

Checks: finite generator eigenvectors and stationary correlations, plus exact endpoint hitting systems. No literature closure of the unoriented high-dimensional IIC problem was verified offline.
