# C0008: an exact common-Gaussian-mode check on covariance comparisons

Label: proof of a limitation of bounded covariance perturbations, pending w347 KEEP. No disproof of Problem 16.8 or full universality result is claimed.

For any finite centred Gaussian field X_N and an independent Z~N(0,sigma^2) with sigma>0, let Y_N(x)=X_N(x)+Z at every site. Then Cov(Y_N(x),Y_N(y))-Cov(X_N(x),X_N(y))=sigma^2 uniformly. For any deterministic centring m_N, max Y_N-m_N=max X_N-m_N+Z exactly. If max X_N-m_N converges in law to M, independence gives max Y_N-m_N -> M+Z. The laws of M and M+Z differ: equality would imply phi_M(t)=phi_M(t) exp(-sigma^2 t^2/2); continuity and phi_M(0)=1 make this impossible at sufficiently small nonzero t.

The full point measure of heights is translated by Z, and the number of sites above a threshold t for Y is the number above t-Z for X. A bounded covariance difference therefore cannot alone force identical centred-maximum, extremal-process, or level-set limit laws. A universality claim must specify any allowed model-dependent smooth mode, centring, constant, intensity or decoration law.

This example changes the off-diagonal O(1) covariance limit. It may fail the exact continuum Green-function hypothesis in the source and is expressly not a counterexample to that complete hypothesis package. The prior conditional interpolation route needs its own theorem with the correct limit identification; an O(1) comparison by itself only provides comparisons, not equality of laws.
