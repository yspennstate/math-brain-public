Continuation: at u=0 the vacant set is all Z^d. Simple random walk has independent increments uniformly distributed on {+/-e_i:1<=i<=d}, mean zero and covariance I_d/d. The standard functional central limit theorem therefore gives n^(-1/2)X_(floor(nt)) -> Brownian motion with covariance t I_d/d. This is the zero-intensity boundary case; it does not cover any positive u.

The interior argument must check separate inputs: stationarity/ergodicity, monotonicity in the framework parameter, sprinkling/decoupling inequalities with their precise scale/error restrictions, local uniqueness, and positive continuous infinite-cluster density. Vacant-set occupancy decreases with the intensity u. If a formulation requires occupancy increasing in its parameter, set v=1/u and verify the transformed interval and sprinkling direction. Equality of thresholds identifies where a strong-percolation property holds; it does not alone establish every independently stated mixing or density-continuity hypothesis.

For each fixed u_0 in (0,u_*), choose a compact interval wholly below u_* around u_0. Any framework constants may depend on this interval; do not infer uniformity as u approaches u_*. A verified full-phase corollary needs exact source quotes for all framework inputs on such an interval, plus the correct walk (simple versus continuous-time), conditioning on 0 belonging to the infinite component, non-degenerate covariance, and dimension range. Neither numeric path simulation nor the zero-intensity boundary case verifies these hypotheses.
