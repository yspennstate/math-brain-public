# P0015: a nonempty open set of failing frames for every M>=2

Status: complete proof of the first clause submitted to w346; no KEEP yet, and no open problem is reported solved. The asymptotic question p_M -> 0 remains unresolved here. No novelty claim.

For every M>=2, among complex frames with N=4M-5 measurement vectors there is a nonempty open set that fails phaseless injectivity modulo global phase. A Gaussian frame has positive probability of belonging to this set. Consequently p_M<1, subject to acceptance of this proof. In addition p_2=p_3=0, with deterministic obstructions for every frame.

## Explicit transverse failing frame

Put H_0=diag(1,-1,0,...,0). Use the following N vectors:

    e_1+e_2, e_1-e_2, e_1+i e_2;
    e_1+e_2+e_j, e_1+e_2+i e_j,
    e_1-e_2+e_j, e_1-e_2+i e_j   for each j=3,...,M.

Every vector a has a^* H_0 a=0. They span C^M.

A real-analytic chart of rank-two Hermitian matrices near H_0, with H_11 fixed at one, is

    H(A,B) = [[A, B], [B^*, B^* A^{-1} B]],
    A=[[1,z],[conjugate(z),beta]], beta real, z complex,
    B an arbitrary complex 2-by-(M-2) matrix.

Here A is invertible with one positive and one negative eigenvalue near diag(1,-1). By its zero Schur complement, H has rank exactly two and the same nonzero inertia. The chart has 3+4(M-2)=N real parameters.

Let G_i(a,A,B)=a_i^* H(A,B)a_i. At H_0 the derivative of the bottom-right block with respect to chart parameters is zero. In the first three rows the derivative in (beta,Re z,Im z) is

    [[1,2,0],[1,-2,0],[1,0,-2]],

which has determinant eight. For each j, subtract the derivative of e_1+e_2 or e_1-e_2 from its corresponding extra row. The remaining four rows, in (Re B_1j,Im B_1j,Re B_2j,Im B_2j), are

    [[2,0,2,0],[0,-2,0,-2],[2,0,-2,0],[0,-2,0,2]],

which has determinant 64. The full chart derivative is therefore invertible, with determinant 8*64^{M-2} in this ordering.

The real implicit function theorem provides, for every frame sufficiently close to this explicit frame, a chart point with all N equations G_i=0. Its matrix H remains nonzero, rank two and indefinite. Its spectral decomposition is H=xx^*-yy^* with noncollinear nonzero x,y. Thus |a_i^*x|^2=|a_i^*y|^2 for every i, while x and y are not equivalent under global phase. This proves an open set of noninjective frames. The Gaussian law on C^{N M} has a strictly positive density everywhere, so this open set has positive probability. No quantitative lower bound uniform in M follows.

## Deterministic low-dimensional cases

If the measurement vectors do not span C^M, a nonzero blind vector and zero give the same measurements. Otherwise a nonzero positive or negative semidefinite Hermitian matrix cannot lie in the measurement kernel: a_i^* H a_i=0 for a spanning set would force its square root, and hence H, to vanish.

For M=2,N=3, the real dimension of the Hermitian space is four, so the kernel contains a nonzero matrix. It is indefinite and rank two, giving the ambiguity above.

For M=3,N=7, the Hermitian space has real dimension nine, so the kernel contains linearly independent H,K. Along the nonzero circle cos(t)H+sin(t)K, the determinant is continuous and changes sign under t -> t+pi, being homogeneous of odd degree three. The intermediate value theorem gives a nonzero singular kernel matrix. It cannot be semidefinite; hence it has rank two and one eigenvalue of each sign. This gives a phaseless ambiguity for every spanning frame too.
