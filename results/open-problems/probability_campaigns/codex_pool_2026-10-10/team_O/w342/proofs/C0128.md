# C0128: robust directional transience under a sublinear adverse-drift budget

Status: proof of a sufficient condition, awaiting w346. Does not settle the general stationary ergodic cookie-environment problem; no novelty claim.

Let X_0=0 and let X_{n+1}-X_n be +/-1. Conditional on the history through n (including the full cookie environment if random), its chance of +1 is p_n. Fix p_*>1/2 and assume

    B_n=sum_{k=0}^{n-1}(p_*-p_k)_+ = o(n) almost surely.

Then liminf X_n/n >= 2p_*-1 almost surely. Thus P(X_n -> +infinity)=P(sup_n X_n=+infinity)=1, and the two corresponding leftward events have probability zero.

Proof: M_n=X_n-sum_{k<n}(2p_k-1) is a martingale with increments bounded by two. Azuma-Hoeffding gives P(|M_n|>=eta n)<=2 exp(-eta^2 n/8). This is summable for each positive rational eta, so Borel-Cantelli implies M_n/n -> 0 almost surely. The predictable drift is at least (2p_*-1)n-2B_n, proving the claim. Divergence to +infinity makes the infimum over all times finite. Mirroring the signs gives the leftward version when positive drift excess over a p_*<1/2 is sublinear.

A concrete cookie-environment condition is

    sum_{z in Z,j>=1}(p_*-omega(z,j))_+ < infinity.

Each cookie is used at most once, so this total bounds B_n on every trajectory. In particular any finite set of arbitrary adverse cookies in an otherwise uniformly right-biased environment preserves directional transience and the lower speed bound. Cookies with zero right probability are allowed among that finite set. This condition is stronger than the sought general classification and typically is not a nontrivial stationary environment assumption.

The proof establishes the zero-one values by an explicit drift calculation. It does not infer a zero-one law just because the environment is ergodic or because a hitting event looks shift-invariant.
