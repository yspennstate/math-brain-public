# C0083: exact cycle counts and the first susceptibility correction

Status: proofs of finite combinatorial identities, awaiting w346. No coefficient of the critical-value expansion beyond the cited literature is claimed.

For the Hamming graph K_n^d with d>=1,n>=2, the degree is D=d(n-1). The numbers of triangles and simple four-cycles through a fixed vertex are

    T=d binom(n-1,2),
    C4=3d binom(n-1,3)+binom(d,2)(n-1)^2.

A triangle changes only one coordinate, giving the first formula. A four-cycle either lies in one coordinate clique (choose three other coordinate values, with three cyclic orders), or alternates two coordinates around a rectangle (choose the coordinates and one alternative value in each). These are exhaustive: a changed coordinate must occur at least twice in a closed walk; four steps therefore use either one coordinate or two coordinates twice each.

For ANY finite simple D-regular graph, let T_v be its triangle count at a vertex v and let chi_v(p)=E_p|C(v)| for independent bond percolation. As p tends to zero,

    chi_v(p)=1+Dp+D(D-1)p^2+[D(D-1)^2-4T_v]p^3+O(p^4).

Proof: sum the connection probabilities over other vertices and use inclusion-exclusion over their simple paths from v. The first coefficient counts length-one paths, the second counts length-two paths. Nonbacktracking length-three walks number D(D-1)^2; the 2T_v walks returning to v must be excluded, leaving the simple length-three paths. The only intersections of distinct connecting paths with a three-edge union are a direct edge and a two-edge alternative around a triangle. There are 2T_v such pairs. Hence another 2T_v is subtracted. All other path terms or intersections contain at least four edges.

In particular triangle contributions first enter this susceptibility Taylor series at p^3, not as a change to the coefficient of p. At p of order 1/[d(n-1)], 4T p^3 is O(1/n) for fixed d. This corrects the earlier heuristic that inferred an order-one change to the leading branching term from counting triangles. Low-order finite susceptibility coefficients do not themselves determine the requested critical-point expansion: criticality needs control of the resummed exploration or lace expansion.
