# P0001: weighted negative correlation on generalized theta graphs

Status: proof awaiting w346. Extends the prior cactus special case to a block with arbitrarily many internally disjoint paths between two terminals. General finite graphs remain unresolved here.

Let G be the union of r>=2 paths between two distinct terminals, with all internal vertices disjoint and no other edges. Give each edge e a positive fugacity w_e; a forest A has probability proportional to product_{e in A} w_e. The uniform-forest law is w_e=1. Parallel single-edge paths are allowed for the proof; simple graphs are included by using longer paths.

Under the independent product measure, an edge is present with p_e=w_e/(1+w_e). A subset is acyclic iff at most one of the r paths is completely present, since every cycle is the union of two complete terminal-to-terminal paths. Write P_j=product_{e in path j}p_e, t_j=P_j/(1-P_j), and S=1+sum_j t_j. Conditional on being a forest, the vector of complete-path indicators has probability 1/S for no complete path and t_j/S for just path j. Conditional on this vector, the paths are independent: a complete path has all its edges, and a noncomplete path has its product edge law conditioned not to be complete.

For edges e and f on distinct paths i and j, let r_e=(p_e-P_i)/(1-P_i) be the occupancy probability conditional on path i not being complete, and similarly r_f. The conditional expectation is r_e+(1-r_e)I_i. Conditional covariance across different paths is zero, so

    Cov(X_e,X_f)=-(1-r_e)(1-r_f)t_i t_j/S^2 <= 0.

For distinct edges e,f on the same path i, integrate out the other paths. The marginal is the original independent product measure on path i tilted by 1-alpha_i 1_{path i complete}, where

    alpha_i=sum_{j!=i}t_j/(1+sum_{j!=i}t_j),
    Z_i=1-alpha_i P_i.

Indeed, when path i is complete all other paths must be incomplete; when it is not complete, the others may have at most one complete path. Thus

    E X_e=(p_e-alpha_i P_i)/Z_i,
    E[X_e X_f]=(p_e p_f-alpha_i P_i)/Z_i,
    Cov(X_e,X_f)=-alpha_i P_i(1-p_e)(1-p_f)/Z_i^2 <= 0.

Every edge pair therefore has nonpositive covariance for all positive fugacities. Graphs whose blocks are bridges or generalized theta graphs inherit this pairwise assertion: cycle constraints and weights factor over blocks, so distinct blocks are independent. This extension does not claim negative association for general increasing functions.

Source and continuation: the prior P0001-A001 cactus proof and its warning that generic decreasing-event conditioning does not preserve negative correlation. Here the complete-path exclusion is explicitly computed rather than assumed to preserve it.
