# C0002: a uniform bulk one-point tail expansion

Status: proof of a one-point expansion, awaiting w346. This continues the corrected Gaussian-tail normalization. It does not identify the fluctuation law or large deviations of the entire DGFF level set.

Fix g>0 and lambda>0. Set L=log N and let a centered Gaussian H have variance gL+b, with B=b/g in a fixed bounded interval. For L sufficiently large,

    P(H >= 2 sqrt(g) lambda L)
      = N^{-2lambda^2} exp(2lambda^2 B)/(2lambda sqrt(2pi L))
        * [1 + (B/2 - 2lambda^2 B^2 - 1/(4lambda^2))/L + O(L^{-2})],

uniformly over that interval of B. The variance is assumed to be exactly gL+b; for a varying finite-N variance, b can be its exact remainder. A limiting b alone is insufficient for the displayed correction without controlling its finite-N error.

Proof: with x=2lambda L/sqrt(L+B), Mills' expansion is Phi_bar(x)=exp(-x^2/2)/(sqrt(2pi)x) * (1-x^{-2}+O(x^{-4})). It follows by repeated integration by parts, with the next integral bounding the remainder uniformly when x tends to infinity. Expand

    x^2/2=2lambda^2 L-2lambda^2 B+2lambda^2 B^2/L+O(L^{-2}),
    1/x=(2lambda sqrt(L))^{-1}[1+B/(2L)+O(L^{-2})],
    1/x^2=1/(4lambda^2 L)+O(L^{-2}),

and multiply. Boundedness of B makes every remainder uniform.

Thus bounded variance corrections enter the LEADING factor exp(2lambda^2 b/g), not merely a vanishing additive correction. On a bulk set of DGFF vertices where b_N(x)=Var(h_x)-g log N is uniformly bounded, linearity of expectation permits summing this expansion with B=b_N(x)/g. No independence of vertices is needed for the expectation. Pair correlations and the scale ladder remain necessary for fluctuation laws.
