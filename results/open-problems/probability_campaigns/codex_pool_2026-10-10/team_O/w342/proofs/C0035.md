# C0035: phase information survives equal two-point marginals

Status: proof and a refuted route, awaiting w346. General block-factor existence remains unresolved in the cached intake. The earlier sublattice result covers reduced degree one; this result examines a genuine degree-two case instead.

Let the Hermitian Toeplitz kernel have diagonal a, first Fourier coefficient b and second coefficient d. For the associated determinantal binary process,

    E X_i=a,
    Cov(X_i,X_{i+1})=-|b|^2,
    Cov(X_i,X_{i+2})=-|d|^2,
    E[X_0 X_1 X_2]=a^3-a(2|b|^2+|d|^2)+2 Re(b^2 conjugate(d)).

These follow by expanding the corresponding one-, two- and three-point determinants. The connected third cumulant is exactly 2 Re(b^2 conjugate(d)).

In particular consider f_plus(theta)=1/2+(4/25)cos(theta)+(2/25)cos(2theta) and f_minus with the last term negated. Both lie in [13/50,37/50], so both define stationary determinantal processes. Their coefficients are a=1/2, b=2/25, d=+/-1/25. Every one- and two-point marginal agrees, including independence at distances greater than two, but their all-one triple probabilities differ by

    4 b^2/25 = 16/15625 = 0.001024.

Thus matching all pair marginals cannot certify a proposed block-factor representation. A genuine degree-two construction must also account for the relative Fourier phase. This does not obstruct block-factor existence for either of these symbols.

For completeness, the earlier valid reduction is: if f(theta)=g(k theta), the kernel vanishes between different residue classes modulo k, making those sublattices independent. If P_g has an r-block iid representation Y_n=h(U_n,...,U_{n+r-1}), then X_i=h(U_i,U_{i+k},...,U_{i+(r-1)k}) represents P_f using a window of length 1+(r-1)k. This is conditional on an already proved representation of g; it is not an induction establishing all degrees.
