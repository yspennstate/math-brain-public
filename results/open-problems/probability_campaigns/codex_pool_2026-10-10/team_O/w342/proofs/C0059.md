# C0059: quartic volume growth does not decide transience

Status: proof of a refuted route, awaiting w346. Neither graph below is claimed to have the Boltzmann-map law. The cached Curien question for a in [2,5/2) remains unresolved here. Recurrence at the excluded endpoint 5/2 does not imply recurrence for nearby parameters.

There are locally finite planar trees of maximum degree three, both with |B(o,R)|=Theta(R^4), with opposite recurrence behavior.

**Recurrent example.** Start with the ray 0,1,2,..., with all edge resistances one. At each spine vertex n>=2 attach by one edge a finite full binary tree of depth floor(3 log_2 n). That finite tree has between n^3-1 and 2n^3-1 vertices. Its depth is O(log n), so for all sufficiently large R the entire trees attached at n<=R/2 lie in B(0,R). Summing their sizes gives an Omega(R^4) lower bound. The sum of all trees at n<=R gives the O(R^4) upper bound. Degrees are at most three.

Every excursion into an attached finite tree returns to the spine almost surely. After such excursions are omitted, the walk on the spine is the symmetric reflected nearest-neighbor walk on a ray, which is recurrent. Equivalently, any unit flow to infinity must carry zero current through finite attached subtrees and current one along every spine edge, so its energy is infinite. An exhaustion including each entire attached tree has effective resistance to spine vertex R equal to R.

**Transient example.** In an infinite binary tree, replace each edge from generation n to generation n+1 by a path of length ell_n=2^{floor(n/3)}. Maximum degree is three. At the end of generation 3k, distance from the root is 3(2^k-1), and the number of vertices through that generation is

    1 + 14(16^k-1)/15.

Successive displayed radii have bounded ratio, which proves quartic ball growth for all large radii. Send unit flow equally among the two children at every branch. An edge between generations n and n+1 carries 2^{-(n+1)}, and there are 2^{n+1} such edges, each with ell_n unit resistances. The total energy is

    sum_{n>=0} ell_n/2^{n+1}
      = sum_{k>=0} (7/8)4^{-k} = 7/6 < infinity.

The finite-energy unit flow proves transience.

Consequently polynomial volume growth, even with the same exponent and a bounded degree assumption, cannot prove the dual Boltzmann-map transience question. A proof must produce finite-energy current (or an equivalent escape criterion) using the actual law of the map. A finite Nash-Williams LOWER bound alone cannot establish transience.
