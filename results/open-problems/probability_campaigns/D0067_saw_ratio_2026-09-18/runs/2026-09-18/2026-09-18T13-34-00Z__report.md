## 7. Nearest solved literature and the precise missing uniformity

### Grimmett--Li weighted self-avoiding walks

Their weighted theory establishes weighted connective constants, bridge constants under appropriate height-function hypotheses, and continuity with respect to weights.

**Has:** the most important structural ingredient missing in the full-plane work — canonical regeneration.
