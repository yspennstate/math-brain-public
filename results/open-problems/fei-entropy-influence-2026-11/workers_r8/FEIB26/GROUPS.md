# Scope and provenance of the group census

The generator catalog is `group_catalog.json`; `group_catalog.py` constructs and checks it. The degree-eight generator block in `gap_trans8_excerpt.txt` is copied without alteration of its mathematical data from the GAP TransGrp source published by Alexander Hulpke, John Conway and John McKay, as mirrored in Debian Sources: [trans8.grp](https://sources.debian.org/src/gap-transgrp/3.6.3-1/data/trans8.grp). The degree-four and degree-six generators are transcribed from [GAP TransGrp lib/trans.grp](https://fossies.org/linux/gap/pkg/transgrp/lib/trans.grp), whose indexed source excerpt was opened by web search. The primary package manual describes the complete catalog: [TransGrp manual](https://docs.gap-system.org/pkg/transgrp/doc/manual.pdf). Hulpke's [small-degree classification page](https://www.math.colostate.edu/~hulpke/smalldeg.html) independently lists the counts used here.

The local code parses every permutation, generates the group, verifies transitivity, records its order, and closes each input orbit under its generators. Degree-six orders are checked against the source sequence. There are five degree-four actions, sixteen degree-six actions and fifty degree-eight actions in the saved catalog. Every invariant Boolean function is encoded by one sign per input orbit. Exhaustive runs fix one sign only when overall output complementation leaves the objective unchanged; unbalanced tower runs enumerate both signs.

All transitive Boolean functions on prime degree p are covered, up to relabeling, by the cyclic census: a transitive group's order is divisible by p; Cauchy's theorem supplies an element of order p; in degree p it is a p-cycle. This covers degrees two, three, five and seven. The full catalog runs at degrees four and six supply the remaining actions. Constants and one-variable functions have zero erasure and are handled separately. Thus the finite-function census and self-tower census through seven variables cover all transitive functions up to relabeling, not merely the cyclic ones. Different group actions can encode the same function, so row counts are not counts of distinct functions across all groups.

At degree eight, all fifty group actions were surveyed. Actions with at most twenty input orbits were exhausted; the remaining actions were annealed with stored seeds and functions. This is not an exhaustive degree-eight census. The first cyclic/dihedral and translation runs in `census/run.log` have narrower scope and some explicitly skipped large actions; the later catalog survey is in `groups/run.log`.

`census/*.tower.csv.gz` and `groups/*.tower.csv.gz` store one row per orbit-sign assignment, with its residual lower approximation, analytic tail bound, iteration count and endpoint flag. Floating arithmetic is not an interval proof of every row. All cyclic maxima were re-evaluated at 140 decimal digits in `census_audit.json`; its 128 flagged degree-seven rows were also re-evaluated there. The extra catalog-action maxima are re-evaluated in `group_audit.json`. The finite headline witness has a separate outward interval certificate.
