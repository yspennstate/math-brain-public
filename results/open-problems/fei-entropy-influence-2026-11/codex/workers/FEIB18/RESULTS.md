## Scope

The run enumerated 18,308 weight/threshold cases. Of these, 2,711 were constant and 15,597 were nonconstant. A case is a weight vector and a threshold, not a distinct truth table up to equivalence. Scaling, repeated thresholds on the same truth table and overlap between families are not deduplicated.

The rational-grid arm contains 15,396 cases. It exhausts nondecreasing positive weight multisets from {1,2,3,4} in dimensions 1 through 8, and from {1,2,3} in dimensions 9 through 16. Division by a common denominator gives the identical rational-weight grids. For each multiset it enumerates every truth table obtained at a nonnegative threshold, plus a constant threshold. Negative thresholds follow by input reversal and output negation. This is a finite grid census, not an exhaustive census of all LTFs.

The remaining families use dimensions 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 14 and 16: equal weights; weights 1 through n; one or two heavy coordinates of weight 4n with all others 1; a single nonzero coordinate; six seeded random integer families in [1,32]; and exact geometric progressions with ratios 1001/1000, 101/100, 11/10, 3/2, 19/10, 199/100, 2 and 201/100. Geometric weights are stored as the exact integer vector p^j q^(n-1-j), not rounded floats. The seed is 181110.

For structured families the requested threshold targets are 0, 0.25, ..., 4 times the full linear form standard deviation. Each is represented by floor(target*sd)+1/2 to remove ties; target collisions are deduplicated within a vector. The grid census uses all attainable thresholds and also covers thresholds above 4 standard deviations. Every saved row gives the actual theta/sd.

Noise products were evaluated at 44 fixed correlations in [0,1], for 686,268 case/correlation evaluations, including both endpoints. In addition 11,414 numerically real stationary-root candidates of the product polynomial were evaluated. Polynomial coefficients come from exact Fourier numerators; root locations and polynomial evaluations use floating point. This root search is not a certified algebraic optimization over the entire real interval.

Lemma checks cover 409 weight sets, with 1,619 window scales: all positive grid multisets in dimensions 1 through 6, equal/linear/heavy families through dimension 16, and 160 seeded integer vectors through dimension 16. The scales are max(w), max(w)+1/4, 5 max(w)/4 and 2 max(w), with duplicates removed. All sign patterns are enumerated. Because atom and window endpoints lie on a rational lattice, the code tests every distinct endpoint cell for c, alpha and t, rather than random locations. The empty sum is proved directly in PROOFS.md.

## Independent verification

verify.py independently recounts the two lemma extremizers using Fraction comparisons, checks each of the 8 recorded extremal metrics, recomputes full-function spectra and influences by edge counting, and applies the noise operator by direct two-point coordinate averaging. It also enumerates 108 small joint pair laws with exact rational probabilities; their residual against coordinate averaging is 0.

A separate 5,000-case sequence check includes zero prefix masses and entries over many scales. Its largest left side divided by the stated upper bound is 0.49017901223715. This is supporting finite evidence for an independently written proof.

The maximum residual in the noise decomposition is 5.5511151231257827e-16; Parseval, B_0=delta^2, the independence endpoints and rho=1 endpoints have zero recorded residual. Integer Walsh numerators, integer sums and square masses are exact at these dimensions. Floating logarithms and dot products account for the nonzero residual. The largest entropy calculation was independently summed with math.fsum.

The controls deliberately replace a closed Lemma A denominator window by an open window (the example R=±a then fails) and drop K=0 from the general two-sequence hypotheses (the unbounded A_0 example then fails). Their clean versions pass. No language-model workers, no model comparison and no brain/no-brain arms were used.

## Constants and the false illustration

constants.json gives the analytic supremum upper bound 7.45570200654455, hence M_cs<=79.5274880698086<80. The same file contains exact rational logarithm enclosures proving C<7579/250=30.316; the decimal display is 30.315745145395. verification.json also contains an exploratory numerical supremum value 5.79521095019814, which is not used to reduce the rigorous constant.

For independent centered Gaussians, Cross Stay/delta^2=(1-delta)^2/2. At half-width 0.001 the computed ratio is 0.49920243388195795, while 1/pi is 0.3183098861837907. Thus the asserted universal 1/pi formula is false. Removing it leaves the proof intact.
