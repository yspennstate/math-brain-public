## Programs and conventions

Every-coordinate payment is a linear program. Best-coordinate payment is a disjunction, so it is solved as a mixed-integer linear program, not misreported as an ordinary LP. The programs include every node from dimensions up to the table column, all census Boolean-root inequalities Phi<=Cprime I, and the scalar-leaf condition. Coefficients are fixed across all included dimensions.

Coefficient bounds are [-16,16], with the N coefficient nonnegative because leaves require it. C and Cprime lie in [0,128]. The total-objective fits do not hit coefficient or upper constant bounds. For every-coordinate LPs, an interior solution to these artificial upper/box bounds is also unrestricted in those bounds by convexity. Best-coordinate results are global numerical optima within the declared coefficient box. Doubling that box to [-32,32] for the six nontrivial families in best mode changes total objectives by at most 1.60568e-09. This is a robustness check, not a proof of optimality outside every finite box. HiGHS reports numerical optimality; these are floating-point programs, not exact rational certificates.

Two objectives are saved. The literal minimum local C appears first. Minimizing C alone permits large root budgets; its auxiliary Cprime is not minimized and can equal 128. The second objective minimizes C+Cprime, which is the quantity that would yield a B/I bound if the inequalities and root budget were universal. Neither finite-census objective proves a universal root bound.

## Proofs and the sign correction

PROOFS.md gives complete arguments, including an adaptive coordinate rule:

- Every bounded dyadic real function belongs to the specified node class.
- For Phi=t Abar+s B+u N, finite universal local C and admissible leaves are possible exactly when u>=0, t>=u, s>=t+1. Then C=0 works at every coordinate, but Phi(f)>=B(f) at every Boolean root. Thus this family only carries the original problem in its root condition.
- No fixed finite polynomial of level-mass moments, with V and N corrections, meets both universal conditions.
- No fixed combination of B1, B2 and N meets both universal conditions.
- No fixed combination of level-profile entropy, marginal entropy and N meets both universal conditions.

With the sign displayed in the brief, Delta B=J makes Phi=+B the tautology at C=0. Phi=-B instead requires 2J<=C||d||². Both signs, and the known H route, are excluded from candidate conclusions. Raw H and A add N to Hbar and Abar; the normalized basis plus N spans those raw features with the leaf constraint intact.

## Checks and resources

audit.py checked 4,353 scalar feature vectors and direct Walsh spectra for 66,324 truth tables. It also checked B and I recursions on all stored edges and accumulated whole trees independently. Maximum scalar feature discrepancy: 7.10543e-15. Maximum B-recursion discrepancy: 8.88178e-16; the I-recursion discrepancy is exactly zero in the saved arithmetic.

verify_fits.py recomputed node potentials for all 200 saved fits, checking 188,290,100 coordinate constraints counting repeated fits. Maximum normalized node residual: 1.23833e-10; maximum root residual: 1.62563e-09. Both are below the declared 2e-8 numerical feasibility tolerance.

proof_checks.py tested the sufficient entropy-potential conditions on 13,553,056 coordinate cases, including the exhaustive small census and larger random/structured samples. Maximum residual: 1.77636e-15. Direct spectra of products of AND_2 gates agree with the closed feature formulas with maximum discrepancy 0.

The compressed census occupies 131,322,091 bytes. Maximum recorded process RSS is 1,178,091,520 bytes. Every numerical process recorded nice 10; each uses one numerical-library/HiGHS thread, with at most two such processes running together. No files outside this worker folder were modified by the research scripts.

A checkable sampled Boolean example is f=-1 on the Hamming ball of radius one in six variables and +1 elsewhere: truth table 0x100010117. Its saved B/I is 1.639579773160, with I=0.93750000. audit.py derives its spectrum with direct character sums, derives I again from hypercube edges, and records both in witnesses.json. This is a sample witness, not a claim of a six-variable maximum. The five-variable witness 0xe reproduces the B/I value quoted in NOTES.md.
