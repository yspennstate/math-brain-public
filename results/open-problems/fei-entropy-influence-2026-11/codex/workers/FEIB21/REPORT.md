# FEIB21 report

The balanced tree claims are proved with explicit finite error bounds: E_root/Inf_root tends to exactly 2; the round-robin one-element witness is log2 D+O(1); the two-element witness is bounded. The full FEI conjecture is not solved. The minimum over all coordinates and unrestricted orders for a fixed window remains open, as does the requested linear-DNF range under only D 2^-w tending to zero.

The main extension is (LIN) in PROOFS.md section 12. For every fixed epsilon>0, a monotone uniform width-w linear DNF with maximum degree D<=2^((1-epsilon)(w-1)) has E_i/Inf_i=2+o(1) at every live coordinate, uniformly in the number of terms. Its finite coefficient is 2 plus an explicit error; coefficient 3 applies whenever that error is at most 1. This uses certificate-supported derivative pieces, Harris conditioning and higher mixed derivatives of the no-background indicator, so cycles do not require independent branches. The theorem is an erasure result. General restrictions need not preserve its uniform-width sparsity assumptions, so it is not presented as a completed FEI restriction induction. The proofs have been audited here but have not received independent mathematical review.

For example, linear_audit.json gives an error bound 0.199770494 at w=32, D=d=9848, k=3, hence coefficient 2.199770494. At w=256, D=d=2^191, k=6, the error is 4.844397831e-8. These are evaluations of a proved bound with ordinary multiprecision arithmetic, not directed-rounding certificates.

Every fixed mate window fails at the root in a different tree scaling, D^2 2^(-(w-1)) tending to c>0. The true root erasure still tends to 2, while for every fixed r the minimum over all mate orders has a positive multiple of log2 D as a lower bound. The explicit lower coefficient is one eighth of P[Poisson(c/4)>=r]. This also covers full windows in orders putting every leaf before every mate. It does not cover arbitrary interleavings. More decisively, private leaves give a one-element witness tending to 2, so the finite tree family is ruled out as a universal minimum-coordinate counterexample. Removing that boundary while controlling all orders is an unresolved mathematical obstacle.
