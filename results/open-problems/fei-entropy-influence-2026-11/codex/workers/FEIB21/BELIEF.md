# The good-coordinate statement I would keep

HEURISTIC / OPEN. There is a finite absolute C such that every Boolean f satisfies

    sum_i sum_T fhat(T+i)^2 log2(1+fhat(T)^2/fhat(T+i)^2) <= C I(f).

Terms with a zero upper coefficient contribute zero. This is the averaged deletion-cost form of FEI, up to the elementary additive I/ln 2 term. It implies that at least half the influence mass lies on coordinates with deletion cost at most 2C Inf_i, hence erasure at most (2C+1/ln 2)Inf_i. Conversely a universal good-coordinate erasure theorem, applied through restrictions, implies FEI and hence such an averaged bound. This is a reformulation of the main conjecture, not a proof or a new conjecture claimed as stronger evidence.

I would retain that averaged conclusion and allow the observing statistic to depend on the function's structure. I would not insist on the largest-influence coordinate: section 11 disproves that rule. I would not insist that a fixed number of last elements suffices at every relevant coordinate: section 8 disproves it even for all mate orders at one tree root. A fixed neighborhood is also insufficient along a PM tower, while its optimized one-element filtration remains bounded. The universal minimum over all coordinates and all orders for a fixed window is still undecided here. The available evidence does not justify declaring it false.

The first lemma I would attack is a mixed-width extension of the high-mate spectral separation lemma in section 12. Choose a variable in a shortest term of a linear monotone DNF, and assume the maximum read number is at most 2^((1-epsilon)(w_min-1)). Split derivative pieces by their first witnessing term. Pieces from much wider terms should be negligible relative to the shortest term after conditioning on the common no-background event; retain only terms up to a constant multiple of the minimum width. Prove a version of (LIN), with a finite coefficient uniform in the number of terms, for this truncated family. The intended tail estimate follows from Harris and the shortest-term lower bound, but the complete mixed-width entropy proof is not supplied here.

That lemma is concrete: all ingredients can be checked using conditional derivative energies and term-intersection counts, and it can fail or succeed independently of a universal FEI proof. It would remove the artificial equal-width hypothesis in the present coordinate theorem. It would not finish a restriction induction, because restrictions can lower the minimum width past the sparsity threshold. The next obstruction would be precisely those small surviving terms and the dense overlap clusters around them. A useful statistic should record their connected overlap structure rather than only their last few variable names.
