# Literature

I did not locate the last-position filtration witness, its optimization over permutations, or the displayed inequality E_i<=sum_k Phi(A_k,B_k) in the checked passages of the named papers; this is a scoped search result, not a novelty claim. The inequality itself is ordinary conditional-entropy data processing once its spectral label is defined. The closest established frameworks are the spectral coding and biased composition methods of Wan–Wright–Wu, [Decision Trees, Protocols, and the Fourier Entropy-Influence Conjecture](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~jswright/papers/readk.pdf), December 2013 manuscript, and O'Donnell–Tan, [A composition theorem for the Fourier Entropy-Influence conjecture](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1304.1347), arXiv:1304.1347; neither checked text supplied this optimized one-coordinate recurrence. Chakraborty–Kulkarni–Lokam–Saurabh, [Upper Bounds on Fourier Entropy](https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2013/052/revision/2/download/), ECCC TR13-052 revision 2, Theorem 5.11 and Remark 5.1, supplies H<=10I for read-once AND/OR/XOR/NOT formulas, which is the entropy theorem used in PROOFS.md. O'Donnell–Wright–Zhou, [The Fourier Entropy–Influence Conjecture for certain classes of Boolean functions](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~odonnell/papers/fei.pdf), ICALP 2011, treats symmetric functions and read-once decision trees. Keller–Mossel–Schlank, [A Note on the Entropy/Influence Conjecture](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1105.2651), arXiv:1105.2651, studies influence-based entropy bounds; Shalev, [On the Fourier Entropy Influence Conjecture for Extremal Classes](https://arxiv.org/html/1806.03646), arXiv:1806.03646, includes the improved read-k decision-tree bound and a min-entropy result for regular read-k DNFs; Kelman–Kindler–Lifshitz–Minzer–Safra, [Towards a Proof of the Fourier–Entropy Conjecture?](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1911.10579), arXiv:1911.10579v2, develops Fourier concentration with the extra logarithmic factor. A later directly relevant reference is González–MacManus–Pereyra, [Further evidence towards the Fourier Entropy-Influence conjecture](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.00246), arXiv:2606.00246v2: its entropy-drop restriction induction is related to FLOW section 7, but I did not locate the section-12 spectral filtration construction there. Primary pages and selected theorem passages were opened on 10 November 2026; searches for "filtration" in all these sources and "martingale" in Wan–Wright–Wu did not return that construction. Exhaustive later literature, alternative terminology and every proof in these papers were not checked. For the independent-tail threshold proof, the auxiliary normal-approximation input is the independent-summand Berry–Esseen bound with constant at most 1; Pinelis, [On the nonuniform Berry–Esseen bound](https://arxiv.org/abs/1301.2828), arXiv:1301.2828v5, records the stronger general constant 0.5600 and references its source.
