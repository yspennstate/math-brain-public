# FEIB29 results

All logarithms are base two. E denotes the sum of coordinate erasure entropies; B=I-W1.

## Initial deductions

PROVED (audited; full proof in PROOFS.md). Multiplication by a Walsh character translates the squared Fourier distribution by symmetric difference, preserving every E_i. Write p_i=Inf_i(f). Since the Boolean derivative takes values in {0,+1,-1}, |fhat({i})|<=p_i, and hence B_i=p_i-fhat({i})^2>=p_i(1-p_i). Translating by the character on {i:p_i>1/2} gives influence sum_i min(p_i,1-p_i)<=2 sum_i B_i. Thus a universal erasure FEI constant K implies E<=2K B. Conversely B<=I. No stability theorem is required.

PROVED (audited; full proof in PROOFS.md). At distance delta<1/2 from a signed dictator x_j, the same shift x_j has all influences at most 2delta. Therefore B>=(1-2delta)I(f*x_j), and erasure FEI gives E/B<=K/(1-2delta). This is conditional on FEI, not an unconditional proof of boundedness near dictators.

PROVED in PROOFS.md. The optimal near-dictator asymptotic supremum is exactly K_E=sup_h E(h)/I(h), allowing infinity. For each feasible dyadic delta, K_E<=A(delta)<=K_E/(1-2delta). Even paths with B->0, I->1, W1->1 retain every unrestricted erasure ratio. A universal bound near dictators is therefore not an easier theorem than FEI.

PROVED class bounds in PROOFS.md. Every symmetric Boolean function has E<=24.08B by the established symmetric FEI theorem and parity minimization.
