## Towers

PROVED: the recurrence separates edges whose deletion leaves their inner block nonempty from edges that empty it. If Z is the vector of unnormalized K, cost and L sums and B its empty-set boundary, then

    Z_parent = M (Z_child - B_child) + E_boundary,
    I_parent = M I_child.

Here M is the summed outer derivative weight. Boundary terms require only singleton spectral masses, noisy singleton norms, the inner variance, and the outer U_v values. Recursing partitions edges by the level where deletion empties a block. No tower truth table is constructed.

PROVED: for a fixed balanced base, successive cost and L differences are bounded by C(h+1)rho^h, and K differences by C rho^h, where rho=W1(base)/I(base)<1. The identity sum_i up_lambda({i})=(phi^h)'(lambda) controls false-positive boundary predictions. This proves convergence, including positivity of the limiting K. The dictator case has overhead zero. The constants depend on the base, so this theorem does not prove P.

## Majority

PROVED: under the size-biased majority spectrum, |U|/n converges to Beta(1/2,1/2), the arcsine law. More precisely q_n(2r+1)=4^(-h)C(2r,r)C(2(h-r),h-r), with n=2h+1. The noisy ratio at k/n approaching x tends to min(x/[(1-lambda)(1-x)],1/lambda). Thus thin tends to p(x)=min(x,1-lambda). For every predictor,

    K_limit = E_arcsine[p(X)/X],
    cost_limit = E_arcsine[-log2 p(X)],
    Ov_limit = log2 K_limit + cost_limit.

PROVED: the summand-ratio and tail bounds in MAJORITY_PROOF.md justify convergence of these expectations. L=0 for every odd majority. The prior heuristic of two nats misses the second endpoint factor in the size-biased density; the small-lambda thin limit is two bits.
