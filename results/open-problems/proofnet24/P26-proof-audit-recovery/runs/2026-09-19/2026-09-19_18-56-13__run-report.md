## 2. Method #2 — ATTACK THE NEGATION FIRST

\[

\[

With `rho=(mu+nu)/2`, `f=dmu/drho`, `g=dnu/drho` and dyadic conditional expectations `f_n,g_n`, one has

\[
A_n=\int\sqrt{f_ng_n}\,d\rho
\downarrow
\int\sqrt{fg}\,d\rho.
\]

Thus hypothetical non-singularity is exactly `A_infinity>0`.

For a level-`n` cell `v`, let

\[
p_v=\frac{\mu(v0)}{\mu(v)},\qquad
q_v=\frac{\nu(v0)}{\nu(v)},\qquad
w_n(v)=\frac{\sqrt{\mu(v)\nu(v)}}{A_n}.
\]

The exact cocycle is

\[
\frac{A_{n+1}}{A_n}
=
\sum_v w_n(v)
\left(\sqrt{p_vq_v}+\sqrt{(1-p_v)(1-q_v)}\right).
\]

Moreover

\[
1-\sqrt{pq}-\sqrt{(1-p)(1-q)}
=\frac12\left[(\sqrt p-\sqrt q)^2+(\sqrt{1-p}-\sqrt{1-q})^2\right]
\ge \frac12(p-q)^2.
\]

Therefore `A_infinity>0` forces the finite loss series

\[

## 3. Overlap-law bridge — PASS

Under `A_infinity>0`, define

\[
\frac{d\lambda_n}{d\rho}=
\frac{\sqrt{f_ng_n}}{A_n}.
\]

Then `lambda_n` is a probability measure and, on each level-`n` dyadic cell,

\[
\lambda_n(v)=w_n(v).
\]

Since `0<=f_n,g_n<=2`, martingale convergence and dominated convergence give

\[
\sqrt{f_ng_n}\to\sqrt{fg}\quad\text{in }L^1(\rho),
\]

hence

\[
\lambda_n\to\lambda
\quad\text{in total variation},\qquad
\frac{d\lambda}{d\rho}=\frac{\sqrt{fg}}{A_\infty}.
\]

In particular `lambda << nu`. Therefore for **varying** measurable events `B_n`,

\[
|\lambda_n(B_n)-\lambda(B_n)|\le\|\lambda_n-\lambda\|_{TV}\to0.
\]

This is the necessary strength; weak convergence alone would not justify the moving good-cell events later in the proof.
