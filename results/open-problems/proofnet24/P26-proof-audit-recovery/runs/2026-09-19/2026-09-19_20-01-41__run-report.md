## 6. Method #20 — FIND WHY THE ALGORITHM FAILS

Try to construct a nonconstant quasiharmonic function by choosing a constant defect `c` and solving the leaf equations first. Writing `a=u(0)` and `b_k=u(k)`, the `k`th leaf equation is

\[
p_ka+(1-p_k)b_k=b_k+c,
\]

so necessarily

\[
\boxed{b_k=a-\frac{c}{p_k}.}
\]

This is the entire obstruction. If `c != 0`, source integrability would require

\[
\sum_{k\ge1}p_k|b_k|
