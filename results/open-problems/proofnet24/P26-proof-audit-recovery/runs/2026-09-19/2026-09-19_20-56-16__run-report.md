## 5. #11 DUALIZE — verify the source equation on both sides of the quotient

The central interface can be checked in two equivalent languages.

\[

\[

On the quotient side, under the equivariant bijection

\[

\]

\[
(Pu)(0)=\sum_{n\ge1}p_nu(n),

\[
(Pu)(k)=p_ku(0)+(1-p_k)u(k).

The two descriptions are exactly equivalent; there is no left/right-coset reversal and no reflected-measure mismatch. The leaf equation for `Pu=u+c` gives

\[
