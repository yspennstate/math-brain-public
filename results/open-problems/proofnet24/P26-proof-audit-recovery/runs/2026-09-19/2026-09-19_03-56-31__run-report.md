E_gE_h^\top

### 3.1 Free-action diagonal and rowwise formula

The clean congruence class is essential for the simplified rowwise statement. The three order-two fixed-point candidates are `1/2,-1,2`; membership in `V_p` would force a quadratic-residue condition on `2`, but `p≡5 (mod 8)` gives `chi(2)=-1`. Order-three fixed points solve

\[
x^2-x+1=0,
\]

and are absent because `p≡2 (mod 3)`. Thus the `S_3` action on `V_p` is free.

If `g!=h`, then `k=gh^{-1}` is nonidentity and `P_k` has zero diagonal. Taking the `(x,x)` entry of (3.3) therefore gives

\[
\boxed{
\sum_y C_{x,gy}C_{x,hy}
=-3-(UU^\top P_{gh^{-1}})_{xx}.
}\tag{3.4}
\]

Equivalently, with the owner's channel notation `epsilon_g(x,y)=C_{x,gy}`, this is exactly Claim 42's rowwise cross-correlation identity.

For `g=h`, one has `P_{gh^{-1}}=I`. Each row of `C` has `n-1` entries of squared magnitude one, so

\[
(C^2)_{xx}=n-1=\frac{p-9}{4}.
\]

Taking the diagonal of (3.1), whose right side is `p-3`, yields

\[
\boxed{
(UU^\top)_{xx}
=p-3-\frac{p-9}{4}
=\frac{3(p-1)}4.
}\tag{3.5}
\]

This independently matches both the owner and P03 normalization.

### 3.2 Representation-sector conservation

For an irreducible representation `rho` of `S_3`, let

\[
\Pi_\rho
=\frac{d_\rho}{6}\sum_{g\in S_3}\chi_\rho(g^{-1})P_g
\]

be the central isotypic projector. Since `C`, `C^2`, and `UU^T=pI-3J-C^2` commute with the action, they commute with every `Pi_rho`. For the sign and standard representations,

\[
J\Pi_\rho=0.
\]

Projecting (3.1) therefore gives

\[
\boxed{
C^2\Pi_\rho+UU^\top\Pi_\rho=p\Pi_\rho.
}\tag{3.6}
\]

Hence every vector `z` in the sign or standard isotypic component satisfies

\[
\boxed{
\|Cz\|^2+\|U^\top z\|^2=p\|z\|^2.
}\tag{3.7}
\]

The same identity holds in the trivial isotypic component after restricting to `1^perp`, because `Jz=0` there. This proves the theorem-sized structural core in full.
