## 4. #4 PROVE SOMETHING WEAKER, COMPLETELY — exact finite certificate lemma

Rather than pretend the finite certificate says anything asymptotic, I isolate the exact finite statement it proves.

Let `H=G_{4177,2}` and `K=\overline H`. Let `B` be a symmetric rational matrix with

- `B_ii=1`;
- `B_ij=1` for every edge `ij` of `H`;
- `B_ij<=0` for every nonedge `ij` of `H`.

Then `A=J-B` has zero diagonal, vanishes on nonedges of `K` (the edges of `H`), and is entrywise nonnegative on edges of `K`. Therefore any exact proof that

` tI-B = tI-J+A \succeq 0 `

is a feasible Szegedy-theta-plus dual certificate for `K`, and hence

`theta(K) <= theta^+(K) <= t`.

The P04 checker uses the integer scale `D=51200`, so `DB=D B`, and sets

`Mint = D(tI-B)`.

With `FACQ=1024` and `SLACK_I=3072`, it constructs an integer matrix `Lq` and the exact symmetric integer residual

`R = FACQ^2 (Mint-SLACK_I I) - Lq Lq^T`.

Thus

`FACQ^2 Mint = Lq Lq^T + SLACK_I FACQ^2 I + R`.

For any real vector `x`, symmetry of `R` and the maximum absolute row-sum bound give

`x^T R x >= -||R||_op ||x||^2 >= -||R||_infty ||x||^2`.
