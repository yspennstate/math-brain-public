# 3. Sharpened quadratic-orbit projection stability

## `P26-T4-QUADRATIC-ORBIT-PROJECTION-STABILITY-8-21 = PROVED`

This is an independent proof, not a vote on P20/P04's status labels.

For the unscaled physical orbit write

\[
q=uu^T-I/d,
\qquad
\nu=\|q\|_F^2=\frac{d-1}{d},
\qquad
\kappa=\frac{d-2}{d}.
\]

Let `R` be an orthoprojector on `E`, `P=I-R`,

\[
y=Rq,
\qquad
s=\|y\|_F^2.
\]

The rank-one-centered orbit obeys exactly

\[
q^2=\kappa q+\frac{d-1}{d^2}I.
\]

On the set `Pq!=0`, expansion under the trace gives the exact P20/P04 quotient

\[
\boxed{
\phi(Pq)-\kappa
=
\frac{-2\kappa s+3\operatorname{tr}(qy^2)-\operatorname{tr}(y^3)}{\nu-s}.
}
\tag{3.1}
\]

At `Pq=0` we use `phi(0)=0` separately.

We prove the uniform constant `8` by splitting at `s=nu/4`.

### Case 1: `0<=s<=nu/4`

Because the eigenvalues of `q` are `nu` and `-1/d`,

\[
\|q\|_{op}=\nu.
\]

Hence

\[
|\operatorname{tr}(qy^2)|\le\nu s,
\qquad
|\operatorname{tr}(y^3)|\le\|y\|_{op}s\le s^{3/2}.
\]

Therefore the numerator in (3.1) is at most

\[
s(2\kappa+3\nu+\sqrt{s})
\le
s\left(2\kappa+3\nu+\frac{\sqrt\nu}{2}\right).
\]

Also `nu-s>=3nu/4`. It is enough to check

\[
2\kappa+3\nu+\frac{\sqrt\nu}{2}\le6\nu.
\]

Since `kappa=2nu-1`, this is equivalent to

\[
\nu+\frac{\sqrt\nu}{2}\le2,
\]

which holds because `0<nu<1`. Thus

\[
|\phi(Pq)-\kappa|\le8s.
\]

### Case 2: `s>=nu/4`

The elementary cubic quotient bound gives

\[
|\phi(Pq)|\le\|Pq\|_{op}\le\|Pq\|_F=\sqrt{\nu-s}.
\]

Therefore

\[
|\phi(Pq)-\kappa|
\le\sqrt{\nu-s}+\kappa
\le\sqrt{3\nu/4}+2\nu-1.
\]

Since `sqrt(3nu/4)<=1`, the right side is at most `2nu`. But `s>=nu/4` gives `8s>=2nu`, so again

\[
|\phi(Pq)-\kappa|\le8s.
\]

This case includes `Pq=0`, for which `s=nu` and the quotient formula is not used.

Hence for every `d>=4` and every orthoprojector `R`,

\[
\boxed{
|\phi((I-R)q)-\kappa_d|
\le8\|Rq\|_F^2.
}
\tag{3.2}
\]

`QED`.

This is a strict quantitative strengthening of `P20-QUADRATIC-ORBIT-PROJECTION-STABILITY-59`.
