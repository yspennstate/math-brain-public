# 3. New proof: intrinsic cubic structure tensor

## 3.1 The tensor is fully symmetric

Define

`C_{ijk}=<A_k,L_{A_i}A_j>`.

Because `A_k` is traceless, the scalar part removed by `Pi_E` is orthogonal to `A_k`. Therefore

`C_{ijk}=tr[A_k(A_iA_j+A_jA_i)/2]`.

For real symmetric matrices, the trace of a triple product is invariant under all permutations: cyclic permutations are immediate, and reversing the order follows by transposition. Hence

`C_{ijk}=tr(A_iA_jA_k)`

and `C` is a fully symmetric rank-three tensor on `R^s`.

## 3.2 Internal Jordan energy equals tensor Frobenius norm

Define

`I_s(P)=sum_{i,j}||P L_{A_i}A_j||_F^2`.

Expanding in the orthonormal basis of `ran P`,

`P L_{A_i}A_j = sum_k C_{ijk} A_k`.

Therefore

`I_s(P)=sum_{i,j,k}C_{ijk}^2 = ||C||_F^2`.

This also proves basis invariance: changing the orthonormal basis acts by the same orthogonal matrix in all three tensor slots and preserves the Frobenius norm.

## 3.3 The tensor trace is exactly `PJ`

Let

`b_k=sum_i C_{iik}`.

Then

`b_k=sum_i tr(A_i^2 A_k)
    =<A_k,C(P)>`

and, since `A_k` is traceless,

`b_k=<A_k,J(P)>`.

Thus `b` is the coordinate vector of `P J(P)` and

`||b||^2=||P J(P)||_F^2`.

## 3.4 Orthogonal trace decomposition

On `Sym^3(R^s)`, use the Frobenius inner product over ordered indices. Define the trace map

`Tr3(T)_k=sum_i T_{iik}`.

Its adjoint is

`(Tr3* v)_{ijk}
 = [delta_ij v_k + delta_ik v_j + delta_jk v_i]/3`.

Indeed, contracting against a symmetric tensor gives exactly `sum_k v_k sum_i T_{iik}`.

A direct calculation gives

`Tr3 Tr3* = (s+2) I/3`.

Therefore the orthogonal projection of `C` onto the trace-generated subspace is

`C_trace = Tr3* [3b/(s+2)]`,

and its squared norm is

`||C_trace||^2 = 3||b||^2/(s+2)`.

Define the trace-free symmetric cubic tensor

`C_harm = C-C_trace`

and

`H_s(P)=||C_harm||^2 >=0`.

Pythagoras gives the exact identity

`I_s(P) = 3||P J(P)||^2/(s+2) + H_s(P)`.

This proves `P26-T4-CUBIC-STRUCTURE-TENSOR-20`.

## 3.5 Intrinsic spherical-harmonic interpretation

For `theta` uniform on `S^{s-1}`, define

`X_theta=sum_i theta_i A_i`

and

`p_P(theta)=tr(X_theta^3)=C(theta,theta,theta)`.

The trace-generated tensor evaluates on the unit sphere as

`C_trace(theta,theta,theta)
 = 3 <b,theta>/(s+2)
 = 3 <PJ,X_theta>/(s+2)`.

Hence

`h_P(theta)=p_P(theta)-3<PJ,X_theta>/(s+2)`

is exactly the restriction of the trace-free homogeneous cubic and is harmonic in the coefficient variables.

For a trace-free symmetric cubic tensor `T`, the sixth spherical moment contraction gives

`E_theta[T(theta,theta,theta)^2]
 = 6||T||^2/[s(s+2)(s+4)]`.

(The nine pairings containing an internal trace vanish; the six all-cross pairings remain.) Applying this to `C_harm` yields

`H_s(P)
 = s(s+2)(s+4) E_theta[h_P(theta)^2]/6`.

As an additional check, for the full cubic `C`,

`E_theta[p_P(theta)^2]
 = [6 I_s(P)+9||PJ||^2]/[s(s+2)(s+4)]`.

No probabilistic approximation enters these identities.
