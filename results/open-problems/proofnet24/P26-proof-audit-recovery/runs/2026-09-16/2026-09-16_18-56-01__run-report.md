### 4.1 Weak metric duality

For nonnegative edge lengths `ell` with conductance budget

\[
\sum_e c_e\ell_e\le1,
\]

take `phi_a(v)=-d_ell(a,v)`. Summation by parts against

\[
\operatorname{div}f^{(a)}=\delta_a-\pi
\]

gives

\[
\sum_b\pi_b d_\ell(a,b)
\le
\sum_e\ell_e|f_e^{(a)}|.
\]

After multiplying by `pi_a`, summing in `a`, and applying the defining edge-congestion bound,

\[
\mathbb E_{\pi\times\pi}d_\ell(X,Y)\le \psi(f).
\]

Thus any feasible `ell` gives a valid lower bound on `F=\inf_f\psi(f)`. Exact LP-duality equality is not needed.

### 4.2 Cone normalization and spectrum

Let `H` be a connected `d`-regular bipartite Ramanujan graph on `n` vertices and set

\[
G=K_1\vee H.
\]

Then `D(G)=2`, the hub stationary mass is `1/(d+2)`, total base mass is `(d+1)/(d+2)`, and every physical edge has conductance `1/(n(d+2))`. On base adjacency eigenvectors orthogonal to constants the cone transition eigenvalue is `mu/(d+1)`; the remaining hub/base-constant nontrivial eigenvalue is `-1/(d+1)`. Hence

\[
\lambda_2\le\frac{2\sqrt{d-1}}{d+1}.
\]

### 4.3 Exact metric certificate

For integer truncation radius `r`, a degree-`d` ball of radius `<r` contains at most

\[
B(d,r)=1+d\sum_{j=0}^{r-2}(d-1)^j.
\]

Choose a sufficiently large member of the infinite fixed-degree Ramanujan family so that `n>=2B(d,r)`. Then independent uniform base vertices satisfy

\[
\Pr\{d_H(U,V)\ge r\}\ge\frac12,
\qquad
\mathbb E\min\{d_H(U,V),r\}\ge\frac r2.
\]

Assign spoke length

\[
x=\frac{r(d+2)}{d+r}
\]

and base-edge length

\[
y=\frac{2(d+2)}{d+r}.
\]

Then `2x=ry`, and the edge-conductance budget is exactly one. The resulting dual lower bound is

\[
F(G)\ge
\frac{r(d+1)(d+3)}{(d+2)(d+r)}.
\]

### 4.4 Divergence

Set `d=s^2`, `r=s`. Using the Ramanujan spectral bound and `D(G)=2`,

\[
\frac{F(G)}{\tau_{\rm rel}(G)D(G)}
>
L_s
:=
\frac{(s-1)^2(s^2+3)}{2(s+1)(s^2+2)}.
\]

The exact identity

\[
L_s-\frac{s-3}{2}
=
\frac{5s^2-2s+9}{2(s+1)(s^2+2)}>0
\]

shows the ratio is unbounded. Given any proposed universal constant `K`, choosing integer `s>2K+3` and then a sufficiently large degree-`s^2` bipartite Ramanujan base produces a finite connected simple graph violating that constant.

Therefore the source-level universal-diameter assertion is mathematically **REFUTED** by the audited claim.
