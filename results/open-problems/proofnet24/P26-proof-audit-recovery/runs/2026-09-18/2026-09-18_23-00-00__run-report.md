## 4. B1 — change category: from rational identities to a function-field quotient

\[

\[
\boxed{\zeta(gx)=\operatorname{sgn}(g)\zeta(x).}

\[

\[
\boxed{
\prod_{g\in A_3}(X-g(y))

The three odd images have signed coordinate `-zeta(y)`, hence similarly

\[
\boxed{
\prod_{g\notin A_3}(X-g(y))
