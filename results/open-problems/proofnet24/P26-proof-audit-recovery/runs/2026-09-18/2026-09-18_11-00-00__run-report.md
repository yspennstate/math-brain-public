## 4. Method #2 — ATTACK THE NEGATION FIRST

Assume the odd-`m` source statement fails. Let `Y` be the odd-cardinality member of `{x,x^c}`, with `s=|Y|`. Failure would force every length-`h` interval to contain at most `(s-1)/2` points of `Y`; otherwise that interval, or its complement, is immediately a source-valid witness.

Summing these hypothetical bounds gives

`hs <= m(s-1)/2`.

Substituting `h=(m-1)/2` gives

`s(m-1) <= m(s-1)`, hence `m <= s`, contradicting `s<m`.

This is an exact contradiction at every source quantifier.

I then attacked the tempting stronger statement obtained by dropping oddness. It is false. At `m=4`, take `x={0,2}`. A cyclic interval of length 1 or 3 has symmetric-difference size at least 1, equal to the right-hand side `min(|W|,4-|W|)=1`; a length-2 cyclic interval has symmetric-difference size 2, equal to the right-hand side 2. Hence no strict witness exists. This confirms that the source's oddness hypothesis is doing real work rather than being an artifact of the proof.

## 5. Method #11 — DUALIZE: regular incidence / cyclic-convolution proof

The same argument has a clean independent dual formulation.

Let `M` be the `m x m` incidence matrix whose rows are the length-`h` cyclic intervals and whose columns are the points of `Z_m`. Every row and every column has exactly `h` ones. For the indicator vector `1_Y`, the vector `M 1_Y` records the `m` intersection counts.

Regularity gives

`1^T M 1_Y = h s`,

so

`max_t |I_t cap Y| >= ceil(hs/m)`.

Now

`hs/m = s(m-1)/(2m) = s/2 - s/(2m)`.

Because `s` is odd and `0<s<m`, this real number lies strictly between `(s-1)/2` and `(s+1)/2`, in fact strictly above `(s-1)/2`. Therefore

`ceil(hs/m) = (s+1)/2 > s/2`.

Thus the strict-majority half-cycle exists. This incidence-matrix/cyclic-convolution derivation is logically independent of the owner's contradiction wording and reproduces the exact strictness threshold.

It also identifies the mechanism as elementary regular-incidence averaging/pigeonhole. I make **no novelty claim** for that mechanism; it is standard combinatorial folklore. What is being audited is its application to the exact 2026 source conjecture.

## 6. Source inequality and complement case checked explicitly

`I^c triangle x = I^c triangle Y^c = I triangle Y`.

`|W triangle x| = h+s-2a < h = min(h+1,h)`.

No endpoint, parity, empty-set, or complement convention is hidden here. For `m=1`, there is no nonempty proper `x`, so the statement is vacuous; odd cycles begin at `m=3`.
