## 4. The corrected Claim 26 theorem

The underlying second-moment argument is sound after one tiny scope/inequality repair.

Let `N_p` be the number of eigenvalues with

\[
|\lambda|>\frac23\sqrt p.
\]

Since `T_p` is real symmetric with zero diagonal and all off-diagonal entries in `{+1,-1}`,

\[
\sum_j\lambda_j^2=\operatorname{tr}(T_p^2)=m(m-1).
\]

If `N_p>0`, then

\[
N_p\frac{4p}{9}<m(m-1).
\]

If `N_p=0`, the non-strict bound below is automatic. Hence for every admissible prime,

\[
\boxed{
N_p\le \frac{9m(m-1)}{4p}
      =\frac{3(p-29)}{32p}\,m.
}
\]

Thus either of the following repairs is complete:

1. replace `fewer than` by `at most` in the displayed count bound; or
2. retain the strict inequality but scope it to `p>=53`.

For `p>=29`, `m>0`, the proportion statement still gives at least `29/32` inside the window, and for `p>=53` the original strict `>29/32` conclusion follows. No source-level consequence changes; this remains a typical-spectrum theorem, not an extreme-edge theorem.

## 6. Independent full proof of Claim 25

Fix a prime `p ≡ 5 (mod 24)`. Let `chi` be the quadratic character of `F_p`. Define

\[
V=\{x\ne0,1:\chi(x)=\chi(x-1)=1\}
\]

and

\[
D_p=\mathbf P^1(\mathbb F_p)\setminus\{0,\infty,t:t^4=1\}.
\]

Because `p ≡ 1 (mod 4)`, the four fourth roots of unity are rational and distinct, so `|D_p|=p-5`.

Define

\[
x(t)=\left(\frac{1-t^2}{1+t^2}\right)^2.
\]

The excluded points are exactly the zeros/poles that would give `x in {0,1,infinity}`. Moreover

\[
1-x(t)=\frac{4t^2}{(1+t^2)^2},
\]

so both `x(t)` and `1-x(t)` are nonzero squares. Hence `x(t) in V`.

### 6.1 The four-sheeted map `D_p -> V`

If `x(s)=x(t)`, then after taking the square root symbolically,

\[
\frac{1-s^2}{1+s^2}=\pm\frac{1-t^2}{1+t^2}.
\]

The plus case gives `s^2=t^2`; the minus case gives `s^2=t^{-2}`. Thus every fiber is exactly

\[
\{t,-t,t^{-1},-t^{-1}\}.
\]

None of the possible fixed points is in `D_p`, so the four points are distinct. The deck group is

\[
K=\langle t\mapsto -t,\ t\mapsto t^{-1}\rangle\cong V_4.
\]

The standard character count gives `|V|=(p-5)/4`, so the four-to-one image has exactly the size of `V`; hence `x:D_p->V` is onto.

### 6.2 Free anharmonic action and the 24-sheeted invariant

On the clean family `p ≡ 5 (mod 24)`, the anharmonic `S_3` action on `V` is free.

The three transposition fixed points are represented by `2`, `1/2`, and `-1`; at least one of `x` or `x-1` is a nonsquare because `(2/p)=-1` for `p ≡ 5 (mod 8)`. The order-three fixed points solve

\[

\]

and do not exist because `p ≡ 2 (mod 3)`.

Use the classical invariant

\[
J(x)=\frac{(x^2-x+1)^3}{x^2(1-x)^2}.
\]

The six anharmonic transforms are precisely the generic fibers of `J`; freeness makes every `J`-fiber inside `V` have size six. Therefore for

\[

Substitution gives

\[

=\frac{(t^8+14t^4+1)^3}{16t^4(t^4-1)^4}.

The lifts

\[

\[

&(X^2-X+1)^3Y^2(1-Y)^2
