## 4. NEW-METHOD #2 — attack the negation first

I first attacked the places where the theorem could most plausibly fail instead of accepting the owner's derivation.

### 4.1 Cell-sign attack

For `x∈V`, the row-sum equation `Q1=0` gives

\[
U_B\mathbf1+U_D\mathbf1+U_F\mathbf1
=-C\mathbf1-2\mathbf1=-v. \tag{1}
\]

The `(x,0)` entry of `Q^2=pI-J` is `-1`. Because the signs of `χ(y)` on `V,B,D,F` are `+,+,-,-` and the `y=1` anchor contributes `+1`,

\[
U_B\mathbf1-U_D\mathbf1-U_F\mathbf1=-v. \tag{2}
\]

The `(x,1)` entry is also `-1`. Because the signs of `χ(y-1)` on `V,B,D,F` are `+,-,+,-` and the `y=0` anchor contributes `+1`,

\[
-U_B\mathbf1+U_D\mathbf1-U_F\mathbf1=-v. \tag{3}
\]

Solving (1)--(3) yields

\[
\boxed{U_B\mathbf1=-v,\qquad U_D\mathbf1=-v,\qquad U_F\mathbf1=v.} \tag{4}
\]

Thus the most fragile sign convention checks independently.

### 4.2 Constant-projection coefficient attack

The three exterior cells each have size `m=(p-1)/4`. If `P_m=J_m/m` is orthogonal projection onto constants in one cell, then

\[
U_B P_m U_B^\top=\frac1m vv^\top,
\]

and the same holds for `D,F`, regardless of the sign in (4), because the sign disappears in the outer product. Summing gives exactly

\[
\frac3mvv^\top=\frac{12}{p-1}vv^\top.
\]

So the coefficient `12/(p-1)` is exact; there is no missing factor of three or four.

### 4.3 The `T=0` boundary

For a PSD trace-one matrix `X`, define

\[
T=\mathbf1^\top X\mathbf1.
\]

If `T=0`, then `X^{1/2}\mathbf1=0`, hence `X\mathbf1=0`. Therefore

\[
R:=v^\top X\mathbf1=0.
\]

So the escape inequality has no hidden division-by-zero gap at `T=0`; the later formula with `1/T` is correctly stated only for `T>0`.

### 4.4 Schur-domain attack

Since `J` has largest eigenvalue `n` and `tr X=1`,

\[
0\le T=\operatorname{tr}(JX)\le n.
\]

Hence the Schur factor `T(n-T)` is nonnegative. At `p=5`, `n=0`, so there is no nonzero trace-one state on the empty localized vertex set; the theorem's optimization content is vacuous rather than contradictory.

### 4.5 Hidden second coarse direction attack

For exterior vectors constant on each cell,

\[
U(a\mathbf1_B,b\mathbf1_D,c\mathbf1_F)
=(-a-b+c)v.
\]

If one tries to negate part (d) by finding a second coarse escape direction, this formula rules it out exactly: the entire three-dimensional cell-constant space maps into `span(v)`, and the two-dimensional plane `c=a+b` lies in the kernel. The failure is structural, not numerical.

## 6. Independent Gram/Schur reconstruction

The `V×V` block of `Q^2=pI-J` has two anchor contributions, each equal to `J`. Therefore

\[
\boxed{C^2+UU^\top=pI-3J.} \tag{7}
\]

For `X\succeq0`, `tr X=1`, define

\[
S=\operatorname{tr}(CX),\qquad
q=\mathbf1^\top X C\mathbf1,
\qquad
T=\mathbf1^\top X\mathbf1,
\qquad
E=\operatorname{tr}(UU^\top X).
\]

Take the Hilbert-Schmidt Gram matrix of

\[
X^{1/2},\quad C X^{1/2},\quad J X^{1/2}.
\]

Using (7) and `J^2=nJ`, its entries are exactly

\[
\boxed{
\begin{pmatrix}
1&S&T\\
S&p-3T-E&q\\
T&q&nT
\end{pmatrix}\succeq0.} \tag{8}
\]

Schur-complementing the upper-left `1` yields

\[
\boxed{
(q-ST)^2\le
(p-3T-E-S^2)T(n-T).} \tag{9}
\]

Since `v=(C+2I)1`,

\[
R=v^\top X\mathbf1=q+2T. \tag{10}
\]

For the theta-plus feasible states in the P04 route, the exact edge accounting is

\[
S=T-1+\Delta,
\qquad \Delta\ge0.
\]

Set

\[
G:=p-\left(T^2+T+1+E+2(T-1)\Delta+\Delta^2\right).
\]

Then `G=p-3T-E-S^2\ge0`, and after substituting (10) into (9),

\[
\boxed{
\left(R-T(T+1+\Delta)\right)^2
\le G\,T(n-T).} \tag{11}
\]

Hence

\[
R\ge T(T+1+\Delta)-\sqrt{GT(n-T)}.
\]

Combining this with (6), for `T>0`, gives exactly

\[
\boxed{
E\ge
\frac{12}{(p-1)T}
\left[
T(T+1+\Delta)-\sqrt{GT(n-T)}
\right]_+^2.} \tag{12}
\]

Thus the proof is complete, including the boundary cases and signs.
