## 5. Independent reconstruction of the full proof

### 5.1 Interior existence, uniqueness, and inverse-zero KKT equations

For each `z<z_c`, the theta-completion equivalence supplies a positive-definite feasible correlation completion. With diagonal fixed to one, the PSD closure of the feasible set is compact. The determinant maximum is positive and hence attained in the positive-definite interior. Strict concavity of `log det` on the positive-definite cone gives a unique maximizer `C_z`.

Let `F_alpha` be any symmetric basis of the free distinct-nonedge coordinates. The derivative of `log det C` in direction `F_alpha` is

\[
\operatorname{Tr}(C^{-1}F_\alpha).
\]

Therefore at the optimum

\[
\boxed{(P_z)_{ij}=0\quad\text{for every distinct nonedge }ij.}
\]

The restricted Hessian is

\[
D^2\log\det(C)[Y,Y]
=-\operatorname{Tr}(C^{-1}YC^{-1}Y)<0
\]

for every nonzero symmetric free direction `Y`. Thus the free KKT Jacobian is nonsingular and the implicit-function theorem gives smooth dependence of all free entries of `C_z` on `z` throughout the interior interval.

### 5.2 First derivative and trace identity

Put

\[
H_z=C_z'.
\]

The diagonal entries of `H_z` are zero and its edge entries are `-1`. The envelope derivative is

\[
\Phi_H'(z)=\operatorname{Tr}(P_zH_z).
\]

All free-nonedge contributions vanish by the inverse-zero KKT equations, so

\[
\Phi_H'(z)=-2\sum_{ij\in E}(P_z)_{ij}.
\]

Hence

\[
\boxed{\mathcal M_H(z)=2\sum_{ij\in E}(P_z)_{ij}.}
\]

Now take the diagonal entries of `P_zC_z=I`. Since `P_z` vanishes on nonedges,

\[
1=(P_z)_{ii}-z\sum_{j\sim i}(P_z)_{ij}.
\]

Summing over `i` gives

\[
n=\operatorname{Tr}P_z-2z\sum_{ij\in E}(P_z)_{ij},
\]

and therefore

\[
\boxed{\mathcal M_H(z)=\frac{\operatorname{Tr}P_z-n}{z}.}
\]

### 5.3 Second derivative and exact dissipation

Differentiate `P_z=C_z^{-1}`:

\[
P_z'=-P_zH_zP_z.
\]

Differentiating `\Phi_H'=\operatorname{Tr}(P_zH_z)` gives

\[
\Phi_H''(z)
=-\operatorname{Tr}(P_zH_zP_zH_z)
+\operatorname{Tr}(P_zC_z'').
\]

The diagonal entries and edge entries of `C_z` are affine/constant in `z`, so `C_z''` is supported only on distinct nonedges. The inverse-zero KKT equations therefore give

\[
\operatorname{Tr}(P_zC_z'')=0.
\]

Thus

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal M_H'(z)
=-\Phi_H''(z)
=\operatorname{Tr}(P_zH_zP_zH_z)
=\|P_z^{1/2}H_zP_z^{1/2}\|_F^2\ge0.
}
\]

### 5.4 Tangent-energy minimization

Differentiate the free-nonedge KKT equations. On each distinct nonedge,

\[
(P_zH_zP_z)_{ij}=0.
\]

Consider

\[
Q_z(Y)=\operatorname{Tr}(P_zYP_zY)
\]

on the affine tangent class `Y_{ii}=0`, `Y_{ij}=-1` on edges. An allowed variation `Z` is supported only on distinct nonedges, and

\[
DQ_z(H_z)[Z]
=2\operatorname{Tr}(P_zH_zP_zZ)=0.
\]

Because `P_z\succ0`,

\[
Q_z(Y)=\|P_z^{1/2}YP_z^{1/2}\|_F^2
\]

is strictly positive for every nonzero symmetric direction. Hence `H_z` is the unique minimizer and

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal M_H'(z)=
\min_Y\operatorname{Tr}(P_zYP_zY).
}
\]

\[
G_{\alpha\beta}=\operatorname{Tr}(PF_\beta PF_\alpha),

\[

## 8. #29 ADD THE POINTS AT INFINITY — exact endpoint blow-up

\[
C_*-\delta A_H\succ0
\]

would have edge entries `-(z_c+\delta)`, contradicting the definition of the critical feasible correlation.

Now let `z_k\uparrow z_c`. The correlation matrices `C_{z_k}` lie in a compact set. If their determinants failed to tend to zero, a subsequence would converge to a critical feasible matrix `C_*` with `\det C_*>0`, hence `C_*\succ0`, contradicting the preceding paragraph. Therefore

\[
\boxed{\det C_z\to0,
\qquad
\Phi_H(z)=\log\det C_z\to-\infty
\quad(z\uparrow z_c).}

\[
-\Phi_H(z)=\int_0^z\mathcal M_H(s)\,ds

\[
\boxed{\mathcal M_H(z)\to+\infty\quad(z\uparrow z_c).}

The trace identity then gives

\[
\boxed{\operatorname{Tr}P_z\to+\infty.}
