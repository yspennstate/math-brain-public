## 4. NEW-METHOD #2 — attack the negation first

For `0<b<=B`, `ell>=100`, and `p=kappa/ell`, C008 uses the logarithmic density

\[

\frac{1-p}{\ell b}\mathbf 1_{[-\ell b,0]}(x)
+
\frac{p}{\varepsilon b}\mathbf 1_{[-\varepsilon b,0]}(x).
\tag{4.1}
\]

Its two summands have masses `1-p` and `p`, so total mass is exactly one. On `[-ell b,-epsilon b)` the density is constant; on `[-epsilon b,0]` it jumps upward by a positive amount; after zero it vanishes. Hence it is nondecreasing up to the mode `0` and nonincreasing afterward. The log-law is genuinely unimodal.

\[
\frac1u\left[
\frac{1-p}{\ell b}\mathbf1_{[e^{-\ell b},1]}(u)
+
\frac{p}{\varepsilon b}\mathbf1_{[e^{-\varepsilon b},1]}(u)

\tag{4.2}

For every finite parameter choice, `e^{-ell b}>0`, so this is an atomless absolutely continuous law with compact support contained in `(0,infinity)`. The claimed regularity survives.

## 5. NEW-METHOD #18 — say it in another language: robust moving-window desingularization

C007 can be viewed as a long logarithmic plateau plus a point mass at the mode. C008 replaces the point mass by a short one-sided uniform plateau. In normalized coordinates the entire new theorem becomes a statement about two moving-window averages of one positive kernel.

The inverse log-law gives

\[
S_\varepsilon(y)
=
\frac p\varepsilon\int_{y-\varepsilon}^{y}K_b(v)\,dv
+
\frac{1-p}{\ell}\int_{y-\ell}^{y}K_b(v)\,dv.
\tag{5.1}
\]

Multiplying by `ell` and using `p=kappa/ell`,

\[
R_\varepsilon(y)
=
\frac\kappa\varepsilon\int_{y-\varepsilon}^{y}K_b(v)\,dv
+
\left(1-\frac\kappa\ell\right)

\tag{5.2}

The proof is therefore a stability statement: the high-low-high geometry of the atomic construction survives replacing the atom by a small window average.

The audit finds a little more robustness than the frozen statement needs. The two epsilon-sensitive estimates are both worst at the largest allowed epsilon. On `0<epsilon<=1/100`,

is increasing. Hence every strict inequality checked at `epsilon=1/100` automatically survives for any smaller positive epsilon. I do **not** count this observation as a separate theorem package; it is proof diagnosis showing that C008 has real slack and is not balanced on a numerical knife edge.
