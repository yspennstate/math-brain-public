## 4. NEW-METHOD #27 — swap the quantifiers: formal ELE is equivalent to a cutoff witness

This is the decisive audit point. Instead of asking whether each source proof can be encoded into ELE, swap the direction and ask whether a chain already known to cut off can always manufacture ELE data.

### Proposition P26-I0110-O1 — every two-time cutoff witness yields the frozen formal ELE certificate

Let `(P_{n,t})` be finite irreducible chains with stationary laws `pi_n` and fixed source-relevant initial laws. Suppose there exist

\[
t_n^-<t_n^+,\qquad t_n^+/t_n^-\to1,
\]

such that

\[
d_n(t_n^-):=\|p_{n,t_n^-}-\pi_n\|_{TV}\to1,
\qquad
d_n(t_n^+)\to0.
\tag{4.1}
\]

Then the frozen ELE definition as written is satisfied.

#### Lower certificate

Put `p_n=p_{n,t_n^-}` and

\[
\delta_n=1-d_n(t_n^-)\to0.
\]

On a finite state space choose a set `A_n` attaining total variation with the positive sign:

\[
p_n(A_n)-\pi_n(A_n)=d_n(t_n^-)=1-\delta_n.
\]

Since `p_n(A_n)<=1`,

\[
p_n(A_n)\ge1-\delta_n,
\qquad
\pi_n(A_n)\le\delta_n.
\tag{4.2}
\]

Take

\[
\Omega_n^-=X_n,\quad q_n^-=p_n,\quad \Phi_n^-=\mathrm{id},\quad E_n^-=A_n.
\]

The representation error is exactly zero, (4.2) is exactly ELE-L, and the lift/map is merely the identity map on the chain's state space.

#### Upper certificate

Put `p_n=p_{n,t_n^+}` and

\[
\delta_n=d_n(t_n^+)\to0.
\]

Irreducibility gives `pi_n(x)>0`. Write

\[
f_n(x)=p_n(x)/\pi_n(x),
\qquad
2\delta_n=\sum_x\pi_n(x)|f_n(x)-1|.
\]

If `delta_n=0`, take `E_n^+=X_n`. Otherwise set `a_n=\sqrt{\delta_n}` and

\[
B_n=\{x:|f_n(x)-1|>a_n\},\qquad E_n^+=X_n\setminus B_n.
\]

Markov's inequality under `pi_n` gives

\[
\pi_n(B_n)\le\frac{2\delta_n}{a_n}=2\sqrt{\delta_n}.
\tag{4.3}
\]

Total variation also gives

\[
p_n(B_n)\le\pi_n(B_n)+\delta_n
\le2\sqrt{\delta_n}+\delta_n=o(1).
\tag{4.4}
\]

Now take

\[
\Omega_n^+=X_n,\quad q_n^+=p_n,\quad \Phi_n^+=\mathrm{id},
\]

and let `r_n=p_n 1_{E_n^+}` with mass `m_n=1-p_n(B_n)`. Then `m_n->1` and the representation error is again exactly zero. Moreover

\[
D_n=\sum_x\pi_n(x)\bigl(f_n(x)1_{E_n^+}(x)-m_n\bigr)^2.
\]

On `E_n^+`,

\[
|f_n-m_n|\le a_n+(1-m_n),
\]

while on `B_n` the square is `m_n^2`. Hence

\[
D_n\le \bigl(a_n+1-m_n\bigr)^2+\pi_n(B_n)m_n^2\longrightarrow0
\tag{4.5}
\]

by (4.3)--(4.4). This is ELE-U.

Thus (4.1) implies formal ELE. R19-C008 proves the converse. Therefore, for the source-relevant initial-law notion frozen by R19,

\[
\boxed{\text{formal ELE}\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad\text{existence of a two-time cutoff witness}.}
\tag{4.6}
\]

No finite computation is involved in this theorem-sized obstruction, so there is no artificial checker to manufacture.
