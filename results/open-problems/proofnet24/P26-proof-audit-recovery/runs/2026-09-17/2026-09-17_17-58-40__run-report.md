## 4. #18 SAY IT IN ANOTHER LANGUAGE — theta SDP as an exact Fourier LP

Let

\[
H=\overline{G_{41,1}}.
\]

Use the standard Lovasz-theta primal

\[
\vartheta(H)=\max\langle J,X\rangle
\]

subject to `X` positive semidefinite, `Tr X=1`, and `X_uv=0` on distinct edges of `H`. Because the edges of `H` are exactly the nonedges of the localized Paley graph, the zero constraints are exactly the difference classes in `N` above.

Average any feasible `X` over cyclic translations and reflection. This preserves feasibility and objective and gives a real symmetric circulant matrix. Its Fourier eigenvalues are nonnegative. Pair conjugate frequencies and write `w_m` for the combined mass of `{m,20-m}` (with the usual singleton conventions). The SDP becomes the exact finite LP

\[
\max 20w_0,
\]

\[
\sum_m w_m=1,
\qquad
\sum_m w_m\cos(2\pi md/20)=0
\quad(d\in\{1,2,3,6,8\}),
\qquad
w_m\ge0.
\]

The ordinary LP dual is

\[
\min 20a
\]

subject to

\[
a+\sum_{d\in\{1,2,3,6,8\}}
\lambda_d\cos(2\pi md/20)
\ge \mathbf 1_{\{m=0\}}
\quad(m=0,\ldots,19).
\]

This translation is important for audit scope: the variables in the half-frequency display are **paired Fourier masses**, not raw unpaired eigenvalues. Matching exact primal and dual objectives therefore prove the actual source-normalized theta value.
