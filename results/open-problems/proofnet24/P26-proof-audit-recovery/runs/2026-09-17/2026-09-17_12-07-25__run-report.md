## 4. NEW-METHOD #11 — independently dualize the source-normalized theta program

\[
q_m\ge0,

\sum_{m=0}^{89}q_m=1.
\tag{4.2}

\[
X_{0d}=\frac1{90}\sum_{m=0}^{89}q_m e^{2\pi i md/90}.
\tag{4.3}

\max &\quad 90w_0,\\

\tag{P}
