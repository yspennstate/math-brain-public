## 3. Mathematical core independently checked

Let `P` be a finite irreducible discrete-time Markov chain on `V`, let `A` be a nonempty absorbing/stopping set, and put `U=V\A`. Write

`L = I-P`, `Pi = 1 pi^T`, `Z=(L+Pi)^(-1)-Pi`.

Let `F_A` be the total weight of directed spanning forests converging to the root set `A`, and let `F_{A∪{j}}(i->j)` be the total weight of forests rooted at `A∪{j}` in which `i` lies in the `j`-rooted component.

### 3.1 Killed Green / forest ratio

The killed Green matrix is

`G_A=(I-P_UU)^(-1)=L_UU^(-1)`.

The all-minors matrix-tree theorem identifies

`det L_UU = F_A`,

and the relevant cofactor of `L_UU` with `F_{A∪{j}}(i->j)`. Hence Cramer's rule gives

`G_A(i,j) = F_{A∪{j}}(i->j) / F_A`.

This is the exact source prototype when `A={k,l}`:

`E_i[# visits to j before min(T_k,T_l)] = F_{ {k,l,j} }(i->j) / F_{ {k,l} }`.

### 3.2 Arbitrary boundary / occupation compiler

If the occupation row vector `x` has initial law `sigma`, and the terminal law is supported on `A`, with boundary occupation values `c=x_A`, then the conservation equation gives

`x_U(I-P_UU)=sigma_U + c P_AU`.

Thus, writing

`mu_q = sigma_q + sum_{a in A} c_a p_{a q}`,

we obtain for `j in U`

`x_j = sum_{q in U} mu_q G_A(q,j)`

and therefore the single rooted-grove rule

`x_j = (1/F_A) sum_{q in U} mu_q F_{A∪{j}}(q->j)`.

This one formula contains the ordinary first-hit, strict-return, stationary-start, and multi-boundary occupation identities as specializations of the boundary data.

### 3.3 Frozen marked `(pi,Z)` bridge

For the marked set `A`, define the bordered source matrix

`M_A = [[Z_A^T, -pi_A], [1^T,0]]`,

and `Delta_A=det M_A`.

The P02/P26/P01 derivations all identify

`Delta_A = F_A / T > 0`,

where `T` is the total rooted-tree weight, and the corresponding inherited marked numerator with

`N_{A;i,j}=F_{A∪{j}}(i->j)/T`.

Consequently the marked `(pi,Z)` determinant ratio equals the rooted-grove ratio exactly. The package is not merely “there exists some forest formula”; it supplies the requested bridge between the combinatorial object and the source's marked matrix variables.

## 4. NEW-METHODS results

### #21 — ASK WHO ELSE HAS THIS SHAPE

I mapped the C5 mechanism against neighboring literature rather than treating the rooted-grove formula as automatically new.

- Chaiken's 1982 all-minors matrix-tree theorem already supplies the determinant-to-directed-forest engine.
- Chebotarev/Agaev and related matrix-forest work place Laplacian inverses/generalized inverses and forest enumerators in the same framework.
- Pitman–Tang, _Tree formulas, mean first passage times and Kemeny's constant of a Markov chain_ (arXiv:1603.09017; Bernoulli 2018), give tree/forest formulas for finite-state Markov Green and hitting quantities.
- Chung–Zeng, _Forest formulas of discrete Green's functions_ (first published 6 September 2022; J. Graph Theory 102 (2023), 556–577), explicitly prove forest formulas for Green functions on directed and weighted graphs and derive hitting-time formulas.
- Recent 2026 work continues to use the same engine: Voits–Schwarz (arXiv:2605.00135) use the all-minors matrix-tree theorem for transient probabilities and hitting-time densities; Nam–Gunawardena (arXiv:2608.08375) develop spanning-forest formulas for first-passage quantities in a continuous-time linear-framework setting.

**Outcome:** the Green/forest mechanism is **KNOWN IN SUBSTANCE**. The package must not advertise “forest formulas for Markov occupation quantities” as the novelty.

**Outcome:** the exact publication-level question is narrower than the theorem's mathematical validity. The proof is good, but the prior-art obligation is to determine whether this exact compiler/packaging already appeared later.
