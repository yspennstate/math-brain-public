## 5. New theorem: semigroup irredundancy of every essential letter

### Claim `P16-SEMIGROUP-IRREDUCIBILITY-52` — PROVED

Let `S` be a finite set of transformations on a finite state set Q. Assume the automaton with letter support S is synchronizing. Let `a in S` and `B=S\{a}`. If B is nonsynchronizing, then a is not in the transformation monoid generated by B (including the identity):

`a notin <B>^1`.

### Proof

Suppose instead that a is induced by a word u over B. If a is the identity, u may be chosen empty; otherwise choose any B-word realizing a. Let w be a reset word over S. Replace every occurrence of the symbol a in w by u. Transformation composition is preserved at each replacement, so the resulting word w' over B induces exactly the same constant transformation as w. Hence B is synchronizing, contradicting the hypothesis. Therefore `a notin <B>^1`. QED.

### Consequence for the missing n=8, q=4 case

Because the ternary n=8 class is already completely verified, any hypothetical four-letter n=8 automaton violating the square bound cannot have a synchronizing three-letter subalphabet: any reset word of that subalphabet would also reset the four-letter automaton within 49 steps. Thus all four letters are essential, and for every letter a,

`a notin <S\{a}>^1`.

In particular, the missing case is not merely distinct-support; it is semigroup-irredundant in all four directions. Identity and rank-one letters are automatically excluded, consistently with the retained reduction.

This theorem is general and exact. It is a pruning condition, not a complete n=8 enumeration, so the metric remains unchanged.

## 6. Pair-trap formulation as an exact extension filter

For a nonsynchronizing ternary support B, define `U_B` to be the graph on Q whose edges are unordered pairs `{x,y}` that cannot be merged by any B-word. Since B is nonsynchronizing, U_B is nonempty.

For every `b in B`, U_B is invariant: if `{x,y}` is unmergeable and its b-image were diagonal or B-mergeable, then prefixing the corresponding merge word by b would merge `{x,y}`. Therefore every b maps each U_B-edge to another U_B-edge.

If `B union {a}` is synchronizing, a cannot also preserve every edge of U_B. Hence there exists `{x,y} in U_B` such that either `a(x)=a(y)` or `{a(x),a(y)}` is B-mergeable.

Equivalently: a synchronizing one-letter extension must fail to be an endomorphism of the loopless unmergeable-pair graph U_B. This is the operational version of the retained pair-trap theorem and gives a cheap exact candidate-letter filter before full subset-BFS reset verification.

## 7. New theorem: exact canonical augmentation and durable child shards

### Claim `P16-CANONICAL-TERNARY-AUGMENTATION-52` — PROVED

Fix n. Consider distinct four-transformation supports S such that:

1. S is synchronizing; and
2. every three-element proper subset of S is nonsynchronizing.

For each state-conjugacy orbit of nonsynchronizing three-supports, choose the lexicographically least serialized representative T. Let `Aut(T)` be the group of state permutations whose conjugation stabilizes T setwise. Enumerate candidate fourth transformations `a notin T` only up to `Aut(T)`-orbits. For each extension `T union {a}`:

- reject unless all four three-subsets are nonsynchronizing;
- test synchronization and exact reset threshold by power-subset BFS;
- canonicalize the resulting four-support under all state relabelings;
- deduplicate by that full canonical four-support serialization.

Then every state-isomorphism orbit satisfying (1)–(2) appears, and every retained canonical child belongs to such an orbit.

### Proof of completeness

Let S be any target support and choose any three-element parent `T0 subset S`; by (2), T0 is nonsynchronizing. There is a state permutation pi taking T0 to the chosen canonical representative T of its orbit. Write `pi S pi^{-1}=T union {a'}`.

The enumeration chooses one representative a from every `Aut(T)`-orbit on transformations outside T. Therefore there is `rho in Aut(T)` with `rho a' rho^{-1}=a` for the representative that is enumerated. Since rho stabilizes T setwise,

`rho (T union {a'}) rho^{-1}=T union {a}`.

Thus this generated child is state-isomorphic to S. It passes the all-three-subsets-nonsynchronizing test and the synchronization test because those properties are invariant under state conjugacy. Its full four-support canonicalization is therefore exactly `can(S)`. Hence every target orbit is generated.

Conversely, every retained generated child is synchronizing and has all proper ternary subalphabets nonsynchronizing by the exact filters, so it lies in the target class. Full canonical serialization identifies exactly the state-conjugacy orbit, giving exact deduplication. QED.

### Durable shard rule

The full canonical child serialization is the certificate identity. Durable shards may be disjoint ranges/prefixes of that serialization. A hash may be used only as a routing convenience if the full serialization is retained and collisions cannot merge identities. The proof does not assume a unique parent: the same child may arise from several ternary parents, and canonical child deduplication deliberately absorbs that overlap.

This specializes prior one-letter-extension methodology to the exact missing P16 class and makes the required shard boundary mathematically explicit. It does not by itself make the n=8 run small enough.
