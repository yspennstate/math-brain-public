## 8. Newly recovered KNOWN pruning from the nearest solved computation

The primary algorithm paper arXiv:1404.3311v4 contains exactly the mechanism missing from the previous P16 engineering plan.

For a generated nonsynchronizing parent A, its Algorithm 1 computes:

- the minimal rank and a shortest word of that rank;
- all compressible pairs;
- an extension upper bound from successive applications of its Theorem 2.2 (Frankl–Pin sequence refinement) and Pin’s rank-reduction theorem;

- reducibility of the current letter transformations as semigroup generators.

Only a parent whose bound remains above the target threshold is stored for the next one-letter extension layer. This is a theorem-backed extension sieve, not a heuristic sample.

Two additional source-certified reductions are especially relevant at n=8:

These are literature imports and should be incorporated into the enumeration engine, rather than rediscovered as new conjectures.
