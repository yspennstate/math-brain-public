# 6. New exact reduction: duplicate letters can be collapsed without changing reset threshold

## Theorem P16-DUPLICATE-SUPPORT-51 — PROVED

Let A=(Q,Σ,δ) be a DFA and let

    S = { delta_a : a in Σ } subset Q^Q

be the set of distinct state transformations induced by its letters. Form a support automaton A_S having one letter for each transformation in S. Then A is synchronizing iff A_S is synchronizing, and

    rt(A) = rt(A_S).

### Proof

Given any word `a_1 ... a_l` over Σ, replace each symbol a_i by the corresponding transformation delta_{a_i}, viewed as a support letter. The resulting support word has the same length l and induces exactly the same transformation of Q. Hence every reset word of A gives a reset word of A_S of the same length.

Conversely, for each support transformation f in S choose one original symbol a_f with delta_{a_f}=f. Replacing every support letter f in a support word by a_f gives an original word of the same length and identical action on Q. Hence every reset word of A_S gives one for A with the same length.

The two shortest lengths are equal. QED.

### Consequence for the fixed q-letter metric

Exactly-q syntactic alphabets with repeated transformations are completely represented behaviorally by support sets of size at most q. Multiplicity patterns matter for the syntactic orbit count, but not for synchronizability or reset threshold. This is a proved quotient, not a silent change of the tracked class.

# 7. New exact minimal-counterexample reduction for n=8, q=4

## Theorem P16-Q4-MINIMAL-SUPPORT-51 — PROVED

Assume every synchronizing eight-state automaton with at most three distinct transformations satisfies the square. If a four-letter eight-state counterexample exists, then there is one with a support S of exactly four distinct transformations such that every proper three-element sub-support is nonsynchronizing. Moreover S contains neither the identity transformation nor a constant/rank-one transformation.

### Proof

Start with a hypothetical four-letter counterexample. Collapse duplicate letters by Theorem P16-DUPLICATE-SUPPORT-51. Repeatedly delete a support transformation whenever the remaining support is still synchronizing. Because the support is finite, this process ends at an inclusion-minimal synchronizing support S'. Removing available letters cannot create a shorter reset word: the set of legal words only shrinks. Therefore

    rt(A_{S'}) >= rt(A) > 49.

So A_{S'} remains a counterexample.

By the verified ternary n=8 result, a synchronizing support of size at most three cannot be a counterexample. Hence |S'|=4, and inclusion-minimality means every three-letter proper sub-support is nonsynchronizing.

If identity belonged to S', deleting it would not change which nonidentity transformation words are available and would preserve synchronizability, contradicting minimality. If a rank-one transformation belonged to S', the automaton would reset in one letter, contradicting `rt>49`. QED.

This is a genuine full-scope reduction for a hypothetical missing n=8 four-letter counterexample, not an SI restriction.

# 8. Pair-trap certificate forced by irreducibility

The original 1964 source proves the pair criterion: a DFA is synchronizing iff every pair of states can be merged by some word. This makes the preceding irreducibility reduction algorithmically useful.

## Theorem P16-ESSENTIAL-LETTER-PAIRTRAP-51 — PROVED

Let S={a,b,c,d} be synchronizing, and suppose every three-letter sub-support is nonsynchronizing. Fix a in S and put B=S\{a}. Let U_a be the set of unordered distinct pairs `{x,y}` that cannot be merged by any B-word. Then:

1. U_a is nonempty.
2. For every p in U_a and every letter e in B, p e is again a distinct pair in U_a.
3. U_a is not closed under a. Therefore there exists p in U_a such that p a is either diagonal or is a pair mergeable by a B-word.

### Proof

Since B is nonsynchronizing, the pair criterion gives a pair that no B-word merges; hence U_a is nonempty.

Take p={x,y} in U_a and e in B. If xe=ye, then e itself merges p, contradiction. Thus pe is distinct. If pe could be merged by a B-word w, then ew would merge p, again a contradiction. Hence pe lies in U_a. This proves B-invariance.

If U_a were also closed under a, then beginning from any p in U_a, every S-word would keep the image as a distinct pair in U_a. The full automaton S could not synchronize, contradicting the hypothesis. So some p escapes under a. QED.

This yields four exact essential-letter witnesses for every hypothetical minimal four-letter counterexample. It is an exact pruning certificate for enumeration, not by itself a bound on reset threshold.

# 9. Proved symmetry reduction and Burnside enumeration formulas

The direct labeled search space on n states and q letters has `(n^n)^q` ordered transition tables. For n=8,q=4 this is hopeless. I therefore derived and exactly checked the full state/letter symmetry quotient and, more importantly, the duplicate-collapsed support quotient.

## Lemma P16-TRANSFORMATION-CENTRALIZER-51 — PROVED

Let pi in S_n have m_d cycles of length d. The number C(pi) of arbitrary transformations f:Q->Q commuting with pi is

    C(pi) = product over d of ( sum over e|d of e*m_e )^(m_d).

### Proof

Consider one domain cycle `(x, pi x, ..., pi^(d-1)x)` of length d. Equivariance `f pi = pi f` means the image of the whole cycle is determined by y=f(x), and consistency after d steps requires `pi^d y=y`. The points fixed by pi^d are exactly the points lying in pi-cycles whose lengths e divide d, so there are `sum_{e|d} e m_e` choices for y. Different domain cycles can be chosen independently. Multiplying over the m_d domain cycles of each length d gives the formula. QED.

## Theorem P16-ORDERED-Q-BURNSIDE-51 — PROVED

Let M_{n,q} be the number of q-letter transition tables modulo simultaneous state relabeling by S_n and alphabet-symbol relabeling by S_q, with duplicate induced transformations allowed. Then

    M_{n,q}
      = 1/(n! q!) * sum_{pi in S_n} sum_{sigma in S_q}
          product over letter-cycles C of C(pi^{|C|}).

### Proof

Apply Burnside to the action of S_n x S_q. Fix (pi,sigma). Around one sigma-cycle of length l, choosing one letter transformation determines the remaining l-1 transformations by repeated conjugation with pi. Returning after l positions is consistent exactly when the chosen transformation commutes with pi^l. There are C(pi^l) choices. Distinct cycles of sigma are independent, giving the product. Averaging fixed-point counts over the group gives the formula. QED.

## Theorem P16-SUPPORT-BURNSIDE-51 — PROVED

Let S_{n,k} be the number of k-element sets of distinct transformations modulo state conjugation. For fixed pi, put

    F_r(pi) = C(pi^r).

For r<=4, the number a_d(pi) of d-cycles of the conjugation action on the full transformation monoid is

    a_1 = F_1,
    a_2 = (F_2-F_1)/2,
    a_3 = (F_3-F_1)/3,
    a_4 = (F_4-F_2)/4.

For k<=4 the number of pi-invariant k-supports is the coefficient of x^k in

    (1+x)^{a_1}(1+x^2)^{a_2}(1+x^3)^{a_3}(1+x^4)^{a_4}.

Consequently S_{n,k} is the average of that coefficient over pi in S_n.

### Proof

For the permutation c_pi acting on transformations by conjugation, F_r counts points fixed by c_pi^r. Thus `F_r = sum_{d|r} d a_d`; the displayed a_d formulas follow for r=1,2,3,4. A subset invariant under c_pi is exactly a union of complete c_pi-cycles. A k-set with k<=4 can use only action cycles of size at most 4. Choosing j cycles of size d contributes `x^{dj}` and gives the binomial factor `(1+x^d)^{a_d}`. Multiply across d and extract x^k. Burnside over state relabelings completes the proof. QED.

# 14. Next attack: canonical augmentation from nonsynchronizing ternary seeds, not raw orbit enumeration

The correct next finite route is to port the de Bondt–Don–Zantema/Kisielewicz style extension-and-pruning architecture to the exact P16 q=4,n=8 scope.

By Theorem P16-Q4-MINIMAL-SUPPORT-51, every hypothetical counterexample has four distinct letters and every three-letter sub-support is nonsynchronizing. This suggests generating candidates by canonical augmentation from nonsynchronizing ternary support seeds rather than traversing all four-subsets of the transformation monoid.

A complete durable-shard implementation must prove and record all of the following before it can move the metric:

1. **Canonical generation theorem.** Every four-support up to state conjugation is generated exactly once (or a proved controlled number of times) from a canonical three-support seed plus one new transformation. Letter order is irrelevant after support collapse.
2. **Irreducibility pruning.** Reject immediately if any three-subset synchronizes. This is exact by the minimal-counterexample theorem.
3. **Pair-trap pruning.** For each essential letter, compute the B-invariant nonmergeable-pair trap U_a and require an a-escape. Failure is a proof of nonsynchronizability of the four-support, not a heuristic.
4. **Exact synchronizability and reset threshold.** For every surviving automaton, run exact BFS in the subset automaton from Q; first singleton distance is the exact reset threshold. Every candidate with threshold >49 must be archived individually as a counterexample certificate; otherwise the maximum threshold and coverage counts are exact.
5. **Durable shards.** Partition by canonical ternary seed (or a collision-free canonical hash of its state-conjugacy class). For each shard archive: seeds, augmentations generated, symmetry duplicates removed, irreducibility failures, pair-trap failures, synchronizing survivors, exact maximum reset threshold, and a cryptographic digest of the candidate/certificate stream. A timed-out shard is incomplete and contributes zero to the complete-n metric.
6. **Coverage recombination proof.** The union of completed shards must be shown equal to the entire irreducibly synchronizing four-support domain. Sampling is not enough.

A secondary structural route is to turn the four pair-trap escape certificates into a provable bound on the number/form of possible fourth letters for a fixed nonsynchronizing ternary seed. That could shrink the augmentation set before subset-BFS.

The cubic-bound branch stays separate. I found no justified improvement to Shitov's coefficient. Any claimed new constant must come with a full theorem proof under the unrestricted DFA hypotheses, not numerical optimization of an unproved recurrence.
