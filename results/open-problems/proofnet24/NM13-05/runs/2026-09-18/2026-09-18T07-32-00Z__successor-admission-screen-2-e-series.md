## 4. Fresh current-literature correction — E0052 / Feige

E0052 was the strongest **unowned-looking** candidate by shape: an exact sharp probability constant for sums of independent nonnegative variables. It fails admission for a stronger reason than category fit: the source problem is now resolved.

First, the repository's own `probability_1000_intake_E_20260908/CORRECTIONS.md` records that the E0052 row is **misstated**. Bandeira's Conjecture 4.33 asks for the Feige upper-tail/small-deviation inequality, equivalently the lower bound

`P(S < E S + 1) >= 1/e`

under independent nonnegative summands with means at most one. The intake row had swapped to a non-complementary `<=` event, which is not source-exact because of possible mass at the threshold.

I then ran a fresh bounded public-literature check dated 2026-09-18 using the exact Feige statement and the `1/e` constant. It recovered multiple 2026 primary preprints:

1. **Weibo Fu, Yanjun Han, Guanyang Wang, Jun Yan, Peng Zhang, Zhengqing Zhou**, *Sharp small-deviation inequalities for sums of independent nonnegative random variables*, arXiv:`2607.23980`, submitted **2026-07-27**. For `delta >= 1` it gives the sharp finite-`n` bound; at `delta=1`,

   `P(S < E S + 1) >= (n/(n+1))^n >= 1/e`,

   with equality in the finite-`n` extremal construction.

2. **Zipei Nie and Jiaye Wei**, *On Feige's conjecture*, arXiv:`2607.24528`, submitted **2026-07-27**, gives a separate short proof of the same sharp finite-`n` Feige bound.

3. **Zhi Ling**, *On Samuels' Conjecture*, arXiv:`2608.18392`, submitted **2026-08-18**, proves the sharper Samuels extremal formula and states that Feige follows immediately from the equal-means case.

Thus E0052 is **not an available open source** in September 2026.
