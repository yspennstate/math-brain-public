## 6. Method #7 — TAKE THE LIMIT: what the arboreal-gas boundary really says about prior art

This method was used as a **prior-art reconciliation**, not as a new theorem claim.

For a finite graph with `n` vertices, write

\[
x=\frac{p}{q(1-p)}.

Up to a positive common factor, a configuration `A` has random-cluster weight

\[
x^{|A|} q^{c(A)},
\qquad
c(A)=|A|-n+k(A).
\]

Thus for fixed `x=beta`, taking `q -> 0` suppresses every cyclic configuration and leaves the arboreal-gas weight `beta^{|A|}` on forests. Equivalently one may take

\[
p(q)=\frac{\beta q}{1+\beta q}.
