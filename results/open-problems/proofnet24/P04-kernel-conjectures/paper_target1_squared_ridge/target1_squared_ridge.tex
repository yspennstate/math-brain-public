\begin{abstract}
We prove the restricted two-augmentation theorem completed by the P04/P21 kernel programme.  The setting has fixed positive augmentation strength and ridge, a smooth bounded odd activation such as \(\tanh\), Gaussian random-feature entries of variance \(1/d\), proportional sample and feature dimensions, and a unit-norm linear teacher.  The two augmentations of an original sample are replaced jointly, preserving their shared latent.  The optimized squared-ridge objective differs from its matched correlated Gaussian surrogate by \(O(d^{-1/2})\).  A separate argument proves clean-test prediction-risk equivalence against a jointly matched clean surrogate.  Exact normalization and the total, rather than per-block, replacement scale are visible throughout.
\end{abstract}

\section{Model and theorem}
Let \(a_i,z_{i1},z_{i2}\stackrel{\mathrm{iid}}{\sim}N(0,I_d)\), independently over \(i=1,\ldots,n\), and
\[
 F=(f_1,\ldots,f_m)\in\R^{d\times m},\qquad F_{kr}\stackrel{\mathrm{iid}}{\sim}N(0,1/d).
\]
Fix \(\Delta>0\), \(\lambda>0\), let \(n/d\to\alpha\in(0,\infty)\), \(m/d\to\beta\in(0,\infty)\), and let \(\|\xi\|=1\), \(t_i=\xi^Ta_i\).  The activation \(\sigma\) is smooth, bounded, odd, and has the bounded derivative norms used below (for example \(\tanh\); \(C_b^3\) suffices for the displayed estimates).  The actual features are
\[
 x_{ij}^{A}=\sigma\!\left(F^T(a_i+\Delta z_{ij})\right),\qquad j=1,2.
\]
Put \(N=2n\).  For either the actual or Gaussian model,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:objective}
 L_M(w)=\frac1{2N}\sum_{i=1}^n\sum_{j=1}^2
 \bigl(t_i-(x_{ij}^{M})^Tw\bigr)^2+\frac\lambda2\|w\|^2.
\end{equation}
Thus the per-row coefficient is exactly \(1/(2N)=1/(4n)\), the \(\ell=2\) specialization of the source coefficient \(1/(2n\ell)\).

For the matched training surrogate set
\[
 \tau=(1+\Delta^2)^{1/2},\qquad \rho=\tau^{-2},\qquad G\sim N(0,1),
\]
\[
 \mu=\E\sigma'(\tau G),\qquad V=\E\sigma(\tau G)^2,
\]
and for standard jointly Gaussian \((G_1,G_2)\) with correlation \(\rho\),
\[
 C=\E\{\sigma(\tau G_1)\sigma(\tau G_2)\}.
\]
The odd Hermite decomposition below gives
\[
 \mu_2^2=C-\mu^2\ge0,
 \qquad
 \mu_3^2=V-C-\Delta^2\mu^2\ge0.
\]
With independent standard Gaussian \(b_i,p_{i1},p_{i2}\in\R^m\), define
\begin{equation}\label{eq:surrogate}
 x_{ij}^{G}=\mu F^Ta_i+\Delta\mu F^Tz_{ij}+\mu_2b_i+\mu_3p_{ij}.
\end{equation}
The variables \(a_i,b_i\) are shared across the two views; \(z_{ij},p_{ij}\) are view-private.  The replacement unit is the entire triple \((t_i,x_{i1},x_{i2})\).

For a fresh clean point \(a^0\sim N(0,I_d)\), put \(t^0=\xi^Ta^0\), \(x_A^0=\sigma(F^Ta^0)\).  The clean test comparator is matched separately: with
\[
 \nu=\E\sigma'(G),\qquad V_0=\E\sigma(G)^2,
\]
let
\begin{equation}\label{eq:cleansur}
 x_G^0=\nu F^Ta^0+\sqrt{V_0-\nu^2}\,p^0,
 \qquad p^0\sim N(0,I_m),
\end{equation}
with \(p^0\) independent of training data and \(a^0\).

\begin{theorem}[Restricted two-view squared-ridge equivalence]\label{thm:main}
Let \(L_M^*=\min_w L_M(w)\).  Under the assumptions above,
\[
 |\E L_A^*-\E L_G^*|=O(d^{-1/2}).
\]
For every \(\Phi\in C_b^2(\R)\),
\[
 |\E\Phi(L_A^*)-\E\Phi(L_G^*)|
 \le C\|\Phi'\|_\infty d^{-1/2}+C\|\Phi''\|_\infty d^{-1}.
\]
The two rows belonging to each original sample are replaced jointly, so shared latent dependence is preserved throughout the hybrid chain.

Separately, the clean population squared risks of the trained actual model and the jointly matched clean comparator \eqref{eq:cleansur} have the same leading \(O(d^{-1/2})\) transfer for bounded smooth scalar tests.  If clean risk is estimated on \(M\) iid test points, the additional sampling term is \(O(M^{-1/2})\).
\end{theorem}

No claim is made for ReLU, nonlinear teachers, vanishing ridge, arbitrary augmentation counts, viewwise replacement, or independence of the nonlinear plus/minus channels.

\section{Exact rank-two leave-block completion}
Fix a hybrid step and delete both rows of original sample \(i\), without renormalizing.  Let
\[
 H_-=\lambda I+\frac1N X_-^TX_-,\qquad B=H_-^{-1},
\]
let \(w_-\) be the leave-block minimizer, and set
\[
 e=t_i\mathbf1_2-X_iw_-,\qquad Q=\frac1N X_iBX_i^T,\qquad A=(I_2+Q)^{-1}.
\]
Then \(\|B\|_{\op}\le\lambda^{-1}\), \(Q\succeq0\), and \(0\preceq A\preceq I_2\).

\begin{lemma}\label{lem:woodbury}
The exact restored optimizer and optimized-value increment are
\[
 w_i-w_-=\frac1N BX_i^TAe,
\qquad
 \boxed{L_i^*-L_-^*=\frac1{2N}e^TAe.}
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
For an increment \(\delta\), quadratic completion around \(w_-\) gives
\[
 L_i(w_-+\delta)=L_-^*+\frac12\delta^TH_-\delta
 +\frac1{2N}\|e-X_i\delta\|^2.
\]
Stationarity yields
\[
 (H_-+N^{-1}X_i^TX_i)\delta=N^{-1}X_i^Te,
\]
and Woodbury gives \(\delta=N^{-1}BX_i^TAe\).  The exact identities
\[
 I_2-QA=A,
 \qquad
 AQA+A^2=A
\]
imply \(e-X_i\delta=Ae\) and then the displayed optimized-value formula.  The accompanying exact symbolic checker verifies these finite algebraic identities independently.
\end{proof}

Deleting one view at a time would be invalid: the other view retains the shared \(a_i\) and, in the surrogate, the shared \(b_i\).  Joint deletion makes the leave environment independent of the complete current original-sample latent.

\section{Finite-feature Hermite--Schur structure}
Write \(f_r=R_r\theta_r\), where \(R_r^2\sim\chi_d^2/d\), \(\theta_r\) is uniform on \(S^{d-1}\), and radius and direction are independent.  Put \(\Theta_{rs}=\theta_r^T\theta_s\).  Conditional on \(F\),
\[
 G_{jr}=\frac{f_r^T(a+\Delta z_j)}{\tau R_r}
\]
is standard normal and
\[
 \operatorname{Corr}(G_{jr},G_{js}\mid F)=\Theta_{rs},
 \qquad
 \operatorname{Corr}(G_{1r},G_{2s}\mid F)=\rho\Theta_{rs}.
\]
For each radius \(r>0\), expand
\[
 \sigma(\tau rG)=\sum_{k\ge0}c_k(r)h_k(G)
\]
in the orthonormal probabilists' Hermite basis.  Oddness gives \(c_k(r)=0\) for even \(k\).  With \(D_k=\operatorname{diag}(c_k(R_1),\ldots,c_k(R_m))\), Mehler's identity gives exactly
\begin{align}
 \Sigma_A^{\mathrm{same}}&=\sum_{k\ \mathrm{odd}}D_k\Theta^{\circ k}D_k,\\
 \Sigma_A^{\mathrm{cross}}&=\sum_{k\ \mathrm{odd}}\rho^kD_k\Theta^{\circ k}D_k.
\end{align}
Every sector is positive semidefinite by the Schur product theorem.  The parity covariances
\[
 \Sigma_\pm=\sum_{k\ \mathrm{odd}}(1\pm\rho^k)D_k\Theta^{\circ k}D_k
\]
are therefore positive semidefinite; this is a covariance statement, not a claim of nonlinear parity independence.

Gaussian integration by parts yields
\[
 c_1(r)=\tau r\kappa(r),\qquad \kappa(r)=\E\sigma'(\tau rG),
\]
and for the teacher
\begin{equation}\label{eq:teacher}
 \E[t\,x_{jr}\mid F]=(\xi^Tf_r)\kappa(R_r).
\end{equation}
Thus teacher--feature dependence is part of the matched endpoint.

\section{Dimension bookkeeping}
The mismatch between actual and surrogate covariances splits into higher odd chaos and radius variation.  For the higher-chaos off-diagonal matrix \(H\), Parseval and boundedness of \(\sigma\) give
\[
 |H_{rs}|\le\|\sigma\|_\infty^2|\Theta_{rs}|^3\qquad(r\ne s).
\]
For independent spherical directions,
\[
 \E\Theta_{rs}^6=\frac{15}{d(d+2)(d+4)}.
\]
Because \(m=\Theta(d)\), the full \(m^2\) pair count gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:highchaos}
 \E\|H\|_F^2=O(d^{-1}),\qquad \|H\|_F=O_{L^2}(d^{-1/2}).
\end{equation}
The crucial cancellation is that oddness removes quadratic chaos, so the first unmatched off-diagonal sector is cubic.

Radius fluctuations are larger globally.  If \(\mu=\kappa(1)\) and \(\delta_r=\kappa(R_r)-\mu\), Gaussian radius concentration and bounded \(\sigma''\) give \(\E|\delta_r|^q=O(d^{-q/2})\) for fixed \(q\).  The radial covariance debt has only \(O_{L^2}(1)\) Frobenius norm, so global \(d^{-1/2}\) Frobenius matching is neither true nor needed.

The ridge solution supplies the needed probe gain.  A leave-feature-coordinate Schur complement, conditioning on all columns except coordinate \(r\), makes the \(r\)-specific Gaussian feature source fresh.  Fixed \(\lambda>0\) bounds the scalar Schur inverse, and Gaussian fourth-moment estimates of the fresh-column linear forms give, uniformly along the hybrid chain,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:coord4}
 \sup_{r\le m}\E|w_{-,r}|^4\le Cd^{-2}.
\end{equation}
The same bound holds for the clean sensitivity direction used below.  Therefore, without assuming independence,
\[
 \E\|\operatorname{diag}(\delta)w_-\|^2
 \le\sum_r(\E\delta_r^4)^{1/2}(\E|w_{-,r}|^4)^{1/2}=O(d^{-1}).
\]
All radial debts probed by \(w_-\) or the clean sensitivity direction are consequently \(O(d^{-1/2})\).  Equation \eqref{eq:teacher} similarly gives
\[
 \E\sum_r|\delta_r\,\xi^Tf_r|^2=O(d^{-1}).
\]
For a leverage mean, normalized trace gives the same gain directly: if \(R_{\mathrm{rad}}\) has \(O_{L^2}(1)\) Frobenius norm,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:normtrace}
 \left|\frac1N\tr(BR_{\mathrm{rad}})\right|
 \le\frac{\sqrt m}{\lambda N}\|R_{\mathrm{rad}}\|_F
 =O_{L^1}(d^{-1/2}).
\end{equation}
The higher-chaos debt in \eqref{eq:highchaos} is smaller still after the normalized trace.

\section{Dependence-aware leverage freezing}
Conditional on the leave-block environment, differentiate
\[
 Q_{ab}=N^{-1}x_a^TBx_b
\]
with respect to the entire shared Gaussian current block \((a_i,z_{i1},z_{i2})\).  The bounded derivative of \(\sigma\), \(\|B\|_{\op}\le\lambda^{-1}\), and \(\|x_j\|=O(\sqrt m)\) give
\[
 \|\nabla Q_{ab}\|\le C\frac{\sqrt m}{N}\|F\|_{\op}.
\]
Fixed moments of \(\|F\|_{\op}\) are bounded, so Gaussian Poincar\'e gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:qfluct}
 \E\|Q-\bar Q\|_F^2=O(d^{-1}),\qquad \bar Q=\E[Q\mid\mathcal F_-],
\end{equation}
for both actual and surrogate blocks.  Ridge coercivity and Gaussian concentration give \(\E\|e\|^4=O(1)\).

No independence between \(e\) and \(Q\) is asserted.  The resolvent identity yields
\[
 \|(I+Q)^{-1}-(I+\bar Q)^{-1}\|_{\op}\le\|Q-\bar Q\|_{\op},
\]
whence Cauchy--Schwarz and \eqref{eq:qfluct} give
\begin{equation}\label{eq:freeze}
 \left|\E e^T(I+Q)^{-1}e-
 \tr\left((I+\bar Q)^{-1}\E[ee^T]\right)\right|=O(d^{-1/2}).
\end{equation}
View-exchange symmetry makes \(\bar Q\) and \(C_e=\E[ee^T]\) commute with the swap matrix.  In the common plus/minus basis they have eigenvalues \(q_\pm,c_\pm\ge0\), so the frozen bracket is exactly
\[
 \frac{c_+}{1+q_+}+\frac{c_-}{1+q_-}.
\]
The finite-feature estimates above match \(q_\pm,c_\pm\) between actual and Gaussian laws at \(O(d^{-1/2})\).  Therefore
\begin{equation}\label{eq:blockcmp}
 \left|\E[e_A^T(I+Q_A)^{-1}e_A]
 -\E[e_G^T(I+Q_G)^{-1}e_G]\right|=O(d^{-1/2}).
\end{equation}

\section{Total training telescope}
Insert the exact factor \(1/(2N)\) from Lemma~\ref{lem:woodbury} before summing.  If the unscaled bound in \eqref{eq:blockcmp} is \(K/\sqrt d\), one original-sample replacement costs
\[
 \frac{K}{2N\sqrt d}.
\]
There are exactly \(n=N/2\) blocks, so the total first-order error is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:total}
 \boxed{\frac{nK}{2N\sqrt d}=\frac{K}{4\sqrt d}=O(d^{-1/2}).}
\end{equation}
This is the total replacement error, not a per-block estimate.

Moreover \(0\preceq(I+Q)^{-1}\preceq I\) and \(\E\|e\|^4=O(1)\) imply
\[
 \E(L_i^*-L_-^*)^2\le C/N^2.
\]
For \(\Phi\in C_b^2\), summing the one-block Taylor remainders gives
\[
 n\,O(N^{-2})=O(N^{-1})=O(d^{-1}),
\]
proving the training assertions of Theorem~\ref{thm:main}.

\section{Separate clean-risk telescope}
Training-objective equivalence does not imply prediction-risk equivalence.  Let an independent clean quadratic functional be
\[
 \widehat R_0(w)=c_0-2r_0^Tw+w^TS_0w.
\]
Set
\[
 v_-=B(S_0w_--r_0),\qquad h=X_iv_-,
\]
\[
 R=N^{-1}X_iBS_0BX_i^T,\qquad A=(I+Q)^{-1}.
\]
Direct substitution of the optimizer update gives the exact clean increment
\begin{equation}\label{eq:cleaninc}
 \boxed{\Gamma_i=\frac1N\{2e^TAh+e^TARAe\}.}
\end{equation}
The factor is \(1/N\), because this is a separate test quadratic.

Conditioning on the leave-training environment and clean summary, let
\[
 \bar Q=\E Q,\quad \bar R=\E R,\quad C=\E[ee^T],\quad M=\E[he^T],\quad A_0=(I+\bar Q)^{-1}.
\]
The resolvent identity gives the deterministic bound
\begin{align*}
&\left|\E\{2e^TAh+e^TARAe\}-\{2\tr(A_0M)+\tr(A_0\bar RA_0C)\}\right|\\
&\quad\le2\E[\|Q-\bar Q\|\|e\|\|h\|]
 +2\E[\|Q-\bar Q\|\|R\|\|e\|^2]
 +\E[\|R-\bar R\|\|e\|^2].
\end{align*}
Since \(R_{ab}=N^{-1}x_a^T(BS_0B)x_b\), the same Poincar\'e derivative calculation gives \(\E\|R-\bar R\|_F^2=O(d^{-1})\).  Equation \eqref{eq:coord4} for \(w_-\) and \(v_-\), together with the same Hermite--Schur decomposition, matches \(\bar Q,\bar R,C,M\) at \(O(d^{-1/2})\) in the fixed \(2\times2\) functional.  Thus the unscaled clean numerator mismatch is \(O(d^{-1/2})\).  The exact \(1/N\) in \eqref{eq:cleaninc} and \(n=N/2\) replacements yield total \(O(d^{-1/2})\), with the same \(O(d^{-1})\) second-order debt.

Finally, a fresh clean latent \(a^0\) is independent of training.  Gaussian Stein matches \(\E[t^0x^0]\), and the odd-Hermite decomposition matches the feature second moments with the comparator \eqref{eq:cleansur}, with the same \(O(d^{-1/2})\) probed radius/higher-chaos remainder.  This is a separate clean endpoint argument.  Averaging \(M\) fresh test points adds the standard \(O(M^{-1/2})\) sampling term.

\section{Prior art and independent audits}
The motivating source is O.~Dhifallah and Y.~M.~Lu, \emph{On the Inherent Regularization Effects of Noise Injection During Training}, PMLR 139 (2021).  Its equation (3) has coefficient \(1/(2n\ell)\), and its correlated Gaussian surrogate shares original-sample latent terms across augmentation index \(j\).  The paper explicitly describes correlated Gaussian equivalence as a conjectural ingredient.  The theorem above proves only the restricted regime stated here.

H.~Hu and Y.~M.~Lu, \emph{Universality Laws for High-Dimensional Learning with Random Features}, arXiv:2009.07669, is the rigorous architectural precedent for leave-one-out/Lindeberg/Stein random-feature universality, but does not justify splitting two noisy views with a common original latent.

A bounded literature check dated 16 September 2026 also found L.~Morisset, A.~Durmus and A.~Hardy, \emph{Characterizing the Generalization Error of Random Feature Regression with Arbitrary Data-Augmentation}, arXiv:2605.10290 (submitted 11 May 2026).  It studies proportional random-feature regression with arbitrary augmentation via population augmentation statistics.  The bounded check did not locate an exact theorem subsuming the finite empirical two-draw, shared-latent replacement statement above; accordingly the package is labelled PROVED rather than KNOWN.
