We now calculate the integrand pointwise.

From (6.6)--(6.9),

\begin{aligned}

\tag{7.2}

On the other hand, using the pointwise identity `\langle a,S\rangle=k`,

\[
\begin{aligned}
\|R\|^2
={}&16e+\frac{16}{d^2}\tau+(d+4)^2\|S\|^2
-\frac{32}{d}\langle J,a\rangle\\
&+8(d+4)\langle J,S\rangle
-\frac{8(d+4)}d k.
\end{aligned}
\tag{7.3}

Adding (7.2) and (7.3), the target Jordan defect cancels **pointwise**:

\[

and so does the unknown Jordan-affine correlation:

\[
\frac{32}{d}\langle J,a\rangle
-\frac{32}{d}\langle J,a\rangle=0.

Thus

\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{div}R+\|R\|^2
={}&16s t_d-\frac{16s(s-1)}d+8\delta
+\frac{8(s+1)}{d^2}\tau\\
&+(d+4)^2\|S\|^2
+8(d+4)\langle J,S\rangle\\
&-(d+4)k(d-1)-\frac{8k(d+3)}d.
\end{aligned}

\tag{7.4}
\]

There is no hidden `e` term left in (7.4).

# 8. New theorem: exact nonlinear weighted-Jordan Ward reduction

=-8\operatorname{tr}(P L_CQ L_J)
