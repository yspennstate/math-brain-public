# 6. New rank-two fresh-projection and selected-Schur local laws

## 6.1 Spectral delocalization of the actual residual plane

For a deterministic rank-two plane `P=span{A,B}` with Frobenius-orthonormal basis, put

\[

\qquad

For every Frobenius-unit `X=aA+bB in P` and every vector `v`,

\[
\|Xv\|^2
\le(a^2+b^2)(\|Av\|^2+\|Bv\|^2)
=v^TC(P)v.

Hence `X^2 preceq C(P)` and, with

\[
\kappa(P):=\sup_{X\in P,\|X\|_F=1}\|X\|_{op}^2,
\]

\[
\kappa(P)^2\le\|C(P)\|_{op}^2
\le\operatorname{tr}C(P)^2
=e(P)+\frac4d.
\tag{6.1}

For the actual residual plane `P_2`, (5.10) therefore yields

## 6.2 Joint CLT for two fresh physical quadratic coordinates
