## `P04-T4-ANISOTROPIC-WARD-SCORE-49` — PROVED

Put

\[

\[

\qquad

\[
S=\sum_{i=1}^kq_i,
\qquad
a=Y^TG^{-1}\mathbf1_k.
\tag{6.1}
\]

Then

\[
\langle q_i,a\rangle=1
\quad\text{for every }i,
\qquad
\boxed{\langle S,a\rangle=k.}
\tag{6.2}
\]

For `C in E`, let

\[
L_C(B)=\Pi_E\frac{CB+BC}{2}
\]

and consider the anisotropic sphere flow

\[
\phi_t^C(u)=\frac{e^{tC}u}{\|e^{tC}u\|}.
\tag{6.3}
\]

Its tangent field is

\[
V_C(u)=Cu-(u^TCu)u.
\]

Writing

\[
q_C(u)=u^TCu=\langle q(u),C\rangle,
\]

direct differentiation gives the exact coefficient-row variation

\[
\boxed{
\frac d{dt}q(\phi_t^C(u))\Big|_{t=0}
=2L_Cq(u)+\frac2dC-2q_C(u)q(u).
}
\tag{6.4}
\]

Because `q_C` is a degree-two traceless spherical harmonic,

\[
\Delta_{S^{d-1}}q_C=-2d\,q_C,
\]

and `V_C=(1/2)\nabla_S q_C`. Therefore

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{div}_S V_C=-d\,q_C.
}
\tag{6.5}
\]

Now differentiate the determinant density. The common linear term `2L_Cq_i` contributes

\[
4\operatorname{tr}((I-P)L_C)
=-4\operatorname{tr}(PL_C),
\]

because `tr_E L_C=0`. For rank two,

\[
\operatorname{tr}(PL_C)=\langle J(P),C\rangle.
\tag{6.6}
\]

The common shift `(2/d)C` contributes `(4/d)\langle a,C\rangle`, and the rowwise scaling term `-2q_C(U_i)q_i` contributes `-4\langle S,C\rangle`. Adding the sphere Jacobian (6.5), the exact logarithmic Ward score is

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal A_C
=-4\langle J(P),C\rangle
+\frac4d\langle a,C\rangle
-(d+4)\langle S,C\rangle.
}
\tag{6.7}
\]

For every smooth integrable test `F`, the compact-sphere change of variables gives

\[
\boxed{
\mathbb E_{Q_k}\big[D_CF+\mathcal A_CF\big]=0,
}
\tag{6.8}
\]

where `D_C` denotes derivative along the simultaneous flow (6.3).

### Rigour at nearly singular Gram matrices

The selected density contains `det G`; the singular set has zero selected mass. More importantly, the derivation need not divide by the determinant before integration: `det(G)G^{-1}=adj(G)` is polynomial in the Gram entries. Thus every first-inverse term above can be obtained by differentiating the unnormalized determinant and then normalizing. Equivalently one may regularize with `G+epsilon I` and pass to the limit. No unproved inverse-moment integrability is used in (6.8).

This score is genuinely physical-orbit-specific: it uses the sphere flow and the exact affine structure of `q(u)`, rather than only the coefficient covariance.
