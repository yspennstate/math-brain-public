# 6. Proof of `P04-T4-POINTWISE-024-SECTOR-37`

## 6.1 Exact spherical eighth-moment identity

Let `A,B` be arbitrary traceless symmetric matrices. Set

\[
f_A(u)=\langle h(u),A\rangle_F
=c_d\,u^\top Au,
\]

and similarly for `B`.

For `g~N(0,I_d)`, the joint quadratic-form cumulant calculation gives

\[
E(g^\top Ag)^2(g^\top Bg)^2
=
4\|A\|_F^2\|B\|_F^2
+8\langle A,B\rangle_F^2
+32\operatorname{tr}(A^2B^2)
+16\operatorname{tr}(ABAB).
\tag{6.1}
\]

One direct derivation uses

\[
\log E\exp\{g^\top(tA+sB)g\}
=-\frac12\log\det(I-2tA-2sB)
\]

and extracts the `t^2s^2` coefficient. The fourth joint cumulant is

\[
32\operatorname{tr}(A^2B^2)
+16\operatorname{tr}(ABAB),
\]

while the three pairings contribute the first two terms in (6.1).

Since `g=ru`, `r` and `u` are independent and

\[
E r^8=d(d+2)(d+4)(d+6),
\]

while

\[
c_d^4=\frac{d^2(d+2)^2}{4},
\]

we obtain

\[
\boxed{
\int f_A(u)^2f_B(u)^2\,d\mu(u)
=
\alpha_d
\left[
\|A\|_F^2\|B\|_F^2
+2\langle A,B\rangle_F^2
+8\operatorname{tr}(A^2B^2)
+4\operatorname{tr}(ABAB)
\right].
}
\tag{6.2}
\]

As a consistency check, setting `A=B`, `||A||_F=1`, gives

\[
E f_A^4
=
3\alpha_d\left(1+4\operatorname{tr}A^4\right),
\]

which matches the previously established exact pure-harmonic fourth-moment formula.
