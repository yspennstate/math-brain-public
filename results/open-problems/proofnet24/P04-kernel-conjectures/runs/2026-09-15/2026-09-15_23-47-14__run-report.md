# 6. New theorem — all-rank exact two-step selected amplitude

## `P04-T4-ALLRANK-TWOSTEP-AMPLITUDE-45` — PROVED

Fix any deterministic rank-s coefficient projection `P` in E, with s>=2. Let

`F_P(u)=||P h(u)||^2`.

Draw the first fresh harmonic row y=h(U) under the exact deletion density

`F_P(U)/s`.

Set

`N = P y / ||P y||`,

`P' = P - |N><N|`.

Draw the second fresh harmonic row x=h(V) under the exact next deletion density

`R/(s-1)`, where `R=||P' x||^2`.

Let

`Rbar_s(P) = E_selected-pair[R | P]`.

Define

`Acal(P)=tr C(P)^2`,

`delta(P)=(1/2) sum_{a,b=1}^s ||[A_a,A_b]||_F^2`,

`b(P)=E[tr N^4 | P]`,

`alpha_d = d(d+2)/[(d+4)(d+6)]`.

Then, exactly at finite d,

`Rbar_s(P)`

` = alpha_d/(s-1) * [ s^2-1 + 12(s-2)Acal(P)/s + 12 b(P) - 4(s-2)delta(P)/s ].`      (6.1)

### 6.1 Proof

For any deterministic rank-r coefficient projection Q, the exact spherical eighth-moment calculation gives

`Integral F_Q(u)^2 dmu(u)`

` = alpha_d [ r^2+2r + 12 Acal(Q) - 4 delta(Q) ].`      (6.2)

The selected-pair density relative to `mu x mu` is

`F_P(U) R(U,V) / [s(s-1)]`.

Therefore

`Rbar_s(P) = (1/(s-1)) E_first-del[ Integral F_{P'}(v)^2 dmu(v) | P ].`      (6.3)

For the Jordan term, `C(P')=C(P)-N^2`. Hence

`Acal(P') = Acal(P) - 2 tr(C(P)N^2) + tr N^4`.

The exact conditional deletion covariance gives

`E[N^2|P]=C(P)/s`,

so

`E[Acal(P')|P] = ((s-2)/s) Acal(P) + b(P).`      (6.4)

For the commutator term, complete N to an orthonormal basis `N,M_1,...,M_{s-1}` of Ran(P). Then

`delta(P)-delta(P') = sum_j ||[N,M_j]||_F^2`.      (6.5)

Conditionally on P and N, the next deletion direction N' has coefficient covariance `P'/(s-1)`, so

`E[||[N,N']||_F^2 | P,N]`

` = (1/(s-1)) sum_j ||[N,M_j]||_F^2`.      (6.6)

On the other hand, averaging first over N and using its coefficient covariance P/s,

`E[ sum_j ||[N,M_j]||_F^2 | P ] = 2 delta(P)/s`.

Thus

`E[delta(P')|P] = ((s-2)/s) delta(P).`      (6.7)

Insert (6.4) and (6.7) into (6.2)-(6.3). This gives (6.1).

At s=3, (6.1) becomes

`Rbar_3(P) = alpha_d/2 [ 8 + 4 Acal(P) + 12 b(P) - (4/3)delta(P) ]`,

exactly P23's rank-three theorem.

# 10. New theorem — exact all-rank weak-Haar identity

## `P04-T4-ALLRANK-WEAK-HAAR-45` — PROVED

Let P_s be the **actual adaptive harmonic residual plane** under the volume/DPP chain. Define

`e_s = E Acal(P_s)-s^2/d`,

`gamma_s = E tr(N_s^4)-1/d`.

`e_{s-1} = ((s-2)/s)e_s + gamma_s`.      (10.1)

` = alpha_d/(s-1) * [ s^2-1 + 12(s-1)^2/d - 2d(s-2)(s-1)/(m-1) + 12 e_{s-1} ].`      (10.3)

`E_actual Rbar_s - R_s^{sph}`

` = [12 alpha_d/(s-1)] ( e_{s-1}-e_{s-1}^H ).`      (10.4)

`E_actual Rbar_s - R_{s,H}^{harm}`

` = [12 alpha_d/(s-1)] (e_{s-1}-e_{s-1}^H) - Delta_orbit(s,d)`,      (10.5)

### What this proves and what it does not

Equation (10.4) shows that **full total-variation Haarization is unnecessary**. It is enough to compare one selected two-step amplitude observable under the actual residual law and the coefficient-spherical/Haar benchmark.

However, (10.4) by itself is not a contraction theorem. It is exactly equivalent to the Jordan defect `e_{s-1}-e_{s-1}^H`. Averaging the known moments and substituting the recurrence merely re-expresses the same unknown. Thus a proof based only on the exact moment identities is circular.

The genuine missing theorem is law-specific: show that the actual adaptive P_s law controls the conditional functional `Rbar_s(P)` (or equivalently its Jordan/fourth-mark part) close enough to Haar, especially in the final ranks that carry the positive fourth-mark budget.
