## 8. Main theorem — all higher sectors collapse to a pair, and the pair is resistance-controlled

### `C31-I0035-PAIR-RESISTANCE-05 = PROVED`

Let `G=(V,E)` be a finite connected simple graph with `n=|V|`. Put

\[
R_*:=\max_{y\in V}\bar R_y,
\qquad
\bar R_y:=\frac1n\sum_{x\in V}R_{x,y}.
\tag{8.1}
\]

For every `r` with `2<=r<=alpha(G)`, let `lambda_D^(r)(G)` have exactly the normalization of `C31-I0035-INDEPENDENTSET-SPARSE-04`: continuous-time simple exclusion, each ordered particle-edge move at rate `1/2`, restricted to independent `r`-sets, with Dirichlet killing on a proposed slide to a non-independent `r`-set.

Then:

\[
\boxed{
\lambda_D^{(r)}(G)\ge \lambda_D^{(2)}(G)
\qquad(2\le r\le\alpha(G)).
}
\tag{8.2}
\]

Consequently, whenever `alpha(G)>=2`,

\[
\boxed{
\min_{2\le r\le\alpha(G)}\lambda_D^{(r)}(G)
=\lambda_D^{(2)}(G).
}
\tag{8.3}
\]

Moreover

\[
\boxed{
\lambda_D^{(2)}(G)\ge \frac1{nR_*}.
}
\tag{8.4}
\]

Thus the stuck-report bridge is true with `c=1`:

\[
\boxed{
\min_{2\le r\le\alpha(G)}\lambda_D^{(r)}(G)
\ge\frac1{n\max_y\bar R_y}.
}
\tag{8.5}
\]

If `alpha(G)=1`, the higher-sector minimum is absent and (8.5) is vacuous.

### 8.1 Labeled lift of killed independent-set exclusion

For `r>=2`, let

\[
\Omega_r=
\{(x_1,\dots,x_r)\in V^r:
\{x_1,\dots,x_r\}\text{ is an independent }r\text{-set}\}.
\]

Consider `r` labeled continuous-time walkers, each attempting every incident edge at rate `1/2`, killed upon leaving `Omega_r`.

This is exactly the labeled lift of the killed SEP block. From an independent tuple, a coordinate cannot jump directly onto another occupied coordinate: such a jump would require those two current occupied vertices to be adjacent, contradicting independence. Hence the only one-step exits are precisely moves that create an adjacency and are exactly the Dirichlet-killing moves in the unlabeled definition.

The symmetric subspace of functions on `Omega_r` is naturally the unlabeled independent-set space. Therefore, writing `lambda_lab^(r)` for the principal labeled Dirichlet eigenvalue,

\[
\lambda_D^{(r)}(G)\ge \lambda_{\rm lab}^{(r)}(G),
\tag{8.6}
\]

because the symmetric Rayleigh quotient minimizes over a smaller function class.

### 8.2 Forget all constraints except one designated pair

Enlarge the labeled domain to

\[
\widetilde\Omega_{12}
=
\{(x_1,\dots,x_r):x_1\ne x_2,\ x_1\not\sim x_2\},
\tag{8.7}
\]

with coordinates `3,...,r` unrestricted. Then

\[
\Omega_r\subseteq\widetilde\Omega_{12}.
\]

For a symmetric killed generator, Dirichlet-domain enlargement can only decrease the principal eigenvalue. Hence

\[
\lambda_{\rm lab}^{(r)}(G)
\ge
\lambda(\widetilde\Omega_{12}).
\tag{8.8}
\]

The enlarged domain factors as the ordered two-particle nonadjacency domain times `V^{r-2}`. The last `r-2` coordinates are unrestricted and contribute a zero ground energy. Therefore

\[
\lambda(\widetilde\Omega_{12})
=\lambda_{\rm pair}(G),
\tag{8.9}
\]

where `lambda_pair` is the principal eigenvalue of two labeled rate-`1/2` walkers killed when they become adjacent.

Finally

\[
\lambda_{\rm pair}(G)=\lambda_D^{(2)}(G).
\tag{8.10}
\]

Indeed the ordered pair domain is invariant under swapping the two coordinates. On any component realizing the principal eigenvalue, choose a nonnegative ground state; if swapping sends that component to a distinct component, add its swapped copy, while if the component is invariant, Perron uniqueness makes the positive ground state swap-invariant. In either case a symmetric principal eigenfunction exists, and the symmetric quotient is exactly the unlabeled two-token killed process.

Combining (8.6)--(8.10) proves (8.2), and (8.3) follows because the `r=2` sector is itself present whenever `alpha(G)>=2`.

### 8.3 Thomson inequality for the ordered pair

Let

\[
D_2=\{(x,y)\in V^2:x\ne y,\ x\not\sim y\}.
\]

Take any real test function `f` on `D_2` and extend it by zero to all of `V^2`. In particular

\[
f(y,y)=0
\]

for every `y`.

For fixed `y`, set

\[
g_y(x)=f(x,y).
\]

The variational characterization of effective resistance (equivalently Thomson/Cauchy along the unit electrical flow) gives, for every `x`,

\[
|g_y(x)-g_y(y)|^2
\le
R_{x,y}
\sum_{\{u,v\}\in E}
(g_y(u)-g_y(v))^2.
\tag{8.11}
\]

Since `g_y(y)=0`, summing in `x` yields

\[
\sum_x f(x,y)^2
\le
n\bar R_y
\sum_{\{u,v\}\in E}
(f(u,y)-f(v,y))^2.
\tag{8.12}
\]

Then summing in `y` and using `bar R_y<=R_*` gives

\[
\|f\|_2^2
\le
nR_*
\sum_y\sum_{\{u,v\}\in E}
(f(u,y)-f(v,y))^2.
\tag{8.13}
\]

For the first coordinate, each ordered particle-edge jump has rate `1/2`. Therefore its Dirichlet contribution is

\[
\mathcal E_1(f,f)
=
\frac12
\sum_y\sum_{\{u,v\}\in E}
(f(u,y)-f(v,y))^2,
\tag{8.14}
\]

where the zero extension automatically includes all killing edges. Hence

\[
\|f\|_2^2\le2nR_*\mathcal E_1(f,f).
\tag{8.15}
\]

The identical argument in the second coordinate gives

\[
\|f\|_2^2\le2nR_*\mathcal E_2(f,f).
\tag{8.16}
\]

Adding (8.15) and (8.16) and dividing by two,

\[
\boxed{
\|f\|_2^2
\le
nR_*\bigl(\mathcal E_1(f,f)+\mathcal E_2(f,f)\bigr)
=nR_*\mathcal E_{\rm pair}(f,f).
}
\tag{8.17}
\]

Taking the infimum of the Rayleigh quotient over nonzero `f` proves

\[
\lambda_{\rm pair}(G)\ge\frac1{nR_*}.
\]

Together with (8.10), this proves (8.4) and therefore (8.5). QED.

### Normalization check

The factor in (8.14) is important. A single labeled walker attempts each oriented edge at rate `1/2`; consequently the standard symmetric Dirichlet form is `1/2` times the unoriented gradient sum. This is exactly the rate-`1/2` normalization frozen in `C31-I0035-INDEPENDENTSET-SPARSE-04`. No differently normalized exclusion gap is being compared under the same symbol.
