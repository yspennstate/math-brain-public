## 4. New theorem: the general `O(n^2/theta)` upper scale is attained on paths

### `C31-I0035-PATH-SHARPNESS-02 = PROVED`

Let `P_n` be the path on vertices `1,...,n`, `n>=2`, and let the Aldous–Fill biased annihilating branching chain have parameter

\[
0<\theta\le \frac1{100n^3}.
\tag{4.1}
\]

Then

\[
\boxed{
\tau_{\rm BAB}(P_n,\theta)
\ge \frac{n^2-1}{13\theta}.
}
\tag{4.2}
\]

Consequently, for any sequence `theta_n` satisfying (4.1), the HMT upper bound together with (2.1) gives

\[
\boxed{
\tau_{\rm BAB}(P_n,\theta_n)
=\Theta\!\left(\frac{n^2}{\theta_n}\right).
}
\tag{4.3}
\]

The constants in the `Theta` are universal. Thus the previously obtained general sparse-regime envelope `O(n^2/theta)` cannot be improved to `o(n^2/theta)` uniformly over source graphs and parameters. This is still a restricted-parameter theorem, hence **PARTIAL** for Open Problem 14.31.

### Proof of (4.2)

Write a nonempty configuration as `S subseteq {1,...,n}`. Since

\[
p=\frac\theta{1+\theta},
\]

the conditioned Bernoulli law has the especially simple form

\[
\mu_\theta(S)=\frac{\theta^{|S|}}{Z_\theta},
\qquad
Z_\theta=(1+\theta)^n-1.
\tag{4.4}
\]

For `v notin S`, let

\[
d_S(v)=|\{u\in S:u\sim v\}|.
\]

The unordered configuration-space edge `S <-> S union {v}` has reversible conductance

\[
c(S,S\cup\{v\})
=\mu_\theta(S)\,\theta d_S(v)
=\frac{d_S(v)\theta^{|S|+1}}{Z_\theta}.
\tag{4.5}
\]

Define the centered linear profile

\[
g(i)=i-\frac{n+1}{2}
\]

and the test function

\[
f(S)=\frac1{|S|}\sum_{i\in S}g(i).
\tag{4.6}
\]

#### Variance

Let `A={S:|S|=1}`. Conditional on `A`, the singleton is uniform on the path, so

\[
\operatorname{Var}(f\mid A)
=\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^n g(i)^2
=\frac{n^2-1}{12}.
\tag{4.7}
\]

Set `x=n\theta`. Under (4.1), `x<=1`. Since

\[
Z_\theta\le e^x-1\le2x,
\]

we have

\[
\mu_\theta(A)=\frac{n\theta}{Z_\theta}\ge\frac12.
\tag{4.8}
\]

By the law of total variance,

\[
\operatorname{Var}_{\mu_\theta}(f)
\ge \mu_\theta(A)\operatorname{Var}(f\mid A)
\ge \frac{n^2-1}{24}.
\tag{4.9}
\]

#### Dirichlet form: singleton level

Enumerating each transition by its smaller occupied set,

\[
\mathcal D_{\rm BAB}(f)
=\sum_{\emptyset\ne S\subsetneq V}
\sum_{v\notin S}
\frac{d_S(v)\theta^{|S|+1}}{Z_\theta}
\left(\frac{g(v)-f(S)}{|S|+1}\right)^2.
\tag{4.10}
\]

For `|S|=1`, every path edge `{i,i+1}` gives exactly two configuration edges, one from `{i}` and one from `{i+1}`. In both cases the change in `f` has magnitude `1/2`. Hence the singleton contribution is exactly

\[
\mathcal D_1
=\frac{(n-1)\theta^2}{2Z_\theta}
\le \frac\theta2,
\tag{4.11}
\]

using `Z_theta >= n theta`.

#### Dirichlet form: all higher particle levels

For `|S|=k>=2`, the path geometry gives

\[
\sum_{v\notin S}d_S(v)\le2k,
\tag{4.12}
\]

because the left-hand side is the number of path edges crossing from `S` to its complement. Also the range of `g` is `n-1`, so

\[
|g(v)-f(S)|\le n.
\tag{4.13}
\]

Therefore the contribution from level `k` obeys

\[
\mathcal D_k
\le
\binom nk\frac{\theta^{k+1}}{Z_\theta}
\frac{2kn^2}{(k+1)^2}
\le
\frac{2n^2\theta^{k+1}}{Z_\theta}\binom nk.
\tag{4.14}
\]

Summing over `k>=2`, allowing the nonexistent full-set births only to enlarge the bound,

\[
\mathcal D_{\ge2}
\le
\frac{2n^2\theta}{Z_\theta}
\left[(1+\theta)^n-1-n\theta\right].
\tag{4.15}
\]

Now

\[
(1+\theta)^n-1-n\theta
\le e^x-1-x
\le \frac{e^x x^2}{2},
\tag{4.16}
\]

and `Z_theta>=x`. Thus

\[
\mathcal D_{\ge2}
\le e^x n^3\theta^2
\le \frac{e}{100}\theta
<\frac3{100}\theta.
\tag{4.17}
\]

Combining (4.11) and (4.17),

\[
\mathcal D_{\rm BAB}(f)
<\frac{53}{100}\theta.
\tag{4.18}
\]

The Rayleigh quotient now yields

\[
\tau_{\rm BAB}(P_n,\theta)
\ge
\frac{(n^2-1)/24}{(53/100)\theta}
=
\frac{25(n^2-1)}{318\theta}
>
\frac{n^2-1}{13\theta},
\]

which proves (4.2). QED.

### Proof of the matching upper order (4.3)

For the path with unit edge resistances,

\[
R_{x,y}=|x-y|,
\qquad
\max_y\bar R_y=\frac{n-1}{2}.
\tag{4.19}
\]

Under (4.1), `p_n=theta_n/(1+theta_n)->0`. HMT Theorem 1(b) therefore gives

\[
\tau_{\rm CB}(P_n,p_n)
\le c n\frac{n-1}{2}.
\tag{4.20}
\]

Applying the sharp C31 comparison (2.1),

\[
\tau_{\rm BAB}(P_n,\theta_n)
\le \frac{c}{2}\frac{n(n-1)}{\theta_n}
=O\!\left(\frac{n^2}{\theta_n}\right).
\tag{4.21}
\]

Together with (4.2), this proves (4.3).

## 7. Directed second-audit duty: P01's new C2 claim

The newest P01 report after the previous C31 audit introduces

`P01-I0005-C2-D4-FINITE-HORIZON-OVERLAP-BARRIER`.

I independently rederived the claim rather than repeating the earlier C1 audit.

Write

\[
G_t(a)=\sum_{m=0}^t p_m(a),
\qquad
q_{x,t}(v)=\mathbb P_v(T_x\le t),
\]

\[
K_t(a)=\sum_yG_t(y)G_t(y-a),
\qquad
Q_t(x,z)=\sum_v q_{x,t}(v)q_{z,t}(v),
\]

and `g=G(0)`.

First, hitting by time `t` implies at least one visit by time `t`, while after the first hit the expected total number of future visits is at most `G(0)`. Thus, pointwise,

\[
\frac1gG_t(v-x)\le q_{x,t}(v)\le G_t(v-x).
\tag{7.1}
\]

Multiplying the two bounds and summing `v` gives exactly

\[
g^{-2}K_t(z-x)\le Q_t(x,z)\le K_t(z-x).
\tag{7.2}
\]

Second, Tonelli and Chapman–Kolmogorov give the exact truncated convolution identity

\[
K_t(a)=\sum_{r,s=0}^t p_{r+s}(a)
=\sum_{m=0}^{2t}c_t(m)p_m(a),
\tag{7.3}
\]

where

\[
c_t(m)=
\begin{cases}
m+1,&m\le t,\\2t-m+1,&t<m\le2t.\end{cases}
\]

The standard four-dimensional heat-kernel upper bound

\[
p_m(a)\le C m^{-2}\exp(-c|a|^2/m)
\]

then yields

\[
K_t(a)\le C'\left[1+\log\left(1+\frac{t}{1+|a|^2}\right)\right].
\tag{7.4}
\]

For the lower bound, choose `m` of the parity compatible with `a` in the interval from a sufficiently large multiple of `max(|a|^2,t^{1/2})` up to `t`. If `|a|<=c_0t^{1/4}` with `c_0` small, the periodic local CLT is uniform on that interval and gives

\[
p_m(a)\ge c m^{-2}
\]

on every compatible parity. Since `c_t(m)=m+1` for `m<=t`, summing over half the integers gives

\[
K_t(a)\ge c'\sum_{m\asymp t^{1/2}}^t\frac1m
\ge c''\log t.
\tag{7.5}
\]

Together with (7.2), this proves P01's stated `Q_t=Theta(log t)` barrier at separations `1<=|x-z|<=c_0t^{1/4}`.
