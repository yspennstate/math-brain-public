## 8. P0213 exact source wording and fresh bounded literature check

The maintained source page states the model literally:

- arrivals form a rate-1 Poisson process;
- task types are uniform on `[0,1]`;
- batch processing time `S(B)` is monotone and strictly subadditive on disjoint batches;

The source asks whether there is an **explicit algorithm**, allowed to depend on `S` and `c`, and a **universal constant `C`** such that

`W / W_* < C`

for **all** admissible `S,c`. The same page says that for `c=1`, the greedy policy that puts everything in one batch has finite `W` whenever any algorithm does. The problem history says it was posted March 2009.
