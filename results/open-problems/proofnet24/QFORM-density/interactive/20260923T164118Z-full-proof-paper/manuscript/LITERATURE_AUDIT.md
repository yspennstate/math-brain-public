# Literature audit

Checked 23 September 2026. This record identifies the material inspected for the accompanying anonymous manuscript. It is a source and scope audit, not a claim to have established priority or to have exhaustively searched the literature. Bibliographic authors name the authors of cited works; no manuscript author is identified.

## Main conclusions

The target under review is a constant uniform over all dimensions, all symmetric matrices with three eigenvalues of modulus at least one, all linear terms, and all independent coordinate densities bounded by one. None of the inspected theorem statements supplies this whole conclusion. The nearby positive results have material restrictions: linear projections, bilinear forms in independent blocks, Gaussian input, log-concavity, compact support for a fixed analytic map, or a weaker anti-concentration exponent.

This does **not** certify novelty. In particular, the semidefinite estimate in the manuscript must be assessed on its proof and precise quantifiers. The inspected Gaussian and analytic-pushforward papers do not give its required constant uniform in arbitrary product laws and ambient dimension. A failed or irrelevant search result is not evidence of absence from the literature.

## Critical attribution and transfer checks

- **Sasha Sodin:** The three directly inspected papers with Sasha Sodin are `FS2007`, `APSSS2017`, and `GHS2026`. These entries use the name printed on the papers. They are not papers by Mikhail Sodin. The separate Nazarov--Sodin--Volberg literature must not be attributed to Sasha without checking its author identity. No source inspected establishes that Sasha Sodin formulated the exact conjecture in this manuscript; the conjecture is not attributed to him.
- **Sharp marginal bound:** `RV2015` proves a universal exponential-in-projection-dimension bound. The explicit factor `2^(k/2)` is supplied by `LPP2016`. Projection density bounds are not arbitrary conditional-density bounds.
- **Rough Fourier bounds:** `RV2015`, Lemma 3.1, estimates the decreasing rearrangement of the characteristic-function modulus. Replacing this by pointwise decay is invalid. `BCG2012` pointwise separation estimates retain variance, quantile, or entropy parameters.
- **Bilinear density:** `BGKS2024`, Theorem 2.1 and scalar Corollary 2.4, concern independent random blocks. The scalar integrability step needs at least two crossing directions. This theorem does not by itself cover a rank-one hub whose transverse quadratic term uses the same light coordinates.
- **Parabola L6:** The estimate with constant `4*pi^3/sqrt(3)` is a classical sharp Schrödinger Strichartz consequence. Foschi's Theorem 1.1 has evolution-operator constant `12^(-1/12)`; converting the Fourier convention gives `(2*pi)^3/sqrt(12) = 4*pi^3/sqrt(3)` for the sixth power. Density at most one implies L2 norm at most one. The elementary coarea proof may be reproduced as a proof of established input.
- **Local endpoint:** `GHS2026` distinguishes all finite Lp integrability from L-infinity. It also records stronger endpoint theorems, including its Corollary 6.2 and the real result of `Reiser2018`. Their constants are not asserted to be uniform over the families required here.
- **Hub analogue:** `APSSS2017`, Lemma 2.1 and Section 6, supplies a strong Gaussian ratio estimate and a dominant-eigenvalue/diffuse-remainder Fourier decomposition. Rotational invariance and the exact Gaussian transform are essential to the published argument. This is a potential methodological starting point, not a completed transfer to rough product densities.

## Review limits and follow-up

The directly used standard theorems should retain their exact normalizations and hypotheses in the main text. For sources checked only through metadata, abstracts, or a primary-paper restatement, this audit does not claim a full proof review. In particular, the bibliography is not evidence that any new-looking lemma has no predecessor.

The most relevant next literature-based mathematical task is to identify a replacement for the Gaussian joint-transform argument in `APSSS2017` that survives arbitrary bounded coordinate densities, or a quantitative analytic-pushforward theorem with constants controlled by the product structure and third spectral scale. Neither transfer has been established in this audit.
