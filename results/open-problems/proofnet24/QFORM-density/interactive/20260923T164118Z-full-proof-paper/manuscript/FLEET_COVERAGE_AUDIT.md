## Corrections that materially affect the proof map

### 1. Interval profiles do not become Borel-set profiles

The frozen-slice proofs in fleet02/B05 `20260923T130427Z` (Sections 5–6) establish an oscillatory estimate and a square-root bound for the measure of an interval. Their Section 7 then invokes a receiver for an arbitrary cap-one independent hub. Such a receiver requires control on every Borel set, or an additional interval-support/regularity hypothesis on the hub. The layer-cake superlevel sets of a translated, rescaled rough hub density need not be intervals. The manuscript states this stronger Borel hypothesis explicitly in `lem:hub-profile`.

Accordingly, that report’s `<55.776` full rough heavy-null conclusion and Corollary 8.1 are not certified by the displayed proof. The same issue occurs in fleet04/B14 `20260923T141430Z`, where the valid affine frozen-slice theorem is followed by the same unsupported transfer. Pure-longitudinal absorption in fleet01/B05 `20260923T160511Z` is valid algebra and remains useful; applications importing the preceding endpoint are conditional. This audit does not disprove the desired heavy-null theorem.

### 2. Krylov rank is not the necessary spectral reserve

Fleet01/B12 `20260923T162217Z` correctly bounds the rank of `(A0+S)^2-A0^2` by `dim(Ran S+A0 Ran S)`, at most twice the rank of S. Its example with a rank-two difference does not show that a reserve loss of twice the rank of S is necessary. Direct singular-value interlacing of `(A0+S)P_H` loses only `rank(SP_H)`. The manuscript proves this stronger result in `supp:rank-reserve`, including an operator-norm remainder and an adaptive spectral threshold.

### 3. Negative results retain their exact scope

The moving-ridge example keeps one hub independent while allowing dependence inside a triple with bounded joint density. It is a counterexample to a weakened independence premise, not to the original product-law conjecture. The L3 plus square-root interval example disproves a general interpolation implication, not the existence of a more structured quadratic-form argument. Both are proved in `fleet_supplement.tex`.
