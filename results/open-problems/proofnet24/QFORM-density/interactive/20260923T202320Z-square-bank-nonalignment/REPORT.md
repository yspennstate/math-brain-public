# Quantitative nonalignment of every separated square bank in a near-hub family

23 September 2026. This is a matrix-only obstruction to forcing a fixed-strength independent square carrier, even with a small approximation tolerance. The same family has a uniform density bound by the separate two-bank theorem cited below.

## Setup

Let \(m\) be a multiple of four, let \(h\) be one original hub coordinate, and on \(m\) light coordinates put
\[
w_j=m^{-1/2},\qquad
v_j=\sqrt{\frac{12}{m(m^2-1)}}
       \left(j-\frac{m+1}{2}\right),\qquad 1\le j\le m.
\]
Then \(w,v\) are orthonormal and \(|v_j|^2<3/m\). Define
\[
A_0=e_hw^\mathsf T+we_h^\mathsf T+vv^\mathsf T.
\]
It has exact rank three and nonzero eigenvalues \(1,-1,1\).

A separated rank-one square carrier on an original-coordinate bank \(S\) has, at the matrix level, \(A_{SS}=\lambda uu^\mathsf T\), \(A_{ST}=uz^\mathsf T\), where \(T=S^c\), \(|u|=1\), \(\lambda\ne0\). To quantify approximation, suppose both
\[
\|A_{SS}-\lambda uu^\mathsf T\|_{\rm op}\le\eta,
\qquad
\|A_{ST}-uz^\mathsf T\|_{\rm op}\le\eta. \tag{1}
\]

## Transversality on every strong light bank

First let \(S\) contain only light coordinates, with size \(s\). If \(\|v_S\|^2\ge c\), then \(s\ge cm/3\). Write \(r_j=\sqrt m\,v_j\), an equally spaced grid with step \(\sqrt{12/(m^2-1)}\). For any \(s\) selected grid points, their empirical variance obeys
\[
\operatorname{Var}_S(r)\ge\frac{s^2-1}{m^2-1}. \tag{2}
\]
Indeed, if the selected indices are \(i_1<\cdots<i_s\), then \(i_b-i_a\ge b-a\). Use
\(\operatorname{Var}(r)=s^{-2}\sum_{a<b}(r_{i_b}-r_{i_a})^2\)
and the sum of squared differences of \(s\) consecutive integers. This shows that consecutive grid points minimize the variance.

Writing \(\alpha=s/m\) and \(\mathbb E_S\) for the selected empirical average,
\[
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{dist}(w_S,\operatorname{span}v_S)^2
&=\alpha-\frac{(w_S\cdot v_S)^2}{\|v_S\|^2}\\
&=\alpha\frac{\operatorname{Var}_S(r)}
                    {\mathbb E_S r^2}\\
&\ge \frac{s}{m}\frac{s^2-1}{3(m^2-1)}. \tag{3}
\end{aligned}
\]
Here \(\mathbb E_S r^2<3\). For \(s\ge2\), the last expression is at least \(\alpha^3/6\). Thus, when \(m\ge6/c\), the condition \(\|v_S\|^2\ge c\) gives
\[
\operatorname{dist}(w_S,\operatorname{span}v_S)
\ge \frac{c^{3/2}}{9\sqrt2}. \tag{4}
\]
The hub column of \((A_0)_{ST}\) is precisely \(w_S\). Hence an exact rank-one square \(v_Sv_S^\mathsf T\) with coefficient \(\|v_S\|^2\ge c\) cannot have an aligned crossing block, even approximately below the threshold (4).

## Uniform approximate statement for all banks

Fix \(0<c\le1\) and \(m\ge12/c\). There is **no** original-coordinate bank \(S\), unit \(u\), scalar \(|\lambda|\ge c\), vector \(z\), and error
\[
\eta<\frac{c^{5/2}}{256} \tag{5}
\]
satisfying both inequalities (1).

For light-only \(S\), a negative \(\lambda\) is impossible under \(\eta<c\) because \((A_0)_{SS}=v_Sv_S^\mathsf T\) is positive semidefinite. A positive \(\lambda\ge c\) and (1) imply \(\|v_S\|^2\ge c-\eta\ge c/2\), so \(s\ge cm/6\ge2\). Equation (3) now gives
\[
d:=\operatorname{dist}(w_S,\operatorname{span}v_S)
\ge c^{3/2}/36. \tag{6}
\]
Apply \(I-uu^\mathsf T\) to the first inequality (1). The angle \(\vartheta\) between \(u\) and \(v_S\) satisfies
\[
\sin\vartheta\le\eta/\|v_S\|^2\le2\eta/c.
\]
The hub column in the second inequality (1) therefore forces
\[
\eta\ge \operatorname{dist}(w_S,\operatorname{span}u)
\ge d-2\eta/c.
\]
Since \(1+2/c\le3/c\), this implies
\(\eta\ge cd/3\ge c^{5/2}/108\), contradicting (5).

Now let \(S\) contain \(h\) and \(s\ge1\) light coordinates. Put \(a=\|w_{S\setminus\{h\}}\|=\sqrt{s/m}\). Its principal matrix is a rank-two hub star of norm \(a\), plus \(v_Sv_S^\mathsf T\) of norm at most \(3a^2\), so
\[
\|(A_0)_{SS}\|\le a+3a^2\le4a.
\]
The first inequality (1) and \(|\lambda|\ge c\), \(\eta<c/2\), require \(a\ge c/8\). Compress \((A_0)_{SS}\) to the plane generated by \(e_h\) and the normalized restricted \(w\). This compression is
\[
\begin{pmatrix}0&a\\a&b\end{pmatrix},
\qquad 0\le b\le1.
\]
Its positive eigenvalue is at least \(a\), and its negative eigenvalue has magnitude at least \(a^2/(1+a)\ge a^2/2\). By minmax, \((A_0)_{SS}\) has both a positive and a negative eigenvalue of magnitudes at least \(a^2/2\). Its distance in operator norm from every rank-one matrix is therefore at least
\[
s_2((A_0)_{SS})\ge a^2/2\ge c^2/128,
\]
which exceeds (5). If \(S=\{h\}\), its principal matrix is zero and cannot approximate \(|\lambda|\ge c\) with \(\eta<c\).

The conclusion also persists for
\[
A_\varepsilon=A_0+\varepsilon(wp^\mathsf T+pw^\mathsf T)
\]
with \(p\perp w,v\), \(|p|=1\): the perturbation has full operator norm \(|\varepsilon|\), so any approximate carrier of error \(\eta\) for \(A_\varepsilon\) would give error \(\eta+|\varepsilon|\) for \(A_0\). In particular, choosing \(\varepsilon=m^{-3}\) and \(m\) sufficiently large leaves no carrier with \(\eta<c^{5/2}/512\).

## Scope

This blocks the proposed *matrix structural implication* that \(s_3(A)\ge1\) forces either a fixed-strength separated square bank or an arbitrarily accurate one. It does **not** disprove bounded output density.
