# Concrete target matrix

Take the symmetric five-coordinate matrix with nonzero entries A13=A31=1, A24=A42=1, and A33=A44=A55=1.

Its eigenvalues are two copies of (1+sqrt(5))/2, two copies of (1-sqrt(5))/2, and 1. Thus exactly three eigenvalues have modulus at least one.

The shared-bank theorem uses sources X1,X2 and shared bank (X3,X4), giving density cap 8 sqrt(2). The account04/B18 theorem uses the literal bank (X3,X4), whose quadratic block is the identity, and gives the sharper density cap 2 pi. Continuity follows from the shared-bank Fourier-L1 proof.
