# Constant calculation

Let D denote the square root of the Gram determinant of the two slope rows. Combining the one- and two-dimensional projection bounds with the mixed half-moment lemma yields the reciprocal half-moment bound 16 times sqrt(2) times K divided by sqrt(D).

The Fourier estimate then has L1 norm at most 32 times sqrt(2) times pi times K squared divided by sqrt(D). Fourier inversion divides this by 2 pi. Therefore the density bound is 16 times sqrt(2) times K squared divided by sqrt(D). Integrability also proves continuity and vanishing at infinity.
