# Barrier construction

Let 0<eps<=1/4 and let U,H,S,T1,T2 be independent Uniform[-1/2,1/2]. Define

Q = eps U^2 + 2 U T1 + eps^(-1) T1^2
    - eps H^2 + 2 H(T2+S) - eps^(-1)(T2+S)^2.

Then

Q = eps(U+T1/eps)^2 - eps(H-(T2+S)/eps)^2.

Choose shared anchor H, star coordinate S, other source U, and columns T1,T2. The physical coefficient A_SH is 1 and the 2 by 2 crossing determinant from rows U,H to columns T1,T2 is also 1. Thus the local mixed star-minor product equals one. The source quadratic block on U,H is diag(eps,-eps).
