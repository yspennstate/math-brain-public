# Partner application

Source: account04/B16 commit 7f2bb2d330022b09a6af6e39ab09d054caeaa346.

Take Q=-4 R^2+6 R Z1+5 Z1^2+3 Z2^2, where R is a literal original coordinate and (Z1,Z2) is an orthonormal 2D projection of a disjoint product bank. Conditioning on R leaves the definite plane diag(5,3). The partner theorem gives density cap 2*pi/sqrt(15), approximately 1.62231147038944.

The effective 3 by 3 matrix has characteristic polynomial (lambda-3)(lambda^2-lambda-29). Its eigenvalues are 3 and (1 plus or minus 3 sqrt(13))/2, so all three have absolute value greater than one. This is therefore an actual strict-target inequality.
