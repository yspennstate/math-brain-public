# r59 scope note

This note is part of the canonical r59 bundle and narrows one live-application sentence in `REPORT.md`.

`QFORM-FIXEDSTAR-PAIRWEIGHT-TRICHOTOMY-v1` is proved exactly for a **three-dimensional Parseval carrier** `U in R^{n x 3}`. It should therefore be applied directly to the r58 fixed-star branch only when the relevant low-rank carrier has rank three (or when a genuine three-dimensional carrier is otherwise present). One must **not** append artificial zero-curvature columns to a rank-one or rank-two carrier and then treat the resulting leverage/pair geometry as curvature supplied by the original quadratic form.

For lower carrier rank, the algebraic proof has parameterized variants, but the strong alternative `excluded leverage trace > 1 => at least two genuine spectral dimensions` is specifically a rank-three conclusion at the threshold `tr G_L<2`. No rank-one/rank-two completion is claimed in r59.

Accordingly, Section 5 of `REPORT.md` should be read as a compiler for the **rank-three subbranch** of the r58 fixed-star obstruction. The rank-<=2 fixed-star endpoint remains part of the next debt unless an already-proved lower-rank receiver applies independently.

All displayed inequalities inside Theorem 2.1 and the partner-scaled consequence (4.5)--(4.9) are unchanged in their stated three-dimensional scope.
