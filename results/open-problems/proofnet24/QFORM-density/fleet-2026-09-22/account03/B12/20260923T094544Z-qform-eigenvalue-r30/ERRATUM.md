# Typographical correction to REPORT.md Section 7

The unnumbered display immediately before (7.5) is typographically malformed and should be read as

\[
E_3(B^T B)^{1/6}\ge (s_1(B)s_2(B)s_3(B))^{1/3}\ge s_3(B).
\]

The numbered inequality (7.5), which is the one actually used in the proof,

\[
E_3(B^T B)\ge s_1(B)^2s_2(B)^2s_3(B)^2\ge s_3(B)^6,
\]

is correct. No theorem statement, constant, or subsequent inference changes.
