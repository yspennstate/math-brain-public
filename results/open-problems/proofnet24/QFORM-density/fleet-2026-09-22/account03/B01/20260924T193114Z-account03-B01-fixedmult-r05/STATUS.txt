PHYSICAL FIVE-COORDINATE CHAMBER

Take five distinct original coordinates U=(U1,U2), V=(V1,V2), H, mutually independent with one-dimensional densities at most one. After conditioning on all remaining original coordinates, write

Q = U^T C U + 2 U^T(BV+cH+beta)
    + V^T D V + 2H r^T V + a H^2
    + affine terms,

where C,D are symmetric 2 by 2. Assume B is invertible and define
d=B^{-1}c, w=r-Dd,
lambda=a-2 r^T d+d^T D d,
M=|det B|^{-1}.
The bordered ratio is D0=a-r^T d and satisfies
D0=lambda+w^T d.
