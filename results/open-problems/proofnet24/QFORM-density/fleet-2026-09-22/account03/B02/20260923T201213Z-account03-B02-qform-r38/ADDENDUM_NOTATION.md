# Notation clarification for REPORT.md equation (3.8)

The congruence in the real-rootedness proof is

`det(P^2+tG)=det(P^2) det(I+t (P^2)^(-1/2) G (P^2)^(-1/2))`

when `P` is invertible. Here `(P^2)^(-1/2)` is the positive-definite inverse square root of the positive-definite matrix `P^2`.

The prose immediately after (3.8) in `REPORT.md` states this intended meaning, but the compact inline symbol there is typographically ambiguous. This addendum supplies the unambiguous formula. No mathematical conclusion or constant changes.
