All symbolic identities below were checked exactly with SymPy; the REPORT proofs are algebraic and do not rely on numerical sampling.

Notation:
B=[[a,c],[c,d]], P=[[alpha,beta],[r,s]], C=P B^{-1} P^T + lambda*m*e1e1^T,
z=B^{-1}(r,s)^T, p=(alpha,r)^T, q=(beta,s)^T,
u=(-r,alpha)^T, v=(-s,beta)^T, U=u^T C v.

1. Mixed Y/Z crossing determinant:
M_YZ=[[C,q],[p^T,c]].
Exact simplification:
  det(M_YZ) = c*det(C)-U
              = lambda*m*(c*r-a*s)*(d*r-c*s)/(a*d-c^2)
              = -lambda*m*det(B)*z1*z2.
Residual after adding lambda*m*det(B)*z1*z2: 0.

H_Z=[[C,q],[q^T,d]].
Exact simplification:
  det(H_Z)=lambda*m*det(B)*z1^2.
Residual: 0.

3. Y-bank split crossing determinant:
H_Y=[[C,p],[p^T,a]].
Exact simplification:
  det(H_Y)=lambda*m*det(B)*z2^2.
Residual: 0.

4. Pluecker vertex identity for a Parseval two-frame Gamma:
  d_i=sum_j det(g_i,g_j)^2
     = ||g_i||^2 tr(Gamma^T Gamma)-g_i^T(Gamma^T Gamma)g_i
     = ||g_i||^2.
Hence sum_i d_i^2 <= 2 q_X and random fair partition gives
E[det(G_I)det(G_J)] >= (1-2q_X)/16.

5. Scalar-bank split:
For a_j=zeta_j^2, sum a_j=1, q=max a_j, fair Bernoulli split t=sum a_j xi_j:
  E[t(1-t)] = (1-sum_j a_j^2)/4 >= (1-q)/4.
Thus ||zeta_K|| ||zeta_L|| >= sqrt(1-q)/2 for some split.

A=(2*pi)^(3/2)*Delta^(-1/2), t0=A^(2/3)=2*pi*Delta^(-1/3).
Integral_R min(1,A|t|^(-3/2)) dt = 12*pi*Delta^(-1/3).
After 1/(2*pi) inversion: density cap = 6*Delta^(-1/3).
