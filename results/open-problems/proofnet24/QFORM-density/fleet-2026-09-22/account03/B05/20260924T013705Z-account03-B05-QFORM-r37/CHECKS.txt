1. Exact root identities for d != 0.
For q=a*x^2+2*c*x*y+d*y^2 and D=c^2-a*d, define
s=sqrt(D*x^2+d*t), y_+=( -c*x+s)/d, y_-=( -c*x-s)/d.
Symbolic differentiation returned exact zero residuals for
  d^2/dx^2 y_+ - D*t/s^3 = 0,
  d^2/dx^2 y_- + D*t/s^3 = 0,
and for
  partial_y q(x,y_+) - 2*s = 0,
  partial_y q(x,y_-) + 2*s = 0.
These are algebraic identities, not numerical evidence.

2. Dyadic constant.
(16*sqrt(2))^(2/3)=8 exactly, since 16*sqrt(2)=2^(9/2).
The dyadic geometric sums are
  sum_{k>=0}2^{-k}=2,
  sum_{j>=1}2^{-j/2}=1/(sqrt(2)-1)=1+sqrt(2).
Hence
  C_H=8(3+sqrt(2))=35.31370849898476... <35.314.

3. Ratio-packet constant.
With scalar density caps <=sqrt(2), the cap factor is 2^(2/3).
With H=10/sqrt(|lambda|m), the one-third Riesz factor is 3*10^(2/3).
Thus
  C_RP=C_H*3*20^(2/3)
      =780.5808866643874... <781.
For m>=3/20,
  C_RP*(20/3)^(1/3)
      =1469.1094756141745... <1470.
