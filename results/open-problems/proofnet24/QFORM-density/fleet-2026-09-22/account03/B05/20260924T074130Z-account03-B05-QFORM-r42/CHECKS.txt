1. Symbolic curvature cancellation.
Represent the rotated residual matrix as
C = alpha*h*h^T + beta*(q*h^T+h*q^T) + gamma*(h*z^T+z*h^T) + delta*z*z^T,
where h,z,q are an orthonormal frame (q=+/- h cross z).
A symbolic expansion gives
h1^2*C22 - 2*h1*h2*C12 + h2^2*C11 = delta*(-h1*z2+h2*z1)^2,
h2^2*C33 - 2*h2*h3*C23 + h3^2*C22 = delta*(-h2*z3+h3*z2)^2.
Thus alpha, beta=a*kappa, and gamma cancel exactly.
