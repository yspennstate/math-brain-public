## 3. The family

Let `m=2^k>=256`, `N=m-2`, let `U=H_m/sqrt(m)` be a normalized Sylvester Hadamard matrix, and set

`delta=N^(-1/2)`,

`M_N = U diag(1,1,delta,...,delta) U^T`  (there are `N` copies of `delta`).

Define the `2m x 2m` physical matrix

`A_N = [[0,M_N],[M_N,0]]`.

It is genuinely rotated in the original coordinates unless `delta=1`. Its spectrum is exactly

`{+1,+1,-1,-1}` together with `N` copies each of `+delta` and `-delta`.

Hence `A_N` is indefinite and satisfies the exact target with four eigenvalues of modulus one. Every physical scalar diagonal is zero.

Using the first two Hadamard columns, write `P=u_1u_1^T+u_2u_2^T`. Then

`M_N = delta I +(1-delta)P`,

`P_ii=2/m`, `P_ij in {0,2/m}` for `i!=j` (after the standard Sylvester sign choice). Therefore

`max_ij |(M_N)_ij| = c_N := delta + 2(1-delta)/m = N^(-1/2)+O(N^(-1))`.

Every nonzero physical two-coordinate determinant of `A_N` is `-(M_N)_{ij}^2`; consequently

`max_{|S|=2} |det A_N[S,S]| = c_N^2 -> 0`.

This is an exact no-scalar-pivot / vanishing-pair-determinant target family.
