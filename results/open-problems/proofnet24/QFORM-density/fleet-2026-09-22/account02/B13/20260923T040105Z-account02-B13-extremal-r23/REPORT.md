## 4. Exact exterior algebra: pair volume cannot disappear under a scalar Schur correction for free

The remaining difficulty in applying (3.3) is that scalar Schur descent changes a physical cross block by a rank-one matrix, and small coefficient error is not a small random perturbation on unbounded supports. Here there is an exact algebraic replacement.

For a real `m x n` matrix `T`, write

`E_2(T)=sum_{|P|=|T_0|=2} det(T[P,T_0])^2=||wedge^2 T||_F^2`.    (4.1)

### Theorem 4.1 — `QFORM-RANKONE-SCHUR-E2-LOSS-IDENTITY-v1`

For every real `S` and vectors `x,y`, put

`N=S+x y^T`.                                                     (4.2)

Then

`boxed: ||wedge^2 N-wedge^2 S||_F^2`
`       =||x||^2||y||^2 ||P_{x^perp} S P_{y^perp}||_F^2.}`      (4.3)

Consequently

`boxed: sqrt(E_2(N)) <= sqrt(E_2(S))`
`       +||x||||y|| ||P_{x^perp}SP_{y^perp}||_F.`               (4.4)

In particular, if `E_2(S)<=theta E_2(N)` with `0<=theta<1`, then

`boxed: ||x||^2||y||^2||P_{x^perp}SP_{y^perp}||_F^2`
`       >=(1-sqrt(theta))^2 E_2(N).`                             (4.5)

#### Proof

Orthogonal row and column changes preserve both sides of (4.3). Rotate so that

`x=||x||e_1`, `y=||y||e_1`.                                    (4.6)

A `2x2` minor of `N` differs from the corresponding minor of `S` only if its selected row pair and selected column pair both contain index `1`. For a remaining row `k>1` and column `l>1`, the determinant difference is exactly

`||x||||y|| S_{kl}`.                                            (4.7)

Summing its square over `k,l` gives (4.3), because the lower-right block in these coordinates is precisely `P_{x^perp}SP_{y^perp}`. The triangle inequality in exterior Hilbert space gives (4.4); rearranging it gives (4.5). QED.

Thus a large pre-Schur pair volume cannot simply be declared lost after pivoting. Either substantial `E_2` survives in the Schur cross block, where Section 3 applies, or the loss itself forces a transverse-star Frobenius resource.
