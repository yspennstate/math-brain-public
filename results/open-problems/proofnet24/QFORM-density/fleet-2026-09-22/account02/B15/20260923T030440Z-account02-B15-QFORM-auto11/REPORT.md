`boxed{

### Proof

`|phi_F(t)-phi_G(t)| <= 2K |t|^{-b}`.                            (3.3)

`|phi_F(t)-phi_G(t)| <= min(Delta,2K |t|^{-b}).`                 (3.4)

For `Delta>0`, put

`T=(2K/Delta)^{1/b}`.

Then direct integration yields

`int_R |phi_F-phi_G| dt`
` <= 2[Delta T + (2K)/(b-1) T^{1-b}]`
` = 2 b/(b-1) (2K)^{1/b} Delta^{1-1/b}.`                        (3.5)
