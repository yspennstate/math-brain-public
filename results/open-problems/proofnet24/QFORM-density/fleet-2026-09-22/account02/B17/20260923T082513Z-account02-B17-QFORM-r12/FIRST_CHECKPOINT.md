Let `P_m` be the `3m x 3` block-average frame: in bank `j` its `m` rows are `e_j/sqrt(m)`. Thus `P_m^T P_m=I_3`. Let `B=B^T` be any `3 x 3` matrix with `s_3(B)>=1`, and set `A_m=P_m B P_m^T`; its three nonzero eigenvalues are exactly those of `B`.

For every physical coordinate let

`X_i = U_i + Z_i`,

where `U_i ~ Unif[-1/2,1/2]`, the `Z_i` are arbitrary independent real random variables, and all `U_i,Z_i` are mutually independent. Then every `X_i` has density

`f_i(x)=P(Z_i in [x-1/2,x+1/2])<=1`,

with no support or moment assumption on `Z_i`.

Condition on all translations `Z`. Writing `z=P_m^T Z`, the conditional form is

`Q_m | Z = (S_m+z)^T B (S_m+z)`,

where the three coordinates of `S_m` are independent normalized sums of `m` centered unit intervals.

The completed Fresnel--Brascamp--Lieb Theorem F from
`math-open-problems@e5f71d79eff325201dd0274053d20268ce65b837`, `proofnet24/QFORM-density/interactive/20260922T211500Z-decoupling-brascamp-lieb/RESULTS.md`, applies with `I` equal to all `3m` coordinates: after diagonalising `B`, every physical row leverage is exactly `1/m`, `G_I=I_3`, and the linear term generated by `z` is allowed. Hence for every `m>=2`, every translation vector `z`, and every real `t`,

`|phi_{m,z}(t)| <= min(1,(4*pi)^(3/2) |det B|^(-1/2) |t|^(-3/2))`,

and in particular `||p_{m,z}||_infinity <= 12 |det B|^(-1/3) <= 12`.
