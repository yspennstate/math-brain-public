### Theorem 2.1 — `QFORM-INDEFINITE-PAIR-PLUS-RANK2-SCHUR-v1`

Let `P={p,q}` be two ORIGINAL coordinates. Write the corresponding physical principal block as an invertible indefinite symmetric matrix `B=A_PP`, and put

`M=S[I,J]`

has exact rank two and nonzero singular values `sigma_1,sigma_2`. Define

`Delta=sigma_1 sigma_2`.

Then for arbitrary independent cap-one input laws, arbitrary coefficients on every other block, and all affine terms generated by conditioning,

`boxed: ||p_(X^T A X)||_infinity <= 16 Delta^(-1/8) (D kappa)^(-1/4).`        (2.1)

Essential boundedness is claimed; continuity is not needed for the full target and is not asserted here.

#### Proof

Condition on the outside bank `W`. Completing the fixed physical pair `P` changes only the remainder quadratic form by the exact Schur complement and produces affine terms. The conditional Schur remainder on the still-random banks `I,J` therefore has the form covered by the bilateral projected-Plancherel theorem: arbitrary same-bank curvature and affine leakage, with cross block exactly `M`.

Hence, uniformly in the conditioned value and uniformly in the affine terms,

`H_(1/2)(R) <= 8 Delta^(-1/4)`.                                      (2.2)

Markov's inequality against the fractional kernel gives, for every `s` and `rho>0`,

`P(|R-s|<=rho) <= rho^(1/2) E|R-s|^(-1/2)`
`                 <= 8 Delta^(-1/4) sqrt(rho)`.                         (2.3)

Thus the pair-log theorem applies with

`H=8 Delta^(-1/4)`.

Its density estimate gives

`||p_Q||_infinity`
` <=4 sqrt(2H)(D kappa)^(-1/4)`
` =4 sqrt(16 Delta^(-1/4))(D kappa)^(-1/4)`
` =16 Delta^(-1/8)(D kappa)^(-1/4)`,

proving (2.1). The bound is uniform in the conditioned bank `W`, so averaging conditional laws preserves it. QED.
