# Addendum: exact coloop classification for the two-hub common-plane endpoint

This proves the exact-coloop sentence in `REPORT.md` rather than leaving it as an informal structural remark.

Use the notation of Theorem 3.1 there. Normalize the two-row cross frame by replacing the columns `m_i` with

`v_i=G^{-1/2}m_i`, `G=MM^T`,

so that

`sum_i v_i v_i^T=I_2`, `ell_i=|v_i|^2`.

This invertible change of the two coefficient rows preserves every relevant row-space condition `r_i=0`.

## One exact coloop

Assume `ell_c=1` and there is no second exact coloop. Rotate the normalized two-plane so that

`v_c=e_1`.

The Parseval identity then forces

`v_j=b_j e_2` for every `j!=c`, `sum_{j!=c} b_j^2=1`.

Because there is no second coloop, after deleting any `j!=c` the remaining columns still have rank two. The condition `r_j=0` therefore says that the off-diagonal row of `D` at `j`, restricted away from `j`, lies in the two-dimensional row span generated by the coordinate vector at `c` and the vector `b=(b_k)_{k!=c}`. Hence for each `j!=c` there are scalars `alpha_j,gamma_j` such that

`D_{jc}=alpha_j`,
`D_{jk}=gamma_j b_k` for `k!=c,j`.

Symmetry gives, for distinct `j,k!=c`,

`gamma_j b_k=gamma_k b_j`.

Choose any index with `b_k!=0`. It follows that `gamma_j=kappa b_j` for every `j!=c` (and `gamma_j=0` whenever `b_j=0`) for one scalar `kappa`. Consequently the off-diagonal light block is

`offdiag(D)=offdiag(kappa b b^T + e_c q^T+q e_c^T)`

for a vector `q` supported away from `c`. Thus

`boxed: D=Delta + kappa b b^T + e_c q^T+q e_c^T}`

for a diagonal `Delta`. The first non-diagonal term is a rank-one piece inside the original two-row cross plane; the only extra freedom is one literal physical star centred at the coloop coordinate `c`.

## Two exact coloops

If `ell_c=ell_d=1`, the Parseval identity forces, after a two-plane rotation,

`v_c=e_1`, `v_d=e_2`, `v_j=0` for every `j notin {c,d}`.

For every `j notin {c,d}`, deleting `j` leaves the two coloop columns and hence rank two. The condition `r_j=0` says the off-diagonal row `D_{j,-j}` is supported only on `{c,d}`. By symmetry there are no off-diagonal entries between two coordinates outside `{c,d}`. Therefore

`boxed: D=Delta + e_c q_c^T+q_c e_c^T + e_d q_d^T+q_d e_d^T + K_{cd}}`

where `Delta` is diagonal, `q_c,q_d` are supported off the corresponding centre (their values at the other centre may be absorbed into `K_{cd}`), and `K_{cd}` is supported on the physical `2x2` coloop block. Thus the exceptional self-curvature is a two-anchor finite star packet.

There cannot be three exact coloops because `sum_i ell_i=2`.

Hence the exact zero-height endpoint of the leaf-transverse compiler has the complete deterministic trichotomy:

1. no coloop: `D=Delta+M^T K M`, `K=K^T`;
2. one coloop: diagonal + same-plane rank one + one physical star;
3. two coloops: diagonal + two physical stars + their `2x2` block.

No probabilistic or post-rotation independence assertion is used here; this is coefficient geometry only.
