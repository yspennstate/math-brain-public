Matrix normal form:
B = [[a,beta,0],[beta,alpha,delta],[0,delta,gamma]].
Assume sigma_min(B)>=1, hence ||B^{-1}||op<=1 and |det B|>=1.
Also ||B e1||^2=a^2+beta^2>=1.

Small-a certificate for 0<|a|<=1/16, |gamma|<1/2:
x=B^{-1}e3, ||x||<=1.
a x1+beta x2=0 => |x2|<=|a|/|beta|.
delta x2+gamma x3=1 => |a delta|/|beta| >=1-|gamma|>1/2.
(B^{-1})31=beta delta/det B => |det B|>=|beta delta|.
Thus |a det B|>|a beta delta|>beta^2/2>=255/512.

Density-cap normalization for scalar S with ||p_S||inf=L:
S'=L S has density cap <=1.
Under B'=diag(1/L,1,1) B diag(1/L,1,1),
beta'=beta/L and det B'=det B/L^2.
Therefore |beta'^2 gamma det B'|^{-1/6}
= L^(2/3)|beta^2 gamma det B|^{-1/6}.
