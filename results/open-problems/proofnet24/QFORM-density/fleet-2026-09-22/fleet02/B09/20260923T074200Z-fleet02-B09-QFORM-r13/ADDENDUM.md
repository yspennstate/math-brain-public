# Addendum — scope sentence in REPORT introduction

The introduction's sentence saying the exact zero-disjoint-volume endpoint is paid "except for the explicitly aligned/diagonal star endpoint" should be read with the full hypotheses stated later in Sections 5--6.

Precisely: when `Omega_5(B)=0`, the exact completion theorem reduces the unconditioned remainder to `D+C` with `rank(C)<=4` after conditioning at most eight literal leaf coordinates. The one-hub theorem then pays this branch only when `rank(C)=r>=1`, the augmented frame `[range(C),c_L]` has full column rank, and its determinant-weighted disjoint-basis mass `rho` is positive. Thus `r=0`, `c_L in range(C)`, **or `rho=0`** remains a localized/aligned survivor requiring a genuine external third-direction payment. No claim is made that `rho>0` follows from transversality alone.

No displayed theorem or constant in REPORT.md changes.
