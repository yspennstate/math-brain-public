In Section 1.1 the sharper partner Case B condition was written as

`V_2(A)<rho^-4<=V_1(A)`.

The source-correct statement is

`boxed: V_2(A)<rho^-4  and  V_1(A)>=rho^-2.`

Under that condition the partner supplies `rank R<=4` and `||E||_F<6/rho`.

The chained shorthand in the report mixed quantities of different homogeneities and should not be read literally. Sections 5 and onward use only the resulting `rank R<=4`, `||E||_F<6/rho` conclusion, so no displayed pair-count or separation inequality changes.
