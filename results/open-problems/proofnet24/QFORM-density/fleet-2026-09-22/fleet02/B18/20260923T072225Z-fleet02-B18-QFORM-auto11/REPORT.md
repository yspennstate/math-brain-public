## 1. Exact target and first unpaid implication

For every dimension, independent real coordinates with probability densities bounded by one, and every real symmetric matrix with at least three eigenvalues of absolute value at least one, the full target asks for a universal essential-density bound for `X^T A X`, or a valid counterexample. No moment, support, log-concavity, identical-law, diagonal-basis, or post-rotation-independence assumption is available. Continuity is a separate conclusion.

The previous B18 run reduced an explicit non-source-separated Schur-isotropic stress family to its minority-sign/two-sheet side. The newest completed partner result fleet03/B18 r25 proved a zero-pivot estimate for an **original-coordinate two-plane bank** with Pluecker currency

\[
\Omega_J=\sum_{i<j\in J}\det(n,v_i,v_j)^2.
\]

The first unpaid implication attacked here is whether a fixed positive `Omega_J` can pay the *whole shear parameter* `r`, rather than only the large-shear endpoint, without inventing independence after spectral rotation.

### 2.3 Primary literature theorem underlying the bank transfer

The partner proof uses Livshyts--Paouris--Pivovarov, *On sharp bounds for marginal densities of product measures*, Israel J. Math. 216 (2016), Theorem 1.1; arXiv:1507.07949v2. I checked the primary statement directly. For a product density `f=prod f_i`, each factor a nonnegative bounded probability density, every `k`-dimensional orthogonal marginal satisfies

\[
\|\pi_E f\|_\infty
\le \min\left\{\left(\frac n{n-k}\right)^{(n-k)/2},\,2^{k/2}\right\}
\prod_i\|f_i\|_\infty^{\gamma_i},
\qquad \sum_i\gamma_i=k.
\]

Hence cap-one factors give the exact `k=2` cap `<=2`. No moments, compact support, log-concavity, iid, symmetry, or smoothness are assumed. For a full-rank nonorthonormal two-dimensional linear image with coefficient Gram matrix `G`, ordinary change of variables gives cap `<=2/sqrt(det G)`. This does **not** assert independence between the two projected coordinates.

## 3. New theorem: `QFORM-PLANAR-BANK-SHEAR-COMPILER-v1`

Assume the canonical packet above, and in addition:

1. the complete physical matrix has exactly three nonzero eigenvalues, each of modulus at least `s>0`;
2. the residual Parseval frame has
   \[
   \delta:=\max_j\|v_j\|_2^2\le\frac12;
   \]
3. some residual original-coordinate bank `J` spans exactly a two-plane and has `Omega_J>=omega>0`.

Then the complete quadratic form has a bounded continuous density and

\[
\boxed{
\|p_Q\|_\infty
\le \frac{12}{s}\left(1+\frac1{2\omega}\right)^{1/3}.
} \tag{3.1}
\]

### Proof

Condition only on the genuine original scalar `X_0`. The residual variables remain independent with the same cap-one laws. Diagonalize the **three-dimensional coefficient matrix** `D=O\Lambda O^T` and write `R=VO`. This rotates only the deterministic three-column spectral factor; it does not rotate the random input vector. Since `R^TR=I_3` and row norms are unchanged,

\[
\max_j\|R_{j,*}\|^2=\delta\le\frac12.
\]

The conditioned form has quadratic part `R Lambda R^T` and an arbitrary linear term from the fixed value of `X_0`. Theorem F therefore gives, uniformly in the conditioned scalar,

\[
\|p_{Q\mid X_0}\|_\infty\le12|\det D|^{-1/3}. \tag{3.2}
\]

The same characteristic-function envelope is uniform in the linear term, so averaging over `X_0` preserves the bound and supplies continuity. By (2.2), with `x=r^2`,

\[
A(x):=\frac{12}{s}(1+x)^{1/3} \tag{3.3}
\]
is a valid bound.

Independently, (2.1), `Omega_J>=omega`, and (2.2) give

\[
B(x):=\frac{6\,2^{2/3}}{s\,\omega^{1/3}}(1+x^{-1})^{1/3}. \tag{3.4}
\]

`A(x)` is increasing and `B(x)` is decreasing on `(0,infinity)`. Their cubes are

\[
A(x)^3=1728s^{-3}(1+x),
\]

\[
B(x)^3=864s^{-3}\omega^{-1}(1+x^{-1}).
\]

After cancelling `1+x`, equality occurs exactly at

\[
x=\frac1{2\omega}. \tag{3.5}
\]

Therefore

\[
\sup_{x>0}\min\{A(x),B(x)\}
=A((2\omega)^{-1})
=\frac{12}{s}\left(1+\frac1{2\omega}\right)^{1/3},
\]
which is (3.1). QED.

### Scope

The theorem is a genuine whole-line statement and uses the partner theorem at an inequality. It does not infer any new independent coordinates after a rotation. It is not full QFORM because neither `delta<=1/2` nor a dimension-free exact two-plane currency `omega` follows from the bare three-large-eigenvalue hypothesis.

## 4. Application: the prior mixed-source Hadamard stress family is fully paid

Let `m>=8` be a Walsh--Hadamard order. Choose three orthonormal Hadamard columns consisting of the constant column and two independent Walsh characters. The residual rows are

\[
v_j=m^{-1/2}(1,a_j,b_j),\qquad a_j,b_j\in\{\pm1\},
\]
with all four sign types occurring `m/4` times. Let `n=e_1`, `r>0`, `s_r=\sqrt{1+r^2}`, and

\[
D_r=
\begin{pmatrix}
0&s_r^{-1}&0\\
s_r^{-1}&0&0\\
0&0&1
\end{pmatrix}. \tag{4.1}
\]

Writing `T=[r e_1,V^T]`, the physical matrix is `A=T^TD_rT`. Since

\[
TT^T=I_3+r^2e_1e_1^T=\operatorname{diag}(1+r^2,1,1),
\]
the three nonzero eigenvalues of `A` equal those of

\[
D_rTT^T=
\begin{pmatrix}
0&s_r^{-1}&0\\
s_r&0&0\\
0&0&1
\end{pmatrix},
\]
namely

\[
\{-1,1,1\}. \tag{4.2}
\]

Thus this is a genuine target family for every `m,r`.

Every residual leverage is `3/m<=1/2`. Take `J` to consist of the two row types

\[
(1,1,1)/\sqrt m,\qquad (1,1,-1)/\sqrt m.
\]

They span `E={(x,x,z)}`. A unit normal is `(1,-1,0)/sqrt2`, so its squared overlap with `n=e_1` is `1/2`. The bank Gram determinant on `E` is also `1/2`, hence

\[
\Omega_J=\frac14. \tag{4.3}
\]

Equivalently, only cross-type pairs contribute to Cauchy--Binet; there are `(m/4)^2` of them, each with squared determinant `4/m^2`.

Putting `s=1`, `omega=1/4` into (3.1) gives the whole-line bound

\[
\boxed{
\|p_{Q_{m,r}}\|_\infty
\le12\,3^{1/3}
=17.3069948436889\ldots
\qquad(m\ge8,\ r>0).
} \tag{4.4}
\]

This includes the minority-sign/two-sheet side that the previous B18 one-sheet result did not pay. The exact source-separated condition still fails row-by-row; closure comes from an original-coordinate tilted plane bank, not from renaming the spectral coordinates as independent.

## 5. New construction barrier: `QFORM-CIRCULAR-FRAME-PLANAR-BANK-BARRIER-v1`

The previous application might suggest trying to prove that every diffuse N3 packet contains a dimension-free exact two-plane bank. That structural implication is false even inside a very explicit target-valid family.

For integer `m>=5`, put `theta_j=2pi j/m` and define the Parseval frame

\[
v_j=\left(
\frac1{\sqrt m},
\sqrt{\frac2m}\cos\theta_j,
\sqrt{\frac2m}\sin\theta_j
\right),\qquad 0\le j<m. \tag{5.1}
\]

The elementary root-of-unity identities give

\[
V^TV=I_3,\qquad \|v_j\|^2=\frac3m. \tag{5.2}
\]

Use the same `n=e_1` and `D_r` from (4.1). The physical matrix again has nonzero spectrum exactly `{-1,1,1}` for every `m,r`, by the same `D_r(I+r^2e_1e_1^T)` calculation.

### 5.1 Every exact two-plane bank has vanishing currency

After harmless column rescaling, the rows in (5.1) correspond to the planar points

\[
(1,\cos\theta_j,\sin\theta_j).
\]

Any three distinct such rows are linearly independent: determinant zero would make three distinct points of the unit circle collinear, impossible because a line meets a circle in at most two points. Hence any subset of rows spanning exactly a two-plane has cardinality at most two.

For a two-row bank `{i,j}`,

\[
\Omega_{\{i,j\}}
=\det(e_1,v_i,v_j)^2
=\frac4{m^2}\sin^2(\theta_j-\theta_i)
\le\frac4{m^2}. \tag{5.3}
\]

Thus

\[
\boxed{
\sup_{J:\,\dim\operatorname{span}\{v_j:j\in J\}=2}\Omega_J
\le\frac4{m^2}\to0.
} \tag{5.4}
\]

So a dimension-free exact-plane extraction cannot be a consequence merely of diffuse leverage or of the target spectrum.

### 5.2 The family enters every fixed N3 no-pivot window

Let `A=T^TD_rT` as above. Since `||D_r||op=1` and `||v_j||^2=3/m`, for residual indices

\[
|A_{ij}|\le\frac3m,
\qquad |A_{ii}|\le\frac3m. \tag{5.5}
\]

The hub satisfies `A_{00}=0`, and

\[
A_{0j}=\frac r{\sqrt{1+r^2}}\sqrt{\frac2m}\cos\theta_j,
\qquad |\det A[\{0,j\}]|=A_{0j}^2\le\frac2m. \tag{5.6}
\]

For two residual coordinates,

\[
|\det A[\{i,j\}]|
\le |A_{ii}A_{jj}|+|A_{ij}|^2
\le\frac{18}{m^2}. \tag{5.7}
\]

Therefore, for every fixed `tau in (0,1]`, all sufficiently large `m` (for example `m>=18/tau`) satisfy

\[
|A_{ii}|<\tau,
\qquad |\det A[\{i,j\}]|<\tau
\]
for every physical coordinate and physical pair. The family is thus genuinely inside the no-pivot N3 regime while (5.4) kills the exact-two-plane extraction mechanism.

This is **not** a QFORM counterexample. It is a counterexample only to the proposed structural shortcut “N3/diffuse implies a dimension-free exact two-plane bank”. In fact Theorem F still gives

\[
\|p_Q\|_\infty\le12(1+r^2)^{1/3}
\]
after conditioning the hub, but that grows with the shear `r` and therefore does not close the family uniformly. The partner bank bound also becomes dimension-dependent because its best possible `Omega_J` is `O(m^{-2})`.
