# Early checkpoint: a sign-specific Dodgson compiler on the rank-two/rank-two bank face

Status: PROVED SUPPORTING LEMMA / FULL TARGET OPEN.

## Setup
Let L be a real symmetric 3x3 matrix of inertia (2,1) whose three eigenvalues satisfy |lambda_k(L)|>=1. In an orthonormal spectral basis write

L = [[a,b,d],[b,c,e],[d,e,f]].

Let
E=L[{1,2},{1,2}], F=L[{2,3},{2,3}], C=L[{1,2},{2,3}], and k=det C=b e-c d.
These are exactly the two self-plane restrictions and their cross pairing when two source banks occupy the complementary spectral planes span(e1,e2) and span(e2,e3).

## Exact identity
Direct expansion (equivalently Desnanot-Jacobi condensation) gives

    det(E) det(F) - k^2 = c det(L).                    (1)

Also, since every |lambda_k(L)|>=1,

    ||L^{-1}||op <= 1.

The two principal cofactors obey

    det(E)=det(L) (L^{-1})_{33},
    det(F)=det(L) (L^{-1})_{11},

hence

    |det(E)|, |det(F)| <= |det(L)|.                    (2)

These two elementary facts give a quantitative sign split without any physical-minor selection.

## Majority-shared direction c>0
Fix eta>0 and assume c>=eta.

If

    k^2 >= (1/2)c|det L|,

then, since |det L|>=1,

    |k| >= sqrt(eta/2).                                (3)

Otherwise (1) gives

    det(E)det(F) = k^2-c|det L| <= -(1/2)c|det L|.

Thus E and F have opposite determinant signs. Both contain the positive vector e2 with quadratic value c>0, so the restriction with positive determinant is positive definite and the other is indefinite. Moreover, using (2) on either factor,

    |det(E)|, |det(F)| >= c/2 >= eta/2.                (4)

So a majority-sign shared direction has an exact alternative: either the bank-to-bank 2x2 cross determinant is quantitative, or one entire bank self-plane is quantitatively positive definite.

## Minority-shared direction c<0
If c<=-eta, then (1) becomes

    det(E)det(F)=k^2+|c||det L| >= |c||det L|.

The determinants have the same sign. Neither can be positive: a positive determinant together with the common negative diagonal c would make that 2-plane negative definite, contradicting the fact that L has only one negative eigenvalue. Hence both E and F are indefinite, and (2) yields

    det(E)<0, det(F)<0,
    |det(E)|, |det(F)| >= |c| >= eta.                  (5)

Thus the minority-shared face does NOT collapse to same-sign self curvature: it contains two quantitative rank-two saddles. This is the genuinely two-sheet branch.

At the null interface c=0 one has det(E)=-b^2, det(F)=-e^2 and k=be, so both self restrictions are indefinite or degenerate. The near-null region |c|<<1 is therefore the natural remaining transition.

## Lift to the balanced two-bank face
Suppose an exact-rank-three Parseval frame is split into disjoint ORIGINAL-coordinate banks S,T and, in the basis above,

    G_S=R_S^T R_S=diag(1,beta,0),
    G_T=R_T^T R_T=diag(0,1-beta,1),
    beta,1-beta >= alpha>0.

After source-preserving polar normalization, the S and T two-dimensional projections have density caps <=2. Their normalized quadratic/cross matrices have determinants

    det B_S = beta det(E),
    det B_T = (1-beta) det(F),
    det K_ST = sqrt(beta(1-beta)) k.                   (6)

Hence the majority-shared alternative gives either

    |det K_ST| >= alpha sqrt(eta/2),                   (7)

or one self bank is positive definite with

    det B_def >= alpha eta/2.                          (8)

In branch (8), condition on the other ORIGINAL-coordinate bank. The cross term becomes only an affine term in the definite bank. The pinned rank-two PSD theorem from math-open-problems@e5f71d79eff325201dd0274053d20268ce65b837, COUNTEREXAMPLE_SEARCH.md, gives for a 2D projection density cap <=2

    ||p_Q||inf <= 2 pi / sqrt(det B_def)
               <= 2 pi sqrt(2/(alpha eta)).            (9)

This is a real target-level conditional-density bound on the exact-rank-three branch; no rotated coordinates are asserted independent.

For the completed fleet03/B11 r28 rank-collapse example beta=1/2, L=diag(1,1,-1), one has c=1, k=0 and det B_S=1/2. Equation (9) gives
