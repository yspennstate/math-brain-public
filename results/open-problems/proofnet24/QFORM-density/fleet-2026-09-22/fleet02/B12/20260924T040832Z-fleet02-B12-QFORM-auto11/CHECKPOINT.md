Latest partner consumed: fleet03/B12 r48 (`math-open-problems@1e1ba593e3832d65ece87d00c7e14cdf48632881`). In the partner notation, let `K` be the right-singular subspace generated by the `q` smallest singular directions of `F`. Define

- `d = inf_{y in K, ||y||=1} ||Fy||`,
- `a = inf_{z in K^perp, ||z||=1} ||Fz||` (so `a>=d`),
- `b = inf_{y in K, ||y||=1} ||Wy||`,
- `c = ||W P_{K^perp}||op`.

Assume `a,b>0`. Put

`H = [[d^2+b^2, -bc],[-bc, a^2+c^2]]`

and let `rhohat^2=lambda_min(H)`, equivalently

`rhohat^2 = (U-sqrt(U^2-4V))/2`,

`U=a^2+b^2+c^2+d^2`,

`V=a^2(d^2+b^2)+c^2 d^2`.

Then for the stacked/orthogonal-output carrier map `M=[F;W]`,

`boxed: s_m(M) >= rhohat.`

Proof. For `x=y+z`, `y in K`, `z in K^perp`, set `Y=||y||`, `Z=||z||`. Since `K` is a right-singular subspace, `Fy perp Fz`, hence `||F x||^2 >= d^2Y^2+a^2Z^2`. Also `||W(y+z)|| >= max(bY-cZ,0)`. If `bY>=cZ`, the claimed lower bound is exactly the Rayleigh lower bound of `H`. If `bY<cZ`, discard `W`; because `a>=d`, `(d^2Y^2+a^2Z^2)/(Y^2+Z^2)` decreases as `Y/Z` increases, so it is bounded below by its boundary value at `Y/Z=c/b`. That boundary value is the Rayleigh quotient of `H` at `(c/b,1)`, hence is at least `lambda_min(H)`. This proves the claim.

Two useful consequences are immediate. First, `H=H_0+d^2 e_1e_1^T`, so `rhohat` dominates the partner r48 bound (recovered exactly at `d=0`). Second,

`H-d^2 I = [[b^2,-bc],[-bc,a^2+c^2-d^2]] >= 0`

because its determinant is `b^2(a^2-d^2)>=0`; therefore `rhohat>=d`. Thus arbitrarily large transverse leakage `c` can no longer collapse the carrier below a positive low-singular floor `d`.

A later dense coefficient perturbation `E` with `||E||op<=delta` costs only by Weyl:

`boxed: s_m(M+E) >= [rhohat-delta]_+.`

No claim is made that the random quadratic perturbation is small.
