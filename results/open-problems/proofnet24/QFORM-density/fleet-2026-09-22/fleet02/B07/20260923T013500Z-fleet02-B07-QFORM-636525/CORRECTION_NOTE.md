# Correction to Theorem 4.1 wording

In `REPORT.md`, the phrase “exactly one of the following structural outcomes occurs” is stronger than needed and should be read as:

> **At least one** of the following outcomes occurs under the stated decision procedure: stop at the first level at which a row or column side is diffuse; if no diffuse stop occurs at either level, outcome 3 (the concentrated physical `3x3` minor) follows.

The three descriptive properties are not claimed to be logically mutually exclusive; for example, a packet could satisfy a diffuse marginal inequality and also happen to contain a comparatively large atom. The proof and every quantitative implication use only the decision-tree statement above, so equations (4.1)–(5.9), the six-coordinate core bound, and the stated unpaid conditional-diffuse branch are unchanged.
