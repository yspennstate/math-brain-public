# fleet02/B14 early calculation — overlapping-driver Lp receiver

Let `1<q<2`, `p=q/(q-1)`, `alpha=q-1`. Let `U,V_1,...,V_m` be independent cap-one original-coordinate variables. Let `Xi` be an auxiliary product block independent of `(U,V)`. Suppose that for every driver realization `v` a variable `T_v=T(Xi;v)` has a density with `||p_{T_v}||_p<=K`, uniformly in `v`; unlike the current fleet03/B14 theorem, `T_v` is allowed to depend on the SAME driver coordinates `v` that enter the receiver row. For arbitrary measurable `R(v)`, fixed `d,c`, and arbitrary affine shift `e`, put

`Q=T_V+dU^2+2(c^T V+e)U+R(V)`.

For fixed `v`, the pushforward `h_v` of `U` by `u -> d u^2+2(c^Tv+e)u` obeys

`||h_v||_q^q <= E_U |dU+c^Tv+e|^{-alpha}`.

(The two-preimage factor `2^(q-1)` cancels exactly against the derivative factor `2^(1-q)`.) Young gives

`||p_{Q|V=v}||_inf <= K [E_U |dU+c^Tv+e|^{-alpha}]^(1/q)`.

Averaging in `V` and using concavity of `x^(1/q)` yields

`||p_Q||_inf <= K [E |dU+c^TV+e|^{-alpha}]^(1/q)`.

The whole original-coordinate projection `dU+c^TV` has density cap

`C(a)=min{1/||a||_inf, sqrt(2)/||a||_2}`, `a=(d,c)`.

The cap-C Riesz bathtub bound is

`E|Y-s|^{-alpha} <= 2^alpha C^alpha/(1-alpha)`.

Therefore

`||p_Q||_inf <= K [2^(q-1)/(2-q)]^(1/q) C(a)^((q-1)/q)`.

At `p=3,q=3/2`, this is

`||p_Q||_inf <= 2K C(a)^(1/3) <= 2^(7/6) K ||a||_2^(-1/3)`.

This closes the *parametric overlap* left by the newest fleet03/B14 theorem: the receiver driver may enter the conditional core arbitrarily, provided fixing that driver leaves a uniform `L^p` core generated by independent remaining variables. The unresolved case is *stochastic overlap*, where the same driver must remain unconditioned to create the core `L^p` smoothing.
