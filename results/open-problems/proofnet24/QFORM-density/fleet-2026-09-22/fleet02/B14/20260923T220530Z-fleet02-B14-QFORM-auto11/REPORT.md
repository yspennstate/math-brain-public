## 2. The affine small-ball functional

For a probability law `mu` on `R^d` and a real symmetric `d x d` matrix `B`, define the extended affine small-ball constant

\[
\Lambda_B(\mu)
=
\sup_{a\in\mathbb R^d,\ c\in\mathbb R}
\sup_{I\subset\mathbb R\text{ bounded open interval}}
\frac{\Pr\{Z^TBZ+a^TZ+c\in I\}}{|I|},
\qquad Z\sim\mu.
\tag{2.1}
\]

If this quantity is finite, every affine quadratic output in (2.1) has an essentially bounded density with the same bound. The formulation in terms of interval probabilities avoids any continuity assumption.
