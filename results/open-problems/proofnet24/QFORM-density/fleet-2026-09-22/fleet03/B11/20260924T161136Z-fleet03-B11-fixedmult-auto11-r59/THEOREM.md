# Incidence-bank theorem

This file records a Cauchy-Binet aggregation lemma for quadratic-form research.

Fix two target coordinates t1,t2 and one source coordinate u. Let P be a finite set of other source coordinates. For each v in P let Bv be the determinant of the two by two crossing matrix on source rows u,v and target columns t1,t2. Let E be the sum of Bv squared.
