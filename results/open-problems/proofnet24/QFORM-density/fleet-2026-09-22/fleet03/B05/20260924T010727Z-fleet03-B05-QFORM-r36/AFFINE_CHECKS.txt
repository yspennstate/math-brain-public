1. Completing a general invertible scalar pair.
For W=(Y,Z)^T and V=(S,R)^T,
 Q=V^T C V+2 V^T P W+W^T B W+ell_V^T V+ell_W^T W+q0.
Set h(V)=B^{-1}(P^T V+ell_W/2). Then
 [W+h]^T B[W+h]
 =W^T B W+2V^T P W+ell_W^T W
  +V^T P B^{-1}P^T V+V^T P B^{-1}ell_W
  +(1/4)ell_W^T B^{-1}ell_W.
Thus the remainder is
 V^T(C-PB^{-1}P^T)V
 +(ell_V-PB^{-1}ell_W)^T V+constant,
which equals lambda*m*S^2+b^T V+constant under the ratio-packet Schur identity.
