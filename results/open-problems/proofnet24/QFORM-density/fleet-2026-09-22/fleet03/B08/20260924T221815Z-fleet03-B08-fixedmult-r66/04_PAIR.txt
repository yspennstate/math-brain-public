PAIR BRANCHES

Let D be the absolute determinant of the physical pair and assume D>=1/287.

Definite case: density <= pi/sqrt(D) <= pi*sqrt(287) < 93181/1750 = 53.2462857.

Indefinite case: the published hyperbolic estimate plus its concentration lemma gives a density cap
D^(-1/2) times [6+L0+positive_part(log(16 sqrt(D)))], where L0=2+2 log(19).
On D from 1/287 to 1/256 the log contribution is zero and the expression decreases.
Above 1/256 direct differentiation remains negative because the bracket is already larger than one and is increasing.
So the maximum is at D=1/287 and is at most

sqrt(287)*(8+2 log(19))
<11766219/50000
=235.32438<236.
