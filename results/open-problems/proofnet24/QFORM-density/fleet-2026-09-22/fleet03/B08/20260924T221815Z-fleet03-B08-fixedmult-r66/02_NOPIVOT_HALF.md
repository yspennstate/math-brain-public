# No-pivot density and the s3 half-potential

Let B=pi+4 log2+4+4 log19. The bounds in 01_PARAMETERS.md give B<37973/1750.

For s_5(A)>=1 and no pivot, the m=3 sign lemma gives E_3>gamma_3^6. The published order-three receiver has

C_3=3 sqrt(2)e(9/2)^(1/6)<207538551/14000000.

Hence

||p_Q||_infinity < C_3/gamma_3
<622615653/2639660
=235.8696396505...<236.

This branch has p_Q in C_0.

For the half-potential under s_3(A)>=1, the same proof with the retuned thresholds has three cases:

no pivot:
(61/5)/(467/1000)=12200/467=26.124197...;

pair pivot:
16 zeta^(-1/4)<16(103/25)=1648/25=65.92;

scalar pivot:
1+eta^(-1/2)B
<63036801/875000
=72.0420582857...<73.

Therefore, in the normalized case,

sup_a E|Q-a|^(-1/2)<73.

Scaling gives 73 K/sqrt(s).
