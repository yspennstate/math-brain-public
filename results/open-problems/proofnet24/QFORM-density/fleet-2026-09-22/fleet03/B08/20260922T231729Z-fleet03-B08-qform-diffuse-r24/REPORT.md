`s_m(C_I)<c`.                                                    (5.3)

`s_(p+q-1)(X+Y) <= s_p(X)+s_q(Y)`                               (5.4)

`L rho_alpha <= s_3(S_I)

`s_(4-m)(E_I)>L rho_alpha-c`.                                   (5.6)

`H_I:=R_I^T B R_J=G_I^(1/2) E_I G_I^(1/2)`.                    (5.7)

`s_(4-m)(H_I) >= alpha s_(4-m)(E_I)
