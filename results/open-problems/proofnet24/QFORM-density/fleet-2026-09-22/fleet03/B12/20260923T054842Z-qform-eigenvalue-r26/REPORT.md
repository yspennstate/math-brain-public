## 2. A new intrinsic cross-energy certificate

Let M be any real physical cross block between disjoint ORIGINAL-coordinate banks. Write its squared singular values as

    lambda_1>=lambda_2>=...>=0,

    E_2=e_2(lambda).                                            (2.1)

Let

    rho_R=max_i ||row_i(M)||_2^2,
    rho_C=max_j ||col_j(M)||_2^2,

#### Proof
