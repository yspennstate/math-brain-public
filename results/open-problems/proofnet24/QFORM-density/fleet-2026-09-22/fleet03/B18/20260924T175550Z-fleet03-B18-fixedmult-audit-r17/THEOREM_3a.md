# Fourier step

Condition on the outside original coordinates. Integrating the two literal sources first leaves the product of their two characteristic functions evaluated at the random slopes T1 and T2, multiplied by a phase of modulus one.

For fixed nonzero slopes s1,s2, Plancherel and Cauchy-Schwarz imply that the integral over frequency of the absolute product is at most

2 pi K / sqrt(|s1 s2|).

This uses only that each source density is a probability density bounded by K, hence its squared L2 norm is at most K. Tonelli then bounds the full Fourier L1 norm by 2 pi K times E |T1 T2|^(-1/2).
