# Physical quadratic-form corollary

Let i,j be two literal original coordinates with A_ii=A_jj=A_ij=0. Let B be the two-row matrix formed by their physical cross coefficients into one disjoint original-coordinate bank. Assume B has rank two.

In X^T A X the two source slopes are twice the two rows of B. Consequently the shared-bank theorem gives

density cap <= 8 sqrt(2) K^2 times det(B B^T)^(-1/4).

The density is continuous and vanishes at infinity. Arbitrary same-bank quadratic curvature and all outside terms surviving original-coordinate conditioning are allowed. Small nonzero source squares are not deleted; this corollary requires the displayed zero source block exactly.
