# QFORM-TWO-SOURCE-SHAREDBANK-L1-v1

Let U1,U2 be independent original scalar coordinates with densities at most K, and V a disjoint original-coordinate product bank with coordinate densities at most K. After conditioning any other disjoint original coordinates, assume

Q = U1 T1(V) + U2 T2(V) + H(V),

where Ti(V)=ci dot V + alpha_i, H is arbitrary measurable, and the 2 by m matrix C with rows c1,c2 has rank two. Put D = sqrt(det(C C^T)).

Then Q has a continuous C0 density and

||p_Q||_infty <= 16 sqrt(2) K^2 D^(-1/2)
              = 16 sqrt(2) K^2 det(C C^T)^(-1/4).

The source-source quadratic block must be exactly zero. The two slopes T1,T2 may be dependent because they use the same original bank V.
