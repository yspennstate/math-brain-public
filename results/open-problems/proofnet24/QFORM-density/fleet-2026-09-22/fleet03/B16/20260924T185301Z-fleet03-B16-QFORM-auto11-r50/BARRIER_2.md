# Barrier density proof, part 1

Put R1=U+T1/eps and R2=H-(T2+S)/eps.

For |r|<=1/2, convolution of U with T1/eps gives exactly p_R1(r)=eps.

The sum T2+S has triangular density 1-|w| on [-1,1]. Hence for |r|<=1/2,

p_R2(r)=eps [1-eps(r^2+1/4)] >= (7/8)eps,

because eps<=1/4.

Set Z1=eps R1^2 and Z2=eps R2^2. For 0<z<eps/4 the change of variables through the two square roots gives

p_Z1(z)=sqrt(eps)/sqrt(z),

p_Z2(z)>=(7/8)sqrt(eps)/sqrt(z).
