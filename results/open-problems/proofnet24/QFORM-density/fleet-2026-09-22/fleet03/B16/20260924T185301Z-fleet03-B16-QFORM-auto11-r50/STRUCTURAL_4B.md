# Four-bank extraction, conclusion

For the retained partition write the sum as sum_h r_h^2 z_h, where r_h^2 is the squared star norm into label 1 and z_h is the sum of squared 2 by 2 crossing determinants through labels 2 and 4. Since sum_h z_h <= V_2, some h with z_h>0 satisfies r_h^2 > 558009/2621440000, hence r_h > 747/51200.
