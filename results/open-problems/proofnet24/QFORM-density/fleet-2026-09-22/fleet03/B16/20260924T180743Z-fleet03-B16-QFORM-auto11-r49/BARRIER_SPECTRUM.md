# Spectrum and scope

In the coordinate order (U1,V1,U2,V2), the coefficient matrix is block diagonal with first block [[eps,1],[1,eps^(-1)]] and second block its negative. Each block has determinant zero and the positive block has trace eps+eps^(-1).

Therefore the spectrum is exactly

{eps+eps^(-1), 0, -(eps+eps^(-1)), 0}.

So this family has only two nonzero eigenvalues and is not a counterexample to the three-large-eigenvalue conjecture.

Its role is narrower and useful: the exact-zero two-source theorem cannot be extended by continuity in the source quadratic block on the indefinite side while arbitrary quadratic curvature on the shared slope bank is allowed. The slope matrix remains rank two with det(B B^T)=1 as eps tends to zero. A valid lower-multiplicity argument must retain and spend the genuine third curvature before this reduction.
