## Theorem S1 (`QFORM-RANK3-RESIDUAL-LARGE-RAYLEIGH-v1`)

Keep the exact-rank-three setup of `REPORT.md`: after completing a nonzero original-coordinate pivot `a=A_ii`, the residual is

`D=(W^T X_R)^T B(W^T X_R)`,

where `W^TW=I_2`, `B` is symmetric invertible, and

`|det B|=ell |det Lambda|/|a|`.

Assume the residual Schur form is indefinite and write

`Delta=-det B>0`.

Choose a residual physical row `w` of leverage `h=|w|^2>0`, put `e_1=w/sqrt(h)`, choose `e_2 perp e_1`, and write

`B=[[beta,gamma],[gamma,delta]]`

in this basis. Thus the selected physical Schur diagonal is `w^TBw=h beta`.

If

`|beta| >= sqrt(Delta)/5`,                                      (S1.1)

then the residual quadratic has an `L^3` density satisfying

`||p_D||_3 <= sqrt(2) 5^(1/3) H_(1/2) H^(1/3) h^(-1/2) Delta^(-1/3)`,   (S1.2)

where `H_(1/2)` is the universal one-dimensional HLS norm

`||I_(1/2)f||_3 <= H_(1/2)||f||_(6/5)`,

and `H` is any valid density cap for the normalized transverse bank below. Using the source-valid two-dimensional product-projection theorem with an independent dummy coordinate gives `H=2`.

Consequently the full rank-three form obeys

`||p_Q||_infinity`

`<=4 [sqrt(2) 5^(1/3) H_(1/2) 2^(1/3)]^(3/4)`

`   * h^(-3/8) [ell |det Lambda| |a|]^(-1/4)`.                 (S1.3)

Thus, in the nested-heavy branch (`h>1/2`), every heavy residual row whose normalized Schur Rayleigh quotient is at least one fifth of the determinant scale is quantitatively paid. No reciprocal physical principal minor appears.

### Proof

Write residual-frame coordinates as

`Y=sqrt(h)X_j+T`, `L=sum_{k!=i,j} l_kX_k`,

where `X_j` is an original physical coordinate independent of the sigma-field generated by all other residual variables. Orthonormality gives `sum l_k^2=1`, hence the bank cap

`||p_L||_infinity<=H`, with `H=2` admissible.

The residual quadratic is

`D=beta Y^2+2gamma YL+delta L^2`.

Fix all variables except `X_j`; equivalently fix `(T,L)=(t,l)`. Solving `D=s` as an equation in `X_j`, the discriminant identity is exact:

`(beta Y+gamma l)^2 = (gamma^2-beta delta)l^2+beta s`

`                         = Delta l^2+beta s`.                   (S1.4)

At every regular root,

`|partial_(X_j) D|=2 sqrt(h) sqrt(Delta l^2+beta s)`.

There are at most two roots and the physical density of `X_j` is at most one, so

`p_(D | T=t,L=l)(s)`

`<= h^(-1/2)(Delta l^2+beta s)_+^(-1/2)`.                       (S1.5)

The key point is that **the leakage `T` cancels completely from the discriminant**. Therefore averaging first over `T|L=l` and then over `L` gives

`p_D(s) <= h^(-1/2) int g_L(l)(Delta l^2+beta s)_+^(-1/2) dl`.  (S1.6)

Let `M` be the density of `U=Delta L^2`. The completed single-square theorem used by account01/B11 (ultimately account02/B11) scales from a cap-one variable to a cap-`H` variable as follows. Put `L'=HL`; then `p_(L')<=1` and

`U=(Delta/H^2)(L')^2`.

Hence

`||M||_(3/2)<=2 H^(2/3) Delta^(-1/3)`.                           (S1.7)

Since `||M||_1=1`, interpolation gives

`||M||_(6/5)<=sqrt(2) H^(1/3) Delta^(-1/6)`.                    (S1.8)

The right side of (S1.6), after the change `u=Delta l^2`, is a one-sided half-order fractional integral of `M` evaluated at the scaled argument `beta s`; it is pointwise dominated by `I_(1/2)M`. HLS and scaling in `s` therefore give

`||p_D||_3`

`<=h^(-1/2)|beta|^(-1/3)H_(1/2)||M||_(6/5)`

`<=sqrt(2)H_(1/2)H^(1/3)h^(-1/2)|beta|^(-1/3)Delta^(-1/6)`.     (S1.9)

Under (S1.1), `|beta|^(-1/3)<=5^(1/3)Delta^(-1/6)`, proving (S1.2).

Finally apply account01/B11's completed dependent-square smoothing lemma to the outer pivot:

`Q=a(X_i+m(R))^2+D(R)`,

`||p_Q||_infinity<=4|a|^(-1/2)||p_D||_3^(3/4)`,

and use `Delta=|det B|=ell|det Lambda|/|a|`. This proves (S1.3). QED.

## Relation to the main report

The main report pays the exact residual-isotropic endpoint `beta=0` by a different affine-mixture rearrangement argument, with

`||p_D||_3<=66^(1/3)h^(-1/3)Delta^(-1/3)`.

The present theorem pays the complementary determinant-scale region `|beta|>=sqrt(Delta)/5`. The still-unpaid localized residual is therefore sharply confined to

`0<|beta|<sqrt(Delta)/5`,

for a residual-heavy row, together with the independent outer-pivot light-cone debt `|a|->0`.

The discontinuity between the exact affine endpoint `beta=0` and the small nonzero range is a real proof gap, not a claim that the density itself is discontinuous in `beta`. A successful next theorem should interpolate between the affine-mixture rearrangement and the discriminant/HLS estimate while retaining the normalization of each physical-coordinate conditional section.
