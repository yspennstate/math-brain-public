# Scope clarification for account01/B14 r28

This addendum narrows one phrase in `REPORT.md` and is part of the final mathematical record.

The pair-log compiler in Sections 1–2 applies to a disintegration of variables **outside the active physical pair**, or more generally to any disintegration under which the two active original-coordinate variables remain conditionally independent and each retains density cap at most one, so that the source-pinned physical-pair logarithmic envelope remains valid slice by slice. It is not a theorem permitting arbitrary conditioning on functions involving the active pair itself.

Under that hypothesis the mass-proportional logarithmic lemma itself needs only `w(u)>=0` and `int w=1`; the auxiliary restriction `w(u)<=1` written in Section 1 is unnecessary. Indeed the proof uses only linearity in `w`. The target-valid example in Section 4 is within the stated safe scope because the conjugate variables involve only `X_3,X_4`, while the active pair is `(X_1,X_2)`.

No displayed bound in `REPORT.md` changes under this clarification.
