## 2. One-dimensional inverse-half lemma

### Lemma 2.1

If a real random variable `V` has density at most `K`, then, uniformly in `a in R`,

`E |V-a|^(-1/2) <= 2 sqrt(2K)`.                                  (2.1)

**Proof.** By layer cake,

`E|V-a|^(-1/2)=integral_0^infinity P(|V-a|<u^(-2)) du`.

The density cap gives

`P(|V-a|<u^(-2)) <= min(1,2K u^(-2))`.

Split at `u0=sqrt(2K)`:

`integral_0^u0 1 du + integral_u0^infinity 2K u^(-2) du`
`=sqrt(2K)+2K/sqrt(2K)=2sqrt(2K)`. QED.

No support or moment hypothesis is used.

## 3. Conditional product half-Riesz theorem

### Theorem 3.1 — `QFORM-CONDITIONAL-PRODUCT-HALF-RIESZ-v1`

Let `(W,Y)` be any real pair such that

* `Y` has a density bounded by `L`;
* for almost every `y`, the conditional law `W|Y=y` has a density bounded by `K`.

Let `b!=0` and let

`R=2bWY+cY^2+dW+eY+f`.                                           (3.1)

Then

`boxed: sup_t E |R-t|^(-1/2) <= 4 sqrt(2KL) |b|^(-1/2).`         (3.2)

**Proof.** Put `y0=-d/(2b)`. For fixed `Y=y`, (3.1) is affine in `W` with slope

`2by+d=2b(y-y0)`.

For `y!=y0`, there is a deterministic center `a_t(y)` such that

`R-t=2b(y-y0)(W-a_t(y))`.

Apply Lemma 2.1 conditionally:

`E(|R-t|^(-1/2) | Y=y)`
`<= |2b(y-y0)|^(-1/2) 2sqrt(2K)`
`=2sqrt(K)|b|^(-1/2)|y-y0|^(-1/2)`.                             (3.3)

The exceptional point `y=y0` is null because `Y` has a density. Applying Lemma 2.1 again to `Y`, uniformly at the translated center `y0`, gives

`E|Y-y0|^(-1/2)<=2sqrt(2L)`.

Average (3.3) to obtain (3.2). QED.

The important point is that `W` and `Y` need not be independent and no joint density cap is needed.

## 4. A nested square smoother

### Theorem 4.1 — `QFORM-TRIANGULAR-CONDITIONAL-SQUARE-SMOOTHER-v1`

`||p_(Z|W=w,Y=y)||_infinity <= H`.                               (4.1)

Let `a!=0` and let `h(W,Y)` be any measurable function. Define

`Q=R+a[Z+h(W,Y)]^2`.                                             (4.2)

Then `Q` has a density and

`boxed: ||p_Q||_infinity <=4 H sqrt(2KL) |a|^(-1/2)|b|^(-1/2).`  (4.3)

**Proof.** Conditional on `(W,Y)`, the signed square in (4.2) has a density bounded by

`H |a|^(-1/2) |u|^(-1/2)`                                      (4.4)

`p_Q(t) <= H |a|^(-1/2) E|t-R|^(-1/2)`.
