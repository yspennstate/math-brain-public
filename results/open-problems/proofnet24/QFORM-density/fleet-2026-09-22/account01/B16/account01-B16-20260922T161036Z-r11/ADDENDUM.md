# Addendum — sharper constant on the singleton-plus-two-banks stress family

The main report's equation (7.4) states the valid bound `2 C_ind` for

`A_m=[1] direct_sum (J_m/m) direct_sum (-J_m/m)`.

The same proof gives the strictly sharper constant `sqrt(2) C_ind`. Indeed, choose the opposite-sign diffuse banks as the binary indefinite base. Each normalized bank has density cap `sqrt(2)`, so Lemma 3.1 gives the inverse-square-root potential bound

`C_ind sqrt(sqrt(2)*sqrt(2)) = sqrt(2) C_ind`.

Use the singleton physical coordinate as the final square innovation; its density cap is `1`, not `sqrt(2)`. Lemma 4.1 therefore gives

`||p_Q||_infinity <= sqrt(2) C_ind
 = 23.390591531...`,

uniformly in `m` and in all cap-one input laws.

The weaker `2 C_ind` statement in the main report is still correct; this addendum only sharpens its stress-family constant.
