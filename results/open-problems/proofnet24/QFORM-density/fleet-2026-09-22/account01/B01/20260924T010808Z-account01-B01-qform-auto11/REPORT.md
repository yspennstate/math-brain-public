## 4. A general Frostman-to-fractional-potential lemma

### Lemma 4.1 — `QFORM-FROSTMAN-TO-RIESZ-v1`

Let `R` be any real random variable satisfying, for some `0<alpha<=1` and `K>0`,

`sup_s P(|R-s|<rho)
 <=min{1,K rho^alpha}`                                          (4.1)

for all `rho>0`. Then for every `0<beta<alpha`,

`boxed:
 H_beta(R)
 <= alpha/(alpha-beta) * K^(beta/alpha).`                       (4.2)

### Proof

For fixed `s`, layer cake gives

`E|R-s|^(-beta)
 =beta int_0^infinity rho^(-beta-1)
       P(|R-s|<rho) d rho`.                                     (4.3)

Split at `rho_0=K^(-1/alpha)`. Using `K rho^alpha` below the split and one above it,

`E|R-s|^(-beta)
 <= beta K rho_0^(alpha-beta)/(alpha-beta)+rho_0^(-beta)
 = [beta/(alpha-beta)+1]K^(beta/alpha)
 = alpha/(alpha-beta)K^(beta/alpha).`                           (4.4)

Take the supremum in `s`. QED.

The endpoint `beta=alpha` diverges logarithmically in this argument; no endpoint is claimed.

`boxed:

### Proof
