# r25 addendum — all-heavy-row consequence

In the right-plane Parseval frame `(q,v)`, the physical leverages satisfy

`ell_j=q_j^2+v_j^2`, `sum_j ell_j=2`.

Hence the heavy set

`H={j: ell_j>1/2}`

has cardinality at most three.

The r25 heavy-hub theorem applies separately to every `r in H`. Therefore, if the full one-sided survivor is not paid by the r25 non-null heavy-hub exit, then necessarily

`|h_r|/rho<1/8` for every `r in H`.

Consequently

`sum_{r in H} h_r^2 < |H| rho^2/64 <=3 rho^2/64`.

Thus, in the still-unpaid heavy-plane state, every coordinate that is heavy for the two-dimensional `(q,v)` plane is simultaneously near-null for the physical square coefficient, and the total square-coefficient energy on all such heavy coordinates is `<3/64` of `rho^2`.

For each `r in H`, the second r25 exit also says that if `ell_r<=7/8` and the residual frame after deleting `r` has normalized leverage at most `1/2`, then the branch is paid by `<26.585/s_3(A)`. Therefore an unpaid state has, for every heavy `r`, either `ell_r>7/8` or another heavy coordinate in the residual whitened frame.

This finite-heavy-set consequence is a structural handoff only. It does not by itself prove the remaining localized-core branch and does not treat a small coefficient as a small random perturbation.
