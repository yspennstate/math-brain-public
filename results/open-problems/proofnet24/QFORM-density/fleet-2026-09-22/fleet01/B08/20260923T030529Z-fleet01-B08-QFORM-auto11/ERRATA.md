# fleet01/B08 auto11 erratum

One wording correction to `REPORT.md`, Section 4: the sentence

> "Moreover exactly one of the following source-valid survivor types occurs (overlaps are harmless)"

must read

> "Moreover at least one of the following source-valid survivor types occurs (overlaps are harmless)."

The three survivor descriptions and all inequalities are unchanged. The alternatives can overlap (for example a matrix can lie in both a rank-one tube and a rank-two tube), so exclusivity was never used in the proof or in Corollary 4.1.
