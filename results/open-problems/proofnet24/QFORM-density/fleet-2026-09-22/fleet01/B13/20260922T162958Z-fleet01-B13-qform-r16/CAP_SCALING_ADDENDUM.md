# r16 addendum — anisotropic density-cap scaling and exact grouped-bank corollary

The main theorem extends without new analysis to unequal one-dimensional density caps, which is the form needed when an exact projective bank is replaced by its normalized product-linear aggregate.

## Theorem `QFORM-2VAR-AFFINE-HALF-RIESZ-CAPS-v1`

Let independent `Y,Z` have density caps `M,N` respectively, and let the affine quadratic Hessian `S` be invertible with `Delta=|det S|`. Then

`sup_t E|R-t|^(-1/2) <= 8sqrt(2) (MN)^(1/2) Delta^(-1/4)`.

Also, with `tau=8 MN eps/sqrt(Delta)<=1`,

`P(|R-t|<=eps) <= tau(1+log(1/tau))`.

### Proof

Translate away the affine term as in the main report. Set `Y'=M Y`, `Z'=N Z`. Their densities are bounded by one. In the new coordinates the quadratic Hessian is

`S'=diag(M^-1,N^-1) S diag(M^-1,N^-1)`,

so

`|det S'|=Delta/(M^2 N^2)`.

Apply the main cap-one theorem. QED.

## Three-coordinate form with unequal caps

Let independent `X_1,X_2,X_3` have density caps `K_1,K_2,K_3`. Let the affine quadratic principal Hessian `T` be invertible, and choose a nonzero diagonal pivot `d=T_11`. Completing the square in `X_1` and applying the theorem above to the Schur residual gives

`||p_Q||_infinity <= 8sqrt(2) K_1 (K_2 K_3)^(1/2) |d det T|^(-1/4)`.

The same bound holds after conditioning arbitrary outside **original independent** coordinates because only affine terms change.

## Exact grouped-bank consequence

Suppose three disjoint physical coordinate banks are each compressed to normalized linear forms `Y_j`; by the standard one-dimensional product-marginal theorem used in the existing finite-direction aggregation work, each aggregate has density cap at most `sqrt(2)`, and the three aggregates remain independent because the physical banks are disjoint. Hence any invertible aggregate `3 x 3` Hessian `T` with a nonzero diagonal pivot `d` satisfies

`||p_Q||_infinity <= 16sqrt(2) |d det T|^(-1/4)`

uniformly in arbitrary affine terms generated by conditioning all other independent banks.

This sharpens the exact-bank side of `QFORM-ZERO-PIVOT-GROUPED-COLLAR-v1`: the remaining instability is not support translation or affine leakage and not near-pure-saddle Schur balance. It is specifically the loss of an **exact disjoint-bank representation** under spectral-direction leakage, or the all-isotropic/zero-pivot aggregate endpoint. Full QFORM remains OPEN.
