Exact/numeric arithmetic checks for REPORT.md

Write x=epsilon^2 and gamma=1/2 for the fleet02/B04 balanced family.

D_(1/2) = 289(1+4x)/64.
m2 = 128(x+2)/(289(4x+1)).
ell_strong/m2 = 17(4x+1)/(32(x+2)).
ell_weak/m2 = 289x/(64(x+2)).

The q=1 transfer gate requires both ratios <=1/2.
Strong-row inequality gives x<=15/52 = 0.28846153846153844...
Weak-row inequality gives x<=64/257 = 0.24902723735408560...
Hence the combined sufficient range is
  epsilon^2 <= 64/257,
  epsilon <= 0.49902628924144427...
