## Exact no-pivot s4 family with V3=0

Fix an integer m>100. Let w=m^(-1/2)(1,...,1)^T and
S_m = [[0,w^T],[w,0]], A_m=S_m direct-sum S_m.
Each star block has nonzero spectrum {1,-1}, because ||w||_2=1. Hence A_m has nonzero spectrum {1,1,-1,-1} and s_4(A_m)=1.

Every diagonal entry is zero. A principal two-coordinate determinant is either zero or -1/m. Thus for pivot threshold eta=10^(-2), A_m has no scalar or pair pivot.

If R,C are disjoint coordinate sets, then in one star S_m[R,C] has rank at most one: the center can occur on at most one side, and all nonzero entries are then confined to one row or one column. Therefore a disjoint submatrix of A_m has rank at most two. Every disjoint 3x3 minor vanishes, so V_3(A_m)=0.

Thus the shortcut
s_4(A)>=1 and no physical pivot => V_3(A)>0
is FALSE. This is a proof-mechanism counterexample, not a QFORM counterexample.

## Uniform bounded density for the natural zero-affine stress

Let all physical coordinates be independent with arbitrary densities bounded by one. Write the two center variables U_1,U_2 and leaves V_(k,j), and put L_k=m^(-1/2) sum_j V_(k,j). With zero affine term,
Q = X^T A_m X = 2 U_1 L_1 + 2 U_2 L_2.
The two summands are independent.

The product-projection lemma gives each L_k a density g_k with ||g_k||_infinity<=sqrt(e), hence ||g_k||_2<=e^(1/4).

For one product Z=2UL, with U density f<=1 and independent L density g, phi_Z(t)=integral f(u) ghat(2tu) du. Minkowski and Plancherel give
||phi_Z||_2 <= sqrt(pi)||g||_2 integral f(u)|u|^(-1/2) du.
For every probability density f<=1, layer cake gives
integral f(u)|u|^(-1/2)du <= integral_0^infinity min(1,2s^(-2)) ds = 2sqrt(2).
Therefore ||phi_Z||_2 <= 2sqrt(2pi)e^(1/4).

For Z_k=2U_kL_k, independence gives phi_Q=phi_(Z_1)phi_(Z_2). Cauchy-Schwarz yields
||phi_Q||_1 <= 8 pi sqrt(e).
Fourier inversion gives a bounded continuous density with
||p_Q||_infinity <= 4 sqrt(e) = 6.5948850828...

So the exact s4/V3=0 obstruction is not itself generated by a density blowup in this natural zero-affine stress. Any s4 proof must consume shared-index/star/hub geometry before passing to disjoint third minors, or use an analytic receiver not requiring V_3>0. The full s4 and s3 density theorems remain open.
