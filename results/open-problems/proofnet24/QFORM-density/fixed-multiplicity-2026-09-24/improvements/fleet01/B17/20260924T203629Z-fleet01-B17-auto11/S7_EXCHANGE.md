# S7 seed-exchange lemma

In the no-pivot chamber with s_7(A)>=1, take an order-two sampled seed whose completion error obeys ||E||_F^2<=18 kappa. Rank six forces one complement diagonal defect. For that coordinate h, the interpolation scalar satisfies |P_hh|>117/200. Writing B=A[S,T], u=A[S,h], v=A[h,T], one has det(B)P_hh=v adj(B)u and |v_j|<1/11. Hence one one-column replacement seed B' obeys

det(B')^2 > (1656369/160000) det(B)^2 > 10.35 det(B)^2.

The same statement holds for a one-row replacement by symmetry. Thus no determinant-local maximum order-two seed can have completion error <=18 kappa.
