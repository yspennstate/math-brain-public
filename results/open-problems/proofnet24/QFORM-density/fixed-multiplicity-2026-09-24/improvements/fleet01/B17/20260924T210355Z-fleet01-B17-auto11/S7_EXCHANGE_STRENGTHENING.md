# S7 exchange strengthening

For a good order-two seed B=A[S,T] and the forced complement coordinate h, let u=A[S,h], v=A[h,T], and P_hh=v B^{-1}u. In the no-pivot chamber |v_j|<1/11 and |P_hh|>117/200. If c=adj(B)u, then det(B)P_hh=v c. Cauchy-Schwarz gives

sum_j c_j^2 > (121/2)(117/200)^2 det(B)^2 = (1656369/80000) det(B)^2 > 20.70 det(B)^2.

Each c_j, up to sign, is the determinant of a one-column replacement of B by h. Hence the total squared determinant mass of the two one-column replacements exceeds 20.70 times the seed mass, and at least one replacement exceeds 1656369/160000 > 10.35 times the seed mass. By symmetry the same holds for row replacements.

This proves that no determinant-local maximum order-two seed can be good. It does not close s7: a target seed can have dimension-unboundedly many inverse exchange predecessors, so this local growth cannot be summed into a dimension-free global mass contradiction without a new congestion estimate. The overlap-one analytic receiver remains the genuine endpoint.
