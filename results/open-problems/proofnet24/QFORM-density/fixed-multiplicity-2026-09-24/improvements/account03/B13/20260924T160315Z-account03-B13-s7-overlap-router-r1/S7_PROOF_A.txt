S7 router proof A.
Normalize to density cap 1 and s7(A)>=1. Set eta=1/100, kappa=4/81, and assume no eta pivot. Then |Aii|<1/100 and |Aij|^2<101/10000. Let a=sqrt(101/10000). Since (91/900)^2-101/10000=1/8100, a<91/900.
If V1<kappa, then ||A||<1/100+sqrt(kappa)=1/100+2/9<1, contradiction. Hence V1>=kappa.
