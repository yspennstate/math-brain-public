## 2. New lemma: two high leverages contradict the no-pivot entry cap

### Lemma `QFORM-S8-TWOHIGH-IMPOSSIBLE-NOPIVOT-v1`

`eta=1/100`.

Let `H={h_1,h_2}` be any two original coordinates and let `C=A[H,H^c]`. Suppose

`g=det(CC^T)>=d0=0.3649442658827020...`.

Define the rank-two projection

`Q_C=C^T(CC^T)^(-1)C`

and leverage scores `q_i=(Q_C)_ii`. Then there cannot exist two distinct complement indices `i,j` with

`q_i>2/3`, `q_j>2/3`.

#### Proof

For every two-set `{a,b}` in the complement define

`w_ab = det(C_{ab})^2/g`.

Cauchy--Binet gives `sum_{a<b} w_ab=1`. Since `Q_C` is the rank-two projection generated by the rows of `C`, its one-point determinantal marginals satisfy

`q_i=sum_{b!=i} w_ib`.

Therefore for distinct `i,j`,

`q_i+q_j <= 1+w_ij`,

because the total weight one consists of `w_ij`, weights incident only to `i`, weights incident only to `j`, and nonnegative weights incident to neither. Thus if both leverages exceed `2/3`,

`w_ij >= q_i+q_j-1 > 1/3`,

and hence

`det(C_{ij})^2 > g/3 >= d0/3
 =0.12164808862756733...`.                                           (2.1)

But no `eta`-pivot means, for every two distinct physical indices,

`|A_ab|^2 < eta^2+eta =0.0101`.

All four entries of the cross minor `C_{ij}` are off-diagonal physical entries, so

`|det(C_{ij})|
 <= |A_{h_1 i} A_{h_2 j}|+|A_{h_1 j} A_{h_2 i}|

Consequently

`det(C_{ij})^2 < 4(eta^2+eta)^2

which contradicts (2.1) by a factor exceeding 298. QED.
