Shortcut FALSE (mechanism only): let B be the zero-diagonal symmetric 5x5 graph with edges (1,5),(2,3),(4,5), and A_N=N^(-1/3) times N disjoint copies. Physical scalar/pair pivots vanish. For distinct blocks a,b, shared h=5_a with S={1_a,2_b}, T={4_a,3_b} gives overlap-minor square N^(-2), so total overlap mass >=1-1/N. But each fixed anchor participates in only O(N) such block-pair minors, each O(N^(-2)), so max fixed-anchor mass ->0. Thus aggregate overlap cannot be reduced to one heavy anchor by dimension-free pigeonhole. The family has no fixed s7 spectral floor and is not a QFORM counterexample.
