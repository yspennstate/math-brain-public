## Decoupling / independent-copy reconstruction

For the cross form

`Z=F(X_I)+2 X_I^T M X_J+G(X_J)`,

Cauchy–Schwarz in `X_I`, followed by an independent copy `X'_J`, gives

`|phi_Z(t)|^2 <= E_D prod_{i in I} |hat f_i(2t(MD)_i)|`,

where `D=X_J-X'_J`. This step never conditions on `D` while treating `X_J+X'_J` as independent; the sum variable is not used. Jensen gives

`|phi_Z(t)|^4 <= E_D prod_i |hat f_i(2t(MD)_i)|^2`.

The regularized graph/BL estimate controls the last expectation by a determinant to the power `-1/2`, so taking the fourth root yields `-1/8`. Retaining an order-`k` determinant term therefore yields decay `|t|^{-k/4}`. This independently confirms the two powers that are decisive for the paper: `k=3 -> 3/4` and `k=5 -> 5/4`.
