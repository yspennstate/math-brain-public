### 3.3 Degree-explicit spherical hypercontractivity

Let `Y_s` be a pure degree-`s` spherical harmonic and let

\[
H_s(z)=\|z\|^sY_s(z/\|z\|)
\]

be its homogeneous harmonic extension. For standard Gaussian `G=R_gU`, `H_s(G)` is in the homogeneous Wiener chaos of degree `s`.

The Gaussian Ornstein-Uhlenbeck hypercontractive criterion is

\[
\|P_tF\|_q\le\|F\|_p
\quad\text{whenever}\quad
q-1\le e^{2t}(p-1),
\]

with dimension-free constant one. Historical sources are Nelson's Gaussian hypercontractivity and Gross's log-Sobolev/hypercontractivity equivalence. A current exact statement was checked against modern scholarly expositions; the historical Nelson/Gross primary PDF was not line-inspected this run, so no fictional primary-page verification is claimed.

For a degree-`s` chaos, `P_tH_s=e^{-st}H_s`. Taking `p=2` and `e^{2t}=q-1` yields

\[
\|H_s(G)\|_q\le(q-1)^{s/2}\|H_s(G)\|_2.
\]

Radial independence gives

\[
\|H_s(G)\|_q=(ER_g^{sq})^{1/q}\|Y_s\|_q,
\qquad
\|H_s(G)\|_2=(ER_g^{2s})^{1/2}\|Y_s\|_2.
\]

Since `q>=2`, monotonicity of radial `L^p` norms gives

\[
(ER_g^{2s})^{1/2}/(ER_g^{sq})^{1/q}\le1.
\]

Therefore

\[
\boxed{
\|Y_s\|_q\le(q-1)^{s/2}\|Y_s\|_2.
}
\tag{3.7}
\]

This is dimension-free and has no hidden `C_s`.

## 5. New theorem: simultaneous sharp rate over the entire growing low block
