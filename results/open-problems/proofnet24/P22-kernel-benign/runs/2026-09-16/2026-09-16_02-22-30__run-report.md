## 5. Growing-degree spherical hypercontractivity and Rosenthal budget

Let `Y_s` be a pure degree-`s` spherical harmonic and let

`H_s(z)=||z||^s Y_s(z/||z||)`

be its homogeneous harmonic extension. For a standard Gaussian `G=R_g U`, `H_s(G)` lies in homogeneous Gaussian chaos of degree `s`.

The Gaussian Ornstein-Uhlenbeck hypercontractive theorem says

`||T_rho F||_q <= ||F||_p`

whenever `1<p<=q<infinity` and

`rho <= sqrt((p-1)/(q-1))`.

A homogeneous degree-`s` chaos is an eigenfunction of `T_rho` with eigenvalue `rho^s`. Choosing `p=2` yields the exact degree-explicit estimate

`||H_s(G)||_q <= (q-1)^(s/2) ||H_s(G)||_2`.

Radial independence gives

`||H_s(G)||_q=(E R_g^(sq))^(1/q)||Y_s||_q`,

`||H_s(G)||_2=(E R_g^(2s))^(1/2)||Y_s||_2`.

Because `q>=2`, monotonicity of radial `L^p` norms shows the radial ratio is at most one, hence

`||Y_s||_q <= (q-1)^(s/2)||Y_s||_2`.              (5.1)

This is dimension-free and has no hidden `C_s`.

For the normalized degree-`s` zonal kernel `r_s=P_s/N_s`, (5.1) gives the degree-explicit moment envelope used in the KRM recursion. The factorial Mercer-tail ratio (4.2) lets the infinite tail be summed before choosing the growing proof moment.
