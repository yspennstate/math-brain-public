## 3. Exact Gaussian-kernel Mercer spectrum and infinite tail

Put `a=h^-2`. On the unit sphere,

`K_h(x,y)=exp(-a) exp(a<x,y>)`.

For normalized spherical measure and the degree-`s` population projection kernel `P_s`, one exact Mercer eigenvalue formula is

`lambda_{d,s}`

`= exp(-a) sum_{r>=0} a^(s+2r)`

`  / [2^r r! d(d+2)...(d+2(s+r)-2)]`.

Equivalently,

`lambda_{d,s}=exp(-a) Gamma(d/2) (2/a)^(d/2-1) I_{s+d/2-1}(a)`.

Primary source refreshed this run: NIST DLMF 10.25.2,

`https://dlmf.nist.gov/10.25.E2`.

It gives the positive series

`I_nu(z)=(z/2)^nu sum_{r>=0}(z^2/4)^r/[r! Gamma(nu+r+1)]`.

Termwise positivity yields for **every** `s>=0`

`lambda_{d,s+1} <= [a/(d+2s)] lambda_{d,s}`.      (3.1)

No large-order Bessel asymptotic and no fixed-degree constant is used.

The exact multiplicity ratio obeys

`N_{d,s+1}/N_{d,s} <= (d+2s)/(s+1)`.

Hence for shell masses

`M_s=N_{d,s}lambda_{d,s}`

we get the exact factorial contraction

`M_{s+1}/M_s <= a/(s+1)`.                         (3.2)

For

`tau=sum_{s>=k}M_s`,

(3.2) gives for all large `k`

`M_k <= tau <= 2 M_k`.                             (3.3)

Since the Mercer eigenvalues are decreasing in the relevant regime and `tau>=N_k lambda_k`, also

`sum_{s>=k} N_s lambda_s^2/tau^2 <= 1/N_k`.        (3.4)

Thus the **whole infinite Gaussian-kernel tail** is controlled. No finite-kernel truncation is inserted.

`E=Sym_d^0`

`A_1=(e_1 e_2^T+e_2 e_1^T)/sqrt(2)`,

`A_2=(e_1 e_3^T+e_3 e_1^T)/sqrt(2)`

and let

`P_*=span{A_1,A_2}`.

For unit `u`,

`f_1(u)=<A_1,h(u)>=sqrt(2)c_d u_1 u_2`,

`f_2(u)=<A_2,h(u)>=sqrt(2)c_d u_1 u_3`.

For independent `U,V` uniform on the sphere, the rank-two determinant is therefore exactly

`W_*(U,V)`

`=f_1(U)f_2(V)-f_2(U)f_1(V)`

`=2 c_d^2 U_1 V_1 (U_2 V_3-U_3 V_2)`

`=d(d+2) U_1 V_1 (U_2 V_3-U_3 V_2)`.             (11.1)

`E W_*^2=2`.

and the inverse-wedge squared radius is
