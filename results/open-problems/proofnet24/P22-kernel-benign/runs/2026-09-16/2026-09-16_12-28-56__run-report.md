\frac{{\rm Tr}Z^2}{n(n-1)\zeta/\tau^2}\xrightarrow{P}1,

The omitted factor `(n-1)/n` changes (6.8) only by `O(D/(nN))=o(D/N)`.

### 6.2 Off-diagonal triple term

Define, for three independent points,

\[
g(x,r,y)
:=\frac{t(x,r)t(r,y)\ell(x,y)}{\tau^2}.
\tag{6.9}
\]

Every first Hoeffding projection is exactly zero. For example, fixing `x` and integrating over `r,y`, the inner integral

\[
\int t(r,y)\ell(x,y)\,d\mu(y)=0
\]

by high/low harmonic orthogonality; the other two fixed-variable cases are the same.

Thus two ordered triple summands have zero covariance if their index triples share at most one sample index. Only `O(n^4)` pairs of triples can contribute to the variance.

For the second moment, condition on `x,y` and use Cauchy in the middle point:

\[
\int t(x,r)^2t(r,y)^2\,d\mu(r)
\le \|t(x,\cdot)\|_4^2\|t(y,\cdot)\|_4^2.
\]

Together with (6.5) and `E\ell(X,Y)^2=D`,

\[
E g(X,R,Y)^2
\le C_h\frac{9^kD}{N^2}.
\tag{6.10}
\]

Hence the off-diagonal term in (6.3), after division by `n^2`, has standard deviation at most

\[
C_h\frac{3^k\sqrt D}{N}.
\tag{6.11}
\]

Relative to `D/N`, this is `C_h3^k/\sqrt D\to0`, since `3^k` is only a fixed power of `d` while `D=d^{R+O(1)}` with `R\to\infty`.

Combining (6.2), (6.8), and (6.11),

\[
\boxed{
\frac1n{\rm Tr}P_2

\tag{6.12}

\tag{6.13}

\[
