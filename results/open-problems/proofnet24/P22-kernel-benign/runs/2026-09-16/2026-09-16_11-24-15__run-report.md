# 5. The low-coefficient trace has first correction exactly `D/n`

Define the normalized matrices

\[

\qquad

\tag{5.1}
\]

\[

=I_D+A_L+P+J.
\tag{5.2}
\]

Also, because `W=I+U`,

\[
\frac1n\Psi^TW^2\Psi
=I_D+A_L+2P+P_2.
\tag{5.3}
\]

Put

\[
E:=A_L+P+J,
\qquad
F:=A_L+2P+P_2.
\tag{5.4}
\]

Then

\[
B=\frac1n(I+E)^{-1}
\]

and the low-noise trace in (4.7) is

\[
L_d:=\operatorname{Tr}(B\Psi^TW^2\Psi B)
=\frac1n\operatorname{Tr}\left[(I+F)(I+E)^{-2}\right].
\tag{5.5}
\]

This cyclic form is useful because `E` is self-adjoint.

## 5.1 Exact first-order cancellation

On `\|E\|\le1/2`, functional calculus gives

\[
(I+E)^{-2}=I-2E+3E^2+\mathcal R_3(E),
\qquad
\|\mathcal R_3(E)\|\le C\|E\|^3.
\tag{5.6}
\]

The formal first-order trace is

\[
\operatorname{Tr}F-2\operatorname{Tr}E.
\]

Using (5.4),

\[
\operatorname{Tr}F-2\operatorname{Tr}E
=-\operatorname{Tr}A_L+\operatorname{Tr}P_2-2\operatorname{Tr}J.
\tag{5.7}
\]

The dangerous linear terms in `P`, hence in the tail fluctuation `U=W-I`, cancel **exactly**. The sample-covariance term cancels because

\[
\operatorname{Tr}A_L=0
\]

exactly by the addition theorem. The only survivors in (5.7) are already second-order (`P_2`) or the very small RKHS penalty (`J`).

This cancellation is the structural reason a mere rank bound was too crude in the preceding report.

## 5.2 Quantitative bounds at the `r_d^2` scale

\qquad

\tag{5.8}
\]

Moreover
