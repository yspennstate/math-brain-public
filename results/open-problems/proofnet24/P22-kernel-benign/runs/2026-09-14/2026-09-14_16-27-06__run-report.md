## 3.1 Fixed-radius feature

Let

\[
E_D=\operatorname{Sym}_D^0,
\qquad
m=\dim E_D=\frac{(D-1)(D+2)}2.
\]

For \(u\) uniform on \(S^{D-1}\), define

\[
h_D(u)
=
c_D
\left(
uu^\top-\frac1D I
\right),
\qquad
c_D=\sqrt{\frac{D(D+2)}2}.
\]

Then \(h_D\) is isotropic on \(E_D\).

Fix traceless \(A\in\operatorname{Sym}_D\) with

\[
\|A\|_F=1.
\]

Define the Jordan operator

\[
J_A(X)
=
P_0\left(\frac{AX+XA}{2}\right)
\]

on \(E_D\).

## 3.2 Hilbert–Schmidt norm

Diagonalize \(A=\operatorname{diag}(a_1,\ldots,a_D)\).

On the full real symmetric space, the diagonal basis vector \(E_{ii}\) has eigenvalue \(a_i\), while the normalized off-diagonal symmetric basis vector has eigenvalue

\[
\frac{a_i+a_j}{2}.
\]

Hence

\[
\begin{aligned}
\|L_A\|_{HS}^2
&=
\sum_i a_i^2
+
\frac14
\sum_{i<j}(a_i+a_j)^2.
\end{aligned}
\]

For \(\operatorname{Tr}A=0\),

\[
\sum_{i<j}(a_i+a_j)^2
=
(D-2)\sum_i a_i^2.
\]

Therefore

\[
\|L_A\|_{HS}^2
=
\frac{D+2}{4}\|A\|_F^2.
\]

The scalar-to-traceless and traceless-to-scalar blocks each have squared Hilbert–Schmidt norm

\[
\frac{\|A\|_F^2}{D}.
\]

Thus

\[
\boxed{
\|J_A\|_{HS}^2
=
\left(
\frac{D+2}{4}-\frac2D
\right)
\|A\|_F^2
=
\frac{(D-2)(D+4)}{4D}
\|A\|_F^2.
}
\tag{3.1}
\]

This part of P04 is exact.

## 3.3 Quadratic fluctuation

Since

\[
\left(
uu^\top-\frac1DI
\right)^2
=
\left(1-\frac2D\right)uu^\top+\frac1{D^2}I,
\]

and \(A\) is traceless,

\[
\begin{aligned}
\langle h_D,J_Ah_D\rangle
&=
\operatorname{Tr}(Ah_D^2)\\
&=
\frac{D(D+2)}2
\left(1-\frac2D\right)
u^\top Au\\
&=
\boxed{
\frac{D^2-4}{2}u^\top Au.
}
\tag{3.2}
\]

For traceless \(A\),

\[
\mathbb E(u^\top Au)^2
=
\frac{2\|A\|_F^2}{D(D+2)}.
\]

Therefore

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{Var}
\langle h_D,J_Ah_D\rangle
=
\frac{(D-2)^2(D+2)}{2D}
\|A\|_F^2.
}
\tag{3.3}
\]

Dividing by (3.1),

\[
\boxed{
\frac{
\operatorname{Var}
\langle h_D,J_Ah_D\rangle
}{
\|J_A\|_{HS}^2
}
=
\frac{2(D-2)(D+2)}{D+4}
\sim2D.
}
\tag{3.4}
\]

Thus Frobenius-normalizing the test operator leaves standard deviation of order \(\sqrt D\).

## 3.4 Constant-probability violation

Take \(D\) even and

\[
A
=
\operatorname{diag}
\left(
\underbrace{D^{-1/2},\ldots,D^{-1/2}}_{D/2},
\underbrace{-D^{-1/2},\ldots,-D^{-1/2}}_{D/2}
\right).
\]

Put

\[
S=\sum_{i\le D/2}u_i^2.
\]

Then

\[
S\sim\operatorname{Beta}(D/4,D/4),
\qquad
X=2S-1.
\]

Direct beta moments give

\[
\mathbb EX^2=\frac2{D+2},
\]
\[
\mathbb EX^4
=
\frac{12}{(D+2)(D+6)}.
\]

Paley–Zygmund applied to \(X^2\) at one-half its mean gives

\[
\Pr\left\{
|X|\ge\frac1{\sqrt{D+2}}
\right\}
\ge
\frac{D+6}{12(D+2)}
\ge
\frac1{12}.
\]

After normalizing \(J_A\) to Hilbert–Schmidt norm one,

\[
Q_{B_A}
=
(D+2)
\sqrt{\frac{D-2}{D+4}}
\,X.
\]

Therefore with probability at least \(1/12\),

\[
\boxed{
|Q_{B_A}|
\ge
\sqrt{D+2}
\sqrt{\frac{D-2}{D+4}}
=
\Theta(\sqrt D).
}
\]

Since the feature dimension is

\[
m=\Theta(D^2),
\]

this is a constant-probability fluctuation of size \(m^{1/4}\) against a deterministic Frobenius-unit test operator.

P04 is therefore correct: the Ma–Misiakiewicz optimal quadratic-form concentration hypothesis fails for this row law.

## 4.2 Exact conditional quadratic form

Put

\[
K=A_GW^{-2}A_G^\top.
\]

Then

\[
M-I=C_G^\top K C_G.
\]

For iid Gaussian columns \(c_1,\ldots,c_d\), standard Wick calculation gives:

- diagonal quadratic form:
  \[
  \operatorname{Var}(c_j^\top Kc_j)
  =
  2\operatorname{Tr}K^2;
  \]
- off-diagonal bilinear form:
  \[
  \mathbb E(c_j^\top Kc_k)^2
  =
  \operatorname{Tr}K^2
  \qquad(j\ne k).
  \]

Therefore

\[
\boxed{
\mathbb E\left[
\left\|
M-(1+\operatorname{Tr}K)I_d
\right\|_F^2
\mid A_G
\right]
=
d(d+1)\operatorname{Tr}K^2.
}
\tag{4.1}
\]

The nonzero eigenvalues of \(K\) equal those of \(W^{-1}\), so

\[
\operatorname{Tr}K^2
=
\operatorname{Tr}W^{-2}.
\]

## 4.3 Inverse-Wishart moments

Here

\[
W\sim\operatorname{Wishart}_m(N,I),
\qquad
N-m-1=d.
\]

The standard inverse-Wishart second moments, covered by Matsumoto's general formulas, are

\[
\mathbb E(W^{-1})_{ii}
=
\frac1d,
\]

\[
\operatorname{Var}(W^{-1})_{ii}
=
\frac{2}{d^2(d-2)},
\]

and, for \(i\ne j\),

\[
\mathbb E(W^{-1})_{ij}^2
=
\frac1{(d+1)d(d-2)}.
\]

Summing all squared entries gives

\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathbb E\operatorname{Tr}W^{-2}
&=
m
\left[
\frac1{d^2}
+
\frac{2}{d^2(d-2)}
\right]\\
&\quad+
\frac{m(m-1)}{(d+1)d(d-2)}.
\end{aligned}
\]

Substituting

\[
m=\frac{d(d-1)}2-1
\]

simplifies exactly to

\[
\boxed{
\mathbb E\operatorname{Tr}W^{-2}
=
\frac{(d-1)(d+2)}{4d}.
}
\tag{4.2}
\]

The algebra has been independently checked.

### Theorem P22-T4-NOSCALAR-01 — PROVED

In the Gaussian first-peel benchmark above, for every \(d\ge3\),

\[
\boxed{
\mathbb E
\left[
\frac1d
\inf_{a\in\mathbb R}
\left\|
\frac Md-aI_d
\right\|_F^2
\right]
=
\frac{(d-1)^2(d+2)^2}{4d^4}.
}
\tag{5.1}
\]

Hence

\[
\boxed{
\lim_{d\to\infty}
\mathbb E
\left[
\frac1d
\inf_{a\in\mathbb R}
\left\|
\frac Md-aI_d
\right\|_F^2
\right]

}
\tag{5.2}
\]

Thus not even the best scalar selected after observing \(M\) can approximate the graph metric in normalized Frobenius mean square.

## Proof

Let

\[
S=M-I=C_G^\top KC_G.
\]

Pointwise in \(M\),

\[
\inf_{a\in\mathbb R}
\left\|
\frac Md-aI
\right\|_F^2
=
\frac1{d^2}
\left\|
M-\frac{\operatorname{Tr}M}{d}I
\right\|_F^2.
\tag{5.3}
\]

Because adding the identity does not change the traceless part,

\[
M-\frac{\operatorname{Tr}M}{d}I
=
S-\frac{\operatorname{Tr}S}{d}I.
\tag{5.4}
\]

Conditional on \(A_G\),

\[
\mathbb E\|S\|_F^2
=
d(\operatorname{Tr}K)^2
+
d(d+1)\operatorname{Tr}K^2.
\tag{5.5}
\]

Also

\[
\operatorname{Tr}S
=
\sum_{j=1}^d c_j^\top Kc_j,
\]

so

\[
\mathbb E[
(\operatorname{Tr}S)^2
\mid A_G]
=
d^2(\operatorname{Tr}K)^2
+
2d\operatorname{Tr}K^2.
\tag{5.6}
\]

Therefore

\[
\begin{aligned}
&
\mathbb E
\left[
\left\|
S-\frac{\operatorname{Tr}S}{d}I
\right\|_F^2
\middle|A_G
\right]\\
&\qquad=
\mathbb E\|S\|_F^2
-
\frac1d
\mathbb E(\operatorname{Tr}S)^2\\
&\qquad=
\left[
d(d+1)-2
\right]
\operatorname{Tr}K^2\\
&\qquad=
\boxed{
(d-1)(d+2)\operatorname{Tr}W^{-2}.
}
\tag{5.7}
\end{aligned}
\]

Now divide by the normalization in (5.3) and the outer \(1/d\):

\[
\begin{aligned}
&
\mathbb E
\left[
\frac1d
\inf_a
\left\|
\frac Md-aI
\right\|_F^2
\right]\\
&\qquad=
\frac{(d-1)(d+2)}{d^3}
\mathbb E\operatorname{Tr}W^{-2}.
\end{aligned}
\]

Using (4.2),

\[
\boxed{
\mathbb E
\left[
\frac1d
\inf_a
\left\|
\frac Md-aI
\right\|_F^2
\right]
=
\frac{(d-1)^2(d+2)^2}{4d^4}.
}
\]

This proves (5.1)–(5.2).
