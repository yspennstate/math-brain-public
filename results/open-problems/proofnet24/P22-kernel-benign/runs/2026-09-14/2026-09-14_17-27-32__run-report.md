## 12.1 Koltchinskii–Mendelson

Vladimir Koltchinskii and Shahar Mendelson,  
*Bounding the smallest singular value of a random matrix without concentration*,  
arXiv:1312.3580; International Mathematics Research Notices.

  \[

\[
\lambda_{\min}(\Gamma)\ge c_3(L)
