# 3. Audit of the exact Gaussian-kernel Mercer spectrum

## `P22-B1` / `P22-B1b` — PROVED / PASS

The Mercer expansion is
\[
K_h(x,y)
=
\sum_{s\ge0}\lambda_{d,s}P_s(x,y),
\]
with
\[
\boxed{
\lambda_{d,s}
=
e^{-\gamma}
\sum_{r\ge0}
\frac{
\gamma^{s+2r}
}{
2^r r!\,
d(d+2)\cdots(d+2(s+r)-2)
}.
}
\tag{3.1}
\]

### Independent derivation

Let
\[
H_s(ru)=r^sY_s(u)
\]
be the homogeneous harmonic extension of \(Y_s\in\mathcal H_{d,s}\).

For \(G\sim N(0,I_d)\),
\[
E\left[
H_s(G)
e^{t\langle x,G\rangle-t^2/2}
\right]
=
t^sH_s(x).
\]

Comparing the coefficient of \(t^{s+2r}\),
\[
E[
\langle x,G\rangle^{s+2r}H_s(G)
]
=
\frac{(s+2r)!}{2^rr!}H_s(x).
\]

Writing
\[
G=\mathcal R U
\]
with \(U\) uniform on the sphere and \(\mathcal R\) independent,
\[
E\mathcal R^{2(s+r)}
=
d(d+2)\cdots(d+2(s+r)-2).
\]

Projecting the Taylor series of
\[
e^{\gamma\langle x,U\rangle}
\]
onto degree \(s\) yields (3.1).

Equivalently, the modified-Bessel form is
\[
\lambda_{d,s}
=
e^{-\gamma}
\Gamma(d/2)
(2/\gamma)^{d/2-1}
I_{s+d/2-1}(\gamma),
\]
and its positive power series is exactly (3.1).

### Whole-tail monotonicity

Compare every \(r\)-term at degrees \(s+1\) and \(s\):
\[
\frac{
\gamma^{s+1+2r}/
[d(d+2)\cdots(d+2(s+r))]
}{
\gamma^{s+2r}/
[d(d+2)\cdots(d+2(s+r)-2)]
}
=
\frac{\gamma}{d+2(s+r)}
\le
\frac{\gamma}{d+2s}.
\]

Therefore
\[
\boxed{
\lambda_{d,s+1}
\le
\frac{\gamma}{d+2s}\lambda_{d,s}
}
\tag{3.2}
\]
for every \(s\), with no fixed-degree restriction.

This exact monotonicity is later used both in the KRM correlation-kernel comparison and in population tail-noise leakage.

### Adversarial check

The ratio
\[
\lambda_{s+1}/\lambda_s
\]
must not be confused with the trace-mass ratio
\[
\tau_{s+1}/\tau_s,
\qquad
\tau_s=N_s\lambda_s.
\]

The multiplicity \(N_s\) nearly cancels the \(d^{-1}\) Mercer drop in the relevant range. The proof keeps these two quantities separate.
