# 4. Growing-degree spherical hypercontractivity

For every pure degree-`s` spherical harmonic `Y_s` and `p>=2`,

\[
\boxed{
\|Y_s\|_p
\le
(p-1)^{s/2}\|Y_s\|_2.
}
\]

The proof retains the full degree dependence.

Lift `Y_s` homogeneously to Gaussian space:

\[
F_s(g)=\|g\|^sY_s(g/\|g\|).
\]

Because the homogeneous lift is a degree-`s` harmonic polynomial, it belongs to the `s`-th homogeneous Wiener chaos. Nelson/Gross Ornstein-Uhlenbeck hypercontractivity gives

\[
\|F_s\|_p\le(p-1)^{s/2}\|F_s\|_2.
\]

Writing a standard Gaussian as `g=RU` with `R` independent of `U`,

\[
\|F_s\|_p=\|R^s\|_p\|Y_s\|_p,
\qquad
\|F_s\|_2=\|R^s\|_2\|Y_s\|_2.
\]

Since `p>=2`, monotonicity of `L^p` norms gives `||R^s||_p >= ||R^s||_2`, so no extra radial factor is lost and the displayed spherical estimate follows.

There is no hidden `C_s`.

A current statement of Nelson hypercontractivity for a homogeneous Wiener chaos gives exactly the factor `(p-1)^{s/2}`; no fixed-degree constant is being imported.
