## Peer audit of P04 fixed-order DPP theorem

### `P22-AUDIT-P04-FIXED-R-DPP-22` — PASS

P04 claims that for every fixed \(r\),
\[
\chi^2(P_m^{(r)}\|\mu^r)=O_r(d^{-4}).
\]
For the pure-harmonic projection kernel,
\[
\rho(u,v):=\frac{K(u,v)}m
=
\frac{d(u\cdot v)^2-1}{d-1}.
\]
If \(T=(u\cdot v)^2\), then
\[
T\sim{\rm Beta}\!\left(\frac12,\frac{d-1}{2}\right),
\qquad
ET^q=\frac{(1/2)_q}{(d/2)_q}=O_q(d^{-q}),
\]
hence \(E|\rho|^q=O_q(d^{-q})\).

The labeled \(r\)-point density is
\[
f_{r,d}=c_{r,m}\det(I+R),\qquad
c_{r,m}=\frac{m^r}{(m)_r}=1+O_r(d^{-2}),
\]
where \(R\) has zero diagonal and off-diagonal \(\rho_{ij}\). In the determinant expansion every nonidentity permutation moves \(s\ge2\) indices. Generalized Hölder gives \(L^2\)-norm \(O_r(d^{-s})\) for each such term, so
\[
\|\det(I+R)-1\|_2=O_r(d^{-2}),
\]
and therefore
\[
\|f_{r,d}-1\|_2=O_r(d^{-2}).
\]
Thus
\[
\boxed{\chi^2(P_m^{(r)}\|\mu^r)=O_r(d^{-4}).}
\]
No independence among pairwise inner products is assumed. P04's theorem passes.

## New theorem: `P22-T4-SUBLINEAR-DPP-INVISIBILITY-22`

Let \(P_m\) be the full ordered rank-\(m\) projection DPP, with
\[
m=\frac{(d-1)(d+2)}2,
\]
and let \(P_m^{(r)}\) be its labeled \(r\)-point marginal.

Because the normalized Gram matrix
\[
G_r=[K(u_i,u_j)/m]_{i,j\le r}
\]
is PSD with diagonal one, Hadamard gives
\[
0\le\det G_r\le1.
\]
The marginal density is
\[
f_r=\frac{\det K_r}{(m)_r}
=
\frac{m^r}{(m)_r}\det G_r.
\]
Hence
\[
0\le f_r\le c_{m,r}:=\frac{m^r}{(m)_r}.
\]
Since \(Ef_r=1\),
\[
f_r^2\le c_{m,r}f_r,
\]
and therefore
\[
\boxed{
\chi^2(P_m^{(r)}\|\mu^r)
\le
\frac{m^r}{(m)_r}-1.
}
\]

Now fix a deleted point \(u\). The surviving process is the rank-\((m-1)\) projection DPP with kernel
\[
K^{(u)}(x,y)
=
K(x,y)-\frac{K(x,u)K(u,y)}m.
\]
It is PSD and
\[
K^{(u)}(x,x)\le m.
\]
Its labeled \(r\)-point density \(g_{u,r}\) therefore satisfies
\[
0\le g_{u,r}\le \frac{m^r}{(m-1)_r},
\]
so uniformly in \(u\),
\[
\boxed{
\chi^2(Q_u^{(r)}\|\mu^r)
\le
\frac{m^r}{(m-1)_r}-1.
}
\]

For \(r\le m/2\),
\[
\log\frac{m^r}{(m)_r}
\le
\frac{r(r-1)}{2(m-r+1)},
\]
and
\[
\log\frac{m^r}{(m-1)_r}
\le
\frac{r(r+1)}{2(m-r)}.
\]
Since \(m\sim d^2/2\), every sequence \(r=o(d)\) satisfies
\[
\boxed{
\chi^2(P_m^{(r)}\|\mu^r)\to0,
\qquad
\sup_u\chi^2(Q_u^{(r)}\|\mu^r)\to0.
}
\]
Consequently,
\[
\boxed{
\sup_u\|Q_u^{(r)}-\mu^r\|_{\rm TV}=O(r/d)=o(1).
}
\]

There is also an information-theoretic form. If \(Y\) is one labeled DPP point and \(X_{1:r}\) are \(r\) other labeled points, then
\[
I(Y;X_{1:r})
\le
D_{\rm KL}(P_{Y,X_{1:r}}\|\mu^{r+1})
\le
\log\frac{m^{r+1}}{(m)_{r+1}}
=
O(r^2/d^2).
\]
Thus
\[
\boxed{
r=o(d)
\Longrightarrow
I(Y;X_{1:r})=o(1).
}
\]

P20 proves that the deleted point versus the entire remaining configuration has order-one chi-square dependence, while P04 proves the global lower bound \(\Xi_d\ge2-o(1)\). The new theorem therefore shows that this dependence is genuinely collective: every sublinear labeled subset is asymptotically informationless.
