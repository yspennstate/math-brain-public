# 3. Exact Gaussian-kernel Mercer spectrum and whole-tail ratio

Set `a=h^(-2)`. On the sphere,

`K_h(x,y)=e^(-a) exp(a x·y)`.

The exact degree-`s` Mercer eigenvalue is

`lambda_{d,s}=e^(-a) sum_{r>=0} a^(s+2r) / [2^r r! d(d+2)...(d+2(s+r)-2)]`.

Equivalently,

`lambda_{d,s}=e^(-a) Gamma(d/2) (2/a)^(d/2-1) I_{s+d/2-1}(a)`.

NIST DLMF 10.25.2 gives the positive power series

`I_nu(z)=(z/2)^nu sum_{r>=0} (z^2/4)^r/[r! Gamma(nu+r+1)]`.

For positive real `a`, every term is nonnegative. Comparing corresponding terms gives, for every `s>=0`,

`lambda_{d,s+1} <= [a/(d+2s)] lambda_{d,s}`.

This is an exact whole-tail inequality, uniform at the growing cutoff.

P26 additionally proves the exact Mercer **mass** contraction. With

`w_s=N_{d,s} lambda_{d,s}`,

the exact multiplicity ratio plus the eigenvalue ratio gives

`w_{s+1}/w_s <= a/(s+1)`.

Thus the tail diagonal is genuinely first-shell dominated:

`w_k <= tau_k=sum_{s>=k}w_s <= w_k[1+O_h(1/k)]`.

It also gives

`sum_{s>=k}N_{d,s}lambda_{d,s}^2 <= lambda_{d,k} tau_k`

and hence

`[sum_{s>=k}N_{d,s}lambda_{d,s}^2]/tau_k^2 <= 1/N_{d,k}`.

No multiplicity explosion is hiding beyond the cutoff.

# 4. Growing-degree spherical hypercontractivity

For every pure degree-`s` spherical harmonic `Y_s` and every `p>=2`,

`||Y_s||_p <= (p-1)^(s/2) ||Y_s||_2`.

The proof is dimension-free and degree-explicit:

1. Lift homogeneously to Gaussian space:
   `F_s(g)=||g||^s Y_s(g/||g||)`.
2. Since the lift is harmonic and homogeneous of degree `s`, it lies in the `s`th Gaussian chaos.
3. Nelson/Gross Ornstein-Uhlenbeck hypercontractivity gives
   `||F_s||_p <= (p-1)^(s/2)||F_s||_2`.
4. Write `g=RU` with Gaussian radius `R` independent of uniform direction `U`.
5. Factor the radial and angular norms. Since `p>=2`, `||R^s||_p >= ||R^s||_2`, so dividing can only improve the constant.

Thus there is no hidden `C_s` and no fixed-degree restriction.
