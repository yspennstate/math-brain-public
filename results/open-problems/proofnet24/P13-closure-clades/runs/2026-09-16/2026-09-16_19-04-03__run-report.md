## 5. Adversarial self-check of the three-region Hilbert tail

The exact starting point retained from the proof is
\[
E\|Q_RZ_n\|_2^2
\le C\sum_{j=R+1}^{n}a(n,j)\frac{1+\log(j+1)}j.
\tag{5.1}
\]
Put
\[
d=\frac{\sqrt5-1}{2},
\qquad
\alpha=d^2=1-d=\frac{3-\sqrt5}{2}.
\]
For `n>R`, the proof partitions `j>R` into three regions.

**Region I, `j<=n^d`.** The BCM long-time approximation controls `a(n,j)-a(j)` in this range. Since `a(j)=O((\log j)/j)`, multiplying by `(1+\log j)/j` produces a summable limiting contribution of order
\[
\sum_{j>R}\frac{(1+\log j)^2}{j^2}
=O(R^{-1}\log^2R),
\]
and the approximation-error term is bounded at the weaker target rate `O(R^{-alpha} log R)` used by the report.

**Region II, `j>n^d` and `n-j>=j^d`.** With `m=floor(j^d)`, monotonicity in the starting state and the BCM short-time estimate give
\[
a(n,j)\le a(j+m,j)\le \frac{C}{j^d h_j}.
\]
Since `h_j` is comparable to `1+log j`, the summand in (5.1) is `O(j^{-1-d})`, and the region contributes `O(R^{-d})`, which is smaller than the target because `d>alpha`.

**Region III, `n-j<j^d`.** The partition forces `j>=n/2`. There are at most `n^d+1` such integers and the factor `(1+log(j+1))/j` is `O(log n/n)`, so this region is
\[
O(n^{d-1}\log n)=O(n^{-\alpha}\log n).
\]
Since this region has `j>R`, also `n>R`, and monotonicity of `x^{-alpha}log x` after a fixed threshold gives `O(R^{-alpha}log R)` (with small `R` absorbed into the constant).

The exponent identities are exact and the region partition is exhaustive. I found no candidate counterexample to the quantifier structure. The only imported non-elementary ingredient is the stated BCM harmonic-descent estimate; that hypothesis remains an explicit checkpoint for the independent audit. **Verdict of this section: SELF-CHECK PASS, closure credit = 0.**
