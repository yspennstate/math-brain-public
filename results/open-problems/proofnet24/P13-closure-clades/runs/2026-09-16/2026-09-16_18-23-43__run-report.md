### 3.2 Exact full joint recursion and maximal covariance block

The exact PGF recursion is

\[

Writing

\[
\mu_r(n)=EN_n(r),\quad

\[
\eta_{rs}(n)=\frac1n\operatorname{Cov}_{I\sim q_n}

C_{rs}(n)=\frac1n\operatorname{Cov}(N_n(r),N_n(s)),

the exact harmonic-descent Green formula is

\[
\boxed{
C_{rs}(n)=\sum_{j=2}^na(n,j)\eta_{rs}(j).

\tag{3.4}

\sup_{r,s\ge2}|C_{rs}(n)-\Sigma_{rs}|=O(n^{-\alpha}).
\tag{3.7}
