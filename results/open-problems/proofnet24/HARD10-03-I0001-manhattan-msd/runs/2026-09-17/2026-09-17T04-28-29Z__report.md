## 5. B1 — change category: from nonselfadjoint generator to a positive odd-chaos block-Jacobi operator

\[
H_\lambda:=\lambda-S.

-(\psi,H_\lambda\psi)

\right\}.
\]

Define the positive self-adjoint form operator

\[
\boxed{
K_\lambda:=H_\lambda+A^*H_\lambda^{-1}A.
}
\tag{5.1}
\]

Then, in form sense,

\[
\boxed{
R(\lambda)=(\phi,K_\lambda^{-1}\phi)_\pi.
}
\tag{5.2}
\]

This is simply completion of the square in the positive quadratic form. It is useful because `K_lambda`, unlike `G`, exposes an exact band structure in Fourier–Walsh chaos.

Let `C_n` denote the Walsh-chaos subspace spanned by degree-`n` products of distinct orientation variables, and let `P_n` be its orthogonal projection. Translations permute orientation variables, so `S` preserves degree. A term of `A` first translates a monomial, preserving its degree, and then multiplies by one local orientation variable; because every orientation variable squares to one, this multiplication toggles that coordinate and changes the Walsh degree by exactly `+1` or `-1`. Hence

\[
S:\mathcal C_n\to\mathcal C_n,
\qquad
A:\mathcal C_n\to\mathcal C_{n-1}\oplus\mathcal C_{n+1}.
\]

Therefore

\[
K_\lambda:\mathcal C_n
\to
\mathcal C_{n-2}\oplus\mathcal C_n\oplus\mathcal C_{n+2}.
\]

In particular, the odd and even chaos sectors are exact reducing subspaces. Since `phi` has degree one, only the odd sector is relevant to (5.2).

For a precise block formula define

\[
D_n:=P_{n+1}AP_n:\mathcal C_n\to\mathcal C_{n+1}.

Skew-adjointness of `A` gives

\[
P_{n-1}AP_n=-D_{n-1}^*.

Writing `H_n=P_nH_lambda P_n`, direct expansion of `A^*H_lambda^{-1}A` gives, on the cylinder core,

\tag{5.4}
