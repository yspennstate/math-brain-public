### Proof

Conditional on `F_(j-1)`, `X_(j,b)` are iid in `[0,B]`. Since

```text
X^2<=B X,
```

one has

```text
Var(X|F_(j-1))<=B q_j.
```

The one-sided Bernstein lower-tail inequality gives, with conditional failure probability at most `exp(-ell_g)`,

```text
q_j-hat q_j
<= sqrt(2 B q_j ell_g/M)
   + 2B ell_g/(3M).                                      (5.5)
```

```text
y^2 - sqrt(2a)y - (hat q_j+2a/3) <=0.
```

The positive root is

```text
y
<= sqrt(a/2)+sqrt(hat q_j+7a/6).                        (5.6)
```

### Predictable smaller envelopes

# 9. Two exact falsifications

## 9.1 `P19-GHOST-PLUGIN-CE-35` — PROVED

A raw plug-in variance estimate cannot replace the UCB correction in `(5.3)`.

Fix a coordinate and let

```text
X=(Delta_j H_T)^2
=B with probability eps,
=0 with probability 1-eps.
```

Then

```text
q_j=eps B.
```

Take

```text
eps=1/(2M).
```

For `M` iid ghost draws,

```text
P{hat q_j=0}
=(1-1/(2M))^M
>=1/2.                                                    (9.1)
```

Yet

```text
q_j=B/(2M)>0.                                             (9.2)
```

Thus with constant probability the naive plug-in reports zero predictable variance when the true variance is positive. Any valid finite-ghost certificate needs an estimation error term, a prior structural tail/range assumption, or both.

The same example shows the sample-complexity issue: to obtain a relative-error variance certificate for rare influence probability `eps`, one necessarily needs `M` on the order of `1/eps` without stronger structure.

## 9.2 `P19-GHOST-FROZEN-MASK-CE-35` — PROVED

Ghost retraining must recompute adaptive hard pruning; freezing the original support can miss the dominant influence.

Let `k=1`, let the feature covariance be exactly

```text
G=I_2,
```

and consider neighboring trained readouts

```text

C_-=(1-eps,1+eps),
0<eps<1.
```

Exact top-1 pruning gives

```text
H_1(C_+)=(1+eps,0),
H_1(C_-)=(0,1+eps).
```

Hence the true fresh prediction RMS difference is

```text
||[H_1(C_+)-H_1(C_-)] G^(1/2)||_F
```

If a ghost calculation incorrectly freezes the support selected by `C_+`, it compares

```text

```

with

```text

```

and reports only

```text

```

As `eps ->0`, the frozen-mask influence tends to zero while the actual adaptive hard-pruning influence tends to `sqrt(2)`.

This is the same-matrix dependence issue in finite-resampling form. It is not repaired by calling the frozen mask “iid”.
