## Main new theorem

### `P19-ALIGNED-SLL-TRANSFER-38` — **PROVED**

Let

\[
\mathcal X_n=\begin{pmatrix}0&X_n\\X_n^*&0\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
R_n(\zeta)=(\zeta I-\mathcal X_n)^{-1}.
\]

Let

\[
\mathcal E_n=\operatorname{diag}(U_n,V_n),
\qquad
\mathcal F_n=\operatorname{diag}(U_n^0,V_n^0)
\]

be two block frames with orthonormal columns of the same dimension. Suppose that for a compact

\[
K\Subset\mathbb C\setminus[-b,b]
\]

the proxy frame satisfies

\[
\sup_{\zeta\in K}
\|\mathcal F_n^*R_n(\zeta)\mathcal F_n-\mathcal M(\zeta)\|_{\rm op}
\le \varepsilon_n,
\]

where

\[
\mathcal M(\zeta)
=\operatorname{diag}(\phi(\zeta)I_{r_n},\widetilde\phi(\zeta)I_{r_n}).
\]

Let

\[
Q_n=\operatorname{diag}(Q_{L,n},Q_{R,n})
\]

be block-unitary and put

\[
\delta_n:=\|\mathcal E_n-\mathcal F_nQ_n\|_{\rm op},
\qquad
R_K:=\sup_{\zeta\in K}\|R_n(\zeta)\|_{\rm op}.
\]

Then **pathwise**

\[
\boxed{
\sup_{\zeta\in K}
\|\mathcal E_n^*R_n(\zeta)\mathcal E_n-\mathcal M(\zeta)\|_{\rm op}
\le
\varepsilon_n+2R_K\delta_n.
}
\tag{0.1}
\]

Because `M` is scalar on each left/right block, it commutes with every such `Q_n`. Hence an honest deterministic/independent proxy subspace local law plus `delta_n=o_P(1)` transfers the source's holomorphic SLL to the actual frame even if the actual frame is chosen from the same realized matrix.

For bounded spike strengths, this immediately supplies the deformation-weighted compatibility required by the source, so the August v1 projector theorem applies on every stable outlier window. This yields a rigorous path from an honest proxy frame to **cluster singular-projector and sign-free cross-projector control for an adaptive trained/pruned signal**.

## Sharp counterexample

### `P19-ADAPTIVE-BBP-COLLAPSE-CE-38` — **PROVED**

Let `X_n` be Gaussian Marchenko–Pastur noise and let `(s_1,u_1,v_1)` be its top singular triplet. Choose the signal **after seeing the same noise matrix**:

\[
P_n=\theta u_1v_1^*,\qquad \theta>0.
\]

Then

\[
W_n=X_n+P_n
=U\operatorname{diag}(s_1+\theta,s_2,\ldots)V^*.
\]

Therefore the global empirical squared-singular-value law is still MP—the perturbation changes exactly one singular value—but

\[
\sigma_1(W_n)=s_1+\theta\to 1+\sqrt c+\theta
\]

for **every** fixed `theta>0`, including all `theta<c^{1/4}` that are subcritical for an independent spike. The planted left/right overlaps equal one identically. Thus an MP-looking bulk does not protect the independent-spike BBP threshold or overlap laws against same-matrix adaptive frame selection.

The source SLL itself fails on this frame. With

\[
\mathcal E_n=\operatorname{diag}(u_1,v_1),
\]

the two-dimensional signal subspace is invariant under `mathcal X_n`, and for real `zeta>b` away from the edge,

\[
\mathcal E_n^*R_n(\zeta)\mathcal E_n
=
\frac1{\zeta^2-s_1^2}
\begin{pmatrix}
\zeta&s_1\\s_1&\zeta
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

The source scalar equivalent has zero off-diagonal blocks. Hence

\[
\boxed{
\|\mathcal E_n^*R_n(\zeta)\mathcal E_n-\mathcal M(\zeta)\|_{\rm op}
\ge
\frac{s_1}{\zeta^2-s_1^2}
\to
\frac{1+\sqrt c}{\zeta^2-(1+\sqrt c)^2}>0.
}
\tag{0.2}
\]

So even rank-one Gaussian MP noise gives an exact failure of the independent/deterministic-frame SLL when the frame is selected spectrally from the same matrix. This is a direct mathematical reason that trained-matrix pruning cannot be replaced by an iid mask or an independent spike merely because the sparsity rate and global ESD look correct.

# 4. `P19-ALIGNED-SLL-TRANSFER-38` — full proof

Let

\[
R(\zeta)=(\zeta I-\mathcal X_n)^{-1}.
\]

Write

\[
\widetilde{\mathcal F}=\mathcal FQ,
\qquad
D=\mathcal E-\widetilde{\mathcal F}.
\]

Because `E` and `FQ` have orthonormal columns,

\[
\|\mathcal E\|_{\rm op}
=
\|\widetilde{\mathcal F}\|_{\rm op}=1,
\qquad
\|D\|_{\rm op}=\delta.
\]

Since

\[
\mathcal M
=
\operatorname{diag}(\phi I,\widetilde\phi I)
\]

and `Q` is block-unitary,

\[
Q^*\mathcal M Q=\mathcal M.
\]

Therefore

\[
\|\widetilde{\mathcal F}^*R\widetilde{\mathcal F}-\mathcal M\|
=
\|Q^*(\mathcal F^*R\mathcal F-\mathcal M)Q\|
\le\varepsilon.
\]

Now use the exact identity

\[
\mathcal E^*R\mathcal E
-
\widetilde{\mathcal F}^*R\widetilde{\mathcal F}
=
D^*R\mathcal E
+
\widetilde{\mathcal F}^*RD.
\tag{4.1}
\]

Hence

\[
\|\mathcal E^*R\mathcal E
-
\widetilde{\mathcal F}^*R\widetilde{\mathcal F}\|
\le
2\delta\|R\|.
\]

Taking the supremum over `zeta in K` and applying the triangle inequality yields (0.1).

No independence statement is used in this deterministic step. The actual frame may be an arbitrary measurable function of `X_n`.

Under the source global edge assumption and fixed `K` away from `[-b,b]`, the resolvent norm is `O_P(1)`: on the event that the spectrum of `mathcal X_n` lies in an arbitrarily small neighborhood of `[-b,b]`,

\[
R_K
\le
\operatorname{dist}(K,\operatorname{spec}\mathcal X_n)^{-1}
=O(1).
\]

Thus

\[
\varepsilon_n\to0,
\qquad
\delta_n\to0
\]

in probability imply the actual-frame holomorphic SLL.

### Quantitative probability form

For any deterministic bounds `e_n,d_n,L_n` and events

\[
A_n=\{\varepsilon_{\rm proxy}\le e_n\},
\quad
B_n=\{\delta_n\le d_n\},
\quad
C_n=\{R_K\le L_n\},
\]

on `A_n cap B_n cap C_n`,

\[
\varepsilon_{\rm actual}
\le e_n+2L_nd_n.
\]

Therefore

\[
P\{\varepsilon_{\rm actual}>e_n+2L_nd_n\}
\le
P(A_n^c)+P(B_n^c)+P(C_n^c).
\tag{4.2}
\]

This is the exact finite-probability composition needed for later source audits.

# 6. `P19-ADAPTIVE-BBP-COLLAPSE-CE-38` — full proof

Let Gaussian MP noise have singular-value decomposition

\[
X_n=\sum_{j=1}^n s_j u_jv_j^*,
\qquad s_1\ge s_2\ge\cdots.
\]

Set

\[
P_n=\theta u_1v_1^*,
\qquad \theta>0.
\]

This is rank one and bounded, but is maximally dependent on `X_n`.

Then exactly

\[
W_n
=X_n+P_n
=(s_1+\theta)u_1v_1^*
+
\sum_{j\ge2}s_ju_jv_j^*.
\tag{6.1}
\]

Hence all singular values except the first are unchanged and

\[
\sigma_1(W_n)=s_1+\theta.
\tag{6.2}
\]

Since

\[
s_1\to b=1+\sqrt c,
\]

we obtain

\[
\sigma_1(W_n)\to b+\theta>b
\]

for every fixed `theta>0`.

Yet the empirical squared-singular-value measures differ by only one atom, so for every bounded continuous test function `f`,

\[
\left|
\int f\,d\mu_{W_nW_n^*}
-
\int f\,d\mu_{X_nX_n^*}
\right|
\le
\frac{2\|f\|_\infty}{n}
\to0.
\]

Thus the global MP law survives exactly while the extreme singular value is changed by an order-one amount.

For an independent spike, the MP threshold is

\[
\theta_\star=c^{1/4}.
\]

Choose any

\[
0<\theta<c^{1/4}.
\]

The independent-spike BBP rule predicts no superedge outlier, whereas (6.2) gives one deterministically conditional on `X_n`. Moreover the planted vectors are exactly `u_1,v_1`, and the observed leading singular vectors remain exactly `u_1,v_1`, so both planted overlaps equal one.

### Direct SLL failure

Let

\[
\mathcal E_n=
\begin{pmatrix}u_1&0\\0&v_1\end{pmatrix}.
\]

The span of its two columns is invariant under

\[
\mathcal X_n=
\begin{pmatrix}0&X_n\\X_n^*&0\end{pmatrix}
\]

and the restriction is

\[
\begin{pmatrix}0&s_1\\s_1&0\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Therefore

\[
\mathcal E_n^*(\zeta I-\mathcal X_n)^{-1}\mathcal E_n
=
\frac1{\zeta^2-s_1^2}
\begin{pmatrix}\zeta&s_1\\s_1&\zeta\end{pmatrix}.
\tag{6.3}
\]

The source MP deterministic equivalent is block diagonal. Since operator norm dominates the absolute value of every matrix entry,

\[
\|\mathcal G_n(\zeta)-\mathcal M(\zeta)\|_{\rm op}
\ge
\frac{s_1}{|\zeta^2-s_1^2|}.
\]

For any fixed real `zeta>b` separated from `b`, the right side tends to a strictly positive constant. Hence Assumption 2.4 fails.

This proves three separate facts at once:

1. MP **ESD** can remain exactly valid;
2. an **extreme singular value** can violate the independent-spike BBP rule;
3. **singular-vector overlap** can be one instead of the independent-spike subcritical value zero.

The distinction is structural, not a finite-sample artifact.

# 7. Structured-pruning interpretation

## `P19-PRUNED-FRAME-SLL-BRIDGE-38` — PROVED conditional composition

The alignment theorem applies to deterministic trained top-`k` or structured pruning without introducing a mask distribution.

Suppose the final deployed signal frame `E_n` is selected from the same trained matrix and an honest proxy frame `F_n` is available for which a source-legal SLL is proved. Let

\[
\delta_n
=
\inf_{Q_L,Q_R}
\|\mathcal E_n-\mathcal F_n\operatorname{diag}(Q_L,Q_R)\|_{\rm op}.
\]

If `delta_n=o_P(1)`, then (0.1) proves the actual-frame SLL. With bounded spikes and a stable window, source Theorem 3.7 then gives the actual trained/pruned cluster projectors and cross term.

A particularly clean special case occurs when the frame is selected from a fixed orthonormal dictionary and a legal score-margin theorem proves that the trained support equals an honest proxy support. Then, up to column permutation/sign/unitary rotation,

\[
\delta_n=0
\]

on the support-lock event, and no extra resolvent debt is paid.

For learned features, support agreement alone need not imply frame agreement; the block-Procrustes debt remains explicit.

### Why leave-one-out does not automatically solve source independence

A leave-one-out learner is independent of its deleted observation, which is enough for the P19 prediction-risk arguments in prior runs. But a frame trained on `n-1` observations is generally still a function of most of the full data/noise object. Therefore one must **not** automatically invoke source Theorem 7.4 on a LOO frame as if it were independent of the full matrix. One needs either:

- a truly source-legal deterministic/independent proxy frame;
- a direct subspace local law for the dependent proxy;
- or a geometric transfer from some frame for which such a law is already proved.

This is the exact conditional obligation left by the present theorem.

# 8. Prediction risk and singular-vector stability remain separate

The new SLL theorem is about compressed resolvents and source spectral projectors. It does not by itself prove that pruning improves prediction risk.

Conversely, the inherited prediction theorem has the form

\[
E\|(C_1-C_2)\psi\|^2\le\overline R_\Delta.
\]

Only with an honest restricted Gram floor on the actual support union,

\[
G_{JJ}\succeq aI,
\qquad a>0,
\]

does this imply

\[
\|C_1-C_2\|_{\rm op}
\le
\sqrt{\overline R_\Delta/a}.
\tag{8.1}
\]

Only if (8.1) is at most half a genuine protected singular-cluster gap `Delta_K` is Wedin/Hermitian dilation invoked, giving the inherited scale

\[
\|\Delta\widehat\Pi_K^L\|_{\rm op},
\|\Delta\widehat\Pi_K^R\|_{\rm op},
\|\Delta\widehat F_K\|_{\rm op}
\lesssim
\frac1{\Delta_K}
\sqrt{\overline R_\Delta/a}.
\]

This perturbative Wedin route and the resolvent/Riesz-projector BBP route answer different questions. Wedin does **not** generate `kappa_L,kappa_R,kappa_LR`.

# 9. Method comparison after the v1 source audit

## Resolvent / Riesz projector

This is now the primary source-exact route for P19 singular subspaces. The source theorem accepts dependent signals at the deterministic transfer level, but the SLL still has to be established on the actual dependent frame.

## Anisotropic local law + net

Source Theorem 7.4 shows how exponential one-vector tails plus LSI concentration lift to `r_n=o(n)` for deterministic/independent frames. Corollary 7.6 shows that polynomially small failure probabilities directly support only `O(log n)` frame dimension through a net. Neither statement licenses a frame selected after inspecting the same noise matrix.

## Alignment/stability

The present theorem gives an exact way to pay for dependence geometrically: prove a source-legal proxy SLL, then pay `2R_K delta_n` for block-subspace misalignment. This is the most concrete route from trained-pruning stability to the source projector theorem.

## Davis–Kahan / Wedin

Use only after an operator perturbation and protected spectral gap. It is a post-processing stability tool, not a substitute for the source overlap calculation.

## Leave-one-out / ghost replacement

Useful for prediction-risk generalization and for proving score/support stability. It is not automatically a source-independent RMT frame. Its role here is to help establish `delta_n`, not to erase the dependence assumption.

## Deterministic equivalents / free probability

The source's Section 9 allows matrix-valued deterministic equivalents when the correct resolvent center is not scalar. Free probability can describe global laws and some outlier heuristics, but a global ESD or scalar deterministic equivalent does not cure an adaptive-frame resolvent bias. Counterexample (6.3) makes this explicit.

## Sparse-RIP / sparsification concentration

A simultaneous coordinate-sparse RIP event can legitimately cover a coordinate support selected from the same realized matrix, because it is uniform over all supports in the class. That is a different statement from the subspace local law for an arbitrary data-dependent spectral frame. Same sparsity fraction is not enough to replace one by the other.
