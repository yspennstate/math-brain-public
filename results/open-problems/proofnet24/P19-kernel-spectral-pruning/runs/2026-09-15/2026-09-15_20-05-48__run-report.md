# 6. Fresh-audit family covariance theorem

\[
\boxed{
\sup_{J\in\mathcal J}
\sup_{\substack{\operatorname{supp}(v)\subseteq J\\\|v\|_2=1}}
\|v^*\xi(X)\|_{\psi_2\mid T}
\le K.

\tag{6.1}

## `P19-FRESH-AUDIT-FAMILY-COV-28` — NEW / PROVED

There is a universal constant `C<infinity` such that, conditional on `T`, with probability at least `1-eta`,

\[
\boxed{
\sup_{J\in\mathcal J}
\|(\widehat H-H)_{JJ}\|_{\rm op}
\le
C K^2
\left[
\sqrt{\frac{L_{\mathcal J}}m}
+

\[
\boxed{

\tag{6.3}

### Proof

Fix `J` and a unit vector `v` supported on `J`. Put

\[
Y_i=(v^*\xi(X_i))^2.
\]

The standard Orlicz implication gives

\[
\|v^*\xi(X)\|_{\psi_2}\le K
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\|Y_i-EY_i\|_{\psi_1}\le C_1K^2.

Conditional Bernstein therefore yields

\[

\[

For a symmetric matrix `A`, the standard net inequality gives

\[
\|A\|_{\rm op}
\le2\max_{v\in N}|v^*Av|.
