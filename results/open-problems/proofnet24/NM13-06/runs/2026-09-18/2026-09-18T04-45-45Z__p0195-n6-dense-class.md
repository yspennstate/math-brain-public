## 9. Main restricted theorem of this run

### Theorem `NM13-06-P0195-N6-DENSE-06`

Let `G` be a simple graph on six vertices with at least 13 edges. For every absent edge `e` and every finite `beta>=0`, zero-field ferromagnetic random-scan one-site heat-bath Glauber dynamics satisfies

`gap_beta(G+e) <= gap_beta(G)`.

Equivalently, the LPW edge-addition monotonicity clause holds for the complete six-vertex dense stratum `|E(G)|>=13`.

This theorem is **PARTIAL** relative to the source and does not imply `n_edge^*>=7`.

### Proof

If `G` has 15 edges there is no absent edge. If `G` has 14 edges, its complement is `K_2`. If `G` has 13 edges, its complement has two edges and is either `P_3` or `2K_2`. In each of these three nonvacuous complement types the absent edges form one automorphism orbit. Therefore it suffices to prove the three representative marked transitions in Section 7.

Set `x=tanh(beta)`. For a local field `h` with `|h|<=5`, the heat-bath update probability is rational in `x`; explicitly the needed hyperbolic tangents are

`tanh(beta)=x`,

`tanh(2 beta)=2x/(1+x^2)`,

`tanh(3 beta)=(3x+x^3)/(1+3x^2)`,

`tanh(4 beta)=4x(1+x^2)/(1+6x^2+x^4)`,

`tanh(5 beta)=(5x+10x^3+x^5)/(1+10x^2+5x^4)`.

All denominators are positive on `0<=x<1`. Hence the exact heat-bath transition matrix and every symmetry quotient have entries in `Q(x)`.

The preceding P0195 report proved the structural lemma needed here: for zero-field ferromagnetic heat-bath dynamics, the true second eigenvalue `lambda_2` is the top eigenvalue of the subspace of functions that are odd under global spin flip and invariant under `Aut(G)`. Briefly, monotonicity gives an increasing `lambda_2` eigenfunction; subtracting its spin flip produces a nonzero odd increasing eigenfunction; averaging over graph automorphisms stays nonzero because all-plus is fixed. Every eigenvalue of the quotient is an eigenvalue of the full chain, so the quotient maximum is exactly `lambda_2`.

For each representative source and target, the checker constructs that exact odd quotient and clears positive denominators from its characteristic polynomial. In these three highly symmetric cases the resulting quotient characteristic polynomial is irreducible over `Q(x)`; therefore its largest real `L`-root is the quotient top eigenvalue and hence the true `lambda_2`.

For a source polynomial `f_s(x,L)` and target polynomial `f_t(x,L)`, the ordering of their largest real roots can change inside `0<x<1` only at an `x` for which they share a root, so only at a zero of the exact resultant `Res_L(f_t,f_s)`. The checker square-frees that resultant, removes endpoint factors at `x=0,1`, isolates every remaining real root in `(0,1)` by exact rational Sturm arithmetic, and compares the largest source and target roots at one exact rational sample in every complementary interval. The exact outcomes are:

- complement `K_2 -> empty`: quotient degrees `7 -> 3`; resultant degree after endpoint removal `75`; `2` interior roots; interval signs `[1,1,1]`;
- complement `P_3 -> K_2`: quotient degrees `12 -> 7`; resultant degree `399`; `5` interior roots; signs `[1,1,1,1,1,1]`;
- complement `2K_2 -> K_2`: quotient degrees `8 -> 7`; resultant degree `273`; `5` interior roots; signs `[1,1,1,1,1,1]`.

Here sign `+1` means `lambda_2(target)>lambda_2(source)` at the exact sample. Because the top-root functions are continuous, the weak inequality remains valid at the finitely many resultant roots. At `x=0` (`beta=0`) every graph has the same independent-spin heat-bath chain, so equality holds. Consequently

`lambda_2(P_{G+e}(beta)) >= lambda_2(P_G(beta))`

for every finite `beta>=0` in all three marked types, hence

`gap_beta(G+e) <= gap_beta(G)`.

The complement classification exhausts all six-vertex graphs with at least 13 edges, proving the theorem. QED.
