## 4. B24 — FIND THE EXTREMAL OBJECT: replace the atomic witness by a canonical continuous slow tail

The previous source counterexample is exact but deliberately sparse/atomic. I tested whether the literal source failure persists for a transparent continuous distribution with no tuned atoms. It does.

### Claim `P11-I0062-CONTINUOUS-SLOWTAIL-WITNESS-08` — PROVED / source-refutation strengthening by simplification

Let `T` have survival function

\[
\bar F(t):=P(T>t)=\frac{1}{\log(e+t)},\qquad t\ge0.
\tag{4.1}
\]

Then `T` is finite a.s., nonnegative, `F(0)=0`, has no positive exponential moment, and there exists a corrected-Kesten-admissible straight-box height function for which

\[
\limsup \frac{\Phi}{kl}=+\infty\qquad\text{a.s.}
\tag{4.2}
\]

Hence the literal Question 36 is refuted even by a smooth monotone heavy tail rather than only by the earlier lacunary atomic law.

### Proof

The function `1/log(e+t)` decreases continuously from 1 to 0, so (4.1) is the survival function of a probability distribution on `[0,infinity)` with `F(0)=0` and no atom at `+infinity`.

For every `gamma>0`, a finite exponential moment would imply by Markov

\[
P(T>t)\le E e^{\gamma T}\,e^{-\gamma t}.
\]

But

\[
\frac{e^{\gamma t}}{\log(e+t)}\to\infty,
\]

so this inequality cannot hold with finite `E e^{gamma T}` for all large `t`. Thus no positive exponential moment exists. In fact `ET=infinity`, since

\[
ET=\int_0^\infty \frac{dt}{\log(e+t)}=\infty.
\]

For integer `r>=1`, set

\[
a_r=e^{r^3},\qquad b_r=r^3+1,
\qquad N_r=b_r^r.
\tag{4.3}
\]

Since `e+e^{r^3} <= 2e^{r^3}` and `log 2<1`,

\[
q_r:=P(T>a_r)
=\frac1{\log(e+e^{r^3})}
\ge \frac1{r^3+1}=b_r^{-1}.
\tag{4.4}
\]

Define a nondecreasing integer height function by

\[
m(k,l)=\max\{1,\max\{r:N_r\le \min(k,l)\}\}.
\tag{4.5}
\]

Then `m(k,l)->infinity` when both horizontal side lengths tend to infinity, and

\[
m(N_r,N_r)=r.
\tag{4.6}
\]

Moreover `N_r>=2^r`, so for `k>=l` we have eventually

\[
m(k,l)\le \log_2 l.
\]

Hence for the corrected Kesten exponent choice `delta=1/2`,

\[
k^{-1/2}\log m(k,l)
\le k^{-1/2}\log\log_2 l
\to0.
\tag{4.7}
\]

Thus the height is safely within the corrected sub-surface-exponential regime.

Now inspect the box with base side lengths `N_r,N_r` and height `r`. It contains

\[
M_r=(N_r+1)^2
\]

pairwise edge-disjoint vertical columns of `r` edges each. Call a column high if all `r` of its vertical edges exceed `a_r`. By (4.4), a column is high with probability

\[
p_r=q_r^r\ge b_r^{-r}.
\tag{4.8}
\]

The high-column indicators are independent. If `Y_r` is their number, then

\[
\mu_r:=EY_r=M_rp_r\ge N_r^2 b_r^{-r}=b_r^r=N_r.
\tag{4.9}
\]

Therefore

\[
P\left(Y_r<\frac{\mu_r}{2}\right)
\le \frac{4\,\operatorname{Var}Y_r}{\mu_r^2}
\le \frac4{\mu_r}
\le \frac4{N_r}.
\tag{4.10}
\]

The series `sum_r 4/N_r` converges. By Borel--Cantelli, almost surely

\[
Y_r\ge\mu_r/2
\]

for all sufficiently large `r`.

Every high column is a bottom-to-top path whose every edge has capacity at least `a_r`. Sending `a_r` units up each high column gives an admissible flow, so

\[
\Phi_r\ge a_rY_r.
\tag{4.11}
\]

Equivalently, by max-flow/min-cut, every cut must intersect every high column in a distinct edge and therefore has capacity at least `a_rY_r`.

Consequently, eventually almost surely,

\[
\frac{\Phi_r}{N_r^2}
\ge
\frac{a_r\mu_r}{2N_r^2}
\ge
\frac{e^{r^3}}{2(r^3+1)^r}.
\tag{4.12}
\]

Taking logarithms of the final expression gives

\[
r^3-r\log(r^3+1)-\log2\to+\infty,
\]

so (4.2) follows. QED.

## 5. B17 — LOOK FOR YOUR NUMBERS SOMEWHERE ELSE: the finite-minimum threshold has a classical FPP ancestor

The quantity

\[
r_*(G)=\inf\{h\ge1:E\min(T_1,\ldots,T_h)<\infty\}
\]

looked at first like a surface-specific invention. The literature search shows that its *mechanism* is classical in point-to-point FPP.

In standard FPP, the local obstruction to integrability is escaping from a vertex through finitely many incident edges. Classical Kesten/shape-theorem formulations therefore use moments of the minimum of independent incident edge weights, rather than moments of one raw edge, and failure of the corresponding minimum-moment condition produces persistent local traps/holes.

P11's surface argument is structurally parallel:

- point-path geometry regularizes by choosing among incident edges;
- surface geometry regularizes by choosing among independent anchored shells/cut surfaces;
- finite `r_*` means sufficiently many parallel surface alternatives have an integrable minimum;
- `r_*=infinity` means no fixed number of such alternatives regularizes the tail, and claim05 then permits a slow-height divergence construction.

I did **not** locate a published maximal-flow theorem that identifies finite `r_*` itself as the sharp universal corrected-Kesten criterion. Therefore the classical FPP analogy is prior-art for the idea of minimum-moment regularization, not a source showing that 07R is already known.

`P04-I0101-SPLIT-KERNEL-FOURIER-SELFDUAL-18`.

P04 defines, for prime `p=1 mod 4`, normalized additive Fourier matrix

\[
F_{a,x}=p^{-1/2}\psi(-ax)
\]

and kernel

\[
L_{x,y}=\chi(x^2-y^2).
\]

The claim is `FLF=L`, hence `FL=LF`; `L` kills the odd subspace; and a nontrivial diagonal localization projector `P_D` cannot commute with `F`.

Independent verification:

Using `u=x-y`, `v=x+y`,

\[
(FLF)_{a,b}
=\frac1p
\sum_u\chi(u)\psi(-(a-b)u/2)
\sum_v\chi(v)\psi(-(a+b)v/2).
\]

For a nontrivial quadratic character, each nonzero-frequency sum is a Gauss sum `chi(c)G`, and for `p=1 mod 4`, `G^2=p`. The zero-frequency cases vanish on both sides. Therefore

\[
(FLF)_{a,b}
=\chi((a-b)(a+b)/4)
=\chi(a^2-b^2)
=L_{a,b},
\]

because `chi(4)=1` and `chi(-1)=1`.

Reflection `R` satisfies `RL=L=LR` because `L` is even in each coordinate, while `F^2=R`. From `FLF=L`, left-multiplication by `F` gives `RLF=FL`, hence `LF=FL`. If `Rf=-f`, then `Lf=LRf=-Lf`, so `Lf=0`: the odd subspace is annihilated.

Finally every Fourier entry is nonzero. If a diagonal `0/1` projector `P_D` commuted with `F`, then entrywise

\[
(P_D)_{aa}F_{a,x}=F_{a,x}(P_D)_{xx}
\]

would force every diagonal entry of `P_D` to be equal, impossible when `D` is nonempty and proper. Thus `P_DF\ne FP_D`.
