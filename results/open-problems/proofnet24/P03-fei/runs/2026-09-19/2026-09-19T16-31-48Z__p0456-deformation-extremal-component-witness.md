## 4. #6 DEFORM TO A POINT YOU CAN SOLVE — what is monotone and what is not

Start after Claim 309's safe root suffixes and Claim 311's global-minimum collision preprocessing. Let `L_0` be the resulting finite set of preliminary retained deadlines. For each positive-degree right vertex `e` of `Gamma_0`, let

`N(e) subset L_0`

be the deadlines strictly contaminated by that one raw edge. For `F` a set of such support edges, define

`O(F)=union_{e in F} N(e)`.

Let `R(F)` denote the event that every deadline in `L_0\O(F)` satisfies its required external rejection constraint using the residual blocks `B_u={{u,x}:x in E}`.

If `F subset F'`, then `O(F) subset O(F')`; therefore `L_0\O(F') subset L_0\O(F)`, and `R(F')` asks for a subset of the constraints asked by `R(F)`. Hence

`R(F) subset R(F')`.                                      (4.1)

Adding the same active support twice changes nothing: the deletion operator is idempotent. There is no iterative creation of new support incidences; removing a left deadline can only delete incidences of `Gamma_0`.

This gives a useful deformation: expose the positive support raw edges one at a time. Conditional on their already realized active values, the residual rejection event can only become easier as contaminated deadlines are deleted.

### 4.1 Why the full charged event is not monotone

The tempting stronger statement is false. Take one preliminary retained deadline `D`, one candidate support raw edge `e`, and let

`A={W(e)<D}`

be its activity event. Let `R` be the external rejection constraint for `D`; conditional on `D in (0,1)`, both `A` and `R` have nontrivial probability, and their raw variables are disjoint in the Claim-315 ordering.

With no prescribed support charge,

`E(empty)=R`.

With `e` prescribed active and its contaminated deadline deleted,

`E({e})=A`.

On `A^c intersect R`, `E(empty)` occurs but `E({e})` does not. On `A intersect R^c`, `E({e})` occurs but `E(empty)` does not. Both sets have positive conditional probability. Therefore neither inclusion holds.

This is the exact obstruction requested by method #6: **omission relief is monotone, but charge acquisition adds a probability constraint, and the two effects trade off.** It validates rather than replaces Claim 315's two-stage conditioning.

| charge | pattern multiplicity | codimension | max omission relief | net factor |
