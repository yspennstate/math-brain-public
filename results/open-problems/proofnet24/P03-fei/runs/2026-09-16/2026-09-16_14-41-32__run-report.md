# 5. `P03-QUADRATIC-UNCERTAINTY-SLACK-POTENTIAL-186` — PROVED

\[

\[
H_z^{in}+H_z^{out}\ge r\ln2,
\tag{5.1}

\[
0\le H_z^{in},H_z^{out}\le r\ln2.
\tag{5.2}
\]

Define the **uncertainty slack**

\[
\boxed{
\mathfrak S_A(f)
:=\sum_zm_z
\left(H_z^{in}+H_z^{out}-r\ln2\right).

\tag{5.3}

Equation (5.1) gives

\[
\mathfrak S_A(f)\ge0.
\]

Using `H_z^{out}<=r ln2`,

\[
H_z^{in}+H_z^{out}-r\ln2\le H_z^{in},
\]

hence

\[
\boxed{
0\le\mathfrak S_A(f)
\le\sum_zm_zH_z^{in}
=H_{\rm nat}(f)-H_{\rm nat}(Z_A).
}
\tag{5.4}
\]

The exact chain rules are

\[
H_{\rm nat}(f)
=H_{\rm nat}(Z_A)+\sum_zm_zH_z^{in},
\tag{5.5}

\[
H_{\rm nat}(fq_A)
=H_{\rm nat}(Z_A)+\sum_zm_zH_z^{out}.
\tag{5.6}

\[
\boxed{

\tag{5.7}

## 6.1 Exact common radical

If `v in K cap K'`, then

\[
Ev=(d\cdot v)e+(e\cdot v)d=0.
\]

Since `e,d` are independent,

\[
e\cdot v=d\cdot v=0.
\]

Conversely, if `v in K cap e^perp cap d^perp`, then `Ev=0`, so `A'v=0`. The same argument with `A,A'` exchanged gives

\[
\boxed{
L:=K\cap K'
=K\cap e^\perp\cap d^\perp
=K'\cap e^\perp\cap d^\perp.
}
\tag{6.2}
\]

Define

\[
a=\dim K-\dim L,
\qquad
a'=\dim K'-\dim L.
\tag{6.3}
\]

Let

\[
T(v)=(e\cdot v,d\cdot v)\in\mathbb F_2^2.
\]

By (6.2), `a=dim T(K)` and `a'=dim T(K')`.

For `v in K` and `w in K'`,

\[
0=v^T(A'-A)w
=(e\cdot v)(d\cdot w)+(d\cdot v)(e\cdot w).
\tag{6.4}
\]

The right side is the nondegenerate symplectic form on `F_2^2` evaluated on `T(v),T(w)`. Hence

\[
T(K')\subseteq T(K)^\perp,
\]

so

\[
a+a'\le2.
\tag{6.5}
\]

Alternating matrices have even rank. Therefore `dim K` and `dim K'` have the same parity, so

\[
a-a'\equiv0\pmod2.
\tag{6.6}
\]

Combining `a,a' in {0,1,2}`, (6.5), and (6.6) gives exactly

\[
\boxed{(a,a')\in\{(0,0),(1,1),(2,0),(0,2)\}.}
\tag{6.7}
\]

Moreover

\[
\operatorname{rank}A'-\operatorname{rank}A
=\dim K-\dim K'=a-a'.
\tag{6.8}
\]

Thus:

- `(0,0)`: no radical/syndrome change;
- `(1,1)`: rank stasis and exchange of one radical direction;
- `(2,0)`: rank grows by two and two radical directions disappear;
- `(0,2)`: rank drops by two and two radical directions are inserted.

This is exactly the algebraic birth/exchange/death geometry sought in the occupation/re-entry ledger.

\[

\[
\frac{\nu}{2^\nu-1}.

Averaging (7.1) therefore yields

\[
\boxed{
H_{\rm nat}(Z_K)
\le
\frac{\nu}{2^\nu-1}
\sum_{0\ne v\in K}h_{\rm nat}(I_v(f)).
}
\tag{7.2}
\]

Now use concavity of binary entropy. Claim `P03-QUADRATIC-KERNEL-LEAKAGE-160` gives, for the radical flat,
