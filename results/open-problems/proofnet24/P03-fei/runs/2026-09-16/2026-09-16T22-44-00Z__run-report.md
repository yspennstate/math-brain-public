## 6. Move #13 — ordinary compression is not the needed monovariant

I tested whether standard downward coordinate compression could monotonically improve the exact FEI comparison gap
\[

It cannot: there are exact examples in both directions.

### Example A: compression makes the gap smaller

\tag{6.3}

So no proof may simply assert that ordinary coordinate compression monotonically helps or hurts the sharp FEI gap.
