`u_T = Fourier(f_0 q_B)(T)`,

# 6. `P03-HADAMARD-MELLIN-LAYERCAKE-141` — PROVED

The scalar kernel `K` above has a strong positivity property that was not used previously.

For `p,q>0`, direct differentiation of the exact Hadamard entropy formula gives

`d^2 K/(dp dq) = [1/(4 sqrt(pq))] log((sqrt(p)+sqrt(q))/|sqrt(p)-sqrt(q)|)`.

Denote the right side by `kappa(p,q)`. It is strictly positive off the diagonal. The logarithmic diagonal singularity is locally integrable. Also

`K(p,0)=K(0,q)=0`.

Therefore, by integrating the mixed derivative from the coordinate axes,

`K(p,q)=integral_0^p integral_0^q kappa(a,b) db da`.

This identity extends continuously to the diagonal and axes.

Consequently, for threshold sets

`P_a={T:p_T>=a}`, `Q_b={T:q_T>=b}`,

one has the exact positive layer-cake representation

`C_V^K(p,q)=integral integral kappa(a,b) Xi_V(P_a,Q_b) da db`,

where

`Xi_V(P,Q)=(1/|V|) sum_{s in V} |P intersect (Q-s)|`.

Thus every ordered-star block is a positive-weight integral of a **signed difference of selector correlations** between global shifts and old-subspace shifts, followed by the exact source-minus-comparator subtraction.

The positivity here is scalar-kernel positivity only. It does not authorize replacing the resulting signed selector difference by its positive part.

# 7. `P03-HADAMARD-MELLIN-PD-142` — PROVED

The layer-cake kernel admits an explicit positive-definite Mellin representation.

By homogeneity write

`K(p,q)=sqrt(pq) psi(log(p/q))`.

Put `t=log(p/q)`. Differentiating this form gives

`d^2 K/(dp dq)=[psi(t)-4 psi''(t)]/(4 sqrt(pq))`.

Comparison with Section 6 yields the scalar ODE

`psi(t)-4 psi''(t)=g(t)`,

where

`g(t)=log coth(|t|/4)`.

For `t != 0`,

`g(t)=2 sum_{j>=0} exp(-(2j+1)|t|/2)/(2j+1)`.

With Fourier convention

`g_hat(xi)=integral_R exp(-i xi t) g(t) dt`,

the elementary transform of `exp(-a|t|)` gives

`g_hat(xi)=2 sum_{j>=0} 1/((j+1/2)^2+xi^2)`.

The standard half-integer partial-fraction identity then gives

`g_hat(xi)=pi tanh(pi xi)/xi`,

with its continuous value at `xi=0`.

The ODE therefore yields

`psi_hat(xi)=pi tanh(pi xi)/[xi(1+4 xi^2)]`.

There is no additional homogeneous `exp(+/-t/2)` term: the earlier scalar bound

`0<=K(p,q)<=2 ln2 sqrt(pq)`

makes `psi` bounded on the whole real line, excluding both nonzero homogeneous solutions.

Fourier inversion now gives the exact positive representation

`K(p,q)=integral_R p^(1/2+i xi) q^(1/2-i xi) mu(dxi)`,

with

`mu(dxi)= [tanh(pi xi)/(2 xi(1+4 xi^2))] dxi`.

The density is even and nonnegative, with continuous value `pi/2` at zero. Its total mass is

`mu(R)=K(1,1)=2 ln2`.

Thus `K` is a positive-definite kernel on `R_+`, with explicit Mellin features

`Phi_xi(p)=p^(1/2+i xi)`.

This is an exact integral identity, not an asymptotic expansion.

# 8. `P03-MELLIN-SELECTOR-MODE-143` — PROVED

The positive Mellin representation diagonalizes the ordered-star selector operator.

Fix `k,B` and let `M=|W_k|`. For one Mellin frequency `xi`, set

`a_xi(T)=p_T^(1/2+i xi)`,

`b_xi(T)=q_T^(1/2+i xi)`.

Use the unnormalized finite-group Fourier transform

`ahat_xi(eta)=sum_T a_xi(T)(-1)^(eta dot T)`,

and similarly `bhat_xi`.

For every subgroup `V<=W_k`, finite-group correlation and subgroup averaging give

`C_V^K=(1/M) integral_R sum_{eta in V^perp} Re(ahat_xi(eta) conjugate(bhat_xi(eta))) mu(dxi)`.

Because `O_k^perp=U_k` and `W_k^perp={0}`,

`G_k(f;B)=-(1/M) integral_R sum_{eta in U_k, eta != 0} Re(ahat_xi(eta) conjugate(bhat_xi(eta))) mu(dxi)`.

For the comparator, let `r_B(T)` be the squared Fourier spectrum of `q_B` and put

`c_xi(T)=r_B(T)^(1/2+i xi)`.

Then the exact paired block response is

`Delta_k(f;B)=(1/M) integral_R sum_{eta in U_k, eta!=0}`

`[ |chat_xi(eta)|^2 - Re(ahat_xi(eta) conjugate(bhat_xi(eta))) ] mu(dxi)`.

The integrand admits the exact signed split

`|c|^2-Re(a conjugate(b))`

`= (1/2)|a-b|^2 + [ |c|^2-(|a|^2+|b|^2)/2 ]`.

Hence

`Delta_k=P_k+S_k`,

where `P_k>=0` is the nontrivial-mode physical difference term and `S_k` is the signed selector/re-entry term.

The crucial cancellation is exact: at every `xi`, Parseval and the normalization

`sum_T p_T=sum_T q_T=sum_T r_B(T)=1`

imply

`(1/M) sum_eta [ |chat_xi(eta)|^2-(|ahat_xi(eta)|^2+|bhat_xi(eta)|^2)/2 ] = 0`.

Therefore `S_k` is exactly the negative of its complementary-mode contribution. It is not an error term. Discarding it destroys a genuine signed conservation law.

This is the precise Mellin-frequency form of the selector/correlation credit demanded by the historical signed-gluing and R32 instructions.

# 9. `P03-ORDERED-COMPARATOR-CORRELATION-144` — PROVED

For the comparator, the selector term can be evaluated exactly in alternating-matrix geometry.

Let `B` be the pure quadratic alternating matrix on `W_k`, of rank `rho` and nullity

`nu=dim ker B`.

The squared Fourier spectrum of `q_B` is uniform, with mass `2^-rho`, on an affine coset of `im B`.

For a shift `s`, the original and shifted support cosets are identical exactly when `s in im B`; otherwise they are disjoint. Since

`K(lambda,lambda)=2 lambda ln2`,

it follows that

`sum_T K(r_B(T),r_B(T+s)) = 2 ln2 * 1_{s in im B}`.

Therefore

`C_{W_k}^K(comparator)=2 ln2 * 2^-nu`.

Put

`kappa0=dim(ker B intersect U_k)`.

Because `im B=(ker B)^perp`,

`dim(O_k intersect im B)=dim(O_k)-nu+kappa0`,

and hence

`C_{O_k}^K(comparator)=2 ln2 * 2^(-nu+kappa0)`.

Thus the centered comparator gap is exactly

`G_k(1;B)=2 ln2 [2^-nu-2^(-nu+kappa0)]`

`= -2 ln2 * 2^(-nu) (2^kappa0-1) <= 0`.

For the actual ordered-star ensemble the matrix has block form

`B=[[B0,C],[C^T,0]]`

`kappa0=dim ker C=dim(U_k)-rC`.

`rank B=2 rC + rank D`,

`eta=nullity D`,

then

`nu=kappa0+eta`,

so the comparator gap has the sharper exact form

`G_k(1;B)=-2 ln2 * 2^-eta (1-2^-kappa0)`.

This identifies the selector/re-entry correction by an explicit pair of nullities. No Gaussianity, independence of projected coordinates, or continuum approximation is used.

# 10. `P03-MELLIN-LIPSCHITZ-LOG-BARRIER-145` — PROVED AS A METHOD LIMITATION

The Mellin representation also explains exactly why a naive absolute estimate recreates the known logarithmic obstruction.

For `x,y>=0`, differentiation of `x^(1+2 i xi)` gives the global Lipschitz bound

`|x^(1+2i xi)-y^(1+2i xi)| <= sqrt(1+4 xi^2) |x-y|`.

Here

`a_xi(T)=|u_T|^(1+2i xi)`,

`b_xi(T)=|v_T|^(1+2i xi)`.

The common comparator factor `q_B` does not affect physical disagreement across coordinate `k`, so Parseval gives exactly

`sum_T |u_T-v_T|^2 = 4 I_k(f)`.

For a Mellin cutoff `L`, subgroup-mode Parseval therefore yields

`P_k(|xi|<=L) <= 2 I_k(f) Lambda(L)`,

where

`Lambda(L)=integral_{|xi|<=L} (1+4xi^2) mu(dxi)`

`=integral_0^L tanh(pi xi)/xi dxi`.

Hence

`Lambda(L)=log L+O(1)` as `L->infinity`.

After the exact gauge, summing the low-frequency estimate over all stars gives

`sum_k P_k(|xi|<=L) <= 2 Lambda(L) J(f)`.

The Mellin tail mass is small:

`mu(|xi|>L) <= integral_L^infinity dxi/[xi(1+4xi^2)]`

`= (1/2) log(1+1/(4L^2)) = O(L^-2)`.

But an absolute tail bound is only dimension-free **per block**. Summing it over all blocks reintroduces a factor comparable to the number of stars. Choosing `L` large enough to suppress that factor makes the low-frequency term grow like `J log L`.

Therefore the route

> bound `P_k` modewise by Lipschitz, discard the signed `S_k`, and pay the Mellin tail absolutely

cannot by itself yield a dimension-free constant. The logarithm is not an artifact of the earlier restriction language; it is the exact high-Mellin-frequency price of discarding the selector/re-entry cancellation.

This is a method limitation, not a counterexample to FEI.
