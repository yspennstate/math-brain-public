## 2. Binary quadratic phases and exact Fourier plateau geometry

Work in `F_2^n`. Let

`Q_A(x)=sum_{i<j} A_ij x_i x_j`,

where `A` is the associated alternating matrix (symmetric with zero diagonal over `F_2`), and put

`q_A(x)=(-1)^{Q_A(x)}`.

Let

`B_A(x,z)=Q_A(x+z)+Q_A(x)+Q_A(z)=x^T A z`.

Write `r=rank(A)` and `K=ker A`, so `dim K=n-r`.

For any `xi in F_2^n`,

`hat q_A(xi)=2^{-n} sum_x (-1)^{Q_A(x)+xi.x}`.

Square the coefficient and set the second point to `x+z`:

`|hat q_A(xi)|^2`

`=2^{-2n} sum_{x,z} (-1)^{Q_A(x)+Q_A(x+z)+xi.z}`

`=2^{-2n} sum_z (-1)^{Q_A(z)+xi.z} sum_x (-1)^{B_A(x,z)}`.

The inner sum is `2^n` exactly when `z in K` and zero otherwise. Hence

`|hat q_A(xi)|^2`

`=2^{-n} sum_{z in K} (-1)^{Q_A(z)+xi.z}`.                      (2.1)

On `K`, the polar form vanishes, so `Q_A|_K` is linear. Therefore the character sum in (2.1) is either `|K|=2^{n-r}` or zero. Parseval then forces exactly `2^r` nonzero Fourier coefficients, each having squared magnitude `2^{-r}`.

Thus the spectral law of every pure quadratic phase is uniform on an affine `r`-flat and

`H_nat(q_A)=r ln 2`.                                             (2.2)

Affine and constant terms only translate/permutate the Fourier support and do not change entropy. This proof is self-contained and uses no unverified stabilizer-state theorem.
