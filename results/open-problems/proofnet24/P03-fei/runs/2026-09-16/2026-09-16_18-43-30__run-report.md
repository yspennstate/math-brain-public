## 5. `P03-C7-HIGH-BOUNDARY-198 — PROVED`

\[

\le\sum_{i=1}^7h_2(I_i)

\[

\[
2e(G)=\sum_v d(v)\ge r|V(G)|.
\tag{8.1}
\]

If the leaf block is a bridge, its endpoint away from the articulation is a non-cut vertex of degree one, contradiction for `r>=2`. Otherwise the leaf block is 2-connected and bipartite and meets the rest of `G` in exactly one articulation `c`.

For `r=3`, write its bipartition as `X union Y`, with `c in X` without loss.
