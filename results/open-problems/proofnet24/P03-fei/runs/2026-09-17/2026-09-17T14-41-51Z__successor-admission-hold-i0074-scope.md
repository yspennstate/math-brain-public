### Claim under audit

For prime `p = 5 mod 24`, let

`V={x in F_p\{0,1}: chi(x)=chi(x-1)=1}`

and

`J(x)=((x^2-x+1)^3)/(x^2(1-x)^2)`.

The anharmonic `S_3=<x->1-x,x->1/x>` acts freely on `V`; `J` is constant on each orbit and separates the orbits. For distinct orbit representatives `x,y`, if

`k(x,y)=#{g in S_3: chi(x-g y)=1}`,

then

`chi(J(x)-J(y))=(-1)^{k(x,y)}`.

Thus parity-even cross-orbit blocks correspond exactly to adjacency in the Paley graph induced on `U_p=J(V)`.

### Independent verification

**(a) Exact factorization.** Independently expanding both sides gives the polynomial identity

`(x^2-x+1)^3 y^2(1-y)^2 - (y^2-y+1)^3 x^2(1-x)^2`

`=(x-y)(xy-1)(x+y-1)(xy-x+1)(xy-x-y)(xy-y+1)`.

I verified the expansion symbolically and then used the factorization algebraically below; no numerical approximation is involved.

**(b) Size of V.** Since `p=5 mod24`, in particular `p=1 mod4`. Therefore

`|V|=(1/4) sum_{x !=0,1}(1+chi(x))(1+chi(x-1))`.

The three nonconstant sums are `-1,-1,-1`: the first two because the full character sum is zero and the omitted nonzero endpoint contributes `1`; the quadratic sum `sum_x chi(x(x-1))=-1` because the quadratic has two distinct roots. Hence

`|V|=(p-2-1-1-1)/4=(p-5)/4`.

**(c) Freeness.** The three transposition fixed points of the anharmonic action are `-1,2,1/2`. For `p=5 mod8`, `(2/p)=-1`, so none lies in `V` (for `-1`, the second required character is `chi(-2)=-1`). **(d) J separates orbits.** Direct substitution gives `J(1-x)=J(x)` and `J(1/x)=J(x)`, hence invariance under the whole anharmonic group. If `J(x)=J(y)`, the exact factorization forces one of the six factors to vanish, yielding exactly

`|U_p|=|V|/6=(p-5)/24`.
