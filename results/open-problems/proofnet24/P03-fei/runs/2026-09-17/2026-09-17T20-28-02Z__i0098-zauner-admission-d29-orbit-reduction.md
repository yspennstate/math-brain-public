## 7. B2 — the coarse ETF signature route does not refute d=29

Let `v_1,...,v_{d^2}` be a hypothetical SIC in `C^d`, normalized to unit length, and let `G` be its Gram matrix. Tightness gives

\[
\sum_{j=1}^{d^2} v_j v_j^*=dI_d.
\]

Hence `G` has nonzero eigenvalue `d` with multiplicity `d` and eigenvalue `0` with multiplicity `d^2-d`.

For a SIC the off-diagonal modulus is

\[
\alpha=\frac1{\sqrt{d+1}}.
\]

Writing

\[
G=I+\alpha S,
\]

the Hermitian signature matrix `S` has zero diagonal, unimodular off-diagonal entries, and exactly two eigenvalues

\[
(d-1)\sqrt{d+1}\quad (\text{mult. }d),
\qquad
-\sqrt{d+1}\quad (\text{mult. }d^2-d).
\]

Therefore it satisfies the exact quadratic identity

\[
S^2=(d-2)\sqrt{d+1}\,S+(d^2-1)I.
\]

For `d=29` this becomes

\[
\operatorname{spec}(S)=\{28\sqrt{30}^{\,(29)},\,-\sqrt{30}^{\,(812)}\}
\]

and

\[
\boxed{S^2=27\sqrt{30}\,S+840I.}
\]

All elementary trace/rank/integrality consequences are internally consistent. Thus the cheapest spectral-negation route gives no obstruction at 29. This is useful route-kill, not evidence of existence.

### Proof

First,

\[
Z-I=\begin{pmatrix}-1&-1\\1&-2\end{pmatrix}
\]

has determinant `3`, which is nonzero modulo 29. Therefore the only fixed point of `Z` on `F_29^2` is `0`. Since `Z^3=I`, every nonzero orbit has length exactly three. There are `29^2-1=840` nonzero labels, hence `840/3=280` orbits.

Next, because `psi` is a `U_Z` eigenvector,

\[
\begin{aligned}
x_{Zu}
&=|\langle\psi,D_{Zu}\psi\rangle|^2\\
&=|\langle\psi,U_ZD_uU_Z^*\psi\rangle|^2\\
&=|\langle\psi,D_u\psi\rangle|^2=x_u,
\end{aligned}
\]

where the displacement phase disappears after taking modulus squared. Hence `x_u` is constant on each 3-cycle.

Let `rho=|psi><psi|`. Orthogonality of the Weyl--Heisenberg operator basis gives

\[
\operatorname{Tr}(\rho^2)
=\frac1{29}\sum_{u\in F_{29}^2}|\operatorname{Tr}(\rho D_u)|^2.
\]

Since `rho` is pure, the left side is one. The identity label contributes `|Tr rho|^2=1`. Thus

\[
\sum_{u\ne0}x_u=29-1=28.
\]

Grouping three equal terms on each Zauner orbit gives

\[
3\sum_Ox_O=28.
\]

Now Cauchy--Schwarz on the 840 nonzero values gives

\[
\sum_{u\ne0}x_u^2\ge\frac{28^2}{840}=\frac{14}{15},
\]

with equality if and only if all 840 nonzero `x_u` are equal. Their common value is then `28/840=1/30`, exactly the SIC overlap value `1/(d+1)` for `d=29`.

Since every orbit has three elements,

\[
\sum_{u\ne0}x_u^2=3\sum_Ox_O^2.
\]

Using `sum_O x_O=28/3`, direct expansion gives

\[
3\sum_O\left(x_O-\frac1{30}\right)^2
=3\sum_Ox_O^2-\frac{14}{15}.
\]

This is nonnegative and vanishes exactly at the SIC point. The stated Zauner eigenspace dimensions are the standard `d=3k+2` dimensions `(k+1,k+1,k)` with `k=9`, giving `(10,10,9)`. QED.
