# 2. Gauge audit — exact equivalence, not compiler payment

\[
A_0=\{i:I_i(f)>1/2\},\qquad F=f\chi_{A_0}.
\]

Then

\[
\widehat F(S)=\widehat f(S\triangle A_0),

\[

\[
I_i(F)=
\begin{cases}
1-I_i(f),&i\in A_0,\\

Therefore

\[
\boxed{I(F)=J(f).}
\tag{2.1}
\]

All physical edge counts below are taken after this one gauge, so \(I_i(F)=j_i\le1/2\). This proves equivalence of the two FEI formulations with the same constant; it pays none of (0.1).

# 4. `P03-SIGNED-UPFLOW-SHARPENING-88` — PROVED

This sharpens the earlier `P03-LOW-SHELL-GOOD-FLOW-17` and removes most of its coarse defect loss.

Let \(A\in C_0\) have weight

\[
4\le w=|A|\le N/4-2,
\]

and write

\[
g_A(x)=(-1)^{1_A(x)},\qquad J_A=J(g_A).
\]

For each original coordinate \(i\), partition the \(N/2\) physical \(i\)-edges into

\[
E_{0,i},\quad E_{1,i},\quad E_{2,i}
\]

according as they contain zero, one, or two vertices of \(A\). Then

\[
w=E_{1,i}+2E_{2,i},
\qquad
E_{0,i}=\frac{N-w-E_{1,i}}2.
\tag{4.1}
\]

Since \(w\le N/4\), every coordinate influence of \(g_A\) is at most \(2w/N\le1/2\), so it is already folded and

\[
\sum_iE_{1,i}=\frac N2J_A.
\tag{4.2}
\]

A physical upward q-flip is specified by one boundary edge and one empty parallel edge. Its four vertices form an affine two-flat \(P\), and replacing \(A\) by

\[
B=A\triangle P
\]

raises the weight from \(w\) to \(w+2\).

The total number of upward channels is

\[
U_{\rm all}(A)=\sum_iE_{1,i}E_{0,i}.
\]

Because \(E_{1,i}\le w\), (4.1) gives

\[
E_{1,i}E_{0,i}
=\frac{(N-w)E_{1,i}-E_{1,i}^2}{2}
\ge
\frac{N-2w}{2}E_{1,i}.
\]

Using (4.2),

\[
U_{\rm all}(A)
\ge
\frac{N(N-2w)}4J_A.
\tag{4.3}
\]

Now use the original Boolean derivative signs. For coordinate \(i\), let

\[
m_i=\frac N2j_i(f)
\]

be the number of physical \(i\)-edges on which the gauged \(f\) changes sign. Let \(t_i\) and \(r_i\) count those negative derivative edges among the boundary and empty edge classes respectively. A q-flip is frustration-destroying exactly when its two edge derivative signs are opposite. The number of such bad pairs in coordinate \(i\) is

\[
t_i(E_{0,i}-r_i)+(E_{1,i}-t_i)r_i.
\tag{4.4}
\]

For \(w\le N/4\), one has \(E_{0,i}\ge E_{1,i}\): indeed

\[
E_{0,i}-E_{1,i}
=\frac{N-w-3E_{1,i}}2
\ge\frac{N-4w}{2}\ge0.
\]

Hence (4.4) is at most

\[
E_{0,i}(t_i+r_i)\le E_{0,i}m_i
\le\frac{N-w}{2}m_i.
\]

Summing over coordinates and using \(\sum_i m_i=(N/2)J(f)\),

\[
U_{\rm bad}(A)
\le
\frac{N(N-w)}4J(f).
\tag{4.5}
\]

Therefore the number \(U_+(A)\) of upward q-flips that both preserve frustration and raise the shell by two satisfies

\[
\boxed{
U_+(A)
\ge
\frac N4\Big[(N-2w)J_A-(N-w)J(f)\Big].
}
\tag{4.6}
\]

If the bracket is negative the inequality is of course vacuous but still true. This strictly improves the old coarse loss
\(\frac{N^2}{8}(J_A-2B_{\rm flat})\) in the regime relevant here and uses only original-coordinate edge counts.

No independence, tensorization, random relation model, or positive-part replacement is used.

# 5. `P03-DOUBLY-EVEN-CREDIT-NOREENTRY-89` — PROVED

The earlier low-shell flow needed a minimum-distance condition to rule out re-entry into the terminal dual. For the actual debit-to-credit move at the half-band, that condition is unnecessary.

Take a deposited debit relation

\[
A\in C_0\setminus D_{d_h},
\qquad
f_A=-1,
\qquad
|A|=w\equiv0\pmod4,
\]

and a good upward physical q-flip \(P\) from Section 4. Since \(P\) is an affine two-flat,

\[
P\in RM(1,n)^\perp=C_0.
\]

Therefore

\[
B=A\triangle P\in C_0.
\]

The upward move has \(|A\cap P|=1\), hence

\[
|B|=w+2\equiv2\pmod4.
\tag{5.1}
\]

But the half-band residual dual \(D_{d_h}\) is doubly even, so every word in it has weight divisible by four. Consequently

\[
\boxed{B\notin D_{d_h}.}
\tag{5.2}
\]

This holds at **every weight**, not just below the dual minimum distance.

For a good channel the two physical edge derivative signs agree, hence

\[
f_P=+1.
\]

Therefore

\[
f_B=f_Af_P=-1.
\tag{5.3}
\]

Thus every good upward q-flip from a deposited \(0\bmod4\) debit lands on a genuine deposited \(2\bmod4\) **negative credit** and cannot re-enter the terminal code.

This is exactly the re-entry statement needed for the adjacent-shell transport below.

# 7. `P03-UNIT-BOREL-SHELL-FLOW-90` — PROVED

Fix an even shell

\[
w\equiv0\pmod4,
\qquad
4\le w\le N/4-2,
\qquad
u:=w+2.
\]

Let

\[
\mathcal D_w
=
\{A\in C_0\setminus D_{d_h}:|A|=w,\ f_A=-1\}
\]

be the debit relations and

\[
\mathcal C_u
=
\{B\in C_0\setminus D_{d_h}:|B|=u,\ f_B=-1\}
\]

be the adjacent negative-credit shell.

Define

\[
\alpha_w
:=
\frac{(N-w)J(f)}{(N-2w)\ell_w},
\qquad
\beta_w
:=
\frac{8n}{N\ell_w},
\qquad
\gamma_w
:=
\frac{1+\beta_w}{1-\alpha_w}.
\tag{7.1}
\]

Assume

\[
\alpha_w<1.
\tag{7.2}
\]

Then by (4.6) and \(J_A\ge\ell_w\), every source \(A\in\mathcal D_w\) has

\[
U_+(A)
\ge
(1-\alpha_w)
\frac{N(N-2w)}4J_A.
\tag{7.3}
\]

Send one unit of source mass from each \(A\) equally across its \(U_+(A)\) good upward channels.

For a target \(B\in\mathcal C_u\), any reverse channel consists of one boundary edge and one full edge of \(B\). Since \(u\le N/4\), its relation influence is folded and

\[
D_-(B)
\le
\sum_i E_{1,i}(B)E_{2,i}(B)
\le
\frac u2\sum_iE_{1,i}(B)
=
\frac{Nu}{4}J_B.
\tag{7.4}
\]

Along one q-flip, \(g_B=g_Ag_P\). The folded influence map is 1-Lipschitz coordinatewise, and an affine four-point phase satisfies

\[
J(g_P)\le\frac{8n}{N}.
\]

Thus

\[
|J_B-J_A|\le\frac{8n}{N}
\]

and, since \(J_A\ge\ell_w\),

\[
\frac{J_B}{J_A}\le1+\beta_w.
\tag{7.5}
\]

If \(B\) receives a channel then \(J_B>0\). Combining (7.3)–(7.5), its total incoming **unit** load is at most

\[
\boxed{
L(B)
\le
\gamma_w\frac{u}{N-2w}.
}
\tag{7.6}
\]

This already gives the unweighted shell inequality

\[
|\mathcal D_w|
\le
\gamma_w\frac{u}{N-2w}|\mathcal C_u|.
\tag{7.7}
\]

Now enter Borel time. Put

\[
x=s/N,
\qquad
\rho_w(x)=\sin^w x\cos^{N-w}x.
\]

By (3.7), \(0<x<\pi/2\) throughout the live corridor, and therefore

\[
\frac{\rho_w(x)}{\rho_{w+2}(x)}=\cot^2x.
\tag{7.8}
\]

Give every source \(A\) its exact debit mass \(\rho_w(x)\), split equally over its good channels. By (7.6), the radial load arriving at any target is at most

\[
\rho_w(x)\gamma_w\frac{u}{N-2w}.
\]

Hence, whenever

\[
\boxed{
\tan^2x
\ge
\gamma_w\frac{w+2}{N-2w},
}
\tag{7.9}
\]

that load is at most the target's exact negative-credit capacity \(\rho_{w+2}(x)\). By Section 5 every target is a genuine spent negative credit, with no terminal re-entry.

Therefore

\[
\boxed{
\rho_w(x)|\mathcal D_w|
\le
\rho_{w+2}(x)|\mathcal C_{w+2}|
}
\tag{7.10}
\]

under (7.2) and (7.9).

Equation (7.10) is a **unit-capacity signed payment** of the entire shell-\(w\) debit sector by the actual adjacent shell of negative credits. It is not a bound on absolute parity frustration and does not reuse a credit more than once: each \(2\bmod4\) shell has only the immediately preceding \(0\bmod4\) shell as its debit source in this construction.

# 8. `P03-SIGNED-BOREL-PAID-CONE-91` — PROVED

The parameters in Section 7 admit a simple universal shell criterion.

For \(w\le N/4\),

\[
\frac{N-w}{N-2w}\le\frac32.
\]

Using \(\ell_w=2w\log_2(N/w)/N\),

\[
\alpha_w
\le
\frac{3NJ(f)}{4w\log_2(N/w)},
\qquad
\beta_w
=
\frac{4n}{w\log_2(N/w)}.
\tag{8.1}
\]

Thus if

\[
\boxed{
 w\log_2\frac Nw
\ge
\max\{3NJ(f),16n\},
}
\tag{8.2}
\]

then

\[
\alpha_w\le\frac14,
\qquad
\beta_w\le\frac14,
\qquad
\gamma_w\le\frac53.
\tag{8.3}
\]

Consequently the whole debit shell is pointwise paid whenever

\[
\boxed{
\tan^2(s/N)
\ge
\frac53\frac{w+2}{N-2w}.
}
\tag{8.4}
\]

Equivalently, if

\[
y=\tan^2(s/N),
\]

then (8.4) is

\[
w\le W(s):=rac{3Ny-10}{5+6y}.
\tag{8.5}
\]

Therefore, at each Borel time \(1\le s\le S_f\), every shell

\[
\boxed{
\begin{gathered}
w\equiv0\pmod4,\qquad
4\le w\le\min\{N/4-2,W(s)\},\\
 w\log_2(N/w)\ge\max\{3NJ(f),16n\}
\end{gathered}
}
\tag{8.6}
\]

has its **entire positive R32 debit contribution paid pointwise** by the adjacent \(w+2\) negative-credit shell.

Let \(\mathcal U(s)\) denote the remaining deposited debit relations after removing all shells certified by (8.6). Since all unused credits are still negative, the exact signed pairing implies the one-sided residual bound

\[
\boxed{
\mathfrak G_{d_h}(s)
\le
2N\sum_{A\in\mathcal U(s)}
\sin^{|A|}(s/N)\cos^{N-|A|}(s/N).
}
\tag{8.7}
\]

This is **not** obtained by replacing the original signed sum with its positive part. It is obtained only after an explicit capacity-respecting cancellation of selected debits against actual negative credits. Equation (8.7) is therefore a legitimate sufficient reduction, not an assertion that discarded credits were unnecessary.

Combining with the exact corridor target, a sufficient remaining inequality is

\[
8N\int_1^{S_f}
\frac1{s^3}
\sum_{A\in\mathcal U(s)}
\sin^{|A|}(s/N)\cos^{N-|A|}(s/N)
\,ds
\le C J(f).
\tag{8.8}
\]

The exact universal obligation remains the signed equation (0.2); (8.8) is the newly pruned sufficient residual.

# 9. `P03-ADJACENT-PREMODE-BARRIER-92` — PROVED AS A METHOD LIMITATION

The new unit-capacity theorem identifies exactly why the local adjacent-shell flow still does not close FEI.

For one radial shell,

\[
\rho_w(x)=\sin^w x\cos^{N-w}x,
\]

and

\[
\frac{d}{dx}\log\rho_w(x)
=w\cot x-(N-w)\tan x.
\]

Thus the first-quadrant radial mode is at

\[
\boxed{
\tan^2x_{\rm mode}=\frac{w}{N-w}.
}
\tag{9.1}
\]

Even in the formal ideal limit with zero defect loss and no source/target influence distortion, \(\alpha_w=\beta_w=0\), the local unit-capacity criterion (7.9) becomes

\[
\tan^2x
\ge
\frac{w+2}{N-2w}.
\tag{9.2}
\]

But

\[
(w+2)(N-w)-w(N-2w)
=N+w^2-w-2>0,
\]

so

\[
\boxed{
\frac{w+2}{N-2w}
>
\frac{w}{N-w}.
}
\tag{9.3}
\]

Therefore this **local equal-split adjacent-shell certificate necessarily begins after the radial mode**. For \(w\to\infty\), \(w=o(N)\), the threshold ratio is only

\[
\frac{(w+2)(N-w)}{w(N-2w)}
=1+O(1/w+w/N),
\]

so the gap is asymptotically small, but it is structurally on the wrong side of the maximum.

This does not disprove a better signed matching. It disproves the hope that the present local adjacent flow, by itself and with no cross-order or cross-time credit, can certify the entire radial shell. The live residual is now specifically the **pre-mode Borel mass**, plus shells failing the source-isoperimetric/defect condition and the heavier shells outside the low-shell range.

This points directly back to the retained R16 odd-order refunds and to global shell/time matching rather than to another positive-part estimate.
