# 4. `P03-POINTWISE-PARITY-RESOLVENT-51` — **PROVED**

The R32 parity-frustration expansion has an exact **pointwise resolvent-scale** version.

For a truth-table phase `v in {+1,-1}^N`, define its original-cube squared Fourier law

\[
p_v(S)=\widehat v(S)^2
\]

and for `t>0`

\[
G_t(v)=\sum_S\frac{p_v(S)}{p_v(S)+t}.
\tag{4.1}
\]

For a truth-table subset `A`, write

\[
v_A=\prod_{x\in A}v_x,
\qquad w=|A|,
\]

and let

\[
\widetilde G_t(A)=E_v[G_t(v)v_A]
\tag{4.2}
\]

be its Fourier coefficient on the `N` independent truth-table signs.

Set

\[
a=N\sqrt t,
\qquad
K_{w,N}(a)
=
\int_0^\infty e^{-as}
\sin^w s\cos^{N-w}s\,ds.
\tag{4.3}
\]

For nonempty `A`,

\[
\boxed{
\widetilde G_t(A)
=
-N a\,(-1)^{w/2}
K_{w,N}(a)
\,1_{\{w\ {m even},\ \oplus A=0\}}.
}
\tag{4.4}
\]

Because `N` is even, for every even `w`, both exponents in (4.3) are even, so

\[
K_{w,N}(a)>0.
\tag{4.5}
\]

## 4.1 Proof

Fix one original Fourier label `S`.  Put

\[
u_x=v_x\chi_S(x),
\qquad
Y=\sum_xu_x.
\]

The map `v -> u` preserves the uniform truth-table law and

\[
\widehat v(S)^2=Y^2/N^2.
\]

Moreover

\[
v_A=u_A\chi_S(\oplus A).
\]

Summing over all original characters gives the exact XOR filter

\[
\sum_S\chi_S(\oplus A)
=N1_{\{\oplus A=0\}}.
\tag{4.6}
\]

For nonempty `A`, `E u_A=0`.  Therefore, with `a=N sqrt(t)`,

\[
E\left[
\frac{Y^2}{Y^2+a^2}u_A
\right]
=-a^2E\frac{u_A}{Y^2+a^2}.
\tag{4.7}
\]

Use the elementary Laplace identity

\[
\frac1{Y^2+a^2}
=
\frac1a\int_0^\infty e^{-as}\cos(sY)ds.
\tag{4.8}
\]

Since the `u_x` are independent signs,

\[
E[u_Ae^{isY}]
=(i\sin s)^w(\cos s)^{N-w}.
\tag{4.9}
\]

Taking the real part gives zero for odd `w` and

\[
E[u_A\cos(sY)]
=(-1)^{w/2}\sin^ws\cos^{N-w}s
\]

for even `w`.  Substitution into (4.7), then summation by (4.6), proves (4.4).

No series interchange is used.  The Laplace integral is absolutely convergent for every `a>0`; the integrand is bounded by `e^{-as}`.

# 7. `P03-SCALE-RADIAL-RECURRENCE-54` — **PROVED**

The new pointwise kernel admits an exact adjacent-shell recurrence and a scale-wise radial ratio estimate.

Write

\[
K_w(a)=K_{w,N}(a),
\qquad
F_w(s)=\sin^w s\cos^{N-w}s.
\]

A direct differentiation gives

\[
F_w''
=w(w-1)F_{w-2}
+(N-w)(N-w-1)F_{w+2}
-[2w(N-w)+N]F_w.
\tag{7.1}
\]

For even `4<=w<=N-2`, `F_w(0)=F_w'(0)=0`, while `e^{-as}` kills the bounded periodic endpoint at infinity.  Two integrations by parts therefore give

\[
\int_0^\infty e^{-as}F_w''(s)ds=a^2K_w(a).
\]

Hence

\[
\boxed{
[a^2+2w(N-w)+N]K_w
=w(w-1)K_{w-2}
+(N-w)(N-w-1)K_{w+2}.
}
\tag{7.2}
\]

Because `N,w` are even,

\[
K_w(a)
=\int_0^\infty
|\tan s|^w e^{-as}|\cos s|^Nds.
\]

Thus the even sequence is a moment sequence of a positive measure, and Cauchy–Schwarz gives

\[
\boxed{K_w^2\le K_{w-2}K_{w+2}.}
\tag{7.3}
\]

Put

\[
x=\frac{K_w}{K_{w+2}},
\quad
A=w(w-1),
\quad
B=(N-w)(N-w-1),
\quad
C=a^2+2w(N-w)+N.
\]

From (7.3), `K_{w-2}/K_w>=x`; dividing (7.2) by `K_w` gives

\[
C\ge Ax+B/x.
\]

Therefore

\[
Ax^2-Cx+B\le0
\]

and

\[
\boxed{
\frac{K_w(a)}{K_{w+2}(a)}
\le
\frac{C+\sqrt{C^2-4AB}}{2A}.
}
\tag{7.4}
\]

For `w->infinity`, `w=o(N)`, and `a^2=o(wN)`, the right side is

\[
\frac Nw(1+o(1)).
\tag{7.5}
\]

In resolvent variables `a=N sqrt(t)`, this sharp radial regime is

\[
t=o(w/N).
\tag{7.6}
\]

This is the first radial comparison in the P03 ledger that is **pointwise in the Shannon resolvent scale**, rather than only after integrating to `I_{w,N}`.  It is compatible with the earlier integrated ratio theorem and can be inserted into physical/SQS flow without changing signs.

It does not close the central flow: for `a^2 >> wN`, the ratio can grow like `a^2/w^2`, so the large-`a` part of a small truth-table shell still needs a separate signed or moment payment.
