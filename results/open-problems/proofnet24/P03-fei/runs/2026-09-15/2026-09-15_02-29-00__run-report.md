## STRONGEST NEW RESULT 1 — P03-SELFORTH-GAUSS-COMPILER-15 — PROVED

The earlier compiler's **unweighted** parity-frustration imbalance is now exactly solved at every individual partial mask state as long as the retained mask code is doubly-even self-orthogonal.

Let \(n\ge3\), \(N=2^n\). Let \(M\) be any binary truth-table mask code satisfying
\[
RM(1,n)\subseteq M\subseteq M^\perp,
\qquad |\sigma|\equiv0\pmod4\quad(\sigma\in M),
\]
and put \(D=M^\perp\subseteq C_0\). Every \(A\in D\) has even weight, so define
\[
q(A)=\frac{|A|}{2}\pmod2.
\]
For a truth-table exponent \(z\in\mathbb F_2^N\), define
\[
S_D(z)=\sum_{A\in D}(-1)^{q(A)+z\cdot A}.
\]
Then
\[
\boxed{
S_D(z)=
\begin{cases}
0,&z\notin D,\\
(-1)^{q(z)}2^{N/2},&z\in D.
\end{cases}}
\tag{3}
\]
In particular, the signed count of frustrated relations inside \(D\),
\[
G_D(z)=\sum_{\substack{A\in D\\z\cdot A=1}}(-1)^{q(A)},
\]
is exactly
\[
\boxed{
G_D(z)=
\begin{cases}
2^{N/2-1},&z\notin D,\\
0,&z\in D,\ q(z)=0,\\
2^{N/2},&z\in D,\ q(z)=1.
\end{cases}}
\tag{4}
\]

### Proof

On even truth-table vectors,
\[
q(A+B)=q(A)+q(B)+A\cdot B\pmod2.
\tag{5}
\]
Because \(M\subseteq D\), \(A\cdot\sigma=0\) for \(A\in D,\sigma\in M\), and because \(M\) is doubly even, \(q(\sigma)=0\).

First evaluate \(S_D(0)\) by finite character orthogonality. Since \(1_D(A)=|M|^{-1}\sum_{\sigma\in M}(-1)^{\sigma\cdot A}\),
\[
\begin{aligned}
S_D(0)
&=\frac1{|M|}\sum_{\sigma\in M}
\sum_{A\in\mathbb F_2^N}(-1)^{\sigma\cdot A}i^{|A|}\\
&=\frac1{|M|}\sum_{\sigma\in M}
(1+i)^{N-|\sigma|}(1-i)^{|\sigma|}.
\end{aligned}
\]
Now \(N\) is divisible by eight and every \(|\sigma|\) is divisible by four, hence every phase is +1 and every term equals \(2^{N/2}\). Therefore
\[
S_D(0)=2^{N/2}.
\tag{6}
\]

If \(z\notin D=M^\perp\), choose \(\sigma\in M\) with \(z\cdot\sigma=1\). Translation \(A\mapsto A+\sigma\) preserves \(q(A)\) but flips \((-1)^{z\cdot A}\), so \(S_D(z)=0\).

If \(z\in D\), translation \(A\mapsto A+z\) and (5) give
\[
q(A)+z\cdot A=q(A+z)+q(z),
\]
so \(S_D(z)=(-1)^{q(z)}S_D(0)\). This proves (3), and (4) follows from
\[
1_{\{z\cdot A=1\}}=\frac{1-(-1)^{z\cdot A}}2.
\]

### Compiler consequence

Take the initial dual \(C_0=RM(1,n)^\perp\), which satisfies the same theorem with \(M=RM(1,n)\). Since \(D\subseteq C_0\), subtraction gives the exact spent unweighted imbalance
\[
\boxed{
\sum_{\substack{A\in C_0\setminus D\\z\cdot A=1}}(-1)^{q(A)}
=
\begin{cases}
0,&z\notin C_0\setminus D,\\
(-1)^{q(z)+1}2^{N/2-1},&z\in C_0\setminus D.
\end{cases}}
\tag{7}
\]
Thus it is never an arbitrary debit/credit cardinality defect: it is either zero or one square-root Gauss quantum.

This applies to **individual actual monomial appends**, not merely complete degree layers, throughout the self-orthogonal regime. Indeed, any span of actual monomial truth tables of degree at most \(k\) with \(2k\le n-1\), after the affine masks have been retained, is self-orthogonal: the product of two generators has degree at most \(2k\le n-1\) and hence even truth-table weight. Every generator has weight divisible by four. Therefore every partial state through degree \(\lfloor(n-1)/2\rfloor\) satisfies (3)–(7).

This is directly on the priority compiler route and retains the full actual code constraints and common Boolean signs. It does **not** prove (1), because R32 weights each shell by the highly nonconstant \(I_{w,N}\).

## STRONGEST NEW RESULT 2 — P03-RADIAL-STEP-16 — PROVED

The exact radial mismatch across one parity-flipping physical move is now quantitatively bracketed.

For even \(4\le w\le N-2\),
\[
\boxed{
(w+1)I_{w,N}-(N-w-1)I_{w+2,N}
=3\int_0^\infty
\frac{\sin^{w+1}t\,\cos^{N-w-1}t}{t^4}\,dt>0.
}
\tag{8}
\]
Hence
\[
\boxed{
\frac{I_{w,N}}{I_{w+2,N}}>
\frac{N-w-1}{w+1}.
}
\tag{9}
\]

For the reverse bound, let \(I^0_{w,N}\) be the integral over \([0,\pi/2]\). Periodicity and monotonicity of \(t^{-3}\) give
\[
I_{w,N}\le\left(2+\frac{\zeta(3)}4\right)I^0_{w,N}.
\tag{10}
\]
With \(x=\sin^2 t\) and \(\sqrt x\le\arcsin\sqrt x\le(\pi/2)\sqrt x\),
\[
\frac12\left(\frac2\pi\right)^3
B\!\left(\frac{w-2}{2},\frac{N-w+1}{2}\right)
\le I^0_{w,N}
\le
\frac12
B\!\left(\frac{w-2}{2},\frac{N-w+1}{2}\right).
\tag{11}
\]
Taking the upper bound for \(w\) and lower bound for \(w+2\), the beta ratio is exactly \((N-w-1)/(w-2)\). Therefore
\[
\boxed{
\frac{I_{w,N}}{I_{w+2,N}}
\le C_{\rm rad}\frac{N-w-1}{w-2},
\qquad
C_{\rm rad}=\left(2+\frac{\zeta(3)}4\right)\left(\frac\pi2\right)^3
<8.917.
}
\tag{12}
\]

For (8), differentiate \(\sin^{w+1}t\cos^{N-w-1}t\), integrate against \(t^{-3}\), and integrate by parts. The boundary terms vanish. The remaining \(t^{-4}\) integral is positive: on each period \([k\pi,(k+1)\pi]\), pair \(k\pi+u\) with \(k\pi+\pi-u\), \(0<u<\pi/2\). Their magnitudes coincide, their signs are opposite, and the positive term has the smaller denominator. The \(k=0\) endpoint is integrable because \(w\ge4\).

This proves rigorously why a one-to-one debit/credit pairing cannot be the right target at low truth-table weight: one weight-\(w\) debit is intrinsically of order \(N/w\) heavier than one adjacent weight-\(w+2\) credit.
