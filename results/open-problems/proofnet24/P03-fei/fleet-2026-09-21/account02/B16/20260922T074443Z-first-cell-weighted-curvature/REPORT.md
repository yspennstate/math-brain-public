## 3. New theorem: the two secants are one weighted slope-jump curvature

### Theorem 3.1 — exact curvature reduction

For all real first-cell slopes `s1,...,s4`,

\[
\boxed{m_{12}-m_1=\kappa.}
\tag{3.2}
\]
Consequently the source gate criterion (2.1) is exactly

\[
\boxed{(1-Q)m_1-Q\kappa\ge\Theta.}
\tag{3.3}

### Proof

Since `a1+a2=1`,

\[
m_1=s_1+a_2d_2.
\]
Since `b2+b3+b4=1`, substitute
` s2=s1+d2`, `s3=s1+d2+d3`, and `s4=s1+d2+d3+d4` to obtain

\[
m_{12}=s_1+d_2+(b_3+b_4)d_3+b_4d_4.
\]
Subtracting gives

\[
m_{12}-m_1=(1-a_2)d_2+(b_3+b_4)d_3+b_4d_4
=a_1d_2+(b_3+b_4)d_3+b_4d_4=\kappa.
\]
Substitute `m12=m1+kappa` in (2.1) to get (3.3). `QED`

The crucial structural point is that all three coefficients in (3.1) are positive. The source gate is therefore testing a concrete positive weighted **forward curvature** of the candidate's first four refined slope cells; no global slope norm or unsigned gate relaxation is needed.
