## 3. Strict prime-gate central reserve

Let \(P\) be an essential composition-prime five-input Boolean gate. By (2.1), \(P\) is not an equality case of (1.2). Therefore the peer's certified strict-gap floor gives

\[
U(P)-U(F_m)\ge\delta_5,
\qquad \delta_5=0.004906502214,
\tag{3.1}
\]

where \(m\) is the output cardinality of \(P\).

The read-once certificate for \(F_m\) gives

\[
C_{\rm cert}I(F_m)-H(F_m)-\Gamma_{.01}(m/32)\ge0.
\tag{3.2}
\]

At uniform input bias, the gate slack is exactly

\[
D_{\Gamma_{.01}}(P;1/2^5)
=C_{\rm cert}I(P)-H(P)-\Gamma_{.01}(m/32).
\tag{3.3}
\]

Combining (3.1)--(3.3),

\[
\boxed{
D_{\Gamma_{.01}}(P;1/2^5)\ge\delta_5.
}
\tag{3.4}
\]

Now use the source-exact scalar-lift identity. Increasing the quadratic coefficient by \(c\) changes gate slack by \(cM_P\), where

\[
M_P=\frac{I(P)}4-p_{\rm out}(1-p_{\rm out})\ge0
\]

by Poincare. Since \(a^\dagger>0.01\),

\[
D_{\Gamma^\dagger}(P;1/2^5)
=D_{\Gamma_{.01}}(P;1/2^5)
+(a^\dagger-0.01)M_P
\ge\delta_5.
\]

Hence

\[
\boxed{
D_{\Gamma^\dagger}(P;1/2,\ldots,1/2)
\ge0.004906502214
}
\tag{3.5}
\]

for **every essential composition-prime five-input gate**.

Degenerate gates reduce to smaller arity; decomposable gates are already handled by the exact composition cocycle once their prime factors are handled. Thus (3.5) is the precise five-input prime statement needed by the common candidate.
