## 1. Functional form of the valuation-tail theorem

Let `r` denote `r_a` under packet law `p`. Let `phi:{0,...,n}->[0,infinity)` be nondecreasing with `phi(0)=0`.

### Theorem B15-VALUATION-FUNCTIONAL-01

For every such `phi`,

`boxed{ E_p phi(r) <= 2 sum_{k=1}^n q_k^down phi(k). }`             (1.1)

### Proof

Put `Delta_m=phi(m)-phi(m-1)>=0`. The layer-cake identity gives

`E phi(r)=sum_{m=1}^n Delta_m Pr(r>=m)`.

The consumed valuation theorem gives

`Pr(r>=m)<=2 sum_{k=m}^n q_k^down`.

Therefore

`E phi(r) <= 2 sum_m Delta_m sum_{k>=m} q_k^down`

`=2 sum_k q_k^down sum_{m<=k}Delta_m`

`=2 sum_k q_k^down phi(k)`.

QED.

No convexity assumption is needed. For `phi(k)=k` this recovers `R<=2J`. For `t>=1`, taking `phi(k)=t^k-1` yields the exponential-moment bound

`boxed{ E_p t^r <= 1+2 sum_{k=1}^n q_k^down (t^k-1). }`            (1.2)

Thus every increasing functional of the signed valuation-depth distribution is controlled by the tail profile of the folded influences, not merely its first moment.

## 2. The canonical valuation escort

For `theta>=0`, define

`Z_theta = E_p 2^{-theta r_a}`                                      (2.1)

and

`p_theta(a)=p_a 2^{-theta r_a}/Z_theta`.                            (2.2)

Write

`R_theta=E_{p_theta} r_a`.

All KL divergences below use base-2 logarithms.

### Theorem B15-VALUATION-ESCORT-02

For every `theta>=0`, exactly

`boxed{ D(p||p_theta)=theta R+log_2 Z_theta, }`                      (2.3)

`boxed{ D(p_theta||p)=-theta R_theta-log_2 Z_theta, }`              (2.4)

and hence

`boxed{ D(p||p_theta)+D(p_theta||p)=theta(R-R_theta). }`            (2.5)

In particular,

`boxed{ D(p||p_theta)+D(p_theta||p) <= theta R <= 2 theta J. }`     (2.6)

Moreover

`boxed{ -log_2 Z_theta <= theta R <= 2 theta J. }`                  (2.7)

### Proof

From (2.2),

`log_2(p_a/p_theta(a))=theta r_a+log_2 Z_theta`.

Taking expectation under `p` gives (2.3). Reversing the likelihood ratio and taking expectation under `p_theta` gives (2.4). Adding proves (2.5). Since exponential tilting by `2^{-theta r}` favors smaller `r`, one has `R_theta<=R`; alternatively this follows directly from the covariance derivative of `log Z_theta`. Thus the left side of (2.5) is nonnegative and at most `theta R`. The consumed theorem gives `R<=2J`, proving (2.6).

For (2.7), convexity of `x -> 2^{-theta x}` and Jensen give

`Z_theta >= 2^{-theta E_p r}=2^{-theta R}`.

Take `-log_2`. QED.

### Canonical arithmetic value `theta=2`

The signed carry normalization uses `4^{-r}=2^{-2r}`. Put

`p^val_a = p_a 4^{-r_a}/Z_val`, `Z_val=E_p 4^{-r_a}`.              (2.8)

`boxed{ D(p||p^val)+D(p^val||p) = 2(R-E_{p^val}r) <= 4J. }`         (2.9)
