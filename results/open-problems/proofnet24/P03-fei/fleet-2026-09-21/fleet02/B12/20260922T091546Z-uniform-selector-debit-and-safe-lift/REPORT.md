## 3. Main theorem: intrinsic selector entropy cancels both endpoints uniformly in bias

The input-coordinate price in the all-gate inequality contains

\[

\tag{3.1}
\]

\[

\tag{3.2}
\]

### Theorem 3.1 — selector-bias-free disagreement envelope

For every genuine Boolean gate, every physical selector coordinate, and every interior selector bias,

\[
\boxed{
Q_s(d)
\le
h_2(d)+\frac2{\ln2}\min\{d,1-d\}.
}
\tag{3.3}
\]

The right side is independent of `s` and vanishes at `d=0` and `d=1`.

### Proof

From (2.6)--(2.7),

\[
Q_s(d)
\le
h_2(V_s d)-d h_2(V_s)
+\kappa(s)V_s\min\{d,1-d\}.
\tag{3.4}
\]

The first difference is itself an information quantity. Let `A~Bernoulli(d)` and `Z~Bernoulli(V_s)` be independent and put `Y=AZ`. Then

\[
H(Y)=h_2(V_s d),
\qquad
H(Y\mid A)=d h_2(V_s),
\]

so

\[
h_2(V_s d)-d h_2(V_s)=I(A;Y)\le H(A)=h_2(d).
\tag{3.5}
\]

For the second coefficient write

\[
x=|1-2s|\in[0,1),
\qquad
V_s=1-x^2.
\]

The continuous extension of (0.2) gives

\[
\kappa(s)V_s
=\frac2{\ln2}\frac{(1-x^2)\operatorname{artanh}x}{x}.
\tag{3.6}
\]

Set

\[
g(x)=x-(1-x^2)\operatorname{artanh}x.
\]

Then `g(0)=0` and

\[
g'(x)=2x\operatorname{artanh}x\ge0.
\]

Hence `g(x)>=0`, which is exactly

\[
\kappa(s)V_s\le\frac2{\ln2}.
\tag{3.7}
\]

Substituting (3.5) and (3.7) in (3.4) proves (3.3). `QED`.
