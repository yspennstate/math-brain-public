# 3. Local Boolean gate estimate with exact terminal-compatible normalization

\[

=-\frac2N\sum_{x\in F\setminus Q}(-1)^{q_x+m_x},

\[

=-\frac2N\sum_{x\in F\cap Q}(-1)^{q_x+m_x}.
\tag{3.1}

Because \(P\supseteq RM(1,n)\), its dual has no nonzero word of weight two. Averaging over \(m\in P\) therefore kills every distinct pair and gives the exact identities
\[

\qquad

\qquad
\mathbb E_P\delta u\,\delta v=0.
\tag{3.2}

\[

\tag{3.3}

\[
