# Completion and source boundary

The final authority is [the complete sharpened proof](bounded_ascending_path_support_fei_proof.md)
with the separate full root and heat audits. The original candidate is
preserved with its older valid weaker constants. Best proved bound:
H(f)<=4 cot^2(pi/[2(k+1)]) I(f) when either label has ascending paths of
at most k VERTICES. For edge-free label classes, constant4 is optimal.
All k-Sperner families qualify, and many families with longer skipped-rank
chains also qualify. The weighted-band corollary permits arbitrary
internal labels but requires every coordinate weight to be nonzero.

The published nilpotent numerical-radius theorem is Haagerup--de la Harpe,
ProcAMS115(1992)371--379, DOI10.1090/S0002-9939-1992-1072339-6:
https://access.archive-ouverte.unige.ch/access/metadata/2e3d55fc-f7d2-42af-95ae-6c06df864dbb/download
It was checked from the primary author archive and is proved again by a
finite-dimensional dilation in the full note; it is credited prior work.

The root checked Shalev's primary arXiv1806.03646v2(24Jan2019) introduction
and theorem statements, including the existing extremal-influence,
high-entropy, spectral-norm and read-k classes:
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1806.03646
This is a bounded prior-art check and does not certify novelty. Current
general FEI formulation was checked against Han's original article
(published20Dec2024) and Gonzalez--MacManus--Pereyra2606.00246v2:
https://doi.org/10.1007/s00493-024-00133-z
https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.00246
No full-FEI or strong I-Var claim is made. Integer energy/path checks and
numerical entropy/operator checks are clearly distinguished.
