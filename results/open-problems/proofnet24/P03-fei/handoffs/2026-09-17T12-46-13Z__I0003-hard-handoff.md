## 1. Exact source model and notation

Write horizontal line orientations as `a_y in {+1,-1}` and vertical line orientations as `b_x in {+1,-1}`. From `(x,y)` the exact source walk moves to `(x+a_y,y)` with probability `1/2` and to `(x,y+b_x)` with probability `1/2`.

Choose anchored one-dimensional primitives

`alpha_y-alpha_{y-1}=a_y`, `beta_x-beta_{x-1}=b_x`, `alpha_0=beta_0=0`,

and face stream

`psi_{x,y}=(alpha_y-beta_x)/4`.

The anchored envelope is

`M(R)=sup_{|x|,|y|<=R}|alpha_y-beta_x|`.

Counting measure is invariant, and the symmetric part of the transition operator is planar simple random walk. Those are source/background facts, not P03 novelty.

## 3. Exact blocker at handoff

For a collapsed finite box the exact Gaudilliere--Landim capacity is an `H^{-1}(-S)` norm of `L^*F`. Every universal P03 estimate currently available turns the skew term into a quadratic stream penalty. In the worst case this produces a resistance/capacity series of the form

`sum_r 1/[r(1+M(r)^2)]`,

whose fixed-power threshold is `M(r)~(log r)^(1/2)`.

The adaptive comparison network can certify recurrence but is one-sided and cannot certify transience. Ordinary continuum scaling sends every polylogarithmic primitive to zero, while scaling by stream amplitude is noncompact. Claim 239 shows large stream can coexist with recurrence when it has favorable geometry. Claim 243 shows even exact iid local statistics do not determine transience.

A proof that moves the frozen metric must therefore add one of the following genuinely new ingredients:

- a nonlocal `H^{-1}` cancellation estimate strong enough to prove universal recurrence for a fixed exponent `gamma>1/2`; or
- a source-valid deterministic transience mechanism producing a field with rigorously controlled slow stream growth, preferably `M(R)=O((log R)^gamma)` for some explicit `gamma>1/2`.

Finite comparison-network resistance by itself is insufficient for the second option.
