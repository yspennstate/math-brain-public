## Main changes

1. For every Boolean f on n bits, a rare Boolean replacement F_t on 3n+t bits preserves H and I in the limit but makes R(F_t) tend to zero. The explicit selector uses 2n bits and distinct dyadic character weights; t fresh flag bits select a rare replacement. Hence D(F_t) tends to H(f)/2.
2. Consequently the optimal universal constants satisfy C_FEI=2 sup_f D(f)/I(f). This removes the additive four from the universal implication, not from a pointwise inequality. H<=2D pointwise is false.
3. The transitive function T_k=2 product_{j=1}^{2^k-1}(1-1_{block_j=0})-1 has R=0, but every coordinate satisfies Delta_i D/Inf_i >= (N-2)(1-1/N)^(N-3)>(N-2)/3. All coordinates are relevant. This refutes the existential one-coordinate D-payment rule for every fixed constant.
4. Every nontrivial adaptive physical restriction tree of any fixed depth d has a positive D drop bounded below independently of N and consumes at most d 2^d/N influence. Thus bounded-depth batching does not repair this potential. Immediate stopping on a separately certified root, unbounded-depth policies, and other potentials are not ruled out. D+lambda R has the same obstruction for any fixed lambda<1.
5. Standard balanced composition of the explicit four-input gate keeps power-of-two Fourier magnitudes and yields H/I tending to six. The coefficient-count and actual-input signed-mask recurrences permit an exact compressed finite D/I>2.18 certificate at depth five without a 2^1024 truth table.
6. The explicit nine-variable two-magnitude example requires quarter-valued magnitude components. Two bands do not imply a half-integer component grid.
7. Band-label entropy alone cannot pay D: on 2^j-1 tensor copies of the earlier five-variable example, D=2^j-1 while band-label entropy is at most j.

The transitive family has H/I tending to four and D/I tending to two. None of these constructions is an FEI counterexample. The surviving global D/I upper bound is not proved.
