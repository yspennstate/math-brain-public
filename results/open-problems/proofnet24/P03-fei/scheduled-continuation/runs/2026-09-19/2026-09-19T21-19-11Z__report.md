### 2. Exact fresh-control affine-drift identity

The rare-controls report gives the exact value of `M(Gamma_c f)` but did not compute the **fixed-domain multiplicative-derivative drift** of the controlled function. That quantity has an exact formula.

Define the unfolded drift

\[
\Delta_0(f)=2M_0(f)-\mathbb E_v M_0(f_v).
\tag{6}
\]

**Theorem.** For every Boolean `f`, every fresh control block of size `c>=3`, `p=2^{-c}`, and `d=1-Ef`,

\[
\boxed{
\Delta_{\rm aff}(\Gamma_c f)
=p^2\Delta_0(f)+2p(1-p)d+c p^2d^2.}
\tag{7}
\]

This is an equality, not a finite-dimensional extrapolation.

**Proof.** Put `G=Gamma_c f`. The existing exact formula is

\[
M(G)=p[M_0(f)+cd].
\tag{8}
\]

Write a derivative shift as `(u,v)`, with `u` in the `c` control coordinates and `v` in the old coordinates.

If `u=0`, then

\[
G_{(0,v)}=\Gamma_c(f_v),
\]

and since `1-E f_v=2\alpha_f(v)`, (8) gives

\[
M(G_{(0,v)})
=p[M_0(f_v)+2c\alpha_f(v)].
\tag{9}
\]

Now suppose `u\ne0`. The derivative is nontrivial only on the two control slices `z=0` and `z=u`, where its values are respectively `f(y)` and `f(y+v)`. For a test direction `(a,b)`, direct counting gives

\[
\alpha_{G_{(u,v)}}(a,b)=
\begin{cases}
2p\alpha_f(b),&a=0,\\
2p\alpha_f(b+v),&a=u,\\
2pd,&a\notin\{0,u\}.
\end{cases}
\tag{10}
\]

For `c>=3`, every value in (10) is at most `1/2`, so folded and unfolded directional costs coincide.

On the `(n+1)`-dimensional subspace with control component in `span{u}`, the direction `(u,v)` has zero cost. Quotienting by that direction identifies the remaining costs with `2p alpha_f(b)`. The minimum basis cost of this subspace is therefore exactly `2p M_0(f)`. The remaining `c-1` independent control quotient directions each have unavoidable cost `2pd`. Hence

\[
\boxed{M(G_{(u,v)})=2p[M_0(f)+(c-1)d],\qquad u\ne0.}
\tag{11}
\]

Average (9) and (11). The elementary autocorrelation identity

\[
\mathbb E_v\alpha_f(v)=\frac{1-(Ef)^2}{2}
=\frac{d(2-d)}2
\tag{12}
\]

then gives (7) after cancellation.

For completeness, `Delta_0(f)>=0`: choose an `M_0`-optimal basis with costs `a_i`. For each basis direction,

\[
\mathbb E_v\alpha_{f_v}(v_i)=2a_i(1-a_i),
\]

so

\[
\mathbb E_v M_0(f_v)
\le2M_0(f)-2\sum_i a_i^2.
\]

Thus

\[
\Delta_0(f)\ge2\sum_i a_i^2\ge0,
\tag{13}
\]

and (7) in particular yields the unconditional gate payment

\[
\Delta_{\rm aff}(\Gamma_c f)
\ge2p(1-p)d+c p^2d^2.
\tag{14}
\]

Equations (7), (11), and (14) connect the owner rare-control construction directly to the derivative-drift route without confusing the operation that **adds fresh variables** with a derivative-chain transition.
