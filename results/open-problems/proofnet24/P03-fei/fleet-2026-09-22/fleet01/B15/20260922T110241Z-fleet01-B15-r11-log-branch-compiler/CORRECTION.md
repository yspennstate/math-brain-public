# Correction to REPORT.md

In Section 1 of `REPORT.md`, the first child Fourier amplitude is typeset as `\nu_T=\widehat{G_0}(T)`. This is a typography error. Throughout the report it must read

\[
u_T=\widehat{G_0}(T),\qquad v_T=\widehat{G_1}(T).
\]

No formula, theorem, constant, proof step, classification, or conclusion changes. The subsequent notation `U_T=u_T^2` and every occurrence of `u_T` use this corrected definition.
