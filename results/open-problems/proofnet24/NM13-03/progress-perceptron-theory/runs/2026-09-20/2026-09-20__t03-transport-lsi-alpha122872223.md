## 3. New theorem — Gaussian transport/log-Sobolev entropy cage

### Theorem NM03-T03-TRANSPORT-LSI-CAGE

Under the strict-domain hypotheses above,

`(m^2/2)(p2-p3) M1 <= D_U <= (m^2/2)(p2-p3) M2`,              (3.1)

and

`(m/2)(1-p2) M2 <= D_H <= 2 m B^2 (1-p2) MB`.                 (3.2)

Consequently, at every full stationary point satisfying (2.1)--(2.2), the two nested c-sector equations obey the purely algebraic necessary inequalities

`boxed: (p2-p3) q3/2 <= A_c3 <= (p2-p3) q2/2`,               (3.3)

`boxed: (1-p2) q2/2 <= A_c2 <= (1-p2)/2`.                    (3.4)

These inequalities involve no nested Gaussian KL or log-partition evaluation.

### Proof

Fix `v` and abbreviate

`h(U)=m u(sqrt(p2-p3)U+sqrt(p3)v-1)`.

Then `nu_v` has density `e^h/E e^h` relative to standard Gaussian measure. The classical one-dimensional Gaussian log-Sobolev inequality in tilted form gives

`KL(nu_v||gamma_U) <= (1/2) E_{nu_v} |h'(U)|^2`.

Since

`h'(U)=m sqrt(p2-p3) u_x(X)`,

we obtain

`KL(nu_v||gamma_U) <= (m^2/2)(p2-p3)<u_x^2>_v`.

Averaging in `V` proves the upper half of (3.1).

For the lower half, use the elementary entropy variational formula. For any probability `nu<<gamma` and any real `t`,

`KL(nu||gamma) >= t E_nu U - log E_gamma e^{tU}`
`                 = t E_nu U - t^2/2`.

Optimizing in `t` gives

`KL(nu||gamma) >= (E_nu U)^2/2`.                              (3.5)

Gaussian integration by parts under the tilted density gives

`E_{nu_v} U = E_{nu_v} h'(U)`
`           = m sqrt(p2-p3)<u_x>_v`.

Insert this into (3.5):

`KL(nu_v||gamma_U) >= (m^2/2)(p2-p3)<u_x>_v^2`.

Average in `V` to get the lower half of (3.1).

Now fix `x` and put

`k(H)=-B(x+sH)_+^2`.

The same Gaussian log-Sobolev inequality gives

`KL(rho_x||gamma_H) <= (1/2) E_{rho_x}|k'(H)|^2`.

Away from the null kink set,

`k'(H)=-2Bs(x+sH)_+`,

so

`KL(rho_x||gamma_H)`
` <= 2 B^2 s^2 E_{rho_x}(x+sH)_+^2`
` = -2 B^2 s^2 u_B(x)`,                                      (3.6)

because `u_B(x)=-E_{rho_x}(x+sH)_+^2`.

Multiply (3.6) by `m`, average first under `nu_V` and then in `V`, and use `s^2=1-p2` and the definition of `MB`. This proves the upper half of (3.2).

For the lower half, apply (3.5) to `rho_x`. Gaussian integration by parts under the `H` tilt yields

`E_{rho_x} H = E_{rho_x} k'(H)`
`            = -2Bs E_{rho_x}(x+sH)_+`
`            = s u_x(x)`,

where the last equality is differentiation of `u(x)=log f(x)` with respect to `x`. Therefore

`KL(rho_x||gamma_H) >= s^2 u_x(x)^2/2`.

Multiply by `m` and average to obtain the lower half of (3.2).

Finally substitute (2.2) into (2.1). For `A_c3`, the factors satisfy `m=c3/c2`, so the lower and upper entropy bounds simplify exactly to

`alpha/c3^2 * (m^2/2)(p2-p3) M1 = (p2-p3)q3/2`,

`alpha/c3^2 * (m^2/2)(p2-p3) M2 = (p2-p3)q2/2`.

For `A_c2`,

`alpha/(c2c3) * (m/2)(1-p2)M2 = (1-p2)q2/2`,

while

`alpha/(c2c3) * 2mB^2(1-p2)MB = (1-p2)/2`.

This proves (3.3)--(3.4). QED.
