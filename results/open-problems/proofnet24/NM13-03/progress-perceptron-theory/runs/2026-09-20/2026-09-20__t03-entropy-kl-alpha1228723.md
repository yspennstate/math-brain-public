## 3. New theorem — c-stationarity is exactly relative-entropy matching

### Theorem NM03-T03-ENTROPY-CSECTOR

Define

`D_U := E_V KL(nu_V || gamma_U)`.

For every `x`, also define the one-step H-tilt

`d rho_x/d gamma_H = exp[-B(x+sH)_+^2]/f(x)`,

and

`D_H := m E_V < KL(rho_X || gamma_H) >_V`.

Then

`D_U = m QM - Q >= 0`,                                       (3.1)

`D_H = B Q_B - m QM >= 0`.                                  (3.2)

Consequently the source third-full c-stationarity equations (2.1)--(2.2) are exactly

`boxed: A_c3 = alpha D_U/c3^2`,                              (3.3)

`boxed: A_c2 = alpha D_H/(c2 c3)`.                           (3.4)

On the strict nondegenerate branch above, both entropy terms are strictly positive, hence every full stationary point satisfies the previously hidden algebraic sign conditions

`A_c3>0`, `A_c2>0`.                                         (3.5)

### Proof

For fixed `v`,

`log(d nu_v/d gamma_U)=m u(X)-log Z_v`.

Therefore

`KL(nu_v||gamma_U)=m<u>_v-log Z_v`.

Average over `V` to obtain (3.1).

For the H-tilt, write `h_x(H)=(x+sH)_+^2`. Since

`f(x)=E exp[-B h_x(H)]`,

ordinary differentiation under the Gaussian integral gives

`u_B(x)=partial_B log f(x)=-E_{rho_x} h_x(H)`.

Hence

`KL(rho_x||gamma_H)`
`=E_{rho_x}[-B h_x(H)-log f(x)]`
`=B u_B(x)-u(x)`.

Averaging under the inner tilt and then over `V` gives

`D_H=m E_V <B u_B-u>_V=B Q_B-m QM`,

which is (3.2).

Now use `m=c3/c2`. Equation (2.1) becomes

`0=A_c3 + alpha(Q-mQM)/c3^2`
`  =A_c3-alpha D_U/c3^2`,

which is (3.3). Equation (2.2) and (3.2) give (3.4).

Relative entropy is nonnegative. Strictness follows because `p2>p3` gives the inner Gaussian `U` positive variance and, for `B>0`, `u(X)` is not almost surely constant, so `nu_v` differs from the standard Gaussian on a positive-measure set. Likewise `s>0` and `B>0` make the one-step exponential tilt nonconstant on a positive-probability H-event. Thus both averaged KL divergences are strictly positive. QED.

### Why this is useful for certification

The previous four-scalar compiler expressed the c-residuals through cancellation-prone combinations of `Q,QM,Q_B`. The new form replaces those combinations by expectations of nonnegative KL integrands. For interval work this provides sign information before root certification and avoids proving positivity by subtracting comparable nested Gaussian terms.

## 5. New lemma — power-mean collapse of the nested comparison to one dimension

### Lemma NM03-T03-POWERMEAN-COLLAPSE

For fixed `V=v`, set `G=f(X)`. Since `0<G<1` and `0<m<1`,

`G <= G^m`.

Also `x -> x^m` is concave, so Jensen gives

`E_U G^m <= (E_U G)^m`.

Taking logarithms and averaging in `V` yields

`boxed: L <= Q <= m L`,                                      (5.1)

where

`L := E_V log E_U f(X)`.                                    (5.2)

Moreover the two independent Gaussians `U,H` combine exactly:

`E_U f(X)`
`= E_{U,H} exp[-B(sqrt(p2-p3)U+sqrt(p3)V-1+sqrt(1-p2)H)_+^2]`
`= f(sqrt(p3)V-1, sqrt(1-p3), B)`.                           (5.3)

Thus `L` is a **one-dimensional Gaussian expectation** and, notably, is independent of `p2` after the collapse.

Finally, on any smooth strict-interior stationary branch the already-proved envelope identity is

`d Psi3/d alpha = -Q/c3`.

Combining this with (5.1) and `m=c3/c2` gives the exact one-dimensional slope sandwich

`boxed: -L/c2 <= d Psi3/d alpha <= -L/c3`.                   (5.4)

### Proof

The pointwise inequality and Jensen argument above prove (5.1). Equation (5.3) follows because the Gaussian sum

`sqrt(p2-p3)U + sqrt(1-p2)H`

has variance `(p2-p3)+(1-p2)=1-p3`. Equation (5.4) follows from (5.1), noting that `L,Q<0` and dividing by `c3>0`. QED.
