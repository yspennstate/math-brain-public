## 1. Cumulative exact finite-network identities

Let `G=(V,E)` be finite and connected, `A,B` disjoint nonempty boundary sets, and `h` the equilibrium voltage `h|_A=0`, `h|_B=1`, minimizing

\[
\mathcal E_c(f)=\sum_{\{x,y\}\in E}c_{xy}(f(x)-f(y))^2.
\]

If a single edge `e={u,v}` is increased by `b>0`, write `d_e=h(u)-h(v)` and let `R_e` be the squared norm of the edge-evaluation functional in the zero-boundary Dirichlet space. Then

\[
C(c+b1_e)-C(c)=\frac{b d_e^2}{1+bR_e},\qquad 0\le R_e\le1/c_e.
\]

Starting from unit conductances and adaptively changing distinct edges `1->1+a`, before each update let `h_j` be the current equilibrium voltage and `e_j={u_j,v_j}` the updated edge. Then

\[
\sum_j(h_j(u_j)-h_j(v_j))^2\le(1+a)C_0.
\]

For a square annulus between `0` and `partial[-R,R]^2`, `C_0=Theta(1/log R)`, so the sum of squared dynamic-voltage gaps over all first traversals is `O_a(1/log R)` pathwise.

The rank-one voltage update is

\[
h'(w)-h(w)=-\frac{a d_e}{1+aR_e}z_e(w),
\]

where `z_e` is the dipole Riesz representer. At the arrival endpoint `y` of a first traversal `x->y`, reinforcement attenuates the old potential increment; the squared environment-update jumps also have total `O_a(1/log R)`, so their centered martingale fluctuation is small. The unresolved term is the predictable signed compensator.

## 2. Discovery-stage Feynman–Kac representation

Fix the currently traversed trace `S` and root `o`. Before the next discovery, old edges have common conductance `c=1+a` and fresh edges conductance `1`. Let `Z_t` be continuous-time simple random walk on the old trace with unit rate on each old edge and killed at `o`. Let `b_S(v)` be the number of fresh directed edges leaving `v` and

\[
A_t=\int_0^t b_S(Z_s)\,ds.
\]

Then the probability of another discovery before return to root is

\[
q_c(S,x)=1-E_xe^{-A_{\tau_o}/c},
\]

and for a particular fresh directed edge `f`,

\[
P_x(f\text{ is next fresh edge before }o)
=
\frac1cE_x\int_0^{\tau_o}e^{-A_t/c}1_{\{Z_t=f^-\}}dt.
\]

Hence stronger reinforcement decreases the total probability of the next discovery for a fixed trace. This does not order the location of the discovery.

## 3. New exact rank-one formula for the moving harmonic potential

For a fresh edge `e={x,y}` traversed from `x` to `y`, orient `z_e` with source `x`, sink `y`. Put

\[
\alpha_x=z_e(x),\qquad \alpha_y=-z_e(y),\qquad R_e=\alpha_x+\alpha_y.
\]

If `d=h(x)-h(y)`, then

\[
h'(y)-h(y)=\frac{a\alpha_y}{1+aR_e}d.
\]

Thus, with

\[
\theta_{x\to y}=\frac{a\alpha_y}{1+aR_e},
\]

one has `0<=theta<1` and

\[
h'(y)-h(x)=(1-\theta_{x\to y})[h(y)-h(x)].
\]

So reinforcement pulls the arrival potential toward the departure potential. If `J_j=h_{j+1}(X_{t_j+1})-h_j(X_{t_j+1})` is the environment-update jump at the `j`th first traversal, then

\[
\sum_jJ_j^2\le(1+a)C_0.
\]

Consequently, after centering these update jumps, the environment-update martingale has annular variance `O_a(1/log R)`. The remaining obstruction is the predictable compensator.

## 4. Candidate finite-graph first-excursion inequality

Let `G` be finite connected, root `o`, singleton target `b`, and `x~o`. Start immediately after the initial step `o->x` with only `{o,x}` reinforced. Let

\[
Q_c(G;o,x,b)=P(\tau_b<\tau_o),
\]

and let `h_0(x)` be the SRW hitting probability. Candidate:

\[
Q_c(G;o,x,b)\le h_0(x).
\]

If true for finite square boxes, planar recurrence follows from the SRW annular escape probability `O(1/log R)`. This remained open.

## 5. Exact unique-path theorem

If `o=v_0,v_1,...,v_R=b` is the unique simple root-target path, start immediately after `o->v_1`. Off-path components are self-loops for the embedded path-coordinate process. When the discovered path reaches `v_k`, the `k` edges behind it have conductance `c`, and the frontier edge has conductance `1`. Therefore

\[
P_{v_k}(\tau_{v_{k+1}}<\tau_{v_0})=\frac{k}{k+c},
\]

and

\[
P_{v_1}^{ORRW}(\tau_b<\tau_o)
=\prod_{k=1}^{R-1}\frac{k}{k+c}
=\frac{\Gamma(R)\Gamma(1+c)}{\Gamma(R+c)}
\le \frac1R.
\]

Thus the candidate first-excursion domination holds on every unique-path graph. Cycles are the genuine obstruction.
