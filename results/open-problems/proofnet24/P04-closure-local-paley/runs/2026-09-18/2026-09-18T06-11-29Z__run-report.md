## 3. B7 TAKE THE LIMIT — the actual six-channel masks are asymptotically Bernoulli-uniform

The earlier clean-family six-channel analysis used a uniform Bernoulli model on the six `S_3` channel signs as a heuristic / rigidity test. The key question was whether that model reflects the actual Paley arithmetic. It does, row by row, with an explicit square-root error.

### Claim 39

\[
\boxed{\texttt{P04-I0101-SIXMASK-EQUIDISTRIBUTION-39 = PROVED / STRUCTURAL-PARTIAL}.}
\]

Let `p == 5 (mod 24)` be prime. Then the anharmonic `S_3` action on the localized vertex set
\[
V_p=\{x\in\mathbb F_p\setminus\{0,1\}:\chi(x)=\chi(x-1)=1\}
\]
is free, and `|V_p|=(p-5)/4`. Fix `x in V_p`. For each `g in S_3`, define the channel sign at `y` by
\[
\epsilon_g(x,y)=\chi(x-g(y)).
\]
For a mask `sigma in {+/-1}^6`, let `M_sigma(x)` count vertices
\[
y\in V_p\setminus S_3x
\]
whose six channel signs equal `sigma`. Then, uniformly in `x` and `sigma`,
\[
\boxed{
\left|M_\sigma(x)-\frac{p-29}{256}\right|
\le \frac{705\sqrt p+1953}{256}
<3\sqrt p+8.
}
\]
In particular,
\[
\boxed{
\frac{M_\sigma(x)}{|V_p|-6}=\frac1{64}+O(p^{-1/2})
}
\]
uniformly in the row `x` and in the mask `sigma`.

### Proof

Use the already-proved level-4 cover
\[
x(t)=\left(\frac{1-t^2}{1+t^2}\right)^2,
\qquad
D_p=\mathbb P^1(\mathbb F_p)\setminus\{0,\infty,t:t^4=1\},
\]
which is exactly four-to-one from `D_p` to `V_p`. Let `K` be the deck Klein four group. Fix a lift `t in D_p` of `x`, and for each `g in S_3` choose one octahedral lift `gamma_g` with
\[
x(\gamma_g s)=g(x(s)).
\]
Then
\[
\epsilon_g(t,s)=\chi\bigl(x(t)-x(\gamma_gs)\bigr).
\]
The exact factorization already established on P04 is
\[
x(t)-x(h)
=
\frac{-4(h-t)(h+t)(ht-1)(ht+1)}{(1+h^2)^2(1+t^2)^2}.
\]
Therefore, as a rational function of `s`, the squareclass defining `epsilon_g(t,s)` has exactly four simple zeros, namely
\[
\gamma_g^{-1}Kt,
\]
and all poles have even order.

Because `p == 5 (mod 24)` is the clean family, the `S_3` action on `V_p` is free. Consequently the six four-point sets
\[
\gamma_g^{-1}Kt,\qquad g\in S_3,
\]
are pairwise disjoint. Hence for every nonempty subset `A subset S_3`, the product
\[
F_A(s)=\prod_{g\in A}\epsilon_g(t,s)
\]
is the quadratic character of a rational function whose squareclass has exactly `4|A|` distinct simple zeros. In particular it is not a square in `F_p(s)`.

The standard Weil bound for a non-square quadratic character sum on `P^1` therefore gives the safe coarse estimate
\[
\left|\sum_{s\in\mathbb P^1(\mathbb F_p)}F_A(s)\right|
\le (4|A|-1)\sqrt p+1.
\]
To make every channel sign equal to `+/-1`, remove the six points outside `D_p` and the 24 collision lifts
\[
O_t=\bigcup_{g\in S_3}\gamma_g^{-1}Kt.
\]
Thus at most 30 points are removed, so
\[
\left|\sum_{s\in D_p\setminus O_t}F_A(s)\right|
\le (4|A|-1)\sqrt p+31.
\]

On `D_p\setminus O_t`, the mask indicator is exactly
\[
1_{\{\epsilon=\sigma\}}
=
2^{-6}\prod_{g\in S_3}(1+\sigma_g\epsilon_g).
\]
Every eligible vertex `y in V_p\setminus S_3x` has exactly four lifts, and
\[
|D_p\setminus O_t|=p-29.
\]
Therefore
\[
4M_\sigma(x)
=
\frac1{64}
\sum_{s\in D_p\setminus O_t}
\prod_{g\in S_3}(1+\sigma_g\epsilon_g(t,s)).
\]
Expanding over subsets gives main term `(p-29)/64` at lift level. For the 63 nonempty subsets the square-root coefficients sum to
\[
\sum_{k=1}^6{6\choose k}(4k-1)
=4\cdot6\cdot 32-63
=705.
\]
The additive deletion / endpoint contribution is at most `63*31=1953`. Dividing by four gives
\[
\left|M_\sigma(x)-\frac{p-29}{256}\right|
\le \frac{705\sqrt p+1953}{256},
\]
as claimed. Since `|V_p|-6=(p-29)/4`, the normalized mask law is `1/64+O(p^{-1/2})`, uniformly in `x` and `sigma`. QED.

### Why this matters

This turns the earlier Bernoulli six-bit model from a heuristic into the correct **rowwise asymptotic law** on the clean family. Combined with the previously proved mask-only local-rule rigidity, it shows that a fixed bounded rule depending only on the six local channel bits cannot hope to beat the obstruction by exploiting a systematic nonuniformity of actual Paley masks: that nonuniformity is only `O(p^{-1/2})` per row.

This does **not** prove Conjecture 27. It instead tells us what a successful derandomization must contain that a mask lookup table does not: dependence on orbit labels, global arithmetic phase, a growing-complexity statistic, or another mechanism that correlates across rows before absolute values / local averaging destroy the useful cancellation.

## 5. B28 REPLACE FOR ALL WITH FOR TYPICAL — any source counterexample dual state must be spectrally concentrated

The previous theta-plus defect theorem narrows a violating state by negative complement-edge mass and conference escape energy. There is also a simple but useful independent concentration obstruction: a source-scale dual optimizer cannot be diffuse/high-rank.

### Claim 40

\[
\boxed{\texttt{P04-I0101-THETAPLUS-PURITY-OBSTRUCTION-40 = PROVED / STRUCTURAL-PARTIAL}.}
\]

Let `X` be any theta-plus dual feasible state for
\[
K_p=\overline{G_{p,2}}:
\qquad
X\succeq0,\quad \operatorname{tr}X=1,\quad X_{ij}\le0\ \ (ij\in E(K_p)).
\]
Write
\[
T=\langle J,X\rangle,
\qquad
\Delta=-2\langle A_{K_p},X\rangle\ge0,
\qquad
n=\frac{p-5}{4}.
\]
Then
\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{tr}(X^2)
\ge
\frac{2[2(T-1)+\Delta]^2}
{n\,(p-15+2\sqrt p)}.
}
\]
In particular, every theta-plus feasible state with
\[
T>\frac23\sqrt p
\]
satisfies
\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{tr}(X^2)
>
\frac{8(\frac23\sqrt p-1)^2}
{n\,(p-15+2\sqrt p)}.
}
\]
Consequently for any asymptotic sequence of such violating states,
\[
\boxed{
\liminf_{p\to\infty}p\,\operatorname{tr}(X^2)\ge\frac{128}{9}.
}
\]
Equivalently, the effective rank
\[
r_{\rm eff}(X)=\frac1{\operatorname{tr}(X^2)}
\]
must obey
\[
\boxed{
r_{\rm eff}(X)\le\left(\frac9{128}+o(1)\right)p
=\left(\frac9{32}+o(1)\right)n.}
\]
The same necessary condition applies, a fortiori, to an ordinary-theta source counterexample dual optimizer (where `Delta=0`).

### Proof

Let `G=G_{p,2}` and let `m=|E(G)|`. Define the unordered off-diagonal sums
\[
s_G=\sum_{\{i,j\}\in E(G)}X_{ij},
\qquad
s_K=\sum_{\{i,j\}\in E(K_p)}X_{ij}.
\]
Since `tr X=1`,
\[
T=1+2(s_G+s_K).
\]
Also
\[
\langle A_{K_p},X\rangle=2s_K,
\qquad
\Delta=-4s_K.
\]
Therefore
\[
\boxed{
s_G=\frac{T-1}{2}+\frac\Delta4
=\frac{2(T-1)+\Delta}{4}.}
\]
By Cauchy-Schwarz on the `m` graph edges,
\[
s_G^2\le m\sum_{\{i,j\}\in E(G)}X_{ij}^2.
\]
Meanwhile
\[
\operatorname{tr}(X^2)
=\sum_iX_{ii}^2+2\sum_{i<j}X_{ij}^2
\ge2\sum_{\{i,j\}\in E(G)}X_{ij}^2.
\]
Thus
\[
\operatorname{tr}(X^2)
\ge\frac{2s_G^2}{m}
=\frac{[2(T-1)+\Delta]^2}{8m}.
\]

Use the earlier exact localized degree formula
\[
d_x=\frac{p-15+S_x}{8},
\qquad |S_x|\le2\sqrt p.
\]
Hence
\[
m\le\frac n2\cdot\frac{p-15+2\sqrt p}{8}
=\frac{n(p-15+2\sqrt p)}{16}.
\]
Substitution yields
\[
\operatorname{tr}(X^2)
\ge
\frac{2[2(T-1)+\Delta]^2}
{n(p-15+2\sqrt p)}.
\]
For `T>2sqrt(p)/3` and `Delta>=0`, this gives the displayed source-scale lower bound. Since `n=(p-5)/4`, multiplying by `p` and taking `p -> infinity` yields
\[
\liminf p\,\operatorname{tr}(X^2)
\ge
\frac{128}{9}.
\]
Taking reciprocals gives the effective-rank bound. QED.

### Interpretation

This is the B28 payoff: a `for typical state` statement is now rigorous. Diffuse/high-effective-rank dual states cannot violate the source edge. Any actual asymptotic obstruction has to concentrate on at most about `9/32` of the ambient effective dimension (in the purity sense), while also satisfying the earlier conference escape / sign-defect restrictions.

This does not prove a density-one prime result. It is a state-space reduction, not a quantifier change on the source theorem. The honest status remains PARTIAL.

## 6. What changed in the research picture

The two structural results fit together in a useful way.

1. **Rows are locally generic.** On the clean family, every six-bit local channel pattern occurs with asymptotic frequency `1/64`, uniformly in the row. There is no persistent local mask bias to exploit.

3. Therefore the missing mechanism is not a local lookup table and not a single global centering mode. It must create **long-range arithmetic coherence across many locally generic rows** while concentrating a dual state in a structured low-dimensional representation / orbit sector.

That is a sharper target than the previous generic phrase “need sign-sensitive cancellation.” The object to hunt is now a globally coherent arithmetic phase or finite family of phases whose local six-bit marginals look uniform but whose representation-theoretic correlations are highly nonuniform.
