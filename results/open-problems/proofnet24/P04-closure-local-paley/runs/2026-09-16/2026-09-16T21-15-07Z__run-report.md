## 4. Move #5 — add a parameter and differentiate: spectral soft-max continuation

The previous `p=257` certificate restricted all nonedge weights to a very low-dimensional polynomial of the vertex degrees. That is convenient but has no reason to contain the true best edge-fixed completion.

Let `O` range over the `S_3`-orbits of unordered nonedge pairs and let `E_O` be the symmetric `0/1` matrix supported on orbit `O`. Keep the diagonal and edge entries of `B` fixed at `1` and write

\[
B(w)=B_0+\sum_O w_O E_O.
\tag{4.1}
\]

The nonsmooth convex objective is `lambda_max(B(w))`. I introduced the standard matrix soft maximum

\[
F_\tau(w)
=
\tau\log\operatorname{Tr}\exp(B(w)/\tau),
\qquad \tau>0.
\tag{4.2}
\]

### Proposition 4.1 — exact smoothing inequalities and gradient

For symmetric `B` of order `n`,

\[
\lambda_{\max}(B)
\le
\tau\log\operatorname{Tr}e^{B/\tau}
\le
\lambda_{\max}(B)+\tau\log n.
\tag{4.3}
\]

Moreover, with

\[
\rho_\tau
=
\frac{e^{B/\tau}}{\operatorname{Tr}e^{B/\tau}},
\tag{4.4}
\]

the derivative in an orbit direction is

\[
\boxed{
\frac{\partial F_\tau}{\partial w_O}
=
\operatorname{Tr}(\rho_\tau E_O)
=
2\sum_{\{i,j\}\in O}(\rho_\tau)_{ij}.
}
\tag{4.5}
\]

#### Proof

If `lambda_1,...,lambda_n` are the eigenvalues of `B`, then

\[
e^{\lambda_{\max}/\tau}
\le
\sum_i e^{\lambda_i/\tau}
\le
n e^{\lambda_{\max}/\tau},
\]

and taking `tau log` gives (4.3).

For the derivative, the Frechet derivative of the matrix exponential under a trace gives

\[
\frac{d}{ds}\Big|_{s=0}
\operatorname{Tr}e^{(B+sE_O)/\tau}
=
\frac1\tau\operatorname{Tr}(e^{B/\tau}E_O).
\]

Differentiating the logarithm proves the first equality in (4.5). Since `E_O` has entries `1` at both `(i,j)` and `(j,i)` for every unordered pair in `O`, the second equality follows. QED.

This is standard convex spectral smoothing and is not claimed novel. Its value here was methodological: it permits a continuation from a coarse dual to the full orbit-completion space and exposes which orbit weights matter before any exact checker is written.
