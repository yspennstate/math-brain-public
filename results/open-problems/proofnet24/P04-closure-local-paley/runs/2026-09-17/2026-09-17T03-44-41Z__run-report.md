## 4.2 Edge-fixed theta lemma

For any graph `H`, suppose `B=B^T` satisfies

`B_ii=1`, and `B_ij=1` for every edge `{i,j}` of `H`.

In the standard primal definition

`theta(complement H) = max <J,X>`

subject to `X >= 0` (PSD), `Tr X=1`, and `X_ij=0` on distinct nonedges of `H`, every feasible `X` obeys

`<J,X> = <B,X>`

because `J-B` is supported only on those distinct nonedges where `X` vanishes. Since `X` is PSD with trace one,

`<B,X> <= lambda_max(B) Tr X = lambda_max(B)`.

Therefore

`theta(complement H) <= lambda_max(B)`.

This is the only theta-duality fact needed for the finite certificate.
