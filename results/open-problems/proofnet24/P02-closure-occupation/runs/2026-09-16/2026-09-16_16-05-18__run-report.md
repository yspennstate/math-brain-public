# 6. Exact bridge theorem

We now prove `P02-OCC-C2-SCHUR-PORTMANTEAU-PRIOR-ART-BRIDGE` completely.

Let `A` be nonempty and choose `a in A`. Put `S=A\{a}` and order `A=(a,S)`.

For states `i,j != a`, define the one-target killed Green function

\[
F^a_{ij}
=E_i\sum_{t<T_a}{\bf1}_{\{X_t=j\}}.
\]

Aldous–Fill Lemma 2.9 followed by Lemma 2.12 gives

\[
\begin{aligned}
F^a_{ij}
&=\pi_j(E_iT_a+E_aT_j-E_iT_j)\\
&=Z_{ij}-Z_{aj}+\frac{\pi_j}{\pi_a}(Z_{aa}-Z_{ia}).
\end{aligned}
\tag{6.1}
\]

This formula is unchanged if `Z` is replaced by `Z+Pi`, because both displayed differences annihilate the rank-one stationary term.

## 6.1 Denominator identity

Start from

\[
M_A=
\begin{pmatrix}
Z_A^T&-\pi_A\\
{\bf1}^T&0
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

For every `s in S`, perform the determinant-preserving column operation

\[
C_s\leftarrow C_s-C_a.
\]

The final row now has entry `1` in column `a` and `0` in every other column. Expanding along this row contributes the cofactor sign

\[
(-1)^{|S|+3}.
\]

The remaining matrix `H` has rows indexed by `b in A`, columns indexed by `s in S` plus the final time column, and entries

\[
H_{b,s}=Z_{sb}-Z_{ab},
\qquad
H_{b,*}=-\pi_b.
\]

For every `b in S`, make the row operation

\[
R_b\leftarrow R_b-\frac{\pi_b}{\pi_a}R_a.
\]

Then the final column vanishes on every row in `S`, and for `b,s in S`,

\[
\begin{aligned}
H'_{b,s}
&=Z_{sb}-Z_{ab}
-\frac{\pi_b}{\pi_a}(Z_{sa}-Z_{aa})\\
&=F^a_{sb}.
\end{aligned}
\]

Thus the `S x S` block is `(F^a_{SS})^T`. The only remaining entry in the final column is `-pi_a` in row `a`. Expanding there gives another factor

\[
(-1)^{|S|+3}\pi_a.
\]

The two signs cancel. Therefore

\[
\boxed{\Delta_A=\pi_a\det F^a_{SS}.}
\tag{6.2}
\]

The principal block `F^a_{SS}` is nonsingular: `F^a` is the inverse of the transient nonsingular M-matrix `I-P_{V\setminus\{a\}}`, and its relevant principal block is again an inverse-M-matrix principal block. Equivalently, nonsingularity is exactly the inverse appearing in Golnari–Zhang–Boley Theorem 3. Hence `Delta_A != 0`.

## 6.2 Numerator identity

Set

\[
K_{A;i,j}
=
\begin{pmatrix}
M_A&b_i\\
r_j^T&Z_{ij}
\end{pmatrix},
\]

where

\[
b_i=(Z_{iA}^T,1)^T,
\qquad
r_j^T=(Z_{Aj}^T,-\pi_j).
\]

Perform the same operations `C_s <- C_s-C_a` for `s in S`, and additionally

\[
C_{\rm extra}\leftarrow C_{\rm extra}-C_a.
\]

Now the normalization row has a single nonzero entry, equal to `1`, in column `a`. Expanding along it again contributes `(-1)^{|S|+3}`.

On the resulting matrix, perform

\[
R_b\leftarrow R_b-\frac{\pi_b}{\pi_a}R_a
\quad(b\in S),
\]

and on the appended bottom row perform

\[
R_j\leftarrow R_j-\frac{\pi_j}{\pi_a}R_a.
\]

The time column then vanishes except at row `a`, where it equals `-pi_a`. The remaining minor, after deleting row `a` and the time column, is

\[
\begin{pmatrix}
(F^a_{SS})^T&(F^a_{iS})^T\\
(F^a_{Sj})^T&F^a_{ij}
\end{pmatrix},
\]

which is the transpose of

\[
\begin{pmatrix}
F^a_{SS}&F^a_{Sj}\\
F^a_{iS}&F^a_{ij}
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Expansion in the time column contributes the same second sign `(-1)^{|S|+3}` and the factor `pi_a`; again the signs cancel. Therefore

\[
\boxed{
\det K_{A;i,j}
=
\pi_a\det
\begin{pmatrix}
F^a_{SS}&F^a_{Sj}\\
F^a_{iS}&F^a_{ij}
\end{pmatrix}.
}
\tag{6.3}
\]

Dividing (6.3) by (6.2) and applying the scalar/block determinant Schur formula gives

\[
\frac{\det K_{A;i,j}}{\Delta_A}
=F^a_{ij}-F^a_{iS}(F^a_{SS})^{-1}F^a_{Sj}.
\tag{6.4}
\]

The right side is precisely Golnari–Zhang–Boley Theorem 3 with `S_1={a}`, `S_2=S`; probabilistically it is the expected number of visits to `j` before hitting `A`.

This proves C2.
