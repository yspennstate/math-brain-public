Fix the integer scale

`Q=10^15`.

Suppose every child state has an integer `U_child` satisfying

`E(child) <= U_child/Q`.

Define

`U(S) := ceil( [Q + sum_j m_j(S) U_child]/m(S) )`.

`E(S) <= U(S)/Q`.
