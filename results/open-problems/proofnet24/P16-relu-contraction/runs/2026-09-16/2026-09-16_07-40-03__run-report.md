# 3. Quantifier and probability ledger before the new proof

## Expectation / mean-field

For deterministic `x` independent of a fresh Gaussian matrix,

\[
E\|F_W(x)\|^2=\frac\alpha2\|x\|^2.
\]

This is annealed and pointwise. It cannot be substituted after maximizing over a direction chosen from `W`.

## Fixed-horizon high probability

The source tied theorem controls a prescribed deterministic start, and its random-start / exceptional-set variants require independence from `W`.

## Uniform tied theorem already validated for `alpha<1/2`

Let

\[
M_+(W)=
\sup_{\substack{u\ge0\\\|u\|=1}}
\|\operatorname{ReLU}(Wu)\|.
\]

Inherited `P16-UNIF-LOW` gives

\[
M_+(W)\le\sqrt{2\alpha}+o_P(1).
\]

For fixed `alpha<1/2`, choose `rho` with `sqrt(2alpha)<rho<1`. With probability tending to one, `M_+(W)<=rho`. For an **arbitrary signed** initial vector `x_0`, the first state

\[
x_1=\operatorname{ReLU}(Wx_0)
\]

is nonnegative and satisfies `||x_1||<=||W|| ||x_0||`. Thereafter,

\[
\|x_t\|
\le \|W\|\rho^{t-1}\|x_0\|
\qquad(t\ge1).
\]

Thus, with

\[
C(W)=\max\{1,\|W\|/\rho\},
\]

\[
\boxed{
\|F_W^t(x_0)\|
\le C(W)\rho^t\|x_0\|
\quad\text{for every }x_0,t.
}
\tag{3.1}
\]

This is the correct every-input/all-time statement; it does **not** assert one-step contraction for arbitrary signed inputs.

## Almost-sure across widths

Summable width-indexed failure probabilities can be combined with Borel--Cantelli under a chosen coupling. That is distinct from infinite-time contraction for one fixed matrix.

## Dead neurons

If `W` is invertible, `x=-W^{-1}1` is killed in one step. Hence no uniform lower radial law is possible. Dead directions help contraction; the hard quantifier is a matrix-dependent noncontracting direction.

# 5. `P16-COMPRESSIBLE-PSEUDORAY-EXCLUSION-43` — PROVED

For `x>=0`, `||x||=1`, `q>=1`, put

\[
e=\|\operatorname{ReLU}(Wx)-qx\|,
\qquad L=\|W\|.
\]

Let `B={i:(Wx)_i<0}`. On `B`, the residual equals `-q x_i`, so `||x_B||<=e/q`. Also

\[
x^T\operatorname{ReLU}(Wx)\ge q-e.
\]

The negative-preactivation loss satisfies

\[
0\le
\sum_{i\in B}x_i[-(Wx)_i]
\le L e/q.
\]

Since `x^T W x=x^T Sx`,

\[
\boxed{
q-x^TSx
\le e\left(1+\frac Lq\right).
}
\tag{5.1}
\]

Now fix

\[
\beta=\frac1{250},
\qquad
\tau=\frac1{800\sqrt\alpha},
\qquad
r=\frac1{40\sqrt\alpha},
\qquad
L_0=8\sqrt\alpha.
\tag{5.2}
\]

For fixed deterministic unit `y`,

\[
y^TSy=y^TWy\sim N(0,\alpha/N),
\]

so

\[
P\{y^TSy\ge1/2\}\le e^{-N/(8\alpha)}.
\]

A `tau`-net over all supports of size at most `beta N` gives

\[
P\{\exists\text{ sparse net point }y:y^TSy\ge1/2\}
\le
(N+1)e^{-c_{\rm sp}(\alpha)N},
\tag{5.3}
\]

where

\[
\boxed{
 c_{\rm sp}(\alpha)
 =\frac1{8\alpha}
 -H(1/250)
 -\frac1{250}\log(1+1600\sqrt\alpha).
}
\tag{5.4}
\]

On `[1/2,2)`, this is minimized at `alpha=2` and

\[
c_{\rm sp}(2)=0.005523069646127948\ldots>0.
\]

On the norm event `L<=L_0`, net interpolation costs

\[
2L_0\tau=1/50,
\]

so every unit vector supported on at most `beta N` coordinates has

\[
y^TSy\le13/25.
\]

Define

\[
\operatorname{Comp}(\beta,r)=
\{x\in S^{N-1}:\operatorname{dist}(x,
\{y\in S^{N-1}:|\operatorname{supp}y|\le\beta N\})\le r\}.
\]

For `x` in this compressible set,

\[
x^TSx
\le13/25+2L_0r
=23/25.
\]

Equation `(5.1)` therefore yields, for every nonnegative compressible unit `x` and `q>=1`,

\[
\boxed{
\|F_W(x)-qx\|
\ge
\varepsilon_{\rm comp}(\alpha)
:=\frac{2}{25(1+8\sqrt\alpha)}.
}
\tag{5.5}
\]

Consequently, except on an event of probability at most

\[
2e^{-(8-\log81)N}
+(N+1)e^{-c_{\rm sp}(\alpha)N},
\tag{5.6}
\]

every `epsilon_comp`-accurate expanding pseudoray is incompressible. This statement is uniform over vectors chosen after observing `W`.

# 6. `P16-INCOMPRESSIBLE-SPREAD-43` — PROVED

Let `u>=0`, `||u||=1`, and `u notin Comp(beta,r)`. For all sufficiently large `N`, there is `J subset [N]` with

\[
|J|\ge c_*N,
\qquad
c_*:=\frac{\beta r^2}{32}
=\frac1{12,800,000\,\alpha},
\tag{6.1}
\]

such that

\[
u_i\ge\frac r{2\sqrt N}\quad(i\in J),
\qquad
\|u_J\|^2\le1/8.
\tag{6.2}
\]

Proof: let `I` be the largest `floor(beta N)` coordinates. If `a=||u_I||`, the nearest unit vector supported on `I` has squared distance `2(1-a)`. Incompressibility gives `a<1-r^2/2`, hence

\[
\|u_{I^c}\|^2>r^2-r^4/4\ge3r^2/4.

Outside `I`, each squared coordinate is at most `2/(beta N)` for large `N`. Coordinates smaller than `r/(2sqrt N)` carry at most `r^2/4`, so the remaining set has mass at least `r^2/2` and cardinality at least `beta r^2 N/4`. Select a subset of cardinality between `beta r^2N/32` and `beta r^2N/16`; its squared mass is at most `r^2/8<=1/8`.

# 7. `P16-SKEW-FIXEDQUERY-SMALLBALL-43` — PROVED

Fix `S`, a nonnegative incompressible unit `u`, and `q>=1`. Under the independent skew law let

\[
Z=Au,
\qquad
\sigma^2=\frac\alpha{2N}.
\]

For every `L subseteq J`,

\[
\operatorname{Cov}(Z_L)=\sigma^2(I_L-u_Lu_L^T),
\]

hence by the determinant lemma and `(6.2)`,

\[
\det\operatorname{Cov}(Z_L)
=\sigma^{2|L|}(1-\|u_L\|^2)
\ge\frac78\sigma^{2|L|}.
\tag{7.1}
\]

Set

\[
\delta=\frac r8=\frac1{320\sqrt\alpha},
\qquad
\varepsilon_0=\frac{\delta\sqrt{c_*}}2.
\tag{7.2}
\]

If

\[
\|F_{S+A}(u)-qu\|\le\varepsilon_0,
\]

then at least half of the indices in `J` have scalar residual at most `delta/sqrt N`. On each such coordinate,

\[
qu_i\ge\frac r{2\sqrt N}>rac\delta{\sqrt N},
\]

so the ReLU is on its positive branch and

\[
(Au)_i\in
\left[qu_i-(Su)_i-\frac\delta{\sqrt N},
qu_i-(Su)_i+\frac\delta{\sqrt N}\right].
\tag{7.3}
\]

For a prescribed subset `L` of at least `|J|/2` coordinates, the Gaussian density bound from `(7.1)` gives

\[
P_A\{(7.3)\text{ for all }i\in L\}

# 12. Sharp obstruction and failed routes

The new theorem does **not** imply that the event “there exists an expanding pseudoray” is exponentially small, because a measurable continuous selector may have order-`N` or infinite exact `I(A;U|S,E)`. Prior P16 work already disproved a general exact-MI upper bound from row-replacement `L2` sensitivity.

A full ambient sphere net also fails quantitatively. ReLU is 1-Lipschitz, and for fixed `q`,

\[
\|F_W(v)-qv\|
\le
\|F_W(u)-qu\|
+(\|W\|+q)\|u-v\|.
\]

Preserving the very small residual `epsilon_*` by an ambient net costs order `N log(C/epsilon_*)`, which overwhelms the deliberately crude small-ball exponent. This is a **proof-route obstruction**, not a counterexample to contraction.
