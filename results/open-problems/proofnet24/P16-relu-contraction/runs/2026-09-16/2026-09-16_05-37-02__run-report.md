# 7. `P16-PERIOD2-COPOSITIVE-POLARIZATION-41` — full proof

Let W be any real square matrix. Let `u,v` be unit nonnegative vectors satisfying

`F_W(u)=g v`,

`F_W(v)=h u`,

with `g,h>0`.

Put

`ρ=<u,v>`,

`κ=sqrt(g h)`.

Because v is nonnegative and supported on the positive output coordinates of `W u`,

`v^T W u = v^T F_W(u) = g`.

Likewise

`u^T W v = h`.

Therefore, with `S=(W+W^T)/2`,

`u^T S v = (g+h)/2 >= sqrt(g h)=κ`.

This is the exact cancellation of the skew-symmetric part over the reversed two-step pair.

Now use polarization:

`4 u^T S v = (u+v)^T S (u+v) - (u-v)^T S (u-v)`.

Because `u+v` is nonnegative,

`(u+v)^T S (u+v) <= Λ_+(W) ||u+v||^2 = 2 Λ_+(W)(1+ρ)`.

By the minimum eigenvalue,

`(u-v)^T S (u-v) >= lambda_min(S) ||u-v||^2 = -2 B(W)(1-ρ)`.

Hence

`u^T S v <= [Λ_+(W)(1+ρ)+B(W)(1-ρ)]/2`.

Combining with the AM-GM lower bound proves

`sqrt(g h) <= [Λ_+(W)(1+ρ)+B(W)(1-ρ)]/2`.

The proof is deterministic, finite-N, and uniform over all W-dependent candidate cycles.

Why it is stronger than the inherited high-cosine Lipschitz certificate: period two reverses the bilinear pair exactly, so the skew part cancels before any norm bound is applied. The resulting mismatch cost is linear in `1-ρ`, not square-root in `1-ρ`.

# 10. `P16-BADORBIT-PROJECTIVE-MOTION-41` — PROVED

Let W be deterministic and consider any live normalized orbit

`u_(t+1) = F_W(u_t)/g_t`,

`g_t = ||F_W(u_t)|| > 0`,

with every `u_t >=0` and `||u_t||=1`.

Because `u_(t+1)` is the normalized positive output,

`g_t = u_(t+1)^T W u_t`.

Add and subtract u_t:

`g_t = u_t^T W u_t + (u_(t+1)-u_t)^T W u_t`.

The first term equals `u_t^T S u_t` and is at most `Λ_+(W)`. Let `L=||W||` and `d_t=||u_(t+1)-u_t||`. Then

`g_t <= Λ_+(W) + L d_t`.

For a block of length T,

`exp[(1/T) sum_(t<T) log g_t]`

`<= (1/T) sum_(t<T) g_t`

`<= Λ_+(W) + L (1/T) sum_(t<T) d_t`.

Therefore, if the block mean log gain is at least ell,

`mean_t d_t >= [exp(ell)-Λ_+(W)]_+ / L`.

If the upper radial Lyapunov exponent is nonnegative and `Λ_+(W)<1`, then along a subsequence

`limsup mean_t d_t >= [1-Λ_+(W)]/||W|| > 0`.

Thus below the Gaussian symmetric-cone threshold α=1, a bad orbit cannot generate nonnegative radial growth while asymptotically freezing in projective space. It must keep crossing projective geometry / activation structure.

This is not a contraction theorem. It is a deterministic recurrence obligation that can be combined later with activation-cell, mask-change, reset, or information costs.

`1 - [1-B_(N,n)(q)]^floor(m/2)`.

# 12. Stress tests and comparison of methods

## Dead neurons

A state that reaches zero stays zero. Dead neurons/states cannot form a nonzero exact projective cycle and are not an obstruction.

## Exceptional W-dependent directions

The original source explicitly does not cover a start chosen after seeing W. The new period-two inequality is deterministic after W is fixed and therefore applies simultaneously to every W-dependent candidate cycle. This is a genuine improvement in the adversarial-input quantifier for that obstruction class.

## Correlations across layers / iterations

No layer independence is used. The key identity is exactly

`v^T W u = g`, `u^T W v = h`.

The same tied W appears in both directions; that is why the skew part cancels after symmetrization. A fresh-layer replacement would destroy the proof.

## Random-matrix products

No product of overlapping tied blocks is approximated by independent Ginibre blocks. Shared entries remain shared. The proof bypasses the dependence via the symmetric bilinear form.

## Mean field

Ordinary starts have fresh radial gain about `sqrt(α/2)` and hence candidate threshold 2. The symmetric positive-cone quadratic optimum is instead about `sqrt(α)` and crosses one already at α=1. This discrepancy precisely shows that large positive-cone quadratic value is weaker than ReLU self-consistency.

## Deterministic Lipschitz

Uniform one-step contraction reaches only α<1/2. The new period-two theorem is not a one-step Lipschitz theorem; it gains power by exploiting exact recurrence and skew cancellation.

## Nonnormality

Exact periods one and two do not exhaust recurrent dynamics. Prior Jordan examples show stable systems may have large transient/pseudospectral growth. A bad tied ReLU orbit could in principle renew such transients without converging to a finite cycle.

## Finite width

The polarization inequality is exact at every N. Finite-width uncertainty enters only through Λ_+ and B. Fixed gaps from their limits have exponential Gaussian concentration. No Tracy-Widom-scale estimate or optimal finite-size edge law is used.

That is the correct next target, not another ambient spectral-norm estimate.
