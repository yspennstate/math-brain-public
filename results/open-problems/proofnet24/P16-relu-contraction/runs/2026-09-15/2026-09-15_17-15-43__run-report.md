# 5. Scalar gate-centered ReLU energy inequality

Let

\[
Z\sim N(0,1)
\]

and define

\[
\psi(a)
=
E(a+Z)_+^2.
\]

The standard Gaussian integral is

\[
\boxed{
\psi(a)
=
(1+a^2)\Phi(a)+a\phi(a),
}
\tag{5.1}
\]

where \(\phi,\Phi\) are the standard normal density and cdf.

Define

\[
h(a)=\Phi(a)-\frac12
\]

and

\[
R(a)
=
\psi(a)
-
\frac{1+a^2}{2}
-
2h(a).
\tag{5.2}
\]

Substituting `(5.1)`,

\[
\boxed{
R(a)
=
(a^2-1)h(a)+a\phi(a).
}
\tag{5.3}
\]

Since \(h\) is odd and \(\phi\) is even, \(R\) is odd.

Differentiate:

\[
\begin{aligned}
R'(a)
&=
2ah(a)
+
(a^2-1)\phi(a)
+
\phi(a)-a^2\phi(a)
\\
&=
\boxed{2ah(a)}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{5.4}
\]

For \(a\ge0\),

\[
0\le h(a)\le\frac12,
\]

hence

\[
0
\le
R(a)
=
\int_0^a2t h(t)\,dt
\le
\frac{a^2}{2}.
\]

Oddness gives the two-sided estimate

\[
\boxed{
|R(a)|\le\frac{a^2}{2}
\qquad
\forall a\in\mathbb R.
}
\tag{5.5}
\]

Therefore:

## `P16-SCALAR-GATE-ENERGY-MAJORANT-28` — NEW / PROVED

For every real \(a\),

\[
\boxed{
E(a+Z)_+^2
\le
a^2+2\Phi(a)-\frac12.
}
\tag{5.6}
\]

The bound is exact at \(a=0\).

It is also structurally sharp among bounds of this form:

- the derivative of \(\psi\) at \(0\) forces the coefficient of \(\Phi(a)-1/2\) to be at least \(2\);
- as \(a\to+\infty\), the remaining quadratic coefficient must be large enough to recover \(\psi(a)\sim a^2+1\).

The important point is not just the scalar constant: the actual **gate score**
\(\Phi(a)\) absorbs the dangerous linear response of the shifted ReLU energy, leaving only a quadratic recycled-mean debt.
