## 3. Conic recurrence notation

For a nonzero tied trajectory put
\[
r_j=\|x_j\|,\qquad u_j=x_j/r_j,\qquad g_j=r_{j+1}/r_j.
\]
For \(j\ge1\), \(u_j\in S^{N-1}\cap\mathbb R_+^N\).

Fix \(t\ge2\), support \(S\subset\{1,\ldots,t-1\}\), \(s=|S|\), coefficients \(a_j\ge0\), and assume
\[
\left\|x_t-\sum_{j\in S}a_jx_j\right\|\le\delta r_t,\qquad0\le\delta<1.
\tag{3.1}
\]
Set
\[
c_j=a_jr_j/r_t,\qquad C=\sum c_j,\qquad p_j=c_j/C.
\]
Define
\[
G^{(0)}_{ij}=\langle u_i,u_j\rangle,\qquad
G^{(1)}_{ij}=\langle u_{i+1},u_{j+1}\rangle,
\]
and \(D_g=\operatorname{diag}(g_j)\). Put
\[
Q_0=p^\top G^{(0)}p,\qquad
Q_1=p^\top G^{(1)}p,\qquad
Q_g=p^\top D_gG^{(1)}D_gp.

## 4. `P16-CONE-GRAM-TRANSFER-20` — PROVED

\[
\boxed{
g_t\le(1+\delta)\sqrt{\frac{Q_g}{Q_0}}+\|W\|\delta.
}
\tag{4.1}
\]

**Proof.** Normalize `(3.1)`:
\[
u_t=C\sum_{j\in S}p_ju_j+e,\qquad\|e\|\le\delta.
\]
The inherited positive-cone ReLU sublinearity gives
\[
F_W(u_t)\le C\sum_jp_jF_W(u_j)+|We|
\]
coordinatewise. Since \(F_W(u_j)=g_ju_{j+1}\),
\[
g_t\le C\sqrt{Q_g}+\|W\|\delta.
\]
Also
\[
|C\sqrt{Q_0}-1|\le\delta,
\]
hence \(C\sqrt{Q_0}\le1+\delta\). Substitute. QED.

For an exact positive recurrence \(\delta=0\),
\[
\boxed{
g_t\le
\sqrt{
\frac{p^\top D_gG^{(1)}D_gp}{p^\top G^{(0)}p}
}.
}
\tag{4.2}
\]
This is a generalized projective-Gram Rayleigh quotient. It is sharper than bounding cone mass and support multipliers separately.

## 5. `P16-CONE-MASS-SUPPORT-20` — PROVED

Because the supporting post-ReLU directions lie in the positive orthant,
\[
\langle u_i,u_j\rangle\ge0.
\]
Therefore
\[
Q_0\ge\sum_jp_j^2\ge1/s.
\]
Since \(|C\sqrt{Q_0}-1|\le\delta\) and \(Q_0\le1\),
\[
\boxed{1-\delta\le C\le(1+\delta)\sqrt{s}.}
\tag{5.1}
\]
Thus on a fixed block of length \(L\), the diffuse-cone mass is at most \((1+\delta)\sqrt L\), independent of width.

## 6. `P16-DIFFUSE-CONE-SYNC-20` — PROVED

Let
\[
G=\max_{j\in S}g_j.
\]
Because \(u_{j+1}\ge0\) and \(g_j\le G\),
\[
Q_g\le G^2Q_1.
\]
Hence
\[
g_t\le G(1+\delta)\sqrt{Q_1/Q_0}+\|W\|\delta.
\tag{6.1}
\]
Define
\[
R_t=\frac{(g_t-\|W\|\delta)_+}{G(1+\delta)}.
\]
Then \(Q_1/Q_0\ge R_t^2\). If \(R_t>1\),
\[
Q_1-Q_0\ge(R_t^2-1)Q_0\ge\frac{R_t^2-1}{s}.
\]
But
\[
Q_1-Q_0=
\sum_{i,j\in S}p_ip_j
[\langle u_{i+1},u_{j+1}\rangle-\langle u_i,u_j\rangle].
\]
Therefore some distinct \(i,j\in S\) satisfy
\[
\boxed{
\langle u_{i+1},u_{j+1}\rangle-\langle u_i,u_j\rangle
\ge\frac{R_t^2-1}{s}.
}
\tag{6.2}
\]
Equivalently,
\[
\boxed{
\|u_i-u_j\|^2-\|u_{i+1}-u_{j+1}\|^2
\ge\frac{2(R_t^2-1)}{s}.
}
\tag{6.3}
\]

A diffuse positive-cone recurrence can therefore create a new radial record only by causing **quantitative one-step projective synchronization** among at least one pair of its support directions.

If \(s\le L\) and
\[
g_t\ge(1+\varepsilon)(1+\delta)G+\|W\|\delta,
\]
then some pair gains correlation by at least
\[
\boxed{\frac{2\varepsilon+\varepsilon^2}{L}.}
\tag{6.4}
\]

So the “diffuse cone” is no longer an unstructured obstruction.

## 7. `P16-CONE-DUAL-SEPARATOR-20` — PROVED

Let
\[
C=\operatorname{cone}(u_1,\ldots,u_{t-1}),\qquad u=u_t,

let \(v=P_Cu\), and let
\[
r=u-v,\qquad\kappa=\|r\|.

\tag{7.1}

This follows from the projection variational inequality and the cone choices \(0,2v\), which imply \(r\perp v\).

Thus conic innovation has an exact dual separating functional.

## 8. `P16-CANCELLATION-EXPOSURE-20` — PROVED
