# 6. Scalar recycled-mean inequality

Put

\[

For

\[

\[
\boxed{

d^2+c_0s|d|.

### Proof

If \(d\ge0\), then using the lower bound in `(6.1)`,

\[
|\mu|-H(\nu,s)

|\mu-\nu|
