# 12. `P16-UNIFORM-GATE-BALANCE-24` — NEW / PROVED

Let

\[
\Gamma_1,\ldots,\Gamma_N\stackrel{\rm iid}{\sim}N(0,I_d),
\]

with \(d\) fixed, and for \(a\ne0\) define

\[
\widehat p_N(a)=\frac1N\sum_i\mathbf1_{\{a\cdot\Gamma_i>0\}}.

For general-position points, the number of strict homogeneous sign patterns is at most

\[
M_{N,d}=2\sum_{j=0}^{d-1}\binom{N-1}{j}.
\tag{12.1}
\]

Decompose each Gaussian point into an unoriented line and an independent Rademacher orientation:

\[
\Gamma_i=\epsilon_iV_i,
\qquad
\epsilon_i\stackrel{\rm iid}{\sim}\operatorname{Rad}(\pm1).
\]

Conditional on the lines, each fixed realizable base sign pattern multiplied by \(\epsilon\) is an iid Rademacher sign vector. Hoeffding plus union bound gives \(2M_{N,d}e^{-2\eta^2N}\).

Almost surely, an origin hyperplane contains at most \(d-1\) Gaussian sample points. Boundary coefficient vectors can therefore change the positive count by at most \(d-1\) relative to a nearby strict cell.

Hence, for every \(\eta>0\),

\[
\boxed{
P\left\{
\sup_{a\ne0}|\widehat p_N(a)-1/2|
>
\eta+\frac{d-1}{N}
\right\}
\le
4\left[\sum_{j=0}^{d-1}\binom{N-1}{j}\right]e^{-2\eta^2N}.
}
\tag{12.2}
\]

For fixed \(d\), this is exponentially small up to a polynomial prefactor.

The key point is **uniformity over all coefficient vectors \(a\)**. The vector may be chosen adaptively from the same Gaussian sample.

Primary sign-pattern source checked: T. M. Cover, `Geometrical and Statistical Properties of Systems of Linear Inequalities with Applications in Pattern Recognition`, IEEE Transactions on Electronic Computers EC-14 (1965), 326--334.
