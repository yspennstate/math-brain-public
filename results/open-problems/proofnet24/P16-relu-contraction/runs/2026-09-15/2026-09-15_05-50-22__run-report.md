# New theorem 1 — sharp shifted-ReLU energy envelope

## `P16-SHIFTED-RELU-ENERGY-17` — PROVED

Let \(Z\sim N(0,1)\) and
\[
h(b)=E[(b+Z)_+^2]=(1+b^2)\Phi(b)+b\phi(b).
\tag{E1}
\]
Then for every \(\delta>0\) and \(\mu\in\mathbb R^N\),
\[
\boxed{\frac1N\sum_{i=1}^NE[(\mu_i+\delta Z_i)_+^2]\le
\delta^2h\!\left(\frac{\|\mu\|}{\delta\sqrt N}\right).}
\tag{E2}
\]
This is sharp if only \(\|\mu\|^2/N\) is retained: equality holds for a flat nonnegative mean vector.

**Derivation.**  Set \(H_\delta(m)=E[(m+\delta Z)_+^2]=\delta^2h(m/\delta)\) and \(g_\delta(u)=H_\delta(\sqrt u)\).  Since \(h\) is increasing, \(H_\delta(m)\le H_\delta(|m|)\).  For \(u>0\), with \(b=\sqrt u/\delta\),
\[
g_\delta''(u)=-\frac{\phi(b)}{2\delta^2b^3}<0.
\]
Hence Jensen applied to \(\mu_i^2\) gives (E2).

The recycled tied-matrix mean is retained exactly; it is not replaced by a centered fresh layer.

# New theorem 3 — the energy-only innovation threshold

## `P16-HIGHINNOV-THRESHOLD-17` — PROVED

At \(\varepsilon=0\), put \(b=\sqrt{1-u^2}/u\).  Then
\[
\psi_0(u)=\Phi(b)+\frac{b\phi(b)}{1+b^2}.
\tag{T1}
\]
Moreover
\[
\frac d{db}\left[\Phi(b)+\frac{b\phi(b)}{1+b^2}\right]
=\frac{2\phi(b)}{(1+b^2)^2}>0.
\tag{T2}
\]
Thus \(\psi_0\) decreases strictly from \(1\) at \(u\downarrow0\) to \(1/2\) at \(u=1\).

For every \(1<\alpha<2\), there is a unique
\[
\boxed{\eta_\alpha\in(0,1):\quad \alpha\psi_0(\eta_\alpha)=1.}
\tag{T3}
\]
For \(\alpha\le1\), every fixed strictly positive innovation ratio has a strict mean-contraction margin (and for \(\alpha<1\), even the limiting \(u\downarrow0\) envelope is subcritical).

Near the edge of chaos,
\[
\boxed{1-\eta_\alpha=\frac\pi{64}(2-\alpha)^2+o((2-\alpha)^2),\qquad\alpha\uparrow2.}
\tag{T4}
\]
Indeed, with \(\Delta=2-\alpha\), \(1/\alpha=1/2+\Delta/4+O(\Delta^2)\), while
\[
\psi_0=\frac12+\sqrt{2/\pi}\,b+O(b^3),\qquad
u=(1+b^2)^{-1/2}=1-b^2/2+o(b^2).
\]
This yields (T4).

Numerical diagnostics only: \(\eta_{1.1}\approx0.81315\), \(\eta_{1.2}\approx0.89359\), \(\eta_{1.5}\approx0.97725\), \(\eta_{1.8}\approx0.99757\), \(\eta_{1.9}\approx0.99946\).

# New theorem 4 — high-probability contraction of one high-innovation tied query

## `P16-HIGHINNOV-STEP-17` — PROVED

Fix \(\alpha<2\).  If \(1<\alpha<2\), choose \(\eta>\eta_\alpha\); if \(\alpha\le1\), choose any fixed \(\eta>0\).  By continuity choose \(\varepsilon>0\) such that
\[
\overline\psi:=\sup_{u\in[\eta,1]}\psi_\varepsilon(u)<1/\alpha.
\]
Put \(q_0=\sqrt{\alpha\overline\psi}<1\), choose \(0<\tau<1-q_0\), and set \(q=q_0+\tau<1\).

On (E3), every nonzero predictable tied query with \(\iota_r\ge\eta\) satisfies
\[
\boxed{P\{\|F_W(v_r)\|\ge q\|v_r\|\mid\mathcal F_{r-1}\}
\le\exp[-N\tau^2/(2\alpha)].}
\tag{S1}
\]
The sharper exponent is \(\exp[-N\tau^2/(2\alpha\iota_r^2)]\).

**Proof.**  Conditionally on the past,
\[
Y(z)=\sqrt{\alpha/N}\,\|(\mu_r+\delta_rz)_+\|
=\sup_{a\ge0,\|a\|\le1}\sqrt{\alpha/N}\langle a,\mu_r+\delta_rz\rangle.
\]
This is the supremum of a finite-dimensional Gaussian process with pointwise variance at most \(\alpha\delta_r^2/N\).  Borell--TIS gives
\[
P\{Y\ge EY+t\mid\mathcal F_{r-1}\}
\le\exp[-Nt^2/(2\alpha\delta_r^2)].
\]
By (E5), \(EY\le\sqrt{EY^2}\le q_0\|v_r\|\).  Take \(t=\tau\|v_r\|\).

Primary-source verification: the Gaussian-supremum concentration is the Borell--TIS inequality; classical sources are Christer Borell, *The Brunn--Minkowski inequality in Gauss space*, Invent. Math. 30 (1975), 207--216, DOI `10.1007/BF01425510`, and B. S. Tsirel'son, *The density of the distribution of the maximum of a Gaussian process*, Teor. Veroyatnost. i Primenen. 20:4 (1975), 865--873; English translation Theory Probab. Appl. 20:4 (1976), 847--856, DOI `10.1137/1120092`.  The process here is finite-dimensional/separable and has the variance bound used above.

# New theorem 5 — finite high-innovation block contraction

## `P16-HIGHINNOV-BLOCK-17` — PROVED

Fix \(\alpha<2\), choose \(\eta,\varepsilon,\tau,q\) as above, and fix \(L\).  For a deterministic unit start, or a random unit start independent of \(W\), let
\[
H_L=\#\{0\le t<L:\iota_t\ge\eta\}.
\]
There are \(C,c>0\), depending on the fixed block parameters but not on \(N\), such that with probability at least \(1-Ce^{-cN}\), simultaneously through the block,
\[
\|x_L\|\le q^{H_L}M_\alpha^{L-H_L}\|x_0\|,
\qquad M_\alpha:=6\sqrt\alpha.
\tag{B1}
\]
Hence if \(H_L\ge\beta L\) and
\[
\boxed{q^\beta M_\alpha^{1-\beta}<1,}
\tag{B2}
\]
then
\[
\boxed{\|x_L\|\le\rho_{\alpha,\eta,\beta}^{L}\|x_0\|,
\qquad \rho_{\alpha,\eta,\beta}=q^\beta M_\alpha^{1-\beta}<1.}
\tag{B3}
\]
When \(M_\alpha>1\), it is sufficient that
\[
\beta>\frac{\log M_\alpha}{\log M_\alpha-\log q}.
\tag{B4}
\]

**Finite-width proof.**  For fixed \(L\), the iid innovation columns satisfy
\[
P\{\lambda_{\max}(N^{-1}Z_L^\top Z_L)>1+\varepsilon\}\le C_{L,\varepsilon}e^{-c_\varepsilon N}
\]
by a fixed net in \(S^{L-1}\) and chi-square Chernoff.  A separate \(1/4\)-net on both \(S^{N-1}\) gives
\[
P\{\|G\|>6\sqrt N\}\le2e^{-c_0N},
\]
because \(\|G\|\le2\max_{x,y\text{ nets}}|y^\top Gx|\), both nets have size at most \(9^N\), and \(P(|y^\top Gx|>3\sqrt N)\le2e^{-9N/2}\).  Thus every low-innovation step is bounded by \(M_\alpha\), while each high-innovation step satisfies (S1).  A conditional union bound over fixed \(L\) completes (B1).

This closes the prior named target `P16-HIGHINNOV-BLOCK-17` only in the **finite-horizon, deterministic/\(W\)-independent-start** sense.  It is not a uniform theorem over starts selected after seeing \(W\).

# New theorem 6 — exact low-innovation volume diagnostic

## `P16-LOWINNOV-GRAM-VOLUME-17` — PROVED

For nonzero queries let \(\widehat v_r=v_r/\|v_r\|\), let \(\widehat K_R\) be their correlation Gram matrix, and let \(\iota_r=\delta_r/\|v_r\|\).  On every linearly independent prefix,
\[
\boxed{\det\widehat K_R=\prod_{r=1}^R\iota_r^2.}
\tag{L1}
\]
Hence if at least \(M\) queries have \(\iota_r\le\eta\),
\[
\boxed{\det\widehat K_R\le\eta^{2M}.}
\tag{L2}
\]
This is exact Gram-volume collapse, but **not** yet a low-rank theorem: a tiny determinant can be absorbed by one tiny singular value while the rest remain macroscopic.  The next low-innovation theorem must control a singular-value profile, exterior powers, stable ranks, or a persistent approximate span.

# Stress tests and sharp obstruction

Dead directions have exponent \(-\infty\) and are harmless.  Exact query repetition gives \(\delta_r=0\) and no fresh Gaussian term.  Near repetition suppresses fresh variance by \(\iota_r^2\).  Even at high innovation, the conditional preactivation is **not centered** because the recycled mean remains.  The theorem cannot start from a \(W\)-dependent adversarial direction; that quantifier gap remains central.

The new quantitative obstruction is strongest near \(\alpha=2\):
\[
1-\eta_\alpha\sim\frac\pi{64}(2-\alpha)^2.
\]
Thus an energy-only smoothing argument needs the query to be almost entirely new near the conjectured edge.  Any proof reaching \(2\) must either exploit much finer sign/cancellation structure of the recycled mean or convert repeated low innovation into a tractable recurrent structure.

# Bottom line

The tied ReLU contraction conjecture remains unresolved, but the high-innovation half of the finite-depth dependence problem is now rigorous.  For a predictable tied query,
\[
Wv_r=\sqrt{\alpha/N}(\mu_r+\delta_rz_r),
\]
and on a typical finite innovation frame,
\[
\boxed{E[\|F_W(v_r)\|^2\mid\mathcal F_{r-1}]\le\alpha\psi_\varepsilon(\iota_r)\|v_r\|^2.}
\]
For \(1<\alpha<2\), the energy-only contraction threshold \(\eta_\alpha\) is defined by \(\alpha\psi_0(\eta_\alpha)=1\), and a tied query above any strictly larger innovation threshold contracts by a fixed factor with conditional failure \(e^{-cN}\).  Consequently a fixed block with enough high-innovation steps contracts exponentially.

But
\[
\boxed{1-\eta_\alpha\sim\frac\pi{64}(2-\alpha)^2}
\]
near \(2\).  The sharp remaining obstruction is therefore the **low-innovation recycled regime**: trajectories whose new directions repeatedly lie near the span of their own past and can reuse already exposed Gaussian information.  Turning that repeated innovation collapse into a controlled-dimensional recurrent structure is now the highest-value P16 theorem.
