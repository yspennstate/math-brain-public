\[

\qquad
T_A(v)=u.

\[
a=\|(Au)_+\|,
\qquad
b=\|(Av)_+\|.

Then

\[
(Au)_+=a v,
\qquad
(Av)_+=b u.
\]

With

\[
T=\operatorname{supp}u,
\qquad
S=\operatorname{supp}v,
\]

the active equations are

\[
A_{S,T}u_T=a v_S,
\]

\[
A_{T,S}v_S=b u_T.
\]

Composing,

\[
A_{T,S}A_{S,T}u_T
=
ab\,u_T.
\]

Thus

\[
\lambda_{\rm prod}:=ab
\]

is a positive real eigenvalue of the reused-block product.

Now let

\[
W=\sqrt\alpha A.
\]

By positive homogeneity,

\[
F_W(u)
=
\sqrt\alpha\,a v.
\]

Applying the same map again,

\[
F_W^2(u)
=
\sqrt\alpha a\,F_W(v)
=
\sqrt\alpha a
\left(
\sqrt\alpha b\,u
\right)
=
\alpha ab\,u.
\]

Therefore

\[
\|F_W^{2k}(u)\|
=
(\alpha ab)^k\|u\|,
\]

and

\[
\chi_2
=
\lim_{k\to\infty}
\frac1{2k}
\log
\frac{\|F_W^{2k}(u)\|}{\|u\|}
=
\frac12\log(\alpha ab).
\]

This proves the theorem.

# 7. Sign symmetrization with reused overlap

The key new finite-\(N\) observation is that overlapping supports still retain a large exact sign group.

For a sign vector

\[
d=(d_j)_{j\in U}\in\{\pm1\}^U,
\]

let \(D\) be the corresponding diagonal sign matrix on \(U\).

On the internal reused block, apply the conjugation

\[
A\mapsto DAD.
\]

Because the entries are independent centered Gaussians, the internal joint law is invariant.

The active equations transform as

\[
u_T\mapsto D_Tu_T,
\qquad
v_S\mapsto D_Sv_S.
\]

The interaction graph with vertex set \(U\) and edges between \(S\) and \(T\) is connected whenever both supports are nonempty.

Hence the only sign vectors acting trivially on every internal edge are the two global signs.

The effective sign group therefore has size

\[
\boxed{
2^{m-1}.
}
\]

For one generic algebraic candidate pair \((u_T,v_S)\):

- on an overlap coordinate \(i\in S\cap T\), both \(u_i\) and \(v_i\) are multiplied by the **same** sign \(d_i\);
- hence the relative overlap signs are invariant;
- if those relative signs are inconsistent, no group element can make both vectors positive;
- if they are consistent, exactly one effective sign class makes both vectors entrywise positive.

Therefore, averaging over the invariant sign group,

\[
\boxed{
E[
\#\{\text{internally positive candidates on }(S,T)\}
]
\le
N\,2^{1-m}.
}
\tag{7.1}
\]

The same inequality remains true after restricting to candidates satisfying

\[
\vartheta
=
\langle u_{S\cap T},v_{S\cap T}\rangle
\le
\vartheta_0,
\]

because \(\vartheta\) is invariant under simultaneous coordinate sign conjugation.

This is the overlap analogue of the exact positivity entropy used in the disjoint-support theorem.
