# 8. Proof of `P16-CYCLE2-DISJOINT-07`

We now prove the new theorem carefully.

Fix nonempty disjoint supports

\[
S,T\subset[N],
\qquad
S\cap T=\varnothing,
\]
with sizes
\[
s=|S|,
\qquad
t=|T|.
\]

Suppose

\[
u\in S_+^{N-1},
\qquad
v\in S_+^{N-1}
\]

form an exact projective two-cycle with

\[
\operatorname{supp}u=T,
\qquad
\operatorname{supp}v=S.
\]

There are gains

\[
a>0,\qquad b>0
\]

such that

\[
(Au)_+=a v,
\qquad
(Av)_+=b u.
\]

## 8.1 Internal equations

On the active rows,

\[
A_{S,T}u_T=a v_S,
\]
\[
A_{T,S}v_S=b u_T.
\]

Set

\[
G=A_{S,T},
\qquad
H=A_{T,S}.
\]

Since \(S\cap T=\varnothing\), these two blocks use disjoint matrix entries and are independent.

Composing gives

\[
HG\,u_T=(ab)u_T.
\]

Thus \(ab>0\) is a positive real eigenvalue of \(HG\).

Conversely, let \(\lambda>0\) be a nonzero positive real eigenvalue of \(HG\), with real eigenvector \(u_T\). Put

\[
a=\|Gu_T\|,
\qquad
v_S=\frac{Gu_T}{a}.
\]

Then

\[
Hv_S
=
\frac{\lambda}{a}u_T.
\]

If the projective pair \(u_T,v_S\) admits a simultaneous positive representative and the off-support inequalities hold, it produces an exact ReLU projective two-cycle.

For Gaussian \(G,H\), degeneracies such as repeated nonzero eigenvalues or zero coordinates of the relevant eigenvectors occur on algebraic null sets and do not affect the count.

Therefore the number of internally possible cycle lines is bounded by

\[
R^+_{s,t},
\]

the number of positive real nonzero eigenvalues of \(HG\).

## 8.2 Internal positivity cost

The joint law of \((G,H)\) is invariant under

\[
G\mapsto D_SGD_T,
\qquad
H\mapsto D_THD_S,
\]

where \(D_S,D_T\) are diagonal sign matrices.

A positive-real product eigenpair transforms as

\[
u_T\mapsto D_Tu_T,
\qquad
v_S\mapsto D_Sv_S.
\]

The simultaneous transformation

\[
(D_S,D_T)\mapsto(-D_S,-D_T)
\]

acts trivially on both matrices and changes only the simultaneous global sign of the eigenpair.

Thus the effective sign group has size

\[
2^{s+t-1}.
\]

For a generic eigenpair with no zero coordinates, exactly one effective sign class admits the representative

\[
u_T>0,\qquad v_S>0.
\]

Hence sign symmetrization gives the exact internal positivity fraction

\[
\boxed{
2^{1-s-t}.
}
\tag{8.1}
\]

## 8.3 Off-support sign cost

The first ReLU equation requires

\[
A_{S^c,T}u_T<0.
\]

The second requires

\[
A_{T^c,S}v_S<0.
\]

Because \(S\cap T=\varnothing\), every matrix entry in these two off-support blocks is disjoint from the entries of \(G\) and \(H\), and the two off-support blocks are mutually disjoint.

Conditional on \(G,H,u_T,v_S\), all scalar constraints are centered nondegenerate Gaussian signs.

There are

\[
N-s
\]

constraints in the first block and

\[
N-t
\]

in the second.

Therefore

\[
\boxed{
P\{
\text{all off-support signs hold}
\mid G,H,u_T,v_S
\}
=
2^{-(2N-s-t)}.
}
\tag{8.2}
\]

## 8.4 Exact support-pair expectation

Multiply (8.1) and (8.2):

\[
2^{1-s-t}\,
2^{-(2N-s-t)}
=
\boxed{
2^{1-2N}.
}
\]

This factor is independent of \(s,t\).

Linearity over positive real eigenvalues therefore gives

\[
\boxed{
E\,\mathcal C_N(S,T)
=
2^{1-2N}
E R^+_{s,t}.
}
\]

Summing over ordered disjoint support pairs proves `P16-CYCLE2-DISJOINT-07a`.

## 8.5 Exponential exclusion

Since

\[
R^+_{s,t}
\le
\min(s,t)
\le
N,
\]

and each coordinate has only three choices for a disjoint ordered pair:

- in \(S\);
- in \(T\);
- in neither,

the number of ordered disjoint support pairs is at most

\[
3^N.
\]

Hence

\[
E\,\mathcal C_N^{\rm disj}
\le
2^{1-2N}N3^N
=
2N(3/4)^N.
\]

Markov gives

\[
P\{\mathcal C_N^{\rm disj}>0\}
\le
2N(3/4)^N.
\]

No random-matrix asymptotic theorem is required.

# 9. Proof of the overlap-row orthant formula

Let an arbitrary candidate projective two-cycle have supports \(S,T\).

Put

\[
I=S\cap T,
\qquad
R=[N]\setminus(S\cup T).
\]

Fix positive unit vectors \(u,v\) supported on \(T,S\), respectively.

For one outside row \(r\in R\), define

\[
X_r=\sum_{j\in T}A_{rj}u_j,
\]
\[
Y_r=\sum_{j\in S}A_{rj}v_j.
\]

Because the entries in row \(r\) are independent \(N(0,1/N)\),

\[
\operatorname{Var}(X_r)
=
\frac1N\|u\|^2
=
\frac1N,
\]

\[
\operatorname{Var}(Y_r)
=
\frac1N\|v\|^2
=
\frac1N,
\]

and

\[
\operatorname{Cov}(X_r,Y_r)
=
\frac1N
\sum_{j\in I}u_jv_j.
\]

Thus the correlation coefficient is exactly

\[
\vartheta
=
\sum_{j\in I}u_jv_j
=
\langle u_I,v_I\rangle.
\]

The centered bivariate Gaussian orthant identity gives

\[
P\{X_r\le0,Y_r\le0\}
=
\frac14
+
\frac1{2\pi}\arcsin\vartheta.
\]

Distinct outside rows use independent Gaussian entries, so the probability that **all** outside-row off-support inequalities hold is

\[
\left[
\frac14+\frac1{2\pi}\arcsin\vartheta
\right]^{|R|}.
\]

This is exact.

It reveals the next obstruction:

\[
\boxed{
\text{support overlap matters through the actual cycle-vector overlap mass, not merely }|S\cap T|.
}
\]

Small combinatorial overlap does not automatically imply small \(\vartheta\): a cycle vector could, in principle, concentrate most of its mass on the intersection.

Any complete period-two Kac–Rice/counting theorem must therefore control eigenvector mass on the overlap as well as support entropy.
