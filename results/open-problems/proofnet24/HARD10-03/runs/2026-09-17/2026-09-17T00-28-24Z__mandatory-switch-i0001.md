## 5. Mathematical idea written before code

The most useful language is not pathwise geometry but the exact environment resolvent. The 2018 variational formula is

`R(lambda) = sup_psi { 2(phi,psi) - (psi,(lambda-S)psi) - (A psi,(lambda-S)^(-1) A psi) }`,

where `G=S+A` is split into symmetric and antisymmetric parts.

The old `rho=1/4` lower bound comes from a restricted first-chaos test function. The `4/3` prediction asks for the stronger singularity `R(lambda) ~ lambda^(-1/3)`. A natural RG/self-consistency heuristic is to interpret `R` through a running effective diffusivity `D_lambda`. Because one component of the drift is constant along an entire lattice line, the transverse low-frequency contribution has codimension one. If the renormalized denominator is of the schematic form `lambda + D_lambda k^2`, then the one-dimensional transverse shell contributes

`int_R dk/(lambda + D_lambda k^2)  ~  (D_lambda lambda)^(-1/2)`.

A one-loop fixed-point relation of the form

`D_lambda ~ 1 + c (D_lambda lambda)^(-1/2)`

has dominant balance

`D_lambda^(3/2) ~ lambda^(-1/2)`,

so

`D_lambda ~ lambda^(-1/3)`.

This reproduces exactly the missing resolvent exponent. **This is a heuristic, not a theorem.** The rigorous problem is to derive an inequality of this self-consistent shape from the exact LTV variational formula, without assuming spatial finite-range dependence that the Manhattan field does not possess.

## 6. B2 — ATTACK THE NEGATION FIRST

### Counterexample to a tempting deterministic pathwise proof template

A naïve route would try to show that every legal directed path in a Manhattan environment must accumulate enough repeated use of line orientations to force a superlinear occupation-square quantity. That deterministic statement is false.

For any `N`, consider the monotone staircase path

`z_(2j)=(j,j),  z_(2j+1)=(j+1,j),  z_(2j+2)=(j+1,j+1)`

for as many steps as desired. Orient every row used by a horizontal step to the east and every column used by a vertical step to the north. No requested orientation conflicts occur: each newly visited row is used for one east step, and each newly visited column is used for one north step. Hence this is an exactly legal directed Manhattan path of arbitrary length.

Yet every line identity is used only `O(1)` times (in this construction, once for the displayed staircase). Thus any purported universal deterministic inequality saying that a legal length-`N` path must have line-local-time square mass of order at least `N^(4/3)` is refuted by this family; its corresponding occupation-square mass is only `O(N)`.

This does **not** refute the source conjecture: the orientations supporting a specific long staircase are exponentially atypical under the annealed product law. It refutes only the proof template that tries to get `4/3` from legality alone with no probabilistic weight.

Outcome B2: useful obstruction; no source counterexample.

## 7. B6 — DEFORM FROM THE SOLVED/CONTROLLED RG REGIME

The source itself asks whether the Armstrong–Bou-Rabee–Kuusi RG can be adapted. The first task is therefore to quantify, rather than blur, the hypotheses that fail.

### Failure 1: raw spatial finite-range dependence fails maximally

In two dimensions write the horizontal line orientation at height `y` as `H_y in {+1,-1}` and the vertical line orientation at abscissa `x` as `V_x`. Then the first drift component at lattice site `(x_1,x_2)` is

`b_1(x_1,x_2)=H_(x_2)`.

Therefore under the product environment law

`E[b_1(0,0) b_1(x_1,x_2)] = 1_{x_2=0}`.

In particular, for every arbitrarily large integer `m`,

`E[b_1(0,0)b_1(m,0)] = 1`.

Thus disjoint spatial boxes separated by an arbitrarily large horizontal distance are still perfectly correlated whenever they see the same horizontal line identity. No finite spatial range can make the raw fields independent. The ABK probabilistic induction uses scale-wise finite-range/independent layers; it cannot be imported verbatim.

The same statement holds with coordinates interchanged for `b_2`.

### Failure 2: the exponent is outside the perturbative theorem regime

ABK's proved exponent is `2/(2-gamma)` for a positive Hurst exponent in a perturbative `gamma << 1` regime. Matching the Manhattan prediction

`2/(2-gamma)=4/3`

forces

`gamma=1/2`,

which is not a small perturbative Hurst parameter. Therefore even an isotropic self-similar identification at `gamma=1/2` would not lie inside the proved small-`gamma` theorem.

### Failure 3: covariance geometry is anisotropic/codimension-one

The Manhattan drift spectrum is concentrated in line-constant sectors: the horizontal component varies only in the transverse coordinate and the vertical component likewise. This is not the isotropic self-similar stream-field structure in the ABK theorem. Hypercube-symmetric scale induction therefore needs a new state space rather than a silent identification.

### What may survive

The deterministic coarse-graining part of the ABK program remains conceptually relevant: it suggests tracking an effective diffusivity and a scale-local defect, then proving that newly added randomness perturbs the coarse operator by a controlled amount. For Manhattan, however, the plausible independent objects are **new line identities or frequency shells**, not spatially separated boxes.

Outcome B6: direct transfer **fails** on explicitly identified hypotheses; the potential survivor is a generator-level coarse-graining argument rebuilt around line-identity/shell decoupling.
