## 2. New theorem P17-M4 — magnetic factor-map, trace, spectral-gap and boundary naturality

Let `(Ω,τ)` and `(Ω',τ')` be compact metrizable `Z^d` systems, and let

`p: Ω → Ω'`

be a continuous **surjective equivariant factor map**:

`p(τ_n ω)=τ'_n(p(ω))`.

Fix the **same** skew matrix `B` on both systems and the same scalar cocycle `σ_B`.

Write

`A_B(Ω') = C(Ω') ⋊_{τ',σ_B,r} Z^d`,
`A_B(Ω)  = C(Ω)  ⋊_{τ, σ_B,r} Z^d`.

### P17-M4(a): exact crossed-product embedding

The coefficient pullback

`p^*: C(Ω') → C(Ω)`,
`p^*f=f∘p`

together with the assignment of twisted implementing unitaries

`U'_n ↦ U_n`

extends to a unital injective *-homomorphism

`Φ_{p,B}: A_B(Ω') → A_B(Ω)`.

**Proof.** Equivariance gives

`U_n p^*(f) U_n^* = p^*(f∘τ'_n)`,

and the same cocycle on the two systems gives

`U_nU_m=σ_B(n,m)U_{n+m}`

with exactly the downstairs multiplication relation. Hence the covariant pair `(p^*,U)` defines a homomorphism of full twisted crossed products. Since `Z^d` is amenable, full and reduced twisted crossed products coincide.

Injectivity can also be checked directly with the canonical conditional expectations. Let

`E_Ω:A_B(Ω)→C(Ω)`,
`E_Ω':A_B(Ω')→C(Ω')`.

Then

`E_Ω ∘ Φ_{p,B} = p^* ∘ E_Ω'`.

Because `p` is surjective, `p^*` is injective. The reduced conditional expectations are faithful. If `Φ_{p,B}(a)=0`, then

`0=E_Ω(Φ(a* a))=p^*(E_Ω'(a*a))`;

hence `E_Ω'(a*a)=0`, and faithfulness gives `a=0`.

This is a genuine operator-algebra map, unlike P01's nonamenable quotient situation.

### P17-M4(b): exact magnetic trace and ordered-K0 naturality

Let `μ` be any `τ`-invariant probability measure on `Ω`, and set

`ν=p_*μ`.

Then `ν` is `τ'`-invariant. The canonical magnetic traces are

`T_{μ,B}= μ∘E_Ω`,
`T_{ν,B}= ν∘E_Ω'`.

They satisfy the exact identity

`T_{μ,B} ∘ Φ_{p,B} = T_{ν,B}`.

Indeed, for a finite Fourier sum `a=Σ_n f_n U'_n`,

`T_{μ,B}(Φ(a))
 = ∫_Ω f_0∘p dμ
 = ∫_{Ω'} f_0 dν
 = T_{ν,B}(a)`,

and density gives the general case.

Therefore on K0,

`T_{μ,B,*}( Φ_{p,B,*}(x) )
 = T_{ν,B,*}(x)`.

Because `Φ_{p,B}` is unital, its K0 map preserves the positive cone and order unit.

**Important caveat.** A C*-algebra monomorphism need not induce an injective map on K0. I do **not** claim `Φ_{p,B,*}` is injective without additional hypotheses.

### P17-M4(c): invariant measures downstairs always lift upstairs

Every `τ'`-invariant probability measure `ν` on `Ω'` has at least one `τ`-invariant probability lift `μ` on `Ω` with

`p_*μ=ν`.

**Proof.** The state `ν` on the unital C*-subalgebra `p^*C(Ω')⊂C(Ω)` extends to a state `μ_0` on `C(Ω)`. Take Følner boxes `F_N⊂Z^d` and average:

`μ_N = |F_N|^{-1} Σ_{n∈F_N} (τ_n)_* μ_0`.

Every `μ_N` still pushes forward to `ν`, because `ν` is invariant and `p` is equivariant. Any weak-* cluster point `μ` is `τ`-invariant and still satisfies `p_*μ=ν`.

Consequently, for every downstairs invariant measure and corresponding magnetic trace,

`T_{ν,B,*}(K0(A_B(Ω')))
 ⊂
 T_{μ,B,*}(K0(A_B(Ω)))`

for some invariant lift `μ`.

If `(Ω,τ)` is uniquely ergodic, every factor `(Ω',τ')` is uniquely ergodic: any invariant `ν` downstairs lifts to an invariant `μ` upstairs, uniqueness forces that lift to be the unique `μ`, hence `ν=p_*μ`.

Thus unique ergodicity gives a clean **magnetic K-theoretic gap-label monotonicity under factors**.

### P17-M4(d): exact spectral and Fermi-projection transport

Let

`h' = h'^* ∈ M_r(A_B(Ω'))`

and set

`h=Φ_{p,B}^{(r)}(h')`.

Because `Φ_{p,B}` is a unital *-monomorphism, spectral permanence gives

`spec(h)=spec(h')`.

Suppose `E_F` lies in a spectral gap of `h'`. The Fermi projection

`P' = 1_{(-∞,E_F)}(h')`

belongs to `M_r(A_B(Ω'))` by continuous functional calculus. Then

`P = 1_{(-∞,E_F)}(h)
   = Φ_{p,B}^{(r)}(P')`.

Hence

`[P]=Φ_{p,B,*}([P'])`

and, for `ν=p_*μ`,

`T_{μ,B}(P)=T_{ν,B}(P')`.

So a Hamiltonian imported from a factor has **exactly the same spectrum, K0 gap class, and IDS trace label** after pullback. No heuristic spectral similarity is used.

This statement distinguishes two notions that are often conflated:

1. the full K0 trace range gives **allowed K-theoretic labels**;
2. a concrete Hamiltonian realizes only labels attached to its actual open gaps.

P17-M4(d) preserves actual gaps only for Hamiltonians related by the exact factor embedding.

## 3. P17-M4(e) — magnetic half-space and PV boundary naturality

Let

`H=Z^{d-1}⊂Z^d`

be the subgroup tangent to the chosen boundary and let `e_d` be the normal generator.

Define the boundary/tangential crossed products

`A_B^H(Ω')
 = C(Ω') ⋊_{τ'|H,σ_B|H,r} H`,

`A_B^H(Ω)
 = C(Ω) ⋊_{τ |H,σ_B|H,r} H`.

The factor map induces the same type of injective homomorphism

`Φ_{p,B}^H:A_B^H(Ω')→A_B^H(Ω)`.

The last magnetic translation gives automorphisms `α_Ω'` and `α_Ω`. On coefficient functions,

`α_Ω(f)=f∘τ_{e_d}`.

For `h∈H`, if `U_h` is the twisted tangential unitary,

`α_Ω(U_h)
 = U_{e_d} U_h U_{-e_d}
 = exp(2π i e_d^T B h) U_h`.

The phase follows exactly from skew-symmetry:

`σ_B(e_d,h) σ_B(e_d+h,-e_d)
 = exp(2π i e_d^T B h)`.

The same formula holds downstairs, and equivariance of `p` gives

`Φ_{p,B}^H ∘ α_Ω'
 = α_Ω ∘ Φ_{p,B}^H`.

Therefore the two Toeplitz extensions form a morphism of short exact sequences:

`0 → A_B^H(Ω')⊗K → T_{α_Ω'} → A_B(Ω') → 0`

maps vertically to

`0 → A_B^H(Ω)⊗K → T_{α_Ω} → A_B(Ω) → 0`.

Naturality of the six-term exact sequence gives the exact boundary-map identity

`∂_Ω ∘ Φ_{p,B,*}
 =
 Φ_{p,B,*}^H ∘ ∂_Ω'`.

This is valid for **general compact hulls** and does not require `α_* = id`.

For `x∈K1(A_B(Ω'))`, and invariant measures `μ`, `ν=p_*μ`, the tangential trace pairing satisfies

`T^H_{μ,B,*}( ∂_Ω Φ_*x )
 =
 T^H_{ν,B,*}( ∂_Ω' x )`.

Thus inherited magnetic bulk classes have inherited boundary index/trace pairings under factor maps.

In toral/NCT models, the previous P17-M3B theorem further identifies `∂` with contraction by the cut-direction exterior generator. P17-M4(e) is strictly more general: it gives naturality even when no exterior-algebra formula is available.

## 4. Why this advances the old manuscript program

The recovered manuscript is fundamentally about constructing observable algebras from dynamical hulls and transporting bulk information to a boundary algebra. P17-M4 now supplies an exact functorial statement for a large class of physically natural changes of hull: **equivariant factors at fixed magnetic field**.

It simultaneously controls:

- the bulk twisted crossed product;
- the canonical invariant-measure trace;
- the K0 ordered pairing;
- the spectrum and Fermi projection of pulled-back Hamiltonians;
- the half-space Toeplitz extension;

This is the theorem-level replacement for merely saying that two patterned systems “look spectrally similar.”

## 5. What P17-M4 does not prove

The fixed-cocycle condition is essential. If one changes `B` to `B'`, the assignment `U_n'↦U_n` does not preserve multiplication unless the cocycles are equal (or are first related by an explicit cohomology/gauge equivalence). Thus P17-M4 is **not** a theorem comparing different magnetic fields. Magnetic-field deformation remains governed by P17-M1's cocycle-homotopy K-theory theorem and a separate continuous-field/index-pairing analysis.

Surjectivity of `p` is also essential for the monomorphism/spectral-permanence conclusion. If `p` is not surjective, `p^*:C(Ω')→C(Ω)` can have a nontrivial kernel.

Equivariance is essential for covariance.

Finally, algebraic injectivity does not automatically imply K0-injectivity, and the general half-space boundary map need not be exterior contraction.

## 6. Current literature route for the still-open general trace formula

The next level beyond P17-M4 is not abstract K-theory but an explicit **measured magnetic index formula** for general hulls.

Guo–Proietti–Wang's mapping-torus framework treats the twisted `Z^d` case by a magnetic connection and a measured twisted foliated index theorem. Their twisted topological pairing is expressed using the magnetic skew form/Pfaffian contributions and tangential characteristic classes. Benameur–Mathai's magnetic gap-labelling work gives the corresponding measured twisted index framework for Cantor-like systems.

This is the right source technology for the remaining theorem:

`T_{μ,B,*}: K0(A_B(Ω)) → R`

should be written geometrically under the cocycle-homotopy / mapping-torus identification, with all normalizations fixed in the user's convention `σ_B(n,m)=exp(π i n^T Bm)`.

I do not yet promote a closed formula for arbitrary `Ω` because the exact sign, `2π`, Pfaffian and tangential-Chern normalization must be specialized line by line.
