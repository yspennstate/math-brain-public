## 2. Exact magnetic target set this run

Let `Ω` be compact metrizable, `τ:Z^d↷Ω` continuous, and `B∈M_d(R)` skew-symmetric. Define the normalized constant magnetic multiplier

`σ_B(n,m) = exp(π i n^T B m)`, `n,m∈Z^d`.

It satisfies the cocycle identity, and

`σ_B(n,m) / σ_B(m,n) = exp(2π i n^T B m)`.

Define the magnetic bulk algebra

`A_B := C(Ω) ⋊_{τ,σ_B,r} Z^d`.

Since `Z^d` is amenable, the full and reduced twisted crossed products agree; I nevertheless keep the reduced notation when invoking groupoid/BC results.

## 3. Theorem P17-M1 — magnetic twist does not change abstract K-groups

**Theorem.** For every compact `Ω`, continuous `τ:Z^d↷Ω`, and real skew `B`,

`K_*( C(Ω) ⋊_{τ,σ_B} Z^d ) ≅ K_*( C(Ω) ⋊_τ Z^d )`.

**Proof.** For `t∈[0,1]`,

`σ_t(n,m)=exp(π i t n^T Bm)`

is a continuous homotopy of normalized 2-cocycles on the transformation groupoid `Z^d⋉Ω`, joining `σ_0=1` to `σ_1=σ_B`. Gillaspy's theorem on K-theory and homotopies of 2-cocycles on transformation groups says that if the acting group satisfies Baum–Connes with coefficients, evaluation at the endpoints induces an isomorphism on the K-theory of the reduced twisted groupoid C*-algebras. `Z^d` is amenable and hence satisfies Baum–Connes with coefficients. Therefore the endpoint K-groups are isomorphic. Amenability also identifies full and reduced twisted crossed products. ∎

Primary source checked: Elizabeth Gillaspy, **“K-theory and homotopies of 2-cocycles on transformation groups,”** Journal of Operator Theory 73 (2015), 465–490, DOI 10.7900/jot.2014feb14.2033. This explicitly proves the transformation-group statement under Baum–Connes with coefficients. Current related literature also treats twisted `Z^d` crossed products and magnetic gap labelling geometrically (Guo–Proietti–Wang, JFA 287 (2024), 110625).

**Interpretation.** A constant magnetic field of this form cannot change the **abstract** `K0/K1` groups of the manuscript's bulk algebra. This does **not** mean magnetic gap labels are unchanged, because the canonical trace pairing can vary with `B`.

## 4. Theorem P17-M2 — toral models become an explicit higher-dimensional noncommutative torus

Assume `Ω=T^q` and

`τ_n(x)=x+A n (mod Z^q)`

for a real `q×d` matrix `A`. Let `U_a(x)=exp(2π i x_a)`, `a=1,...,q`, and let `V_j` be the twisted implementing unitary for `e_j∈Z^d`. Fix the convention

`V_j f V_j^* = f∘τ_{e_j}`.

Then

`U_a U_b = U_b U_a`,

`V_j U_a = exp(2π i A_{aj}) U_a V_j`,

`V_j V_k = exp(2π i B_{jk}) V_k V_j`.

Thus `A_B` is exactly the `N=q+d` dimensional noncommutative torus `A_Θ` with

`Θ = [[0_q,-A],[A^T,B]]`

under the convention `W_r W_s=exp(2π i Θ_rs)W_s W_r`.

Consequently the standard Pimsner–Voiculescu/Elliott computation gives

`K0(A_B) ≅ Λ^{even} Z^N`,

`K1(A_B) ≅ Λ^{odd} Z^N`,

so for `N≥1` both are free abelian of rank `2^{N-1}`. This recovers the old manuscript's K-groups at `B=0` and proves that they remain unchanged for arbitrary constant magnetic `B`.

Rieffel's higher-dimensional noncommutative-torus work explicitly notes that the K-groups are the same as for the ordinary torus of the same dimension and may be obtained from the Pimsner–Voiculescu machinery.

## 5. Magnetic gap labels: K-group invariance does not mean trace invariance

For `A_Θ` the canonical trace `τ` has K0-range generated by Pfaffians of the even principal minors:

`τ_*(K0(A_Θ)) = Σ_{J even} Pf(Θ_J) Z`,

with `Pf(Θ_∅)=1`. This is exactly the Pfaffian gap-labelling formula already used in the user's manuscript and in Prodan's later “Topological gaps by twisting” framework.

### Concrete 2+2 case

Let `q=d=2` and

`A = [[a11,a12],[a21,a22]]`,

`B = [[0,b],[-b,0]]`.

Then

`Θ = [[0,0,-a11,-a12],
      [0,0,-a21,-a22],
      [a11,a21,0,b],
      [a12,a22,-b,0]]`.

The canonical gap-label group is therefore generated by

`1, a11, a12, a21, a22, b, det(A)`

(up to irrelevant signs of generators). The four-dimensional Pfaffian is

`Pf(Θ)= -det(A)`,

so the constant magnetic flux contributes a **new linear label `b`** while leaving the top Pfaffian magnitude `|det A|` unchanged in this block geometry.

This is a useful exact prediction: adding constant magnetic flux preserves `K0/K1` as abstract groups but generally enlarges/changes the trace image and hence the allowed IDS gap labels.

**DISPROVED AS AN OVERSTRONG CLAIM:** “magnetic twisting leaves the gap-labelling group unchanged because K-theory is homotopy invariant.” False. K-groups can be isomorphic while the trace pairing changes; the explicit `q=d=2` formula already exhibits the extra `b` generator.

## 6. Theorem P17-M3 — magnetic half-space extension and explicit boundary map for toral models

Write `Z^d=Z^{d-1}×Z`, with the last coordinate the physical direction normal to the cut. Restrict the action and cocycle to `H=Z^{d-1}` and set

`A'_B = C(Ω) ⋊_{τ|H,σ_B|H} H`.

The last magnetic translation induces an automorphism `α_B` of `A'_B`, including both the last spatial action and the cocycle commutation phases. The full magnetic bulk algebra is an iterated crossed product

`A_B ≅ A'_B ⋊_{α_B} Z`.

Hence the standard Toeplitz algebra gives the exact half-space sequence

`0 → A'_B ⊗ K → T_{α_B} → A_B → 0`,

and its six-term exact sequence is precisely the magnetic counterpart of the manuscript's bulk–boundary engine.

For the toral/noncommutative-torus case, reorder generators so the last generator `W_N` is the spatial boundary direction and write `A_Θ=A_{Θhat}⋊_α Z`. The automorphism satisfies

`α(W_j)=exp(2π i Θ_{Nj}) W_j`, `j<N`.

The path

`α_s(W_j)=exp(2π i s Θ_{Nj}) W_j`, `0≤s≤1`,

is a norm-continuous homotopy from `id` to `α`, hence `α_*=id` on K-theory. The PV six-term sequence therefore collapses to short exact sequences

`0 → K_i(A_{Θhat}) → K_i(A_Θ) →∂ K_{i-1}(A_{Θhat}) → 0`.

Under the standard inductive exterior-algebra identification

`K_*(A_Θ) ≅ Λ^* Z^N`,

this boundary map is, up to the conventional PV sign, contraction by the last basis vector `e_N`:

`∂(η)=0` if `η` contains no `e_N`,

`∂(ξ∧e_N)= ± ξ`.

**Proof.** Start at `N=1`, where the Toeplitz index sends the K1 generator `[z]` to `[1]∈K0(C)`; then induct on `N` through the PV sequence. Because `α` is homotopic to `id`, the new K-theory summand is exactly the suspended/crossed-product summand represented by wedging with `e_N`, and the connecting map is the inverse identification on that summand.

This gives the concrete magnetic bulk–boundary rule: a bulk K-class has a nontrivial boundary image exactly through the component carrying the cut-direction exterior generator. Constant magnetic phases change the algebra and trace pairing but do not alter this abstract boundary-map pattern.

For a general hull `Ω`, `α_{B,*}` need not be the identity, so one must retain the full PV sequence with `id−α_{B,*}`; the exterior-contraction formula is asserted only for the toral/NCT models where the above homotopy is explicit.

## 7. Continuity / spectral consequences

The cocycle path `t↦σ_{tB}` gives a deformation connecting the untwisted and magnetic algebras with K-theory-isomorphic endpoint evaluations. Therefore a norm-continuous family of bulk Hamiltonian sections `h_t` whose chosen Fermi gap remains open transports a single K0 gap class across `t`. However the trace of that transported class can vary according to the Pfaffian formula above. This cleanly separates:

1. topological class persistence under an open gap;
2. abstract K-group invariance under magnetic cocycle homotopy;
3. numerical IDS/gap-label variation with magnetic flux.

No claim is made that heuristic spectral similarity alone identifies K-classes.
