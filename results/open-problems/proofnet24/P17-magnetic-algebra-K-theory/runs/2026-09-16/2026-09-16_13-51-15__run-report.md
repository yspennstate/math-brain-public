# 4. Coordinate PV subcube

Let `A_Theta` be an `n`-generator noncommutative torus with coordinate generators globally ordered

\[
1<2<\cdots<n.
\]

Fix a set of cut coordinates

\[
P\subset\{1,\ldots,n\}.
\]

For `J subset P`, let `A_{Theta,J}` be the coordinate torus generated by the uncut coordinates `j notin J`, with the deformation matrix restricted to those coordinates.

In the retained exterior basis,

\[
K_0(A_{\Theta,J})
\cong
\bigoplus_{\substack{I\subset\{1,\ldots,n\}\setminus J\\|I|\text{ even}}}
\mathbb Z e_I,
\]

\[
K_1(A_{\Theta,J})
\cong
\bigoplus_{\substack{I\subset\{1,\ldots,n\}\setminus J\\|I|\text{ odd}}}
\mathbb Z e_I.
\]

The calibrated coordinate boundary in direction `j` is right contraction:

\[
R_j(e_I)=0\quad(j\notin I),
\]

and, for `j in I`,

\[
\boxed{
R_j(e_I)
=(-1)^{\#\{i\in I:i>j\}}e_{I\setminus\{j\}}.
}
\tag{4.1}
\]

This convention reproduces the already retained source formulas, e.g.

\[
R_1(e_{12})=-e_2,
\qquad
R_2(e_{12})=e_1,
\]

and

\[
R_4R_3(e_{34})=-1,
\qquad
R_4R_3(e_{1234})=-e_{12}.
\]

Right contractions anti-commute:

\[
R_iR_j+R_jR_i=0.
\tag{4.2}
\]

For fixed `q in Z/2`, define

\[
\boxed{
C_{P,q}^k
=
\bigoplus_{\substack{J\subset P\\|J|=k}}
K_{q-k}(A_{\Theta,J}).
}
\tag{4.3}
\]

The differential on the `J` component is

\[
\delta_J=
\sum_{j\in P\setminus J}R_j,
\tag{4.4}
\]

with each term inserted in the `J union {j}` component. Equation `(4.2)` gives `delta^2=0`; this is the toral coordinate form of M52.

The augmentation is the coordinate-subtorus inclusion

\[
\eta_P:K_q(A_{\Theta,P})\longrightarrow K_q(A_\Theta),
\tag{4.5}
\]

which sends every exterior monomial supported outside `P` to the identically labelled monomial in the full torus.

# 5. `P17-M53-TORAL-PV-SUBCUBE-EXACT-49` — proof

## 5.1 Basis states

A basis element in degree `k` is a pair

\[
(J,I),
\qquad
J\subset P,\quad |J|=k,\quad I\cap J=\varnothing,
\]

with parity

\[
|I|\equiv q-k\pmod2.
\tag{5.1}
\]

Define two subsets associated with the basis state:

\[
U:=I\setminus P,
\qquad
S:=J\cup(I\cap P).
\tag{5.2}
\]

`U` records the exterior indices outside the chosen cut set. `S` records each cut coordinate which has either already been cut or still appears in the exterior monomial.

A nonzero differential in direction `j` replaces

\[
(J,I)
\longmapsto
(J\cup\{j\},I\setminus\{j\}),
\qquad j\in(P\setminus J)\cap I.
\]

Therefore both `U` and `S` are preserved by `delta`.

Moreover,

\[
|I|+|J|
=|U|+|S|,
\]

so `(5.1)` is equivalent to

\[
\boxed{|U|+|S|\equiv q\pmod2,}
\tag{5.3}
\]

independently of the degree `k`.

Hence the entire PV cube decomposes integrally as a direct sum of finite subcomplexes indexed by admissible pairs `(U,S)` satisfying `(5.3)`.

## 5.2 The `S=empty` summands

If `S=empty`, then `J=empty` and `I=U` is disjoint from `P`. Thus this summand occurs only in degree zero, and it is exactly the image of the augmentation `(4.5)`.

Consequently the entire possible cohomology after augmentation comes from summands with

\[
S\ne\varnothing.
\]

## 5.3 Explicit integral contraction for `S!=empty`

Fix an admissible nonempty `S`, and choose

\[
s_0=\min S.
\]

For a basis state `(J,I)` in this summand, define `h(J,I)=0` if `s_0 notin J`.

If `s_0 in J`, set

\[
J'=J\setminus\{s_0\},
\qquad
I'=I\cup\{s_0\}.
\]

Let

\[
\epsilon(J,I)
=(-1)^{\#\{i\in I':i>s_0\}}.
\tag{5.4}
\]

This is exactly the coefficient of the distinguished `s_0`-edge

\[
(J',I')\xrightarrow{\delta}(J,I).
\]

Define

\[
\boxed{
h(J,I)=\epsilon(J,I)(J',I').
}
\tag{5.5}
\]

All coefficients are `0,+1,-1`.

### Case 1: `s_0 notin J`

Then `s_0 in I` because `s_0 in S`. The `s_0` term of `delta(J,I)` is the unique target on which `h` is nonzero in the distinguished direction. By `(5.4)` its coefficient is inverted by the same sign, so

\[
h\delta(J,I)=(J,I).
\]

Every other `j`-edge still has `s_0 notin J union {j}`, hence `h` vanishes on those targets. Since `h(J,I)=0`,

\[
(\delta h+h\delta)(J,I)=(J,I).
\]

### Case 2: `s_0 in J`

Now `delta h(J,I)` contains the distinguished `s_0`-edge, giving `(J,I)` with coefficient `+1`. It also contains cross terms obtained by first moving `s_0` backward with `h` and then contracting some `j!=s_0`.

The term `h delta(J,I)` has exactly the same targets, obtained in the opposite order: first contract `j`, then move `s_0` backward. Their coefficients are opposite because the signed coordinate contractions anti-commute. Equivalently, the two orders differ by one swap of the degree-one operations `R_j` and `R_{s_0}`.

Thus all cross terms cancel and

\[
(\delta h+h\delta)(J,I)=(J,I).
\]

We have therefore proved on every nonempty-`S` summand

\[
\boxed{\delta h+h\delta=1.}
\tag{5.6}
\]

This is an integral contraction, not just a rational acyclicity argument.

## 5.4 Exactness

The `S=empty` degree-zero summands are precisely the augmented coordinate-subtorus group `K_q(A_{Theta,P})`; every `S!=empty` summand is contractible by `(5.6)`.

Therefore

\[
H^0(C_{P,q}^\bullet)
\cong K_q(A_{\Theta,P}),
\qquad
H^k(C_{P,q}^\bullet)=0\quad(k>0),
\]

and the augmented sequence

\[
\boxed{
0\to K_q(A_{\Theta,P})
\xrightarrow{\eta_P}C_{P,q}^0
\xrightarrow{\delta}C_{P,q}^1
\to\cdots\to C_{P,q}^{|P|}\to0
}
\tag{5.7}
\]

is exact over `Z`.

This proves `P17-M53-TORAL-PV-SUBCUBE-EXACT-49` and `P17-M53-INTEGRAL-PV-LIFTING-HOMOTOPY-49`.
