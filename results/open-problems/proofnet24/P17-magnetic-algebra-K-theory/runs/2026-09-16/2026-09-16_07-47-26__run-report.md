# 5. Proof of `P17-M47-ANTILINEAR-PV-GRADED-NATURALITY-43`

## 5.1 Conjugate-linear action on the exterior K-basis

Let `e_I` denote the retained exterior K-theory basis element indexed by

\[
I=\{i_1<\cdots<i_m\}\subset\{1,2,3,4\}.
\]

A conjugate-linear symmetry with

\[
\mathfrak J(u_k)=u_k^{\varepsilon_k}
\]

can be factored as ordinary coordinate inversion/sign action followed by coefficient conjugation. In complex K-theory, coefficient conjugation acts on exterior degree `m` by

\[
(-1)^{m(m+1)/2}.
\]

The coordinate part contributes the product of its generator signs. Expressed directly in the `\varepsilon_k` used above, the resulting exact sign is

\[
\boxed{
\mathfrak J_*(e_I)
=s(I)e_I,
\qquad
s(I)=(-1)^{m(m+3)/2}\prod_{i\in I}\varepsilon_i.
}
\tag{5.1}
\]

Check of the first degrees:

\[
\begin{array}{c|c}
m&s(I)/(\prod\varepsilon_i)\\ \hline
0&+1\\
1&+1\\
2&-1\\
3&-1\\
4&+1
\end{array}
\tag{5.2}
\]

For `m=2`, `(5.1)` is exactly M46's Chern sign `-\varepsilon_i\varepsilon_j`.

## 5.2 PV boundary as right exterior contraction

In the retained plus-exponential convention, the cut normal to `u_j` acts on a homogeneous exterior generator by

\[
\partial_j(e_I)
=
(-1)^{\#\{i\in I:i>j\}}e_{I\setminus\{j\}}
\quad(j\in I),
\tag{5.3}
\]

and is zero when `j\notin I`.

Let `m=|I|` and suppose `j\in I`. The ratio of the bulk and boundary anti-linear signs is

\[
\frac{s(I)}{s(I\setminus\{j\})}
=
(-1)^{m+1}\varepsilon_j.
\tag{5.4}
\]

Indeed the exponent difference is

\[
\frac{m(m+3)-(m-1)(m+2)}2=m+1.
\]

Since `K_0` consists of even exterior degrees, `m` is even and `(5.4)` becomes

\[
\frac{s(I)}{s(I\setminus\{j\})}=-\varepsilon_j.
\]

The contraction sign `(5.3)` is the same on both sides. Therefore

\[
\boxed{
\partial_j\mathfrak J_*
=-\varepsilon_j\mathfrak J_*^\partial\partial_j
\quad\text{on }K_0.
}
\tag{5.5}
\]

This proves `(M47.1)`.

For completeness, the same calculation on odd exterior degree gives

\[
\partial_j\mathfrak J_*
=+\varepsilon_j\mathfrak J_*^\partial\partial_j
\quad\text{on }K_1,
\tag{5.6}
\]

so the sign is genuinely parity graded.
