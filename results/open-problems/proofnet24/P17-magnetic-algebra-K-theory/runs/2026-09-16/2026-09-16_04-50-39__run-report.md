# 5. Proof of the sharp residual-hull phase theorem

For one rational direction, use the exact identity inherited from M43:

\[
t(n;\alpha)
=\exp[-1-a\cos x],
\qquad
x=2\pi n\theta+\alpha/Q+\pi\theta,
\qquad
a=2r\sin(\pi\theta)>0.
\tag{5.1}
\]

Differentiate:

\[
|\partial_\alpha t|
=\frac{a}{Q}e^{-1}|\sin x|e^{-a\cos x}.
\tag{5.2}
\]

It remains to maximize

\[
g_a(x)=|\sin x|e^{-a\cos x}.
\]

Put `y=cos x`. Squaring does not change the maximizer, so maximize

\[
F_a(y)=(1-y^2)e^{-2ay},\qquad -1\le y\le1.
\]

Interior critical points satisfy

\[
0=\frac{d}{dy}\log F_a(y)
=-\frac{2y}{1-y^2}-2a,
\]

or

\[
a y^2-y-a=0.
\tag{5.3}
\]

The root

\[
\frac{1+\sqrt{1+4a^2}}{2a}>1
\]

lies outside the interval; the unique admissible root is

\[
y_*(a)=\frac{1-\sqrt{1+4a^2}}{2a}\in(-1,0).
\]

The endpoints give `F_a=0`, so this unique interior critical point is the global maximum. Hence

\[
\sup_x g_a(x)=m(a)=\sqrt{1-y_*(a)^2}e^{-ay_*(a)},
\]

which proves `(M44.1)`.

For a bilateral weighted shift `S_t`,

\[
\|S_t-S_{t'}\|\le\|t-t'\|_\infty.
\]

The self-adjoint nearest-neighbor direction is `S_t+S_t^*`, so its variation is at most twice the sup variation. This proves `(M44.3)`.

# 7. Proof of the singular-inertia box certificate

For every ordered singular value,

\[
|s_j(B(x))-s_j(B(g))|
\le\|B(x)-B(g)\|
\le\epsilon_g.
\tag{7.1}
\]

Hence a singular value of `B(x)` in `[a,b]` would force a singular value of `B(g)` into

\[
[a-\epsilon_g,b+\epsilon_g].
\tag{7.2}
\]

Now

\[
C_t(g)=B(g)^*B(g)-t^2I
\]

has eigenvalues `s_j(B(g))^2-t^2`. If `C_t(g)` is nonsingular and `t>0`, then

\[
\nu_-(C_t(g))
=\#\{j:s_j(B(g))<t\}.
\tag{7.3}
\]

Therefore, provided `a>epsilon_g`, equality of negative inertias at `t=a-epsilon_g` and `t=b+epsilon_g`, together with nonsingularity at both endpoints, is equivalent to the absence of singular values in the expanded interval. Combining `(7.1)`-`(7.3)` proves `(M44.10)` and the full phase-box statement.
