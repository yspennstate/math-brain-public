# 5. `P17-M52-CANONICAL-FACE-INCIDENCE-SIGN-48` — PROVED

## 5.1 Canonical normalization

For each subset

\[
J=\{j_1<\cdots<j_k\},
\]

choose the corner T-duality map

\[
\mu_J^{\uparrow}
\]

by taking the one-dimensional Connes–Thom/Paschke factors in the **increasing normal order** `j_1,...,j_k`.

This removes the ordered-normalization ambiguity left implicit when one writes a multi-cut theorem for an arbitrary ordered list.

## 5.2 One-step sign

Fix `j notin J` and first append `j` after the increasing list for `J`. In that append-order normalization, the retained M51 single-step theorem gives exactly

\[
(\mu_{J\cup\{j\}}^{(J,j)})^{-1}
\partial_{J,j}^+
\mu_J^\uparrow
=
\iota_{J,j}^*.
\tag{5.1}
\]

To pass from the append order

\[
(j_1,\ldots,j_k,j)
\]

to the increasing order on `J union {j}`, the new coordinate `j` must cross exactly

\[
N(J,j)=\#\{i\in J:i>j\}
\]

one-dimensional Thom/Bott factors.

M51 proved that every adjacent interchange of two degree-one factors contributes `-1`. Hence

\[
\mu_{J\cup\{j\}}^{(J,j)}
=
(-1)^{N(J,j)}\mu_{J\cup\{j\}}^\uparrow
\]

at the K-theory normalization level relevant to `(5.1)`. Therefore

\[
\boxed{
(\mu_{J\cup\{j\}}^\uparrow)^{-1}
\partial_{J,j}^+
\mu_J^\uparrow
=
(-1)^{N(J,j)}\iota_{J,j}^*.
}
\tag{5.2}
\]

This is the claimed incidence formula.

## 5.3 Square sign check

Let `i<j`, with both outside `J`. The two geometric routes to the common codimension-two face are equal as pullbacks:

\[
\iota_{J\cup\{i\},j}^*\iota_{J,i}^*
=
\iota_{J\cup\{j\},i}^*\iota_{J,j}^*.
\tag{5.3}
\]

The incidence signs satisfy

\[
(-1)^{N(J,i)+N(J\cup\{i\},j)}
=
-
(-1)^{N(J,j)+N(J\cup\{j\},i)},
\tag{5.4}
\]

because in the second route the already-added `j` contributes one extra element larger than `i`.

Combining `(5.2)`–`(5.4)` gives

\[
\boxed{
\partial_{J\cup\{i\},j}^{+}\partial_{J,i}^{+}
+
\partial_{J\cup\{j\},i}^{+}\partial_{J,j}^{+}
=0.
}
\tag{5.5}
\]

Thus the M51 two-cut anti-commutation is not an isolated sign identity: it is exactly the boundary-of-a-boundary cancellation for the full coordinate-face cube.
