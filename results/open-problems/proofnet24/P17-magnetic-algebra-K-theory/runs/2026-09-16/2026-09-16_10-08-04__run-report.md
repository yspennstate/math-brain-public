## 7. Proof of `P17-M49-PV-SQUARE-EXACT-GLUING-45`

### 7.1 Anti-commutation

For distinct `i,j`, right contractions satisfy the exterior identity

\[
R_iR_j+R_jR_i=0.
\tag{7.1}
\]

This follows immediately on every ordered monomial: deleting two different generators in opposite orders changes the deletion parity by one.

Under the M48 identifications, the second cut of `D_i` by generator `j` is again the corresponding right contraction. Thus

\[
\partial_j^{(i)}\partial_i
+
\partial_i^{(j)}\partial_j=0,
\]

which proves `(M49.2)` and shows

\[
\operatorname{im}(\partial_i,\partial_j)
\subseteq
\ker(\partial_j^{(i)}+\partial_i^{(j)}).
\]

### 7.2 Integral surjectivity onto the compatibility kernel

The reverse inclusion must be proved over `Z`, not merely by a rank count.

Take a pair

\[
(a,b)\in K_1(D_i)\oplus K_1(D_j)
\]

satisfying the corner compatibility equation

\[
\partial_j^{(i)}a+\partial_i^{(j)}b=0.
\tag{7.2}
\]

Work coefficientwise in the odd exterior bases.

An odd basis monomial in `D_i` falls into two cases.

1. **It does not contain `j`.** Its coefficient in `a` is unconstrained by `(7.2)`. It is lifted, with coefficient changed only by a sign `±1`, by the unique even monomial obtained by adjoining `i`.
2. **It contains `j`.** Removing `j` gives a basis monomial in `D_ij`. Equation `(7.2)` identifies its coefficient, up to the right-contraction sign, with the coefficient of the corresponding odd monomial in `D_j` obtained by adjoining `i`. These two compatible coefficients are lifted simultaneously by the single even monomial containing both `i` and `j`.

The symmetric statement handles the components of `b` not containing `i`. Every lift uses only coefficients `0,±1`; no division occurs. Hence every integral compatible pair has an integral bulk lift. Therefore

\[
\operatorname{im}(\partial_i,\partial_j)
=
\ker(\partial_j^{(i)}+\partial_i^{(j)})
\]

over `Z`.

### 7.3 Kernel and surjectivity at the ends

M48's common-kernel theorem gives

\[
\ker(\partial_i,\partial_j)
=
\bigoplus_{\substack{I\subset\{1,2,3,4\}\setminus\{i,j\}\\|I|\text{ even}}}
\mathbb Z[p_I].
\]

Since the complement has two generators, this is precisely the canonical included copy of

\[
K_0(D_{ij})\cong\mathbb Z^2.
\]

At the other end, any even basis monomial in `D_ij` is the contraction of an odd monomial formed by adjoining `j` inside `D_i`. Hence

\[
\partial_j^{(i)}+\partial_i^{(j)}
\]

is surjective.

This proves the entire four-term exact sequence.
