let

\widetilde F(z)=\sum_{k=-S}^S A_kz^k

### Audit step 1 — uniqueness of the coefficient lift

If two Laurent polynomials of degree at most \(S\) agree on all \(L\)-th roots, their difference multiplied by \(z^S\) is an ordinary polynomial of degree at most \(2S<L\) with \(L\) distinct roots. Hence it is zero. The lift is unique.

### Audit step 2 — self-adjoint dilation

Define

\[
D(e^{i\theta})
=
\begin{pmatrix}
0&\widetilde F(e^{i\theta})\\
\widetilde F(e^{i\theta})^*&0
\end{pmatrix}.
\tag{7.2}
\]

Then \(D\) is a self-adjoint matrix trigonometric polynomial of degree \(S\), and

\[
\|D(e^{i\theta})\|=\|\widetilde F(e^{i\theta})\|.
\tag{7.3}
\]

At an \(L\)-th root, its norm is exactly the matrix sample norm of \(F_L\).

### Audit step 3 — independent scalar secant argument

Let

\[
M=\max_\theta\|D(e^{i\theta})\|
\]

and choose \(\theta_0\) attaining it. Since \(D(\theta_0)\) is self-adjoint, choose a unit eigenvector \(v\) with eigenvalue \(\pm M\). Then

\[
f(\theta)=\langle v,D(e^{i\theta})v\rangle
\]

is a real scalar trigonometric polynomial of degree at most \(S\), \(|f(\theta)|\le M\), and \(|f(\theta_0)|=M\).

The pointwise Bernstein–Szegő inequality for a real trigonometric polynomial of degree \(S\) gives

\[
f'(\theta)^2+S^2f(\theta)^2\le S^2M^2.
\tag{7.4}
\]

Choose the nearest \(L\)-th root \(e^{i\theta_j}\); then

\[
|\theta_j-\theta_0|\le\frac\pi L.
\]

Since \(S<L/2\), the interval stays before the first zero of the comparison cosine. Integrating the differential inequality for \(\arccos(|f|/M)\) yields

\[
|f(\theta_j)|
\ge
M\cos\!\left(S|\theta_j-\theta_0|\right)
\ge
M\cos\!\left(\frac{\pi S}{L}\right).
\tag{7.5}
\]

Therefore

\[
\max_{\zeta^L=1}\|F_L(\zeta)\|
=
\max_j\|D(e^{i\theta_j})\|
\ge
M\cos\!\left(\frac{\pi S}{L}\right),
\]

which rearranges to (7.1).

No estimate depends on the matrix size.
