## Proof

\[
k_\beta(x)=e^{-\beta|x|},\qquad x\in\mathbb R^2.

\[
\widehat{k_\beta}(\xi)
=\frac{2\pi\beta}{(\beta^2+|\xi|^2)^{3/2}}>0.
\tag{4.1}
\]

Let \(P=\{p_n\}\subset\mathbb R^2\) be any source pattern and let \(c\) have finite support. Then

\[
\begin{aligned}
\sum_{n,m}\overline{c_n}c_m e^{-\beta|p_n-p_m|}
&=(2\pi)^{-2}\int_{\mathbb R^2}
\widehat{k_\beta}(\xi)
\left|\sum_n c_ne^{ip_n\cdot\xi}\right|^2d\xi\\
&\ge0.
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.2}
\]

Thus every finite Gram compression is positive. Passing to the bounded infinite operator gives

\[
H^{\rm pub}_\omega\ge0.
\tag{4.3}

c_0=0.00871847141603228\ldots .

## Claim audited

For the standard \(N\times N\) clock/shift (Weyl) realization of the torus Bott pair, commutator tolerance \(\delta\) forces matrix dimension \(N=\Omega(\delta^{-1})\); conversely the standard pair achieves \(N=O(\delta^{-1})\).

## Independent derivation

Let

\[
U_N=\operatorname{diag}(1,\zeta,\ldots,\zeta^{N-1}),
\qquad
V_Ne_j=e_{j+1},
\qquad
\zeta=e^{2\pi i/N}.
\]

Up to orientation convention,

\[
U_NV_N=\zeta V_NU_N.
\]

Therefore

\[
\|U_NV_N-V_NU_N\|
=|\zeta-1|
=2\sin\frac\pi N.
\tag{7.1}
\]

For \(N\ge2\), \(\pi/N\in(0,\pi/2]\), and concavity of sine on this interval gives

\[
\sin x\ge\frac{2x}{\pi}.
\]

Hence

\[
2\sin\frac\pi N\ge\frac4N.
\tag{7.2}
\]

If the defect is at most \(\delta\), then

\[
\boxed{N\ge4/\delta.}
\tag{7.3}
\]

Conversely, \(\sin x\le x\) gives

\[
2\sin\frac\pi N\le\frac{2\pi}{N},
\tag{7.4}
\]

so \(N\ge2\pi/\delta\) suffices. Thus the standard Bott/Weyl pair has exact order

\[
\boxed{N=\Theta(\delta^{-1}).}
\tag{7.5}
\]

The standard clock/shift pair carries the expected nontrivial Bott winding in the small-defect regime. The audit is deliberately scoped to the theorem actually claimed: the standard Weyl/clock-shift realization. It does not assert a universal lower bound for every imaginable finite-dimensional approximate representative of the Bott class unless additional hypotheses are supplied.
