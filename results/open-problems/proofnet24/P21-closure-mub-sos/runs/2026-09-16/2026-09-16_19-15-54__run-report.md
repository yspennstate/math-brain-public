## 7. Three genuinely different fleet moves on I0035 screening

Following `FLEET_RULES.md`, I used three Section-B moves not used in the previous P21 run.

### Move 18 — SAY IT IN ANOTHER LANGUAGE: BAB as weighted constrained heat bath

Let `k_v(eta)` be the number of occupied neighbors of `v`. The source rates are

\[
0\to1:\ \theta k_v(\eta),
\qquad
1\to0:\ k_v(\eta).
\]

Since `p=theta/(1+theta)`, these are exactly the rates obtained by resampling the spin at `v` from Bernoulli(`p`) at total clock rate

\[
(1+\theta)k_v(\eta).
\]

Let `mu` be Bernoulli(`p`) conditioned on the nonempty state space. For an outside configuration with `k_v>0`, the conditional spin law at `v` is still Bernoulli(`p`). If the outside configuration is all zero, then `k_v=0`, so the exceptional conditioning contributes zero anyway. Therefore the BAB Dirichlet form is exactly

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal D_{\rm BAB}(f)
=(1+\theta)\sum_{v\in V}
\mu\big[k_v(\eta)\operatorname{Var}_v(f)\big].
}
\tag{7.1}
\]

This translation exposes the model as a weighted FA-1f-type heat-bath chain rather than a mysterious branching-annihilating process.

### Move 21 — ASK WHO ELSE HAS THIS SHAPE: FA-1f / CBSEP

Hartarsky-Martinelli-Toninelli define FA-1f on the same nonempty Bernoulli state space by resampling `v` at rate one whenever it has at least one neighboring particle. Thus

\[
\mathcal D_{\rm FA}(f)
=\sum_v\mu\big[\mathbf 1_{\{k_v\ge1\}}\operatorname{Var}_v(f)\big].
\tag{7.2}
\]

If `Delta` is the maximum graph degree, the pointwise integer bound

\[
\mathbf1_{\{k_v\ge1\}}
\le k_v
\le \Delta\mathbf1_{\{k_v\ge1\}}
\]

gives the exact comparison

\[
\boxed{
(1+\theta)\mathcal D_{\rm FA}
\le \mathcal D_{\rm BAB}
\le (1+\theta)\Delta\mathcal D_{\rm FA}.
}
\tag{7.3}
\]

No asymptotics or coupling are used.

The HMT paper then proves for its **different** CBSEP chain an absolute-constant Dirichlet comparison

\[
c^{-1}\mathcal D_{\rm FA}
\le \mathcal D_{\rm CB}
\le c\Delta p^{-1}\mathcal D_{\rm FA}.
\tag{7.4}
\]

The distinction matters: CBSEP has exclusion moves in addition to the branching/coalescing moves. The value of (7.4) is as a transfer bridge, not an identification of the two chains.

### Move 11 — DUALIZE: Poincare/Rayleigh quotients transfer the bounds

For any reversible chain with common stationary law `mu`,

\[
T_{\rm rel}
=\sup_{f\not\equiv\mathrm{const}}
\frac{\operatorname{Var}_\mu(f)}{\mathcal D(f)}.
\]

Applying this to (7.3) gives immediately

\[
\boxed{
\frac{T_{\rm FA}}{(1+\theta)\Delta}
\le T_{\rm BAB}
\le \frac{T_{\rm FA}}{1+\theta}.
}
\tag{7.5}
\]

Applying the same variational rule to (7.4) gives

\[
\frac{T_{\rm CB}}{c}
\le T_{\rm FA}
\le c\Delta p^{-1}T_{\rm CB}.
\tag{7.6}
\]

Since `p=theta/(1+theta)`, composition yields the source-model comparison

\[
\boxed{
\frac{T_{\rm CB}}{c(1+\theta)\Delta}
\le T_{\rm BAB}
\le \frac{c\Delta}{\theta}T_{\rm CB}.
}
\tag{7.7}
\]

This is a concrete route by which the general-graph CBSEP bounds of HMT may transfer to Aldous-Fill's exact chain. For example HMT prove `T_CB=O(1)` for density bounded away from zero and the general sparse-density upper bound

\[
T_{\rm CB}\le c n\max_y\bar{\mathcal R}_y
\]

when `p->0`; inserting those into (7.7) gives explicit BAB bounds with fully visible `theta` and `Delta` losses.

This screen is promising enough to justify a dedicated admission check. It is **not yet a source-level solution claim**: the source's wording “Give bounds” is broad, and before P21 claims I0035 the fleet must decide whether these comparison bounds meet a nontrivial source-matching closure criterion or merely give a useful partial theorem. That category decision cannot be silently invented by the worker.
