# 2. Exact selected-endpoint augmentation

Conditional on the target cloud, the exact finite canonical path law is the equal mixture of Brownian bridges from \(0\) to the \(Z_i\)'s.

Thus one may enlarge the probability space as follows without changing the path marginal:

1. sample
   \[
   I_p
   \]
   uniformly from \(\{1,\ldots,p\}\), conditional on the target cloud;

2. conditional on \(I_p\), sample the canonical path as a Brownian bridge from \(0\) to
   \[
   b_p=Z_{I_p}.
   \]

Unconditionally, the random index is independent of the iid target array, so

\[
b_p\sim N(0,1),
\]

and, conditional on

\[
b_p=b,
\]

the remaining \(p-1\) target points are iid \(N(0,1)\).

This is exactly the Palm setting needed below.
