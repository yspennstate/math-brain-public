# 3. Proof by a conditional characteristic-Laplace functional

Fix one realization of \(\mathcal E\).

Write

\[
g=g_{\mathcal E},
\qquad
b=b(\mathcal E),
\qquad
\sigma^2=\int g^2f.
\]

Let

\[
t\in\mathbb R
\]

and let

\[
h\ge0
\]

be continuous with compact support.

By iid factorization,

\[
\begin{aligned}
& E\left[
\exp\left(
itL_p-\langle h,\Pi_p\rangle
\right)
\mid\mathcal E
\right]
\\
&\quad=
\left[
\int
e^{it g(z)/\sqrt p}
e^{-h((z-b)/s_p)}
f(z)\,dz
\right]^p.
\end{aligned}
\tag{3.1}
\]

Put

\[
A_p
=
\int
e^{it g(z)/\sqrt p}
f(z)\,dz.
\]

Since

\[
\int gf=0,
\qquad
g\in L^2(f),
\]

the ordinary \(L^2\) characteristic-function expansion gives

\[
\boxed{
p(A_p-1)
\to
-\frac{t^2\sigma^2}{2}.
}
\tag{3.2}
\]

Indeed,

\[
p\left(
e^{itg/\sqrt p}
-
1
-
\frac{itg}{\sqrt p}
\right)
\to
-\frac{t^2g^2}{2}
\]

pointwise, and its absolute value is bounded by a constant multiple of \(t^2g^2\).

Now define the local correction

\[
C_p
=
\int
e^{itg(z)/\sqrt p}
\left[
e^{-h((z-b)/s_p)}-1
\right]
f(z)\,dz.
\]

Its version with the CLT factor removed is

\[
C_p^{(0)}
=
\int
\left[
e^{-h((z-b)/s_p)}-1
\right]
f(z)\,dz.
\]

Changing variables

\[
z=b+s_p\eta
\]

gives

\[
\begin{aligned}
pC_p^{(0)}
&=
ps_p
\int
\left(
e^{-h(\eta)}-1
\right)
f(b+s_p\eta)\,d\eta
\\
&\longrightarrow
\rho f(b)
\int
\left(
e^{-h(\eta)}-1
\right)\,d\eta.
\end{aligned}
\tag{3.3}
\]

It remains to show that the CLT factor is invisible inside the local window.

Let \(K\) be a compact interval containing the support of \(h\), and set

\[
A_{p,K}
=
\{z:(z-b)/s_p\in K\}.
\]

Using

\[
|e^{ix}-1|\le|x|,
\]

\[
\begin{aligned}
p|C_p-C_p^{(0)}|
&\le
|t|\sqrt p
\int_{A_{p,K}}
|g(z)|f(z)\,dz
\\
&\le
|t|\sqrt p
\left(
\int_{A_{p,K}}
g(z)^2f(z)\,dz
\right)^{1/2}
P\{Z\in A_{p,K}\}^{1/2}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{3.4}
\]

By continuity of \(f\) at \(b\),

\[
P\{Z\in A_{p,K}\}
=
O(s_p)
=
O(p^{-1}).
\]

Hence

\[
p|C_p-C_p^{(0)}|
\le
C_{\mathcal E,h,t}
\left(
\int_{A_{p,K}}
g^2f
\right)^{1/2}.
\tag{3.5}
\]

Since

\[
g^2f\in L^1
\]

and the interval \(A_{p,K}\) shrinks to \(b\),

\[
\int_{A_{p,K}}g^2f\to0.
\]

Therefore

\[
\boxed{
p(C_p-C_p^{(0)})\to0.
}
\tag{3.6}
\]

Combining (3.2), (3.3), and (3.6),

\[
\begin{aligned}
&p\left[
\int
e^{it g(z)/\sqrt p}
e^{-h((z-b)/s_p)}
f(z)\,dz
-
1
\right]
\\
&\qquad\to
-\frac{t^2\sigma^2}{2}
+
\rho f(b)
\int
(e^{-h(\eta)}-1)\,d\eta.
\end{aligned}
\tag{3.7}
\]

Taking the \(p\)-th power in (3.1),

\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
&
E\left[
e^{itL_p-\langle h,\Pi_p\rangle}
\mid\mathcal E
\right]
\\
&\qquad\longrightarrow
\exp\left(
-\frac{t^2\sigma^2}{2}
\right)
\exp\left[
\rho f(b)
\int(e^{-h(\eta)}-1)\,d\eta
\right].
\end{aligned}
}
\tag{3.8}
\]

The limit factors into:

- the Gaussian characteristic function;
- the Poisson Laplace functional.

This proves conditional independence.

Because every conditional transform has modulus at most one, dominated convergence also gives the corresponding stable/joint statement with the environment.

QED.
