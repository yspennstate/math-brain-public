## 5. Full proof of `P24-AV-P3-DIRECT-G-PULSE-59`

Fix `T>0` and a nonzero scalar `h`. Let `phi_n` be an `L^2(T)`-normalized eigenfunction of `Lambda`:

`Lambda phi_n = lambda_n phi_n`,

where `lambda_n=1+n^2` and `lambda_n -> infinity`.

Choose `n` large enough that `1/lambda_n < T`. Set, with `a=0` and deterministic `tau=T`,

`f_n(t)=0`,

`g_n(t)=h 1_[0,1/lambda_n](t) phi_n`.

This `g_n` is deterministic, hence progressive, and belongs to every source `L^p_t gamma(U,X_{1/2})` space. Its input norm is exactly

`||g_n||^p_{L^p(Omega;L^p(0,T;L^2))} = |h|^p/lambda_n`.      (5.1)

Because all coefficients preserve the one-dimensional eigenspace, the strong solution has the form

`u_n(t)=x_n(t) phi_n`,

where

`dx_n = -lambda_n x_n dt + beta sqrt(lambda_n) x_n dW_t`

`       + h 1_[0,1/lambda_n](t) dW_t`,

`x_n(0)=0`.                                                   (5.2)

Now put

`s=lambda_n t`,

`Wtilde_s=sqrt(lambda_n) W_{s/lambda_n}`,

`Z_s=sqrt(lambda_n) x_n(s/lambda_n)`.

Brownian scaling converts (5.2) exactly into the lambda-free SDE

`dZ_s = -Z_s ds + (beta Z_s + h 1_[0,1](s)) dWtilde_s`,

`Z_0=0`.                                                       (5.3)

The source output norm on this eigenspace also scales exactly because

`||u_n(t)||_{H^1} = sqrt(lambda_n)|x_n(t)| = |Z_{lambda_n t}|`.

Therefore

`||u_n||^p_{L^p(Omega;L^p(0,T;H^1))}`

`= (1/lambda_n) integral_0^(lambda_n T) E|Z_s|^p ds`.          (5.4)

Combining (5.1) and (5.4), the p-th power of the output/input norm ratio is exactly

`R_n(p)^p = |h|^{-p} integral_0^(lambda_n T) E|Z_s|^p ds`.     (5.5)

### 5.1 The pulse leaves nonzero mass

For `0<=s<=1`, let `m(s)=E Z_s` and `v(s)=E Z_s^2`. From (5.3),

`m'(s)=-m(s)`, `m(0)=0`,

hence `m(s)=0`.

Ito's formula gives

`v'(s)=(-2+beta^2)v(s)+2 beta h m(s)+h^2`

`      =(-2+beta^2)v(s)+h^2`.

Since `beta^2<2`,

`v(1)=h^2 [1-exp(-(2-beta^2))]/(2-beta^2) > 0`.               (5.6)

Thus `Z_1` is not almost surely zero. The scalar linear SDE has moments of every finite order on `[0,1]`; consequently

`0 < m_p := E|Z_1|^p < infinity`                              (5.7)

for every finite `p>=2`.

### 5.2 Exact post-pulse moment exponent

For `s>=1`, the additive pulse is off, so

`dZ_s=-Z_s ds+beta Z_s dWtilde_s`.

Conditionally on `F_1`,

`Z_s=Z_1 exp(-(1+beta^2/2)(s-1)+beta(Wtilde_s-Wtilde_1))`.

Hence

`E(|Z_s|^p | F_1)=|Z_1|^p exp(gamma_{p,beta}(s-1))`,           (5.8)

where

`gamma_{p,beta}`

`= -p(1+beta^2/2) + beta^2 p^2/2`

`= (p/2)[ beta^2(p-1)-2 ]`.                                  (5.9)

The critical exponent is therefore exactly

`p_c(beta)=1+2/beta^2`.                                      (5.10)

At `p=p_c(beta)`, `gamma=0`, and (5.8) becomes the exact conservation law

`E|Z_s|^{p_c} = E|Z_1|^{p_c}` for every `s>=1`.               (5.11)

Above the threshold the same moment grows exponentially.

### 5.3 Contradiction to source SMR

If `p=p_c(beta)`, (5.5), (5.7), and (5.11) give

`R_n(p)^p >= (m_p/|h|^p)(lambda_n T-1)`.

Thus `R_n(p)->infinity` as `n->infinity`.

If `p>p_c(beta)`, then `gamma_{p,beta}>0`, and the tail integral in (5.5) contains

`m_p integral_1^(lambda_n T) exp(gamma_{p,beta}(s-1)) ds`,

which diverges exponentially in `lambda_n`.

Definition 3.7 would require a single finite constant `C_T` controlling all these deterministic inputs for the same pair and the same fixed physical horizon `T`. No such constant exists. Therefore

`(Lambda,beta Lambda^(1/2)) notin SMR_{p,0}` for every `p>=1+2/beta^2`.

Since bullet membership implies base membership, bullet membership also fails there. This completes the proof.

### 5.4 Frozen endpoint

For the frozen pair `beta=1`,

`gamma_p = p(p-3)/2`.

In particular,

`gamma_2=-1`, `gamma_3=0`, `gamma_4=2`.

Thus `p=3` is the exact conserved-moment endpoint and the fixed upper certificate is source-direct.

## 6. Quantitative consequence below the endpoint

The same pulse gives information below the endpoint and explains why constants must deteriorate as `p` approaches three.

For `p<p_c(beta)`, put

`kappa_{p,beta} = -gamma_{p,beta}`

`= (p/2)[2-beta^2(p-1)] > 0`.

Letting `lambda_n -> infinity` in the tail of (5.5) yields the lower bound, for any valid base-SMR constant at exponent p,

`C_T(p)^p >= (m_p/|h|^p) / kappa_{p,beta}`.                  (6.1)

For the frozen pair `beta=1`, `Z_1/h` does not depend on `h`, has all finite moments, and its p-th absolute moment is continuous at `p=3`. By (5.6), its third absolute moment is strictly positive. Hence there are `p_0<3` and `c>0` such that

`C_T(p) >= c (3-p)^(-1/3)` for `p_0<p<3`.                    (6.2)

The preceding Bellman proof gives a nonsharp upper blow-up of order `(3-p)^(-1)`. The exact near-critical operator-norm exponent therefore remains an interesting auxiliary question, but it is not the frozen task metric.

## 7. Explicit proof construction that fails, and the exact failure step
