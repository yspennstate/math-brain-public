### Continuity for positive \(\alpha\)

First prove
\[

\quad\text{uniformly}
\Longrightarrow
K_{b_n}\to K_b

The right side is integrable in \(s\). Therefore Minkowski plus dominated convergence gives (5.4).

\to
\Theta_\alpha b
