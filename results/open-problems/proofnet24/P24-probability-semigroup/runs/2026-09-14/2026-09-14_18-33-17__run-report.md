# 5. Gaussian lower-max lemma with arbitrary shifts

We first isolate the elementary Gaussian mechanism.

### Lemma P24-GAUSS-MAX-SHIFT-02 — PROVED

Let
\[
X^{(M)}
\]
be a centered Gaussian vector whose covariance satisfies
\[
\Sigma_M\succeq \sigma_0^2I_M.
\]

Then there is a representation in law
\[
X^{(M)}
=
\sigma_0Z^{(M)}+Y^{(M)},
\]
where \(Z^{(M)}\) has iid \(N(0,1)\) coordinates and is independent of the centered Gaussian vector \(Y^{(M)}\).

For every \(u>0\),
\[
\boxed{
P\left\{
\max_{1\le j\le M}|X_j|\le u
\right\}
\le
\left(
1-2\overline\Phi(u/\sigma_0)
\right)^M
\le
\exp\left[
-2M\overline\Phi(u/\sigma_0)
\right].
}
\tag{P24-GAUSS-MAX-SHIFT-02}
\]

### Proof

Condition on \(Y^{(M)}=y\).

For one coordinate,
\[
P\{|\sigma_0Z+y_j|\le u\}
\]
is the Gaussian mass of an interval of fixed length \(2u\).

Because the centered Gaussian density is symmetric and unimodal, this interval mass is maximized when the interval is centered at zero:
\[
P\{|\sigma_0Z+y_j|\le u\}
\le
P\{|\sigma_0Z|\le u\}
=
1-2\overline\Phi(u/\sigma_0).
\]

Conditional independence of the \(Z_j\)'s gives the product bound.

Average over \(Y\).

QED.

No independence among the coordinates of \(X\) itself is assumed.

# 13. Why this still does not prove the sharp original log modulus

The natural original-path target is
\[
q^{-1/4}|\Xi_{\beta q}-\Xi_0|
\asymp
\sqrt{\log\log(1/q)}
\]
along large excursions.

On the geometric grid
\[
q_m=e^{-mL},
\]
this is the same as a target of order
\[
\sqrt{\log m}.
\]

The diagonal construction above proves a threshold
\[
\sqrt{\log j}
\]
inside a block of length \(j\), but the chosen starting index \(m_j\) may be arbitrarily larger than \(j\).

Without a quantitative convergence rate, nothing proves
\[
\log j
\asymp
\log m_j.
\]

Therefore:

\[
\boxed{
\text{P24-XI-SPARSE-GROWING-WINDOW-02 does not imply a }
\sqrt{\log\log(1/q)}
\text{ lower modulus.}
}
\]

What it proves is that the missing bridge is no longer **existence** of a growing window.

The remaining obstruction is **rate**:

> how large a logarithmic window \(N(q)\) can be coupled at base scale \(q\)?

This is a sharper formulation of the next problem.
