## 4. New unrestricted reduction: the exact zero set can be put into the complete Hadamard atlas

The preceding C29 run produced exact obstructions only on cyclotomic/Butson strata. Those are useful branch prunings but are not an unrestricted cover. The August 2026 classification now permits a genuinely unrestricted **exact-solution** reduction, provided the relative row monomials are retained.

Let `Mon(6)` be the group of `6 x 6` monomial unitaries. Standard complex-Hadamard equivalence is

\[
H'\;=\;D_{\rm r}P_{\rm r}\,H\,P_{\rm c}D_{\rm c},
\tag{4.1}
\]

where the `D`'s are diagonal unitaries and the `P`'s are permutation matrices.

Let \(\mathcal A_6^{\rm fc}\) denote the exact branch-complete dephased finite-corner atlas of Cárdenes Wuttig–Tindall. Their Theorem 6 proves that every order-six complex Hadamard equivalence class has a representative in this atlas.

### Theorem `C29-ATLAS-GAUGE-04` — complete exact-zero normal form

**Theorem.** If seven mutually unbiased bases exist in \(\mathbb C^6\), then they are gauge-equivalent, by one common unitary change of ambient coordinates and independent permutations/rephasings of the vectors inside each basis, to a tuple of the form

\[
\boxed{
\left(
I,\;
\frac{A_1}{\sqrt6},\;
\frac{M_2A_2}{\sqrt6},\ldots,
\frac{M_6A_6}{\sqrt6}
\right),
}
\tag{4.2}
\]

where

\[
A_j\in\mathcal A_6^{\rm fc}
\]

is a dephased order-six complex Hadamard atlas representative and

\[
M_j=D_jP_j\in\operatorname{Mon}(6)
\tag{4.3}
\]

is a relative row monomial. After an allowed common scalar rephasing of the columns of the \(j\)-th basis, one may normalize

\[
D_j=\operatorname{diag}(1,z_{j2},\ldots,z_{j6}),
\qquad |z_{jr}|=1.
\tag{4.4}
\]

Thus, after anchoring one nonidentity basis in the complete Hadamard atlas, the relative gauge that must **not** be discarded for each of the other five bases consists of a finite row permutation and five circle phases.

Conversely, any tuple of the form (4.2) satisfying the remaining pairwise cross-unbiasedness equations is a set of seven MUBs.

#### Proof

Let the seven bases be represented by unitary matrices

\[
U_0,U_1,\ldots,U_6,
\]

whose columns are the basis vectors.

Apply the common unitary \(U_0^\dagger\) on the left. This preserves every inner product and sends the first basis to the identity. Set

\[
V_j=U_0^\dagger U_j,\qquad j=1,\ldots,6.
\]

Because \(V_j\) is unbiased to the identity, every entry of \(V_j\) has modulus \(1/\sqrt6\). Hence

\[
H_j=\sqrt6\,V_j
\]

has unimodular entries. Since \(V_j\) is unitary,

\[
H_jH_j^\dagger=6I,
\]

so every \(H_j\) is an order-six complex Hadamard matrix.

By the complete Cárdenes Wuttig–Tindall classification, the standard-equivalence class of \(H_1\) has a dephased finite-corner atlas representative \(A_1\). Therefore there are monomial unitaries \(L,R\) such that

\[
A_1=L H_1 R.
\tag{4.5}
\]

Apply the **same** left monomial \(L\) to all seven bases. The identity basis becomes \(L\), but right multiplication of that basis by the allowed monomial \(L^\dagger\) restores it exactly to \(I\). Right multiplication of the anchor basis by \(R\) turns it into \(A_1/\sqrt6\). Common left multiplication cancels from cross Gram matrices, while right monomials merely permute and phase basis vectors, so all MUB relations are preserved.

Now fix \(j\ge2\). After the common left operation, let

\[
K_j=L H_j.
\]

This is still a complex Hadamard matrix. By completeness of the same atlas, there exist monomial unitaries \(L_j,R_j\) and \(A_j\in\mathcal A_6^{\rm fc}\) such that

\[
A_j=L_jK_jR_j.
\tag{4.6}
\]

Right multiplication of the \(j\)-th basis by the allowed monomial \(R_j\) gives

\[
K_jR_j=L_j^{-1}A_j.
\]

Set

\[
M_j=L_j^{-1}.
\]

Every monomial unitary factors as a diagonal unitary times a permutation,

\[
M_j=D_jP_j.
\]

Finally, multiplying all columns of the \(j\)-th basis by one common unit complex scalar is an allowed right rephasing and scales \(D_j\) by that scalar. Choose it so the first diagonal entry of \(D_j\) is one, giving (4.4).

This proves the forward normal form.

For the converse, every \(A_j/\sqrt6\) is unitary and flat, and left multiplication by \(M_j\) preserves unitarity and flatness. Therefore every nonidentity basis in (4.2) is unbiased to \(I\). If the remaining pairwise cross equations also hold, all 21 basis pairs are mutually unbiased, giving seven MUBs. QED.

### Why the relative row monomials are essential

Equation (4.2) is precisely where an unsafe shortcut would lose solutions. Standard Hadamard equivalence permits independent **left** monomial transformations of individual Hadamards, but in an MUB tuple those are changes of ambient coordinates and cannot be made independently without altering overlaps with the other bases. They survive as the \(M_j\)'s.

Independent **right** monomials are harmless because they only permute/rephase vectors inside a basis.

Therefore it would be invalid to replace all six nonidentity bases simultaneously by independently dephased atlas representatives and then test their raw overlaps. The relative \(M_j\) factors are part of the exact unrestricted problem.

## 6. A literature-certified branch deletion inside the unrestricted atlas

The new normal form allows an immediate source-compatible pruning that was not available from the Butson pair obstruction alone.

Huang–Liang–Chen define an “MUB trio” as the three nonidentity CHMs occurring when four MUBs contain the identity. Their Theorem 19 states:

- every order-six CHM having exactly three distinct entries is complex equivalent to either Tao's \(S_6^{(0)}\) or a specific \(H_2\)-reducible matrix \(H^{(1)}\);
- no CHM having exactly three distinct entries is a member of an MUB trio.

Membership in an MUB trio is invariant under complex-Hadamard standard equivalence: a left monomial can be applied as a common ambient unitary to all four bases, and a right monomial only rephases/reorders the basis vectors.

Therefore:

### Corollary `C29-THREE-ENTRY-PRUNE-05`

In any exact seven-MUB tuple, **none** of the six nonidentity Hadamard equivalence classes can be the Tao class or the \(H^{(1)}\) class identified by Huang–Liang–Chen.

#### Proof

Take any one nonidentity basis from a hypothetical seven-MUB tuple and select any two of the other five nonidentity bases. Together with the identity they form four MUBs, so the selected Hadamard is a member of an MUB trio.

If its equivalence class were Tao's class or the \(H^{(1)}\) class, choose the corresponding three-entry representative. Huang–Liang–Chen prove that such a CHM cannot be a member of an MUB trio. Since membership is invariant under standard equivalence, this contradicts the selected basis being part of the four-MUB subset. QED.

This is a **KNOWN-literature branch pruning combined with the new gauge normal form**, not a claim that C29 newly proved the Huang–Liang–Chen theorem.

It excludes two exact atlas classes from every one of the six Hadamard slots. It does not exclude the whole Karlsson sector and does not move the global residual metric.
