# 0. Executive result

The original problem remains **OPEN**:

\[
\mu_i^G:K_i^{\mathrm{top}}(G)\longrightarrow K_i(C_r^*(G)),
\qquad i=0,1,
\]

for every second-countable locally compact group `G`.

This run does **not** replace the scalar conjecture by Baum–Connes with coefficients and does **not** claim that a discrete test-family theorem settles the locally compact problem.

The main new work attacks the analytic bottleneck left explicitly by P58:

> for a fixed finite group-ring matrix, produce a genuinely finite certificate that forces an isolated zero in the **reduced** spectrum and that can be transported across marked approximations without any reduced-group-C*-algebra quotient map.

The resulting package is:

## `P01-P59A-FINITE-SOS-REDUCED-GAP-CERTIFICATE` — **PROVED**

Let `F=F(S)` be a finitely generated free group, let

\[
C\in M_{u,v}(\mathbb C F),
\qquad
A=C^*C\in M_v(\mathbb C F),
\]

and let `G` be an `S`-marked quotient. Suppose that for some `delta>0` there are finitely many matrices

\[
B_j\in M_{r_j,v}(\mathbb C F)
\]

such that after evaluation in the group ring of `G`,

\[
\boxed{
A_G^2-\delta A_G
=\sum_j B_{j,G}^*B_{j,G}.
}
\tag{P59.1}
\]

Then in the reduced matrix algebra

\[
\boxed{
\sigma(A_G)\subset\{0\}\cup[\delta,\infty).
}
\tag{P59.2}
\]

Consequently

\[
\boxed{
P_G=\mathbf 1_{\{0\}}(A_G)\in M_v(C_r^*(G)).
}
\tag{P59.3}
\]

Thus `(P59.1)` is an exact finite algebraic certificate for the missing passage

\[
\text{finite matrix}
\Longrightarrow
\text{actual reduced-C* kernel projection with a certified gap}.
\]

The abstract sum-of-squares spectral-gap idea is not claimed as new: Li–Nowak–Pooya explicitly point to Bader–Nowak's group-algebra criteria and state that the relevant Laplacian summands can be certified by writing expressions of the form `(Delta-epsilon)Delta` as sums of squares. The new P01 point is the exact **marked-local portability radius** and the quantitative trace/resolvent consequences below.

## `P01-P59B-LOCAL-RELATION-SOS-PORTABILITY` — **PROVED**

Write the formal defect

\[
E=A^2-\delta A-\sum_jB_j^*B_j\in M_v(\mathbb C F).
\]

Let `W` be the maximum free-word length occurring in an entry of `E`. If another `S`-marked quotient `H` has the same trivial relations as `G` for every reduced free word of length at most `2W`, then

\[
\boxed{E_G=0\Longrightarrow E_H=0.}
\tag{P59.4}
\]

Hence the **same** reduced gap `delta` holds for `A_H`.

If `R_C` is the support radius of `C` and

\[
R_B=\max_j R_{B_j},
\]

then a coarse explicit bound is

\[
\boxed{
W\le\max\{4R_C,2R_B\},
\qquad
L_{\rm SOS}:=2W
\le\max\{8R_C,4R_B\}.
}
\tag{P59.5}
\]

Therefore any marked approximation whose relation agreement depth eventually exceeds `L_SOS` inherits the exact same reduced spectral gap, with no reduced quotient homomorphism and no norm-convergence assertion.

## `P01-P59C-CERTIFIED-GAP-TRACE-LOCK-RADIUS` — **PROVED**

Let

\[
A=\sum_w A_w w,
\qquad
R_A=\max\{|w|:A_w\ne0\},
\]

and choose

\[
M\ge\sum_w\|A_w\|,
\qquad M>\delta.
\]

Then every quotient evaluation satisfies `||A_H||<=M`. Once `(P59.1)` has transferred, all such evaluations have the same positive gap `delta`, so uniformly in the quotient

\[
\boxed{
\|e^{-tA_H}-P_H\|\le e^{-t\delta}
}
\tag{P59.6}
\]

and

\[
\boxed{
\|\eta(A_H+\eta I)^{-1}-P_H\|
\le\frac{\eta}{\delta+\eta}.
}
\tag{P59.7}
\]

Let `G,H` also have the same trivial relations through depth `nR_A`. Their canonical matrix moments agree through degree `n`. For the exact Chebyshev cutoff one gets

\[
\boxed{
|\tau_v(P_H)-\tau_v(P_G)|
\le 2v\varepsilon_n
\le4v\exp\!\left(-2n\sqrt{\delta/M}\right),
}
\tag{P59.8}
\]

where `tau_v=Tr_v tensor tau` is the unnormalised matrix canonical trace.

Thus for any tolerance `0<Delta<4v`, put

\[
n_\Delta
:=1+\left\lceil
\frac12\sqrt{\frac M\delta}
\log\frac{4v}{\Delta}
\right\rceil.
\]

The explicit finite relation depth

\[
\boxed{
L_\Delta
=\max\{\,2W,\,n_\Delta R_A\,\}
}
\tag{P59.9}
\]

suffices simultaneously for:

1. transport of the exact reduced gap `delta`;
2. the uniform heat and resolvent estimates `(P59.6)`–`(P59.7)`;
3. canonical kernel-trace locking
   \[
   |\tau_v(P_H)-\tau_v(P_G)|<\Delta.
   \]

This is a fixed-matrix quantitative permanence theorem. It does **not** claim a map

\[
C_r^*(G)\to C_r^*(H)
\]

and it does not solve moving-relation K-theory control.

## `P01-P59D-APPROX-SOS-ZERO-ISOLATION-NOGO` — **PROVED falsification certificate**

A merely approximate SOS residual is not enough to isolate zero.

If

\[
\|A^2-\delta A-Q\|\le\epsilon,
\qquad Q\ge0,
\qquad 0<\epsilon<\delta^2/4,
\]

then only

\[
\boxed{
\sigma(A)\subset
[0,r_-]\cup[r_+,\infty),
\qquad
r_\pm=\frac{\delta\pm\sqrt{\delta^2-4\epsilon}}2
}
\tag{P59.10}
\]

follows. The whole low interval `[0,r_-]` remains allowed.

Indeed in the C*-algebra `C([0,r_-])`, with `A(s)=s` and `Q=0`,

\[
\|A^2-\delta A\|=\epsilon
\]

while

\[
\sigma(A)=[0,r_-],
\]

so zero is not isolated. Therefore a floating-point / approximate semidefinite SOS output is **not**, by itself, the reduced-C* projection certificate needed for a scalar Baum–Connes counterexample. One needs an exact certificate, an independently certified low-spectrum exclusion, or another rigorous enclosure argument.

# 3. Mandatory spectral-stability seed — rederived

Let `A>=0` in a unital matrix C*-algebra and assume

\[
\sigma(A)\subset\{0\}\cup[\delta,M],
\qquad \delta>0.
\]

The function on `sigma(A)` which is `1` at zero and `0` on `[delta,M]` is continuous, hence continuous functional calculus gives

\[
\boxed{P_A=\mathbf1_{\{0\}}(A)}
\]

inside the algebra.

For the heat cutoff,

\[
\|e^{-tA}-P_A\|
=
\sup_{\lambda\in\sigma(A)\setminus\{0\}}
e^{-t\lambda}
\le e^{-t\delta}.
\]

Thus

\[
\boxed{
\|e^{-tA}-P_A\|\le e^{-t\delta}.
}
\tag{3.1}
\]

For the resolvent cutoff,

\[
\eta(A+\eta I)^{-1}-P_A
\]

vanishes at zero and has scalar value `eta/(lambda+eta)` at every positive spectral value. Therefore

\[
\boxed{
\|\eta(A+\eta I)^{-1}-P_A\|
\le\frac{\eta}{\delta+\eta}.
}
\tag{3.2}
\]

Now let `A,B>=0` both have positive spectral gap at least `delta`. Define the bounded pseudoinverse

\[
A^\dagger(0)=0,
\qquad
A^\dagger(\lambda)=\lambda^{-1}\quad(\lambda\ge\delta).
\]

Because `BP_B=0`,

\[
(1-P_A)P_B
=A^\dagger AP_B
=A^\dagger(A-B)P_B,
\]

and hence

\[
\|(1-P_A)P_B\|
\le\frac{\|A-B\|}{\delta}.
\]

Interchanging `A,B` gives the symmetric estimate. Consequently the requested sufficient bound is

\[
\boxed{
\|P_A-P_B\|
\le\frac{2\|A-B\|}{\delta}.
}
\tag{3.3}
\]

The retained sharper factor-one two-projection estimate is compatible with this, but `(3.3)` is the required seed.

The common positive gap is indispensable. If `A` has nonzero kernel and

\[
B=A+\epsilon I,
\]

then

\[
\|A-B\|=\epsilon\to0
\]

while `P_B=0`. The positive gap of `B` begins at `epsilon`, so the common uniform gap collapses. This is precisely why norm closeness alone does not transport kernel projections.

# 4. P59A — finite SOS certificate for an actual reduced gap

Let

\[
A_G=C_G^*C_G\ge0
\]

and suppose `(P59.1)` holds:

\[
A_G^2-\delta A_G
=\sum_jB_{j,G}^*B_{j,G}.
\]

Every term on the right is positive in the matrix algebra over `C_r^*(G)`, so

\[
A_G^2-\delta A_G\ge0.
\tag{4.1}
\]

Since the left side is the continuous functional-calculus value of

\[
f(\lambda)=\lambda(\lambda-\delta)
\]

at the positive operator `A_G`, positivity of `(4.1)` forces

\[
f(\lambda)\ge0
\]

for every `lambda` in `sigma(A_G)`.

Because `lambda>=0`, this means

\[
\lambda=0
\quad\text{or}\quad
\lambda\ge\delta.
\]

Hence

\[
\sigma(A_G)\subset\{0\}\cup[\delta,\infty),
\]

which proves `(P59.2)`.

The singleton `{0}` is therefore clopen in the spectrum. Continuous functional calculus gives

\[
P_G=\mathbf1_{\{0\}}(A_G)
\in M_v(C_r^*(G)).
\]

This proves `(P59.3)`.

### What is and is not obtained

Obtained:

- a finite group-ring matrix;
- an actual isolated zero in the **reduced** spectrum;
- an actual reduced-C* projection;
- an explicit lower spectral edge `delta`;
- all heat/resolvent/Chebyshev projection estimates with that `delta`.

Not obtained merely from the SOS identity:

- a forbidden scalar-assembly trace;
- a proof that the projection class is outside the assembly image;
- a construction of a matrix satisfying the certificate on a host with forbidden trace;
- arbitrary-coefficient Baum–Connes information.

That distinction is essential.

# 5. P59B — exact finite-relation portability

The key point is that `(P59.1)` is a **finite algebraic identity**, so its validity is determined by finitely many equality relations among finitely many words.

Let

\[
E=A^2-\delta A-\sum_jB_j^*B_j
\in M_v(\mathbb C F).
\]

Fix one entry and write it as a finite formal sum

\[
E_{ab}=\sum_{w\in\mathcal W_{ab}}c_w w,
\]

with each `w` a reduced free word. Let

\[
W=\max\{|w|:w\in\mathcal W_{ab}
\text{ for some }a,b\}.
\]

The evaluation `E_G=0` says exactly that, in each matrix entry and for each element `g in G`, the sum of coefficients of the words evaluating to `g` is zero.

Now suppose `G` and `H` have the same trivial free-word relations through depth `2W`.

For two support words `u,v`,

\[
u=_Gv
\iff
uv^{-1}=_Ge.
\]

The reduced word `uv^{-1}` has length at most `2W`. Therefore

\[
uv^{-1}=_Ge
\iff
uv^{-1}=_He,
\]

and hence

\[
\boxed{
u=_Gv\iff u=_Hv
\quad\text{for all support words }u,v.
}
\tag{5.1}
\]

Thus `G` and `H` induce the **same partition of the finite formal support into equal group elements**. Every coefficient cancellation that makes `E_G` zero therefore occurs identically in `H`, proving

\[
E_H=0.
\]

This proves `(P59.4)`.

### Explicit radius

If the support radius of `C` is `R_C`, then

\[
R_A\le2R_C,
\qquad
R_{A^2}\le4R_C.
\]

If

\[
R_B=\max_jR_{B_j},
\]

then

\[
R_{B_j^*B_j}\le2R_B.
\]

Hence

\[
W\le\max\{4R_C,2R_B\}.
\]

Agreement of trivial relations through

\[
L_{\rm SOS}=2W
\]

is sufficient. This gives `(P59.5)`.

### Why this is stronger than marked convergence as usually invoked

The invalid shortcut would be:

> marked/local convergence implies upper reduced-norm convergence.

P59B does not say that.

Instead it says:

> one **specific finite algebraic positivity identity**, once known exactly, is eventually literally the same identity in every marked quotient with sufficiently deep relation agreement.

The reduced gap then follows from positivity **inside each reduced algebra separately**.

No C*-algebra map between the different groups is introduced.

# 6. P59C — quantitative trace and resolvent lock

Assume the P59A identity has transferred to both `G` and `H`. Then both `A_G` and `A_H` have the same gap `delta`.

Write

\[
A=\sum_wA_ww.
\]

The group elements act unitarily in every reduced representation, so

\[
\|A_K\|
\le\sum_w\|A_w\|
\]

for every marked quotient `K`. Choose once and for all

\[
M>\delta,
\qquad
M\ge\sum_w\|A_w\|.
\]

Then

\[
\sigma(A_K)\subset\{0\}\cup[\delta,M]
\]

for every quotient to which the certificate transfers.

The uniform heat and resolvent estimates `(P59.6)` and `(P59.7)` now follow directly from Section 3.

## 6.1 Moment agreement

If `R_A` is the formal support radius of `A`, then every word occurring in `A^j` has length at most `jR_A` before free reduction.

Therefore if `G,H` have the same trivial relations through depth `nR_A`, then

\[
\tau_v(A_G^j)=\tau_v(A_H^j),
\qquad0\le j\le n.
\tag{6.1}
\]

No quotient C*-map is needed: the canonical trace is just the identity coefficient, and the identity status of every contributing formal word is locally identical.

## 6.2 Chebyshev cutoff

Let `q_n` be the degree-`n` Chebyshev minimax cutoff with

\[
q_n(0)=1
\]

and

\[
\sup_{x\in[\delta,M]}|q_n(x)|
=\varepsilon_n
=
\frac1{T_n((M+\delta)/(M-\delta))}.
\]

The retained explicit estimate is

\[
\varepsilon_n
\le2\exp\!\left(-2n\sqrt{\delta/M}\right).
\tag{6.2}
\]

Functional calculus yields

\[
\|q_n(A_K)-P_K\|\le\varepsilon_n.
\]

Since the unnormalised matrix trace has total mass `v`,

\[
|\tau_v(q_n(A_K))-\tau_v(P_K)|
\le v\varepsilon_n.
\tag{6.3}
\]

By `(6.1)`,

\[
\tau_v(q_n(A_G))=\tau_v(q_n(A_H)).
\]

Combining the two instances of `(6.3)` gives

\[
|\tau_v(P_H)-\tau_v(P_G)|
\le2v\varepsilon_n
\le4v e^{-2n\sqrt{\delta/M}},
\]

which is `(P59.8)`.

The explicit relation radius `(P59.9)` follows by requiring simultaneously

\[
L\ge2W
\]

for the SOS identity and

\[
L\ge n_\Delta R_A
\]

for the Chebyshev moments.

## 6.3 Exact obstruction produced by a persistent trace mismatch

A useful contrapositive is immediate.

Suppose `G_i->G` is a marked approximation with relation depth tending to infinity, `A` is fixed, and

\[
|\tau_v(P_{G_i})-\tau_v(P_G)|\ge\Delta>0
\]

infinitely often.

Then **no fixed finite SOS certificate of the form `(P59.1)` can witness the limit gap with any fixed positive `delta` and finite support**. Otherwise P59B would transport it eventually and P59C would force the trace difference below `Delta`.

This does **not** prove that the limit gap is absent. It rules out only a finite locally portable SOS witness for that gap. A non-SOS gap proof could still exist.

This is the exact certificate-level complement to `P25-SMC`.

# 7. P59D — why approximate SOS output is insufficient

Suppose only

\[
\|A^2-\delta A-Q\|\le\epsilon,
\qquad Q\ge0.
\]

Then

\[
A^2-\delta A\ge-\epsilon I.
\]

For every `lambda in sigma(A)`,

\[
\lambda^2-\delta\lambda+\epsilon\ge0.
\]

If `epsilon<delta^2/4`, the roots are

\[
r_\pm
=\frac{\delta\pm\sqrt{\delta^2-4\epsilon}}2.
\]
