# 3. Mandatory spectral-stability seed

Let `A>=0` in a unital C*-algebra and suppose

\[
\sigma(A)\subset\{0\}\cup[\delta,M],\qquad \delta>0.
\]

Since `0` is isolated, the function on the spectrum equal to `1` at zero and `0` on `[delta,M]` is continuous. Hence continuous functional calculus gives

\[
P_A=\mathbf1_{\{0\}}(A)\in C^*(A,1).
\]

For `t>0`, the function `e^{-t\lambda}-1_{\{0\}}(\lambda)` vanishes at zero and has absolute value at most `e^{-t\delta}` on the positive spectrum. Therefore

\[
\boxed{
\|e^{-tA}-P_A\|\le e^{-t\delta}.
}
\tag{3.1}
\]

Similarly, at zero

\[
\eta(0+\eta)^{-1}-1=0,
\]

while for `lambda>=delta`,

\[
0\le\frac{\eta}{\lambda+\eta}\le\frac{\eta}{\delta+\eta}.
\]

Thus

\[
\boxed{
\|\eta(A+\eta I)^{-1}-P_A\|
\le\frac{\eta}{\delta+\eta}.
}
\tag{3.2}
\]

Now let positive `A,B` both have spectra in `{0} union [delta,infinity)`. Their Moore–Penrose inverses lie in the generated C*-algebras and satisfy

\[
\|A^\dagger\|,\|B^\dagger\|\le\delta^{-1}.
\]

Since `BP_B=0`,

\[
(1-P_A)P_B=A^\dagger A P_B
=A^\dagger(A-B)P_B,
\]

and hence

\[
\|(1-P_A)P_B\|\le\frac{\|A-B\|}{\delta}.
\]

Interchanging `A,B` gives the symmetric estimate. Therefore

\[
\boxed{
\|P_A-P_B\|
\le\frac{2\|A-B\|}{\delta}.
}
\tag{3.3}
\]

The standard two-projection identity yields the previously retained sharper factor `1`, but (3.3) is the sufficient estimate requested by the program.

The common-gap assumption is essential. If `A` has nonzero kernel and positive spectrum bounded below by `delta`, then

\[
B=A+\varepsilon I
\]

satisfies `||A-B||=epsilon->0` but `P_B=0` while `P_A!=0`; the positive gap of `B` above zero has shrunk to `epsilon` and there is no common uniform gap. This seed is therefore a **sufficient norm-plus-uniform-gap hypothesis**, not a proof that a family of group approximants possesses such a gap.

# 5. Proof of the new height-boundary theorem

## 5.1 Construction of the central-slice map

The stable-letter exponent gives the retained height map on the Rips complex. The overlap `X_D^0` contains the height-zero slice.

The subgroup `C=<a>` is contained in `H`, and the word metric on its `a`-cycle embeds with the same edge lengths into the ambient Cayley metric. Therefore the map

\[
H\times_C P_D C\to X_D^0,
\qquad [h,c]\mapsto hc,
\]

is well-defined, `H`-equivariant, and simplicial. Denote it by `j_D`.

No orbit-quotient simplicial shortcut is used here: `j_D` is defined before taking any quotient, on a genuine induced `H`-simplicial complex.

## 5.2 Mackey reduction of the torus source

The Bott source `z_{2,L}` was constructed from the torus subgroup

\[
A=C\times\langle t\rangle
\]

as the induced external product of the cyclic class with the line/Bott class. The group decomposition has a single Mackey double coset:

\[
\Gamma=HA,
\qquad H\cap A=C.
\]

Therefore restricting the induced torus source from `Gamma` to `H` reduces to induction from `C` to `H`.

On the line factor, the Mayer–Vietoris boundary for the two half-lines sends the one-dimensional Dirac/Bott generator `eta` to the point class:

\[
\partial_{\mathbb R}(\eta)=\pm[\mathrm{pt}].
\tag{5.1}
\]

External-product naturality and induction compatibility therefore give at the initial controlled scale

\[
\partial\operatorname{Res}_H^\Gamma(z_{2,L})
=\pm\operatorname{Ind}_C^H(x_{L,1})
\]

on the central height slice.

Now apply naturality under the Rips inclusion `P_2Gamma -> P_DGamma`. The cyclic factor changes exactly by

\[
x_{L,1}\mapsto x_{L,D}=q^C_{1,D,*}(x_{L,1}),
\]

while the central-slice inclusion becomes `j_D`. Hence

\[
\boxed{
\delta_D
=\pm(j_D)_*\operatorname{Ind}_C^H(x_{L,D}).
}
\tag{5.2}
\]

This proves `P01-P53A-CENTRAL-SLICE-BOUNDARY-FACTOR`.

## 5.3 Exact cyclic upper bound

By definition of `kappa_L`,

\[
x_{L,\kappa_L}=0.
\]

Naturality in the directed Rips system implies `x_{L,D}=0` for every `D>=kappa_L`. Substituting into (5.2),

\[
\delta_D=0\qquad(D\ge\kappa_L).

Therefore

\[
\boxed{\Theta_L^\partial\le\kappa_L.}
\tag{5.3}

## 5.4 Arithmetic lower bound from exact freeness

\]

Therefore for every integer `2<=D<tau_L`, the restricted `H`-action on the overlap `X_D^0` is free. Put

Then the quotient map is a principal `H`-bundle, classified by

On the central induced slice `H x_C P_D C`, this is exactly the classifying map of the cyclic principal bundle. Consequently (5.2) maps to the retained nonzero cyclic class

\boxed{
