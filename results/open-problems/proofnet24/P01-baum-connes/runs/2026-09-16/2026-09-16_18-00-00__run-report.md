## 7.1 Independent derivation of the conservation identity

Let

\[
x_k=E_\sigma\sum_{t=0}^{T-1}\mathbf1_{\{X_t=k\}},
\qquad
\tau_j=P_\sigma(X_T=j),
\qquad
s=E_\sigma T.
\]

For each `j`, since `{T>t}` is measurable at time `t`,

\[
\begin{aligned}
(xP)_j
&=\sum_kx_kP_{kj}\\
&=E_\sigma\sum_{t<T}P(X_{t+1}=j\mid\mathcal F_t)\\
&=E_\sigma\sum_{t<T}\mathbf1_{\{X_{t+1}=j\}}.
\end{aligned}
\]

Comparing times `1,...,T` with `0,...,T-1` gives

\[
(xP)_j=x_j-\sigma_j+\tau_j,
\]

hence

\[
\boxed{x(I-P)=\sigma-\tau.}
\tag{7.2}
\]

For the fundamental matrix `Z`,

\[
(I-P)Z=I-\Pi,
\qquad \Pi=\mathbf1\pi^T.
\]

Multiplying (7.2) by `Z` and using `x\mathbf1=s` gives

\[
\boxed{x=\sigma Z-\tau Z+s\pi^T.}
\tag{7.3}
\]

If `supp tau subseteq A` and `c_a=x_a` for `a in A`, then for every `a in A`,

\[
\sum_{b\in A}Z_{ba}\tau_b-\pi_as=z_{\sigma a}-c_a,
\]

while `sum_{b in A}tau_b=1`. This is exactly (7.1) with P02's

\[
M_A=
\begin{pmatrix}
Z_A^T&-\pi_A\\
\mathbf1^T&0

So the displayed boundary system is correct.

## 7.2 Independent derivation of invertibility

Assume

\[
M_A\binom{u}{t}=0.
\tag{7.4}
\]

Then

\[
\sum_{b\in A}Z_{ba}u_b=\pi_at\quad(a\in A),
\qquad
\sum_{b\in A}u_b=0.
\tag{7.5}
\]

Define on the full state space

\[
d_b=\begin{cases}u_b/\pi_b,&b\in A,\\0,&b\notin A.\end{cases}
\]

Then

\[
\pi^Td=\sum_{b\in A}u_b=0.
\tag{7.6}
\]

For the reversed chain

\[
\widehat P_{ab}=\frac{\pi_bP_{ba}}{\pi_a},
\qquad
\widehat Z=D_\pi^{-1}Z^TD_\pi,
\]

one has

\[
(\widehat Zd)_a
=\frac1{\pi_a}\sum_{b\in A}Z_{ba}u_b
=t
\qquad(a\in A).
\tag{7.7}
\]

Set

\[
f=\widehat Zd-t\mathbf1.
\]

By (7.7), `f=0` on `A`. Also

\[
(I-\widehat P)\widehat Z=I-\widehat\Pi,
\]

and (7.6) gives `widehat Pi d=0`, so

\[
(I-\widehat P)f=d.
\tag{7.8}
\]

But `d=0` on `A^c`. Therefore `f` is `widehat P`-harmonic on `A^c` and zero on `A`. A finite irreducible Markov chain hits every nonempty set almost surely. Applying optional stopping to the bounded harmonic function `f` at the first hit of `A` yields

\[
f(v)=E_vf(X_{T_A})=0
\]

for every `v notin A`. Thus `f=0` everywhere. Equation (7.8) gives `d=0`, hence `u=0`; then (7.7) gives `t=0`.

So `M_A` has trivial kernel:

\[
\boxed{\det M_A\ne0.}
