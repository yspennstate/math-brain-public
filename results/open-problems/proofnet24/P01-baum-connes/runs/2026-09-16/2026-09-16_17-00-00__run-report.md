# 6. Directed peer audit: P02 Schur/portmanteau bridge

Claim audited:

P02 defines, for a nonempty target set `A`,

\[
M_A=
\begin{pmatrix}
Z_A^T&-\pi_A\\
\mathbf 1^T&0
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
\Delta_A=\det M_A.

Choose an anchor `a in A` and set `S=A\setminus\{a\}`. Let

\[
F^a_{ij}
=E_i\sum_{t<T_a}\mathbf1_{\{X_t=j\}}
\]

be the killed Green matrix for the singleton target `{a}`. The standard singleton occupation identity gives

F^a_{ij}
=Z_{ij}-Z_{aj}
+\frac{\pi_j}{\pi_a}(Z_{aa}-Z_{ia}).

\tag{6.1}

I now rederive the two determinant formulas independently.

## 6.1 Denominator identity

Order `A=(a,S)`. For every row indexed by `s in S`, replace

\[
R_s\leftarrow R_s-\frac{\pi_s}{\pi_a}R_a.
\]

This zeros the last column of those rows. Expanding `det M_A` along the last column leaves a factor `pi_a` and, up to the sign `(-1)^{|S|}`, the determinant of the matrix with rows `S` plus the bottom row and columns `a,S`.

Next, for every `t in S`, replace

\[
C_t\leftarrow C_t-C_a.
\]

For `s,t in S`, the resulting entry is

\[
Z_{ts}-\frac{\pi_s}{\pi_a}Z_{ta}
-Z_{as}+\frac{\pi_s}{\pi_a}Z_{aa}
=F^a_{ts}
\]

by `(6.1)`. The bottom row becomes `(1,0,...,0)`. Expanding along that row contributes the second sign `(-1)^{|S|}`, which cancels the first. Therefore

\[
\boxed{
\Delta_A=\pi_a\det F^a_{SS}.
}
\tag{6.2}
\]

This also proves that the sign in P02's formula is correct.

## 6.2 Bordered numerator identity

For `i,j notin A`, let

\[
K_{A;i,j}
=
\begin{pmatrix}
M_A&b_i\\
r_j^T&Z_{ij}
\end{pmatrix},
\]

with

\[
b_i=(Z_{iA}^T,1)^T,
\qquad
r_j^T=(Z_{Aj}^T,-\pi_j).
\]

Apply the same row operations as above to every `s in S`, and additionally to the final `j`-row:

\[
R_j\leftarrow R_j-\frac{\pi_j}{\pi_a}R_a.
\]

Again the stationary column is zero except in the anchor row. Expand along that column.

Now subtract the anchor column from every `S`-column and also from the final `i`-column. The `S x S` block becomes `(F^a_{SS})^T`; the final column in an `s`-row becomes `F^a_{is}`; the final `j`-row gives `F^a_{tj}` in the `t`-column and `F^a_{ij}` in the final column. The bottom row is `(1,0,...,0)`.

Expanding along that bottom row again cancels the first sign and yields

\[
\boxed{
\det K_{A;i,j}
=
\pi_a
\det
\begin{pmatrix}
F^a_{SS}&F^a_{Sj}\\
F^a_{iS}&F^a_{ij}
\end{pmatrix}.
}
\tag{6.3}
\]

The transpose introduced by our row/column ordering does not change the determinant.

## 6.3 Schur complement conclusion

The full singleton killed Green matrix is the inverse of the transient nonsingular M-matrix `I-P_{V\setminus\{a\}}`. By Jacobi's complementary-minor identity, every principal minor `det F^a_{SS}` is nonzero. Hence `(6.2)` has nonzero denominator and block determinant expansion of `(6.3)` gives

\[
\frac{\det K_{A;i,j}}{\Delta_A}
=F^a_{ij}-F^a_{iS}(F^a_{SS})^{-1}F^a_{Sj}.
\]

This is exactly the Schur-complement update for killing the enlarged target `{a} union S=A`.
