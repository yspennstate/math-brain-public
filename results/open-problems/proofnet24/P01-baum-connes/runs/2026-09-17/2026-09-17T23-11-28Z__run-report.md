## 1. Idea before code: a scalar clopen-transposition unitary in the rational group ring

P86 used a \(2\times2\) projection splice. P88 asks whether one can break symmetry enough to obtain a **scalar** unitary already in the finite-support rational group ring \(\mathbb QG\), while keeping exact control of its free quotient.

Let \(a_x\in B\) denote the nontrivial order-two lamp at position \(x\in F_2\). Put
\[
p_x=\frac{1+u_{a_x}}2,
\qquad
q_x=\frac{1-u_{a_x}}2.
\]
These are commuting complementary spectral projections for the lamp involutions. Fix \(s=s_1\), and deliberately choose the asymmetric cylinder projection
\[
\boxed{p=p_e q_s.}
\]
Conjugating by \(u_s\) gives
\[
r=u_s p u_s^*=p_s q_{s^2}.
\]
The asymmetry is the key: \(p\) contains \(q_s\), while \(r\) contains \(p_s\). Hence
\[
pr=rp=0.
\]
Now set
\[
v=u_s p.
\]
Then
\[
v^*v=p,
\qquad
vv^*=r.
\]
So \(v\) is a finite-support partial isometry carrying the lamp cylinder \(p\) onto its disjoint translate \(r\).

Define
\[
\boxed{
W=v+v^*+(1-p-r).
}
\]
This is the scalar crossed-product analogue of a transposition of two disjoint clopen pieces. The mechanism is standard partial-isometry/full-group technology; no novelty is claimed for that mechanism. The point here is the concrete finite-support specialization inside the frozen wreath-product group ring and its use as an explicit Baum–Connes representative.

## 2. Full proof that \(W\) is a finite-support null-homotopic unitary with trivial free quotient

Because \(p,r\) are orthogonal projections and \(v^*v=p\), \(vv^*=r\), we have
\[
pv^*=v^*,\quad rv=v,\quad
vp=v,\quad v^*r=v^*,
\]
while all products of \(v\) or \(v^*\) with \(1-p-r\) vanish. Moreover
\[
v^2=u_s p u_s p
   =u_s p (u_s p u_s^*)u_s
   =u_s(pr)u_s=0,
\]
and similarly \((v^*)^2=0\). Therefore
\[
\begin{aligned}
W^2
 &=vv^*+v^*v+(1-p-r)^2\\
 &=r+p+(1-p-r)\\
 &=1.
\end{aligned}
\]
Also \(W^*=W\). Thus
\[
\boxed{W\in \mathbb QG\subset C_r^*(G)\text{ is a selfadjoint unitary}.}
\]
It has finite support because \(p,r,v\) are finite linear combinations of group elements.

Every selfadjoint unitary is null-homotopic in the unitary group of the containing unital \(C^*\)-algebra: with
\[
P=\frac{1-W}{2}
\]
a projection,
\[
W=e^{i\pi P},
\]
and
\[
t\longmapsto e^{i\pi tP},\qquad0\le t\le1,
\]
joins \(1\) to \(W\). Consequently
\[
\boxed{[W]=0\in K_1(C_r^*(G)).}
\]

Now apply the group quotient \(\pi:G\to F_2\), which kills every lamp. Under the induced reduced \(C^*\)-quotient \(q\),
\[
q(p_e)=1,
\qquad
q(q_s)=0,
\]
so
\[
q(p)=q(r)=q(v)=0.
\]
Hence
\[
\boxed{q(W)=1.}
\]
Thus the lamp transposition is completely invisible in the free quotient, in exact agreement with P87’s relative-kernel screen.

## 3. One further explicit assembly-accounted class for the frozen group and degree

Choose the reduced free word
\[
w=s_1^2s_2^{-1}s_1^4s_2^{-4}.
\]
Its exponent-sum vector is
\[
\operatorname{ab}(w)=(6,-5).
\]
Define the **scalar finite-support unitary**
\[
\boxed{U=u_wW\in \mathbb QG.}
\]
Because \(u_w\) and \(W\) are unitaries, \(U\) is a unitary. Its quotient is
\[
q(U)=u_wq(W)=u_w.
\]
Since \([W]=0\),
\[
[U]=[u_w]
   =6[u_{s_1}]-5[u_{s_2}].
\]
Pooya explicitly computes the topological and analytic \(K\)-groups for \(F\wr F_n\) and identifies the degree-one assembly generators with the free-group generator unitaries. Writing \(v_1,v_2\) for the corresponding topological generators for the frozen \(G=C_2\wr F_2\), we obtain
\[
\boxed{
[U]=6[u_{s_1}]-5[u_{s_2}]
   =\mu_1^G(6v_1-5v_2).
}
\]
This explicitly decides assembly membership of a further class for the **same group and the same degree**.
