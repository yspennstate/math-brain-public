# 4. Move 18 — rewrite the instruction covariance as Dirichlet energy

The exact source location is Proposition 8.3, Step 3. For an instruction based at `y`, after normalizing by the conditional probabilities in the process with the particle deleted, the source obtains values

\[
1-\alpha_v,\qquad 1-\beta_v,\qquad v\sim y,
\]

with only

\[
0\le\alpha_v\le q_x(v),\qquad 0\le\beta_v\le q_z(v).
\tag{4.1}
\]

Writing `m=2d`, `\bar\alpha=m^{-1}\sum\alpha_v` and similarly for `beta`, the factor is controlled by

\[
\frac1m\sum_{v\sim y}\alpha_v\beta_v-\bar\alpha\bar\beta.
\tag{4.2}
\]

For arbitrary real arrays on a finite set of size `m`,

\[
\boxed{
\frac1m\sum_v\alpha_v\beta_v-\bar\alpha\bar\beta
=
\frac1{2m^2}\sum_{v,w}(\alpha_v-\alpha_w)(\beta_v-\beta_w).
}
\tag{4.3}
\]

This is the exact algebraic cancellation that the previous I0005 report was looking for. It changes the natural language from “positive overlap” to a carré-du-champ / Dirichlet-energy quantity.

## 4.1 What happens for the Green envelopes themselves

Let

\[
q_x(y)=G(y-x)/g,\qquad g=G(0),
\]

for transient simple random walk. The Green equation is

\[
(I-P)q_z=\delta_z/g.
\]

For the harmonic envelopes, the signed global gradient identity is

\[
\boxed{
\sum_y\frac1m\sum_{v\sim y}
(q_x(v)-q_x(y))(q_z(v)-q_z(y))
=
\frac{2G(z-x)}{g^2}.
}
\tag{4.4}
\]

Indeed, the left side is `2 <q_x,(I-P)q_z>` by reversibility of counting measure.

In `d=4`, the standard Green asymptotic gives the neighbor-gradient bound

\[
|q_x(v)-q_x(w)|\le C(1+|y-x|)^{-3}
\tag{4.5}
\]

for neighbors `v,w` of `y`, after absorbing finitely many near-target cases into `C`. Hence

\[
\sum_y (1+|y-x|)^{-3}(1+|y-z|)^{-3}
\le C(1+|x-z|)^{-2}.
\tag{4.6}
\]

The proof is the usual three-region split: within half the separation of `x`, within half the separation of `z`, and dyadic shells outside both balls. Therefore an instruction covariance with genuine neighbor-difference control would have exactly the decay missing from the positive Green-product route.

This Dirichlet-energy mechanism is classical potential theory; no novelty claim is made for (4.3)–(4.6). Its value here is diagnostic: it identifies the precise structural estimate that would make the source architecture four-dimensional.
