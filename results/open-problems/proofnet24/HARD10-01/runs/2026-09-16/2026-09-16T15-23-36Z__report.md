## 2. Original numbered locator recovered

The campaign alias `Grimmett Conjecture 4.2` is the 2022/2023 re-statement in Geoffrey Grimmett's *Selected Problems in Probability Theory*. The original source locator is:

> **Grimmett--Winkler, Conjecture 1.1 (2003/2004), uniform-forest half.** For every finite graph, the uniform forest is edge-negatively-associated.

The paper defines edge-negative association for distinct edges `e,f` by

\[
\Pr(e,f\in F)\le \Pr(e\in F)\Pr(f\in F),
\]

where `F` is uniform over all acyclic edge subsets. The paper assumes simple graphs in its finite-graph encoding and separately states a stronger general negative-association notion. Thus the target here is the pairwise edge statement, not full negative association.

Primary source checked 2026-09-16:

* G. R. Grimmett and S. N. Winkler, arXiv:math/0302185 (submitted 17 February 2003), *Random Structures & Algorithms* 24 (2004), 444--460, DOI `10.1002/rsa.20012`.
* On paper page 2, Conjecture 1.1 is the original numbered locator.
* On paper page 3, Theorem 1.5 proves by direct numerical calculation that the forest statement holds for all graphs with at most 8 vertices and for 9-vertex graphs with at most 18 edges.
* The same page then introduces weighted extensions, beginning with equation (1.6). Those weighted questions are stronger than Conjecture 1.1 and are not silently substituted for the unit-weight target.

## 5. Refutation-first exact search

The run first tried to refute the conjecture in the entire unresolved nine-vertex sector. No negative Rayleigh numerator was found.

For a graph `G`, write

\[
Z=\#\{\text{forests of }G\},\quad
Z_e=\#\{F:e\in F\},\quad
Z_{ef}=\#\{F:e,f\in F\}.
\]

The source inequality is exactly

\[
\Delta_G(e,f)=Z_eZ_f-ZZ_{ef}\ge0.
\]

The executed checker enumerated a complete isomorphism-class census and then tested every distinct present edge pair in every class with 19--36 edges.

### 5.2 Exact forest arithmetic

For every induced vertex set `T`, the checker computes the spanning-tree count by the Matrix--Tree theorem. It also computes tree counts conditioned on one or two distinguished edges from exact transfer-current determinants. Reduced-Laplacian inversion is done modulo

\[
p=1,000,000,007.
\]

This is exact, not probabilistic: every induced spanning-tree count is at most

\[
\tau(K_9)=9^7=4,782,969<p,
\]

and every one-/two-edge-conditioned tree count lies between 0 and the total tree count. The canonical residue is therefore the unique true integer.

All-forest counts are recovered by the exact component recurrence that chooses the component containing the least vertex. `Z_e` and `Z_{ef}` are then assembled over same-component and, for endpoint-disjoint `e,f`, different-component cases. Final products use signed 128-bit integer arithmetic; every forest count is below `2^36`.
