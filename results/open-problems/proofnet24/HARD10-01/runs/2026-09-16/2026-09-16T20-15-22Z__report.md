## 2. Exact original source locator recovered again

The original source is not campaign `Conjecture 4.2`; that is a later Grimmett locator. In the 2003/2004 Grimmett--Winkler paper, the source statement is the **forest half of Conjecture 1.1**.

The paper fixes a finite labelled simple graph `G=(V,E)` with neither loops nor multiple edges. It defines `F` to be the set of **all subsets of `E` containing no cycle**. All subgraphs retain the full vertex set `V`, so isolated vertices are included. A uniform forest is a uniformly chosen element of this full acyclic-edge-subset family.

Conjecture 1.1 states that, for every finite graph, the uniform forest and the uniform connected subgraph are edge-negatively associated. The forest half is therefore exactly

\[
\Pr(e,f\in F)\le \Pr(e\in F)\Pr(f\in F),\qquad e\ne f.
\tag{2.1}
\]

The paper then rewrites the forest half as the corresponding exact count inequality. Its Theorem 1.5 proves the forest statement for every graph with at most eight vertices and every nine-vertex graph with at most eighteen edges.

The inherited HARD10 dense-sector exact certificate covers the complementary nine-vertex sector `19..36` edges: all `120,314` unlabeled classes in that sector and all `25,304,635` present-edge pairs passed exact integer checking. Hence the frozen all-graph metric remains exactly

\[
\boxed{9}.
\tag{2.2}
\]

The published `n<=8` result is prior art, not a new campaign result; the new campaign contribution remains only the exact completion of the previously uncovered dense nine-vertex sector.

## 3. Source reconciliation: restore the fixed-graph Cocks implication

### 3.1 Why the predecessor suspended it

The preceding HARD10 report found an accessible proof in Erickson's thesis of the **class-level** implication

\[
[\text{every graph satisfies unit forest NC}]
\Longrightarrow
[\text{every graphic matroid is independence correlated}],
\tag{3.1}
\]

implemented by graph-expanding gadgets for rational weights and a limiting argument. That proof does not preserve the original graph's vertex count. On that basis the predecessor suspended a stronger graph-local inference.

That was a correct reason not to infer more from Erickson alone, but it does not settle what Cocks proved.

### 3.2 The final Semple--Welsh paper resolves the ambiguity

Semple--Welsh define a matroid `M` to be **independence correlated** when, for every positive real edge weighting `y` and every distinct `e,f`, its independent-set polynomial satisfies

\[
I_e(M;y)I_f(M;y)-I_{ef}(M;y)I(M;y)\ge0.
\tag{3.2}
\]

For a graphic matroid, independent sets are exactly forests, so this is the all-positive-edge-weight forest Rayleigh inequality.

Their Section 5 asks, as open Question (3):

> Does there exist a graph `G` that satisfies Conjecture 1.1, but `M(G)` is not independence correlated?

The final published note then states that Cocks showed:

> if Conjecture 1.1 holds for a graph `G`, then `M(G)` is independence correlated,

and says this settles Question (3).

Because Question (3) is explicitly **fixed graph `G`**, the cited Cocks result is graph-local, not merely the class-level statement (3.1). Cocks's published abstract independently says that the Grimmett--Winkler conjecture is equivalent to statements about correlated graphic matroids and that correlated matroids are closed under 2-sum.

I therefore restore, as a **published dependency attributed to Cocks via the final Semple--Welsh note**, the implication

\[
\boxed{
G\text{ satisfies the unit forest pair inequalities for all pairs}
\Longrightarrow
M(G)\text{ is independence correlated for all positive edge weights}.}
\tag{3.3}
\]

This is not an automatic equivalence from definitions and is not being re-proved here. No theorem number from the inaccessible body of Cocks's paper is invented.

Since the all-positive-weight property includes the unit point, (3.3) makes the two properties equivalent **for each fixed graphic forest problem**, by a nontrivial theorem. This does not contradict the campaign warning that unit negative correlation is not *automatically* the stronger Rayleigh statement.

### 3.3 Consequence for the finite campaign

## 7. New exploratory representation of the singleton star — exact two-point triangle quadrature

This subsection is an **exploratory route**, not a promoted closure theorem.

Delete the degree-three vertex `w` and call the remaining nine-vertex graph `A`, with terminals `x,y,z`. For an `A`-forest, record the partition it induces on the three terminals:

- `d`: all three terminals separate;
- `a,b,c`: exactly one terminal pair connected;
- `t`: all three connected.

Adding the unit three-spoke star at `w` has exactly

\[
(8,6,6,6,4)
\tag{7.1}
\]

admissible star-edge subsets in these five states, respectively. This is direct: with three terminal components every subset of the three spokes is acyclic; with exactly two terminal components exactly two spoke subsets create a cycle; with one terminal component only the empty set and the three singleton spokes are allowed.

Now instead add a triangle on `x,y,z` with all three triangle edges assigned common positive weight `s`. After summing over the triangle-edge forest states, the five transfer multipliers are

\[
m(s)=\bigl(1+3s+3s^2,\ 1+2s,\ 1+2s,\ 1+2s,\ 1\bigr).
\tag{7.2}
\]

Let

\[
s_- = \frac{3-\sqrt3}{12},\qquad
s_+ = \frac{3+\sqrt3}{12}.
\tag{7.3}
\]

They satisfy

\[
s_-+s_+=\frac12,
\qquad
s_-^2+s_+^2=\frac16.
\tag{7.4}
\]

Substitution into (7.2) gives the exact identity

\[
\boxed{(8,6,6,6,4)=2m(s_-)+2m(s_+).}
\tag{7.5}
\]

Consequently, for every constraint `C` involving only physical edges of `A` (in particular `C=empty,{e},{f},{e,f}`), the constrained forest count in the original star graph obeys

\[
\boxed{
Z_C(G)=2Z_C(A+K_3(s_-))+2Z_C(A+K_3(s_+)),
}
\tag{7.6}
\]

where `K_3(s)` denotes the terminal triangle with all three virtual edges of weight `s`.

This is a two-node positive quadrature representation of the degree-three star transfer by **two nine-vertex all-positive-weight problems**, each individually covered by the Cocks upgrade of the complete order-nine unit certificate.

### Why this does not yet prove the star case

Negative correlation is not preserved by an arbitrary positive mixture. Let `mu_-` and `mu_+` be the two normalized physical-edge marginals arising from the two triangle fields, and let `lambda` be the normalization-dependent mixture coefficient. The law of total covariance gives exactly

\[
\operatorname{Cov}_{\rm star}(e,f)
=
(1-\lambda)\operatorname{Cov}_{-}(e,f)
+\lambda\operatorname{Cov}_{+}(e,f)
+\lambda(1-\lambda)
\bigl(p_e^- - p_e^+\bigr)
\bigl(p_f^- - p_f^+\bigr).
\tag{7.7}
\]

The first two terms are nonpositive by the all-positive-weight order-nine theorem. The last environmental term has no automatic favorable sign.

In fact the all-weight theorem shows why the obstacle is genuine rather than cosmetic. Along the symmetric triangle field,

\[
\frac{d}{ds}p_e(s)
=
\frac1s\sum_{g\in E(K_3)}\operatorname{Cov}_s(1_e,1_g)
\le0,
\tag{7.8}
\]

because every physical/virtual edge pair in the nine-vertex weighted graph is negatively correlated. Thus both `p_e(s)` and `p_f(s)` are nonincreasing, so

\[
(p_e^- - p_e^+)(p_f^- - p_f^+)\ge0.
\tag{7.9}
\]

The mixture creates exactly the **positive environmental covariance** that has to be quantitatively absorbed by the negative conditional covariances. This is the same obstruction seen algebraically in the mixed coefficient `q1` of (6.2), now expressed using only two explicit positive triangle fields.

The useful next inequality is therefore not “each endpoint measure is Rayleigh” — that is already known — but the quantitative bridge

\[
\lambda(1-\lambda)
(p_e^- - p_e^+)(p_f^- - p_f^+)
\le
-(1-\lambda)\operatorname{Cov}_{-}(e,f)
-\lambda\operatorname{Cov}_{+}(e,f).
\tag{7.10}
\]

A proof of (7.10) for every nine-vertex `A` would close the degree-three singleton three-cut case. A violation would kill this quadrature route without by itself refuting the source conjecture unless the resulting physical ten-vertex graph also has a negative unit pair.

No code was written for (7.5): it is exact two-line algebra and does not warrant another execution artifact before a nontrivial inequality has been found.
