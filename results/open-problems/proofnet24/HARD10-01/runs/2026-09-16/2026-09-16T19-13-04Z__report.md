## 2. Source and current-literature check, dated 2026-09-16

### Original source and locator

Primary source rechecked:

G. R. Grimmett and S. N. Winkler, *Negative association in uniform forests and connected graphs*, arXiv:`math/0302185`, *Random Structures & Algorithms* 24 (2004), 444--460, DOI `10.1002/rsa.20012`.

The source explicitly assumes finite graphs with neither loops nor multiple edges. Its `F` is the family of **all acyclic edge subsets**; every subgraph has the full vertex set, so isolated vertices are allowed. The original numbered locator behind the later campaign alias is **Conjecture 1.1**, whose forest half asks whether a uniform forest is edge-negatively-associated:

\[
\Pr(e,f\in F)\le \Pr(e\in F)\Pr(f\in F),\qquad e\ne f.
\]

Theorem 1.5 gives the published baseline: all graphs on at most eight vertices, and all nine-vertex graphs having at most eighteen edges. The source separately formulates stronger weighted variants in (1.6)--(1.7); those are not silently substituted for the frozen unit-weight metric.

The repository's exact complementary dense-sector certificate covers the remaining simple nine-vertex graphs, so the inherited frozen metric remains exactly `9`.

## 3. Critical correction: the preceding graph-local Cocks inference is not secure

The preceding HARD10 report promoted the following graph-local implication as a published theorem:

> if a fixed graph `G` satisfies the unit-weight Grimmett--Winkler inequality for every pair, then the same `G` is independence correlated for every positive edge weighting.

It then combined that with P14's two-cut identity to claim that a vertex-minimal counterexample must be 3-connected and that the ten-vertex search could be restricted unconditionally to the `5,114,079` unlabeled 3-connected classes.

That promotion is **suspended from the current cumulative chain**. I do not use it in this run and it must not be treated as a certified reduction until the exact Cocks statement is recovered from the body of his paper or independently reproved with the same finite-size scope.

Why the correction is necessary:

1. The final note of Semple--Welsh literally says that Cocks showed that if Conjecture 1.1 holds for a graph `G`, then `M(G)` is independence correlated. That sentence is what the preceding HARD10 report relied on.

2. However, the accessible proof in Alejandro Erickson's 2008 thesis states a **class-level** result, not a fixed-graph result. In Chapter 5.2 he constructs, from a weighted graphic matroid `M` and a rational weight vector, a **larger graphic matroid** `M[Q^{E-{e,f}}]` by two-summing explicit path gadgets. Proposition 5.2.2 says that if all those enlarged matroids are unit I-negatively correlated, then `M` is I-Rayleigh. Corollary 5.2.3 is:

   `Graphs are all I-negatively correlated if and only if they are all I-Rayleigh.`

   The construction does not preserve the vertex order of the original graph.

3. Erickson's later published exposition describes the Cocks/Erickson conclusion in the same class-level form: **if all graphs** satisfy the unit condition, then they are all I-Rayleigh.

4. Oxley's current textbook formulation likewise states that Cocks proved the **class of graphic matroids** is independence-correlated iff the unit conjecture holds for **all graphs**.

5. Cocks's abstract says the Grimmett--Winkler conjecture is equivalent to a statement about correlated graphic matroids and proves 2-sum closure, but the article body was not openly accessible in this run. Therefore I cannot use the ambiguous Semple--Welsh endnote to justify a finite-order-preserving graph-local theorem against the explicit larger-graph construction in Erickson.

Accordingly, the following predecessor labels are **RETRACTED / NOT LOAD-BEARING pending direct-source resolution**:

- `HARD10-COCKS-FINITE-UPGRADE-61` as a fixed-graph finite-order implication;
- the deduction `every simple graph on <=9 vertices is independence correlated` from the unit `n<=9` certificate alone;
- `HARD10-MINIMAL-3CONNECTED-61` insofar as it depended on that fixed-graph upgrade;
- the unconditional restriction of the complete ten-vertex search to exactly `5,114,079` 3-connected classes.

This is a scope correction, not a claim that a graph-local implication is false. The exact issue is that the accessible proof/equivalence does not preserve graph order, and the directly inspected Cocks body needed to settle the wording conflict was unavailable.

The frozen source metric remains `9` either way.
