# 4. B22 — READ THE FAILURES: the first broken line in each major technology

## 4.1 Information Percolation

Lubetzky--Sly's arbitrary-geometry theorem succeeds at sufficiently high temperature by making the backward update-history clusters subcritical enough to obtain strong exponential control. Salez's current notes explicitly say the same framework reaches other geometries only under a strong decoupling assumption valid at high enough temperature, and call overcoming this an intrinsic limitation.

**First broken line beyond high temperature:** tree uniqueness contracts **cavity/message influence**, but it need not make the raw all-neighbor backward dependency cluster subcritical. Counting ancestors without weights loses exactly the cancellation/attenuation that uniqueness sees.

## 4.2 Equilibrium spectral independence / entropy factorization

These methods control the equilibrium Gibbs law and its conditioned/pinned Gibbs descendants, and thereby prove optimal rapid mixing far into uniqueness regimes.

**First broken line out of equilibrium:** `mu_t^x` is not a Gibbs measure for the same local specification. Worse, its conditioned laws do not evolve as independent copies of the original Glauber chain: the previously derived pinning equation has boundary injection from neighboring pinning sectors. Therefore an equilibrium influence-matrix bound has no direct propagation rule for the entire dynamic pinning hierarchy.

## 4.3 Pedrotti--Salez evolving-reference chi-square method

This is the closest general cutoff mechanism. In the proof of their Lemma 1, after the variance identity, the key estimate explicitly inserts `gamma(mu P_s)` and then applies Gronwall. In other words, the evolving-reference proof **consumes** a lower bound on the non-equilibrium Poincare constants; it does not produce that lower bound from equilibrium rapid mixing.

Their positive-curvature estimate supplies such a lower bound in the perturbative regime. Their own Remark 1 says it becomes useless in negative curvature and proposes a relaxed `psi(t) <= C exp(-2 kappa t)` route as future work. Thus the literature itself points to the same missing bridge rather than silently resolving it.

## 4.4 Absolute-value influence closure

The exact covariance/score equations can be closed by absolute values only under Dobrushin-type contraction. **First broken line:** taking absolute values destroys the nonbacktracking/cavity cancellations that separate the tree-uniqueness threshold from the smaller Dobrushin/high-temperature region.

**B22 outcome:** all four routes fail at the same structural location: they do not transfer a tree-uniqueness **equilibrium message contraction** into a **uniform non-equilibrium functional inequality for every relevant time marginal and start**.

# 5. B21 — ASK WHO ELSE HAS THIS SHAPE

Three neighboring languages expose what a successful proof would have to add.

### 5.1 Strong data processing / filtering

`mu_t^x` is the visible marginal of the graphical Glauber construction. The conditional probability of a spin given the rest can be regarded as a posterior quantity in a hidden space-time factor graph. Dynamic approximate tensorization would follow from a sufficiently uniform strong-data-processing statement for those posterior channels. Equilibrium entropy factorization is not such a statement because the input is a deterministic state pushed through an interacting channel.

### 5.2 Tree cavity and nonbacktracking message contraction

On a tree, uniqueness is naturally expressed through contraction of directed cavity messages along nonbacktracking branches. This is strictly more refined than counting the number of ancestors in an Information Percolation history. The obvious porting target is therefore **weighted history**, where a backward branch carries its local message derivative rather than unit mass. In the strict uniqueness regime the weight can plausibly contract even while the unweighted history branches supercritically.

This is only a route diagnosis here, not a theorem: on graphs with cycles, histories can merge and reuse vertices, while under conditioning the weights themselves change. Those are exactly the complications absent on a finite tree.

### 5.3 Evolving-reference functional inequalities

Pedrotti--Salez shows that a non-equilibrium local Poincare bound is sufficient. The right analogue of a filtering stability theorem would be an estimate that transports a functional inequality from a simple initial law through an interacting semigroup after allowing a finite burn-in or a prefactor. Their Remark 1 (`psi(t) <= C e^{-2 kappa t}`) has exactly this flavor.

**B21 outcome:** the closest plausible bridge is not another equilibrium SI inequality. It is a **dynamic strong-data-processing / weighted-cavity inequality** for the space-time graphical channel.

# 6. B29 — ADD THE POINTS AT INFINITY: what would a bad limiting object look like?

Suppose `(BLOCK)` fails badly along a sequence, so that at times `t_n` and starts `x_n` the auxiliary gaps approach zero. By the already-proved identity `C_AT(mu)=1/gap(H_mu)`, the variational meaning is that there are normalized observables `f_n` with

\[
\operatorname{Var}_{\mu_{n,t_n}^{x_n}}(f_n)=1,
\qquad
\sum_i\mathbb E_{\mu_{n,t_n}^{x_n}}
\operatorname{Var}(f_n\mid X_{-i})\longrightarrow0.
\tag{6.1}
\]

Thus a bad point at infinity is not merely a large pairwise correlation. It is a **global latent mode** which remains almost measurable after deleting any single coordinate. A local weak limit of bounded neighborhoods could look perfectly benign while such a global near-kernel survives. This explains why local influence bounds alone need not be enough unless they are propagated uniformly through all pinnings or upgraded to a global SDPI.

A compactification based only on raw local log-density scores is also too small. Near `t=0` those scores approach boundary values corresponding to almost-deterministic coordinates, yet the auxiliary gap tends to `1`, not `0`. A useful boundary object must therefore retain at least two pieces simultaneously:

1. the directed/pinned posterior-message profile of the time marginal; and
2. the energy profile of a potential near-kernel `f_n` as in (6.1).

On an actual tree there is a canonical hierarchy of sigma-fields by subtrees, making this pair much more structured. On a loopy graph there is no such exact decomposition.

**B29 outcome:** a hypothetical counterexample to dynamic factorization would have to create a global latent mode invisible to one-site resampling while equilibrium uniqueness remains good. The cleanest place to decide whether that can happen is the finite regular tree, where cavity messages and subtree sigma-fields are exact.
