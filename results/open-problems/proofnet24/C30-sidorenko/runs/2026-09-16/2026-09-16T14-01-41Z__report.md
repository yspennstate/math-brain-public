## 4. New exact transfer-matrix identity

`T[(a,b),(b,c)] = C(a,b,c)`,

### 10.1 Source normalization

Aldous–Fill §4.3.5 defines, for a family `f=(f^(a))` of unit flows from state `a` to stationary distribution `pi`,

`psi(f)=max_edges(i,j) sum_a pi_a |f_ij^(a)|/(pi_i p_ij)`.

Open Problem 4.49(ii) asks whether

`inf_f psi(f) <= K tau_2 Delta`

can hold with a universal `K`. The source discrete relaxation time uses the second-largest eigenvalue, `tau_2=(1-lambda_2)^(-1)`; it is not the inverse absolute spectral gap.

### 10.2 Direct weak duality independently checked

For any nonnegative edge lengths `ell` with conductance budget

`sum_e c_e ell_e <= 1`,

where `c_uv=pi_u p_uv`, put `phi_a(v)=-d_ell(a,v)`. Summation by parts with

`div f^(a)=delta_a-pi`

gives

`sum_b pi_b d_ell(a,b) <= sum_e ell_e |f_e^(a)|`.

Multiplying by `pi_a`, summing in `a`, and using the defining edge bound in `psi(f)` yields

`E_{pi x pi} d_ell(X,Y) <= psi(f)`.

Thus every feasible length metric supplies a valid lower bound on `inf_f psi(f)`. The refutation does not require the stronger exact all-pairs LP duality.

### 10.3 Cone spectrum and distance certificate

Let `H` be a connected `d`-regular bipartite Ramanujan graph on `n` vertices and form the cone `G=K_1 vee H`. For simple random walk on `G`:

- `Delta(G)=2`;
- the hub mass is `beta=1/(d+2)`;
- total base mass is `alpha=(d+1)/(d+2)`;
- on a base adjacency eigenvector of eigenvalue `mu` orthogonal to constants, the cone transition eigenvalue is `mu/(d+1)`;
- the remaining nontrivial hub/base-constant eigenvalue is `-1/(d+1)`.

Hence the Ramanujan bound gives

`lambda_2 <= 2 sqrt(d-1)/(d+1)`.

For an integer truncation radius `r`, a degree-`d` ball of radius `<r` has at most

`B(d,r)=1+d sum_{j=0}^{r-2}(d-1)^j`

vertices. Taking `n>=2B(d,r)` from an infinite fixed-degree Ramanujan family gives, for independent uniform base vertices `U,V`,

`P(d_H(U,V)>=r)>=1/2`

and therefore

`E min(d_H(U,V),r)>=r/2`.

Choose spoke and base-edge lengths

`x=r(d+2)/(d+r)`,

`y=2(d+2)/(d+r)`.

Then `2x=ry` and, because every physical edge has conductance `1/(n(d+2))`,

`n x+(nd/2)y=n(d+2)`

is exactly the unit dual budget. Base-base distance is

`y min(d_H(u,v),r)`,

and hub-base distance is `x`. Including both base-base and hub-base ordered pairs therefore gives

`F(G) >= r(d+1)(d+3)/((d+2)(d+r))`.      (10.1)

### 10.4 Divergence

`lambda_2 < 2s/(s^2+1)`

`F/(tau_rel Delta) > L_s`,

`L_s=((s-1)^2(s^2+3))/(2(s+1)(s^2+2))`.
