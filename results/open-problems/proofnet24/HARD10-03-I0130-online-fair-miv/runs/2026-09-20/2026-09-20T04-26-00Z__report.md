## 4. Exact affine-interval rigidity theorem

### Lemma
Let `f:[0,1]->R` be concave. If the right derivative `f'(0+)` exists and

\[
f(1)-f(0)=f'(0+),
\]

then

\[
f(t)=f(0)+t f'(0+)\qquad(0\le t\le1).
\]

### Proof
Concavity gives the chord lower bound

\[
f(t)\ge (1-t)f(0)+t f(1)=f(0)+t f'(0+).
\]

For a concave function, the right derivative at the left endpoint is a supergradient, so

\[
f(t)\le f(0)+t f'(0+).
\]

The two inequalities coincide. QED.
