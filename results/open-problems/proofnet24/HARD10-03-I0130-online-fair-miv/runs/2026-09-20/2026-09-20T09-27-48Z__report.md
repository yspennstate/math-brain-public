## 7. Transferable lemma for the probability workers

The proof gives a general max-min Lyapunov device.

> **Variable-slope Hamiltonian patching.** Suppose a one-dimensional adversarial recursion admits two valid linear Lyapunov slopes, one for a reflected compact core and one for the non-reflected tail. If the non-reflected two-branch Hamiltonian tolerates a bounded mismatch between the average forward and backward slopes, then the two linear certificates can be glued by a slowly varying staircase whose jump spacing exceeds the largest combined branch displacement. If the tail slope pays state decrement exactly, the staircase can then be clipped at zero. The result is one compactly supported supersolution controlling every finite horizon at once.

This is useful in reflected random walks, finite-state robust control, adversarial stopping, and extremal-distribution dynamic programs: it replaces a horizon-dependent family of barriers by one static Lyapunov envelope.
