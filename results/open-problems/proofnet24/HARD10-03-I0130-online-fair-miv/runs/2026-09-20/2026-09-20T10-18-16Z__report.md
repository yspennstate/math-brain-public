## 7. Transferable dual/Lyapunov lemma for probability tracks

The proof gives a reusable deterministic control lemma.

> **Delayed staircase lemma.** Suppose a one-dimensional max-min Bellman operator only compares states within total span `S`, and a local two-slope certificate is valid whenever `q_H<=q_L` and `q_L-q_H<=D(q_H)`. Then any nonincreasing edge-slope profile whose consecutive breakpoints are more than `S` apart and whose adjacent slope jumps obey the local `D` bound is a global supersolution before clipping. If a terminal constant slope pays for backward motion and the terminal constant-slope zone has length at least `S`, positive-part clipping preserves the certificate. Hence all slope changes may be delayed until near the desired cutoff without affecting the compact core.

This is directly applicable to martingale/Lyapunov and robust-control DPs in which local drift inequalities tolerate a bounded mismatch between forward and backward average slopes. The main design principle is to spend Lyapunov curvature only near the stopping boundary, not near the origin.
