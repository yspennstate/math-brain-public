## 6. Recovery #2 research: the pendant-defect identity isolates the exact missing comparison

This subsection records a route identity and diagnosis only; it is **not promoted to a new theorem claim or claim ID** in this mandatory no-new-claim recovery.

Take a positive equilibrium with a pendant leaf `ell` attached to a root `r`. Let

\[
q:=\frac{A_r}{A_\ell}.
\]

Because the leaf equilibrium is

\[
A_\ell(A_r+A_\ell)^{-(p+1)}=1,
\]

the incidence of the pendant edge at the root is exactly `q`. Full support at the root gives `0<q<1`. Deleting only the pendant edge while keeping the same core potentials changes the core equilibrium equations in one place:

\[
A_v\sum_{u\sim v,\,u\ne\ell}(A_u+A_v)^{-(p+1)}=
\begin{cases}
1-q,&v=r,\\
1,&v\ne r.
\end{cases}
\]

Thus **a WARM pendant leaf is exactly a one-vertex demand defect on the remaining core**. Writing `theta=1-q`, the core has demand `theta` at `r` and demand `1` elsewhere. The pendant conductance itself simplifies to

\[
 c_{r\ell}
 =\frac{A_rA_\ell}{(A_r+A_\ell)^{p+2}}
 =\frac{q}{1+q}
 =\frac{1-\theta}{2-\theta},
\]

independent of `p` once expressed through the root incidence.

This reformulation explains exactly why the fixed-weight signless/Laplacian pendant literature is close but not yet sufficient. If the core conductances were held fixed and a pendant edge of conductance `d` were attached at `r`, then the full weighted Laplacian has block form

\[
L_{\rm full}=
\begin{pmatrix}
L_{\rm core}+d e_re_r^T&-d e_r\\
-d e_r^T&d
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

By principal-submatrix interlacing and positive-semidefinite rank-one monotonicity,

\[
\lambda_{\max}(L_{\rm full})
\ge \lambda_{\max}(L_{\rm core}+d e_re_r^T)
\ge \lambda_{\max}(L_{\rm core}).
\]

So **fixed-weight pendant attachment cannot cure an already-large top Laplacian eigenvalue**. The WARM obstruction is now sharply isolated: deleting the leaf does **not** leave a uniform-firing equilibrium on the core; it leaves the `theta`-defect equilibrium above. All core conductances may re-adjust with `theta`, and no previous run proves the needed monotonicity through that nonlinear deformation.

A source-capable pendant-elimination theorem would therefore need a comparison of the form

\[
\theta\mapsto \mathcal S_{G,r,p}(\theta)
\]

for the relevant stability spectral functional of the defect equilibrium, strong enough to show that moving from the defect demand `theta<1` toward the uniform core demand `theta=1` cannot hide the instability. On odd unicycles the natural functional is the top eigenvalue of `L_c`; on trees the analogous statement must be phrased for the nontrivial Gram/spectral-gap eigenvalue rather than naively reusing the unicycle top-eigenvalue statement.

This is more precise than “try pendant elimination”: the missing object is now **root-demand deformation monotonicity under equilibrium-dependent conductances**.
