## 2. Exact current mathematical target and verified status

The Hadwiger–Nelson problem asks for

\[
\chi(\mathbb R^2),
\]

where points at Euclidean distance one must have different colors. Current checked status remains

\[
5\le \chi(\mathbb R^2)\le 7.
\]

The direct finite target is therefore

\[
\boxed{
\exists G=(V,E),\ V\subset\mathbb R^2\text{ finite},\
xy\in E\Rightarrow \|x-y\|_2=1,\quad \chi(G)\ge6.
}
\]

As of the current source check on 2026-09-16, no finite unit-distance graph realized in the Euclidean plane with chromatic number greater than five is known. The unrestricted smallest known 5-chromatic example remains Jaan Parts' 509-vertex graph. Primary/reference links rechecked this run:

## 4. Mandatory stuck diagnosis

### (a) Exact blocking statement

The most compact relation-level obstruction is the existence of a **pure unit-distance, 5-colorable forced-equality gadget**:

\[
\boxed{
\exists U\text{ finite unit-distance graph in }\mathbb R^2,
\quad a\ne b\in V(U),
\quad U\text{ is 5-colorable},
\quad
\operatorname{SAT}_5\!\left(U\wedge[c(a)\ne c(b)]\right)=\mathrm{UNSAT}.
}
\]

Equivalently, every proper 5-coloring of `U` gives `c(a)=c(b)` although `a != b`. As noted above, the inherited argument shows this already has the strength to imply `chi(R^2)>=6` when geometrically composed correctly.

The direct whole-graph version is

\[
\boxed{
\exists G\subset\mathbb R^2\text{ finite unit-distance graph with }\chi(G)\ge6.
}
\]

Neither statement is proved.

### (b) Routes tried and precise failure of each

**Route 1 — fractional chromatic improvement.** Current fractional-chromatic improvements above four do not imply ordinary chromatic number at least six. The integrality gap is exactly the missing implication, so this cannot certify the target.

**Route 2 — off-diagonal six-color phenomena.** Results phrased as “six-color” for a different forbidden-distance/off-diagonal condition do not certify the ordinary Hadwiger–Nelson condition `distance 1 => different colors`. Importing them would change the problem.

**Route 3 — six-terminal `K_6-e` two-distance scaffold.** The desired geometry cannot be realized in the required planar two-distance form; the inherited classification obstruction kills this route before any coloring gadget is attached.

**Route 4 — Parts `G16` forced equality.** `G16` has exactly the logical relation wanted at the 5-color level, but its edges use lengths `1` and the golden ratio. It is therefore evidence for the combinatorial mechanism, not a pure-unit-distance certificate.

**Route 5 — Haugland relation gadget.** Haugland's pure unit-distance gadget gives a strong `sqrt(3)` terminal relation under 4-colorings. The P19 target needs a relation surviving all 5-colorings. The known gadget is exactly one color short.

**Route 6 — pairwise black-box scaffold.** Exact scaffold search showed that the six-terminal and seven-terminal black-box designs still require at least six non-unit relation gadgets. No certified pure-unit-distance 5-color terminal gadgets at the needed squared distances 3 and 4 are currently available, so the combinatorial skeleton cannot yet be instantiated.

**Route 7 — mine the exact 509-vertex Parts graph for terminal relations.** This remains the most concrete finite route. The public exact coordinates, CNF/DRAT infrastructure, and verifier repository were located and rechecked. However, this runtime does not have `pysat`, `z3`, `pycosat`, `ortools`, or `pulp`; the container also has no external DNS. The GitHub connector can read the public repository, but this run did not obtain a clean full local graph-to-SAT pipeline capable of issuing and independently certifying thousands of pair-conditioned 5-color queries. Therefore **no pair-relation UNSAT claim is made this run**.
