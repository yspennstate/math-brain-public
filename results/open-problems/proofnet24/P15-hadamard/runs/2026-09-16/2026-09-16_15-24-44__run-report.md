# 6. New exact finite result: no H64-invariant bordered-GS family

**Claim `P15-H64-BGS-2060-NEG-01` — PROVED, restricted scope.**

Let H be the order-64 subgroup of Z_514^* generated by 81. There is no quadruple `(X1,X2,X3,X4)` satisfying the bordered-GS difference equations above if every Xi is invariant under multiplication by H.

## Proof

First, 81 has multiplicative order exactly 64 modulo 514. The action of H on Z_514 has two fixed points, 0 and 257, and eight other orbits of cardinality 64. The executable checker verifies the complete orbit profile

\[
1+1+8\cdot64=514.
\]

An H-invariant set of size 256 cannot contain a fixed point: its size must be a sum of orbit sizes, so it is exactly a union of four of the eight 64-orbits. Hence there are

\[
\binom84=70
\]

possible X1.

An H-invariant set of size 257 must be a union of four 64-orbits together with exactly one of the two fixed points. Hence there are

\[
2\binom84=140
\]

possible sets for each of X2,X3,X4.

For any H-invariant X and any h in H,

\[
\nu_X(ht)=\nu_X(t),
\]

because multiplication by h is a bijection of X and of Z_514. The 513 nonzero shifts therefore reduce exactly to nine multiplicative H-orbits: eight of size 64 and the singleton {257}. Thus each candidate has an exact nine-coordinate difference signature.

The checker computes all 70 signatures of the size-256 candidates and all 140 signatures of the size-257 candidates. Since X2,X3,X4 enter the equations symmetrically, it forms every pair sum of two size-257 signatures, allowing repetition; these collapse to 945 distinct exact pair-sum signatures. For each of the `70*140` choices of `(X1,X2)`, it computes the required complementary nine-vector

\[
(512,\ldots,512)-\operatorname{sig}(X_1)-\operatorname{sig}(X_2)
\]

and tests exact membership among the pair sums. No match occurs. This exhausts all H-invariant quadruples of the required sizes. Therefore no such H64-invariant cyclic bordered-GS difference family exists. QED.
