# 3. Exact Gaussian OU reuse theorem

## 3.1 Setup

Let `H` be a separable Hilbert space of fresh-query predictions. For example,

\[
H=L^2(P_U;\mathbb R^q).
\]

Let `Z` be the whitened Gaussian latent vector for the complete source training/model-selection budget; data-dependent standardization, neural training, feature construction, validation selection and KRR are all simply part of the measurable map from raw `Z` to prediction.

Let

\[
b,g\in L^2(\gamma;H),
\]

where `gamma` is the standard Gaussian law of `Z`. Let

\[
b=Eb+\sum_{k\ge1}b_k,
\qquad
g=Eg+\sum_{k\ge1}g_k
\tag{3.1}
\]

be their orthogonal Hilbert-valued Wiener-chaos decompositions.

Let `Z'` be independent and use `(0.2)`.

## 3.2 Mehler diagonalization

The Ornstein–Uhlenbeck/Mehler operator

\[
(P_tg)(Z)=E[g(Z_t)\mid Z]
\]

acts diagonally on chaos degree `k`:

\[
P_tg_k=t^kg_k.
\]

Therefore, using orthogonality across chaos degrees,

\[
\begin{aligned}
E\langle b_Z-Eb,g_{Z_t}-Eg\rangle
&=E\langle b-Eb,P_t(g-Eg)\rangle\\
&=\sum_{k\ge1}t^kE\langle b_k,g_k\rangle.
\end{aligned}
\]

This proves `(0.3)` with no smoothness assumption.

## 3.3 Squared-risk identity

Let fresh target `T=G(U)` be independent of training. Expand

\[
R_t=E\|T-b_Z-g_{Z_t}\|^2.
\]

The marginal law of `g_{Z_t}` is independent of `t`, so `E||g_{Z_t}||^2` is constant. Because `T` is independent of the training sample, `E<T,g_{Z_t}>` is also constant. The only `t`-dependent term is the baseline/correction covariance:

\[
R_t-R_0
=2\left(E\langle b_Z,g_{Z_t}\rangle-\langle Eb,Eg\rangle\right).
\]

Substitution of `(0.3)` proves `(0.5)` and `(0.6)`.

This proof is exact for the entire complete learner and therefore prices dependence from learned means, learned representations and the residual correction simultaneously.

## 3.4 Finite-correlation transfer

By stationarity and reversibility of the pair `(Z,Z_t)`,

\[
\begin{aligned}
E\langle b_Z-b_{Z_t},g_Z-g_{Z_t}\rangle
&=2E\langle b_Z,g_Z\rangle-2E\langle b_Z,g_{Z_t}\rangle\\
&=2\sum_{k\ge1}(1-t^k)E\langle b_k,g_k\rangle.
\end{aligned}
\]

This equals `R_same-R_t`, proving `(0.7)`. Cauchy–Schwarz gives `(0.8)` and `(0.9)`.

For self-distance,

\[
D_t(F)=2\sum_{k\ge1}(1-t^k)E\|F_k\|^2,
\]

which proves `(0.10)`. Since

\[
1-t^k\ge1-t
\]

for every `k>=1`,

\[
D_t(F)\ge(1-t)D_0(F),
\]

proving `(0.11)`.

# 4. Smooth Gaussian interpolation / Jacobian certificate

The exact chaos theorem handles arbitrary `L^2` learners. When the complete branch is additionally Gaussian-Sobolev, it has a useful derivative form.

\[
C(t)=E\langle b(Z)-Eb,g(Z_t)-Eg\rangle.
\]

Then

\[
\boxed{
C'(t)
=
E\langle \nabla b(Z),\nabla g(Z_t)\rangle_{\rm HS}.
}
\tag{4.1}
\]

One proof is termwise differentiation of `(0.3)`. A direct proof differentiates `g(Z_t)` and applies Gaussian integration by parts separately in `Z` and `Z'`; the second-derivative terms cancel.

Integrating from zero to one gives

\[
\boxed{
R_{\rm same}-R_{\rm ghost}
=
2\int_0^1
E\langle \nabla b(Z),\nabla g(Z_t)\rangle_{\rm HS}\,dt.
}
\tag{4.2}
\]

For raw Gaussian loads with covariance

\[
\Sigma_N=I_N\otimes C,
\]

this becomes

\[
\boxed{
R_{\rm same}-R_{\rm ghost}
=
2\int_0^1
E\langle J_Ub\,\Sigma_N^{1/2},
J_Ug_t\,\Sigma_N^{1/2}\rangle_{\rm HS}\,dt.
}
\tag{4.3}
\]

Hence

\[
\boxed{
|R_{\rm same}-R_{\rm ghost}|
\le
2
\sqrt{E\|J_Ub\Sigma_N^{1/2}\|_{\rm HS}^2}
\sqrt{E\|J_Ug\Sigma_N^{1/2}\|_{\rm HS}^2}.
}
\tag{4.4}
\]

This is `P15-SOURCE-GAUSSIAN-JACOBIAN-39 = PROVED` under the displayed Sobolev hypotheses.

0\le R_{\rm same}-R_{\rm cf}
\le2\tau\operatorname{tr}(HA)
\le\frac{n\tau}{2},
\tag{7.3}
\]

because every eigenvalue `h(1-h)<=1/4`.

## 7.1 Exact commuting spectral criterion

If `S` commutes with `K`, write

\[
h_j=\frac{\kappa_j}{\kappa_j+\rho},
\qquad
a_j=1-h_j,
\qquad s_j=\text{eigenvalue of }S,
\]

and let `f_j` be the signal row in this common basis. Then

\[
R_{0,j}=a_j^2\|f_j\|^2+\tau h_j^2,
\tag{7.4}
\]

\[
R_{{\rm same},j}
=a_j^2(1-s_j)^2\|f_j\|^2
+\tau(h_j+a_js_j)^2,
\tag{7.5}
\]

\[
R_{{\rm cf},j}
=a_j^2(1-s_j)^2\|f_j\|^2
+\tau(h_j^2+a_j^2s_j^2).
\tag{7.6}
\]

The same-response correction improves the mode iff, for `a_j s_j>0`,

\[
\boxed{
a_j\|f_j\|^2>\tau h_j
\quad\text{and}\quad
0<s_j<
\frac{2(a_j\|f_j\|^2-\tau h_j)}{a_j(\|f_j\|^2+\tau)}.
}
\tag{7.7}
\]

By contrast, the independent-copy mean improves iff

\[
0<s_j<\frac{2\|f_j\|^2}{\|f_j\|^2+\tau}.
\tag{7.8}
\]

The same-sample threshold is smaller exactly because of the covariance debt. This is `P15-COMMUTING-SPECTRAL-REUSE-39 = PROVED`.

## 7.2 Double-KRR rational filter

Take the structured mean itself to be KRR with ridge mass `nu`:

\[
S_\nu=K(K+\nu I)^{-1}.
\]

Then `(0.18)` follows from

\[
H_\rho+(I-H_\rho)S_\nu
=I-(I-H_\rho)(I-S_\nu).
\]

In the Gram eigenbasis the transfer factor is

\[
t_j
=1-\frac{\rho\nu}{(\kappa_j+\rho)(\kappa_j+\nu)}.
\]

Equation `(0.19)` follows from `(0.17)`. This is `P15-DOUBLEKRR-RATIONAL-FILTER-39 = PROVED`.

The scalar calculation `(0.20)` is exact and is recorded as `P15-DOUBLEKRR-SIGNFLIP-CE-39`.

## 7.3 Exact response-only leave-one-out attribution

Replace response row `i` by an independent copy while holding the design, `K` and `S` fixed. If `delta_i=Y_i-Y_i'`, then

\[
\Delta_i b=He_i\delta_i,
\qquad
\Delta_i g=ASe_i\delta_i.
\]

Since `E||delta_i||^2=2tau`,

\[
C_i
:=E\langle\Delta_i b,\Delta_i g\rangle
=2\tau(H^TAS)_{ii}.
\]

Therefore

\[
\boxed{
\sum_i C_i
=2\tau\operatorname{tr}(HAS)
=R_{\rm same}-R_{\rm cf}.
}
\tag{7.9}
\]

This is `P15-LINEAR-LOO-REUSE-39 = PROVED`. There is no higher-order Efron slack here because both maps are linear in the response rows. It remains a response-only benchmark, not the source full-pair deletion law.

## 7.4 Operator perturbation of the linear reuse debt

Let

\[
\mathcal D(H,S)=2\tau\operatorname{tr}(H(I-H)S).
\]

For symmetric contractions `H,H'`,

\[
\|H(I-H)-H'(I-H')\|_F
\le2\|H-H'\|_F.

Hence

\[
\boxed{
|\mathcal D(H,S)-\mathcal D(H',S')|
\le
4\tau\|H-H'\|_F\|S\|_F
+2\tau\|H'(I-H')\|_F\|S-S'\|_F.
}
\tag{7.10}
\]

Also `||H'(I-H')||_F<=sqrt(n)/4`. This is `P15-LINEAR-REUSE-PERTURB-39 = PROVED` and provides a branch-frozen representation/ridge perturbation benchmark.
