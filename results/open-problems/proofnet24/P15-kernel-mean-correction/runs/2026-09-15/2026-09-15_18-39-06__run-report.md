# 5. Rank repair: row-rank one is not full vectorized rank one

## `P15-SOURCE-OOF-RANK-REPAIR-27` — NEW / PROVED

The previous P15 report said that the direct leakage is “rank one per fold and rank at most four over the entire 19000-row OOF field.” That sentence is correct **only as a row-space statement**. It is too strong if interpreted as the rank of the full vector-output linear operator.

For

\[
Y_H\in\mathbb R^{h\times q},
\qquad h=|H|,
\]

the direct leakage map is

\[
\mathcal L_H:Y_H\mapsto L_HY_H.
\tag{5.1}
\]

On vectorization,

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{vec}(L_HY_H)
=
(I_q\otimes L_H)\operatorname{vec}(Y_H)
}
\tag{5.2}
\]

(up to the harmless Kronecker ordering convention).

Therefore

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{rank}_{\rm row}(L_H)\le1,
}
\tag{5.3}
\]

but

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{rank}(I_q\otimes L_H)
=q\,\operatorname{rank}(L_H)
\le q.
}
\tag{5.4}
\]

Across four folds, the block-diagonal row operator has row-space rank at most four, while the full vectorized operator has rank at most

\[
\boxed{4q.}
\tag{5.5}
\]

For the source

\[
q=1681,
\]

so the full vectorized bound is

\[
\boxed{4q=6724.}
\tag{5.6}
\]

The useful structure is therefore **four row directions shared across all output coordinates**, not literal rank four in the full 19,000-by-1,681 field space.

### Norms and divergence

\[

# 7. Exact OOF optimism identity: no Gaussian assumption needed

The source labels are deterministic simulator outputs, so I do **not** impose a stochastic noise model on the actual mechanics experiment. The following is an explicit conditional benchmark explaining what the pooled-centering leakage would do under a row-independent response-noise model.

\[

\]

where, conditional on the design and the fold-fitting data,

\[
E[E_H]=0,
\]

and the held-out rows are uncorrelated with common output covariance

\[
E[E_iE_j^\top]
=
\begin{cases}
\Omega,&i=j,\\
0,&i\ne j.
\end{cases}
\tag{7.1}
\]

No Gaussian assumption is used.

Consider any predictor of the form

\[
\widehat Y_H=G(Y_F)+L Y_H,
\tag{7.2}
\]

where `G(Y_F)` is independent of the held-out noise.

Define total observed squared loss and signal risk

\[
\mathcal E_{\rm obs}
=E\|Y_H-\widehat Y_H\|_F^2,
\]

\[
\mathcal R_{\rm sig}
=E\|M_H-\widehat Y_H\|_F^2.
\]

## `P15-OOF-LINEAR-OPTIMISM-27` — NEW / PROVED

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal R_{\rm sig}
=
\mathcal E_{\rm obs}
-h\operatorname{tr}(\Omega)
+2\operatorname{tr}(L)\operatorname{tr}(\Omega).
}
\tag{7.3}
\]

### Proof

Expand

\[
\|Y_H-\widehat Y_H\|_F^2
=
\|M_H-\widehat Y_H\|_F^2
+
\|E_H\|_F^2
+2\langle E_H,M_H-\widehat Y_H\rangle_F.
\]

The first noise cross term against `G(Y_F)` and deterministic signal pieces vanishes by conditional centering. The only own-response term is

\[
-E\langle E_H,LE_H\rangle_F.
\]

By `(7.1)`,

\[
E\langle E_H,LE_H\rangle_F
=
\operatorname{tr}(L)\operatorname{tr}(\Omega),
\]

and

\[
E\|E_H\|_F^2
=h\operatorname{tr}(\Omega).
\]

Rearrange.

QED.
