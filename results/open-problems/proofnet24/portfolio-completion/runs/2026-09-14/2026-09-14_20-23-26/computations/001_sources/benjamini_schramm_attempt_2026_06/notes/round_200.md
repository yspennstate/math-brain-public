# Round 200

Round 200 audits the half-to-full profile criterion against the unavoidable
geodesic lower bound.

On a connected infinite graph,
\[
  \bar\tau_p^{\rm sph}(n)\ge p^n
\]
because every vertex at distance \(n\) is connected to the root if one fixed
geodesic is open.  Therefore the half-to-full profile supremum \(\Psi_p(s)\)
has lower exponent at least
\[
  \left(\frac{1}{2\alpha_\omega}-3s\right)\log_\Delta p_c.
\]
The target exponent from Round 199 is compatible with this lower bound when
\[
  (2+7\alpha_\omega)s
  <
  -\left(\frac{1}{2\alpha_\omega}-3s\right)\log_\Delta p_c.
\]
Since \(p_c<1\) in the nonamenable quasi-transitive setting, this holds for
sufficiently small \(s>0\).  Thus the current spherical-average target is not
immediately killed by the open-geodesic baseline, unlike strict global
pointwise-supremum criteria.
