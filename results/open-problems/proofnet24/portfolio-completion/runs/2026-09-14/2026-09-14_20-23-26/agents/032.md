## Objects that must be kept distinct

For a finite positive contraction \(K\), let \(X_K\) be its random subset and let

\[
H_{\rm set}(K)=H(X_K),\qquad H_{\rm count}(K)=H(|X_K|),\qquad
S(K)=\sum_i h(\lambda_i(K)),
\]

where \(h(u)=-u\log u-(1-u)\log(1-u)\). Precisely, the session target is

\[
H_{\rm set}((1-t)K_0+tK_1)\ge
(1-t)H_{\rm set}(K_0)+tH_{\rm set}(K_1)
\]

for all complex Hermitian \(n\times n\) matrices \(0\le K_0,K_1\le I_n\) and \(t\in[0,1]\). The two-site restriction is \(n=2\). The other two functionals depend only on the spectrum; \(H_{\rm set}\) generally does not.

For example, the kernels

\[
\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
\frac12\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&1\end{pmatrix}
\]

both have eigenvalues \(1,0\) and deterministic cardinality one. Their full subset entropies are respectively \(0\) and \(\log 2\). This exact example explains why a cardinality theorem or an eigenvalue-only entropy bound does not identify the target theorem. It also prevents promoting a theorem for one fixed coordinate block to every direction of the same matrix rank through an arbitrary unitary change of basis.

## The Gu cardinality proof: statement versus displayed inference

In S3, the displayed concavity step on p. 2 splits differently scaled terms without the Jensen weights. That displayed inequality does not follow from concavity. The theorem's cardinality conclusion nevertheless has the following direct repair using the representation written there and S5.

**Independent derivation, not a quotation of Gu's proof.** Let

\[
F(a)=H\!\left(\sum_i\operatorname{Ber}(a_i)\right),\quad
\lambda^j=\lambda(K_j),\quad K_t=(1-t)K_0+tK_1.
\]

The spectral majorization representation used in the report is

\[
\lambda(K_t)=\sum_\sigma c_\sigma
 \big[(1-t)\lambda^0+t\sigma\lambda^1\big],
\qquad c_\sigma\ge0,\quad\sum_\sigma c_\sigma=1.
\]

Joint concavity and permutation symmetry of \(F\) give the properly weighted steps

\[
F(\lambda(K_t))
\ge\sum_\sigma c_\sigma
F((1-t)\lambda^0+t\sigma\lambda^1)
\ge(1-t)F(\lambda^0)+tF(\lambda^1).
\]

Thus the invalid displayed step should not be copied, nor should its repair be described as a proof of full DPP entropy concavity. This observation is only a source/proof transcription issue; it supplies no priority claim for the session's target.

## The adjugate/Fisher lemma and the known geometric mean

For the two-site probabilities \(x=p_{00},y=p_{10},z=p_{01},w=p_{11}>0\), agent 016 introduces

\[
A=\frac{\operatorname{adj}K}{\sqrt w},\qquad
B=\frac{\operatorname{adj}(I-K)}{\sqrt x},\qquad C=A+B.
\]

Both \(A,B\) are positive definite with determinant one. The optimization in that report is

\[
\min_{D>0,\,\det D=1}
\big[(\operatorname{tr}AD)^2+(\operatorname{tr}BD)^2\big]
=2\det C,
\qquad D_*=\sqrt{\det C}\,C^{-1}.
\tag{032.1}
\]

The elementary estimate behind it is

\[
(\operatorname{tr}AD)^2+(\operatorname{tr}BD)^2
\ge\frac12(\operatorname{tr}CD)^2
\ge2\det C\det D.
\]

The first step is scalar Cauchy–Schwarz. The second is the arithmetic–geometric mean inequality for the two positive eigenvalues of \(C^{1/2}DC^{1/2}\); commutativity is unnecessary. At \(D_*\), the equal-determinant hypothesis yields \(\operatorname{tr}(AC^{-1})=\operatorname{tr}(BC^{-1})=1\), so both inequalities are attained. This verifies the comparison used here without depending on a newly named matrix lemma.

Applying S9's Proposition 2.1 gives the exact identity

\[
\boxed{D_*=(A\#B)^{-1}.}
\tag{032.2}
\]

Equation (032.2) is our algebraic identification of the session optimizer using a known formula. It would be inaccurate to say S9 proved the DPP Fisher bound merely because the optimizer agrees with its geometric-mean expression.

The minimized quantity in (032.1) is only the empty/full contribution

\[
\frac{(p'_{00})^2}{p_{00}}+\frac{(p'_{11})^2}{p_{11}}.
\]

It is not the exact minimum of the whole four-atom Fisher information. The distinction matters both mathematically and in any priority statement. No exact earlier trace-square optimization or Fisher application was identified by the targeted search. That negative search outcome does not establish originality. Appropriate wording is “an elementary Cauchy–Schwarz and eigenvalue AM–GM estimate; its optimizer can be written as the inverse matrix geometric mean, using Ando–Li–Mathias, Proposition 2.1.” Do not call this the Hadamard–Fischer determinant inequality, which is a different result.
