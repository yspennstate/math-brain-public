## Result A: universal fixed-MASA positive compression needs a growing coefficient bound

### Theorem

Let \(d\ge2\) be even, and \(v_1,\ldots,v_N\in\mathbb C^d\) satisfy
\[
\sum_jv_jv_j^*=I_d.
\]
Put \(\Phi(\lambda)=\sum_j\lambda_jv_jv_j^*\) for real \(\lambda\).
If every Hermitian contraction \(A\) has a preimage \(\Phi(\lambda)=A\) with
\(\|\lambda\|_\infty\le K\), then
\[
\boxed{K\ge\sqrt{d+1}.}
\]
No restriction on \(N\), or linearity/regularity of preimage selection, is needed.

### Proof

Let \(H_0\) have \(d/2\) eigenvalues \(+1\) and \(d/2\) eigenvalues \(-1\), and set \(H=UH_0U^*\) for Haar unitary \(U\). Then \(\operatorname{tr}H=0\), \(H^2=I\), \(\|H\|_{\rm op}=1\), and \(\operatorname{tr}H^2=d\).

For any fixed vector \(v\),
\[
\mathbb E|v^*Hv|^2=\|v\|^4/(d+1).
\]
Indeed, for a uniform complex unit vector \(z\),
\[
\mathbb E|z_i|^4=2/[d(d+1)],\qquad
\mathbb E|z_i|^2|z_j|^2=1/[d(d+1)]\ (i\ne j).
\]
These follow by normalizing independent complex Gaussians (squared moduli are normalized independent exponentials). Expanding the quadratic form gives
\[
\mathbb E(z^*H_0z)^2=
\frac{(\operatorname{tr}H_0)^2+\operatorname{tr}H_0^2}{d(d+1)}
=\frac1{d+1}.
\]
Rotational invariance proves the vector identity. Hence
\[
\mathbb E\sum_j|v_j^*Hv_j|
\le\frac{\sum_j\|v_j\|^2}{\sqrt{d+1}}
=\frac d{\sqrt{d+1}}.
\]
Some such \(H\) therefore has the same upper bound without expectation. Represent it as \(H=\Phi(\lambda)\). Taking trace against \(H\),
\[
d=\operatorname{tr}H^2
=\sum_j\lambda_jv_j^*Hv_j
\le K\sum_j|v_j^*Hv_j|
\le Kd/\sqrt{d+1}.
\]
Cancel \(d\). ∎

For odd \(d\ge3\), use equal numbers of \(+1,-1\) eigenvalues and one zero. The calculation gives \(K\ge\sqrt{(d^2-1)/d}\). Even dimensions suffice to disprove an absolute universal constant.

### Exact failure in the manuscript

Proposition prop:masalift, source lines 126–145 in paper12/universal_family_obstruction.tex, asserts precisely the forbidden conclusion with fixed \(U,V\), all contraction tuples, and absolute \(K_0\). Absorb \(U\) into \(V\); its rows provide the theorem's \(v_j\).

The proof's frame-operator normalization is independently impossible. Define on the real Hilbert space \(\operatorname{Herm}(d)\)
\[
T(A)=\sum_j\operatorname{tr}(Av_jv_j^*)v_jv_j^*.
\]
Its superoperator trace is
\[
\operatorname{Tr}T=\sum_j\|v_jv_j^*\|_{\rm HS}^2=\sum_j\|v_j\|^4.
\]
In the proof's equal-norm construction, \(\|v_j\|^2=d/N\), so
\[
\operatorname{Tr}T=d^2/N,\qquad\lambda_{\min}(T)\le1/N.
\]
The claimed \(T\succeq\kappa I\) with absolute \(\kappa>0\) cannot hold as \(N\) grows. A good condition number does not imply a dimension-independent lower spectral scale. The proposition's “equivalent” formula also loses \(K_0\): if \(D_i=\Lambda_i/K_0\), then \(\Phi(D_i)=A_i/K_0\), not \(A_i\).

This disproves that positive-compression proposition, not Matrix Spencer or every possible tuple-dependent nonpositive lift.

## Result B: exact unit-ball approximation obstruction and quantifier correction

If \(L\) is a proper real linear subspace of \(\operatorname{Herm}(d)\), then
\[
\sup_{\|A\|_{\rm op}\le1}\inf_{B\in L}\|A-B\|_{\rm op}=1.
\]

Choose nonzero Hermitian \(H\) Hilbert–Schmidt orthogonal to \(L\), and \(A=\operatorname{sgn}H\), zero on the kernel. Then \(\|A\|_{\rm op}=1\), \(\operatorname{tr}(HA)=\|H\|_1\). For \(B\in L\),
\[
\|H\|_1=\operatorname{tr}(H(A-B))
\le\|H\|_1\|A-B\|_{\rm op}.
\]
Thus the distance is at least one, and at most one by \(B=0\). ∎

Consequently, if a real linear image \(\Pi(W)\) approximates every Hermitian contraction to error \(\delta<1\), its image equals \(\operatorname{Herm}(d)\), so \(\dim_{\mathbb R}W\ge d^2\).
For a self-adjoint block-algebra domain this gives
\[
\sum_\alpha r_\alpha^2\ge d^2,\qquad
(\max_\alpha r_\alpha)\sum_\alpha r_\alpha\ge d^2.
\]
Use the real dimension of the self-adjoint source, avoiding real/complex dimension ambiguity.

This strengthens the addendum's approximate-span lemma and bypasses its invalid suggestion that arbitrary \(\delta>1/2\) approximation can be iterated to smaller error inside the same proper subspace.

However, its definition of universal reduction allows the source algebra and map to depend on the tuple. A tuple of \(n=d\) Hermitian matrices spans at most \(d\) real dimensions. A quadratic lower bound obtained by supplying a spanning tuple of \(d^2\) matrices does not give a quadratic bound for every tuple of length \(d\). This is the exact quantifier gap in extrapolating the full-span obstruction to square Matrix Spencer. A lower bound on entropy inside an upper-bound formula is also not a lower bound on actual discrepancy: it only limits what that formula certifies.

These are standard finite-dimensional separation facts; no novelty claim.

## Result C: signed-star Matrix Komlós obstruction

For \(d\ge2\), let
\[
A_j=(E_{1j}+E_{j1})/\sqrt2,\qquad 2\le j\le d.
\]
Every \(A_j\) is real symmetric and has Hilbert–Schmidt norm one. Given arbitrary signs, put
\[
w=(0,\varepsilon_2,\ldots,\varepsilon_d)/\sqrt2,\quad
S=\sum_{j=2}^d\varepsilon_jA_j=e_1w^\top+we_1^\top.
\]
Since \(w\perp e_1\), the only nonzero eigenvalues of \(S\) are
\(\pm\|w\|\). Thus
\[
\boxed{\operatorname{disc}_{\rm op}(A_2,\ldots,A_d)=\sqrt{(d-1)/2}.}
\]
Append a zero matrix to obtain \(n=d\) without changing discrepancy. Therefore the unrestricted HS-normalized matrix \(O(1)\) analogue in the addendum is false.

This does not refute classical vector Komlós; these matrices do not share a diagonal basis. Primary literature search found the already-known lower bound in Dadush–Jiang–Reis, arXiv:2111.03171, Corollary 1.8. This run's explicit family is a self-contained rediscovery, not a new solution.

## Result D: a sufficient weighted-reservoir lemma

Let \(R\) have \(e\) sites, and for each \(r\) let \(\mathcal B_r\) be a finite/countable decoration class with nonnegative weights satisfying
\(\sum_{b\in\mathcal B_r}w_r(b)\le D_r\).
Suppose configurations \(c\) admit an injective encoding
\[
c\mapsto(S_c,(b_r(c))_{r\in S_c}),\qquad S_c\subseteq R,
\]
and \(W(c)\le\prod_{r\in S_c}w_r(b_r(c))\). Then
\[
\sum_cW(c)\le\prod_{r\in R}(1+D_r).
\]
Proof: sum over all independently decorated subsets, a larger class, and factor the nonnegative series. In particular \(D_r\le D\) gives \((1+D)^e\). ∎

An injection of bubbles into sites *within each configuration* does not establish an injective encoding of all configurations, nor a per-site total decoration bound. If two sites carry at most one bubble but the order of two bubbles is retained, the weight sum is \(1+2D+2D^2>(1+D)^2\) for \(D>0\). This toy example is not asserted to be an actual percolation trace; it identifies a missing condition.

Round 518 must prove canonical reconstruction, multiplicity/decoration bounds, and compatibility of charges across different record heights. Its decimal slack table does not supply these facts. No claim is made that its proposed trace encoding is impossible.

## E548 exact bounded experiment

The source key inequality is
\[
2e(G)(n-1)!\le C(T,r;G)+(t-2)n!,
\]
where \(T\) is a \(t\)-vertex tree and \(C\) counts adjacency-marked permutation prefixes supporting an injective copy of \(T\) with prescribed root.

The independently implemented counting formula is as follows. For host root \(b\), outer support \(S\subseteq V(G)\setminus\{b\}\), \(k=|S|\ge1\), the exact contribution is
\[
|N_G(b)\cap S|\,(k-1)!\,(n-1-k)!
\]
when \(G[\{b\}\cup S]\) has a copy of \(T\) rooted at \(b\), and zero otherwise. This follows by choosing the marked final prefix vertex, ordering the other prefix vertices, then ordering the suffix. Enumerating injective tree maps supplies the support predicate; upward closure evaluates supersets. This does not use the proposed inductive proof.
