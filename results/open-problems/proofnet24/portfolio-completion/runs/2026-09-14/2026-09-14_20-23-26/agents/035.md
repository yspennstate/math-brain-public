## Comparison target

The unrestricted statement under comparison concerns
\[
S^3=\{x\in\mathbb R_{\ge0}^{4}:\sum_i x_i=1\},\qquad
V_k(x)=\sum_{i,j=1}^{4}P_{ij,k}x_ix_j,
\]
with exactly
\[
P_{ij,k}=P_{ji,k}\ge0,\qquad \sum_kP_{ij,k}=1,\qquad
P_{ij,k}=0\quad(k\in\{i,j\}). \tag{U}
\]
Allowed coefficients may vanish. Interior fixed points are not assumed.
There are fourteen independent coefficient parameters: four diagonal rows,
each distributing mass to three possible offspring types, and six unequal
parent rows, each distributing mass to two possible offspring types.

To compare the 2016 theorem with (U), one needs its actual extra equalities
or inequalities, any positivity restrictions, and whether uniqueness is
asserted on the closed simplex. None of these missing items can be supplied
from the retrieved abstract.

## Independent exclusion: full type-permutation symmetry is too restrictive

This is an elementary derivation by Agent 035, **not a recovered assertion
or proof from Jamilov's DNC paper**.

Suppose (U) holds and additionally
\[
P_{\sigma(i)\sigma(j),\sigma(k)}=P_{ij,k}
\quad\text{for every permutation }\sigma\text{ of the four types}. \tag{F}
\]
The permutation group acts transitively on allowed diagonal triples
\((i,i,k)\), and on allowed triples with three distinct entries.
Consequently there are constants \(a,b\) with
\[
P_{ii,k}=a\ (k\ne i),\qquad
P_{ij,k}=b\ (i,j,k\text{ distinct}).
\]
Normalization forces \(a=1/3\), \(b=1/2\). Thus (F) leaves the single
equiprobable operator \(E\), with
\[
E_i(x)=\frac13\sum_{j\ne i}x_j^2+
\sum_{\substack{j<k\\j,k\ne i}}x_jx_k
=\frac12-x_i+\frac23x_i^2-\frac16\sum_jx_j^2. \tag{E}
\]
For every pair \(i,j\),
\[
E_i(x)-E_j(x)=-(x_i-x_j)
\left(1-\frac23(x_i+x_j)\right). \tag{D}
\]
Let \(w(x)=\max_i x_i-\min_i x_i=\max_{i,j}|x_i-x_j|\).
If \(x_i\ne x_j\), then \(0<x_i+x_j\le1\), so the factor in
parentheses lies in \([1/3,1)\). If \(x_i=x_j\), the output difference
is zero. There are only finitely many pairs, hence
\[
w(E(x))<w(x)\quad\text{whenever }w(x)>0.
\]
The center \((1/4,1/4,1/4,1/4)\) is fixed by (E). Every other point
strictly decreases the nonnegative width and therefore cannot be periodic.
Thus the center is the only periodic point of the fully type-permutation
invariant strict operator.

This provides a stronger reason than parameter counting to avoid the
unsupported description that the DNC family has coefficients depending
only on whether its parents coincide: on allowed triples that description
gives exactly (E), which cannot exhibit the periodic-point abundance
announced in the DNC abstract. Subject to the abstract applying its class
terminology consistently, full type-permutation invariance cannot describe
that entire family. **This observation neither proves that DNC symmetry is
only parental symmetry nor determines any intermediate symmetry condition.**
