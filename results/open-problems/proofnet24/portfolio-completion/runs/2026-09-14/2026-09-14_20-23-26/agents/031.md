## 3. Coefficient conventions and equations (1)–(5)

The intended assumptions are exactly

\[
V_k(x)=\sum_{i,j=1}^4P_{ij,k}x_ix_j,\quad
P_{ij,k}=P_{ji,k}\ge0,\quad \sum_kP_{ij,k}=1,\quad
P_{ij,k}=0\quad(k\in\{i,j\}).
\]

The double sum is over ordered pairs. Thus a distinct unordered pair contributes
twice, and a repeated pair `(i,i)` contributes once. The exclusion applies to
repeated pairs, including `P_{ii,i}=0`; it does not require strictly positive
allowed entries. Parent symmetry is distinct from invariance under relabelling
the four types, which is not assumed. Nonnegativity and row sum one imply the
coefficient upper bound one used throughout.

* **Simplex preservation:** summing outputs gives
  `sum_k V_k(x)=(sum_i x_i)^2=1`. Polynomial continuity and compact convex
  nonemptiness justify Brouwer, even when some coordinates always vanish.
* **(2), fixed-point cap:** `x_k<=(1-x_k)^2` yields
  `x_k^2-3x_k+1>=0`. On `[0,1]` only the lower-root branch is available, giving
  `c=(3-sqrt(5))/2<1/2`. This is a cap on fixed points, not on all raw images.
* **(3), matrix convention:** rows are offspring and columns are one parent.
  `A(m)_{rj}=sum_a P_{aj,r}m_a` is nonnegative, column-stochastic, and
  `A(m)_{jj}=0`. It preserves the zero-sum subspace.
* **(4), polarization:** writing `z=x-y`, the identity
  `x_ax_b-y_ay_b=m_a z_b+z_a m_b` and parent symmetry make the two sums equal.
  The factor is exactly **two**, including repeated parents.
* **(5), transfer:** for `G={i,k}`, `H={j,l}`, the coefficients obey
  `p_ki=0`, `p_lj=1`, and `p_ij=p_ji`. If `D=1_G^T A(m)(e_i-e_j)` and
  `U=m_i(1-p_ij)+m_j p_ij`, then the exact slack is

\[
U-D=m_i(1-p_{ii})+m_jp_{jj}+m_kp_{kj}+m_\ell(1-p_{\ell i})\ge0.
\]

Since `p_ij in [0,1]`, `U<=max(m_i,m_j)`. This verifies every index and sign;
the coefficient shared by the two parent orders has not been split into two
independent variables. Both groups having exactly two types is essential.

## 7. Corollary 5 and equations (13)–(15)

The manuscript correctly distinguishes the nonnegative ambient matrix
`J=2A(x*)` from its restriction to `E={z:sum_i z_i=0}`. The latter is invariant
because column sums of `J` equal two. If a **positive real** tangent eigenvalue
`rho` exists, choose its nonzero real eigenvector in `E`. At a sole positive
entry `i`, zero diagonal and nonnegativity would give `(Jz)_i<=0<rho z_i`;
the negative-singleton case follows by changing sign. The eigenvector thus
has two entries of each sign. The transfer decomposition gives

\[
\rho h=\mathbf1_G^TJz\le2h\max_i x_i^*,
\]

which proves (13). A singular `I-tJ|_E` for `0<t<=1` would give an excluded
positive eigenvalue `1/t>=1`. The determinant is continuous, never zero,
and starts at one, proving (14). No diagonalizability is needed.

For a four-by-four matrix with trace zero,
`det(I-uA)=1+u^2 e_2-u^3 e_3+u^4 det(A)`. Since `1` is an eigenvalue,
`e_2=e_3-det(A)-1`; substitution at `u=2` gives
`det(I-2A)=-3-4e_3+12 det(A)`. At a fixed point the radial line is
complementary to `E` and has `J`-eigenvalue two, so the ambient determinant
is minus the tangent determinant. Equation (15) has the correct sign and
answers the archived `q:det`, even for boundary fixed points.

The conclusion does **not** bound moduli of all eigenvalues by one, establish
attraction for raw iteration, or identify a boundary fixed point's simplex
index with the sign of an ambient tangent determinant. The integrated note
makes none of those invalid inferences.

### Every overlap case

Take `m=(T^3x+T^3y)/2`. Equal column indices have overlap one. For distinct
indices `i,j` with `m_i,m_j>=epsilon`, both columns contain the same
parental row `P_ij,·`, with weights `m_j` and `m_i`. Their overlap is at

`z_r<=2t`, `V_r(z)<=2t`, and `z in K_q`, so `z_a>=d-2t>0` for `a!=r`.

\[

\[
A_{kj}\ge m_\ell(1-\theta(t)),\quad
A_{\ell j}\ge m_k(1-\theta(t)),\quad

\[
h(t)\ge h(\varepsilon)=\frac{598335}{19668992},\qquad
h(\varepsilon)-\varepsilon=\frac{29055}{9834496}>0.

All column pairs therefore overlap by at least `epsilon` at the required
midpoints. This is stronger than claiming overlap merely on an unverified
