## 2. Precise sufficient theorem and integrability

Let \(n\ge3\). Let \(Z_1,\ldots,Z_n,Z^\circ\) be independent with common law \(P\), and let \(W\) be independent of all these observations. For each \(m\in\{n-2,n-1,n\}\), let
\[
(T,w,z)\longmapsto f_{T,w}(z)
\]
be a jointly measurable real-valued map on ordered training sequences of length \(m\). A deletion preserves order. The same pair \((T,W)\) always gives the same output, including when a double deletion is reached through two different deletion orders. This is the actual joint coupling of the fits.

For an iid ordered \(T_m\), an independent uniform deletion index \(I_m\), and a fresh query, define
\[
b_m^2=E_{T_m,W,I_m}P(f_{T_m,W}-f_{T_m^{-I_m},W})^2,\qquad m=n,n-1,
\]
\[
B^2=E_{T_{n-1},W}P f_{T_{n-1},W}^2.
\]
Assume these three nonnegative numbers are finite. They are deterministic population quantities.

The marginal law of a uniformly deleted iid ordered sequence is again an iid ordered sequence of the smaller size. The inequalities \((a+b)^2\le2a^2+2b^2\), applied in both directions to a fit and its deletion, therefore give
\[
E P f_{T_n,W}^2\le2B^2+2b_n^2,\qquad
E P f_{T_{n-2},W}^2\le2B^2+2b_{n-1}^2.
\]
Thus every size used in the proof has finite averaged second moment. Every fixed deletion-position energy is finite, because it is one of finitely many nonnegative terms in a finite average. For each fixed omitted index, the honest evaluation has the same second moment as an independent deployment query. All centered variables and all covariance products below are consequently integrable.

Fubini and Jensen define \(Pf_{S,W}\) as a finite random variable almost surely, with finite second moment. An arbitrary measurable definition on an exceptional null event is harmless. The phrase "for every realized full fit" in Agent 047 should be read with this almost-sure convention; averaged hypotheses alone do not assert integrability for every hypothetical training sequence.

The conclusion is
\[
\left\|Pf_{S,W}-\frac1n\sum_i f_{S^{-i},W}(Z_i)\right\|_2
\le b_n+\sqrt{\frac{B^2+(n-1)b_{n-1}^2}{n}}=:K.
\tag{A}
\]
The norm and all probabilities are joint over \(S,W\). No symmetry of the learner is assumed.

## 3. Independent proof, including every conditioning step

Suppress \(W\) in the function subscripts. Write
\[
F=Pf_S-\frac1n\sum_i f_{S^{-i}}(Z_i)=D+H,
\]
\[
D=\frac1n\sum_i P(f_S-f_{S^{-i}}),\qquad
H=\frac1n\sum_i h_i,\qquad
h_i=Pf_{S^{-i}}-f_{S^{-i}}(Z_i).
\]
This is an identity for the actual random full-fit target, not for \(E Pf_S\).

Jensen over the deletion index and then over \(P\) yields
\[
ED^2\le\frac1n\sum_i E[P(f_S-f_{S^{-i}})]^2
\le\frac1n\sum_i EP(f_S-f_{S^{-i}})^2=b_n^2.
\tag{B}
\]
There is no pointwise claim \(|D|\le b_n\).

Given \((W,S^{-i})\), the variable \(Z_i\) is independent with law \(P\); hence
\[
E[h_i\mid W,S^{-i}]=0,\qquad
Eh_i^2=E\operatorname{Var}_P(f_{S^{-i}})\le B^2.
\tag{C}
\]

Fix distinct, deterministic indices \(i,j\), put \(T=S^{-\{i,j\}}\), and set
\[
a_i=Pf_T-f_T(Z_i),\qquad a_j=Pf_T-f_T(Z_j),
\]
\[
\Delta_{ij}=f_{S^{-i}}-f_T,\qquad
d_{ij}=P\Delta_{ij}-\Delta_{ij}(Z_i),
\]
with \(d_{ji}\) defined by interchanging \(i,j\). Then \(h_i=a_i+d_{ij}\) and \(h_j=a_j+d_{ji}\).

The three cross terms vanish for distinct reasons:

1. Given \((W,T)\), \(Z_i,Z_j\) are independent queries with law \(P\), so \(E[a_i a_j\mid W,T]=0\).
2. Given \((W,S^{-j})\), the ordered positions identify \(Z_i\) and \(T\). Thus \(a_i\) is measurable. Both functions defining \(d_{ji}\) are also fixed, and its remaining evaluation \(Z_j\) is independent with law \(P\). Therefore \(E[a_i d_{ji}]=0\).
3. Conditioning on \((W,S^{-i})\) gives \(E[d_{ij}a_j]=0\) by the same argument.

Consequently,
\[
E h_i h_j=E d_{ij}d_{ji}.
\tag{D}
\]
The second cancellation does not claim that \(a_i\) is independent of \(d_{ji}\); it uses a measurable multiplier times a conditionally centered variable.

Let
\[
e_{ij}=EP(f_{S^{-i}}-f_T)^2.
\]
The same held-out independence as in (C) gives
\[
E d_{ij}^2=E\operatorname{Var}_P(\Delta_{ij})\le e_{ij}.
\]
Thus
\[
E h_i h_j\le |E d_{ij}d_{ji}|
\le\sqrt{e_{ij}e_{ji}}
\le\frac{e_{ij}+e_{ji}}2.
\tag{E}
\]

Here is the exact ordered-sample counting argument. For fixed \(i\), the position of \(Z_j\) in \(S^{-i}\) is
\[
r_i(j)=j-\mathbf1_{\{j>i\}}.
\]
The map \(j\mapsto r_i(j)\) is a bijection from the indices other than \(i\) onto \(1,\ldots,n-1\). Since \(S^{-i}\) has the ordered iid size-\((n-1)\) distribution, summing over \(j\ne i\) gives
\[
\sum_{j\ne i}e_{ij}=(n-1)b_{n-1}^2.
\]
In particular,
\[
\sum_{i\ne j}e_{ij}=n(n-1)b_{n-1}^2.
\tag{F}
\]
Individual \(e_{ij}\) need not be equal. Permutation invariance of the learner is unnecessary.

Combining (C), (E), and (F),
\[
EH^2\le\frac{nB^2+n(n-1)b_{n-1}^2}{n^2}
=\frac{B^2+(n-1)b_{n-1}^2}{n}.
\]
Minkowski and (B) prove (A). In particular,
\[
\Pr\{|F|>K/\sqrt\alpha\}\le\alpha,\qquad 0<\alpha<1.
\tag{G}
\]
If \(K=0\), \(F=0\) almost surely. Dependence between \(D\) and \(H\) does not matter.

All conditioning above establishes identities and is then integrated over \(W\). It does not replace unconditional stability constants by conditional ones.

## 4. The actual random full-fit risk gate

For measurable vector-valued fitted functions \(m_T,c_T\), apply (A) to
\[
\ell_T(u)=\langle G(u)-m_T(u),c_T(u)\rangle,\qquad
v_T(u)=\|c_T(u)\|^2.
\]
Assume the three finite averaged quantities separately for each functional, and additionally assume
\[
R_S(0)=P\|G-m_S\|^2<\infty\quad\text{almost surely}.
\]
Agent 047 explicitly includes this last assumption. It is not implied by the functional moments: \(c_T=0\) makes them all zero even when \(G-m_S\) has infinite second moment.

Let \(A_S=P\ell_S\), \(V_S=Pv_S\), and let \(\widehat A,\widehat V\) be their honest LOO estimates. For fixed \(0<\delta<1\), choose deterministic \(\alpha_A,\alpha_V>0\) with sum at most \(\delta\), and put
\[
\epsilon_j=\frac1{\sqrt{\alpha_j}}
\left[b_{j,n}+
\sqrt{\frac{B_j^2+(n-1)b_{j,n-1}^2}{n}}\right].
\]
By (G) and a union bound, with probability at least \(1-\delta\),
\[
A_S\ge L:=\widehat A-\epsilon_A,\qquad
V_S\le U:=\widehat V+\epsilon_V.
\tag{H}
\]
Since \(v_T\ge0\), \(U\ge0\).

The moment lemma above implies \(P v_S^2<\infty\) almost surely, and hence \(V_S<\infty\). Finite baseline risk and Cauchy-Schwarz make the exact risk expansion legitimate:
\[
R_S(\theta)=R_S(0)-2\theta A_S+\theta^2V_S.
\]
On (H), simultaneously for all \(0\le\theta\le1\),
\[
R_S(0)-R_S(\theta)\ge2\theta L-\theta^2U.
\]
Take \(\widehat\theta=0\) if \(L\le0\) or \(U=0\), and otherwise take \(\min(1,L/U)\). If \(U>0\), this maximizes the lower quadratic. If \(U=0\), (H) forces \(V_S=0\), whence \(c_S=0\) \(P\)-almost everywhere and \(A_S=0\). Therefore
\[
R_S(\widehat\theta)\le R_S(0)-
[2\widehat\theta L-\widehat\theta^2U]\le R_S(0).
\tag{I}
\]
For \(L,U>0\), the subtracted certificate is \(L^2/U\) when \(L\le U\), and \(2L-U\) when \(L\ge U\); both are positive.

The gate can depend on the same sample because the deterministic quadratic inequality holds for all scalars on the single event (H). Choosing an unaccounted-for candidate pipeline or a data-dependent confidence allocation is a different selection problem. Agent 047 does not claim those operations are covered.

## 5. Gaussian separation checked independently

Let \(X_i\) be iid \(N(0,1)\), \(G=1\), \(m_T=0\), and \(c_{T_m}=1+\bar X_m\), constant in its query. Then
\[
B_{A,m}^2=1+1/m,\qquad
B_{V,m}^2=1+6/m+3/m^2.
\]
For one uniform deletion, write \(H=\bar X_{m-1}\), \(D=\bar X_m-H\). Their centered Gaussian covariance parameters are
\[
s=EH^2=\frac1{m-1},\quad
d=ED^2=\frac1{m(m-1)},\quad EHD=-d.
\]
Thus \(b_{A,m}^2=d\). The energy difference is \(2D+2HD+D^2\). Its square has expectation
\[
4d+4EH^2D^2+4EHD^3+ED^4
=4d+4(sd+2d^2)-12d^2+3d^2
=4d+4sd-d^2.
\]
This equals
\[
b_{V,m}^2=\frac4{m(m-1)}
+\frac{4m-1}{m^2(m-1)^2},
\]
as stated in Agent 047.

Taking one deleted observation arbitrarily large while the others stay in bounded intervals makes both deletion norms unbounded. Taking all observations large makes both functional moments unbounded uniformly over fits. These sets have positive Gaussian probability, so the failure also concerns essential suprema. All averaged quantities remain finite, establishing strict separation.

Every observation occurs in \(n-1\) LOO training means, giving exactly \(\widehat A=1+\bar X_n=A_S\). At \(n=1000,\delta=0.05\), independent numerical evaluation gives
\[
\epsilon_A=0.20652792027218428,\qquad
\epsilon_V=0.21366137710761055.
\]
Here \(U>0\). Chebyshev implies
\[
\Pr(L\le0)\le [n(1-\epsilon_A)^2]^{-1}.
\]
Intersecting \(L>0\) with (H) proves positive certified actual risk improvement with probability at least
\[
1-\delta-[n(1-\epsilon_A)^2]^{-1}
=0.9484116847683409.
\]
The reported number is correct. For bounded \(B_j\), the averaged widths vanish whenever both relevant deletion norms tend to zero. The asserted \(O(n^{-1/2})\) rate under \(b_{j,m}=O(m^{-1})\) is also correct.

## 6. Exact failure of observed-energy substitution

Use \(n=100,\delta=1/25\), \(P(C)=p=97/100\), \(q=3/100\), \(G=1,m_T=0\), and
\[
r(u)=\begin{cases}1&u\in C,\\-34&u\notin C,\end{cases}
\qquad c_T(u)=\mathbf1_{\{T\subset C\}}r(u).
\]
This is a deterministic, bounded, symmetric, honest learner. Direct calculation gives
\[
Pr=-1/20,\quad M_2=Pr^2=713/20,\quad M_4=Pr^4=801821/20.
\]
At size \(m\),
\[
B_{A,m}^2=p^mM_2,\quad B_{V,m}^2=p^mM_4,
\]
\[
b_{A,m}^2=qp^{m-1}M_2,\quad b_{V,m}^2=qp^{m-1}M_4.
\]
Indeed, deleting one row changes the activation indicator precisely when the deleted row is outside \(C\) and all remaining rows are inside.

On \(E=\{S\subset C\}\), every full, single-deleted, and double-deleted function equals \(r\). Both observed size-\(n\) energies and both observed size-\((n-1)\) energies are exactly zero, even with exact deployment integration. Nevertheless,
\[
\widehat A=\widehat V=1,\qquad A_S=-1/20,\quad V_S=713/20.
\]

Supply the true averaged moment constants but substitute those observed zero energies. The balanced widths become
\[
(\epsilon_A^{\rm plug})^2=\frac{713}{40}p^{99},\qquad
(\epsilon_V^{\rm plug})^2=\frac{801821}{40}p^{99}.
\]
The exact integer comparisons
\[
20\cdot97^{99}<100^{99},\qquad
25\cdot97^{100}>100^{100}
\]
were independently verified with JavaScript BigInt. The first implies
\((\epsilon_A^{\rm plug})^2<713/800<1\), so \(L^{\rm plug}>0\) and the gate is strictly positive on \(E\). Every positive scalar then has
\[
R_S(\theta)-R_S(0)=\theta/10+(713/20)\theta^2>0.
\]
The second comparison gives
\[
\Pr(E)=p^{100}=0.0475525079254057\ldots>\delta.
\]
Thus the claimed nonworsening probability fails. Approximate values independently reproduced are \(\theta^{\rm plug}=0.00201578729089327\) and risk increase \(0.00034643888212515\).

This is a counterexample to that automatic substitution, not to (A) or (I).

A sufficient repair is exactly the one in Agent 047. Suppose random, finite, nonnegative upper bounds simultaneously dominate the six deterministic theoretical parameters with probability at least \(1-\eta\). Use these bounds in the same monotone width formulas with fixed confidence allocation. On their domination event, computed widths are at least oracle widths. Intersect with (H) and apply the deterministic gate argument to obtain probability at least \(1-\delta-\eta\). No independence between these two events is required. Unbiasedness of the observed energies supplies no such domination event.

## 7. Independent exact enumeration and assumption stress checks

For an additional ordered-learner check, I executed a new exact rational enumeration, independently of Agent 047's random-table script. Let
\[
P(Z=-1)=2/3,\quad P(Z=2)=1/3,\qquad
P(W=-1)=3/4,\quad P(W=3)=1/4,
\]
independently, and define the explicitly order-sensitive family
\[
f_{T,w}(z)
=wz\sum_{k=1}^{m}kT_k+
\left(\sum_{k=1}^{m}T_k\right)^2+mw.
\]
Enumerating all \(2^{n+1}\) sample/seed configurations gives:

| \(n\) | \(B^2\) | \(b_n^2\) | \(b_{n-1}^2\) | \(EF^2\) | \(ED^2\) | \(EH^2\) | \(Q=(B^2+(n-1)b_{n-1}^2)/n\) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 3 | 108 | 109 | 61 | \(203/3\) | 29 | \(116/3\) | \(230/3\) |
| 4 | 285 | 165 | 109 | 111 | \(63/2\) | \(159/2\) | 153 |

For each ordered pair, all three separate cancellations in Section 3 and identity (D) were checked exactly: 18 cancellations and 6 pair identities at \(n=3\); 36 cancellations and 12 identities at \(n=4\). The summed pair energies were respectively \(366\) and \(1308\), equal to \(n(n-1)b_{n-1}^2\). The component inequalities and final bound were also checked using integer fractions, without floating-point sign decisions. These finite checks supplement the analytic proof.

Three boundary checks help identify what the theorem actually assumes:

- **Iid cannot be replaced by mere sample exchangeability.** Let all \(Z_i\) equal one Rademacher variable and let \(f_T(z)=z\). With an independent Rademacher deployment query, deletion energies vanish and \(B=1\), but \(\|F\|_2=1>1/\sqrt n\). The report correctly assumes iid data.
- **Unconditional seed bounds do not give every-seed guarantees.** Take \(n=3\), independent Rademacher data, independent \(W\sim\operatorname{Bernoulli}(1/100)\), and \(f_{T,W}(z)=Wz\). Then both deletion norms vanish and \(B^2=1/100\). At \(\alpha=1/4\), the joint threshold is \(1/\sqrt{75}<1/3\). Conditional on \(W=1\), \(|F|=|\bar Z_3|\ge1/3\) always, so the conditional failure probability is one. The joint theorem remains valid. Agent 047 expressly avoids the false conditional claim.
- **Full-fit stability cannot be omitted.** Define \(f_T=1\) at size \(n\) and \(f_T=0\) at smaller sizes. Then \(F=1\), \(B=b_{n-1}=0\), and \(b_n=1\). Formula (A) is exact; a bound based only on the LOO distribution would be false.

Full-pipeline honesty and matching fit coupling are likewise substantive. Reusing data-dependent tuning or a data-containing "seed" after deletion would violate the displayed measurable dependence on the retained sample and independent \(W\). The report already disallows those shortcuts.
