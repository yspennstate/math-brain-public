A root trace consists of distinct integer portal heights with labels V or a child index. V labels represent maximal root-column vertical intervals. They cannot be consecutive; their endpoint pairs must form a matching of adjacent gaps in the sorted union of portals. Child pieces must be mutually avoiding within each child cone. Packets are ordered lists of such path pieces, not independent scalar walks.

In the one-sided regime, root-column legality is \(h\ge u-r\), and \(e_r(h)=r+h-u\). Round 500 defines
\[
 E_r(\Pi)=\sum_{(a,b)\in\Pi}(e_r(a)+e_r(b)).
\]
Rounds 501 and 508 propose, in pointwise form,
\[
 E_z(\Pi)\le C^{|\Pi|}w^{E_r(\Pi)}
 \alpha^{\sum|a-b|}G_\eta(A(\Pi)),
 \qquad
 G_\eta(A)=\prod_{\text{adjacent gaps }g}(1+\eta^g).
\]
Round 502 calibrates \(w=w_*(z)=2z/(1+z^2)<1\).

## 2. The raw endpoint-excess norm is impossible

Fix a root cone at depth \(r\), ball radius \(u\), and \(0<z<1\). For every sufficiently large integer H, the path along the single root-column edge from height H to H+1 is legal. If boundary crossing edges are included, adjoin the parent-to-root edge at H and the root-to-parent edge at H+1. This three-edge path is self-avoiding and stays outside the forbidden ball when H is large.

Thus, under the two conventions described in rounds 499 and 505,
\[
 E_z(((H,H+1)))\ge z
 \quad\hbox{or}\quad
 E_z(((H,H+1)))\ge z^3.
\]
Both are positive lower bounds independent of H.

But
\[
 E_r(((H,H+1)))=2(r+H-u)+1,\qquad
 L=1,\qquad G_\eta=1+\eta.
\]
For fixed finite C and fixed \(\alpha,\eta\), the proposed upper bound tends to zero:
\[
 Cw^{2(r+H-u)+1}\alpha(1+\eta)\longrightarrow0.
\]
This is a contradiction. In particular, the k=1 supremum in the displayed Fock-type norm is already infinite. Factorials in packet size do not help.

The suggested reserve correction does not cure this example. Its only compressed record levels are H and H+1. Even charging both entire reserves gives at most \(R_*=E_r(\Pi)\). Whenever \(w(1+D)<1\),
\[
 w^{E_r(\Pi)}(1+D)^{R_*}
 \le [w(1+D)]^{E_r(\Pi)}\longrightarrow0.
\]
There are no reversal bubbles in this path.

This disproves the stated uniform norm for the raw \(E_z\) defined in rounds 499 and 503. A different conjugated kernel, a restriction of the state domain, or an entrance/exit weighting could change the problem, but must be defined and its recursion proved. No such alteration is assumed here.

## 3. Explicit loss of weighted information in leg collapse

Round 512 observes that \(a^m\le a\) for \(0<a<1\), where \(a=Cqz^2w_*^2\), and collapses an entire monotone leg with m child atoms to one child atom. This is valid for one term. It does not bound the sum over terms mapping to that image.

Here is a family realizable by actual self-avoiding cone paths, not merely unrestricted scalar words. Fix two child columns A and B of the root column v, and a descendant column B0 immediately below B. Fix a legal lower height H, put \(R=H+L\), and choose any composition
\[
 L=\ell_1+\cdots+\ell_m,\qquad \ell_i\ge1.
\]
Descend from R to H by m child excursions, with labels alternating A,B and chosen so that the final descending label is A. Each excursion crosses to its child column, descends vertically through its prescribed height interval, and crosses back to v.

From \((v,H)\), return above the record using the path
\[
 (v,H)\to(B,H)\to(B0,H)
 \to(B0,R+1)\to(B,R+1)\to(v,R+1),
\]
where the middle arrow is a vertical run.

These paths are self-avoiding. Repeated uses of A or B on descent have separated height intervals; consecutive descents use different children. The return uses the previously unused B0 column. Its B vertices at H and R+1 were not visited on descent, because the final descending child is A and R+1 exceeds every descent height. All root portals are distinct, there are no root V intervals, and every path vertex has height at least H. Choosing H large enough verifies annular legality including parent edges.

There is one height reversal: a monotone descent followed by a monotone ascent crossing R. The record-envelope procedure cuts the final child jump virtually at R. Collapsing the descent produces the same child-type leg of length L for every composition; the other bubble leg has length L and the forward remainder has length 1.

In the archive's scalar weights, the original trace has weight
\[
 a^{m+1}z^{2L+1}.
\]
Therefore its whole collapsed fiber has weight
\[
 \sum_{m=1}^{L}\binom{L-1}{m-1}a^{m+1}z^{2L+1}
 =
 a^2z^{2L+1}(1+a)^{L-1}.
\]
The one collapsed image has weight \(a^2z^{2L+1}\). Their ratio grows exponentially.

Adding one reserve-slot label yields at most \(e_r(R)=e_r(H)+L\) images with the same collapsed payload. This linear number cannot absorb \((1+a)^{L-1}\). Retaining a fixed finite number of collapsed child-colour choices changes only a constant factor.

The exact raw path weights exhibit the same erased-subdivision phenomenon. Descent length is \(L+2m\); return length is \(L+5\). Hence the family has raw generating mass
\[
 z^{2L+7}(1+z^2)^{L-1},
\]
whereas its m=1 representative has mass \(z^{2L+7}\).

This disproves the claimed weighted use of the specified collapse-plus-one-slot code. It does not disprove the bare assignment of distinct bubbles within one trace to distinct lower levels: here there is only one bubble, so that assignment is vacuous. Nor does it rule out a new encoding retaining subdivisions or using additional decorations. The missing distinction is between injectivity of a bubble-to-level assignment inside one object and injectivity of the code for the entire family of objects.

## 4. A valid local replacement: retain all subdivisions

The collapse defect can be repaired locally without increasing the archived D budget.

**Lemma.** Give a vertical atom of positive length \(\ell\) weight \(z^\ell\) and a child atom weight \(az^\ell\). A monotone leg is a nonempty composition into these atoms, with no consecutive vertical atoms. A marked neutral bubble consists of a downward leg of span d and an upward leg of the same span d, for \(d\ge1\), with no V–V pair at their turnaround. Retain all subdivisions. Root avoidance, child feasibility, and collision of portals between legs may be discarded for this upper bound.

Set
\[
 \lambda(a)=\frac{2+a+\sqrt{a^2+4a}}2.
\]
If \(z\lambda(a)<1\), the total bubble inventory is
\[
 \boxed{\mathcal B(a,z)=
 \frac{a(a+2)z^2}
 {1-(a^2+4a+2)z^2+z^4}.}
 \tag{1}
\]
This is an exact identity for the stated scalar class, and an upper bound for any subclass of marked primitive bubbles satisfying those hypotheses.

**Proof.** Temporarily omit the common factor \(z^d\). Let \(C_d,V_d\) be the colour-weight sums of span-d monotone legs ending in C,V, and put \(F_d=C_d+V_d\). With \(P=x/(1-x)\), decomposition at the last atom gives
\[
 C(x)=aP(1+C(x)+V(x)),\qquad V(x)=P(1+C(x)).
\]
Thus, with \(Q(x)=1-(2+a)x+x^2\),
\[
 C(x)=\frac{ax}{Q(x)},\quad
 V(x)=\frac{x(1-x)}{Q(x)},\quad
 F(x)=\frac{x(1+a-x)}{Q(x)}.
 \tag{2}
\]
Reversal preserves a monotone leg's weight, so the total inventory with its first atom V also equals \(V_d\). Consequently the valid two-leg coefficient is \(F_d^2-V_d^2\).

For completeness, write \(k=2+a\) and let
\[
 U_{-1}=0,\quad U_0=1,\quad U_n=kU_{n-1}-U_{n-2}.
\]
Then \(C_d=aU_{d-1}\), \(V_d=U_{d-1}-U_{d-2}\). Summing geometric series using
\[
 U_n=\frac{\lambda^{n+1}-\lambda^{-n-1}}{\lambda-\lambda^{-1}}
\]
gives, for \(t\lambda^2<1\),
\[
 \sum_{n\ge0}U_n^2t^n
 =\frac{1+t}{(1-t)[1-(k^2-2)t+t^2]},
\]
\[
 \sum_{n\ge0}U_nU_{n-1}t^n
 =\frac{kt}{(1-t)[1-(k^2-2)t+t^2]}.
\]
Therefore
\[
 \sum_{d\ge1}(F_d^2-V_d^2)t^d
 =
 \frac{ak t}{1-(k^2-2)t+t^2}.
\]
Taking \(t=z^2\) proves (1). All series converge absolutely by the stated hypothesis. This counts every subdivision; no many-to-one collapse or independence assumption occurs. QED.

A physical bubble returning to a virtual record cut fits this lemma only after that interface and the allocation of the crossing atom are specified. The lemma does not encode the forward overshoot, record ownership, or nested bubbles automatically.

## 5. Rigorous comparison with the archive's budget

Round 511 uses
\[
 D(a,z)=a(a+2)\frac{z^2}{(1-z)^2}.
\]
For
\[
 0<a\le8/25,\qquad0<z\le2/5,
 \tag{3}
\]
one has \(\lambda(a)<2+a\le58/25\), so
\(z\lambda(a)<116/125<1\).
Furthermore the difference between the denominator of (1) and \((1-z)^2\) is
\[
 z[2-(a^2+4a+3)z+z^3]>0,
\]
since
\[
 2-(a^2+4a+3)z
 \ge 2-\frac{2739}{625}\frac25
 =\frac{772}{3125}>0.
\]
Thus
\[
 \mathcal B(a,z)\le D(a,z).
 \tag{4}
\]

These bounds cover every integer \(q\ge2\), not only the five displayed residual cases. Put
\[
 z\le M_q^{-1},\quad w_*=\frac{2z}{1+z^2},
 \quad a=2qz^2w_*^2.
\]
The quantity a increases with z. At the endpoint, writing \(s=\sqrt q\), its values are
\[
 a=\frac{2s^2}{(1+2s)^2}\quad(s\le2),\qquad
 a=\frac{8s^2}{(s^2+2s+2)^2}\quad(s\ge2).
\]
The first expression increases to \(8/25\); the second decreases for \(s\ge2\). Also \(M_q\) increases with q and \(M_2>5/2\), proving (3).

Since \(w_*\le20/29\) and \(D\le1856/5625\), the reserve multiplier has the rigorous uniform bound
\[
 w_*(1+\mathcal B)\le w_*(1+D)
 \le\frac{29924}{32625}<1.
 \tag{5}
\]
Thus the useful numerical slack is real even after retaining all two-leg subdivisions. The obstruction is global encoding and the choice of norm, not this local inventory.

For fixed ordered reserve slots I, if a decoration is an occupied subset with one full bubble payload per occupied slot, if its placement/order is determined by the slots, and if every local payload inventory is at most \(\mathcal B\), its total product-weight is at most
\[
 \prod_{\ell\in I}(1+\mathcal B)=(1+\mathcal B)^{|I|}.
\]
This follows by expanding the product. An injective reconstruction-preserving map into these decorations with dominating weights would transfer the bound to actual traces. These conditions are hypotheses, not consequences of distinct portal heights. Additional nesting, record-owner choices, time order, or attachment positions must be recovered or paid for.

## 6. Endpoint excess does not equal total record reserve

There is another explicit accounting gap if the reserve is charged only to the endpoint exponent from round 500. Take \(r=u\), start at height 1, and make n unit-height upward child excursions alternating A and B. This is a valid self-avoiding compressed trace, with portals \(1,2,\ldots,n+1\).

Its endpoint excess is \(E=n+2\), but summing available reserve over its record portals gives
\[
 R_*=\sum_{j=1}^{n+1}j=\frac{(n+1)(n+2)}2.
\]
Omitting the initial record changes only 1. Therefore \(R_*\) cannot be bounded by a universal constant times this endpoint E. The trace has no bubbles; inserting all unused reserves is itself potentially expensive. A fuller recursive sum of internal-port excesses might supply a different budget, but that identity is absent from the claimed scalar charging step and must be proved explicitly.
