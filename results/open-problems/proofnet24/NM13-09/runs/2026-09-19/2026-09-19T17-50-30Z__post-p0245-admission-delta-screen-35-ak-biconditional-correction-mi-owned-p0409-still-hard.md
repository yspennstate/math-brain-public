`phi(t)=Vol_n(e^{t Lambda}K cap B_2^n)`

### 5.1 HARD7-01 false-negative witness

HARD7-01's exact two-dimensional witness is

`K=[-1/2,1/2] x [-2,2]`, `Lambda=diag(0,1)`, `t=2 log 2`.

### 5.2 R7-05 dimension-one biconditional correction

A later independent source-logic audit by R7-05 noticed that the printed wording is a biconditional, so one must also test whether each listed alternative is sufficient for equality. In dimension one every diagonal `Lambda` is scalar, but scalar dilation need not give equality if the radius crosses the unit-ball boundary between the midpoint endpoints.

The exact witness is

`K=[-1/2,1/2]`, `Lambda=[1]`, `t=2 log 2`.

Then

`phi(0)=1`, `phi(t/2)=2`, `phi(t)=2`,

hence

`phi(t/2)^2=4 > 2 = phi(0)phi(t)`.

The inequality is strict although two printed alternatives hold (`K subset B_2^1` and scalar `Lambda`). R7-05 further gives the complete one-dimensional criterion

`phi(t/2)^2=phi(0)phi(t) iff (log a)(log a+lambda t)>=0`

for `K=[-a,a]`.
