## 3. Corrected four-cycle factorization

The scale audit in the preceding PROB10 work gave the true standardized cyclic four-vector correlations

\[

\[

\[

\qquad
A=\frac{1+v}{1-v}=\frac{22490051}{214595},
\tag{3.2}

\[

\tag{3.3}

\[
p_4=\mathbb E[g_A(Z_1)g_A(Z_2)],
\qquad \operatorname{Corr}(Z_1,Z_2)=q.
\tag{3.4}
\]

No unit-variance-before-noise normalization enters this identity.

Let `He_n` be the probabilists' Hermite polynomials and

\[
d_n=\mathbb E[g_A(Z)He_n(Z)],
\qquad w_n=\frac{d_n^2}{n!}\ge0,
\qquad m=\mathbb E g_A(Z).
\tag{3.5}
\]

Mehler's identity gives the positive expansion

\[
\boxed{p_4=m^2+\sum_{n\ge1}q^n w_n.}
\tag{3.6}
\]

Parseval gives

\[
\sum_{n\ge1}w_n=\mathbb E g_A(Z)^2-m^2.
\tag{3.7}
\]

The two endpoint moments have the exact elementary forms

\[
m=\frac1\pi\arctan\sqrt A,
\qquad
\mathbb E g_A(Z)^2
=\frac1\pi\arctan\frac{A}{\sqrt{1+2A}}.
\tag{3.8}
\]

For `M>=1`, positivity plus `q^n<=q^{M+1}` for `n>M` yields

\[
\begin{aligned}
p_4
&\le m^2+\sum_{n=1}^M q^n w_n
+q^{M+1}\sum_{n>M}w_n\\
&=(1-q^{M+1})m^2+q^{M+1}\mathbb E g_A(Z)^2
+\sum_{n=1}^M(q^n-q^{M+1})w_n.
\end{aligned}
\tag{3.9}
\]

This tail inequality is rigorous and monotone in the sense needed here; no guessed decay rate for the Hermite coefficients is used.

The coefficients are evaluated from

\[
d_n=\frac{\sqrt A}{\pi}\int_0^\infty
He_{n-1}(x)e^{-(1+A)x^2/2}\,dx,
\tag{3.10}
\]

whose monomial half-Gaussian moments are elementary. The checker evaluates (3.8)--(3.10) with exact `Fraction` arithmetic and outward rational enclosures for `sqrt`, `pi`, and `arctan`.
