`C = ker(A-20 I)`

and let `E` be the orthogonal projection onto `C`, with Euclidean representing points `x_i=E e_i`.

The exact inner products are the standard Bondarenko ones: adjacent vertices are at the shorter distance and nonadjacent vertices are at the diameter. `dim span{x_i:i notin Z} <= 65-j`.
