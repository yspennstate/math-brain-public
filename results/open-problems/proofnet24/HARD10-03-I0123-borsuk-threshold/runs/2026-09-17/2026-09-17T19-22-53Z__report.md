## 5. What a successful proof must contain that no route so far contains

For a source-level negative-endpoint move `U_B<=62`, a successful construction must simultaneously provide:

1. a certified realization in dimension at most 62;

The existing work repeatedly achieves item 1. It has exact control of item 2 for several restricted families. It has never achieved item 3.
