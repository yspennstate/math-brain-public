# Clarification of one notation phrase in REPORT.md

In Section 2, the phrase “one-bit `1/2`-multiplicity tilt” is shorthand for a **{1,2}-valued multiplicity tilt**: after deleting the incomparability-leaf vertex `x`, a quotient extension has either one or two lifts to `P`, according to whether the unique incomparable neighbor `b` lies outside or inside the admissible insertion corridor. It does **not** mean a probability-one-half tilt.

The exact identity used in the report is unchanged:

`e(P-x) = ell*u + d*r - d*u = d*u*(A+B-1)`.

No theorem statement, count, probability, or conclusion changes.
