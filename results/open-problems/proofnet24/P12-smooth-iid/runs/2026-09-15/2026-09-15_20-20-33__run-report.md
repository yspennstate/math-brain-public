## 3. New operator-norm lemma for microscopic jitter

Let `U∈F^{N×m}`, `F=R` or `C`, have independent centered entries with `|U_ij|≤δ`, and `N≤Γm`.

### `P12-MICROJITTER-OPNORM-33` — NEW / PROVED

There are `C_Γ,c_Γ>0` such that

\[
\boxed{P\{\|U\|_{op}>C_\Gamma\sqrt m\,\delta\}\le e^{-c_\Gamma m}.}
\tag{3.1}
\]

**Proof.** For fixed unit `x,y`,

\[
y^*Ux=\sum_{ij}\overline y_i x_jU_{ij}.
\]

Every real projection of this sum is a sum of independent centered variables bounded by `δ|y_i||x_j|`, and

\[
\sum_{ij}\delta^2|y_i|^2|x_j|^2=\delta^2.
\]

Hoeffding gives `P{|y*Ux|>tδ}≤4e^{-ct²}`. Take `1/4`-nets of the underlying real unit spheres; their sizes are at most `9^{d_Fm}` and `9^{d_FN}`, where `d_R=1`, `d_C=2`. The standard net inequality gives

\[
\|U\|_{op}\le2\max_{x,y\text{ in nets}}|y^*Ux|.
\]

Choosing `t=C_Γ√m` pays the net entropy and proves `(3.1)`. QED.

One global norm event controls every candidate submatrix because `||(U)_T||op≤||U||op`; no exponential row-set union is required.

## 6. Direct complex scale-local projection small ball

\[

\tag{6.1}

Add uniform-disk jitter of radius `δ` only to the coordinates in `J`. If `||LX-z||≤t√k`, then for every jitter realization the smoothed output lies in the ball of radius `(t+δ)√k`, since `||L_J||op≤1`.

\[
|\det_C L_J|^{-2}
\left[\frac{Q_\xi^{\mathbb C}(\delta)}{\pi\delta^2}\right]^k.
\tag{6.2}

The `2k`-real-dimensional ball of radius `(t+δ)√k` has volume

\[
\frac{\pi^k[k(t+\delta)^2]^k}{k!}

Multiplying `(6.1)`–`(6.3)` proves `(0.4)`, hence `(0.5)`.
