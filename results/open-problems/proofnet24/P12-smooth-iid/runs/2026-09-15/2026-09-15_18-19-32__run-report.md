# 4. New complex projective two-column obstruction

Let \(\xi_m'\) be an independent copy of the complex entry \(\xi_m\).

Define

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal Q_m^{\mathbb C}(\rho)

## `P12-COMPLEX-TWOCOLUMN-PROJECTIVE-OBSTRUCTION-31` — NEW / PROVED

Assume

\[
N_m/m\to\gamma>1,
\qquad
k_m=r_m+1,
\qquad
k_m\log m=o(m).
\]

Suppose along a subsequence there are coefficients \(a_m,b_m\in\mathbb C\), radii \(\rho_m\ge0\), and one fixed

\[
p>1/\gamma
\]

such that

\[
|a_m|^2+|b_m|^2=1
\]

and

\[
P\{|a_m\xi_m+b_m\xi_m'|\le\rho_m\}
\ge p.
\tag{4.2}
\]

Then there is \(c=c(\gamma,p)>0\) such that

\[
\boxed{
P\left\{
M^{\mathbb C}_{m,r_m}(A_m)
\le
\sqrt{m+r_m}\,\rho_m
\right\}
\ge
1-e^{-cm}
}
\tag{4.3}
\]

for all large indices of the subsequence.

### Proof

Use only columns 1 and 2.

For row \(i\), let

\[
E_i
=
\{|a_mA_{i1}+b_mA_{i2}|\le\rho_m\}.
\]

Because the entries in the two columns are independent copies, the row events \(E_i\) are iid and

\[
P(E_i)\ge p.
\]

Let

\[
C_m=\sum_{i=1}^{N_m}\mathbf1_{E_i}.
\]

Since

\[
k_m\log m=o(m)
\]

implies \(k_m=o(m)\),

\[
\frac{m+r_m}{N_m}
=
\frac{m+k_m-1}{N_m}
\longrightarrow
\frac1\gamma<p.
\]

A binomial Chernoff bound gives

\[
P\{C_m<m+r_m\}
\le e^{-cm}.
\tag{4.4}
\]

On \(C_m\ge m+r_m\), choose any \(T\) of exactly \(m+r_m\) successful rows and put

\[
x_m=(a_m,b_m,0,\ldots,0)\in\mathbb C^m.
\]

Then

\[
\|x_m\|_2=1
\]

and every selected row satisfies

\[
|(A_Tx_m)_i|
\le\rho_m.
\]

Hence

\[
\|A_Tx_m\|_2
\le
\sqrt{m+r_m}\,\rho_m.
\]

Therefore

\[
M^{\mathbb C}_{m,r_m}(A_m)
\le
\sigma_m(A_T)
\le
\sqrt{m+r_m}\,\rho_m.
\]

QED.
