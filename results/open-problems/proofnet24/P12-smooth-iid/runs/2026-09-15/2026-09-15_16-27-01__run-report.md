# 4. Finite convolution gives tunable coordinate concentration

Let \(Q_\xi(r)\) denote the Lévy concentration function. Assume
\[
\|f^{*q}\|_\infty\le M.
\]

## `P12-FC-CONCENTRATION-29` — NEW / PROVED

### Real field

\[
\boxed{Q_\xi(r)\le(2Mq\,r)^{1/q}.}
\tag{4.1}
\]

### Complex field

\[
\boxed{Q_\xi(r)\le(\pi Mq^2r^2)^{1/q}.}
\tag{4.2}
\]

### Proof

Take \(q\) independent copies. If every copy lies in a real interval centered at \(z\) of radius \(r\), the sum lies in the interval centered at \(qz\) of radius \(qr\). Therefore \(Q_\xi(r)^q\le2qrM\). In the complex plane, \(q\) disks \(B(z,r)\) sum into \(B(qz,qr)\), whose area is \(πq^2r^2\). Thus \(Q_\xi(r)^q\le\pi q^2r^2M\). QED.

### Consequence: symmetrized annulus

Let \(~\xi=\xi'-\xi''\). Then
\[
P\{|\widetilde\xi|<b\}\le Q_\xi(b).
\tag{4.3}
\]
If \(E|\xi|^2=1\), then \(E|\widetilde\xi|^2\le2\), so
\[
P\{|\widetilde\xi|>b^{-1}\}\le2b^2.
\tag{4.4}
\]
Choose fixed \(b=b(q,M)>0\) so small that \(Q_\xi(b)\le1/4\), \(2b^2\le1/4\), and \(b\le1/2\). Then
\[
\boxed{P\{b\le|\widetilde\xi|\le b^{-1}\}\ge1/2\ge b.}
\tag{4.5}
\]
This is exactly the fixed symmetrized-annulus input used by inherited heavy-tail random-rounding geometry. No centering is needed.
