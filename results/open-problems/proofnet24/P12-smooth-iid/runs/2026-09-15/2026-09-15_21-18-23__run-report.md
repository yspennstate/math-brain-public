\[
p_0=P\{X=0\},

## `P12-COMPLEX-INFINITE-LINEAR-ATOM-CAP-34` — NEW / PROVED

For centered finite-variance complex `X`,

\[
\boxed{

\le
\max\{p_0,\kappa\}.
}
\tag{6.1}
\]

### Proof

If exactly one coefficient is nonzero, `(6.1)` is equality with `p_0`.

Otherwise choose two nonzero coefficients `c_i,c_j`. The remainder

\[
R=\sum_{\ell\ne i,j}c_\ell X_\ell

\[
c_iX_i+c_jX_j=-R.

\[
