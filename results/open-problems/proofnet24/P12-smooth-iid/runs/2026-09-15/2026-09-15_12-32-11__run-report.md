# 6. New theorem: complex useful normals for every finite \(L^p\), \(p>1\)

Fix \(1<p<\infty\). Let
\[
B_m\in\mathbb C^{N_m\times m},\qquad N_m\ge c_*m,
\]
have iid entries \(\xi_m\) satisfying uniformly
\[
\boxed{E\xi_m=0,\qquad E|\xi_m|^2=1,\qquad \|f_{\xi_m}\|_{L^p(\mathbb C)}\le K_p.}
\tag{6.1}
\]

## `P12-COMPLEX-RECT-COMP-LP-20` — NEW / PROVED

There are fixed \(\delta,\rho,c>0\), depending only on \(p,K_p,c_*\), such that
\[
\boxed{P\left\{\inf_{z\in\operatorname{Comp}_{\mathbb C}(\delta,\rho)}\|B_mz\|_2\le c\sqrt{N_m}\right\}\le e^{-cm}.}
\tag{6.2}
\]

### Proof

Let \(\widetilde\xi_m=\xi_m'-\xi_m''\). For any \(b>0\), condition on \(\xi_m''\). Hölder gives
\[
P\{|\widetilde\xi_m|<b\}\le K_p(\pi b^2)^{1-1/p}.
\tag{6.3}
\]
Also \(E|\widetilde\xi_m|^2=2\), hence
\[
P\{|\widetilde\xi_m|>b^{-1}\}\le2b^2.
\tag{6.4}
\]
Choose fixed \(b=b(p,K_p)\in(0,1/4]\) so both right sides are at most \(1/8\). Then
\[
\boxed{P\{b\le|\widetilde\xi_m|\le b^{-1}\}\ge3/4\ge b.}
\tag{6.5}
\]
This is the Jain--Silwal annulus hypothesis. Lemma 3.1 gives for each fixed complex unit vector \(v\)
\[
P\{\|B_mv\|_2\le c_0\sqrt{N_m}\}\le e^{-c_0N_m}.
\]
Theorem 3.2 supplies a deterministic complex random-rounding net with approximation error controlled by
\[
\epsilon m^{-1/2}\sqrt{E\|B_m\|_{HS}^2}=\epsilon\sqrt{N_m}.
\]
Choose \(\epsilon\) small and then fixed \(\delta,\rho\) so the compressible entropy \(\exp\{O(\delta m\log(C/\delta))\}\) is dominated by the fixed-vector exponent. This proves `(6.2)`. QED.

Thus useful-normal incompressibility works uniformly for **every** fixed \(p>1\). The endpoint \(p=1\) is qualitatively different because `(6.3)` no longer forces small fixed-radius concentration to zero uniformly.

# 8. New theorem: one-normal microscopic disk for every finite \(L^p\), \(p>1\)

Let \(X=(\xi_1,\ldots,\xi_m)\in\mathbb C^m\) have iid entries satisfying uniformly
\[
\boxed{E\xi_m=0,\qquad E|\xi_m|^2=1,\qquad \|f_{\xi_m}\|_{L^p(\mathbb C)}\le K_p}
\tag{8.1}
\]
for fixed \(1<p<\infty\). Let \(u\) be a useful spread normal: for fixed \(c_0,a_0,b_0,v_0>0\) there is \(I\subset[m]\) with \(|I|\ge c_0m\), \(a_0/\sqrt m\le|u_i|\le b_0/\sqrt m\) on \(I\), and \(\sum_{i\in I}|u_i|^2\ge v_0\).

## `P12-COMPLEX-LP-DENSITY-DISK-20` — NEW / PROVED

There are \(c>0,m_0<\infty\), depending only on \(p,K_p,c_0,a_0,b_0,v_0\), such that for all \(m\ge m_0\), every useful \(u\), and every \(0<s\le1\),
\[
\boxed{P\{|\langle X,u\rangle|\le s\}\ge c s^2.}
\tag{8.2}
\]

### Proof

Choose fixed \(T\) so tail probability \(P\{|\xi_m|>T\}\le T^{-2}\) is small relative to the useful spread mass. Reveal on \(I\) every tail indicator and contaminated value. The unrevealed clean law \(\zeta_m=\mathcal L(\xi_m\mid|\xi_m|\le T)\) has \(L^p\) density norm bounded uniformly. Recenter \(\eta_m=\zeta_m-E\zeta_m\). Then \(|\eta_m|\le2T\), and `P12-PLANAR-LP-ELLIPTIC-20` gives
\[
\kappa I_2\preceq\operatorname{Cov}(\eta_m)\preceq4T^2I_2.
\tag{8.3}
\]
Chernoff and weighted Hoeffding show with probability \(1-e^{-cm}\) that the clean spread set has linear cardinality and retains fixed positive coefficient mass.

Set
\[
\boxed{b=\left\lceil\frac p{p-1}\right\rceil,\qquad q_b=\frac b{b-1}.}
\tag{8.4}
\]
Then \(q_b\le p\). Interpolation between \(L^1\) and \(L^p\) gives a uniform \(L^{q_b}\) bound for the clean density. Group all but fewer than \(b\) clean spread coordinates into blocks of exactly \(b\) coordinates; reveal the finitely many leftovers. The number of blocks \(q\asymp m\). Put \(a_i=\sqrt q\,\overline{u_i}\). Useful spread gives fixed \(0<c_-\le|a_i|\le c_+\).

In two real dimensions,
\[
\|f_{a_i\eta_i}\|_{q_b}=|a_i|^{-2/b}\|f_{\eta_i}\|_{q_b}\le C.
\]
For a block \(Y_\ell=\sum_{i\in\ell}a_i\eta_i\), the \(b\)-fold Young inequality gives \(\|f_{Y_\ell}\|_\infty\le M_0\). Block supports are uniformly bounded, and covariance is uniformly elliptic by `(8.3)` and the coefficient bounds. Thus the clean centered sum
\[
Z=q^{-1/2}\sum_{\ell=1}^qY_\ell
\]
satisfies `P12-PLANAR-TRIANG-LOCALCLT-19`; its density is uniformly close to a uniformly elliptic planar Gaussian and hence has a fixed positive floor on \(|z|\le3\) for large \(m\).

Let \(M_I\) be the conditional mean after tail/leftover reveal and \(R_I\) the independent outside-spread sum. Conditional-expectation contraction gives \(E|M_I+R_I|^2\le1\), so \(P\{|M_I+R_I|\le2\}\ge3/4\). On the intersection with the clean good event, every radius-\(s\le1\) target disk lies inside the fixed density-floor region. Integrating yields `(8.2)`. QED.

Therefore the constructive **one-normal** regularity frontier is now verified for every fixed \(p>1\), not merely \(p=2\).

and normalize variance to one. Then the law is centered and compactly supported, has all moments finite, belongs to \(L^p(\mathbb C)\), and satisfies \(P\{|\xi|\le t\}\asymp t^{2-\alpha}\) for small \(t\).

Use
\[

## 9.2 Smooth triangular all-moments version

The cusp is unnecessary. Let \(\psi\) be a fixed radial \(C^\infty\) density supported in the unit disk and \(g\) another fixed radial \(C^\infty\) compactly supported density of variance one. Put
\[
t_m=e^{-\lambda m},\qquad \beta=2-\frac1p,\qquad \varepsilon_m=t_m^\beta,
\tag{9.4}

\[
\delta_m=\varepsilon_m^{p/[2(p-1)]}.
\tag{9.5}

Let \(\psi_{\delta_m}(z)=\delta_m^{-2}\psi(z/\delta_m)\), and choose a bounded central scale \(s_m\to1\) so the radial mixture
\[
\boxed{f_m=(1-\varepsilon_m)g_{s_m}+\varepsilon_m\psi_{\delta_m}}

\|\varepsilon_m\psi_{\delta_m}\|_p

# 10. New deterministic theorem: robust two-plane pruning

Let \(E\subset\mathbb C^m\) be a two-plane and let \(A\in\mathbb C^{2\times m}\), \(AA^*=I_2\), have rows forming an orthonormal basis of \(E\). Write columns \(a_j\in\mathbb C^2\). For \(R\subset[m]\), put
\[
G_{R^c}=\sum_{j\notin R}a_ja_j^*.
\]
Assume there are \(\tau\in(0,1)\), \(L\in\mathbb N\) such that
\[
\boxed{G_{R^c}\succeq\tau^2I_2\quad\text{for every }|R|\le L.}
\tag{10.1}
\]
Set \(L_\tau=\lceil\log_2(\tau^{-4})\rceil+1\).

## `P12-COMPLEX-RANK2-PRUNE-20` — NEW / PROVED

If \(L\ge L_\tau\), there is \(J\subset[m]\) with
\[
\boxed{G_J:=\sum_{j\in J}a_ja_j^*\succeq\tau^2I_2}
\tag{10.2}
\]
and every internal leverage satisfies
\[
\boxed{a_j^*G_J^{-1}a_j\le\frac12\quad(j\in J).}
\tag{10.3}
\]

### Proof

Start with \(J_0=[m]\), \(G_0=I_2\). If some \(j\in J_k\) has leverage \(\ell_j=a_j^*G_k^{-1}a_j>1/2\), remove it. The matrix determinant lemma gives
\[
\det G_{k+1}=\det G_k(1-\ell_j)<\frac12\det G_k.
\]
If this continued for \(L_\tau\) steps, \(\det G_{L_\tau}<2^{-L_\tau}<\tau^4\). But the robust hypothesis gives \(G_{L_\tau}\succeq\tau^2I_2\), hence determinant at least \(\tau^4\), contradiction. Therefore the process stops earlier, giving `(10.3)`, and fewer than \(L\) removed coordinates gives `(10.2)`. QED.

\[
\boxed{P\{|\langle X,u\rangle-z_1|\le s,|\langle X,v\rangle-z_2|\le t\}
\le4\pi^2e^2K_2^4\frac{\tau^{-4}}{\Delta}s^2t^2.}
\tag{11.1}

### Proof

Multiply by the target complex bidisk volume \(\pi s^2\pi t^2\). QED.
