# 5. New theorem: q=3 general-incidence normal form

Let \(B\in\mathbb C^{n\times p}\), \(n=p+r\), have full column rank. Fix pairwise-disjoint \(r\)-sets \(S_1,S_2,S_3\subset[n]\) and an arbitrary numerator omitted \(r\)-set \(U\).

Put
\[
O=U\cup S_1\cup S_2\cup S_3,\qquad C=O^c.
\]
After freezing \(B_C\), project rows indexed by \(O\) onto
\[
W=\operatorname{span}(B_C)^\perp.
\]
Write residual rows \(y_i\in\mathbb C^d\), where \(d=|O|-r\).

For \(a=1,2,3\), set
\[
q_a=|U\cap S_a|,\qquad m_a=r-q_a,
\]
\[
A_a=S_a\setminus U,\qquad F_a=U\setminus S_a.
\]
Then \(|A_a|=|F_a|=m_a\) and
\[
d=m_1+m_2+m_3.
\]

Order the numerator residual matrix as
\[
S=Y_{A_1\cup A_2\cup A_3}\in\mathbb C^{d\times d},
\]
with coordinate blocks \(V_a\) of dimensions \(m_a\). Let \(E_a:\mathbb C^{m_a}\hookrightarrow\mathbb C^d\) be the block embedding and define
\[
\boxed{H_a=Y_{F_a}S^{-1}E_a\in\mathbb C^{m_a\times m_a}.}
\tag{5.1}
\]
For \(m_a=0\), set \(\det H_a=1\).

## `P12-COMPLEX-Q3-GENERAL-INCIDENCE-NORMALFORM-18` — PROVED

For each \(a\),
\[
\boxed{\det Y_{O\setminus S_a}=\pm\det S\,\det H_a.}
\tag{5.2}
\]
Hence
\[
\boxed{
\frac{|\det Y_{O\setminus U}|^2}{\prod_{a=1}^3|\det Y_{O\setminus S_a}|^{2/3}}
=
\prod_{a=1}^3|\det H_a|^{-2/3}.
}
\tag{5.3}
\]

### Proof

The numerator is exactly \(S\). For fixed \(a\), the denominator row set \(O\setminus S_a\) consists of all numerator rows \(A_b\), \(b\ne a\), plus the \(m_a\) replacement rows \(F_a\). Right-multiply by \(S^{-1}\). The \(A_b\) rows become standard coordinate rows. After a simultaneous row/column permutation putting block \(V_a\) last, the normalized denominator is
\[
\begin{pmatrix}I_{d-m_a}&0\\ *&H_a\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Thus its determinant is \(\pm\det H_a\), proving (5.2). The three factors \(|\det S|^{2/3}\) multiply to \(|\det S|^2\), cancelling the numerator and giving (5.3). QED.

This covers fully distinct, partial-overlap, and coincident incidence in one formula.

# 6. New theorem: unequal-block complex Hodge identity

Expose one fresh row \(x\in U\). Denominator \(a\) is active exactly when \(x\in F_a=U\setminus S_a\).

up to sign.

\[

## `P12-COMPLEX-Q3-OVERLAP-HODGE-18` — PROVED

# 9. New theorem: coordinate L2 suffices for complex useful normals

Primary source checked: Vishesh Jain and Sandeep Silwal, *A note on the universality of ESDs of inhomogeneous random matrices*, arXiv:2006.05418. The current source gives the complex symmetrized annulus framework, fixed-vector invertibility (Lemma 3.1), and the random-rounding net directly in \(\mathbb C^n\) for random \(N\times n\) matrices with independent columns (Theorem 3.2).

## `P12-COMPLEX-NORMAL-L2-18` — PROVED

Let \(B_m\in\mathbb C^{(m-1)\times m}\) have iid entries \(\xi_m\) satisfying uniformly
\[
\boxed{E\xi_m=0,\qquad E|\xi_m|^2=1,\qquad \|f_{\xi_m}\|_{L^2(\mathbb C)}\le K_2.}
\tag{9.1}
\]
Then there exist fixed \(\delta,\rho,c>0\), depending only on \(K_2\), such that
\[
\boxed{
P\left\{\inf_{z\in\operatorname{Comp}_{\mathbb C}(\delta,\rho)}\|B_mz\|_2\le c\sqrt m\right\}
\le e^{-cm}.
}
\tag{9.2}
\]
Thus any unit null direction \(u_m\) satisfies
\[
\boxed{P\{u_m\in\operatorname{Comp}_{\mathbb C}(\delta,\rho)\}\le e^{-cm}.}
\tag{9.3}
\]

### Proof

Let \(\widetilde\xi_m=\xi_m'-\xi_m''\). Its density is \(g_m=f_m*\widetilde f_m\), so Cauchy–Schwarz gives
\[
\|g_m\|_\infty\le\|f_m\|_2^2\le K_2^2.
\]
Hence
\[
P\{|\widetilde\xi_m|<b\}\le\pi K_2^2b^2.
\]
Also \(E|\widetilde\xi_m|^2=2\). Set
\[
b=\min\left\{\frac14,\frac1{4\sqrt\pi K_2}\right\}.
\]
Then
\[
P\{|\widetilde\xi_m|<b\}\le1/16,
\qquad
P\{|\widetilde\xi_m|>b^{-1}\}\le2b^2\le1/8.
\]
Therefore
\[
\boxed{P\{b\le|\widetilde\xi_m|\le b^{-1}\}\ge13/16\ge b.}
\tag{9.4}
\]
This is the Jain–Silwal uniform annulus hypothesis.

Their fixed-vector theorem plus tensorization gives exponential lower-norm failure for every fixed unit vector. Their complex random-rounding theorem supplies a deterministic net whose approximation error is controlled by
\[
\frac\epsilon{\sqrt m}\sqrt{E\|B_m\|_{\rm HS}^2}
\]
rather than by the operator norm. Here
\[
E\|B_m\|_{\rm HS}^2=(m-1)m.
\]
Choose fixed net parameters and then \(\delta,\rho\) small enough that the compressible covering entropy is dominated by the fixed-vector exponential rate. This yields (9.2). Absolute continuity gives full row rank almost surely, so (9.3) follows. QED.

By exchangeability, the fraction of compressible normals in the exponential base-hyperplane family tends to zero in probability without an exponential-family union bound.

# 10. New theorem: fractional coordinate-basis L2 smoothing

Let \(X=(\xi_1,\ldots,\xi_n)\in\mathbb C^n\) have independent coordinate densities with \(\|f_{\xi_j}\|_2\le K_2\). Let \(L:\mathbb C^n\to\mathbb C^r\) have full row rank.

Let \(\mathcal B\) be a family of invertible \(r\)-coordinate bases, with weights \(\theta_B\ge0\), \(\sum_B\theta_B=1\). Define coordinate loads
\[
\lambda_j=\sum_{B\ni j}\theta_B.
\]
Assume
\[
\boxed{\lambda_j\le1/2\quad\forall j.}
\tag{10.1}
\]

## `P12-COMPLEX-FRACTIONAL-BASE-L2-18` — PROVED

\[
\boxed{
\|f_{LX}\|_\infty
\le
K_2^{2r}
\prod_{B\in\mathcal B}|\det_{\mathbb C}L_B|^{-2\theta_B}.
}
\tag{10.2}
\]

### Proof

Let \(\Phi(t)=\prod_j\phi_j(l_j^*t)\). For a basis \(B\), define
\[
G_B(t)=\prod_{j\in B}|\phi_j(l_j^*t)|^2.
\]
Then
\[
\prod_BG_B(t)^{\theta_B}=\prod_j|\phi_j(l_j^*t)|^{2\lambda_j}\ge|\Phi(t)|
\]
because \(|\phi_j|\le1\) and \(2\lambda_j\le1\). Generalized Hölder gives
\[
\int|\Phi|\le\prod_B\left(\int G_B\right)^{\theta_B}.
\]
For one basis, the complex change of variables \(y=L_B^*t\) and planar Plancherel give
\[
\int G_B\le|\det L_B|^{-2}(4\pi^2K_2^2)^r.
\]
Taking the weighted geometric mean and applying Fourier inversion in \(2r\) real dimensions proves (10.2). QED.

### Projection-DPP corollary

If \(AA^*=I_r\) and \(q_B=|\det A_B|^2\), then \(\sum_Bq_B=1\) and the coordinate loads are the leverage scores \(\|a_j\|^2\). If every leverage is at most \(1/2\), choosing \(\theta_B=q_B\) gives
\[
\boxed{
\|f_{AX}\|_\infty\le K_2^{2r}e^{H(q)}\le K_2^{2r}\binom nr.
}
\tag{10.3}
\]

For the two-normal map, the remaining L2 geometry is now a fractional-basis problem: construct a distribution over nonzero 2x2 coordinate minors whose coordinate loads are at most 1/2 and whose determinant geometric mean is not too small. A star of nonzero minors around one coordinate violates this condition, matching the known obstruction to arbitrary-projection L2 smoothing.
