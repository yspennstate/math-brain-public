# 7. Lattice event and rank-k microscopic failure

\tag{7.1}

Among all four-category count vectors of total size `s`, the balanced vector maximizes the multinomial coefficient. There are at most `(s+1)^3` possible count vectors. Since their probabilities sum to one,

\boxed{p_s\ge(s+1)^{-3}.}
\tag{7.2}

The `k` group events are independent, so
