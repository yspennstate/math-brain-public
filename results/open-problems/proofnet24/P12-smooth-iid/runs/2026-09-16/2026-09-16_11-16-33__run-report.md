For every scalar `sigma`, each candidate submatrix satisfies

\[
\sigma_{\min}(\sigma A_T)=|\sigma|\sigma_{\min}(A_T).
\]

Taking the minimum over row candidates gives the identity

\[
\boxed{M_{m,r}(\sigma A)=|\sigma|M_{m,r}(A).}
\tag{2.1}

\[
\frac{k_m}{m}\log M_{m,r_m}(A_m)

\[
e^{-a_1n}.
\tag{5.2}

\[
D_\kappa^n

Now prescribe a large number `U_0`. Corollary 5.10 allows its lattice fraction to be made `U_0^{-n}` by shrinking the positive auxiliary parameter `vartheta`. Choose

\[
U_0\ge D_\kappa U

with `U` so large that

\[
(e/(cU))^n\le e^{-a_1n}.
\tag{5.3}

# 6. Uniform fourth moment supplies the fixed Fernandez hypotheses

Let `xi'` be an independent copy and put

\[
Z=\xi-\xi'.
\]

For centered variance-one real `xi` with `E xi^4<=B`,

\[
EZ^2=2,
\qquad
EZ^4=2E\xi^4+6\le2B+6.
\]

Paley--Zygmund applied to `Z^2` gives

\[
P\{|Z|\ge1\}\ge\frac1{2B+6}.
\tag{6.1}
\]

If

\[
p_y=P\{|\xi-y|\le1/4\},

then two independent copies in the same interval force `|Z|<=1/2`, so

\[
p_y^2\le1-P\{|Z|\ge1\}.

\[
\boxed{

# 8. Source-scale least-singular-value implication and exact remaining constant debt

Let `B_T=(A_m)_T` be an `n_m x m` candidate and `C_j` its columns. Let

\[
H_j=\operatorname{span}\{C_\ell:\ell\ne j\},
\qquad
E_j=H_j^\perp,
\qquad
\dim E_j=k_m.
\]

The negative second moment identity gives

\[
\sum_{j=1}^m\operatorname{dist}(C_j,H_j)^{-2}
=
\sum_{i=1}^m\sigma_i(B_T)^{-2}.
\tag{8.1}
\]

Thus

\[
\sigma_m(B_T)\le\tau
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\exists j:\operatorname{dist}(C_j,H_j)\le\sqrt m\,\tau.
\tag{8.2}
\]

Fernandez Corollary 4.3 converts an RLogLCD lower bound

\[
D(E_j)\gtrsim\sqrt{k_m}\,e^{c_Rm/k_m}
\]

into a quenched fresh-column projection estimate whose arithmetic radius floor is

\[
t_0\lesssim e^{-c_Rm/k_m}.
\tag{8.3}
\]

At the source lower-tail threshold

\[
\tau_m(\varepsilon)
=
\exp\!\left[-(h(\Gamma)+\varepsilon)\frac{m}{k_m}\right],
\tag{8.4}
\]

the normalized projection radius is

\[
t_m
=
\sqrt{m/k_m}\,\tau_m
=
\exp\!\left[-(h(\Gamma)+\varepsilon)\frac{m}{k_m}
+o(m/k_m)\right].
\tag{8.5}
\]

Therefore the current RLogLCD architecture proves the source **lower-extreme half** whenever the height constant can be certified to satisfy

\[
\boxed{c_R>h(\Gamma).}
\tag{8.6}
\]

If `(8.6)` holds, choose `0<epsilon<c_R-h(Gamma)`. Then the fresh-column probability is

\[
\le\exp[-(h(\Gamma)+\varepsilon)m+o(m)],
\]

which beats candidate entropy `exp[h(Gamma)m+o(m)]`. Larger fixed `epsilon` follow by monotonicity from a smaller one.

If `c_R<=h(Gamma)`, the additive arithmetic floor contributes only `e^{-c_Rm+o(m)}` after taking the `k_m`-dimensional product, and candidate entropy is not paid.

So the lower-side debt is now sharply separated:

\[
\boxed{
\text{structural reliability: PROVED at every fixed aspect;}
\qquad
\text{source-sharp height: OPEN.}
}
\tag{8.7}
\]

P13’s near-square bounded-fourth lower theorem remains valid: for each finite `B`, fix one moderate reliability target, obtain one positive `c_R(B)`, and use `h(Gamma)\downarrow0` as `Gamma\downarrow1` to get a nonempty aspect interval where `(8.6)` holds. The present run does not enlarge that interval quantitatively because the primary source does not expose a usable lower bound for `vartheta(U,b,K)`.

Choose a `[0,1]`-valued Lipschitz cutoff `phi` equal to one on `|t|<=1/(2sqrt2)` and zero on `|t|>=1/sqrt2`. Uniformly in unit `x`,

\[
E\phi(\langle C_1,x\rangle)\le1-q_B.
\tag{9.3}
\]

For the compressible class `Comp_N(alpha,eta)`, symmetrization and contraction reduce the empirical deviation to the Rademacher vector

\[
H=\frac1m\sum_{j=1}^m\varepsilon_jC_j.
\]

If `s=floor(alpha N)`, then deterministically

\[
\sup_{x\in\operatorname{Comp}}|\langle H,x\rangle|
\le
\max_{|J|\le s}\|H_J\|_2+\eta\|H\|_2.
\tag{9.4}
\]

Moreover

\[
EH_i^4
=
\frac{mE\xi^4+3m(m-1)}{m^4}
\le\frac{B+3}{m^2}.
\tag{9.5}
\]

Hence

\[
E\max_{|J|\le s}\|H_J\|_2
\le
(B+3)^{1/4}\alpha^{1/4}\sqrt{N/m},
\qquad
E\|H\|_2\le\sqrt{N/m}.
\tag{9.6}
\]

For `N/m` in a fixed bounded interval, choose fixed `alpha,eta>0` so small that the expected empirical-process deviation is `<q_B/4`. Changing one column changes the supremum by at most `1/m`; McDiarmid then gives failure at most

\[
\exp[-q_B^2m/8]
\]

up to harmless constant choices.

On the good event, every compressible `x` has empirical soft-slab mass at most `1-q_B/2`. But if

\[
A_{-j}^Tx=0,

then `m-1` of the `m` column inner products are exactly zero, so the empirical soft-slab mass is at least `(m-1)/m`, a contradiction for all large `m`.

Finally, a deleted-column null vector for any row candidate can be zero-extended to `R^N`; it then belongs to the corresponding global deleted-column kernel. A sparse approximant inside the candidate remains sparse after zero extension. Thus one global event indeed covers every candidate without an exponential candidate union.

Uniform B4 admits rare matrix-level spikes with non-exponentially-small full-operator-norm tails. The present proof deliberately uses expected Hilbert--Schmidt control plus regularized nets, not a global subgaussian norm event.

The present primary RLogLCD theorem is real. I do not infer a planar/complex analogue from formal similarity. Complex fixed-FC universality is retained from its separate proof; complex B4 arithmetic globalization remains OPEN.

### 12.5 Noncentered extrapolation rejected

Variance rescaling preserves the mean up to multiplication. None of the B4 statements here is promoted to noncentered laws.
