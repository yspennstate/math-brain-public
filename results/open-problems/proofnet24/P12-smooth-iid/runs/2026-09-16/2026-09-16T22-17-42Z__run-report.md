## 6. NEW-METHOD #26 — HUNT THE SPORADIC CASE: CAN PLANAR COVARIANCE COLLAPSE UNDER `(FC)`?

The most dangerous sporadic complex case would be a sequence of laws whose variance remains `E|xi|^2=1` but whose real `2 x 2` covariance becomes almost rank one, potentially invalidating a uniform planar local CLT. The following elementary derivation shows that **one fixed `(q,M)` itself prevents this collapse**. This is an audit self-check of the frozen assumptions, not a newly numbered P12 theorem.

Write `Sigma=Cov_R2(xi)` and let

\[
S_q=\xi_1+\cdots+\xi_q.
\]

Then `S_q` has planar density at most `M` and covariance `q Sigma`. Let `sigma^2` be the smaller eigenvalue of `q Sigma`, with unit eigenvector `e`; let `e_perp` be the orthogonal direction. Since

\[
\operatorname{tr}(q\Sigma)=q,
\]

we have

\[
\operatorname{Var}(e_\perp\cdot S_q)\le q.
\]

Chebyshev gives

\[
P\{|e\cdot S_q|\le2\sigma\}\ge\frac34,
\qquad
P\{|e_\perp\cdot S_q|\le2\sqrt q\}\ge\frac34.
\]

Hence their intersection has probability at least `1/2`. It lies in a rectangle of area

\[
(4\sigma)(4\sqrt q)=16\sigma\sqrt q.
\]

The density ceiling therefore forces

\[
\frac12\le16M\sigma\sqrt q,
\qquad
\sigma\ge\frac1{32M\sqrt q}.
\]

Thus

\[
\lambda_{\min}(q\Sigma)
\ge\frac1{1024M^2q},
\qquad
\boxed{
\lambda_{\min}(\Sigma)
\ge\frac1{1024M^2q^2}.
}
\tag{6.1}
\]

The zero-eigenvalue case is automatically excluded: a bounded planar density cannot put probability at least one-half in a zero-area rectangle.

There is a useful stability corollary for the existing proof architecture. Multiplication by a complex coefficient `c` is a real rotation followed by scaling by `|c|`, so

\[
\operatorname{Cov}_{\mathbb R^2}(c\xi)
\succeq
\frac{|c|^2}{1024M^2q^2}I_2.
\tag{6.2}
\]

For independent copies and coefficients `c_j`, covariance adds, giving

\[
\operatorname{Cov}_{\mathbb R^2}\!\left(\sum_j c_j\xi_j\right)
\succeq
\frac{\sum_j|c_j|^2}{1024M^2q^2}I_2.
\tag{6.3}
\]

In particular a normalized spread direction cannot lose one real dimension merely because the coordinate law is noncircular. This is exactly the kind of uniform ellipticity required by the planar local-CLT module, after the proof's clean-block/reveal operations are checked at their stated conditioning level.
