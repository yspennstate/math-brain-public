# 0. Executive result

The exact source target remains Conjecture 8.2, **“Smooth iid universality.”** Put

\[
M_{m,r}(A_m)
:=
\min_{\substack{T\subset[N_m]\\ |T|=m+r}}
\sigma_m((A_m)_T),
\qquad
h(\Gamma)
:=
\Gamma\log\Gamma-(\Gamma-1)\log(\Gamma-1).
\]

The source-certified recovery assumes iid **real** entries with

\[
E\xi=0,
\qquad
E\xi^2=1,
\]

a bounded sufficiently smooth density, and sufficiently many finite moments, and conjectures for every fixed integer `r>=0`, when `N_m/m -> Gamma>1`,

\[
\boxed{
\frac1m\log M_{m,r}(A_m)
\xrightarrow{\mathbb P}
-\frac{h(\Gamma)}{r+1}.
}
\tag{0.1}
\]

The recovered manuscript predicts the complex exponent

\[
-\frac{h(\Gamma)}{2(r+1)}.
\tag{0.2}
\]

The exact differentiability order and exact number of moments are not present on the currently accessible source surface, so they are **not guessed** here.

This run proves two new real results that materially sharpen the current P12 state.

## `P12-BD-ALLSURPLUS-LOWER-51` — NEW / PROVED

For the **lower extreme** (no candidate can be much more singular than the source scale), subgaussianity, smoothness, centering, variance normalization, fourth moments, RLogLCD, compressible/incompressible analysis, and a local CLT are all unnecessary.

Let `A_m` have iid real entries with a Lebesgue density `f_m` and put

\[
K_m=\|f_m\|_\infty.
\]

Assume

\[
N_m/m\to\Gamma>1,
\qquad
k_m=r_m+1,
\qquad
k_m\log m=o(m),
\tag{0.3}
\]

and, for a triangular family,

\[
\boxed{
\frac{k_m}{m}\log_+K_m\to0.
}
\tag{0.4}
\]

Then for every fixed `eps>0`,

\[
\boxed{
P\left\{
M_{m,r_m}(A_m)
\le
\exp\!\left[-(h(\Gamma)+\varepsilon)\frac{m}{k_m}\right]
\right\}\to0.
}
\tag{0.5}
\]

In particular a single fixed bounded-density law needs **no moment assumption and no centering assumption** for `(0.5)`.

The proof is a one-line conceptual replacement of the previous RLogLCD lower route by the current primary Rudelson--Vershynin projection-density theorem. For a candidate `B` of size

\[
n_m\times m,
\qquad
n_m=m+k_m-1,
\]

negative second moment gives

\[
\sigma_m(B)\le\tau
\Longrightarrow
\exists j:\ 
\operatorname{dist}(C_j,H_j)\le\sqrt m\,\tau.
\tag{0.6}
\]

Conditioning on the other columns leaves `C_j` fresh and

\[
\dim H_j^\perp=k_m.
\]

Rudelson--Vershynin Theorem 1.1 says that every `k_m`-dimensional orthogonal projection of a product vector with coordinate-density ceiling `K_m` has density at most `(C K_m)^{k_m}`. Therefore

\[
P\{\operatorname{dist}(C_j,H_j)\le s\mid A_{-j}\}
\le
(CK_m)^{k_m}v_{k_m}s^{k_m},
\tag{0.7}
\]

where `v_k` is the Euclidean `k`-ball volume. Since

\[
v_k\le(C/\sqrt k)^k,
\tag{0.8}
\]

union over all candidates and all `m` columns yields

\[
P\{M_{m,r_m}\le\tau\}
\le
\binom{N_m}{n_m}
\,m\,(CK_m)^{k_m}v_{k_m}
(\sqrt m\,\tau)^{k_m}.
\tag{0.9}
\]

With

\[
\tau=
\exp[-(h(\Gamma)+\varepsilon)m/k_m],
\]

and

\[
\log\binom{N_m}{n_m}=h(\Gamma)m+o(m),
\tag{0.10}
\]

the logarithm of the right-hand side is

\[
-\varepsilon m
+O(k_m\log_+K_m)
+O\!\left(k_m\log\frac{em}{k_m}\right)
+o(m)
=
-\varepsilon m+o(m).
\tag{0.11}
\]

This closes the real bounded-density lower half for the full moderate-surplus window. The previous conditional growing-surplus RLogLCD height requirement is still relevant to **density-free B4 laws**, but it is no longer an obligation inside the bounded-density smooth-iid source class.

## `P12-BD-B4-BASE-PARENT-INCOMP-51` — NEW / PROVED

The constructive upper extreme needs right-null normals of many overlapping `(m-1)`-row bases. This run proves a filtration-safe common-parent geometry theorem under bounded density plus a fourth moment.

Let

\[
B_m\in\mathbb R^{N_b\times m},
\qquad
N_b/m\to\beta\in(1,\infty),
\]

have iid entries satisfying

\[
E\xi^2=1,
\qquad
E|\xi|^4\le B,
\qquad
\|f_\xi\|_\infty\le K.
\tag{0.12}
\]

Centering is **not** needed for this parent-geometry statement. Fix any

\[
0<\delta<1.
\]

Then there exist fixed

\[
\alpha,\eta,c>0
\]

depending only on `(B,K,beta,delta)` such that with probability at least `1-e^{-cm}`, simultaneously for every row set

\[
A\subset[N_b],
\qquad
|A|\ge(1-\delta)m,
\]

every unit vector in the right kernel

\[
H_A:=\ker(B_m)_A
\]
has distance `>eta` from the unit vectors supported on at most `alpha m` coordinates:

\[
\boxed{
H_A\cap\operatorname{Comp}(\alpha,\eta)=\varnothing.
}
\tag{0.13}
\]

The event is measurable with respect to the base pool `B_m`; any disjoint reserved probe pool therefore stays exactly fresh.

The proof uses the sharp one-dimensional density ceiling from Rudelson--Vershynin together with the P12 soft-slab/Rademacher argument. For every unit `x`,

\[
\left\|f_{\langle R,x\rangle}\right\|_\infty
\le\sqrt2K.
\tag{0.14}
\]

Choose an upper aspect bound `beta_+` valid for large `m` and put

\[
q_*:=\frac{1-\delta}{\beta_+}>0.
\]

Choose `rho>0` so small that

\[
\sup_{\|x\|=1}P\{|\langle R,x\rangle|\le\rho\}
\le2\sqrt2K\rho
\le q_*/8.
\tag{0.15}
\]

Let `phi` be a `[0,1]` cutoff equal to one on `[-rho/2,rho/2]`, zero outside `[-rho,rho]`, and with Lipschitz constant at most `2/rho`. For the compressible class `C_m=Comp(alpha,eta)` define

\[
F_m
=
\sup_{x\in C_m}
\left[
\frac1{N_b}\sum_{i=1}^{N_b}
\phi(\langle R_i,x\rangle)
-E\phi(\langle R,x\rangle)
\right].
\]

Symmetrization and contraction give

\[
EF_m
\le
\frac8\rho
E\sup_{x\in C_m}|\langle H,x\rangle|
+\frac2{\sqrt{N_b}},
\qquad
H=\frac1{N_b}\sum_i\varepsilon_iR_i.
\tag{0.16}
\]

If `s=floor(alpha m)`, then deterministically

\[
\sup_{x\in C_m}|\langle H,x\rangle|
\le
\max_{|J|\le s}\|H_J\|_2
+\eta\|H\|_2.
\tag{0.17}
\]

Moreover

\[
EH_j^4
=
\frac{N_bE\xi^4+3N_b(N_b-1)(E\xi^2)^2}{N_b^4}
\le
\frac{B+3}{N_b^2},
\tag{0.18}
\]

so

\[
E\max_{|J|\le s}\|H_J\|_2
\le
s^{1/4}\left(\sum_jEH_j^4\right)^{1/4}
\le
C_{B,\beta}\alpha^{1/4},
\tag{0.19}
\]

and

\[
E\|H\|_2\le\sqrt{m/N_b}=O_\beta(1).
\tag{0.20}
\]

Choose fixed `alpha,eta` sufficiently small that the right side of `(0.16)` is at most `q_*/8` for all large `m`. Changing one row changes `F_m` by at most `1/N_b`, hence McDiarmid gives

\[
P\{F_m>q_*/4\}\le e^{-c_{B,K,\beta,\delta}m}.
\tag{0.21}
\]

On the complement, every compressible `x` has strictly fewer than `q_*N_b` rows in its `rho/2` slab. But if `x in ker(B_m)_A` with `|A|>=(1-delta)m`, then all rows in `A` lie in that slab, while

\[
|A|/N_b\ge q_*.
\]

This contradiction proves `(0.13)`.

The theorem trades a fixed bounded-density ceiling for the strict-subcritical fourth-moment restriction in earlier P12 residual-space results: for bounded-density B4 laws, any fixed overlap fraction `D/m<=delta<1` is allowed, at any finite base aspect `beta>1`.

# 4. Full proof of `P12-BD-ALLSURPLUS-LOWER-51`

Let

\[
n=m+k-1,
\qquad
k=k_m.
\]

Fix one row candidate `T`, `|T|=n`, and write

\[
B=(A_m)_T=[C_1\ \cdots\ C_m].
\]

Absolute continuity implies every `(n x (m-1))` deleted-column matrix has rank `m-1` almost surely, so for

\[
H_j=\operatorname{span}\{C_\ell:\ell\ne j\}
\]

we have

\[
\dim H_j^\perp=n-(m-1)=k.
\tag{4.1}
\]

The negative second-moment identity gives

\[
\sum_{i=1}^m\sigma_i(B)^{-2}
=
\sum_{j=1}^m
\operatorname{dist}(C_j,H_j)^{-2}.
\tag{4.2}
\]

If `sigma_m(B)<=tau`, then the left side is at least `tau^-2`. If every distance exceeded `sqrt(m) tau`, the right side would be strictly below `tau^-2`; hence `(0.6)`.

Conditional on all columns except `j`, `H_j^perp` is deterministic and `C_j` is a product vector with independent coordinate densities bounded by `K_m`. The primary projection-density theorem therefore yields `(0.7)`.

Union over `j` gives

\[
P\{\sigma_m(B)\le\tau\}
\le
m(CK_m)^kv_k(\sqrt m\,\tau)^k.
\tag{4.3}
\]

Union over all candidates gives `(0.9)`.

For `k log m=o(m)`, one has `k=o(m)` and Stirling entropy gives

\[
\log\binom{N_m}{m+k-1}
=h(\Gamma)m+o(m).
\tag{4.4}
\]

The unit-ball volume satisfies `(0.8)`, so the non-exponential prefactor in `(0.9)` contributes at most

\[
O(\log m)
+O(k\log_+K_m)
+O\!\left(k\log\frac{em}{k}\right)
=o(m)
\tag{4.5}
\]

under `(0.3)--(0.4)`. At the source-lower radius the `tau^k` term is exactly

\[
\exp[-(h(\Gamma)+\varepsilon)m].
\]

This proves `(0.5)`.

### What this theorem does and does not use

**Used:** real product structure, absolute continuity, density ceiling, candidate entropy, negative second moment.

**Not used:** centering, variance one, finite moments, smooth derivatives, subgaussian tails, operator norm, normal delocalization, RLogLCD, smoothing, truncation, Lindeberg replacement.

For a fixed law the density ceiling is fixed, so `(0.4)` is automatic. For a triangular family `(0.4)` is the exact density-growth condition appearing in this proof; it must not be silently omitted.

# 5. Full proof of `P12-BD-B4-BASE-PARENT-INCOMP-51`

The proof is already summarized in Section 0; here the quantifiers are checked carefully.

Let `beta_+>beta` be fixed such that for all large `m`,

\[
N_b\le\beta_+m.
\]

Set

\[
q_*=(1-\delta)/\beta_+.
\]

By the sharp density-of-sums theorem, `(0.14)` holds for every deterministic unit vector `x`. Choose

\[
\rho\le \frac{q_*}{16\sqrt2K}.
\tag{5.1}
\]

Then `(0.15)` holds.

For the empirical process, standard symmetrization and contraction with `psi=phi-phi(0)` give `(0.16)`. The `2/sqrt(N_b)` term is the Rademacher constant contribution from `phi(0)`.

For `H=N_b^{-1}sum eps_iR_i`, the fourth-moment calculation `(0.18)` is exact because the Rademacher signs make the summands centered even if `xi` itself is noncentered. For every `s`-set `J`,

\[
\|H_J\|_2
\le
s^{1/4}\left(\sum_{j=1}^mH_j^4\right)^{1/4},
\]

which gives `(0.19)` after Jensen. Thus fixed `alpha,eta` can be chosen so the expected empirical-process error is an arbitrarily small fixed fraction of `q_*`.

McDiarmid applies because replacing one row changes every empirical slab average by at most `1/N_b`; taking the supremum does not increase that bounded difference. Therefore `(0.21)` holds.

Finally, if a compressible unit `x` belongs to `ker(B_m)_A`, then

\[
\phi(\langle R_i,x\rangle)=1
\]

for every `i in A`, hence its empirical slab fraction is at least

\[
|A|/N_b
\ge
(1-\delta)m/N_b
\ge q_*.
\]

This contradicts the uniform empirical cap. The theorem follows.

### Counterexample check

The conclusion cannot hold for `delta=1` uniformly: a sufficiently high-dimensional subspace must contain sparse vectors by dimension counting. The theorem correctly requires one fixed `delta<1`, and its constants are allowed to deteriorate as `delta up 1`.

The theorem also does not claim a density-free B4 result at arbitrary aspect. Without the bounded-density population gap, earlier P12 exact fourth-moment thresholds show genuine collision obstructions.

# 6. Full proof of `P12-SMOOTH-COMMONPARENT-PROJECTIVE-51`

Fix an allowed parent `H_A` on the global event `(0.13)` and a fixed-dimensional subspace

\[
S\subset H_A,
\qquad
\dim S=d\le d_0.
\]

Let `W` have orthonormal rows spanning `S`. For any coordinate set `J` of size at most `alpha m` and any unit `a in R^d`, put

\[
x=W^Ta.
\]

Then `x` is a unit vector in `H_A`, so its distance from the sparse unit sphere exceeds `eta`. If `v=||x_{J^c}||`, normalization of `x_J` gives

\[
\operatorname{dist}(x,x_J/\|x_J\|)^2
=2-2\sqrt{1-v^2}.
\]

Therefore

\[
\boxed{
\|x_{J^c}\|_2^2
>\eta^2-\eta^4/4=\theta^2.
}
\tag{6.1}
\]

Choose `L_0>d_0/alpha` and let

\[
J=\{i:\|w_i\|_2^2>L_0/m\}.
\]

Because the leverage scores sum to `d`, `|J|<alpha m`. Applying `(6.1)` to every unit `a` gives `(0.23)`.

The covariance-subset theorem from `P12-SMOOTH-LOWLEVERAGE-LOCALCLT-50` now gives one set of constants uniform over every `A,S,m`.

For the pair statement, set

\[
R=\begin{pmatrix}u^T\\v^T\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
G=RR^T.
\]

Whitening `W=G^{-1/2}R` gives

\[
WW^T=I_2
\]

and leaves the row span equal to `span{u,v}`. Thus the whitened vector

\[
Z=WX
\]

has a uniformly bounded `C^1` density with a positive lower bound on one fixed neighborhood of the origin. Since

\[
Y=RX=G^{1/2}Z,
\]

the density transforms as

\[
f_Y(y)
=(\det G)^{-1/2}
f_Z(G^{-1/2}y),
\tag{6.2}
\]
