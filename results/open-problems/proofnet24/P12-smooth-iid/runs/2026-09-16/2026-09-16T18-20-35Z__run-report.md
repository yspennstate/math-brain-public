# 5. New-method move #29 — add the heavy-tail boundary and determine the exact fourth-moment assumption

To test whether a fourth moment is secretly needed by `(T-FC)`, move all the way to the heavy-tail boundary while keeping `q=1`.

## 5.1 Real exact witness

Define the smooth bounded density

\[
f_{\mathbb R}(x)=\frac{2}{\pi(1+x^2)^2},\qquad x\in\mathbb R. \tag{5.1}
\]

It is symmetric, hence centered, and

\[
\int_{\mathbb R}\frac{dx}{(1+x^2)^2}=\frac\pi2,
\]

so it is normalized. Also

\[
\|f_{\mathbb R}\|_\infty=\frac2\pi.
\]

Its variance is exactly one:

\[
\begin{aligned}
E X^2
&=\frac2\pi\int_{\mathbb R}\frac{x^2}{(1+x^2)^2}\,dx\\
&=\frac2\pi\left(\int_{\mathbb R}\frac{dx}{1+x^2}-\int_{\mathbb R}\frac{dx}{(1+x^2)^2}\right)\\
&=\frac2\pi\left(\pi-\frac\pi2\right)=1.
\end{aligned}
\tag{5.2}
\]

But

\[
E X^4=\frac2\pi\int_{\mathbb R}\frac{x^4}{(1+x^2)^2}\,dx=\infty,
\tag{5.3}
\]

because the integrand tends to `2/pi` at infinity. Thus this law is `C^infinity`, bounded-density, centered and variance-one, satisfies `(FC)` with `q=1,M=2/pi`, and has **no finite fourth moment**.

## 5.2 Complex exact witness

With Lebesgue area measure on `C`, define

\[
f_{\mathbb C}(z)=\frac{2}{\pi}(1+|z|^2)^{-3}. \tag{5.4}
\]

Radial symmetry gives mean zero. Polar coordinates show

\[
\int_{\mathbb C}f_{\mathbb C}(z)\,dz=1,
\qquad
E|Z|^2=1,
\qquad
\|f_{\mathbb C}\|_\infty=\frac2\pi,
\tag{5.5}
\]

while

\[
E|Z|^4
=2\pi\frac2\pi\int_0^\infty\frac{r^5}{(1+r^2)^3}\,dr
=\infty
\tag{5.6}
\]

(logarithmically), since after `s=r^2` the integrand behaves like `1/s`.

## 5.3 Exact conclusion for the rewrite

These are not counterexamples to `(T-FC)`; they are adversarial witnesses against **adding an unnecessary B4 hypothesis** to its statement. The frozen theorem, if accepted, genuinely reaches smooth bounded-density laws with finite second moment and infinite fourth moment.

Therefore the exact fourth-moment statement for the final rewrite is:

\[
\boxed{\text{No fourth-moment assumption is part of }(T\text{-FC});\quad E|\xi|^2<\infty\text{ is enough on the moment side.}}
\]

At the same time,

\[
\boxed{\text{B4 alone, without uniform microscopic smoothing, is not promoted to universality.}}
\]
