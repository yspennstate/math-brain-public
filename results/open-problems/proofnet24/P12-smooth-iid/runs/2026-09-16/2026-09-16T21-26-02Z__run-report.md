## 2. Frozen theorem and exact assumption boundary

The candidate-complete theorem remains unchanged. Let `F` be `R` or `C`, with real dimension `d_R=1`, `d_C=2`. Let `A_m` be `N_m x m` with iid centered variance-one entries having density `f_m`. Assume there exist **fixed** `q>=1` and `M<infinity` such that

\[
\sup_m \|f_m^{*q}\|_\infty\le M,
\qquad
N_m/m\to\gamma>1,
\qquad
k_m:=r_m+1,
\qquad
k_m\log m=o(m).
\tag{2.1}
\]

Then the frozen package asserts

\[
\boxed{
\frac{k_m}{m}\log M^{\mathbb F}_{m,r_m}(A_m)
\xrightarrow{\mathbb P}
-\frac{h(\gamma)}{d_{\mathbb F}},
\qquad
h(\gamma)=\gamma\log\gamma-(\gamma-1)\log(\gamma-1).
}
\tag{2.2}
\]

No fourth moment is assumed in (2.1). The historical P13 route instead imposed a uniform B4 bound for coarse geometry/RLogLCD work and did **not** complete the source-scale constructive upper extreme. That B4 route remains a preserved incomplete route, not the hypothesis of (2.2).

Scope freeze:

- **square / rectangular:** `r=0` square candidates are included; moderate surplus is allowed only through `k_m log m=o(m)`;
- **real / complex:** (2.2) is frozen for both `R` and `C`; the owner's exact source Conjecture 8.2 is real;
- **centered / noncentered:** centered is required; scaling never centers a nonzero mean;
- **variance:** normalized to one. If an unnormalized triangular law has scale `sigma_m`, then `M(sigma_m A)=|sigma_m|M(A)`, so exponent preservation additionally requires `(k_m/m)|log|sigma_m|| -> 0`;
- **density regularity:** only the uniform finite-convolution ceiling in (2.1) is used. For a fixed bounded-density law, take `q=1`, `M=||f||_infty`; differentiability is not needed by the frozen theorem;
- **anti-concentration:** for `q>1`, raw coordinate density or a raw `O(t^{d_F})` small-ball bound is not assumed and must not be silently inserted;
- **tails:** finite variance is present through normalization; subgaussianity and B4 are not theorem assumptions;
- **uniformity:** triangular laws require one fixed `q` and one uniform `M`. Per-size smoothness with constants deteriorating with `m` is outside the frozen theorem.

The accessible owner source file is
`paper19/sources/extracted_text/extreme_least_singular_values_radial_phase_retrieval_20260628_171349.txt`.
Its Conjecture 8.2 assumes a fixed real law with mean zero, variance one, bounded sufficiently smooth density, sufficiently many finite moments, fixed `r`, and predicts `(1/m) log M_{m,r} -> -h(gamma)/(r+1)`. The source does not specify the differentiability order or the exact number of moments in the accessible text, so this report does not invent them. Since bounded density gives (2.1) with `q=1`, the fixed-law source class is contained in (2.2); the unsupported enlargement is the arbitrary triangular/B4 class, not the fixed-law source class.

## 5. Move #12 — FIND WHAT IS CONSERVED

**SELF-CHECK ONLY.** The conserved quantity is the leading exponential slack.

For the lower extreme, under `k log m=o(m)`,

\[
\log\binom{N_m}{m+k_m-1}=h(\gamma)m+o(m).
\tag{5.1}
\]

At the lower-test scale

\[
\tau_m=\exp\!\left[-\frac{(h(\gamma)+\varepsilon)m}{d_{\mathbb F}k_m}\right],
\tag{5.2}
\]

the frozen projection small-ball bound contributes

\[
d_{\mathbb F}k_m\log\tau_m
=-(h(\gamma)+\varepsilon)m,
\tag{5.3}
\]

while all fixed-`q` basis-extraction, volume, and polynomial losses are `o(m)`. Thus the union bound retains `-epsilon m+o(m)` slack. This is a re-expression of the existing lower proof, not a new result.

For the constructive upper extreme, write the target exponent as `lambda=h(gamma)-epsilon`. Reserving a fixed `delta m` probe reservoir changes the base aspect ratio to `gamma-delta`. Because `h` is continuous on `(1,infinity)`, one can choose `delta>0` after `epsilon` so that `gamma-delta>1` and

\[
h(\gamma-\delta)>h(\gamma)-\varepsilon/4>\lambda.
\tag{5.4}
\]

The frozen proof then has a fixed positive order-`m` entropy margin available to absorb the rare-event cost, while its fixed-`q` smoothing loss and `k`-stage bookkeeping remain `O(k log m)=o(m)`. The order of quantifiers matters: `delta` is chosen after the fixed accuracy parameter `epsilon`, not uniformly for all `epsilon` at once.

**Outcome:** the audit ledger exposes one concrete P26 checkpoint: verify that every upper-branch thinning/reservoir step spends only the declared fixed `delta` entropy plus `o(m)`, never an unrecorded additional order-`m` loss.
