# 4. Half-cylinder K-homology vanishes

Let `K` be compact and consider

\[
T^+=K\times[0,\infty).
\]

Analytically,

\[
K_*^{\mathrm{lf}}(T^+)
=KK_*(C(K)\otimes C_0([0,\infty)),\mathbb C).
\]

The C*-algebra `C_0([0,infinity))` is contractible. An explicit homotopy from the identity to zero is obtained by translation to infinity.

For `0<=u<1`, set

\[
R_u=\frac{u}{1-u}
\]

and define

\[
(\beta_u g)(s)=g(s+R_u).
\]

Set `beta_1=0`. Each `beta_u` is a *-homomorphism. Norm continuity for `u<1` follows from uniform continuity of `C_0` functions; as `u->1`,

\[
\|\beta_u g\|
=\sup_{s\ge0}|g(s+R_u)|\to0
\]

because `g` vanishes at infinity.

Tensoring with `id_{C(K)}` gives a C*-homotopy from the identity on

\[
C(K)\otimes C_0([0,\infty))
\]

to zero. Homotopy invariance of KK/K-homology therefore gives

\[
\boxed{K_*^{\mathrm{lf}}(K\times[0,\infty))=0.}
\tag{4.1}
\]

The same proof gives

\[
\boxed{K_*^{\mathrm{lf}}(K\times(-\infty,0])=0.}
\tag{4.2}
\]

Translations of the endpoint do not change the statement.

This elementary calculation is the key point missed by the P40 detector package: compact data paired only with a half-line carries no locally finite K-homology class capable of detecting a halfspace preimage.

# 6. Proof of `P18-PROPER-HEIGHT-MV-FACTOR-41`

\[

\[

\[
\begin{CD}
K_0^{\mathrm{lf}}(Q) @>{\partial_Q}>> K_1^{\mathrm{lf}}(Q^0)\\

K_0^{\mathrm{lf}}(T) @>{\partial_T}>> K_1(T^0).
