\[

Equivalently, `\sigma` is the simplex on `C\times\{0,1\}`. Its boundary is denoted

\[
X=\partial\sigma.

The only points of `X` fixed by all of `C` are the two barycenters

\qquad

\tag{3.1}

X^C=F:=\{p_0,p_1\},
\tag{3.2}

and every nonfixed point of `X` has a proper stabilizer in `C`.

\tag{3.3}

# 4. `P18-P51-PROPER-FACE-COLLAPSE-NOGO-52` — PROVED

\[
K_0^C(X,P)
\stackrel?\cong
K_0^C(\tau_0,\partial\tau_0)
\oplus
K_0^C(\tau_1,\partial\tau_1).
\tag{4.1}
\]

That construction is impossible.

Choose any vertex `v\in\tau_1`. The face

\[
\rho_0:=\tau_0\cup\{v\}
\]

is a proper face of `\sigma`: it omits all the other vertices of `\tau_1`. Its setwise stabilizer is proper, because a nontrivial element carrying `v` to another vertex of the free regular orbit does not preserve `\rho_0`.

Therefore the `P` required for `(4.1)` is not the stated subcomplex, and P51 equation `(5.1)` does not follow.

This is a structural defect, not a sign convention.

# 9. Why isotropy-null does NOT yet prove source survival

The P25/P18 proper-family Wang theorem excludes a death relation that is genuinely realized inside a proper-family CW complex. Equation `(8.2)` says only that the **boundary class** has an abstract proper-family lift in the isotropy-separation exact sequence. \boxed{
