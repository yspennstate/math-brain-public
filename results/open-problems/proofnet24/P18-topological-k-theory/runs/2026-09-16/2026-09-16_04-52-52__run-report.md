# 3. Proof of `P18-ISOTROPY-CELL-PARITY-SPLIT-45`

Let

\[
X=A\cup_{\{\phi_\lambda\}}
\coprod_\lambda G/K_\lambda\times D^n,
\]

with each `K_λ` finite. Collapse `A` to a point. Equivariant excision gives a wedge decomposition of the relative quotient:

\[
X/A
\simeq_G
\bigvee_\lambda
G_+\wedge_{K_\lambda}S^n.
\tag{3.1}
\]

Because there are finitely many cell orbits, equivariant K-homology turns this finite wedge into a direct sum. Induction/Shapiro gives

\[
\widetilde K_j^G
\bigl(G_+\wedge_{K_\lambda}S^n\bigr)
\cong
\widetilde K_j^{K_\lambda}(S^n).
\tag{3.2}
\]

Suspension gives

\[
\widetilde K_j^{K_\lambda}(S^n)
\cong
K_{j-n}^{K_\lambda}(\mathrm{pt}).
\tag{3.3}
\]

Combining `(3.1)`–`(3.3)` proves `(0.2)`.

For finite `K_λ`, compact equivariant K-homology of a point is concentrated in even degree:

\[
K_{2r}^{K_\lambda}(\mathrm{pt})\cong R(K_\lambda),
\qquad
K_{2r+1}^{K_\lambda}(\mathrm{pt})=0.
\tag{3.4}
\]

If `n` is even, `(3.4)` gives `(0.4)`. The pair exact sequence contains

\[
K_1^G(X,A)
\longrightarrow
K_0^G(A)
\longrightarrow
K_0^G(X),
\tag{3.5}
\]

so the vanishing of the left term proves `(0.5)`. It also contains

\[
K_0^G(X,A)
\xrightarrow{\partial_{\rm cell}}
K_1^G(A)
\longrightarrow
K_1^G(X)
\longrightarrow
K_1^G(X,A)=0,
\tag{3.6}
\]

which proves `(0.6)` and `(0.7)`.

If `n` is odd, `(3.4)` gives `(0.8)`. Now

\[
K_0^G(X,A)=0
\longrightarrow
K_1^G(A)
\longrightarrow
K_1^G(X)
\tag{3.7}
\]

proves `(0.9)`, while

\[
K_1^G(X,A)
\xrightarrow{\partial_{\rm cell}}
K_0^G(A)
\longrightarrow
K_0^G(X)
\longrightarrow
K_0^G(X,A)=0
\tag{3.8}
\]

proves `(0.10)` and `(0.11)`.

Finally, take a skeletal filtration

\[
X^{(0)}\subset X^{(1)}\subset\cdots\subset X^{(N)}.
\]

At the first stage where a class disappears, apply the one-step result to

\[
(X^{(q)},X^{(q-1)}).
\]

A `K_0` class cannot disappear at an even `q` because `(0.5)` is injective; a `K_1` class cannot disappear at an odd `q` because `(0.9)` is injective. This proves `(0.12)`–`(0.13)`.

No assumption on stable rank, cancellation, matrix size, or analytic assembly is present: this is a source-side generalized-homology calculation.

# 4. Proof of `P18-COMPLEX-REPRESENTATION-ATTACHMENT-45`

Let `W` be a finite-dimensional complex representation of a finite subgroup `K≤G`. For the induced pair

\[
(X,A)=G\times_K(D(W),S(W)),
\]

induction/Shapiro gives

\[
K_j^G(X,A)
\cong
K_j^K(D(W),S(W)).
\tag{4.1}
\]

Complex equivariant Bott–Thom periodicity gives a canonical degree shift by the real dimension of `W`:

\[
K_j^K(D(W),S(W))
\cong
K_{j-2d}^K(\mathrm{pt}),
\qquad d=\dim_\mathbb C W.
\tag{4.2}
\]

Since `2d` is even and `K` is finite,

\[
K_1^K(\mathrm{pt})=0,
\]

hence `(0.16)` follows. The long exact sequence of `(X,A)` then gives the injectivity `(0.17)`.

For finitely many disjoint induced representation-disk attachments, excision replaces the one relative group by a finite direct sum and the same conclusion holds.

The theorem does **not** say the attachment is harmless. The even relative group is

\[
K_0^G(X,A)\cong R(K),
\]

so the connecting homomorphism can have nonzero image in `K_1^G(A)`. This point is essential for compatibility with the rotating-disk counterexample from P18-P44.

# 5. Proof of the finite representation-ring killer certificate

Consider one even-dimensional standard proper cell

\[
e_\lambda=G/K_\lambda\times D^{2d_\lambda}
\]

attached by

\[
\phi_\lambda:
G\times_{K_\lambda}S^{2d_\lambda-1}\to A.
\]

Let

\[
q_\lambda:
(D^{2d_\lambda},S^{2d_\lambda-1})
\to D^{2d_\lambda}/S^{2d_\lambda-1}
\cong S^{2d_\lambda}
\]

be the quotient. Under Bott periodicity, every relative class is the Bott generator twisted by a unique virtual representation

\[
r_\lambda\in R(K_\lambda).
\]

Write this class as

\[
\beta_{\lambda,r_\lambda}
\in
K_0^{K_\lambda}(D^{2d_\lambda},S^{2d_\lambda-1}).
\]

The boundary in the pair exact sequence is natural under maps of pairs. Therefore the boundary of the induced relative class is the pushforward, along the attaching map, of the sphere boundary of the corresponding Bott class:

\[
\partial_{\rm cell}(r_\lambda)
=
(\phi_\lambda)_*\bigl(
\partial\beta_{\lambda,r_\lambda}
\bigr).
\tag{5.1}
\]

For finitely many cells, additivity gives

\[
\partial_{\rm cell}((r_\lambda)_\lambda)
=
\sum_\lambda
(\phi_\lambda)_*
\bigl(
\partial\beta_{\lambda,r_\lambda}
\bigr).
\tag{5.2}
\]

Exactness of `(3.6)` then says precisely that

\[
\eta\mapsto0\text{ in }K_1^G(X)
\]

if and only if `η` is equal to the right side of `(5.2)` for some finite virtual-representation vector `(r_λ)`. This proves `(0.19)`.

This is the explicit certificate form requested by the P18-P44 next attack. A future computation need not guess a global topological nullhomotopy: it can enumerate the finite cell stabilizers, write the representation-ring generators, and compute the attaching-map boundary matrix.
