# 3. Proof of `P18-FREE-ORIGIN-ISOTROPY-SEPARATION-44`

## 3.1 The free approximation class

Because `P` is free, there exists a `C`-map

\[
s:P\to EC.
\]

Any two such maps are `C`-homotopic because `EC` is terminal up to `C`-homotopy among free `C`-CW complexes. Hence the class

\[
\widehat x_f=(s,f)_*x
\]

is independent of `s`.

The diagonal action on `EC\times Y` is free because the `EC` coordinate is free. Thus free-action change of groups gives

\[
K_1^C(EC\times Y)
\cong
K_1((EC\times Y)/C).
\]

Let

\[
p:(EC\times Y)/C\to BC
\]

be induced by projection to `EC`.

The composite

\[
C\backslash P
\xrightarrow{(s,f)/C}
(EC\times Y)/C
\xrightarrow{p}
BC
\]

is `s/C`, which classifies the principal bundle `P->C\backslash P`. Therefore it is homotopic to `lambda`, and

\[
p_*(\widehat x_f/C)
=\lambda_*(x/C)
=\xi\ne0.
\]

This proves `(0.1)` and in particular

\[
\widehat x_f\ne0.
\]

## 3.2 Isotropy separation

Take the universal free-space cofiber sequence

\[
EC_+\to S^0\to\widetilde EC.
\]

Smash with `Y_+`. Since

\[
EC_+\wedge Y_+\cong(EC\times Y)_+,
\]

we obtain a cofiber sequence of based `C`-spaces

\[
(EC\times Y)_+
\to Y_+
\to \widetilde EC\wedge Y_+.
\]

Complex equivariant K-homology is a 2-periodic equivariant generalized homology theory, so the long exact sequence contains

\[
K_0^C(\widetilde EC\wedge Y_+)
\xrightarrow{\partial_{\rm iso}}
K_1^C(EC\times Y)
\xrightarrow{\varepsilon_*}
K_1^C(Y).
\]

The map `epsilon` is projection

\[
EC\times Y\to Y.
\]

Therefore

\[
\varepsilon_*(\widehat x_f)
=\varepsilon_*(s,f)_*x
=f_*x.
\]

Exactness yields `(0.3)`.

Because `\widehat x_f` is nonzero, any preimage under `\partial_iso` is itself nonzero. This proves `(0.4)`.

## 3.3 Why the obstruction is exactly isotropy-supported

For the trivial subgroup,

\[
(EC)^e\simeq *,
\]

so the nonequivariant cofiber

\[
(\widetilde EC)^e
\]

is contractible.

For any nontrivial subgroup `K<=C`,

\[
(EC)^K=\varnothing.
\]

Taking `K`-fixed points in the cofiber sequence therefore gives

\[
(\widetilde EC)^K\simeq S^0.
\]

After smashing with `Y_+`, the nontrivial fixed-point sectors of the cofiber are precisely the fixed-point sectors of `Y`, while the trivial-subgroup sector vanishes.

If `Y` is free, then `Y^K=\varnothing` for every `K ne e`, hence `\widetilde EC\wedge Y_+` is equivariantly contractible. The exact sequence then makes

\[
K_1^C(EC\times Y)\to K_1^C(Y)
\]

injective on all classes, and in particular

\[
f_*x\ne0.
\]

This recovers the conceptual content of P18-P43 from a standard isotropy-separation sequence and explains exactly why that argument stops at the first singular stratum.
