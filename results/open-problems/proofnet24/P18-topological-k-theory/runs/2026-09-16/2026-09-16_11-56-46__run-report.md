## 4.1 `C`-CW structure of the sign circle

On

the kernel `N` acts trivially and `C/N\cong C_2` acts freely by the antipodal half-turn. A one-dimensional `C`-CW structure therefore has one orbit of zero-cells and one orbit of one-cells, both of orbit type `C/N`.

Since `C` is abelian, the attaching translation acts trivially by conjugation on `R(N)`. The equivariant cellular chain differential in degree one is therefore zero. Thus, compatibly with the periodic K-homology AHSS,

\[
K_0^C(S(W))\cong R(N),
\qquad
K_1^C(S(W))\cong R(N).
\tag{4.1}
\]

## 4.2 The zero-cell map is induction

The disk `D(W)` is `C`-equivariantly contractible to its fixed center, so

\[
K_0^C(D(W))=R(C),
\qquad
K_1^C(D(W))=0.
\]

Under `(4.1)`, the map

\[
K_0^C(S(W))\to K_0^C(D(W))
\]

is induction from the isotropy group `N` to `C`:

\[
\operatorname{Ind}_N^C:R(N)\to R(C).
\]

If `eta=chi|_N`, then

\[
\operatorname{Ind}_N^C(\eta^j)
=\chi^j+\chi^{j+r}
=\chi^j(1+\varepsilon).
\tag{4.2}
\]

These character pairs have disjoint support, so induction is injective and

\[
\operatorname{im}(\operatorname{Ind}_N^C)
=(1+\varepsilon)R(C).
\tag{4.3}
\]

## 4.3 Euler map and pair boundary

Complex Bott periodicity identifies

\[
K_0^C(D(W),S(W))\cong R(C).
\]

Under the Thom identification, the map from the fixed center into the Thom pair is multiplication by the complex Euler class

\[
1-\varepsilon
\]

up to the standard harmless character-unit convention. Its kernel is exactly `(1+epsilon)R(C)`, agreeing with `(4.3)`.

The pair exact sequence therefore gives a surjection

\[
\partial_W:R(C)\twoheadrightarrow R(N)
\]

with kernel

\[
(1-\varepsilon)R(C).
\tag{4.4}
\]

But restriction

\[
\operatorname{Res}_N^C:R(C)\to R(N)
\]

is also surjective, and

\[
\ker(\operatorname{Res}_N^C)
=(1-\varepsilon)R(C),
\tag{4.5}
\]

because `chi^j` and `chi^{j+r}` have the same restriction and these are the only identifications.

Both maps are `R(C)`-linear, with the target module structure on `R(N)` through restriction. Hence they differ by an `R(N)`-module automorphism, i.e. multiplication by a unit:

\[
\boxed{
\partial_W(q)=u\,\operatorname{Res}_N^C(q),
\qquad u\in R(N)^\times.
}
\tag{4.6}

For `r=1`, `N` is trivial and `(4.6)` is exactly the P46 formula

\[
