# 3. Canonical monomial perturbation lemma

Let

`U=(U_1,...,U_d)`, `V=(V_1,...,V_d)`

be two tuples of unitaries on the same Hilbert space. For `m=(m_1,...,m_d) in Z^d`, use the fixed ordered monomials

`U^m=U_1^(m_1)...U_d^(m_d)`,

and similarly for `V^m`.

## Lemma 3.1 — monomial telescope

Let

`gamma=max_j ||U_j-V_j||`.

Then

`||U^m-V^m|| <= |m|_1 gamma`.

### Proof

For one unitary pair and integer `n`,

`||U^n-V^n|| <= |n| ||U-V||`.

For positive `n`, telescope

`U^n-V^n = sum_{k=0}^{n-1} U^k(U-V)V^(n-1-k)`.

For negative `n`, use

`||U^{-1}-V^{-1}||=||U^*(V-U)V^*||=||U-V||`

and apply the positive case to inverses.

Now telescope the ordered product one generator at a time. Every unaffected factor is unitary, so the norm contributions add to

`sum_j |m_j| ||U_j-V_j|| <= |m|_1 gamma`.

QED.

This proof does not use commutativity of either tuple and therefore works for noncommutative tori with different multipliers.

# 4. Quantitative Wiener spectral modulus

Let `sigma,sigma'` be two multiplier matrices and choose Gao's canonical generator tuples on the common Hilbert space. Define

`gamma_G(sigma,sigma')=max_j ||U_j(sigma)-U_j(sigma')||`.

Let

`H_sigma=sum_m A_m(sigma) tensor U_sigma^m`

and

`H_sigma'=sum_m A_m(sigma') tensor U_sigma'^m`,

where the coefficient matrices can have any fixed finite matrix size.

Assume

`D_0 = sum_m ||A_m(sigma)-A_m(sigma')|| < infinity`

and

`W_1(sigma)=sum_m |m|_1 ||A_m(sigma)|| < infinity`,

`W_1(sigma')=sum_m |m|_1 ||A_m(sigma')|| < infinity`.

Absolute convergence makes both series norm-convergent.

## Theorem 4.1 — `P18/P17-CANONICAL-WIENER-SPECTRAL-MODULUS-25`

One has

`||H_sigma-H_sigma'|| <= D_0 + gamma_G min{W_1(sigma),W_1(sigma')}`.

If both elements are self-adjoint, then

`d_H(spec(H_sigma),spec(H_sigma')) <= D_0 + gamma_G min{W_1(sigma),W_1(sigma')}`.

All constants are independent of coefficient matrix size.

### Proof

Use the decomposition

`H_sigma-H_sigma'`

`= sum_m (A_m(sigma)-A_m(sigma')) tensor U_sigma'^m`

`  + sum_m A_m(sigma) tensor (U_sigma^m-U_sigma'^m)`.

The first sum has norm at most `D_0`, because every monomial is unitary. Lemma 3.1 bounds the second by

`gamma_G W_1(sigma)`.

Interchanging the roles of `sigma` and `sigma'` gives the same estimate with `W_1(sigma')`; take the smaller one.

For self-adjoint operators on one Hilbert space, the Hausdorff spectral distance is at most the operator-norm distance. Since Gao's tuples are canonical, the represented spectra are the spectra in the abstract noncommutative tori.

Tensoring with `M_N` changes neither the generator estimate nor the argument, so the bound is matrix-stable.

QED.

## Gao parameter form

Theorem 4.2 gives

`gamma_G(sigma,sigma') <= C_G max_j sum_k |sigma_jk-sigma'_jk|^(1/2)`.

If

`sigma_jk=exp(2 pi i Theta_jk)`

and `d_T` denotes circular distance modulo integers, then

`|sigma_jk-sigma'_jk| <= 2 pi d_T(Theta_jk,Theta'_jk)`.

Hence

`gamma_G <= C_G sqrt(2 pi) max_j sum_k d_T(Theta_jk,Theta'_jk)^(1/2)`.

The square-root modulus is expected: norm-continuous faithful generator realizations of irrational rotations are only one-half Hölder in general.

# 5. Exact finite-Fourier gap certificate

The previous theorem is useful only if the infinite source Hamiltonian can be reduced to certified finite data. The following statement makes the needed obligations explicit.

Let `sigma_s`, `0<=s<=1`, be a continuous multiplier path. Let `h_s` be self-adjoint elements in the corresponding tori. Choose one finite support `F subset Z^d` and polynomials

`P_s=sum_{m in F} A_m(s) U_{sigma_s}^m`

with certified errors

`||h_s-P_s|| <= tau_s`.

At the reference endpoint let

`delta_0=dist(E,spec(P_0))`.

The word **spectrum** here means the full spectrum of the polynomial in the rational/reference noncommutative torus, equivalently in a faithful representation. A single periodic matrix at one Bloch/central character is not automatically sufficient.

Define

`D_0(s)=sum_{m in F} ||A_m(s)-A_m(0)||`,

`W_1(s)=sum_{m in F} |m|_1 ||A_m(s)||`,

and

`M_s=D_0(s)+gamma_G(sigma_s,sigma_0) min{W_1(s),W_1(0)}`.

## Theorem 5.1 — `P18/P17-FINITE-FOURIER-GAP-CERTIFICATE-25`

If

`delta_0>0`

and

`sup_s [tau_s+M_s] < delta_0`,

then

`dist(E,spec(h_s)) >= delta_0-tau_s-M_s > 0`

for all `s`.

Consequently the Fermi projections

`p_s=1_{(-infinity,E]}(h_s)`

form a continuous projection field, and the K0 class at `s=0` transports unchanged to `s=1`.

### Proof

Realize the polynomials at all parameters in Gao's common faithful canonical representation. By Theorem 4.1,

`||P_s-P_0|| <= M_s`.

Therefore

`||h_s-P_0|| <= ||h_s-P_s||+||P_s-P_0|| <= tau_s+M_s`.

Self-adjoint spectral perturbation gives

`dist(E,spec(h_s)) >= dist(E,spec(P_0))-||h_s-P_0||`.

The assumed strict inequality proves the gap.

The Prodan–Shmalo continuous-field statement then applies along the entire parameter path: while the common Fermi energy remains outside the spectrum, functional calculus produces a continuous field of Fermi projections. Thus the endpoint projections represent the transported K0 class.

QED.

## Matrix/stabilization audit

If the Hamiltonian has `N` internal degrees of freedom, the coefficients `A_m` lie in `M_N`. The proof is unchanged. No stable-rank or cancellation theorem is required: the conclusion is equality under the continuous-field K0 transport, not equality of finite-rank representatives inside one fixed matrix algebra.

# 6. What a rigorous rational-fiber certificate must actually certify

The manuscript's rational approximants are essential computationally, but a rigorous proof needs one more distinction.

For rational noncommutative tori, a small finite-dimensional irreducible representation is generally one fiber over residual central/Bloch parameters. Its spectrum need not equal the full C*-spectrum unless the representation used is faithful or the residual parameter family has been exhausted.

Therefore a usable `delta_0` in Theorem 5.1 can be supplied by any of the following:

1. a faithful representation with interval arithmetic;
2. a Bloch/central-parameter family whose eigenvalue intervals are enclosed uniformly over the full parameter torus;
3. a direct resolvent certificate in the abstract rational torus;
4. an equivalent faithful crossed-product computation.

A plotted gap in one finite periodic matrix is not, by itself, a proof of `delta_0>0` for the full rational C*-fiber.

This is the precise missing source material in the current repository for Figures 8.2/8.3.

# 8. Coefficient drift for Example 4.3.3

The source coefficient also changes with `theta`. The derivative is controlled explicitly before Fourier expansion.

For `q=(q_1,q_2)` and a path in `theta`, differentiating the displacement gives

`|partial_{theta_j} p_-q| <= r |q_j|`.

Since the function `x -> exp(-|x|)` is 1-Lipschitz after multiplication by the local exponential factor away from the origin, and the previous lower bound keeps `q!=0` uniformly away from zero, one obtains

`||partial_{theta_j} w_q||_infinity <= r |q_j| exp(-c ||q||_infinity)`.

Consequently, along a straight parameter segment,

`||w_q(theta)-w_q(theta')||_infinity`

`<= r (|q_1||Delta theta_1|+|q_2||Delta theta_2|) exp(-c||q||_infinity)`.

On the max-norm shell `M`,

`sum_{||q||_infinity=M} (|q_1|+|q_2|)=12M^2`,

and separately

`sum_{shell M}|q_1|=sum_{shell M}|q_2|=6M^2`.

Thus the total crossed-product coefficient drift obeys

`sum_q ||w_q(theta)-w_q(theta')||_infinity`

`<= 6r (|Delta theta_1|+|Delta theta_2|) sum_{M>=1} M^2 e^{-cM}`

`= 6r (|Delta theta_1|+|Delta theta_2|) x(1+x)/(1-x)^3`.

For the Figure 8.3 values and `r=0.4`, the exact parameter errors are approximately

`|Delta theta_1| = 1.2331889014e-5`,

`|Delta theta_2| = 1.3950299557e-4`.

The above very coarse coefficient-drift bound is about `9.11e-2`. It is therefore rigorous but probably too wasteful to certify all visible gaps without sharpening.

This calculation is useful because it separates two analytically different contributions:

1. coefficient drift of the physical hopping amplitudes;
2. deformation of the noncommutative-torus multiplication/generator tuple, handled by Gao's one-half-Hölder theorem.

Neither may be silently dropped.
