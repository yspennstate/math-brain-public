\[

## 15.1 Latała — uniform fourth moment and coarse operator norm

Primary source:

Rafał Latała, *Some estimates of norms of random matrices*,  
Proc. Amer. Math. Soc. **133** (2005), 1273–1282, DOI `10.1090/S0002-9939-04-07800-1`.

For independent mean-zero entries, Latała gives the nonasymptotic expectation bound
\[
E\|A\|
\le
C\left[
\max_i\sqrt{\sum_j EA_{ij}^2}
+
\max_j\sqrt{\sum_i EA_{ij}^2}
+
\left(\sum_{ij}E|A_{ij}|^4\right)^{1/4}
\right].
\tag{L1}
\]
Thus bounded aspect ratio, variance one and a **uniform** fourth-moment bound imply
\[
E\|A_m\|=O(\sqrt m),

\]
