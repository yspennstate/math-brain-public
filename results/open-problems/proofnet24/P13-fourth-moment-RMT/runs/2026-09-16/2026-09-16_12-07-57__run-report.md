\[

\kappa_T=\kappa_0^{N_m/n_m}.
\tag{7.1}

\[
\prod_{i\in T}\alpha_i
\ge

=
\kappa_T^{-n_m}.
\tag{7.2}

\[
\alpha_T\in\Omega_{\kappa_T}.

\tag{7.3}

\[
B_{\kappa_T}(C_{j,T})

which is `(0.11)`.

# 11. Route comparison / proof-role separation

## 11.1 Truncation / replacement

Polynomial truncation plus Weyl remains invalid at the source scale: a polynomial perturbation is vastly larger than `exp[-Theta(m/k)]`. None of the new RLogLCD corrections uses such a perturbation. Row-aware trimming remains potentially useful only for the constructive upper branch.

## 11.2 Operator norm

Uniform B4 gives coarse `O_P(sqrt(m))` norm tightness, but the retained rare-spike theorem shows there is no uniform subgaussian-style `e^{-cm}` full-matrix norm event over triangular B4 laws. The regularized-HS machinery is specifically designed to avoid this need.

The new row-restriction theorem is sharper still: it globalizes the relevant weighted Hilbert--Schmidt event over all row candidates before candidate selection.

## 11.3 Bai--Yin

Bai--Yin is a macroscopic fixed-law spectral-edge theorem under finite fourth moment. It remains useful for one fixed law and coarse norms, but does not provide source-scale triangular anti-concentration or the candidate-uniform arithmetic structure needed here.

## 11.4 Lindeberg

Generic low-moment matrix Lindeberg remains nonprimary. Exact row relations, narrow clusters, and Rademacher arithmetic can change while low moments remain similar. Any replacement strong enough here would have to control the same microscopic structure the moment-only problem is testing.

## 11.5 Robust small ball / leave one out

This remains the live lower route. Conditioning on all columns except one leaves a genuinely fresh iid product column. Law-adapted RLogLCD converts the arithmetic of the adaptive nullspace into `t^k` small-ball power plus the arithmetic height floor. The current run corrects the net reliability bookkeeping without changing this architecture.

## 11.6 Delocalization

Classical incompressibility is globally available under uniform B4, but deterministic spread remains insufficient: the retained flat-Rademacher resonant-cap theorem gives highly delocalized vectors with only polynomial RLogLCD. Random-kernel structure is essential.

## 11.7 Expectation versus high probability

The retained rare-contamination example shows that correct convergence in probability does not imply normalized logarithmic expectation convergence, even under smooth compact support, uniform subgaussianity, and uniform B4. Expectation requires separate deep-scale concentration / uniform integrability.

## 11.8 Finite fourth moment versus uniformly bounded fourth moment

The distinction is exact. Uniform B4 gives `(8.2)` and uniform coarse geometry. A triangular family can have finite fourth moment at every level while `E|xi_m|^4->infinity`; the retained sparse heavy-tail example then destroys the source exponent.
