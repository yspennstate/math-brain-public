# HARD10-04 — same-run erratum

This append-only erratum corrects a notation typo in

`proofnet24/HARD10-04/runs/2026-09-16/2026-09-16T00-30-04Z__report.md`

at commit `f1c234eaca683925df2103297e9f74bf54c09183`.

In equation (3.3), the term printed as
\[
\nu^{-1}+Cu^{-2}
\]
should read
\[
\boxed{u^{-1}+Cu^{-2}.}
\]

The intended argument is unchanged: the block begins with at most \(u^{-1}\) sites of harmonic measure at least \(u\), and at most \(Cu^{-2}\) additional hot sites can be born during the unit-capacity block. Hence
\[
|\mathcal H_j(u)|\le u^{-1}+Cu^{-2}\le C'u^{-2},
\qquad 0<u\le1.
\]

No other claim or formula in the supplement is changed by this correction.
