Fix

\tag{3.1}

\[
h:=R^{-a},
\qquad
M:=\lfloor R\rfloor,
\qquad
\ell:=\frac{1-h}{M}.
\tag{3.2}
\]
The active probability vector consists of one distinguished **hot lineage** of mass \(h\) and \(M\) **diffuse lineages**, each of mass \(\ell\):
\[
p=(h,\ell,\ldots,\ell),
\qquad h+M\ell=1.
\tag{3.3}
\]

At every particle step:

1. select one active lineage according to its current mass;
2. retire its current active lattice label \(x\);
3. create a fresh child label \(x'\) at a nearest-neighbor unused lattice site;
4. give the child exactly the same mass as its parent;
5. declare the capacity increment to be
   \[
   \Delta C=p(x)^2.
   \tag{3.4}
   \]

All unselected active labels and masses remain unchanged. Consequently the multiset of active masses is stationary within the block.

This is an abstract growth law. The lattice embedding and genealogy below are genuine nearest-neighbor / one-use geometric objects, but (3.3) is **not claimed to equal random-walk harmonic measure of that geometry**.

## 4. Correct cubic scale for arbitrary \(D\)

The moments of the active law are
\[
S_q:=h^q+M\ell^q.
\tag{4.1}
\]
For the cubic moment,
\[
S_3
=R^{-3a}+(1+o(1))R^{-2}
=R^{-D}+R^{-2+o(1)}.
\tag{4.2}

Hence, uniformly for \(D\in[3/2,2]\),
\[
\boxed{S_3=\Theta(R^{-D}).}
\tag{4.3}

(The two terms are of the same order at \(D=2\).)

Because a particle of mass \(p_i\) is selected with probability \(p_i\) and contributes \(p_i^2\), the predictable capacity drift per particle is exactly
\[
\mathbb E[\Delta C]=\sum_i p_i^3=S_3.
\tag{4.4}
\]
Choose a deterministic block length
\[
N_R:=\left\lfloor S_3^{-1}\right\rfloor.
\tag{4.5}
\]
Then
\[
\boxed{N_R=R^{D+o(1)}.}
\tag{4.6}
\]
Thus the construction realizes every candidate particle-per-capacity exponent \(D\in[3/2,2]\).

### Capacity concentration

The one-step second moment of the capacity increment is
\[
\mathbb E[(\Delta C)^2]
=\sum_i p_i^5
=S_5.
\tag{4.7}

Now
\[
N_RS_5
\lesssim
R^{3a}R^{-5a}

# 16. Concrete next attack: a realizability / shielding inequality

The countermodel's hot lineage repeatedly regenerates a mass
\[

These are now the strongest next targets.
