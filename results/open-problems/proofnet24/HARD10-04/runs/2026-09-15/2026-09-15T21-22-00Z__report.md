The full target remains **OPEN**: this run does not prove that

converges almost surely, does not determine \(\beta\), and does not refute existence/determinism.

This run makes a substantive advance on the screening/renormalization route. The immediately preceding checkpoint introduced, for an old growth shell, an ancestry posterior
\[
f_{k,n}(Y_n)=\mathbb P(A_k\mid Y_n)
\]
and its screening variance \(V_{k,n}\), then reduced two-time lineage correlations to decay of that variance. The previous report left even \(V_{k,\infty}=0\) as open.

Here I prove a **uniform quantitative screening theorem** using two classical, shape-uniform planar-random-walk inputs:

1. quantitative convergence of finite-set entrance measure to harmonic measure from infinity;
2. the discrete Beurling annular-penetration estimate.

If \(r\) is the radius of the old closure \(K_m\), \(R\) is the radius of a later closure \(K_n\), and \(Q=R/r\), then for every bounded observable \(g\) of the old genealogy state and every \(p\ge1\),
\[
\mathbb E\Big|\mathbb E[g(Y_m)\mid Y_n]-\mathbb E g(Y_m)\Big|^p
\le
C_p(1+\log^p Q)Q^{-p/(2p+1)}.
\]
In particular, for the old-shell ancestry bit,
\[
\boxed{
V_{k,n}
\le
C(1+\log^2 Q)Q^{-2/5}.
}
\]
Thus
\[
\boxed{V_{k,\infty}=0}
\]
for every old shell, uniformly over all DLA geometries.

Using
\[
\operatorname{Cap}(K)=\frac2\pi\log R(K)+O(1),
\]
this becomes an **exponential screening law in intrinsic capacity separation**. If
\[
t=C_n-C_m,
\]
then
\[
\boxed{
V_{k,n}\le C(1+t^2)e^{-\pi t/5}.
}
\]
For the whole genealogy, the conditional absolute-regularity/strong-mixing error between a fixed old state and a future state is
\[
O((1+t)e^{-\pi t/6}).
\]
Consequently the capacity-sampled backwards harmonic-measure genealogy is exponentially mixing in capacity separation, and its tail sigma-field is trivial, conditional on every realized DLA trajectory.

This is a genuine resolution of one previously open structural subproblem. It still does **not** close the DLA exponent: the target concerns how many physical particles are needed to accumulate capacity, while the new theorem mixes only after a prescribed amount of capacity has already elapsed. The remaining obstruction is therefore more sharply localized to the particle-count/capacity conversion and to possible bursty dependence inside bounded capacity windows.

### Input A: quantitative entrance-measure convergence

Lawler, *Random Explorations*, Proposition 4.30 states, in dimension two: if a finite set \(K\subset B_r\) and \(|w|\ge2r\), then for every \(z\in K\),
\[
\mathbb P^w\{S_{T_K}=z\}
=
\omega_K(z)\,[1+O(\varepsilon(r,w))],
\tag{2.1}
\]
where
\[
\varepsilon(r,w)
=
\frac r{|w|}\log\frac{|w|}{r}.
\tag{2.2}
\]
The constant is universal. In \(\mathbb Z^2\) recurrence removes the transient conditioning appearing in the general \(d\)-dimensional formulation.

Consequently, for every bounded \(g:K\to\mathbb R\), \(\|g\|_\infty\le1\),
\[
\left|
\mathbb E^w g(S_{T_K})
-
\sum_{z\in K}\omega_K(z)g(z)
\right|
\le
C\varepsilon(r,w).
\tag{2.3}
\]
For an indicator of a subset \(S\subset K\), with \(\delta=\omega_K(S)\), the relative estimate improves this to
\[
\left|
\mathbb P^w\{S_{T_K}\in S\}-\delta
\right|
\le
C\delta\varepsilon(r,w).
\tag{2.4}
\]

### Input B: annular Beurling penetration

The standard discrete Beurling estimate implies the annular form used here: if a connected lattice set crosses an annulus from radius \(s\) to radius \(R\), then a simple random walk crossing from the outer side to the inner side without hitting that connected crossing has probability at most
\[
C\left(\frac sR\right)^{1/2}.
\tag{2.5}
\]
This is the same square-root Beurling exponent used in the classical Kesten DLA bound and in the archived HARD10-04 reports.

No result below assumes the unknown DLA exponent, a scaling limit, a shape theorem, or a multifractal spectrum.

# 3. Harmonic-measure penetration into an inner ball

Write
\[
\mathcal R_n:=\max_{z\in K_n}|z|_2.
\tag{3.1}
\]
This differs from the cluster radius \(R_n\) by at most an additive lattice constant.

### Lemma 3.1 — PROVED

There is a universal \(C<\infty\) such that for every sufficiently large connected DLA closure \(K_n\), and every
\[
2\le s\le \mathcal R_n/4,
\]
its harmonic measure from infinity satisfies
\[
\boxed{
\mu_n(B_s)
:=
\sum_{z\in K_n\cap B_s}\mu_n(z)
\le
C\left(\frac{s}{\mathcal R_n}\right)^{1/2}.
}
\tag{3.2}
\]

### Proof

Because \(K_n\) is connected, contains the seed near the origin, and reaches radius \(\mathcal R_n\), it contains a nearest-neighbor path \(\gamma\) crossing the annulus between radii comparable to \(s\) and \(\mathcal R_n\).

If a random walk coming from infinity first hits \(K_n\) at a point in \(B_s\), then before that first hit it must penetrate from the outer side of this annulus to radius \(s\) while avoiding \(\gamma\). The annular discrete Beurling estimate (2.5), uniformly over the entrance point on the outer circle and over the shape of \(\gamma\), bounds this probability by
\[
C(s/\mathcal R_n)^{1/2}.
\]
Taking the pole to infinity gives (3.2). QED.

### Check

This is stronger than bounding inner mass by “number of inner sites times maximal harmonic measure”: that cruder estimate would lose a factor \(s^2\). The connected annular crossing is the essential geometric input.

# 4. General posterior-screening theorem

Fix two levels
\[
m<n.
\]
Let
\[
K:=K_m,
\qquad
r:=\mathcal R_m,
\qquad
R:=\mathcal R_n,
\qquad
Q:=R/r.
\tag{4.1}
\]
Assume \(Q\) is larger than a universal constant so that the intermediate radii below are meaningful.

Let \((Y_j)\) be the canonical backwards harmonic-measure genealogy from the archived reports. For a bounded observable
\[
g:K_m\to[-1,1],
\]
define
\[
\bar g:=\mathbb E[g(Y_m)]
=\sum_{z\in K_m}\mu_m(z)g(z)
\tag{4.2}
\]
and, for \(y\in K_n\),
\[
h_{m,n}^g(y)
:=
\mathbb E[g(Y_m)\mid Y_n=y].
\tag{4.3}
\]
By the exact nested first-hit semigroup,
\[
h_{m,n}^g(y)
=
\mathbb E^y[g(S_{T_{K_m}})]
\tag{4.4}
\]
for \(y\notin K_m\), with the direct-hit convention on \(K_m\).

### Theorem 4.1 — PROVED

For every fixed \(p\ge1\), there is \(C_p<\infty\) such that
\[
\boxed{
\mathbb E_{Y_n\sim\mu_n}
\left|h_{m,n}^g(Y_n)-\bar g\right|^p
\le
C_p(1+\log^p Q)Q^{-p/(2p+1)}.
}
\tag{4.5}
\]
The estimate is deterministic conditional on the DLA trajectory and uniform over all cluster shapes and all bounded \(g\).

### Proof

Choose an intermediate radius \(s\) with
\[
2r\le s\le R/4.
\tag{4.6}
\]
Split according to \(|Y_n|<s\) and \(|Y_n|\ge s\).

On the far event \(|Y_n|\ge s\), quantitative entrance convergence (2.3) gives
\[
|h_{m,n}^g(Y_n)-\bar g|
\le
C\frac rs\log\frac sr.
\tag{4.7}
\]
Hence its \(p\)-th power contributes at most
\[
C_p\left(\frac rs\log\frac sr\right)^p.
\tag{4.8}
\]

On the near event, the difference is bounded by \(2\), while Lemma 3.1 gives
\[
\mu_n(B_s)
\le
C(s/R)^{1/2}.
\tag{4.9}
\]
Therefore
\[
\mathbb E|h_{m,n}^g(Y_n)-\bar g|^p
\le
C_p
\left[
\left(\frac rs\log\frac sr\right)^p
+
\left(\frac sR\right)^{1/2}
\right].
\tag{4.10}
\]

Write
\[
s=rQ^a.
\]
Ignoring the harmless logarithm, the two powers are
\[
Q^{-ap}
\quad\text{and}\quad
Q^{-(1-a)/2}.
\]
Balancing gives
\[
ap=\frac{1-a}{2},
\qquad
\boxed{a=\frac1{2p+1}}.
\tag{4.11}
\]
Then both terms have exponent
\[
\frac{p}{2p+1},
\]
and (4.5) follows after absorbing the logarithm into \(1+\log^pQ\). QED.

# 10. Exponential strong mixing of the full backwards genealogy

Let
\[
\mathscr P_m:=\sigma(Y_1,\ldots,Y_m),
\qquad
\mathscr F_n:=\sigma(Y_n,Y_{n+1},\ldots).
\tag{10.1}
\]
Conditional on \(Y_n\), the reverse-Markov factorization makes the old segment \(\mathscr P_m\) independent of the future tail \(\mathscr F_n\) beyond its dependence through \(Y_n\).

Define the usual strong-mixing coefficient
\[
\alpha(m,n)
:=
\sup_{A\in\mathscr P_m,\ B\in\mathscr F_n}
\left|
\mathbb P(A\cap B)-\mathbb P(A)\mathbb P(B)
\right|.
\tag{10.2}
\]
All probabilities here are under the auxiliary genealogy law conditional on the fixed DLA trajectory.

### Theorem 10.1 — PROVED

There is a universal \(C<\infty\) such that
\[
\boxed{
\alpha(m,n)
\le
C(1+t)e^{-\pi t/6},
\qquad
t=C_n-C_m,
}
\tag{10.3}
\]
for all sufficiently large \(t\).

### Proof

Fix \(A\in\mathscr P_m\). Set
\[
g(y):=\mathbb P(A\mid Y_m=y),
\qquad 0\le g\le1.
\tag{10.4}
\]
For \(B\in\mathscr F_n\), let
\[
b(y):=\mathbb P(B\mid Y_n=y),
\qquad0\le b\le1.
\tag{10.5}
\]
By the reverse Markov property,
\[
\mathbb P(A\cap B)-\mathbb P(A)\mathbb P(B)
=
\mathbb E\left[
 b(Y_n)
 \left(
 \mathbb E[g(Y_m)\mid Y_n]
 -\mathbb E g(Y_m)
 \right)
\right].
\tag{10.6}
\]
Taking absolute values and using \(b\le1\), the right-hand side is bounded by the \(p=1\) case of Theorem 7.1. This yields (10.3). QED.

### Corollary 10.2 — capacity-sampled exponential mixing

At capacity hitting times
\[
\sigma_j:=\inf\{n:C_n\ge j\},
\]
put
\[
Z_j:=Y_{\sigma_j}.
\]
Since capacity overshoots vanish and a separation of \(L\) capacity units means \(t=L+o(1)\), the process \((Z_j)\), conditional on the DLA trajectory, has exponentially decaying strong-mixing coefficient:
\[
\boxed{
\alpha_Z(j,j+L)
\le
C(1+L)e^{-\pi L/6+o(L)}.
}
\tag{10.7}
\]
No stationarity is asserted.

# 11. Tail triviality of the canonical harmonic-measure genealogy

### Theorem 11.1 — PROVED

Conditional on every DLA trajectory for which the standard radius/capacity properties above hold, the tail sigma-field
\[
\mathscr T
:=
\bigcap_{n\ge1}\sigma(Y_n,Y_{n+1},\ldots)
\tag{11.1}
\]
is trivial.

### Proof

Let \(B\in\mathscr T\). Fix \(m\) and \(A\in\mathscr P_m\). For every \(n>m\), \(B\in\mathscr F_n\), so Theorem 10.1 gives
\[
|\mathbb P(A\cap B)-\mathbb P(A)\mathbb P(B)|
\le\alpha(m,n)\to0.
\]
Therefore \(B\) is independent of every \(\mathscr P_m\). The increasing union of the finite-coordinate sigma-fields \(\mathscr P_m\) generates the full genealogy sigma-field, hence \(B\) is independent of the full sigma-field and in particular of itself. Thus
\[
\mathbb P(B)=\mathbb P(B)^2,
\]
so \(\mathbb P(B)\in\{0,1\}\). QED.

### Important limitation

This tail triviality belongs to the **auxiliary backwards genealogy conditional on the DLA trajectory**. It is not a tail-triviality theorem for the DLA cluster process itself and does not imply determinism of the radial exponent.

## 12. Why this still does not solve the DLA exponent

The new theorem is strong: actual planar first-hit kernels erase old genealogy information exponentially fast in **capacity time**. But the desired exponent is precisely about how physical particle time compares with capacity/radius time.

A unit-capacity block can contain anywhere in the currently rigorous range from about \(R^{3/2}\) to nearly \(R^2\) particles. The new mixing theorem says that once several units of capacity have elapsed, old ancestry is forgotten. It does **not** yet control how many particles are required to produce those capacity units.

There is also a local obstruction to immediately converting (8.2) into the pair-overlap second-moment bound sought in preceding reports. The correlation error contains only one power of the later shell probability after squaring,
\[
|c_{k\ell}-\delta_k\delta_\ell|^2
\lesssim
\delta_\ell e^{-a\Delta C},
\]
and \(\sum\delta_\ell\) inside a unit-capacity block can be much larger than \(\sum\delta_\ell^2\asymp1\). Thus the present estimate controls correlations across separated capacity scales but does not rule out a large burst of strongly correlated tiny-TV renewals inside one bounded-capacity window.

This is now the principal gap in the genealogy route.

The accumulated work now rules out or closes several routes:

1. **Pure scalar inequalities are insufficient.** The preceding scalar-sharpness family satisfies Makarov, Beurling, \(d\asymp p^2\), Kesten and higher moments for every candidate block dimension \(D\in[3/2,2]\).

4. **What remains is intra-window burst control.** To turn genealogy mixing into particle-count self-averaging, one needs a theorem preventing one unit of pair-overlap/capacity from being generated by a rare, highly correlated burst of enormous particle multiplicity inside a bounded capacity interval.

This obstruction is narrower than the earlier generic phrase “annular mixing.” Cross-annular/cross-capacity mixing is now proved for the auxiliary lineage; the missing estimate lives primarily **inside bounded capacity windows**.

## 14. Next concrete attack

The next useful target should combine the new cross-capacity mixing with the previously proved higher-overlap/anti-condensation hierarchy.

For a unit-capacity block \(B_j\), let
\[
L_{2,j}
=
\sum_{k\in B_j}
J_{k+1}^{(1)}J_{k+1}^{(2)}.
\tag{14.1}
\]
Earlier reports proved
\[
\mathbb E[L_{2,j}\mid\text{trajectory}]\asymp1,
\]
but did not control \(\mathbb E[L_{2,j}^2]\). The new screening theorem controls interactions between well-separated capacity blocks. Therefore the remaining finite lemma is an **intra-block burst estimate**, for example any uniform-integrability statement of the form
\[
\sup_j
\mathbb E\left[
L_{2,j}\mathbf1_{\{L_{2,j}>M\}}
\mid\text{trajectory}
\right]
\xrightarrow[M\to\infty]{}0,
\tag{14.2}
\]
or the stronger
\[
\sup_j\mathbb E[L_{2,j}^{1+\eta}\mid\text{trajectory}]<\infty
\tag{14.3}
\]
for some \(\eta>0\).

A successful estimate would give a nondegenerate probability of a genuine pair common renewal per capacity block; the exponential cross-block mixing proved here would then provide a true regenerative/mixing framework in intrinsic capacity time.

The most promising inputs for (14.2) are:

- the archived fact that \(q\ge3\) simultaneous-lineage overlaps have finite future expectation and strong radius tails;
- the higher conditional moments of one-step capacity increments;
- the local finite-dimensional response formula, which limits how much one attachment can reorganize the immediate harmonic-measure state;
- the present exponential forgetting once a burst produces nontrivial capacity separation.

This is the next attack. It is materially narrower than proving a full DLA shape theorem or multifractal spectrum.

## 16. Adversarial checking pass

1. **No circular use of \(\beta\).** Radius ratios are converted to capacity differences only through the deterministic capacity-radius comparison, not through particle-time growth.
2. **Entrance estimate orientation.** Lawler Proposition 4.30 compares a walk started far away with harmonic measure from infinity on the **fixed old set** \(K_m\), exactly the posterior kernel needed here.
3. **Near/far split.** Far-field smoothing alone is insufficient because later harmonic measure may still charge sites near the old set; Lemma 3.1 is needed to make that near mass small.
4. **Beurling geometry.** Connectedness of \(K_n\) supplies an annulus-crossing obstacle from the seed to the current outer radius. A hit of \(K_n\) deep inside must penetrate the annulus while avoiding that obstacle.
5. **Optimization exponent.** For \(L^p\), balancing \(Q^{-ap}\) and \(Q^{-(1-a)/2}\) gives \(a=1/(2p+1)\), hence exponent \(p/(2p+1)\). For \(p=2\), this is \(2/5\); for posterior TV/L1 it is \(1/3\).
6. **Capacity exponent.** Since \(\log Q=(\pi/2)t+O(1)\), \(Q^{-2/5}\) becomes \(e^{-\pi t/5+O(1)}\), and \(Q^{-1/3}\) becomes \(e^{-\pi t/6+O(1)}\).
7. **Tail triviality scope.** The 0–1 statement is only for the auxiliary genealogy conditional on a fixed DLA path. It is not asserted for the DLA process.
8. **No false regeneration claim.** Cross-capacity mixing does not bound the number of highly correlated tiny renewals occurring within one unit-capacity block; this remains open.

These checks are self-review, not independent/formal verification. Novelty is unassessed.
