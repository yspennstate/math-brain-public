# 3. Old-shell ancestry posterior

Let
\[
S_k:=K_{k+1}\setminus K_k
\tag{3.1}
\]
be the newly exposed shell created at DLA step \(k+1\). The preceding genealogy report constructs, conditional on the fixed DLA trajectory, a process \((Y_n)_{n\ge1}\) with
\[
Y_n\sim\mu_n,
\]
where \(\mu_n\) is harmonic measure from infinity on \(K_n\), and reverse transition kernel \(\Pi_n:K_{n+1}\to K_n\) equal to direct first-hit balayage.

Fix \(k\). Define the old-shell ancestry event
\[
A_k:=\{Y_{k+1}\in S_k\}.
\tag{3.2}
\]
Its probability is
\[
\delta_{k+1}:=\mu_{k+1}(S_k)
=\|\mu_{k+1}-\mu_k\|_{\rm TV}.
\tag{3.3}
\]

For every \(n\ge k+1\), define
\[
f_{k,n}(y)
:=
\mathbb P^y\{S_{T_{K_{k+1}}}\in S_k\},
\qquad y\in K_n,
\tag{3.4}
\]
with the direct-hit convention on \(K_{k+1}\), so
\[
f_{k,k+1}=\mathbf 1_{S_k}.
\tag{3.5}
\]
By the exact first-hit semigroup from `ce6eeded...`,
\[
\boxed{
f_{k,n+1}=\Pi_n f_{k,n}.
}
\tag{3.6}
\]
Equivalently, in the lineage,
\[
\boxed{
f_{k,n}(Y_n)
=
\mathbb P(A_k\mid Y_n).
}
\tag{3.7}
\]

The mean is independent of \(n\):
\[
\sum_{y\in K_n}\mu_n(y)f_{k,n}(y)
=\mathbb P(A_k)
=\delta_{k+1}.
\tag{3.8}
\]

Define the **screening variance**
\[
V_{k,n}
:=
\sum_{y\in K_n}
\mu_n(y)
\bigl(f_{k,n}(y)-\delta_{k+1}\bigr)^2.
\tag{3.9}
\]
This is the \(L^2(\mu_n)\) nonuniformity of the probability that a present harmonic-measure point descends to the old shell \(S_k\).

At the initial level,
\[
\boxed{
V_{k,k+1}
=\delta_{k+1}(1-\delta_{k+1}).
}
\tag{3.10}
\]

# 4. Exact screening-variance dissipation identity

### Theorem 4.1 — PROVED

For every \(n\ge k+1\),
\[
\boxed{
V_{k,n}-V_{k,n+1}
=
\sum_{y\in S_n}
\mu_{n+1}(y)
\operatorname{Var}_{Z\sim\Pi_n(y,\cdot)}
\bigl(f_{k,n}(Z)\bigr)
\ge0.
}
\tag{4.1}
\]
Hence \(V_{k,n}\) is nonincreasing in \(n\) and has a limit
\[
V_{k,\infty}:=\lim_{n\to\infty}V_{k,n}
\in[0,\delta_{k+1}(1-\delta_{k+1})].
\tag{4.2}
\]
Moreover, for every \(N>k+1\),
\[
\boxed{
\delta_{k+1}(1-\delta_{k+1})
=
V_{k,N}
+
\sum_{n=k+1}^{N-1}
\sum_{y\in S_n}
\mu_{n+1}(y)
\operatorname{Var}_{\Pi_n(y,\cdot)}
(f_{k,n}).
}
\tag{4.3}
\]
Letting \(N\to\infty\),
\[
\boxed{
\delta_{k+1}(1-\delta_{k+1})
=
V_{k,\infty}
+
\sum_{n=k+1}^{\infty}
\sum_{y\in S_n}
\mu_{n+1}(y)
\operatorname{Var}_{\Pi_n(y,\cdot)}
(f_{k,n}).
}
\tag{4.4}
\]

### Proof

Set
\[
M_n:=f_{k,n}(Y_n).
\]
By (3.6) and the reverse Markov transition,
\[
\mathbb E[M_n\mid Y_{n+1}]
=f_{k,n+1}(Y_{n+1})
=M_{n+1}.
\tag{4.5}
\]
Thus the law of total variance gives
\[
\operatorname{Var}(M_n)
=
\operatorname{Var}(M_{n+1})
+
\mathbb E[\operatorname{Var}(M_n\mid Y_{n+1})].
\tag{4.6}
\]
The first two terms are \(V_{k,n}\) and \(V_{k,n+1}\). If \(Y_{n+1}\in K_n\), the reverse kernel is the identity, so the conditional variance is zero. Only \(Y_{n+1}\in S_n=K_{n+1}\setminus K_n\) contributes. Conditioning on its value gives (4.1). Telescoping proves (4.3), and monotone convergence proves (4.4). QED.

### Interpretation

The initial binary ancestry information has variance \(\delta(1-\delta)\). Future DLA growth can dissipate that ancestry information only when a lineage sits on a newly exposed site. The exact amount dissipated at step \(n+1\) is the later harmonic mass of that new shell times the variance, under its first-hit distribution on the previous closure, of the old-shell ancestry probability.

The residual \(V_{k,\infty}\) is a precise **tail-memory parameter**. If it is positive, then even arbitrarily late harmonic-measure points retain nontrivial information about whether their ancestor at level \(k+1\) lay in \(S_k\). If it vanishes, the old shell is completely screened in this \(L^2\) ancestry sense.

# 5. Exact information-dissipation identity

Define
\[
\mathcal I_{k,n}:=I(A_k;Y_n)
\tag{5.1}
\]
under the conditional genealogy law, with natural logarithms. Since \(A_k\) is a deterministic function of \(Y_{k+1}\),
\[
\mathcal I_{k,k+1}=h_{\rm b}(\delta_{k+1}),
\tag{5.2}
\]
where
\[
h_{\rm b}(u)=-u\log u-(1-u)\log(1-u).
\]

### Theorem 5.1 — PROVED

For every \(n\ge k+1\),
\[
\boxed{
\mathcal I_{k,n}-\mathcal I_{k,n+1}
=I(A_k;Y_n\mid Y_{n+1})
\ge0.
}
\tag{5.3}
\]
Thus \(\mathcal I_{k,n}\downarrow\mathcal I_{k,\infty}\ge0\), and
\[
\boxed{
h_{\rm b}(\delta_{k+1})
=
\mathcal I_{k,\infty}
+
\sum_{n=k+1}^{\infty}
I(A_k;Y_n\mid Y_{n+1}).
}
\tag{5.4}
\]
Also
\[
\boxed{
2V_{k,n}
\le
\mathcal I_{k,n}
\le
\frac{V_{k,n}}{\delta_{k+1}(1-\delta_{k+1})}.
}
\tag{5.5}
\]

### Proof

The reverse genealogy has the Markov relation
\[
Y_{n+1}\longrightarrow Y_n\longrightarrow A_k.
\]
Therefore
\[
I(A_k;Y_n,Y_{n+1})=I(A_k;Y_n)
\]
and the chain rule gives
\[
I(A_k;Y_n)-I(A_k;Y_{n+1})
=I(A_k;Y_n\mid Y_{n+1}).
\]
This proves (5.3) and telescoping gives (5.4).

For (5.5), the posterior probability of \(A_k\) given \(Y_n=y\) is \(f_{k,n}(y)\). Hence
\[
\mathcal I_{k,n}
=
\sum_y\mu_n(y)
D_{\rm KL}(\operatorname{Ber}(f_{k,n}(y))\|\operatorname{Ber}(\delta_{k+1})).
\]
Pinsker's inequality gives the lower bound
\[
D_{\rm KL}(\operatorname{Ber}(p)\|\operatorname{Ber}(q))
\ge2(p-q)^2.
\]
For the upper bound, KL is bounded by \(\chi^2\), and for Bernoulli laws
\[
\chi^2(\operatorname{Ber}(p)\|\operatorname{Ber}(q))
=\frac{(p-q)^2}{q(1-q)}.
\]
Average over \(\mu_n\). QED.

Thus variance screening and information screening are equivalent up to the explicit fixed-shell factor \(\delta_{k+1}(1-\delta_{k+1})\).

# 6. Two-time correlation error is controlled by remaining screening variance

Let, as in the previous checkpoint,
\[
c_{k\ell}
:=
\mathbb P(A_k\cap A_\ell)
=
\mathbb P(J_{k+1}=1,J_{\ell+1}=1),
\qquad k<\ell.
\tag{6.1}
\]

### Theorem 6.1 — PROVED

For every \(k<\ell\),
\[
\boxed{
\left|c_{k\ell}
-\delta_{k+1}\delta_{\ell+1}\right|^2
\le
\delta_{\ell+1}(1-\delta_{\ell+1})
V_{k,\ell+1}.
}
\tag{6.2}
\]
Consequently
\[
\boxed{
\left|c_{k\ell}
-\delta_{k+1}\delta_{\ell+1}\right|^2
\le
\frac18\,\mathcal I_{k,\ell+1}
}
\tag{6.3}
\]
is also valid up to the universal Pinsker constant stated below; the sharper small-\(\delta_{\ell+1}\) estimate is (6.2).

### Proof

By the posterior identity,
\[
c_{k\ell}
=\mathbb E[J_{\ell+1}f_{k,\ell+1}(Y_{\ell+1})].
\]
Subtract the product of means:
\[
c_{k\ell}-\delta_{k+1}\delta_{\ell+1}
=
\mathbb E\left[
(J_{\ell+1}-\delta_{\ell+1})
(f_{k,\ell+1}(Y_{\ell+1})-\delta_{k+1})
\right].
\]
Cauchy–Schwarz gives (6.2).

For the information form, data processing gives
\[
I(A_k;J_{\ell+1})\le I(A_k;Y_{\ell+1})=\mathcal I_{k,\ell+1}.
\]
For two Bernoulli variables the total-variation distance between their joint law and the product law is \(2|c_{k\ell}-\delta_{k+1}\delta_{\ell+1}|\). Pinsker therefore gives
\[
\mathcal I_{k,\ell+1}
\ge8|c_{k\ell}-\delta_{k+1}\delta_{\ell+1}|^2.
\]
QED.

### Consequence

The weighted-screening problem from the preceding report is no longer an undefined mixing condition. It is enough to prove quantitative decay of the monotone functional \(V_{k,n}\), uniformly in the scale-relevant regime. For fixed \(k\), complete ancestry screening
\[
V_{k,n}\to0
\]
forces absolute two-time factorization. For the DLA exponent, however, one needs a **scale-uniform** estimate when \(k\) itself tends to infinity and \(n-k\) spans capacity blocks.

# 7. New local gradient lower bound on screening dissipation

Let \(x=X_{n+1}\) be the actual site attached when passing from \(A_n\) to \(A_{n+1}\). Every newly exposed site \(y\in S_n=K_{n+1}\setminus K_n\) is a nearest neighbour of \(x\). Since \(x\in\partial A_n\subset K_n\), a random walk started at \(y\) hits \(K_n\) at \(x\) on its first step with probability \(1/4\). Therefore
\[
\Pi_n(y,x)\ge\frac14.
\tag{7.1}
\]

### Theorem 7.1 — PROVED

For every fixed old shell \(k\) and every \(n\ge k+1\),
\[
\boxed{
V_{k,n}-V_{k,n+1}
\ge
\frac14
\sum_{y\in S_n}
\mu_{n+1}(y)
\bigl(f_{k,n}(x)-f_{k,n+1}(y)\bigr)^2.
}
\tag{7.2}
\]
Whenever \(x,y\notin K_{k+1}\), both values are evaluations of the same exterior harmonic function
\[
h_k(z):=\mathbb P^z\{S_{T_{K_{k+1}}}\in S_k\},
\tag{7.3}
\]
so the contribution becomes
\[
\mu_{n+1}(y)\bigl(h_k(x)-h_k(y)\bigr)^2.
\]

### Proof

Fix \(y\in S_n\), and let \(Z\sim\Pi_n(y,\cdot)\). Its mean ancestry value is
\[
\mathbb E[f_{k,n}(Z)]=f_{k,n+1}(y).
\]
The single outcome \(Z=x\) has probability at least \(1/4\). Therefore
\[
\operatorname{Var}(f_{k,n}(Z))
\ge
\Pi_n(y,x)
\bigl(f_{k,n}(x)-f_{k,n+1}(y)\bigr)^2
\ge
\frac14\bigl(f_{k,n}(x)-f_{k,n+1}(y)\bigr)^2.
\]
Insert this into Theorem 4.1 and sum over \(y\in S_n\). QED.

### Interpretation

An old-shell ancestry profile can retain variance only if, on most future growth steps carrying appreciable new-shell harmonic mass, it is nearly flat across the edge from the attached site to each newly exposed neighbour. Thus the missing screening theorem can be attacked as a **weighted Dirichlet-energy accumulation problem** for the old-shell hitting function along the actual DLA growth front.

Equation (7.2) is the first local coercive inequality in this genealogy route: it turns an abstract variance loss into a squared spatial gradient sampled by DLA geometry.

# 8. Dependence-sharpness countermodel: projective balayage alone does not mix

The previous checkpoint proved that the current one-time scalar inequalities permit every candidate dimension \(D\in[3/2,2]\). The present construction proves a complementary statement: even the exact projective/balayage genealogy algebra and the exact \(q=2\) variation threshold do **not** imply screening or bounded pair-overlap second moments.

Choose any nonincreasing sequence
\[
q_1=1\ge q_2\ge q_3\ge\cdots\downarrow0.
\tag{8.1}
\]
Let
\[
K_n:=\{1,2,\dots,n\}.
\]
Define a probability measure \(\nu_n\) on \(K_n\) by
\[
\nu_n(j)=q_j-q_{j+1},\quad j<n,
\qquad
\nu_n(n)=q_n.
\tag{8.2}
\]
The sum telescopes to one.

Define a reverse kernel \(P_n:K_{n+1}\to K_n\) by
\[
P_n(j,j)=1\quad(j\le n),
\qquad
P_n(n+1,n)=1.
\tag{8.3}
\]
Then
\[
\boxed{\nu_n=\nu_{n+1}P_n.}
\tag{8.4}
\]
Indeed all old masses are unchanged except at \(n\), where
\[
(q_n-q_{n+1})+q_{n+1}=q_n.
\]

The new-shell mass is
\[
\delta_{n+1}=\nu_{n+1}(n+1)=q_{n+1},
\tag{8.5}
\]
and extending \(\nu_n\) by zero to \(K_{n+1}\) gives
\[
\boxed{
\|\nu_{n+1}-\nu_n\|_{\rm TV}=q_{n+1}.
}
\tag{8.6}
\]
Thus choosing
\[
q_n=n^{-1/2}
\]
for \(n\ge1\) gives exactly the same variation threshold as the actual DLA harmonic-measure path:
\[
\sum_n\delta_n^p
\begin{cases}
=\infty,&p\le2,\\
<\infty,&p>2.
\end{cases}
\tag{8.7}
\]

Yet the genealogy has **zero screening**. Fix \(k\). A state \(j\ge k+1\) deterministically descends through
\[
j\to j-1\to\cdots\to k+1.
\]
Therefore the old-shell ancestry posterior is exactly
\[
f_{k,n}(j)=\mathbf1_{\{j\ge k+1\}}.
\tag{8.8}
\]
Under \(\nu_n\),
\[
\nu_n\{j\ge k+1\}=q_{k+1}=\delta_{k+1}.
\]
Hence for every \(n\ge k+1\),
\[
\boxed{
V_{k,n}
=\delta_{k+1}(1-\delta_{k+1}),
}
\tag{8.9}
\]
so every dissipation term in (4.4) is zero and
\[
V_{k,\infty}=V_{k,k+1}.
\]
Likewise
\[
\mathcal I_{k,n}=h_{\rm b}(\delta_{k+1})
\]
for every \(n\): the tail retains the full old-shell ancestry bit.

For \(k<\ell\), renewal at the later shell forces renewal at every earlier shell, so
\[
\boxed{
 c_{k\ell}=\delta_{\ell+1},
}
\tag{8.10}
\]
which is maximally non-factorized when \(\delta_{k+1}\ll1\).

### Unit-quadratic-variation block version

The obstruction persists even at the block scale used in the DLA project. Fix a large integer \(N\), and let a plateau of \(N\) consecutive levels have
\[
q=N^{-1/2}.
\]
Then every step has TV displacement \(q\), so
\[
\sum_{\rm block}\delta^2=Nq^2=1.
\tag{8.11}
\]
This exactly mimics a unit-capacity/unit-quadratic-variation block. Yet for every two levels \(k<\ell\) inside the plateau,
\[
c_{k\ell}=q.
\]
The exact pair-overlap second-moment formula from the previous report gives
\[
\mathbb E[L_2^2]
=Nq^2+2\binom N2q^2
=1+(N-1)
=N.
\tag{8.12}
\]
Thus the pair clock has unbounded second moment even though its first moment is one and the block quadratic variation is exactly one.

### Logical conclusion

The following inputs, by themselves, cannot yield the desired pair-clock mixing theorem:

- nested/projectively consistent harmonic-measure-type measures;
- exact first-hit-style balayage algebra;
- the exact \(q=2\) variation threshold;
- unit quadratic variation per intrinsic block;
- the lineage semigroup and overlap identities.

A successful proof must use a property absent from the spine model: **genuine spatial spreading of planar simple-random-walk first-hit kernels**. The local gradient coercivity (7.2), quantitative Harnack/Poisson-kernel smoothing, Beurling screening, or a related planar geometric input is therefore essential.

This abstract countermodel is not a DLA counterexample and does not refute exponent existence. It is a proof-strategy no-go theorem.

# 9. Reformulated screening target

The new variance functional turns the previous weighted correlation target into a clean two-stage program.

For a unit-capacity block beginning near level \(k\), one would like a scale-uniform estimate of the form
\[
\boxed{
V_{k,n}
\le
\Psi(C_n-C_{k+1})\,
\delta_{k+1}(1-\delta_{k+1})
+\operatorname{Err}_{k,n},
}
\tag{9.1}
\]
where \(\Psi(t)\to0\) as \(t\to\infty\) and the errors are summable in the pair-overlap second-moment calculation.

Theorem 6.1 would then immediately give
\[
|c_{k\ell}-\delta_{k+1}\delta_{\ell+1}|^2
\lesssim
\delta_{\ell+1}\Psi(C_{\ell+1}-C_{k+1})\delta_{k+1}
+\cdots.
\tag{9.2}
\]
Combined with \(\sum_{B_j}\delta^2\asymp1\), a sufficiently integrable \(\Psi\) would prove the bounded pair-clock second moment sought in the previous checkpoint.

Theorem 7.1 says that proving (9.1) reduces to showing that a nonconstant old-shell hitting function cannot remain almost flat, in the harmonic-mass-weighted sense, across too many future newly exposed edges.

A concrete next analytic lemma is therefore:

> **Weighted front-energy lemma.** For an exterior harmonic function \(h\) arising as the hitting probability of an old DLA shell, show that across a positive fraction of intrinsic capacity time, the DLA-selected attachment edge satisfies a lower bound on \(|h(x)-h(y)|^2\) sufficient to dissipate a fixed fraction of the current \(L^2(\mu)\) variance.

A quantitative discrete Harnack/Poisson-kernel estimate can handle genuinely far future tips. The remaining hard component is to show that tips which stay in the near field of the old shell cannot carry almost all of the relevant later harmonic mass for too long. That is now the precise screening geometry obstruction.
