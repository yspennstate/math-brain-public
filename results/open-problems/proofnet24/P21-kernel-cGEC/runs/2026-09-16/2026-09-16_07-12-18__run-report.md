\[
\boxed{

\frac1{2n\ell}=\frac1{4n}=\frac1{2N}.

The paper explicitly calls contribution C.1 a **correlated Gaussian equivalence conjecture**. Its Gaussian row is

\[
q_{ij}

## 7.2 Coordinate-deleted row sum

Let `w^{(-r)}` be the coordinate-deleted optimizer from Section 6 and define

\[
d_{k,t}^{(-r)}
=w^{(-r)}\odot\sigma'(F_{-r}^Tq_{k,t}).
\]

Put

\[
Z_r^{(-r)}
=\sum_{s\ne r}(f_r^Tf_s)
 w_s^{(-r)}\sigma'(f_s^Tq_{k,t})
=f_r^TF_{-r}d_{k,t}^{(-r)}.
\tag{7.3}
\]

Conditional on

- all training sources except coordinate `r`;
- all feature columns `F_{-r}`;
- the fresh current/OU latent `q_{k,t}`;

both `w^{(-r)}` and the vector

\[
v_r:=F_{-r}d_{k,t}^{(-r)}
\]

are fixed relative to

\[
f_r\sim N(0,I_d/d).
\]

Hence the Gaussian moment is **exact**:

\[
E_{f_r}|Z_r^{(-r)}|^4
=\frac3{d^2}\|v_r\|^4,
\tag{7.4}
\]

and more generally

\[
E_{f_r}|Z_r^{(-r)}|^8
=\frac{105}{d^4}\|v_r\|^8.
\tag{7.5}
\]

Bounded `sigma'` gives

\[
\|v_r\|
\le
\|F_{-r}\|_{op}\|\sigma'\|_\infty
\|w^{(-r)}\|.
\tag{7.6}
\]

Ridge coercivity gives

\[
\|w^{(-r)}\|^2
\le {\|y\|^2\over\lambda N}.
\tag{7.7}
\]

Gaussian feature matrices have dimension-uniform fixed moments of `||F||_op`, and the label energy in (7.7) has every fixed moment bounded after division by `N`. Therefore, uniformly in `r,j,k,t` and hybrid position,

\[
\boxed{
E|Z_r^{(-r)}|^4\le Cd^{-2},
\qquad
E|Z_r^{(-r)}|^8\le Cd^{-4}.
}
\tag{7.8}
\]

The OU parameter does not enter the constant: only the bounded derivative `sigma'` was used.

## 7.3 Main deleted-coordinate term

Use `|sigma''|<=K_2`. First ignore the difference between `Z_r` and `Z_r^{(-r)}`. By Cauchy--Schwarz,

\[
E\left[w_r^2|Z_r^{(-r)}|^2\right]
\le
(Ew_r^4)^{1/2}
(E|Z_r^{(-r)}|^4)^{1/2}
\le Cd^{-2}.
\tag{7.9}
\]

Thus before restoring the outer `F`, summing over `m asymp d` gives

\[
E\sum_r w_r^2\sigma''(f_r^Tq_j)^2
|Z_r^{(-r)}|^2
\le C/d.
\tag{7.10}
\]

To retain the outer matrix without any hidden independence assumption, use one more fixed moment. By (7.5), Gaussian operator-norm moments, and Holder,

\[
E\left[
\|F\|_{op}^4|Z_r^{(-r)}|^4
\right]
\le
(E\|F\|_{op}^8)^{1/2}
(E|Z_r^{(-r)}|^8)^{1/2}
\le Cd^{-2}.
\tag{7.11}
\]

Therefore

\[
\begin{aligned}
&E\left[
\|F\|_{op}^2
w_r^2\sigma''(f_r^Tq_j)^2
|Z_r^{(-r)}|^2
\right]\\
&\qquad\le
K_2^2(Ew_r^4)^{1/2}
\left(
E\|F\|_{op}^4|Z_r^{(-r)}|^4
\right)^{1/2}
\le Cd^{-2}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{7.12}
\]

Summation in `r` yields `C/d`.

\[

\qquad

\]

The full and coordinate-deleted normal equations give the exact vector identity

\[
\boxed{
 w_{-r}-w^{(-r)}=-A_r^{-1}c_r\,w_r.
}
\tag{7.15}
\]

Since `A_r >= lambda I`,

\[
\|A_r^{-1}c_r\|^2
=c_r^TA_r^{-2}c_r
\le
\lambda^{-1}c_r^TA_r^{-1}c_r.
\tag{7.16}
\]

Positivity of the scalar Schur complement gives

\[
c_r^TA_r^{-1}c_r\le a_r-\lambda
={1\over N}\|x_r\|^2.
\tag{7.17}
\]

Hence

\[
\boxed{
\|w_{-r}-w^{(-r)}\|^2
\le
{1\over\lambda}
\left({\|x_r\|^2\over N}\right)w_r^2.
}
\tag{7.18}
\]

This is deterministic and works for actual, Gaussian and mixed training designs. Boundedness of the actual activation is not needed at this step.

## 7.5 Correction to the off-diagonal row sum

Let

\[
E_r:=Z_r-Z_r^{(-r)}.
\]

Because the derivative factors `sigma'(f_s^Tq_{k,t})` are the same for the full and coordinate-deleted optimizer,

\[
E_r
=f_r^TF_{-r}
\left[
(w_{-r}-w^{(-r)})
\odot\sigma'(F_{-r}^Tq_{k,t})
\right].
\tag{7.19}
\]

Thus

\[
|E_r|^2
\le
\|f_r\|^2\|F_{-r}\|_{op}^2
\|\sigma'\|_\infty^2
\|w_{-r}-w^{(-r)}\|^2.
\]

By (7.18),

\[
|E_r|^2
\le
C
\|f_r\|^2\|F\|_{op}^2
\left({\|x_r\|^2\over N}\right)
w_r^2.
\tag{7.20}
\]

Restoring both the outside `w_r^2` from `C_j` and the outer `F`,

\[
\|F\|_{op}^2
w_r^2\sigma''(f_r^Tq_j)^2|E_r|^2
\le
C\,
\underbrace{\|F\|_{op}^4\|f_r\|^2
\left({\|x_r\|^2\over N}\right)}_{=:G_r}
w_r^4.
\tag{7.21}
\]

The factor `G_r` has dimension-uniform second moment by Gaussian feature operator/column moments and (6.8). By Holder and (6.6),

\[
E[G_rw_r^4]
\le
(EG_r^2)^{1/2}(Ew_r^8)^{1/2}
\le Cd^{-2}.
\tag{7.22}
\]

Summing over `m asymp d`,

\[
\boxed{
E\sum_r
\|F\|_{op}^2
w_r^2\sigma''(f_r^Tq_j)^2|E_r|^2
\le C/d.
}
\tag{7.23}
\]

This is exactly where the fixed eighth moment from `P21-RIDGE-COLQ-43` cleanly removes any need for a max-coordinate or logarithmic event.

## 7.6 Finish

Since

\[
|Z_r|^2
\le2|Z_r^{(-r)}|^2+2|E_r|^2,
\]

combine (7.12) and (7.23), sum in `r`, and use

\[
\|FC_jRd_{k,t}\|^2
\le
\|F\|_{op}^2\|C_jRd_{k,t}\|^2.

We obtain

\[
\sup_{t\ge0}\max_{j,k}
E\|FC_jRd_{k,t}\|^2
\le C/d.
\]

This proves `P21-OFFDIAG-CONTRACT-43`.

# 8. Log-free fixed-dimensional block Gaussianization

The predecessor proved `P21-CONTRACTED-SOPI-42`: for a centered `C^2` Gaussian functional `V=f(G)`, with

\le

\tag{8.1}

\[
Y_A=(t,u_+,u_-),

the Hessian-gradient contraction decomposes, up to constants depending only on `Delta`, into

\[

\qquad
H_jFd_{k,t},

where

H_j=FC_jF^T.
\]

The preceding run already proved

\[
E\|H_j\xi\|^2\le C/d
\tag{8.2}

\[
E\|FC_jDd_{k,t}\|^2\le C/d.
\tag{8.3}
\]

The new theorem (0.1) supplies the off-diagonal piece. Therefore every unit projection `theta` satisfies

\[
\boxed{
\|\Gamma_{\theta}-E(\theta\cdot Y_A)^2\|_2
\le Cd^{-1/2}.
}
\tag{8.4}
\]

By fixed-dimensional polarization, the Stein-kernel matrix discrepancy for `Y_A` is `O(d^-1/2)`. Hence for every bounded-Hessian test `Phi`,

\le C\|\nabla^2\Phi\|_\infty d^{-1/2},
\tag{8.5}

The matched surrogate block `Y_G` is itself jointly Gaussian conditional on the leave-block environment. A one-parameter Gaussian covariance interpolation in fixed dimension then gives

|E\Phi(Z_A)-E\Phi(Y_G)|

Combining (8.5)-(8.7) proves `P21-BLOCK-C2-LOGFREE-43`.

# 9. Log-free fourth cumulant

=3E\left[V^2(\Gamma_V-EV^2)\right].

Every unit projection `V=theta dot Y_A` is Gaussian-Lipschitz with dimension-free fixed moments because `||w||` has fixed ridge moments, `||F||_op` has fixed Gaussian moments, and `sigma'` is bounded. Thus

This proves `P21-JOINT-CUM4-LOGFREE-43` and removes the predecessor's `sqrt(log d)` loss without asserting a false log-free Hessian operator bound.

# 10. Method comparison and dependence audit

## Whole-original-sample block Lindeberg

**Primary and sharp for the requested theorem.** It preserves the shared `a_i`, teacher scalar `t_i`, and source-Gaussian shared `b_i`. The exact rank-two Woodbury identity contributes `1/N` before summation and yields total `O(d^-1/2)`.

## Correlated Stein / odd-Hermite

**Target-aligned.** Once the rank-two leverage is frozen and parity is used, squared ridge needs only three same-parity second moments. A strong full-vector CLT is unnecessary.

## Contracted second-order Poincare / interpolation

**Now log-free as a higher-order extension.** The correct quantity is the Hessian acting on the OU-correlated gradient. The generic Hessian operator norm can remain `sqrt(log d/d)` and was rigorously rejected in the predecessor. Coordinate deletion closes the only off-diagonal contracted term.

## Positive-real interpolation

For a clean quadratic, adding `zS_0` with `z>=0` preserves `lambda`-strong convexity and gives a useful stability cross-check. Negative-real interpolation remains rejected because the Hessian can lose positive definiteness.

## Resolvents

Only local resolvents are needed for Target 1: `H_-^{-1}`, rank-two Woodbury, and `(I_2+Q_i)^{-1}`. Global nonlinear-Gram universality is stronger than necessary and can introduce spurious dimensions.

## Naive CLTs

Rejected for three independent reasons:

1. the two views share `a_i` and the teacher;
2. the actual plus/minus channels can be dependent despite zero mixed covariance;
3. one bounded `tanh` coordinate stays a positive total-variation distance from its Gaussian surrogate.

The valid theorem is a target-functional block comparison, not coordinatewise Gaussianization.
