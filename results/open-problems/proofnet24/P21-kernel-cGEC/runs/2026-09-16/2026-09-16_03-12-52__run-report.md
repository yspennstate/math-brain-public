## `P21-COORDINATE-TV-NOGO-39` — PROVED

For `tanh`, one actual feature coordinate is almost surely bounded in `(-1,1)`, whereas the matched Gaussian surrogate coordinate is conditionally a nondegenerate Gaussian. Consequently the single-coordinate actual and surrogate laws stay a positive distance apart in total variation; in particular, no coupling can make an individual feature coordinate converge in probability to its matched Gaussian coordinate.

More precisely, for a feature column `f` and one view,

\[
X_A=\tanh(f^T(a+\Delta z))\in(-1,1)\quad a.s.,
\]

while the source-matched Gaussian coordinate is

\[
X_G=\mu_e f^Ta+\mu_b f^Tz+\mu_2b+\mu_3p.
\]

Conditional on `f`,

\[
X_G\sim N(0,s_f^2),
\qquad
s_f^2=(\mu_e^2+\mu_b^2)\|f\|^2+\mu_2^2+\mu_3^2.
\]

For `tanh`, Gaussian Stein gives

\[
\mu_e=E[Z\tanh(Z+\Delta V)]
      =E[\operatorname{sech}^2(Z+\Delta V)]>0,
\]

\[
\mu_b=E[V\tanh(Z+\Delta V)]
      =\Delta E[\operatorname{sech}^2(Z+\Delta V)]>0.
\]

Thus on the ordinary Gaussian-column event `||f||^2>=1/2`,

\[
s_f\ge \mu_e/\sqrt2.
\]

Taking the event `E={|x|>1}` gives the exact lower bound

\[
\boxed{
 d_{TV}(\mathcal L(X_A\mid f),\mathcal L(X_G\mid f))
 \ge P(|X_G|>1\mid f)
 \ge 2\Phi\!\left(-\frac{\sqrt2}{\mu_e}\right)>0.
}
\tag{0.1}
\]

Moreover, under **any** coupling of `X_A,X_G` and for every fixed `eps>0`,

\[
\boxed{
P(|X_A-X_G|>\varepsilon\mid f)
\ge
P(|X_G|>1+\varepsilon\mid f)
\ge
2\Phi\!\left(-\frac{\sqrt2(1+\varepsilon)}{\mu_e}\right)>0.
}
\tag{0.2}
\]

Since `||f||^2->1` in probability for entries `N(0,1/d)`, these lower bounds persist asymptotically after averaging over `F`.

Hence **pointwise feature Gaussianization is false**. The valid theorem is an optimized-objective / clean-risk universality statement after aggregate block replacement, not a coordinatewise total-variation theorem.

## `P21-PARITY-INDEPENDENCE-NOGO-39` — PROVED

The preceding P21 run proved exact plus/minus **orthogonality** by view-swap parity. This run proves that, for the actual nonlinear `tanh` block, plus/minus are nevertheless **not independent**.

For one feature coordinate define

\[
X_1=\tanh(G_1),\qquad X_2=\tanh(G_2),
\]

\[
X_+=\frac{X_1+X_2}{\sqrt2},
\qquad
X_-=\frac{X_1-X_2}{\sqrt2}.
\]

Conditional on any nonzero feature column `f`,

\[
(G_1,G_2)
=
(f^T(a+\Delta z_1),f^T(a+\Delta z_2))
\]

is a nondegenerate bivariate Gaussian because `Delta>0`; its covariance determinant is

\[
\|f\|^4\big((1+\Delta^2)^2-1\big)>0.
\]

Therefore `(X_1,X_2)` has positive density throughout `(-1,1)^2`, and `(X_+,X_-)` has support equal to the open diamond

\[
\mathcal D
=
\{(u,v): |u+v|<\sqrt2,\ |u-v|<\sqrt2\}.
\tag{0.3}
\]

Both marginals have support `(-sqrt(2),sqrt(2))`. Choose any

\[
a_0\in(1/\sqrt2,\sqrt2).
\]

Then

\[
P(X_+>a_0)>0,
\qquad
P(X_->a_0)>0,
\]

but

\[
P(X_+>a_0,X_->a_0)=0,
\]

because `X_++X_->sqrt(2)` would violate `(0.3)`. Thus independence is impossible:

\[
\boxed{X_+\not\!\perp X_-\quad\text{for the actual nonlinear block}.}
\tag{0.4}
\]

By contrast, for the matched Gaussian surrogate,

\[
x_j^G=c+v_j,
\]

with common Gaussian `c` and iid private Gaussians `v_1,v_2`. Hence

\[
x_+^G=\sqrt2\,c+\frac{v_1+v_2}{\sqrt2},
\qquad
x_-^G=\frac{v_1-v_2}{\sqrt2}
\]

are jointly Gaussian with zero cross covariance, and therefore are conditionally independent given `F`.

So the parity basis produces this exact structural contrast:

\[
\boxed{
\text{actual: uncorrelated but dependent};
\qquad
\text{Gaussian surrogate: independent}.
}
\tag{0.5}
\]

This is not a defect in cGEC. It is a certificate that the proof may only use the low-order parity moments actually needed by squared ridge; it must **not** silently import surrogate independence into the actual block.
