# 5. `P21-CONTRACTED-SOPI-42` — full proof

Let \(G\sim N(0,I_D)\), \(V=f(G)\), and assume \(EV=0\). Let \((P_t)_{t\ge0}\) be the Ornstein-Uhlenbeck semigroup. The standard Mehler representation gives

\[
-DL^{-1}V=\int_0^\infty e^{-t}P_t(DV)\,dt.
\tag{5.1}
\]

Define

\[
u(G):=-DL^{-1}V,
\qquad \Gamma_f=\langle Df(G),u(G)\rangle.
\]

Gaussian integration by parts gives \(E\Gamma_f=EV^2\). By Gaussian Poincare,

\[
\|\Gamma_f-EV^2\|_2\le \|D\Gamma_f\|_{L^2}.
\tag{5.2}
\]

Differentiate the scalar product:

\[
D\Gamma_f=D^2f(G)u(G)+(Du(G))^TDf(G).
\tag{5.3}
\]

For the first term, insert (5.1), apply Minkowski, and use the Mehler representation

\[
P_tDf(G)=E'[Df(G_t)\mid G],
\quad G_t=e^{-t}G+\sqrt{1-e^{-2t}}G'.
\]

Jensen gives

\[
\|D^2f(G)u(G)\|_2
\le \int_0^\infty e^{-t}
\left(E\|D^2f(G)Df(G_t)\|^2\right)^{1/2}dt.
\tag{5.4}
\]

For the second term, differentiate under the semigroup. Since

\[
D(P_tDf)=e^{-t}P_t(D^2f),
\]

we have

\[
Du(G)=\int_0^\infty e^{-2t}P_t(D^2f)(G)\,dt.
\tag{5.5}
\]

Therefore, again by Minkowski and Jensen,

\[
\|(Du)^TDf(G)\|_2
\le \int_0^\infty e^{-2t}
\left(E\|D^2f(G_t)Df(G)\|^2\right)^{1/2}dt.
\tag{5.6}
\]

The Gaussian pair \((G,G_t)\) is exchangeable, hence

\[
E\|D^2f(G_t)Df(G)\|^2
=E\|D^2f(G)Df(G_t)\|^2.
\tag{5.7}
\]

Combining (5.2)–(5.7) proves (0.1). Since

\[
\int_0^\infty e^{-t}dt=1,
\qquad
\int_0^\infty e^{-2t}dt=\frac12,
\]

(0.2) follows.

### Vector consequence
