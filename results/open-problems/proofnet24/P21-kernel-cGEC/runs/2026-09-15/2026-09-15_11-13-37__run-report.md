## New theorem: `P21-SURROGATE-COEFFICIENT-CERT-23`

This run derives the common/private Gaussian residual coefficients from first principles.

Set

\[
s=\sqrt{1+\Delta^2},
\qquad
\rho_\Delta=s^{-2}.
\]

Let \(h_k\) be the orthonormal probabilists' Hermite basis and define, for a feature column of squared norm \(q\),

\[
a_k(q)=E[\sigma(s\sqrt q\,G)h_k(G)],
\qquad
\kappa(q)=E[\sigma'(s\sqrt q\,G)].
\tag{7}
\]

Oddness gives

\[
a_k(q)=0
\quad\text{for every even }k.
\tag{8}
\]

Gaussian integration by parts gives

\[
\boxed{
a_1(q)=s\sqrt q\,\kappa(q).}
\tag{9}
\]

Now take iid \(G_0,G_1,G_2\sim N(0,1)\),

\[
X_j=G_0+\Delta G_j,
\qquad
Y_j=\sigma(X_j).
\]

Stein gives

\[
E[G_0Y_j]=\kappa(1),
\qquad
E[G_jY_j]=\Delta\kappa(1).
\]

Thus the exact first-order projection coefficients are

\[
\boxed{
\mu_e=\kappa(1),
\qquad
\mu_b=\Delta\kappa(1).
}
\tag{10}
\]

Oddness also gives \(\mu_0=0\).

Parseval gives the higher-Hermite residual variance

\[
\eta(1)
=
\sum_{\substack{k\ge3\\k\ {\rm odd}}}a_k(1)^2.
\tag{11}
\]

The standardized \(X_1/s,X_2/s\) have correlation \(\rho_\Delta\). Mehler gives

\[
E[Y_1Y_2]
=
\sum_{\substack{k\ge1\\k\ {\rm odd}}}
a_k(1)^2\rho_\Delta^k.
\tag{12}
\]

The \(k=1\) term equals \(\mu_e^2\), so the higher-Hermite shared residual covariance is

\[
\gamma(1)
=
\sum_{\substack{k\ge3\\k\ {\rm odd}}}
a_k(1)^2\rho_\Delta^k.
\tag{13}
\]

Because \(0<\rho_\Delta<1\),

\[
0\le\gamma(1)\le\eta(1).
\]

Hence

\[
\boxed{
\mu_2^2=\gamma(1),
\qquad
\mu_3^2=\eta(1)-\gamma(1)
}
\tag{14}
\]

are automatically nonnegative.

If \(B,P_1,P_2\) are independent standard Gaussians, with \(B\) shared across views, then

\[
S_j=\mu_eG_0+\mu_bG_j+\mu_2B+\mu_3P_j
\tag{15}
\]

satisfies exactly

\[
ES_j=EY_j=0,
\]

\[
E[G_0S_j]=E[G_0Y_j],
\qquad
E[G_jS_j]=E[G_jY_j],
\]

\[
\operatorname{Var}(S_j)=\operatorname{Var}(Y_j),
\qquad
\operatorname{Cov}(S_1,S_2)=\operatorname{Cov}(Y_1,Y_2).
\]

Thus:

\[
\boxed{
\text{the common Gaussian residual is exactly the higher-Hermite cross-view covariance,}
}
\]

and

\[
\boxed{
\text{the private residual is exactly the remaining higher-Hermite variance.}
}
\]

This proves `P21-SURROGATE-COEFFICIENT-CERT-23`.
