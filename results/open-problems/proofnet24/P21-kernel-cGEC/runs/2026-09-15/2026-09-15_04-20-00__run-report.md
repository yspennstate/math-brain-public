### Step 2: clipping and mollification lemma

Suppose real X,Y satisfy

\[
EX^2+EY^2\le M_2
\]

and for bounded C^2 psi,

\[
|E\psi(X)-E\psi(Y)|
\le
a\|\psi'\|_\infty+b\|\psi''\|_\infty+\delta\|\psi\|_\infty.
\]

For a 1-Lipschitz phi with phi(0)=0, clip its VALUE at level R:

\[
\phi_R(x)=(-R)\vee\phi(x)\wedge R.
\]

Then

\[
|\phi(x)-\phi_R(x)|\le(|x|-R)_+\le x^2/R.
\]

Mollify in the argument with Z~N(0,1):

\[
\phi_{R,\eta}(x)=E_Z\phi_R(x+\eta Z).
\]

Then

\[
\|\phi_{R,\eta}'\|_\infty\le1,\qquad
\|\phi_{R,\eta}''\|_\infty\le C/\eta,\qquad
\|\phi_{R,\eta}\|_\infty\le R,
\]

and

\[
\sup_x|\phi_{R,\eta}(x)-\phi_R(x)|\le C\eta.
\]

Therefore Kantorovich-Rubinstein duality gives

\[
W_1(\mathcal L(X),\mathcal L(Y))
\le
a+C b/\eta+C\eta+C M_2/R+C\delta R.
