# 4. Leave-one-original-sample-block identity and moments

Delete both views of original sample \(i\). Let

\[

# 5. Conditional Efron--Stein for the optimized value

Fix \(F\). Under either marginal law \(M\in\{A,G\}\), the \(n\) original-sample blocks are independent conditional on \(F\).

Let

\[
V_M=L_M^\star,
\qquad
m_M(F)=E[V_M\mid F].
\]

For Efron--Stein, replace block \(i\) by an independent copy while keeping all other blocks fixed. Denote the resulting optimum by \(V_M^{(i)}\).

With the two alternative blocks deleted, the leave environment is common. Therefore

\[
V_M-V_M^{(i)}
=
\delta_i(B_i;\text{leave})
-
\delta_i(B_i';\text{leave}).
\tag{5.1}
\]

Using `(4.4)`,

\[
E(V_M-V_M^{(i)})^2
\le
2E\delta_i(B_i)^2+2E\delta_i(B_i')^2
\le
CN^{-2}.
\tag{5.2}
\]

Conditional Efron--Stein and then averaging \(F\) give

\[
\begin{aligned}
E\operatorname{Var}(V_M\mid F)
&\le
\frac12\sum_{i=1}^n
E(V_M-V_M^{(i)})^2
\\
&\le
C\frac n{N^2}
=
O(d^{-1}),
\end{aligned}
\tag{5.3}
\]

since \(N=2n\asymp d\).

Thus

\[
\boxed{
E|V_M-m_M(F)|
\le
\left(E\operatorname{Var}(V_M\mid F)\right)^{1/2}
\le
Cd^{-1/2}.
}
\tag{5.4}
\]

This is a concentration statement around a **common-feature conditional mean**, not around a deterministic limit.

# 6. Conditional mean comparison by whole-block Lindeberg

We next compare

\[
m_A(F)=E[V_A\mid F],
\qquad
m_G(F)=E[V_G\mid F].

\[
E\left|
E_A[\delta_i^A\mid\mathcal F_{-i}]
-
E_G[\delta_i^G\mid\mathcal F_{-i}]
\right|
\le
\frac{C}{N\sqrt d}.
\tag{6.1}

This is the exact place where the correlated teacher/two-view Stein comparison enters.

After the \(2\times2\) leverage is frozen, the statistic is quadratic in

\[
(t_i,u_1,u_2),
\qquad
u_j=(x_{ij})^\top w_-.

Only the second-moment matrix matters:

\[

\qquad

\qquad

Teacher-feature terms are exact Gaussian-Stein quantities; the two-view covariance keeps the shared latent and uses odd-Hermite/Price matching. The source-exact outer factor \(1/(2N)\) is already included in `(6.1)`.

\boxed{Cd^{-1/2}}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{6.2}

The TOTAL replacement arithmetic is therefore again

\[
\boxed{

\qquad N=2n.

\tag{6.3}
