# 14. Why a naïve correlated CLT is the wrong target

A tempting route is to prove that, conditional on the optimizer direction,

\[

\]

is asymptotically Gaussian.

That statement is false uniformly over arbitrary optimizer directions.

For example, with \(\sigma=\tanh\), take \(w\) equal to one coordinate vector.

Then the actual logit is bounded:

\[
