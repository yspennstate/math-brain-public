## 5.1 Price expansion

For \(r\ne s\), fix the two marginal variances and interpolate in the preactivation covariance \(c\).

Price differentiation gives
\[
\frac{d}{dc}
E[\sigma(X_c)\sigma(Y_c)]
=
E[\sigma'(X_c)\sigma'(Y_c)].
\]

At \(c=0\),
\[
\frac{d}{dc}\bigg|_{0}
E[\sigma(X_c)\sigma(Y_c)]
=
a_ra_s.
\]

The second derivative at zero is
\[
E\sigma''(\sqrt{q_r}Z)\,
E\sigma''(\sqrt{q_s}Z).
\]

Since \(\sigma\) is odd, \(\sigma''\) is odd, so both Gaussian expectations vanish.

The third derivative is bounded uniformly by
\[
\|\sigma'''\|_\infty^2.
\]

Hence
\[
E[\sigma(X_c)\sigma(Y_c)]
=
a_ra_s\,c
+
O(|c|^3).
\tag{5.2}
\]

# 15. Why the theorem is not claimed for growing \(\ell\)

No growing-\(\ell\) theorem is claimed.
