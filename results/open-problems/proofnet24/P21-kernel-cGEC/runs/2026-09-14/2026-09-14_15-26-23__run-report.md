\[
a_i,z_{i1},z_{i2}\stackrel{\rm iid}{\sim}N(0,I_d),
\qquad
t_i=\xi^\top a_i,
\qquad
\|\xi\|=1.

The two augmented nonlinear feature rows are

\[
x_{ij}
=

j=1,2.

The exact squared-ridge objective is

\[
\boxed{
L_A(w)
=
\frac1{4n}
\sum_{i=1}^n\sum_{j=1}^2

\frac1{2N}\|y-Xw\|^2+\frac\lambda2\|w\|^2.

\boxed{\lambda_{04}=2\lambda},

\boxed{\Gamma=N\lambda}.
\tag{2.2}

This normalization is the source of the decisive \(1/N\) factor in each original-sample-block replacement.

## 3. Exact matched correlated Gaussian surrogate

For odd \(\sigma\), \(\mu_0=0\). Let

\[
U=z+\Delta v,
\qquad
U_j=z+\Delta v_j,
\]

where \(z,v,v_1,v_2\) are iid standard normal.

Define

\[
\mu_e=\mathbb E[z\sigma(U)]
=\mathbb E\sigma'(U),
\]

\[
\mu_b=\mathbb E[v\sigma(U)]
=\Delta\mathbb E\sigma'(U),
\]

\[
\mu_2^2
=
\mathbb E[\sigma(U_1)\sigma(U_2)]-\mu_e^2,
\]

\[
\mu_3^2
=
\mathbb E\sigma(U)^2
-
\mathbb E[\sigma(U_1)\sigma(U_2)]
-
\mu_b^2.
\]

The matched pair is

\[
\boxed{
\widetilde x_{ij}
=
\mu_eF^\top a_i
+\mu_bF^\top z_{ij}
+\mu_2b_i
+\mu_3p_{ij},
}
\tag{3.1}
\]

where \(b_i\sim N(0,I_m)\) is **shared by \(j=1,2\)** and the \(p_{ij}\)'s are private iid \(N(0,I_m)\).

The replacement unit must be the complete original-sample block. Replacing the two augmentations separately destroys the shared \(a_i\), duplicated teacher scalar \(t_i\), and shared surrogate residual \(b_i\).

\[
\max_r\left|\|f_r\|-1\right|

=

\[
C_{ab}^A
=
\mathbb E[x_{ia}x_{ib}^\top\mid F],
\qquad
C_{ab}^G
=
\mathbb E[\widetilde x_{ia}\widetilde x_{ib}^\top\mid F].

Price expansion in the off-diagonal preactivation covariance gives

\[
\mathbb E[\sigma(X)\sigma(Y)]
=
(c_{ab}f_r^\top f_s)a_ra_s
+

where

\[
c_{ab}
=
\begin{cases}
1+\Delta^2,&a=b,\\
1,&a\ne b,

and

\[
a_r
=
\mathbb E\sigma'(
\sqrt{1+\Delta^2}\,\|f_r\|Z

The off-diagonal remainder obeys

\|R\|_{\rm op}
\le

## 6. P21-NOSTEIN-01 — a uniform teacher/logit Gaussian CLT is false

A tempting route is:

> Conditional on the leave-block base, prove that
> \[
> (t_i,u_{i1},u_{i2})
> \]
> is asymptotically jointly Gaussian with the matched cGEC triple, uniformly over all bounded \(w_-\).

This is false.

Take the activation \(\sigma=\tanh\), and consider one deterministic coefficient vector

\[
w=e_1.
\]

Then the first actual logit is

\[
u_1^A
=
\tanh\big(f_1^\top(a+\Delta z_1)\big),
\]

so

\[
|u_1^A|\le1
\quad\text{almost surely}.
\]

The matched surrogate coordinate is

\[
u_1^G
=
\mu_e f_1^\top a
+
\mu_b f_1^\top z_1
+
\mu_2 b_1
+
\mu_3p_{1}.
\]

Conditional on \(F\), this is Gaussian. On the good feature event,

\[
\operatorname{Var}(u_1^G)
\longrightarrow
v_{\sigma,\Delta}
:=
\mathbb E[\tanh(Z+\Delta V)^2]
>0.
\]

Therefore \(u_1^G\) has a nondegenerate unbounded Gaussian limit, while \(u_1^A\) remains supported on \([-1,1]\).

Choose any \(\psi\in C_b^\infty(\mathbb R)\) with

\[
\psi(x)=0\quad(|x|\le1),
\]

and

\[
\psi(x)=1\quad(|x|\ge2).
\]

Then

\[
\mathbb E\psi(u_1^A)=0,
\]

whereas

\[
\liminf_{d\to\infty}
\mathbb E\psi(u_1^G)
\ge
\Pr\{
|N(0,v_{\sigma,\Delta})|\ge2
\}
>0.
\]

Thus

\[
\boxed{
\liminf_d
\sup_{\varphi\in\mathcal T}
\left|
\mathbb E\varphi(t,u_1^A,u_2^A)
-
\mathbb E\varphi(t,u_1^G,u_2^G)
\right|
>0
}
\tag{P21-NOSTEIN-01}
\]

for any smooth test class \(\mathcal T\) containing
\(\varphi(t,u_1,u_2)=\psi(u_1)\).

### Interpretation

A **uniform** correlated-Stein/logit CLT over all bounded leave-block coefficient vectors cannot be a valid proof ingredient for this theorem unless one separately proves delocalization or a Lindeberg-small-coordinate property of \(w_-\).

This does not contradict Hu–Lu. Their general universality theorem uses a more structured leave-one-out/Lindeberg/Stein argument for weakly correlated variables. The present counterexample only rules out the naïve claim that the complete teacher/logit triple is itself uniformly Gaussian for arbitrary bounded \(w\).

For the squared-ridge target, no such CLT is needed.

## 7. P21-MOM2-02 — exact second-moment comparison is sufficient

Condition on \(F\), the leave-block Hessian, and a fixed vector \(w\) independent of the current block. Define

\[
u_a^M=(x_{ia}^M)^\top w,
\qquad
M\in\{A,G\}.
\]

All three variables \(t,u_1,u_2\) are centered in both models.

The teacher variance is identical:

\[
\mathbb E_A t^2=\mathbb E_G t^2=1.
\]

For teacher-logit covariance,

\[
\left|
\mathbb E_A[t\,u_a]
-
\mathbb E_G[t\,u_a]
\right|
=
\left|
w^\top(c_a^A-c_a^G)
\right|
\le
C\varepsilon_d\|w\|.
\tag{7.1}
\]

For logit-logit covariance,

\[
\left|
\mathbb E_A[u_au_b]
-
\mathbb E_G[u_au_b]
\right|
=
\left|
w^\top(C_{ab}^A-C_{ab}^G)w
\right|
\le
\varepsilon_d\|w\|^2.
\tag{7.2}
\]

Consequently, for every fixed quadratic polynomial
\(Q_2(t,u_1,u_2)\),

\[
\boxed{
\left|
\mathbb E_AQ_2(t,u_1,u_2)
-
\mathbb E_GQ_2(t,u_1,u_2)
\right|
\le
C_{Q_2}\varepsilon_d(1+\|w\|^2).
}
\tag{P21-MOM2-02}
\]

This is exactly the comparison needed after leverage freezing.

## 12. Route comparison

### Block Lindeberg / leave-one-original-sample-block-out — preferred and proved

It preserves the shared original latent and shared surrogate residual, exposes the complete \(2\times2\) leverage matrix, and keeps the decisive \(1/N\) normalization visible.

### Direct correlated Stein for \((t,u_1,u_2)\) — false if demanded uniformly in arbitrary bounded \(w\)

P21-NOSTEIN-01 gives an explicit \(\tanh\), \(w=e_1\) counterexample.

A Stein theorem may become valid under an additional delocalization/Lindeberg-small-coordinate assumption on \(w\), but that hypothesis has not been proved for the leave-block optimizer and is not needed here.

### Continuous interpolation — no gain

Without the quadratic simplification it reintroduces third-derivative bookkeeping. With the simplification it is merely a longer version of the same moment comparison.

### Global resolvent comparison — unnecessarily strong

The proof needs only the local Woodbury resolvent at the current block. Full Gram matrices need not be close in operator norm under a natural coupling.
