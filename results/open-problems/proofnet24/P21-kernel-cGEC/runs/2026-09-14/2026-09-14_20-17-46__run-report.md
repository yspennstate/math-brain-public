Expand

\[
\sigma(\sqrt q\,x)

\sum_{k\ge0}
\widehat\sigma_k(q)h_k(x).

Because \(\sigma\) is odd,

\[
\widehat\sigma_k(q)=0
\qquad
\text{for every even }k.

\[

=
\sum_{k\ge1,\ k\ {\rm odd}}
\widehat\sigma_k(q)
\widehat\sigma_k(q')

\tag{6.1}

For the \(k=1\) coefficient,

\[
\widehat\sigma_1(q)

\[

\begin{aligned}
