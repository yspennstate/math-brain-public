## 1. Parent problem before/after, separately from mechanism gain

For a finite graph `G=(V,E)`, let

\[
I_G(\mathbf y)=\sum_{F\subseteq E:\,F\text{ acyclic}}\prod_{e\in F}y_e,
\qquad
\Delta_G(e,f)=I_eI_f-I_GI_{ef}.
\]

At unit weights, `Delta_G(e,f)>=0` is exactly pairwise negative correlation of distinct edges `e,f` when a forest is chosen uniformly from **all** acyclic spanning edge subsets, with all component counts mixed.

## 4. New all-parameter theorem

### Theorem `T07-TRIANGLE-LINEAR-FOREST7-STRIP-16`

Let `H` be any finite linear forest all of whose connected components have at most seven vertices. Then for

\[
G=K_3\vee H
\]

and every two distinct present edges `e,f`,

\[
\boxed{\Delta_G(e,f)>0.}
\]

Hence the uniform measure on all spanning forests of every such `G` has strict pairwise negative edge correlation.

If `H` is nonempty, `G` is 3-connected: deleting any two vertices leaves at least one triangle vertex, which is adjacent to every surviving right vertex, and the surviving triangle vertices are mutually adjacent. Every nonempty member contains a `K_4`, so the family is not series-parallel. Thus this is structurally inside the region left after P14's minimal-counterexample reduction but outside Erickson's class.

## 5. Five-state algebra and the exact response strip

Use terminal partitions

\[
D=0|1|2,\quad P_{12}=0|12,\quad P_{01}=01|2,\quad P_{02}=02|1,\quad T=012.
\]

For one isolated right vertex,

\[
S=4I+N,\qquad N^3=0,\quad N^2\ne0.
\]

Direct frontier enumeration gives, for `1<=l<=7`,

\[
T_{P_\ell}=A_\ell I+B_\ell N+C_\ell N^2,
\]

with

| `l` | `A_l` | `B_l` | `C_l` |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
|1|4|1|0|
|2|23|12|5/3|
|3|130|103|85/3|
|4|734|780|961/3|
|5|4144|5524|9050/3|
|6|23396|37512|25562|
|7|132088|247492|202132|

Normalize a layer by

\[
T_{P_\ell}/A_\ell=I+a_\ell N+c_\ell N^2,
\qquad a_\ell=B_\ell/A_\ell,\quad c_\ell=C_\ell/A_\ell.
\]

Define

\[
\rho_\ell=\frac{a_\ell^2/2-c_\ell}{a_\ell}.
\]

The exact values are

\[
\rho_1=\frac18,
\quad \rho_2=\frac{101}{828},
\quad \rho_3=\frac{9727}{80340},
\quad \rho_4=\frac{103613}{858780},
\]

\[
\rho_5=\frac{1033583}{8584296},
\quad \rho_6=\frac{13190815}{109703844},
\quad \rho_7=\frac{70123811}{583762916},
\]

and exact arithmetic gives

\[
\rho_1>\rho_2>\cdots>\rho_7>0.
\]

Now expose the one or two right components containing a chosen marked edge pair. For all remaining unmarked components, nilpotence gives

\[
\prod_j(I+a_jN+c_jN^2)=I+uN+vN^2,
\]

where

\[
u=\sum_j a_j,
\qquad
v=\sum_j c_j+\sum_{i<j}a_i a_j.
\]

Therefore

\[
\frac{u^2}{2}-v
=\sum_j\left(\frac{a_j^2}{2}-c_j\right)
=\sum_j\rho_j a_j.
\]

If `u>0`, set

\[
h=\frac{u^2/2-v}{u}.
\]

This is a weighted average of the `rho_j` with positive weights `a_j`, hence

\[
\boxed{\rho_7\le h\le\frac18},
\qquad
\boxed{v=\frac{u^2}{2}-hu}.
\]

If `u=0`, there are no unmarked components and `v=0`. This is the physical two-dimensional **response strip**. It replaces a seven-dimensional lattice of component counts.

## 6. Every Rayleigh numerator becomes one quadratic `Q(u,v)`

The triangle seed response is

\[
q=(1,1,1,1,3).
\]

If a core edge is required, the seed row is replaced by its exact required-edge response; if one or two right components contain required edges, their exact marked transfer matrices are inserted. After the marked components are exposed, every forest count has form

\[
q_XM_1\cdots M_s(I+uN+vN^2)\mathbf1,
\]

so each of `I,I_e,I_f,I_ef` is affine in `(u,v)`. Consequently

\[
\Delta(e,f)=Q_{e,f}(u,v)
\]

for an exact rational polynomial of degree at most two in `(u,v)`.

A universal labelled witness consisting of the triangle and **two copies of every `P_l`, `1<=l<=7`**, has 213 edges and 22,578 unordered edge pairs. It is exhaustive for local pair types: an edge pair meets at most two right components, and two labelled copies of each possible component order represent the same-component and two-component cases, with every path position and terminal label present.

The exact checker evaluates all 22,578 witness pairs. After exact polynomial deduplication there are

\[
\boxed{749}
\]

distinct `Q(u,v)` polynomials.

## 7. Strip positivity: 736 Bernstein certificates and 13 discriminant certificates

Parameterize the strip by

\[
h=\rho_7+y\left(\frac18-\rho_7\right),\qquad 0\le y\le1,
\]

and substitute

\[
v=\frac{u^2}{2}-hu.
\]

For each local polynomial write

\[
R(u,y)=Q\!\left(u,\frac{u^2}{2}-hu\right)
=\sum_{i=0}^4 r_i(y)u^i.
\]

Each `r_i` has degree at most two in `y`.

For **736 of the 749** distinct local polynomials, every `r_i(y)` has nonnegative degree-two Bernstein coefficients on `[0,1]`. Also `r_0=Q(0,0)` is a positive constant. The minimum positive Bernstein coefficient is `1`, and the minimum constant is `2`. Hence all 736 are strictly positive for every `u>=0` and every `y in [0,1]`.

Exactly 13 local polynomials fail that coefficientwise test. Up to the labelled witness conventions they are the same-path, different-terminal spoke pairs

- `P4`: `(0,3)`;
- `P5`: `(0,3),(0,4)`;
- `P6`: `(0,3),(0,4),(0,5),(1,4)`;
- `P7`: `(0,3),(0,4),(0,5),(0,6),(1,4),(1,5)`.

For every exceptional polynomial the strip substitution has the exact shape

\[
R(u,y)=a_4u^4-b_3(y)u^3+a_2(y)u^2+a_1(y)u+a_0,
\]

where `a_4>0`, `a_0>0`, and the exact degree-two Bernstein coefficients of `a_2(y)`, `a_1(y)`, and `b_3(y)` are all strictly positive on `[0,1]`. More importantly,

\[
\boxed{4a_4a_2(y)-b_3(y)^2=D>0}
\]

is **independent of `y`** in every one of the 13 cases. The exact positive constants are, in the order listed above,

\[
16476912,
12901593600,
1156460544,
12802993007616,
907023960000,
76503301056,
10096206840000,
\]

\[
12954731194211328,
902212338069504,
60028920979200,
4954651893504,
10019454655795200,
710839190880000.
\]

Thus the quadratic

\[
a_4u^2-b_3(y)u+a_2(y)
\]

has negative discriminant and positive leading coefficient, hence is positive for every real `u`. Therefore

\[
u^2(a_4u^2-b_3u+a_2)>0
\]

for `u>0`, while `a_1(y)u+a_0>0`; at `u=0`, `R=a_0>0`. Every exceptional polynomial is therefore strictly positive on the full response strip.

Combining the 736 Bernstein cases and 13 discriminant cases proves `T07-TRIANGLE-LINEAR-FOREST7-STRIP-16`.

The finite computation is only the exhaustive finite classification of local marked responses. The component multiplicities are not bounded or enumerated; their entire infinite parameter space is covered analytically by the response-strip argument.

## 9. What failed and what was learned

The first attempted global cone `v>=u^2/2-u/8` was too large: many exact local `Q(u,v)` polynomials are not positive by coefficientwise certification on that half-plane. The decisive correction is that physical path mixtures occupy a **narrow strip**, not an upper half-plane:

\[
\rho_7u\le \frac{u^2}{2}-v\le\frac18u.
\]

Even on that sharp strip, 13 same-path spoke geometries still leave the Bernstein cone. Their failure is real and recovers the same exceptional sector seen separately in the preceding `P4/P5/P6/P7` computations. The new fact is that all 13 share a uniform **negative-discriminant mechanism**, eliminating the need for separate lattice-corner rectangles.

This suggests that the useful invariant is not positivity of every mixed coefficient, which P14 already showed is too strong, but positivity after projecting the feasible three-terminal response to its actual low-dimensional region.

## 10. Residual obstruction and next attack

The theorem still does not control arbitrary 3-connected environments: a general right component need not have a transfer response whose normalized `(a,c)` ratio lies in the path strip, and marked responses need not share the 13-exception discriminant structure.

The next materially broader route is therefore:

1. use NM13-08's scalar path recurrences to study
   \(\rho_n=(a_n^2/2-c_n)/a_n\) for **all** path lengths `n`, asking whether `rho_n` decreases to an explicit positive limit;
2. derive recurrences for the exceptional marked-spoke discriminants as path length and positions vary;
3. prove the strip/Bernstein-plus-discriminant argument uniformly in `n`, which would yield `K_3` joined with an **arbitrary finite linear forest**, with no component-order cutoff;
4. refutation-first: if either `rho_n` exits the strip pattern or an exceptional discriminant becomes nonpositive, evaluate the corresponding exact physical graph immediately as a source-valid counterexample candidate.

That route uses the new recurrence theory rather than simply enumerating `P8` as an isolated next case.
