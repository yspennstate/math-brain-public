\[
\boxed{\kappa(T)<\kappa(S_*)}
\]

\]

### Proof

Let `C` be the symmetric Paley conference matrix in the already-verified projective normalization, and let

\[
A_T=C[T,T].
\]

Every `A_T` is a real symmetric `14 x 14` Seidel matrix with zero diagonal and off-diagonal entries in `{+1,-1}`. Its corresponding Gram matrix is

\[
G_T=I+\frac1{\sqrt{73}}A_T.
\]

The executed exact checker enumerates all `161070` two-swap neighbors and, for each one, checks positive definiteness of the integer matrix

\[
20A_T+161I.
\tag{3.1}
\]

The positive-definiteness test uses Sylvester's criterion with exact fraction-free Bareiss elimination. No floating eigenvalue enters the finite proof. Every leading principal pivot is positive for every neighbor. Therefore

\[
20A_T+161I\succ0,
\]

hence

\[
\lambda_{\min}(A_T)>-\frac{161}{20}.
\tag{3.2}
\]

Because each row of `A_T` has thirteen off-diagonal entries of absolute value one, Gershgorin gives

\[
\lambda_{\max}(A_T)\le 13.
\tag{3.3}
\]

Equations (3.2)–(3.3) imply

\[
\kappa(T)^2
<
\frac{1+13/\sqrt{73}}
{1-\frac{161}{20\sqrt{73}}}

\frac{71060+8420\sqrt{73}}{3279}.
\tag{3.4}

The checker also verifies exactly, in the quadratic-radical field generated by `sqrt(73)` and `sqrt(13)`, that

\[
