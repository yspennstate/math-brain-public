# 3. NEW-METHODS work: ideas before code

The research work preceded the finite checker. The checker was added only after the abstract route and a concrete noncommuting block had been identified.

## 3.1 Move #1 — CHANGE THE CATEGORY

The previous P15 spectral Bellman theorem is global and operator-theoretic: it uses a positive self-adjoint `A` and coefficients that strongly commute with the spectral resolution of `A`. The new route changes category.

Instead of looking first for a functional calculus identity on the infinite-dimensional space, ask for a **finite-dimensional homogeneous stochastic Lyapunov function** for a single critical block. Then repeat that same block over infinitely many spatial scales `lambda_k`, weighting the Lyapunov energy so that its dissipative term becomes exactly the full `X_1` norm.

The key finite-dimensional condition is

\[
DV(y)[My]-\frac12\sum_{j=1}^mD^2V(y)[C_jy,C_jy]\ge c_*|y|^p.
\tag{3.1}
\]

This condition sees the **joint nonlinear geometry** of drift and all noise matrices. It does not require `[M,C_j]=0` or `[C_i,C_j]=0`.

The infinite-dimensional idea is then to place the `k`th copy at parabolic scale `lambda_k`:

\[
A_k=\lambda_kM,
\qquad
B_{j,k}=\lambda_k^{1/2}C_j.
\]

The half-order scaling is critical: in Itô's formula both the drift and quadratic multiplicative-noise Hessian term acquire the same factor `lambda_k`.

## 3.2 Move #8 — DISCRETIZE, THEN LIFT

Rather than first proving an abstract Banach theorem, solve finite truncations exactly and demand an estimate independent of the truncation. The spatial truncation is not being used as a numerical approximation heuristic; it is a proof device that lets every Itô term be written componentwise with no hidden domain issue.

The correct energy weight turns out to be

\[
\lambda_k^{p-1}V(u_k).
\]

Multiplying (3.1) by the extra `lambda_k` contributed by the equation gives

\[
\lambda_k^p|u_k|^p,
\]

which is exactly the `ell^p` contribution to `||u||_{X_1}^p`.

The forcing and inhomogeneous stochastic terms land at precisely the source data scales `X_0` and `X_{1/2}` by Young inequalities; this is the mechanism that makes the finite block lift into a source-SMR estimate rather than only a homogeneous stability statement.

## 3.3 Move #22 — READ THE FAILURES

The nearest recent negative result is not a failed version of this exact theorem but a warning against overgeneralization: Lorist–van Neerven–Veraar 2026 exhibit stochastic maximal-regularity failure despite strong deterministic-looking operator hypotheses and show the relevance of Banach geometry.

So the theorem below deliberately fixes the geometry to weighted `ell^p(R^d)`, where the finite-block Lyapunov energy and the Gaussian square function have exactly the needed summability. I do **not** infer an arbitrary-UMD-space theorem from the calculation.

This move also prevents a false metric advance: a theorem obtained merely by replacing one Hilbert spectral identity with an unsupported general UMD assertion would not count. The new family counts only because the complete `ell^p` proof below closes.

# 4. New theorem

## P15-I0066-BLOCK-LYAPUNOV-05 — PROVED / PARTIAL TOWARD SOURCE

Fix `p>=2`, positive integers `d,m`, and a sequence `lambda_k>=lambda_0>0`. Let

\[
X_0=\ell^p(\mathbb N;\mathbb R^d),
\]

\[
X_1=\Bigl\{x=(x_k): (\lambda_kx_k)_k\in\ell^p\Bigr\},
\qquad
\|x\|_{X_1}=\Bigl(\sum_k\lambda_k^p|x_k|^p\Bigr)^{1/p},
\]

and

\[
X_{1/2}=\Bigl\{x=(x_k): (\lambda_k^{1/2}x_k)_k\in\ell^p\Bigr\}.
\]

Let `M` be a real symmetric positive-definite `d x d` matrix, and let `C_1,...,C_m` be arbitrary real `d x d` matrices. Define

\[
(Ax)_k=\lambda_kMx_k,
\qquad
(B_jx)_k=\lambda_k^{1/2}C_jx_k.
\tag{4.1}
\]

Assume there is a function `V in C^2(R^d;[0,infty))` and constants
`c_0,C_0,C_1',C_2',c_*>0` such that for all `y in R^d`,

\[
c_0|y|^p\le V(y)\le C_0|y|^p,
\tag{4.2}
\]

\[
|DV(y)|\le C_1'|y|^{p-1},
\qquad
\|D^2V(y)\|\le C_2'|y|^{p-2},
\tag{4.3}
\]

with the evident bounded-Hessian interpretation when `p=2`, and

\[
\boxed{
DV(y)[My]-\frac12\sum_{j=1}^mD^2V(y)[C_jy,C_jy]
\ge c_*|y|^p.
}
\tag{4.4}
\]

Then on every finite horizon `T`, for every deterministic starting time `s in [0,T]`, every bounded stopping time `tau in [s,T]`, zero initial data, and arbitrary progressive data

\[
f\in L^p(\Omega\times(s,\tau);X_0),
\qquad
g\in L^p(\Omega\times(s,\tau);\gamma(\mathbb R^m,X_{1/2})),
\]

the stochastic evolution equation

\[
du(t)+Au(t)\,dt=f(t)\,dt+(Bu(t)+g(t))\,dW(t)
\tag{4.5}
\]

has a unique adapted solution satisfying

\[
\boxed{
\|u\|_{L^p(\Omega\times(s,\tau);X_1)}
\le C
\left(
\|f\|_{L^p(\Omega\times(s,\tau);X_0)}
+
\|g\|_{L^p(\Omega\times(s,\tau);\gamma(\mathbb R^m,X_{1/2}))}
\right),
}
\tag{4.6}
\]

where `C` depends only on `p,d,m,M,C_j,V,c_*,lambda_0,T` through the displayed bounds, not on `s,tau`, the truncation, or the data.

Consequently `(A,B)` has the **base stochastic maximal `L^p`-regularity estimate in the source scale** for this weighted-block class.

No pairwise commutation of the `C_j` is assumed, and no commutation between `M` and the `C_j` is assumed. No separate source-style smallness hypothesis `||B||<epsilon_{A,p}` is imposed.

**Scope warning.** This theorem proves base SMR in this structured class. It does **not** assert the full stronger `SMR^bullet` package of temporal fractional estimates. It is PARTIAL toward Problem 1, not a resolution of the source problem.

# 5. Proof of P15-I0066-BLOCK-LYAPUNOV-05

## 5.1 Operator scale and boundedness of the noise map

Because `M` is positive definite on finite-dimensional `R^d`, there are constants `0<m_-<=m_+<infty` with

\[
m_-|y|\le |My|\le m_+|y|.
\]

Hence the diagonal operator `A` in (4.1) is closed and sectorial on `X_0`, and its graph domain is exactly `X_1` with equivalent graph norm. Since finite-dimensional functional calculus for `M` only changes norms by constants, the half-domain/interpolation scale is exactly `X_{1/2}` up to equivalent norms.

For `x in X_1`,

\[
\|B_jx\|_{X_{1/2}}^p
=
\sum_k\lambda_k^{p/2}
|\lambda_k^{1/2}C_jx_k|^p
=
\sum_k\lambda_k^p|C_jx_k|^p
\lesssim
\|x\|_{X_1}^p.
\]

Since the noise dimension `m` is finite, Gaussian Khintchine/Fubini gives the standard equivalence

\[
\|h\|_{\gamma(\mathbb R^m,X_{1/2})}^p
\simeq_{p,d,m}
\sum_k\lambda_k^{p/2}
\left(\sum_{j=1}^m|h_{j,k}|^2\right)^{p/2}.
\tag{5.1}
\]

Therefore

\[
B:X_1\longrightarrow\gamma(\mathbb R^m,X_{1/2})
\]

is bounded, as required by the source setup.

## 5.2 Finite truncations

Let `P_N` project onto blocks `k<=N`. Solve the finite-dimensional linear SDE with projected data. For each `k<=N`,

\[
du_k+\lambda_kMu_k\,dt
=f_k\,dt
+\sum_{j=1}^m
\bigl(\lambda_k^{1/2}C_ju_k+g_{j,k}\bigr)dW_t^j.
\tag{5.2}
\]

The coefficients are globally Lipschitz, so the finite system has a unique global adapted strong solution.

Define the weighted energy

\[
\mathcal V_N(u)=\sum_{k=1}^N\lambda_k^{p-1}V(u_k).
\tag{5.3}
\]

Apply Itô's formula on `[s,t wedge tau]`, initially after the standard localization of the martingale term.

## 5.3 Main drift and multiplicative quadratic variation

For a fixed block, the drift contribution from `-lambda_kMu_k` is

\[
-\lambda_k^p DV(u_k)[Mu_k]\,dt.
\]

The pure multiplicative part of the quadratic variation contributes

\[
\frac12\lambda_k^p
\sum_{j=1}^mD^2V(u_k)[C_ju_k,C_ju_k]\,dt.
\]

By (4.4), the sum is bounded above by

\[
-c_*\lambda_k^p|u_k|^p\,dt.
\tag{5.4}
\]

This is the exact critical-scale cancellation on which the theorem rests.

## 5.4 Deterministic forcing term

By (4.3),

\[
\lambda_k^{p-1}|DV(u_k)[f_k]|
\lesssim
\lambda_k^{p-1}|u_k|^{p-1}|f_k|.
\]

Writing this as `(lambda_k|u_k|)^{p-1}|f_k|` and applying Young's inequality gives, for every `epsilon>0`,

\[
\lambda_k^{p-1}|DV(u_k)[f_k]|
\le
\epsilon\lambda_k^p|u_k|^p+C_\epsilon|f_k|^p.
\tag{5.5}
\]

## 5.5 Cross term between multiplicative and additive noise

Put

\[
G_k=\left(\sum_{j=1}^m|g_{j,k}|^2\right)^{1/2}.
\]

Expanding the Hessian term in (5.2), the mixed part is bounded by

\[
C\lambda_k^{p-1/2}|u_k|^{p-1}G_k.
\]

Indeed one factor of the multiplicative noise contributes `lambda_k^{1/2}|u_k|`, while `D^2V` contributes `|u_k|^{p-2}` and the energy weight contributes `lambda_k^{p-1}`.

Now

\[
\lambda_k^{p-1/2}|u_k|^{p-1}G_k
=(\lambda_k|u_k|)^{p-1}\lambda_k^{1/2}G_k.
\]

Young with conjugate exponents `p/(p-1)` and `p` yields

\[
C\lambda_k^{p-1/2}|u_k|^{p-1}G_k
\le
\epsilon\lambda_k^p|u_k|^p
+C_\epsilon\lambda_k^{p/2}G_k^p.
\tag{5.6}
\]

## 5.6 Pure additive quadratic-variation term

For `p>2`, (4.3) gives a contribution bounded by

\[
C\lambda_k^{p-1}|u_k|^{p-2}G_k^2.
\]

Factor it as

\[
(\lambda_k|u_k|)^{p-2}\,\lambda_kG_k^2.
\]

Young with exponents `p/(p-2)` and `p/2` gives

\[
C\lambda_k^{p-1}|u_k|^{p-2}G_k^2
\le
\epsilon\lambda_k^p|u_k|^p
+C_\epsilon\lambda_k^{p/2}G_k^p.
\tag{5.7}
\]

When `p=2`, the Hessian is uniformly bounded and the pure additive term is directly `C lambda_k G_k^2`, exactly the same right-hand scale `lambda_k^{p/2}G_k^p`.

## 5.7 Summation, stopping, and the uniform estimate

Sum (5.4)–(5.7) over `k<=N`. Choose `epsilon` small enough that all occurrences of `epsilon lambda_k^p|u_k|^p` are absorbed by a fixed fraction of the coercive constant `c_*`.

After localization, stop at the arbitrary bounded `tau`, take expectation, and then remove the localization. The stochastic integral has expectation zero, and the terminal energy is nonnegative by (4.2). Thus

\[
\mathbb E\int_s^\tau
\sum_{k=1}^N\lambda_k^p|u_k(t)|^p\,dt
\le C
\mathbb E\int_s^\tau
\left[
\sum_{k=1}^N|f_k(t)|^p
+
\sum_{k=1}^N\lambda_k^{p/2}G_k(t)^p
\right]dt.
\tag{5.8}
\]

By (5.1), this is exactly

\[
\|u_N\|_{L^p(\Omega\times(s,\tau);X_1)}
\le C
\left(
\|P_Nf\|_{L^p(\Omega\times(s,\tau);X_0)}
+
\|P_Ng\|_{L^p(\Omega\times(s,\tau);\gamma(\mathbb R^m,X_{1/2}))}
\right).
\tag{5.9}
\]

The constant is independent of `N`, `s`, `tau`, and the data.

## 5.8 Infinite-dimensional limit

Because the equation is block diagonal in the spatial index, the difference `u_N-u_M` for `N>M` solves the same finite system on the tail blocks with data `(P_N-P_M)f` and `(P_N-P_M)g`. Applying (5.9) gives

\[
\|u_N-u_M\|_{L^p(\Omega\times(s,\tau);X_1)}
\le C\left(
\|(P_N-P_M)f\|_{L^p(X_0)}
+
\|(P_N-P_M)g\|_{L^p(\gamma(\mathbb R^m,X_{1/2}))}
\right),
\]

whose right-hand side tends to zero. Hence `u_N` converges to some

\[
u\in L^p(\Omega\times(s,\tau);X_1).
\]

The boundedness of `A:X_1->X_0` gives convergence of the drift terms in `L^p(\Omega\times(s,\tau);X_0)`. The boundedness

\[
B:X_1\to\gamma(\mathbb R^m,X_{1/2})
\hookrightarrow\gamma(\mathbb R^m,X_0)
\]

and the finite-horizon inequality

\[
\left(\int_s^T h(t)^2dt\right)^{p/2}
\le (T-s)^{p/2-1}\int_s^T h(t)^pdt
\]

show that the stochastic integrands converge in the space needed for the Burkholder–Davis–Gundy estimate. Since `X_0=ell^p(R^d)` is UMD for `1<p<infty`, the stochastic integrals converge in `L^p(\Omega;C([s,T];X_0))` after the standard localization/stopping argument. Coordinatewise passage to the limit gives (4.5).

Estimate (4.6) follows from (5.9) by lower semicontinuity, or directly by taking the limit.

## 5.9 Uniqueness

If `f=g=0`, apply (4.6) to the difference of two solutions. It gives

\[
\|u\|_{L^p(\Omega\times(s,\tau);X_1)}=0.
\]

Thus the solutions agree almost everywhere, and the mild/continuous `X_0` representatives agree indistinguishably. Uniqueness follows.

Because the estimate was proved after an arbitrary deterministic start `s` and an arbitrary bounded stopping time `tau`, with a constant independent of both, it has the stopping-time uniformity required for the base source SMR formulation in this class.

This completes the proof. `square`

# 6. Why this is a genuinely new counted family for P15

This is not family 4 with notation changed.

- Family 4 lives on a Hilbert spectral scale and relies on every `C_j` strongly commuting with the spectral projections of `A`.
- Here one may take `p!=2`, so `X_0=ell^p(R^d)` is non-Hilbert.
- Here `[M,C_j]` may be nonzero, so `B_j` need not commute with the block drift `A` even inside a common eigenscale.
- The noises `C_i,C_j` may also fail to commute with one another.
- The proof uses a finite-dimensional nonlinear Lyapunov inequality and componentwise Itô/Young estimates, not the semigroup Bellman identity from family 4.

Nor is it simply the source's fixed-point criterion. The theorem has no separate assumption that the operator norm of `B` be smaller than a universal `epsilon_{A,p}` derived from an additive maximal-regularity constant. In fact the family is stable under the scaling

\[
M\mapsto R^2M,
\qquad
C_j\mapsto RC_j,
\]

for which the Lyapunov inequality scales by `R^2`, while the critical noise operator norm grows like `R`.

Accordingly this theorem advances the frozen P15 metric from `4` to `5`.

# 8. What failed, what remains conditional, and what is not being claimed

## 8.1 Geometry-free extrapolation — rejected

A tempting route would be: finite-dimensional Lyapunov coercivity + UMD/type-2 ambient space should automatically give general stochastic maximal regularity. Current 2026 necessary-condition literature shows that such geometry-free claims are unsafe. The present theorem therefore fixes a weighted `ell^p` geometry where every step is explicit.

Status: **DISCARDED ROUTE**, not a theorem.

## 8.2 Full `SMR^bullet` upgrade — not proved here

The source's stronger bullet properties contain temporal fractional regularity/trace assertions beyond the base `L^p(X_1)` estimate. The current proof gives base SMR plus the usual `X_0` stochastic-integral continuity needed for existence/uniqueness. It does not establish every bullet estimate.

Status: **OPEN extension**.

## 8.3 Novelty — not claimed
