# Prior-art checkpoint for the general H2 continuation

Checked 2 October 2026. This is a targeted comparison, not an exhaustive novelty
certificate.

1. Çiçeksiz–Ravichandran, arXiv:2610.00549v1 (30 September 2026), primary full
   text: https://arxiv.org/html/2610.00549v1. Theorem 1.5 proves the second
   coefficient's sum of squares when the first vanishes. Theorem 4.8 supplies
   the separated-current-support/equipotential-interface classification.
   The discussion immediately preceding Theorem 4.8 explicitly leaves the
   next coefficient H2 or the full Rayleigh difference for investigation.
   Thus the current source does not state the global H2 theorem proved in
   `GENERAL_H2_SOS_PROOF.md`. Its K4-chain examples also show why an H2 theorem
   alone cannot resolve every remaining high-fugacity case.

2. Huang, arXiv:2311.00965v1 (2 November 2023), primary full text:
   https://arxiv.org/html/2311.00965v1. Theorems 2.1–2.2 prove large-fugacity
   negative correlation for adjacent edges of arbitrary finite graphs and
   arbitrary pairs on complete graphs with additional size assumptions. The
   published 2024 version is *On negative correlation of Arboreal Gas for
   specific graphs*, Statistics & Probability Letters,
   https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0167715224001433 (publisher result title located;
   exact journal/version contents not separately checked here). The checked
   preprint's stated results do not give a general third-coefficient formula.

3. Caracciolo–Jacobsen–Saleur–Sokal–Sportiello,
   *Fermionic Field Theory for Trees and Forests*, PRL 93, 080601 (2004),
   https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0403271,
   https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.93.080601. The Grassmann forest
   representation is established prior art, not a new ingredient of this
   continuation. The proof also supplies elementary all-minors counting for
   the specific Q2,Q3 identities, so its sign theorem does not rely on a
   newly asserted all-orders field-theory formula.

Targeted searches included “forest Rayleigh third coefficient”, “arboreal gas
H_2 correlation”, and “forests negative correlation Grassmann”. No older primary
source for this exact global conditional third-coefficient formula was located.
Absence from a targeted search does not establish novelty; specialist review
and a broader citation search are still required.

The theorem established is only H0=H1=0 implies H2>=0, with an explicit
electrical SOS. Neither all-fugacity forest negative correlation, general
high-fugacity negative correlation in the remaining zero cases, negative
association, nor q<1 random-cluster negative correlation is proved.
