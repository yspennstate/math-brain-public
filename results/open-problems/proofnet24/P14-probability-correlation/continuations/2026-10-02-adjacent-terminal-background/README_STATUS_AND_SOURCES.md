# Completion boundary for adjacent marked terminals

The shorter proof is forest_adjacent_terminal_core3_bernstein_proof.md;
its pending-at-creation wording is superseded by the complete separate
ADJACENT_BERNSTEIN_ANT_INDEPENDENT_AUDIT.md and adjacent_terminal_root_audit.md.
The original complete large-certificate proof and its independent audit
are retained. All eleven primitive variables of the 11,063-term
certificate are nonnegative; Gamma has no required upper bound.

Two adjacent marked edges have arbitrary positive activities. Rank-one
spokes, a weighted three-core, and an arbitrary finite graph on OTHER
terminals are allowed. No extra terminal edge touches the three marked
endpoints. Strictness is exactly outer rows positive and B>0; the middle
row may vanish. This is pairwise correlation, not negative association.

series-parallel SOS paper1008.3660 supply prior restricted cases:
