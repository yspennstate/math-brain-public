# Source and scope check: disjoint rank-one pair over a forest backbone

Checked 3 October 2026 UTC.  This is a bounded primary-source search, not a
historical priority certificate.

* Grimmett--Winkler, *Negative association in uniform forests and connected
  graphs*, primary manuscript: <https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0302185>.  This
  states the general uniform-forest edge negative-correlation conjecture and
  records finite computations.  The full conjecture remains strictly broader
  than the attachment family proved here.
* Borcea--Branden--Liggett, *Negative dependence and the geometry of
  polynomials*: <https://arxiv.org/abs/0707.2340>.  Strongly Rayleigh and
  determinantal negative-dependence results are established prior work.
  The unrooted forest law in the present family is not asserted strongly
  Rayleigh; the proof instead uses a family-specific determinant identity.
* Anari--Liu--Oveis Gharan--Vinzant, primary matroid log-concavity
  manuscript: <https://arxiv.org/abs/1811.01600>.  Ordinary homogeneous
  independent-set polynomial log-concavity is credited in preceding
  checkpoints.  The present disjoint-pair proof obtains the stronger
  one-variable apex factorization directly from tree covariance; it does not
  infer real-rootedness for cyclic backbones.
* Gupta, *Edges of the uniform random forest of K_n are pairwise negatively
  correlated for every n*: <https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26134>.  This proves
  the unweighted complete-graph case.  It neither contains nor is implied by
  the weighted forest-backbone proportional-attachment theorem here.
* Ciceksiz--Ravichandran, *Negative Correlations for Forests and the q<1
  Random Cluster Model*, v1 submitted 30 September 2026:
  <https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00549>.  It gives the first two general
  high-fugacity coefficients/equality geometry and complete-graph q<1
  regimes.  Its abstract still treats arbitrary finite-graph arboreal edge
  correlation as unresolved.  The all-fugacity proportional-attachment
  configuration in this checkpoint is different and uses a full exact
  Rayleigh identity.

Targeted searches for proportional/rank-one four-terminal attachments,
forest-backbone apex polynomials, and disjoint marked pairs found no primary
source stating the precise theorem.  This negative result is weak evidence
only and cannot certify originality.

The proved graph has a weighted forest backbone, one common nonnegative
profile, four new vertices with proportional spoke rows, and exactly two
disjoint marked mutual edges.  It allows arbitrary finite backbone size,
arbitrary number of common neighbors, nonnegative activities, positive
fields on the backbone/marked edges, and coordinate/cylinder conditioning
of backbone edge indicators.  It does **not** cover cyclic backbones,
nonproportional spoke rows, individual spoke fields, other edges incident to
the new vertices, arbitrary-event conditioning, a whole matching vector, or
the general forest negative-correlation/association conjecture.

Historical novelty and external specialist review remain unverified.
