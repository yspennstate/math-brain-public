# Primary-source and scope checkpoint

Checked 2 October 2026. This record distinguishes the mathematical induction
from research priority. No contact with authors or outside publication was
performed.

1. Caracciolo, Jacobsen, Saleur, Sokal and Sportiello, *Fermionic field
   theory for trees and forests*, arXiv:cond-mat/0403271v2, revised
   4 September 2004. The full five-page primary manuscript was inspected,
   especially formula (13):
   <https://arxiv.org/pdf/cond-mat/0403271>.
   The forest/Grassmann representation is classical. The present grounded
   all-orders feature argument explicitly verifies the convention used here.

2. Huang, *On Negative Correlation of Arboreal Gas on Some Graphs*,
   arXiv:2311.00965v1, submitted 2 November 2023; journal version *On negative
   correlation of Arboreal Gas for specific parameters*, Statistics &
   Probability Letters 213 (2024), 110174:
   <https://arxiv.org/pdf/2311.00965>,
   <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.spl.2024.110174>.
   The full primary preprint, especially Theorems 2.1 and 2.2, was inspected.
   It gives an arbitrary-graph sufficiently-large-parameter theorem for
   adjacent edges, and additional complete-graph regimes. Those statements
   do not cover every nonadjacent pair in every weighted graph.

3. Çiçeksiz and Ravichandran, *Negative Correlations for Forests and the
   q<1 Random Cluster Model*, arXiv:2610.00549v1, submitted 30 September
   2026 at 18:26:54 UTC. On the primary abstract page checked today, v1 is
   the listed submission; do not infer a new version from a crawl date:
   <https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00549>,
   <https://arxiv.org/html/2610.00549v1>.
   The actual theorem and equality statements were read in full. Theorem
   1.5 and Corollary 1.7 cover the first two large-fugacity coefficients and
   isolate their simultaneous equality. Section 4.2 explicitly discusses
   arbitrarily long leading-zero strings in K4 chains. The new induction
   continues through this equality instead of assuming a finite coefficient
   list is sufficient. Section 6.1 concerns connectivity-event covariance;
   its leading-order dichotomy is not the edge-Rayleigh inequality.

The complete graph results, current two-coefficient formula and equality
geometry, older forest representation, and classical supersymmetry tools
are credited. The new claimed mathematical content is the invariant-compatible
all-orders marked-score absorption and its finite termination.

Targeted current searches included arboreal-gas negative correlation at large
fugacity on arbitrary graphs, forest negative edge correlation on every
finite graph, and supersymmetric/Ward approaches. No earlier complete proof
of the precise all-pairs high-fugacity statement was located in the primary
sources reviewed. Search coverage was incomplete: absence of a hit is not
proof of absence. Historical novelty and definitive priority remain
unverified, and the full all-positive-fugacity Kahn conjecture remains outside
this theorem. The q<1 random-cluster and negative-association conjectures are
also not settled.
