# Source and scope: one-distinguished-arm theta stability

Checked 3 October 2026.

The proof uses the established multiaffine Rayleigh characterization and
strong-Rayleigh consequences from Borcea--Branden--Liggett,
arXiv:0707.2340. Huang, arXiv:2311.00965, and Ciceksiz--Ravichandran,
arXiv:2610.00549, are credited for the broader weighted-forest conjecture,
reductions, identities, and current established regimes.

Targeted primary-source searches for combinations of `theta graph`,
`K_{2,3}`, `apex forest polynomial`, `real stable`, and `strongly Rayleigh`
did not locate this theorem. That is a bounded search, not a priority or
novelty certification. No external specialist review has occurred.

Proved here: one `K_{2,3}` arm has activities `rt,rt`, the other four edges
have activity `t`, for arbitrary `r,t>0`; all five apex-spoke variables are
independent.

Not proved: simultaneous endpoint-side and arm asymmetry; six unrelated
backbone activities; arbitrary theta subdivisions; two-terminal or
series-parallel closure; backbone-edge conditioning; nonproportional rows;
joint negative association involving backbone or marked edges; the full
forest negative-correlation conjecture.
