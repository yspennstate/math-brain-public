# 11. New low-dimensional symmetry counterexample

\[

Z_2^2 & -Z_1Z_2\\
-Z_1Z_2 & Z_1^2
\end{pmatrix}.
\tag{11.1}

By coordinate exchangeability each diagonal expectation is \(1/2\).  The off-diagonal expectation is

\[
\begin{aligned}

+

\frac19\left(-\frac12\right)

\tag{11.2}

\[
\boxed{

=
\begin{pmatrix}

-1/10 & 1/2
\end{pmatrix}

\tag{11.3}
\]

Its purity is

\[

=
