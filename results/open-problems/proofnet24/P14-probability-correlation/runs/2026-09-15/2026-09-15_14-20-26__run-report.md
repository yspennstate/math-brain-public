## `P14-ORDINARYPREFIX-HOEFFDING-20` — PROVED

For every pair of ranks \(d\ge e\), let \(b_d=E\Delta_d(F_d)\). Couple the two ordinary-prefix histories by sharing exactly the first \(k\) ordinary residual rows, \(0\le k\le D-d\), and complete the two histories independently afterwards. If \(T_k^{d,e}\) denotes the resulting Hilbert--Schmidt pairing, then

\[
\boxed{
\langle b_d,b_e\rangle_F
=T_0^{d,e}
=T_{D-d}^{d,e}-\sum_{k=1}^{D-d} I_k^{d,e}.
}
\tag{0.4}
\]

Each correction is an exact one-row conditional covariance. More precisely, after the first \(k-1\) common rows are frozen, let \(\Phi_{k,d}(x)\) and \(\Phi_{k,e}(x)\) be the future-averaged rank-\(d\) and rank-\(e\) signed defects when the kth fresh ordinary direction equals \(x\). Then

\[
\boxed{
I_k^{d,e}
=E\langle \Phi_{k,d}(X)-E_X\Phi_{k,d}(X),
          \Phi_{k,e}(X)-E_X\Phi_{k,e}(X)\rangle_F.
}
\tag{0.5}
\]

Equivalently, with \(X,X'\) iid fresh ordinary residual directions,

\[
\boxed{
I_k^{d,e}
=\frac12 E\left\langle
\Phi_{k,d}(X)-\Phi_{k,d}(X'),
\Phi_{k,e}(X)-\Phi_{k,e}(X')
\right\rangle_F.
}
\tag{0.6}
\]

This is an exact exchangeable-row localization of the missing history cancellation.

# 4. Signed transition variables

Fix the realized apex \(A\). Let \(\nu_A\) be the ordinary residual row-direction law.

For a rank-\(d\) projector \(F\), one fresh direction \(q\sim\nu_A\) removes

\[
u_F(q)=\frac{Fq}{\sqrt{q^\top Fq}}
\]

and produces

\[
F'=F-u_F(q)u_F(q)^\top.
\tag{4.1}
\]

Define

\[
R_{\nu_A}(F)
=E_{q\sim\nu_A}\left[\frac{Fqq^\top F}{q^\top Fq}\right].
\tag{4.2}
\]

Then

\[
\boxed{
\Delta_d(F)
=E\left[\frac{F'}{d-1}\middle|F\right]-\frac Fd
=\frac1{d-1}\left[\frac Fd-R_{\nu_A}(F)\right].

\tag{4.3}
\]

b_d=E\Delta_d(F_d).

The inherited signed Duhamel theorem gives

\[
\boxed{M_A-I_D/D=\sum_{d=2}^{D}b_d.}
\tag{4.4}

Because \(\operatorname{tr}M_A=1\),

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{tr}(M_A^2)-1/D

\tag{4.5}

In the Haar/Gaussian model, \(R_{\nu_A}(F)=F/d\) for every deterministic \(F\), so every \(\Delta_d(F)\) vanishes pointwise.

# 6. Proof of the ordinary-prefix Hoeffding telescope

Fix \(d\ge e\ge2\) and set

\[
n_d=D-d,
\qquad
n_e=D-e.
\]

Let

\[
h_d(x_1,\ldots,x_{n_d})=\Delta_d(F_d(x_1,\ldots,x_{n_d})),
\]

and similarly \(h_e\).

Take three mutually independent iid ordinary-direction sequences \(C_i,X_i,Y_i\). For \(0\le k\le n_d\), define

\[
H_d^{(k)}=h_d(C_1,\ldots,C_k,X_{k+1},\ldots,X_{n_d}),
\]

\[
H_e^{(k)}=h_e(C_1,\ldots,C_k,Y_{k+1},\ldots,Y_{n_e}),
\]

and

\[
T_k^{d,e}=E\langle H_d^{(k)},H_e^{(k)}\rangle_F.
\tag{6.1}
\]

At \(k=0\), the histories are independent:

\[
T_0^{d,e}=\langle b_d,b_e\rangle_F.
\tag{6.2}
\]

Fix \(1\le k\le n_d\), freeze \(C_1,\ldots,C_{k-1}\), and let

\[
\Phi_{k,d}(x)
=E[h_d(C_{<k},x,X_{>k})\mid C_{<k},x],
\]

\[
\Phi_{k,e}(x)
=E[h_e(C_{<k},x,Y_{>k})\mid C_{<k},x].
\]

Then

\[
T_k^{d,e}
=E E_x\langle\Phi_{k,d}(x),\Phi_{k,e}(x)\rangle_F,
\]

whereas

\[
T_{k-1}^{d,e}
=E\langle E_x\Phi_{k,d}(x),E_x\Phi_{k,e}(x)\rangle_F.
\]

Subtracting gives

\[
\boxed{
T_k^{d,e}-T_{k-1}^{d,e}=I_k^{d,e},

\tag{6.3}
\]

with \(I_k^{d,e}\) as in `(0.5)`. Telescoping proves `(0.4)`.

For iid \(X,X'\), the Hilbert covariance identity

\[
\operatorname{Cov}(\Phi(X),\Psi(X))

gives `(0.6)`.

For \(d=e\),

\[

and

\[
\sum_{k=1}^{D-d}I_k^{d,d}
=E\|\Delta_d(F_d)\|_F^2-\|b_d\|_F^2.
\tag{6.4}

# 11. Low-dimensional exact counterexample to an isotropy shortcut
