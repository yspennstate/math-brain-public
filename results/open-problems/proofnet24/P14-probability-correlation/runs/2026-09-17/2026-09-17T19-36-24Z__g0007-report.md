## 3. Move #2 — attack `Theta_I(lambda_p)>0` and derive what any counterexample must look like

The first goal was not to prove critical nonpercolation but to assume its negation and extract rigid consequences.

### Claim `G0007-GEODESIC-PERSISTENCE-EXPONENTS-02`

For a fixed `d>=3` and `lambda>lambda_1`, let `v_0,...,v_n` be any geodesic segment in `T_d`, and define

\[
q_n^I(\lambda)
:=\nu_\lambda(v_0,\ldots,v_n\text{ all infected}),
\]

\[
q_n^H(\lambda)
:=\nu_\lambda(v_0,\ldots,v_n\text{ all healthy}).
\]

Then the limits

\[
\alpha_I(\lambda):=\lim_{n\to\infty}(q_n^I(\lambda))^{1/n},
\qquad
\alpha_H(\lambda):=\lim_{n\to\infty}(q_n^H(\lambda))^{1/n}
\]

exist and equal the corresponding suprema over `n>=1`. Moreover:

1. `alpha_I` is nondecreasing and left-continuous in `lambda`;
2. `alpha_H` is nonincreasing and right-continuous in `lambda`;
3. if `(d-1) alpha_I(lambda)<1`, then `Theta_I(lambda)=0`;
4. if `(d-1) alpha_H(lambda)<1`, then `Theta_H(lambda)=0`;
5. conversely, `Theta_I(lambda)>0` implies `alpha_I(lambda)>=1/(d-1)`, and `Theta_H(lambda)>0` implies `alpha_H(lambda)>=1/(d-1)`.

This is a repository lemma/reframing of the tree first-moment mechanism, not a novelty claim against the literature.

### Proof

The upper invariant measure of the attractive contact process is associated. Equivalently, increasing cylinder events are positively correlated; the same is true for pairs of decreasing events by changing signs of their indicators.

Let

\[
A=\{v_0,\ldots,v_m\text{ all infected}\},
\quad
B=\{v_m,\ldots,v_{m+n}\text{ all infected}\}.
\]

Both are increasing, so association gives

\[
q_{m+n}^I=\nu_\lambda(A\cap B)
\ge \nu_\lambda(A)\nu_\lambda(B)
=q_m^I q_n^I.
\]

The same argument applies to all-healthy events because both are decreasing. Therefore `log q_n^I` and `log q_n^H` are superadditive (the probabilities are positive at finite `lambda`), and Fekete's lemma gives existence of both exponential-rate limits and equality with the suprema.

For fixed `n`, `q_n^I(lambda)` and `q_n^H(lambda)` are finite cylinder probabilities, hence continuous in `lambda` by the previous P14 theorem. Consequently each exponent is a supremum of continuous functions and is lower semicontinuous. `alpha_I` is nondecreasing by attractiveness. For a nondecreasing function, lower semicontinuity forces equality with its left limit, hence left continuity. Similarly `alpha_H` is nonincreasing and lower semicontinuity forces equality with its right limit.

Let `Z_n^I` count vertices `x` at graph distance `n` from the root `o` for which `o` and `x` are connected by an infected path. Because `T_d` is a tree, there is a unique path from `o` to `x`, so

\[
\mathbb E Z_n^I
=d(d-1)^{n-1}q_n^I.
\]

If `(d-1)alpha_I<1`, choose `epsilon>0` with `(d-1)(alpha_I+epsilon)<1`. For all sufficiently large `n`, `q_n^I <= (alpha_I+epsilon)^n`, so `E Z_n^I ->0`. Since an infinite infected root cluster requires `Z_n^I>=1` for every `n`,

\[
\Theta_I(\lambda)
\le \mathbb P(Z_n^I\ge1)
\le \mathbb E Z_n^I\to0.
\]

Thus `Theta_I=0`. The healthy statement is identical.

Conversely, if `Theta_I(lambda)>0`, then for every `n`,

\[
\mathbb E Z_n^I
\ge \mathbb P(Z_n^I\ge1)
\ge \Theta_I(\lambda),
\]

so

\[
q_n^I\ge
\frac{\Theta_I(\lambda)}{d(d-1)^{n-1}}.
\]

Taking `n`th roots and passing to the limit gives `alpha_I>=1/(d-1)`. Again the healthy proof is identical. `square`

### Consequence for a putative critical infected cluster

If the desired conclusion fails, i.e.

\[
\Theta_I(\lambda_p)>0,
\]

then necessarily

\[
\alpha_I(\lambda_p)\ge \frac1{d-1}.
\]

This does **not** contradict the definition of `lambda_p`: the reverse implication `alpha_I>1/(d-1) => percolation` is not available for this dependent measure. The point is to isolate the exact missing reverse direction instead of assuming a Bernoulli branching recursion.

### Häggström multiplicity constraint under the same negation

There is a second independent structural consequence. Given an infected site configuration, declare a tree edge open exactly when both endpoints are infected. This produces an automorphism-invariant dependent bond percolation whose infinite edge clusters are exactly the infinite infected site clusters.

At finite `lambda`, the upper invariant law is not the all-infected configuration: by time reversal, the one-site infection density is the survival probability from one infected site, and the initial site recovers before producing any infection with probability at least `1/(1+d lambda)>0`. Thus the induced bond process is not the trivial all-open process.

Häggström's 1997 theorem for nontrivial automorphism-invariant percolation on a homogeneous tree says the number of infinite clusters is almost surely either zero or infinity. Therefore

\[
\boxed{
\Theta_I(\lambda)>0
\Longrightarrow
\text{there are a.s. infinitely many infinite infected clusters.}
}
\]

In particular, a critical counterexample at `lambda_p` cannot be a Bernoulli-like “single emerging giant cluster”; it would have to create infinitely many infinite stationary infected clusters at the transition.

This is a structural reduction, not `C_crit` movement.

## 4. The naive tree-cavity independence claim is false after conditioning on the root state

The immediate prompt asked whether conditioning on an appropriate root time-line makes branches independent enough for a recursion. The first version of that idea—“condition on the root being infected and then multiply branch probabilities”—fails for a basic collider reason.

Consider two independent branch messages `E_1,E_2 ~ Bernoulli(p)`, `0<p<1`, and suppose the root is reinfected exactly when at least one branch succeeds:

\[
U=E_1\lor E_2.
\]

Then

\[
\mathbb P(E_1\mid U)=\frac{1}{2-p},
\qquad
\mathbb P(E_1\cap E_2\mid U)=\frac{p}{2-p},
\]

so

\[
\operatorname{Cov}(1_{E_1},1_{E_2}\mid U)
=-\frac{(1-p)^2}{(2-p)^2}<0.
\]

Thus conditioning on a root state that is an OR of independent incoming branch messages **creates dependence between the branches**. This is exactly the local logical shape of a Harris construction at a root that can be reinfected from more than one child branch after a recovery.

This does not rule out every cavity argument. Conditioning on primitive Poisson marks before conditioning on the resulting root infection state may preserve a product decomposition, and one-way/severed branch processes can be genuinely independent. What is ruled out is the shortcut that treats branch states as independent merely because the underlying subtree Poisson processes were independent before conditioning on the common root event.

## 5. Move #6 — deform to finite time, where independence is local, and identify the quantity that must survive the limit

A productive deformation is to replace the upper invariant time slice by the contact process started from full infection and observed after a finite time `t`. On a finite time slab, the Harris construction is a genuine product of finitely/relevantly many local Poisson variables after truncation, and decision-tree methods have a clean product-space input.

This is where the valid part of the Beekenkamp program lives. His 2021 dissertation proves sharpness for the finite-time percolation transition. In the later upper-invariant discussion, the intended route is to compare

\[
\theta_n(t,\lambda)
=\mathbb P_{t,\lambda}(o\leftrightarrow S_n)
\]

with its stationary limit as `t -> infinity` and to control a differential inequality schematically of the form

\[
-\partial_t\theta_n(t,\lambda)
\lesssim
\varepsilon(t)\,\partial_\lambda\theta_n(t,\lambda),
\qquad \varepsilon(t)\to0,
\]

uniformly in the spatial radius `n` and in `lambda` near the transition. Such an estimate would let one transfer the finite-time threshold information to the upper invariant threshold.

The dissertation explains why the obvious proof does not close: a revealment algorithm that exposes the whole backward dependency cluster has a cost/revealment constant that deteriorates as `t` grows, and the key derivative comparison would require decoupling a pivotal event from the event that the finite-time and stationary configurations disagree at a site. That is precisely a dependence estimate, not a missing line of OSSS algebra.

The regular tree gives a possible new angle, but not a free solution. Removing a vertex separates space into branches, yet the root state feeds infections back into each branch, and those feedback arrows re-couple the branch histories. The exact collider calculation above shows why conditioning only on the final root state is insufficient.

A tree-specific finite-time target that would actually advance the proof is therefore:

> Condition on a **primitive interface skeleton** (root recovery process and root-edge arrow times) and formulate branch histories as temporal messages. Prove a decoupling/revealment bound for the pivotal event using those messages that remains uniform as the time slab grows, or prove rigorously that no such uniform bound can hold.

This is stronger and more precise than “use branch independence.”
