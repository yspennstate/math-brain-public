## 5. New theorem: continuity reduces exactly to critical nonpercolation

### Claim `G0007-CRITICAL-NONPERCOLATION-REDUCTION-01`

Fix a regular tree `T_d` in the source setting. Let

\[
\Theta_I(\lambda)=\nu_\lambda(o\leftrightarrow\infty\text{ through infected vertices}),
\]

and

\[
\Theta_H(\lambda)=\nu_\lambda(o\leftrightarrow\infty\text{ through healthy vertices}).
\]

Then:

1. every finite-dimensional cylinder probability of `nu_lambda` is continuous in `lambda`;
2. `Theta_I` is nondecreasing and **right-continuous**;
3. `Theta_H` is nonincreasing and **left-continuous**;
4. consequently, under the source threshold conventions,

\[
\Theta_I\text{ is continuous at }\lambda_p
\iff
\Theta_I(\lambda_p)=0,
\tag{5.1}
\]

and

\[
\Theta_H\text{ is continuous at }\lambda_{p^\complement}
\iff
\Theta_H(\lambda_{p^\complement})=0.
\tag{5.2}
\]

Thus the first Fernley–Jacob source question is exactly the pair of critical-nonpercolation statements counted by `C_crit`.

### Proof

The proof uses one external theorem and standard contact-process identities, with every logical step spelled out.

**Step 1: continuity of finite-set extinction probabilities.** Xiangying Huang, *Exponential growth and continuous phase transitions for the contact process on trees* (2019), proves that on periodic trees the single-site contact-process survival probability

\[
\rho_{\{x\}}(\lambda)
:=\Pr_\lambda^{\{x\}}(\xi_t\neq\varnothing\ \forall t\ge0)
\]

is continuous in `lambda`. A regular tree is periodic.

For any finite set `A`, use the standard basic graphical coupling simultaneously in the initial set and in `lambda`. Contact-process additivity gives, in one graphical realization,

\[
\xi_t^A=\bigcup_{x\in A}\xi_t^{\{x\}}.
\]

Because `A` is finite, survival from `A` is exactly the union of the individual survival events. If `lambda' < lambda`, monotonicity of the graphical coupling therefore yields

\[
0\le \rho_A(\lambda)-\rho_A(\lambda')
\le
\sum_{x\in A}
\bigl(\rho_{\{x\}}(\lambda)-\rho_{\{x\}}(\lambda')\bigr).
\tag{5.3}
\]

By transitivity of `T_d`, the summands are all the same single-site difference. Huang's continuity therefore implies continuity of `rho_A(lambda)` for every finite `A`.

**Step 2: self-duality gives continuity of finite-set void probabilities.** Start the contact process from all infected sites and let time tend to infinity. Standard contact-process self-duality gives, for finite `A`,

\[
\nu_\lambda(\eta\cap A=\varnothing)
=\Pr_\lambda^A(\text{extinction})
=1-\rho_A(\lambda).
\tag{5.4}
\]

Hence every finite-set void probability is continuous in `lambda`.

**Step 3: void probabilities determine every cylinder probability.** For finite `B` and `S\subseteq B`, the event `eta\cap B=S` has indicator

\[
\mathbf 1_{\{\eta\cap(B\setminus S)=\varnothing\}}
\prod_{x\in S}
\bigl(1-\mathbf 1_{\{x\notin\eta\}}\bigr).
\]

Expanding gives the exact inclusion–exclusion formula

\[
\nu_\lambda(\eta\cap B=S)
=
\sum_{T\subseteq S}(-1)^{|T|}
\nu_\lambda\bigl(\eta\cap((B\setminus S)\cup T)=\varnothing\bigr).
\tag{5.5}
\]

Each summand is continuous by (5.4), so every finite-dimensional cylinder probability is continuous.

**Step 4: finite-radius connection probabilities are continuous.** Let `B_n` be the radius-`n` ball around the root and `S_n` its sphere. Let `I_n` be the cylinder event that the root is joined to `S_n` by an infected path entirely in `B_n`, and `H_n` the analogous healthy-path event. Since these depend only on finitely many vertices, (5.5) implies that

\[
i_n(\lambda):=\nu_\lambda(I_n),
\qquad
h_n(\lambda):=\nu_\lambda(H_n)
\]

are continuous in `lambda`.

The events decrease in `n`, and an infinite locally finite tree has an infinite root cluster exactly when the root connects to every sphere. Therefore

\[
\Theta_I(\lambda)=\inf_{n\ge1} i_n(\lambda),
\qquad
\Theta_H(\lambda)=\inf_{n\ge1} h_n(\lambda).
\tag{5.6}
\]

**Step 5: one-sided continuity.** An infimum of continuous functions is upper semicontinuous. Attractiveness of the contact process makes `nu_lambda` stochastically increasing in `lambda`. Thus `Theta_I` is nondecreasing, while `Theta_H` is nonincreasing.

A nondecreasing upper-semicontinuous real function is right-continuous: its right limit is at least its value by monotonicity and at most its value by upper semicontinuity. Similarly a nonincreasing upper-semicontinuous function is left-continuous. This proves assertions 2 and 3.

**Step 6: threshold equivalence.** By definition of the infected percolation threshold, `Theta_I(lambda)=0` for `lambda<lambda_p`. The only possible discontinuity at `lambda_p` is therefore a jump from zero on the left to `Theta_I(lambda_p)>0`; right continuity is already known. Hence (5.1).

Likewise, by definition of the healthy threshold, `Theta_H(lambda)=0` for `lambda>lambda_{p^complement}`. Since left continuity is already known, continuity at the threshold is equivalent to the threshold value itself being zero, giving (5.2).

This completes the proof. `square`

It does, however, replace an apparently two-sided continuity problem by two sharply defined one-point percolation problems and proves the reduction on the page without a numerical grid.

## 7. Method #21 — who else has this shape?

Three neighboring techniques were checked against their actual hypotheses.

1. **Huang (2019): survival transition on trees.** Huang proves continuity of the contact-process survival probability and extinction at the weak-survival critical value on periodic trees. This supplies the finite-dimensional continuity input in the theorem above. But `lambda_p` concerns spatial percolation of the *upper invariant snapshot*, not survival of the space-time infection process from one finite seed. Huang's theorem therefore does not itself prove `Theta_I(lambda_p)=0`.

2. **Beekenkamp (2018): upper-invariant percolation on `Z^d`.** Beekenkamp proves sharpness of the upper-invariant contact-process percolation transition on `Z^d`, with exponentially small clusters throughout the subcritical regime and a mean-field lower bound above threshold, using an OSSS/decision-tree argument. This is much closer in shape to G0007 than generic FKG. The regular-tree geometry may simplify exploration but changes the revealment bookkeeping; importing the result requires an actual tree-adapted decision-tree inequality, not a citation by analogy.

3. **Benjamini–Lyons–Peres–Schramm / Hutchcroft: critical Bernoulli percolation on nonamenable or exponential-growth graphs.** These results prove absence of critical infinite clusters for independent Bernoulli percolation in broad nonamenable/quasi-transitive settings. The regular tree is in their geometric world, but `nu_lambda` is a dependent field generated by the contact process. Independence is not a disposable hypothesis.

The nearest solved mechanism is therefore not 'nonamenability implies the answer'. It is **sharpness for a dependent upper-invariant contact measure**, with Beekenkamp's OSSS machinery the closest model match and Huang's tree survival continuity providing the local-parameter continuity already used.
