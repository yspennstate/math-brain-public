# 10. `P14-DIAGONAL-REVEAL-PYTHAGORAS-34` — PROVED

Fix a deterministic rank-`n` parent `P`, diagonal `p_i`, and let

\[
\mathcal F_0\subset\mathcal F_1\subset\cdots\subset\mathcal F_{n-1}
\]

be the filtration generated by successively revealing the remaining future original rows. Put

\[
M_j=E[\Lambda\mid P,\mathcal F_j],
\]

so `M_0=M_P` and `M_{n-1}=Lambda=zz^T`.

On diagonal vectors define the fixed-parent weighted Hilbert norm

\[
\|x\|_{p^{-1}}^2
=\sum_{i:p_i>0}\frac{x_i^2}{p_i}.
\]

The diagonal processes `diag(M_j)` form a square-integrable Hilbert-valued martingale: `0<=Lambda_ii<=p_i` makes every weighted second moment finite. Martingale increments are orthogonal in `L^2`. Therefore

\[
\begin{aligned}
&\sum_{j=1}^{n-1}
E\|\operatorname{diag}(M_j-M_{j-1})\|_{p^{-1}}^2\\
&\qquad=
E\|\operatorname{diag}(\Lambda-M_P)\|_{p^{-1}}^2\\
&\qquad=
E\sum_i\frac{(z_i^2-(M_P)_{ii})^2}{p_i}.
\end{aligned}
\]

Expanding and using `E z_i^2=(M_P)_ii` gives

\[
\boxed{
\sum_{j=1}^{n-1}
E\sum_i
\frac{((M_j-M_{j-1})_{ii})^2}{p_i}
=J_2(P)-\sum_i\frac{(M_P)_{ii}^2}{p_i}.
}
\tag{10.1}
\]

The first increment is `K_U=M_{P,U}-M_P`, so `(10.1)` implies

\[
E_U\Theta_P(K_U)
\le J_2(P),
\]

recovering the earlier P14 Jensen bound and strengthening it to a **global future-row budget**.

By P24's pointwise theorem, for every fixed `epsilon>0`, the full left side of `(10.1)` is at most `C_{epsilon,xi}n^{-1+epsilon}`.

For Haar geometry,

\[
J_2(P)=\frac3{n+2},
\qquad
\sum_i\frac{(M_P)_{ii}^2}{p_i}=\frac1n,
\]

so the exact total diagonal martingale energy is

\[
\frac{2(n-1)}{n(n+2)}.
\]

This theorem does not control the full matrix frame defect in `(3.3)`; in particular it does not replace the off-diagonal `q=3` sibling geometry.

# 11. `P14-FIXED-RENYI-ORDERS-NOGO-34` — PROVED / ROUTE-KILL
