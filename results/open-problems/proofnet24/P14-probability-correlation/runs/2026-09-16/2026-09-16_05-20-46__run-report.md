# 8. `P14-TIGHTFRAME-NO-H2-36` — PROVED

The P24 quartic cancellation is the first case of a general deterministic fact.

For an integer `a>=1`, define

\[
F_{2a,P}(x)
=
\sum_i p_i\langle u_i,x\rangle^{2a}.
\tag{8.1}
\]

Then

\[
\Delta F_{2a,P}
=2a(2a-1)F_{2a-2,P}.
\tag{8.2}
\]

Because the frame is tight,

\[
F_{2,P}(x)=\|x\|^2.
\tag{8.3}
\]

Iterating `(8.2)` gives

\[
\Delta^{a-1}F_{2a,P}(x)
=C_a\|x\|^2
\tag{8.4}
\]

for an explicit positive constant `C_a`.

Now take the Fischer decomposition of the homogeneous polynomial `F_{2a,P}`:

\[
F_{2a,P}(x)
=
H_{2a}(x)+\|x\|^2H_{2a-2}(x)+\cdots
+\|x\|^{2a-2}H_2(x)
+c\|x\|^{2a},
\tag{8.5}
\]

where each `H_k` is harmonic homogeneous of degree `k`.

After applying `Delta^{a-1}`:

- every term with harmonic degree at least four is killed after its radial powers are exhausted;
- the last radial term becomes a multiple of `||x||^2`;
- the `||x||^{2a-2}H_2` term becomes a nonzero multiple of `H_2`.

But `(8.4)` is purely radial. By uniqueness of the harmonic decomposition,

\[
\boxed{H_2\equiv0.}
\tag{8.6}
\]

Therefore **every weighted even tight-frame moment has no degree-two spherical harmonic**.

This does not solve P14, but it clarifies why higher relative moments are not measuring covariance anisotropy directly: after the Haar constant, they begin at degree four.

# 10. `P14-J3-NOT-NECESSARY-36` — PROVED AS A LOGICAL COUNTEREXAMPLE

The previous run correctly proved that `J_3=O(r^{-2})` is sufficient for `J_2=O(r^{-1})`. It is not necessary, even for an even permutation-invariant line law on the coordinate parent.

Take

\[
P=I_r
\]

and let the random line direction `Z` have law

\[
\mu_r
=
\left(1-\frac1r\right)\sigma_r
+
\frac1r\nu_r,
\tag{10.1}
\]

where `sigma_r` is Haar measure on `S^{r-1}` and `nu_r` is uniform on the `2r` signed coordinate vectors `\{\pm e_i\}`.

Then

\[
J_2=E\sum_iZ_i^4
=
\left(1-\frac1r\right)\frac3{r+2}
+
\frac1r
=
\boxed{
\frac{4r-1}{r(r+2)}
}
=O(r^{-1}).
\tag{10.2}
\]

But

\[
\begin{aligned}
J_3
&=E\sum_iZ_i^6\\
&=
\left(1-\frac1r\right)
\frac{15}{(r+2)(r+4)}
+
\frac1r\\
&=
\boxed{
\frac{r^2+21r-7}{r(r+2)(r+4)}
}
\asymp r^{-1}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{10.3}
\]

So sharp Rayleigh can hold while the sharp sixth moment fails by a factor of order `r`.

This is **not** a counterexample to the iid-source P14 theorem and is not claimed to arise from a bounded-density product-row null-line law. It is a rigorous logical certificate that the sixth-moment route imposes unnecessary degree-six regularity. If desired, the atomic component may be replaced by sufficiently small smooth caps to obtain an absolutely continuous spherical line law with the same order separation.

Accordingly, `P14-PALM-Q3-SIXTHMOMENT-36` is retained as a valid sufficient route but retired as the preferred minimal target.
