## 5.1 Parent row-volume law and graph chart

For every `s`-set `T` with `A_T` invertible, define

\[
\nu_A(T)
=
\frac{\det(A_T)^2}{\det(AA^T)}.
\tag{5.1}
\]

By Cauchy--Binet, `nu_A` is a probability law. It is the projection-DPP basis law of the row space of `A`.

Set

\[
U=T^c,
\qquad |U|=r,
\]

and define the graph matrix and graph metric

\[
H_T=A_T^{-1}A_U,
\qquad
G_T=I_r+H_T^TH_T.
\tag{5.2}
\]

Every vector in `ker A` has the chart form

\[
z=(-H_Ty,y),\qquad y\in\mathbb R^r,

so its Euclidean metric is `G_T`.

For the future genuinely fresh rows define

\[
Y_T=X_U-X_TH_T
\in\mathbb R^{n\times r}.
\tag{5.3}
\]

Then elementary graph-coordinate algebra gives

\[
\boxed{
X P X^T
=Y_TG_T^{-1}Y_T^T.

\tag{5.4}

Schur complement in the stacked row Gram gives

\[
\boxed{
\det(BB^T)
=
\det(AA^T)
\det(Y_TG_T^{-1}Y_T^T).
}
\tag{5.5}

## 5.2 Nested child row-basis law

Let `W` be a row-volume basis of the stacked matrix `B`, so `|W|=s+n=m-q` and

\qquad J\subset U,

\tag{5.6}

Summing `(5.6)` over `J` and using Cauchy--Binet, the induced marginal of `T` is

\boxed{

\tag{5.9}

inside the `r` chart coordinates.

# 8. `P14-GAUSSIAN-FLAG-DPP-43` — PROVED

The Gaussian calibration is orientation-free for **every** deterministic parent.

## 8.1 Statement

Let `P` be any deterministic rank-`r` orthogonal projector in any ambient dimension. Let `R<=P` be a Haar rank-`q` subprojection of `Ran(P)`. Then for every `1<=d<=q`,

\[
\boxed{
E_R\mathcal A_q^{(d)}(P,R)
=
\prod_{j=0}^{d-1}
\frac{(r-j)(q-j+2)}{(q-j)(r-j+2)}.
}
\tag{8.1}
\]

The right side depends only on `(r,q,d)`, not on the leverage profile or orientation of `P`.

For Gaussian fresh original rows, conditional on every frozen parent `P`, the residual rank-`q` subspace is exactly Haar in `Ran(P)`, so `(8.1)` is the exact Gaussian fixed-parent benchmark.

## 8.2 Proof

Choose an isometry

\[
U_0:\mathbb R^r\to\operatorname{Ran}P,
\qquad P=U_0U_0^T.
\]

Write

\[
R=U_0QU_0^T,
\]

where `Q` is a Haar rank-`q` projector in `R^r`.

Fix a coordinate `d`-set `S` with `det(P_S)>0` and put

\[
B=U_0^TE_S\in\mathbb R^{r\times d}.
\]

Then

\[
B^TB=P_S,
\qquad
R_S=B^TQB.
\]

By polar decomposition and Haar invariance,

\[
B^TQB
\stackrel d=
P_S^{1/2}Q_dP_S^{1/2},
\]

where `Q_d` is the upper-left `d x d` principal block of a Haar rank-`q` projection in `R^r`. Hence

\[
\boxed{
E\frac{\det(R_S)^2}{\det(P_S)}
=
\det(P_S)\,E\det(Q_d)^2.
}
\tag{8.2}
\]

The principal block `Q_d` has the real matrix-beta law with parameters `q/2` and `(r-q)/2`. Its determinant second moment is

\[
E\det(Q_d)^2
=
\prod_{j=0}^{d-1}
\frac{(q-j)(q-j+2)}{(r-j)(r-j+2)}.
\tag{8.3}
\]

This follows immediately from the multivariate-gamma ratio

\[
\frac{\Gamma_d(q/2+2)\Gamma_d(r/2)}
{\Gamma_d(q/2)\Gamma_d(r/2+2)}.
\]

Finally, because the nonzero eigenvalues of `P` are `r` ones,

\[
\sum_{|S|=d}\det(P_S)=\binom rd.
\tag{8.4}
\]

Insert `(8.2)--(8.4)` into the definition `(4.1)`:

\[
E\mathcal A_q^{(d)}
=
\frac{\binom rd^2}{\binom qd^2}
E\det(Q_d)^2,
\]

which simplifies exactly to `(8.1)`.

## 8.3 Rank-three calibrations

For `q=3`,

\[
E\mathcal A_3^{(1)}
=
\frac{5r}{3(r+2)},
\]

\[
\boxed{
E\mathcal A_3^{(2)}
=
\frac{10}{3}
\frac{r(r-1)}{(r+1)(r+2)},
}
\tag{8.5}
\]

and

\[
E\mathcal A_3^{(3)}
=
10\frac{(r-1)(r-2)}{(r+1)(r+2)}.
\]

These are exactly the coordinate-parent Haar constants from previous P14 work, now proved to hold for arbitrary deterministic parents.

The key interpretation is:

\[
\boxed{
\text{adaptive parent orientation creates no Gaussian debt at all.}
}
\]

Any successful finite-variance proof should therefore compare the source law to this parent-uniform Gaussian benchmark without paying a deterministic parent condition number.
