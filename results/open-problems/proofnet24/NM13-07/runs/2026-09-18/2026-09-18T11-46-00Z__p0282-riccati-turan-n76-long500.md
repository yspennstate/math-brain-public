## 6. Method #1 — CHANGE THE CATEGORY: a positive first-order transfer and exact Turán identity

Write `a_{n,k}=ell_{n,k}`. In the long-LIS sector, Kauers–Wang recurrence (2) is

`(n+1)^2(a_{n,k}+a_{n,k+1})`
` = (n-k+1)a_{n+1,k} + (n+k+1)a_{n+1,k+1}`.          (6.1)

For one transfer `n -> n+1`, define

`b_k := a_{n+1,k}`,  
`d_k := (n+1)^2(a_{n,k}+a_{n,k+1})`,  
`alpha := n-k+1`, `beta := n+k+1`.

Then (6.1) is the positive first-order linear relation

`alpha b_k + beta b_{k+1} = d_k`.                    (6.2)

This changes category: instead of viewing (6.1) as merely a bivariate permutation recurrence, treat it as a backward one-dimensional transfer for a positive sequence `b_k`, with exact boundary `b_{n+1}=1`.

The adjacent equation at `k-1` is

`(n-k+2)b_{k-1} + (n+k)b_k = d_{k-1}`.              (6.3)

Let

`T_{n+1,k}:=b_k^2-b_{k-1}b_{k+1}`.                  (6.4)

### Exact identity

For every `k` for which both adjacent recurrences are valid,

`(n-k+2)(n+k+1) T_{n+1,k}`
` = 2(n+1)b_k^2`
`   + [(n-k+1)d_{k-1}+(n+k)d_k] b_k`
`   - d_{k-1}d_k`.                                  (6.5)

### Proof

From (6.2) and (6.3),

`b_{k+1} = (d_k-(n-k+1)b_k)/(n+k+1)`,

`b_{k-1} = (d_{k-1}-(n+k)b_k)/(n-k+2)`.

Substitute these into (6.4) and multiply by `(n-k+2)(n+k+1)`. The coefficient of `b_k^2` becomes

`(n-k+2)(n+k+1) - (n-k+1)(n+k) = 2(n+1)`.

The two cross terms are exactly `(n-k+1)d_{k-1}b_k` and `(n+k)d_k b_k`, while the constant term is `-d_{k-1}d_k`. This is (6.5). No asymptotics or sign estimate is used.

Equivalently, with `x_k=b_k/d_k` and `q_k=d_{k-1}/d_k`, the sign of `T_{n+1,k}` is the sign of

`F_{n,k}(x_k,q_k)`
` := 2(n+1)x_k^2 + [(n-k+1)q_k+n+k]x_k - q_k`.       (6.6)

For `x>=0`, `F_{n,k}` is strictly increasing in `x`. This exposes a scalar threshold/invariant-region formulation for the long-sector Chen inequality.

**Outcome of #1:** successful. The long-sector Turán sign has been reduced exactly to a one-dimensional transfer/threshold identity instead of another shape sum.
