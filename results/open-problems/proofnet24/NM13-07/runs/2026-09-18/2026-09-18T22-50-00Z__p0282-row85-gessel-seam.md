## 5. Gessel/Toeplitz exact low-LIS certificate

Define the cumulative LIS counts

`u_k(n) = #{sigma in S_n : LIS(sigma)<=k}`.

Gessel's classical identity is

`sum_{n>=0} u_k(n) x^(2n)/(n!)^2 = det(I_{|i-j|}(2x))_{1<=i,j<=k}`,

where

`I_d(2x) = sum_{r>=0} x^(2r+d)/(r!(r+d)!)`.

Therefore

`ell_{n,k} = u_k(n)-u_{k-1}(n)`.

The production checker exploits the fact that every Bessel entry has fixed parity. It stores a series as

`x^epsilon * sum_r a_r (x^2)^r`

and truncates at `(x^2)^85`. Because the constant matrix of the Toeplitz determinant is the identity, formal Gaussian elimination has unit pivots in every modular calculation; the product of the first `k` pivots gives the leading `k x k` determinant, so a single elimination through size 19 yields all `u_k(85)`, `1<=k<=19`.

The arithmetic is exact modulo **16 pairwise distinct primes**, each independently primality-checked by the checker. Their product has **479 bits**, whereas `85!` has **427 bits**, and the checker explicitly verifies

`M > 2*85!`.

After CRT, the known combinatorial bound `0<=u_k(85)<=85!` makes every reconstructed count unique. No floating-point value is used to decide an inequality.
