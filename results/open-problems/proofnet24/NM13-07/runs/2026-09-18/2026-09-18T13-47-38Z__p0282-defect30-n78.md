## 6. Method #7 — TAKE THE LIMIT: an all-defect positive-coefficient lemma for individual fixed-defect counts

Put

`E_j(n) := ell_{n,n-j}`.                                               (6.1)

The preceding report used the Kauers–Wang recurrence to define exact integer polynomials

`P_0(n)=1`, `P_1(n)=(n-1)^2`,

`P_{j+1}(n)`
` = n^2[P_j(n-1)+jP_{j-1}(n-1)]-(2n-j-1)P_j(n)`,                    (6.2)

with

`P_j(n)=j! E_j(n)`                                                     (6.3)

through the recurrence-valid fixed-defect range. This run asks what the large-`n` polynomial itself is made of.

### Residual-partition dimension formula

Let `mu=(mu_1,...,mu_r)` be a partition of `j`, and let

`lambda=(N,mu_1,...,mu_r)`, `N>=mu_1`, `n=N+j`.

Then

`f^lambda`
` = (f^mu/j!)`
`   * prod_{i=1}^r (N-mu_i+i)`
`   * prod_{s=r+1}^j (N+s)`.                                         (6.4)

**Proof.** Use the Frobenius form of the hook-length formula

`f^lambda = n! * prod_{a<b}(lambda_a-lambda_b+b-a)`
`           / prod_a (lambda_a+L-a)!`

for a partition with `L` rows. Divide the formula for `lambda=(N,mu)` by the formula for `mu`. All Vandermonde factors internal to `mu` cancel, as do the factorial denominator factors belonging to the `mu` rows. The new cross factors are exactly `N-mu_i+i`, and the only new factorial denominator is `(N+r)!`. Since

`n!/(N+r)! = prod_{s=r+1}^j (N+s)`,

(6.4) follows. `square`

Now put

`N=m+j-1`, equivalently `n=m+2j-1`.                                  (6.5)

Every factor in (6.4) is then a linear polynomial in `m` with nonnegative constant term:

`N-mu_i+i = m + (j-1+i-mu_i)`,

`N+s = m + (j-1+s)`.                                                   (6.6)

Because `mu_i<=j`, the first constants are nonnegative; the second are positive. At the one boundary case where `N<mu_1` (the excluded shape `mu=(j)` at `m=0`), the first factor in (6.6) is zero, so the polynomial continuation contributes zero exactly as required.

Squaring (6.4), summing over `mu|-j`, and multiplying by `j!` gives:

### Lemma `NM13-07-P0282-INDIVIDUAL-CONE-08`

For every `j>=1`, the polynomial

`P_j(m+2j-1)`                                                        (6.7)

has coefficientwise nonnegative expansion in `m`.

This is an **all-defect analytic lemma**, not a finite extrapolation. It does not by itself imply Turán positivity because a difference of products of coefficient-positive polynomials need not be coefficient-positive. It does explain why a stable shifted positive cone is plausible and why the shift near `2j` is structurally natural.

The exact checker independently tested the nonnegative constants in (6.6) over **35,471 residual partitions** for defects `0..31`, and directly cross-checked formula (6.4) against ordinary hook-length dimensions at **3,140** `(mu,N)` instances. Those checks are self-checks of the proved identity, not substitutes for the argument above.

**Outcome of #7:** success. The large-`n` route produced a general structural lemma rather than another isolated finite row.
