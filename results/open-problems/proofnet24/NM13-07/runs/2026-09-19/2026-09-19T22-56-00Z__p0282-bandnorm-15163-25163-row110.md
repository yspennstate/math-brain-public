## 4. New lemma: exact normalization under a first-row cap

### Lemma `NM13-07-P0282-PACKED-NORMALIZATION-19A`

Let `mu |- s` with `mu_1<=m`, and assume the stable regime `K>s`. Write `s=q m+r`, `0<=r<m`. Then

`R_{s,n}(mu) >= rho_m(s,K)`,

where

`rho_m(s,K)=prod_{i=1}^q (K+i-m)/(K+i)`

and, when `r>0`, there is one additional factor

`(K+q+1-r)/(K+q+1)`.

Equality is attained by the packed partition

`lambda*(s,m)=(m^q,r)`.

### Proof

Rowwise telescoping gives the exact identity

`R_{s,n}(mu)=prod_i (K+i-mu_i)/(K+i)`.

Suppose `mu` is not packed. Choose the earliest row `i` that is not full (`mu_i<m`) while some later row is nonempty, and choose the last nonempty row `h>i`. Moving one cell from row `h` to row `i` preserves the partition inequalities: row `i` can increase because every earlier row is full or at least as large, and the last nonempty row can decrease without violating the lower neighbor.

Set

`A=K+i-mu_i`, `B=K+h-mu_h`.

Because `h>i` and `mu_i>=mu_h`,

`B-A=(h-i)+(mu_i-mu_h)>=1`.

Only two row factors change. The ratio new/old is

`[(A-1)/A]*[(B+1)/B]`.

This is strictly below one because

`(A-1)(B+1)<AB <=> A-B-1<0`,

and `B>=A+1`. Thus every upward packing move strictly decreases `R`. Iterating terminates exactly at `(m^q,r)`. Evaluating the rowwise product there gives the displayed `rho_m`. QED.

The exact checker also runs a small-integer transfer sanity test over many admissible `K,i,h,mu_i,mu_h` combinations; this is a self-check of the algebra, not the proof itself and not an independent audit.
