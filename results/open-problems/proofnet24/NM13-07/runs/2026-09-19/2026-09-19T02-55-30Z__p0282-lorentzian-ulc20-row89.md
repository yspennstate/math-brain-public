## 3. #1 CHANGE THE CATEGORY — from real stability to bivariate Lorentzian / ultra-log-concavity

Instead of asking for real-rootedness of `P_n`, homogenize the coefficient sequence:

`L_n(x,y)=sum_{k=1}^n ell_{n,k} x^(k-1) y^(n-k)`.

For a positive bivariate homogeneous polynomial with full interval support, the natural coefficient form of Lorentzianity is log-concavity after division by the binomial coefficients. Here that is the explicit inequality

`ell_{n,k}^2 * C(n-1,k-2) * C(n-1,k)`

`> ell_{n,k-1} ell_{n,k+1} * C(n-1,k-1)^2`.                 (ULC)

Equivalently,

`ell_{n,k}^2 > ell_{n,k-1}ell_{n,k+1} * k(n-k+1)/((k-1)(n-k))`.

The multiplicative factor on the right is strictly greater than 1 for every source-valid interior index, so (ULC) strictly implies Chen. Thus the real-rootedness failure at `n=12` does not kill this weaker but still source-useful algebraic target.

## 4. #5 ADD A PARAMETER / STRENGTHEN — exact all-n ULC theorem for defects `j<=20`

Set the defect `j=n-k` and write

`E_j(n)=ell_{n,n-j}`.

### Theorem `NM13-07-P0282-ULC-DEFECT20-01`

For every integer `1<=j<=20` and every source-valid `n>=j+2`, with `k=n-j`,

`ell_{n,k}^2 * C(n-1,k-2) * C(n-1,k)`

`> ell_{n,k-1} ell_{n,k+1} * C(n-1,k-1)^2`.

Consequently Chen's source inequality holds strictly throughout this sector.

### Proof

Fix `j`. When `n>=2j` every partition of `n` whose first row has length `n-j` has the unique form

`lambda=(n-j,mu)`,  `mu |- j`.

By RSK,

`E_j(n)=sum_{mu|-j} (f^(n-j,mu))^2`.                        (1)

Let `mu=(mu_1,...,mu_{r-1})` and `lambda=(n-j,mu_1,...,mu_{r-1})`. The Weyl/hook formula gives

`f^lambda = n! * prod_{0<=a<b<r}(lambda_a-lambda_b+b-a)`

`           / prod_{a=0}^{r-1}(lambda_a+r-a-1)!`.           (2)

All factors involving only the lower rows are constant in `n`. The quotient

`n!/(n-j+r-1)!`

is a product of `j-r+1` linear factors in `n`, and the Vandermonde factors coupling the first row to the lower rows contribute the remaining `r-1` linear factors. Hence each `f^(n-j,mu)` is exactly a degree-`j` rational polynomial in `n`, and (1) is an exact rational polynomial `E_j(n)` of degree at most `2j` on `n>=2j`.

Now substitute `k=n-j` into (ULC). Since

`C(n-1,k-1)^2 / (C(n-1,k-2) C(n-1,k))`

`= k(n-k+1)/((k-1)(n-k))`

`= (n-j)(j+1)/((n-j-1)j)`,

(ULC) is equivalent to

`M_j(n) := j(n-j-1)E_j(n)^2`

`          - (j+1)(n-j)E_{j-1}(n)E_{j+1}(n) > 0`.          (3)

For the three residual polynomials in (3) to be simultaneously valid it suffices that `n>=2j+2`. Put

`m=n-(2j+2)`.
