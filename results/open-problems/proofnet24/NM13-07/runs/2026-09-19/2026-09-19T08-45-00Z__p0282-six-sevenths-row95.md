`F_mu(t)=2 sum_c [log(1-a_c t)-log(1-(a_c-h_c)t)]`,                 (6.34)

`A_j(t)=E[e^{F_mu(t)}]`, `G_j(t)=log A_j(t)`.                       (6.35)

`<= 2 sum_c h_c |t|/(1-2j|t|)`

`<= 2j r/(1-2jr)=1/2`.                                             (6.38)

`|A_j(t)-1| <= E[|e^{F_mu(t)}-1|] <= e^{1/2}-1 < 2/3`.             (6.39)

`|G_j(t)| < log 3 < 2` on `|t|<=r`.                                (6.40)

`G_j(t)=sum_{m>=1} D_m(j)t^m`.                                     (6.41)

`|D_m(j)| <= 2 r^{-m}`.                                             (6.42)

`<=2^20 (j+1)^6/n^6`.                                               (6.44)

Under (6.1) with `j>=67`, the lower bound on `n` is much stronger than `n>=12j`, so (6.43)--(6.44) apply.
