## 4. Retained factorization and growth identity

Put

`j=n-k`, `F_j(n)=ell_{n,n-j}`.

Retain the stable factorization

\[
F_j(n)=\frac{(n)_j^2}{j!}A_j(n),
\qquad
A_j(n)=\mathbb E_{\mu\sim\mathrm{Plancherel}(j)}R_{j,n}(\mu)^2,
\]

where

\[
R_{s,n}(\mu)=\prod_{x\in\mu}\frac{K-c(x)}{K+1-c(x)},
\qquad K=n-s.
\]

If `lambda` covers `mu` by one box of content `c_0`, the exact retained edge identity is

\[
\frac{R_{s+1,n}(\lambda)}{R_{s,n}(\mu)}
=
\prod_{x\in\mu}
\left(1-\frac1{(K-c(x))^2}\right)
\left(1-\frac1{K-c_0}\right).
\]

The standard Plancherel growth transition carries Plancherel measure at size `s` to Plancherel measure at size `s+1`, so pointwise edge bounds can be averaged exactly.

## 5. New lemma: the one-row edge is the exact worst edge

Consider the transition `j-1 -> j`. Set

\[
d=n-2j,
\qquad
K=n-j+1=j+d+1.
\]

Let `mu` have `j-1` boxes. Enumerate its boxes in row-major order as

`x_p=(r,q)`, `p=1,...,j-1`.

Because `p>=q`,

\[
c(x_p)=q-r\le q-1\le p-1.
\]

Hence

\[
K-c(x_p)\ge K-p+1.
\]

The factor `1-1/y^2` is increasing for `y>1`, so

\[
\prod_{x\in\mu}
\left(1-\frac1{(K-c(x))^2}\right)
\ge
\prod_{p=1}^{j-1}
\left(1-\frac1{(K-p+1)^2}\right).
\]

The right side telescopes:

\[
\begin{aligned}
\prod_{p=1}^{j-1}
\left(1-\frac1{(K-p+1)^2}\right)
&=
\prod_{m=d+3}^{j+d+1}\left(1-\frac1{m^2}\right)\\
&=
\frac{(d+2)(j+d+2)}{(d+3)(j+d+1)}.
\end{aligned}
\]

The added box has `c_0<=j-1`, so

\[
1-\frac1{K-c_0}\ge\frac{d+1}{d+2}.
\]

Therefore every growth edge satisfies

\[
\boxed{
\frac{R_{j,n}(\lambda)}{R_{j-1,n}(\mu)}
\ge
q_j(d):=
\frac{(d+1)(j+d+2)}{(d+3)(j+d+1)}.
}
\]

For `mu=(j-1)` and `lambda=(j)`, the old contents are exactly `0,1,...,j-2` and the new content is `j-1`. Every inequality above is then equality. Thus:

### Lemma `NM13-07-P0282-ONE-ROW-WORST-EDGE-13`

In the stable positive-factor regime, `q_j(d)` is the **exact minimum** of the Plancherel-growth correction ratio over all edges `mu |- j-1 -> lambda |- j`, and it is attained by the one-row edge.

The exact self-check independently enumerates 283 growth edges for `2<=j<=10` and verifies the formula and exact one-row minimum in every checked rank.
