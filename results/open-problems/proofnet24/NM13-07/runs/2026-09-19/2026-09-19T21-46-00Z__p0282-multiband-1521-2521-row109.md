\tag{2.2}

where `nu_s(mu)` is proportional to `Pl_s(mu) R_{s,n}(mu)^2`, and

\[

\qquad

\tag{2.3}

\[

\mathbb E_\mu C=0,

\tag{2.4}

\[

\tag{2.5}

so for every parent event `E`,

\tag{2.6}

\tag{2.7}
