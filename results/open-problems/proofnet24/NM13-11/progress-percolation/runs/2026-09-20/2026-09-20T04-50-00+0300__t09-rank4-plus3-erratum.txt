Erratum/clarification for commit 2674144c49153a675aa473b3c1ed80d430778d74:

In Section 1, the sentence saying that the hypothesis "can be stated more concretely as" Gamma being generated by an involution s and one further element t with B={s,t,t^{-1}} should read "a useful sufficient subcase is" that form. A symmetric 3-element generating set can also consist of three involutions. Theorem A itself is stated and proved for an arbitrary symmetric generating set B of cardinality 3, so this wording correction changes no theorem, bound, application, or checker result.
