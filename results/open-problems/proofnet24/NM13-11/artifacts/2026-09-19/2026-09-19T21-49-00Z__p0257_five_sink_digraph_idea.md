## #1 — change category: Euclidean geometry -> finite 2-out digraph topology

The preceding P0257 runs have already charged all three size-4 directed-two-nearest-neighbor (D2) sink-SCC topology classes that were found and exactly realized. Instead of refining another four-point coordinate box, pass to the abstract finite digraph carried by a five-point D2 sink component.

For a five-point ordered configuration, each vertex has outdegree two. If an exact positive-measure Euclidean chart has a fixed strongly connected D2 topology and an empty external guard beyond every internal second-neighbor radius, then those five vertices are a sink SCC of the global D2 graph. The previously proved Hamiltonian-boundary lemma then gives an L-side surplus `(H-U) r` for every such non-endpoint component, without requiring consecutive NUV consumption.

The useful counting refinement is topological. If every ordered tuple in a chart has one fixed labeled digraph `A`, then two labelings of the same unordered geometric five-set that both lie in that chart differ by an automorphism of `A`. Hence the factorial-moment integral needs division only by `|Aut(A)|`, not the crude `5!`. This is exact and is potentially the main gain.
