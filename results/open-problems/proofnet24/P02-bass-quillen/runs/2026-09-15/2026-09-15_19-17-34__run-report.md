# 3. Required elementary audits, rederived

## 3.1 Rank-one stable-to-actual freeness

Let `A` be commutative and `P` rank one projective. If

`P ⊕ A^m ≅ A^(m+1)`,

then taking top exterior powers gives

`P ≅ det(P ⊕ A^m) ≅ det(A^(m+1)) ≅ A`.

This proves exactly that implication. It does not prove higher-rank cancellation or Bass–Quillen.

## 3.2 Localization at a parameter need not be local

Let

`R=k[x,y]_(x,y)`, `A=R[1/x]`.

Then

`A/(y) ≅ k[x]_(x)[1/x] ≅ k(x)`

and, substituting `y=x`,

`A/(y-x) ≅ k(x)`.

Hence `(y)A` and `(y-x)A` are distinct maximal ideals. They are distinct because their equality would contain their difference `x`, impossible since `x` is a unit in `A`.

Therefore “projectives over a local ring are free” cannot be applied to `R[1/r]` without proving locality.

## 3.3 Stable `K_0` does not solve ranks two or three

For the declared H4 ring, Quillen localization gives

`K_0(R/r) -> K_0(R) -> K_0(A) -> 0`.

Both local regular rings on the left have `K_0 ≅ Z`, and the first map sends `[R/r]` to zero because

`0 -> R --r--> R -> R/r -> 0`.

Thus `K_0(A) ≅ Z`. Consequently every finite projective over `A` is stably free after adding a finite free complement. Since `dim A=3`, ordinary stable range only closes stably free ranks `>3`. It does **not** close ranks `2` or `3`.

This is exactly why the inherited low-rank `Um_3` / `Um_4` orbit obligations remain meaningful in mixed characteristic.

# 4. `P02-C98-EQUICHARACTERISTIC-QUILLEN-CLOSURE` — PROVED / literature-verified

## Statement

Let `(R,m)` be a regular local ring of **equicharacteristic**, and let

`r in m\m^2`

be a regular parameter. Then every finite projective

`R[1/r]`

module is free.

There is no rank restriction and no dimension restriction.

## Source check

Česnavičius, §2.2.2 (2), states explicitly:

> The case when `R` is of equicharacteristic was settled by Bhatwadekar–Rao [BR83, Theorem 2.5] (see also [Rao85, Theorem 2.9]) and Popescu.

The finite-type theorem is independently visible in the Bhatwadekar–Rao abstract: for a regular local ring of an affine algebra over a field and a regular parameter `r`, all projective `R[1/r]`-modules are free.

The passage to arbitrary equicharacteristic regular local rings is through Popescu approximation; the same survey records that a regular ring containing a perfect field is a filtered direct limit of smooth algebras over that field.

## Consequence for the P02 laboratory

All of the inherited C50–C97 rank-three machinery is **not needed to close Quillen localization in equicharacteristic**. In particular, the unit-numerator prestabilization obstruction C97 is a real unstable object, but in equicharacteristic it must ultimately vanish because the whole projective module is already known to be free by C98.

This is a route-pruning theorem, not a retraction of C50–C97.

# 6. `P02-C100-r-ADIC-COMPLETION-REDUCTION` — PROVED / literature-verified

## Statement

For the Quillen localization target, it is enough to work after replacing `R` by its `r`-adic completion.

Equivalently for the proof strategy: given a finite projective `P` over `R[1/r]`, formal gluing reduces extension/freeness of `P` to the corresponding question over the `r`-adically complete base.

## Exact source

Česnavičius, immediately after Conjecture 2.2.1:

- freeness over `R[1/r]` is equivalent to extension to a finite projective `R`-module because `R` is local;
- formal gluing allows one to replace `R` by its `r`-adic completion “without losing generality.”

Source:
`https://www.imo.universite-paris-saclay.fr/~kestutis.cesnavicius/torsors-regular.pdf`, §2.2, lines surrounding Conjecture 2.2.1.

## Derivation

Let `P` be finite projective over `R[1/r]`. To prove it free it is enough to prove it extends to a finite projective `E` over `R`; then `E` is free because `R` is local, so `P=E[1/r]` is free.

Formal gluing for the pair `(Spec R, V(r))` identifies vector bundles obtained by gluing data on the complement `D(r)` and the formal completion along `V(r)`. Thus the extension problem can be tested after replacing the formal neighborhood by the `r`-adic completion. In particular, a proof of the completed case supplies the formal piece needed to glue an extension over `R`.

The key point is that this is an **extension/gluing reduction**, not the invalid statement that `R[1/r]` itself is local.

## Strategic consequence

Every remaining mixed-characteristic H4 attack should first ask whether its unstable invariant is compatible with the `r`-adic completion reduction. Infinite `r`-adic constructions are now legitimate proof objects provided the formal gluing descent back to `R` is kept explicit.

# 8. `P02-C102-BQ-UNRAMIFIED-CLOSURE` — PROVED / literature-verified, SEPARATE TARGET

## Statement

Let `R` be a regular ring such that every localization `R_m` at a maximal ideal is an **unramified regular local ring**. Then, for every `d>=0`, every vector bundle on

`A_R^d = Spec R[T_1,...,T_d]`

descends to `R`.

In particular, if `R` itself is an unramified regular **local** ring, then every finite projective `R[T_1,...,T_d]`-module is free.

## Exact source

Česnavičius, Theorem 5.2.2 (Quillen, Suslin, Lindel, Popescu):
`https://www.imo.universite-paris-saclay.fr/~kestutis.cesnavicius/torsors-regular.pdf`, §5.2.

The theorem explicitly includes unramified mixed characteristic: the proof uses Popescu approximation to local rings of schemes smooth over either a field or `Z_(p)`.

## Why this must stay separate from Quillen localization

C102 is a theorem about polynomial extension / descent. It does not say that every projective over `R[1/r]` is free for an arbitrary regular parameter `r` in an unramified mixed-characteristic `R`.

The historical implication goes the other way: a positive Quillen localization theorem implies the local Bass–Quillen statement. We do not reverse this implication.

## Consequence for P02 priority

For the **Bass–Quillen** target, the entire unramified branch—including equicharacteristic and unramified mixed characteristic—is already closed. The generic unresolved BQ branch is ramified.

For the **Quillen localization** target, C98 closes equicharacteristic, but mixed-characteristic localization remains the hard branch; no blanket unramified-localization theorem is imported here.

# 9. `P02-D31-COMPLETION-DOES-NOT-CLOSE-BQ` — DISPROVED METHOD CLAIM

A tempting strengthening is:

> “If a regular local ring is complete (or local, Henselian, excellent), then Bass–Quillen follows from Popescu.”

The current survey explicitly records this as an **incorrect claim** in Lam and in an earlier Popescu account:

This is a route falsification, not a counterexample to Bass–Quillen itself.

The leading route requested by the original program remains valid, but its scope is now correctly pruned.

## 10.1 Elementary transitivity / unimodular rows

In mixed-characteristic H4, rank-three freeness still asks for actual completion/cancellation of a row in `Um_4(A)`; stable `K_0` and stable `SK_1` do not suffice. C96–C97 isolate a one-step prestabilization obstruction in a useful restricted branch.

This distinction is now mandatory when interpreting Vaserstein/Mennicke calculations.

## 10.2 Vaserstein / Mennicke / Euler classes

The earlier blanket dimension-three Vaserstein-injectivity route remains killed: explicit literature supplies noninjectivity examples over dimension-three real affine rings. Such symbols can still detect specific unstable classes, but no generic “dimension three implies injective” theorem may be used.

For the mixed-characteristic H4 hard core, these symbols remain plausible at ranks two and three. Their hypotheses—especially invertibility of `2`, smoothness/finite type, and cohomological-dimension assumptions—must be checked after C100 completion rather than silently inherited.

## 10.3 Hermitian K-theory

The inherited elementary-Witt / Grothendieck–Witt bridge remains certified only under its recorded `2 in A^x` hypotheses. Characteristic two / residue characteristic two remains a separate unresolved interface. C98 closes equicharacteristic Quillen even in characteristic two, but that does **not** magically validate the old Hermitian argument in characteristic two; it merely makes that argument unnecessary for closing the equicharacteristic target.

## 10.4 Localization sequences

The stable localization calculations remain useful diagnostics. They establish stable freeness in H4 and, in special residual rings such as C93, can show `SK_1=0`. They do not cancel a free summand at rank two or three.

## 10.5 Descent and approximation

This run substantially sharpens the route split:

- equal characteristic Quillen: approximation route already closes the target (C98);
- unramified Bass–Quillen: approximation + Lindel/Quillen patching already closes the target (C102);
- ramified Bass–Quillen: Popescu's finite-type presentation alone is explicitly not known to close the problem;
- mixed-characteristic Quillen localization: C100 formal completion is an exact reduction, but an unstable cancellation theorem on the completed side is still missing.

Thus future descent work must preserve the **specific unstable module/row**, not merely regularity of the approximating rings.

**AUDIT_FOR=P04**  
**Source path:** `proofnet24/P04-kernel-conjectures/runs/2026-09-15/2026-09-15_18-44-04__run-report.md`  
**Creation commit:** `11c206cf765dbcd4fc6268325e5ab88e80243a20`  
**Blob:** `072d965a714c26cd500d737b1d2d4378b2ebb8c3`  
**Claim:** `P04-T4-HAAR-OVERLAP-SPREAD-40`  
**Verdict:** **PASS** for the exact finite-dimensional Haar benchmark theorem. This does not prove P04 Target 4 and makes no isotropy-to-inverse-Gram inference.

Let

`E=Sym_d^0`, `m=dim E=(d-1)(d+2)/2`,

and `{T_mu}` a Frobenius-orthonormal basis of `E`.

The orthogonal projector from all symmetric matrices to traceless symmetric matrices gives the completeness identity

`sum_mu (T_mu)_{ij}(T_mu)_{kl} = 1/2(delta_ik delta_jl + delta_il delta_jk) - (1/d) delta_ij delta_kl`.

Contracting against a symmetric matrix `X` yields

`sum_mu T_mu X T_mu = ((d-2)/(2d)) X + (1/2) tr(X) I`.

This confirms the corrected P04 equation (7.4) from its erratum.

For a Haar coefficient direction

`N=sum_mu n_mu T_mu`, `n~Haar(S^{m-1})`,

the spherical fourth moment

`E[n_mu n_nu n_rho n_sigma] = (delta_mu,nu delta_rho,sigma + delta_mu,rho delta_nu,sigma + delta_mu,sigma delta_nu,rho)/(m(m+2))`

and the identities

`sum_mu T_mu^2=(m/d)I`,

`sum_{mu,nu} tr(T_mu T_nu T_mu T_nu)=m(d-2)/(2d)`

give

`E tr(N^4) = (2d^2+3d-6)/(d(d^2+d+2))`.

Therefore

`c_H := E tr(N^4)-1/d = (d-2)(d+4)/(d(d^2+d+2))`.

The P04 recurrence

`e_{s-1}=((s-2)/s)e_s+c_H`, `e_m=0`,

has the exact solution

`e_s = s(m-s)/(m-1) c_H`.

Hence

`D_s = e_{s-1}/(s-1)-e_s/s = c_H/(m-1)`

for every `3<=s<=m`, exactly as claimed.

The theorem is therefore algebraically correct. The audit does not estimate the original quadratic-feature inverse Gram matrix, does not transfer volume-biased statements to the ordinary law, and does not close the hard edge.
