# ProofNet24 P02 erratum — C77 hypothesis terminology

**Applies to:** `2026-09-15_14-01-54__run-report.md`  
**Track:** P02 Bass–Quillen / Quillen localization

During post-commit adversarial readback, one terminology sentence in `P02-C77-UNMIXED-EXACT-COLON-DEPTH` was found to be stronger than the proof needs and its stated “equivalently” was not correct in full generality.

The report says:

> Assume in addition that `R/K` is unmixed, equivalently in the chosen minimal primary decomposition every `P_i` is a minimal prime of `K`.

The correct statement is:

> Assume that the chosen irredundant primary decomposition of `K` has **no embedded primary components**, i.e. every radical `P_i=sqrt(Q_i)` is a minimal prime of `K`. In particular this hypothesis holds if `R/K` is unmixed.

Under this weaker and precise hypothesis, the proof in the report is unchanged: distinct minimal primes are pairwise incomparable, so after localizing at a vertical minimal prime `P_i`, every other minimal primary component becomes the unit ideal while `(Q_i:r^q)_{P_i}` remains proper for `q<e_i`. Therefore

\[
\min\{q\ge0:K:r^q=K:r^\infty\}
=
\max_{r\in P_i}\min\{e\ge1:r^e\in Q_i\}.
\]

Thus:

- the mathematical conclusion `P02-C77` remains **PROVED**;
- its hypothesis can be weakened from “unmixed” to “no embedded primary components”;
- `P02-C78` remains valid because a Cohen–Macaulay quotient has no embedded associated primes (indeed in the height-two regular-local setting it is unmixed as used there).

No other claim or dependency in the run report is changed.
