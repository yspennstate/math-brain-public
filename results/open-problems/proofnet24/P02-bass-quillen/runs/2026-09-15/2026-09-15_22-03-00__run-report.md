# 3. Required elementary audits, rederived

## 3.1 Rank one

Let `A` be commutative and `P` a rank-one finite projective. If

`P direct_sum A^m ~= A^(m+1)`,

then top exterior powers give

`P ~= det(P direct_sum A^m) ~= det(A^(m+1)) ~= A`.

This proves only this rank-one cancellation. It says nothing about rank-two or rank-three stably free modules without further unstable input.

## 3.2 Localization at a parameter need not be local

Let

`R=k[x,y]_(x,y)`, `A=R[1/x]`.

Then

`A/(y) ~= k[x]_(x)[1/x] ~= k(x)`

and, after substituting `y=x`,

`A/(y-x) ~= k(x)`.

Thus `(y)` and `(y-x)` are maximal. They are distinct because equality would put their difference `x` into a proper ideal although `x` is a unit in `A`.

Therefore local-ring freeness cannot be applied to `R[1/r]` merely because `R` was local.

# 5. Primary source check: Vaserstein pre-stabilization

The key current source is Wilberd van der Kallen,

**A Group Structure on Certain Orbit Sets of Unimodular Rows**, *Journal of Algebra* 82 (1983), 363–397, DOI `10.1016/0021-8693(83)90158-8`.

The original PDF was inspected directly. In the comfortable absolute case its setup includes a commutative Noetherian ring of dimension `d`. Corollary 2.8 states Vaserstein's pre-stabilization theorem: the kernel of the surjective map

`GL_{d+1}(A,I) -> K_1(A,I)`

is the smallest subgroup normalized by `E_{d+1}(A)` containing matrices

`(1+D Y)(1+Y D)^(-1)`,

where

`D=diag(q,1,...,1)`

and `1+D Y` is invertible. Remark 2.9(a) says that for `d>=2` this subgroup is actually generated by the relative elementary group together with these displayed matrices.

For the absolute case `I=A`, `d=2`, this is exactly a description of the kernel

`GL_3(A) -> K_1(A)`

by `E_3(A)` and the displayed Vaserstein generators.

The source was checked at the actual theorem page, not from a secondary synopsis.

A second source check is van der Kallen,

**Vaserstein's pre-stabilization theorem over commutative rings**, *Communications in Algebra* 15 (1987), 657–663, DOI `10.1080/00927878708823435`.

Its abstract explicitly says that the theorem describes `GL_n(A) intersect E(A)` just below stable range and that, for commutative rings, the newer proof requires only the relevant stable-range condition rather than a Krull-dimension hypothesis.

This is exactly the unstable level at which C111 lands.

# 7. `P02-C113-VASERSTEIN-DETERMINANT-LIFT-CERTIFICATE` — PROVED

C112 reduces the rank-three problem to lifting finitely many explicit Vaserstein factors. Each factor has a simple sufficient certificate.

## Statement

Let

`B=A/(F)`

and let

`Vbar=(I_3+Dbar Ybar)(I_3+Ybar Dbar)^(-1) in SL_3(B)`

be one C112 Vaserstein generator, with

`Dbar=diag(lambdabar,1,1)`.

Choose arbitrary lifts

`lambda in A`, `Y in M_3(A)`,

and put

`D=diag(lambda,1,1)`,

`delta=det(I_3+D Y)`.

Then

`det(I_3+D Y)=det(I_3+Y D)=delta`.  (C113.1)

If

`delta in A^x`,  (C113.2)

then

`V=(I_3+D Y)(I_3+Y D)^(-1) in SL_3(A)`  (C113.3)

is a lift of `Vbar`.

If every Vaserstein factor in one C112 factorization admits lifts satisfying (C113.2), then the entire residual completion `M` lifts to `SL_3(A)`.

Finally, because `A=R[1/r]`, write

`delta=a/r^m`, `a in R`, `m>=0`.

Then

`boxed{delta in A^x iff there exists L>=0 with r^L in aR}.`  (C113.4)

Thus every individual factor-lift condition is a finite principal ideal-membership certificate in `R`.

## Proof

For square matrices over a commutative ring, Sylvester's determinant identity gives

`det(I+DY)=det(I+YD)`.

If the common determinant `delta` is a unit, both matrices are invertible. Their quotient (C113.3) is defined, reduces to `Vbar`, and has determinant one by (C113.1).

Every elementary matrix over `B` lifts to an elementary matrix over `A`, because `A->B` is surjective on entries. Therefore, if all `V_i` in (C112.3) lift, the finite product of chosen elementary and Vaserstein lifts gives

`Mtilde in SL_3(A)`

reducing to `M`.

For (C113.4), if `a/r^m` is invertible in `A`, choose inverse `c/r^n`. Then

`ac=r^(m+n)`,

so a power of `r` lies in `aR`. Conversely, if `ac=r^L`, then

`(a/r^m) * (c r^(m-L))=1`

inside `R[1/r]` (negative powers are allowed), proving invertibility.

QED.

## Why this is useful for descent

The data in C113 are finite:

- finitely many `3 x 3` matrices;
- finitely many scalar parameters `lambda_i`;
- determinant identities;
- finitely many equations `a_i c_i=r^{L_i}`.

Thus a successful factorization/lift package is a finite algebraic certificate that can in principle be carried through approximation/descent. No abstract inverse in an infinite localization is required.

# 8. `P02-C114-EXPLICIT-HB-LIFT-CLOSURE` — PROVED

C94 already recorded a general liftable-completion closure. Here the special C110 frame permits a direct proof, making clear that no cancellation is hidden.

## Statement

Retain C110–C111. Suppose the residual completion

`M=[pbar qbar xbar] in SL_3(B)`

has a lift

`Mtilde=[p q x] in SL_3(A)`.

Then the two columns of

`Theta_A=[-F I_2; Phi]:A^2 -> A^5`

extend to a basis of `A^5`. Hence

`boxed{coker(Theta_A) ~= A^3}`

and the original rank-three projective is free.

## Proof

Because `p,q` reduce to the two columns of `Phibar`, there are `a,b in A^3` with

`p=Phi_1+F a`,

`q=Phi_2+F b`.

Write the two columns of `Theta_A` as

`c_1=(-F,0,Phi_1)^T`,

`c_2=(0,-F,Phi_2)^T`.

For each of the three bottom rows, subtract the appropriate component of `a` times the first top row and the appropriate component of `b` times the second top row. These are elementary row operations on `A^5`. They transform the frame to

`c'_1=(-F,0,p)^T`,

`c'_2=(0,-F,q)^T`.  (C114.1)

Let

`e_1=(1,0,0,0,0)^T`,

`e_2=(0,1,0,0,0)^T`,

`X=(0,0,x)^T in A^5`.

Consider the five columns in the order

`e_1, e_2, c'_1, c'_2, X`.

With rows split as `2+3`, their matrix is block upper triangular:

```
[ I_2    -F I_2   0 ]
[  0      p q     x ]
```

and its lower-right `3 x 3` block is exactly `Mtilde`. Therefore its determinant is

`det(I_2) det(Mtilde)=1`.

Thus these five columns are a basis. Undoing the elementary row operations shows that the original two columns of `Theta_A` also extend to a basis.

By C110,

`coker(Theta_A)`

is the original rank-three projective. Hence it is free.

QED.

## Corollary

C112 + C113 + C114 give a finite sufficient closure package:

> Find one Vaserstein pre-stabilization factorization of the C111 residual completion and choose lifts of its finitely many generators whose determinant certificates (C113.4) all succeed. Then the rank-three Quillen module is free.

This is strictly sharper than stable `SK_1=0` and strictly weaker than demanding `SL_3(B)=E_3(B)`.

# 9. `P02-C115-ROW-COFACTOR-DETERMINANT-CORRECTION` — PROVED

The C113 determinant is not rigid under choice of lift. Its exact first correction can be written without approximation.

## Statement

Retain one lifted Vaserstein factor. Put

`L=I_3+D Y`,

`delta=det L`,

and write

`D=diag(d_1,d_2,d_3)=diag(lambda,1,1)`.

Let `C_{ij}` be the `(i,j)` cofactor of `L`. Fix a row `i` and replace that row of `Y` by

`Y_{i*} + F(z_1,z_2,z_3)`.

Then the new determinant is exactly

`boxed{delta' = delta + F d_i sum_j z_j C_{ij}.}`  (C115.1)

In particular, for `i=2` or `3`, where `d_i=1`, if there are `z_j in A` and a unit `epsilon in A^x` satisfying

`epsilon-delta = F sum_j z_j C_{ij}`,  (C115.2)

then the corrected lift satisfies the C113 unit-determinant certificate.

## Proof

Multiplying `Y` on the left by the diagonal matrix `D` scales row `i` by `d_i`. Therefore the row modification of `Y` changes row `i` of `L` by

`F d_i(z_1,z_2,z_3)`.

The determinant is linear in one row when all other rows are fixed. Expansion along the changed row gives exactly (C115.1). Condition (C115.2) then makes the corrected determinant equal to the unit `epsilon`, so C113 applies.

QED.

## Residual cofactor structure

Because `Lbar=I+Dbar Ybar` is invertible over `B`, its adjugate is invertible. Hence every row of the cofactor matrix (equivalently every column of the adjugate) is a unimodular row over `B`.

Thus the lifted cofactor ideal for each row satisfies

`(F,C_{i1},C_{i2},C_{i3})A=A`.  (C115.3)

This does **not** by itself solve (C115.2), because `F` is not in the Jacobson radical of the nonlocal ring `A`. But it identifies an exact finite patching problem: force a unit into the affine coset

`delta + F(C_{i1},C_{i2},C_{i3})A`.
