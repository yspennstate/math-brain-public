# 2. Mandatory elementary audits — rederived

## 2.1 Rank one only

Let `A` be commutative and let `P` be a rank-one finite projective. If

\[
P\oplus A^m\cong A^{m+1},
\]

then top exterior power gives

\[
\det(P\oplus A^m)\cong P
\]

and

\[
\det(A^{m+1})\cong A.
\]

Hence

\[
P\cong A.
\]

This proves **only** the stated rank-one cancellation. It gives no rank-two or rank-three cancellation.

## 2.2 Localization at a parameter is not automatically local

Let

\[
R=k[x,y]_{(x,y)},
\qquad
A=R[1/x].
\]

Then

\[
A/(y)\cong k[x]_{(x)}[1/x]\cong k(x)
\]

and likewise

\[
A/(y-x)\cong k(x).
\]

Thus `(y)` and `(y-x)` are maximal ideals of `A`. They are distinct because equality would force their difference `x` into the common ideal, but `x` is a unit in `A`.

Therefore `R[1/r]` need not be local. No local-ring freeness theorem is used below without first producing an actual local ring.

# 4. New theorem `P02-C172-AFFINE-MODIFICATION-REGULARITY` — PROVED

Assume the inherited H4 hypotheses and fix `N>=2`. Define

\[
\boxed{
T_N:=R[t]/(u-r^{N-1}t).
}
\]

Let

\[
g=u-r^{N-1}t\in R[t].
\]

Then:

1. `T_N` is a Noetherian domain;
2. `dim T_N=4`;
3. `T_N` is a regular ring;
4. `r` remains prime in `T_N`;
5. there is a canonical isomorphism
   \[
   \boxed{T_N[1/r]\cong R[1/r]=A}
   \]
   carrying
   \[
   t\longmapsto u/r^{N-1}.
   \]

This is the exact affine modification along the parameter pair `(r,u)` needed to remove the denominator exponent.

## 4.1 Domain and dimension

A regular local ring is a UFD. Since `r,u` are tangent-independent parameters, `u notin (r)`; hence

\[
\gcd(u,r^{N-1})=1.
\]

Thus the linear polynomial

\[
g=u-r^{N-1}t
\]

is primitive in the UFD `R[t]`. Over `Frac(R)` it is nonconstant linear, hence irreducible. Gauss' lemma makes it irreducible in `R[t]`; because `R[t]` is a UFD, it is prime.

Therefore `T_N` is a domain. Since `dim R[t]=5` and `(g)` is a nonzero principal prime of height one,

\[
\dim T_N=4.
\]

## 4.2 Regularity away from `r=0`

Let `Q` be a prime of `R[t]` containing `g`, and let `q=Q/(g)` be the corresponding prime of `T_N`.

If

\[
r\notin Q,
\]

then `r` is invertible. Solving the defining equation gives

\[
t=u/r^{N-1},
\]

so

\[
T_N[1/r]\cong R[1/r].
\]

A localization of a regular ring is regular. Hence `(T_N)_q` is regular at every such prime.

## 4.3 Regularity over `r=0`

Now suppose

\[
r\in Q.
\]

Because `g in Q`,

\[
u=g+r^{N-1}t\in Q.
\]

Set

\[
U=R[t]_Q,
\qquad
\mathfrak q=QU.
\]

The ring `U` is regular local. Moreover

\[
U/(r,u)U
\]

is a localization of

\[
(R/(r,u))[t].
\]

Since `r,u` are tangent-independent members of a regular system of parameters of `R`, `R/(r,u)` is regular. Hence `U/(r,u)U` is regular.

Because both `U` and its codimension-two quotient are regular, the classes

\[
[r],[u]\in\mathfrak q/\mathfrak q^2
\]

are linearly independent.

We now inspect the class of `g` modulo `q^2`.

### Case `N>=3`

Since `r^(N-1) in q^2`,

\[
g\equiv u\pmod{\mathfrak q^2},
\]

so `g notin q^2`.

### Case `N=2`, `t in Q`

Then `rt in q^2`, so again

\[
g\equiv u\pmod{\mathfrak q^2}
\]

and `g notin q^2`.

### Case `N=2`, `t notin Q`

Now `t` is a unit of `U`. In the cotangent space,

\[
[g]=[u]-\overline t[r].
\]

Because `[r]` and `[u]` are linearly independent, this class is nonzero. Hence again

\[
g\notin\mathfrak q^2.
\]

A hypersurface quotient of a regular local ring by an element not in the square of the maximal ideal is regular. Therefore

\[
U/(g)U=(T_N)_q
\]

is regular for every prime over `r` as well.

Thus `T_N` is regular globally.

## 4.4 `r` is prime in `T_N`

Modulo `r`, the relation `u-r^(N-1)t` becomes `u`, so

\[
T_N/(r)
\cong
(R/(r,u))[t].
\]

The ring `R/(r,u)` is a regular local domain. Hence the polynomial ring above is a domain, proving that `(r)` is prime in `T_N`.

## 4.5 Exact localization isomorphism

The map

\[
R[t]\to R[1/r],
\qquad
t\mapsto u/r^{N-1}
\]

kills `g`, and after inverting `r` the relation uniquely solves for `t`. Hence

\[
\boxed{T_N[1/r]\simeq R[1/r].}
\]

No completion, Henselian assumption, characteristic assumption, or approximation theorem is used.

# 7. New theorem `P02-C175-FINITE-PRINCIPAL-PATCH-CERTIFICATE` — PROVED

\[
P=\ker(v)\cong\ker(w).

Then `P` is a finite projective `A`-module of rank three.

\[
S=T_N\setminus\mathfrak n.

\[
S^{-1}A=(T_N)_\mathfrak n[1/r].

\[
S^{-1}P\cong(S^{-1}A)^3.

\[
\phi:S^{-1}P\to(S^{-1}A)^3
\]

\[
\psi:(S^{-1}A)^3\to S^{-1}P.

\[
\psi\phi=1_P,
\qquad
\phi\psi=1_{A^3}

\[
s\in S=T_N\setminus\mathfrak n

\boxed{
P_s\cong A_s^3
\quad\text{for some }s\in T_N\setminus\mathfrak n.

Thus the remaining nonfree locus, if any, is contained in the explicit principal closed set

V(s)\subset\operatorname{Spec}A.

This is an exact finite patch certificate. It is not yet a global cancellation theorem because freeness on one principal open does not itself trivialize the clutching data on its complement.

# 8. New theorem `P02-C176-FINAL-RESIDUAL-SINGULAR-LOCUS-CRITERION` — PROVED

Let

\[
F=r^N+u\alpha,
\qquad
f=F/r^N,
\qquad
B_N=A/fA.
\]

Since `r` is a unit in `A`,

\[
\boxed{
B_N\cong R[1/r]/(F).
}
\]

Let `p` be a prime of `R` representing a prime of `B_N`. Then

\[
F\in p,
\qquad
r\notin p.
\]

In the domain `R/p`,

\[
r^N=-u\alpha.
\]

The left side is nonzero because `r notin p`. Hence the product `u alpha` is nonzero in the domain `R/p`, so

\[
\boxed{
u\notin p,
\qquad
\alpha\notin p.
}
\]

Thus every prime of the final residual surface already lies in the torus-like locus

\[
D(ru\alpha).
\]

Localizing at such `p`, `R_p` is a regular local ring and

\[
(B_N)_p\cong R_p/(F).
\]

The element `F` is a nonzero nonunit in the regular local ring `R_p`. Its quotient has dimension one less than `R_p`.

If

\[
F\notin p^2R_p,
\]

then `F` is part of a minimal system of generators of the maximal ideal, so `R_p/(F)` is regular.

If

\[
F\in p^2R_p,
\]

then quotienting does not lower the embedding dimension, while Krull dimension drops by one; hence the quotient is not regular.

Therefore

\[
\boxed{
(B_N)_p\text{ regular}
\iff
F\notin p^2R_p.
}
\]

Equivalently, the final singular locus is exactly the set of surviving primes at which the hypersurface equation loses its first-order class.

This gives the precise regularity gate that any Euler/Vaserstein/Hermitian or patching argument must respect. It also shows why merely inverting `u` and `alpha` does not force smoothness.

# 9. Method falsification `P02-D51-SINGLE-AFFINE-MODIFICATION-CHART-NOGO`

A tempting overstatement after C174 would be:

> Since the exponent-one theorem applies to the distinguished local chart of `T_N`, it solves the original projective over `A=T_N[1/r]`.

This is false.

Take

\[
R=k[r,u,x,y]_{(r,u,x,y)},
\qquad
N=2,
\]

and

\[
T=R[t]/(u-rt).
\]

Let

\[
\mathfrak n=(r,u,x,y,t)T=(r,x,y,t)T.
\]

The element

\[
1+t
\]

is not in `n`, so it is a unit in

\[
T_\mathfrak n[1/r].
\]

Under

\[
T[1/r]\cong A=R[1/r],
\qquad
t=u/r,
\]

it becomes

\[
1+t=\frac{r+u}{r}.
\]

But this element is **not** a unit of `A`. Indeed

\[
\mathfrak q=(r+u,x,y)A
\]

is maximal, because

\[
A/\mathfrak q
\cong
k[r]_{(r)}[1/r]
\cong k(r).
\]

Thus

\[
A\subsetneq T_\mathfrak n[1/r].
\]

So the distinguished chart is a strict further localization. C174 is a true local patch certificate, not a global solution.

# 10. Method falsification `P02-D52-RESIDUAL-SINGULARITY-NOT-OBSTRUCTION`

A second tempting overstatement is:

> If the final residual surface `B_N` is singular or nonreduced, that singularity is the actual rank-three Quillen obstruction.

This is also false.

Take a characteristic-zero field, for definiteness `k=Q`, and

\[
R=k[r,u,x,y]_{(r,u,x,y)},
\qquad
N=2,
\qquad
\alpha=u-2r.
\]

Then

\[
F=r^2+u(u-2r)
=(u-r)^2.
\]

Hence

\[
B_2
=A/((u-r)^2)
\]

is generically nonreduced along the prime

\[
\mathfrak p=(u-r),
\]

which survives after inverting `r`. In particular

\[
F\in\mathfrak p^2R_\mathfrak p,
\]

so C176 certifies that the residual local ring is nonregular.

Now choose the actual normal-form row

\[
v=(f,u,0,0),
\qquad
f=1+\frac{u\alpha}{r^2}.
\]

There is an explicit Bezout identity

\[
\boxed{
1=f-\frac{\alpha}{r^2}u.
}
\]

Thus `(f,u)` is a unimodular pair. Its length-two kernel is free rank one (indeed it is generated by `(u,-f)` up to sign), and the two zero tail coordinates add two free summands. Hence

\[
\boxed{
\ker(v)\cong A^3.
}
\]

So an actually free rank-three row may have a generically nonreduced final residual surface.

**Conclusion:** residual singularity/nonreducedness is a **method warning**, not an obstruction class. Any genuine obstruction must still be tied to the actual unimodular orbit / Euler / Vaserstein / Hermitian boundary data.
