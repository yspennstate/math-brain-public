# 3. Mandatory elementary audits rederived

## 3.1 Rank-one stable cancellation

Let `P` be projective of constant rank one and suppose

\[
P\oplus A^m\simeq A^{m+1}.
\]

Taking the top exterior power gives

\[
\det(P\oplus A^m)
\simeq P\otimes\det(A^m)
\simeq P,
\]

whereas

\[
\det(A^{m+1})\simeq A.
\]

Hence

\[
\boxed{P\simeq A}.
\]

This proves **only** rank-one cancellation under the displayed stable-freeness premise. It is not rank-two or rank-three cancellation and does not prove that an arbitrary projective is stably free.

## 3.2 `R[1/r]` need not be local

Take

\[
R=k[x,y]_{(x,y)},\qquad A=R[1/x].
\]

Then

\[
A/(y)
\simeq k[x]_{(x)}[1/x]
\simeq k(x),
\]

and similarly

\[
A/(y-x)\simeq k(x).
\]

Thus `(y)` and `(y-x)` are maximal ideals. They are distinct: equality would put

\[
y-(y-x)=x
\]

in their common proper ideal, but `x` is a unit in `A`.

Therefore localization at a parameter need not be local. No projective-freeness theorem for local rings may be invoked on `A` without separately proving locality.

# 5. `P02-C119-ONE-ROW-CORRECTION-IFF-COFACTOR-UNIMODULAR` — PROVED

## 5.1 Statement

Let `A` be a commutative ring, `F in A`, and

\[
B=A/FA.
\]

Assume the unit map

\[
A^\times\twoheadrightarrow B^\times
\]

is surjective. Let

\[
L=(\ell_{ij})\in M_n(A)
\]

have invertible reduction `Lbar in GL_n(B)`, and let

\[
\delta=\det L.
\]

Fix a row `i`. Let `C_{ij}` be the cofactors belonging to that row and put

\[
J_i=(C_{i1},\ldots,C_{in})A.
\]

For `z=(z_1,...,z_n)` let `L(z)` be obtained by replacing row `i` by

\[
\operatorname{row}_i(L)+Fz.
\]

Then

\[
\boxed{
\exists z\in A^n:\ \det L(z)\in A^\times
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
J_i=A.
}
\tag{C119}
\]

In the C92 branch the unit-surjectivity hypothesis is exactly C116.

## 5.2 Exact determinant image

The cofactors `C_{ij}` are independent of row `i`. Determinant multilinearity therefore gives the exact formula

\[
\det L(z)
=
\delta+F\sum_jz_jC_{ij}.
\tag{5.1}
\]

Moreover Laplace expansion along row `i` gives

\[
\delta=\sum_j\ell_{ij}C_{ij}\in J_i.
\tag{5.2}
\]

Hence every reachable determinant lies in `J_i`:

\[
\delta+FJ_i\subseteq J_i.
\tag{5.3}
\]

## 5.3 Necessity

If some correction has unit determinant, then by (5.3) the ideal `J_i` contains a unit. Thus

\[
J_i=A.
\]

## 5.4 Sufficiency

Because `Lbar` is invertible,

\[
\bar\delta\in B^\times.
\]

By unit surjectivity choose

\[
\varepsilon\in A^\times
\]

lifting `bar(delta)`. Then

\[
\varepsilon-\delta=Fh
\]

for some `h in A`.

If `J_i=A`, choose coefficients `z_j` such that

\[
h=\sum_jz_jC_{ij}.
\]

Equation (5.1) then gives

\[
\det L(z)
=
\delta+Fh
=
\varepsilon\in A^\times.
\]

This proves C119.

## 5.5 Consequence for C118

C118 said that for a C112 Vaserstein factor a globally unimodular unrestricted cofactor row gives a successful determinant repair. C119 upgrades that result:

> for one fixed lifted presentation and one fixed unrestricted row, global cofactor-row unimodularity is not merely sufficient; it is exactly necessary and sufficient for any `F`-multiple correction of that row to make the determinant a unit.

Thus failure of `J_i=A` is a genuine certificate of failure of **that fixed one-row repair problem**. D36 still proves that it is not an invariant obstruction to lifting the underlying group element.

# 6. `P02-C120-COFACTOR-ROW-EQUALS-RANK2-FRAME-COMPLETION` — PROVED

C119's condition can be identified exactly with a low-rank completion problem.

## 6.1 Statement

Let

\[
L\in M_3(A).
\]

Delete row `i` and call the resulting `2 x 3` matrix

\[
U_i.
\]

Let `J_i` be the ideal generated by the three `2 x 2` minors of `U_i`, with the usual cofactor signs. Then the following are equivalent:

\[
\boxed{
J_i=A;
}
\tag{6.1}
\]

\[
\boxed{
U_i:A^3\to A^2\text{ is surjective (hence split surjective)};
}
\tag{6.2}
\]

\[
\boxed{
\text{the two rows of }U_i\text{ extend to a matrix in }GL_3(A).
}
\tag{6.3}
\]

## 6.2 `(6.1) => (6.3)`

The determinant obtained by inserting a row

\[
x=(x_1,x_2,x_3)
\]

in position `i` is

\[
\sum_jx_jC_{ij}.
\]

If `J_i=A`, choose `x_j` with

\[
\sum_jx_jC_{ij}=1.
\]

The resulting `3 x 3` matrix has determinant one. Hence the two rows extend to an `SL_3(A)` basis.

## 6.3 `(6.3) => (6.1)`

Conversely, the determinant of any completion is an `A`-linear combination of the same cofactors. If a completion is invertible, that determinant is a unit, so the cofactor ideal contains a unit. Therefore `J_i=A`.

## 6.4 Equivalence with split surjectivity

The maximal minors of a `2 x 3` matrix generate the unit ideal exactly when the associated map

\[
A^3\to A^2
\]

has rank two at every prime, equivalently is surjective. Since `A^2` is projective, every surjection onto it splits.

Conversely, a split surjection is locally rank two everywhere, so its maximal-minor ideal is not contained in any prime and therefore equals `A`.

This proves C120.

## 6.5 Strategic meaning

The C118/C119 cofactor condition is not a mysterious determinant trick. It is exactly the statement that the **other two rows already form a split rank-two frame**.

Thus the factorwise cofactor route is a genuine low-rank reduction, but it does not bypass low-rank cancellation. It detects when one rank-two frame is already completable.

# 7. `P02-C121-FROZEN-ROW-COMPLETION-EQUALS-RANK2-CANCELLATION` — PROVED

The previous interpretation has an even more direct row form.

## 7.1 Statement

Let

\[
a=(a_1,a_2,a_3)\in Um_3(A).
\]

Then the following are equivalent:

1. there exist rows `b,c in A^3` such that

   \[
   \begin{pmatrix}
   a\\b\\c
   \end{pmatrix}
   \in GL_3(A);
   \]
2. the unimodular row `a` is completable;
3. the rank-two stably free module

   \[
   K=\ker(a:A^3\to A)
   \]

   is free.

## 7.2 Completion implies free kernel

If `M in GL_3(A)` has first row `a`, then multiplication by `M` identifies the kernel of `a` with the coordinate plane

\[
0\oplus A^2.
\]

Thus `K ~= A^2`.

## 7.3 Free kernel implies completion

Because `a` is unimodular, choose

\[
s\in A^3,\qquad a(s)=1.
\]

The exact sequence

\[
0\to K\to A^3\xrightarrow{a}A\to0
\]

splits. If `K` is free with basis `k_1,k_2`, then

\[
(s,k_1,k_2)
\]

is a basis of `A^3`. Let `B` be the invertible matrix with these columns. Then

\[
aB=(1,0,0),
\]

so the first row of `B^{-1}` is `a`. Hence `a` is completable.

This proves C121.

## 7.4 Consequence for determinant repair

Allowing arbitrary changes of two rows while freezing the third is already equivalent to a rank-two cancellation problem. Restricting those changes to `F`-multiples can only make the problem harder.

Therefore a proof of C117 cannot legitimately say “there are enough free row parameters, so force the determinant to a unit” without proving the corresponding rank-two completion theorem.

# 8. `P02-D37-UNIMODULAR-FROZEN-ROW-NOT-ENOUGH` — method claim DISPROVED

The overstrong claim

> every unimodular frozen row of length three can be completed by suitably choosing the other two rows

is false even for a smooth two-dimensional real affine ring.

Take

\[
S=\mathbb R[x,y,z]/(x^2+y^2+z^2-1)
\]

and

\[
a=(x,y,z)\in Um_3(S).
\]

The row is unimodular because

\[

\[
K_1(B)\simeq B^\times.

\[
\det\bar M=1,

\bar M\oplus I_k\in E_{3+k}(B).

\bar M\oplus I_k
=
\prod_{\nu=1}^t e_{i_\nu j_\nu}(\bar a_\nu).

\[
A\twoheadrightarrow B=A/(F)

E=
\prod_{\nu=1}^t e_{i_\nu j_\nu}(a_\nu)

reduces exactly to `Mbar+I_k`.

This proves C123.

# 10. `P02-C122-RELATIVE-PRESTABILIZATION-GATE` — CONDITIONAL, source verified

Van der Kallen's 1987 primary paper gives a precise theorem for exactly the kind of one-step unstable kernel appearing here, but its stable-range hypothesis must not be omitted.

## 10.1 Primary theorem checked

W. van der Kallen, *Vaserstein's pre-stabilization theorem over commutative rings*, Communications in Algebra 15 (1987), 657–663.

In the paper's notation, let `B` be a commutative ideal in a ring `A`. For `n>=2`, if

\[
\operatorname{sr}(B)\le n,
\]

then

\[
GL_n(B)\cap E(A,B)=\widetilde E_n(A,B),
\]

where `tilde(E)_n` is generated by the relative elementary subgroup, the relevant commutators, and explicit Vaserstein factors of the form

\[
(I+XY)(I+YX)^{-1}
\]

with the prescribed diagonal shape for `Y`.

The primary paper explicitly says that no Krull-dimension hypothesis is needed **once the relevant stable-range condition is available**.

## 10.2 Application gate for C117

For the C117 overlap set

\[
C=A_\delta,
\qquad
I=FC.
\]

A direct use at size three requires at least the following two items:

1. the relative class to be destabilized must lie in the stable relative elementary group;
2. the relative ideal must satisfy

   \[
   \operatorname{sr}(I)\le3.
   \]

If both hold, van der Kallen's theorem gives an explicit size-three prestabilization description in terms of relative elementary/commutator/Vaserstein generators. That is exactly the architecture needed to attack the C117 double-coset class.

## 10.3 Why the gate is not automatic

The working ring `C=A_delta` has dimension at most three. Generic noetherian dimension estimates only place a dimension-three ring in stable range at most four; they do not automatically prove the sharper relative condition

\[
\operatorname{sr}(FC)\le3.
\]

Nor does C123 by itself prove that the discrepancy between a chosen size-three local lift and the global stable elementary lift lies in the **relative** stable elementary subgroup on the overlap.

Therefore the following is retained only as a conditional gate:

`P02-C122-RELATIVE-PRESTABILIZATION-GATE` — **CONDITIONAL**.

A future proof must either establish the two missing hypotheses from the special C92 geometry or bypass the generic stable-range theorem.

# 12. Stable-range and low-rank applicability audit

## 12.1 Stable `K_0` is not cancellation
