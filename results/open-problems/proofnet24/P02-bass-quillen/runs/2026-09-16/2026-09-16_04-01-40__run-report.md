# 2. Mandatory imported-label audits

## 2.1 Rank-one cancellation is only rank one

Let `P` be a constant-rank-one projective over a commutative ring `A` and assume

\[
P\oplus A^m\cong A^{m+1}.
\]

Take `m+1`st exterior powers. Since

\[
\det(P\oplus A^m)
\cong
P\otimes\det(A^m)
\cong P
\]

and

\[
\det(A^{m+1})\cong A,
\]

we get

\[
P\cong A.
\]

This proves only the displayed rank-one cancellation. It neither proves stable freeness for an arbitrary projective nor cancels ranks two or three.

## 2.2 `R[1/r]` need not be local

Let

\[
R=k[x,y]_{(x,y)},
\qquad
A=R[1/x].
\]

Then

\[
A/(y)
\cong
k[x]_{(x)}[1/x]
\cong k(x),
\]

and, after imposing `y=x`,

\[
A/(y-x)
\cong k[x]_{(x)}[1/x]
\cong k(x).
\]

Hence `(y)` and `(y-x)` are maximal ideals of `A`. They are distinct: equality would put

\[
y-(y-x)=x
\]

in a proper ideal even though `x` is a unit in `A`.

Thus local-ring freeness cannot be applied to `A` merely because `R` was local.

## 2.3 Higher rank still requires an unstable theorem

The inherited stable `K_0`, `K_1`, `K_2`, completion, and localization statements are retained only at their actual strength. None is promoted to rank-two or rank-three freeness without an unstable cancellation/completion theorem.

# 4. `P02-C135-HB-RIGHT-INVERSE-ORBIT-RIGIDITY` — PROVED

## Statement

Let `B` be a commutative ring. Suppose

\[
\Delta\in Um_3(B),
\qquad
M=[a\ b\ z]\in SL_3(B),
\qquad
\Delta M=(0,0,1).
\tag{4.1}
\]

Then every right inverse `z'` of `Delta`, i.e.

\[
\Delta z'=1,
\]

has

\[
(z')^T\in z^T E_3(B).
\tag{4.2}
\]

Consequently all right inverses determine the same class in

\[
Um_3(B)/E_3(B).
\]

Moreover, for every surjection of commutative rings

\[
\pi:A\twoheadrightarrow B,
\]

the property

> “`z` has a lift in `Um_3(A)`”

is independent of the chosen right inverse `z`.

## Proof

Because `M` is invertible and

\[
\Delta M=(0,0,1),
\]

we have

\[
\ker(\Delta)=Ba+Bb.
\tag{4.3}
\]

If `z'` is another right inverse, then

\[
\Delta(z'-z)=0,
\]

so there are `s,t in B` with

\[
z'=z+as+bt.
\tag{4.4}
\]

Put

\[
E=e_{13}(s)e_{23}(t)\in E_3(B).
\]

Then

\[
M E=[a\ b\ z'].
\]

Equivalently,

\[
z'=N z,
\qquad
N:=M E M^{-1}.
\tag{4.5}
\]

For a commutative ring, Suslin's normality theorem gives

\[
E_3(B)\triangleleft GL_3(B).
\]

Hence `N in E_3(B)`. Transposing (4.5),

\[
(z')^T=z^T N^T,
\]

and `N^T in E_3(B)` because the transpose of an elementary matrix is elementary. This proves (4.2).

Now suppose `z` lifts to `y in Um_3(A)`. Choose an elementary factorization of `N` and lift its finitely many coefficients through `pi`; this gives

\[
\widetilde N\in E_3(A),
\qquad
\pi(\widetilde N)=N.
\]

Then

\[
\widetilde N y\in Um_3(A)
\]

and reduces to `z'`. The converse follows from `N^{-1}`. Therefore liftability is independent of the right inverse.

QED.

## Why this matters

The residual affine family of right inverses has many representatives but only **one unstable elementary orbit**. The orbit cannot be optimized by changing the Hilbert–Burch syzygy coefficients.
