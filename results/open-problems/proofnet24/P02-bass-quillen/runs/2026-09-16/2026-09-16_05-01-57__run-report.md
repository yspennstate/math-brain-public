# 2. Mandatory imported-label audits

## 2.1 Rank-one cancellation proves only rank one

Let `P` be a constant-rank-one projective over a commutative ring `A` and suppose

\[
P\oplus A^m\cong A^{m+1}.
\]

Taking `(m+1)`st exterior powers gives

\[
\det(P\oplus A^m)
\cong P\otimes\det(A^m)
\cong P,
\]

while

\[
\det(A^{m+1})\cong A.
\]

Hence

\[
P\cong A.
\]

Nothing in this argument cancels rank two or rank three, and it does not prove that an arbitrary projective is stably free.

## 2.2 `R[1/r]` need not be local

Let

\[
R=k[x,y]_{(x,y)},
\qquad
A=R[1/x].
\]

Then

\[
A/(y)\cong k[x]_{(x)}[1/x]\cong k(x),
\]

and imposing `y=x` gives

\[
A/(y-x)\cong k[x]_{(x)}[1/x]\cong k(x).
\]

Thus `(y)` and `(y-x)` are maximal. They are distinct because equality would force

\[
x=y-(y-x)
\]

into a proper ideal although `x` is a unit of `A`.

So no local-ring projective-freeness theorem may be applied to `A` merely because `R` is local.

# 4. `P02-C139-LIFTABLE-GL3-IMAGE-INVARIANCE` — PROVED

## Statement

Let

\[
\pi:A\twoheadrightarrow B
\]

be a surjection of commutative rings. Let

\[
\pi_*:Um_3(A)/E_3(A)\to Um_3(B)/E_3(B)
\]

be reduction. Suppose

\[
G\in GL_3(B)
\]

has a lift

\[
\widetilde G\in GL_3(A).
\]

Then right multiplication by `G` is a well-defined bijection on `Um_3(B)/E_3(B)`, and

\[
\boxed{
\rho\in\operatorname{Im}\pi_*
\iff
\rho G\in\operatorname{Im}\pi_*.
}
\tag{4.1}
\]

## Proof

For a commutative ring, Suslin normality gives

\[
E_3(B)\triangleleft GL_3(B).
\]

If two unimodular rows represent the same orbit,

\[
y'=y\varepsilon,
\qquad \varepsilon\in E_3(B),
\]

then

\[
y'G
=yG(G^{-1}\varepsilon G),
\]

and `G^{-1}epsilon G in E_3(B)`. Hence right multiplication by `G` is well-defined on the orbit set. Its inverse is right multiplication by `G^{-1}`.

If

\[
\rho=\pi_*([x]),
\]

then

\[
\rho G
=
\pi_*([x\widetilde G]),
\]

because reduction commutes with matrix multiplication. Therefore the image of `pi_*` is stable under `G`. Applying the same argument to the lift `\widetilde G^{-1}` proves the converse.

QED.

## Interpretation

Any orbit modification induced by a matrix that already lifts to `A` cannot turn a non-liftable orbit class into a liftable one. To alter the obstruction one must use genuinely non-liftable quotient geometry or change the underlying source/residual problem rather than merely its liftable coordinates.

# 5. `P02-C140-HB-PRESENTATION-LIFTABILITY-INVARIANCE` — PROVED

This is the main new Bass–Quillen reduction.

## Statement

Work in the C92 branch, so

\[
S=R/(F)
\]

is local and

\[
B=S[1/r].
\]

Let an oriented Hilbert–Burch matrix over `S` be

\[
\Phi=[a\ b]\in M_{3\times2}(S),
\]

and let its oriented minors row be characterized by

\[
\Delta(v)=\det[a,b,v].
\tag{5.1}
\]

Take a standard presentation-equivalence

\[
\Phi'=U\Phi V,
\qquad
U\in GL_3(S),
\qquad
V\in GL_2(S).
\tag{5.2}
\]

Let `rho_HB` and `rho'_HB` be the canonical C135 right-inverse orbit classes after localizing to `B`.

Then

\[
\boxed{
\rho_{HB}\in\operatorname{Im}\pi_*
\iff
\rho'_{HB}\in\operatorname{Im}\pi_*.
}
\tag{5.3}
\]

Equivalently, the existence of the C133 unimodular completion-vector certificate is invariant under every standard `GL_3(S) x GL_2(S)` change of Hilbert–Burch bases.

## Proof: transformation of the minors row

Put

\[
\lambda=\det(U)\det(V)\in S^\times.
\]

For `v in S^3`, first changing the two source columns by `V` multiplies their wedge by `det(V)`. Then

\[
\det[Ua',Ub',v]
=\det(U)\det[a',b',U^{-1}v].
\]

Therefore

\[
\boxed{
\Delta'=\lambda\,\Delta U^{-1}.
}
\tag{5.4}
\]

If `z` is a right inverse of `Delta`, define

\[
z'=\lambda^{-1}Uz.
\tag{5.5}
\]

Then

\[
\Delta'z'
=\lambda\Delta U^{-1}\lambda^{-1}Uz
=1.
\]

With row convention,

\[
(z')^T
=z^T G,
\qquad
G:=\lambda^{-1}U^T\in GL_3(S)\subset GL_3(B).
\tag{5.6}
\]

Thus

\[
\rho'_{HB}=\rho_{HB}G
\]

in `Um_3(B)/E_3(B)`.

## Proof: `G` lifts automatically in the C92 local quotient

Because `R` is local and `F in m`, every matrix in `GL_n(S)` lifts to `GL_n(R)`.

Indeed, lift its entries arbitrarily. The determinant reduces to a unit of `S`. If the lifted determinant lay in the maximal ideal `m`, then its residue in `S` would lie in `m/(F)` and could not be a unit. Hence the lifted determinant is outside `m`, therefore is a unit of `R`.

Choose lifts

\[
\widetilde U\in GL_3(R),
\qquad
\widetilde V\in GL_2(R).
\]

Then

\[
\widetilde\lambda
=\det(\widetilde U)\det(\widetilde V)
\in R^\times
\]

lifts `lambda`, and

\[
\widetilde G
=\widetilde\lambda^{-1}\widetilde U^T
\in GL_3(R)\subset GL_3(A)
\]

lifts `G`.

C139 now gives (5.3).

QED.

## Symbolic/adversarial check

For arbitrary invertible rational test matrices `U` and `V` and random rank-two `3 x 2` matrices `Phi`, direct symbolic determinant calculation verified

\[
\Delta(U\Phi V)
=\det(U)\det(V)\,\Delta(\Phi)U^{-1}.
\]

This is only a consistency test; the proof above is determinant multilinearity and does not rely on computation.

# 8. `P02-D43-ARBITRARY-QUOTIENT-GL3-LIFT-NOGO` — overstrong generalization DISPROVED

C140 relies crucially on the C92 local quotient `R -> S=R/(F)`.

It is false for an arbitrary surjective ring map that every `GL_3` matrix downstairs lifts invertibly.

Take

\[
A_0=\mathbb Z,
\qquad
B_0=\mathbb Z/8\mathbb Z,
\]

and

\[
G=\operatorname{diag}(3,1,1)\in GL_3(B_0).
\]

If `G` lifted to some `\widetilde G in GL_3(Z)`, then

\[
\det(\widetilde G)=\pm1.
\]

Reducing modulo `8` would give determinant `1` or `7`, whereas

\[
\det(G)=3\pmod 8.
\]

Contradiction.

So the condition “`G` has an invertible lift” in C139 is genuine. The reason it becomes automatic in C140 is precisely that the quotient ideal `(F)` lies in the Jacobson radical of the local ring `R`.

Again, this is a hypothesis stress test, not a counterexample to Quillen.

\[

The nontrivial characters occur in conjugate pairs, so

\[
V_q\cong(W_q)_\mathbb R,
\qquad
W_q=\bigoplus_{j=1}^{(q-1)/2}\chi^j.

Complex equivariant Bott periodicity therefore gives

\[
K_0^K(D(V_q),S(V_q))\cong R(K),

K_1^K(D(V_q),S(V_q))=0.

\[
H=C_r,
\]

and

\[
V_q\cong\sigma\oplus(W_q)_\mathbb R.
\]

\[
D(\sigma)\simeq_K\mathrm{pt},
\qquad
S(\sigma)\cong K/H,
\]

the pair long exact sequence is

\[
0\to K_1^K(D\sigma,S\sigma)
\to R(H)
\xrightarrow{\operatorname{Ind}_H^K}
R(K)
\to K_0^K(D\sigma,S\sigma)

\operatorname{Ind}_H^K(\eta_j)

\[
K_1^K(\Delta^{q-1},\partial\Delta^{q-1})=0
