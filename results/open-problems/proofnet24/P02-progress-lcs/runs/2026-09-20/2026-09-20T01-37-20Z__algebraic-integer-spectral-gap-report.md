## 4. New theorem: arithmetic strictification of a rational cone bound

### Theorem P02-T02-A (algebraic-integer gap for a finite rational covering operator)

Let `A` be an `N x N` nonnegative rational matrix and suppose `B=CA` is an integer matrix for some positive integer `C`. Suppose

\[
\rho(A)\le\lambda=\frac pq<1
\]

with `p/q` rational. Reduce

\[
C\lambda=\frac ab,
\qquad \gcd(a,b)=1.
\]

If `b>1`, define

\[
\delta_R:=\frac{1}{b(2a)^{N-1}}
\]

and

\[
\varepsilon:=\frac{\delta_R}{2C}
=\frac{1}{2Cb(2a)^{N-1}}.
\]

Then

\[
\boxed{\rho(A)<\lambda-\varepsilon<\lambda.}
\tag{4.1}
\]

In particular, for any finite covering machine whose accepted-list asymptotics are controlled by `A`, one may replace `lambda` by `lambda-epsilon` in the exponential finite-to-infinite transfer.

### Proof

Put

\[
\mu:=\rho(B)=C\rho(A),
\qquad
R:=C\lambda=\frac ab.
\]

Because `B` is an integer matrix, every eigenvalue of `B`, in particular the Perron root `mu`, is an algebraic integer. The rational number `R=a/b` is not an integer because `b>1`. A rational algebraic integer is an integer, hence

\[
\mu\ne R.
\]

The inherited bound `rho(A)<=lambda` gives `mu<=R`, therefore in fact

\[
\mu<R.
\tag{4.2}
\]

Let

\[
\chi_B(z)=\det(zI-B)\in\mathbb Z[z]
\]

be the monic characteristic polynomial, of degree `N`. Since `R` is not an eigenvalue,

\[
\chi_B(R)\ne0.
\]

Substituting the reduced fraction `a/b` into a monic integer polynomial of degree `N` shows that `b^N chi_B(a/b)` is a nonzero integer. Consequently

\[
|\chi_B(R)|\ge b^{-N}.
\tag{4.3}
\]

Write the eigenvalues of `B`, with algebraic multiplicity, as

\[
\mu=\mu_1,\mu_2,\ldots,\mu_N.
\]

Because `B` is nonnegative, its spectral radius is `mu`, so `|mu_j|<=mu<R` for every `j`. Hence

\[
|R-\mu_j|\le R+|\mu_j|<2R.
\]

Using one Perron factor separately in the factorization of the characteristic polynomial,

\[
b^{-N}
\le |\chi_B(R)|
=(R-\mu)\prod_{j=2}^N|R-\mu_j|
\le (R-\mu)(2R)^{N-1}.
\]

Therefore

\[
R-\mu\ge
\frac{1}{b^N(2R)^{N-1}}
=
\frac{1}{b(2a)^{N-1}}
=\delta_R.
\tag{4.4}
\]

Dividing by `C`,

\[
\rho(A)\le\lambda-\frac{\delta_R}{C}
<\lambda-\frac{\delta_R}{2C}
=\lambda-\varepsilon.
\]

This proves (4.1). No irreducibility, independent-cell assumption, or numerical eigenvalue computation is used. QED.
