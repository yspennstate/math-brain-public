By Section 7,

\[
\|J_{L,R_L}\|_{cb},
\|J_{L,R_L}^{-1}\|_{cb}
\to1.
\]

Thus the expanding finite-propagation operator spaces converge in a two-sided matrix-stable near-isometric sense.

Nevertheless

\[
[u_L]=0
\quad\text{for every finite stage},
\]

while

\[
[u_\infty]\ne0.
\]

Therefore the statement

> two-sided asymptotically isometric completely bounded convergence on expanding marked balls forces ordinary K1 continuity

is false.

The missing datum is exactly the propagation of the stable K-relation.

This is a nonamenable analogue of the earlier cyclic warning and shows why P01 must track relation complexity separately from representative/norm control.
