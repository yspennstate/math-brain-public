\[
\|J_{L,R}\|_{cb}
\le\Gamma_+(R,L)

whenever the P40 support condition, in particular `L>=8R+1`, holds.

\[
C_r^*(\Gamma)
= C_r^*(F_2)\otimes_{\min}C_r^*(\mathbb Z)
= C_r^*(F_2)\otimes_{\min}C(\mathbb T),

and similarly

\[
C_r^*(\Gamma_L)

X\in M_n(E_R\otimes C(\mathbb T)),

\[
z\mapsto X(z)\in M_n(E_R).

Then

\[
\|X\|=\sup_{z\in\mathbb T}\|X(z)\|,

and the coefficient map on the first factor satisfies

\[
\|(J_{L,R}\otimes\mathrm{id})^{(n)}X\|
=
\sup_z\|J_{L,R}^{(n)}(X(z))\|

\boxed{
\|J_{L,R}\otimes\mathrm{id}_{C(\mathbb T)}\|_{cb}

The extra `Z`-propagation costs nothing in this norm theorem.

# 4. Non-free quotient kernel-rank monotonicity

## `P25-F2xZ-QUOTIENT-KERNEL-RANK-MONOTONICITY-26` — **PROVED**

Let

\[
R=\mathbb C[F_2\times\mathbb Z]
\]

and

\[
C\in M_{u,v}(R).
\]

Let

\[
\alpha=\dim_{L(\Gamma)}\ker C_\infty.
\]

Then

\[
\boxed{
\rho_R(C)=v-\alpha,
}
\tag{4.1}
\]

where `rho_R` is Cohn inner rank.

Consequently, for **every** group quotient

\[
\Gamma\twoheadrightarrow H,
\]

with coefficientwise evaluated matrix `C_H`, one has

\[
\boxed{
\dim_{L(H)}\ker C_H\ge\alpha.
}
\tag{4.2}
\]

### Proof

By Section 2, `R` is a Sylvester domain. Its inner rank is therefore the integer-valued Sylvester rank induced by its universal division ring `D_R`.

Henneke–López-Álvarez identify `D_R` with the Linnell division closure of `R` in the affiliated-operator algebra `U(Gamma)`.

The canonical von-Neumann rank on `U(Gamma)` restricts to a Sylvester rank function on the division ring `D_R`. A division ring has only one Sylvester rank function: ordinary division-ring matrix rank. Hence, on matrices over `R`,

\[
\operatorname{rank}_{L(\Gamma)}C
=
\operatorname{rank}_{D_R}C
=
\rho_R(C).
\]

Rank-nullity in the finite von-Neumann module `L(Gamma)^v` gives `(4.1)`.

Set

\[
r=v-\alpha=\rho_R(C).
\]

By definition of inner rank there are group-ring matrices

\[
A\in M_{u,r}(R),
\qquad
B\in M_{r,v}(R)
\]

such that

\[
C=AB.
\]

Under any group quotient the algebraic factorization survives:

\[
C_H=A_HB_H.
\]

No map of reduced group `C*`-algebras is asserted.

As an `L(H)`-module operator, the closed image of `C_H` is contained in the closed image of `A_H`, which has dimension at most `r`. Thus

\[
\operatorname{rank}_{L(H)}C_H\le r.
\]

Therefore

\[
\dim_{L(H)}\ker C_H
=v-\operatorname{rank}_{L(H)}C_H
\ge v-r
=\alpha.
\]

This proves `(4.2)`.

### Scope

The theorem is stronger than needed for the specific cyclic filling: the lower kernel-dimension bound holds for every group quotient of `F_2 x Z`. What is special to the family `Gamma_L` is the matching analytic upper control constructed below.

M_L(C_r^*(N_L))

\boxed{

The same formula holds after finite matrix stabilization and therefore on `K_0`.

### Step 2 — trace image of `N_L`

\[
A=C_r^*(F_L).

\[
C_r^*(N_L)=A\otimes C(\mathbb T)=C(\mathbb T,A).

\[
\boxed{
\tau_*K_0(C_r^*(N_L))=\mathbb Z.

\tag{5.3}

# 6. Quantized controlled spectral cutoff

We now prove the main theorem.

Let

\[
D=C^*C
\]

satisfy the fixed limit gap `(0.1)`. Let `rho` be the first-factor support radius of `D`.

## 6.1 Chebyshev cutoff

The P01-P38 minimax polynomial is

\[
p_N(x)
=
\frac{
T_N\!\left(\frac{M+\delta-2x}{M-\delta}\right)
}{
T_N\!\left(\frac{M+\delta}{M-\delta}\right)
}.
\tag{6.1}
\]

It satisfies

\[
p_N(0)=1
\]

and

\[
E_N
:=
\sup_{x\in[\delta,M]}|p_N(x)|
=
\operatorname{sech}(N\theta).
\tag{6.2}
\]

With `N=N_L` from `(0.2)`,

\[
\operatorname{sech}(N_L\theta)
\le2e^{-N_L\theta}
\le
\boxed{
\frac1{12vL}.
}
\tag{6.3}
\]

Define

\[
A_{\infty,L}=p_{N_L}(D_\infty).
\]

On the zero spectral subspace it is exactly `1`, while on the positive spectrum it has norm at most `E_L:=E_{N_L}`. Thus

\[
\boxed{
\|A_{\infty,L}^2-A_{\infty,L}\|
\le
E_L(1+E_L).
}
\tag{6.4}
\]

Its first-factor support radius is at most `N_L rho`, while the idempotent defect has first-factor support radius at most `2N_L rho`.

## 6.2 Transfer of the idempotent defect

Evaluate the same finite group-ring polynomial at the stage group `Gamma_L` and call it

\[
A_L=p_{N_L}(D_L).
\]

Algebraically,

\[
A_L^2-A_L
=
(A_{\infty,L}^2-A_{\infty,L})_L.
\]

By `P25-F2xZ-TENSOR-P40-CB-26`, under `(0.3)`,

\[
\eta_L
:=
\|A_L^2-A_L\|
\le
2E_L(1+E_L).
\tag{6.5}
\]

Since `E_L<=1/(12vL)<=1/12`, one has

\[
\eta_L<\frac14.
\]

Therefore the genuine reduced-`C*` projection

\[
Q_L
=
\mathbf1_{[1/2,\infty)}(A_L)
\in M_v(C_r^*(\Gamma_L))
\tag{6.6}
\]

is well defined.

For a self-adjoint element `x` with

\[
\|x^2-x\|\le\eta<1/4,
\]

spectral calculus gives

\[
\left\|
\mathbf1_{[1/2,\infty)}(x)-x
\right\|
\le
\frac{1-\sqrt{1-4\eta}}2
\le2\eta.
\tag{6.7}
\]

Hence

\[
\boxed{
\|Q_L-A_L\|
\le2\eta_L.
}
\tag{6.8}

## 6.3 Exact trace comparison for the polynomial

Because `(0.3)` is far stronger than `N_L rho<L`, no nontrivial word in the support of `A_{\infty,L}` can collapse to the identity under the relation `a^L=1`.

The extra direct-product `Z` coordinate is unchanged by the quotient. Therefore the identity coefficient is preserved exactly:

\[
\boxed{

=
(\operatorname{Tr}_v\otimes\tau_{\Gamma})(A_{\infty,L}).

\tag{6.9}

Let

\[

\]

By `(6.2)`,

\[
\|A_{\infty,L}-P_\infty\|
\le E_L.

Thus, with the unnormalized matrix trace,

\[
\left|
(\operatorname{Tr}_v\otimes\tau_{\Gamma})(A_{\infty,L})
-
\alpha
\right|
\le vE_L.
\tag{6.10}

Using `(6.8)` and `(6.9)`,

\[
\left|

\right|
\le

\]

By `(6.5)`,

\[
2\eta_L+E_L
\le
E_L(5+4E_L).
\]

Since `E_L<=1/(12vL)<=1/12`,

\[
\boxed{
\left|
(\operatorname{Tr}_v\otimes\tau_{\Gamma_L})(Q_L)-\alpha

\frac1{2L}.

\tag{6.11}

But Section 5 says the trace of every reduced-`C*` `K_0` class lies in `L^{-1}Z`, while Section 4 says `alpha` is an integer. Therefore `(6.11)` forces the exact equality

\boxed{

This is the quantization step.

# 7. Explicit stage gap

The identity `P_L=Q_L` turns the fixed polynomial threshold into a genuine spectral gap.

Let

\[
y_0=\frac{M+\delta}{M-\delta}=\cosh\theta.
\]

Since `N_L theta >= log(24vL)`, one has `cosh(N_L theta)/2>1`. Define `s_L` and `delta_{*,L}` by `(0.8)`.

On `[0,delta]` the affine Chebyshev variable

\[
y(x)=\frac{M+\delta-2x}{M-\delta}
\]

decreases from `cosh theta` to `1`, and `T_{N_L}` is strictly increasing on `[1,infinity)`. Hence

\[
p_{N_L}(x)>1/2
\qquad
(0\le x<\delta_{*,L}).
\tag{7.1}
\]

If a positive spectral point of `D_L` lay in `(0,delta_{*,L})`, every sufficiently small spectral neighborhood of that point would give a nonzero spectral projection contained in `Q_L` and orthogonal to `P_L`, contradicting `Q_L=P_L`.

If `alpha=0`, the same argument excludes zero itself from the spectrum: otherwise every sufficiently small neighborhood of zero would give a nonzero projection inside `Q_L=P_L=0`.

This proves `(0.10)`.

## 7.1 Explicit lower bound

Set

\[
x=N_L\theta.
\]

For `x>arcosh 2`,

\[
\cosh(x-\log2)
=
\frac{e^x}{4}+e^{-x}
>
\frac{e^x+e^{-x}}4
=
\frac{\cosh x}{2}.
\]

Therefore

\[
\operatorname{arcosh}(\cosh x/2)
<x-\log2,
\]
