## `P25-SECONDARY-TRACE-SMITH-10` — PROVED

Fix even \(n\ge2\). Put

\[
A_{n-1}=K_0^G(X^{(n-1)}),\qquad
A_n=K_0^G(X^{(n)}),
\]

and write

\[
T_{n-1}=D^{-1}\mathbb Z.
\]

The cellular exact sequence gives

\[
0\to A_{n-1}\to A_n\to Z_n\to0,
\]

where

\[
Z_n=
\ker\!\left[
C_n\to K_1^G(X^{(n-1)})
\right],
\qquad
C_n=\bigoplus_eR_{\mathbb C}(H_e).
\]

Since \(C_n\) is free abelian, so is \(Z_n\). Choose an integral basis \(z_1,\ldots,z_k\) and arbitrary integral lifts \(\widetilde z_j\in A_n\). Define

\[
\beta_j
=
D\,\tau_*\mu_{X^{(n)}}(\widetilde z_j).
\]

Then

\[
\boxed{
s_n=[T_n:T_{n-1}]
=
\operatorname{lcm}_j\operatorname{den}(\beta_j).
}
\tag{1}
\]

Equivalently,

\[
\boxed{
v_p(s_n)=\max_j[-v_p(\beta_j)]_+.
}
\tag{2}
\]

If \(\beta_j=v_j/B\) for one common \(B\ge1\), then

\[
\boxed{
s_n=\frac{B}{\gcd(B,v_1,\ldots,v_k)}.
}
\tag{3}
\]

### Proof

Two lifts of one \(z_j\) differ by \(A_{n-1}\), whose trace lies in \(D^{-1}\mathbb Z\). Hence \(\beta_j+\mathbb Z\in\mathbb Q/\mathbb Z\) is lift-independent.

Multiplication by \(D\) identifies

\[
\mathbb Q/(D^{-1}\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Q/\mathbb Z.
\]

Under this identification the secondary trace sends \(z_j\) to \(\beta_j+\mathbb Z\). The order of \(\beta+\mathbb Z\) is the reduced denominator of \(\beta\). A finite subgroup of \(\mathbb Q/\mathbb Z\) is cyclic, so the subgroup generated by the basis images has order equal to the lcm of the individual orders. This proves (1), and (2) is the \(p\)-primary form. Formula (3) follows by taking the gcd of the common-denominator numerators.

The generated subgroup is unchanged under \(GL_k(\mathbb Z)\)-basis change, and changing lifts only adds an integral vector. Thus the formulas are intrinsic.

QED.

\qquad

\[

Choose a full integral Chern-data lattice \(\mathcal C_2\cong\mathbb Z^r\). In integral bases let

\[

\]

be Lück's rational Chern matrix and let

\[
\ell_2\in\mathbb Q^{1\times r}
\]

be the canonical trace-after-assembly row on Chern coordinates.

From `P25-TRACE-FUNCTIONAL-SMITH-08`, the trace row on integral \(K\)-homology coordinates is

\[
t_2=\ell_2(A_2^{\rm Ch})^{-1}.
\]

Choose a basis of \(Z_2\), integral lifts in \(A_2\), and let

\[
Y\in M_{r\times k}(\mathbb Z)
\]

be the matrix of the lift coordinates in \(\overline A_2\). Then

\[
\boxed{
(\beta_1,\ldots,\beta_k)
=
L_0\,\ell_2(A_2^{\rm Ch})^{-1}Y.
}
\tag{4}
\]

Therefore

\[
\boxed{
s_2
=
\operatorname{lcm}_j
\operatorname{den}
\left(
[L_0\,\ell_2(A_2^{\rm Ch})^{-1}Y]_j
\right).
}
\tag{5}
\]

This is the finite Chern-character formula requested by P01.

### Exact adjugate form

Choose positive integers \(a,b\) such that

\[
M:=aA_2^{\rm Ch}\in M_r(\mathbb Z)
\]

and

\[
w:=b\ell_2\in\mathbb Z^{1\times r}.
\]

Assume \(\det M\ne0\), and set

\[
c:=w\,\operatorname{adj}(M)\,Y
=
(c_1,\ldots,c_k)\in\mathbb Z^{1\times k}.
\]

Because

\[
(A_2^{\rm Ch})^{-1}
=
aM^{-1}
=
\frac{a}{\det M}\operatorname{adj}(M),
\]

we have

\[
\beta_j=\frac{aL_0c_j}{b\det M}.
\]

Hence

\[
\boxed{
s_2
=
\frac{
b|\det M|
}{
\gcd(
b|\det M|,
aL_0c_1,\ldots,aL_0c_k
)
}.
}
\tag{6}
\]

In particular,

\[
s_2\mid b|\det M|,
\]

but determinant size alone is not sharp: the trace row and the relative-cycle lift lattice can cancel any part of it.

Prime-by-prime,

\[
\boxed{
v_p(s_2)
=
\max\left\{
0,\
v_p(b|\det M|)
-
\min_jv_p(aL_0c_j)
\right\},
}
\tag{7}
\]

with \(s_2=1\) if every \(c_j=0\).
