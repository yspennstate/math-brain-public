## `P25-DUAL-TRACE-COMPRESSION-11` — PROVED

Let
\[
A_n=K_0^G(X^{(n)}),\qquad
\lambda_n=\tau_*\mu_{X^{(n)}}:A_n\to\mathbb Q.
\]
Assume
\[
T_{n-1}=\lambda_{n-1}(A_{n-1})=D^{-1}\mathbb Z,
\qquad
T_n=\lambda_n(A_n)=(s_nD)^{-1}\mathbb Z,
\]
where for odd \(n\) one has \(s_n=1\) by P01-P27.

Write
\[
A_n/\operatorname{tors}\cong\mathbb Z^r
\]
and let
\[
t_n=(t_1,\ldots,t_r)\in\mathbb Q^{1\times r}
\]
be the full canonical trace row in any integral basis.

Then
\[
\boxed{
s_n=\operatorname{lcm}_{j}\operatorname{den}(Dt_j).
}
\tag{1}
\]
Equivalently,
\[
\boxed{
s_n=\min\{s\ge1:sDt_n\in\mathbb Z^{1\times r}\}.
}
\tag{2}
\]
Prime by prime,
\[
\boxed{
v_p(s_n)=\max_j[-v_p(Dt_j)]_+.
}
\tag{3}
\]

### Proof

The full trace image is
\[
T_n=\sum_j\mathbb Z t_j=(s_nD)^{-1}\mathbb Z.
\]
Multiplying by \(D\),
\[
DT_n=s_n^{-1}\mathbb Z.
\]
Modulo \(\mathbb Z\), this is the cyclic subgroup of \(\mathbb Q/\mathbb Z\) of order \(s_n\), generated by the classes \(Dt_j+\mathbb Z\). The order of a rational class modulo \(\mathbb Z\) is its reduced denominator, so the order of the generated subgroup is the lcm in (1). Equations (2) and (3) follow immediately. QED.
