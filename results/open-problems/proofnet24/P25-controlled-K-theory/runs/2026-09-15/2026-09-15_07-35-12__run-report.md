The remaining obstruction is moving witness complexity.

If ordinary assembly relations differ across the marked family, their nullhomotopy/equivalence witnesses must escape through relation depth, reduced-norm distortion, tolerance, or matrix size.

The next P25 theorem should therefore bound the **full moving assembly witness budget**, not merely the endpoint propagation.
