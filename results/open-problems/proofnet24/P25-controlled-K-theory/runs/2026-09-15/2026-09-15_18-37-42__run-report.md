# 4. Nielsen normal-form growth

Fix the semidirect-product convention

\[
t h t^{-1}=\alpha(h)
\qquad(h\in F).
\]

Then every group element of `Gamma` has a unique normal form

\[
h t^k,
\qquad h\in F,\ k\in\mathbb Z.
\]

The automorphism iterates are explicit:

\[
\alpha^n(a)=a,
\qquad
\alpha^n(b)=a^n b,
\qquad
\alpha^n(b^{-1})=b^{-1}a^{-n}.
\tag{4.1}
\]

## `P25-NIELSEN-NORMAL-FORM-GROWTH-29` — **PROVED**

If `g in Gamma` has standard word length at most `Q` with respect to

\[
\{a^{\pm1},b^{\pm1},t^{\pm1}\},
\]
and

\[
g=h t^k
\]
is its normal form, then

\[
\boxed{|k|\le Q}
\tag{4.2}
\]
and

\[
\boxed{|h|_F\le Q(Q+1).}
\tag{4.3}
\]

### Proof

Choose a representative word of length at most `Q`. Read it from left to right while moving every `t`-letter to the right. At every point, the current `t`-height `n` has

\[
|n|\le Q.
\]

A base letter encountered at height `n` contributes:

- `alpha^n(a^{\pm1})`, of free length `1`;
- `alpha^n(b)=a^n b`, of free length `|n|+1`;
- `alpha^n(b^{-1})=b^{-1}a^{-n}`, of free length `|n|+1`.

There are at most `Q` base letters, and every contribution has free length at most `Q+1`. Before free reduction the base word therefore has length at most

\[
Q(Q+1).
\]
Free reduction can only decrease length. This proves (4.3). The exponent bound (4.2) is immediate. QED.

This quadratic conversion from semidirect word radius to free-base radius is the only monodromy-growth loss in the norm theorem below.

# 5. Crossed-product cb base theorem

Let

\[
X\in M_n(\mathbb C\Gamma)
\]
be supported in the standard `Gamma`-ball of radius `Q`.

Using the normal form, write its finite Fourier expansion

\[
X=\sum_{|k|\le Q}x_k t^k,
\qquad
x_k\in M_n(\mathbb C F).
\tag{5.1}
\]

By Section 4, every `x_k` is supported in the free ball of radius

\[
S_Q=Q(Q+1).
\tag{5.2}
\]

Let `E` be the canonical conditional expectation from the reduced crossed product to the base reduced group C*-algebra. Then

\[
x_k=E(Xt^{-k}).
\]
Because `E` is completely contractive,

\[
\boxed{\|x_k\|\le\|X\|}
\tag{5.3}
\]
at every matrix level.

The quotient evaluation `F -> H_L` is algebraic and intertwines the descended automorphism, so

\[
X_L=\sum_{|k|\le Q}(x_k)_L t^k.
\tag{5.4}
\]

## `P25-NIELSEN-CROSSED-PRODUCT-CB-BASE-29` — **PROVED**

Assume

\[
L\ge8Q(Q+1)+1.
\tag{5.5}
\]
Then

\[
\boxed{
\|X_L\|
\le
(2Q+1)\Gamma_+(Q(Q+1),L)\|X\|.
}
\tag{5.6}
\]
The same bound holds at every matrix size.

### Proof

Apply inherited P40 to each Fourier coefficient:

\[
\|(x_k)_L\|
\le
\Gamma_+(S_Q,L)\|x_k\|.
\]
Since `t^k` is unitary,

\[
\|X_L\|
\le
\sum_{|k|\le Q}\|(x_k)_L\|
\le
(2Q+1)\Gamma_+(S_Q,L)\|X\|.
\]
All ingredients are completely bounded uniformly in the matrix size: the conditional expectation is cpc, P40 is cb, and the final finite triangle inequality does not depend on matrix size. QED.

### What this proof deliberately avoids

The regular crossed-product matrix for `X` has entries involving

\[
\alpha^{-i}(x_{j-i})

for unbounded `i`. Because

\[

\]
there is no fixed coefficient radius on that infinite matrix. Thus the periodic-monodromy tensor argument cannot simply be copied. The finite Fourier decomposition above is the replacement.

# 8. Stable inner rank is enough: pseudo-Sylvester quotient monotonicity

Let `R` be a pseudo-Sylvester domain. Write

\[
\rho_R^*(A)
\]
for stable inner rank.

By stable-rank preservation in the universal division ring `D_R`,

\[
\operatorname{rk}_{D_R}(A)=\rho_R^*(A).
\tag{8.1}
\]

By eventual stabilization of inner rank, there exists `s>=0` such that

\[
\rho_R(A\oplus I_s)
=
\rho_R^*(A)+s.
\tag{8.2}
\]
Hence there are finite matrices over `R`

\[
U\in M_{m+s,r+s}(R),
\qquad
V\in M_{r+s,n+s}(R),
\]
where

\[
r=\rho_R^*(A),
