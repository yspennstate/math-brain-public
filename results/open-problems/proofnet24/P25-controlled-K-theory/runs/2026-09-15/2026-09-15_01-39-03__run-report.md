# 3. Proof of `P25-SECONDARY-TRACE-SMITH-10`

Because \(n\) is even, equivariant excision and Bott periodicity give

\[
K_1^G(X^{(n)},X^{(n-1)})
=
0
\]

\[

\[

\to
A_{n-1}
\stackrel i\to
A_n
\stackrel\pi\to
C_n
\to
K_1^G(X^{(n-1)}).
\]

Its image in \(C_n\) is exactly

\[
Z_n.
\]

Hence `(S1)`.

Since \(Z_n\) is a subgroup of the free abelian group \(C_n\), it is free.

Let \(z_j\) be a basis and \(\widetilde z_j\) chosen lifts.

If \(\widetilde z_j'\) is another lift, then

\[
\widetilde z_j'-\widetilde z_j
=
i(a)
\]

for some \(a\in A_{n-1}\).

Naturality of assembly gives

\[
\lambda_n(i(a))
=
\lambda_{n-1}(a)
\in
\frac1D\mathbb Z.

Therefore

\[
D\lambda_n(\widetilde z_j')
-
D\lambda_n(\widetilde z_j)
\in
\mathbb Z.
\]

Thus `(S3)` is lift-independent.

By definition, P01's secondary trace map is

\[
z
\mapsto
\lambda_n(\widetilde z)
\mod
\frac1D\mathbb Z.
\]

Multiplication by \(D\) identifies its image with the subgroup of

\[
\mathbb Q/\mathbb Z
\]

generated by the classes

\[
\beta_j+\mathbb Z.
\]

An element

\[
\beta+\mathbb Z
\]

of \(\mathbb Q/\mathbb Z\) has order exactly

\[
\operatorname{den}(\beta).
\]

Every finite subgroup of \(\mathbb Q/\mathbb Z\) is cyclic.

The subgroup generated by finitely many elements of orders \(d_j\) therefore has order

\[
\operatorname{lcm}_j d_j.
\]

Hence `(S5)`.

The \(p\)-valuation formula `(S6)` follows because

\[
v_p(\operatorname{den}\beta)

[-v_p(\beta)]_+.

g=\gcd(B,v_1,\ldots,v_k),

and that element has order

\[
B/g.

This proves `(S7)`.

QED.
