# 2. P25-CYCLIC-NORMING-16 — PROVED

For a scalar trigonometric polynomial \(T\) of degree at most \(r\), Bernstein's inequality gives
\[
\|T'\|_\infty\le r\|T\|_\infty.
\]
Applying this to \(\langle F(\theta)\xi,\eta\rangle\) gives the matrix version
\[
\|F'\|_\infty\le r\|F\|_\infty.

Every circle point is within angular distance \(\pi/L\) of an \(L\)-th root. Hence, if \(\pi r<L\),
\[
\boxed{

\tag{2.1}

The reverse inequality is automatic.
