# 7. Exact Smith refinement of the prime local block

The preceding P25 report proved only the total torsion order of the prime representation-sphere block and left its full invariant-factor pattern conditional. This run closes that gap.

Let

\[
H=C_p,
\qquad

\qquad

\]

and write

\[
R=R(C_p)=\mathbb Z[t]/(t^p-1),
\]

\[
e_W=\prod_{j=1}^{m}(1-t^{-j}).
\]

The previous theorem gives

\[
K_1^{C_p}(S(W))\cong R/e_WR.
\]

## 7.1 Cyclotomic pullback for the representation ring

Let `zeta` be a primitive `p`-th root of unity,

\[
\mathcal O=\mathbb Z[\zeta],
\qquad
\pi=1-\zeta.
\]

The map

\[
R\longrightarrow \mathbb Z\times\mathcal O,
\qquad
f\longmapsto(f(1),f(\zeta))
\]

identifies `R` with the fiber product

\[
\boxed{
R
\cong
\{(a,b)\in\mathbb Z\times\mathcal O:
 a\bmod p=b\bmod\pi\}.
}
\tag{7.1}
\]

Indeed the congruence is immediate from `zeta=1 mod pi`. Conversely, if `(a,b)` satisfies the congruence, choose `g in Z[t]` with `g(zeta)=b`. Then `a-g(1)` is divisible by `p`; adding the corresponding multiple of

\[
\Phi_p(t)=1+t+\cdots+t^{p-1}
\]

changes the value at `1` by a multiple of `p` and leaves the value at `zeta` unchanged.

Let `I=ker(aug:R->Z)` be the augmentation ideal. From `(7.1)`, evaluation at `zeta` gives an isomorphism

\[
\boxed{I\cong \pi\mathcal O.}
\tag{7.2}
\]

Injectivity holds because the kernel of evaluation at `zeta` inside `R` is generated by `Phi_p`, whose nonzero multiples have nonzero augmentation. Surjectivity follows by subtracting a suitable multiple of `Phi_p` to correct the value at `1` to zero.

## 7.2 The Euler ideal inside the augmentation ideal

At `zeta`,

\[
e_W(\zeta)
=\prod_{j=1}^{m}(1-\zeta^{-j}).
\]

For every `j not congruent 0 mod p`,

\[
\frac{1-\zeta^j}{1-\zeta}
\]

is an algebraic unit: it is an algebraic integer, and the product of all its conjugates is `1`, because multiplication by `j` permutes the nonzero residues modulo `p`.

Hence

\[
\boxed{e_W(\zeta)=u\pi^m}
\tag{7.3}
\]

for a unit `u in O^*`.

Since evaluation `R->O` is surjective and `e_WR subset I`, `(7.2)` gives

\[
\boxed{
I/e_WR
\cong
\pi\mathcal O/\pi^m\mathcal O.
}
\tag{7.4}
\]

The augmentation splits `R/e_WR` by the class of `1`, so

\[
R/e_WR
\cong
\mathbb Z\oplus(I/e_WR).
\tag{7.5}
\]

## 7.3 The torsion is elementary `p`-torsion

Because

\[
\mathcal O/\pi\mathcal O\cong\mathbb F_p,
\]

the filtration

\[
\pi\mathcal O\supset\pi^2\mathcal O\supset\cdots\supset\pi^m\mathcal O
\]

has `m-1` successive quotients, each isomorphic to `F_p`.

Moreover

\[
p=\prod_{j=1}^{p-1}(1-\zeta^j)
=u_0\pi^{p-1}
\]

for a unit `u_0`. Therefore

\[
p\,\pi\mathcal O
\subseteq
\pi^p\mathcal O
\subseteq
\pi^m\mathcal O,
\]

so the quotient in `(7.4)` is killed by `p`.

It follows that

\[
\boxed{
\pi\mathcal O/\pi^m\mathcal O
\cong
(\mathbb Z/p)^{m-1}.
}
\tag{7.6}
\]

Thus:

## `P25-PRIME-ORBIT-SLICE-SMITH-21` — PROVED

For every odd prime `p`,

\[
\boxed{
K_1^{C_p}(S(W))
\cong
\mathbb Z
\oplus
(\mathbb Z/p)^{(p-3)/2}.
}
\tag{7.7}
\]

Equivalently, the Smith normal form of multiplication by `e_W` on the rank-`p` lattice `R(C_p)` is

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{diag}
(\underbrace{1,\ldots,1}_{(p+1)/2},
\underbrace{p,\ldots,p}_{(p-3)/2},
0).
}
\tag{7.8}
\]

This upgrades the previous conditional pattern to a theorem.

Exact symbolic Smith checks for

\[

\]

match `(7.8)`.
