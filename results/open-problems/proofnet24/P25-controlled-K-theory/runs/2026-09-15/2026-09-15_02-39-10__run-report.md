## `P25-DUAL-TRACE-COMPRESSION-11` — PROVED

Let
\[
A_n=K_0^G(X^{(n)}),\qquad
T_{n-1}=D^{-1}\mathbb Z,\qquad
T_n=(s_nD)^{-1}\mathbb Z.
\]
Write
\[
A_n/\operatorname{tors}\cong\mathbb Z^r
\]
and let
\[
t_n=(t_1,\ldots,t_r)\in\mathbb Q^{1\times r}
\]
be the canonical trace row in any integral basis. Then
\[
\boxed{
s_n=\operatorname{lcm}_j\operatorname{den}(Dt_j).
}
\tag{1}
\]
Equivalently,
\[
\boxed{
s_n=\min\{s\ge1:sDt_n\in\mathbb Z^{1\times r}\}.
}
\tag{2}
\]
Prime by prime,
\[
\boxed{
v_p(s_n)=\max_j[-v_p(Dt_j)]_+.
}
\tag{3}
\]

### Proof

The trace image is
\[
T_n=\sum_j\mathbb Zt_j=(s_nD)^{-1}\mathbb Z.
\]
Hence
\[
DT_n=s_n^{-1}\mathbb Z.
\]
Modulo \(\mathbb Z\), this is the cyclic subgroup of \(\mathbb Q/\mathbb Z\) of order \(s_n\), generated by \(Dt_j+\mathbb Z\). A rational class modulo \(\mathbb Z\) has order equal to its reduced denominator, so the generated subgroup has order given by (1). QED.

## `P25-DUAL-CHERN-ORDER-11` — PROVED

Choose an integral Chern-data lattice \(\mathcal C_n\cong\mathbb Z^r\). Let
\[
C_n\in GL_r(\mathbb Q)
\]
be the rational Chern matrix from Chern coordinates to the integral free \(K\)-homology coordinates, and let
\[
\ell_n\in\mathbb Q^{1\times r}
\]
be the canonical trace row on Chern coordinates. Then
\[
t_n=\ell_nC_n^{-1}.
\]
Therefore
\[
\boxed{
s_n=\min\{s\ge1:sD\ell_nC_n^{-1}\in\mathbb Z^{1\times r}\}.
}
\tag{4}
\]
Equivalently,
\[
\boxed{
s_n=\min\{s\ge1:sD\ell_n\in\mathbb Z^{1\times r}C_n\}.
}
\tag{5}
\]

Thus \(s_n\) is the order of one dual Chern-lattice class.
