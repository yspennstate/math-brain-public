\[
T_{n-1}

\[

\[

\[

\[

\[
\boxed{

\[
\boxed{

\max_j
\left[-v_p(Dt_j)\right]_+.

This formula uses the full trace row only.

# Proof of `P25-DUAL-TRACE-COMPRESSION-11`

The image of the full trace row is

\[
T_n
=
\sum_{j=1}^r\mathbb Z t_j.
\]

Multiplying by the preceding denominator \(D\),

\[
D T_n
=
\sum_j
\mathbb Z(Dt_j).
\]

Because

\[
T_n
=
\frac1{s_nD}\mathbb Z,
\]

we have

\[
D T_n
=
\frac1{s_n}\mathbb Z.
\]

Modulo \(\mathbb Z\), this is the unique cyclic subgroup of

\[
\mathbb Q/\mathbb Z
\]

of order \(s_n\).

But the same subgroup is generated by the classes

\[
Dt_j+\mathbb Z.
\]

The order of one rational class \(x+\mathbb Z\) is its reduced denominator.

Hence the generated subgroup has order

\[
\operatorname{lcm}_j\operatorname{den}(Dt_j).
\]

This proves (11.1).

Equation (11.2) is the statement that the order of a finite subgroup of \(\mathbb Q/\mathbb Z\) is the least positive integer killing all its generators.

Equation (11.3) is the primewise form.

QED.
