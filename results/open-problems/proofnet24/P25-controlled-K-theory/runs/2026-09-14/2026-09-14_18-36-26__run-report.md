# 15. Full/reduced, norm/strong and stable/unstable distinctions

### Magnetic P17/P18/P25

### P01

The marked groups can be nonamenable. Complete strong convergence compares evaluations of common formal matrices. It does not induce
\[

\]

### Norm versus strong/von Neumann

Every invertibility, spectral cut and controlled \(K\)-class in the new theorems is a norm-\(C^*\) statement.

Von Neumann kernel projections remain relevant to \(L^2\)-traces but are not identified with reduced-\(C^*\) projections without a reduced spectral gap.

### Stable versus unstable

`P25-UE-CONTROLLED-HOMOTOPY-02` transfers a homotopy at its fixed matrix size, hence certainly the stable relation. It does not assert that every stable relation can be realized in one preassigned matrix size.
