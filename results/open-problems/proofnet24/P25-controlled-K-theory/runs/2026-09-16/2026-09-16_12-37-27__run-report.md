## `P25-MARKED-L1-RESIDUAL-TRANSFER-52` — **PROVED**

Let `F=F(S)` be a finitely generated free group and let `π_G:F→G`, `π_H:F→H` be marked quotients. For a finite matrix

\[
X=\sum_w X_w w\in M_n(\mathbb C F)
\]

and a quotient `K`, collect equal quotient words and write

\[
X_K=\sum_{k\in K} A_k^{(K)} k,
\qquad
\|X\|_{1,\mathrm{op};K}:=\sum_k\|A_k^{(K)}\|_{M_n}.
\]

If `prop_F(X)≤r` and `G,H` agree on marked relations through radius `2r`, then

\[
\boxed{
\|X\|_{1,\mathrm{op};G}
=
\|X\|_{1,\mathrm{op};H}.
}
\]

Consequently

\[
\boxed{
\|X_H\|_{M_n(C_r^*(H))}
\le
\|X\|_{1,\mathrm{op};G}.
}
\]

The factor `2` is sharp in general: for `G=C_{2r}`, `H=\mathbb Z`, and `X=a^r-a^{-r}`, the marked groups agree through radius `2r-1`, but the collected residual is `0` in `G` and has coefficient norm `2` in `H`.

## `P25-ROBUST-STRUCTURAL-SOS-KERNEL-PORTABILITY-52` — **PROVED**

Let

\[
p,C,D_1,\ldots,D_m\in M_n(\mathbb C F),
\qquad A=C^*C,
\qquad \delta>0.
\]

Assume in a reference marked quotient `G` that

\[
p_G=p_G^*=p_G^2,
\qquad p_GA_G=0.
\]

Define the finite complement residual

\[
E:=A-\delta(1-p)-\sum_jD_j^*D_j
\]

and suppose

\[
\boxed{
\|E\|_{1,\mathrm{op};G}\le\varepsilon<\delta.
}
\]

Put

\[
r_p=\operatorname{prop}_F(p),
\quad r_C=\operatorname{prop}_F(C),
\quad r_E=\operatorname{prop}_F(E),
\]

\[
W_{\rm rob}
=
\max\{2r_p,\ r_p+2r_C,\ r_E\},
\qquad
R_{\rm rob}=2W_{\rm rob}.
\]

If `H` agrees with `G` on marked relations through `R_rob`, then

\[
p_H=p_H^*=p_H^2,
\qquad p_HA_H=0,
\qquad \|E_H\|\le\varepsilon,
\]

and, with `q_H=1-p_H`,

\[
\boxed{
A_H\ge(\delta-\varepsilon)q_H.
}
\]

Therefore

\[
\boxed{
\mathbf1_{\{0\}}(A_H)=p_H,
\qquad
\sigma(A_H)
\subset
\{0\}\cup[\delta-\varepsilon,M_C],
}
\]

where

\[
M_C=
\left(\sum_w\|C_w\|_{M_n}\right)^2.
\]

Most importantly,

\[
\boxed{\operatorname{prop}(p_H)\le r_p,}
\]

so for every quantitative-K tolerance `0<η_K<1/4`, `p_H` itself is an exact `(η_K,r_p)`-projection representing the stable class `[p_H]∈K_0(C_r^*(H))`. No Chebyshev `\sqrt{M/\delta}` radius appears.

The strict threshold `ε<δ` is sharp: in `M_2(\mathbb C)`, take

\[
p=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&0\end{pmatrix},
\qquad A=0,
\qquad D_j=0.
\]

Then `pA=0` and

\[
E=-\delta(1-p),
\qquad \|E\|=\delta,
\]

but the kernel projection of `A` is `1`, not `p`.

A useful special case is stronger still. If the **formal free-group** coefficient norm already satisfies

\[
\|E\|_{1,\mathrm{op};F}\le\varepsilon<\delta,
\]

then no marked-relation budget is needed to transfer the residual estimate at all; the only local relations needed are those transferring `p^2=p=p^*` and `pA=0`. Thus one may replace `R_rob` by

\[
\boxed{
R_{\rm free}
=2\max\{2r_p,\ r_p+2r_C\}.
}
\]

## `P25-ROBUST-STRUCTURAL-LEFT-INVERSE-PORTABILITY-52` — **PROVED**

Let

\[
p,C,B\in M_n(\mathbb C F),
\qquad A=C^*C,
\]

and assume in `G`

\[
p=p^*=p^2,
\qquad pA=0.
\]

Set

\[
E:=BA-(1-p),
\qquad
\|E\|_{1,\mathrm{op};G}\le\eta<1.
\]

If

\[
H\text{ and }G
\text{ agree through }
R_{\rm inv,rob}
=2\max\{2r_p,\ r_p+2r_C,\ r_E\},
\]

then on `q_H=1-p_H`, for every vector `ξ`,

\[
(1-\eta)\|\xi\|
\le
\|B_HA_H\xi\|
\le
\|B\|_{1,\mathrm{op};F}\,\|A_H\xi\|.
\]

Since `A_H` is positive and self-adjoint,

\[
\boxed{
\mathbf1_{\{0\}}(A_H)=p_H,
\qquad
\sigma(A_H|_{q_H})
\subset
\left[
\frac{1-\eta}{\|B\|_{1,\mathrm{op};F}},\infty
\right].
}
\]

The threshold `η<1` is sharp: at `η=1`, `p=0`, `A=0`, `B=0` has residual `BA-1=-1`, but no complementary gap.

## `P25-LOCAL-RELATIONS-NOT-REDUCED-NORM-UPPER-52` — **PROVED**

There is no theorem of the form “agreement on longer and longer finite relation balls gives upper reduced-norm control.” Let

\[
F_2=\langle a,b\rangle,
\qquad
M=rac14(a+a^{-1}+b+b^{-1}).
\]

The regular representation of `F_2` is the simple random-walk operator on the 4-regular tree, so Kesten's exact computation gives

\[
\boxed{
\|M\|_{C_r^*(F_2)}=\frac{\sqrt3}{2}<1.
}
\]

For every finite radius `R`, residual finiteness of `F_2` gives a finite quotient `Q_R` in which no nontrivial word of length at most `R` dies. Thus `F_2` and `Q_R` have identical marked relations through `R`. But in the finite regular representation the constant vector is an eigenvector of `M_{Q_R}` with eigenvalue `1`, and `M_{Q_R}` is a convex average of unitaries, hence

\[
\boxed{\|M_{Q_R}\|=1.}
\]

So arbitrarily large local-relation agreement leaves a fixed upper reduced-norm gap

\[
1-\frac{\sqrt3}{2}>0.
\]

This counterexample explains why the robust theorem is deliberately formulated with a coefficient-ℓ1 residual, whose norm bound is representation-independent, rather than with an unverified reduced norm transported across marked quotients.

# 3. Proof of marked coefficient-ℓ1 residual transfer

Let

\[
X=\sum_{w\in\Omega}X_w w,
\qquad |w|\le r.
\]

For a quotient `K`, two support words `u,v∈Ω` contribute to the same collected coefficient exactly when

\[
\pi_K(u)=\pi_K(v)
\iff
uv^{-1}\in\ker\pi_K.
\]

Since `|uv^{-1}|≤2r`, agreement of relation kernels through `2r` makes this equivalence relation on `Ω` identical for `G` and `H`. The equivalence classes are therefore the same finite subsets `C⊂Ω`, and their matrix coefficients are exactly

\[
A_C=\sum_{w\in C}X_w
\]

in both quotients. Summing `\|A_C\|` proves equality of the collected coefficient norms.

For every unitary representation `λ_K`,

\[
\left\|\sum_C A_C\otimes\lambda_K(k_C)\right\|
\le
\sum_C\|A_C\|,
\]

which gives the reduced-norm bound.

Sharpness is the P51 relation-length example upgraded from exact vanishing to residual size: in `C_{2r}`, `a^r=a^{-r}`; in `\mathbb Z`, the two words stay distinct. The first new relation has length `2r`.

# 4. Proof of robust SOS kernel portability

The formal propagations satisfy

\[
\operatorname{prop}(p^2-p)\le2r_p,
\]

\[
\operatorname{prop}(pA)\le r_p+2r_C.
\]

Hence relation agreement through `R_rob=2W_rob` is more than enough for P51's exact finite-identity transfer to give

\[
p_H=p_H^*=p_H^2,
\qquad p_HA_H=0.
\]

It is also enough to apply the new residual-transfer lemma to `E`, because `R_rob≥2r_E`, giving

\[
\|E_H\|
\le
\|E\|_{1,\mathrm{op};H}
=
\|E\|_{1,\mathrm{op};G}
\le\varepsilon.
\]

Because `A_H=C_H^*C_H` and `p_H=p_H^*`, taking adjoints of `p_HA_H=0` gives `A_Hp_H=0`. Thus with `q_H=1-p_H`,

\[
A_H=q_HA_Hq_H.
\]

The residual identity is

\[
A_H
=
\delta q_H+\sum_jD_{j,H}^*D_{j,H}+E_H.
\]

The element `E_H` is self-adjoint, since every other term in its definition is self-adjoint. Therefore

\[
E_H\ge-\|E_H\|1\ge-\varepsilon1.
\]

Compressing to `q_H` gives

\[
q_HA_Hq_H
\ge
(\delta-\varepsilon)q_H.
\]

Since `δ-ε>0`, `A_H` is invertible on the `q_H` corner and vanishes on the `p_H` corner. Therefore its Borel zero-spectral projection is exactly `p_H`.

Finally

\[
\|C_H\|
\le
\sum_w\|C_w\|,
\]

so `\|A_H\|≤M_C`. The propagation and quantitative-K statements are immediate because `p_H` itself is a finite group-ring matrix projection.

The finite-dimensional threshold example at `ε=δ` proves that the strict inequality cannot be replaced by `ε≤δ` in general.

# 5. Proof of robust left-inverse portability

Exact projection and annihilation transfer as above. The residual-transfer lemma gives

\[
\|E_H\|\le\eta<1.
\]

For `ξ∈q_H\ell^2(H)^n`, the identity

\[
BA=q+E

becomes

\[
\xi=B_HA_H\xi-E_H\xi.

Thus

\[
(1-\eta)\|\xi\|
\le
\|B_HA_H\xi\|
\le
\|B_H\|\,\|A_H\xi\|.

The universal coefficient estimate

\[
\|B_H\|
\le
\|B\|_{1,\mathrm{op};F}
\]

therefore yields the displayed lower bound. Because `A_H|_{q_H}` is positive self-adjoint, a uniform lower norm bound implies

\[
\inf\sigma(A_H|_{q_H})
\ge
(1-\eta)/\|B\|_{1,\mathrm{op};F}.
\]

Again `A_H` vanishes exactly on the `p_H` corner, so the reduced kernel projection is `p_H`.

The `η=1` example shows sharpness of the strict threshold.

# 6. Counterexample: finite marked locality does not control upper reduced norms

Fix `R`. The set

\[
B_R(F_2)\setminus\{e\}
\]

is finite. Residual finiteness of `F_2` gives, for every nontrivial `w` in this set, a finite quotient in which `w` survives. Taking the product of these finitely many quotients gives one finite quotient `Q_R` in which **all** nontrivial words of length at most `R` survive. Hence `F_2` and `Q_R` agree on marked relations through `R`.

For

\[
M=\tfrac14(a+a^{-1}+b+b^{-1}),
\]

Kesten's free-group calculation gives

\[
\|M\|_{C_r^*(F_2)}=\sqrt3/2.
\]

In the finite quotient, the regular representation contains the constant vector and each group element fixes it, so `M_{Q_R}` has eigenvalue `1`. On the other hand it is an average of four unitaries, so `\|M_{Q_R}\|≤1`; hence equality holds.

This is an adversarial test against an overstrong approximation principle: even exact marked-ball agreement at an arbitrarily large prescribed radius does not imply upper reduced-norm convergence. The robust residual theorem escapes the obstruction because coefficient-ℓ1 control is algebraic and representation-independent.

### Independent derivation

Let `G≤H` and `x∈M_n(\mathbb CG)`.

Choose left coset representatives for `G\backslash H`. Then

\[
\ell^2(H)
\cong
\bigoplus_{G h\in G\backslash H}\ell^2(Gh),
\]

and left multiplication by every element of `G` preserves each summand. After identifying each `\ell^2(Gh)` with `\ell^2(G)`, the operator `λ_H(x)` is a direct sum of copies of `λ_G(x)`. Therefore

\[
\|x\|_{C_r^*(H)}=\|x\|_{C_r^*(G)},
\]

and the same holds for matrices. This proves the canonical reduced subgroup inclusion is isometric.

A unital C*-subalgebra is spectrally invariant in its ambient C*-algebra: if `x-λ` is invertible in the ambient algebra, continuous functional calculus of `(x-λ)^*(x-λ)` gives its inverse in the generated C*-subalgebra. Hence the spectra agree.

For the Borel kernel projection of `x^*x`, the same coset decomposition shows that the projection in `L(H)` is the direct sum of copies of the projection in `L(G)`. If `P_G∈C_r^*(G)`, its canonical inclusion in `C_r^*(H)` is therefore exactly `P_H`.

Conversely, if `P_H∈C_r^*(H)`, apply the canonical subgroup conditional expectation

\[
E_G:C_r^*(H)\to C_r^*(G),
\qquad
E_G\Bigl(\sum_h a_h\lambda_h\Bigr)
=
\sum_{g\in G}a_g\lambda_g.
\]

On the von Neumann level the Fourier support of the direct-sum kernel projection in the `G` corner is precisely `P_G`, so

\[
E_G(P_H)=P_G.
\]

Thus `P_H∈C_r^*(H)` implies `P_G∈C_r^*(G)`.

**Verdict:** PASS exactly as stated. The proof is a **subgroup** argument. It gives no reduced map for a quotient `H→H/N`, and it does not use or imply marked-convergence norm continuity.

1. **Exact algebraic kernel side:** find `p` with `p^2=p=p^*` and `pA=0`, or prove such a finite-support full-kernel projection cannot exist.
2. **Complement side:** certify either a coefficient-ℓ1 SOS residual `<δ` or inverse residual `<1`; do not substitute a reduced-norm estimate unless independently proved in the target representation.
3. **K-theoretic side:** once `p` exists, identify whether `[p]` lies outside the scalar assembly image; finite propagation alone does not provide the assembly obstruction.

For PSZ, P01-P62D settles obligation 1 negatively for the **full** kernel projection in the concrete finitely-presented host. The next useful P25 work should therefore not spend another run optimizing PSZ gap approximants.

# 12. Next concrete attack

The highest-value next P25 target is

\[
\boxed{\texttt{P25-FESHBACH-SPECTRAL-PROJECTION-STABILITY-53}.}
\]

The goal is a quantitative spectral-projection theorem for the practically important intermediate regime where one has a finite-propagation projection `p` and a certified complementary gap, but only **approximate annihilation** `\|pA\|≤α` rather than `pA=0`.

A useful target statement is to assume

\[
p=p^*=p^2,
\qquad
qAq\ge\delta q,
\qquad
\|pAq\|\le\alpha,
\qquad
\|pAp\|\le\beta,
\]

derive an explicit spectral window separated by a Schur/Feshbach estimate, and bound the low-energy spectral projection `P` by an explicit Davis–Kahan/Riesz estimate

\[
\|P-p\|\le F(\alpha,\beta,\delta),
\]

with hypotheses guaranteeing `F<1/2`. That would produce a stable `K_0` identification while keeping finite-propagation `p` as the controlled representative. It is directly relevant to magnetic/topological spectral-projection stability in P17/P18 and to numerically certified P01 candidates, while correctly **not** claiming `p` is the exact kernel projection when annihilation is only approximate.
