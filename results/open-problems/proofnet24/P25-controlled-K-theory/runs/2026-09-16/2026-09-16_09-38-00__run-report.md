# 4. Proof of `P25-SOS-CHEBYSHEV-CONTROLLED-KERNEL-49`

\[

\[
\frac{M+\delta}{M-\delta}

\[
\operatorname{arcosh}\frac{M+\delta}{M-\delta}

\ge2s.

Using `T_n(\cosh u)=\cosh(nu)` and `\cosh y\ge e^y/2`, equations `(4.2)`–`(4.3)` imply

The finite group-ring element `A_H` has propagation at most `R_A`. A degree-`n` polynomial in `A_H` therefore has propagation at most `nR_A`. Hence

\[
\operatorname{prop}(e_{\beta,H})
