# 3. Proof of `P25-MARKED-FINITE-IDENTITY-TRANSFER-51`

Let the finite support of `X` be

\[
S_X=\{w_1,\ldots,w_N\}\subset B_r(F).
\]

In a quotient `K` of `F`, the coefficient at a group element `g\in K` is the matrix sum

\[
\sum_{i:\pi_K(w_i)=g}X_{w_i}.
\]

Thus `X_K=0` is equivalent to saying that each equivalence-class sum vanishes for the partition

\[
w_i\sim_K w_j
\iff
\pi_K(w_i)=\pi_K(w_j).
\tag{3.1}
\]

But

\[
w_i\sim_Kw_j
\iff
w_i^{-1}w_j\in\ker\pi_K,
\]

and

\[
|w_i^{-1}w_j|\le |w_i|+|w_j|\le2r.
\]

If the kernels agree through radius `2r`, the partitions `~_G` and `~_H` are identical. Therefore the same finite matrix coefficient sums vanish in `G` and `H`, proving `(0.2)`.

## Sharpness of the factor `2`

Take the one-generator free group `F=<a>`,

\[
G=C_{2r}=\langle a\mid a^{2r}=1\rangle,
\qquad
H=\mathbb Z=\langle a\rangle.
\]

The shortest nontrivial relation in `G` has free word length `2r`, so the marked kernels agree through `2r-1`. Yet

\[
X=a^r-a^{-r}
\]

has free propagation `r` and satisfies `X_G=0` because `a^r=a^{-r}` in `C_{2r}`, whereas `X_H\ne0`. Hence the radius `2r` in the general finite-identity lemma cannot be reduced uniformly.

## 7.3 Scope boundary

These are certificate **exclusion** results, not proofs that every finite-support projection related to the matrix is impossible. A proper kernel subprojection can exist without controlling the whole complementary spectrum. What is ruled out is exactly the finite structural data strong enough to identify the entire reduced kernel projection and enforce a bounded complement.
