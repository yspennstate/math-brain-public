## 4.1 Height is a proper quotient coordinate

The height homomorphism

\[
\operatorname{ht}:\Gamma\to\mathbb Z,
\qquad
\operatorname{ht}(h t^n)=n,
\]

changes by at most one under multiplication by a standard generator. Extend it affinely over Rips simplices. It is `H`-invariant and therefore descends to

\[
\bar h_D:Q_D\to\mathbb R.
\tag{4.2}
\]

We claim `(4.2)` is proper.

Let `I subset R` be compact. Only finitely many integer heights occur in simplices meeting `bar h_D^{-1}(I)`: every simplex has height diameter at most `D`, so its vertex heights lie in a fixed finite integer interval depending on `I,D`.

At any chosen height `n`, `H` acts transitively on the vertices `h t^n`. Normalize one vertex of a simplex to `t^n` by left multiplication in `H`. Every other vertex then lies in the finite word ball `B_D(t^n)`. Hence there are only finitely many `H`-orbits of simplices whose heights lie in the fixed finite interval.

Therefore `bar h_D^{-1}(I)` is a finite subcomplex of `Q_D`, hence compact. Thus

\[
\boxed{\bar h_D\text{ is proper}.}
\tag{4.3}
\]

This is the control feature that the naive cyclic coordinate projection lacks.

## 4.2 Finite dimension

\[
\dim Q_D\le |B_D(e)|-1<\infty.
\tag{4.4}

This permits a finite-skeleton classifying detector.
