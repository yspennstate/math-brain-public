# C077 — gauge tangent entropy curvature (working checkpoint)

Date: 2026-09-15. This is a bounded creative proof attempt. The general Lyons conjecture remains open.

For diagonal real D, diagonal-unitary invariance gives, at any strict Hermitian contraction K,

    A = i[D,K],  Dp_S(K)[A] = 0,
    D²H(K)[A,A] = DH(K)[D,[D,K]].

Thus the entire Fisher contribution vanishes on gauge tangent directions, and concavity on this family must be certified by the frozen supermodular expectation alone. The investigation seeks a sign and quantitative identity, not merely this orbit identity. A first route is to determine whether every frozen supermodular expectation has nonpositive gauge curvature. This would produce a universal restricted theorem if true; a finite-dimensional obstruction would identify the precise entropy-specific input still needed.
