## Exact new construction

Put \(K_{e,h}=ezz^*+(1-h)ww^*+VMV^*\), \(N=I-M\), \(d=\det M\), \(d_0=\det N\). All atoms except the empty atom, the full atom, and the singleton \(\{4\}\) are positive at \(K_0\). The three singular atoms are denoted \(q_0,q,q_4\).

The decisive identity, valid for every kernel, is

\[
R:=q_0+q_4=\det(I-K)_{\{1,2,3\}}.
\]

Choose a four-by-two isometry \(T\) spanning \(\{w,e_4\}^{\perp}\), and put
\[
F_e=T^*[(1-e)zz^*+VNV^*]T,\qquad D_e=\det F_e.
\]
Then \(R=hD_e\), \(q_0=h(1-e)d_0\), and \(q_4=h[D_e-(1-e)d_0]\). Writing \(s=|z_4|^2\), \(v=V_{4,:}\), and \(g=vN^{-1}Mv^*>0\), cofactor expansion gives
\[
D_e=d_0[(1-e)(1+g)+es],\qquad
q_4=hd_0[(1-e)g+es].
\]

Write an arbitrary affine Hermitian direction in the \([z,w,V]\) frame as
\[
A=\begin{pmatrix}a&u&x^*\\\bar u&b&y^*\\x&y&B\end{pmatrix}.
\]
The logarithmic singularity from \(q_0,q_4\) together is exactly the one belonging to \(R\). Its mixing term involves only
\[
f=T^*Aw=T^*V r,\qquad r=y-\frac{v^*}{\bar z_4}u.
\]
The cancellation uses \(zu+V(v^*/\bar z_4)u=e_4u/\bar z_4\). Consequently its two real null mixing modes are precisely the Hermitian directions generated by \(e_4w^*\); the full atom controls them because \(z_4\ne0\).

The exact grouped entropy Hessian is
\[
H''=Q_{13}-\frac{(q')^2}{q}-q''\log q
-\frac{(R')^2}{R}-R''\log R+C,
\]
where \(Q_{13}\) is the contribution of the thirteen regular atoms. If \(\theta=q_0/R\),
\[
C=-R\frac{(\theta')^2}{\theta(1-\theta)}
-q_0''\log\theta-q_4''\log(1-\theta).
\]
For this family, \(\theta=(1-e)d_0/D_e\) stays strictly between zero and one on compact parameter families. The singular terms \(-b/h\) cancel in the quotient derivative \(\theta'\), leaving it bounded; hence \(C\) is a bounded quadratic form. Its restriction to \(B\) tends to zero.

## Strict active face gap

ROOT_DIAMOND_ACTIVE_MATRIX gives \(T(B)=-\mathrm{FI}(B)+2\det(B)\Lambda\), \(\Lambda\ge0\), for every rank-one background. Here strictness has a particularly direct proof.

Hodge duality gives the active-plane pair masses
\(h_{ij}=|z_4|^2|w_k|^2\) for \(\{i,j,k\}=\{1,2,3\}\). In every C033 inclusion transport the bottom mass at site 4 is zero. Thus the entire mass of addition pair \(ij\subset123\) is forced into top \(123\), with center \(k\). It follows that
\[
\Lambda\ge s\sum_{k=1}^3|w_k|^2
\log\frac{p_{ki}p_{kj}}{p_kp_{123}}.
\]

Condition on site 4 being absent and complement the remaining three occupations. Its kernel is the Schur complement of \((I-K_0)_{44}\) in \(I-K_0\), hence has rank two. Its empty atom and every singleton are positive, so its finite odds matrix \(L\) is positive semidefinite, has rank two, and has positive diagonal. The three displayed gaps are
\(-\log(1-|L_{ij}|^2/(L_{ii}L_{jj}))\).

Normalize \(L\) to diagonal one. Since its rank is two and its trace is three, its squared trace norm gives
\(\sum_{i<j}|L_{ij}|^2/(L_{ii}L_{jj})\ge3/4\). Jensen's inequality therefore proves
\[
\boxed{\Lambda\ge3s\min_{k\le3}|w_k|^2\log(4/3)>0.}
\]
With \(C_1=8-6\log2\), the active theorem supplies
\[
T(B)\le-\min\{\Lambda,2C_1/(C_1+2\Lambda)\}\|B\|_F^2.
\]

\[
a=\sqrt e\,\alpha,\quad b=\sqrt h\,\beta,\quad

leaving \(B\) unscaled. The grouped determinant jets yield
\[

Here \(T^*V\) is invertible, with squared singular values \(1,s\). All displayed blocks are strictly negative.
