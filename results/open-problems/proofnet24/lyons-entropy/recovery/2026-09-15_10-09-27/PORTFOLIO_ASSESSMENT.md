## What the P22 theorem actually says

For a fixed Gaussian bandwidth, use iid uniform spherical inputs, independent Gaussian label noise, uniformly bounded signals of a fixed maximal harmonic degree, and

`d_l=2^(2^l)`, `n_l=2^(2^(2l))`.

The claimed conclusion is that the realized clean-test conditional excess risk of the actual minimum-RKHS-norm interpolant converges to zero in probability. The dimension is subpolynomial in sample size. The construction deliberately keeps the special sequence and signal restrictions.

The relevant external paper, [Medvedev–Vardi–Srebro, Overfitting Behaviour of Gaussian Kernel Ridgeless Regression](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.03891), gives related predictions under a Gaussian-universality ansatz. Thus removing that ansatz for the stated actual model is meaningful if the full candidate proof survives verification. The source does not establish priority for the repository's exact result; this recovery did not perform an exhaustive novelty audit.

The P22 report dated 12:27:02 and P04 report dated 12:44:48 both keep T2 candidate-complete. P22 explicitly says that the shared protocol's two other-worker complete end-to-end audits have not been established by its repeated component/recheck records. This qualification must be retained. T4, the dependent quadratic-feature hard-edge problem, remains a separate open target; the T2 proof does not solve it.

## A resource plan with a meaningful stopping point

For Lyons, fund a concrete deliverable first: a complete unrestricted three-site proof or an exact feasible counterexample, with a reproducible certificate where computation is used. The existing Schur/Fisher criterion, eight-atom chart, phase counterexamples, boundary normal forms, and finite replica method make this a well-defined task.

In parallel with mathematical construction, recover the final terminal outputs of C076–C081 and distinguish them from the checkpoint drafts. Use the current source files and the execution ledger; this recovery did not inspect their live states.

If three-site concavity closes, require an explicit theorem explaining how the argument extends in dimension. The unresolved arbitrary projection diamond, arbitrary background mixing, and conditional-Hessian remainder are separate obstacles. Restricted theorems in arbitrarily large dimensions do not automatically supply that extension.

For P22, the next deliverable can instead be a self-contained manuscript and two complete independent implication-chain checks, with every growing-degree estimate, decoupling hypothesis, normalization, signal assumption, and limit visible. This has a more concrete finishing path than the universal Lyons conjecture, although the theorem's recognition and scope are different.
