# X038 — a deleted-loop bound proves phase damping on every balanced unicyclic graph

Status: COMPLETE bounded theorem and exact deterministic verifier, 2026-09-15.
General Lyons concavity and arbitrary affine cycle-edge motion remain open.

## 1. The theorem and its precise scope

Let G be a connected unicyclic graph, with unique simple cycle C of length
m>=3 and arbitrary trees attached to its vertices. In the measured basis let
K=I/2+B be a Hermitian positive contraction supported on G. All edge
magnitudes and the complex phase around C are unrestricted by the theorem.
For a supermodular table f on {0,1}^V write F_f(J)=E_(P_J) f(X).

**Theorem.** At every strict base and every real diagonal D,

    D²F_f(K)[i[D,K],i[D,K]] <= 0.                              (1)

For arbitrary vectors y_i in R^d, define the Gaussian phase-damping flow

    (K_t)_ij = exp(-t ||y_i-y_j||²) K_ij,     t>=0.            (2)

Then F_f(K_t) is nondecreasing for every supermodular f, and the measured
entropy H(P_(K_t)) is nondecreasing. Boundary kernels are included. The exact
nonnegative production decomposition is (9) below. Forests follow directly
from the bridge terms, and entropy additivity gives direct sums of these
classes. The trees may have arbitrary size, depth, and edge strengths; their
entries need not remain fixed under (2).

This gives longer cycles and coupled tree reservoirs beyond X025's balanced
four-cycle result. It is a gauge-Hessian and damping theorem, not concavity in
arbitrary affine edge directions. No arbitrary measured-basis unitary change,
positive-real-cycle hypothesis, or small-coupling assumption is used.

## 2. The deleted-loop ratio lemma

**Lemma.** Let K_R be any strict balanced forest kernel, U⊆R, and p_R,p_U its
atom and marginal-atom tables. For every occupation configuration s on R,

    p_R(s)/p_U(s_U) >= det K_R / det K_U.                       (3)

Empty determinants and empty atom probabilities are one. This is a lower
bound on every conditional atom, not an assertion of entrywise inverse signs.

**Proof.** A diagonal unitary gauges every forest edge of 2B_R to a nonnegative
real number; denote this adjacency matrix by C_R and its compression by C_U.
The forest is bipartite. Its centered spectrum is symmetric, so strict
contraction implies ||C_R||<1. Let S_R=diag(2s_i-1), and S_U its compression.
The signed atom determinant gives

    p_R(s)=2^(-|R|) det(I+S_R C_R).

The trace-log expansion is absolutely convergent since ||S_R C_R||<1.
Only even closed walks occur, and therefore

    log[p_R(s)/p_U(s_U)]
      =-(|R|-|U|)log 2
       -sum_(k>=1) [tr((S_R C_R)^(2k))-tr((S_U C_U)^(2k))]/(2k).

Subtracting the two traces removes precisely the closed walks contained in
U. Each remaining signed walk has weight at most its unsigned nonnegative
weight. Replacing all those signs by +1 consequently lowers this logarithm.
The resulting expression is

    -(|R|-|U|)log 2 + log det(I-C_R)-log det(I-C_U)
       = log(det K_R/det K_U).

The last equality uses bipartite conjugation C↦-C. This proves (3). All series
manipulations were within an absolutely convergent forest expansion. In
particular no unsigned-walk convergence on the full cyclic graph is assumed.

## 3. Exact frozen curvature, including the acceleration

Orient C cyclically. Put U=V\C, M=det K_U>0, r_e=|K_e|²,
Q=product_(e in C) K_e, q=Re Q, and x_e=d_i-d_j on an oriented edge.
For R_e=V\endpoints(e) define

    h_e(T)=Delta_i Delta_j f(T) >=0,
    W_e=sum_T p_(R_e)(T) h_e(T),
    Psi=sum_(T on U) p_U(T) Delta_C f(T).                       (4)

Every difference Delta_i is occupied value minus unoccupied value. The
function f is fixed throughout these derivatives; no odds path is substituted.

**Exact identity.** For A=i[D,K],

    P_f(D):=-D²F_f(K)[A,A]
      =2 sum_(all e) r_e W_e x_e²
       +2(-1)^m Psi q sum_(e in C) x_e².                       (5)

To prove it, expand inclusion determinants into permutation cycles and use
F_f(J)=sum_T (Delta_T f)(0) det J_T. A transposition on e has weight -r_e;
formally differentiating its weight gives partial_(r_e)F_f=-W_e. The two
orientations of C give partial_q F_f=2(-1)^(m-1)Psi, with the complementary
permutations summing to the marginal p_U. These account for all permutations
of a unicyclic graph, including a cycle and disjoint outside transpositions.
On the affine line K+tA,

    r'_e=0,  r''_e=2r_e x_e²,
    Q'=iQ sum_C x_e=0,  Q''=Q sum_C x_e².

The last equality uses sum_C x_e=0. Substitution proves (5) and retains the
full nonzero atom acceleration. Every atom first derivative is zero because
the direction is tangent to diagonal-unitary conjugation.

For a cycle edge e, R_e is a forest and U⊆R_e. Apply (3) and then the triangle
inequality to the alternating sum Delta_C f=Delta_(C\endpoints(e))h_e. This gives

    W_e >= (det K_(R_e)/M) sum_(T on U) p_U(T)|Delta_C f(T)|
        >= (|Psi|/M) det K_(R_e).                              (6)

The first inequality uses h_e>=0 before taking the alternating sum. It is
the needed bound on the frozen logarithmic loop coefficient with reservoirs.

## 4. A feasible phase comparison supplies the compensating square

Choose Khat with the same diagonal, magnitudes, and tree entries as K, and
cycle real part qhat=(-1)^(m-1)|q|. It suffices to multiply one cycle edge
by -1 if the real part has the wrong sign; otherwise leave K unchanged.

**Khat is a strict contraction.** Every principal subgraph omitting a cycle
vertex is a forest and its determinant is unchanged. If T contains C, write

    det K_T = b_T+2(-1)^(m-1)q det K_(T\C),
    det(I-K)_T = b_T+2(-1)^(m-1)(-1)^m q det K_(T\C).           (7)

Here b_T is the matching contribution; the forest determinant on T\C is
positive and unchanged. Balanced diagonals make the two matching terms equal.
For even m, qhat=-|q| increases both determinants in (7). For odd m, changing
the sign of q interchanges the two determinants for every T. Thus every
principal minor of Khat and I-Khat stays positive, proving the assertion.

Extend the cycle values of d constantly through each attached tree and call
the resulting diagonal Dhat. Then Ahat=i[Dhat,Khat] has only cycle edges.
The determinant gauge score tr(Khat^-1 Ahat) vanishes. Its full second
derivative, computed both by (5) for f=product_i X_i and by determinant
differentiation, gives

    2 sum_(e in C) [r_e det K_(R_e)-M|q|] x_e²
      =det Khat ||Khat^(-1/2) Ahat Khat^(-1/2)||F² >=0.          (8)

The edge coefficients on the left may be negative. The comparison controls
their combined cycle quadratic form, so it does not assume the false claim
that entropy decreases with every individual edge radius.

Set lambda=|Psi|/M and, for cycle edges, delta_e=W_e-lambda det K_(R_e)>=0.
Also put eta=(-1)^m Psi q+|Psi||q|>=0. Equations (5)-(8) yield the exact identity

    P_f(D)=lambda det Khat ||Khat^(-1/2) Ahat Khat^(-1/2)||F²
          +2 sum_(e in C) r_e delta_e x_e²
          +2 eta sum_(e in C) x_e²
          +2 sum_(bridges e) r_e W_e x_e² >=0.                  (9)

This proves (1), including arbitrary tree gauge motion and either sign of
Psi or q. The only loop interaction has been paid without discarding it.

## 5. From gauge curvature to global measured entropy production

For strict K, freeze f_K(S)=-log p_S(K). It is supermodular: writing
L=K(I-K)^-1>0 gives p_S=det(I-K)det L_S. The two-coordinate Schur complement
of L proves p_Ti p_Tj>=p_T p_Tij, which is exactly Delta_i Delta_j f_K>=0.
The full entropy identity is H''[A,A]=-sum_S(p'_S)²/p_S+D²F_(f_K)[A,A].
For A=i[D,K] the first term is zero, so (9) is exactly -H''[A,A].

Put D_alpha=diag(y_i,alpha). Gaussian averaging of diagonal unitaries gives
(2), hence preserves both contraction bounds and the support graph. Its
generator is dot K_t=-sum_alpha[D_alpha,[D_alpha,K_t]]. Twice differentiating
the constant gauge-orbit entropy therefore gives the exact production law

    dH(P_(K_t))/dt=sum_alpha P_(f_(K_t))(D_alpha) >=0,            (10)

where every summand has decomposition (9) at the current kernel. The same
argument with a fixed supermodular f gives dF_f(K_t)/dt>=0. Integrating proves
both monotonicity statements. Finally apply the strict proof to
I/2+(1-epsilon)(K-I/2) and let epsilon decrease to zero. Finite atom-polynomial
and x log x continuity give the boundary assertion.
