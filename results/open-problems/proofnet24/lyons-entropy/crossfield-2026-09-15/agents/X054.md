## 1. Domain and exact target

Write the positive atoms as `p0,p1,p2,p3,p12,p13,p23,p123`, with sum one. Put

    g_ij=p_i p_j-p0 p_ij,
    C=[p0^2 p123+2p1p2p3-p0(p1 p23+p2 p13+p3 p12)]/2.

The exact complex-Hermitian feasibility conditions are

    g_ij>=0,                 C^2<=g12 g13 g23.             (1)

Here is the algebraic justification, recalled from C013. The positive odds
matrix has `Lii=p_i/p0`, `|Lij|^2=g_ij/p0^2`, and real cycle product
`Re(L12 L23 L31)=C/p0^3`. Condition (1) permits precisely these edge phases.
All principal determinants are the prescribed positive `p_S/p0`; hence `L>0`.
Also `det(I+L)=sum_S det L_S=1/p0`, so `K=L(I+L)^(-1)` has these exact atoms.
Necessity follows from the same formulas. A vanishing edge forces `C=0`.
The feasible domain will not be replaced by the weaker six face inequalities.

For complementary `{i,j,k}={1,2,3}`, define

    c_k=log[p_i p_j/(p0 p_ij)],
    tau=log[p0 p12 p13 p23/(p1 p2 p3 p123)],
    M=diag(c_k)+tau K.

Both `c_k` and `c_k+tau` are nonnegative conditional face log ratios. With
`LM(a,b)=(a-b)/(log a-log b)`, the exact coefficients are

    c_k=g_ij/LM(p_i p_j,p0 p_ij),
    c_k+tau=(p_ki p_kj-p_k p123)/LM(p_ki p_kj,p_k p123). (2)

The equality case uses the continuous logarithmic mean. All directions below
are Hermitian affine velocities `A` of the original measured kernel. Write
`q_S=Dp_S[A]`, `s_S=q_S/p_S`, and `FI=sum p_S s_S^2`. The complete identity is

    -H''_K(A)=FI-2 tr(M adj A).                           (3)

Boolean moment inversion proves it: singleton acceleration is zero, pair
acceleration is `2 det A_ij`, and full-atom acceleration is `2tr(K adj A)`.
Normalization and the signed atom acceleration are retained throughout.

## 2. Sharp three-category Fisher inequality on two sites

Let `0<J<I_2`, with empty, two mixed, and full masses `x,y,z,w`.
Put `u=y+z=1-x-w`, `r=sqrt(xw)`, and `Delta=u^2-4xw>=0`. For a Hermitian
velocity `E`, aggregate the two mixed outcomes and retain their Fisher term:

    C_J(E)=x'^2/x+w'^2/w+(x'+w')^2/u.

Thus `C_J` is the Fisher information of the actual count in this two-site law.
Define the explicit coefficient

    eta(J)=[1+sqrt(1+u Delta/(xw))]/u.                    (4)

**Theorem 1.** For every Hermitian `E`, including indefinite velocities,

    C_J(E)=2 eta(J) det E+rho_count,J(E),
    rho_count,J(E)>=0.                                  (5)

The coefficient `2 eta(J)` is the largest possible determinant coefficient.

**Proof.** Diagonalize `J` for this two-site calculation only, with eigenvalues
`lambda,mu`. This preserves the count, `x,w`, and `det E`; it is not a change
of the measured entropy problem. Write `E=[[b1,h],[conj(h),b2]]` in this basis.
The empty/full/middle derivatives are respectively

    -(1-mu)b1-(1-lambda)b2,
    mu b1+lambda b2,
    (1-2mu)b1+(1-2lambda)b2.

Consequently `C_J=G11 b1^2+2G12 b1b2+G22 b2^2`, where

    G11=(1-mu)^2/x+mu^2/w+(1-2mu)^2/u,
    G22=(1-lambda)^2/x+lambda^2/w+(1-2lambda)^2/u,
    G12=1/u,       G11 G22=1/u^2+Delta/(xwu).

These are direct expansions; `Delta=(lambda-mu)^2`. Therefore
`eta=G12+sqrt(G11 G22)`, and the exact remainder is

    rho_count,J(E)=(sqrt(G11)b1-sqrt(G22)b2)^2
                       +2 eta |h|^2.                  (6)

Choosing `h=0` and `b1/b2=sqrt(G22/G11)>0` proves sharpness. At repeated
eigenvalues the same identity holds in any chosen eigenbasis. This proves (5).

The coefficient improves X015's endpoint coefficient

    omega(J)=2+u/r,             eta(J)>=omega(J),         (7)

with equality exactly when `x=w`. Put `T=1+u Delta/r^2` and
`R=2u+u^2/r-1`. The following probability-sensitive identities prove this:

    T-R^2=u^2(2r+u)(1-u-2r)/r^2,
    eta-omega=u(2r+u)(sqrt(x)-sqrt(w))^2
                       /[r^2(sqrt(T)+R)].              (8)

The denominator is positive: if `x!=w`, the first line gives
`sqrt(T)>|R|`; if `x=w`, use `u>=2r` and `u+2r=1` to get `R>0`.
For `lambda_face=log(yz/(xw))`, arithmetic–geometric mean further gives
`omega>=2+2exp(lambda_face/2)`. Thus (4) strictly improves X015's
logarithmic majorant whenever the conditional empty/full masses differ.

## 3. Six actual conditionals and the two triangle remainders

At pivot `k`, write `a=Kkk,v=K_Rk,d=Akk,wvec=A_Rk`.
For branch `s`, put `r_s=a-(1-s)` and `pi_1=a,pi_0=1-a`. The actual velocity is

    J_s=K_RR-vv*/r_s,
    E_s=A_RR-(wvec v*+v wvec*)/r_s+d vv*/r_s^2.          (9)

The moving denominator is included. With `g_k1=K e_k,g_k0=(I-K)e_k`, define

    D=sum_k Akk^2/[Kkk(1-Kkk)],
    Ccount=sum_ks pi_ks C_Jks(E_ks),
    Ncount=sum_ks [eta(J_ks)/pi_ks] g_ks g_ks*,
    Rcount=sum_ks pi_ks rho_count,Jks(E_ks).

X015's compression identity `det E_ks=g_ks*(adj A)g_ks/pi_ks^2` and (5) give

    Ccount=2 tr(Ncount adj A)+Rcount.                   (10)

For two positive mixed masses the elementary identity

    y'^2/y+z'^2/z=(y'+z')^2/(y+z)
                     +yz/(y+z)(y'/y-z'/z)^2

shows exactly what aggregation discards. Pivot scores cancel in the contrast.
Take one triangle on `{1},{2},{3}` and one on `{12},{13},{23}`, and sum over
their six unordered edges:

    Wtriangle=sum_edges p_S p_T/(p_S+p_T) (s_S-s_T)^2.

**Theorem 2 (complete score identity).** At every feasible strict three-site base,

    3 FI=D+Ccount+Wtriangle,                            (11)
    3(-H'')=D+2tr((Ncount-3M)adj A)+Rcount+Wtriangle.    (12)

Indeed, apply the conditional Fisher chain rule at each pivot and the preceding
two-mass identity. Each singleton pair occurs once, at its missing pivot's absent
branch; each doubleton pair occurs once, at its common pivot's occupied branch.
This proves (11); equations (3) and (10) then prove (12).
Equivalently, `D+Ccount=sum_k FI(N,Xk)`, with `N=|X|`; changing pivot
probabilities are included. No independent atom tangents are substituted.

Thus `D+Ccount>=6tr(M adj A)` is a sufficient full-direction test.
It is pointwise at least as strong as X021's endpoint test, since the added terms
are `sum_ks pi_ks(x'_ks+w'_ks)^2/(y_ks+z_ks)>=0`. Also `Ncount>=N`, so
X015's logarithmic matrix comparison strengthens to

    Ncount>=3.5M+sigma I+(Ncount-N),
    sigma=9-7log(3.5)>0,           Ncount-N>=0.           (13)

The gain is explicit in (8). Pairing (13) with an indefinite adjugate is still
invalid; neither (11) nor (13) proves the sufficient test universally.

## 4. Exact obstruction to separating the three logarithmic payments

`d_k+Ccount,k>=6tr(M_k adj A)` separately. This fails on feasible atoms. Take

    K=[[1/5,1/10,i/20],[1/10,3/5,1/10],[-i/20,1/10,4/5]],
    A=[[0,200,799/2-400i],[200,0,i],[799/2+400i,-i,0]].    (14)

The mask-order atoms are `(106,34,209,51,514,146,771,169)/2000`.

    (adj A)11=-1,       Re(K adj A)11=-151/5,
    c1=log(209/159),    tau=log(34821/35321)<0.

The integral bounds `log(1+t)<t` and `log(1+t)>t/(1+t)` yield

    tr(M_1 adj A)=-c1-(151/5)tau

This disproves the separate-pivot inequality, including its endpoint-only
version. It does not disprove their sum, (12), or entropy concavity. Contributions
