## 1. Statement in the original measured model

Let `V:C^3 -> C^n` be isometric, with nonzero rows
`sqrt(t_i)n_i*`, `||n_i||=1`, and `sum_i t_i P_i=I`, `P_i=n_i n_i*`.
Set

    F_v(A)=sum_i t_i [tr(P_i A)]^2.

Assume the following finite second-moment condition:

    F_v(B)>=(1/8)||B||_F^2 whenever B=B*, tr B=0.       (1)

**Theorem.** For every `0<s<1` and every Hermitian `A`,

    -D^2 H(P_{V(sI)V*})[VAV*,VAV*] >= ||A||_F^2/26.   (2)

The derivative is along the actual affine kernel `V(sI+tA)V*`, with the
original coordinate occupation measurement. No eigenbasis entropy is used.
For each fixed scalar base, the full active Hessian remains negative definite
in a neighborhood by continuity. A claim on the entire nonscalar domain is
not made.

**Robust design corollary.** Let `V_D` be any weighted complex projective
2-design frame in dimension three, meaning

    F_D(A)=[(tr A)^2+tr(A^2)]/4,

and let `V_R` be any Parseval frame in dimension three. For every
`1/2<=theta<=1`, form the stacked measured isometry

    V=[sqrt(theta)V_D ; sqrt(1-theta)V_R].             (3)

Its frame moment is `F_v=theta F_D+(1-theta)F_R`. On traceless matrices this

weight as the design part. It may itself have any finite number of rows,
complex phases, and arbitrarily nearparallel distinct rays. Zero effects
are omitted throughout. Parallel splitting does not affect the conclusion.

## 3. A weaker first-measurement condition forces second-measurement Fisher

The following lemma generalizes the design-specific bound in X062.

**Lemma 1.** Suppose, for some `0<=gamma<=1`,
`F_v(B)>=gamma||B||^2` on traceless Hermitian matrices. Then

    F_w(B)>=gamma(2+gamma)||B||^2/3                 (7)

on that same space.

**Proof.** Since `F_v(I,B)=tr B`, removing the trace gives, for every
Hermitian X,

    F_v(X)>=gamma tr(X^2)+(1-gamma)(tr X)^2/3.       (8)

Put `Q_i=I-P_i` and `B_i=Q_i A Q_i`, viewed on its two-dimensional range.
Let `F_i` be the first-frame measurement form of the projected rank-two
Parseval frame `sqrt(t_j) Q_i n_j`. If
`d_ij=1-|n_i*n_j|^2`, direct normalization gives

    F_i(B_i)=sum_j:d_ij>0 t_j(n_j*B_i n_j)^2/d_ij
             >= F_v(B_i).                           (9)

A parallel omitted row contributes zero on the right. The exact contraction
of X062 is

    sum_i t_i F_i(B_i)=2F_w(A).                      (10)

For a direct check, write `h=tr A`, `x_i=tr(P_i A)`,
`y_ij=tr(P_ij A)`. The unit projected row, `n_i`, and the pair-orthogonal
vector form an orthonormal basis, so its A-measurement is `h-x_i-y_ij`.
Thus the left side is `sum_i!=j r_ij(h-x_i-y_ij)^2`. Expanding and using

    sum_j r_ij=2t_i,
    sum_j r_ij y_ij=t_i(h-x_i),
    sum_i t_i x_i=h

gives (10), including every cross term. Further direct contractions give

    sum_i t_i (tr B_i)^2=h^2+F_v(A),
    sum_i t_i tr(B_i^2)=tr(A^2)+F_v(A).              (11)

Apply (8)-(9) inside (10). The result is

    2F_w(A)>=gamma tr(A^2)+(1-gamma)h^2/3
                       +(1+2gamma)F_v(A)/3.

For `h=0`, substitute `F_v(A)>=gamma||A||^2` to obtain (7). This proves
the lemma without any design assumption on the second measurement. ∎

With `gamma=1/8`, (1) and (7) give, for traceless B,

    FI_s(B)=F_v(B)/s+F_w(B)/q
            >=[1/(8s)+17/(192q)]||B||^2.            (12)

## 4. Sharp payment of the positive traceless-adjugate part

**Lemma 2.** For every traceless Hermitian three-by-three B,

    tr((adj B)_+)<=||B||^2/6.                        (13)

Consequently, for every positive matrix T,

    tr(T adj B)<=lambda_max(T)||B||^2/6.             (14)

**Proof.** Apart from zero eigenvalues and an overall sign, the eigenvalues
of B are `a,b,-a-b`, with `a,b>=0`. Its adjugate has exactly one possible
positive eigenvalue, `ab`; the other two are nonpositive. Also

    ||B||^2=2(a^2+ab+b^2)>=6ab.

This proves (13), including the singular case by the same expression.
Discarding the nonpositive spectral part of `adj B` proves (14), because
T is positive. The constant `1/6` is sharp: take B with eigenvalues
`1,1,-2` and T the unit projection onto its `-2` eigenspace. This is a
three-dimensional matrix inequality, not an entropy measurement rotation. ∎

Apply (14) to the actual positive `T_s` and use (6):

    2tr(T_s adj B)<=[(1+2s/3)L/3]||B||^2.           (15)

In particular no positive matrix comparison is traced against an indefinite
adjugate without explicitly removing its negative part.
