# X055 — complete Hessian transport through one arbitrary external field

15 September 2026. **Completed new full-Hessian class.** One arbitrary positive activity preserves the complete measured-entropy Hessian sign at every equal-strength particle–hole symmetric three-site seed. A matrix inequality extends the result to unequal strengths. The field can approach zero or infinity; no bound on its size or on the seed's distance to the spectral boundary is imposed. General simultaneous external fields, unrestricted three-site concavity, and Lyons's universal conjecture remain open.

The contribution is a finite field polynomial and a concave Schur-complement interpolation. C008 supplies the already-proved center sign for the unequal-strength extension; the equal-strength theorem below includes its own center proof. C013/C045 already cover parts of these field orbits, and C052 covers strong-interaction regions with controlled fields. No historical-priority claim outside the supplied project record is made.

## 1. The completed theorem

Put H(K)=H(P_K), using natural logarithms in the fixed measured basis. Let

    K_b=I/2+i b [[0,1,-1],[-1,0,1],[1,-1,0]],   12 b²<1,
    L_b=K_b(I-K_b)^(-1),   D=diag(d,1,1),   0<d<infinity,
    J_b,d=D^(1/2)L_b D^(1/2)[I+D^(1/2)L_b D^(1/2)]^(-1).

**Theorem 1.** At every J_b,d, for every Hermitian three-by-three V,

    -D²H(J_b,d)[V,V] >= 0.                                      (1)

The inequality is strict for V≠0 when b≠0. Coordinate permutations, diagonal unitary conjugation, and conjugation preserve the conclusion. Thus any one of the three activities can vary independently over its entire positive range. The theorem concerns all nine affine Hermitian directions at each base, including directions leaving the displayed family. It does not assert that arbitrary chords between points of this nonlinear family stay in a certified region.

Here is the larger, computable version. For a strict PH seed K=I/2+iB, B real skew, define complementary atom masses and coefficients by

    q0=p_empty=p_123,   qi=p_i=p_jk,
    c_i=log(q_j q_k/(q0 q_i)),   C=diag(c_1,c_2,c_3).             (2)

Choose pivot i, let v=B e_i, and suppose

    C-c_i e_i e_i* -4c_i v v* >= 0.                            (3)

**Theorem 2.** Every positive change of activity at pivot i has the complete Hessian sign (1). If the seed is connected, the sign is strict. Condition (3) is only a two-by-two real matrix test on the coordinates other than i. When c_j,c_k>0 it is equivalently

    4c_i [B_ji²/c_j+B_ki²/c_k] <= 1.                           (4)

Theorem 2 uses C008's full PH-center theorem. For Theorem 1, all c_i coincide, and (3) follows from 4||v||²=8b²<1. Section 5 proves that center directly, so Theorem 1 is self-contained.

## 2. Exact field transport of the full Hessian

For an arbitrary strict three-site K, set p_S=P_K(S), s_S(A)=Dp_S(K)[A]/p_S and

    c_i=log[p_j p_k/(p_empty p_jk)],
    tau=log[p_empty p_12 p_13 p_23/(p_1 p_2 p_3 p_123)].

Inclusion–exclusion and D²det K[A,A]=2tr(K adj A) give the complete identity

    -H''_K(A)=sum_S p_S s_S(A)²-2tr[(C+tau K)adj A].             (5)

Indeed the constant and one-coordinate terms of the Boolean expansion of -log p have zero second expectation derivative; its pair and triple terms give the displayed adjugates. Both parts of the entropy Hessian are retained.

For a positive activity matrix D=diag(d_1,d_2,d_3), put E=D-I and

    Z=det(I+EK),   J=D^(1/2)K(I+EK)^(-1)D^(1/2),
    U=D^(1/2)(I+KE)^(-1),   A_D=UAU*.                         (6)

The tilted atom law is p_D(S)=p_S d^S/Z. Its c_i and tau are unchanged. Differentiating the rational kernel map once shows that A_D is the image of A. Therefore its actual affine score at J is s_S(A)-E_D s(A). Set k_i=Ke_i, h_i=(I-K)e_i. Weighted variance and adjugate congruence yield the exact polynomial

    Q_D(A):=Z²[-H''_J(A_D)]
      =sum_{S<T}p_S p_T d^(S+T)(s_S-s_T)²
       -2sum_i (product_{j!=i}d_j) tr({c_i h_i h_i*
          +d_i(c_i+tau/2)(h_i k_i*+k_i h_i*)
          +d_i²(c_i+tau)k_i k_i*} adj A).                    (7)

For clarity, the frozen congruence is

    det(U)²=det(D)/Z²,
    U^(-1)(C+tau J)U^(-*)
       =(I+KE)CD^(-1)(I+EK)+tau(K+KEK).                    (8)

Expanding columns h_i+d_i k_i and using K-K²=(1/2)sum_i(h_i k_i*+k_i h_i*) proves (7). This is the actual Hessian at J evaluated in A_D, not the second derivative of H along the nonlinear field image of K+tA. The latter would also contain DH(J)[-2UA E(I+KE)^(-1)AU*]; that acceleration has not been substituted for the affine Hessian.

## 3. Two endpoints and a concave Schur complement

Now K=I/2+iB, pivot i=1, D=diag(d,1,1), and tau=0. Split A=R+iF, with R real symmetric and F real skew. The PH involution K→I-conjugate(K) fixes the seed, reverses R, and fixes iF. Complementing atom labels gives, in these two real coordinate spaces,

    Q(d)=Q0+d Q1+d² Q2,
    Q0=[[P,-W],[-W^T,R0]],  Q2=[[P,W],[W^T,R0]],
    Q1=[[S,0],[0,T]].                                      (9)

Here matrices represent quadratic forms; R0 is unrelated to the direction R. Q(1) is the original full Hessian quadratic form, so its diagonal blocks 2P+S and 2R0+T are nonnegative by the PH center sign.

Both Q0 and Q2 are nonnegative. To verify this rather than assume boundary continuity, write a=K_11=1/2, k=K_{2:3,1}, delta=A_11, w=A_{2:3,1}, A0=A_{2:3,2:3}. The actual conditional kernels and velocities are

    J_-=K_{2:3,2:3}+2kk*,  E_-=A0+2(wk*+kw*)+4delta kk*,
    J_+=K_{2:3,2:3}-2kk*,  E_+=A0-2(wk*+kw*)+4delta kk*.     (10)

They are strict two-site contractions. Conditioning the score in (7), and expanding a two-by-two determinant, give exactly

    Q0(A)=(1/4)[-H''_(J_-)(E_-)],
    Q2(A)=(1/4)[-H''_(J_+)(E_+)].                           (11)

For example det E_-=4 h_1*adj(A)h_1 and det E_+=4 k_1*adj(A)k_1. Each conditional log interaction equals c_1. Thus (11) follows directly from (5), without taking the entropy Hessian at a singular three-site kernel.

Here is the needed elementary two-site sign. For a strict two-site kernel with atom table (x,y,z,w), lambda=log(yz/(xw))>=0 and any Hermitian E,

    -H''(E)=FI(E)-2lambda det E >=0.                         (12)

If det E<=0 this is immediate. Otherwise replace E by -E to make it positive. The empty/full Fisher terms, with P0=adj(J)/sqrt(w) and P1=adj(I-J)/sqrt(x), give

    FI(E)>=[tr(P0 E)]²+[tr(P1 E)]²
         >=2det(P0+P1)det E,
    det(P0+P1)=2+(y+z)/sqrt(xw)>=2+2exp(lambda/2)>lambda.

The middle inequality is two-dimensional trace–determinant arithmetic–geometric mean. This proves (12) for every direction, including singular ones.

We use the following finite interpolation lemma. Suppose (9) has Q0,Q2,Q(1)>=0 and T-2R0>=0. Then Q(d)>=0 for every d>0. To prove it, put x=(d-1)/(d+1), t=x². After division by (d+1)², (9) becomes

    [[A0+t A1, x W],[x W^T,C0+t C1]],
    A0=(2P+S)/4, C0=(2R0+T)/4, C1=(2R0-T)/4<=0.             (13)

Its Schur complement is A0+tA1-t W(C0+tC1)^(-1)W^T. This is concave in t∈[0,1]. Indeed, after conjugating by C0^(-1/2), the subtracted function is t(I-tG)^(-1), G>=0; diagonalizing G reduces convexity to the scalar second derivative 2g/(1-tg)^3>=0. The Schur complement is nonnegative at t=0 by Q(1), and at t=1 by Q2. Concavity proves its sign between them. Singular blocks are handled by adding epsilon I to (13), applying the argument, then letting epsilon decrease to zero. If Q(1)>0, the Schur complement at t<1 is at least (1-t)A0>0 and the lower block is at least (1-t)C0>0. This also proves strictness for every finite d>0.

## 4. The precise field condition

For a purely imaginary direction A=iF, its scores agree on complementary atoms. Both conditional score means are zero since A_11=0, and the two conditional Fisher informations agree. Consequently the tilted Fisher information is independent of d and Z² times that Fisher information equals (1+d)² FI_K(A)/4. Its contribution to T-2R0 therefore cancels exactly.

Every real skew three-by-three F has a real axial vector w with adj(iF)=-ww*. Since 2k_1-e_1=2i v for v=B e_1, expansion of the frozen part of (7) gives

    (T-2R0)(iF)=2w*[C-c_1 e_1e_1* -4c_1 vv*]w.             (14)

Condition (3) is exactly what makes (14) nonnegative. Equations (9)–(14), the center sign, and invertibility of U prove Theorem 2. For equal strengths C=cI, the nonzero block in (14) is c(I_2-4v v*), positive because 4||v||²=8b²<1. Thus Theorem 1 follows once its center sign is established.

## 5. A self-contained equal-strength center proof

Put rho=12b², q0=(1-rho)/8, q=(3+rho)/24, and c=log(q/q0)>=0. These are the empty/full and six middle atom masses. For imaginary A, (5) is FI(A)+c||A||_F²>=0. Complement symmetry makes the real and imaginary sectors orthogonal in both the Fisher and frozen forms.

For real symmetric A, set s=tr A, v=(b,b,b), z=v*Av. Differentiating the determinant and the singleton probabilities gives a full-atom derivative s/4-z and singleton derivatives A_ii/2-s/4-z. Cauchy–Schwarz after aggregation by particle count therefore gives

    FI(A)>=2[(s/4-z)²/q0+(-s/4-3z)²/(3q)].                 (15)

For b≠0, write n=v/||v|| and decompose orthogonally

    A=aI+u(3nn*-I)+A_perp,  tr A_perp=n*A_perp n=0.

Then s²-||A||_F²=6(a²-u²)-||A_perp||_F². The right side of (15) is alpha a²+2beta au+gamma u², where, with Delta=(1-rho)(3+rho),

    alpha=(36-12rho²-8rho³)/Delta,
    beta=-16rho³/Delta,  gamma=16rho²(3-2rho)/Delta,
    alpha gamma-beta²=576rho²/Delta,
    alpha-gamma=12L,  L=(3+3rho-2rho²)/(3+rho)>0.            (16)

The full Hessian is bounded below by c||A_perp||² plus the two-by-two quadratic matrix [[alpha-6c,beta],[beta,gamma+6c]]. Its lower diagonal is positive. The elementary inequality log x<=(x-1)/sqrt(x), x>=1, gives c²<=16rho²/(3Delta). Hence its determinant satisfies

    576rho²/Delta+72cL-36c²
        >=384rho²/Delta+72cL>0.                            (17)

This proves strict center positivity for b≠0. At b=0 every field-scaled kernel is diagonal, and directly -H''=sum_i V_ii²/[J_ii(1-J_ii)]>=0. Theorem 1 is proved without an imported center theorem.
