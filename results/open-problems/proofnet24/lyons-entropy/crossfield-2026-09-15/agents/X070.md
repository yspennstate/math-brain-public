# X070 — positive odds shifts, a compensated heat representation, and the CM obstruction

15 September 2026. **Completed exact constructive reduction and rigorous failed-bridge theorem.**
The full measured-entropy curvature has an explicit signed Laplace representation
under additive positive odds shifts, with the physical tangent congruence retained.
Its complete monotonicity fails already on a rational three-site tree, even in a
direction whose entropy curvature is strictly favorable at every positive shift.
An integrated heat-density inequality gives a weaker sufficient replacement.
That inequality, the six-variable three-site comparison, and general Lyons

## 1. The actual shift semigroup and both physical jets

Let `L>0`, `L_s=L+sI`, `T_s=I+L_s`, `R_s=T_s^(-1)`, `K_s=I-R_s`, and fix
an arbitrary Hermitian odds velocity `B`. All principal determinants and Shannon
entropies use the original measured coordinate basis. Put

    A_s=R_s B R_s,   q_B(s)=-D²H(P_(K_s))[A_s,A_s].                (1)

In kernel coordinates the shift is the semigroup

    F_s(K)=I-(I-K)[I+s(I-K)]^(-1),
    DF_s(K)[A]=U_s A U_s,   U_s=[I+s(I-K)]^(-1).

Thus (1) is its true pullback of the affine-kernel Hessian, taking
`B=(I-K)^(-1)A(I-K)^(-1)` at the initial point. The nonlinear odds image
of `L_s+tB` has kernel second derivative `-2R_s B R_s B R_s`.
Conversely the odds image of the **affine physical line** `K_s+tA_s` has

    L'(0)=B,              L''(0)=2 B R_s B.                       (2)

These follow by two differentiations of the inverse matrix; neither acceleration
is dropped. The tangent also moves with the shift:
`A_s'=-R_s A_s-A_s R_s`, while `K_s'=R_s²`.

For any number of sites define

    w_S=det(L_s)_S, Z=det T_s, p_S=w_S/Z,
    b_S=tr((L_s)_S^(-1)B_S),  g=tr(R_s B),
    c_S=tr(((L_s)_S^(-1)B_S)^2),
    d_S=tr((L_s)_S^(-1)(B R_s B)_S), h=tr((R_s B)^2).

Empty-subset traces are zero. Determinant differentiation and (2) give the
**normalized, physical affine** atom jets

    p'_S/p_S=b_S-g,
    p''_S/p_S=(b_S-g)^2-c_S+2d_S-h.                              (3)

Indeed ordinary affine-odds differentiation first gives
`(b_S-g)^2-c_S+h`; the acceleration (2) adds `2d_S-2h`.
Consequently the full exact curvature is

    q_B(s)=Var_p(b)+sum_S p_S[(b_S-g)^2-c_S+2d_S-h]log w_S.        (4)

The normalizer logarithm cancels because the actual second atom jets sum to zero.
Formula (4) retains all atom derivatives, normalization, and physical acceleration.

## 2. A finite three-site normal form

Let `adj B` denote the ordinary three-by-three adjugate. For complementary
indices `{i,j,k}={1,2,3}` set

    c_i(s)=log[w_j w_k/w_jk],
    tau(s)=log[w_12 w_13 w_23/(w_1 w_2 w_3 w_123)],
    C_s=diag(c_i(s)),   G=I+L,  T_s=sI+G.

The frozen Boolean-log identity established in X055 is
`-H''_K(A)=FI_K(A)-2tr[(C+tau K)adj A]`. It also follows directly by
expanding the log atom table in inclusion indicators: singleton acceleration
vanishes, pair acceleration is `2det A_jk`, and triple acceleration is
`2tr(K adj A)`. Adjugate congruence in (1) therefore gives

    q_B(s)=F_B(s)-2tr[N(s)adj B],
    N(s)=[T_s C_s T_s+tau(s)(T_s²-T_s)]/Z(s)²,                   (5)
    F_B(s)=sum_S (D_B w_S)²/[Z w_S]-(D_B Z)²/Z².

Equivalently `Z²F_B=sum_(S<T)w_S w_T(b_S-b_T)²`. This is the complete
measured Fisher information, not a quantum or occupation-only surrogate.

## 3. Explicit heat densities for every logarithmic term

Write `a_i=Lii`; write `lambda_+,lambda_-` for the eigenvalues of `L_jk`.
The elementary identity
`log((s+b)/(s+a))=integral_0^infinity e^(-st)(e^(-at)-e^(-bt))dt/t`
follows by integrating `(s+x)^(-1)` from `a` to `b`. It yields

    c_i(s)=integral_0^infinity e^(-st) k_i(t)dt,
    k_i(t)=[tr exp(-t L_jk)-exp(-t a_j)-exp(-t a_k)]/t >=0,       (6)
    tau(s)=integral_0^infinity e^(-st) k_3(t)dt,
    k_3(t)=[tr exp(-t L)-sum_(i<j)tr exp(-t L_ij)
                         +sum_i exp(-t a_i)]/t.                (7)

The sign in (6) follows from
`tr exp(-t L_jk)=2exp[-t(a_j+a_k)/2]cosh[t sqrt((a_j-a_k)²+4|Ljk|²)/2]`.
There is no general sign assertion for (7). Its first two Taylor orders cancel.
If a diagonal gauge makes all off-diagonal entries of L real nonpositive,
then `k_3>=0`: choose `alpha>=max a_i`, so `D=alpha I-L` is entrywise
nonnegative. Expand `exp(-tL)=exp(-alpha t)sum_m t^m D^m/m!`.
Inclusion–exclusion in (7) retains exactly closed walks visiting all three
vertices, with nonnegative weights. This argument also covers every real tree.

Let `z(t)` be the sixfold convolution of exponentials
`exp[-(1+lambda_r)t]`, each eigenvalue `lambda_r` of L occurring twice.
Thus its Laplace transform is `Z(s)^(-2)`, and `z(0)=z'(0)=0`. Define

    P_i(t)=Eii z''+(G Eii+Eii G)z'+G Eii G z,
    P_3(t)=I z''+(2G-I)z'+(G²-G)z.                              (8)

Ordinary integration by parts shows their transforms are respectively
`T_s Eii T_s/Z²` and `(T_s²-T_s)/Z²`. There are no missing boundary terms;
in fact `z(t)=t^5/120+O(t^6)` at zero. The rational function F_B has poles
only at negative principal eigenvalues of L and at `-1-lambda_r`.
It is proper and tends to zero at infinity. Its inverse Laplace transform
`f_B(t)` is therefore the explicit finite exponential polynomial obtained
by replacing each partial fraction `c/(s+a)^m` with
`c t^(m-1)exp(-at)/(m-1)!`. Coincident poles are handled by multiplicity.

Equations (5)–(8) give the **compensated full-Hessian identity**

    q_B(s)=integral_0^infinity e^(-st) rho_B(t)dt,
    rho_B=f_B-2tr[{sum_i(P_i*k_i)+P_3*k_3}adj B].                (9)

Here `*` means ordinary convolution on the positive half-line. Everything
in (9) is explicitly determined by L and B. All densities are integrable,
with exponential decay. Neither `f_B`, `P_i`, nor `rho_B` is asserted positive.
The signed contributions cannot be separated before integration.

## 4. Complete monotonicity fails for the full physical pullback

Take the rational positive definite tree odds and real vector

    L=[[1/10,9/100,1/100],[9/100,1/10,0],[1/100,0,10]],
    v=(1,-1,-1/1100)^T,
    B=i [[0,-v3,v2],[v3,0,-v1],[-v2,v1,0]].                     (10)

Its leading principal minors are `1/10,19/10000,1899/100000>0`.
Hence every `K_s`, `s>=0`, is a strict physical contraction. Since L is
real and B is imaginary skew, all first atom jets vanish exactly; thus
`F_B(s)=0`. Also `adj B=-vv^T`, so the full curvature is exactly

    q_B(s)=2v^T[T_s C_s T_s+tau(s)(T_s²-T_s)]v/Z(s)².            (11)

This quantity is **strictly positive for every s>=0**. Indeed c_2,c_3>0;
with `x_i=s+a_i`, the tree determinant identity gives
`tau=log[1+|L12|²|L13|²/(x_1 det L_s)]>0`, and `T_s²-T_s>0`.
Thus even all scalar logarithmic densities in (6)–(7) are favorable here.
Nevertheless exact differentiation of (11) gives

    -10 < (-1)^5 q_B^(5)(0) < -7,                              (12)
    numerically: -8.270513442793671564549459720996581268234.

The exact derivative has form `r+alpha log(10000/9999)+beta log(100/19)
+gamma log(21109/21100)`, with rational coefficients preserved in the certificate.
Exact arithmetic gives `-9<r<-8`, `0<alpha<5`, `0<beta<1/10000`,
`-30<gamma<0`. The elementary bounds `0<log x<x-1`, for x>1, prove
`-9-270/21100 < derivative < -8+5/9999+81/190000`, hence (12).
A positive atanh series with its rational geometric tail bounds supplies the
printed decimal; the elementary inequalities already certify the strict sign.
The scalar quantity is analytic around zero, so failure persists for some
strictly positive shifts. Therefore the full tensor pullback (1) is not
completely monotone. By differentiating (9), (12) also proves that its density
rho_B changes sign: the fifth moment of rho_B is negative, while its zeroth
moment is the strictly positive curvature (11). This is an analytic failure
of the stronger transport claim, not an unfavorable entropy direction.

## 5. A weaker positive-shift certificate and the remaining gap

Define the cumulative **full compensated density**

    R_B(t)=integral_0^t rho_B(u)du.                              (13)

One further integration by parts gives, for every s>0,

    q_B(s)=s integral_0^infinity exp(-st) R_B(t)dt,
    q_B(0)=lim_(t->infinity)R_B(t).                              (14)

Consequently `R_B(t)>=0` for all t is a sufficient certificate for favorable
curvature along the entire positive-shift orbit, including its initial base.
A matrix version requires this quadratic form nonnegative for every Hermitian
B at every t. It allows a signed rho_B, unlike the disproved complete-
monotonicity route. It is a concrete sufficient lemma, not a proved universal
property, and no sign transport is inferred merely from a large-shift sign.
For calibration, if L is diagonal then all off-diagonal B terms vanish and

    q_B(s)=sum_i Bii²/[(s+a_i)(s+a_i+1)²],
    rho_B(t)=sum_i Bii² exp(-a_i t)[1-(1+t)exp(-t)]>=0.           (15)

This recovers the elementary diagonal case; it is not claimed as a new domain.
The new obstruction (12) shows why positive pair/connected heat traces alone
cannot close the mixed-direction problem: physical congruence and its matrix
polynomial factors must be compensated together with the entire Fisher density.
A proof of (13)'s matrix sign on a nontrivial new domain remains the precise
constructive target. No claim is made that the witness satisfies that stronger
primitive criterion, nor that the criterion is necessary for Lyons concavity.
