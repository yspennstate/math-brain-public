## 1. New all-dimensional theorem

Let K be a complex Hermitian matrix with

    I/10 <= K <= 9I/10.

For every real diagonal D=diag(v), with arbitrary signs, put

    FI=sum_x (p'_x)^2/p_x,
    chi_i=E_[X_-i] 1/[q_i(X_-i)(1-q_i(X_-i))],
    C=sum_i chi_i v_i^2.

Then the actual affine-kernel entropy Hessian obeys

    -H''(K;D) >= (4/45) C >= (16/45)||v||_2^2,
    -H''(K;D) >= (4/125) FI.                              (1)

In particular its signed second-order score chaos satisfies the strict payment

    -sum_x p''_x log p_x <= (121/125) sum_x (p'_x)^2/p_x.  (2)

No absolute value is placed on the acceleration in (2). For a fixed Hermitian
zero-diagonal B, the measured entropy of B+diag(d) is strongly concave on its
entire convex slice through this slab. For any two kernels there with diagonal
difference, integration gives the finite Jensen lower gap

    H(P_((1-t)K0+tK1))-(1-t)H(P_K0)-tH(P_K1)
        >= (8/45)t(1-t)||diag(K1-K0)||_2^2.               (3)

This expands X057's uniform slab [1/6,5/6] to [1/10,9/10] and strengthens its
explicit uniform lower metric. It covers arbitrary n, complex phases, and
simultaneous mixed-sign diagonal motion. General off-diagonal motion and
unrestricted diagonal motion remain open.

## 2. The new determinant-feasible two-site inequality

Let J be any two-site Hermitian kernel with eigenvalues in [1/10,9/10], and
let (a,b,c,d) be its actual table in order 00,01,10,11. Define

    h=bc-ad=|J12|^2, F=h(1/a+1/b+1/c+1/d),
    lambda=log(bc/ad), Q=F-lambda.

All four probabilities are positive. If h=0 then F=lambda=Q=0. Otherwise,

    39/80 <= lambda/F <= 25/sqrt(369),
    -(25/sqrt(369)-1)F <= Q <= (41/80)F,
    |Q| <= (41/80)F.                                     (4)

The two-sided nonlinear comparison (4) is the new ingredient. The physical
constraint on the complete conditional table, not just negative correlation,
is essential to the proof.

### 2a. Spectral parameterization and a sharp bound on F

Write e1,e2 for the two eigenvalues of I-2J, so |e_i|<=rho=4/5, and put
delta=e1e2 and t=lambda. The total empty/full mass is

    u=a+d=(1+delta)/2.

The exact algebra of the four probabilities gives

    F=sinh(t)+delta[cosh(t)-1].                           (5)

At fixed eigenvalues a,d are fixed, and h ranges from zero to
(e1-e2)^2/16 as the measured basis varies. The singletons satisfy
b+c=1-u and bc=ad+h. Thus

    F=h[u/(ad)+(1-u)/(ad+h)]

increases with h. At maximal h the diagonal entries of J coincide and
b=c=(1-delta)/4. Direct simplification then gives

    F <= (e1-e2)^2/(1-e1e2)
              * [1/(2(1-e1^2))+1/(2(1-e2^2))].           (6)

For -rho<=y<=x<=rho, the ratio (x-y)^2/(1-xy) increases with x and
decreases with y: its derivatives have signs determined respectively by
(x-y)(2-xy-y^2) and -(x-y)(2-xy-x^2). The bracket factors are positive.
Therefore its maximum is 4rho^2/(1+rho^2). The bracket in (6) is at most
1/(1-rho^2). Consequently

    F <= 4rho^2/(1-rho^4)=1600/369.                       (7)

Equality occurs at e1=-e2=rho and equal measured diagonals. Thus (7) is sharp,
although the logarithmic constants in (4) need not be.

### 2b. The high-log regime forces opposite spectral signs

At fixed eigenvalues the largest t occurs at maximal h, and

    exp(-t) >= 1-[(e1-e2)/(1-e1e2)]^2.                    (8)

If delta>=0, the eigenvalues have the same sign. After a common sign change
and interchange, 0<=e2<=e1<=rho, and

    (e1-e2)/(1-e1e2) <= e1 <= rho.

It follows that delta>=0 implies t<=log(25/9)<log 3. Hence

    t>=log 3 implies delta<=0.                           (9)

This sign constraint is what a comparison on arbitrary negatively correlated
binary tables would miss.

### 2c. Three elementary ranges pay the logarithm

The elementary expansion log 3=2 sum_(k>=0) (1/2)^(2k+1)/(2k+1) gives

    log 3 >= 13/12.

For 0<t<=log 3, use delta<=16/25 in (5). The function
[sinh t+(16/25)(cosh t-1)]/t is increasing, as is clear from its integral
representation integral_0^1[cosh(st)+(16/25)sinh(st)]ds. Therefore

    F/t <= (44/25)/log 3 <= 528/325 < 80/39.

For log 3<=t<=log 9, (9) and the monotonicity of sinh(t)/t imply

    F/t <= sinh(log 9)/log 9 =20/(9 log 3) <=80/39.

For t>=log 9, (7) implies

    F/t <= (1600/369)/log 9 <=3200/1599 <80/39.

Thus lambda/F>=39/80. In the other direction differentiate the right side
of (5) with delta fixed. For every s>=0,

    cosh s+delta sinh s >= sqrt(1-delta^2) >=sqrt(369)/25.

The first bound follows either by minimizing the elementary hyperbolic
expression or from (cosh s+delta sinh s)^2
-(sinh s+delta cosh s)^2=1-delta^2. Both first expressions are positive.
Integrating from zero to t gives F/t>=sqrt(369)/25, proving the upper
ratio in (4). Finally sqrt(369)>19 gives
25/sqrt(369)-1<6/19<41/80, completing the absolute bound.

## 3. Proof of the all-dimensional signed chaos payment

Every actual kernel conditioned on any present/absent pattern retains the
same slab. To see this directly, presence conditioning takes a Schur
complement of K. If K>=aI, minimizing its quadratic form over the removed
coordinate proves that this Schur complement is at least aI. Its upper
bound follows from the principal submatrix. Absence conditioning applies
the same argument to I-K. Iteration proves the claim. Thus (4) applies to
every actual two-site conditional required below.

For completeness retain X057's full fixed-basis calculus. With

    Q_x=diag(1-x), R_x=(K-Q_x)^(-1),
    p_x=(-1)^(n-|x|)det(K-Q_x),

diagonal determinant differentiation and normalization yield

    J_ii=E(R_X)_ii^2=chi_i,
    J_ij=E(R_X)_ii(R_X)_jj=E|(R_X)_ij|^2, i!=j,
    FI=C+2 sum_(i<j)v_i v_j J_ij.                         (10)

For fixed outside configuration y, the two-site mixed acceleration is its
marginal mass times (1,-1,-1,1). Its log pairing is minus that mass times
lambda. Summing the four conditional Fisher cross terms gives F, so

    J_ij=E_y F_ij,y,
    -H''=C+2 sum_(i<j)v_i v_j E_y Q_ij,y.                 (11)

The outside marginal is independent of the two diagonal entries being
differentiated. Equations (10)-(11) therefore keep the actual affine path,
the full measured Fisher energy, and every second derivative correction.

The signed-resolvent row budget from X057 also has an elementary proof.
Let E=2K-I, S_x=diag(2x-1), and w=R_x e_i. Since
(S_x+E)w=2e_i, projection away from i gives

    ||w_-i||<=||Ew||<=rho||w||,
    sum_(j!=i)|(R_x)_ij|^2 <= rho^2/(1-rho^2)|(R_x)_ii|^2.

Hermiticity and averaging give

    sum_(j!=i)J_ij <=(16/9)chi_i.                        (12)

Now use (4), (11), and 2|v_i v_j|<=v_i^2+v_j^2:

    -H'' >= C-(41/80)sum_i v_i^2 sum_(j!=i)J_ij
          >=[1-(41/80)(16/9)]C=(4/45)C.                 (13)

The conditional Bernoulli variance bound gives chi_i>=4. Also (10),(12)
give FI<=(1+16/9)C=(25/9)C. These prove (1), and rearranging the exact
identity -H''=FI+sum p'' log p proves (2). Integration proves (3).

## 4. A full-dimensional domain beyond the earlier slab

    K*=I_(2m)/2+(3/8)[[0,U],[U*,0]].
