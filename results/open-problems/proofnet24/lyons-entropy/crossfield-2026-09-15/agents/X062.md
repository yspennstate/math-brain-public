# X062 — three-step flags and all rank-three projective 2-design frames

Status: constructive proof, 15 September 2026. The complete scalar-base active
Hessian is negative for every weighted complex projective 2-design frame in
dimension three, with no entropy-isotropy assumption. This is a new general
frame class relative to X004/X008/X028/X032, not a proof for arbitrary frames
or arbitrary nonscalar active bases. A universal conditional-entropy trace
bound and an exact orientation-defect identity are also proved below.
## 1. Statement and notation

Let `sum_i t_i P_i=I_3`, where `P_i=n_i n_i*`, `||n_i||=1`, and `t_i>0`.
Let the measured isometry have rows `sqrt(t_i)n_i*`. As in X004/X008, write
`b_ij=det(VV*)_{ij}`, `r_ijk=|det V_ijk|^2`, and let `P_ij` be the unit
projection orthogonal to `n_i,n_j` for every independent pair. Set

    W=sum_ij b_ij c_ij P_ij,  c_ij=log(t_i t_j/b_ij),
    tau=sum_ijk r_ijk c_ijk,
    c_ijk=log[b_ij b_ik b_jk/(t_i t_j t_k r_ijk)].       (1)

Only nonzero effects enter logarithms. Define `F_v,F_w` as in the root scalar
formula. A weighted complex projective 2-design means precisely

    F_v(A)=sum_i t_i(n_i* A n_i)^2
          =[(tr A)^2+tr(A^2)]/4                     (2)

for every Hermitian `A`. This algebraic identity is our hypothesis; no design
classification theorem is imported.

**Theorem.** Under (2), for every `0<s<1` and every Hermitian active `A`,

    -D^2 H(P_{V(sI)V*})[VAV*,VAV*] >= ||A||_F^2/10.  (3)

In particular all nine real active directions are controlled, including
changes of active eigenvectors. Every fixed scalar base has a neighborhood
of strict full-Hessian concavity in the active covariance, by continuity.
No assertion of concavity throughout the whole nonscalar active domain is
made. Arbitrary weighted unions of unitarily rotated 2-designs satisfy (2).
## 2. The actual three-step projection flag

Draw an ordered distinct triple `(i,j,k)` with probability `r_ijk/6`.
Equivalently, its consecutive transition probabilities are

    P(i)=t_i/3,
    P(j|i)=b_ij/(2t_i),
    P(k|i,j)=r_ijk/b_ij.                            (4)

All normalizations follow from exterior contraction:
`sum_j b_ij=2t_i`, `sum_k r_ijk=b_ij`, and `sum_T r_T=1`.
Thus (4) preserves the singleton, pair, and triple cardinality weights.
Its nested coordinate subsets form an elementary three-dimensional cube
after the three selected sites are labeled by their ordering. Its active
orthonormal flag is obtained by Gram–Schmidt:

    u1=n_i,
    u2=Q_i n_j/sqrt(d_ij),
    u3 spans ran P_ij,
    Q_i=I-P_i,  d_ij=b_ij/(t_i t_j).

The third site still has its full conditional law in (4); it is not replaced
by a uniform site. No individual spectral-mode marginal is asserted for
this cube. Such a labeling would be an extra transport requirement.

For fixed `i`, the projected rows `sqrt(t_j)Q_i n_j` form a rank-two Parseval
frame on `ran Q_i`. Their row weights are `ell_j=b_ij/t_i`; their pair
weights are `rho_jk=r_ijk/t_i`. Define its log-Gram entropy

    beta_i=sum_{j<k} rho_jk log(ell_j ell_k/rho_jk).

Every summand is nonnegative by the two-dimensional Gram determinant bound.
Put `Z_i=sum_{j<k}ell_j ell_k<=2`, and normalize these pair products to a
law `pi_i`. Since `sum rho=1`, direct expansion yields

    0<=beta_i=log Z_i-D(rho||pi_i)<=log 2.           (5)

This uses only the finite log-sum inequality. Its supported pairs have
positive `ell_j ell_k`, so the relative entropy is finite.
## 3. Two universal consequences of this flag

For `B_i=Q_i A Q_i` on its two-dimensional range, let `F_i(B_i)` be the
first-frame Fisher form of that conditional frame. Write `h=tr A`,
`x_i=n_i* A n_i`, and `y_ij=tr(P_ij A)`. Gram–Schmidt gives
`u2* A u2=h-x_i-y_ij`. Therefore

    sum_i t_i F_i(B_i)
      =sum_{i!=j}b_ij(h-x_i-y_ij)^2=2F_w(A).         (6)

To check every cross term, use `sum_j b_ij y_ij=t_i(h-x_i)`,
`sum_i t_i x_i=h`, and `sum_ij b_ij y_ij^2=F_w` with unordered pairs.
These give `sum_{i!=j}b_ij x_i y_ij=h^2-F_v`, and expansion proves (6).

Define `G=sum_i t_i beta_i P_i`. The exact coefficient relation
`log(ell_j ell_k/rho_jk)=c_ijk+c_jk` gives

    0<=G<=(log 2)I,
    tr G=tr W+3tau.                               (7)

For the trace, each triple supplies three copies of `c_ijk`, and each pair
coefficient is counted with total weight `sum_k r_ijk=b_ij`.
Consequently, for **every** rank-three frame, independently of (2),

    tr W+3tau<=3log 2.                             (8)

This strengthens the supplied bound on the scalar cubic coefficient. In
particular `tau<=log2-tr(W)/3`; it does not assert `tau>=0`.

For completeness there is a full signed Fisher comparison for `G`:

    tr(G adj A)<= (log 2)F_w(A),                    (9)

valid for every Hermitian `A`. Indeed `det B_i=tr(P_i adj A)` and
`F_i(B_i)>=(tr B_i)^2/2>=2det B_i`. Multiply by `t_i beta_i/2`,
use (5), and sum (6). This proof retains indefinite compressed directions.
## 4. The 2-design forces enough second-layer Fisher information

For the conditional frame, `0<d_ij<=1` gives

    F_i(B_i)=sum_{j:d_ij>0}t_j(n_j*B_i n_j)^2/d_ij
             >=sum_j t_j(n_j*B_i n_j)^2
             =[(tr B_i)^2+tr(B_i^2)]/4.             (10)

Parallel omitted rows contribute zero on the right. The last equality is
exactly hypothesis (2), applied to the ambient Hermitian matrix `B_i`.
The contractions `sum_i t_i P_i=I` imply

    sum_i t_i(tr B_i)^2=h^2+F_v(A),
    sum_i t_i tr(B_i^2)=tr(A^2)+F_v(A).

Combining (6), (10), and (2) proves the new pair-frame bound

    F_w(A)>=3[(tr A)^2+tr(A^2)]/16.                 (11)

In particular, for traceless `B` and `q=1-s`,

    FI_s(B)>= [1/(4s)+3/(16q)]||B||_F^2
              >=gamma||B||_F^2,
    gamma=(7+4sqrt(3))/16.                         (12)

The last minimization is the scalar identity
`a/s+b/q>=(sqrt(a)+sqrt(b))^2`. Both measured layers are used.
## 5. Paying the complete anisotropic orientation term

Put `L=log2`, `c=tr(W)/3`, `E=W-cI`, `d=c+s tau`, and
`A=(h/3)I+B`, `tr B=0`. X008 proves `0<=W<=LI`; X004 proves
`W+tau I>=0`. These are the supplied finite exterior results, not a
unitary-invariance assertion about measured entropy. Equation (8) implies

    0<=d<=L,
    2lambda_max(E)-d<=lambda_max(W)-lambda_mid(W)<=L,
    tr(E^2)<=2L^2/3.                               (13)

For the middle bound, `d>=c-lambda_min(W)`, from `tau>=-lambda_min(W)`
and `0<s<1`; substitute and use `3c=sum lambda_j(W)`.
For the last bound maximize the convex variance of three numbers in `[0,L]`
at the eight corners of that cube.

The exact scalar two-frame identity, with its full count Fisher retained,
completes to the square

    -H''=Q_s(B)+(L_s/3)[h+tr(E B)/L_s]^2,
    L_s=1/(sq)-2d,
    Q_s(B)=FI_s(B)+d||B||_F^2-2tr(E B^2)
                    -[tr(E B)]^2/(3L_s).           (14)

Since `L_s>=4-2L`, (12)-(13) imply

    Q_s(B)>=kappa||B||_F^2,
    kappa=gamma-L-2L^2/[9(4-2L)]>3/25.             (15)

The strict rational certificate uses only `L<7/10` and `sqrt3>17/10`:
`kappa>69/80-7/10-49/1170=1129/9360>3/25`.
The first bound follows from the positive exponential series at `7/10`.
Writing `u=h+tr(E B)/L_s`, Cauchy–Schwarz and (13) give
`[tr(E B)/L_s]^2<||B||_F^2/20`. Hence

    ||A||_F^2<= (31/30)||B||_F^2+(2/3)u^2,
    -H''>=(3/25)||B||_F^2+(13/15)u^2>=||A||_F^2/10.

This proves (3) for the original affine active-matrix direction.
## 6. The precise arbitrary-frame cube gap

One must not replace `G` by `C=W+tau I`: they have the same trace, but
need not be equal. Let `Phi_v(X)=sum_i t_i tr(P_i X)P_i`. Exact contraction
gives the traceless orientation defect
    D:=G-C=(Phi_v-I)(W)
        +sum_T r_T c_T[sum_{i in T}P_i-I].           (16)
Indeed `sum_{i notin {j,k}}r_ijk P_i=b_jk Phi_v(P_jk)`. Group the
`c_jk` and `c_T` terms in `G` separately to obtain (16). The second
sum cannot be discarded: its coefficients can be signed. Thus (9) controls
`C+D`, and the unresolved term for a general frame is `tr(D adj A)`.
The present theorem pays that term through (11)-(15), without requiring its
sign. This also explains why a flag coupling alone does not settle the
arbitrary-frame Hessian. All triple and cardinality weights remain present.
## 7. Explicit anisotropic frames beyond the overlap certificate
Put `omega=exp(2pi i/3)`. The nine rays
`n_{jk,a}=(e_j-omega^a e_k)/sqrt2`, `j<k`, `a=0,1,2`, with weights `1/3`
satisfy (2): the three phase sums for a fixed pair give
`(A_jj+A_kk)^2/4+|A_jk|^2/2`, and summing the three pairs proves it.
Take the equal-weight union of this design and its image under
`R=diag(1,1,(1-eps^2+2i eps)/(1+eps^2))`, `0<eps<=1/10000`.
All eighteen row weights are `1/6`; parallel duplicates may be merged.
Let `z=eps^2/(1+eps^2)`, `f(x)=-x log x`, and
`d_±=3/4-z/2 ±sqrt(3z(1-z))/2`. Direct pair grouping gives diagonal `W` with
`W11=W22` and `W11-W33=[f(z)+f(d_+)+f(d_-)-2f(3/4)]/12`.
Taylor's inequality `f''>=-2` on `[1/2,1]` bounds this difference below by
`z[log(1/z)-3/2]/12>0`. Thus these frames are not entropy-isotropic.
Their matched nearparallel rows give X028's row sum
`R_overlap>=(1/6)log(1/z)>8/3>56/25`: indeed `z<2^-24` and `log2>2/3`.
This escapes exact entropy-isotropy and X028's overlap condition; X008's
broader anisotropy criterion can still cover it. There are fifteen distinct
rays after merging duplicates. The advance is the entire 2-design theorem
and the universal trace bound, not a claim of escaping every prior criterion.
