# X081 — Bounded reverse-exchange entropy closes quadrature flag concavity

Date: 2026-09-15. **Status: COMPLETE bounded all-rank theorem; exact verification PASS.** All logarithms are natural. No universal fixed-flag inequality or general Lyons theorem is asserted.

## 1. Main result, scope, and credits

Let a unitary frame be assembled from blocks of the following two kinds:

1. One rank-one phase factor `Q=I+(z−1)hh*`, with `||h||=1`, `|z|=1`.
2. A pair `Q₂Q₁` of Householder factors, `Q₁=I−2hh*`, `Q₂=I−2gg*`, whose coordinate supports intersect in exactly two sites `c,d` and satisfy

   `Re[(conj(g_c)h_c) conj(conj(g_d)h_d)]=0`.

The support of each new **block** must intersect the union of previous block supports in at most one coordinate. There may be arbitrarily many blocks, arbitrarily large supports, arbitrary zero entries, and arbitrary complex phases satisfying the displayed quadrature condition. Call the final frame `V`.

**Theorem.** For every column set `I` and distinct `p,q` outside it, let `W=V C^I` and `U=span(Ve_p,Ve_q)`. Their projection laws satisfy

`beta := H(mu_(W+Ve_p))+H(mu_(W+Ve_q))−H(mu_W)−H(mu_(W+U)) >= −2 log 2`.   (1)

Consequently the orientation-independent fair-middle law `m` satisfies

`−2 log 2 <= beta_* := 2H(m)−H(mu_W)−H(mu_(W+U)) <= 2 log 2`.             (2)

Thus, for this fixed measured-coordinate flag, the entire map

`M ↦ H(P_(P_W+U M U*))`, `0<=M<=I₂`,                                      (3)

is concave in all four real entries of the active Hermitian covariance. Here `U` in (3) denotes the isometry with columns `Ve_p,Ve_q`. Endpoints may be noncommuting. At every strict active covariance and definite Hermitian active direction `A`,

`−H''[A,A] >= (8−8 log 2) det A > 0`.                                    (4)

The coefficient in (4) is uniform in the number of measured sites, bottom rank, top codimension, number of factors, and all factor parameters.

**What is new in this report.** The lower bound (1), obtained from actual exchange-path conditional entropy, and its propagation without accumulated error. These supply the previously missing lower orientation bound for the quadrature blocks and all their tree gluings. The result does not assert projection-entropy submodularity for the pair.

**Credited inputs.** C056 proves projection-entropy submodularity for one rank-one phase factor. C035 gives its full-phase unlabeled diamond; C036 proves quadrature channel composition and the composed diamonds for exactly the class above. `ROOT_PROJECTION_ENTROPY_GLUE.md` gives the exact one-coordinate entropy identity. X017 gives the invariant active-covariance criterion and Fisher bound. None of those diamond constructions, local submodularity statements, or active calculus identities are claimed as new. Their hypotheses and the needed elementary arguments are supplied below.

## 2. A general entropy lemma for a composed exchange channel

For a unitary `Q`, define the occupation channel

`T_Q(Y|I)=|det Q[Y,I]|²` if `|Y|=|I|`, and zero otherwise.

Its column entropy is `F_Q(I)=H(T_Q(.|I))`. An elementary diamond `(S,T)` has `S⊂T`, `|T\S|=2`, and two middle sets `S+i,S+j`.

Suppose two channels `T₁,T₂` have:

- submodular column-entropy functions `F₁,F₂`;
- a first-channel unlabeled diamond for every input elementary diamond;
- physical composition `T_V=T₂T₁` for the frame under study.

Sample the actual finite channel path `I → R → Y`, conditionally on fixed input `I`. Set

`G(I)=H(R,Y|I)=F₁(I)+Σ_R T₁(R|I)F₂(R)`,
`ell(I)=H(R|Y,I)`,
`F_V(I)=G(I)−ell(I)`.                                                     (5)

Every term is an ordinary finite Shannon entropy. No latent label is discarded in (5).

For input `I,I+p,I+q,I+p+q`, write `Delta F=F(I+p)+F(I+q)−F(I)−F(I+p+q)`. Then

`Delta G = Delta F₁ + E_D[ F₂(S+i)+F₂(S+j)−F₂(S)−F₂(T) ] >= 0`,           (6)

where `D` is the first-channel diamond. Indeed its marginal equations identify the four expectations in the second term of (5). Submodularity of `F₂` makes its integrand nonnegative, and submodularity of `F₁` pays the first term. This needs only the aggregate two-middle marginal, not prescribed middle labels.

Consequently the exact entropy balance and its retained-cost lower bound are

`Delta F_V = Delta G + ell(I)+ell(I+p+q)−ell(I+p)−ell(I+q)`,                (7)
`Delta F_V >= −ell(I+p)−ell(I+q)`.                                        (8)

More generally, a known lower bound `Delta F₁>=−L` gives the right side `−L−ell(I+p)−ell(I+q)` in (8). If the posterior fibers for the two middle inputs have at most `N_p,N_q` elements, then

`Delta F_V >= −log N_p−log N_q`.                                         (9)

This is an entropic property of the actual exchange chain, not a spectral-gap bound. The endpoint posterior entropies and the local submodular productions in (7) remain available as positive slack.

**Useful general criterion.** If the final physical frame also has a diamond, and the sum of its two middle posterior entropies in (8) is at most `4−2 log 2`, then its full two-active-covariance map is concave by X017. In particular a uniform posterior fiber bound of three suffices, since `2 log 3 < 4−2 log 2` (equivalently `6<e²`). This criterion concerns actual channel composition; arbitrary stochastic decompositions of four unrelated laws do not meet its hypotheses.

## 3. The posterior ambiguity is exactly controlled for one quadrature pair

Let factors `Q₁,Q₂` be supported on coordinate sets `A,B`, with `A∩B={c,d}`. Consider a fixed initial subset `I`, final subset `Y`, and a possible intermediate subset `R` with

`T_(Q₁)(R|I)T_(Q₂)(Y|R)>0`.

Because `Q₁` fixes coordinates outside `A`,

`R\A=I\A`.

Because `Q₂` fixes coordinates outside `B`,

`R\B=Y\B`.

These two equations determine `R` at **every coordinate outside `{c,d}`**. The conserved cardinality `|R|=|I|` fixes the number of overlap sites it contains. Thus the full global posterior fiber is of size one when that number is zero or two and of size at most two when it is one. This proves

`ell(I)=H(R|Y,I)<=log 2` for every fixed input I,                           (10)

regardless of all support sizes and ranks. This is a bound on the complete global intermediate set; there is no hidden second ambiguity in a single pair.

There is also a sharper bound retaining the probability that an ambiguity is possible. Write `K_J=Q₁P_JQ₁*` and `D={c,d}`. The event `|R∩D|=1` is determined by `I,Y`, so

`ell(J)<=eta_J log 2`, `eta_J=tr((K_J)_D)−2det((K_J)_D)`.                 (10a)

The expression for `eta_J` is the exact projection-DPP probability of one occupied overlap site, obtained by inclusion–exclusion on two sites. Thus a single pair satisfies the more informative bound `beta>=−(eta_(I+p)+eta_(I+q))log 2`. The exact identity (7) still retains the endpoint posterior entropies and the two local entropy productions as additional slack.

For completeness, the physical channel composition used here is exact. Write each Householder as `Q_j=D_j O_j D_j*`, where `O_j` is real orthogonal and `D_j` diagonal unitary. Cauchy–Binet gives at most two intermediate amplitudes by the same support argument. If two occur, their intermediate sets exchange `c,d`, so their cross product is a real multiple of

`(D₁_cc conj(D₂_cc)) conj(D₁_dd conj(D₂_dd))`.

The quadrature hypothesis makes its real part zero. Squared modulus therefore equals the sum of squared moduli, proving `T_(Q₂Q₁)=T_(Q₂)T_(Q₁)` on all subset ranks. Zero-coordinate limits and unique-path cases cause no division or phase obligation. This is C036's composition lemma, reproduced with credit.

Both local entropy functions are submodular by C056 and the first factor has the C035 diamond. Applying (8) and (10) proves (1) for a **single quadrature pair**, in every rank.

## 4. The defect does not accumulate under one-coordinate block gluing

A multi-block network can have several independent ambiguous path choices. **We do not extend (10) to its complete history.** The uniform bound instead follows from a separate exact entropy identity.

Suppose `V₁,V₂` are supported on sets `A,B` with `A∩B={c}`. For input `I`, put `J=I∩A`, `K=I∩(B\{c})`, and

`s(J)=Σ_(j∈J) |(V₁)_(cj)|²`.

The unique intermediate subset in Cauchy–Binet is determined by `I,Y`: outside `A` it agrees with `I`, outside `B` with `Y`, and cardinality fixes `c`. Hence its posterior entropy is zero, and

`F_(V₂V₁)(I)=F_(V₁)(J)+(1−s(J))F_(V₂)(K)+s(J)F_(V₂)(K+c)`.              (11)

This is the exact identity in the root's prior gluing report. It holds for arbitrary local unitaries, without any submodularity assumption. Its elementary entropy differences are:

- Both input additions in `A`: exactly the corresponding difference of `F_(V₁)`, since `s` is modular.
- Both additions in `B\{c}`: a convex combination, with weights `1−s(J),s(J)`, of the corresponding two differences of `F_(V₂)` at backgrounds `K,K+c`.
- One addition `p∈A`, the other `q∈B\{c}`: `|(V₁)_(cp)|²` times the difference of `F_(V₂)` at background `K` and additions `c,q`.
- An addition outside the union: zero.

Therefore if all elementary differences of the two local functions are at least `−L₁` and `−L₂`, respectively, then all differences of their composition are at least

`−max(L₁,L₂)`.                                                          (12)

Every coefficient used above lies in `[0,1]`; this is why there is no sum of errors. Disjoint supports give additivity and the same conclusion. Iteration of (12), with `L=0` for one phase factor and `L=2 log 2` for each quadrature pair, proves (1) for the full block family stated in Section 1.

## 5. From this lower bound to the full active theorem

For the two labeled middle laws `c,d`, finite entropy gives exactly

`beta_* = beta + 2 JS_(1/2)(c,d) >= beta`.                                (13)

The Jensen–Shannon term is retained; nonnegativity is the ordinary log-sum inequality. Thus (1) gives `beta_*>=−2 log 2`.

C036's already proved full-phase diamonds compose inside each quadrature pair by its exact channel composition, and compose between blocks by the unique-intermediate channel identity. Therefore the final frame has a genuine unlabeled diamond for every input flag. At scalar active covariance `I₂/2`, DPP atom log-submodularity gives

`a_S b_T <= 4 m_(S+i)m_(S+j)`.

Averaging the logarithm over that diamond yields `beta_*<=2 log 2`. One may obtain the displayed atom inequality by a two-by-two positive Schur complement for a strict odds matrix and then regularize the projection-background kernel. Every positive diamond atom has all four required probabilities positive. This is the credited X017 upper bound, not a new diamond result.

Since `log 2<1`,

`−2 log 2 > 2 log 2−4`.

Thus (2) is contained in X017's sufficient interval. Its actual Fisher calculation is

`−H'' >= det A [8−4 log 2+2 beta_*+4(T−1−log T)+4D(m||nu_M)]`             (14)

for definite `A`, where `T>=1` and `nu_M` is the normalized actual middle law. Both correction terms are nonnegative and remain in (14). Substituting the lower bound in (2) proves (4). For `det A<=0`, the diamond upper bound and the exact information inequality `D(m||nu_M)<=log T` make the frozen acceleration nonnegative, so the actual Hessian is nonpositive. Rank-one directions are also covered.

Integration over the convex strict active domain proves concavity. Finite entropy continuity extends it to `0<=M<=I₂`. No measured-basis average, unitary diagonalization of the measurement, or omitted score-information term is used. This theorem has a projection background; forgetting a fractional background still creates the two correction terms in X017 and is outside the claim.

## 6. Local inputs, recorded in self-contained form

The rank-one phase factor `Q=I+(z−1)hh*`, with `s_i=|h_i|²` and `gamma=|z−1|²<=4`, has exact exchange channel

`T_Q(J|I)=1−gamma s(I)(1−s(I))` if `J=I`,
`T_Q(I−i+j|I)=gamma s_i s_j` for `i∈I,j∉I`,

and no other transitions. This follows directly from the rank-one determinant lemma and proportionality of two exchanged rows.

Here is C056's local submodularity argument in compressed form, to make the input checkable. Put `L_I=Σ_(i∈I)s_i log s_i`, `L=Σ_i s_i log s_i`, `k(x)=1−gamma x(1−x)`, and

`f(x)=−k(x)log k(x)−gamma x(1−x)log gamma`.

Then

`F_Q(I)=f(s(I))−gamma[(1−2s(I))L_I+s(I)L]`.

For additions of masses `a,b` at background mass `x`, its elementary gap equals

`f(x+a)+f(x+b)−f(x)−f(x+a+b)−2gamma ab log(ab)`.

For `0<gamma<4`, put `delta=1/gamma−1/4`. Direct differentiation gives

`−f''(x)=2gamma V_delta(x−1/2)`,
`V_delta(t)=log(t²+delta)+1+2t²/(t²+delta)`.

This even function increases on `t>=0`, because its derivative is `2t(t²+3delta)/(t²+delta)²`. Integrating over the `a`-by-`b` rectangle shows that the gap is minimized at `x=(1−a−b)/2`. (The integral of an even increasing function over a centered interval increases with the absolute center; apply twice.) At that center the gap is

`2[X log X−Y log Y−D log D]=2X h(D/X)>=0`,

where `X=1−gamma/4+gamma(a+b)²/4`, `Y=1−gamma/4+gamma(a−b)²/4`, `D=gamma ab`, and `X=Y+D`. Zero masses and `gamma=0,4` follow by finite entropy continuity. This proves the needed all-subset local submodularity.

For the C035 local diamond, let the input blocks be bottom `R`, active pair `P`, complement `C`, of masses `alpha,beta,chi`. Put

`c₀=1−gamma(alpha beta+alpha chi+beta chi)`, `tau=max(−c₀,0)`.

Put `D_(S,T)=delta_S+delta_T−delta_(S+i)−delta_(S+j)`, where `T\S={i,j}`. The original signed common-transposition lift has weight `c₀` on the unchanged diamond and weight `gamma s_i s_j` on each cross-block transposition. For each `a∈R,k∈P,c∈C`, the six-diamond identity

`D_(R,H)+D_(R−a+c,H−k+c)+D_(R−a+k,H−a+c)`
`=D_(R−a+c,H−a+c)+D_(R−a+k,H)+D_(R,H−k+c)`

holds as a signed occupation-vector identity, with `H=R∪P`. Add this identity with coefficient `tau s_a s_k s_c/(alpha beta chi)`. It raises the unchanged coefficient by `tau`, replaces each cross-block coefficient by `s_i s_j(gamma−tau/(mass₁ mass₂))`, and adds the two nonnegative three-index terms. All coefficients are nonnegative because, for example,

`tau−gamma alpha beta <= gamma chi(1−chi)−1 <=0`,

and cyclically. Their sum is one and their signed marginals are unchanged. If a block mass is zero then `tau=0` and no division is used. This is the actual full-phase unlabeled diamond required in Sections 2 and 5, reproduced from C035/C036 rather than asserted from a spectral-gap theorem.
