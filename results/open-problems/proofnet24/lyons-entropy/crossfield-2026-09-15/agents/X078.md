Indeed `E prod_(i in S)(X_i-a_i)=det(K+tA-diag(a))_S` by expanding both
sides in inclusion moments. Orthogonal Fourier inversion proves (2).
At the reference only the three pair coefficients and the triple coefficient

    f:=p_K/mu-1
       =-sum_(i<j) |K_ij|^2/sqrt(q_i q_j) Y_iY_j
          +2 Re(K12 K23 K31)/sqrt(q1 q2 q3) Y1Y2Y3.       (3)

## 2. Logarithmic means and the automatic signed real-cycle domain

Use mask atoms `p0,p1,p2,p3,p12,p13,p23,p123`. For complementary `{i,j,k}` set

    c_i=log(p_j p_k/(p0 p_jk)),
    tau=log(p0 p12 p13 p23/(p1 p2 p3 p123)),
    m_i=c_i+tau a_i,   M=diag(c_i)+tau K.                 (5)

Conditioning on site i gives a two-site DPP. Its negative association implies
`c_i>=0` and `c_i+tau>=0`; directly, each conditional odds numerator minus
denominator is a squared conditional coherence times the squared branch mass.
With `LM(x,y)=(x-y)/(log x-log y)` and its continuous equal-argument value,

    c_i=(p_j p_k-p0 p_jk)/LM(p_j p_k,p0 p_jk),
    c_i+tau=(p_ij p_ik-p_i p123)/LM(p_ij p_ik,p_i p123),
    m_i=(1-a_i)c_i+a_i(c_i+tau).                          (6)

Thus all pair logarithmic coefficients in (4) are actual logarithmic-mean
energies. Boolean inversion of the pair and triple second moments gives the

    Q(A)=FI(A)-2 tr(M adj A),  FI=sum_S (p'_S)^2/p_S.      (7)

For zero-diagonal A orient edges as `z=(A23,A31,A12)` and
`k=(K23,K31,K12)`. Define the real comparison matrix

    C_ii=m_i,  C_ij=-|tau| |K_ij|  (i!=j).               (8)

Then, writing `r_i=|z_i|`, exact expansion gives

    -2tr(M adj A)
      =2 r^T C r
       +4|tau| sum_cyclic |k_i| r_j r_k
           [1-sign(tau) cos(arg(k_i)+arg(z_j)+arg(z_k))]. (9)

Zero factors are interpreted by continuity; for `tau=0` the second line is
zero. Every bracket is nonnegative and equals twice a squared sine or cosine.
If C is positive semidefinite, its first term is also a sum of squares.
For positive definite C, with `D2=C11 C22-C12^2`, its explicit completion is

    r^T C r
       =C11(r1+C12 r2/C11+C13 r3/C11)^2
        +(D2/C11)(r2+(C11 C23-C12 C13)r3/D2)^2
        +(det C/D2)r3^2.                                (10)
