## 1. Family and conditional reflection

Take one hub `0` and `m>=1` disjoint triples `(l_j,v_j,r_j)`. Fix real
`a_j,b_j>=0` with `c_j=a_j²+b_j²<1`. Define a Hermitian zero-diagonal matrix
`J(z)` by the only nonzero upper-orientation entries

    J_(l_j,v_j)=a_j,  J_(v_j,r_j)=b_j,
    J_(0,l_j)=z_j=x_j+i y_j,  J_(0,r_j)=conjugate(z_j).

Set `K(z)=(I+J(z))/2`. Every nondegenerate petal is a four-cycle, distinct
cycles intersect exactly at the hub, and its oriented cycle product is
`a_j b_j z_j²/16`. Thus the phases `2 arg z_j` move independently. The map
from all real `(x_j,y_j)` to the Hermitian kernel is affine.

Put `d_j=(a_j-b_j)²`, `e_j=(a_j+b_j)²`. Define four nonnegative quadratic
forms `r_(j,t)(v)=v^T M_(j,t) v`, where `v=(x,y)` and

    M_++ = diag(2-d,2-e)/[2(1-c)],       weight w_++=(1-c)/4,
    M_-- = diag(2+d,2+e)/[2(1+c)],       weight w_--=(1+c)/4,
    M_+- = diag(d,e)/[2(1-a²+b²)],       weight w_+-=(1-a²+b²)/4,
    M_-+ = diag(d,e)/[2(1+a²-b²)],       weight w_-+=(1+a²-b²)/4.

All weights are positive and sum to one. The first two matrices are positive
definite and the last two are positive semidefinite. Indeed `e<=2c<2`.

**Exact domain.** The strict positive-contraction domain is

    sum_j r_(j,++)(x_j,y_j) < 1/2.                         (1)

The closed domain replaces `<` by `<=`. This is a convex ellipsoid. To see
this, remove the hub and Schur-complement the disjoint positive blocks
`I_3 +/- C_j`, where `C_j` is the fixed weighted path adjacency. Their
eigenvalues are `1,1+sqrt(c_j),1-sqrt(c_j)`. The hub Schur complement of
both `I+J` and `I-J` is `1-2 sum_j r_(j,++)`. The two signs agree by the
bipartition involution; no measured-basis unitary diagonalization is used.

Let `Y_j` be the occupation of exterior triple j, with occupation signs
`s_l,s_v,s_r`. Its exact atom probability is

    P(Y_j=s)=[1-a² s_l s_v-b² s_v s_r]/8.                  (2)

The exterior triples are independent. Set `t=(s_l s_v,s_r s_v)`. Then t
has the four weights above and `s_v` is an independent fair sign. The signed
conditional DPP Schur complement gives

    P(X_0=1 | Y)=1/2 - sum_j q_j(Y_j),
    q_j= (1/2) (z_j,0,conjugate z_j)
                  (diag(s_l,s_v,s_r)+C_j)^(-1)
                  (conjugate z_j,0,z_j)^T.               (3)

Here is a direct proof of the conditional formula, including the absent
coordinates. For a full occupation sign vector s, the exact DPP atom is
`(product_i s_i) det(K-I_(unoccupied))`. The signed exterior matrix is
block diagonal with invertible blocks `(diag(s_l,s_v,s_r)+C_j)/2`.
Its determinant, multiplied by its exterior signs, is exactly `P(Y)`.
Its hub Schur complement is `s_0/2-sum_j q_j`; multiplying also by `s_0`
gives `P(X_0=s_0,Y)=P(Y)[1/2-s_0 sum_j q_j]`. This proves (3), rather
than assuming a conditional independence across petals.

Direct inversion gives `q_j=s_v r_(j,t)` except at `t=--`, where
`q_j=-s_v r_(j,--)`. Consequently each q_j is exactly `sigma_j r_(j,T_j)`,
where the `sigma_j` are independent fair signs, independent also of every
type `T_j`. This is an equality of the actual conditional occupation law.
Every choice of types and signs has positive exterior probability. In the
strict domain (1), choosing all resulting signs positive proves
`sum_j r_(j,T_j)<1/2` for every type profile. Thus every argument of phi
below is in its domain. Convex combinations of nonnegative amplitude
vectors also remain in that domain.

For verification of the inversion, its numerator after extracting `s_v` is

    [(t_l-b² t_l t_r)|z|²+(t_r-a² t_l t_r)|z|²
                           +2ab t_l t_r Re(z²)]/[2(1-a²t_l-b²t_r)].

The equal endpoint moduli cancel the indefinite diagonal part for the two
mixed types. Their remaining numerator is `d x²+e y²`, with positive sign.
This cancellation is the key restriction; arbitrary independent endpoint
attachments do not satisfy it.

## 2. Exact entropy potential and affine concavity

Let `phi(u)=log2-h(1/2+u)` on `|u|<1/2`, where h is binary entropy. Then

    H(P_K)=sum_j H(P_(K_j))+log2
                -E_(T,sigma) phi(sum_j sigma_j r_(j,T_j)). (4)

The exterior entropy is fixed. For any fixed types the function

    G(r)=E_sigma phi(sum_j sigma_j r_j)

is convex because phi is convex. It is coordinatewise nondecreasing for
`r_j>=0`, since, with `S=sum_(k!=j) sigma_k r_k`,

    partial_j G=E_(sigma_-j)[phi'(S+r_j)-phi'(S-r_j)]/2>=0.

Convexity and coordinatewise monotonicity therefore prove that G composed
with the nonnegative convex quadratic amplitudes is convex on (1). Taking
the positive weighted average in (4) proves concavity along every actual
affine Hermitian kernel segment in this entire family.

There is an exact stronger quartic theorem. Define

    Q(z)=2 sum_j E_(T_j) r_(j,T_j)(x_j,y_j)².              (5)

Since `phi(u)-2u²` is even and convex (`phi''(u)=1/(1/4-u²)>=4`), the same
composition argument shows **H(P_K(z))+Q(z) is concave** on the full closed
ellipsoid (1). Here sign independence gives
`E_sigma (sum sigma_j r_j)²=sum r_j²`. Thus, for all feasible endpoints,

    H(K_t)-(1-t)H(K_0)-tH(K_1)
        >=(1-t)Q(z_0)+tQ(z_1)-Q((1-t)z_0+t z_1).          (6)

Q is strictly convex, because every `w_(j,++)` is positive and the square
of a positive definite quadratic form is strictly convex. Therefore every
nonconstant chord has a strictly positive entropy Jensen gap. Boundary
extension follows by scaling all hub attachments by `rho<1`, applying the
strict result, and letting rho increase to one using finite-atom continuity.

For equal fixed path legs `a=b` and `c=2a²`, the forms simplify to

    r_++=x²/(1-c)+y²,  r_--=x²/(1+c)+y²,
    r_+-=r_-+=c y²,

and the quartic contribution of one petal is exactly

    Q_j=x⁴/(1-c²)+2x²y²+(1+c²)y⁴.                        (7)

## 3. Positive curvature identity retaining both terms

On an arbitrary affine parameter line `v_j(t)=v_j+t u_j`, freeze types,
put `R_j=v_j^T M_j v_j`, `B_j=u_j^T M_j u_j`, and

    Z=sum_j sigma_j R_j,
    Z'=2 sum_j sigma_j v_j^T M_j u_j,
    S_j=sum_(k!=j) sigma_k R_k.

Differentiating the actual entropy (4) and pairing sign j gives the exact
identity

    -H'' = E[(Z')²/(1/4-Z²)]
       +sum_j E_(T,sigma_-j) B_j
           integral_(S_j-R_j)^(S_j+R_j) du/(1/4-u²).      (8)

Every displayed term is nonnegative. The first term is precisely the full
occupation Fisher information: exterior probabilities are fixed and the
conditional hub Bernoulli parameter has derivative `-Z'`. The second term
is the entire logarithmic atom acceleration, paired by the exact independent
conditional reflections. No affine odds substitution or omitted conditional
mutual information occurs. Subtracting the second derivative of (5) gives

    -(H+Q)'' = E[(1/(1/4-Z²)-4)(Z')²]
       +sum_j E B_j integral_(S_j-R_j)^(S_j+R_j)
                                 [1/(1/4-u²)-4] du >=0.  (9)

This is also a direct Hessian proof of the strengthened theorem.
