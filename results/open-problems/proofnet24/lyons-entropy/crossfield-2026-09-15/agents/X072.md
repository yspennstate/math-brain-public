## 1. Exact measured symmetry and the complete frozen curvature

Let P exchange measured coordinates 2 and 3, and put Pi(K)=(K+PKP)/2.
Write e_+=(e2+e3)/sqrt(2), e_-=(e2-e3)/sqrt(2), and Q_-=e_-e_-*.
A swap-symmetric kernel has the operator decomposition

    K = M on span(e1,e_+) direct-sum lambda on span(e_-),
    M=[[a,conj(r)],[r,u]],       0<M<I_2, 0<lambda<1.       (1)

This decomposition is used for algebra; the measured sites remain e1,e2,e3.
In particular their two leaf diagonal entries are (u+lambda)/2, their joining
entry is (u-lambda)/2, and their two pivot links are r/sqrt(2).

For any strict three-site J and Hermitian A, let FI_J(A)=sum p'^2/p and define

    c_k=log[p_i p_j/(p_empty p_ij)],
    tau=log[p_empty p12 p13 p23/(p1 p2 p3 p123)],
    Mlog(J)=diag(c_k)+tau J.

The full exact identity, credited to X015, is

    -H''_J(A)=FI_J(A)-2 tr(Mlog(J) adj A).                 (2)

Both c_k and c_k+tau are nonnegative, since they are actual conditional
two-site log odds. Thus Mlog>=0: use its displayed expression for tau>=0,
and diag(c_k+tau)+(-tau)(I-J) otherwise. Equation (2) follows by Boolean
inversion of the pair and triple inclusion accelerations, so no signed
atom acceleration or moving conditional term is suppressed.

Decompose A=A_++A_-, where A_+=(A+PAP)/2 and A_-=(A-PAP)/2.
In the decomposition (1), A_- is [[0,z],[z*,0]] with z in C^2. Hence
its eigenvalues are ||z||,-||z||,0, and adj A_- is negative semidefinite.
Equation (2) proves -H''_J(A_-)>=FI_J(A_-) at EVERY strict J.
At a swap-symmetric K the mixed Hessian vanishes exactly:

    H''_K[A_+,A_-]=H''_K[PA_+P,PA_-P]=-H''_K[A_+,A_-].   (3)

This follows from permutation invariance of the original measured entropy.
It permits addition of the two signed quadratic forms, not addition of
arbitrary separately permitted directions at a nonsymmetric base.

## 2. The fixed measured mean Bellman envelope

Put f(x,lambda)=H(P_(x P_+ + lambda P_-)) on the two measured leaf sites.
The established two-site theorem makes f concave jointly in its two arguments.
Conditioning on the actual pivot bit gives, with b=1-a and rho=|r|^2,

    F_a(u,lambda,r)=h(a)+a f(u-rho/a,lambda)
                             +b f(u+rho/b,lambda).        (4)

In (u,lambda,rho), this is concave on the convex, downward-closed domain
0<=lambda<=1, rho>=0, rho<=au, rho<=b(1-u). Moreover,

    partial_rho F_a=-f_x(u-rho/a,lambda)
                           +f_x(u+rho/b,lambda)<=0.      (5)

The inequality follows from scalar concavity of f in x. Combining joint
concavity with |(1-t)r0+tr1|^2 <= (1-t)|r0|^2+t|r1|^2 proves concavity
of (4) on its ENTIRE physical (u,lambda,r) domain. This is the symmetric
specialization of the credited C062/C059 scalar-amplitude input.

The local formula retains its full acceleration. Set x1=u-rho/a,
x0=u+rho/b, g=f_x(x1,lambda)-f_x(x0,lambda)>=0. For a fixed a,

## 4. Global upper envelopes and a finite joint Jensen theorem

For every physical three-site K,

    H(P_K)<=H(P_(Pi K)).                                 (11)

Indeed D=K-Pi K is odd. The line Pi K+tD, -1<=t<=1, is physical, because its
endpoints are K and PKP. Equation (2) makes its entropy concave; permutation

    H(P_(Pi K))-H(P_K)
      =integral_0^1 (1-s)[FI_(Pi K+sD)(D)
                    -2tr(Mlog(Pi K+sD) adj D)] ds>=0.    (12)

Fix either L(K)=K11 or L(K)=tr(Q_- K), and fix its value c. Then

    B_c(K):=H(P_(Pi K)),  on {0<=K<=I, L(K)=c},           (13)

is a concave function, by (4)-(5) or (8)-(9), respectively. It dominates the
actual entropy by (11), and agrees with it at every swap-symmetric K. This

claimed to agree with entropy at nonsymmetric kernels.

**Finite theorem.** Suppose K0,K1 are contractions with L(K0)=L(K1), and
Kt=(1-t)K0+tK1 is swap-symmetric for a specified t in [0,1]. Then

    H(P_Kt)>=(1-t)H(P_K0)+tH(P_K1).                       (14)
