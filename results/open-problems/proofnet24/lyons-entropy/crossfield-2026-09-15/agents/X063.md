## 1. Fixed flag and the actual remaining constant

Let W and U be orthogonal subspaces of C^n, of dimensions r and 2, and let c=n-r-2.
Write a=mu_W, b=mu_(W+U), and

    m = (mu_(W+u) + mu_(W+v))/2,
    beta_* = 2 H(m) - H(a) - H(b),

for any orthonormal basis u,v of U. The exterior rows e_L satisfy
sum_L e_L^* e_L=I_2 and m_L=||e_L||^2/2, so m is basis independent.
X017 proves that all definite active directions are concave exactly when

    beta_* >= 2 log 2 - 4.                                      (1)

Its upper bound beta_*<=2 log 2 controls the other directions and follows from a certified diamond.
The original rank-dependent entropy-cover estimate is in ROOT_DIAMOND_ACTIVE_MATRIX.
X019 and X039 supply actual global coupling families; X018's harmonic bounds and X020's capped
cuts do not themselves supply a general costed diamond. These distinctions are retained here.
X060 separately studies adjacent-cover bounds, which are not rederived below.

## 2. Universal exact active-orientation correction

Choose a Haar-uniform unit vector z in C^2 and put w_z=U z. Then

    E_z H(mu_(W+w_z)) = H(m) + 1/2 - log 2.                    (2)

This is exact for every fixed complex flag, all dimensions, and all zero-minor patterns.
Indeed the atom at L is |e_L z|^2=2m_L X_L. For each fixed nonzero row, X_L is uniform on [0,1].
One elementary proof writes z=(g_1,g_2)/sqrt(|g_1|^2+|g_2|^2), with independent standard complex
Gaussians; their squared moduli are independent exponentials, whose normalized first share is uniform.
Thus E(2X)=1 and E[2X log(2X)]=log 2-1/2. Summing the atom entropy proves (2).
Independence between the different X_L is neither asserted nor needed.

Consequently the fixed-basis target is exactly

    H(a)+H(b)-2 E_z H(mu_(W+w_z)) <= 3.                        (3)

In particular a bound on the unmeasured two-mode entropy alone cannot replace this measured quantity.
The number 3 is the full corrected projection-orientation defect, not a discarded measurement term.

## 3. Mean measured entropy of a Haar projection: self-contained evaluation

Let F_(n,k)=E_Q H(mu_(Q C^k)), where Q is Haar unitary in the measured coordinate space.
Set H_j=1+1/2+...+1/j and H_0=0. Then

    F_(n,k) = sum_(j=1)^k [H_(n-k+j)-H_j],                    (4)
    F_(n,k+1)-F_(n,k)=H_(n-k)-H_(k+1).                       (5)

Here F_(n,0)=F_(n,n)=0, so all endpoint cases are included.
For completeness, let G be an n-by-k matrix of independent standard complex Gaussians and write G=VR,
where V has orthonormal columns and R is the triangular Gram-Schmidt factor. Left-unitary invariance
implies that V is Haar and independent of R: conditional on R, the law of V is invariant under every
left unitary, and this action is transitive on the frame space.
For a fixed k-set S, p=det(V_S^* V_S) satisfies

    det(G_S^*G_S)=p det(G^*G).

The k-by-k Gaussian matrix G_S has determinant-square moment product_(j=1)^k Gamma(j+t)/Gamma(j).
The n-by-k matrix G has determinant-square moment product_(j=1)^k Gamma(n-k+j+t)/Gamma(n-k+j).
These formulas follow by Gram-Schmidt: successive squared residual norms have independent Gamma
laws of dimensions k,k-1,...,1 or n,n-1,...,n-k+1. The Gamma density follows by adding independent
exponential squared complex-Gaussian coordinates. Therefore, for t in a neighborhood of 1,

    E p^t = product_(j=1)^k [Gamma(j+t) Gamma(n-k+j)
                            / (Gamma(j) Gamma(n-k+j+t))].     (6)

At t=1 this equals 1/binom(n,k). Log differentiation at t=1, using
psi(j+1)=H_j-gamma (which also follows by differentiating Gamma(x+1)=x Gamma(x)), proves (4).
Every measured k-set has the same law, so F_(n,k)=-binom(n,k) E[p log p].
Differentiation under expectation is justified by |p^t log p| bounded for t in a compact positive
interval, since 0<=p<=1. Subtracting (4) at successive k gives (5).

## 4. The exact mean lower bound is substantially above the target

Rotate the whole fixed flag by the same Haar Q, always retaining the measured coordinate basis.
Use beta_*(Q) for its fair-middle contrast. Equations (2), (4), and (5) imply

    E_Q beta_*(Q)
      = 2 log 2 - 1 + 1/(r+2) + 1/(c+2).                     (7)

Indeed the mean middle entropy is F_(n,r+1)+log 2-1/2. The centered second difference of F is
1/(r+2)+1/(n-r)=1/(r+2)+1/(c+2).
In particular

    2 log 2 - 1 < E_Q beta_*(Q) <= 2 log 2,                    (8)

with the upper equality exactly when r=c=0. Formula (7) is a universal identity, not a concentration
estimate and not a pointwise bound on a particular measured flag.

## 5. Full concavity after retaining the complete average measurement correction

Define the averaged measured entropy

    Fbar(M)=E_Q H(P_(Q[P_W+U M U^*]Q^*)),  0<=M<=I_2.          (9)

**Theorem.** The function Fbar is concave in all four real entries of M, for every n,r,c.
For a strict M and a definite Hermitian active direction A,

    -Fbar''_M[A,A] >= [6+2/(r+2)+2/(c+2)] det A.              (10)

The endpoints M may be noncommuting. This theorem concerns the averaged measurement experiment (9).
It does not claim concavity for every individual Q or an affine finite DPP realization of that average.

Here is a proof retaining the corrections from X017. Write delta=det A, d=det M, d0=det(I-M),
B=M-dI, f=tr B, T=f/(2 sqrt(dd0)), and nu_Q(L)=e_L B e_L^*/f. Let I_Q denote the actual full
measured Fisher information. For each flag the exact formulas are

    H_Q''=-I_Q+2 delta Lambda_Q,
    Lambda_Q=2 log(2T)-beta_*(Q)-2 D(m_Q||nu_Q),
    0<=D(m_Q||nu_Q)<=log T.                                  (11)

Thus E Lambda_Q>=2 log 2-E beta_*=1-1/(r+2)-1/(c+2)>=0.
For delta<=0, averaging the first equality in (11) proves nonpositive curvature.
For delta>0, the extreme-cardinality Fisher bound I_Q>=4(1+T)delta gives

    -Fbar'' >= delta [6+2/(r+2)+2/(c+2)
                     +4(T-1-log T)+4 E D(m_Q||nu_Q)].         (12)

Every correction on the second line is nonnegative; this proves (10).
On a compact strict active domain, e_L B e_L^* is bounded above and below by positive multiples of
||e_L||^2. Every first derivative is at most a fixed multiple of ||e_L||^2, as is each second derivative.
The extreme layers have analogous bounds by a_S,b_T. The potentially singular entropy terms reduce
to x|log x| on [0,1], which is bounded. A finite-sum dominated differentiation therefore justifies
averaging Hessians. Integrate on the convex strict domain and use bounded finite entropy and
continuity to extend concavity to the closed domain.

## 6. An explicit complete averaged entropy formula

The proof above can also be checked against the following formula. Let rho=B/f have eigenvalues
lambda_1,lambda_2, and put

    J(rho)=integral_0^1 2[lambda_2+(lambda_1-lambda_2)x]
                  log(2[lambda_2+(lambda_1-lambda_2)x]) dx.     (13)

Then

    Fbar(M)=H(d0,f,d)+d0 F_(n,r)+d F_(n,r+2)
                   +f [F_(n,r+1)+log 2-1/2-J(rho)].           (14)

To prove it, additionally average over an independent active right unitary, which leaves Haar Q
unchanged in law. For each fixed exterior row its normalized direction is then uniform on C^2.
Since nu_L=2m_L z_L^*rho z_L, averaging its entropy gives H(m)-J(rho). Apply the exact count chain
rule and the mean-middle identity from Section 4. At rho=I/2, J=0; at a pure rho, J=log 2-1/2.
Consequently all unobserved active-orientation entropy has been evaluated, rather than omitted.

## 7. A deterministic large class from the middle distribution alone

Put k=r+1, N=binom(n,k), R=(k+1)(n-k+1)/(k(n-k)), and let u_k be the uniform k-set law.
Since endpoint entropies are at most their log support sizes, every flag satisfies

    beta_* >= log R - 2 D(m||u_k).                            (15)

Thus **every flag** with D(m||u_k)<=tau:=2-log 2+(log R)/2 has nonpositive entropy curvature
in all definite active directions at every strict M. If it also has a certified diamond, X017
promotes this to full noncommuting active concavity. No coupling is needed for the definite conclusion.
This is a criterion involving only the middle law; it does not restate the original three-law test.
A sufficient condition requiring no logarithms of minors is

    N sum_L m_L^2 <= (e^2/2) sqrt R.                          (16)

Indeed Jensen under m gives D(m||u_k)=E_m log(Nm)<=log(N sum m^2).
Both criteria are independent of the background rank apart from the harmless explicit ratio R.
More generally, positive weights w_i define q_j(S)=product_(i in S)w_i/e_j(w). The one-site means
obey 2 E_m 1_i=E_a 1_i+E_b 1_i, so the common modular log weights cancel exactly and give

    beta_*=log[e_k(w)^2/(e_(k-1)(w)e_(k+1)(w))]
                 +D(a||q_(k-1))+D(b||q_(k+1))-2D(m||q_k).     (17)

Dropping only the two nonnegative endpoint divergences gives the weighted version of (15)-(16).
This is valid for any positive weights, including weights selected using the fixed middle law.

The preceding class has an explicit positive Haar measure. Its mean middle divergence is

    dbar=log binom(n,k)-F_(n,k)-log 2+1/2.

Markov's inequality gives probability at least max(0,1-dbar/tau) for (15)'s criterion.
For n=12,r=c=5, F_(12,6)=167477/27720, tau=2-log 2+log(7/6), and this lower bound is
0.5935491772... . The exact expression 1-[log(924)-167477/27720-log 2+1/2]/tau is the rigorous bound;
the decimal is only its evaluation. This covers at least 59.35% of all such flags without assuming
a diamond, and it controls all definite active directions. Almost every such flag has all relevant
minors nonzero, since each vanishing minor is the zero set of a nonzero polynomial on the unitary group.

## 8. An exact twelve-site high-rank example, including the upper bound

Let C be the 12-by-12 integer matrix with first row and column all one. For 0<=i,j<11 set
C_(i+1,j+1)=-1 if i=j, and otherwise the Legendre sign of i-j modulo 11. Direct integer multiplication
gives CC^T=12I. Let Q=C/sqrt(12), W=span(Qe_0,...,Qe_4), and U=span(Qe_5,Qe_6).
The verifier enumerates the integer maximal minors and proves exactly

    924 sum m_L^2 = 53053/26244 < 4 < (e^2/2)(7/6).            (18)

The last inequality follows already from e>1+1+1/2+1/6=8/3. Thus (16) proves the definite conclusion.
The exact atom histograms additionally give 4/5<beta_*<9/10, using rational logarithm enclosures.
This lies strictly inside X017's sufficient interval, so **the full noncommuting active map is concave**
for this flag without a diamond assumption. Its background rank and top codimension are both five.
Continuity gives an open neighborhood of flags with the same conclusion, including complex flags
with full minor supports. The continuity principle itself is standard and is not claimed as new.
The claim here is this exact high-rank certificate together with the general criteria (15)-(17).
