# X057 — a compensated diagonal slab through the threshold

Date: 2026-09-15. **COMPLETE arbitrary-dimensional theorem.**
The unrestricted Lyons conjecture and the unrestricted diagonal-direction

## 1. The theorem

Let K be any n-by-n complex Hermitian matrix satisfying

    (1/6) I <= K <= (5/6) I.                                    (1)

For every real diagonal direction D=diag(v), with arbitrary mixed signs,

    -D^2 H(P_K)[D,D] >= beta sum_i chi_i v_i^2
                       >= 4 beta ||v||_2^2 > (1/50)||v||_2^2,    (2)
    chi_i=E_[X_-i] 1/[q_i(X_-i)(1-q_i(X_-i))],
    beta=[324-169 log(169/25)]/180 > 1/200,

where q_i is the actual conditional probability of occupation at i.
The strict final inequality applies when v is nonzero; beta is about
0.0057618421. No assumption on n, support, complex phases, commutativity,
exchangeability, or the normalized odds determinant is used.

Consequently, fix any Hermitian zero-diagonal B. The function

    d -> H(P_(B+diag(d)))

is strongly concave throughout the convex slice of (1). For endpoints
K0,K1 in this slice, with K1-K0 diagonal, and 0<=t<=1,

    H(P_((1-t)K0+tK1))-(1-t)H(P_K0)-t H(P_K1)
       >= 2 beta t(1-t)||diag(K1-K0)||_2^2.                     (3)

This closes a dimension-independent part of C070's threshold-straddling
gap, and also covers mixed-sign diagonal directions. C070's separate
all-one-side theorem is still needed outside the slab. A direction
starting at a nondiagonal base is not being replaced by a chord with a
diagonal endpoint. Prior old-104's normalized-odds condition-number-three
criterion and X015's three-site theorem are distinct, credited baselines.

## 2. Exact atom calculus and the complete pair form

For x in {0,1}^n write Q_x=diag(1-x), R_x=(K-Q_x)^(-1), and

    p_x=(-1)^(n-|x|) det(K-Q_x).

Strictness gives p_x>0. Diagonal differentiation gives the exact identities

    partial_i p_x=p_x (R_x)_ii,
    partial_ij p_x=p_x[(R_x)_ii(R_x)_jj-| (R_x)_ij |^2], i!=j,
    partial_ii p_x=0.                                          (4)

Normalization, including its second derivatives, therefore gives

    J_ii=E (R_X)_ii^2=chi_i>=4,
    J_ij=E (R_X)_ii(R_X)_jj=E |(R_X)_ij|^2>=0, i!=j.             (5)

Here J is the full atom Fisher matrix of all diagonal parameters.
The conditional Bernoulli expression in (2) follows from
partial_i p_x=(2x_i-1)p_(-i)(x_-i), so none of the conditional information
in J_ii is discarded.

Fix i<j and an exact configuration y on the other n-2 sites. Let m_y
be its actual marginal probability and (a,b,c,d) the normalized two-bit
table in order 00,01,10,11. Put

    r=ad/(bc), h=bc-ad,
    F=h(1/a+1/b+1/c+1/d), lambda=-log r, Q=F-lambda.              (6)

The conditional DPP kernel C has off-diagonal magnitude squared h, hence
0<r<=1 and h>=0. Directly summing the four Fisher cross terms gives

    J_ij=E_y F.                                                 (7)

The mixed atom acceleration is m_y(1,-1,-1,1), so its logarithmic
pairing is m_y log r. Thus the normalized entropy Hessian is exactly

    -H''(K;D)=sum_i chi_i v_i^2
                 +2 sum_(i<j) v_i v_j E_y Q.                   (8)

Equation (8) retains both -sum (p'_x)^2/p_x and -sum p''_x log p_x.
No entropy term or normalization correction is omitted. In particular,
Q need not be positive: C070 supplies an explicit physical adverse pair.

## 3. Conditional spectral bounds and a scalar compensation inequality

Conditioning a present site k gives the Schur complement
C^(1)=C_rest-C_rest,k C_k,rest/C_kk; conditioning an absent site gives
C^(0)=C_rest+C_rest,k C_k,rest/(1-C_kk). For any diagonal positive Delta,
B>=Delta implies Schur_k(B)>=Delta_rest: minimize its quadratic form over
the eliminated coordinate. Apply this to K and I-K; the other bound in
each case follows from its principal submatrix. Iterating proves that
every full conditional kernel also satisfies (1).

The two-site conditional odds matrix L_C=C(I-C)^(-1) therefore has its
eigenvalues in [1/5,5]. If its eigenvalues are l1<=l2, then

    |(L_C)_12|^2/[(L_C)_11(L_C)_22]
       <= ((l2-l1)/(l2+l1))^2 <= (12/13)^2.                    (9)

Indeed the diagonal product is at most (l1+l2)^2/4, while the determinant
is l1*l2. The four unnormalized odds weights are 1,L11,L22,det L_C;
therefore r=1-|L12|^2/(L11L22), and

    r>=25/169.                                                 (10)

This odds calculation is used only to bound a conditional table at a
fixed base. The differentiated affine path remains K+tD throughout.

Writing u=a+d and z=h/(ad), elementary algebra gives

    F=z(1+u z)/(1+z)
      =(1-r)[1+u(1/r-1)] >=1-r.                                (11)

The function -log(1-s)/s=sum_(k>=0) s^k/(k+1) increases on [0,1).
Consequently (10)-(11) imply

    0<=lambda<=c F, c=(169/144)log(169/25),
    -eta F<=Q<=F, eta=c-1.                                     (12)

The explicit constant eta is about 1.242797697 and is greater than one.
Hence the crucial absolute, fully compensated pair estimate is

    |Q|<=eta F.                                                (13)

This is weaker than the false claim Q>=0, but it will be sufficient.
For r=1 both F and lambda vanish, so no division by zero is required.

## 4. A dimension-free signed-resolvent row budget

Put E=2K-I and S_x=diag(2x-1). Hypothesis (1) says ||E||_op<=2/3,
and 2(K-Q_x)=S_x+E. Fix a column w=R_x e_i. Then

    (S_x+E)w=2e_i.

Project off coordinate i. Since S_x is diagonal unitary,

    ||w_-i||=||(Ew)_-i||<=(2/3)||w||.

Squaring and rearranging yields the pointwise row inequality

    sum_(j!=i) |(R_x)_ij|^2 <= (4/5)|(R_x)_ii|^2.               (14)

Hermiticity identifies its column and row versions. Average against the
actual probabilities p_x and use (5):

    sum_(j!=i) J_ij <= (4/5) chi_i.                             (15)

This is the global cancellation budget missing from a purely pairwise
threshold sign argument. It does not grow with n. An equivalent averaged
bound can also be obtained from the exact Ward identity
E[R_X K(I-K)R_X]=I; the elementary pointwise proof above is sufficient.

Combining (8), (13), (7), 2|v_i v_j|<=v_i^2+v_j^2, and (15) gives

    -H'' >= sum_i chi_i v_i^2-2eta sum_(i<j)|v_i v_j|J_ij
          >= sum_i v_i^2[chi_i-eta sum_(j!=i)J_ij]
          >= [1-(4/5)eta] sum_i chi_i v_i^2.                   (16)

The bracket equals beta, proving (2). Integrating the second-derivative
bound along the affine diagonal chord proves (3).

For an exact elementary check of constants, let x=4/9. The series
log(169/25)=4 sum_(k>=0)x^(2k+1)/(2k+1), with a geometric tail bound,
gives

    4(x+x^3/3+x^5/5) < log(169/25)
      < 4(x+x^3/3+x^5/5)+4x^7/[7(1-x^2)]
      =51347312/26867295.                                      (17)

The lower bound proves eta>1; the upper bound proves beta>1/200 by
rational arithmetic. No decimal sign is a proof input.

## 5. A full-dimensional diagonal domain outside the old criterion

Let U be any m-by-m unitary and set

    K*=I_(2m)/2+(5/16)[[0,U],[U*,0]].                            (18)

Its eigenvalues are 3/16 and 13/16. Thus every independent real diagonal
perturbation diag(d) with max_i|d_i|<=1/48 remains in (1), by the elementary
operator-norm perturbation bound. Equation (3) proves joint concavity on
this entire 2m-dimensional box, including arbitrary mixed-sign chords.
It permits every m and every complex unitary U.

At the center, L=K*(I-K*)^(-1) has eigenvalues 3/13 and 13/3, and all its
diagonal entries equal 89/39. Its diagonal-normalized odds condition
number is therefore 169/9>3. The eigenvalues of K* straddle 1/2. Choosing
a dense generic U makes the coherent blocks nonexchangeable; no diagonal
endpoint or active-support bound is needed. The example is outside the
hypotheses of old-104 and C070, although it may overlap other known cases.
