## Exact balanced full-direction reduction

Use X088's measured-law identity with
`lambda=(a+x,b+y,a-x,b-y)`. At `a=b=1/2`, put
`R=4x^2`, `S=4y^2`, so `0<=R,S<1`. The six orbit masses collapse to

| Orbit | multiplicity | atom probability |
|---|---:|---:|
| empty/full | 2 | `p=(1-R)(1-S)/16` |
| singleton/triple | 8 | `q=(1-RS)/16` |
| adjacent pair | 4 | `c=(1+RS)/16` |
| opposite pair | 2 | `o=(1+R)(1+S)/16` |

Particle-hole complementation followed by the diagonal gauge that changes
the sign of the cycle edges preserves the base. On tangent coordinates it
fixes `(x',y')` and negates `(a',b')`. Therefore the mixed Hessian block
between those two pairs of directions vanishes. X037 already certifies the
edge block. The possible new assertion is positivity of the complete mean
block, including simultaneous changes in the scalar diagonal and opposite
edge.

For the mean direction `(a',b')=(u,v)`, the exact Fisher information is

`I=2[(1-S)u+(1-R)v]^2/[(1-R)(1-S)]`
`  +2[(1+S)u+(1+R)v]^2/(1-RS)`.

The complete acceleration contribution `sum_atom p_atom'' log p_atom` is

`L_S u^2+L_R v^2+4uv log[(1-R)(1-S)/(1+RS)]`,

where

`L_S=(1-S)log[(1-R)(1-S)] +4S log(1-RS)`
`    -2S log(1+RS) -(1+S)log[(1+R)(1+S)]`,

and `L_R` interchanges R and S. Thus the exact remaining inequality for
this full-direction balanced-base theorem is the positivity of the explicit
two-by-two quadratic form

`M_R,S(u,v)=I+L_S u^2+L_R v^2+4uv log[(1-R)(1-S)/(1+RS)]`.

No acceleration has been omitted. At `R=S`, the Fisher information vanishes
in the difference direction `(u,v)=(1,-1)`, but its remaining curvature is

`M_R,R(1,-1)=4(1-R)log[(1+R^2)/(1-R^2)]>=0`.

Consequently deleting Fisher-null directions is not a valid shortcut,
