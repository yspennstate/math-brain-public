## 2. A sharp endpoint identity, including the discarded squares

Let `J` be any strict Hermitian two-site contraction. Denote its four atom probabilities by

    x=p00=det(I-J), y=p10, z=p01, w=p11=det J.

For any Hermitian velocity `E`, define

    P=adj(J)/sqrt(w),  Q=adj(I-J)/sqrt(x),
    L=P+Q,            T=P-Q,
    omega=det L=2+(y+z)/sqrt(xw).

The endpoint part of the actual two-site Fisher information is

    F_end,J(E)=(tr(P E))^2+(tr(Q E))^2.               (5)

The signs of the empty/full probability derivatives disappear after squaring. Set `X=L^(1/2) E L^(1/2)` and `X_0=X-(tr X)I/2`.

**Theorem 1 (exact endpoint-square identity).** For every Hermitian `E`, including indefinite and rank-one velocities,

    F_end,J(E) = 2 omega det E + rho_J(E),            (6)

where

    rho_J(E) = ||X_0||_F^2 + (tr(T E))^2/2 >=0.      (7)

The identity is quadratic and exact; no determinant-sign truncation occurs.

**Proof.** Put `u=tr(P E)` and `v=tr(Q E)`. Then

    u^2+v^2 = (tr(L E))^2/2+(tr(T E))^2/2.

For a Hermitian two-by-two matrix `X`,

    ||X_0||_F^2 = (tr X)^2/2-2 det X.

Here `tr X=tr(L E)` and `det X=det(L)det(E)=omega det E`. Substitution proves (6)–(7). Positivity follows because `X_0` is Hermitian and `tr(T E)` is real. ∎

An equivalent expression, convenient for exact computation without a matrix square root, is

    rho_J(E)=tr(L E L E)-(tr(L E))^2/2+(tr(T E))^2/2. (8)

If `det E=0` and `E!=0`, then `rho_J(E)>0`: a nonzero Hermitian rank-one matrix has one sign, so `tr(P E)` and `tr(Q E)` are nonzero because `P,Q>0`. Thus (6) retains precisely the rank-one conditional information lost by (4).

## 3. Universal three-site refinement and the remaining lemma

Use the actual branch probabilities and velocities in (2), and define

    N=sum_ks [omega(J_ks)/pi_ks] g_ks g_ks*,
    S=N-3M,
    R_K(A)=sum_ks pi_ks rho_Jks(E_ks),
    E_K(A)=sum_ks pi_ks F_end,Jks(E_ks).              (9)

All these quantities are explicit functions of `K,A`. The symbol `R_K(A)` in (9) is a nonnegative quadratic remainder, not a residual coordinate set.

**Theorem 2 (universal Fisher and entropy bounds).** For every strict three-site base and every Hermitian direction,

    3 I_K(A)-D_K(A)
      >=2 tr(N adj A)+R_K(A)=E_K(A),                (10)

and hence

    H''_K(A)
      <=-[D_K(A)+2 tr(S adj A)+R_K(A)]/3.            (11)

In particular every direction satisfying

    D_K(A)+2 tr(S adj A)+R_K(A)>=0                   (12)

has nonpositive entropy curvature.

**Proof.** Sum the exact Fisher chain rule (3) over the three pivots. Keep the empty/full Fisher terms of each two-site conditional law. Apply (6), then the first identity in (3), to get (10). Substitution into the exact ambient formula (1) gives (11). Every conditional weight and every velocity is evaluated along the genuine affine-kernel path; no claim is made that the conditional kernels themselves form affine paths. ∎

Since `R_K(A)>=0`, (11) is everywhere at least as strong as X015's bound

    H''<=-[D_K(A)+2 tr(S adj A)]/3.

Section 4 proves strict improvement even after the original marginal term `D_K(A)` is retained.

One can also select a pivot instead of averaging. Put

    E_k(A)=sum_s pi_ks F_end,Jks(E_ks),
    d_k(A)=Akk^2/[Kkk(1-Kkk)].

Then the same exact chain rule gives the additional valid bound

    H''_K(A)<=2 tr(M adj A)-max_k {d_k(A)+E_k(A)}.    (13)

This uses only quantities already present in (2) and (5).

**Finite Jensen consequence.** On any strict segment `K(u)=K0+uA`, define

    C(u)=D_K(u)(A)+2 tr(S_K(u) adj A)+R_K(u)(A).

For `0<t<1`, let `G_t(u)=(1-t)u` for `u<=t` and `G_t(u)=t(1-u)` for `u>=t`. Twice integrating (11) gives the rigorous finite bound

    H(K(t))-(1-t)H(K0)-t H(K1)
      >=(1/3) integral_0^1 G_t(u) C(u) du.          (14)

Thus pointwise nonnegativity of (12) on a segment proves its Jensen inequality, with a quantitative correction. The dependence of all weights on `u` remains inside the integral.

**Exact unresolved sufficient lemma.** To close unrestricted three-site concavity through this endpoint refinement, it would suffice to prove

    D_K(A)+E_K(A)>=6 tr(M adj A)                    (15)

for every strict `K` and Hermitian `A`. By (9)–(10), this is exactly (12) for all directions. A still stronger candidate is `E_K(A)>=6 tr(M adj A)`. Neither candidate is proved here.
