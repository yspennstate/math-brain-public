## 1. The acceleration cone and its exact positive face decomposition

Let `0<K<I` be a Hermitian three-site kernel, `A=A*`, and

    p=P_K,  v=Dp_K[A],  a=D²p_K[A,A],
    I_K(A)=sum_x v_x²/p_x,  B=adj A.

All atoms are positive. Define, for the pair complementary to `k`,

    r_ij=-det A_{ij}=|Aij|²-Aii Ajj,
    R=sum_(i<j) r_ij=-tr B,  q=-tr(KB).                 (1)

**Theorem 1 (negative-face acceleration cone).** Suppose

    r_ij>=0 for all pairs,  0<=q<=R.                    (2)

If `R>0`, put `rho=q/R` and assign to each pair and outside bit

    w_ij,0=(1-rho)r_ij,  w_ij,1=rho r_ij.               (3)

On its square face denote the states `00,10,01,11` by `a,b,c,d`, respectively, and let `D_face=delta_a-delta_b-delta_c+delta_d`. Then the complete atom acceleration is

    a=-2 sum_faces w_face D_face.                      (4)

Consequently, with the actual conditional cross-ratio logarithms

    Lambda_face=log[p_b p_c/(p_a p_d)]>=0,

one has the exact identity and sign

    -H''_K(A)=I_K(A)+2 sum_faces w_face Lambda_face
              >=I_K(A).                              (5)

If `R=0`, (2) forces all `r_ij=q=0`, and the conclusion is `a=0`, `-H''=I_K(A)`.

**Proof.** Every one-site inclusion probability is affine, so its second derivative vanishes. Every pair inclusion has second derivative `2 det A_ij=-2r_ij`, and the full inclusion has second derivative `2 tr(K adj A)=-2q`. The right side of (4) has exactly these same moments: a face contributes only to its pair moment, and to the triple moment precisely when its outside bit is one. Equations (3) give the required sums. Normalization and all seven nonempty inclusion moments determine a signed measure on three bits by Boolean inversion, proving (4).

Each conditional two-site DPP has atoms `x,y,z,w` satisfying `yz-xw=|J12|²>=0`, where `J` is its strict conditional kernel. Multiplying by the square of its conditioning probability proves `p_b p_c>=p_a p_d`. Finally the full affine entropy identity is

    -H''=sum_x v_x²/p_x+sum_x a_x log p_x.

Insert (4), using `D_face dot log p=-Lambda_face`, to obtain (5). No conditional velocity or affine-kernel acceleration is omitted. ∎

The face weights (3) deliberately redistribute the canonical determinant face currents from X002. They are not asserted to equal the conditioned row-replacement determinants. Their atom acceleration is identical, as the complete moment proof establishes. This redistribution is what permits a nonnegative certificate even when the canonical face current has the wrong sign.

The cone has the equivalent endpoint form

    det A_ij<=0,  D²p_111[A,A]<=0,  D²p_000[A,A]<=0,   (6)

because `D²p_000=2 tr((I-K)B)=-2(R-q)`. Conditions (6) are sufficient, not claimed necessary for entropy concavity.

## 2. An actual reversible generator and an exact dissipation identity

Freeze `K,A`, take two independent samples, and write `mu=p tensor p`. For a face with `U=p_a p_d<V=p_b p_c`, place these two undirected edges in the 64-state pair space:

    (a,d) <-> (b,c),     (d,a) <-> (c,b),

with the same rate in both directions,

    c_face=2 w_face/(V-U)>=0.                          (7)

Sum the rates when edges coincide, and define the diagonal by zero row sums. This is a finite continuous-time Markov generator `Q`, reversible with respect to counting measure. Its exchanges preserve the coordinatewise total of the two occupation samples; the generator also commutes with swapping the copies.

For a positive pair law `nu`, its entropy dissipation is

    D_Q(nu)=sum_(unoriented edges uv)
          c_uv (nu_u-nu_v)(log nu_u-log nu_v)>=0.      (8)

Indeed, for `nu'=Q^T nu`, summation over each edge gives

    d/ds sum_u nu_u log nu_u = -D_Q(nu).

At `mu=p tensor p`, one face has flux `c_face(V-U)=2w_face` toward `(a,d)` from `(b,c)`, and the swapped edge gives the corresponding flux toward `(d,a)`. Its first marginal drift is `2w_face D_face`. Thus, if every used face has `U<V`,

    a=-marginal_1(Q^T mu),
    -H''_K(A)=I_K(A)+(1/2)D_Q(p tensor p).              (9)

The factor `1/2` is exact: each of the two pair-space edges contributes `2w_face log(V/U)` to (8). Equivalently, copy symmetry and `log mu_(x,y)=log p_x+log p_y` give the same factor directly.

If `U=V`, that face has zero logarithmic work. Set its rate to zero in (7). Formula (9)'s entropy identity remains exact; the marginal-drift identity then has a residual supported on these zero-work faces, whose pairing with `log p` is exactly zero. Alternatively, approximate away from the equality faces and use continuity in (5). No infinite rate is needed to state or prove the Hessian result.

This is a generator for an auxiliary entropy-dissipating collision flow. The prescribed affine DPP acceleration is the **negative** marginal of its drift. It is not claimed that the affine parameter itself is forward Markov time. Formula (9) matches the actual Hessian because the Fisher term uses the actual first derivative and the entire second derivative is accounted for by the drift and, when present, the explicitly zero-work residual.

## 3. A uniform spectral region for every fixed-diagonal direction

    {K=K*: I/5<=K<=4I/5, diag K=d}.                    (10)

**Proof.** Here `r_ij=|Aij|²>=0`, `tr A=0`, and `R=tr(A²)/2`. For every traceless three-by-three Hermitian matrix,

    ||adj A||_1 <= (5/3)R.                            (11)

To prove (11), change the sign of `A` if needed and write its eigenvalues as `x,y,-x-y`, with `x,y>=0`. Its adjugate eigenvalues are `-y(x+y),-x(x+y),xy`. Thus

    ||adj A||_1=R+2xy,
    R=x²+xy+y²>=3xy,

which proves (11), including zero eigenvalues. Write `K=I/2+E`, `||E||op<=3/10`. Trace duality gives

    |q-R/2|=|tr(E adj A)|

## 4. Why adapting an ordinary product bridge still fails

This obstruction explains why the two-copy generator is useful. Let `L` be any product birth–death generator, with rates `b_i` from zero to one and `d_i` back, and positive stationary product law `m`. A reversible ordinary bridge has `p=mfg` and

    v=m(gLf-fLg),
    a_0=m[gL²f+fL²g-2(Lf)(Lg)].                       (13)

If `v=0`, reversibility gives

    0=sum_(edges xy) m_x L_xy g_x g_y
                         (f_x/g_x-f_y/g_y)².

Hence `f/g` is constant on each active component; componentwise gauge gives `f=g=sqrt(p/m)`. Set `psi=sqrt(p)` and `S=diag(sqrt m)L diag(1/sqrt m)`. Then

    a_0=2[psi .* S²psi-(Spsi).*(Spsi)],
    a_0 dot h=<psi,[S,[S,diag h]]psi>.                (14)

Writing `q_i=P(X_i=1)` and `z_i=sum_outside sqrt(p_0 p_1)>0`, the one-site commutator is

    a_0 dot X_i=2b_i d_i(2q_i-1)
                   +2sqrt(b_i d_i)(d_i-b_i)z_i.       (15)

Thus matching the zero marginal accelerations of an affine DPP forces `b_i=d_i=t_i` on every active coordinate with `q_i=1/2`. Inactive coordinates have `t_i=0`.

For balanced marginals put `chi_ij=(2X_i-1)(2X_j-1)`. Commuting the coordinate flips in (14) gives

    a_0 dot chi_ij=4(t_i²+t_j²)E_p chi_ij+8t_i t_j T_ij,
    T_ij=sum_x sqrt(p_x p_(x^i^j))chi_ij(x)
        =2 sum_outside [sqrt(p_a p_d)-sqrt(p_b p_c)]<=0. (16)

For a DPP, `E chi_ij=-4|Kij|²`. Therefore an active rate touching a nonzero coherence forces strictly negative acceleration of that pair.

Take `K=I/2+(11*-I)/10` and let `A12=i`, `A21=-i`, with all other entries zero. This is a strict real triangle, with eigenvalues `2/5,2/5,7/10`. Every atom score is zero. The pair accelerations vanish on `13,23` and are nonzero only on `12`. But (16) forces `t_1=t_3=0` from pair `13` and `t_2=t_3=0` from pair `23`. All rates vanish, contradicting the nonzero target acceleration. Consequently **no product birth–death reference, even adapted to this kernel and direction, matches its instantaneous two-jet**. Reducible product generators do not repair the contradiction.

The two-copy construction does match it: `R=1`, `q=1/2`, and the exact entropy curvature is `-H''=log(88/63)>0`, entirely supplied by recombination dissipation despite zero Fisher information.
