# X030 — a two-count touching envelope with a strict full Hessian

Date: 2026-09-15. **Complete restricted theorem.** Unrestricted Lyons concavity remains open. No historical priority is asserted. This proof uses elementary determinant calculus and scalar inequalities; it imports no specialized external theorem.

## 1. The new base family and the actual envelope conclusion

Partition the four measured coordinates into A={0,1}, B={2,3}, and let
u=(1,1,0,0)/sqrt(2), v=(0,0,1,1)/sqrt(2). For complex c define

    K(c)=I_4/2+c uv*+conj(c) vu*,           0<|c|<1/2.                    (1)

**Theorem.** At every base (1), D²H(P_K)[D,D]<0 for every nonzero complex Hermitian D. Moreover, the explicit two-count envelope below is strictly concave on some full-dimensional neighborhood of each such base.

The eigenvalues of (1) are 1/2+|c|, 1/2-|c|, 1/2, 1/2; hence the full displayed domain is strict. A diagonal coordinate gauge makes c real positive and preserves every measured atom and the entire Hermitian tangent space. Write rho=4c² in (0,1).

For S contained in A and T contained in B, direct mixed-column determinants give

    p(S,T)=1/16 [1-rho xy],       x=|S|-1, y=|T|-1.                    (2)

Thus the law is uniform given the two block counts. It is not uniform given total count: the atom A has probability (1+rho)/16, while every set taking one site from each block has probability 1/16. Consequently the C074 count-uniform theorem does not apply.

For any strict four-site kernel J let f_rs(J)=Pr_J(N_A=r,N_B=s),
w_rs=binom(2,r)binom(2,s), and define the actual envelope

    Phi(J)=-sum_{r,s=0}^2 f_rs(J) log[f_rs(J)/w_rs].                   (3)

Entropy within each count fiber gives Phi(J)>=H(P_J), with equality at (1). More precisely, at an equality base and along any affine J+tD,

    D²(Phi-H)[D,D]
      =sum_atoms (p')²/p -sum_counts (f')²/f >=0.                    (4)

The last sign is Cauchy–Schwarz on each fiber. To verify the identity, use log p=log(f/w) at the base and f''=sum_fiber p''; the full acceleration terms cancel exactly. We will prove D²Phi(K(c)) negative definite. Smoothness then gives a ball of strict concavity around each base, and (4) proves the stated measured-entropy Hessian theorem. No global concavity claim for (3) at arbitrary J is needed or asserted.

## 2. Complete count calculus, with all Hermitian directions

Use the block mode basis (u,u_perp,v,v_perp), where u_perp=(1,-1,0,0)/sqrt(2) and similarly for v_perp. This changes the basis only to compute the counts: block-count generating functions are invariant under block unitaries, because diag(z,z,w,w) commutes with them. It does not identify measured entropy with entropy in this mode basis.

In that basis let the arbitrary Hermitian tangent be D=(D_ij), with diagonal
(d,s,e,t), and put T_A=d+s, T_B=e+t. Set h(z)=(1+z)/2. The count generating function at the base is

    G(z,w)=h(z)^2 h(w)^2-c²(z-1)(w-1)h(z)h(w).                       (5)

Let pi_rs=w_rs/16, F(x,y)=1-rho xy, U(x)=2x²-1, V(y)=2y²-1.
A count derivative divided by pi will be denoted g=f'/pi. Differentiating the determinant det[I+K diag(z-1,z-1,w-1,w-1)] gives exactly

    g=2[T_A x+T_B y-rho s U(x)y-rho t xV(y)]
      -4 sqrt(rho) Re(D_02) xy.                                     (6)

The part of f''/pi even under (x,y)->(-x,-y) is exactly

    8[(ds-|D_01|²)U +(et-|D_23|²)V
      +(T_A T_B-|D_02|²-|D_03|²-|D_12|²-|D_13|²)xy
      -rho(st-|D_13|²)UV].                                         (7)

Here is a determinant-level check that (7) has not omitted any relevant acceleration. Terms with zero base off-diagonal entries are the two-by-two principal determinants of D. Terms containing exactly one base c have degree three in z-1,w-1, and are odd under simultaneous count complementation. Terms containing two base entries c use the active indices 0,2, leaving exactly the spectator determinant st-|D_13|². Those are all possible terms with two tangent factors in a four-by-four determinant. Odd terms integrate to zero against log F. Equations (6)–(7) thus retain all sixteen real tangent coordinates and every acceleration contributing to the envelope Hessian.

Write

    L=-log(1-rho²)>0,           R=log[(1+rho)/(1-rho)]>0.              (8)

Under pi the variables x,y are independent, each taking -1,0,1 with probabilities 1/4,1/2,1/4. Only the four corners contribute to log F, so

    E_pi[U log F]=E_pi[V log F]=E_pi[UV log F]=-L/8,
    E_pi[xy log F]=-R/8.                                             (9)

The diagonal part of (6) is odd, and its D_02 part is even; hence their Fisher cross term vanishes. Directly evaluating the remaining nine-point Fisher sum gives the following complete identity:

    -D²Phi[D,D]=Q(d,s,e,t)
      +L(|D_01|²+|D_23|²)
      +R(|D_02|²+|D_03|²+|D_12|²)
      +(R-rho L)|D_13|²
      +[4rho/(1-rho²)](Re D_02)²,                                   (10)

where P_A=T_A-rho t, P_B=T_B-rho s and

    Q=(P_A²+P_B²+2rho P_A P_B)/(1-rho²)
      +(T_A+rho t)²+(T_B+rho s)²
      +L(s²+t²+rho st-T_A s-T_B t)-R T_A T_B.                         (11)

For clarity, the first two terms of (11) are precisely the diagonal count Fisher information. The last line is the complete frozen-logarithmic acceleration from (7)–(9). In (10), each complex off-diagonal entry represents both its real and imaginary directions.

All displayed off-diagonal coefficients are strictly positive. The only less immediate one is

    R-rho L=(1+rho)log(1+rho)-(1-rho)log(1-rho)>0.                    (12)

It remains to prove Q positive definite; this is done analytically next.

## 3. The scalar two-by-two inequality

Set T_epsilon=(T_A+epsilon T_B)/sqrt(2), S_epsilon=(s+epsilon t)/sqrt(2), for epsilon=+1,-1. Algebra in (11) gives

    Q=q_rho(T_+,S_+)+q_{-rho}(T_-,S_-),                              (13)

where for any -1<x<1, x!=0, with ell=-log(1-x²),

    q_x(T,S)=(T-xS)²/(1-x)+(T+xS)²
      +ell(1+x/2)S²-ell TS-atanh(x)T².                              (14)

**Lemma.** Every quadratic form (14) is positive definite.

Proof. Substitute Z=T-S and write q_x=aT²+2bTZ+c_0 Z². Then

    a=2+x+x²+x ell/2-atanh(x),
    b=-x²-(1+x)ell/2,
    c_0=x²(2-x)/(1-x)+(1+x/2)ell>0.                                 (15)

The convergent power series

    x+x ell/2-atanh(x)=sum_{k>=1} x^(2k+1)/[2k(2k+1)]

and sum_{k>=1}1/[2k(2k+1)]=1-log 2 give

    a>=2+(log 2)x²>=2.                                              (16)

We also have the elementary logarithmic bound

    ell²<=x^4/(1-x²).                                                (17)

Indeed, with sqrt(1-x²)=exp(-s), (17) is s<=sinh s, which follows by integrating cosh s>=1. Applying (u+v)²<=2u²+2v² and then (17),

    b²<=2x^4+(1+x)²ell²/2
       <=x^4(5-3x)/[2(1-x)]
       <=(4/3)x²(2-x)/(1-x)<=(4/3)c_0.                              (18)

The penultimate inequality is exactly

    8(2-x)-3x²(5-3x)=(x+1)(3x-4)²>=0.

Therefore the Schur complement satisfies a-b²/c_0>=2/3, and

    q_x(T,S)>=(2/3)T²+c_0[Z+(b/c_0)T]².                             (19)

Equality on the right can hold only when T=Z=0, equivalently T=S=0. This proves the lemma.

By (13), Q is positive definite in all four diagonal entries. Together with (10)–(12), this proves negative definiteness of the full sixteen-dimensional envelope Hessian and, by (4), the theorem. Continuity of these smooth Hessians proves the asserted full-dimensional neighborhoods. Their radii are not made explicit. The case c=0 is the familiar product kernel and has off-diagonal affine-Hessian null directions.

## 4. Verification, scope, and remaining target

`computations/X030/verify.py` independently constructs all sixteen atom probabilities and their first and second derivatives from the mixed-column determinant formula, sums them into the nine count probabilities, and compares the full 16-by-16 Hessian with (10)–(11). It also checks (2) and the touching identity (4). The saved run at rho=0.01,0.25,0.36,0.81,0.99 has maximum Hessian discrepancy below 9.3e-12; both negative Hessians are positive definite in every sample. Fisher-loss eigenvalues down to -2.3e-15 are roundoff. These finite checks corroborate the algebra; the all-parameter sign follows from (15)–(19).

The proved family has a coherent cross block, fractional background eigenvalues 1/2, and a non-count-uniform measured law. The conclusion controls every ambient Hermitian perturbation, including changes to both blocks, both spectators, and every complex cross entry. It does not follow from the earlier fixed-exterior conditional-core theorem. No claim is made that every member is outside every other restricted theorem in the campaign.

The precise next envelope problem is to extend the explicit positive two-count Hessian beyond balanced blocks of size two. For blocks of sizes m,n at the analogous base, the count law is still binomially weighted 1-rho XY, but X and Y take more than three values; the logarithmic averages in (9) no longer collapse to two scalars. The present proof does not claim their signs suffice for the remaining diagonal sector, and does not assert global concavity of (3).
