# X040 — Boolean OR convolution of a rank-two DPP

Date: 15 September 2026. **Status: proved finite concavity family; exact verification below.** The unrestricted conjecture and definite active directions remain open.

The new occupation-preserving transform gives an exact nonnegative face decomposition for every indefinite two-mode direction over an arbitrary fixed diagonal background. Consequently measured Shannon entropy is concave on every feasible fixed-expected-size section, including complex and noncommuting active matrices. The active matrix need not be positive. No quantum or spectral entropy is substituted. Historical priority is not claimed.

## 1. Precise theorem

Fix `D=diag(d_i)`, `0<=d_i<=1`, and any complex `n`-by-`2` matrix `B`. Put

    K(M)=D+BMB*,
    Ω={M=M*: 0<=K(M)<=I},  F(M)=H(P_K(M)).                         (1)

**Theorem.** If `M0,M1∈Ω` and `det(M1−M0)<=0`, then for `0<=s<=1`,

    F((1−s)M0+sM1) >= (1−s)F(M0)+sF(M1)
                      +s(1−s)||diag(B(M1−M0)B*)||₂².             (2)

In particular, for every nonzero positive semidefinite Hermitian `C` and real `c`, `F` is concave on the entire convex section

    Ω∩{M: tr(CM)=c}.                                             (3)

Taking `C=B*B` proves concavity at fixed expected occupation count, since `E|X|=tr D+tr(B*BM)`. For rank-two `B` this is a three-real-dimensional active section, with no noise-size, commutativity, or cycle-phase condition. Rank-deficient `B` only makes the image lower-dimensional. The theorem includes fractional full-rank physical kernels; structural zero or one eigenvalues are not required.

## 2. Exact Bernoulli determinant transform

First assume `0<d_i<1` and `0<K(M)<I`. Let

    q_z=∏_i d_i^(z_i)(1−d_i)^(1−z_i),
    η_i(z_i)=z_i/d_i−(1−z_i)/(1−d_i),
    T_z=B*diag(η_i(z_i))B.                                      (4)

The exact atom transform is

    p_z(M)=q_z det(I₂+M T_z)
          =q_z[1+tr(M T_z)+det M det T_z].                        (5)

**Proof.** In the signed-column atom determinant, the diagonal part is `diag(d_i)` in occupied columns and `diag(1−d_i)` otherwise. Factoring this invertible diagonal matrix and applying `det(I+UV)=det(I+VU)` gives (5). The last equality is the two-by-two determinant identity. This is an identity for arbitrary Hermitian `M`; its positive factor equals `p_z/q_z` at every strict physical kernel. Neither `M` nor `T_z` is assumed positive. ∎

The variables `η_i` are independent and centered under `q`. Their exact Bernoulli integration-by-parts rule is

    E_q[η_i η_j g(Z)]
       =E_{q,−ij}[g(11,Z_−ij)−g(10,Z_−ij)
                                −g(01,Z_−ij)+g(00,Z_−ij)].       (6)

For one coordinate the two signed masses are `d_i/d_i=1` and `−(1−d_i)/(1−d_i)=−1`; applying this twice proves (6), with no residual noise factor.

Writing `B_ij` for the two rows indexed by `i,j`, Cauchy–Binet gives

    det T_z=∑_(i<j) c_ij η_i(z_i)η_j(z_j),
    c_ij=|det B_ij|²>=0.                                       (7)

Thus this transform retains the complete occupation table while its second active cumulant is exactly a positive combination of Boolean mixed differences. Its rank-two truncation is exact, not asymptotic.

## 3. Every face has the required logarithmic sign

For every fixed occupation pattern `R` outside `i,j`, set

    ℓ_ij(R)=log[p_(R+i)p_(R+j)/(p_R p_(R+i+j))].                  (8)

Here `R` itself has both `i,j` unoccupied. Every strict DPP satisfies `ℓ_ij(R)>=0`.

For completeness, set `L=K(I−K)^−1>0`. The atom formula is `p_S=det(I−K)det L_S`. Eliminate the principal block `L_R` in the four determinants. Its remaining positive two-by-two Schur complement is `[[a,z],[conj z,b]]`, so the ratio in (8) equals `ab/(ab−|z|²)>=1`. Empty `R` uses the empty determinant. This proves the sign for every face, including arbitrary complex phase.

Define the finite nonnegative interaction

    J_D,B(M)=∑_(i<j)c_ij E_(q,−ij)[ℓ_ij(R)] >=0.                 (9)

Equations (6)–(8) give the exact identity

    E_q[det T_Z log p_Z]=−J_D,B(M).                              (10)

One can replace `log p` by `log(p/q)` because `log q` is modular and every mixed difference of it vanishes. The expectation in (9) is under the actual fixed Bernoulli background on the remaining coordinates, not under an incorrectly frozen conditional DPP.

## 4. Complete entropy curvature and the proof of concavity

Along `M+tE`, let `δ=det E` and let

    I_M(E)=∑_z (p'_z)²/p_z
          =E_q[(tr(E T_Z)+tr(adj(M)E)det T_Z)²
                            /det(I₂+M T_Z)].                    (11)

Differentiating (5) gives exactly `p''_z=2δ q_z det T_z`. Therefore the full measured Shannon Hessian is

    −F''_M[E,E]=I_M(E)+∑_z p''_z log p_z
               =I_M(E)−2 det(E) J_D,B(M).                       (12)

No conditional entropy or lost Fisher term has been discarded. If `det E<=0`, both terms on the final right side are nonnegative, proving local concavity in all such directions.

There is a simple uniform quantitative bound. For the occupation covariance matrix `Σ`,

    Σ_ii=K_ii(1−K_ii),  Σ_ij=−|K_ij|² (i≠j),  Σ<=(1/2)I.       (13)

Indeed, `K²<=K` gives `sum_(j≠i)|K_ij|²<=K_ii(1−K_ii)`, and `−2a_i a_j<=a_i²+a_j²` gives `a*Σa<=2sum_i K_ii(1−K_ii)a_i²<=(1/2)||a||²` for real `a`. If `u_z=p'_z/p_z` is the score, then `Cov(u,X_i)=(BEB*)_ii`. Cauchy–Schwarz with `a=diag(BEB*)` yields

    I_M(E)>=2||diag(BEB*)||₂².                                 (14)

Thus (12) gives the uniform curvature bound `−F''>=2||diag(BEB*)||₂²`. Integrating on the endpoint segment proves (2).

For (3), every tangent direction satisfies `tr(CE)=0`. If `C>0`, the Hermitian matrix `C^(1/2)EC^(1/2)` has trace zero and hence nonpositive determinant, which gives `det E<=0`. If `C` has rank one, choose its eigenbasis: the constraint is `E_11=0`, so `det E=−|E_12|²<=0`. This proves (3).

For boundary `D`, boundary kernels, or lower-dimensional feasible sections, regularize the whole segment by

    D_ε=(1−2ε)D+εI,  B_ε=sqrt(1−2ε)B,
    K_ε(M)=(1−2ε)K(M)+εI,  0<ε<1/2.                            (15)

The already proved strict inequality applies with the same `M0,M1`. Finite Shannon entropy and the displayed gap are continuous as `ε→0`, proving all stated closed-domain claims. No logarithm or score is assigned at a changing zero atom.

## 5. A full-rank complex interval with a rational Jensen gap

Here is an explicit four-site family. Let

    D=diag(1/5,1/4,3/10,1/3),
    B=(1/2)[[1,0],[0,1],[1,1],[1,i]],
    M0=[[1/4,1/20],[1/20,1/3]],
    E=[[1/10,(1−i)/12],[(1+i)/12,−1/10]],
    K(t)=D+B(M0+tE)B*,  −1<=t<=1.                              (16)

The active matrices do not commute: `[M0,E]≠0`. Here `det E=−43/1800`, `||E||=sqrt(86)/60<1/6`, `M0>=I/5`, and `M0<=23I/60`. Consequently `M0+tE>0`, `M0+tE<11I/20`, and

    ||B*B||=(3+sqrt 2)/4<9/8,
    I/5<=K(t)<[1/3+(9/8)(11/20)]I=(457/480)I.                  (17)

Thus every kernel on the entire interval is strictly between zero and one. The diagonal velocity is `(1/40,−1/40,1/24,−1/24)`, whose sum is zero and squared norm is `17/3600`. Hence the expected number of occupied sites is fixed, while complex coherences and individual means change. The theorem gives throughout the interval

    −d²H(P_K(t))/dt² >=17/1800,
    H(P_K(0))−[H(P_K(−1))+H(P_K(1))]/2 >=17/3600.                (18)

This illustrates arbitrary unequal diagonal noise and an entire noncommuting segment. No claim that the example avoids every earlier sufficient neighborhood is needed for the theorem's structural scope.

## 6. Scope, Boolean convolution, and the exact remaining lemma

When `M>=0`, the construction has root's literal OR interpretation: put `N=(I−D)^−1/2 BMB*(I−D)^−1/2<=I`. The union of `X∼DPP(N)` and independent `Y∼Bernoulli(D)` has avoidance probability `prod_(i∈S)(1−d_i)det(I−N_S)=det(I−K_S)`, hence exactly the occupation law `P_K`. The input has support dimension at most two. Equations (5)–(12) remain valid beyond this probabilistic interpretation, for every Hermitian feasible `M`.

The prior zero-background rank-two theorem and X001's marked-erasure identity are not claimed anew. This proof handles unmarked independent OR noise in **all indefinite active directions**. Its three-dimensional affine sections are substantive finite concavity domains, but it does not prove concavity on the entire four-dimensional active domain.

For definite active `E`, the exact missing inequality is

    I_M(E) >= 2 det(E) J_D,B(M),   det(E)>0.                     (19)

Both sides are explicit full-atom expressions (9), (11). The signed acceleration and conditional-log correction are retained completely; generic data processing does not settle (19). The exact Boolean identity is the new bridge, not a claim that OR noise universally preserves entropy curvature.
