## 1. The controlled seed and its complete curvature

Distinguish measured coordinate 0, and let
\[
 M_t=\begin{pmatrix}a+td&(k+tw)^*\\ k+tw&R+tF\end{pmatrix},
 \qquad F=\lambda kk^*,\quad 0\le t\le1,                         \tag{1}
\]
where d and lambda are real, w is arbitrary complex, and
\[
 0\le M_0,M_1\le I,\qquad 0\le R+3F\le I,\qquad \lambda d\le0. \tag{2}
\]
X034 proves, on strict inputs,
\[
 -H''(P_{M_t})=\mathcal I_{M_t}
 -2\lambda d\,k^*Q_{g_t}(R+tF)k
 +2w^*Q_{g_t}(R+3tF)w\ \ge\ \mathcal I_{M_t}.                \tag{3}
\]
Here \(Q_g(B)=\nabla_B\mathbb E_{P_B}g\), \(g_t(T)=\log[p_t(T)/p_t(T+0)]\), and \(\mathcal I=\sum(p')^2/p\).

For completeness, the specialized mechanism in (3) has a short proof. For any increasing fixed table g, \(Q_g(B)\ge0\): test its derivative against any positive semidefinite direction using determinantal stochastic domination. Its hypotheses are precisely ordered positive contractions; this is [Lyons, Theorem 2.9](https://arxiv.org/html/1406.2707v1), inspected during this attempt. Continuity extends the polynomial gradient to closed contractions.

For a fixed table f on the full cube, put \(f_0(T)=f(T)\), \(g(T)=f(T+0)-f(T)\). Expanding bordered inclusion determinants proves
\[
 \mathbb E_{P_M}f=\mathbb E_{P_R}f_0+a\mathbb E_{P_R}g-k^*Q_g(R)k.
\]
The polynomial \(Q_g(R+s kk^*)\) is affine in s and its derivative annihilates k. Indeed expand g in inclusion monomials; each gradient is an embedded adjugate, and
\(D\operatorname{adj}(V)[uu^*]=(u^*V^{-1}u)\operatorname{adj}(V)-\operatorname{adj}(V)uu^*V^{-1}\)
annihilates u. Multilinearity gives affinity; polynomial continuation removes singularities. Twice differentiating the bordered formula along (1) consequently gives
\[
 D^2\mathbb E_{P_{M_t}}f
 =2\lambda d\,k^*Q_g(R+tF)k-2w^*Q_g(R+3tF)w.               \tag{4}
\]
Freeze \(f=-\log p_t\). It is supermodular: \(p_S=\det(I-M)\det L_S\), \(L=M(I-M)^{-1}>0\), and a two-by-two Schur complement gives \(p_{T+i}p_{T+j}\ge p_Tp_{T+i+j}\). Thus g is increasing. Since \(H''=-\mathcal I+D^2\mathbb E f\), (4) proves (3). This retains the entire atom acceleration. Regularization \(M\mapsto(1-2\epsilon)M+\epsilon I\) preserves (1)–(2), with \(\lambda_\epsilon=\lambda/(1-2\epsilon)\), so continuity handles boundaries.

## 2. Every conditional coordinate channel preserves the seed class

Let D and S be fixed nonnegative diagonal matrices satisfying \(D+S^2\le I\). Write \(S=\operatorname{diag}(\sqrt{\sigma_0},S_e)\). The affine image \(N_t=D+SM_tS\) is a positive contraction. If \(\sigma_0>0\), its seed parameters obey

 \widetilde w=\sqrt{\sigma_0}S_ew,\quad
 \widetilde d=\sigma_0d,\quad
 \widetilde\lambda=\lambda/\sigma_0,\quad

Therefore
\[

So (3) applies. Coordinates with zero \(\sigma_i\) are independent constant Bernoulli bits and may be removed. If \(\sigma_0=0\), the remaining exterior varies in the rank-one direction \(S_eFS_e\); all its atom probabilities are affine, hence \(-H''=\mathcal I\). In all cases
\[
 -H''(P_{D+SM_tS})\ge\mathcal I_{D+SM_tS}.                  \tag{6}
\]
This stability under arbitrary diagonal noise is the new bridge; active-support rank-two theorems do not have this closure automatically.

## 3. Exact tensor refinement and its affine correction

We include C061's finite-channel algebra to specify exactly what is transferred. For a channel \(C(y|x)=c_y+x\delta_y\), split rows into positive, negative, and zero \(\delta_y\). Pair positive i and negative j by any nonnegative transport \(r_{ij}\) with row sums \(\delta_i\) and column sums \(-\delta_j\). Split row i toward pair (i,j) with probability \(r_{ij}/\delta_i\), and row j with probability \(r_{ij}/(-\delta_j)\). These are fixed splitting laws \(\ell(g|y)\), independent of x.

The pair label has an input-independent probability
\[
 w_g=r_{ij}(c_i/\delta_i+c_j/(-\delta_j)),
\]
and, given the label, the positive-row bit has probability \(\rho_g+\sigma_gx\), where
\[
 \rho_g=r_{ij}c_i/(\delta_iw_g),\qquad
 \sigma_g=r_{ij}/w_g,\qquad 0\le\rho_g\le\rho_g+\sigma_g\le1.
\]
Zero-difference rows are singleton labels with constant bit zero. Labels have total weight one, and
\[
 \sum_gw_g\sigma_g=\tfrac12\sum_y|\delta_y|=:\tau_C.          \tag{7}
\]
The refinement retains the original row, so for input mean m,
\[
 H(\widetilde Y)-H(Y)=A_C(m)
 :=\sum_y(c_y+m\delta_y)H(\ell(\cdot|y))\ge0.               \tag{8}
\]
This correction is affine, including its sign; no posterior entropy is silently discarded.

For m independent channels applied to \(X\sim P_{M_t}\), the labels are mutually independent and independent of X. Given their vector g, the resulting bit law is \(P_{D_g+S_gM_tS_g}\), with \(D_g=\operatorname{diag}(\rho_{i,g_i})\), \(S_g=\operatorname{diag}(\sqrt{\sigma_{i,g_i}})\). Indeed its inclusion probability on A is
\[
 \mathbb E\prod_{i\in A}(\rho_i+\sigma_iX_i)
 =\sum_{J\subseteq A}\det(M_J)\prod_{i\in J}\sigma_i
                         \prod_{i\in A\setminus J}\rho_i
 =\det((D_g+S_gMS_g)_A).
\]
With \(W_g=\prod_iw_{i,g_i}\), the entropy identity is exactly
\[
 H(Y_t)=\sum_iH(w_i)+\sum_gW_gH(P_{D_g+S_gM_tS_g})
                    -\sum_iA_{C_i}((M_t)_{ii}).            \tag{9}
\]
Both the full Fisher information and the full acceleration contraction split:
\[
 \mathcal I_Y=\sum_gW_g\mathcal I_g,\qquad
 \sum_yp_y''\log p_y=\sum_gW_g\sum_zp_{g,z}''\log p_{g,z}.  \tag{10}
\]
For Fisher, refinement leaves each score unchanged; then condition on the fixed labels. For acceleration, use \(\log(p_y\ell)=\log p_y+\log\ell\); the extra sum is \(-\sum_iA_{C_i}''((M_t)_{ii})=0\), because the one-site means are affine. These equalities are on the fixed positive support; closed endpoints follow by continuity.

Combining (6), (9), and (10) proves the **arbitrary tensor-channel theorem**
\[
 -H''(Y_t)\ge\mathcal I_Y\ge4\eta_0d^2\ge4\tau_{C_0}^2d^2,
 \qquad \eta_0:=\sum_gw_{0,g}\sigma_{0,g}^2.                \tag{11}
\]
For the middle inequality marginalize each conditional score to its pivot Bernoulli bit: its derivative is \(\sigma_{0,g}d\), and \(p(1-p)\le1/4\). Jensen gives \(\eta_0\ge\tau_{C_0}^2\). Integration yields a chord improvement \(2\eta_0d^2t(1-t)\). A stronger pointwise bound is \(\mathcal I_Y\ge d^2\sum_y\delta_{0,y}^2/[c_{0,y}+(a+td)\delta_{0,y}]\). The number of channels is unrestricted.

## 4. Arbitrary coherent physical reservoirs

Partition measured coordinates into any m nonempty blocks. Fix strict contractions \(B_i\) and nonzero guide vectors \(u_i\) on their blocks, with no eigenvector condition. Put
\[
 \alpha_i=(u_i^*B_i^{-1}u_i)^{-1},\quad
 \beta_i=(u_i^*(I-B_i)^{-1}u_i)^{-1},\quad \ell_i=\alpha_i+\beta_i,
\]
let U embed the guides, \(L=\operatorname{diag}(\ell_i)\), and \(A_0=\operatorname{diag}(\alpha_i)\). Define the physical affine path
\[
 \mathcal K_t=B+U(L^{1/2}M_tL^{1/2}-A_0)U^*.               \tag{12}
\]
Then \(0\le\mathcal K\le I\) **if and only if** \(0\le M\le I\). To see this, the disjoint supports give \(U^*B^{-1}U=A_0^{-1}\); positivity of \(B+UTU^*\), restricted after congruence to the guide range, is equivalent to \(T+A_0\ge0\). The complement similarly gives \(T\le\operatorname{diag}(\beta_i)\).

Its fixed local channel endpoints are
\[
 C_i(\cdot|0)=P_{B_i-\alpha_iu_iu_i^*},\qquad
 C_i(\cdot|1)=P_{B_i+\beta_iu_iu_i^*}.                      \tag{13}
\]
The exact law is \(P_{\mathcal K_t}=(\bigotimes_iC_i)P_{M_t}\). For a block inclusion set \(E_i\), let \(h_i(x)\) be its determinant in endpoint x and \(\Delta_i=h_i(1)-h_i(0)\). The block determinant lemma, expanded in principal minors, gives
\[
 \det(\mathcal K_E)=\sum_{J\subseteq[m]}\det(M_J)
                   \prod_{i\in J}\Delta_i\prod_{i\notin J}h_i(0)
 =\mathbb E_{P_M}\prod_i[h_i(0)+X_i\Delta_i].
\]
First use invertible baseline blocks, then polynomial continuation; empty inclusion sets have \(h_i(0)=1,\Delta_i=0\). Thus all inclusion laws agree. Equations (9)–(11) prove concavity of (12) on every seed segment (1)–(2). These reservoirs may be arbitrarily large, coherent, fractional, and unrelated to the guides. This statement concerns measured occupation entropy throughout.

## 7. Exact obstruction to universal low-rank ancillary refinement

**Proposition.** For a nonconstant affine Hermitian DPP path \(K+tA\) on an interval of positive length, the largest polynomial degree among its atom probabilities is exactly \(\operatorname{rank}A\).

**Proof.** Let \(r=\operatorname{rank}A\). Determinant multilinearity gives degree at most r for every inclusion moment and hence every atom. Some principal r-by-r minor of A is nonzero: the sum of these minors is the product of its nonzero eigenvalues, the nonzero coefficient of \(z^{n-r}\) in \(\det(zI+A)\). For that principal set S, \(\det(K_S+tA_S)\) has degree r. It is a sum of atom probabilities, so at least one atom has degree r. This proves equality. ∎

A fixed stochastic channel preserves the degree upper bound; so does a finite fixed mixture. Therefore no affine rank-at-most-r DPP source, or fixed mixture of such sources, can refine a target path with direction rank greater than r while recovering its measured law by any fixed coarse graining. This includes fixed independent ancillas and recoverable measured flags. It is an obstruction before entropy is considered.

The construction above respects this invariant: the physical and conditional nondegenerate directions still have rank three. Higher-rank tensor products retain a higher total degree, so controlling their separate factors cannot erase the total-rank issue. Nonlinear auxiliary paths, parameter-dependent labels, or entropy identities without recovery of the target law lie outside this proposition and would require their own acceleration and entropy corrections. Those are precise remaining alternatives; none is established here.
