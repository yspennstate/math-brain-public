# X051 — a block-count envelope in arbitrary dimension

Date: 2026-09-15. **COMPLETE bounded theorem.** The unrestricted Lyons conjecture remains open. All logarithms are natural, and every entropy below refers to measured occupation bits.

## 1. The new theorem

Partition the measured coordinates into blocks A,B of sizes p,q>=2. Let u,v be their normalized constant vectors, and set

    K = I_(p+q)/2 + [[0,c u v*],[conj(c) v u*,0]],
    0<rho=4|c|^2<1.                                               (1)

**Theorem.** For every Hermitian direction D=[[U,E],[E*,V]] satisfying

    tr U=tr V=0,

one has the strict full measured-entropy curvature bound

    -H''(K;D) >= FI_K(D)
      +4(1-rho)[alpha ||U||F^2+beta ||V||F^2+2 gamma ||E||F^2],      (2)
    alpha >= rho^2/(p^2 q), beta >= rho^2/(p q^2),
    gamma >= rho/(pq).

Here FI_K(D)=sum_S (p'_S)^2/p_S is the **entire atom Fisher information**. Thus the Hessian is negative definite on the (p+q)^2-2 dimensional space preserving both block traces. There is a relative neighborhood of every base (1), in the affine plane tr J_A=p/2, tr J_B=q/2, on which the actual entropy is strictly concave. Endpoints in a sufficiently small convex such neighborhood may have arbitrary diagonal imbalances, within-block coherences, and cross matrices of any rank.

For p=q=2, X030 already proves the stronger full Hessian theorem. The new scope here is arbitrary block sizes, especially p+q>=5, with simultaneous unrestricted trace-preserving changes in both blocks. Section 6 gives the exact remaining four-scalar form for the two omitted trace directions. No statement about arbitrary distant endpoints is inferred from the local theorem.

## 2. Physical symmetrization and the touching upper envelope

For any kernel J, let f_kl(J)=Pr_J(N_A=k,N_B=l), w_kl=binom(p,k)binom(q,l), and

    Phi(J)=-sum_kl f_kl log(f_kl/w_kl).

Uniformly and independently permuting the two occupation blocks is a fixed doubly stochastic classical channel, sending P_J to Q_J(S,T)=f_(|S|,|T|)/w_(|S|,|T|). Thus Q_J is majorized by P_J and

    Phi(J)-H(P_J)=D(P_J||Q_J)>=0.                                 (3)

It need not be a DPP at a general J. There is, however, an exact physical intermediate law on the larger scalar-block family

    J(a,b,c)=[[a I_p,c u v*],[conj(c) v u*,b I_q]],
    0<[[a,c],[conj(c),b]]<I_2.

Sample the two-site DPP with the displayed active kernel, append p-1 independent Bernoulli(a) bits to its first bit and q-1 independent Bernoulli(b) bits to its second bit, then independently uniformly permute the blocks. The output is exactly P_J. Indeed both constructions give

    p(S,T)=a^k(1-a)^(p-k)b^l(1-b)^(q-l)
      *[1-|c|^2(k-pa)(l-qb)/(pq a(1-a)b(1-b))].                    (4)

For the DPP this follows by a rank-one block determinant; for the channel it follows by averaging the locations of the two active bits, whose covariance is -|c|^2. Hence its entropy also has the lower envelope

    H(P_J)>=H(P_active)+(p-1)h(a)+(q-1)h(b).

This lower envelope need not touch the endpoints of an affine chord and is not used to infer Jensen. Prior ROOT_CONDITIONAL_SYMMETRIZATION.md already develops uniform-mode symmetrization with fixed coherent exteriors; the mechanism in this section is credited, not presented as a new standalone result.

At (1), write X=(2k-p)/p, Y=(2l-q)/q, pi_kl=w_kl/2^(p+q). Formula (4) becomes

    f_kl=pi_kl F, F=1-rho XY, p(S,T)=2^(-p-q)F.                    (5)

Thus Phi touches H there. Along every affine kernel tangent, exact differentiation of (3) gives

    (Phi-H)''=FI_atom-FI_count>=0.                                 (6)

All second atom derivatives cancel in this difference because log p is constant on each count fiber and sum_fiber p''=f''. The moving conditional measurement correction is exactly the conditional Fisher variance, not an omitted entropy term.

## 3. Six logarithmic coefficients and their signs

Under the independent fair-sign reference law, let

    Q_j^(p)(k)=binom(p,j)^(-1) sum_a (-1)^(j-a)binom(k,a)binom(p-k,j-a),

with Q_0=1 and Q_j=0 if j>p. This is the conditional mean of the product of j distinct signs given their count; Q_1=X. Write A_j=Q_j^(p), B_j=Q_j^(q), and let E_0 denote expectation with weights pi_kl. Define

    alpha=-E_0[A_2 log F], beta=-E_0[B_2 log F],
    gamma=-E_0[XY log F], delta=-E_0[A_3 Y log F],
    epsilon=-E_0[X B_3 log F], zeta=-E_0[A_2 B_2 log F].             (7)

All six are nonnegative. Expand -log F=sum_(m>=1)rho^m X^mY^m/m. Each relevant fair-sign moment is nonnegative: after expanding a power of a sum of signs, each monomial has a positive coefficient and expectation either zero or one. The expansion converges absolutely because rho<1. Its m=2,2,1 terms respectively give the three explicit lower bounds in (2).

We also have the decisive comparisons

    alpha-rho delta >=(1-rho)alpha,
    beta-rho epsilon >=(1-rho)beta,
    0<=zeta<=min(alpha,beta),
    gamma-rho zeta >=(1-rho)gamma.                                (8)

Here is a direct proof retaining the actual log-count potential. Its same-block second derivative is nonpositive,

    d^2/dX^2 log(1-rho XY)=-rho^2 Y^2/F^2,

and its mixed derivative is strictly negative,

    d^2/(dX dY) log(1-rho XY)=-rho/F^2.

Consequently the corresponding discrete second differences in any two distinct underlying signs have the same signs. If p>=3, alpha-rho delta equals

    -E_0[xi_1 xi_2 (1-rho xi_3 Y) log F].

Condition on all signs except xi_1,xi_2. The weight is independent of these two signs and at least 1-rho; multiplying their nonpositive second difference proves the first inequality. At p=2, delta=0 and it is immediate. The second inequality is identical. For zeta<=alpha use the nonnegative independent weight 1-eta_1 eta_2 in that same-block comparison; interchange blocks for zeta<=beta. Finally gamma-rho zeta equals

    -E_0[xi_1 eta_1 (1-rho xi_2 eta_2) log F].

Conditioning on the two selected signs and using the mixed second difference proves the last comparison. This is a positive finite-difference certificate, not an assumption of full atom majorization along kernel chords.

## 4. Exact count jets and the complete frozen acceleration

Only for computing Phi, choose fixed block-unitary coordinates in which u,v are the first basis vectors. Count laws are invariant under these changes; measured H is not assumed invariant. Decompose

    U=[[a,w*],[w,D0]], V=[[b,z*],[z,F0]],
    T=tr U, R=tr V, d=tr D0=T-a, e=tr F0=R-b.

For the within-block tangent E=0, the count generating polynomial is exactly

    det(h_x I+t(x-1)U)det(h_y I+t(y-1)V)
     -|c|^2(x-1)(y-1)det(h_x I+t(x-1)D0)det(h_y I+t(y-1)F0),
    h_x=(1+x)/2, h_y=(1+y)/2.

The cofactor identity behind the second term eliminates all active/dark off-diagonal entries w,z from its two determinants. Differentiating yields

    f'/pi=2[T X+R Y-rho d A_2 Y-rho e X B_2],
    f''/pi=4[(T^2-tr U^2)A_2+(R^2-tr V^2)B_2+2TR XY]
      -4rho[(d^2-tr D0^2)A_3Y+(e^2-tr F0^2)XB_3+2de A_2B_2].      (9)

Thus the full logarithmic acceleration, with no deletion, is

    sum f''log(f/w)=-4alpha(T^2-tr U^2)-4beta(R^2-tr V^2)-8gamma TR
      +4rho delta(d^2-tr D0^2)+4rho epsilon(e^2-tr F0^2)+8rho zeta de.
                                                                    (10)

For a pure cross tangent E, let E_dd=(I-uu*)E(I-vv*). Expansion of the block determinant by squared cross minors gives

    f''/pi=-8||E||F^2 XY+8rho||E_dd||F^2 A_2B_2,
    sum f''log(f/w)=8gamma||E||F^2-8rho zeta||E_dd||F^2.              (11)

For example, the only nonzero second derivative of a higher cross-minor coefficient at rank one is e_2(ZZ*)''=2|c|^2||E_dd||F^2. All e_j with j>=3 vanish through order two. The affine involution J -> I-diag(I_p,-I_q)Jdiag(I_p,-I_q) fixes (1), preserves Phi, fixes cross tangents and negates within-block tangents. Hence the complete Hessian has no mixed term between these sectors.

## 5. Proof of the theorem

Under T=R=0, one has d=-a,e=-b. Equation (10) becomes

    4[(alpha+rho delta)a^2+(alpha-rho delta)tr D0^2+2alpha||w||^2
      +(beta+rho epsilon)b^2+(beta-rho epsilon)tr F0^2+2beta||z||^2
      +2rho zeta ab].                                             (12)

Since delta,epsilon>=0 and zeta<=sqrt(alpha beta), the active scalar terms are at least (1-rho)(alpha a^2+beta b^2). By (8) the remaining terms in (12) therefore give

    sum f''log(f/w)>=4(1-rho)[alpha||U||F^2+beta||V||F^2].           (13)

Likewise (8) and ||E_dd||<=||E|| turn (11) into the lower bound 8(1-rho)gamma||E||F^2. Add the two sectors and use the exact normalized entropy calculus

    -Phi''=FI_count+sum f''log(f/w),
    -H''=-Phi''+FI_atom-FI_count.

This proves (2). Its constants are strictly positive, so continuity of the restricted Hessian gives the stated relative neighborhoods. Boundary limits are available for finite inequalities inside any such established concavity region, but no positive lower bound is claimed at rho=0 or rho=1.

## 6. The exact four-scalar obstruction to full ambient curvature

All remaining possible sign issues lie in U=a uu*+[d/(p-1)](I-uu*) and V=b vv*+[e/(q-1)](I-vv*). The dark traceless matrices and active/dark off-diagonal vectors decouple with positive coefficients 4(alpha-rho delta), 4(beta-rho epsilon), 8alpha, 8beta; all cross tangents were settled above. Put kappa_p=(p-2)/(p-1), kappa_q=(q-2)/(q-1). The full count-envelope Hessian is nonpositive if and only if, for every real a,d,b,e,

    Q_pq,rho(a,d,b,e)
      =4 E_0[((a+d)X+(b+e)Y-rho d A_2Y-rho e XB_2)^2/F]
       -8alpha ad-4kappa_p(alpha-rho delta)d^2
       -8beta be-4kappa_q(beta-rho epsilon)e^2
       -8gamma(a+d)(b+e)+8rho zeta de >=0.                          (14)

This is an explicit four-by-four real quadratic form consisting of actual count Fisher information and the full acceleration. It is proved above on a+d=b+e=0. X030/X044 settle the p=q=2 case by a different scalar certificate. A proof of (14) for arbitrary p,q would promote this report to a full ambient Hessian theorem at every base (1); that general sign is **unproved here**. Even that result would remain local and would not establish unrestricted Lyons concavity.
