## 1. Complete conditional calculus

    K=[[a,v*],[v,R]], A=[[d,w*],[w,C]], b=1-a, q=ab,
    J1=R-vv*/a, J0=R+vv*/b, D=J0-J1=vv*/q.

Every J_s is a strict two-site contraction, by Schur complementation in K and
I-K. With H2 the measured two-site entropy, put

    E1=C-(wv*+vw*)/a+d vv*/a²,
    E0=C+(wv*+vw*)/b+d vv*/b²,
    G=grad H2(J1)-grad H2(J0), T=d C-ww*.

The full identity from C057, obtained by twice differentiating
H3(K)=h(a)+a H2(J1)+b H2(J0), is

    Q3(A):=-H3''(A)=d²/q+a Q1(E1)+b Q0(E0)-2tr(GT),       (1)
    Q_s(E)=-H2''_(Js)(E).

Indeed J1''=-2(w-dv/a)(w-dv/a)*/a and
J0''=2(w+dv/b)(w+dv/b)*/b. The changing-weight terms cancel against these
accelerations to leave exactly 2tr(GT) in H3''. They are never discarded.
X054 (5)–(8) proves Q_s>=0 in every Hermitian direction: its conditional-count
bound dominates 2 lambda det E when det E>0; if det E<=0, the exact identity
Q_s=FI_s-2lambda det E is already nonnegative. Thus only the last term in (1)
requires a new payment.

## 3. Exact positive-minus-rank-one structure of every two-site secant

Assume v!=0, and write J=J1 and D=uu*, u=v/sqrt(q). For each of the four
original two-site atoms x, let p_x(s)=P_(J+sD)(x), p_x=p_x(0),
tilde p_x=p_x(1), and Y_x=(J-I_(x^c))^-1. Set

    LM(x,y)=(y-x)/(log y-log x), LM(x,x)=x,
    P=sum_x [p_x²/LM(p_x,tilde p_x)] Y_x D Y_x >=0,
    Lbar=integral_0^1 log[p10(s)p01(s)/(p00(s)p11(s))] ds>=0.

**Lemma 1.** The actual measured entropy gradient secant is exactly

    G=P-Lbar adj D.                                      (4)

Thus its entire adverse part has rank at most one and is transverse to v;
in particular G>=-Lbar adj D. This is stronger structural information than
ordinary concavity, while still permitting C057's negative-gradient witness.

**Proof.** Let Phi_J(B)=sum_x p_x Y_x B Y_x. Differentiating normalization
twice gives FI_J(B,E)=tr(E Phi_J(B)); the map Phi is completely positive.
For two sites, -H2''(E)=FI_J(E)-2lambda(J)det E, where
lambda(J)=log(p10 p01/(p00 p11)). Polarization yields

    -D(grad H2)(J)[D]=Phi_J(D)-lambda(J)adj D.              (5)

Integrate along J+sD. The rank-one inverse formula gives
Y_x(s)u=Y_x u/(1+s m_x), p_x(s)=p_x(1+s m_x),
where m_x=u*Y_xu. Thus the integral of p_x(s)Y_x(s)DY_x(s)
is p_x log(1+m_x)Y_xDY_x/m_x, exactly the summand in P, with its continuous
value if m_x=0. Integrating (5) proves (4). ∎

Everything in (4) can be evaluated from the two endpoint conditional laws:
if B(x,y)=(y log y-x log x)/(y-x)-1, with B(x,x)=log x, then
Lbar=B(p10,tilde p10)+B(p01,tilde p01)
      -B(p00,tilde p00)-B(p11,tilde p11).
No unmeasured eigenbasis entropy is substituted in this formula.

There is also a **sharp** one-dimensional cost. Put alpha=tr(PD)>0 and

    U=PDP/alpha >=0, rank U=1,
    gamma=Lbar-det(P)/alpha. Then G=U-gamma adj D.          (4a)

To prove this, complete the two-by-two Gram square in a basis whose first
vector is u/||u||; it gives P=U+[det(P)/alpha]adj D exactly. Positivity of
alpha follows from (3): a nonzero D changes the count mean, so its Fisher
information is positive. Therefore gamma_+=max(gamma,0) is the smallest
nonnegative beta for which G+beta adj D>=0. Sufficiency follows from (4a);
necessity follows by testing the line perpendicular to the range of U, on
which adj D is positive. This costs only the actual transverse defect,
instead of its generally larger upper bound Lbar.

## 4. What a nonlinear simultaneous Schur affinization cannot remove

**Lemma 2 (kinematic obstruction).** Consider any C² curve K(t) through K
with velocity A, with arbitrary pivot, incident, and exterior accelerations.
If both conditional kernels J0(t), J1(t) have zero second derivative at zero,
then w is complex-collinear with v (real-collinear in the real case).
If both are affine on an interval, their nonzero incident direction is fixed
on that interval.

**Proof.** Normalize u(t)=v(t)/sqrt(a(t)(1-a(t))). Exactly,
J0(t)-J1(t)=u(t)u(t)*. Let Pi project orthogonally to v at zero. Then

    Pi[J0''-J1'']Pi=2Pi u' u'^*Pi
                       =(2/q)Pi ww*Pi.                  (6)

Neither a'', v'', nor R'' appears in this identity. Vanishing of both Schur
accelerations forces Pi w=0. On an interval, an affine positive rank-one
matrix has constant range: if two endpoint ranges differ, their strictly
positive convex combination has rank at least two. Apply this to J0-J1. ∎

For dense real three-site K write Aij=u_ij Kij. At pivot k the obstruction
vanishes exactly when its two incident radial ratios agree. Choosing three
distinct ratios defeats simultaneous conditional affinization at every pivot,
irrespective of moving pivot or exterior choices. This is not a concavity
counterexample; it rules out the proposed free-affinity step.
