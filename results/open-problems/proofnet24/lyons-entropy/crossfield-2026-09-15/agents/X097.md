## 1. Exact hyperbolic form for arbitrary spectator

Set t=1-2x, l=1-2lambda, X=atanh(t), L=atanh(l), C=log cosh(X-L). Direct differentiation of the four categorical atoms gives

    f_x = X+L-l C,      f_lambda = X+L-t C,
    J11=2(1-2tl+l²)/[(1-t²)(1-tl)],
    J12=2/(1-tl)-2C,
    J22=2(1-2tl+t²)/[(1-l²)(1-tl)].

These identities expose simultaneous leaf activity as a common translation of X,L; the entropy Hessian is NOT translation invariant. In particular, shifting both hyperbolic coordinates leaves C unchanged but changes every rational Hessian entry and the two coefficient factors t,l in the gradient. Thus directly transporting the half-spectator scalar lemma is insufficient.

## 2. A flexible centered-gradient endpoint certificate

Write p_j=f_x(x_j,lambda), r_j=f_lambda(x_j,lambda), with x_1<x_0; g=p_1-p_0>0, k=r_1-r_0, d=x_0-x_1, s=1+dg/2. For any real constants c,tau, define

    psi_j=(p_j-c,r_j-tau),
    F_j=psi_j dot (e_j,delta),
    V=sqrt(g/(2d))(e_0-e_1),
    b=(p_1+p_0-2c)sqrt(d/(2g)).

Then exactly

    h=g(e_1+e_0)/2+k delta = F_1-F_0+b V.

Consequently, if a number kappa>=0 satisfies the two endpoint matrix inequalities

    psi_j psi_j^T <= kappa J_j (j=0,1),
    kappa+b² <= s,

then X085 equation (5) holds. This follows from |F_1-F_0|<=sqrt(kappa)(sqrt(Q_1)+sqrt(Q_0)), followed by the two-dimensional Cauchy inequality. Unlike a fixed entropy-gradient centering, the two common centers c,tau can adapt to the endpoint pair. The freedom in tau exploits the physically shared spectator velocity; its contribution cancels exactly between endpoints. The condition may be stronger than equation (5); equivalence is not asserted here.

The remaining work is to select and control these centers for every lambda, or identify the exact obstruction to this certificate.
