# X080 — real-form nonlinear Fisher payment with a blind three-dimensional sector

15 September 2026. **Completed constructive theorem.** The full
Hermitian scalar-base entropy Hessian is nonpositive for every phase-real
rank-three frame whose first measurement has moment at least `1/8` only on
the five-dimensional real traceless symmetric sector. Its other three
traceless directions are exactly Fisher-blind, but have a favorable frozen
logarithmic curvature. A `5/16` real projective design plus arbitrary real
Parseval contamination therefore suffices. An explicit fifteen-ray family
escapes the existing all-Hermitian moment, entropy-isotropy, X008 anisotropy,
and X028 overlap certificates. The unrestricted complex-frame problem and
generic four-row rank-three problem remain open.

This is a real-form extension of the completed X075 theorem, using its
conditional-frame proof on an invariant sector. X004/X008 provide the exact
scalar curvature and exterior positivity; X062 provides the conditional
trace bound. The distinction from X075 is essential: every frame in the
present class has a three-dimensional nullspace in its measured Fisher
operator and fails X075's hypothesis on all traceless Hermitian matrices.

## 1. The exact blind-sector decomposition

A frame is phase-real if, after one fixed unitary change of active coordinates
and individual measured-row phases, all its rows are real. Such coordinate
choices do not change the measured DPP: the active unitary is absorbed into
the variable active covariance, and row phases conjugate its measured kernel
by a diagonal unitary. Work in these real coordinates.

Let the rows be `sqrt(t_i)n_i^T`, `sum_i t_i n_i n_i^T=I_3`. Define the
real symmetric matrices and the measured forms as in X004/X008:

    W=sum_{i<j} b_ij c_ij P_ij,
    c_ij=log[t_i t_j/b_ij], T_s=W+s tau I, q=1-s,
    F_v(A)=sum_i t_i(n_i^T A n_i)^2,
    F_w(A)=sum_ij b_ij[tr(P_ij A)]^2,
    -H''_s(A)=F_v(A)/s+F_w(A)/q-2tr(T_s adj A).       (1)

Here `P_ij` is the real rank-one projection perpendicular to the pair,
`b_ij=t_i t_j(1-(n_i^T n_j)^2)`, and `tau` is the actual triple logarithmic
coefficient of X004. Omit all zero effects. The supplied finite exterior
identities, including all supported triple weights, give

    0<=W<=(log2)I, W+tau I>=0,
    tr W+3tau<=3log2, hence T_s>=0.                   (2)

Write an arbitrary Hermitian active direction uniquely as `A=X+iY`, where
`X` is real symmetric and `Y` real skew-symmetric. Choose the real vector y
such that `Y z=y cross z`. Then `Y^2=y y^T-|y|^2 I`, and the polynomial
identity `adj A=A^2-(tr A)A+[(tr A)^2-tr A^2]I/2` proves

    F_v(A)=F_v(X), F_w(A)=F_w(X),
    adj(iY)=-y y^T,
    tr(T_s adj(X+iY))=tr(T_s adj X)-y^T T_s y.

For the last identity, the mixed term is imaginary and skew-symmetric, so
its trace against real symmetric `T_s` is zero. Consequently

    -H''_s(X+iY)=-H''_s(X)+2y^T T_s y.              (3)

This is exact. In particular it does not replace a positive matrix inside a
trace against an indefinite adjugate. All three imaginary-skew directions
have zero measured Fisher information; their curvature comes entirely from
the frozen logarithmic acceleration. Also `||iY||_F^2=2|y|^2`.

## 2. Full real-sector theorem, retaining the trace square

**Theorem.** Suppose

    F_v(B)>=(1/8)||B||_F^2                           (4)

for every real symmetric traceless `B`. Then every Hermitian `A=X+iY`
satisfies, at every `0<s<1`,

    -H''_s(A)>=(1/26)||X||_F^2+2y^T T_s y>=0.       (5)

Thus the full active Hessian is nonpositive. It is negative definite if
`T_s>0`; the quantitative real-sector assumption alone is not used to assert
a uniform positive imaginary-sector metric.

Here is the complete invariant-sector version of X075's argument. Put
`Q_i=I-n_i n_i^T` and `B_i=Q_i B Q_i`. Because all rows are real, these
compressions remain in the real symmetric sector, where (4) applies. If the
real traceless gap is gamma, trace removal gives

    F_v(Z)>=gamma tr(Z^2)+(1-gamma)(tr Z)^2/3

for every real symmetric Z. The conditional rank-two frame on `ran Q_i`
has Fisher form

    F_i(B_i)=sum_{j:d_ij>0} t_j(n_j^T B_i n_j)^2/d_ij
             >=F_v(B_i), d_ij=1-(n_i^T n_j)^2.

Parallel omitted rows have zero numerator. The actual conditional-frame
contractions of X062, obtained by expanding Gram–Schmidt measurements, are

    sum_i t_i F_i(B_i)=2F_w(B),
    sum_i t_i(tr B_i)^2=(tr B)^2+F_v(B),
    sum_i t_i tr(B_i^2)=tr(B^2)+F_v(B).

Combining these identities proves, for real traceless B,

    F_w(B)>=gamma(2+gamma)||B||^2/3.

At `gamma=1/8`, the full two-layer Fisher is therefore

    FI_s(B)>= [1/(8s)+17/(192q)]||B||^2.             (6)

For completeness, the contraction for the first line follows by putting
`x_i=n_i^T B n_i`, `y_ij=tr(P_ij B)`, and `h=tr B`. The normalized
projected row measures `h-x_i-y_ij`. Expand
`sum_i!=j b_ij(h-x_i-y_ij)^2` and use
`sum_j b_ij=2t_i`, `sum_j b_ij y_ij=t_i(h-x_i)`, and
`sum_i t_i x_i=h`. This gives `2F_w(B)` with all cross terms retained.

Put `L=log2`, `c=tr W/3`, `E=W-cI`, `d=c+s tau`, and
`L_s=1/(sq)-2d`. Equation (2) gives

    0<=d<=L, tr E^2<=2L^2/3,
    lambda_max(T_s)<=(1+2s/3)L, L_s>=4-2L>13/5.     (7)

The eigenvalues of a real traceless B are, up to an overall sign,
`a,b,-a-b`, with `a,b>=0`. Thus `tr((adj B)_+)=ab<=||B||^2/6`.
Since the actual `T_s` is positive,

    2tr(T_s adj B)<=(1+2s/3)L||B||^2/3.             (8)

Now write `X=(h/3)I+B`, where `tr B=0`. The exact trace completion is

    -H''_s(X)=Q_s(B)+(L_s/3)[h+tr(E B)/L_s]^2,
    Q_s(B)=FI_s(B)-2tr(T_s adj B)
                         -[tr(E B)]^2/(3L_s).       (9)

In particular the trace/traceless coupling has not been discarded.
The following rational inequality is valid throughout `0<s<1`:

    1/(8s)+17/(192q)>=19/60+7s/45.                  (10)

After multiplication by `2880sq`, its numerator is
`P(s)=360-1017s+464s^2+448s^3`. For `r=s-1/2>=-1/2`,

    P(s)=47/2-217r+1136r^2+448r^3
          >=47/2-217r+912r^2>0,

because `4*912*(47/2)-217^2=38639>0`. Using `L<7/10`, (6)-(10)
give

    Q_s(B)>[1/12-49/1170]||B||^2>||B||^2/25.

Also `[tr(E B)/L_s]^2<||B||^2/20`. If
`u=h+tr(E B)/L_s`, then

    ||X||^2<=31||B||^2/30+2u^2/3,
    -H''_s(X)>=||B||^2/25+13u^2/15>=||X||^2/26.

This proves the real-sector part of (5), and (3) finishes its full-Hermitian
statement. The elementary bound `log2<7/10` follows from the positive
exponential series; no external design or operator theorem is imported.

## 3. A robust real-design theorem, with an explicit imaginary metric

A weighted real projective 2-design in dimension three means precisely

    F_D(X)=[(tr X)^2+2tr(X^2)]/5

for every real symmetric X. If `V_R` is any real Parseval frame, then

    V=[sqrt(theta)V_D ; sqrt(1-theta)V_R],
    theta>=5/16,                                    (11)

satisfies (4), since its real traceless moment is at least `2theta/5`.
No restriction on the row count, overlap, nearparallel rays, or anisotropy
of the arbitrary real contaminating frame is needed.

For an explicit design, put `phi=(1+sqrt5)/2` and take the six rows

    (0,1,phi), (0,1,-phi), (1,phi,0), (1,-phi,0),
    (phi,0,1), (phi,0,-1),

each divided by `sqrt[2(1+phi^2)]`. The rows are Parseval and have weight
`1/2`; their distinct normalized squared overlaps are `1/5`. The relation
`phi^2=phi+1`, followed by pairwise sign cancellation, verifies
`F_D(X)=[(tr X)^2+2tr(X^2)]/5` directly. Thus

    W_D=log(5/4) I.

The pairs entirely inside the design part of (11) contribute
`theta^2 W_D`; every other contribution to W is positive. From (2),

    T_s=qW+s(W+tau I)>=q theta^2 log(5/4) I.

For this six-row design and every arbitrary real contaminating Parseval
frame, the full result therefore has the explicit strict bound

    -H''_s(A)>=min{1/26, q theta^2 log(5/4)}||A||_F^2. (12)

This holds for all Hermitian directions, including all three Fisher-blind
ones. It is uniform over the contaminating frame. The displayed imaginary
constant depends on s and tends to zero as s tends to one; a uniform positive
constant for that entire endpoint interval is not asserted. Every fixed
scalar base has a neighborhood of strict full-Hessian concavity by the
supported-atom continuity argument in X004.

## 4. An explicit family outside the earlier certificates

Fix `theta=5/16`, `rho=11/16`, and `0<epsilon<=10^-4`. As the contaminating
frame take the eight planar rows

    (1/2)(cos(j pi/4+sigma epsilon),
          sin(j pi/4+sigma epsilon),0),
    j=0,1,2,3, sigma=+1,-1,

together with the row `e3^T`. Each four-row planar subframe has operator
`I_2/2`, so their union plus `e3` is Parseval. Stack it with the six design
rows according to (11). There are fifteen distinct rays for sufficiently
small positive epsilon.

This frame is invariant, up to row permutations and signs, under each
coordinate sign change. Thus W is diagonal. Write
`Delta=W33-W11-W22`. The following exact calculation at epsilon zero uses
parallel merging, which leaves W unchanged. Set

    alpha=(5-sqrt5)/10, beta=1-alpha,
    delta_±=(1±2/sqrt5)/2, f(x)=-x log x,
    H_alpha=f(alpha)+f(beta), H_delta=f(delta_+)+f(delta_-),
    C=[H_delta+(3alpha-2)log(1/(1-alpha/2))
                       +(3beta-2)log(1/(1-beta/2))]/2-H_alpha.

Grouping the within-design pairs, the within-planar pairs, the cross pairs
with the two axis-aligned planar rays, the two diagonal planar rays, and e3
gives

    Delta(0)=-theta^2 log(5/4)+rho^2(log2)/2+theta rho C. (13)

The axis-aligned planar cross contributions cancel in Delta. The within-
planar entropy coefficient is `(log2)/2`. Each diagonal planar ray gives
the two `delta_±` values and the displayed alpha/beta terms. The e3 cross
pairs contribute `-H_alpha`. This accounts for every pair in (13).

The exact rational logarithm certificate in `certificate.py` proves

    C>-67/125, log(5/4)<224/1000, log2>693/1000,
    Delta(0)>13693/512000.

These bounds use `log x=2 sum_{k>=0} z^(2k+1)/(2k+1)` for
`z=(x-1)/(x+1)`, with the explicit geometric tail bound, and rational
upper/lower bounds for sqrt5. In particular this is a finite rational
certificate, not a floating-point sign assertion.

There is a simple uniform perturbation estimate. For an evolving pair of
unit real rays with each speed at most one, write `v=n_i cross n_j`,
`d=|v|^2`, `J=diag(-1,-1,1)`. Its contribution to Delta is
`t_i t_j[-log d] v^T J v`. Since `|v'|<=2`,

    |d/d epsilon {[-log d] v^T J v}|
       <=4 sqrt(d)(1-log d)<=8/sqrt(e)<5.

At d=0 the same bound follows by continuity. The total pair weight is at
most `(sum t_i)^2/2=9/2`. Hence

    |Delta(epsilon)-Delta(0)|<23epsilon,
    Delta(epsilon)>1/50 for 0<epsilon<=10^-4.          (14)

Thus W is not scalar. Moreover, if `c=tr W/3` and `e_max` is the largest
eigenvalue of `W-cI`, then `c-2e_max=-Delta<0`. X008's frame-only
anisotropy criterion fails, since its `d_-<=c` and its additional variance
term is nonnegative. Its pointwise criterion also fails for every
`0<s<=1/100`: by (2), `tau<=log2`, so
`d-2e_max=-Delta+s tau<-1/50+7/1000<0`.

Choose concretely `epsilon=10^-8`. A matched pair of planar rays has
`d=sin^2(2epsilon)<=4epsilon^2`, and each row has total weight `rho/4=11/64`.
Thus its contribution alone to X028's overlap row bound is

    R>=(11/64)log[1/(4epsilon^2)]>56/25.

For an elementary certificate, `epsilon<2^-20` gives the logarithm at least
`38log2>76/3`, and hence `R>209/48`. Therefore the X028 overlap hypothesis
fails. All these frames have zero Fisher on every imaginary-skew direction,
so the all-Hermitian moment hypotheses of X062/X075 fail identically. The
full-Hermitian theorem (12) still applies.

The novelty claim is limited to this proved class and its stated escape
from those particular sufficient criteria. No claim of historical priority
or exclusion from every theorem in the wider literature is made.

## 5. Unresolved route and verification scope

The first attempted general route retained the exact pair identity

    tr(W adj A)=sum_i<j t_i t_j c_ij
             [(n_i* A n_i)(n_j* A n_j)-|n_i* A n_j|^2].

This is the identity already present in X028, not a new result. It implies
that the pair potential is nonpositive on the nullspace of `F_v`, but it
does not prove any uniform quantitative Fisher payment. The unrestricted
first separate comparison is false by X032. For a generic four-row frame
with Gram projection `I-u u*`, the minors are `1-a_i`, `1-a_i-a_j`, and
`a_l`, where `a_i=|u_i|^2`; those explicit formulas did not close the full
six-real-direction comparison in this attempt.

The proved result instead uses a real-form symmetry to identify an entire
blind sector with favorable logarithmic payment and applies the conditional
joint Fisher inequality only on its complementary real sector. The exact
remaining general-frame lemma is still X008's eight-dimensional Schur form
`Q_s(B)>=0`, without a real-form or moment-gap hypothesis. Nothing here
proves arbitrary nonscalar covariance concavity or the full Lyons conjecture.

`verify.py` checks the design moments, exact real/imaginary decomposition,
complete real trace square, and full nine-dimensional Hessian bounds on
specified structured frames. It also independently differentiates the odds
atom probabilities using supported row subspaces. On the fifteen-ray
example, at s=0.01, 0.1, 0.5, and 0.9, the largest independent atom-curvature
identity error was below `1.9e-13`. The explicit example has
`W=diag(0.1481934289,0.1351598719,0.3104514806)`,
`tau=0.05842419946`, and measured overlap row maximum `7.225285591`.
These numerical values are not used in the proof: `certificate.py` supplies
exact rational logarithmic and perturbation certificates for (13)-(14).
Results are retained in `results.json`, `explicit_results.json`, and
`certificate.json`. Numerical checks are supporting algebra checks, not
universal proof.
