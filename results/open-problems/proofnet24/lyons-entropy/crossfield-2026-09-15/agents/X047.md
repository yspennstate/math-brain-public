# X047 — synchronized moving leaves and a compensated sequential sampler

15 September 2026. **Status: COMPLETE bounded theorem; exact verification passed.** Full Lyons concavity remains open. This is a full-domain family in arbitrary dimension, with moving Bernoulli means and directions of arbitrarily large mixed inertia. No historical priority outside the supplied record is asserted.

## 1. The full-domain theorem

Fix an integer m≥1, a strict Hermitian reservoir 0<D<I_r, and a nonzero vector u∈C^r. In the measured coordinate basis, put

    K(p,z) = [[p I_m,             z 1_m u*],
              [conj(z) u 1_m*,    D       ]].                 (1)

The m leaves are independent in their marginal law. Their common mean p and common complex attachment z vary; D and u remain fixed. No eigenvector relation between D and u is assumed.

**Theorem 1.** H(P_K(p,z)) is jointly concave on the entire closed positive-contraction domain of (1). Every nonconstant admissible affine chord in (p,Re z,Im z) has a strictly positive interior Jensen gap.

With s=|z|², a_D=u*D^(-1)u, and b_D=u*(I-D)^(-1)u, the two Schur complements give the exact strict physical domain

    0<p<1,     m s a_D<p,     m s b_D<1-p.                   (2)

This is convex in (p,s), s≥0. In kernel coordinates (p,z), convexity also follows from the affine matrix (1).

For m≤2 this is contained in the prior free-pair/fixed-reservoir theorem C062. The additional scope is m≥3: the moving independent block has dimension m. If p'=α≠0 and z'=η≠0, the direction has rank m+1 and, after possibly reversing sign, inertia (m positive, one negative, r−1 zero). C061 fixes each reservoir up to a rank-one update, whereas pI_m here has a rank-m derivative. The proof below is self-contained and does not import either result.

## 2. The actual two-stage sampler

Sample m independent Bernoulli(p) leaves X_i, then sample the reservoir from its exact conditional DPP. Define

    R_x = sum_i [x_i/p − (1−x_i)/(1−p)].

The signed determinant Schur complement gives

    K_(reservoir | leaves=x)=D−s R_x uu*.                   (3)

Every conditional matrix in (3) is a strict contraction under (2). For a reservoir atom y define

    d_y=P_D(y)>0,
    a_y = (d/dv) P_(D+v uu*)(y)|_(v=0).

A determinant with a rank-one update is affine in v. Thus its actual conditional atom probability is

    q_y(x;p,s)=d_y−s a_y R_x>0,
    H(P_K)=m h(p)+E_p[−sum_y q_y(X) log q_y(X)].             (4)

Here sum_y d_y=1, sum_y a_y=0, and E_p R_X=0. Consequently E_p q_y=d_y stays fixed as p and s move. This cancellation includes the moving leaf weights.

Along the actual affine kernel line p'=α, z'=η,

    s'=σ=2 Re(conj(z)η),      s''=2|η|²,
    R'_x=−α sum_i [x_i/p²+(1−x_i)/(1−p)²],
    q'_y=−a_y(σR_x+sR'_x),
    q''_y=−a_y(2|η|²R_x+2σR'_x+sR''_x).                  (5)

All these derivatives, together with both derivatives of the leaf-history probabilities, are incorporated below. Neither conditional kernels nor Bernoulli weights are treated as affine.

## 3. Laplace compensation and exact accumulated squares

For real λ define

    F(p,s;λ)=p exp(λs/p)+(1−p)exp(−λs/(1−p)).              (6)

Independence in the first stage gives, for t>0,

    E_p exp(−t q_y(X))=exp(−t d_y) F(p,s;t a_y)^m.          (7)

This integrates the entire moving first stage before assigning a sign. For any real α,σ and fixed λ, direct differentiation gives the exact scalar perspective square

    D²F[(α,σ)]
      = λ² { exp(λs/p)/p · (σ−αs/p)²
             + exp(−λs/(1−p))/(1−p) · (σ+αs/(1−p))² }
      =: V_λ ≥0.                                          (8)

Indeed each summand is a perspective a exp(b/a), whose second derivative is exp(b/a)(b'−a'b/a)²/a. Also

    F_s=λ[exp(λs/p)−exp(−λs/(1−p))]≥0                    (9)

for every real λ and s≥0. Along an actual affine kernel line put

    W_λ=V_λ+2|η|²F_s,       dot F=αF_p+σF_s.              (10)

Both terms of W_λ are nonnegative. The second is the retained acceleration of |z|². The complete power satisfies

    (F^m)''=m F^(m−1)W_λ + m(m−1)F^(m−2)(dot F)².         (11)

The last term is the compensated cross-stage contribution of distinct identical leaves. It is a square because the leaf parameters move synchronously. Individual reveal levels need not have a sign, consistent with X010 and C068.

**Theorem 2 (exact nonnegative curvature integral).** At every strict point,

    −H'' = m α²/[p(1−p)]
      +sum_y integral_0^infinity exp(−t d_y)/t²
         {m F_y^(m−1)W_(t a_y)
           +m(m−1)F_y^(m−2)(dot F_y)²} dt ≥0,               (12)

where F_y=F(p,s;t a_y). For m=1 the second term in braces is zero. This is the complete atom Fisher plus frozen-log acceleration, regrouped by the sampler; the full Fisher is not replaced by leaf-score information.

**Proof.** The following elementary identity follows by matching second derivative 1/x and value and slope at x=1:

    x log x−x+1
      = integral_0^infinity [exp(−tx)−exp(−t)
                            +(x−1)t exp(−t)]/t² dt.         (13)

Apply (13) to every q_y in (4). Its affine correction disappears after two derivatives because E_p q_y=d_y is fixed. Substitute (7), then (10)–(11), and add −m h''(p)α². This proves (12). On a compact neighborhood of a strict point, all conditional atoms have a common positive lower bound. At infinity, differentiated (7) is a finite sum of uniformly decaying exponentials times polynomials in t. At zero, F=1+O(t²), dot F=O(t²), W=O(t²). These bounds justify both differentiations under the integral.

With s affine, omit 2|η|²F_s from (10). The same argument proves joint concavity of the conditional entropy in (p,s); (9) proves it is nonincreasing in s. Substituting the convex |z|² therefore preserves concavity. Equivalently integrate (12) along a strict chord. For boundary endpoints use K_ρ=ρK+(1−ρ)diag(I_m/2,D), 0<ρ<1. These remain in the family, are strict, and converge to K. Continuity of determinant probabilities and −x log x proves the closed-domain statement. ∎

## 4. Finite quantitative gap and a coherent five-site example

Let A_1=sum_y|a_y|>0. Positivity follows because the inclusion marginal of reservoir coordinate j has rank-one derivative |u_j|², and u≠0. At fixed p, direct differentiation of (4) and Cauchy–Schwarz give

    −H_ss=E R_X² sum_y a_y²/q_y(X)
           ≥A_1² E R_X²=m A_1²/[p(1−p)],                 (14)

using sum_y q_y(X)=1. Also H_s(p,0)=0. For endpoints define p_θ=(1−θ)p_0+θp_1, z_θ=(1−θ)z_0+θz_1, s_θ=|z_θ|², and bar s=(1−θ)|z_0|²+θ|z_1|². Joint strength concavity and the integral of (14) yield

    H(K_θ)−(1−θ)H(K_0)−θH(K_1)
      ≥m[h(p_θ)−(1−θ)h(p_0)−θh(p_1)]
        +m A_1²/[2p_θ(1−p_θ)] · (bar s²−s_θ²).            (15)

Indeed first compare H(p_θ,bar s) with the endpoint interpolation, then integrate −H_s≥m A_1²s/[p_θ(1−p_θ)] from s_θ to bar s. Since bar s−s_θ=θ(1−θ)|z_1−z_0|², the second term is positive for different z endpoints; the first is positive for different p endpoints. Boundary limits prove the same strictness. The displayed denominator is used when 0<p_θ<1; if p is identically zero or one, feasibility forces z=0 and the family is constant.

An explicit example has m=3 and

    D=[[2/5,1/10+i/20],[1/10−i/20,3/5]],   u=(1,1/2)^T,
    (p_0,z_0)=(1/3,(2+i)/20),
    (p_1,z_1)=(2/3,(3+2i)/20).                            (16)

Here a_D=240/91 and b_D=20/7. The two rational margins in (2) are respectively (64/273,47/84) and (43/105,23/420), so both endpoints are strict. The direction has inertia (3,1,1) and rank four; D does not commute with uu*. Its complex attachment never vanishes along its real affine line, so this chord has no diagonal measured-basis intersection. This is a mixed-inertia example with coherent exterior beyond the fixed-pair scope.

## 5. Exact remaining inequality and verification

Synchronization is used at (11). If leaf means p_i and strengths s_i move independently while retaining guide u, replace F^m by G_y=product_i F(p_i,s_i;t a_y). Write l_i=dot F_i/F_i and w_i=F_i''/F_i, including s_i''=2|η_i|². The same exact sampler calculation gives

    −H''=sum_i α_i²/[p_i(1−p_i)]
       +sum_y integral_0^infinity exp(−td_y)G_y/t²
           [sum_i w_i+2sum_(i<j)l_i l_j] dt.               (17)

Each w_i≥0, but mixed products can have either sign. The precise remaining compensation inequality is

    −2sum_y integral exp(−td_y)G_y/t²
         sum_(i<j: l_i l_j<0)l_i l_j dt
      ≤sum_i α_i²/[p_i(1−p_i)]
        +sum_y integral exp(−td_y)G_y/t²
           [sum_i w_i+2sum_(i<j: l_i l_j≥0)l_i l_j] dt.    (18)

No universal nonnegativity of the individual Laplace integrands is asserted. Moving D also introduces nonfixed d_y and a_y terms. Arbitrary three-site mixed-inertia directions, unsynchronized moving leaves, and full Lyons concavity remain unresolved.
