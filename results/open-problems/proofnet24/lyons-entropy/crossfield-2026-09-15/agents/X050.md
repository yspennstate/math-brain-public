## Exact conditional-volume bound

Write `L=K(I-K)^(-1)>0`, so `p_S=det(I-K)det L_S`. For an outside configuration T and distinct i,j, the second difference of the frozen potential `f=-log p` is

`h_ij(T)=log[p_Ti p_Tj/(p_T p_Tij)]=-log(1-|b12|^2/(b11 b22))>=0`,

where B is the two-by-two Schur complement of `L_T` in `L_(Tij)`. This is an exact ratio of conditional volume weights, not an eigenvalue substitution for measured entropy.

The eigenvalues of K are `1/2 +/- sigma_1(Z), 1/2 +/- sigma_2(Z)`: squaring its centered block matrix proves this directly. Thus the eigenvalues of L lie in `[m,M]`, where `m=(1-sqrt(x))/(1+sqrt(x))` and `M=1/m`. Principal restriction preserves these quadratic-form bounds. The variational identity `v*Bv=min_u (u,v)*L_(Tij)(u,v)` proves `mI<=B<=MI`; the upper bound uses u=0. If the eigenvalues of B are mu,nu, then `b11 b22<=(mu+nu)^2/4`, so

`|b12|^2/(b11 b22) <= ((nu-mu)/(nu+mu))^2 <= ((M-m)/(M+m))^2 =4x/(1+x)^2`.

Consequently every face has `0<=h_ij(T)<=g(x):=4 atanh(x)`.

The identities `I-K=DKD` and `P_(I-K)(S)=P_K(S^c)`, together with diagonal-gauge invariance of every principal minor, give `p_S=p_(S^c)`. Hence f is an even Boolean polynomial. Its centered form is

`f=c+sum_(i<j) alpha_ij (X_i-1/2)(X_j-1/2)+tau product_i(X_i-1/2)`.

Here `alpha_ij` is the average of its four face values h_ij and tau is their alternating sum. The four centered values of the other two bits are +/-1/2, so these assertions follow by applying the two Boolean differences to the displayed expansion. Since the h values are nonnegative,

`|tau|/4 <= alpha_ij <= g(x)`.
