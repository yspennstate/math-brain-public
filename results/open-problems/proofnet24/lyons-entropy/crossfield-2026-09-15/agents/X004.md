# X004 — Hodge–Riemann geometry on arbitrary rank-three supports

Status: completed constructive theorem, 15 September 2026. The universal entropy conjecture is not proved. ## 1. A new mixed-exterior representation

Let V:C^3→C^n be an isometry, with rows v_i, and let 0<M<I_3. Set K=VMV*. Its positive atoms are exactly the sets S of linearly independent rows, together with the empty set. This family is downward closed and has cardinalities at most three. At this fixed base, set f(S)=−log p_S on that support and define c_S there by f(S)=Σ_{T⊆S}c_T. Assign c_S=0 on dependent sets. Those assigned coefficients never enter expectations because their inclusion minors vanish identically along VMV*. Thus no nonzero-minor assumption is needed.

Put w_ij=(v_i×v_j)^T, the column whose entries are the signed two-row minors, and r_ijk=|det V_{ijk}|². The exterior Cauchy–Binet identities are

    det (VMV*)_{ij} = w_ij* adj(M) w_ij,
    det (VMV*)_{ijk} = r_ijk det M,
    Σ_{ij} w_ij w_ij* = I_3,
    Σ_{ijk} r_ijk = 1.

Define the frozen quantities

    W = Σ_{i<j} c_ij w_ij w_ij*,
    τ = Σ_{i<j<k} c_ijk r_ijk,
    T = W + τM.

For every Hermitian active direction A, the frozen expected surprisal F_f(M')=E_{P_{VM'V*}}f(X) is a polynomial of degree at most three. Its quadratic and cubic terms are exactly

    tr(W adj M') + τ det M'.

Consequently

    D²F_f(M)[A,A] = 2 tr(T adj A),
    H'' = −FI + 2 tr(T adj A).                         (1)

This extends the three-measured-site diagonal-plus-scalar formula to every measured embedding of a three-dimensional support. Here W need not be diagonal and n may be arbitrary.

## 2. Positivity of the exterior polarization

Let L=M(I−M)^{-1}>0 and G=VLV*. The exact atoms are p_S=det(I−M)det G_S for |S|≤3. Thus f differs from −log det G_S by a constant on the support. The two-by-two Schur complement proves the local supermodular inequalities on supported pairs and triples:

    c_ij ≥ 0,
    c_ij+c_ijk ≥ 0 whenever {i,j}⊂{i,j,k}.             (2)

Hence W≥0. The less immediate fact is

    W+τI ≥ 0.                                        (3)

Fix a unit a∈C^3. The nested projection kernels

    P_2=V(I−aa*)V* ≤ P_3=VV*

have ranks two and three. The standard stochastic domination theorem for determinantal measures gives a coupling (Y,Z) with Y⊂Z almost surely. Their exact laws are

    P(Y={i,j})=|a*w_ij|²,
    P(Z={i,j,k})=r_ijk.

Therefore

    a*Wa+τ = E[c_Y+c_Z] ≥ 0

by (2). This proves (3), with every theorem hypothesis checked: both kernels are orthogonal projections on the same finite coordinate space, and their ranges are nested with dimension difference one.

If τ≥0, T=W+τM≥0. If τ<0,

    T=(W+τI)+(−τ)(I−M)≥0.                            (4)

This is a rigorous positivity theorem beyond rank-two support. In particular, τ≠0 implies T>0. If τ=0, strict positivity is equivalent to the vectors w_ij for c_ij>0 spanning C^3.

Imported theorem: Russell Lyons, *Determinantal probability measures*, Publ. Math. IHÉS 98 (2003), Theorem 6.2, https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0204325. The primary-source check and a direct specialized exterior proof appear below. No Hodge–Riemann theorem is imported for the following decomposition, which is proved by three-dimensional determinant algebra.

## 3. Primitive decomposition

Suppose T>0. Write δ=det T, B=T^{-1/2}AT^{-1/2}, b=tr B/3, and B_0=B−bI. Adjugate congruence gives

    2 tr(T adj A)
      = δ[(tr B)²−tr(B²)]
      = δ[6b²−||B_0||_F²].                           (5)

The primitive space is tr(T^{-1}A)=0. On it the entropy curvature has the explicit decomposition

    −H'' = FI + δ||T^{-1/2}AT^{-1/2}||_F² ≥ 0.       (6)

Thus every such rank-three-support base has an eight-dimensional real hyperplane of nonpositive directions, strictly negative when T>0 and A≠0. More generally H''≤0 on the whole double-sided cone (tr B)²≤||B||_F². The frozen form has exactly one positive direction and eight negative directions when T>0; adding −FI cannot create another positive direction.

The remaining universal step is Fisher compensation for the one nonprimitive direction. Equation (5) alone does not prove it. Section 5 completes compensation on equiangular frames at every scalar active base; Section 7 states the exact remaining general inequality.

## 4. Zero minors and an explicit exterior domination proof

The primary stochastic domination input in Section 2 is precisely Lyons, Theorem 6.2 (finite nested subspaces), in https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0204325, page 18 of the PDF. Its monotone-coupling consequence is stated in Section 10 of the same paper using Strassen's theorem. Both assertions were checked in the primary source. The imported assertion is only ordinary two-law stochastic domination, not a simultaneous projection diamond.

One can avoid any imported coupling theorem in the inequality actually needed. For a fixed unit a, write u for the unit decomposable 2-form of V(a^⊥), and v=Va, so u∧v is the unit 3-form of ran V. For any collection A of pairs, let U_A be the coordinate projection retaining those pair basis vectors, and let T_A be the coordinate projection retaining every triple containing a pair from A. Since wedging a retained pair by v can produce only a retained triple,

    ||U_A u||²
       = <(U_A u)∧v, u∧v>
       = <(U_A u)∧v, T_A(u∧v)>
       ≤ ||U_A u|| · ||T_A(u∧v)||.

The first equality uses contraction by v and the fact that u is perpendicular to v in every factor. Exterior multiplication by a unit vector is a contraction. Cancelling proves the weighted Hall inequalities

    Σ_{ij∈A}|a*w_ij|² ≤ Σ_{ijk containing some pair of A} r_ijk.

Finite transportation feasibility (or the elementary finite max-flow/min-cut theorem) then gives the pair/triple coupling used in Section 2. This is the exterior-algebra proof underlying the specialized instance of Lyons's theorem. The proof does not presume an explicit fixed Gram sum-of-squares coupling.

Zero minors cause no difficulty: omit each impossible atom and assign its coefficient zero as in Section 1. All subsets of a supported atom are supported. The projection coupling also uses only supported pairs and triples: a pair dependent among the original rows is dependent after projection into a⊥, so it has probability zero under the lower-rank law as well. Thus (1)–(4) hold directly for completely arbitrary frames, including zero rows and dependent pairs.

The Hessian also depends continuously on the frame at vanishing minors, which is useful for neighborhood statements. Keep M in a compact subinterval of 0<M<I. For a k-row matrix R, factor R=CU with UU*=I_k when it has row rank k. Then

    p_S=det(RR*) · [det(I−M) det(U M(I−M)^{-1}U*)].

The bracket and its first two M-derivatives are uniformly bounded, with the bracket bounded away from zero, over all row subspaces and the fixed compact active domain. Thus contributions of a vanishing row Gram determinant x to H, H', and H'' are O(x(1+|log x|)). They vanish continuously. In W and τ the potentially divergent logarithms multiply pair or triple Gram determinants; a triple Gram determinant is bounded by each of its pair Gram determinants times a bounded row norm. Hence the same x log x bound applies. The formula using T^{-1/2} is used only when T>0. If T is singular, approximating it by T+εI shows that the frozen quadratic form still has at most one positive direction.

## 5. Full Fisher compensation on equiangular rank-three frames

**Theorem.** Let V:C^3→C^n be an equal-norm equiangular Parseval frame, with n>3. Thus V*V=I_3, each row has squared norm a=3/n, and the normalized squared inner product of every distinct row pair is the same number ρ². At every active scalar base M=sI_3, 0<s<1, the entropy Hessian of M↦H(P_{VMV*}) is negative definite in all nine real Hermitian directions. This includes directions with changing active eigenvectors.

No assumption of nonzero triple minors is made. The theorem gives open neighborhoods of these scalar bases with negative-definite full active Hessian by continuity. It does not claim the whole active domain is concave.

**Frame constants.** Parseval gives

    ρ²=(n−3)/(3(n−1)),
    a=3/n,
    b=det(VV*)_{ij}=6/[n(n−1)]  for every i≠j.

Indeed Σ_j |(VV*)_ij|²=(VV*)_ii=a, so a²+(n−1)a²ρ²=a. The pair Gram determinants are positive. Their logarithmic Möbius coefficient is constant:

    c=c_ij=log(a²/b)=log[3(n−1)/(2n)]>0.

The exterior Parseval identity therefore gives W=cI_3. Let r_T=|det V_T|² on triples. These are the exact top-projection probabilities, with Σ_T r_T=1. Write H(r)=−Σ_{T:r_T>0}r_T log r_T. On supported triples,

    c_T=log[b³/(a³r_T)]
       =log[8/((n−1)³r_T)].

Consequently

    τ=H(r)−log[(n−1)³/8],
    T(s)=t(s)I_3,       t(s)=c+sτ.                   (7)

The lower exterior bound W+τI≥0 says τ≥−c. Thus

    t(s)≥(1−s)c>0.                                  (8)

For the upper bound, H(r)≤log binom(n,3), even if some triples are absent. Hence

    τ≤log[4n(n−2)/(3(n−1)²)].

The right side is nonnegative for n≥3. It follows that

    t(s)≤c+log[4n(n−2)/(3(n−1)²)]
         =log[2(n−2)/(n−1)]<log 2.                 (9)

This crude entropy bound already suffices; no detailed knowledge of the triple probabilities is required.

**Exact positive decomposition.** Let N=|X| under the base law. Its distribution is Binomial(3,s), since the active eigenvalues are all s, so Var(N)=3s(1−s). Let σ_A(X)=D log p_X[A] be the affine active score and α=tr A/[3s(1−s)]. The score identity gives Eσ_A=0 and

    E[(N−3s)σ_A]=tr A.

The Fisher information therefore decomposes exactly as

    FI=E[(σ_A−α(N−3s))²]+(tr A)²/[3s(1−s)].

Using (1) and 2tr(adj A)=(tr A)²−||A||_F², we obtain the explicit sum of nonnegative terms

    −H''[A,A]
      = E[(σ_A−α(N−3s))²]
        + [1/(3s(1−s))−t(s)](tr A)²
        + t(s)||A||_F².                             (10)

By (8)–(9), all coefficients are positive, and in particular

    −H''[A,A]
      ≥ (4/3−log 2)(tr A)² + (1−s)c||A||_F².       (11)

This completes a full Fisher compensation proof in a genuinely rank-three family. No rank-one or scalar-ray entropy-concavity theorem is used. The primitive metric and the count score supply separate positive terms.

## 6. A concrete nonuniform nine-coordinate family

Let ω=exp(2πi/3), ζ=exp(iθ), and take the nine rows

    (0,1,−ζω^j)/sqrt(6),
    (−ζω^j,0,1)/sqrt(6),
    (1,−ζω^j,0)/sqrt(6),             j=0,1,2.         (12)

Direct summation gives V*V=I_3. Each row has squared norm 1/3, and every distinct pair has squared inner product 1/36. Thus this is an equal-norm equiangular frame, c=log(4/3), and Theorem 5 applies. These elementary verifications are the only properties of the familiar qutrit equiangular construction used here.

Three triples, those consisting of all rows from one displayed group, have zero determinant identically. The 54 triples with two rows from one group and a third row from another have r_T=1/72. The 27 triples with one row from each group split into three groups of nine, with weights

    [1−cos(3θ+2πm/3)]/108,           m=0,1,2.

For θ=π/9, the positive triple weights are exactly

    1/72 (54 triples),
    1/216 (18 triples),
    1/54 (9 triples),

in addition to the three fixed zero triples. The weights sum to one. The actual occupation atom at M=sI for a triple is s³r_T, so these conditional count-three probabilities are not uniform, even on their support. This concrete case is therefore not a count-uniform-occupation-law application.

Here

    H(r)=(3/4)log72+(1/12)log216+(1/6)log54,
    τ=H(r)−log64 ≈ 0.161387047636762.

The theorem nevertheless holds for every θ, every 0<s<1, and every active Hermitian direction A. The affine measured direction is VAV*, and it can have rank three and be noncommuting with nearby active covariances.

## 7. Exact remaining lemma for arbitrary rank-three frames

For a strict general active base M, let J=(I−M)^{-1}, G=V M(I−M)^{-1}V*, and define, for each supported atom S,

    R_S=J V_S* G_S^{-1}V_S J−J,

where the first term is zero for S=∅. Direct differentiation of the exact odds atom gives σ_A(S)=tr(R_S A). This provides a finite explicit nine-dimensional Fisher operator without conditioning on any moving pivot.

When T>0, put δ=det T, define B=T^{-1/2}AT^{-1/2}, and let the self-adjoint positive semidefinite operator F on Herm_3 satisfy

    <B,FB>=Σ_S p_S [tr(R_S T^{1/2}BT^{1/2})]².

Let R=F+δ Id, u=R^{-1}I, and g=<I,u>>0. For b=tr B, put B_perp=B−(b/g)u. Then tr B_perp=0, and completing the R-inner-product square gives the exact identity

    −H''[A,A]
       = <B_perp,RB_perp> + (1/g−δ)(tr B)².         (13)

Thus the **remaining universal inequality** on every strict rank-three support with T>0 is

    δ <I,(F+δ Id)^{-1}I> ≤ 1.                       (14)

Equation (14) is a conditional reduction, not a proved inequality. It extends the earlier three-measured-coordinate scalar criterion to arbitrary measured embeddings. The positive form T itself is now established for these embeddings. Section 5 proves compensation in its stated equiangular class, rather than assuming (14) in general.

For scalar bases M=sI and arbitrary V, root separately derived the following useful exact simplification, checked by this worker's independent atom derivative program. Define t_i=||v_i||², b_ij=||w_ij||², omitting zero denominators, and

    F_v(A)=Σ_i (v_i A v_i*)²/t_i,
    F_w(A)=Σ_{ij} (w_ij* A w_ij)²/b_ij.

Then

    FI=F_v(A)/s+F_w(A)/(1−s).                        (15)

A direct verification uses the empty, singleton, pair, and triple derivatives

    p_∅'=−(1−s)² tr A,
    p_i'=(1−s)[v_i A v_i*−s t_i tr A],
    p_ij'=s[(1−s)b_ij tr A−w_ij* A w_ij],
    p_ijk'=s²r_ijk tr A,

and exterior Parseval; the trace cross terms cancel exactly. This calculation keeps the full measured Fisher information.

With Q_W(A)=tr(W adj A) and Q_I(A)=tr(adj A), scalar-base concavity is therefore equivalent to positivity, for every Hermitian A, of the cubic matrix polynomial

    G_s(A)=(1−s)F_v(A)+sF_w(A)
              −2s(1−s)[Q_W(A)+sτQ_I(A)].            (16)

It has the exact finite decomposition, writing q=1−s,

    G_s=q³F_v+q²s C_1+qs² C_2+s³F_w,
    C_1=2F_v+F_w−2Q_W,
    C_2=F_v+2F_w−2Q_W−2τQ_I.                        (17)

Consequently C_1≥0 and C_2≥0 are a sufficient frame-only certificate for all scalar bases. Their positivity for arbitrary V is not asserted or used. This gives later workers a concrete finite route without conflating the affine kernel path with affine odds.
