## 1. Explicit full-Hessian theorem and convex family

Fix `0<κ≤1/2`, put `m=κ²/8`, and take
\[
 B=\operatorname{diag}(x,y),\quad x,y\in[\kappa,1-\kappa],\quad
 0<a<1,\quad b=1-a,\quad q=ab,
 \quad K=\begin{pmatrix}a&z^*\\z&B\end{pmatrix},\quad z=\sqrt q\,v.
\]
Write `ri=|vi|²`, `r=r1+r2≤m`, and `R=r1r2`. For every Hermitian affine direction
\[
 A=\begin{pmatrix}\alpha&w^*\\w&C\end{pmatrix},
 \qquad
 \boxed{-D^2H(K)[A,A]\ge\frac14\left[
 \frac{\alpha^2}{q}+C_{11}^2+C_{22}^2+
 r_1|w_1|^2+r_2|w_2|^2+qR|C_{12}|^2\right].}\tag{1}
\]
The kernel is a strict contraction. If `R>0`, (1) is a positive metric on the entire nine-dimensional real Hermitian space, and
\[
 -D^2H(K)[A,A]\ge g_K\|A\|_F^2,\qquad g_K=qR/8>0.\tag{2}
\]
There is **no comparison between `a`, `1−a`, `|v1|`, and `|v2|`**. Either edge may be smaller than every power of the other or of the rare marginal. The estimate remains valid when an edge vanishes, with its actual degeneracy retained.

A consequence is strict entropy concavity on the convex seven-real-dimensional set
\[
 \mathcal E_\kappa=\left\{\begin{pmatrix}a&z^*\\z&\operatorname{diag}(x,y)\end{pmatrix}:
 x,y\in[\kappa,1-\kappa],\ 0\le a\le1,\ \|z\|^2\le m a(1-a)\right\}.\tag{3}
\]
For endpoints in (3), write their differences as `Δa,Δx,Δy,Δz1,Δz2`. At `0<t<1` the Jensen gap is at least
\[
 \boxed{t(1-t)\left[\frac{(\Delta a)^2}{2}+
 \frac{(\Delta x)^2+(\Delta y)^2}{8}+
 \frac{1-t+t^2}{36}\sum_{i=1}^2|\Delta z_i|^4\right].}\tag{4}
\]
Thus distinct endpoints give strict inequality, even if a chord crosses a disconnected locus. Section 6 supplies explicit full-dimensional interior balls, including points with a nonzero leaf-to-leaf entry.

## 2. Exact binary grouping resolves the singular logarithm

The actual conditional kernels are `F1=B−bvv*`, `F0=B+avv*`. Their eigenvalues lie in `[κ−r,1−κ+r]⊂[κ/2,1−κ/2]`, so they are strict. Schur complementation proves `0<K<I`. The exact entropy chain rule is
\[
 H(K)=h(a)+aH_2(F_1)+bH_2(F_0).\tag{5}
\]
All four rare atoms have a common marginal factor `a`; their apparent `log a` acceleration cancels because the marginal is affine. Equation (5) groups that cancellation before any bound is taken.

Let `G=∇H2(F1)−∇H2(F0)`, with real Hermitian pairing `tr(GC)`. Direct differentiation gives
\[
 E_1=C-\frac{wz^*+zw^*}{a}+\frac{\alpha zz^*}{a^2},\qquad
 E_0=C+\frac{wz^*+zw^*}{b}+\frac{\alpha zz^*}{b^2},
\]
\[
 \boxed{H''=aH_2''(F_1)[E_1]+bH_2''(F_0)[E_0]
 -\frac{\alpha^2}{q}+2\operatorname{tr}\{G(\alpha C-ww^*)\}.}\tag{6}
\]
For a self-contained acceleration check, set `η1=w−αz/a`, `η0=w+αz/b`. Then `F1''=−2η1η1*/a`, `F0''=2η0η0*/b`, while `αE1−η1η1*=αC−ww*` and `−αE0+η0η0*=−(αC−ww*)`. These identities combine all four changing-weight/gradient terms in the derivative of (5), proving (6). All paths in (6) originate from the affine measured kernel `K+tA`.

## 3. An anisotropic two-site estimate, proved directly

For `J=[[u,c],[conj(c),v]]`, write `s=|c|²`. Its four atoms are
\[
 p_{00}=(1-u)(1-v)-s,\ p_{10}=u(1-v)+s,\
 p_{01}=(1-u)v+s,\ p_{11}=uv-s.
\]
Let `I(u,v,s)=h(u)+h(v)−H2(J)`. Then `I(u,v,0)=0` and
\[
 I_s=\lambda=\log\frac{p_{10}p_{01}}{p_{00}p_{11}},\qquad
 \lambda_s=\sum_{ij}p_{ij}^{-1}\ge16.\tag{7}
\]
Assume `u,v∈[κ/2,1−κ/2]` and `s≤m²/4`. At every strength between `0` and `s`, each atom is at least `κ²/4−m²/4≥m`. Differentiating the four explicit atoms, with `d=(u,v)`, gives
\[
 16s\le\lambda\le4s/m,\qquad
 \|I_{ds}\|\le4\sqrt2\,s/m^2,\qquad
 \|I_{dd}\|\le12s^2/m^3.\tag{8}
\]
Here `|∂u λ_s|,|∂v λ_s|≤4/m²`. Each entry of `∂dd λ_s` is at most `12/m³` in absolute value, so its operator norm is at most `24/m³`. Integrate from `s=0`, where `λ` and all its `d` derivatives vanish, and then integrate once more for `I_dd`; this proves every derivative bound in (8).

For a Hermitian direction `E` with diagonal vector `e` and off-diagonal entry `f`, the exact second derivative is
\[
 -H_2''[E]=\sum_i\frac{e_i^2}{u_i(1-u_i)}+I_{dd}[e,e]
 +4I_{ds}[e]\Re(\bar c f)+4I_{ss}\Re(\bar c f)^2+2\lambda|f|^2.
\]
The mixed term has absolute value at most `16sqrt(2)s^(3/2)||e|| |f|/m²`. Young's inequality pays it with `16s|f|²+8s²||e||²/m⁴`. Since `s≤m²/4` and `m≤1`, the remaining diagonal coefficient is at least `4−(12/m³+8/m⁴)s²≥4−20/16>2`. Therefore
\[
 \boxed{-H_2''(J)[E,E]\ge2(E_{11}^2+E_{22}^2)+16|c|^2|E_{12}|^2.}\tag{9}
\]
This includes the imaginary off-diagonal direction, even when its first atom score is zero.

## 4. The gradient secant retains every vanishing factor

Put `D=vv*`, and consider `F(t)=B+tD`, `−b≤t≤a`. Write `λt=λ(F(t))`. The diagonal entropy gradient is `h'(Fii)−I_di`; its off-diagonal entry is `−λt tD12`. Hence
\[
 G_{12}=\ell D_{12},\qquad \ell=b\lambda_{-b}+a\lambda_a,
 \qquad 0\le\ell\le4R/m\le m.\tag{10}
\]
The conditional strengths are `b²R,a²R≤r²/4≤m²/4`, so (8) applies. Also `|I_di|≤2s²/m²`. It follows that
\[
 G_{ii}=\int_{-b}^a\frac{r_i\,dt}{(B_{ii}+tr_i)(1-B_{ii}-tr_i)}+\epsilon_i,
 \qquad |\epsilon_i|\le2R^2/m^2\le(2r^3/m^2)r_i.
\]
Since `r≤m≤1/32`, this gives `3ri≤Gii≤5ri/κ`. Normalizing by `diag(sqrt(r1),sqrt(r2))`, the off-diagonal in (10) has magnitude `ℓ≤m`, whence
\[
 \boxed{G\succeq2\operatorname{diag}(r_1,r_2),\qquad
 \|(G_{11},G_{22})\|\le5r/\kappa.}\tag{11}
\]
If an `ri` is zero, the corresponding row/column is exactly zero and the same conclusion follows directly. Thus (11) never divides by a lower bound on either incident edge.

## 5. Weighted square completion proves the full metric

Set `δ=a−b`, `W=wv*+vw*`, and
\[
 \dot D=W/\sqrt q+\alpha\delta D/q,\quad M=C+\alpha D,
 \quad N=M_{12}+\delta\dot D_{12}
 =C_{12}+\delta W_{12}/\sqrt q+(1-3q)\alpha D_{12}/q.
\]
Then `E1=M−b dotD`, `E0=M+a dotD`. The diagonal conditional square has mean `Mdiag`, while the off-diagonal square has reversed weights:
\[
 a|(E_1)_{ii}|^2+b|(E_0)_{ii}|^2=|M_{ii}|^2+q|\dot D_{ii}|^2,
\]
\[
 a b^2|(E_1)_{12}|^2+b a^2|(E_0)_{12}|^2
 =q\{|N|^2+q|\dot D_{12}|^2\}.\tag{12}
\]
Let `U²=Σri|wi|²`. Combining (6), (9), (11), and (12), discarding only nonnegative squares, yields
\[
 -H''\ge2\|M_{\rm diag}\|^2+16qR|N|^2+\alpha^2/q+4U^2-2\alpha\operatorname{tr}(GC).\tag{13}
\]
Substitute `Cdiag=Mdiag−αdiag(D)` and `C12=N−αD12−δ dotD12` into the last term. Every resulting scalar `α²` term in `2αtr(GC)` is nonpositive, since `Gii≥0`, `ℓ≥0`. Moreover `|Re(conj(D12)W12)|≤rU`. Consequently
\[
 2\alpha\operatorname{tr}(GC)\le
 2|\alpha|\|G_{\rm diag}\|\|M_{\rm diag}\|+
 4|\alpha|\ell\sqrt R|N|+4|\alpha|\ell rU/\sqrt q.
\]
Three Young bounds pay these terms with `||Mdiag||²`, `8qR|N|²`, and `2U²`; their combined scalar cost in units of `α²/q` is at most
\[
 q\|G_{\rm diag}\|^2+\ell^2/2+2\ell^2r^2
 \le\frac{25}{1024}+\frac1{2048}+\frac1{524288}
 =\frac{13057}{524288}<\frac12.
\]
No division by `R` is required when it is zero: the middle mixed term then vanishes identically. We have proved the stronger sheared metric
\[
 -H''\ge\|M_{\rm diag}\|^2+8qR|N|^2+2U^2+\alpha^2/(2q).\tag{14}
\]
To remove the shear, use `|W12|²≤2(r2|w1|²+r1|w2|²)` and `|1−3q|≤1`:
\[
 \|C_{\rm diag}\|^2\le2\|M_{\rm diag}\|^2+(r^2/2)\alpha^2/q,
 \quad qR|C_{12}|^2\le3qR|N|^2+6r^2U^2+3R^2\alpha^2/q.
\]
Because `r≤1/32`, the bracket in (1) is at most four times the right side of (14). This proves (1). Its smallest coefficient relative to the Frobenius norm is at least `qR/8`, proving (2).

## 6. Independent rates, explicit interior overlap, and finite chords
