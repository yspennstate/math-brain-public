## 1. Arbitrary background rates with a geometric decoding condition

Let [W,V] be a unitary four-by-four matrix, with W=[w_1,w_2] and two active
columns V. Choose distinct measured anchor coordinates i_1,i_2 and put

    eta=sum_(j=1,2) [1-|w_j(i_j)|^2],
    K(M)=W diag(lambda_1,lambda_2) W*+V M V*,
    a I_2<=M<=(1-a)I_2,             0<a<1/2.

The fixed rates lambda_1,lambda_2 are ARBITRARY in [0,1]. Write

    f(e)=h(e)+e log3,
    g=2/3-[4 eta+f(eta)/2]/a^2.                                (1)

**Theorem 1.** If eta<=3/4 and g>=0, M maps to H(P_K(M)) concavely on
the entire displayed matrix interval. Every definite Hermitian E satisfies

    -H''_M[E,E]>=g (tr E)^2.                                   (2)

The indefinite and rank-one directions satisfy -H''>=FI without (1), by
the credited four-site marginal-log diamond argument in X061. Endpoints
may be noncommuting. For a definite Delta=M_1-M_0, integrating (2) gives
the Jensen improvement (g/2)t(1-t)(tr Delta)^2.

A simple rate-uniform domain is

    a=1/4,               eta<=2^-12.                          (3)

Then g>=241/384>5/8. Unlike a small-H(Z) hypothesis, (3) permits, for
example, lambda_1=1/2 and any lambda_2, or both rates equal to 1/2.
It concerns an open family of general measured embeddings, not only
product guides, exactly readable labels, or erasure outputs.

## 2. Exact posterior identity, with both forgetting terms retained

Let Z consist of the independent Bernoulli background labels and w_z their
fixed probabilities. Conditional on Z=z, the measured flag law is p_z(M).
The spectral expansion gives p(M)=sum_z w_z p_z(M)=P_K(M). For a direction E
write s_z=p_z'/p_z and r_z=p_z''/p_z on each conditional positive support.
The complete normalized entropy identity from X017/X061 is

    H(p)''=sum_z w_z H(p_z)''+L+R,
    L=E[Var(s_Z(X)|X)],
    R=sum_(z,x) w_z p_z''(x) log[p_z(x)/p(x)].                  (4)

There is a useful exact cancellation not used in the prior-entropy bound:
with pi_z(x)=P(Z=z|X=x), normalization gives sum_x p_z''(x)=0, so

    R=E[r_Z(X) log pi_Z(X)].                                  (5)

Indeed log(p_z/p)=log pi_z-log w_z, and every log w_z term cancels
separately. Zero-weight labels are omitted. This is an identity for the
actual moving posterior, not a frozen posterior-weight approximation.

X061 proves the following geometry-independent conditional bounds on the
active slab; its flag atom calculation is recalled below:

    |s_z(x)|<=||E||_1/a,          |r_z(x)|<=2|det E|/a^2.        (6)

Since log pi_Z<=0, (5) now proves

    |R|<=2|det E| H(Z|X)/a^2.                                  (7)

This improves the prior-entropy estimate in two ways: the factor two is
exactly retained, and the uncertainty is POSTERIOR uncertainty. In
particular the bound vanishes for exactly recoverable labels even when
H(Z)=log4. No sign for R itself is asserted.

For completeness the conditional layers are d0 alpha, e B e*, d beta,
where d=det M, d0=det(I-M), B=M-dI. Their second derivatives are 2det E
times alpha, -||e||^2, beta. All three positive factors d,d0, and the
eigenvalues of B are at least a^2, proving the second bound in (6).
The extreme scores are tr(M^-1 E) and -tr((I-M)^-1 E). For a unit middle
row u, put q=u*Bu and N=uu*-adj M. Its positive and negative eigenvalue
magnitudes are at least a, and det N=-q; hence ||N||<=q/a.
Thus |tr(NE)|/q<=||E||_1/a, proving the first bound as well.

## 3. A complete classification-error curvature certificate

Take ANY deterministic decoder zhat(X), with error epsilon. Define
s_zhat(x)=0 when that conditional atom is impossible. Conditional variance
minimizes squared prediction error. On a correct classification its error
is zero, and on an incorrect one its absolute error is at most 2||E||_1/a.
Therefore the entire posterior Fisher loss, including all scores, obeys

    L<=4 epsilon ||E||_1^2/a^2.                                (8)

An elementary chain rule also gives

    H(Z|X)<=h(epsilon)+epsilon log3.                           (9)

To prove this, append A=1{Z!=zhat(X)}. Conditional on X and A=0 there is
one possible label; conditional on X and A=1 there are at most three.
Thus H(Z|X)=H(A|X)+H(Z|A,X)<=h(epsilon)+epsilon log3.
There is no independent-label assumption about the posterior.

The conditional four-site flag margin proved in X061 is

    -H(p_z)''>=(2/3)(tr E)^2,                det E>0.           (10)

Its projection input is the rank-one-bottom diamond theorem, which applies
to all ranks 0,1,2 on four sites, and its fair-middle bound beta_*>=-log2.
X017's full Fisher formula then gives -H(p_z)''>=FI_z/3, and active-count
variance at most 1/2 gives (10). This input is credited, not claimed anew.

For definite E, ||E||_1=|tr E| and 4det E<=(tr E)^2. Combining (4),(7),
(8),(10) proves the finer, observable-error certificate

    -H(p)'' >= {2/3-[4epsilon+H(Z|X)/2]/a^2}(tr E)^2.          (11)

Replacing the posterior entropy by (9) gives an explicit scalar condition.
Equations (5),(7),(8),(11) also hold for any finite conditional-flag mixture
with the corresponding proved conditional margin, replacing log3 by
log(m-1) for m labels. They do not assume arbitrary mixture concavity.

## 4. Anchor occupations decode every background rate uniformly

Choose zhat_j(X)=X_(i_j). Set r_j=1-|w_j(i_j)|^2 and

    b_j=sum_(k!=j)lambda_k |w_k(i_j)|^2+(VMV*)_(i_j,i_j).

Orthogonality and 0<=M<=I give 0<=b_j<=r_j. Conditional on Z_j, the
one-site mean is |w_j(i_j)|^2 Z_j+b_j. Consequently

    P(X_(i_j)!=Z_j)=lambda_j r_j+(1-2lambda_j)b_j
                  <=max(lambda_j,1-lambda_j)r_j<=r_j.          (12)

The union bound gives epsilon<=eta. This calculation is exact for the
measured DPP; it does not pretend occupations equal spectral labels.
The function f is increasing on [0,3/4], since f'(e)=log[3(1-e)/e].
Equations (9),(11),(12) prove (1),(2). All physical boundary rates follow
on the fixed conditional union support, or by finite entropy continuity.

For (3), use h(e)<=e(1+log(1/e)), monotonicity of this last expression
on [0,1], and log2<3/4, log3<3/2. Then

    4eta+f(eta)/2 <10*2^-12,
    g>2/3-16*10*2^-12=241/384>5/8.                             (13)

This proves the claimed finite domain uniformly over all background rates.

## 5. Definite directions through seven sites

Let 3<=n<=7, with n-2 background eigenvectors, n-2 distinct anchor sites,
and eta the sum of their leakage probabilities as above. Put

    P=floor(n^2/4), h_n=2-(1/2)log(4P)>0, c_n=h_n/2.

X060 proves beta_*>=-log P for every conditional projection flag. Its
X017 curvature formula gives -H_z''>=FI_z-4det(E)log(2sqrt(P)T), while
FI_z>=4(1+T)det(E). Put alpha=1-h_n/4. For T>=1 the minimum of
alpha(1+T)-log(2sqrt(P)T) is 3h_n/4+log(1-h_n/4)>=h_n/4>0;
the last inequality uses 0<h_n<2 and log(1-x)>=-2x for 0<=x<=1/2.
Thus -H_z''>=(h_n/4)FI_z>=c_n(tr E)^2 for definite E.

The same posterior proof therefore gives concavity in every comparable
active direction whenever eta<=1-2^(-(n-2)) and

    4eta+[h(eta)+eta log(2^(n-2)-1)]/2 <= c_n a^2.             (15)

All background rates are arbitrary. Full active concavity additionally
requires the conditional marginal-log diamond input for indefinite
directions. It is available automatically for n<=5 by the credited
rank-one-bottom theorem; it is not claimed for every six- or seven-site
flag. This corollary needs no new numerical hypothesis or count sampling.

    t=1/1024, Q=(I-tS)(I+tS)^-1, W=Q[:,1:2], V=Q[:,3:4].

    eta<=224/1024^2=7/32768<2^-12.                             (14)
