## 1. Exact affine-kernel transfer

Let K be a strict Hermitian tridiagonal contraction on n measured coordinates, with diagonal p_i and upper-diagonal z_i. For a fixed occupation word x, put epsilon_i=2x_i-1, d_i=x_i p_i+(1-x_i)(1-p_i), and s_i=|z_i|^2. The exact atom determinant is the continuant

    P_0=1, P_1=d_1,
    P_j=d_j P_(j-1)-epsilon_(j-1)epsilon_j s_(j-1)P_(j-2).

This follows by expanding the tridiagonal mixed-column atom determinant at its last row. In particular these are K entries, not odds entries. Along any actual affine K(t), write a_i=epsilon_i p_i', b_i=s_i'=2 Re(conj(z_i)z_i'), c_i=s_i''=2|z_i'|^2, and tau_i=-epsilon_i epsilon_(i+1). Then

    P_j'=d_j P_(j-1)'+a_j P_(j-1)
           +tau_(j-1)[s_(j-1)P_(j-2)'+b_(j-1)P_(j-2)],
    P_j''=d_j P_(j-1)''+2a_j P_(j-1)'
           +tau_(j-1)[s_(j-1)P_(j-2)''+2b_(j-1)P_(j-2)'
                                      +c_(j-1)P_(j-2)].

Consequently the exact measured curvature is

    -H''=sum_x [(P_n')^2/P_n+P_n'' log P_n].

The logarithmic acceleration is retained. These identities require no imported specialized theorem.

## 2. Balanced-chain reduction

If p_i=1/2, set w_i=4s_i and edge signs t_i=-epsilon_i epsilon_(i+1). The spin-to-(root spin,edge signs) map is bijective. With independent uniform edge signs,

    Q_0=Q_1=1, Q_j=Q_(j-1)+w_(j-1)t_(j-1)Q_(j-2),
    F_n:=n log 2-H=E Q_n log Q_n.

All prefix Q_j are positive. Put R_(j-1)=Q_(j-2)/Q_(j-1) and

    phi(u)=[(1+u)log(1+u)+(1-u)log(1-u)]/2.

Averaging only the last edge sign gives the exact entropy increment

    F_j-F_(j-1)=E Q_(j-1) phi(w_(j-1)R_(j-1)).

The cavity ratio evolves as R_j=1/[1+w_(j-1)t_(j-1)R_(j-1)]. This is a two-state linear transfer for (Q_j,Q_(j-1)) but a continuum of possible positive ratios. It must not be called a fixed two-state Markov entropy formula.

## 3. Unpaid induction term, explicit

For a fixed prefix history put A=Q_(j-1), B=Q_(j-2), w=w_(j-1), u=wB/A, and J=A phi(u). Direct perspective differentiation along an affine kernel line gives

    J''=phi''(u)/A * [(wB)'-u A']^2
             +[phi(u)-u phi'(u)]A''+phi'(u)(wB)'',
    phi''(u)=1/(1-u^2),
    phi(u)-u phi'(u)=log(1-u^2)/2,
    (wB)''=wB''+2w'B'+w''B.

Thus the complete chain induction is

    F_j''=F_(j-1)''+E { [(wB)'-u A']^2/[A(1-u^2)]
                  +(log(1-u^2)/2) A''
                  +atanh(u)[wB''+2w'B'+w''B] }.

The first term and the w''B term are nonnegative, since 0<=u<1 and w''>=0. The remaining boundary current is not signed by prefix entropy convexity alone. Establishing a payment for it would extend the fixed-reservoir X023 theorem to moving reservoirs. X014's positive entropy series does not individually sign mixed monomial Hessians; X022 already provides an exact failure of that proposed shortcut.

This identity already answers the transfer question exactly: the boundary current must be paid after summing the sign histories and retaining the earlier prefix curvature. The next sections isolate two statements that would have made that payment automatic but are false, and an amplitude-corrected alternative that remains viable.

## 4. A strict five-path counterexample to convexity of the entropy increment in strengths

Consider successive strengths and a strength direction

    w=(1/10,11/25,11/25,1/10),    v=(-3,7,-6,1).

For a balanced five-site path the strict physical condition is

    w_2/(1-w_1)+w_3/(1-w_4)<1,  w_1,w_4<1.

Here its left side is exactly 44/45. This follows by eliminating the two outer coordinates from I minus the normalized adjacency matrix, or by taking the determinant of its two-by-two bipartite Gram complement. Thus a neighborhood of this strength line consists of actual strict balanced path kernels.

Let I_5(w)=F_5(w)-F_4(w_1,w_2,w_3), with the fifth coordinate appended at the right. The exact continuant jets give

    -1771/1000 < D^2 I_5(w)[v,v] < -1770/1000 <0.            (4.1)

The proof of this strict sign is finite and rational. For each of 16 edge-sign words, compute Q,Q',Q'' by Section 1 with w''=0 and sum Q'^2/Q+Q''log Q; subtract the corresponding 8-word prefix sum. To bound each logarithm, rescale its positive rational argument by an integer power of 2 into [1/2,2], then use

    log r=2 sum_(j=0)^(N-1) x^(2j+1)/(2j+1)+error,
    x=(r-1)/(r+1),
    |error|<=2 |x|^(2N+1)/[(2N+1)(1-x^2)].

This identity follows by integrating the geometric series for 2/(1-x^2). With N=40, the exact rational interval in results.json lies strictly inside (4.1). The verifier checks the rational inequalities, not a floating-point sign.

Thus even the complete last-step entropy increment, after all sign averaging, need not be convex in the strengths. This is stronger than merely observing an indefinite individual mixed monomial. It rules out an induction in which all strength increments are separately nonnegative.

**This is not a counterexample to affine-kernel entropy concavity.** Along an actual complex affine entry, the missing acceleration is w_i''=8|z_i'|^2. For a prescribed radial strength velocity v_i at w_i>0, its minimum is v_i^2/(2w_i). These terms were deliberately absent from the false strength assertion (4.1). Even proving every previous prefix entropy convex would not make the increment positive in strengths.

## 5. A coefficientwise matrix closure retaining the actual amplitude acceleration

At a fixed strict prefix and for a new rightmost edge w, the perspective expansion is locally absolutely convergent:

    F_j-F_(j-1)
       =sum_(k>=1) w^(2k) C_(2k-1)/(2k(2k-1)),
    C_m(w_1,...,w_(j-2))
       =E [Q_(j-2)^(m+1)/Q_(j-1)^m],       m=1,3,5,... .    (5.1)

The expectation is over all prefix edge signs; the oldest sign histories are included even when Q_(j-2) does not depend on the newest one. Formula (5.1) follows directly from phi(u)=sum_(k>=1)u^(2k)/[2k(2k-1)]. Its uniform local convergence follows from 0<=u<1 on finitely many strict histories. C_m is positive.

For odd m, each C_m is coordinatewise nondecreasing in nonnegative prefix strengths. One way to prove this without an additional conjecture is to use X014's proved full-domain nonnegative connected entropy series: C_m is exactly the coefficient of the new leaf strength to power m+1 in that series, divided by its positive scalar prefactor. Given any strict prefix, a small positive new leaf strength stays physical, so the cited coefficient series converges on a neighborhood of that prefix. All its coefficients are nonnegative. This uses X014 for monotonicity, not for an unsupported mixed Hessian sign.

Write g=grad C_m and let all prefix strengths be positive. Define

    M_m=Hess C_m+diag_i[g_i/(2w_i)]
                  -[(2m+2)/(2m+1)] g g^T/C_m.              (5.2)

**Exact reduction.** The individual summand w^(m+1) C_m in (5.1) is convex under arbitrary actual affine complex path entries if M_m is positive semidefinite. Conversely, at a nonzero base, nonnegative curvature of this summand in all real radial directions implies M_m is positive semidefinite.

To prove this, for any smooth G of the strengths, put v_i=w_i'. The exact amplitude second variation is

    G''=v^T [Hess G+diag_i(G_i/(2w_i))]v
             +sum_i G_i [w_i''-v_i^2/(2w_i)].              (5.3)

The square-bracketed scalar in the last sum is nonnegative by
(Re(conj(z_i)z_i'))^2<=|z_i|^2|z_i'|^2. Coordinate monotonicity makes this entire angular remainder nonnegative, and it vanishes in a real radial direction.

For G=w^p C_m with p=m+1, the radial matrix in (5.3) has prefix block

    w^p [Hess C_m+diag_i(g_i/(2w_i))],

mixed block p w^(p-1)g, and final diagonal

    p(p-1/2) w^(p-2) C_m>0.

Its Schur complement is exactly w^p M_m. This proves the claimed equivalence. Zero entries follow by limits whenever the inequalities are proved at nonzero bases.

If (5.2) held at every strict prefix for every odd m, summing (5.1), adding the single-edge phi term, and inducting would prove arbitrary independent complex affine-entry concavity on every balanced path. All derivative interchanges are local within the strict physical region. Integrating along affine K chords and approaching boundary endpoints through I/2+rho(K-I/2) would give the entire closed contraction domain. **The universal positive-semidefiniteness of (5.2) is not proved here.** It is an explicit amplitude-corrected moment problem, stronger than the full chain entropy target but consistent with the bounded diagnostics performed here.

## 6. The stronger strength-moment closure fails exactly

Dropping the amplitude acceleration from (5.3) would instead require

    C_m Hess C_m-[(m+1)/m] grad C_m grad C_m^T >=0.          (6.1)

This condition means C_m^(-1/m) is concave. It fails already at a three-site prefix. With its two strengths a=b=1/4, put

    C_7(a,b)=E_(t,s) (1+a t)^8/(1+a t+b s)^7,
    D=-partial_a+partial_b.

The prefix is strict since a+b=1/2. Exact differentiation of four rational terms gives

    C_7 D^2 C_7-(8/7)(D C_7)^2
       =-1025122761666235553/5991619501228032 <0.            (6.2)

The verifier reproduces this fraction exactly. It excludes a plausible negative-power mean closure that would have made each strength coefficient convex. It does not address the different corrected matrix (5.2); both its diagonal correction and its smaller rank-one coefficient must remain.

## 7. Nonbalanced chains and the finite jet transfer

Section 1 extends to every strictly physical tridiagonal kernel, including independently moving means. The atom and its first two jets are propagated by the six-by-six block matrix

    [[T,0,0],[T',T,0],[T'',2T',T]],
    T=[[d_j,tau_(j-1)s_(j-1)],[1,0]],
    T'=[[a_j,tau_(j-1)b_(j-1)],[0,0]],
    T''=[[0,tau_(j-1)c_(j-1)],[0,0]].

The state is ((P_(j-1),P_(j-2)),(P_(j-1)',P_(j-2)'),(P_(j-1)'',P_(j-2)'')). This is a fixed-dimensional transfer for each observed history. Entropy still requires averaging the histories, and the state of the cavity ratio is generally not a finite set.

There is an exact nonbalanced perspective recursion too. Given a prefix history, set A=P_(j-1), B=P_(j-2), and

    C=p_j A-epsilon_(j-1) s_(j-1) B,    theta=C/A.

Then theta is the actual conditional chance of occupation at coordinate j, and 0<theta<1 by strictness. The chain rule is

    H_j-H_(j-1)=sum_(prefix histories) A h(theta).

Its exact second derivative is

    (A h(C/A))''
       =-[(C'-theta A')^2]/[A theta(1-theta)]
                  -log(1-theta) A''+log((1-theta)/theta) C'',
    C''=p_j A''+2p_j' A'
         -epsilon_(j-1)[s B''+2s' B'+s''B].                (7.1)

The first term is a negative square. The two remaining logarithmic terms are the exact unpaid history-weight/conditional-mean current. This is the chain counterpart of the two-leaf corrections in X022 and the changing-measure obstruction in X047/X058. No full-domain theorem for independent moving means follows from (7.1) alone.
