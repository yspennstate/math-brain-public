## 1. The theorem and the measured conditional-information correction

Let A={1,2}, B={3,4}, and K(Z)=I_4/2+[[0,Z],[Z*,0]], with 0<||Z||op<1/2.
For any strict four-site kernel J let f_ab(J)=Pr_J(N_A=a,N_B=b), w_ab=binom(2,a)binom(2,b), and

    Phi(J)=-sum_(a,b) f_ab(J) log[f_ab(J)/w_ab].                         (1)

**Theorem.** The full sixteen-dimensional Hermitian Hessian of Phi is negative definite at every K(Z) with Z nonzero. If all four entries of Z have the same modulus, then the full Hermitian Hessian of the actual measured entropy H(P_J) is also negative definite at K(Z). Every such base has a full-dimensional neighborhood on which H is strictly concave. The modulus and cycle phase are arbitrary subject only to strict contraction.

The first assertion concerns an entropy envelope; actual measured-entropy equality is required for the second. Block-unitary invariance of Phi is not asserted for H. This extends X030's rank-one equal-modulus case and X037's twelve zero-diagonal directions to every ambient direction at equal-modulus bases, including arbitrary nonzero determinant and cycle phase.

For any finite occupation law P, let Q(S,T)=f_(|S|,|T|)/w_(|S|,|T|). Then exactly

    Phi-H(P)=D(P||Q)
      =d_A+d_B+I(X_A;N_B|N_A)+I(N_A;X_B|N_B)
                 +I(X_A;X_B|N_A,N_B),                                (2)
    d_A=E log binom(2,N_A)-H(X_A|N_A), and similarly d_B.

This follows by subtracting conditional entropies and applying the mutual-information chain rule; every term is nonnegative by the elementary log-sum inequality. At a balanced bipartite kernel the two marginal laws are uniform, so d_A=d_B=0 and the correction equals I(X_A;X_B)-I(N_A;N_B). Thus the measurement correction is an actual sum of conditional informations, not a quantum-Markov assumption about measured bits.

At a touching law P=Q, differentiation along an arbitrary affine kernel direction D gives

    (Phi-H)''=sum_atoms (p')^2/p-sum_counts (f')^2/f >=0.                (3)

Indeed log p=log(f/w), and sum_atoms_in_fiber p''=f'', so **every atom acceleration cancels exactly** in the difference. Equation (3) keeps the complete atom Fisher information. It does not drop the adverse moving-conditional terms exhibited in C057/C068.
