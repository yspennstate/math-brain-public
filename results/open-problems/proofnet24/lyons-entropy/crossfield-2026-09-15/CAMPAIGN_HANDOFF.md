whose connecting segment leaves that family. Arbitrary measured-coordinate

The analytic boundary arguments are indispensable: the compact computation

## What to avoid repeating

Keep the original measured occupation entropy and the actual affine kernel
path. Count entropy, spectral Gaussian entropy, affine odds, and an arbitrary
refinement of outcome labels solve different problems. Retain both terms

    -H'' = sum (p')²/p + sum p'' log p.

The record contains exact failures of separate pivot payment, discarded
posterior-level positivity, real-phase extremality, first-frame Fisher
payment, complete monotonicity under odds shifts, and stronger path moment
closures. Their failures do not refute the target conjecture. Consult the
full report before attempting the same strengthening again.

## Resource recommendation

The most concrete next milestone in this record is the unrestricted
three-site case of Lyons's conjecture. Several entire direction sectors
and base families are now settled, and the remaining comparisons have exact
formulas. That is a reason to concentrate creative work on a sharply stated
lemma; it is not evidence that more agents alone will settle the full
all-dimensional conjecture.

The broader repository assessment is preserved in
[PORTFOLIO_ASSESSMENT.md](../recovery/2026-09-15_10-09-27/PORTFOLIO_ASSESSMENT.md).
It distinguishes famous conjectures from narrower paper-scale targets.
No famous full conjecture is responsibly designated a likely solution merely
because resources are available. In particular the recent Matrix Spencer
proof claim must be considered before treating that target as untouched:
[Akbas–Sra preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28816).

The primary target is [Lyons's paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1406.2707);
[Gu's project report](https://sevenkplus.com/data/dpp.pdf) supplies established
rank-one-direction and zero-endpoint results. Local developments above are
research artifacts with their own explicit arguments and limitations.
