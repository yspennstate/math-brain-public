# Exact scope of the current research results

**Full Lyons entropy concavity: OPEN in this campaign.** A full proof or a certified counterexample for arbitrary finite Hermitian contractions has not been produced. The table records proved restricted results, not a claim of historical priority. Agent execution counts are maintained separately in `execution_ledger.json`.

This is a selected mathematical frontier; `AGENT_INDEX.md` links every completed report, including all exact method obstructions and subsidiary constructions.

The [prior-scope crosswalk](PRIOR_FRONTIER.md) identifies results already proved in the earlier archive. In particular C027/C029 are new Markov constructions for an already covered two-coordinate-block/common-cross-scalar entropy family. They are not evidence of newly resolving that family.

## Exact obstructions that do not refute Lyons

Additional completed families: [C026](agents/C026.md) proves arbitrary mixed-sign outer-edge interpolation on balanced four-site paths; [C027](agents/C027.md) and [C029](agents/C029.md) prove coherent higher-rank block-scaling families by reversible and nonreversible Markov generators; [C024](agents/C024.md) proves explicit commuting spectral corridors, including a strongly coherent four-mode example; [C069](agents/C069.md) proves equal-modulus rank-two commuting squares in all dimensions and complementary families. Their full reports give exact restrictions; none establishes arbitrary two-endpoint concavity.

- C025 proves that five retention bits are necessary and sufficient in its augmented coherent coupling class; the resulting mutual-information correction can be convex.
- C069 gives a rational four-bit random-shuffle Jensen violation with positive covariance, explicitly outside determinantal scope.

- A covariance-only Fisher comparison can fail, even where the full entropy Hessian is negative: C003, C013, C017, and ROOT_FISHER_IDENTITY.md.
- Every three-feature trial can fail at a kernel where a seven-feature trial succeeds: C010. An optimized determinant-product-plus-parity span can also fail: C011.
- The normalized three-site Schur danger scalar need not attain its maximum at real, quadrature, or entropy-maximizing phase: C014. Its corrected certificate and discarded provisional error are explicit.
- A pointwise matrix-resolvent integral can have the wrong sign even on a decomposable two-form: C005.
- Coherent block attenuation can increase measured Jeffreys divergence and mutual information: C007. C022 identifies the exact rank-one condition making it literal classical replacement.
- A common invariant law alone does not imply Shannon concavity for commuting resets: C023 proves the exact higher-degree condition on log(mu).
- Mixed-sign resampling amplitudes can have positive entropy curvature for a non-DPP parity law: C021. This does not settle the DPP-specific question.
- A globally affine six-site coupling formula is impossible even in a family admitting explicit nonlinear positive couplings: C012.

All items have their hypotheses in the linked full reports. Numerical diagnostics, exact arithmetic certificates, analytic proofs, and external theorem inputs are identified there. No failed sufficient criterion is promoted to a counterexample to the full conjecture.
