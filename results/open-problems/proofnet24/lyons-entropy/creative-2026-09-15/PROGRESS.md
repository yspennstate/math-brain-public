## Selection

Lyons states the target as Conjecture 2.6 in his 2014 International Congress of Mathematicians paper, *Determinantal Probability: Basic Properties and Conjectures*, https://arxiv.org/abs/1406.2707 . It is an established specialist conjecture. It was selected for the concrete algebraic frontier in our archive. The more broadly famous Benjamini-Schramm nonuniqueness problem still has substantial unproved geometric steps, while the archive already identifies an external 2026 Matrix Spencer proof claim. No evidence establishes that any broadly famous unresolved problem in the portfolio is close to a full solution. The finite three-site criterion makes Lyons a bounded productive target; it does not guarantee proximity to a proof in all dimensions.
