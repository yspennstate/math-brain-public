An analytic route now gives the following candidate (constants and atom identities are undergoing an independent calculation before being labeled complete): for a star on center 0 and m leaves,

`K = I/2 + [[0,z*],[z,0]]`, `w_i=4|z_i|²`, `sum w_i<1`,

all diagonal directions should satisfy `D²H[diag a] <= -4 sum a_i²`. This permits every independent edge strength and every number of leaves, at arbitrary physical coupling.

Gauge-transforming spins on the leaves gives independent reference signs `t_i` and atom density `q=1+sum w_i t_i`. Let

`M(u)=exp(-u) product cosh(w_i u)`, `v_i(u)=tanh(w_i u)/u`, `d_i=w_i-v_i`.

The proposed exact excess Fisher/log-odds matrix `(J-C-4I)/4` has an integral whose integrand is

```
[[ M-exp(-u), M d^T ],
 [ M d, M (d d^T + diag(2 w_i v_i-v_i²)) ]].
```

Its Schur complement is nonnegative if `product cosh(x_i) >= 1+sum (x_i-tanh x_i)²/(2x_i tanh x_i-tanh² x_i)`, where `x_i=w_i u`. The latter follows coordinatewise from `sinh x>=x`, `tanh x<=x`, and expansion of the product. This is a full analytic inequality, not a sampled sign claim.
