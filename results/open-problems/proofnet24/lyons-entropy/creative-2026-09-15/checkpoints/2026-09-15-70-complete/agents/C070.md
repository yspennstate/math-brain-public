# C070 — a diagonal threshold theorem for nonphysical diagonal intersections

Date: 2026-09-15. Status: analytic theorem established; self-contained proof and exact verifier being finalized. This is a restricted entropy-concavity theorem, not the full Lyons conjecture.

The curvature bound further proves convexity of total correlation along every certified line. Its entropy Jensen gap is at least the sum of the Bernoulli marginal Jensen gaps, hence at least 2 theta(1-theta) sum_i (K1_ii-K0_ii)^2 for a unit-parameter segment.

## Main theorem (checkpoint statement)

Let 0<K<I be Hermitian, let alpha be real, and D=diag(beta_i) with beta_i>0 and alpha+beta_i>0 for every i. Define A=alpha K+D and T=diag(beta_i/(alpha+2 beta_i)). If K<=T or K>=T, then

    D^2 H(P_K)[A,A] <= -sum_i A_ii^2/[K_ii(1-K_ii)].

Reversing all slopes does not change the Hessian. The result integrates along any affine segment K(t)=(1+alpha t)K+tD that stays physical and on one side of the fixed matrix T. The physical segment through t=0 has 1+alpha t>0, so the same rate ratios and the same T apply at every point.

For K(t)=a(t)P+b(t)(I-P), arbitrary P, the remaining same-sign slopes have a nonphysical scalar intersection d outside [0,1], or equal slopes. Put tau=d/(2d-1), with tau=1/2 for equal slopes. Entropy is concave on every subinterval where both spectral values stay <=tau, or both stay >=tau. No rank, dimension, flatness, or odds condition-number restriction is imposed.

## Constructive identity behind the theorem

The affine occupation path is generated by commuting two-state operators G_i=[[-r_i,-s_i],[r_i,s_i]], where r_i=beta_i and s_i=alpha+beta_i. For a full two-coordinate conditional table (a,b,c,d)=(p00,p01,p10,p11), normalized to sum one, put R=ad/(bc)<=1. Its entropy cross term is

    Q_ij = X F(R)+Y G(R)
         = (X-Y)F(R)+Y J(R),

where X=r_i r_j a+s_i s_j d, Y=r_i s_j b+s_i r_j c,

    F(R)=R^(-1)-1+log R,
    G(R)=1-R+log R,
    J(R)=R^(-1)-R+2log R.

Both F and J are nonnegative on (0,1]. If C is the conditional 2x2 DPP kernel, then

    X-Y=(r_i+s_i)(r_j+s_j)det(T_{ij}-C)>=0.

Conditioning preserves either diagonal Loewner bound K<=T or K>=T. Hence every cross term is nonnegative. The own-coordinate Fisher terms remain, and conditional Jensen gives the displayed quantitative theorem.

## Scope relative to baselines

C006 already covers all physical diagonal intersections, including opposite spectral slopes. C075 already covers exchangeable bases in all Hermitian directions. This theorem concerns nonphysical diagonal intersections and their explicit spectral comparison regions. For example a(t)=2/5+t, b(t)=1/100+(6/5)t has d=47/20, tau=47/74. For every projection P, entropy is concave throughout 0<=t<=1/5; its initial odds ratio is 66 and its direction is generally nondiagonal. An arbitrary rational rank-two projection in six measured coordinates will be used in the exact verifier.

The straddling region b<tau<a remains open under this argument.
