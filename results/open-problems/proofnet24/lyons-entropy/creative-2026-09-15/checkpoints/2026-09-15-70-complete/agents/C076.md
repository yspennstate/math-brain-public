Proposed new family: a pivot p is rank-one coupled by a complex scalar z to the plus mode of a measured coherent pair
B(p)=y(p)P_+ + x(p)P_-, where x(p)=x_*−c_−(p−1/2), y(p)=y_*−c_+(p−1/2), and c_±≥0.
Both reservoir modes may move. Require p in [epsilon,1−epsilon], x in [1/4,3/4], and the affine LMIs
[[p,z],[conj(z),y−1/4]]≥0 and [[1−p,z],[conj(z),3/4−y]]≥0.
The domain is convex and ensures both actual conditional pair kernels have both mode eigenvalues in [1/4,3/4].

For two endpoints, C067 gives F1=M1+V/a, F0=M0−V/b,
V=t(1−t)(|Delta z|²P_+ +(Delta p)²T)≥0, T=c_+P_++c_−P_-.
Let Q1(s)=M1+sV/a and Q0(s)=M0−sV/b. The sum of the two weighted finite entropy corrections is exactly
integral_0^1 <grad H(Q1(s))−grad H(Q0(s)),V> ds.
Both Q_i(s) lie in the balanced mode square, and Q0−Q1=|z_t|²P_+/(ab)+(1−s)V/(ab)≥0.
C057's bounds −H_uu,−H_vv≥2 and −H_uv≥4/15 imply that this integral is nonnegative, with lower bound
(4/(15ab)) tr(V)|z_t|² +(2/(15ab))(tr V)².
This is a new combined finite correction, requiring no opposite monotone-atom signs.
