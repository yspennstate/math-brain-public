## 1. Completed result

A natural scalar atom-resolvent representation of the **entire negative entropy curvature** is not pointwise nonnegative, even after removing all product-law logarithms before making the sign comparison. The counterexample below has all of the features that the simpler product/null-direction obstruction lacks:

- the base is a dense, connected, rational strict three-site kernel;
- the Hermitian direction has rank three and inertia `(2 positive, 1 negative)`;
- its full measured Fisher information is exactly `300/19`;
- its frozen-log curvature is strictly positive, so Fisher is needed to prove the entropy sign;
- the product-recentered rational integrand is strictly negative at the rational parameter `s=5`, and on an entire terminal interval;
- its usual integration-by-parts representative also fails.

The actual entropy curvature of the witness is strictly negative. Section 6 gives an explicit positive rational integral for that curvature **after integrated logarithmic cancellation**. Thus the obstruction is to the specified pointwise certificates, rather than to every possible rational integral representation.

This goes beyond C005's frozen matrix-resolvent obstruction. It addresses scalar atom resolvents with the complete Fisher contribution, and it survives the natural repair that cancels modular logarithms first. It does not duplicate C043's frozen-only sign problem.

## 2. Full scalar-resolvent identity, with product terms canceled first

Fix any strict `n`-site kernel `K`, an affine Hermitian direction `A`, and write

\[
p_S=p_S(K),\qquad g_S=Dp_S(K)[A],\qquad h_S=D^2p_S(K)[A,A].
\]

All atoms are positive. Let `μ` be any **frozen positive product law**, and put

\[
r_S=p_S/\mu_S>0.
\]

For the counterexample we choose the product law with the actual marginals of `K`. The reference `μ` is held fixed during differentiation.

Normalization and affine one-site marginals give

\[
\sum_Sh_S=0,\qquad
\sum_{S\ni i}h_S=0\quad(i=1,\ldots,n).
\tag{1}
\]

Consequently every modular table is annihilated by `h`, in particular

\[
\sum_Sh_S\log\mu_S=0.
\tag{2}
\]

The complete entropy identity is

\[
-H''_K(A)=\sum_S\frac{g_S^2}{p_S}+\sum_Sh_S\log p_S.
\tag{3}
\]

Using (2), the scalar logarithm integral, and
`∫_0^∞ (r+s)^{-2} ds=1/r`, obtain

\[
\boxed{
-H''_K(A)=\int_0^\infty Q_s^\mu(K,A)\,ds,
\quad
Q_s^\mu=\sum_S\left[
\frac{g_S^2/\mu_S}{(r_S+s)^2}
-\frac{h_S}{r_S+s}
\right].}
\tag{4}
\]

Both the Fisher term and frozen curvature in (3) have been included, with their exact coefficients. The second summand has zero `1/s` coefficient by (1). Thus (4) is absolutely integrable at infinity and is bounded at zero.

At every product kernel, when `μ=p`, this repaired density satisfies

\[
Q_s^\mu=\frac{1}{(1+s)^2}\sum_S\frac{g_S^2}{p_S}\ge0.
\tag{5}
\]

It therefore avoids the familiar spurious pointwise signs at a diagonal kernel in a Fisher-null off-diagonal direction.

There is a second natural exact representative. Integration by parts gives

\[
\boxed{
-H''_K(A)=\int_0^\infty R_s^\mu(K,A)\,ds,
\quad R_s^\mu=-s\,\frac{d}{ds}Q_s^\mu
=\sum_S\left[
\frac{2s\,g_S^2/\mu_S}{(r_S+s)^3}
-\frac{s h_S}{(r_S+s)^2}
\right].}
\tag{6}
\]

The boundary term `s Q_s^μ` vanishes at both endpoints. Equivalently, (6) is obtained by differentiating the scalar resolvent representation of `−p log(p/μ)` twice before integrating. These equalities hold for arbitrary finite `n` and use no sign conjecture.

## 4. The direction has positive frozen curvature and nonzero Fisher

The table gives

\[
\boxed{\operatorname{FI}_K(A)=\sum_S\frac{g_S^2}{p_S}=\frac{300}{19}.}
\tag{9}
\]

Define the positive rational numbers

\[
C_0=\frac{58081}{43681},\qquad
T_0=\frac{19^2\,11^6}{241^3},
\]

and set

\[
c=\log C_0,\qquad\tau=\log T_0.
\tag{10}
\]

These are precisely the common pair Möbius coefficient and triple Möbius coefficient of the frozen negative log-atom table. Indeed

\[
L=K(I-K)^{-1}=19I-\frac{120}{19}J,
\]

whose diagonal is `241/19` and normalized edge magnitude is `120/241`. Consequently the usual three-site matrix is `M=cI+τK`. Direct substitution into either the atom table or `2 tr(M adj A)` yields

\[
\boxed{
F''_{-\log p}(K)[A,A]= -\sum_Sh_S\log p_S
=-\frac92c+\frac{27}{40}\tau.}
\tag{11}
\]

The following entirely rational comparisons suffice for the needed signs:

\[
0<c<\frac{14400}{43681}<\frac13,
\qquad
27<T_0<64.
\tag{12}
\]

The first uses `log(1+x)<x`. The second is the integer comparison

\[
27\cdot241^3<19^2 11^6<64\cdot241^3.
\]

The scalar inequalities `log 3>1` and `log 2<1` then give

\[
3<\tau<6.
\tag{13}
\]

For the first one, integrate `1/x` or use
`log x>2(x−1)/(x+1)` for `x>1`; the second follows from `log(1+x)<x`.
Combining (11)–(13),

\[
\boxed{\frac{21}{40}<F''_{-\log p}(K)[A,A]<\frac{81}{20}.}
\tag{14}
\]

This puts the witness in the positive frozen-curvature cone. Dropping Fisher would give the wrong entropy sign. Keeping the complete Fisher term gives

\[
\boxed{

\tag{15}
\]

The exact logarithmic values are approximately

## 6. A positive rational integral after the cancellation

The witness admits an explicit replacement density once its integrated logarithmic coefficients have been combined. This demonstrates concretely what the preceding obstruction does and does not prohibit.

Keep `C_0,T_0` from (10), and define for `0≤x≤1`

\[
\begin{aligned}
W(x)={}&\frac{4461}{380}
+\frac92\frac{C_0-1}{1+(C_0-1)x}\\
&+\frac{27}{40}\left[
\frac{6x}{1+x}
+\frac{64-T_0}{T_0+(64-T_0)x}
\right].
\end{aligned}
\tag{21}
\]

All coefficients and denominators are rational, and (12) gives

\[
\boxed{W(x)\ge\frac{4461}{380}>0\quad(0\le x\le1).}
\]

Direct integration proves the exact identity

\[
\boxed{-H''_K(A)=\int_0^1W(x)\,dx.}
\tag{22}
\]

Indeed, the second term integrates to `(9/2)c`, and the bracket integrates to

\[
6(1-\log2)+\log(64/T_0)=6-\tau.
\]

The constant in (21) is `300/19−(27/40)·6`. Expansion therefore gives exactly (15). This is a full-curvature certificate for the witness, with Fisher and frozen contributions both retained. It uses the integrated relations between logarithms; it is not a universal prescription for all kernels or directions.
