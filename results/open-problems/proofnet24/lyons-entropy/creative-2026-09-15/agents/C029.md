## 1. A finite algebraic certificate

Let a finite probability family have the form

\[
p_\eta(x)=\mu_x r_\eta(x),\qquad
r_\eta=1+\eta^2h+\eta^4g,
\]

where every \(\mu_x>0\), \(\langle1,h\rangle_\mu=\langle1,g\rangle_\mu=0\), and \(h,g\) are linearly independent. Suppose also

\[
\langle h,\log\mu\rangle_\mu=
\langle g,\log\mu\rangle_\mu=0. \tag{1}
\]

Set

\[
H_2=\langle h,h\rangle_\mu,\quad
G_2=\langle g,g\rangle_\mu,\quad C=\langle h,g\rangle_\mu,
\qquad
\ell=\frac{G_2h-Cg}{H_2G_2-C^2}.
\]

Thus \(\langle\ell,1\rangle_\mu=0\), \(\langle\ell,h\rangle_\mu=1\), and \(\langle\ell,g\rangle_\mu=0\). Define

\[
R f=\langle1,f\rangle_\mu1,\qquad
P f=h\langle\ell,f\rangle_\mu.
\]

The exact sufficient certificate is

\[
\boxed{\quad h_x\ell_y\ge-\frac65\quad\text{for every }x\ne y.\quad} \tag{2}
\]

Under (2), both

\[
L=4(R-I)+2P,\qquad S=12(R-I)+10P
\]

are Markov generators on functions, with invariant probability \(\mu\). Indeed, their off-diagonal entries are respectively

\[
L_{xy}=\mu_y(4+2h_x\ell_y),\qquad
S_{xy}=\mu_y(12+10h_x\ell_y).
\]

They have zero row sums because \(P1=0\); invariance follows from \(\mu^TP=0\). Their positivity follows directly from (2). Also

\[
R^2=R,\quad P^2=P,\quad RP=PR=0,
\quad Lh=-2h,\quad Lg=-4g,\quad S=-(L^2+L). \tag{3}
\]

This supplies an explicit positive occupation-space semigroup. With source state \(y\) and destination state \(x\), its transition probabilities are

\[
\boxed{\quad
Q_t(y,x)=e^{-4t}\mathbf1_{x=y}
+\mu_x\left[1-e^{-4t}+(e^{-2t}-e^{-4t})h_x\ell_y\right].\quad} \tag{4}
\]

Indeed \(e^{tL}=R+e^{-2t}P+e^{-4t}(I-R-P)\), and (4) is its action on probability masses, obtained by conjugation with \(\operatorname{diag}\mu\). In particular,

\[
\sum_y p_\eta(y)Q_t(y,x)=p_{e^{-t}\eta}(x). \tag{5}
\]

No reversibility was used. When \(C\ne0\), these generators are not reversible: self-adjointness of the rank-one operator \(P=h\otimes\ell\) would force \(\ell\) to be proportional to \(h\), contradicting \(\langle\ell,g\rangle_\mu=0\) and \(C\ne0\).

The canonical Gram formula for \(\ell\) is convenient, but unnecessary: any vector satisfying the three displayed duality constraints and (2) works. Finding such an \(\ell\) is a finite linear feasibility problem for fixed \(\mu,h,g\).

## 2. Entropy concavity without detailed balance

For any stationary Markov generator \(S\) and positive \(f\),

\[
\langle Sf,\log f\rangle_\mu
=-\sum_{x\ne y}\mu_xS_{xy}
\left[f_y\log\frac{f_y}{f_x}-f_y+f_x\right]\le0. \tag{6}
\]

The equality follows by expanding and using both zero row sums and stationarity; the bracket is nonnegative by convexity. Thus (6) needs no symmetric edge weights.

By (1), the cross entropy \(-\sum_xp_\eta(x)\log\mu_x\) is constant. By (3), for \(\eta\ne0\),

\[
Sr_\eta=-2\eta^2h-12\eta^4g=-\eta^2r_\eta''.
\]

Consequently, wherever all atoms are positive,

\[
\boxed{\quad
\frac{d^2}{d\eta^2}H(p_\eta)
=-\sum_x\mu_x\frac{(r_\eta'(x))^2}{r_\eta(x)}
+\frac1{\eta^2}\langle Sr_\eta,\log r_\eta\rangle_\mu
\le0.\quad} \tag{7}
\]

At \(\eta=0\), direct differentiation gives \(H''(0)=0\). Independence of \(h,g\) makes the first term strictly negative when \(\eta\ne0\). Concavity extends to any admissible endpoints with zero atoms by continuity of finite entropy.

## 3. Why the certificate applies to block DPPs

Consider an affine kernel

\[
K_\eta=\begin{pmatrix}A&\eta B\\\eta B^*&D\end{pmatrix},\qquad
0\prec A\prec I,\quad0\prec D\prec I,
\]

where one block has two sites and the other has any finite size. Every exact occupation atom has degree at most four in \(\eta\): a determinant permutation crosses from the two-site block to its complement as many times as it crosses back, and this number is at most two. Hence the powers are only \(1,\eta^2,\eta^4\).

At zero, \(\mu=\mathsf P_A\otimes\mathsf P_D\), with all atoms positive. Both block marginals stay fixed. Therefore each nonconstant coefficient sums to zero under either block marginal. Since \(\log\mu\) is a sum of one function of each block, (1) follows. Thus (2) proves affine-parameter entropy concavity on the whole physical interval \(0\preceq K_\eta\preceq I\).

## 4. Explicit all-phase theorem

Take

\[
K_\eta=\begin{pmatrix}
1/2&c&\eta a&0\\
c&1/2&0&\eta b e^{i\phi}\\
\eta a&0&1/2&d\\
0&\eta b e^{-i\phi}&d&1/2
\end{pmatrix},
\]

with \(0\le c,d<1/2\) and \(a,b>0\). Write

\[
\alpha=4c^2,\quad\beta=4d^2,\quad\gamma=4a^2,\quad\delta=4b^2,
\quad\zeta=-32cdab\cos\phi.
\]

For spins \(y_i=1-2X_i\), let \(z=y_1y_2\), \(w=y_3y_4\), \(s=y_1y_3\), and \(\Delta=(1-\alpha z)(1-\beta w)\). Direct determinant expansion gives

\[
\mu=\Delta/16,\qquad
h=h_0+\zeta v,\qquad g=\gamma\delta v,
\quad h_0=-\frac{s(\gamma+\delta zw)}\Delta,
\quad v=\frac{zw}{\Delta}.
\]

The variable \(s\) is fair conditional on \(z,w\), so \(\langle h_0,v\rangle_\mu=0\). Therefore the oblique dual simplifies to

\[
\ell=\frac{h_0}{N},\qquad
N=\frac{\gamma^2+\delta^2+2\alpha\beta\gamma\delta}
{(1-\alpha^2)(1-\beta^2)}.
\]

Because the largest absolute values of both \(h\) and \(h_0\) occur at \(z=w=1\), with suitable opposite choices of \(s\), the exact off-diagonal minimum is

\[
\min_{x\ne y}h_x\ell_y=-M,\qquad
M=\frac{(1+\alpha)(1+\beta)}{(1-\alpha)(1-\beta)}
\frac{(\gamma+\delta)(\gamma+\delta+|\zeta|)}
{\gamma^2+\delta^2+2\alpha\beta\gamma\delta}. \tag{8}
\]

Thus \(M\le6/5\) proves entropy concavity for this entire affine family, including generic complex phases. Moreover,

\[
\langle h,g\rangle_\mu=
\frac{\zeta\gamma\delta}{(1-\alpha^2)(1-\beta^2)},
\]

so the construction is nonorthogonal and nonreversible whenever \(cd\cos\phi\ne0\).

A new rational seed, distinct from C027's, is

\[
c=1/30,\qquad d=1/24,\qquad a=1/8,\qquad b=1/80.
\]

For **every** phase, substituting \(|\zeta|\le1/14400\) gives

\[
M\le\frac{74469825}{71287172}<\frac{21}{20}<\frac65.
\]

For \(e^{i\phi}=(3+4i)/5\),
\(\langle h,g\rangle_\mu=-2187/1343601459200\ne0\).
For all \(|\eta|\le1\), \(\|K_\eta-I/2\|_{\rm op}\le1/6\), so these kernels are strictly positive contractions.

## 5. Dense complex unequal-block example

The following rational blocks also satisfy the general certificate:

\[
A=\begin{pmatrix}
99/200&1/30+i/1000\\
1/30-i/1000&101/200
\end{pmatrix},\qquad
D=\begin{pmatrix}
199/400&1/24-i/1200\\
1/24+i/1200&201/400
\end{pmatrix},
\]

\[
B=\begin{pmatrix}
1/8&(1+2i)/1000\\
(-2+i)/1200&(3+4i)/400
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

All four diagonal entries differ, every off-diagonal entry is nonzero when \(\eta\ne0\), and phases are not restricted to the previous four-cycle support. The exact rational verifier establishes

\[
\|h\|_\infty<\frac{13}{200},\qquad
\|\ell\|_\infty<\frac{83}{5},\qquad
-\frac{2}{10^9}<\langle h,g\rangle_\mu<-\frac1{10^9}.
\]

Hence \(h_x\ell_y>-1079/1000>-6/5\). Every off-diagonal entry of \(S\) exceeds \((121/100)\mu_y\). A rational row-sum bound gives \(\|K_\eta-I/2\|_{\rm op}\le69/400<1/2\) for \(|\eta|\le1\).

All inequalities are strict. Continuity therefore gives an open neighborhood in the full \((A,D,B)\) parameter space with the same conclusion. Appending independent fair sites to the second block preserves the certificate, and small arbitrary Hermitian perturbations of that enlarged block and arbitrary complex perturbations of its cross block preserve it. This proves nonempty open families of such block-scaling directions in every dimension \(n\ge4\).
