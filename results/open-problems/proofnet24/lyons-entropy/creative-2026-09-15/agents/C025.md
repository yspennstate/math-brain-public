# C025 — a sharp five-bit coherent coupling, and its entropy-curvature obstruction

> Root source crosswalk, 2026-09-15: the prior session already proves entropy concavity for this two-coordinate-block/common-cross-scalar scope. This report contributes the stated constructive method or obstruction, not a newly enlarged entropy scope; see [PRIOR_FRONTIER](../PRIOR_FRONTIER.md).

Date: 2026-09-15. Creative construction for the first two-active-orbital block. No subagents, external messages, pushes, or random sampling.

## Result and precise scope

This report gives a positive classical augmentation of Gaussian beam-splitter occupation laws for an explicit four-site family with **two coherent reservoir blocks, noncommuting first-block endpoints, and rank-two cross correlation**. Exactly five independent Bernoulli retention bits are necessary and sufficient within the class of parameter-independent augmented retention couplings defined below. Four physical-mode retention bits do not suffice, even if the augmentation retains the entire covariance and all its phases.

The resulting coupling has an exact Shannon-entropy curvature identity. Its mutual-information correction has strictly **positive** curvature in a rational, strictly positive, rank-two example. Thus a proof that constructs this positive lift and then treats its information correction as concave fails. This is an exact obstruction to that method, not a counterexample to Lyons concavity. Global entropy concavity for the displayed family is not claimed.

The construction is different from C007's probability-only obstruction: the conditional output laws here may depend on all endpoint coherence data. The obstruction is to the number and parameter dependence of independent retention variables. It also goes beyond a one-active-orbital block: both singular values of each inter-block coupling are nonzero.

## 1. The strict rank-two family

Put
\[
A=\begin{pmatrix}1/10&-1/4\\-1/4&4/5\end{pmatrix},\qquad
B=\begin{pmatrix}1/10&1/4\\1/4&4/5\end{pmatrix},\qquad
C=\begin{pmatrix}1/2&1/8\\1/8&1/2\end{pmatrix},
\]
and, for \(0<|\varepsilon|\le1/25\), define
\[
D=B\oplus C,\qquad
K_\varepsilon=\begin{pmatrix}A&\varepsilon I_2\\
\varepsilon I_2&C\end{pmatrix},\qquad
K_{\eta,\varepsilon}=D+\eta(K_\varepsilon-D).
\tag{1}
\]
All these endpoint kernels are strict positive contractions. Indeed,
\[
\lambda_{\min}(A)=\lambda_{\min}(B)>\frac{\det A}{\operatorname{tr}A}
=\frac7{360},\qquad
\lambda_{\min}(I-A)>\frac{47}{440},
\]
whereas \(\lambda_{\min}(C)=\lambda_{\min}(I-C)=3/8\). The Schur-complement perturbation in either \(K_\varepsilon\) or \(I-K_\varepsilon\) has norm at most
\[
\frac83\varepsilon^2\le\frac8{1875}
<\min\left(\frac7{360},\frac{47}{440}\right).
\tag{2}
\]
This proves strictness, and convexity proves it along the entire segment.

The reservoir is correlated in the measured basis:
\(\operatorname{Cov}_D(X_1,X_2)=-1/16\) and
\(\operatorname{Cov}_D(X_3,X_4)=-1/64\).
Moreover,
\[
[A,B]=\begin{pmatrix}0&-7/20\\7/20&0\end{pmatrix}\ne0,
\]
and \(\varepsilon I_2\) has rank two in any choice of orbital bases.
The standard Gaussian beam splitter with a fresh reservoir \(\rho_D\) has measured output law \(P_{K_{\eta,\varepsilon}}\), by the covariance formula in C006.

## 2. Which augmented classical couplings are being considered?

An **\(m\)-bit augmented retention coupling** consists of:

* arbitrary parameter-independent auxiliary data \(Z\), whose distribution may depend on the full matrices \(K_\varepsilon,D\), including their phases;
* independent \(E_1,\ldots,E_m\sim\operatorname{Bernoulli}(\eta)\), independent of \(Z\);
* a parameter-independent conditional output rule for \(X\), given \((Z,E_1,\ldots,E_m)\).

There is no locality restriction on the output rule. It can use all the retained bits and all the auxiliary data together. Thus this class is broader than modewise coordinate replacement. The restriction is that \(\eta\) enters through the independent retention bits alone.

Let \(b_{m,j}(\eta)=\binom mj\eta^j(1-\eta)^{m-j}\). Averaging the output rule over \(Z\) and over bit strings of the same weight gives probability laws \(Q_0,\ldots,Q_m\) satisfying
\[
p_\eta=\sum_{j=0}^m b_{m,j}(\eta)Q_j.
\tag{3}
\]
Conversely, whenever the Bernstein coefficients \(Q_j\) in (3) are probability laws, sample the bits, put \(J=\sum_iE_i\), and sample \(X\sim Q_J\). This constructs the coupling. One additional fixed uniform variable can supply the sampling randomness. Using fixed independent uniforms to generate all \(E_i(\eta)\) simultaneously gives a coupling of the laws for the whole interval.

Because the degree-\(m\) Bernstein basis is linearly independent, (3) is possible exactly when its unique coefficient vectors are nonnegative and normalized. Allowing more coherence in \(Z\) cannot change this criterion. This criterion also applies to a fixed quantum augmentation followed by bit-conditioned channels and a measurement: after that measurement its conditional output laws are still nonnegative \(Q_j\).

## 3. Four bits are impossible, including with arbitrary coherence data

The event \(X_1=1,X_2=0\) has probability determined by the first-block marginal alone. Its probability along (1) is
\[
f(\eta)=\frac1{50}+\frac{(1-2\eta)^2}{16}
=\frac{33}{400}-\frac\eta4+\frac{\eta^2}4.
\tag{4}
\]
Its degree-four Bernstein coefficients are exactly
\[
\left(\frac{33}{400},\frac1{50},-\frac1{1200},
\frac1{50},\frac{33}{400}\right).
\tag{5}
\]
If a four-bit augmented coupling existed, each entry in (5) would be the probability of this event under a corresponding coefficient law. The negative middle entry is impossible. A coupling with fewer bits could be enlarged to four by adding ignored independent bits, so it is impossible as well.

This obstruction is independent of \(\varepsilon\), and consequently persists throughout the strict rank-two family.

Already at degree two the mixed coefficient law of the first block, ordered as \(00,10,01,11\), is
\[
Q_{\mathrm{mixed}}=\frac1{400}(97,-17,263,57).
\tag{6}
\]
This is the coherent interference in the attempted “retain exactly one orbital” component. Degree elevation to the four physical modes still leaves (5) negative. The issue is positivity even after all coherence information has been supplied, rather than failure to identify a phase from occupation probabilities.

## 4. Five bits suffice: an explicit uniformly positive construction

The positive coefficient laws can be given without numerical matrix diagonalization. Write an atom as \(x=(x_1,x_2,x_3,x_4)\), and set
\[
d=(1/10,4/5,1/2,1/2),\quad
h_i=d_i-\mathbf1_{\{x_i=0\}},\quad
u_x=h_2h_4+h_1h_3,
\]
\[
\chi_x=(-1)^{|x|},\qquad
\chi_U=(-1)^{x_1+x_2}.
\]
Let \(\mu_U\) be the product law with means \((1/10,4/5)\), and let \(\mu_C=P_C\). In the order \(00,10,01,11\),
\[
\mu_U=\frac1{50}(9,1,36,4),\qquad
\mu_C=\frac1{64}(15,17,17,15).
\]
For \(j=0,\ldots,5\), define
\[
\alpha_j=\frac{\binom j2}{10},\qquad
\beta_j=\frac{\binom j3}{10},\qquad
\gamma_j=\frac{\binom j4}{5},\qquad
r_j=1-\frac{4j}5+4\alpha_j.
\]
Thus \(r=(1,1/5,-1/5,-1/5,1/5,1)\). Define six laws by
\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
Q_j(x)={}&\left[\mu_U(x_1,x_2)-\frac{\chi_U r_j}{16}\right]
\mu_C(x_3,x_4)\\
&+\chi_x\left\{\varepsilon^2\left[-u_x\alpha_j+
\frac{2\beta_j-\alpha_j}{16}\right]+\varepsilon^4\gamma_j\right\}.
\end{aligned}}
\tag{7}
\]

**Exactness.** Put \(c_\eta=(1-2\eta)/4\). The signed determinant for the atom is the determinant of the four-cycle matrix with diagonal \(h_i\), local edges \(c_\eta,1/8\), and cross edges \(\varepsilon\eta,\varepsilon\eta\). Its expansion is
\[
\begin{aligned}
p_{\eta,\varepsilon}(x)
={}&P_{B+\eta(A-B)}(x_1,x_2)\mu_C(x_3,x_4)\\
&+\chi_x\left\{-\varepsilon^2\eta^2
\left(u_x+\frac{1-2\eta}{16}\right)
+\varepsilon^4\eta^4\right\}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{8}
\]
The degree-five Bernstein coefficients of \(\eta^2,\eta^3,\eta^4\) are respectively \(\alpha_j,\beta_j,\gamma_j\). Applying this to (8) proves
\[
P_{K_{\eta,\varepsilon}}=\sum_{j=0}^5b_{5,j}(\eta)Q_j.
\tag{9}
\]
Normalization of every \(Q_j\) follows by summing (9) and using uniqueness of Bernstein coefficients of the constant polynomial 1.

**Positivity.** Each first-block factor in square brackets in (7) is at least \(3/400\), and \(\min\mu_C=15/64\). Therefore its product is at least \(9/5120\). Also
\[
|u_x|\le\frac{17}{20},\qquad
0\le\beta_j\le\alpha_j\le1,\qquad0\le\gamma_j\le1.
\]
It follows that
\[
\left|-u_x\alpha_j+\frac{2\beta_j-\alpha_j}{16}\right|
\le\frac{17}{20}+\frac1{16}=\frac{73}{80}.
\]
For every atom and every \(j\), throughout the family,
\[
\boxed{
Q_j(x)\ge\frac9{5120}-\frac{73}{80}\varepsilon^2-\varepsilon^4
\ge\frac{118101}{400000000}>0.
}
\tag{10}
\]
Equations (7), (9), and (10) prove the promised positive coupling. Together with (5), they prove that **five is the exact minimum number of independent retention bits in this augmented class** for every member of (1).

The extra bit is an artificial random variable in the classical representation. The construction is not a claim that a physical four-mode beam splitter performs five local replacement operations.

## 5. The exact entropy correction and its wrong sign for a concavity proof

For the coupling (9), let \(E=(E_1,\ldots,E_5)\), \(J=|E|\), and define
\[
G(\eta)=\sum_{j=0}^5b_{5,j}(\eta)H(Q_j),\qquad
\mathcal I(\eta)=I(J;X)=I(E;X),\qquad
\mathcal C(\eta)=H(E\mid X).
\]
The equality of the two mutual informations follows from the conditional independence of \(X\) and \(E\) given \(J\). The entropy chain rule gives the exact identities
\[
\boxed{
H(P_{K_{\eta,\varepsilon}})=G(\eta)+\mathcal I(\eta)
=5h(\eta)+G(\eta)-\mathcal C(\eta).
}
\tag{11}
\]
Since all coefficient laws and interior weights are positive, these functions are smooth on \((0,1)\). In particular,
\[
\boxed{
H''=20\sum_{j=0}^3b_{3,j}(\eta)
\big[H(Q_{j+2})-2H(Q_{j+1})+H(Q_j)\big]+\mathcal I''.
}
\tag{12}
\]
Equivalently,
\[
H''=-\frac5{\eta(1-\eta)}+G''-\mathcal C''.
\tag{13}
\]
These are corrections to the actual measured Shannon entropy, not to the von Neumann entropy of a Gaussian state.

For the fixed rational example \(\varepsilon=1/100\), at \(\eta=1/2\), rational logarithm bounds prove
\[
-\frac{11}{10^7}<H''<-\frac1{10^6},
\qquad
-\frac{4850}{10000}<G''<-\frac{4849}{10000},
\]
and
\[
\boxed{\frac{4849}{10000}<\mathcal I''<\frac{4850}{10000}.}
\tag{14}
\]
Numerical summaries of the rigorous intervals are
\[
H''\simeq-0.00000106209123238735,\qquad
G''\simeq-0.484915010376173,\qquad
\mathcal I''\simeq0.484913948284940.
\]
Consequently \(\mathcal C''=-20-\mathcal I''<-20.4849\).
The information correction is strictly convex here and cancels nearly all the negative curvature of the conditional component entropy. Positivity of the augmented coupling therefore supplies neither the inequality \(H''\le G''\) nor the needed concavity of \(\mathcal I\).

### Rational sign certificate for (14)

The program computes the atom probabilities, first derivatives, and second derivatives by exact symbolic determinants. At the midpoint all are rational. It uses
\[
H''=-\sum_x\frac{(p'_x)^2}{p_x}-\sum_xp''_x\log p_x,
\qquad
G''=\frac52\big[H(Q_0)+H(Q_1)+H(Q_4)+H(Q_5)\big]
-5\big[H(Q_2)+H(Q_3)\big].
\tag{15}
\]
For each rational logarithm argument, write \(q=2^k y\), \(1\le y<2\). With \(z=(y-1)/(y+1)\in[0,1/3)\), the certificate uses 28 terms in
\[
\log y=2\sum_{r=0}^{27}\frac{z^{2r+1}}{2r+1}+R,
\qquad
0\le R\le\frac{2z^{57}}{57(1-z^2)}.
\tag{16}
\]
The same bound at \(z=1/3\) encloses \(\log2\). Bounds are rounded outward to denominator \(10^{24}\), then all of (15) is evaluated by rational interval arithmetic. The code asserts the displayed rational intervals for \(H'',G'',\mathcal I'',\mathcal C''\). The floating-point values above are summaries, not sign evidence.

## 6. What this resolves and what remains

The positive coupling (7)–(10) handles a genuinely correlated reservoir and a noncommuting block with two active orbitals. It retains all needed coherence through its explicit coefficient laws. It also demonstrates two exact limitations of extending C006 through a classical retention lift:

1. One independent retention variable per physical mode is insufficient even with unrestricted parameter-independent augmentation. The forced event coefficient (5) is negative.
2. Adding enough hidden variables restores positivity, but the exact information correction in (12) can have positive curvature. It cannot be discarded or declared concave.

Neither limitation is the universal probability-only obstruction in C007. Neither refutes a different augmentation with parameter-dependent output rules, or the original Gaussian quantum channel. Such alternatives must still control their exact measured-entropy correction.

The remaining obligation for this five-bit route is to bound the positive part of \(\mathcal I''\) by \(-G''\), or to find a different representation with a usable correction. This report does not establish that global bound, and makes no claim to solve Lyons concavity or to prove global concavity even for (1). It supplies a complete positive construction, a sharp obstruction to the natural number of retention variables, and an exact failure of the proposed sign of the entropy correction. No bibliographic priority is asserted.
