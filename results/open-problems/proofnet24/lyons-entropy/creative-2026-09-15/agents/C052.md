## 2. A quantitative lower frame from the rare and middle count bins

**Theorem 1 — complete Fisher frame.** Suppose

\[
0<\delta\le\tfrac12,\qquad
0<\varepsilon\le\delta/64,
\]

\[
\kappa_1\le\varepsilon,\qquad
\delta\le\kappa_2\le1-\delta,\qquad
\kappa_3\ge1-\varepsilon.
\tag{6}
\]

Then, for every Hermitian (A),

\[
\boxed{
\sum_{h=1}^7\omega_h(w_h^*Aw_h)^2
\ge\mathcal I_N(A)
\ge\tfrac32d_2^2+\frac{\delta}{3\varepsilon}(d_1^2+d_3^2).}
\tag{7}
\]

In particular (\mathcal I_K(A)\ge\tfrac32\sum_i d_i^2).

**Proof.** Put (a=\kappa_1,b=\kappa_2,d=\kappa_3), temporarily reserving (d_i) for the directional entries. Apply

\[
(x+y)^2\ge\tfrac12x^2-y^2
\tag{8}
\]

to the full-atom derivative, with its main term (bd\,d_1). Equation (4) and (6) give

\[
\frac{Q_3^2}{P_3}
\ge\frac{\delta(1-\varepsilon)}{2\varepsilon}d_1^2
-\frac{2\varepsilon}{\delta(1-\varepsilon)}(d_2^2+d_3^2).
\tag{9}
\]

For example, before bounding the coefficients, the right side of (8) is

\[
\tfrac12\frac{bd}{a}d_1^2
-\frac{a}{bd}(d\,d_2+b\,d_3)^2.
\]

The empty atom gives the complemented inequality

\[
\frac{Q_0^2}{P_0}
\ge\frac{\delta(1-\varepsilon)}{2\varepsilon}d_3^2
-\frac{2\varepsilon}{\delta(1-\varepsilon)}(d_1^2+d_2^2).
\tag{10}
\]

For the two middle bins, weighted Cauchy–Schwarz and (P_1+P_2\le1) give

\[
\frac{Q_1^2}{P_1}+\frac{Q_2^2}{P_2}
\ge\frac{(Q_2-Q_1)^2}{P_1+P_2}
\ge(2s+3Q_0-3Q_3)^2.
\tag{11}
\]

Write the last linear form as (h_1d_1+h_2d_2+h_3d_3), where

\[
h_i=2-3\left[(1-\kappa_j)(1-\kappa_k)+\kappa_j\kappa_k\right]
\quad(\{i,j,k\}=\{1,2,3\}).
\]

The bracket lies in ([0,1]), so (|h_1|,|h_3|\le2). For the extreme pair in (h_2), that bracket is at most (2\varepsilon), hence (h_2\ge2-6\varepsilon>0). A second use of (8) gives

\[
(2s+3Q_0-3Q_3)^2
\ge\tfrac12(2-6\varepsilon)^2d_2^2-8(d_1^2+d_3^2).
\tag{12}
\]

Adding (9)–(12) proves

\[
\mathcal I_N(A)\ge D(d_1^2+d_3^2)+B d_2^2,
\]

\[
D=\frac{\delta(1-\varepsilon)}{2\varepsilon}
-\frac{2\varepsilon}{\delta(1-\varepsilon)}-8,
\qquad
B=\tfrac12(2-6\varepsilon)^2
-\frac{4\varepsilon}{\delta(1-\varepsilon)}.
\tag{13}
\]

Since (\varepsilon\le\delta/64\le1/128), the following wholly rational estimates suffice:

\[
B\ge\frac{15625}{8192}-\frac8{127}>\frac32,
\tag{14}
\]

\[
D-\frac\delta{3\varepsilon}
\ge\frac{32}{3}-\frac{33}{4}-\frac4{127}>0.
\tag{15}
\]

Equations (2)–(3) finish the proof. The final assertion uses (\delta/(3\varepsilon)\ge64/3>3/2). \(\square\)
