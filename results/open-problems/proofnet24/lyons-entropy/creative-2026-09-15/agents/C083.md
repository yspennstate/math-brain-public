# C083 — phase-frustrated full-Hessian counterexample search

Status: completed, 2026-09-15. **No Lyons counterexample found or claimed.**

The constructive output is an exact phase-sector Hessian formula for every
chordless cycle, a complete scalar sign criterion for that sector, and an open
family showing why nonpositivity of every edge's radial entropy derivative
cannot be the general proof mechanism. A rational four-site instance has one
strictly positive radial derivative but a rigorously negative entire phase
sector. The strongest six-site numerical search direction was rationalized;
its curvature was rigorously bounded above by `-1/500000` on an exactly feasible
affine line. This is a negative directional certificate, not a full-Hessian
certificate at that six-site kernel.

## 1. Exact phase-sector reduction for a chordless cycle

Let `n>=4` and let the only nonzero off-diagonal entries of an interior
Hermitian contraction be

\[
K_{i,i+1}=z_i\ne0,\qquad i\pmod n.
\]

The diagonal entries may be arbitrary. Put

\[
r_i=|z_i|^2,\quad Q=\prod_i z_i=q+iv,
\quad c_S=2(-1)^{|S|+1}.
\]

Expanding the exact atom determinant by permutation cycles gives

\[
p_S=b_S(r_0,\ldots,r_{n-1})+c_S q.
\tag{1}
\]

Here `b_S` is the matching polynomial

\[
(-1)^{n-|S|}
\sum_{M\text{ a matching of }C_n}(-1)^{|M|}
\prod_{e\in M}r_e\prod_{j\notin V(M)}
\bigl(K_{jj}-1_{j\notin S}\bigr).
\]

The only permutation cycles of length at least three are the two orientations
of the full cycle. Their coefficient is exactly `c_S`, independently of `n`.
One may differentiate entropy as a formal function of `(r,q)` on the open set
where these atoms are positive. Normalization holds identically in these
variables. Define

\[
H_q=-\sum_S c_S\log p_S
 =2\log\frac{\prod_{|S|\text{ even}}p_S}
                   {\prod_{|S|\text{ odd}}p_S},
\qquad F_q=\sum_S\frac{c_S^2}{p_S}=4\sum_Sp_S^{-1}.
\]

Let `w_i` be the entropy derivative when just edge `i` is multiplied by `1+s`:

\[
w_i=2r_iH_{r_i}+qH_q.
\tag{2}
\]

Consider every phase tangent simultaneously:

\[
(A_x)_{i,i+1}=ix_i z_i,\qquad x_i\in\mathbb R.
\]

Along the **affine** line `K+tA_x`,

\[
r_i'=0,\quad r_i''=2r_ix_i^2,\quad
q'=-v\sum_i x_i,\quad q''=-2q\sum_{i<j}x_ix_j.
\]

Since `H_qq=-F_q`, the complete phase-sector Hessian is

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{Hess}H_K[A_x,A_x]
=\sum_iw_ix_i^2-\gamma\Bigl(\sum_ix_i\Bigr)^2,
\qquad \gamma=qH_q+v^2F_q.
}
\tag{3}
\]

Thus an `n`-dimensional block of the full Hessian is exactly
`diag(w)-gamma 11^T`. This is a sign criterion, not a universal claim that it
is nonpositive.

### Complete scalar criterion

Formula (3) yields the following necessary and sufficient conditions.

* If all `w_i<=0` and `gamma>=0`, the phase block is nonpositive.
* If all `w_i<0` and `gamma<0`, it is nonpositive precisely when
  `(-gamma) sum_i 1/(-w_i)<=1`. If any `w_i=0` and `gamma<0`, it fails.
* If exactly one weight `w_k` is positive, all the other weights must be
  strictly negative, `gamma` must be positive, and the exact condition is
  \[
  \boxed{w_k\left(\frac1\gamma+
                       \sum_{i\ne k}\frac1{-w_i}\right)\le1.}
  \tag{4}
  \]
* Two positive weights, or one positive and another zero weight, always give
  positive phase curvature.

For (4), put `D=diag(-w_i:i!=k)` and
`S=sum_{i!=k}1/(-w_i)`. The Schur complement of
`D+gamma 11^T` in the negative Hessian is
`gamma/(1+gamma S)-w_k`, proving the assertion exactly.

In particular, gauge tangents satisfy `sum x_i=0`: write
`x_i=d_i-d_{i+1}`. Their Fisher term vanishes, and their curvature is exactly
`sum w_i x_i^2`. A useful necessary condition for Lyons is therefore

\[
w_k>0\implies
w_i<0\ (i\ne k),\qquad
w_k\sum_{i\ne k}\frac1{-w_i}\le1.
\tag{5}
\]

An exact violation of either (4) or (5) would be a genuine counterexample,
with an explicit phase direction obtained from the Schur complement. No such
violation was found here. The more general identity behind (5) is
`Hess H[i[D,K],i[D,K]]=sum_edges w_ij(d_i-d_j)^2`, obtained by twice
differentiating the diagonal-unitary invariance of measured entropy.

## 2. Symbolic obstruction: a frustrated closing edge can increase entropy

Take a balanced four-site path with positive edge entries `a,b,c`, zero closing
edge, and a strict contraction. Write `t_i=s_i s_{i+1}`, where
`s_i=2 1_{i in S}-1`. Its atoms are

\[
p(t_1,t_2,t_3)
 =(1/4-a^2t_1)(1/4-c^2t_3)-(b^2/4)t_2.
\]

Each triple occurs twice, and subset parity equals `t_1t_3`. Put

\[
P_{\sigma\tau}
 =\bigl[(1/4-a^2\sigma)(1/4-c^2\tau)\bigr]^2-b^4/16.
\]

All these quantities are positive because they are products of positive atoms.
The cycle derivative in (1) is exactly

\[
H_q\big|_{q=0}
 =4\log\frac{P_{++}P_{--}}{P_{+-}P_{-+}}<0,
\]

because

\[
\boxed{P_{++}P_{--}-P_{+-}P_{-+}=-a^2b^4c^2/16<0.}
\tag{6}
\]

Now close the cycle with a real edge `d`. At `d=0`,
`partial H/partial d=abc H_q<0`. Consequently, for all sufficiently small
negative `d`, the radial derivative `w_d=d partial H/partial d` is **positive**.
Strict feasibility persists by openness. This proves an open family of
frustrated examples, not just a numerical anomaly. It rules out an argument
requiring every individual edge radius to decrease entropy. It does not
contradict entropy concavity along the individual edge line, and supplies no
counterexample to Lyons.

### Exact rational instance and compensating phase curvature

The certificate uses

\[
K=\begin{pmatrix}
1/2&3/20&0&-1/1000\\
3/20&1/2&9/50&0\\
0&9/50&1/2&1/5\\
-1/1000&0&1/5&1/2
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Gershgorin proves both `K>=3/25 I` and `I-K>=3/25 I`.
The full cycle product is `Q=-27/5000000`. Exact rational logarithm enclosures
give

| Quantity | Approximate value; sign certified rationally |
|---|---:|
| `w_0` | `-0.0165332038656345` |
| `w_1` | `-0.0349888488323836` |
| `w_2` | `-0.0525742360594418` |
| `w_3` | `+0.0000000456145786901117` |
| `gamma` | `+0.0000000613941757757391` |
| Left side of (4) | `<3/4` (approximately `0.742983854806730`) |

In particular `w_3>1/25000000`. Despite this positive derivative, (4) proves
strict negativity in **all four phase directions jointly**. This is an exact
instance of the compensating rank-one term, not an extension claim about a
larger already-solved entropy family.

This example is not count-uniform: the two-particle probability range is
`1/32`. For its odds `L=K(I-K)^-1`, the normalized C071 matrix has negative real
triangle products. For instance, before the positive diagonal normalization,

\[
-L_{01}L_{12}L_{20}
 =-4628922749226562500000/15259430529402674419169<0.
\]

The phase directions are imaginary while the nonzero kernel edges are real,
so nonzero phase lines have no real affine intersection with a diagonal
kernel. The example is strictly interior with the explicit margin above, and
the phase space contains rank-four perturbations. These distinctions prevent
misidentifying the construction with the C006 diagonal-intersection argument,
the C071 positive-cycle argument, or C074 count-uniformity.

## 3. Bounded adversarial full-Hessian search

The numerical tool uses the entire Frobenius-orthonormal Hermitian basis
(`n^2` real coordinates), not selected edge directions. For each atom,

\[
M_S=K-I_{S^c},\quad T_S=M_S^{-1},\quad
g_{S,a}=p_S\operatorname{tr}(T_SE_a),
\]
\[
h_{S,ab}=p_S\{\operatorname{tr}(T_SE_a)
\operatorname{tr}(T_SE_b)-\operatorname{tr}(T_SE_aT_SE_b)\}.
\]

It diagonalizes
`-sum_S g_S g_S^T/p_S - sum_S h_S log p_S` and optimizes its largest
eigenvalue. Every saved endpoint includes the kernel, eigenvector direction,
both spectra, atom-mass residuals, minimum atom probability, Fisher/frozen
decomposition, count-level probability ranges, and a negative-real odds
triangle. No positive eigenvalue was obtained.

Searches were deterministic and bounded before execution:

* Seed `830915`; two unequal-weight cycle starts for each of `n=4,5,6` and two
  dense bipartite starts for `n=4,6`.
* Cycle moduli lie in `[exp(-2),exp(0.8)]`, phase in `[0.08 pi,0.92 pi]`, and a
  fixed heterogeneous diagonal is included before spectral normalization.
  Every iterate is normalized to prescribed positive lower and upper gaps.
* Bipartite kernels are `I/2 + [[0,B],[B*,0]]`; independent log moduli and
  phases parameterize `B`, rescaled by its largest singular value to preserve
  the contraction exactly in the mathematical parameterization.
* Each local optimization uses Powell, at most two iterations and 240
  optimizer evaluations, plus its initial evaluation. Ten relaxed starts
  used 2,410 evaluations. Several drifted toward nearly diagonal kernels;
  those tiny curvatures were **not** retained as near-counterexamples.
* A second, prebounded set of the same ten starts restricted both extreme
  gaps to `[10^-7,10^-2]`, enforcing spectral spread at least `0.98`.
  This used 2,410 evaluations and is the substantive near-boundary search.
* To address the excluded generic four-site boundary explicitly, two further
  starts use `B=diag(1/2-epsilon,s) R(theta)` with orthogonal rows,
  `theta in [0.2,1.3]`, `s in [0.1,0.44]`, and
  `epsilon in [10^-7,10^-2]`. Both endpoint eigenvectors have coordinate 1
  exactly zero. Thus these limits are outside the fully-supported endpoint
  hypothesis of `ROOT_FOUR_SITE_BOUNDARY`. The two bounded optimizations used
  126 and 124 evaluations. C080 subsequently extended that boundary scope to
  exactly one zero coordinate in one endpoint vector when the other endpoint
  vector is fully supported. Here **both** endpoint vectors have coordinate 1
  zero, so this construction also lies outside C080's stated hypothesis. These
  searches are not generic root-boundary rediscoveries.

The bounded optimizations used **5,070 objective evaluations**, each computing
the full Hessian; saved endpoint reports also reevaluate their matrices.
The guarded maxima
over the two starts in each family were:

### Rationalized strongest candidate

The best guarded candidate was the second six-site bipartite start. Its
numerical endpoint has extreme gaps `10^-5`, minimum atom probability about
`6.24096e-7`, and normalized direction curvature
`-2.397027927e-6`. Fisher and frozen contributions along that direction are
respectively `8.05609e-7` and `-1.59142e-6`.

Round the kernel's real and imaginary entries to denominator `10^8` and the
direction's entries to denominator `10^6`, preserving Hermitian symmetry.
`rational_candidate.py` proves, using exact positive leading principal minors,

\[
K\ge10^{-6}I,\quad I-K\ge10^{-6}I,\quad\|A\|_F<2.
\]

Therefore the entire affine line segment `|t|<=1/2000000` is feasible.
It computes all 64 exact rational atom jets and verifies their mass,
first-derivative, and second-derivative sums exactly. Rational logarithm
enclosures then prove

\[
\boxed{H''(K;A)<-1/500000.}
\]

The enclosed value is approximately `-2.397074625244392e-6`. This resolves the
actual strongest retained direction without calling roundoff a positive
candidate or claiming an exact proof about every untested direction.
