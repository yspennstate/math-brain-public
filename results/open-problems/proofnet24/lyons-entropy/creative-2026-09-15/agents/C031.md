# C031 — a full-rank principal-block endpoint theorem from conditional beam splitting

Date: 2026-09-15. This is a completed restricted **endpoint Jensen theorem**, with an exact nonnegative gap decomposition. It is not a theorem that the whole entropy curve is concave on every subinterval. No subagents, external messages, or pushes were used.

## 1. Main result and what is new

Let the measured coordinates be \(E=U\sqcup O\), where \(|U|=2\). Write
\[
D=B\oplus D_O,\qquad D_O=\operatorname{diag}(d_j:j\in O),
\qquad
K=\begin{pmatrix}A&V\\V^*&C\end{pmatrix},
\qquad 0\preceq D,K\preceq I.
\tag{1}
\]

The two-mode reservoir \(B\) is arbitrary, including genuinely coherent \(B_{12}\ne0\). The matrix \(A-B\), the cross matrix \(V\), and the entire exterior \(C\) are unrestricted subject to contraction. In particular both \(\operatorname{rank}(A-B)\) and \(\operatorname{rank}V\) can equal two, and \(C\) need not be diagonal or fixed along the interpolation.

**Theorem A (one coherent pair endpoint).** For every \(t\in[0,1]\),
\[
\boxed{
H(P_{(1-t)D+tK})
\ \ge\ (1-t)H(P_D)+tH(P_K).
}
\tag{2}
\]
If \(0\prec D\prec I\), \(K\ne D\), and \(0<t<1\), the inequality is strict.

This proves the original Lyons endpoint inequality for every pair of endpoints for which one endpoint has at most one nontrivial measured component, of size two. It allows an arbitrary other endpoint. The statement is invariant under coordinate permutations and coordinate diagonal unitaries.

This extends the endpoint scope of C022, whose full-curve result requires rank at most one in every perturbation block row. The full rank of the present pair row is unrestricted. It also changes the exterior kernel, whereas the prior result in *research-session/LYONS_PARTIAL_RESULTS.md*, Section 6, freezes the entire exterior and scales the cross matrix by one scalar. The only preexisting entropy theorem needed here is full two-site concavity from Section 1 of that note, reproduced in Section 8 below.

The mechanism is an actual two-mode Gaussian attenuation performed **after** a genuinely classical exterior replacement channel. Its measured conditional law is a two-site determinantal interpolation. The proof uses concavity in that conditional fiber; it does not require a probability-only realization of coherent attenuation, a positive two-bit retention lift, or a sign for C007's mixed Jeffreys terms.

## 2. The intermediate beam-splitter state

Initially take strict contractions. Put
\[
C_t=D_O+t(C-D_O),\qquad
M_t=
\begin{pmatrix}
A&\sqrt t\,V\\
\sqrt t\,V^*&C_t
\end{pmatrix}.
\tag{3}
\]
This is the result of attenuating only the exterior modes against the diagonal reservoir \(D_O\). With
\(S_t=\operatorname{diag}(I_U,\sqrt t\,I_O)\),
\[
M_t=S_tKS_t+(1-t)(0_U\oplus D_O),
\]
\[
I-M_t=S_t(I-K)S_t+(1-t)(0_U\oplus(I-D_O)).
\tag{4}
\]
Thus it is a positive contraction, strict for \(t>0\) under the current assumptions.

Let \((X,Y)\sim P_K\), where \(X\) is the measured pair and \(Y\) the exterior configuration. Independently for each \(j\in O\), retain \(Y_j\) with probability \(t\); otherwise replace it with an independent Bernoulli variable of mean \(d_j\). Denote the resulting exterior configuration by \(Y_t\). Then
\[
\boxed{(X,Y_t)\sim P_{M_t}.}
\tag{5}
\]

Here is a direct determinant proof. For an inclusion set \(W\subset U\), \(T\subset O\), condition on the set \(J\subset T\) of retained exterior sites. The output inclusion probability is
\[
\sum_{J\subset T}
t^{|J|}(1-t)^{|T\setminus J|}
\det K_{W\cup J}\prod_{j\in T\setminus J}d_j.
\tag{6}
\]
Expand the diagonal reservoir contributions in the principal determinant of (4). Factoring \(\sqrt t\) from both rows and columns of every retained exterior site gives exactly (6). Inclusion moments determine the finite law, proving (5).

In particular the exterior law \(q_t=P_{C_t}\) is obtained by the same classical product replacement applied to \(q_1=P_C\). The pair marginal of \(M_t\) is \(P_A\), unchanged by the operation.

Now attenuate the pair of \(M_t\) against the coherent reservoir \(B\), with retention parameter \(t\). Its covariance becomes
\[
\begin{pmatrix}
B+t(A-B)&\sqrt t(\sqrt tV)\\
\sqrt t(\sqrt tV)^*&C_t
\end{pmatrix}
=D+t(K-D)=:K_t.
\tag{7}
\]
This is the exact covariance of the two successive beam-splitter operations.

## 3. Exact conditional control of both pair modes

For each outside atom \(y\subset O\), put
\[
Q_y(t)=C_t-I_{O\setminus y},\qquad
q_t(y)=(-1)^{|O\setminus y|}\det Q_y(t)>0.
\]
The conditional kernel of the pair in \(M_t\), given \(Y_t=y\), is
\[
F_{t,y}=A-tVQ_y(t)^{-1}V^*.
\tag{8}
\]
The conditional kernel of the pair in the final \(K_t\), with the same outside configuration, is
\[
\begin{aligned}
G_{t,y}
&=B+t(A-B)-t^2VQ_y(t)^{-1}V^*\\
&=\boxed{(1-t)B+tF_{t,y}.}
\end{aligned}
\tag{9}
\]
Both \(F_{t,y}\) and \(G_{t,y}\) are genuine two-site positive contractions.

For completeness, the conditional DPP statement follows by inclusion-exclusion in the absent outside coordinates:
\[
P(W\subseteq X,\;Y_t=y)
=(-1)^{|O\setminus y|}
\det\begin{pmatrix}
A_{WW}&\sqrt t\,V_{W,O}\\
\sqrt t\,V^*_{O,W}&Q_y(t)
\end{pmatrix}
=q_t(y)\det(F_{t,y})_{WW}.
\tag{10}
\]
For \(K_t\), the same calculation gives (9). Positivity of the conditional kernels can also be seen by successive conditioning: conditioning on included sites takes a Schur complement of the positive kernel; conditioning on excluded sites takes the complementary Schur complement. These operations preserve the interval \([0,I]\). Approximation covers boundary kernels.

Equation (9) controls both retained modes together even when \(F_{t,y}-B\) has rank two. It is the step at which two-site entropy concavity replaces an unavailable classical coherent-block channel.

Define its exact two-site Jensen gap
\[
\mathcal J_{t,y}
=H_2((1-t)B+tF_{t,y})
-(1-t)H_2(B)-tH_2(F_{t,y})\ge0.
\tag{11}
\]
There is no claim that the probability vector itself is an affine mixture of its two endpoint laws.

## 4. Two elementary classical facts

The exterior replacement argument needs only an endpoint entropy inequality, for which there is a short proof valid on arbitrary finite alphabets.

**Lemma 1 (product replacement endpoint inequality).** Let \(Y=(Y_1,\ldots,Y_m)\) have any finite law, and let \(\mu=\bigotimes_j\mu_j\). Retain each \(Y_j\) with probability \(t\), otherwise replace it independently from \(\mu_j\), and call the output \(Y_t\). Then
\[
H(Y_t)\ge tH(Y)+(1-t)H(\mu).
\tag{12}
\]

**Proof.** Let \(S\) be the random retained subset. Conditional on \(S\), the output law is the product of the original marginal \(P_{Y_S}\) with fresh independent coordinates on \(S^c\). Therefore
\[
H(Y_t)\ge H(Y_t\mid S)
=\mathbb E_S H(Y_S)+(1-t)\sum_jH(\mu_j).
\]
Order the coordinates. The chain rule and the fact that conditioning reduces entropy give
\[
H(Y_S)
=\sum_{j\in S}H(Y_j\mid Y_{S\cap\{1,\ldots,j-1\}})
\ge\sum_{j\in S}H(Y_j\mid Y_1,\ldots,Y_{j-1}).
\]
Taking expectation gives \(\mathbb E H(Y_S)\ge tH(Y)\), proving (12). This proof also works for entire finite-alphabet blocks. \(\square\)

**Lemma 2 (the retained pair loses measured information).** In the coupling (5),
\[
H(X\mid Y_t)\ge H(X\mid Y),
\qquad
I(X;Y)-I(X;Y_t)\ge0.
\tag{13}
\]
This is ordinary data processing for \(X\longrightarrow Y\longrightarrow Y_t\). It applies before the coherent pair operation.

If \(m=|O|\), a useful explicit bound is
\[
\boxed{
I(X;Y)-I(X;Y_t)\ge(1-t)^m I(X;Y).
}
\tag{14}
\]
Indeed the independent event that all exterior sites are replaced has probability \(\alpha=(1-t)^m\). Reveal that event to the observer. It is independent of \(X\); in that event the output carries no information about \(X\), and on its complement data processing bounds the information by \(I(X;Y)\). Hence
\(I(X;Y_t)\le(1-\alpha)I(X;Y)\).

## 5. The exact nonnegative entropy-gap decomposition

Define
\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal E_t
&=H(P_{C_t})-(1-t)H(P_{D_O})-tH(P_C),\\
\mathcal L_t
&=I_{P_K}(X;Y)-I_{P_{M_t}}(X;Y_t),\\
\mathcal G_t
&=\sum_yq_t(y)\mathcal J_{t,y}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{15}
\]
Lemmas 1 and 2 give \(\mathcal E_t,\mathcal L_t\ge0\), and (11) gives \(\mathcal G_t\ge0\).

**Theorem B (exact gap identity).**
\[
\boxed{
H(P_{K_t})-(1-t)H(P_D)-tH(P_K)
=\mathcal E_t+t\mathcal L_t+\mathcal G_t.
}
\tag{16}
\]

**Proof.** Entropy conditioning and (9) give
\[
\begin{aligned}
H(P_{K_t})
&=H(P_{C_t})+\sum_yq_t(y)H_2(G_{t,y})\\
&=H(P_{C_t})+(1-t)H_2(B)
+tH_{P_{M_t}}(X\mid Y_t)+\mathcal G_t.
\end{aligned}
\]
Subtract
\[
(1-t)\{H_2(B)+H(P_{D_O})\}
+t\{H(P_C)+H_{P_K}(X\mid Y)\}.
\]
The exterior terms are \(\mathcal E_t\), and the conditional-entropy difference is \(t\mathcal L_t\), since the marginal of \(X\) is \(P_A\) in both \(K\) and \(M_t\). This proves (16), and hence Theorem A. \(\square\)

The two-mode correction in (16) is the nonnegative **conditional entropy Jensen gap**, rather than the mutual-information correction of a hidden retention lift. The only data-processing step is the classical exterior operation (5). Thus C007's negative coherent cross dissipation and C025's positive curvature of a hidden-variable information correction do not invalidate any sign used here.

From (14),
\[
\boxed{
H(P_{K_t})-(1-t)H(P_D)-tH(P_K)
\ge\mathcal E_t+t(1-t)^mI_{P_K}(X;Y).
}
\tag{17}
\]
The prior sharp two-site bound also gives
\[
\mathcal G_t\ge
4t(1-t)\sum_y q_t(y)\,[\det(F_{t,y}-B)]_+.
\tag{18}
\]
Here \([a]_+=\max(a,0)\): when the determinant is positive, the two-site direction is definite and the sharp determinant improvement applies; otherwise (11) supplies zero as the lower bound.

### Boundary kernels

Apply the strict argument to
\[
K^\varepsilon=(1-2\varepsilon)K+\varepsilon I,\qquad
D^\varepsilon=(1-2\varepsilon)D+\varepsilon I.
\]
These preserve the endpoint form and converge to \(K,D\). Every atom and every finite-law entropy or mutual information is continuous, proving (2) and (17) for closed contractions. Atoms of probability zero are omitted in conditional expressions. Identity (16) has the corresponding continuous weighted interpretation. No inversion of a singular outside matrix is required.

### Strictness

Assume \(D\) is strict and \(0<t<1\).

If \(C\ne D_O\), their DPP laws differ: equality with the product law would force equal singleton means and zero pair covariances, hence every off-diagonal entry of \(C\) would vanish. In Lemma 1 the retained-set mixture includes the distinct laws \(P_C\) and \(P_{D_O}\), each with positive weight. Strict concavity of ordinary finite Shannon entropy makes \(\mathcal E_t>0\).

If \(C=D_O\) but \(V\ne0\), some pair covariance across the cut is \(-|V_{ij}|^2<0\), so \(I_{P_K}(X;Y)>0\). Equation (17) makes the gap strict.

The remaining case is \(C=D_O,V=0\). If \(K\ne D\), then \(A\ne B\), and the gap is precisely the strict two-site Jensen gap between \(B\) and \(A\). Strict two-site concavity when one endpoint is strict is proved in Section 8. This exhausts the possibilities, proving the strictness assertion.

## 6. Extension: one unrestricted pair among larger coherent blocks

There is a direct extension beyond a diagonal exterior endpoint. Partition
\[
E=U\sqcup O_1\sqcup\cdots\sqcup O_m,\quad |U|=2,\qquad
D=B\oplus D_1\oplus\cdots\oplus D_m.
\]
Let \(K=D+\Delta\) be a positive contraction and assume only
\[
\boxed{\operatorname{rank}\Delta_{O_j,E}\le1\quad (j=1,\ldots,m).}
\tag{19}
\]
There is no restriction on the pair block row \(\Delta_{U,E}\).

**Theorem C.** The endpoint inequality (2) still holds. The exact identity (16) applies with each entire \(O_j\) as one replacement alphabet, and the bound (17) uses this number \(m\) of exterior blocks.

**Proof of the needed replacement realization.** Attenuate one exterior block \(O_j\) toward \(D_j\), leaving its complement unchanged. Conditional on every complement configuration, its kernel has the form
\[
D_j+E,\qquad \operatorname{rank}E\le1,
\]
because the columns of both its internal perturbation and its cross matrix lie in the common line from (19); the Schur-complement correction has the same range. At attenuation parameter \(s\) this becomes \(D_j+sE\). All inclusion determinants, and hence all atoms, are affine in a rank-one displacement. Thus the measured block update is exactly \(sI+(1-s)R_j\), where \(R_j\) replaces the whole block independently with \(P_{D_j}\).

The row condition remains valid after attenuating other blocks, and these block attenuations commute at covariance level. Applying all exterior updates with \(s=t\) gives exactly (3), with \(D_O=\bigoplus_jD_j\), and on occupation laws gives the product of these commuting classical block replacements. This is the sufficiency part of C022's rank-one criterion; its necessity result is not needed. Sections 3–5 apply unchanged because the final unattenuated block still has exactly two measured sites. Approximation preserves (19). \(\square\)

This extends C022 by allowing one unrestricted rank-two pair block. It does not establish arbitrary larger unrestricted coherent blocks or arbitrary products of unrestricted coherent pair reservoirs.

More generally, the same proof is a conditional lifting principle for a block \(U\) of any size: if a proved convex kernel region contains \(B\) and every conditional \(F_{t,y}\), and measured entropy is concave on that region, then its endpoint theorem follows from exactly (16). The unconditional result here uses size two, for which the whole contraction set is such a region. This observation does not assume any larger-dimensional entropy concavity.

## 7. Exact full-rank example with a changing coherent exterior

Take
\[
D=\frac1{60}
\begin{pmatrix}
24&6&0&0\\6&30&0&0\\0&0&20&0\\0&0&0&36
\end{pmatrix},
\quad
K=\frac1{60}
\begin{pmatrix}
30&-3+2i&6&2+i\\
-3-2i&27&1+3i&-4\\
6&1-3i&24&3-i\\
2-i&-4&3+i&33
\end{pmatrix}.
\tag{20}
\]
Rational row-sum bounds, using \(|a+ib|\le|a|+|b|\), give
\[
\operatorname{spec}(D)\subset[3/10,3/5],\qquad
\operatorname{spec}(K)\subset[1/6,11/15].
\]
Thus both endpoints are strict. The relevant exact determinants are
\[
\det(A-B)=-\frac{103}{3600},\qquad
\det V=\frac{-23-7i}{3600},\qquad
\det(C-D_O)=-\frac{11}{1800}.
\tag{21}
\]
Both retained modes are active, the cross matrix is full rank, the first principal block is changing, and the coherent exterior is changing. This lies outside both C022's pair-row rank-one hypothesis and the prior frozen-exterior theorem. All off-diagonal entries of \(K\) are nonzero.

It also verifies the exact rational feasibility and rank certificates, and records three deterministic high-precision checks of the exact entropy decomposition. ## 8. Included proof of the existing two-site entropy input

This section records the known input for completeness; it is not counted as new.

For a strict two-site kernel
\[
F=\begin{pmatrix}a&c\\\bar c&b\end{pmatrix},
\quad E=\begin{pmatrix}u&v\\\bar v&s\end{pmatrix},
\quad \delta=\det E,
\]
write the four probabilities as
\[
x=(1-a)(1-b)-|c|^2,\quad y=a(1-b)+|c|^2,\quad
z=(1-a)b+|c|^2,\quad w=ab-|c|^2.
\]
They obey \(yz-xw=|c|^2\), and along \(F+\tau E\),
\[
H_2''=-\mathcal I+2\delta L,\qquad
\mathcal I=\sum_\alpha\frac{(p_\alpha')^2}{p_\alpha},
\qquad L=\log\frac{yz}{xw}\ge0.
\tag{22}
\]
This proves the sign when \(\delta\le0\).

When \(\delta>0\), negate \(E\) if needed so that \(E>0\), and set
\[
P=\frac{\operatorname{adj}F}{\sqrt w},\qquad
Q=\frac{\operatorname{adj}(I-F)}{\sqrt x},\qquad
T=\frac{y+z}{2\sqrt{xw}}\ge1.
\]
Both \(P,Q\) have determinant one. The two extreme atom terms and the arithmetic-geometric mean inequality give
\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal I
&\ge(\operatorname{tr}PE)^2+(\operatorname{tr}QE)^2\\
&\ge\tfrac12[\operatorname{tr}(P+Q)E]^2
\ge2\det(P+Q)\det E.
\end{aligned}
\]
The two-dimensional determinant identity gives
\(\det(P+Q)=2+2T\), while \(L\le2\log T\). Hence
\[
H_2''\le-4\delta(1+T-\log T)\le-8\delta<0.
\tag{23}
\]
Integrating proves concavity and the chord improvement \(4\delta t(1-t)\) for positive \(\delta\). Approximation extends these conclusions to closed contractions.

For strictness on a nonconstant line, \(\delta>0\) is covered by (23). If \(\delta=0\), nonzero rank-one Hermitian \(E\) has a nonzero diagonal entry, so a singleton marginal changes and \(\mathcal I>0\). If \(\delta<0\), (22) is strict wherever \(c\ne0\), and it is also strict when \(c=0\) and some diagonal marginal changes. The only possible exceptional points therefore have a pure off-diagonal direction and \(c=0\); a nonconstant affine \(c\) can vanish at at most one real parameter. Thus the entropy is strictly concave on every nonconstant segment whose interior is strict. In particular this applies whenever one endpoint is strict.

## 9. Scope of the completed proof

Theorem A establishes the Lyons endpoint inequality with one arbitrary coherent two-mode reservoir and arbitrarily many exterior singleton reservoirs, arbitrary cross rank, and an arbitrary changing exterior kernel. Theorem C permits additional coherent exterior blocks under their explicit rank-one row condition.

An interior point \(K_s\) of a segment in Theorem A generally has cross entries and a coherent exterior. It need not itself have the permitted endpoint form. Therefore (2), even though valid for every \(t\), cannot be applied automatically to two arbitrary interior points. No global Hessian sign, no universal bound for C007's mixed curvature terms, and no proof of full Lyons concavity are asserted.

The new proof is a finite-chord alternative to the unresolved curvature decomposition. Its three nonnegative terms have precise measured meanings and give an exact formula rather than discarding an unknown coherent correction.
