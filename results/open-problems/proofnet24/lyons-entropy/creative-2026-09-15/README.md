# Creative proof campaign for Lyons entropy concavity

Started 15 September 2026. **The full conjecture remains open in this campaign.** The user requested 100 new GPT-6 Astra workers at max reasoning, exclusively for creative proof methods. The available six worker slots are used in successive waves. This campaign is separate from the preceding 107-launch/100-completion campaign.

The target is [Conjecture 2.6 in Lyons's 2014 ICM paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1406.2707): Shannon entropy of a finite determinantal law should be concave in its Hermitian positive-contraction marginal kernel. This is an established specialist conjecture; the current algebraic reductions make it a productive target, but do not establish that a full solution is near.

Research proofs, numerical diagnostics, formal verification, and historical priority are distinct. Every report must state its exact hypotheses and remaining open obligations. The publication of a report does not itself establish a universal conjecture or worldwide novelty.
