## 3. Depth-three arm operator and the new tail-closure lemma

Fix three independent terminals `0,1,2` and retain one spider center `c`. The frontier consists of four labelled points, hence has `B_4=15` connectivity partitions.

A length-`d` arm means a path
\[
c-v_1-\cdots-v_d
\]
whose every new arm vertex `v_i` is also adjacent to all three terminals. Let `L_1,L_2,L_3` be the exact 15-by-15 transfer matrices for adding one length-one, length-two, or length-three arm. Exact partition DP gives diagonal scalars
\[
L_1=5I+J,\qquad L_2=28I+K,\qquad L_3=158I+M.
\]

The previously known two-tail algebra has
\[
J^4=K^4=0,\qquad JK=KJ,
\]
and dimension seven with basis
\[
I,\ K,\ K^2,\ K^3,\ J,\ JK,\ J^2.
\]

### Lemma 3.1 — length-three arm closure

The new nilpotent part `M` satisfies
\[
M^4=0,\qquad
(\operatorname{rank}M,\operatorname{rank}M^2,\operatorname{rank}M^3,\operatorname{rank}M^4)
=(8,2,1,0),
\]
commutes with `J` and `K`, and lies exactly in the old seven-dimensional algebra:
\[
\boxed{
M=
\frac{82}{7}K+\frac{73}{1029}K^2-\frac{1376}{893025}K^3
-\frac{254}{7}J+\frac{282}{343}JK-\frac{4185}{343}J^2.
}
\]

**Proof.** The 15 frontier partitions are enumerated exactly. For each state, every subset of the twelve edges of a new length-three arm is traversed; a subset is retained iff adding it creates no cycle in the represented connectivity state. This gives `L_3`. Direct exact integer matrix multiplication yields `M^4=0` and the rank profile. Exact commutators with `J,K` vanish. Finally the displayed equality is verified entrywise over `Q`; the seven basis matrices have rank seven when vectorized, so the representation is unique. The executed checker reconstructs all matrices from the partition recurrence and verifies every identity. ∎

Consequently
\[
158^{-r}L_3^r
=
I+\frac r{158}M+\frac{\binom r2}{158^2}M^2+\frac{\binom r3}{158^3}M^3.
\tag{3.1}
\]
Thus arbitrary arm multiplicity is controlled by a cubic polynomial in `r`. The result is a genuine decomposition/closure statement, not interpolation from finitely many spider sizes.

## 6. Cross-component closure and strict sign

For `x=(x_1,x_2,x_3)`, `t=sum x_i`, define
\[

0&0&1&0&t-x_2\\
0&0&0&1&t-x_3\\
0&0&0&0&1
\end{pmatrix},
\]
and
\[
\kappa(x,y)=\Bigl(\sum_i x_i\Bigr)\Bigl(\sum_i y_i\Bigr)-x\cdot y.
\]
The inherited exact algebra identity is
\[
C(x,z)C(y,w)=C(x+y,z+w+\kappa(x,y)).
\tag{6.1}

## 9. Honest residual obstruction and next attack
