## 3. New all-length arm-tail closure

Fix three independent terminals `0,1,2` and retain a spider center `c`. The 15 center-frontier states are the set partitions of these four labelled points.

For an arm of length `d`,
\[
c-v_1-v_2-\cdots-v_d,
\]
every arm vertex `v_i` is also adjacent to all three terminals. Let `L_d` be the exact 15-by-15 transfer obtained by inserting the whole arm and then forgetting all arm vertices.

After the first arm vertex is inserted, retain the terminals, center, and current arm endpoint. There are `B_5=52` partitions. Let `E` be the one-step unmarked endpoint-extension matrix. There are fixed start and close matrices `A,F` such that
\[
\boxed{L_d=A E^{d-1}F.}
\tag{3.1}
\]
Exact partition DP gives, for
\[
p(\lambda)=\lambda^3-7\lambda^2+8\lambda-2
=(\lambda-1)(\lambda^2-6\lambda+2),
\]
\[
\boxed{p(E)^3=0},
\qquad
\operatorname{rank}p(E)=20,
\quad \operatorname{rank}p(E)^2=5.
\tag{3.2}
\]
Therefore every entry of `L_d` satisfies the order-nine recurrence associated with
\[
p(\lambda)^3=
\lambda^9-21\lambda^8+171\lambda^7-685\lambda^6+1452\lambda^5
-1734\lambda^4+1196\lambda^3-468\lambda^2+96\lambda-8.
\tag{3.3}
\]

Let `s_d=(L_d)_{0,0}`. Exact values through twelve are
\[
5,28,158,892,5036,28432,160520,906256,5116496,
28886464,163085792,920741824.
\]
The residual sequence `s_{d+2}-6s_{d+1}+2s_d` also obeys (3.3), and nine consecutive exact residuals vanish. Hence
\[
\boxed{s_{d+2}=6s_{d+1}-2s_d\quad(d\ge1).}
\tag{3.4}
\]
Equivalently,
\[
s_d=\left(\frac12+\frac1{\sqrt7}\right)(3+\sqrt7)^d
+\left(\frac12-\frac1{\sqrt7}\right)(3-\sqrt7)^d.
\]

Now set
\[
J=L_1-5I,\qquad K=L_2-28I,
\]
and
\[
V=\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb Q}\{K,K^2,K^3,J,JK,J^2\}.
\]
The six displayed matrices are linearly independent. Exact multiplication verifies that they commute, every product of two basis elements lies again in `V`, and
\[
\boxed{V^4=0.}
\tag{3.5}
\]
For `M_d=L_d-s_dI`, exact rational linear algebra verifies
\[
M_1,M_2,\ldots,M_9\in V.
\]
Because both `L_d` and `s_d I` obey (3.3), so does `M_d`; passing to the quotient of the 225-dimensional matrix space by `V`, the first nine terms vanish, hence every term vanishes. Therefore:

### Lemma 3.1 — all-length arm-tail closure

For every integer `d>=1`,
\[
\boxed{L_d=s_d I+M_d,\qquad M_d\in V,}
\]
where `s_d` obeys (3.4), all `M_d` commute, and
\[
\boxed{M_d^4=0.}
\]
More generally any product of four non-scalar arm-tail increments is zero. Thus arbitrary multiplicities of arbitrary arm lengths are controlled by one fixed seven-dimensional commutative algebra `QI \oplus V`; no arm-length cutoff is present in this decomposition theorem.

This is the main new closure result of the run. It does **not by itself** prove p-NC for arbitrary mixtures of arm lengths; the marked-pair sign cone is the remaining issue.

## 5. Same-component marked-pair sign theorem

Expose the depth-four spider containing the marked pair `e,f`. Every other unmarked component compresses to `U=I+uN+vN^2` with (4.3). For each of `I,I_e,I_f,I_ef`, the exposed response is affine in `(u,v)`, so the normalized Rayleigh numerator is a quadratic `Q(u,v;m)`. Substitution of (4.3) gives a quartic
\[
R(u,h;m)=\sum_{j=0}^4c_j(h;m)u^j,
\]
where each `c_j` has degree at most two in `h`.

Local edge types are a center-terminal spoke `c`, terminal spokes `s_1,s_2,s_3,s_4` along an arm, and internal arm edges `i_1,i_2,i_3,i_4`. Terminal `S_3`, arm permutations and exchange of the marked pair reduce all possibilities to exactly
\[
\boxed{97\text{ symbolic same-component schemes}.}
\]

For **82/97** schemes, every `c_j` has nonnegative degree-two Bernstein coefficients on `[43/360,1/8]`, and after shifting `m=m_0+X` to the physical minimum arm count, every cleared numerator is coefficientwise nonnegative in `X`.

Exactly **15/97** schemes require the strip-discriminant lemma. They are the different-terminal spoke pairs represented by

- `c` with `s_2,s_3,s_4` on one arm;
- `s_1` with `s_3,s_4` on the same arm;
- `s_1` with `s_2,s_3,s_4` on a distinct arm;
- `s_2` with `s_4` on the same arm;
- `s_2` with `s_2,s_3,s_4` on a distinct arm;
- `s_3` with `s_3,s_4` on a distinct arm;
- `s_4` with `s_4` on a distinct arm.

For each exception,
\[
R=u^2(c_4u^2+c_3u+c_2)+c_1u+c_0,
\]
with `c_4>0`, `c_0>=0`, `c_1>=0` throughout the strip and
\[
\mathcal K=4c_4c_2-c_3^2>0.
\]
The `h` dependence cancels identically from `K`; after the physical `m` shift, every exact numerator has strictly positive coefficients. The executed output archives all fifteen factorizations. Consequently the quadratic core has negative discriminant and is positive for every real `u`.

The only same-component boundary zero is two distinct center spokes when `m=0`; there the right component is one isolated vertex.

## 8. What did and did not move

**Parent theorem scope:** `OPEN -> OPEN`; complete all-graph order `9 -> 9`.

**Demonstrated mechanism gain:** one verified arm length inside the seven-dimensional tail algebra `->` **all arm lengths** inside the same algebra, plus a complete depth-four arbitrary-component p-NC theorem.

The all-length decomposition theorem is strictly stronger than the depth-four application. What remains missing is an arm-length-uniform **marked-pair sign cone**. The fact that every unmarked arm length lies in the same nilpotent algebra does not by itself imply that every one-/two-marked transfer lies in one sign-stable cone.

The next materially distinct attack is therefore to derive a recurrence in arm length for the one- and two-marked response increments, with the goal of proving the Bernstein/discriminant sign conditions uniformly in the marked positions and arm lengths. A successful version would upgrade separate depth-three/depth-four theorems to arbitrary pure-depth spiders, and potentially to spiders with mixed arm lengths. A failure should produce an exact first arm length/marked-position obstruction rather than another blind finite cutoff.
