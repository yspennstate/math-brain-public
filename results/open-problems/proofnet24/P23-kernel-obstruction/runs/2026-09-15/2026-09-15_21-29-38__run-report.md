## 4. Proof of the nullspace coupling

Let \(V_S=\ker S^T\), \(V_G=\ker G^T\). Since \(G^T=S^TD\),

\[
z\in V_G\iff Dz\in V_S,
\]

so \(V_G=D^{-1}V_S\).

For any unit \(w\in V_G\), \(Dw\in V_S\), hence

\[
\operatorname{dist}(w,V_S)\le\|(I-D)w\|\le\delta.
\]

For any unit \(v\in V_S\), \(D^{-1}v\in V_G\), hence

\[
\operatorname{dist}(v,V_G)\le\|(I-D^{-1})v\|\le\delta/(1-\delta).
\]

Equal-dimensional subspaces have projector gap equal to their largest principal-angle sine, proving

\[
\|K_S-K_G\|_{op}\le\delta/(1-\delta).
\]

For \(X_i=R_i^2\sim\chi_m^2\), the mgf Chernoff inequalities

\[
P\{X_i-m\ge2\sqrt{mx}+2x\}\le e^{-x},\qquad
P\{m-X_i\ge2\sqrt{mx}\}\le e^{-x}
\]

with \(x=4\log m\), followed by a union bound over \(n\le2m\), give \(\delta=O_P(\sqrt{\log m/m})\).

Because \(G\) is Gaussian, \(K_G\) is Haar rank \(r\) in \(\mathbb R^n\), so \(E\,1^TK_G1=r\). On \(\delta<1/2\),

\[
\|K_S1\|\le\|K_G1\|+2\delta\sqrt n,
\]

and therefore

\[
1^TK_S1=O_P(r+\log m).
\]

The rank-one Hotelling conclusion follows immediately as stated above.

A key point is that this proof incurs **no hard-edge condition number**. The row scaling acts directly on the ambient left-null subspace.
