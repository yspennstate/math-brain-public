# 7. General multiwindow convexity theorem

The fixed-offset leverage lower bound has a useful deterministic structure.

Let

\[
0<y_0<y_1<\cdots<y_J,
\]

and let \(A_j\) denote cumulative normalized leverage through dimension \(ny_j\).

The disjoint-window risk functional is

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal E_{\mathbf y}(\mathbf A)
=
\frac{A_0^2}{y_0}
+
\sum_{j=1}^J
\frac{(A_j-A_{j-1})^2}{y_j-y_{j-1}}.
}
\tag{7.1}
\]

Define

\[
\boxed{f(y)=\frac{y}{1+y}.}
\tag{7.2}
\]

## `P23-MULTIWINDOW-OBSTACLE-10` — PROVED

Over the relaxed feasible region

\[
A_j\ge f(y_j),
\qquad
0\le j\le J,
\]

one has

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal E_{\mathbf y}(\mathbf A)
\ge
\mathcal E_{\mathbf y}
\big(f(y_0),\ldots,f(y_J)\big).
}
\tag{7.3}
\]

Thus all cumulative leverage lower bounds can be saturated **simultaneously** when minimizing the exact quadratic risk energy.

### Proof

The function \(f\) is increasing and strictly concave, with \(f(0)=0\).

Define its secant slopes

\[
s_0=\frac{f(y_0)-f(0)}{y_0},
\]

and, for \(j\ge1\),

\[
s_j
=
\frac{f(y_j)-f(y_{j-1})}{y_j-y_{j-1}}.
\]

Concavity gives

\[
s_0>s_1>\cdots>s_J>0.
\]

The gradient of \(\mathcal E\) at the obstacle point \(A_j=f(y_j)\) is

\[
\partial_{A_0}\mathcal E=2(s_0-s_1)>0,
\]

\[
\partial_{A_j}\mathcal E=2(s_j-s_{j+1})>0,
\qquad
1\le j<J,
\]

and

\[
\partial_{A_J}\mathcal E=2s_J>0.
\]

The functional \(\mathcal E\) is convex quadratic.

Hence, for every coordinatewise increment \(\delta_j\ge0\),

\[
\mathcal E(f+\delta)
\ge
\mathcal E(f)
+
\nabla\mathcal E(f)\cdot\delta
\ge
\mathcal E(f).
\]

This proves (7.3).

QED.
