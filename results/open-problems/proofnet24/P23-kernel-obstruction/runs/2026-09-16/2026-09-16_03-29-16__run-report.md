## 5.1 Physical tangent geometry

\[

\qquad

}
\tag{5.1}
\]

The spherical gradient of \(f_a\) is

\[
\nabla f_a(u)
=2c_d\left(A_au-(u^TA_au)u\right).
\]

Hence

\[
\sum_{a=1}^2|\nabla f_a|^2
=4c_d^2G_0(u)-4F(u).
\tag{5.2}

For a uniform unit tangent in the \((d-1)\)-dimensional tangent space,

\[

=
\frac{4c_d^2G_0(u)-4F(u)}{d-1}.
\tag{5.3}
