# 5. Exact Schur complement for the matrix tail defect

## `P23-T3-SCHUR-TAIL-DEFECT-48 = PROVED`

Let `M>=0` have rank `D`, let `B>=0`, and assume `M+B>0`. Decompose the ambient space as

```text
R=Ran(M),
N=Ker(M).
```

In this decomposition,

```text
M = [[M_R,0],[0,0]],
B = [[B_RR,B_RN],[B_NR,B_NN]].
```

Because `M+B` is positive definite and `M` vanishes on `N`, `B_NN` is positive definite. Since `B>=0`, its Schur complement

```text
B_eff=B_RR-B_RN B_NN^(-1)B_NR
```

is PSD and satisfies `0<=B_eff<=B_RR`.

The `RR` block of `(M+B)^(-1)` is

```text
(M_R+B_eff)^(-1).
```

Hence

```text
T(M,B)
 = Tr[(M+B)^(-1)M]
 = Tr[(M_R+B_eff)^(-1)M_R].
```

With

```text
C=M_R^(-1/2)B_eff M_R^(-1/2),
```

cyclicity gives

```text
T(M,B)=Tr[(I+C)^(-1)],
D-T(M,B)=Tr[C(I+C)^(-1)].
```

Since `0<=C(I+C)^(-1)<=C`,

```text
D-T(M,B)
 <= Tr C
 = Tr(M_R^(-1)B_eff)
 <= Tr(M_R^(-1)B_RR)
 = Tr(M^+B).
```

This proves

```text
boxed:
D-Tr(M^+B) <= T(M,B) <= D.
```




# 7. Why first two trace moments cannot finish the de-regularization

## `P23-T3-TWOMOMENT-MATRIX-NOGO-48 = PROVED`

The actual flat harmonic floor has

```text
Tr A = nD
```

pointwise and

```text
E Tr A^2 = nD(D+n-1).
```

Thus its normalized nonzero empirical eigenvalues have mean exactly `1` and expected second moment asymptotically `1+y` when `D/n->y`.

One might hope these two moments plus the vanishing Mercer tail trace already force `T(M,B)` above the Cauchy level. They do not.

Fix `y in (0,1]` and `delta in (0,1)`. Define

```text
a=delta,
c=1+y/(1-delta),
p=y/[y+(1-delta)^2].
```

A direct calculation gives

```text
pa+(1-p)c=1,
pa^2+(1-p)c^2=1+y.
```

As `delta->0`,

```text
p->y/(1+y),
c->1+y.
```

Choose asymptotically a `p` fraction of the `D` nonzero eigenvalues of `M/(lambda n)` equal to `a`, the rest equal to `c`, with a one-eigenvalue rounding correction if needed. Add a PSD tail `B/(lambda n)` that equals `sqrt(delta)` on the small-eigenvalue group, zero on the large group, and an arbitrarily smaller positive number on the `n-D` null coordinates to make `M+B` invertible.

Then

```text
Tr B/Tr M
 = p sqrt(delta)+o(1)
 ->0,
```

but

```text
T(M,B)/D
 = p * delta/(delta+sqrt(delta)) + (1-p)+o(1)
 ->1-p
 ->1/(1+y).
```

The scalar Cauchy fraction is therefore minimax-sharp under exactly this moment information, even when the tail trace vanishes.

So a successful matrix-level improvement must prove something that rules out this microscopic spectral cloud for the **actual harmonic sample covariance**. First and second trace moments alone cannot do it.
