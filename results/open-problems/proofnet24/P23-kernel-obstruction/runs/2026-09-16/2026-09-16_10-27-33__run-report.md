\[
t_s=\operatorname{Tr}(K^{-1}K_s),
\qquad
q_s=\operatorname{Tr}(K^{-1}K_sK^{-1}).
\]

For zero signal and independent training noise of variance `sigma^2`, integrating the squared test prediction over test `x` gives

\[
\boxed{
\frac{V(X)}{\sigma^2}
=\sum_s\lambda_s q_s.
}
\tag{2.1}
\]

Indeed,

\[
\int k_X(x)k_X(x)^T\,d\mu(x)
=\sum_s\lambda_s^2\Phi_s\Phi_s^T
=\sum_s\lambda_s K_s.
\]

Next,

\[
t_s
=\lambda_s\langle K^{-1}\Phi_s,\Phi_s\rangle_F.
\]

Cauchy--Schwarz gives

\[
t_s^2
\le
\lambda_s^2\|K^{-1}\Phi_s\|_F^2\|\Phi_s\|_F^2.

The addition theorem gives, pointwise on the sphere,

\[
\sum_{j=1}^{N_s}\phi_{s,j}(x)^2=N_s,

hence

\[
\|\Phi_s\|_F^2=nN_s.
\]

Since

\[
q_s=\lambda_s\|K^{-1}\Phi_s\|_F^2,

we recover exactly

\[
\boxed{t_s^2\le nN_s\lambda_s q_s.}
\tag{2.2}

# 3. Precise Gaussian Mercer eigenvalues and a two-sided ratio estimate

This section proves `P23-T3-MERCER-RATIO-TWOSIDED-45`.

## 3.1 Exact eigenvalue

On the unit sphere,

\[
\|x-y\|^2=2-2x\cdot y,
\]

so with `a=h^-2`,

\[
K_h(x,y)=e^{-a}e^{a x\cdot y}.
\]

Let

\[
\nu=d/2-1.
\]

The normalized Funk--Hecke formula gives the degree-`s` eigenvalue

\[
\boxed{
\lambda_{d,s}
=e^{-a}\Gamma(d/2)(2/a)^\nu I_{\nu+s}(a).
}
\tag{3.1}
\]

Sanity checks:

- for `d=2`, `nu=0`, this is the usual circle Fourier coefficient `e^-a I_s(a)`;
- for `d=3,s=0`, `I_{1/2}(a)=sqrt(2/(pi a)) sinh(a)`, so (3.1) becomes `e^-a sinh(a)/a`, exactly the direct average `(1/2)int_{-1}^1 e^{-a}e^{at}dt`.

## 3.2 Positive-series factorization

The final NIST DLMF 10.25.2 gives

\[
I_\mu(a)
=(a/2)^\mu
\sum_{k=0}^\infty
\frac{(a^2/4)^k}{k!\Gamma(\mu+k+1)}.
\tag{3.2}
\]

Set

\[
c=a^2/4,
\qquad
S_\mu
=\sum_{k=0}^\infty
\frac{c^k}{k!(\mu+1)_k}.
\]

Then

\[
I_\mu(a)
=\frac{(a/2)^\mu}{\Gamma(\mu+1)}S_\mu
\]

and therefore

\[
\frac{I_{\mu+1}(a)}{I_\mu(a)}
=
\frac{a}{2(\mu+1)}
\frac{S_{\mu+1}}{S_\mu}.
\tag{3.3}
\]

Termwise positivity gives `S_{mu+1}<=S_mu`, reproducing the retained exact upper bound.

## 3.3 Quantitative lower bound

For `k>=1`,

\[
\frac1{(\mu+1)_k}-\frac1{(\mu+2)_k}
=
\frac{k}{\mu+k+1}\frac1{(\mu+1)_k}.
\]

Hence

\[
0\le S_\mu-S_{\mu+1}
\le
\frac1{\mu+1}
\sum_{k\ge1}
\frac{k}{k!}
\left(\frac{c}{\mu+1}\right)^k.
\]

The last sum equals

\[
\frac{c}{\mu+1}
\exp\!\left(\frac{c}{\mu+1}\right),
\]

so

\[
0\le S_\mu-S_{\mu+1}
\le
\frac{c}{(\mu+1)^2}
\exp\!\left(\frac{c}{\mu+1}\right).
\tag{3.4}
\]

Since `S_mu>=1`,

\[
\frac{S_{\mu+1}}{S_\mu}
\ge
1-
\frac{c}{(\mu+1)^2}
\exp\!\left(\frac{c}{\mu+1}\right).
\tag{3.5}
\]

Take `mu=nu+s`, so `2(mu+1)=d+2s`, and substitute (3.5) into (3.3). This is precisely the two-sided bound in Section 0.2.

No external large-order Bessel asymptotic is used.

## 3.4 Exact shell-mass consequence

For `d>=3`,

\[
N_{d,s}
=\binom{d+s-1}{s}-\binom{d+s-3}{s-2},

and direct cancellation gives

\[
\frac{N_{d,s+1}}{N_{d,s}}
=
\frac{(d+2s)(d+s-2)}{(s+1)(d+2s-2)}.
\tag{3.6}
\]

Multiplying (3.6) by the ratio in Section 3.3 gives the shell-mass formula of Section 0.2. This independently revalidates the fixed-offset Mercer-tail input used by the expectation-only Target-3 proof.

# 4. General fixed-rank Gaussian quadratic-vector density

This section proves the Gaussian part of `P23-T4-FIXED-RANK-LOWJORDAN-DENSITY-45`.

Let `theta in S^{r-1}` and define

\[
A_\theta=\sum_{j=1}^r\theta_jA_j.
\]

Because the `A_j` are Frobenius-orthonormal,

\[
\operatorname{tr}A_\theta^2=1.
\tag{4.1}
\]

For every `v in R^d`, vector Cauchy--Schwarz gives

\[
\|A_\theta v\|^2
=\left\|\sum_j\theta_jA_jv\right\|^2
\le\sum_j\|A_jv\|^2
=v^TC(P)v.
\]

Thus

\[
0\preceq A_\theta^2\preceq C(P).
\tag{4.2}
\]

For positive semidefinite `0<=X<=Y`, `tr X^2<=tr Y^2`; applying this to (4.2),

\[
\operatorname{tr}A_\theta^4\le\operatorname{tr}C(P)^2=\eta.
\tag{4.3}
\]

Let the eigenvalues of `A_theta` be `lambda_1,...,lambda_d`. Then

\[
\sum_i\lambda_i^2=1,
\qquad
\sum_i\lambda_i^4\le\eta,
\qquad
\lambda_i^2\le\sqrt\eta.
\tag{4.4}
\]

For `t=rho theta`, the exact characteristic function of the centered Gaussian quadratic vector has magnitude

\[
|\varphi_P(\rho\theta)|
=
\prod_{i=1}^d
(1+2\rho^2\lambda_i^2)^{-1/4}.
\tag{4.5}
\]

The function `x -> log(1+2rho^2 x)` is concave and vanishes at zero. Therefore, for `0<=x<=sqrt(eta)`,

\[
\log(1+2\rho^2x)
\ge
\frac{x}{\sqrt\eta}
\log(1+2\rho^2\sqrt\eta).
\]

Summing and using (4.4),

\[
\boxed{
|\varphi_P(\rho\theta)|
\le
(1+2\rho^2\sqrt\eta)^{-1/(4\sqrt\eta)}.
}
\tag{4.6}
\]

Put

\[
\alpha=1/(4\sqrt\eta),
\qquad b=2\sqrt\eta.
\]

The right side is integrable in `R^r` exactly when `alpha>r/2`, i.e.

\[
\eta<1/(4r^2).
\]

The exact radial integral is

\[
\int_{\mathbb R^r}(1+b\|t\|^2)^{-\alpha}dt
=
\pi^{r/2}b^{-r/2}
\frac{\Gamma(\alpha-r/2)}{\Gamma(\alpha)}.
\]

Fourier inversion therefore gives

\[
\|g_P^G\|_\infty
\le
(2\pi)^{-r}
\pi^{r/2}b^{-r/2}
\frac{\Gamma(\alpha-r/2)}{\Gamma(\alpha)},
\]

which is exactly `M_r(eta)`.

### Rank-two check

For `r=2`,

\[
\frac{\Gamma(\alpha-1)}{\Gamma(\alpha)}
=\frac1{\alpha-1},
\]

and the general formula collapses to

\[
M_2(\eta)
=\frac1{2\pi(1-4\sqrt\eta)},
\]

exactly P23/P04's audited rank-two density bound.

# 7. Diffuse-plane CLT and the actual fixed-rank residual chain

## 7.1 Deterministic diffuse planes

Suppose `r` is fixed and `eta(P_d)->0`. For fixed `t in R^r`, set

\[
A_t=\sum_jt_jA_j.

Then

\[
\operatorname{tr}A_t=0,
\qquad
\operatorname{tr}A_t^2=\|t\|^2,
\qquad
\|A_t\|_{op}\le\|t\|\eta^{1/4}.

The exact Gaussian quadratic-form characteristic function is

\[

\]

For fixed `t`, the logarithm may be expanded for all sufficiently large `d`. The linear term vanishes by tracelessness, the quadratic term is `-||t||^2/2`, and every term of order at least three is bounded by

\[
O_t(\|A_t\|_{op}\operatorname{tr}A_t^2)
=O_t(\eta^{1/4}).
\]

Therefore

\[
\varphi_d(t)
\to e^{-\|t\|^2/2}
\]

uniformly on compact `t`-sets, so

\[
Q_{P_d}(G)\Longrightarrow N(0,I_r).
\tag{7.1}
\]

Since in (5.1) the radial multiplier `S->1` in probability,

\[

\tag{7.2}

## 7.2 Uniform integrability needed for determinant selection

For any Frobenius-unit traceless `A`, with

\[

\tag{7.3}

=E\left(\sum_{j=1}^rf_{A_j}^2\right)^2
\le

\tag{7.4}

For fixed `r`, the size-bias weight `R_P/r` is therefore uniformly integrable. From (7.2), under the selected law,

\tag{7.5}

The moment envelope from Section 6 gives uniform higher inverse moments below `1+r/2`, so (7.5) upgrades to moment convergence at every fixed order `p<1+r/2`.

## 7.3 Actual residual planes

For a residual `r`-plane with orthonormal basis, define

\tag{7.7}

Insert P04's current

Sections 7.1--7.2 apply conditionally and uniformly on the event `eta(P_r)` small. This proves the conditional convergence-in-probability statement in Section 0.5.
