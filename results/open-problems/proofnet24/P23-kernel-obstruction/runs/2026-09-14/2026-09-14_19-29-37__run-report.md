Fix a cumulative block through degree \(L\).

\[
\mathcal K_L(x,y)
=

\[
\boxed{
\|\phi(x)\|^2=D
}
\tag{5.1}

for every \(x\).

Hence

\[
\boxed{
|\mathcal K_L(x,y)|\le D.

\tag{5.2}

\[
\begin{aligned}

\sum_{a,b}
\phi_a(x)\phi_b(x)

for **every fixed \(x\)**.

# 7. New variance theorem

Let

\[
H_{ij}=H_L(X_i,X_j).
\]

If two unordered pairs are disjoint, the corresponding \(H\)'s are independent and centered.

If they share exactly one index, for example \((i,j)\) and \((i,k)\), then by conditional independence and (5.5),

\[
\begin{aligned}
E[H_{ij}H_{ik}]
&=
E_{X_i}
\left[
E_{X_j}(H_{ij}\mid X_i)
E_{X_k}(H_{ik}\mid X_i)
\right]
\\
&=0.
\end{aligned}
\tag{7.1}
\]

Therefore **every covariance between two distinct unordered pairs vanishes exactly**.

Hence

\[
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Var}(W)
&=
\frac4{n^4}
\binom n2
E H_L(X,Y)^2

&=
\boxed{
\frac{2(n-1)}{n^3}

\end{aligned}
\tag{7.2}
\]

\[
\boxed{

\le

\tag{7.3}

# 11. Why this does not automatically give realized-risk probability one

Condition on the design \(X\).

The fully realized pure-noise risk has the form

\[
\mathcal R(X,\varepsilon)
=
\varepsilon^\top M_X\varepsilon,
\qquad
M_X\succeq0,
\]

with

\[
E_\varepsilon[\mathcal R\mid X]=V(X).
\]

Knowing that \(V(X)\) is bounded below with probability tending to one does not, by itself, imply

\[
P_\varepsilon\{
\mathcal R\ge cV(X)
\mid X
\}
\to1.
\]

For example, a rank-one Gaussian quadratic form has order-one lower-tail probability below a fixed fraction of its mean.

The previous general second-moment estimate

\[
E[\mathcal R^2\mid X]
\le3V(X)^2
\]

gives only a fixed Paley--Zygmund probability.

Therefore the strongest realized-risk statement retained is still a positive fixed probability, unless one proves a new effective-rank theorem for \(M_X\).

This distinction between

- design-conditional mean;
- fully realized training noise;
- expectation over all training randomness

is exact and preserved.
