## 5. Exact degenerate U-statistic structure

Fix a cumulative block through degree \(L\), of dimension

\[
D=D_{d,L}.
\]

Let

\[
\phi(x)\in\mathbb R^D
\]

be the concatenated orthonormal harmonic feature map and

\[
\mathcal K_L(x,y)
=
\langle\phi(x),\phi(y)\rangle.
\]

The addition theorem gives

\[
\|\phi(x)\|^2=D
\]

for every \(x\). Thus

\[
|\mathcal K_L(x,y)|\le D.
\tag{5.1}
\]

Orthonormality gives, for every fixed \(x\),

\[
\begin{aligned}
E_Y\mathcal K_L(x,Y)^2
&=
\sum_{a,b}
\phi_a(x)\phi_b(x)
E[\phi_a(Y)\phi_b(Y)]
\\
&=
\sum_a\phi_a(x)^2
\\
&=
\boxed{D}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{5.2}
\]

Therefore

\[
\boxed{
H_L(x,y)
=
\mathcal K_L(x,y)^2-D
}
\]

is exactly first-order degenerate:

\[
\boxed{
E_YH_L(x,Y)=0
\quad
\text{for every }x.
}
\tag{P23-UDEG-05}
\]

## 6. Exact Frobenius-defect representation and variance

Let

\[
\Phi
=
\begin{pmatrix}
\phi(X_1)^\top\\
\vdots\\
\phi(X_n)^\top
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
S=\frac1n\Phi^\top\Phi,
\]

and

\[
W=\|S-I_D\|_F^2.
\]

Because \(\operatorname{Tr}S=D\),

\[
W=\operatorname{Tr}(S^2)-D.
\]

The diagonal projection-kernel terms equal \(D^2\), hence

\[
\boxed{
W
=
\frac{D(D-1)}n
+
\frac{2}{n^2}
\sum_{i<j}H_L(X_i,X_j).
}
\tag{6.1}
\]

Thus

\[
\boxed{
EW=\frac{D(D-1)}n.
}
\tag{6.2}
\]

If two distinct unordered pairs are disjoint, their centered kernels are independent. If they share one index, for example \((i,j)\) and \((i,k)\), then degeneracy gives

\[
E[H_{ij}H_{ik}]
=
E_{X_i}\!\left[
E(H_{ij}\mid X_i)
E(H_{ik}\mid X_i)
\right]
=0.
\]

So all distinct pair covariances vanish exactly. Therefore

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{Var}(W)
=
\frac{2(n-1)}{n^3}
\left(
E\mathcal K_L(X,Y)^4-D^2
\right).
}
\tag{P23-FROB-CONC-05}
\]

Using (5.1),

\[
\mathcal K_L^4
\le
D^2\mathcal K_L^2,
\]

and using \(E\mathcal K_L^2=D\),

\[
E\mathcal K_L^4\le D^3.
\]

Hence

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{Var}(W)
\le
\frac{2(n-1)D^3}{n^3}.
}
\tag{6.3}
\]

Dividing by the squared mean,

\[
\boxed{
\frac{\operatorname{Var}(W)}
{(EW)^2}
\le
\frac{
2(n-1)D
}{
n(D-1)^2
}.
}
\tag{6.4}
\]

Consequently, whenever \(D\to\infty\),

\[
\boxed{
\frac{W}{EW}\to1
}
\]

in \(L^2\) and in probability.

At the critical blocks \(D\asymp n=2^d\), the relative variance is \(O(1/n)=O(2^{-d})\).

The coefficient

\[
\frac4{n^4}\binom n2
=
\frac{2(n-1)}{n^3}
\]

was checked symbolically. No numerical simulation is used as proof.

## 9. Expectation versus fully realized noise

Condition on \(X\). The realized pure-noise risk is

\[
\mathcal R(X,\varepsilon)
=
\varepsilon^\top M_X\varepsilon,
\qquad
M_X\succeq0,
\]

and

\[
E_\varepsilon[\mathcal R\mid X]=V(X).
\]

A high-probability lower bound on the conditional mean \(V(X)\) does **not** by itself imply a high-probability lower bound on \(\mathcal R\). A rank-one Gaussian quadratic form already shows why.

The existing bound

\[
E[\mathcal R^2\mid X]\le3V(X)^2

gives only a fixed Paley--Zygmund probability.

Thus the new theorem is exactly a **design-conditional mean** theorem. A realized-risk probability-\(1-o(1)\) result requires a separate effective-rank/concentration argument for \(M_X\).
