Core calculation to prove:

For `Phi_alpha(T)=n^{-alpha}(B_n-p_n)` on a finite `n`-leaf DTCS tree and `0` on an infinite fringe tree, the finite normalized contribution is

`Y_n^(alpha)=n^{1/2-alpha}(B_n-p_n)`.

The exact root-split formula already proved for P0342 gives `p_n->1/2` and eventually `min(p_n,1-p_n)>1/3`.

For the size-three orientation mark, write `X_n=n^{-1/2} sum_i Psi(T_i^(n))`. Conditional on the unoriented size-three-clade scaffold, `X_n` is a Rademacher sum divided by `sqrt(n)`, with at most `n/3` summands. Hence

- `E[X_n | scaffold]=0`, so `Cov(X_n, B_n-p_n)=0` exactly because `B_n` is scaffold-measurable;
- `E X_n^2 <= 1/3`;
- using `E( sum_{r=1}^K eps_r )^4 = 3K^2-2K`, `E X_n^4 <= 1/3`.

Expected phase diagram:

1. `0<alpha<1/2`: `|Y_n^(alpha)| >= (1/3)n^{1/2-alpha}->infinity`, so `X_n+Y_n^(alpha)` diverges in absolute value in probability by the same second-moment bound used in the frozen witness.
2. `alpha=1/2`: `Y_n=B_n-p_n` is order one. Exact covariance zero gives `Var(X_n+Y_n) >= p_n(1-p_n)->1/4`. The fringe CLT variance is `lambda_3=3/(2*pi^2)<1/4`. Uniform fourth-moment control makes squares uniformly integrable, so convergence to the source fringe Gaussian would force variance convergence and is impossible.
3. `alpha>1/2`: `|Y_n^(alpha)|<=n^{1/2-alpha}->0` deterministically. Thus this perturbation cannot change any subsequential finite `sqrt(n)` limit of `Psi`.

If the proof survives checking, it gives an exact square-root stability boundary for this perturbation mechanism: qualitative continuity, and even `O(n^{-1/2})` pointwise approach to the infinite-fringe value, do not force second-order transfer; `o(n^{-1/2})` makes this particular perturbation asymptotically invisible. This is a new strengthening/generalization, not movement of the frozen source metric and not campaign closure.
