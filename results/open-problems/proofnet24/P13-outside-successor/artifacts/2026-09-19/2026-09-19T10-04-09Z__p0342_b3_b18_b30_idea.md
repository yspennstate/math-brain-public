## B3 — prove the finite-tree failure in a stronger form

Do not merely prove non-tightness.  Let

- `Z_n = sum_i Psi(T_i^(n))`, where each size-3 clade contributes its fair left/right orientation sign;
- `X_n = Z_n/sqrt(n)`;
- `Y_n = n^(1/4)(B_n-p_n)`, the historical global root-split perturbation contribution.

Conditional on the sigma-field that exposes the tree down to the existence of its size-3 clades but not their final size-3 orientations, distinct size-3 clade signs are iid Rademacher.  Since size-3 clades are disjoint, `N_3(n) <= n/3`.  Therefore

`E X_n = 0`, `E X_n^2 = E N_3(n)/n <= 1/3`.

The exact root split law gives `p_n -> 1/2`, so eventually `|Y_n| >= n^(1/4)/3` almost surely.  Hence for each fixed `M`,

`P(|X_n+Y_n| <= M) <= P(|X_n| >= n^(1/4)/3-M) -> 0`

by Chebyshev.  Thus the normalized finite `Theta` sum diverges in absolute value in probability.  This should establish the frozen FINITE-FAILURE obligation without any independence assumption between `X_n` and `Y_n`.

## B18 — say the fringe problem as a signed point-process problem

For the stationary fringe leaves `0,...,m-1`, let `K_m` be the number of size-3 clade intervals wholly contained in that leaf block.  Same-size clades are disjoint intervals.  Every complete size-3 clade contributes one fair orientation sign `epsilon_C`; at most one size-3 clade crosses each endpoint of the block.  Therefore

`sum_{i=0}^{m-1} Psi(T_i) = sum_{C complete in block} epsilon_C + R_m`,

with `|R_m| <= 2`.

Conditional on the unoriented size-3 clade scaffold, the complete-clade signs are independent Rademachers.  Thus the fringe CLT is controlled by one scalar random density, `K_m/m`.

## B30 — solve one scale/direction and isolate the exact missing glue

The occupation-measure formula in the source gives

`a(3)=6 H_2/(pi^2(3-1)) = 9/(2 pi^2)`.

Because each size-3 clade contains exactly three leaves, the stationary intensity of size-3 clades is

`lambda_3 = a(3)/3 = 3/(2 pi^2)`.

If `K_m/m -> lambda_3` in probability, then the conditional characteristic function

`cos(t/sqrt(m))^{K_m}`

converges to `exp(-lambda_3 t^2/2)`, while the bounded boundary remainder vanishes after division by `sqrt(m)`.  Hence `Psi` has a strictly-positive-variance fringe CLT.

Conversely, because `0 <= K_m/m <= 1/3`, every subsequence has a weakly convergent subsubsequence.  If the normalized `Psi` sums had a Gaussian limit with deterministic variance, the same conditional-characteristic-function calculation forces every subsequential limit of `K_m/m` to be a constant.  Since stationarity gives `E K_m/m -> lambda_3`, that constant must be `lambda_3`.  So for this frozen witness, the remaining FRINGE obligation is equivalent to the density law

`K_m/m -> 3/(2 pi^2)` in probability,

or to any sufficient route such as ergodicity of the fringe shift or a direct `Var(K_m)=o(m^2)` bound.
