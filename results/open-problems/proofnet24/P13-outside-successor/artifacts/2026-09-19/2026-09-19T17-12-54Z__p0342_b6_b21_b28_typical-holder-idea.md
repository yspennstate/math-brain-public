# P13 / P0342 research idea — B6, B21, B28

UTC run: 2026-09-19. Target: the current exclusive P13 successor P0342, Aldous–Janson Open Problem 12. Frozen source metric is already `M_P0342=2/2`; the work below is a strengthening around the counterexample and must not be counted as a third source obligation.

The previous run solved the worst-case deterministic Kraft extremal problem. Its threshold for a boundary-invisible Hölder perturbation `g` was `beta>1/2`: uniformly over all n-leaf full binary trees, `n^{-1/2} sum_i |g(T_i)| -> 0` only above that exponent. The new idea is to replace worst-case trees by an actual random `DTCS(n)` tree and use its typical leaf depth. This should make the stability threshold much stronger.

## B28 — replace “for all” with “for typical”

If `L_n` is the edge-height of a uniformly chosen leaf of `DTCS(n)`, a boundary-invisible beta-Hölder perturbation obeys, up to the fixed local-metric convention constant,

`|g(T_i^(n))| <= C_beta 2^{-beta d_i}`.

Hence

`E[n^{-1/2} sum_i |g(T_i^(n))|] <= C_beta sqrt(n) E[2^{-beta L_n}]`.

So the question reduces to a negative exponential moment of the *actual* random leaf height, rather than the extremal Kraft profile.

## B6 — deform to the solvable Laplace point

Aldous–Pittel give the exact recurrence for `f_n(u)=E[e^{uL_n}]`, `u<0`:

`f_1=1`,
`f_n(u)= e^u/H_{n-1} * sum_{k=1}^{n-1} f_k(u)/(n-k)`.

Try a power supersolution `C n^{-a}`. For any fixed `a in (0,1)`, the induction reduces to

`e^u/H_{n-1} * sum_{j=1}^{n-1} (1-j/n)^(-a)/j <= 1`.

The sum is `H_{n-1}+O_a(1)`: for `j<=n/2`, concavity/Taylor control gives an O(1) correction, while for `j>n/2`, writing `r=n-j` gives `(2/n) sum_{r<=n/2} (n/r)^a=O_a(1)` because `a<1`. Thus the ratio tends to `e^u<1`; after finitely many base cases, induction gives

`E[e^{uL_n}] <= C(u,a)n^{-a}`

for every `a<1`.

Taking `u=-beta log 2` and any `a in (1/2,1)` proves `sqrt(n) E[2^{-beta L_n}] -> 0` for every `beta>0`. Therefore every boundary-invisible perturbation with *any positive Hölder exponent* is negligible at the finite `sqrt(n)` scale in `L^1`. This is much stronger than the deterministic beta>1/2 threshold.

The preceding P0342 witness is continuous but was already shown to have no positive Hölder exponent, so it sits exactly outside this stability class. In the Hölder scale, the typical-DTCS boundary is therefore at exponent 0: every beta>0 is safe for this mechanism, while mere continuity can fail.
