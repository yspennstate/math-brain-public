## 4. C27-FB-MGF-01 — finite-torus exponential-moment block theorem

### Theorem

For the finite-torus setup of §0, for every `u>=1`,

\[
\boxed{
\mathbf P(\mathcal V(\mathcal B))
\le
\left[
\frac{2}{1+u}
\exp\left(
\frac{(u^2-1)\Lambda_\beta(\mathcal B)}{n}
\right)
\right]^n.
}
\tag{4.1}
\]

Consequently, under (0.1),

\[
\boxed{
\mathbf P(\mathcal V(\mathcal B))
\le 2^{-\varepsilon_{\rm MGF}(k-1)}
\le 2^{-\varepsilon_{\rm cert}(k-1)}.
}
\tag{4.2}
\]

### Proof

Fix a permutation realization in `V(B)`. Its root cycle visits every block, so the undirected graph on block labels obtained from root-cycle transitions is connected. Choose a spanning tree by a deterministic tie-breaking rule.

Independently color the `k` blocks red/blue with fair iid bits. Let `Z` be the number of bichromatic tree edges. On a tree, the root color together with the edge XORs is a bijective parametrization of vertex colorings, hence the `n=k-1` edge XORs are independent fair bits and

\[
Z\sim\operatorname{Bin}(n,1/2),
\qquad
\mathbf E_\xi u^Z=\left(\frac{1+u}{2}\right)^n.
\tag{4.3}
\]

For a coloring `xi`, let `R_xi` and `B_xi` be the unions of red and blue blocks and let

\[
N_\xi=\#\{x\in R_\xi:\pi_\beta(x)\in B_\xi\}.
\]

Every bichromatic tree edge is represented by at least one root-cycle transition across the color cut. Thus the root cycle has at least `Z` color-changing directed transitions. On a directed cycle the numbers of red-to-blue and blue-to-red transitions are equal, so the root-cycle red-to-blue count is at least `Z/2`. The global count `N_xi` is no smaller. Hence, pathwise on `V(B)`,

\[
u^{2N_\xi}\ge u^Z.
\]

Averaging over the fair coloring and then over interchange gives

\[
\mathbf P(\mathcal V)
\left(\frac{1+u}{2}\right)^n
\le
\mathbf E_{\pi,\xi}u^{2N_\xi}.
\tag{4.4}
\]

Now freeze the coloring and put `t=u^2>=1`. Mark the labels initially in `R_xi` red. Their occupied set at time `beta` is the symmetric exclusion process on the finite torus, started from a deterministic set. Borcea–Brändén–Liggett's strongly-Rayleigh preservation theorem applies in exactly this finite symmetric-exclusion setting: a deterministic initial set is strongly Rayleigh, the exclusion dynamics preserves that property, and therefore the final site indicators are negatively associated.

If `eta_y(beta)` indicates that site `y` contains a red label, then

\[
N_\xi=\sum_{y\in B_\xi}\eta_y(\beta).
\]

Negative association, applied to the increasing nonnegative factors `1+(t-1)eta_y`, gives

\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathbf E_\pi t^{N_\xi}
&=\mathbf E_\pi\prod_{y\in B_\xi}
(1+(t-1)\eta_y(\beta))\\
&\le\prod_{y\in B_\xi}
(1+(t-1)\mathbf E\eta_y(\beta))\\
&\le\exp\{(t-1)\mu_\xi\},
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.5}
\]

where

\[
\mu_\xi
=\sum_{x\in R_\xi,y\in B_\xi}p_\beta(x,y)
\le\Lambda_\beta(\mathcal B).
\tag{4.6}
\]

The one-particle kernel is symmetric on the torus, so every cut contributes equally in the two directions; therefore

\[
\Lambda_\beta(\mathcal B)\le\frac12\mathcal H_\beta(\mathcal B).
\tag{4.7}
\]

Using (4.5)--(4.6) in (4.4) and rearranging proves (4.1).

Under (0.1), (4.7) gives `Lambda_beta<=n/(8e)`, hence

\[
\mathbf P(\mathcal V)
\le b(u)^n,
\qquad
b(u)=\frac2{1+u}
\exp\left(\frac{u^2-1}{8e}\right).
\tag{4.8}
\]

Set

\[
\phi(u)=\log b(u)=\log2-\log(1+u)+\frac{u^2-1}{8e}.
\]

Then

\[
\phi'(u)=-\frac1{1+u}+\frac{u}{4e},
\qquad
\phi''(u)=\frac1{(1+u)^2}+\frac1{4e}>0.
\]

Thus the unique minimizer on `u>=1` satisfies

\[
u_*(1+u_*)=4e,
\qquad
u_*=(\sqrt{1+16e}-1)/2.
\]

At this point `(u_*^2-1)/(8e)=(u_*-1)/(2u_*)`, yielding the displayed formula for `epsilon_MGF=-log_2 b(u_*)`. This proves the analytic inequality in (4.2).

Finally, the independent exact-rational checker takes

\[
u_0=11340541/4000000
\]

and rigorous rational upper/lower bounds for `log 2`, `log((1+u_0)/2)`, and `e` to certify

\[
\frac{\log((1+u_0)/2)-(u_0^2-1)/(8e)}{\log2}
>
\frac{590451719447}{1250000000000}.
\]

Hence the literal certified exponent in (4.2) follows. `square`
