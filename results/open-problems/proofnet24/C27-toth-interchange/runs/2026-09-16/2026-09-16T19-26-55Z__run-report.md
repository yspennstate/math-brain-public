### Proposition `C27-GUARDED-TYPICAL-PHASE-02` — PROVED / PARTIAL auxiliary theorem

Let `U subset Z^3`, let `I=[s,s+G]` with `G>=1`, and let `F` be a sigma-field which reveals no Poisson clock on any nearest-neighbor edge incident to `U` during `I`. Let `x in U` and finite sets

\[
A_1,\ldots,A_m\subset U
\]

be `F`-measurable, and assume

\[
d_\infty(x,U^c)\ge b.
\]

For `r>=0` write

\[
A_\ell^{+r}:=\{z\in\mathbb Z^3:d_2(z,A_\ell)\le r\},
\qquad
\mathfrak R_r:=\sum_{\ell=1}^m|A_\ell^{+r}|.
\tag{3.1}
\]

Let `X` be the tagged interchange strand starting from `x` at time `s`. Conditional on `F`, until it exits `U` during `I`, `X` is ordinary continuous-time nearest-neighbor simple random walk with rate one on each incident edge.

There are absolute constants `C,c>0` such that:

1. If `J` is uniform on `{1,...,m}` and sampled independently after `F`, then

\[
\boxed{
\mathbb P\bigl(d_2(X_{s+G},A_J)\le r\mid\mathscr F\bigr)
\le
\frac{C\mathfrak R_r}{mG^{3/2}}
+6\exp\left[-\min\left\{\frac{b^2}{8G},\frac b2\right\}\right].
}
\tag{3.2}
\]

2. At least `m/2` indices `l` satisfy

\[
|A_\ell^{+r}|\le\frac{2\mathfrak R_r}{m}.
\tag{3.3}
\]

Consequently one may choose an `F`-measurable index from this positive-density family and obtain

\[
\boxed{
\mathbb P\bigl(d_2(X_{s+G},A_\ell)\le r\mid\mathscr F\bigr)
\le
\frac{2C\mathfrak R_r}{mG^{3/2}}
+6\exp\left[-\min\left\{\frac{b^2}{8G},\frac b2\right\}\right].
}
\tag{3.4}
\]

#### Proof

Because the edge-time slab incident to `U` is untouched, conditional on `F` its Poisson clocks retain their original independent law. Thus the tagged strand, stopped on exiting `U`, agrees with rate-one-per-edge continuous-time SRW on `Z^3` up to that exit.

The standard three-dimensional heat-kernel estimate for this walk is

\[
\sup_{u,v}p_G(u,v)\le C G^{-3/2},
\qquad G\ge1.
\tag{3.5}
\]

For any fixed `F`-measurable `l`, coupling the stopped strand to the unrestricted walk gives

\[
\begin{aligned}
&\mathbb P\bigl(X_{s+G}\in A_\ell^{+r},\ \tau_{U^c}>G\mid\mathscr F\bigr)\\
&\qquad\le
\sum_{y\in A_\ell^{+r}}p_G(x,y)
\le C|A_\ell^{+r}|G^{-3/2}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{3.6}
\]

The same coordinate exponential-martingale estimate already used in the C27/HARD10 fresh-tube arguments gives

\[
\mathbb P(\tau_{U^c}\le G\mid\mathscr F)
\le
6\exp\left[-\min\left\{\frac{b^2}{8G},\frac b2\right\}\right].
\tag{3.7}
\]

Average (3.6) over the independent uniform `J` and add (3.7), proving (3.2). Equation (3.3) is deterministic Markov counting: more than `m/2` indices cannot each exceed twice the average contribution to (3.1). Apply (3.6)--(3.7) to any `F`-measurable choice among the indices satisfying (3.3), proving (3.4). `square`
