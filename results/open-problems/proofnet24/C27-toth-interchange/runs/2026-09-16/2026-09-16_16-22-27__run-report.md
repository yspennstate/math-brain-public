# 4. New theorem: explicit torus first-interaction constant 756

The previous C27 theorem gave

\[
\mathbb P(L_{m,\beta}(0)\le K)
\le15461\frac{K}{\sqrt\beta}
\]

under `m>=max{5,sqrt(K beta)}`. Its constants used two deliberately coarse replacements: a lag-independent torus heat-kernel bound and the whole `B_2` cube of 125 sites as the pre-entry set. Both can be sharpened without changing the cyclic-time argument.

Let `X` be the ordinary tagged-particle walk on `T_m^3`; each of its six nearest-neighbor directions has rate one. Before the first same-phase interaction of the cyclic-time walk, the cyclic-time driver agrees with `X`. For lag `r beta`, put

\[
D_r(t)=X(t)-X(t-r\beta),
\qquad r\beta\le t\le K\beta.
\]

If a same-phase interaction occurs by time `K beta`, then for some `1<=r<=K`, `D_r` either begins inside

\[
B_1:=\{z:\|z\|_\infty\le1\}
\]

or enters `B_1` at an upper- or lower-endpoint jump.

## 4.1 A lag-sensitive exact torus heat bound

For the one-dimensional continuous-time walk on the cycle of length `m`, Fourier inversion gives

\[
\sup_{a,b}p_t^{(m)}(a,b)
\le
\frac1m+\frac{\sqrt\pi}{4\sqrt t}.
\tag{4.1}
\]

Indeed, after choosing Fourier representatives with `|theta|<=pi`, use

\[
1-\cos\theta\ge\frac{2\theta^2}{\pi^2}
\]

and bound the remaining Gaussian sum by its integral. Since

\[
\pi<\frac{22}{7}<\frac{256}{81},
\]

we have

\[
\frac{\sqrt\pi}{4}<\frac49.
\]

Assume throughout

\[
m\ge\sqrt{K\beta},\qquad \beta\ge1.
\]

At time `t=r beta`, (4.1) and independence of the three coordinate processes give

\[
\boxed{
\sup_{x,y}p_{r\beta}^{T_m^3}(x,y)
\le
\beta^{-3/2}
\left(K^{-1/2}+\frac49r^{-1/2}\right)^3.
}
\tag{4.2}

Write

\[
a_{K,r}=K^{-1/2}+\frac49r^{-1/2}.
\]

## 4.2 Exact first-entry shell: 54 rather than 125

The cube `B_1` has 27 vertices. If a nearest-neighbor jump makes its **first** entrance into `B_1`, the pre-jump difference cannot be an arbitrary point of `B_2`. It lies in the outer graph boundary

\[
\partial^+B_1
=\{z\notin B_1:\operatorname{dist}_{\rm graph}(z,B_1)=1\}.
\]

For `m>=5`, there is no wrap-around ambiguity and

\[
\boxed{|\partial^+B_1|=6\cdot3^2=54.}
\tag{4.3}
\]

The archived exact checker enumerates this set as well.

The upper endpoint of `D_r` changes at total rate six and the lower endpoint changes at total rate six. For a lower-endpoint jump at physical time `s`, the pre-change difference has the form

\[
J+[X(s+r\beta)-X(s)],
\]

where `J` is the nearest-neighbor jump at `s` and the future increment is independent; translating by `J` does not change the supremum heat-kernel bound. Thus both endpoint mechanisms admit the same shell estimate.

## 4.3 Reduction to one explicit finite sum

The probability that `D_r(r beta)` starts in `B_1` is at most

\[
27\beta^{-3/2}a_{K,r}^3.
\]

For the endpoint-entry part, Poisson compensation over the interval of length `(K-r) beta` gives at most

\[
12\cdot54\,(K-r)\beta\cdot
\beta^{-3/2}a_{K,r}^3
=648\beta^{-1/2}(K-r)a_{K,r}^3.
\]

Summing over lags yields

\[
\mathbb P(\tau_{\rm int}\le K\beta)
\le
27\beta^{-3/2}I_K
+648\beta^{-1/2}S_K,
\tag{4.4}
\]

where

\[
I_K=\sum_{r=1}^K a_{K,r}^3,
\qquad
S_K=\sum_{r=1}^{K-1}(K-r)a_{K,r}^3.
\]

For the initial term, `K>=r` implies

\[
a_{K,r}\le\frac{13}{9}r^{-1/2}\le\frac{13}{9},

so

\[
I_K\le K\left(\frac{13}{9}\right)^3
=\frac{2197}{729}K

and therefore

\[
27\beta^{-3/2}I_K
\le\frac{2197}{27}\frac K{\sqrt\beta}.
\tag{4.5}
