# 3. B7/B21 positive result: a filtration-safe two-tag local-escape lemma

The point of this lemma is not to prove the source conjecture. It isolates a d=3 transience mechanism that is valid for the actual unweighted interchange measure and, unlike the tree/loop imports above, survives the measure and geometry checks.

### Proposition `C27-FRESH-PAIR-01` — PROVED / PARTIAL auxiliary result

Work either on Z^3 or inside a finite torus region `U` that is isometric to a subset of Z^3. Fix a physical-time interval `I=[s,s+T]`. Let `F` be an admissible exploration history that may reveal arbitrary information outside `U` and at other physical times but has revealed **no Poisson clock on any edge incident to U during I**.

Let `x,y in U` be `F`-measurable starting vertices and let

\[
D=|x-y|_2.
\]

Let `X_t,Y_t` be the two physical interchange strands during `I`, and let

\[
\tau_U=\inf\{t:\ X_t\notin U\text{ or }Y_t\notin U\}.
\]

For an integer `r>=2` with `D>=2r`, put

\[
\tau_r=\inf\{t:\ |X_t-Y_t|_2\le r\}.
\]

Then there is an absolute constant `C` such that

\[
\boxed{
\mathbf P(\tau_r\le T\mid\mathscr F)
\le C\frac rD+
\mathbf P(\tau_U\le T\mid\mathscr F).
}
\tag{3.1}
\]

In particular, if each start is at buffer distance at least `b` from `U^c`, the second term is at most twice the ordinary continuous-time SRW exit probability from a radius-`b` buffer over duration `T`.

### Proof

Until `tau_U`, every incident edge clock seen by either tag during `I` is untouched under the admissible history. Until `tau_r`, the two tags are farther than `r>=2`; in particular they are not adjacent and do not share an interchange edge. Consequently, up to `tau_r wedge tau_U`, the difference process

\[
Z_t=X_t-Y_t
\]

is exactly continuous-time nearest-neighbor simple random walk on Z^3 with rate two for each signed coordinate jump. Time speed does not affect its hitting probabilities.

It remains to bound the probability that this relative walk, started at `z` with `|z|=D`, ever hits the radius-r ball. Let `G(z,u)` be its Green kernel. The standard d=3 Green estimate used already in the archived C27/HARD10 capacity work gives

\[
G(z,u)\le C(1+|z-u|)^{-1}.
\]

By the last-exit representation,

\[
\mathbf P_z(\tau_{B(0,r)}<\infty)
=\sum_{u\in B(0,r)}G(z,u)e_{B(0,r)}(u),
\]

where `e_A` is equilibrium measure. Since `D>=2r`, the Green factor is at most `C/D`. Also

\[
\operatorname{Cap}(B(0,r))\le Cr.
\]

For completeness, the latter follows directly from the Dirichlet principle using a cutoff equal to one on the radius-r ball, decreasing linearly to zero on the annulus from `r` to `2r`: there are `O(r^3)` relevant edges and squared gradient `O(r^{-2})`, giving energy `O(r)`.

Therefore

\[
\mathbf P_z(\tau_{B(0,r)}<\infty)\le C r/D.
\]

If the actual two-tag process approaches within `r` before time `T`, either it does so before leaving `U`, in which case the relative-walk bound applies, or `tau_U<=T`. This proves (3.1). `square`

This is a nonanticipative fresh-region theorem. It says nothing if `x,y` were selected by looking into the same clocks in `I`. That qualification is load-bearing, not cosmetic.
