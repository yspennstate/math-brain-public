# 4. New theorem: the entire volume-biased left nullspace has exact isotropic first moment

## `P20-VOLUME-NULL-PROJECTOR-48`

Let `H` be `n x m` with

\[
n=m+r,
\qquad r\ge1,
\]

and iid centered rows `h_i in R^m`. Assume `H^T H` and every leave-one-row Gram matrix below are invertible almost surely and the determinant moments are finite.

Let

\[
G=H^\top H,
\qquad
D=\det G,
\]

and define the volume law

\[
\frac{dQ}{dP}=\frac D{E_PD}.
\]

Let

\[
K=I_n-HG^{-1}H^\top
\]

be the orthogonal projector onto `ker(H^T)`. Then

\[
\boxed{E_QK=\frac rnI_n.}
\tag{4.1}
\]

### 4.1 Diagonal identity

Fix `i` and write

\[
A_i=H_{-i}^\top H_{-i},
\qquad
G=A_i+h_ih_i^\top,
\qquad
a_i=h_i^\top A_i^{-1}h_i.
\]

The matrix determinant lemma gives

\[
D=\det(A_i)(1+a_i).
\tag{4.2}
\]

Sherman--Morrison gives

\[
h_i^\top G^{-1}h_i=\frac{a_i}{1+a_i}.
\]

Therefore

\[
\boxed{
D K_{ii}
=D\left(1-h_i^\top G^{-1}h_i\right)
=\det(A_i).
}
\tag{4.3}
\]

By iid row exchangeability the expectation of the right side is the same for all `i`.

### 4.2 Off-diagonal cancellation

For `i != j`, Sherman--Morrison also gives

\[
h_i^\top G^{-1}h_j
=\frac{h_i^\top A_i^{-1}h_j}{1+a_i}.
\]

Hence

\[
\boxed{
D K_{ij}
=-\det(A_i)\,h_i^\top A_i^{-1}h_j.
}
\tag{4.4}
\]

Condition on all rows except `h_i`. Then `A_i`, `h_j`, and `det(A_i)` are fixed, while the right side is linear in the centered fresh row `h_i`. Therefore

\[
E_P[D K_{ij}]=0.
\tag{4.5}
\]

### 4.3 Normalize the diagonal

Pointwise,

\[
\operatorname{tr}K=r.
\]

All volume-weighted diagonal expectations are equal, so

\[
\sum_{i=1}^n E_QK_{ii}=r
\]

forces

\[
E_QK_{ii}=\frac rn.
\]

Together with (4.5), this proves (4.1).

### Remarks

1. No row covariance normalization is used.
2. No rotational invariance is used.
3. For `r=1`, `K=vv^T`, and (4.1) reduces exactly to the strengthened audit identity (3.6).
4. The theorem is a first-moment statement under **volume bias**. It does not transfer to the ordinary product law without reciprocal-volume control.
