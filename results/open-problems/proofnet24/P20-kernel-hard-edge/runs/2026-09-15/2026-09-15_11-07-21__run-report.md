\operatorname{Tr}(A^2)\operatorname{Tr}(B^2)

\tag{4.1}

\[
S_g=\sum_{a=1}^sq_{A_a}(g)^2,

\[
R(F)=\sum_{a,b=1}^s\operatorname{Tr}(A_aA_bA_aA_b).

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{Var}S_g
=
2s+8\operatorname{Tr}(C^2)+4R(F).

\tag{4.2}

For symmetric `A,B`,
\[
|\operatorname{Tr}(ABAB)|

\[
|R(F)|\le\operatorname{Tr}(C^2).
\tag{4.3}

q_{A_a}(g)^2

# 5. Sequential projection-DPP contraction recursion

Let `P_s` denote the residual coefficient projection of rank `s` after `m-s` ordered points have been exposed from the full rank-`m` projection DPP.

Conditional on `P_s=P`, the next point has density
\[
\boxed{

\tag{5.1}
\]
with respect to `mu`.  This is the Gram-determinant Schur complement.

If
\[
q(u)=\|Ph(u)\|^2
\]
and the next point is `U`, define
\[
v=\frac{Ph(U)}{\sqrt{q(U)}}\in\operatorname{Ran}P.
\]
Then
\[
P_{s-1}=P_s-vv^\top.
\tag{5.2}
\]

Let
\[
N=T(v)\in\operatorname{Sym}_d^0,
\qquad
\|N\|_F=1.
\]
Define the linear coefficient-to-physical contraction map by
\[
\mathcal C(vv^\top)=T(v)^2.
\]
For a projection, this is exactly `C(F)` from (2.3). Put
\[
C_s=\mathcal C(P_s).
\]
Then
\[
C_{s-1}=C_s-N^2.
\tag{5.3}
\]

The DPP density cancels the normalization in `v`:
\[
\begin{aligned}
E[vv^\top\mid P_s=P]
&=
\frac1s\int
\frac{Ph(u)h(u)^\top P}{q(u)}q(u)d\mu(u)\\
&=
\frac Ps.
\end{aligned}
\tag{5.4}
\]
Therefore
\[
\boxed{
E[N^2\mid P_s]=\frac{C_s}{s}.
}
\tag{5.5}
\]

Since `Tr N^4<=1`,
\[
\begin{aligned}
E[\operatorname{Tr}C_{s-1}^2\mid P_s]
&=
\operatorname{Tr}C_s^2
-2\operatorname{Tr}\left(C_sE[N^2\mid P_s]\right)
+E\operatorname{Tr}N^4\\
&\le
\left(1-\frac2s\right)\operatorname{Tr}C_s^2+1.

\tag{5.6}

At the top,
\[

\qquad

\[
