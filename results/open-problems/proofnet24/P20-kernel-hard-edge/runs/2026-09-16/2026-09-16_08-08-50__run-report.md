## 4.1 A uniform positive mixed fourth moment

Take any two orthonormal coefficient matrices `A,B in E`. Put

```text

y = tr(ABAB),
D_d = d(d+2)/[(d+4)(d+6)].
```

I first derive the exact mixed fourth moment.

Let `G~N(0,I_d)` and set `X=G^T A G`, `Y=G^T B G`. Because `tr A=tr B=tr(AB)=0` and `||A||_F=||B||_F=1`, the Gaussian second moments give `E X^2=E Y^2=2` and `E XY=0`.

From

```text
log E exp(tX+uY)
 = sum_{r>=2} [2^(r-1)/r] tr(tA+uB)^r,
```

the coefficient of `t^2 u^2` in the fourth cumulant is obtained from the six cyclic words with two `A`'s and two `B`'s. Four give `tr(A^2B^2)=x` and two give `tr(ABAB)=y`. Hence

```text
cum(X,X,Y,Y)=32x+16y,
E X^2 Y^2 = 4+32x+16y.
```

Using `G=R U`, with `R` independent of uniform `U`,

```text

c_d^4 = d^2(d+2)^2/4,
```

so

```text
E f_A(U)^2 f_B(U)^2
 = D_d [1+8x+4y].
```

Now

```text
x = ||AB||_F^2 >= 0,
y = <AB,BA>_F,
|y| <= x.
```

Therefore

```text
1+8x+4y >= 1+4x >= 1.
```

For `d>=2`, `D_d>=1/6`. Thus

```text
E f_A^2 f_B^2 >= 1/6.                  (4.1)
```

This lower bound is orientation-free.
