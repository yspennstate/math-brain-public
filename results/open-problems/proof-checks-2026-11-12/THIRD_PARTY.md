# Third-party material

The `lit/openai*` folders, and the Lean files under `t115_gotsman_linial_2026_11_12/lit/openai/` and
`nt02_landau_siegel_2026_11_12/lit/lean/`, are copies of files from OpenAI's mathematics release
(github.com/openai/math, commit 1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a). They are redistributed under the
Apache License 2.0, a copy of which is in `LICENSE-openai-math.txt`, and are unchanged.

The `lit/search` folders hold arXiv API search results (metadata only). No paper texts, and no source code from
other authors' repositories, are included; the checks cite those works by author, title and arXiv number or
repository address, and the directory listings under `lit/repo_listing` and `lit/*/tree.json` record which files
were read.
