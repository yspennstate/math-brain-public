# Source note: low-influence admission is an established mechanism

30 September 2026. Full FEI remains OPEN.

The tensorization and rare-activation transport used in `COLLISION_ADMISSION.md` belongs to an established FEI reduction mechanism. Guy Shalev, *On the Fourier Entropy Influence Conjecture for Extremal Classes*, arXiv:1806.03646v2 (24 January 2019), Theorem 3 and Section 3.2, proves that a universal FEI theorem on any class `I(f)<=2^{-s(n)}` with `s(n)=o(n)` implies full FEI. The paper attributes the underlying activation construction to Wan–Wright–Wu and uses tensorization to remove a sublinear additive error.

Primary source: [arXiv PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1806.03646), especially printed pages 5 and 12–13; Theorem 3 is the introductory statement and Theorem 19 records the final reduction.

Our artifact adds the particular normalized-collision premise, its constants, and the exact full-support perturbation needed for that premise. It does not establish the collision inequality. Neither the general low-influence hardness principle nor tensor-plus-activation is claimed as new. This attribution note preserves the earlier proof and its scope unchanged.
