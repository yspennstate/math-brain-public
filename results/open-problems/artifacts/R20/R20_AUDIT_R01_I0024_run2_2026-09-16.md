## Checks performed

R01 defines, for a finite irreducible reversible discrete-time chain,

\[
L=BCB^T=\Pi(I-P),
\]

with one orientation of every non-loop edge, conductance
\(c_{xy}=\pi_xP(x,y)=\pi_yP(y,x)\), demand space
\(\mathcal H_0=\{b:1^Tb=0\}\), demand norm
\(b^T\Pi^{-1}b\), and flow-energy norm \(\theta^TC^{-1}\theta\).

I independently checked the three steps on which the claimed identity depends.

1. **Arbitrary-demand Thomson principle.**  The candidate
\[
\theta_*(b)=CB^TL^+b
\]
has divergence \(B\theta_*=LL^+b=b\).  If \(\theta=\theta_*+\eta\) with \(B\eta=0\), then the cross term in the energy is
\[
(L^+b)^TB\eta=0,
\]
so
\[
\min_{B\theta=b}\theta^TC^{-1}\theta=b^TL^+b.
\]
The treatment of self-loops is correct: they contribute nothing to the incidence/flow graph, and the diagonal of \(BCB^T\) is \(\pi_x(1-P(x,x))\), exactly matching \(\Pi(I-P)\).

2. **Relaxation-time Rayleigh quotient.**  With
\[
S=\Pi^{-1/2}L\Pi^{-1/2}=I-\Pi^{1/2}P\Pi^{-1/2},
\]
reversibility makes \(S\) symmetric with nonzero eigenvalues \(1-\lambda_i(P)\).  Under \(b=\Pi h\), \(1^Tb=0\) is \(\pi^Th=0\), and
\[
\frac{b^TL^+b}{b^T\Pi^{-1}b}
=
\frac{z^TS^+z}{z^Tz},\qquad z=\Pi^{1/2}h\perp\sqrt\pi.
\]
Its supremum is therefore \((1-\lambda_2)^{-1}=\tau_2\) in the source's discrete-time normalization.

3. **Minimum-norm right inverse.**  Every linear routing right inverse has pointwise energy at least the Thomson minimum, so its operator norm is at least \(\tau_2^{1/2}\).  The electrical routing operator attains the Thomson minimum simultaneously for every demand, hence its squared routing norm is exactly \(\tau_2\).  Equivalently, after the stated isometries this is the standard minimum-norm right-inverse identity for
\[
D=\Pi^{-1/2}BC^{1/2},
\]
whose least positive singular value squared is \(1-\lambda_2\).

No hidden minimax exchange is used, and I found no factor-of-two, orientation, self-loop, or stationary-weight normalization error.
