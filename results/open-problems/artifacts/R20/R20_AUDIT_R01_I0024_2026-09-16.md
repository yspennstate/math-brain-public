## Independent rederivation of the four proof points

Let `B` be an oriented incidence matrix, `C=diag(c_e)`, `L=BCB^T=Pi(I-P)`, and let an admissible antisymmetric all-pairs flow family satisfy `B phi^{xy}=e_x-e_y`. For centered `h`, R01 defines

`Theta(h)=(1/2) sum_{x,y} pi_x pi_y (h_x-h_y) phi^{xy}`.

1. **Divergence.** Applying `B` and taking the `z` coordinate gives two equal contributions, from `x=z` and `y=z`, each `(1/2) pi_z(h_z-pi(h))`. Since `pi(h)=0`,

`B Theta(h)=Pi h`.

The factor `1/2` and the ordered-pair convention are therefore correct.

2. **Thomson principle.** For any zero-sum demand `b`, set `u=L^+b` and `theta_*=CB^T u`. Then `B theta_*=b`. If `theta=theta_*+eta` is any other feasible flow, `B eta=0` and

`theta^T C^{-1} theta = theta_*^T C^{-1} theta_* + eta^T C^{-1} eta`,

because the cross term is `u^T B eta=0`. Hence the minimum is `b^T L^+ b`. This part is exact.

3. **Rayleigh normalization.** For `b=Pi h`, define `S=Pi^{-1/2} L Pi^{-1/2}=I-P_sym` and `z=Pi^{1/2}h`. Although a pseudoinverse is not generally invariant under arbitrary congruence, no invalid pseudoinverse-congruence step is needed here: for any solution `Lu=Pi h`, `v=Pi^{1/2}u` solves `Sv=z`, and `b^T u=z^T v` is unchanged by adding the one-dimensional null vector because `z` is orthogonal to `sqrt(pi)`. Therefore the minimum energy is `z^T S^+ z`, whose largest Rayleigh quotient on `sqrt(pi)^perp` is `(1-lambda_2)^{-1}`. R01's discrete-time normalization is correct.

4. **Simultaneous attainment.** The electrical pair family

`phi_*^{xy}=CB^T L^+(e_x-e_y)`

is admissible and linear in the pair demand. Combining the pairs with R01's coefficients gives

`Theta_*(h)=CB^T L^+ Pi h`,

the Thomson minimizer for demand `Pi h`, simultaneously for every centered `h`. Hence

`inf_Phi sup_h Energy(Theta_Phi(h))/||h||_pi^2 = tau_2`.

No minimax interchange is being assumed: every family is pointwise bounded below by the Thomson minimum, and one fixed electrical family attains that lower bound for every `h`.
