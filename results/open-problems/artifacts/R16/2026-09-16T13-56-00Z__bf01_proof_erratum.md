# R16-BF-01 proof correction / authoritative separation argument

This immutable artifact corrects one sentence in the run report `runs/R16/2026-09-16T13-56-00Z__bf-dtv-sparse-bound.md`. The theorem statement is unchanged. The report's phrase about obtaining strict separation by replacing `b_i` with `(1-epsilon)b_i` has the scaling direction backwards. The correct argument enlarges the target box by a factor `a>1` below the assumed optimal violation. No later claim depends on the mistaken phrase; use the proof below as authoritative.

## Correct proof

Let `K` be a nonempty bounded open convex subset of `R^m`. Let `u_1,...,u_N` be finitely many directions, including the coordinate basis, and let `b_i>0`. For `beta_i>=0`, put

\[
H_\beta(\xi)=\sum_i\beta_i |u_i\cdot\xi|.
\]

Assume

\[
h_{H_\beta}(K)\le \sum_i\beta_i b_i
\qquad\text{for every }\beta\in[0,\infty)^N. \tag{E.1}
\]

Claim: there is a probability density `rho in BV`, supported in `K`, such that

\[
V_{u_i}(\rho)\le b_i\qquad (1\le i\le N). \tag{E.2}
\]

Define

\[
M_*:=\inf_\rho\max_i\frac{V_{u_i}(\rho)}{b_i}, \tag{E.3}
\]

where the infimum is over probability densities supported in `K` with finite coordinate BV variation. Assume for contradiction that `M_*>1`, and choose a scalar

\[
1<a<M_*.
\]

Let

\[
\mathcal S=\{z\in\mathbb R^N:\exists\rho,\ z_i\ge V_{u_i}(\rho)\text{ for all }i\}
\]

and

\[
Q_a=\{z\in\mathbb R^N:z_i<a b_i\text{ for all }i\}.
\]

By the definition of `M_*`, `S` and `Q_a` are disjoint. `S` is convex because directional variation is convex under mixing of densities, and it is coordinatewise upward closed. The coordinate directions are included with finite budgets, so a bounded sequence of relevant variation vectors yields a uniformly BV-bounded sequence of densities on the fixed bounded set; BV compactness and lower semicontinuity show that `S` is closed in the portion relevant to separation. Equivalently one may first separate `Q_a` from the closed upward hull of attainable variation vectors.

Finite-dimensional strong separation gives a nonzero vector `beta` and a scalar `c` such that

\[
\beta\cdot z\ge c>\beta\cdot q
\qquad (z\in\mathcal S,\ q\in Q_a). \tag{E.4}
\]

Every coordinate of `beta` is nonnegative. Indeed, if `beta_j<0`, upward closure permits replacing any `z in S` by `z+R e_j` for arbitrary `R>0`, sending the left side of (E.4) to `-infinity`, impossible.

For `beta>=0`, minimizing over the upward hull gives

\[
\inf_{z\in\mathcal S}\beta\cdot z
=
\inf_\rho\sum_i\beta_iV_{u_i}(\rho)
=
h_{H_\beta}(K), \tag{E.5}
\]

where the last equality is exactly the anisotropic Cheeger variational definition for the finite integrand `H_beta`. On the other hand,

\[
\sup_{q\in Q_a}\beta\cdot q
=a\sum_i\beta_i b_i. \tag{E.6}
\]

Hence (E.4)-(E.6) imply

\[
h_{H_\beta}(K)\ge a\sum_i\beta_i b_i
>\sum_i\beta_i b_i,
\]

contradicting (E.1). Therefore `M_*<=1`.

Choose a minimizing sequence with `max_i V_{u_i}(rho_j)/b_i -> M_*`. Because the coordinate directions occur in the finite list, the ordinary BV norms of `rho_j` are uniformly bounded. The densities have fixed mass one and support in the same bounded set. BV compactness gives an `L^1` convergent subsequence, and lower semicontinuity of every fixed directional variation yields a limiting probability density satisfying (E.2).

This also handles non-unit directions: replace each nonzero `u_i` by `u_i/||u_i||` and its coefficient by the corresponding homogeneous factor. Zero directions are vacuous.

Thus R16-BF-01 is proved with the correct scaling direction. The prescribed-section and transform-stability deductions in the run report use only the theorem statement and remain unchanged.
