## Threshold-function / projective-arrangement literature checked for method #17

1. A. A. Irmatov, *Estimates for the number of threshold functions*, Discrete Math. Appl. 6 (1996), 569--583. Its abstract explicitly relates asymptotic saturation of the Schlaefli upper bound to typical spans of random sign vectors containing only the signed generators. This is directly relevant to the projective-matroid route, but it does not give the sharp `n^2/2^n` relative deficit rate derived in run33.

2. Thomas Voigt and Guenter M. Ziegler, *Singular 0/1-Matrices, and the Hyperplanes Spanned by Random 0/1-Vectors*, CPC 15 (2006), 463--471 / arXiv:math/0308050. Run32 already corrected the novelty ledger: the conceptual random cube-hyperplane equivalence is prior art.

3. Taylor Brysiewicz, Holger Eble, Lukas Kuehne, *Computing Characteristic Polynomials of Hyperplane Arrangements with Symmetries* (Discrete Comput. Geom. 2024 / preprint lineage). Their threshold-arrangement computation reports Whitney numbers through dimension 8; the chamber numbers through dimension 9 are the OEIS A000609 values. Their public MathRepo documentation does not provide a dimension-10 threshold-arrangement count.

4. Martin Anthony, *Chamber geometry and specification numbers of Boolean threshold functions*, arXiv:2606.29477 (28 Jun 2026). It identifies specification number with chamber facet number and proves average specification number `Theta(n)` (in particular at most `2n`). This controls average chamber facets, not the exponentially precise Schlaefli deficit required by run33's quantitative NBC reduction.

5. OEIS A000609 currently lists exact threshold-function counts only through `n=9`, with `T_9=144130531453121108`; no `T_10` term is present in the current entry.
