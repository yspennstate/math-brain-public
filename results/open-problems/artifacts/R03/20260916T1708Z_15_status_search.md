## Route takeaway

Voigt-Ziegler shows the sharp problem is equivalent, after 0/1 normalization, to proving that non-fat spanned cube hyperplanes contribute o(d^2) to E(d). JSS 2021 rank-deficiency gives corank >=2 probability 2^{-2d+o(d)}, so on the target 2^{-d} scale only corank-one hyperplanes survive. The new R03 modular route uses F_3 to identify an exact analogue of the fat objects: k-subspaces containing the full 2^k Boolean cube are precisely partition/Boolean subspaces; every other k-subspace contains at most (3/4)2^k Boolean points.
