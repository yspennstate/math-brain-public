## Primary source and current source status

1. Vishesh Jain, Ashwin Sah, Mehtaab Sawhney, *Singularity of discrete random matrices*, arXiv:2010.06554, current arXiv page checked 2026-09-17. The abstract still separates the sharp theorem for finite-support laws that are not uniform on their support from the uniform-support case. For uniform-support laws it states only `(1+o_n(1))^n` precision relative to equal-row/column mechanisms, plus a sharp compressible-vector analysis. R03 uses Conjecture 1.1 specialized to uniform Rademacher.
2. Julian Sahasrabudhe, *Probabilistic combinatorics at exponentially small scales*, arXiv:2512.15077, current arXiv page checked 2026-09-17. This is the 2026 ICM survey used in prior R03 reports; the exact iid sign prefactor is recorded there in Section 4 / Eq. (4.1) as the sharp target discussed by the survey.
3. Historical lineage: J. Komlós, *On the determinant of (0,1)-matrices* (1967). The R03 target is the sharp modern iid sign formulation, not merely the historical exponential nonsingularity statement.

Exact Rademacher normalization retained after source match:
`P(det B_n=0)=(1+o(1))*2*n*(n-1)*2^(-n)=(1+o(1))*n*(n-1)*2^(1-n)`.
Tikhomirov's `2^{-n+o(n)}` rate is not this asymptotic.
