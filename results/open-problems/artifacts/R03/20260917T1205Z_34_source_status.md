## Current primary literature checked

- Van H. Vu, *Recent progress in combinatorial random matrix theory*, arXiv:2005.02797: https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.02797
- Vishesh Jain, Ashwin Sah, Mehtaab Sawhney, *Singularity of discrete random matrices*, arXiv:2010.06554: https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.06554. Their sharp full theorem applies when the finite-support law is not uniform on its support. For a law uniform on its support, including iid Rademacher, the paper gives the correct exponential scale for full singularity and a sharp structured/compressible analysis, not the polynomial prefactor above.
- Konstantin Tikhomirov, *Singularity of random Bernoulli matrices*, Annals of Mathematics 191 (2020): https://annals.math.princeton.edu/2020/191-2/p06. This proves `(1/2+o(1))^n`, the exponential rate.
- Zeyan Song, Hanchao Wang, *Rank deficiency of Bernoulli random matrices for growing corank*, arXiv:2607.00495: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.00495. This determines growing-corank probabilities for Bernoulli(p), but does not give the missing sharp corank-one prefactor for the iid sign problem.
- Zach Hunter, Matthew Kwan, Lisa Sauermann, Mehtaab Sawhney, *On random matrices with large corank*, arXiv:2510.12933 / IMRN 2026: https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.12933. This gives `P(corank >= k) <= exp(-c n k)` for iid signs, but again does not give the sharp corank-one prefactor.

This is a dated bounded-search statement, not an exhaustive novelty certificate.
