# R03 run25 dated source / prior-route search — 2026-09-17

Scope: iid nonsymmetric n x n Rademacher sign matrices. This is not the symmetric Bernoulli ensemble.

## Primary/source lineage rechecked

1. János Komlós, *On the determinant of (0-1) matrices*, Studia Scientiarum Mathematicarum Hungarica 2 (1967), 7–21. Rutgers' current record was checked. This is the historical nonsingularity lineage.
2. Van H. Vu, *Recent progress in combinatorial random matrix theory*, arXiv:2005.02797 (2020), Section 2 / Conjecture 2.2 in the R03 lineage. The iid Rademacher problem is separate from the symmetric-matrix discussion.
3. Vishesh Jain, Ashwin Sah, Mehtaab Sawhney, *Singularity of discrete random matrices*, arXiv:2010.06554 / GAFA 31 (2021), Conjecture 1.1. Current arXiv/search records were checked. Their sharp full theorem applies when the finite-support law is not uniform on its support. For a law uniform on its support they prove only exponential-scale full singularity precision, while giving sharp control of the structured/compressible contribution. Their paper explicitly says the uniform-support resolution is the main question left open by their work.
4. The same Jain--Sah--Sawhney source explicitly discusses Irmatov's claimed Rademacher resolution and records unresolved issues, including Theorem 3, and says a slight modification of the claimed proof would appear to give impossibly good error terms. I therefore do not use Irmatov's claimed resolution as source closure.
5. Konstantin Tikhomirov, *Singularity of random Bernoulli matrices*, Annals of Mathematics 191 (2020), 593–634, current Annals page checked. It proves P(singular)=(1/2+o(1))^n, i.e. the exponential rate, not the sharp polynomial prefactor.
6. Julian Sahasrabudhe, *Probabilistic combinatorics at exponentially small scales*, arXiv:2512.15077, submitted 2025-12-17 and appearing in the ICM 2026 invited-lecture volume, current arXiv/search record checked. This survey remains a current reference for the exponentially-small-scale formulation and inverse Littlewood--Offord viewpoint.

The retained source specialization is

P(det B_n=0)=(1+o(1))*n*(n-1)*2^(1-n)=(1+o(1))*2*n*(n-1)*2^(-n).

## Later / current literature rechecked

7. Zach Hunter, Matthew Kwan, Lisa Sauermann, Mehtaab Sawhney, *On random matrices with large corank*, International Mathematics Research Notices 2026(12), rnag126, published June 2026. It proves P(rank(M)<=n-k)<=exp(-c n k) for iid uniform sign matrices. This strongly suppresses high corank but does not determine the sharp corank-one singularity prefactor.
8. Zeyan Song and Hanchao Wang, *Rank deficiency of Bernoulli random matrices for growing corank*, arXiv:2607.00495 (July 2026), surfaced in the bounded search. It sharpens growing-corank probabilities in its Bernoulli setting; it does not close the iid sign prefactor target.
9. Samuel Orellana Mateo, John Urschel, Nicholas West, *LU Factorization of Discrete Random Matrices*, arXiv:2608.08998 (August 2026), surfaced again. It concerns strong nonsingularity/LU factorization and exact binary counts, not a primary proof of the sharp iid sign singularity asymptotic.

Bounded fresh searches on 2026-09-17 for the exact iid Rademacher sharp singularity asymptotic, later Jain/Sah/Sawhney papers, 2026 citing work, and Irmatov follow-up did not surface a later accepted primary-source resolution.
