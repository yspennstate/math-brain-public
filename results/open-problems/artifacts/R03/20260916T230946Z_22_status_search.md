## Primary/source chain recovered

1. János Komlós, *On the determinant of (0,1) matrices* (1967): historical lineage.
2. Van H. Vu, *Recent progress in combinatorial random matrix theory*, arXiv:2005.02797 (2020), later Probability Surveys 18 (2021), Section 2 / Conjecture 2.2: modern iid Rademacher sharp-singularity formulation in the R03 lineage.
3. Vishesh Jain, Ashwin Sah, Mehtaab Sawhney, *Singularity of discrete random matrices*, arXiv:2010.06554 / GAFA 31 (2021), Conjecture 1.1. The abstract still states the sharp full result for finite-support laws not uniform on their support, while for uniform-support laws it states only exponential-scale precision for the full singularity probability plus sharp compressible analysis.
4. Julian Sahasrabudhe, *Probabilistic combinatorics at exponentially small scales*, arXiv:2512.15077v1 (17 Dec 2025), Section 4, Eq. (4.1). The current arXiv HTML explicitly states `P(det B_n=0)=(1+o(1)) 2 n^2 2^{-n}`, calls the corresponding upper bound an extremely challenging open problem, records Tikhomirov's `2^{-n+o(n)}` theorem, and says the iid `{-1,1}` prefactor remains perhaps the central open problem in the area. It then introduces the symmetric ensemble separately.
5. Konstantin Tikhomirov's Bernoulli singularity theorem remains exponential-scale only for this purpose.

The exact collision count used by R03 is `2 n(n-1) 2^{-n}` before lower-order overlaps, equivalently `n(n-1)2^{1-n}`. Retained C225 already handles the matching lower bound with overlaps.
