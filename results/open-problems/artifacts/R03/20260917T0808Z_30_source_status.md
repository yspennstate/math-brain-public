## Source identity and normalization

Primary modern source: Vishesh Jain, Ashwin Sah, Mehtaab Sawhney,
*Singularity of discrete random matrices*, arXiv:2010.06554 (GAFA 31, 2021).
The current arXiv abstract still distinguishes:
- non-uniform finite-support entry laws: sharp elementary-mechanism asymptotic;
- entry laws uniform on their support: only `(1+o(1))^n`-level precision for
  the full singularity probability, with a sharp compressible-vector analysis.

For the uniform sign law, the elementary source conjecture specializes to

    P(det B_n = 0) = (1+o(1)) n(n-1) 2^(1-n).

Repository intake locator: F0001.  The exact R03 metric remains

    r(n) = 2^n P(det B_n = 0, T_n^c),

where T_n is the exact equal/opposite row/column collision union.  Retained
R03 collision-overlap work reduces source closure to `r(n)=o(n^2)`.

Fresh bounded searches on 2026-09-17 found no later primary proof of this
sharp iid nonsymmetric Rademacher prefactor.  This is a bounded search, not a
worldwide novelty certificate.

## Current nearby literature checked

1. Zeyan Song, Hanchao Wang, *Rank deficiency of Bernoulli random matrices
   for growing corank*, arXiv:2607.00495 (2026): determines
   `P(corank >= k)` for `k=O(sqrt(log n))`; it does not supply the sharp
   corank-one Rademacher prefactor.

2. Samuel Orellana Mateo, John Urschel, Nicholas West,
   *LU Factorization of Discrete Random Matrices*, arXiv:2608.08998
   (10 Aug 2026): studies strong nonsingularity / LU and gives exact binary
   strongly-nonsingular counts through size 9.  It does not resolve the sharp
   ordinary singularity asymptotic.

3. Thomas Voigt, Guenter M. Ziegler,
   *Singular 0/1-Matrices, and the Hyperplanes Spanned by Random
   0/1-Vectors*, arXiv:math/0308050; CPC 15 (2006): relates binary
   singularity to the expected number of cube vertices on a random
   hyperplane.  Therefore the generic "translate singularity into cube
   hyperplane incidence" idea is prior art.  Run30's rank-switch / affine-basis
   filter must not be described as establishing novelty without a deeper
   citation audit.

4. Julian Sahasrabudhe, *Probabilistic combinatorics at exponentially small
   scales*, arXiv:2512.15077 (17 Dec 2025): current survey context for the
   exponentially-small-scale singularity problem and inverse
   Littlewood--Offord obstruction.
