## Original/current source identity

Canonical outside target: sharp asymptotics for the singularity probability of an iid Rademacher matrix. Repository intake locator: `F0001`, sourced through Van H. Vu, *Recent progress in combinatorial random matrix theory*, arXiv:2005.02797, Section 2, and the modern primary treatment:

- Vishesh Jain, Ashwin Sah, Mehtaab Sawhney, *Singularity of discrete random matrices*, arXiv:2010.06554 / GAFA 31 (2021).

For iid uniform signs the source-matched conjectural leading term is

`P(det B_n=0) = (1+o(1)) n(n-1) 2^(1-n)`.

Jain–Sah–Sawhney prove the sharp elementary-mechanism asymptotic for finite-support laws that are not uniform on their support. For laws uniform on their support, including iid Rademacher, their full singularity theorem gives only exponential-scale precision while Theorem 1.5 sharply handles the low-entropy / almost-constant contribution. Their introduction explicitly says the uniform-support case is the main question left open by their work. It also records unresolved issues in A. A. Irmatov's 2020 claimed Rademacher resolution, including Theorem 3, and notes that a slight modification of that proof appears to give impossibly good error terms.

Fresh primary-source check used: https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.06554 .

## New directly relevant prior art for the run31 formulation

### Irmatov 1996: random cube spans / threshold functions

A. A. Irmatov, *Estimates for the number of threshold functions*, Diskr. Mat. 8:4 (1996), 92–107 / Discrete Math. Appl. 6:6, 569–583, DOI 10.4213/dm550.

The published abstract states that asymptotic equality in the Schlaefli threshold-function upper bound is equivalent to the proportion tending to one of collections of `n-1` different sign vectors whose linear span meets the sign cube only in the signed generators. This is extremely close in shape to run31's normalized affine-section variable. It is not itself the sharp singularity theorem, and a 0/1 typicality statement does not by itself control the mean excess required by run31 because rare cube sections can be exponentially large.

Primary page checked: https://www.mathnet.ru/eng/dm550 .

### Ferber–Jain–Luh–Samotij counting inverse Littlewood–Offord

Asaf Ferber, Vishesh Jain, Kyle Luh, Wojciech Samotij, *On the counting problem in inverse Littlewood–Offord theory*, arXiv:1904.10425.

Their main counting theorem is advertised as effective down to concentration `exp(-c sqrt(n log n))`. Run31's source-scale affine-section concentration is of order `poly(n) 2^(-n)`, much smaller. Therefore their theorem is highly relevant machinery but cannot be inserted as a black box at the final scale.

Primary page checked: https://arxiv.org/abs/1904.10425 .

## Current route conclusion

The most useful new language is to quotient the rowwise sign gauge in the Schur representation. If `R` is a uniformly random invertible `d x d` sign matrix, `d=n-1`, set

`w_R = R^{-1} 1`,

`C_R = {x in {+/-1}^d : <x,w_R>=1}`,

`M_R = |C_R|`.

The `d` rows of `R` always lie in `C_R`. Row-collision deletion is exactly the subtraction `M_R-d`, while a trivial collision of the distinguished deterministic column with a random column is exactly `w_R=+/-e_j`. Run31 proves that, after the retained JSS structured-sector and higher-corank removals, the source conjecture is equivalent to the basis-weighted mean-excess condition

`E[(M_R-d) 1_{w_R notin {+/-e_j}}] = o(n^2)`

(up to deleting a JSS-structured contribution already proved negligible in the retained R03 chain).

This puts the remaining problem in the geometry/combinatorics of random affine hyperplane sections of the sign cube. Irmatov 1996 is the nearest directly shaped older source found this run, while FJLS 2019 explains why standard counting inverse-LO technology does not yet reach the needed very-small-concentration regime.
