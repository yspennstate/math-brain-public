## Current primary literature checked

- V. Jain, A. Sah, M. Sawhney, *Singularity of discrete random matrices*, arXiv:2010.06554 / GAFA 2021. Their sharp elementary-mechanism theorem covers finite-support laws that are not uniform on their support. For entry laws uniform on their support, including iid Rademacher, the paper obtains exponential-scale precision for total singularity and a sharp compressible-sector analysis, not the sharp polynomial prefactor.
- Z. Song, H. Wang, *Rank deficiency of Bernoulli random matrices for growing corank*, arXiv:2607.00495 (2026). This determines a growing-corank Bernoulli regime and does not supply the missing iid-sign corank-one prefactor.
- Z. Hunter, M. Kwan, L. Sauermann, M. Sawhney, *On random matrices with large corank*, International Mathematics Research Notices 2026, rnag126, published 18 June 2026. This proves `P(corank >= k) <= exp(-c n k)` for iid sign matrices; it does not determine the sharp `k=1` prefactor.
- T. Voigt, G. M. Ziegler, *Singular 0/1-matrices, and the hyperplanes spanned by random 0/1-vectors*, Combinatorics, Probability and Computing 15 (2006), 463–471, arXiv:math/0308050. Their expected-hypercube-section formulation is prior art for the qualitative/asymptotic hyperplane equivalence. Run37 therefore makes no novelty claim for the general cube-hyperplane viewpoint.
