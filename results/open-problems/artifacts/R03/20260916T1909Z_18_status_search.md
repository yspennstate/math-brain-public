1. `arXiv 2010.06554 Jain Sah Sawhney Singularity of discrete random matrices uniform support Rademacher Conjecture 1.1`.
   - Primary arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.06554 .
   - Authors: Vishesh Jain, Ashwin Sah, Mehtaab Sawhney.
   - Conjecture 1.1 gives the general discrete target.  For uniform Rademacher, the zero-row term vanishes and the two signed pair terms give `2 n(n-1) 2^{-n}`.  The paper proves the sharp full asymptotic for finite-support laws not uniform on support; uniform-support full singularity remains only exponential-scale, although the compressible/structured contribution is treated sharply.

   - Kenneth Maples, arXiv:1012.2372.  Theorem 1.2 gives finite-field singularity universality with `O(exp(-c alpha n))` error for alpha-dense entries, with absolute constants. This is relevant negative prior art for the growing-modulus route.
