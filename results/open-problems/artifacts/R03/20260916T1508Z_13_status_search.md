Checked on 2026-09-16:

1. Van H. Vu, *Recent progress in combinatorial random matrix theory*, arXiv:2005.02797, Section 2, Conjecture 2.2: iid Rademacher sharp singularity target; the symmetric model is separate in Section 3.
2. Vishesh Jain, Ashwin Sah, Mehtaab Sawhney, *Singularity of discrete random matrices*, arXiv:2010.06554 / GAFA 31 (2021), Conjecture 1.1 and Theorem 1.5. The sharp theorem for the full singularity probability assumes the entry law is not uniform on its support. For uniform support, including Rademacher, the paper gives exponential-scale precision and a sharp structured/almost-constant theorem, but leaves the unstructured contribution as the residual obstruction.
