Vishesh Jain, Ashwin Sah, Mehtaab Sawhney, *Singularity of discrete random matrices*, arXiv:2010.06554 / GAFA 31 (2021): Conjecture 1.1 is the elementary-mechanism conjecture for iid discrete matrices. The paper proves the sharp full asymptotic for distributions not uniform on their support; the uniform-support case, including Rademacher, is left with exponential-scale precision. ## Important prior-art correction

Richard Arratia and Stephen DeSalvo, *On the singularity of random Bernoulli matrices - novel integer partitions and lower bound expansions*, arXiv:1105.2834 (2011), already classify the only novel partitions with at most six parts as

`11, 1111, 21111, 111111, 221111, 311111, 322111`

and state that there are fourteen novel partitions with seven parts. Therefore the core support-pattern classifications independently rediscovered in R03-C226--C236 are not novel mathematical classifications. Their exact checkers remain useful independent verification, and R03's explicit direction counts/target-scale bookkeeping may be additional, but the partition-type classification should be labelled KNOWN/REDISCOVERED in future reports. Arratia--DeSalvo also study interactions of left and right null vectors, so the earlier R03 observation that naive left/right independence fails is not a novel research direction.
