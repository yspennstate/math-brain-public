## JSS proof bottleneck located

The full text of Jain–Sah–Sawhney was inspected around Theorem 2.1, Lemma 2.10, Proposition 3.7 and Theorem 3.8. Theorem 3.8 has the unstructured error `exp((epsilon-H(p)) n)` for each fixed `epsilon>0`; for Rademacher `H(p)=log 2`, so target-scale multiplication by `2^n` leaves `exp(epsilon n)`.

Lemma 2.10 explicitly chooses `lambda=lambda(xi,epsilon)`, then `gamma=gamma(xi,epsilon)<lambda^2`, and in equation (2.7) pays the factor `(e/lambda)^(2 k lambda ell)`. Its statement has `ell in [(1-gamma)n,n]`. Thus the published theorem is not uniform in a sequence `epsilon=epsilon_n`; direct substitution of `epsilon_n=O(log n/n)` is a quantifier error. The current proof architecture also drives the guaranteed robust-step fraction to zero when one forces the entropy slack to polynomial scale. This is recorded precisely in the run report as a killed naive route, not as a refutation of a sharpened JSS theorem.
