## Source match

The assigned outside target remains intake `F0001`, Van H. Vu, *Recent progress in combinatorial random matrix theory*, arXiv:2005.02797 / Probability Surveys 18 (2021), Conjecture 2.2. The intake statement is

\[
p_n=(2+o(1))n^2 2^{-n}
\]

for an iid uniform Rademacher `n x n` matrix. This is asymptotically equivalent to the campaign normalization

\[
\Pr(\det B_n=0)=(1+o(1))n(n-1)2^{1-n},
\]

because `n(n-1)2^{1-n}=(2+o(1))n^2 2^{-n}`. The latter is retained only after this source match.

Let `T_n` be the exact union of equal/opposite signed row-pair and column-pair events. Retained C225 pays the overlaps and the frozen R03 remainder remains

\[
r(n)=2^n\Pr(\det B_n=0,T_n^c),
\]

with source closure equivalent to `r(n)=o(n^2)`.

## Current primary literature checked

1. Vishesh Jain, Ashwin Sah, Mehtaab Sawhney, *Singularity of discrete random matrices*, arXiv:2010.06554 / GAFA 31 (2021). Their sharp elementary-mechanism asymptotic is for finite-support laws that are **not uniform on their support**. For laws uniform on their support they prove only exponential-scale full singularity precision, together with sharp analysis of the compressible sector. Uniform Rademacher is therefore still the excluded sharp-prefactor case.
2. Zeyan Song and Hanchao Wang, *Rank deficiency of Bernoulli random matrices for growing corank*, arXiv:2607.00495 (2026). They determine `P(corank >= k)` for Bernoulli(p), `k=O(sqrt(log n))`, at `(1-p+o(1))^(kn)` scale. This does not supply the missing Rademacher corank-one polynomial prefactor.
3. Zach Hunter, Matthew Kwan, Lisa Sauermann, Mehtaab Sawhney, *On random matrices with large corank*, arXiv:2510.12933 (2025; later 2026 publication status referenced in the campaign). They prove `P(corank >= k) <= exp(-c n k)` for iid Rademacher matrices. Again this suppresses higher corank, not the sharp corank-one prefactor.
4. A bounded 2026-09-17 search for later Rademacher-singularity sharp-prefactor papers did not locate a primary-source proof of Vu/JSS's uniform-Rademacher target. This is dated search evidence, not a worldwide novelty certificate.
