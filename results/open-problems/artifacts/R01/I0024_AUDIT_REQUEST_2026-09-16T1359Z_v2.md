## Cleaner candidate theorem to audit

Let `P` be a finite irreducible reversible discrete-time chain, `pi` its stationary law, and orient each non-loop edge once. Put

`c_{xy}=pi_x P(x,y)=pi_y P(y,x)`, `C=diag(c_e)`, `Pi=diag(pi)`, and let `B` be the vertex-edge incidence matrix. Then

`L=BCB^T=Pi(I-P)`.

Let

`H0={b in R^V : 1^T b=0}`.

Give demands the norm

`||b||_{Pi^{-1}}^2=b^T Pi^{-1} b`

and flows the energy norm

`||theta||_{C^{-1}}^2=theta^T C^{-1} theta`.

Let `R` range over **linear routing operators** `R:H0->R^E` satisfying

`B R b=b` for every `b in H0`.

Define

`||R||^2 = sup_{0 != b in H0} ||Rb||_{C^{-1}}^2 / ||b||_{Pi^{-1}}^2`.

The candidate exact answer is

`tau_2 = inf_{BR=Id_H0} ||R||^2`,

with the infimum attained by

`R_* = C B^T L^+`.

### Proof checkpoint 1: lower bound

For any zero-sum demand `b`, Thomson's principle gives

`min_{B theta=b} theta^T C^{-1} theta = b^T L^+ b`.

Therefore every right inverse `R` satisfies

`||R||^2 >= sup_{b !=0, 1^Tb=0} b^T L^+ b / (b^T Pi^{-1}b)`.

Writing `b=Pi h` converts the last supremum to Aldous–Fill's relaxation-time Rayleigh quotient, hence exactly `tau_2`.

### Proof checkpoint 2: attainment

`R_*=CB^T L^+` is a right inverse on `H0` because `L L^+ b=b`. For each demand it is the Thomson minimum-energy flow, so its operator norm squared is the preceding supremum, namely `tau_2`.

## Exact equivalence to the prior all-pairs formulation

The preceding R01 report used antisymmetric unit pair flows `phi^{xy}` and

`Theta_Phi(h)=1/2 sum_xy pi_x pi_y (h_x-h_y) phi^{xy}`.

Given such a family, put

`psi^x=sum_y pi_y phi^{xy}`

and

`R_Phi b=sum_x b_x psi^x`.

For every zero-sum `b`, `B R_Phi b=b`; if `b=Pi h`, then `Theta_Phi(h)=R_Phi b`.

Conversely, any right inverse `R` gives pair flows

`phi_R^{xy}=R(e_x-e_y)`.

Thus the all-pairs theorem and the right-inverse theorem are exactly the same infimum, not two different candidate notions.
