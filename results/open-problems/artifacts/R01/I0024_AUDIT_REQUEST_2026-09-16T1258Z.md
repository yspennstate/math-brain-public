## Candidate source-resolution theorem

Let `P` be a finite irreducible reversible discrete-time Markov chain with stationary law `pi`. For every undirected edge `e={x,y}` with `P(x,y)>0`, set conductance `c_e=pi_x P(x,y)=pi_y P(y,x)` and orient the edge once. Let `B` be the incidence matrix and `C=diag(c_e)`.

An admissible all-pairs flow family is an antisymmetric family `(phi^{xy})` such that `B phi^{xy}=e_x-e_y` and `phi^{yx}=-phi^{xy}`. For `h` with `sum_x pi_x h_x=0`, define

`Theta_phi(h) = (1/2) sum_{x,y} pi_x pi_y (h_x-h_y) phi^{xy}`.

Define

`Cost(phi) = sup_{h != 0, pi(h)=0} [ Theta_phi(h)^T C^{-1} Theta_phi(h) / sum_x pi_x h_x^2 ]`.

Claim:

`tau_2 = inf_phi Cost(phi)`

with Aldous–Fill's discrete-time normalization `tau_2=(1-lambda_2)^{-1}`. Equality is attained by the electrical pair flows

`phi_*^{xy}=C B^T L^+ (e_x-e_y)`, where `L=B C B^T=Pi(I-P)`.

The continuous-time analogue is identical with `c_{xy}=pi_x q_{xy}` and `tau_2=lambda_2^{-1}`.

## Proof points to audit

1. For every admissible family, `B Theta_phi(h)=Pi h`.
2. Thomson principle: `min_{B theta=b} theta^T C^{-1}theta = b^T L^+ b` for zero-sum `b`.
3. For `b=Pi h`, the supremum of the Thomson minimum divided by `||h||_pi^2` equals `(1-lambda_2)^{-1}` by conjugating `(I-P)` with `Pi^{1/2}`.
4. Electrical pair flows make `Theta_phi(h)=C B^T L^+ Pi h`, the pointwise Thomson minimizer, so the lower bound is attained simultaneously for every `h`.
5. Check that the formulation genuinely matches the source domain and normalization, and assess whether it is already explicit in prior literature. The underlying right-inverse-of-divergence principle is standard and appears abstractly in Babuška–Aziz theory; novelty is NOT claimed.

## First-run exact counterexamples already certified

- Aldous–Fill Corollary 4.33 randomized path congestion is not exact: simple random walk on `P4` has `tau_2=2`, while the infimum of that path-congestion expression is `5/2` (ratio `5/4`).
- The naive stationary average of pairwise minimum Thomson energies is not exact: simple random walk on `K3` has value `4/3` and `tau_2=2/3` (ratio `2`).
