# Notational correction to the relation-restricted gate note

In the proof of R19-LR2, after independent coordinate sign changes are used to normalize the harmful relation, every occurrence of the finite-checking product for that signed representative is to be read as

`|u_1 ... u_14| = |u_1| ... |u_14|`.

Equivalently, retain the original positive speed tuple for the product bound and use the sign-changed tuple only for the relation equation. Coordinate sign changes do not affect the LR property, divisibility of the absolute product by a prime, primitivity, or the finite-checking upper bound. Thus the two inequalities in the final contradiction are

`|u_1...u_14| >= product_{p in P_a} p >= B_14`

and

`|u_1...u_14| < B_14`.

This is a notation correction only; it does not alter R19-LR1, the relation-shape count, or the quantifier-swapped criterion.
