Proposed model C069. Put the N labels at `0,...,N-1` in `Z/NZ`. Freeze a permutation `rho_N` of ranks `1,...,N`. For every threshold `u=1,...,N-1` and cyclic shift `t`, a current clade has an independent Poisson proposal clock of rate

`c_{u,t}=1/[2 u (N-u)]`.

On that proposal, label `ell` is red iff `rho_N(t+ell mod N) <= u`; split by red/blue when nontrivial. Descendants use fresh clocks but the same frozen ranking.

For a specified unordered visible split `A|B`, with `|A|=i, |B|=j`, write `Q_rho(A|B)` for its conditional split rate. If `rho` is uniform over `S_N`, then for each shift the ranks at the visible positions are a sample without replacement, and Vandermonde gives

`E Q_rho(A|B) = B(i,j)` **exactly**.

If two rankings differ by a transposition, only thresholds between the two exchanged ranks are affected. At each such threshold at most `2(i+j)` shifts can change, so

`|Delta Q| <= 2(i+j) H_{N-1}/N`.

The reveal martingale for a uniform permutation should therefore give

`P(|Q_rho-B(i,j)|>eps) <= 2 exp(- eps^2 N / [8(i+j)^2 H_{N-1}^2])`

(up to an inessential constant depending on the exact martingale coupling). A union bound over all disjoint nonempty `A,B subset [K_N]` with `K_N=floor(N^(1/8)/log N)` and target `eps_N=N^(-1/8)` tends to zero. Hence deterministic rankings exist with all split tests on `[K_N]` within `eps_N` of CTCS. Take the lexicographically first such ranking for large N and define finitely many small N separately.

This directly refutes the proposed necessity of growing exact OA strength: for threshold `u=1`, every translated mask has exactly one red ambient label, so the mask orbit is not even exact strength 2, yet the weighted split generator converges on every fixed restriction.

Finite distinction candidate: define `rho_4=(1,2,3,4)`. Exact calculation predicts singleton split rates all `1/3`, balanced split-rate multiset `{0,1/4,1/4}`, whereas CTCS(4) has balanced multiset `{1/6,1/6,1/6}`. A positive time rescaling cannot remove a zero and relabeling only permutes the multiset. This should give a clean frozen-convention witness distinct from the source and C068.
