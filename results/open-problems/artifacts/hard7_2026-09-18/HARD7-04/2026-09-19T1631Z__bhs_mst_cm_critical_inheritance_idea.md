## Negation attempt and why it fails

Let `G` be a finite connected graph with distinct edge weights, let `T` be its MST, and for a threshold `p` let `G_{<=p}` contain exactly the edges of weight at most `p`. A natural obstruction to importing critical-window geometry into the final MST would be:

> a component `C` of `G_{<=p}` has large graph diameter, but after edges of weight `>p` appear the final MST leaves `C`, uses a later shortcut, and re-enters `C`, destroying the lower bound.

That obstruction is impossible. If `x,y in C` and the unique `T`-path from `x` to `y` contained an edge `e` with weight `>p`, deleting `e` would split `T` into two sides separating `x,y`. Since `x,y` are connected in `G_{<=p}`, an `x-y` path in `G_{<=p}` crosses this cut in some edge `f` with weight `<=p<w(e)`. Replacing `e` by `f` gives a strictly lighter spanning tree, contradicting minimality. Thus every `T`-path between vertices of `C` stays entirely in `C` and uses only edges of weight `<=p`.

Consequently

`T[C] is connected`,

and for all `x,y in C`,

`d_T(x,y) >= d_{G_{<=p}}(x,y)`.

Hence

`diam(T) >= diam(C)`

for **every** threshold component `C`, simultaneously for every threshold `p`.

This is a deterministic filtration-inheritance lemma. The mechanism is a standard cut/Kruskal principle and should be treated as folklore unless a novelty search shows otherwise. The useful point here is its source-specific combination with the already-proved heavy-tailed critical configuration-model metric limit.

## Source-specific consequence to prove on the page

For a supercritical heavy-tailed configuration model with exponent `tau in (3,4)`, put

`eta=(tau-3)/(tau-1)`.

At the critical percolation threshold

`p_c(lambda)=1/nu_n + lambda n^{-eta}+o(n^{-eta})`,

Bhamidi--Dhara--van der Hofstad--Sen prove GHP convergence of each fixed ordered critical percolation cluster under their heavy-tailed assumptions, and in particular convergence of its diameter on the `n^eta` scale. For the retained subclass `min_i d_i>=3`, the underlying configuration model is connected whp (the standard configuration-model connectivity theorem, as summarized by Federico--van der Hofstad and traced there to Luczak). On that whp event the final giant-component MST is the full MST, so the deterministic lemma transfers the critical diameter lower scale into the MST.

The quantitative target for this run is therefore to move the frozen lower-exponent certificate from the trivial `beta_MST^- >= 0` to

`beta_MST^- >= eta=(tau-3)/(tau-1)`

for the retained minimum-degree-three subclass.

This does **not** prove the source's full GH/GHP scaling limit, does not prove an upper bound of order `n^eta`, and does not cover arbitrary low-degree mass. The complement/tail control emphasized by Bhamidi--Sen remains the hard direction.
