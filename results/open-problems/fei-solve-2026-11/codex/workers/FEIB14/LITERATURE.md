# Literature checked for FEIB14

CHECKED: the primary sources below were opened on 10 November 2026. The scope is the named theorem passages, their hypotheses and relevant definitions. This is not a claim to have refereed the complete papers or exhausted later literature. Web discovery was especially useful here because the older local reports predate or omit a recent threshold result. Reported new proofs are in PROOFS.md; inherited conclusions are identified there.

PROVED in the cited paper: [O'Donnell–Wright–Zhou, The Fourier Entropy–Influence Conjecture for certain classes](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~odonnell/papers/fei.pdf), Theorems 1 and 3, proves symmetric FEI with coefficient 12.04 and read-once decision-tree FEI with 4.88. The latter means globally read-once. The supplied local v4 manuscript states 4.657435. Its later v4.1 counterpart ../FSYM47/repository/paper/main.tex and ../../inputs/fsym44_cert/ledger_certificate.json state 4.650431; inherited_audit.json replays the exact finite ledger. FEIB02 uses the later H benchmark. Our count and path statistics are sufficient, so these H constants transfer unchanged to their erasure cost.

PROVED in the cited paper: [Chakraborty–Kulkarni–Lokam–Saurabh, Upper Bounds on Fourier Entropy](https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2013/052/revision/2/download/), Theorem 5.11 and Remark 5.1, gives H<=10I for AND/OR/XOR/NOT read-once formulas. Its complexity bounds include H<=2 E[tree depth]<=2 log(number of leaves). Its LTF entropy bound is O(sqrt(n)); this alone is not H=O(I) for every LTF. The author list here contains Kulkarni, not Kundu. This should not be conflated with the separate random-LTF paper.

PROVED in the cited paper: [Chakraborty–Karmalkar–Kundu–Lokam–Saurabh, Random Linear Threshold Functions](https://arxiv.org/abs/1903.11635), appearing at LATIN 2018, treats random weights drawn from the normal distribution or uniformly from [-1,1]. It is a random-LTF result, rather than a bound for every LTF or arbitrary DNF. Our small random samples are checks of witnesses, not a reproduction of its asymptotic probability theorem.

PROVED in the cited paper: [O'Donnell–Tan, A composition theorem](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1304.1347), Theorems 1–2 and Lemma 4.1, establishes product-biased FEI+ composition and a bound for arbitrary gates of bounded arity. Intermediate means matter. Their coefficient lemma supplies the conservative explicit bounded-arity coefficient derived in PROOFS.md. Uniform FEI for an outer gate alone is not the hypothesis of the composition theorem.

PROVED in the cited paper: [Wan–Wright–Wu, Decision Trees, Protocols, and FEI](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~jswright/papers/readk.pdf), Theorem 2.8 gives H<=[2+(2k+2)log_2 3]I<=9kI for read-k trees. Lemmas 1.5 and 1.7 convert an almost-prefix-free protocol of length L over the ternary alphabet into H<=L log_2 3+2I, with L<=4I+2Cov(T). The separate expected-depth statement H<=12 E[depth] I assumes I>=1. It is not an unrestricted depth-d statement without that qualification.

PROVED in the cited paper: [Shalev, On the FEI Conjecture for Extremal Classes](https://arxiv.org/html/1806.03646), Theorem 31 bounds Cov(T)<=2sqrt(k) I. Combining with the preceding protocol conversion gives the explicit coefficient 2+(4+4sqrt(k))log_2 3. Its DNF result, Corollary 30, is FMEI for regular read-k DNFs, not Shannon FEI for all width-w DNFs. Its constant-Fourier-L1 theorem also does not cover that unrestricted class. These distinctions prevent importing a stronger DNF claim than the source proves.

PROVED in a recent cited preprint, not independently re-proved here: [the author, Fourier entropy of threshold functions and their compositions](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.16069v1), Theorem A gives H<=C_reg I for all LTFs, C_reg=log_2(4+e)+2log_2(e pi)<8.937. Theorem D and Corollary 7.3 give H<=744I for arbitrary-fan-in symmetric-threshold/parity read-once formulas. This includes the full AND/OR/XOR/MAJ gate set. The paper's product-measure threshold estimate is the substantive extra ingredient. These transfers are cited results, not a new proof by our cheap witness search. The local source ~/papers/fourier-entropy-threshold-functions/paper/main.tex was also read to confirm the constants and composition normalization. Its DNF/union estimate depends on read number; it does not supply the requested unrestricted width-only coefficient.

OPEN in this report: a verified general width-only Shannon/erasure coefficient, all-monotone FEI, and a uniform upper bound for the optimized parity-plus-block or subset-count families. No novelty or exhaustive bibliography claim is made for the elementary degree bound; its role is a direct explicit witness proof, compared with FEIB12's stated coefficient. The remembered monotone-width down-set paper remains a provisional lead because its proof was not audited here.
