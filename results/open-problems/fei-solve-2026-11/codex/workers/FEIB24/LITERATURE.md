# Sources opened for FEIB24

Read on 10 Nov 2026 through the web tool; the GitHub pull-request file list was also opened through the connected GitHub read tool. Each entry states one narrow result. These are literature statements, not claims that their numerical certificates were independently replayed here.

- O'Donnell, Wright and Zhou, *The Fourier Entropy–Influence Conjecture for certain classes of Boolean functions*: proves FEI for symmetric functions and bounded-part symmetric functions, and notes that KKL implies the min-entropy version for monotone functions. Opened author PDF, abstract and page 1: https://yuanz.web.illinois.edu/papers/fei-icalp.pdf .
- Hod, *Improved Lower Bounds for the Fourier Entropy/Influence Conjecture via Lexicographic Functions*: constructs monotone functions giving a universal-constant lower bound above 6.454784. Opened abstract: https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.00762 .
- Numaro, the published eighteen-variable certificate and its merged Optimization Problems update: gives a balanced monotone seed with I=261/128 and amplified H/(I-1)>6.521845710923046575, explicitly including the monotone class. Opened research page, certificate/checker and merged PR file diff: https://numaro.tech/research/fourier-entropy-influence-2026/ ; https://numaro.tech/assets/research/fei/check_c71_n18.py ; https://github.com/teorth/optimizationproblems/pull/130/files . No local replay of this published certificate was performed; the local n18_balanced file used in structured.json is a different, earlier worker's seed and is identified by its own hash.
- Arunachalam, Chakraborty, Koucký, Saurabh and de Wolf, *Improved bounds on Fourier entropy and Min-entropy*: proves FMEI for constant-read DNFs, and bounds Shannon Fourier entropy by average unambiguous parity certificate complexity. Opened abstract: https://arxiv.org/abs/1809.09819 .
- Shalev, *On the Fourier Entropy Influence Conjecture for Extremal Classes*: proves FMEI for regular read-k DNFs and proposes a route toward Shannon FEI for read-k DNFs. Opened abstract: https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.03646 .
- González, MacManus and Pereyra, *Further evidence towards the Fourier Entropy-Influence conjecture*: proves FEI for selected monotone functions with the tribe separation property using stopping binary trees. Opened 2026 abstract: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.00246 .
- Kelman, Kindler, Lifshitz, Minzer and Safra, *Towards a Proof of the Fourier–Entropy Conjecture?*: obtains concentration on 2^{O(I log I)} characters at constant variance, retaining a logarithmic loss. Opened abstract: https://arxiv.org/abs/1911.10579 .
- Han, *A new bound for the Fourier-Entropy-Influence conjecture*: bounds H by O(I + sum_i Inf_i log(1/Inf_i)), retaining a potentially unbounded small-influence loss. Opened revised abstract: https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.08271 .

General monotone Shannon FEI and unrestricted overlapping-DNF Shannon FEI are still unresolved in the opened sources. The min-entropy statements do not settle them. This is a bounded literature search, not a proof of the absence of all later results.

The brief's classification of the 6.5218 construction as nonmonotone is contradicted by the published certificate's own statement and the merged primary repository diff. Its number is an amplified lower bound, not the seed's raw H/I and not its minimum coordinate erasure ratio. The weaker assertion that some monotone functions have raw H/I>4 is also independently demonstrated here by the finite functions in profiles.json and structured.json.
