# Opened primary sources

The source statements below are deliberately narrow. They were opened through the web tool on 10 November 2026. No exhaustive novelty or later-literature claim follows from this search.

- O'Donnell, Wright and Zhou, *The Fourier Entropy–Influence Conjecture for certain classes of Boolean functions*, Theorem 1 and Theorems 4–5: FEI holds for symmetric Boolean functions with constant 12.04. Their proof bounds the entropy of the spectral level by 3I and the within-level entropy by 9.04I. [Author PDF](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~odonnell/papers/fei.pdf).
- Transitive functions: the opened sources establish special cases, including symmetric functions and bounded-arity read-once compositions; none of the opened sources gives a theorem covering all transitive Boolean functions. O'Donnell–Tan also describes the symmetry motivation of FEI. This is a statement about the checked sources, not a claim of an exhaustive current-status survey. [O'Donnell–Tan, introduction](https://arxiv.org/html/1304.1347).
- O'Donnell and Tan, *A composition theorem for the Fourier Entropy-Influence conjecture*, Proposition 3.2, Lemma 4.1, Theorem 2, Corollary 4.2 and Theorem 3: biased Fourier composition preserves their strengthened entropy–influence bound and proves FEI for read-once formulas over any fixed bounded-arity gate basis. Section 5 gives a balanced six-variable base with amplified ratio at least 6.278944; the HTML formula preserves negation bars missing from PDF text extraction. [Full paper](https://arxiv.org/html/1304.1347), [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1304.1347).
- Chakraborty, Kulkarni, Lokam and Saurabh, *Upper Bounds on Fourier Entropy*, Theorem 5.11 and Remark 5.1: read-once AND/OR/XOR/NOT formulas satisfy H<=10I, with a stronger bias-dependent additive term in the theorem. [ECCC revision 2](https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2013/052/revision/2/download/).
- Weissman and Verdú, *Erasure Entropy*, ISIT 2006, Definition 1: the established term is the sum of single-variable entropies conditional on all the other variables. Verdú–Weissman further develops it in *The Information Lost in Erasures* (2008). A search for its earlier application specifically to Boolean Fourier spectral samples did not locate one in the passages opened; this is not a novelty claim. [2006 author PDF](https://web.stanford.edu/~tsachy/pdf_files/Erasure%20Entropy.pdf), [2008 author PDF](https://web.stanford.edu/~tsachy/pdf_files/The%20Information%20Lost%20in%20Erasures.pdf).
- The Numaro certificate page explicitly describes its 18-variable seed as balanced and monotone, and gives its amplified lower bound as 6.521845710923046575. This page alone does not assert coordinate transitivity; our supplied-helper lift has two distinct influences in pm_audit.json. The brief's identification of the nine-variable host with the balanced record seed is therefore not used. [Published research page](https://numaro.tech/research/fourier-entropy-influence-2026/).

The recurrence in PROOFS.md uses ordinary Shannon entropy of the full squared Fourier law. O'Donnell–Tan's biased adjusted entropy is a different expression; its variance factors and empty atom are explicitly restored in section 6 before invoking their composition theorem. The web PDF text contains some extraction errors, including omitted negation bars; the HTML version was used for the six-variable base.

Reconstruction audit: the literal six-variable DNF rendered in the opened O'Donnell–Tan HTML gives mean 1/8 and total influence 27/16 in `python3 odonnell_tan.py`, whereas its following sentence specifies a balanced base with influence 13/8. This discrepancy is already noted by Rani Hod, section 1.2 of [Improved Lower Bounds via Lexicographic Functions](https://arxiv.org/html/1711.00762): he says the displayed original is biased and supplies a balanced six-variable replacement with the same stated entropy and influence. `hod_reconstruct.py` replays that replacement and his nearby improved base. Their influences are unequal, so neither is treated as transitive. `ot_tower_dp.json` computes the minimum over all leaf-path types rather than a purported common G. The failed literal searches are retained as diagnostics; they are not evidence against the amplification theorem.
