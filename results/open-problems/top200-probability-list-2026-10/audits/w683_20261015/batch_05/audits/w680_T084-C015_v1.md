I read every line. The law is the unrooted forest law on a simple complete graph with one common finite positive t. Put N=n-1 and a=1/t. Deleting the distinguished vertex leaves unattached unrooted trees and attached rooted trees, each attached by exactly one edge; two attachments would create a cycle. The formal labelled exponential formula gives N!t^N[w^N]exp(aU+zT), with the weights stated in the proof. From T=w expT and U'=T/w, U=T-T^2/2 follows with equal constant terms. The residue substitution w=u exp(-u) is a valid formal inverse substitution and yields the factor(1-u) and exponential((N+a+z)u-au^2/2); no analytic convergence or limit is used.

The generating-function derivative gives P_k=bP_(k-1)-a(k-1)P_(k-2). Hence H(z)=t^N[P_N-NP_(N-1)] at b=N+a+z, with the correct factorial cancellation. I independently enumerated every labelled unrooted forest of K1..K6 by edge decisions and union-find cycle rejection. Counts are1,2,7,38,291,2932. Every coefficient of the full bivariate edge-count/vertex-degree polynomial agrees with this Hermite formula. Receipt checks/t084_kn_counts_own.json; no solver code imported.

The determinant recurrence for the Jacobi matrix has off-diagonal squares a,2a,...,(N-1)a and therefore equals P_N. Changing the last diagonal entry by N subtracts exactly N P_(N-1), since its cofactor is the smaller Jacobi determinant. The matrix is real symmetric, so all roots of H are real. The nonnegative coefficients and H(0)>=1 exclude every root z>=0, giving strictly negative roots. The leading coefficient t^N is the single star forest. For N>=2, a>0 makes the matrix non-scalar; a symmetric matrix whose eigenvalues all coincide would be scalar. Thus the root parameters are not all equal. No eigenvalue simplicity assumption is needed.

Logarithmic differentiation at1 gives E[Y(Y-1)]=(EY)^2-sum p_j^2, with p_j=1/(1+lambda_j). Strict Cauchy-Schwarz then gives E[Y(Y-1)]<(N-1)(EY)^2/N. Exchangeability of the N incident edges gives EY=N rho and the ordered-pair moment=N(N-1)eta. Dividing by the positive N(N-1) yields eta<rho^2. Edge-indicator independence is never assumed. Finite t>0 is essential to the positive off-diagonal argument; unequal weights are not claimed.

## Prior work and scope

Gupta, arXiv2609.26134v1, Theorem1.2 (PDFp2, followed by the strictness statement), already proves the complete-graph uniform-forest case t=1. That slice is known.
