Read Sections 8.1 to 8.3 in full. No new row.
- 8.3 (8), the scaling limit and critical exponents of critical oriented percolation on Z^{1+1}: already T099.
- 8.1, voter-model perturbations in d = 1, 2 (the Cox-Durrett-Perkins program below d = 3): the Lotka-Volterra coexistence row already carries the nearest famous instance.
- 8.3 (4), Tracy-Widom GUE fluctuations of -log K_{0,t}(0,[tx,tx+1]) for Howitt-Warren flows: proved for one specific sticky interaction by Barraquand and Rychnovsky (arXiv:1905.10280, large deviation regime); the moderate deviation regime gives the stochastic heat equation for every flow with finite nonzero characteristic measure (Das, Drillick and Parekh, arXiv:2304.14279). - Conjectures 8.1-8.3 (Brownian net bubbles, potential MRCAs) and 8.3 (1)-(3), (5)-(7): specialist questions, not top-200.

The list has no bootstrap-percolation row. One arXiv search (bootstrap percolation with open problems, newest first)

Their open questions: Question 1, whether the second-order constant in the infection-time expansion of modified

## 15 Nov 2026 (an agent): polluted bootstrap percolation

Read: Gravner-Holroyd-Sivakoff, Polluted bootstrap percolation in three dimensions (arXiv:1706.07338), Theorems 1-3 and
Section 11 "Open Problems"; the abstract and introduction of Gravner-Holroyd-Lee-Sivakoff, Polluted modified bootstrap
percolation (arXiv:2503.15746v1, 2025). Searched arXiv for "polluted bootstrap percolation" (eight results).

What the sources state. On Z^3 with threshold 3 the modified model has final density tending to 1 when
q = o(p^3 (log 1/p)^-3) and to 0 when q > C p^3; for the standard model the 0 side is proved only for q > C p^2, and
Section 11, problem (1), asks whether the density goes to 0 when log q / log p tends to some alpha < 3. Problem (2) asks for
the exponent gamma of the logarithmic correction in the modified model on Z^d with threshold d; the 2025 paper settles d = 2
up to a log log factor (density to 0 for q >= C p^2 / log(1/p), to 1 for q <= p^2 / (log 1/p)^(1+o(1))), answering a 1997
conjecture of Gravner and McDonald, and leaves the log log factor open. Problem (3) asks whether the double-exponential
occupation time of the unpolluted modified model survives pollution q < p^gamma, gamma > 3.

Decision: no row. These are sharp-scaling questions inside one model family, asked by the authors who introduced the
three-dimensional results; no survey or problem list we read ranks them among the field's central problems. The
standard-model gap on Z^3 (p^3 against p^2) is the strongest candidate if a later source raises it.
