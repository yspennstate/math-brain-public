\title{A097 stage 2: prescribed references on the B05 tree}
\author{A097, B20}\date{12 October 2026}\maketitle
Information units are bits. Full FEI remains open. This is a scoped
application of A097 v1; the complete derivation and finite certificates are
in DERIVATION.md and out/RESULTS.json.

Let $k\ge2$ be even, $\delta=2^{-k}$, $e=2^{-k/2}$, $r=2k$, $p=2^{-r}$.
Let $b$ be the quadratic bent sign, $b'=b-2\mathbf1_0$, and $h$ the fair
physical selector between them. Activate $h$ on a point of $r$ fresh controls.
The tree queries controls until inactive or completely active, then queries
the selector and stops at the seed. Put $\mu=e-\delta$, $c=1-\mu$.

\begin{theorem}
Assign either exact seed Fourier laws or uniform data-subset laws
$Q_b(T)=Q_{b'}(T)=\delta$ to the leaves, with $\delta_\varnothing$ at constant
leaves, and propagate by the A097 compatible lift. Then
\[
 I_{\rm removed}=p(2kc+\delta),\quad
 I_{\rm terminal}=p(k/2+k\delta),\quad I(F)=I_{\rm removed}+I_{\rm terminal}.
\]
The exact erasure/credit residual is
\[
 R_k=\frac{B_{\rm sel}-\kappa_{\rm sel}+
     \sum_{s=1}^{2k}2^s(B_s-\kappa_s)}{2kc+\delta},
\]
with the explicit kernels below. For all even $k\ge2$,
$B_{\rm tree}\le3 I_{\rm removed}$, and the constructed complete root
reference obeys
\[
 \operatorname{CE}(P_F,Q_F)\le(3+2/\ln2)I_{\rm removed}+2I_{\rm terminal}.
\]
Under uniform seed references its correction satisfies
\[
 0\le\frac{K_{\rm tree}}{I_{\rm removed}}
 \le\frac{8\delta(1-\delta)}{(2kc+\delta)\ln2}=O(2^{-k}/k).
\]
Both corrected and uncorrected erasure rates tend to $2$. Exact seed
references give identically zero correction.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The signs of $\widehat b(T)$ have counts
$N_\pm=(2^k\pm2^{k/2})/2$. The flip subtracts $2\delta$ from every
coefficient. The core selector coefficients are $\sigma e-\delta,\delta$,
with marginal $q_\sigma=\delta-2\sigma\delta e+2\delta^2$. Thus
\[
B_{\rm sel}=\sum_\sigma N_\sigma
 \phi((\sigma e-\delta)^2,\delta^2),\qquad
J_{\rm sel}=H(q)-[k+H(b')]/2.
\]
In uniform mode the compatible core reference is
$Q_h(z,T)=\delta P_h(z,T)/q_\sigma$, so
$z_0=\delta\mu^2/q_+$ and $\kappa_{\rm sel}=J_{\rm sel}$.
In exact mode $z_0=\mu^2$ and all corrections vanish.

Set $u_s=2^{-s}$, $d_s=u_s c$, $q_s=1-2d_s+2d_s^2$.
The B20 activation formula's actual control-coordinate summand is
\[
B_s=\phi((1-d_s)^2,d_s^2)+1-q_s.
\]
The complete-reference recurrence and its actual physical-child correction are
\[
z_s=\frac{(1-d_s)^2}{q_s}\frac{1+z_{s-1}}2,\quad
w_s=\frac{d_s^2}{q_s}\frac{1+z_{s-1}}2,
\]
\[
\kappa_s=q_s D_2\!\left(
\frac{(1,(1-2d_s)^2)}{2q_s}\,\middle\Vert\,
\frac{(1,z_{s-1})}{1+z_{s-1}}\right).
\]
Only the residual-empty fiber contributes. Multiply by actual reach
$p/u_s$ to obtain the ratio and by $p$ for the selector. Point-flip edge
counting gives $I(b)=k/2$, $I(b')=k/2+2k\delta$, proving the influence split.

Exactly $\chi^2(P_{b'}\Vert\mathrm{uniform})=16\delta(1-\delta)$,
by expanding $P_{b'}(T)-\delta=\delta(-4\sigma e+4\delta)$ and using the
average sign $e$. The elementary $\ln t\le t-1$ and A097 v1 give
$K_{\rm tree}\le p[k-H(b')]/2\le8p\delta(1-\delta)/\ln2$.

For $a+b\le1$, $\phi(a,b)\le b\log_2(e_{\rm Euler}/b)$. Consequently
\[
 B_{\rm tree}/p\le4kc+C_0+\delta[k+\log_2 e_{\rm Euler}],
 \quad C_0=\log_2 e_{\rm Euler}+4+2\log_2(4/3)<13/2.
\]
Here $c\in[3/4,1)$, $\sum2^{-s}\le1$, $\sum s2^{-s}\le2$, and
$B_{\rm sel}\le h_2(\delta)$. For $k\ge6$,
$2kc\ge9$ and $\delta(k+3/2)\le15/128$, so the ratio is less than $3$.
The outward rational certificates at $k=2,4$ complete the proof.
The lower bound $B_{\rm tree}/p\ge4kc-2$ gives the limit $2$;
the credit bound gives the corrected limit. Both seed reference costs
are at most $2I(\mathrm{seed})$. Insert these bounds into A097 v1 to finish.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Unresolved universal line.}
This family already has entropy certificates. No reference payment for an
arbitrary Boolean seed, or for overlapping control histories under a single
exclusive influence budget, is proved here. The exact terminal costs remain
part of every transfer.
