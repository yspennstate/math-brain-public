After the first full run certified supremum intervals and strict real gaps,
the checker was extended to identify exact Boolean maximizers rather than
leave overlapping numerical intervals as possible ties. Each entropy is
expanded as rational coefficients times log2(prime), by integer factorization
of the exact rational probability. All interval-overlapping candidates must
have the same symbolic prime-log coefficient vector as the recorded witness.
This is an equality certificate, not a claim of linear independence of logs.
All excluded candidates have upper endpoints below the maximizing lower
endpoint. Search domain, case count, precision and resource cap are unchanged.
