\section*{A029: scoped child-deficit transport}
All entropies are in bits, all physical inputs fair, and all functions
Boolean signs. Put $D_K(f)=KI(f)-H(f)$. We use the established B06 R2
identity, rather than claim it anew:
\[
D_K(f)=\mathbb E_{\rho\in L}D_K(f_\rho)
 +K\Pi_I(T)-\Pi_H(T).
\]
Internal deficits are replaced, not added, when the terminal cut is
refined. Each terminal deficit and each signed increment has its unique
physical node and reach probability. For parity on $m$ variables, the
sum of all internal weighted deficits is $Km(m+1)/2$, whereas the
actual root deficit is only $Km$; the former is not a reserve.

\paragraph{Certified finite theorem.}
For every nonconstant Boolean $f$ on at most four variables, let $r_1$
be the minimum relevant coordinate entropy-drop/influence ratio and
$r_2$ the minimum ratio of a nonempty adaptive tree of depth at most two.
Then $0\le r_1-r_2\le2$, with equality exactly for signed AND2 (allowing
irrelevant variables) and the four-variable bent functions. The exact
census and a separately implemented direct-transform/min-cost certificate
are saved in \texttt{code/census.py} and \texttt{code/verify.py}; the
rational logarithm remainder proofs are given in \texttt{REPORT.md}.
All 65,804 nonconstant truth tables were verified, and the equality set
has $8+24+48+896=976$ elements.

\paragraph{Analytic explanation.}
Signed AND2 has $H=2$, $I=1$, both local ratios $4$, and a complete
two-level tree ratio $2$. At $K=2$, root debt is $1$, paid by weighted
actual child deficits $(0+2)/2$. Four-variable bent functions are
quadratic signs with nonsingular alternating adjacency matrix; their
root entropy is $4$, influence $2$, and every root coordinate has
entropy decrement $2$ and influence $1/2$. The rank-two children have
entropy $2$. If the graph has a leaf, querying its neighbor makes that
leaf isolated in both children, with influences $0$ and $1$; query it
in the influence-one child. If the graph is complete, each child is a
triangle; querying any coordinate preserves a quadratic edge and
entropy $2$, and has influence $1/2$. Either construction has
$\Pi_H=2$, $\Pi_I=1$.
For any two-level tree, the weighted influence $Z$ of entropy-preserving
second queries is at most $1/2$ (isolated sibling derivatives are
complementary). Entropy-dropping second queries have ratio $4$; let
$E$ be their weighted influence. Therefore
\[
\frac{\Pi_H}{\Pi_I}=
 \frac{2+4E}{1/2+Z+E}\ge\frac{2+4E}{1+E}\ge2.
\]

\paragraph{B05 family, all even $k\ge4$.}
Let $r=2k$, $\delta=2^{-k}$, $p=2^{-r}$,
$b(x)=(-1)^{x_1x_2+\cdots+x_{k-1}x_k}$, and let $b'$ be its sign
flip at zero. With $A$ the all-zero event on $r$ new controls, set
$G=1+A(b-1)$ and $H=1+A(b'-1)$. Their means are determined by
$\mathbb Eb=\sqrt\delta$, $\mathbb Eb'=\sqrt\delta-2\delta$;
their core influences are $k/2$ and $k/2+2k\delta$.
The fair selector of $G,H$ has selector influence $d=p\delta$.
For $F=1+A(u-1)$ with $\mu=\mathbb Eu\in[0,1]$, $c=1-\mu$, and
$h(t)=-t\log_2t$, exact Fourier coefficient counting gives
\begin{align*}
H(F)&=p[H(u)-h(\mu^2)]
 +2rp[(1-\mu^2)+(1-p)c^2]\\
&\quad+p(1-p)h(c^2)+h((1-pc)^2).
\end{align*}
Since $h(t)\le(1-t)/\ln2$ on $[0,1]$ and
$(1-\mu^2)+c^2=2c$, we have
\[
H(F)\le pH(u)+4rpc+2p/\ln2,
\qquad I(F)=p[I(u)+rc].
\]
Thus $D_4(G),D_4(H)\ge p(k-2/\ln2)\ge p(k-3)$,
using $H(u)\le k$ and $\ln2>2/3$. These child reserves are proved
directly, not assumed inductively. The established erasure/JS split
gives $\Delta_yH\le h_2(d)+2d/\ln2$, hence
\[
\Delta_yH-4d\le p\delta(3k+1/2).
\]
Because $2^{-k}(3k+1/2)$ decreases for $k\ge4$ and equals $25/32$
at $k=4$, the actual child reserve pays the split:
\[
D_4(f)\ge p[k-3-\delta(3k+1/2)]>0.
\]
This is scoped to the B05 family. Arbitrary terminal deficits remain
unpaid in the universal conjecture. In particular, a complete AND2
factor tree for $F=g_{\rm AND2}h$ has $(\Pi_H,\Pi_I)=(2,1)$ but
retains signed copies of $h$ at the leaves, so
$D_K(F)=D_K(h)+(K-2)$.
