\section*{Exact ledger}
For a Boolean sign $g$ of mean $\mu$, a point indicator $A_r$ on $r\ge1$
fresh controls, $p=2^{-r}$, and $F=1+A_r(g-1)$, put
$a=1-\mu$, $v=1-\mu^2$, and $L(q)=q\log_2(1/q)$, $L(0)=0$. Then
\begin{align*}
H(F)&=L((1-pa)^2)+(p-p^2)a^2\log_2\frac1{p^2a^2}
       +p[H(g)-L(\mu^2)]+2rpv,\\
I(F)&=pI(g)+rpa.
\end{align*}
Zero terms have their continuous interpretation. For balanced $g$,
\[
H(F)=pH(g)+2rp(2-p)+L((1-p)^2),\qquad I(F)=p[I(g)+r].
\]
For $g_t=z\prod_{j=1}^t f(x^{(j)})$ and
$r_t=\lceil2\log_2(t+1)\rceil$, these identities give
$I(F_t)\to0$, $\Pr(F_t=-1)\to0$, and
$H(F_t)/I(F_t)\to H(f)/I(f)$.

\begin{theorem}[Hereditary reflection]
Let $\mathcal C$ contain literals and constants and be closed under sign
reversal, physical restrictions, disjoint products, and point activation
on fresh controls. For every Boolean $f$ and integers $t,r\ge1$,
\[
f\in\mathcal C\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
z\prod_{j=1}^t f(x^{(j)})\in\mathcal C\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
1+A_r\left[z\prod_{j=1}^t f(x^{(j)})-1\right]\in\mathcal C.
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Forward implications are the stated closures. Restrict the controls to the
selected point. Fix all but one data block, obtaining a sign product $c$;
then fix $z=c$. The surviving restriction is exactly $f$.
\end{proof}

\section*{A nonvacuous certified class}
Let $\cR$ consist of finite AND/OR/parity/negation formulas with literals
and constants as leaves and globally distinct original variable labels.
No opaque Boolean leaves are permitted. Restrictions, sign reversal,
disjoint substitution, and disjoint products preserve the certificate.
Point activation is $F=\operatorname{OR}(-\ell_1,\ldots,-\ell_r,g)$ for
fresh matching control literals $\ell_i$. Thus hereditary reflection applies.

Under a product law with coordinate variances $v_i$, define
\[
E(f)=\sum_{S\ne\varnothing}\widehat f(S)^2
\log_2\frac{\prod_{i\in S}v_i}{\widehat f(S)^2},
\qquad \Delta(f)=I(f)-\operatorname{Var}(f).
\]
Disjoint substitution satisfies
\[
E(b(f_i))=E(b)+\sum_i\frac{I_i(b)}{\operatorname{Var}(f_i)}E(f_i),
\quad
\Delta(b(f_i))=\Delta(b)+\sum_i\frac{I_i(b)}{\operatorname{Var}(f_i)}\Delta(f_i).
\]
Remove deterministic children. For $a_i\in(0,1]$, write
$Z=\prod_i a_i$, $R_0=\sum_i(1-a_i)-1+Z$ and
$\Theta=\sum_i(a_i-Z)\ln(1/a_i)$. Appending $b$ changes $R_0$ by
$(1-b)(1-Z)$ and $\Theta$ by
$(1-b)Z\ln(1/Z)+b(1-Z)\ln(1/b)\le2(1-b)(1-Z)$.
Hence $\Theta\le2R_0$.
OR has $\Delta=4ZR_0$ and $E_{\rm nat}=\ln4\,\Delta+8Z\Theta$;
parity has $\Delta=R_0$ and $E_{\rm nat}=\Theta$ (use respectively
$a_i=\Pr(X_i=-1)$ and $a_i=(EX_i)^2$).
Therefore $E(f)\le C_0\Delta(f)$ for $f\in\cR$,
$C_0=2+4/\ln2$. For uniform original inputs,
$L((Ef)^2)\le\log_2(e)\operatorname{Var}(f)$ gives
\[
H(f)\le C_0 I(f),\qquad C_0<8.
\]

\section*{An actual excluded core}
The multiplexer
$M=(1+x)y/2+(1-x)z/2$ has four coefficients of magnitude $1/2$,
$H(M)=2$, and all three influences $1/2$. A root of a putative
read-once certificate is AND/OR or parity. If three singleton children
occur, AND/OR is unbalanced and parity has influences one. If a singleton
and a two-variable child occur, balance at AND/OR forces the other child
to be constant; parity again has singleton influence one. Thus
$M\notin\cR$, and all its activated balanced tensors are excluded.
Yet $M=\operatorname{OR}(\operatorname{AND}(x,y),
\operatorname{AND}(-x,z))$: overlapping substitution is not a closure.
Universal representation and reuse payment remain unproved.
\section*{Consumed A011 dependency: rare transcript cost}
For the control-first activation of the literal $y$, stop at the first
control mismatch with output $+1$, and output $y$ only on the matching
point. Then $D=\sum_{j=1}^r2^{-(j-1)}=2(1-p)$ and
$I_{\rm sel}=rp$, $I=p(r+1)$. The uncompressed two-bits-per-query
transcript costs $2D=4(1-p)$, hence
\[
\frac{2D}{I}=\frac{4(2^r-1)}{r+1}\longrightarrow\infty.
\]
This is an explicit failure of that transcript payment, not FEI. If an
exact spectral witness $W$ retains its physical leaf label $J$, then
$H(J)=D$ and $H(W)\ge D$. Consequently
\[
H(W\mid S)\ge D-H(F)\ge2(1-p)-4rp-2p\log_2(e)\longrightarrow2.
\]
This exposes a necessary posterior erasure on a certified literal core.
It consumes A011's low-boundary continuation while leaving its pending
claim as an unreviewed source interface, not a proof premise.
