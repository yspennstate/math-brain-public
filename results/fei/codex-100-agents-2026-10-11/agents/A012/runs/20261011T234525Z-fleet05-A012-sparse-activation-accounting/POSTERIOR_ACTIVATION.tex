\begin{theorem}[Sharp complete-reference transfer]
Let $g$ be balanced Boolean, $r\ge1$, $p=2^{-r}$,
$F=1+A_r(g-1)$ for a fresh point indicator $A_r$.
Let $R_g$ be a nonempty spectral subprobability reference of full cost
$c_g=\sum_{T\ne\varnothing}\widehat g(T)^2\log_2(1/R_g(T))$.
Define
\[
Q_F(\varnothing,\varnothing)=(1-p)^2,\qquad
Q_F(U,\varnothing)=p^2\quad(U\ne\varnothing),\qquad
Q_F(U,T)=p^2R_g(T)\quad(T\ne\varnothing).
\]
This is a complete subprobability reference of mass
$1-p+p\sum_T R_g(T)\le1$ and exact cost
\[
\operatorname{CE}(P_F,Q_F)=pc_g+A_r,\quad
A_r=2rp(2-p)+(1-p)^2\log_2\frac1{(1-p)^2}.
\]
The sharp constant in $A_r\le C_{\rm act}rp$ for integer $r\ge1$ is
\[
C_{\rm act}=\frac{15}{4}+\frac{49}{12}\log_2\frac87,
\qquad 4.536<C_{\rm act}<4.537,
\]
attained at $r=3$. Thus $c_g\le K I(g)$ implies
$\operatorname{CE}(P_F,Q_F)\le\max(K,C_{\rm act})I(F)$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The actual spectrum consists of $(1-p)^2$ at the vacuum,
$p^2$ at every nonempty control-only set, and
$p^2\widehat g(T)^2$ at each $(U,T)$ with $T\ne\varnothing$.
Summing the displayed reference and its logarithmic cost proves the
identity. No reference deficit in $R_g$ is discarded.
Set $c_r=A_r/(rp)$. Since $-(1-p)\ln(1-p)\le p$,
$c_r\le4+2/(r\ln2)<4+3/r$. For $r\ge6$ this is at most $4.5$.
Outward rational atanh-series certificates for $r=1,\ldots,5$ prove
$c_3>4.5$ and $c_j<c_3$ for $j\ne3$.
The exact formula at $r=3$ is as stated. For an exact core reference,
Gibbs' inequality and the exact activation entropy imply that no smaller
coefficient can satisfy the same universal reference-transfer bound.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Exact physical-label posterior]
Let $W$ be the control-first true spectral witness that retains its
physical stopping/core leaf $J$, actual leaf spectral set and queried
spectral membership bits. For $k=1,\ldots,r$ put
\[
s_k=2^{-(k-1)},\quad m_k=1-s_k,\quad q_k=\frac{1+m_k^2}{2},\quad
\epsilon_k=\frac{s_k^2}{2(1+m_k^2)},\quad
B_k=q_k h_2(\epsilon_k)+\frac{1-m_k^2}{2}.
\]
Then, with $D=2(1-p)$,
\[
H(W\mid S)=D+\sum_{k=1}^r2^{-(r-k)}B_k-A_r.
\]
The formula is independent of the balanced core, and
$H(W\mid S)\to2$ as $r\to\infty$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The physical leaf probabilities are $2^{-j}$ for mismatch $j=1,\ldots,r$
and $p$ for the core, giving $H(J)=D$. Nonempty residual Fourier packets
have queried membership probability $1/2$; the empty residual packet
has mass $q_k$ and membership probability $\epsilon_k$.
Entropy chain rule yields
$H(W)=D+pH(g)+\sum_k2^{-(r-k)}B_k$.
Subtract $H(F)=pH(g)+A_r$. Moreover $W$ is exactly $(J,S)$, so
$0\le H(W\mid S)\le H(J)=D$, and
$H(W\mid S)\ge D-A_r$. Since $D\to2$ and $A_r\to0$, the limit follows.
\end{proof}
This ideal output reference does not transmit a physical leaf label.
One-symbol integer prefix rounding is separate. Arbitrary tensor cores
retain the full $ptc_f$ cost and $ptH(f)$ entropy. No universal bound for
$c_f$ or the original incidence queue is established here.
