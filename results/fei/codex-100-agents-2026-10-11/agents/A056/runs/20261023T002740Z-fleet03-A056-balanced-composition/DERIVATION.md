## 1. General dominated-reset theorem

The fresh law can depend on the actual restricted phase and remaining coordinate labels. It is not a mixture with the inherited reference. Assumption (2) must be checked for the chosen target; it is not a consequence of small TV or matching root masses.

    ej = wj(1-aj) Delta cj.                            (4)

## 2. Why the allowance is original and cannot be renewed

The weighted source influence wj I(j) is harmonic under private/selector queries and a supermartingale under shared-data queries. At a data query the expected drop is exactly wj Inf_i(j). This follows directly from product subcubes and the physical influence telescope. At every physical node the children retain the parent's unspent/reset flag. A reset consumes its source flag. Finite optional stopping gives

    sum_(j-reset frontier) reach*wj I(j)
                                  <= wj,root I(j_root).             (5)

Consequently total positive reset booking is at most

    sum_j Lj wj,root I(j_root).                       (6)

It is invalid to reapply (6) to a newly reset source and reserve the same original influence again. A second reset needs another genuinely unused allowance, a signed refund, or a new theorem. Separate source flags also prohibit spending a u ticket on both its sibling continuations twice in expectation: their actual reaches remain in (5).

    Cphys + T = CEroot + sum reach*ej

    K I(root),  K=max(Cu+1+Lu,Cv+1+Lv,7).             (8)

The query's opposite child, old conditional KL, actual reference normalizers and all off-path terminals remain in Cphys+T. Positive reset bookings have not been hidden in a renewed bank. A finite no-reset continuation only disintegrates this current full bill.

This supplies the parallel original-potential statement. The physical-mark statement and the actual CE-release statement (7) are two ledgers; their costs are not added together.
