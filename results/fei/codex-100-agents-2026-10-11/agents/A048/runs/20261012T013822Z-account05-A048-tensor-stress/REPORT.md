Under nonuniform physical inputs distinguish I_phys=sum_i Pr(f(X)!=f(X^i)) from spectral energy I_spec=sum_S |S| fhat_p(S)^2. They coincide only on the uniform cube. A collapsed mask with induced bias is a different coordinate system from its physical controls. These are exact scope results, not a Shannon FEI disproof or a universal tree theorem.

# complete derivation or exact first unsupported line

**1. What is unconditional about tau*.** Its angular definition alone gives tau*(p)<=h2(p). Choosing an eigenstate gives min F<=h2(p), so tau*>=0. For any actual unit-norm child pair, the exact posterior/parent-mass identity gives

    E=J-M=h2(p)-sum_T q_T F_p(t_T)<=tau*(p).

This elementary reduction is rebuilt from the actual two child coefficient vectors as in the lead report. Thus the genuine biased AND instance at p=1/8,q=1/4, whose independent exact certificate gives E>1195/10000, proves tau*(1/8)>1195/10000. No numerical minimization, claim of attaining tau* by a Boolean pair, or unaccepted half-tax bound is needed.

At p=1/2, tau*=0 exactly. To see this without a theorem citation, expand h2((1+z)/2)=1-sum_{k>=1} z^(2k)/(ln2*2k(2k-1)). The coefficients are positive and sum to one (evaluate at z=1 by monotone convergence), so h2(x)>=4x(1-x). With x=sin^2 t and y=sin^2(t-pi/4), the two lower bounds sum to sin^2(2t)+cos^2(2t)=1. F>=1 and F(0)=1. This proves min F=1 and tau*=h2(1/2)-1=0.

**2. Essential biased point-defect tax is unpayable.** Let f_r=1-2 product_{i=1}^r X_i, with independent P(X_i=1)=q=1/8. Every physical coordinate is essential. Its exceptional input has probability delta=q^r. Each physical pivotal probability is q^(r-1), so

    I_phys=r q^(r-1),   I_spec=4r(1-q)q^r.

In every exact essential singleton computing tree, the unique live all-one path must query every remaining bit until it sees a zero or exhausts the bits. A zero makes the restriction constant; before seeing a zero every remaining bit is essential. The reach weights along the exceptional path are 1,q,...,q^(r-1). All minimal essential policies therefore have the identical expected query count (1-q^r)/(1-q). More queries cannot improve a nonnegative tax bill. Randomized policies are mixtures and obey the same lower bound. Consequently

    A_T=tau*(q) (1-q^r)/(1-q),
    A_T/I_phys=tau*(q) (1-q^r)/[(1-q)r q^(r-1)] -> infinity.

This fails a payment by any finite C even though the dummy-coordinate repair has been made, all queried bits are essential, and the tree is already optimal for the tax. It also fails payment by spectral energy, which differs here by the fixed factor 4q(1-q). For t disjoint copies, influence and the expected tax bill both multiply by t; the unbounded parameter is defect dimension r, not tensor count. The same tax payment failure holds under a common arbitrary independent unrevealed factor when charged only on the defect coordinates; the factor's terminal entropy must remain.

The corresponding physical path entropy is

    H(transcript)=h2(q)(1-q^r)/(1-q).

Hence A_T<=H(transcript) follows from tau*<=h2, but this path entropy also has unbounded ratio to influence. Since the tree computes f, H(transcript|f)=H(transcript)-h2(q^r), which tends to h2(q)/(1-q)>0. C024 path entropy is an explicit possible accounting source, not an influence-paid free credit. This closes the lead's unqualified essential-bit tax payment question negatively; it does not refute a reserve-corrected ledger.

**3. Exact residual and uncertainty-defect cancellation.** For any actual selector node set U(p)=min F_p and

    D_v=sum_T q_T[F_p(t_T)-U(p)]>=0.

Then tau*(p)-D_v=E_v=J_v-M_v exactly. This is the uncertainty-defect reserve proposed by the lead/A047. For the actual tree,

    A_T-sum_v w_v D_v=sum_v w_v E_v,
    H(f)-E H(leaf)=sum_v w_v(M_v+J_v).

A reserve must be transported on the same physical tree; it cannot be replaced by a second independent copy of path entropy or an unrelated KL reference.

For a point-defect split with selected bias p and residual exceptional probability eps, the inactive child is constant and the active child has mean m=1-2eps, variance v=4eps(1-eps). From direct masses,

    J=h2(pv)-p h2(v),
    M=e((1-p+pm)^2)+e(p(1-p)(1-m)^2)-e(1-pv)+pv h2(p).

These are exact for all eps, including the final eps=1 split. The residual data sample is empty for X=0; for X=1 it is nonempty with probability v, and its nonempty label law is independent of X. Enrich the experiment with an independent Bernoulli(v) erasure flag and that nonempty label. The enriched channel reveals X only when the flag is one, so data processing gives

    [E]_+ <= J <= v h2(p) <=4eps h2(p).

For a heterogeneous point defect with delta=product_i p_i, in any essential short-circuit ordering the reach weight before querying i is w=delta/(p_i eps). Thus

    sum_v w_v [E_v]_+ <=sum_v w_v J_v
           <=4 delta sum_i h2(p_i)/p_i <=4 I_phys.

No ancestral influence is reused: w_v Inf_{i_v}(f_v)=delta/p_i, and the sum is precisely I_phys. The same proof applies factor by factor to finite disjoint point-defect tensors, because wholly unrevealed factors preserve the local M,J and actual pivotal probability. This pays the positive *reserve-corrected* tax debt on this family. It does not pay the conditional M sum for arbitrary children, prove general FEI under arbitrary biases, or admit overlapping control events.

For homogeneous q fixed, the exact spectrum also quantifies cancellation. For nonempty S,

    P_S=4delta^2 product_{i in S}(1-q)/q,
    P_empty=(1-2delta)^2,
    H(f_r)=e((1-2delta)^2)+e(4delta)+4delta r h2(q)-e(4delta^2).

Therefore, as r tends to infinity,

    H/I_phys ->4q[log2(1/q)+h2(q)],
    J_tree/I_phys ->4q log2(1/q),
    E_tree/I_phys ->4q[log2(1/q)-h2(q)].

For the second limit, write s for the number of residual bits after each query. Uniformly for sufficiently large s at fixed q, J_s=4q^(s+1)log2(1/q)+O_q(q^(2s)); this follows from h2(qv)-q h2(v)=qv log2(1/q)+O_q(v^2). Multiply by reach q^(r-s-1) and sum s=0,...,r-1; the finite small-s terms and the geometric remainder are O_q(q^(r-1)). Thus J_tree=4r q^r log2(1/q)+O_q(q^(r-1)). The entropy formula has remainder O_q(delta)+O_q(rdelta^2). Division proves the three limits, with errors O_q(1/r)+O_q(delta). At q=1/8 the last limit is positive: h2(q)<1<3=log2(1/q), so actual positive debt remains even though it tends to zero in absolute units.

Since |E_tree|<=H (M_tree,J_tree>=0 and H=M_tree+J_tree), E_tree tends to zero while A_T tends to tau*(q)/(1-q)>0. Thus sum wD_v/A_T ->1. Almost all of the blanket tax must be canceled by actual uncertainty defect; simply dropping the reserve changes an influence-scale charge into a constant charge. This is the exact correction this verification supplies to A050/A064.

**4. Fair activation versus logical-mask pricing, inside A036's original class.** Let g_n be a fair n-bit point defect, let A_3 be a point indicator on three fresh fair controls, and F_n=1+A_3(g_n-1). Then F_n is exactly a fair point defect on N=n+3 bits. Every bit is essential, and it has full Fourier support for N>=2. Each actual physical query has p_i=1/2 and tax zero; A036's K_KL>=T/4-8I holds at its stated uniform class if that proof is accepted. This present verification does not need to assume that inequality.

If the three controls are instead priced as one logical selector of bias p=1/8, the assigned tax is at least 1195/10000 independently of n. The actual influences are

    d=2^(1-N),   I_total=N d,   I_controls=3d.

Both tend to zero, so the collapsed tax cannot be paid by C I_total or C I_controls for any finite C. The prior general-mean activation bound pays the actual physical block decrement by 8I_controls; it does not pay the collapsed scalar upper-bound tax. The logical M of the mask and physical M of the three-control block differ, even though their J channels agree because all inactive assignments have the same child.

Nor does the original product-marginal KL budget supply a missing constant reserve. For this uniform point defect, K_KL=N h2(d)-H(F_n)>=0, and

    K_KL<=N h2(d)<=N d[log2(1/d)+log2 e]<N(N+1)d ->0.

Here log2 e<2. Thus for any fixed a,b>=0, aK_KL+bI_total tends to zero and cannot pay tau*(1/8). A finite rational certificate is N=13: K_KL+8I_total <13*22/4096=143/2048<1195/10000<tau*(1/8). This lies within A036's point-defect class and exposes only the extra collapsed-mask tax payment, not the collision inequality or recovery inequality themselves. It cannot be repaired by rescaling the raw collision L, which is retired. On disjoint tensors K_KL and I both add; any repeated collapsed-mask bill adds, so no tensor normalization changes this comparison.

For contrast, a genuinely balanced core under k fair point controls has I_controls=kp, p=2^-k. A single logical-mask tax obeys the unconditional bound tau*(p)<=h2(p)<=p[k+log2 e], so it is paid by (1+log2 e/k)I_controls. Conditional on acceptance of the lead's half-tax theorem it is at most (kp)/2. The near-constant-core counterexample shows precisely why essentiality alone does not provide this balanced-core payment. Neither statement bounds arbitrary terminal H(g).

The first unsupported line for a universal repair is: transport the actual D_v reserve (and any compatible KL credit) on one physical tree so that the remaining M_v and terminal entropy are paid from exclusive physical influence accounts. This stage proves that transport/payment for the positive E_v part on point-defect tensors only; it does not establish the general residual bound.
