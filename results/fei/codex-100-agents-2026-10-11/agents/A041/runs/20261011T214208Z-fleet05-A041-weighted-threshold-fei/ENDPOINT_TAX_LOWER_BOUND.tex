\section{An actual Boolean lower bound for the one-bit uncertainty tax}
Let $X\sim\operatorname{Ber}(p)$ and $Y\sim\operatorname{Ber}(q)$ be
independent, with fixed $0<q<1$, and let $f=1-2XY$. Reveal only $X$.
Write $v=4q(1-q)$ and use base-two Shannon entropy throughout this
section. In the order $\varnothing,\{X\},\{Y\},\{X,Y\}$, the exact
orthonormal product Fourier masses are
\[
 P=((1-2pq)^2,\ 4p(1-p)q^2,\ p^2v,\ p(1-p)v).
\]
The marginal spectral $Y$ mass is $pv$. The $X=0$ child is constant,
and the $X=1$ child has spectral masses $1-v,v$. Consequently
\[
 M=H(P)-h_2(pv),\qquad J=h_2(pv)-p h_2(v),
\]
and the signed difference is exactly
\[
 J-M=2h_2(pv)-H(P)-p h_2(v).
\]
For fixed $c>0$, $h_2(cp)=cp\log_2(1/p)+O_c(p)$.
The empty spectral mass contributes $O_q(p)$ to $H(P)$; the
$\{X\}$ and $\{X,Y\}$ masses contribute respectively
$4q^2p\log_2(1/p)+O_q(p)$ and
$vp\log_2(1/p)+O_q(p)$. The $\{Y\}$ mass contributes
$O_q(p^2\log(1/p))=o_q(p)$. Therefore
\[
 J-M=4q(1-2q)p\log_2(1/p)+O_q(p).
\]
In particular, at fixed $q=1/4$,
\begin{equation}\label{a041:necessarytax}
 \lim_{p\downarrow0}\frac{J-M}{p\log_2(1/p)}=\frac12.
\end{equation}
Thus every scalar $\tau(p)$ making $J\le M+\tau(p)$ valid for all
Boolean functions, product biases, and one-coordinate physical reveals
satisfies $\liminf_{p\downarrow0}\tau(p)/[p\log_2(1/p)]\ge1/2$.
In particular no finite fixed $K$ makes $\tau(p)=Kp$ sufficient.
This is an actual Boolean lower bound, not an inference from the
asymptotic size of an available upper bound. It repairs the precise
unsupported endpoint implication in the recovered B09 report, section
5(4). It neither proves optimality of the coefficient-one
Maassen--Uffink tax nor pays the tax through arbitrary composition.
The known upper tax
$h_2(p)+\log_2(1-p)=p\log_2(1/p)+O(p)$ leaves an asymptotic
coefficient gap between $1/2$ and $1$.
