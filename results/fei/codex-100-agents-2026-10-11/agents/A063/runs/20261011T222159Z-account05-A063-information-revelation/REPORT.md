## complete derivation or exact first unsupported line

The full derivation is in QUERY_INFORMATION_IDENTITY.tex. Here are its mathematical steps and their dependencies.

1. For block A and residual Fourier index w, let a_{u,w}=fhat(u union w), c_{x,w}=sum_u a_{u,w}chi_u(x), and q_w=sum_u a_{u,w}^2. The actual joint physical/residual experiment has P(X_A=x,W=w)=2^-|A| c_{x,w}^2. Boolean Parseval gives a uniform physical marginal; partial Walsh Parseval gives residual marginal q. The spectral parent conditional law is nu_w(u)=a_{u,w}^2/q_w and the physical posterior is r_w(x)=2^-|A|c_{x,w}^2/q_w. Therefore B_A=sum q H(nu)=H(S_A|S_C), J_A=|A|-sum q H(r)=I(X_A;W), and H(f)-E H(f_x)=B_A+J_A. In the packet's collision notation B_W=B_A and M_W=J_A. Physical outcome entropy |A| is a different quantity.

2. The experiment does not specify how the deleted parent label U is coupled to the branch X_A. Every extension with the correct (U,W) and (X_A,W) marginals has I(U,W;X_A)=J_A+I(U;X_A|W). The canonical A037 lift imposes conditional independence and attains the minimum J_A. The three-bit point defect in PHYSICAL_SPECTRAL_DISTINCTION.md gives two exact nonidentical lifts with unchanged physical/residual marginals; the additional information in the second is strictly positive by nonindependence and Gibbs. This demonstrates why a kernel is required before assigning full-label information.

3. Independently reconstruct A037's finite adaptive common lift. Starting with root S~fhat^2, maintain current S_h=S intersect R_h. At node h draw outcome x with kernel r_{h,w}(x), w=S intersect (R_h\A_h), and erase A_h from the current set. Induction gives actual fair physical branches and actual conditional child spectra. Since the kernel depends on the root S only through w and history, I(S;next outcome|h)=J_h. For stopped transcript L, sum p_h J_h=I(S;L) and H(S|L)=sum p_h B_h+E H(g_L). These are baseline identities already proved by A037 and are independently accepted here at this finite-tree scope. They measure actual information under the stated coupling; they do not erase root posterior uncertainty when current coordinates disappear.

4. New fixed-block coarsening refund. Fix A and query all and only its coordinates, possibly choosing their order from physical history. For deterministic policy the full transcript is in bijection with X_A. The final joint (X_A,S_C) law equals the actual direct-block experiment: each leaf has uniform probability and the actual child spectrum. Under the sequential lift put R_A=I(S_A;X_A|S_C). The chain rule gives sum p_h J_h=I(S;X_A)=J_A+R_A. The total entropy decrement is order independent, so sum p_h B_h=B_A-R_A. Equivalently sum p_h B_h=H(S_A|S_C,X_A). Thus 0<=R_A<=B_A. The direct-block lift has R_A=0 by construction; singleton refinement can reveal erased spectral information and reduce residual erasure. The sum is invariant; its two pieces can depend on query order. This is a conditional-information identity, not an influence-payment theorem.

5. Reference audit. For normalized supported child references Q_x, mixture Q=E Q_x and posterior t_w(x)=2^-a Q_x(w)/Q(w), the joint KL chain rule gives E D(P_x||Q_x)=D(q||Q)+sum q D(r_w||t_w). Moreover sum q D(r_w||t_w)=J_A+E log2(2^-a/t_W(X_A)). The last term is signed and cannot be suppressed unless its cancellation is proved. This is the precise local reference/mixing correction; A037's general channel/reset ledger applies across incompatible node references.

The refund has a sharp quantitative ceiling. The self-contained positive-series proof of h2(t)>=4t(1-t), applied to the erased-bit and branch posteriors in each singleton fiber, gives J_h<=B_h. Hence R_A<=(B_A-J_A)/2. For any canonical singleton stopping tree, I(S;L)<=[H(f)-E H(terminal)]/2 and H(S|L)>=[H(f)+E H(terminal)]/2. Two-bit AND attains the ceiling with direct block charges (2,0), sequential charges (1,1), and refund 1; disjoint AND products show sharpness in arbitrarily large dimensions. Thus paying only released root information leaves at least half the original entropy unpaid. This is a coupling-mechanism barrier, not an FEI refutation.
