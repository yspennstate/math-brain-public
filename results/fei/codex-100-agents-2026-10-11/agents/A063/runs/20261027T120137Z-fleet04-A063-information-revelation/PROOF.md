## 1. The root product target itself can exhaust the same rate-four owner

Let U,V be nonempty disjoint sets of physical data coordinates and y a new
physical selector. Over F2 put u=sum_(j in U) x_j and v=sum_(j in V) x_j,
and define F=(-1)^(yuv). Write d=1+|U|+|V|>=3. Its selector children are the
constant one and the rank-two quadratic phase (-1)^(uv). The latter has all
data influences 1/2. Every ORIGINAL influence of F, including y, is 1/4,
since a derivative of yuv is the product of two independent fair parity bits.
Thus I=d/4 and the ORIGINAL actual spectral product has marginal entropy
M=d h2(1/4). With product reference K=M-H exactly.

One original S=4I account that must pay full root H+K and an already booked
omega=S/4=I necessarily needs M<=3I. This fails:

    h2(1/4)=2-(3/4)log2(3)>3/4,

because 3^3=27<32=2^5. It fails already at d=3 and grows linearly with d.
This refutes universal SAME-rate-four ORIGINAL-PRODUCT admission for this
ledger, not FEI, not the preceding dense/sparse fixture, and not nonproduct
admission or a sufficiently large original rate.

For orientation the sharp marginal-entropy coefficient for selectors between
two quadratic phases is M/I<=4h2(1/4). Each data influence belongs to
{0,1/4,1/2,3/4,1}; the selector derivative is a quadratic phase, whose nonzero
probability is either 1 or in [1/4,3/4]. To justify the latter, the bias of a
quadratic phase is zero or has magnitude 2^(-r/2) for even alternating rank r;
rank zero gives an affine character or a constant. For r>=2 the magnitude is
at most 1/2. The function h2(t)/t is strictly decreasing for 0<t<1, since its
derivative is log2(1-t)/t^2<0. Thus h2(t)<=4h2(1/4)t for every influence in
this class. The family above attains equality on EVERY coordinate. With an
old relative quarter, original product-root capital therefore needs rate at
least (16/3)h2(1/4)=32/3-4log2(3)>4. This sharp root comparison is not a new
all-history product-reset theorem.

## 2. A fixed nonproduct template, independent of the ambient block sizes

Let L be the three-dimensional spectral subspace generated by masks {y},U,V.
It has eight distinct atoms because these three masks are nonempty and disjoint.
The Fourier coefficient at the empty atom is 3/4 and all seven other
coefficients have magnitude 1/4. Indeed F is one except at the single point
(y,u,v)=(1,1,1), so F=1-2*1_(111) on the three parity bits. Fourier expansion
on those independent parities gives exactly these coefficients, and replacing
a parity bit by its physical block merely relabels the spectral atoms.

Consequently p(0)=9/16 and p(t)=1/16 for the seven nonempty t in L. Choose ONE
original normalized reference Q with

    Q(0)=1/2; Q(t)=1/14 for t in L\{0}; Q(t)=0 outside L.

This is an explicit vacuum-plus-uniform-nonempty template, not the actual
source law p, not a marginal product, and not a fresh reference at descendants.
It is positive on the complete actual source support. Its full ORIGINAL
cross entropy is

    CE(p,Q)=H(p)+KL(p||Q)=9/16+(7/16)log2(14)=:c.

The exact integer certificate 14^28<2^107 gives c<9/4-1/64. This can be checked
without a logarithmic approximation: multiply the inequality by 64, exponentiate,
and reduce to 28log2(14)<107.

With S=4I=d and any already booked 0<=omega<=S/4=d/4, the SAME original bank
retains at least

    S-omega-c >= 3d/4-c > 3(d-3)/4+1/64 >=1/64.       (A)

There is no product admission debit to add: Q is the original reference selected
before any query, paid by CE. This is a different eligible original template
for the stated Boolean family; it is not a retroactive conversion of an
already-consumed product account. The failed product source in section 1 and

## 3. Every physical history retains all query invoices and both terminals

Retain Q under its ACTUAL source-induced physical query kernel. Exercise no
product resets. Query orders, adaptive choices, independent randomization and
stopping are arbitrary. For any reached node with source law p, erase query i
to pbar and Qbar. The physical child spectral laws p_b have fair average pbar.
Use k_b(t)=p_b(t)/(2pbar(t)) on positive-source rows and 1/2 on zero-source
rows. Let pi_b=sum_t Qbar(t)k_b(t), and Q_b=Qbar*k_b/pi_b.
Positive Q on actual support ensures pi_b>0 for each physical child.

Write K=KL(p||Q), E=K-KL(pbar||Qbar)>=0, and
O=KL((1/2,1/2)||pi)>=0. Finite KL disintegration gives

    K=E+O+(K_0+K_1)/2.                                (B)

This retains the full physical-query invoice E+O. O is a reference outcome
mismatch, not the physical mutual information J, and E is conditional erasure
KL, not the spectral erasure entropy B. The ordinary spectral identity

Telescoping (B) on ANY stopped finite physical tree yields

    sum_reached physical_weight*(E+O)
        + E_physical[K_terminal+H_terminal]

There are at most d distinct coordinate queries, so no infinite stopping or

the root, stopping on different branches at different depths, and every complete
