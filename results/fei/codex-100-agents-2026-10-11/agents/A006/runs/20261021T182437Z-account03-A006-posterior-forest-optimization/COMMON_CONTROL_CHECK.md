# Why a tempting all-weight obstruction does not follow

A015's leafless diagnostic concerns a signed transferred surrogate bill, not
an infimum over actual complete prime-table codes. This stage checks a tempting
attempt to strengthen that conclusion and rejects its unpaid step.

For B_m=(OR_i x_i) AND (OR_j y_j), a=2^-m, add a common positive literal z
and keep the primes {z,x_i,y_j}. Their global packing imposes total weight
Lambda<=1. If X is a nonempty set on the left side, every full partition has
one singleton owned block per selected x_i, no selected attachment edges and
therefore only roots. For every ordered cover, singleton subset probability
is at most one. Root rarity 1/8, size-one probability 1/2 and zero-child
probability 1/2 give

    Q_X <= (32^-|X| / D) product_(i in X) alpha_i,
    alpha_i=sum_j lambda_ij, D>=Lambda/8.

Writing alpha_i=Lambda a_i with sum a_i=1 yields
-log2 Q_X >= sum_(i in X) log2(1/a_i)+5|X|-3.
Thus posterior multiplicity cannot erase this row-name charge. But this is
not an FEI or variational-cost divergence: the Boolean function represented
by these positive primes is F=2[z AND B_m]-1, whose influence is

    I(F)=2ma(1-a)+(1-a)^2.

The z influence is (1-a)^2, not the rare false probability 2a-a^2. Its
within-side squared coefficients are a^2(1-a)^2. The AM-GM row-name lower
charge is therefore ma(1-a)^2 log2 m, whose ratio to influence is at most
m2^-m log2 m <= m^2 2^-m ->0. The last sequence decreases geometrically
from m>=4 since its adjacent ratio is at most25/32. This lower bound cannot
establish unbounded complete cost. Nothing here asserts a complete upper
bound for that prime table.

The rare-false activation 1+1[z active](g_m-1) has a different Boolean truth
table and different prime implicants (it includes the inactive-control literal
as an alternative). Replacing one activation by the other silently loses a
physical influence charge and changes the fixed-reference problem. The first
unsupported line of the attempted all-weight obstruction is precisely that
activation/prime-table identification. It is rejected, not recorded as an
unresolved FEI counterexample. A015's correctly scoped fixed-reference signed
transfer obstruction is unchanged.
