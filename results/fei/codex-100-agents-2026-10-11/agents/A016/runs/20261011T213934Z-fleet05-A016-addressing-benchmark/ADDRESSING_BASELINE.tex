\title{A016: Addressing baseline and explicit cost definitions}
\author{A016, stream B04}\date{12 October 2026}\maketitle
All costs are in bits. This is a baseline reproduction; unrestricted FEI remains open.
Let $a\geq0$, $N=2^a$, and $f(x,y)=y_x$, where $x$ is a fresh uniform selector block and the $y_u$ are distinct fair coordinates.
\[
f=N^{-1}\sum_{u\in\{-1,1\}^a}\sum_{T\subseteq[a]}\chi_T(u)\chi_T(x)y_u.
\]
The $N^2$ Fourier sets are distinct, so the spectrum is uniform on $T\cup\{y_u\}$ and
\[
H=2a,\quad I=1+a/2,\quad H=4(I-1).
\]
This includes $a=0$, when $f=y_0$ and $(H,I)=(0,1)$.

An actual repeated-label code first transmits the $a$-bit mask $T$, then the $a$-bit address once for each of the $s=1+|T|$ selected coordinates. Its expectation is $a(1+I)$, with ratio $a+2a/(a+2)$. Transmitting the address once followed by the mask costs $2a$, with ratio $4a/(a+2)\to4$. Its latent witness $(u,T)$ is determined by the output set; posterior entropy is zero.

Under the recovered forest source's coordinatewise fractional definitions, selector witnesses have $q_{j,u}=1/N$, whereas data witnesses are deterministic. Convexity minimizes the pressure at $\lambda_u=1/N$, giving
\[
P_{\min}=\frac72a^2+\frac{11}{5}a,\qquad
P_{\min}/I=7a-\frac{48}{5}+\frac{96}{5(a+2)}.
\]
Pressure is a component of the source's upper bound, not a complete literal code.

For a literal single-run queue set $d=a+1$, $\gamma=6/5$,
\[
\theta_s(r)=\min\{1,(s/r)^\gamma\},\quad
E_{d,s}=e_s(\theta_s(1),\ldots,\theta_s(d)),\quad
W_s(B)=\prod_{r\in B}\theta_s(r),
\]
where $B=T\cup\{d\}$. Write
\[
L_a=\mathbb E_T\left[\log_2(s(s+1))+\log_2 E_{d,s}-\log_2 W_s(B)\right].
\]
The original scalar-root generator with parameter one has complete cost
\[
C_0=\Lambda_0+a+2+L_a,\qquad
\Lambda_0=N\log_2(1+1/(2N)).
\]
The incidence-weighted generator with $\lambda_u=1/N$, conditioned on nonempty roots, has
\[
C_* =\Lambda_f+\log_2 Z_f+2a+2+L_a,
\quad \Lambda_f=N\log_2(1+1/(2N^2)),\quad Z_f=1-2^{-\Lambda_f}.
\]
Exactly one single-root partition run produces each set. Optimizing its posterior subtracts zero entropy. This assertion is restricted to single-root runs; multiple-gate partition runs can exist. At $a=0$, $C_0=\log_2 12$ and $C_*=2$.

Let $w(t)=1$ for $t\leq1/2$ and $w(t)=(2t)^{-\gamma}$ otherwise. There is a unique $z>0$ with
\[
\int_0^1\frac{zw(t)}{1+zw(t)}\,dt=\frac12.
\]
Define
\[
G=\int_0^1\log_2(1+zw(t))\,dt-\frac12\log_2z
-\frac12\int_0^1\log_2w(t)\,dt.
\]
Then
\[
C_0/I,C_*/I\longrightarrow2+2G,\qquad
4\leq2+2G\leq4+\gamma\left(1-\frac1{2\ln2}\right).
\]
The coefficient proof uses a Bernoulli generating function with mean $s$: its atom at $s$ has negative logarithm $o(d)$ by Chebyshev and bounded adjacent atom ratios in an $O(\sqrt d)$ central window. Riemann sums give the displayed integral, and binomial concentration transfers it to $\mathbb E_T$. The complete argument, including error control, appears in REPORT.md, Section 5.

Finally set
\[
R_a=\frac12\sum_{r=1}^{a}\mathbb E\log_2\frac r{1+\operatorname{Bin}(r-1,1/2)}
+\mathbb E\log_2\frac{a+1}{1+\operatorname{Bin}(a,1/2)}.
\]
The source's coarsened forest residual is
\[
D_a=\Lambda_f+\log_2 Z_f+2a+1+\gamma R_a.
\]
The reciprocal-binomial identity and Jensen give $R_a\leq I$; concentration and Cesaro averaging give $R_a/I\to1$. Thus $D_a/I\to16/5$, while the complete forest theorem cost has limit
\[
K_{\rm ref}+16/5,\qquad K_{\rm ref}=3+2\log_2 3+\log_2(6e).
\]
The source's separate scalar-queue upper envelope has limit $\log_2(6e)+16/5$. Neither coarsened expression is the literal queue cost.
