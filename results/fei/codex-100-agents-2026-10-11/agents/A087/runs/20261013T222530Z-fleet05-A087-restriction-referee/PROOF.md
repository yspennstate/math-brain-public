Let C be a 0/1 selector of physical probability q independent of a Boolean core u, and CW=0, W^2=1-C. F=Cu+W. At an actual core query m=Eu, beta=hat u(i), t=beta^2, v=1-m^2. Put a_T=hat W(T)+m hat C(T), c_T=hat C(T), Phi(A,B)=phi(A)+phi(B)-phi(A+B), phi(A)=A log2(1/A). Define M(s)=sum Phi(a_T^2,s c_T^2). Splitting the core frequency into empty/nonempty sectors gives exactly B_i(F)=q[B_i(u)-Phi(m^2,t)]+M(t). This uses sum c_T^2=q and includes the signed empty pair. Nonempty core frequencies tensor with C; the empty-frequency packet uses a_T, not separate fallback spectra.

Independently define J=sum [phi(a_T^2+t c_T^2)-(phi((a_T+beta c_T)^2)+phi((a_T-beta c_T)^2))/2]. The analogous physical entropy identity is D_i(F)=q[D_i(u)-d(m,beta)]+M(t)+J, where d(a,b)=phi(a^2)+phi(b^2)-(phi((a+b)^2)+phi((a-b)^2))/2. Actual children have physical weights 1/2. Since sum a_T c_T=mq, their empty-core-sector masses need not agree when m beta is nonzero. J is the unnormalized concavity gap, so it is nonnegative and bounded by 2qt/ln2 by the scalar JS/Hellinger inequality. Describing it as a fixed-mass normalized JS without the mass correction is invalid at biased descendants.

For completeness the scalar bound is reconstructed: for z=2|ab|/(a^2+b^2), write d(z)=[(1+z)ln(1+z)+(1-z)ln(1-z)]/2. The derivative of z/sqrt(1-z^2)-atanh(z) is (1-z^2)^(-3/2)-(1-z^2)^(-1)>=0. Integration from zero proves d(z)<=1-sqrt(1-z^2). Multiplication by a^2+b^2 gives the natural-unit scalar gap <=2 min(a^2,b^2), hence <=2b^2. Summation proves J<=2qt/ln2 without assuming equal projected-sector masses.

For v>0, t<=v. Monotonicity of Phi(A,s) and the derivative of Phi(A,s)/s+log2(s), equal to [s-A ln(1+s/A)]/(s^2 ln2)>=0, prove M(t)<=min(M(v),t M(v)/v+qt log2(v/t)). Parseval and disjointness give sum a_T^2=1-qv and sum v c_T^2=qv. Conditional entropy concavity then gives M(v)<=h2(qv). As h2(x)<=x log2(e/x), the second branch is at most qt[log2(1/q)+1/ln2]+qt log2(1/t), with exact variance-log cancellation. Boolean derivatives give |beta|<=Inf_i(u), and t log2(1/t)<=2 Inf_i(u)/(e ln2). Thus cap<=c_data q Inf_i(u)+q log2(1/q)t, c_data=1/ln2+2/(e ln2)<4. All zero endpoints follow by continuity.

Adaptive data histories form rectangles in the two independent core cubes. Therefore every actual conditional core remains uniform on its unrevealed coordinates, even under interleaving. At a query v-E v_child=beta^2. Per-core sum w beta^2 equals its root variance minus its expected actual terminal variance; sum w Inf_i(u_node) equals spent core influence. Queries in the other core have no local variance decrement. These martingale identities remain valid under history-dependent stopping and coordinate choice on finite cubes. Controls are not queried during this prefix, so q=P or R stays fixed.

For priority events C={x=0,A=1}, D={x=o,B=1} excluding C, p=2^-r,k=2^-s, P=p/2, R=(1-p)k/2 (o=0) or k/2 (o=1). Let a=1-Eu,b=1-Ev. Each private A edge has reach probability p and fallback mismatch (1-k)a/2+k(1-Eu Ev)/2 for same orientation; each private B edge has influence Rb; the shared bit has influence Pa+Rb. Hence Inf_A>=Pa/2 for k<=1/2. This proves I_ctrl>=(r/2+1)Pa+(s+1)Rb. The reserve P(r+1)Var(u)+R log2(1/R)Var(v) is at most 2(r+1)Pa+2(s+2)Rb<=4I_ctrl, since r,s>=1 and log2(1/(1-p))<=1. Opposite orientation uses Inf_A=Pa, Inf_B=Rb, so its reserve is <=2(r+1)Pa+2(s+1)Rb<=4(rPa+sRb)<=4I_ctrl. The common-bit influence is not charged to two accounts. The prefix bank is therefore <=c_data I_data+4I_ctrl<=4I(F), for complete or partial data-only trees; for partial trees the sharper variance-drop and spent-influence form is retained.

## New source revision: arbitrary exclusive controller

The live A057 proof gained sections 3--4 during this audit; a separate byte-pinned snapshot is out/source_A057_revision2.txt. Its arbitrary-selector reserve argument is independently reconstructed here, without any fresh-event or private-width assumption. A controller on an arbitrary finite uniform physical cube chooses finitely many Boolean cores on independent disjoint data cubes, or constant signs. Let C_j be its disjoint core selectors, q_j=E C_j, V_j=1-m_j^2. Indicator influence means probability of change, not the Fourier energy of a 0/1 indicator.

The cube edge-isoperimetric inequality in the exact normalization needed is I(C)>=2q log2(1/q). Proof by induction: for slice measures q0,q1, the crossing probability is >=|q0-q1|, and the average inductive slice influences are >=q0 log2(1/q0)+q1 log2(1/q1). Their sum minus 2q log2(1/q), q=(q0+q1)/2, is 2q[h2(z)-2min(z,1-z)], z=q0/(2q), which is nonnegative by entropy concavity. Empty endpoints are immediate. Thus no outside theorem or uncertain influence convention is needed.

At a control edge between distinct independent cores j,k, mismatch probability is (1-m_j m_k)/2. Since V_j+V_k=2-m_j^2-m_k^2<=2(1-m_j m_k), the sum of the variances of the two selector indicators changing at that edge is at most four times the actual pivotal probability. Constants have variance zero and means +/-1 and satisfy the same inequality. Edges within a selector state have no charge even though two independent copies of that core would mismatch: it is the same physical core on both ends. Summing original physical edges once yields sum_j V_j I(C_j)<=4 I_ctrl(F), hence sum_j q_j log2(1/q_j)V_j<=2 I_ctrl(F).

The per-core variance-drop identity on interleaved data-only trees therefore proves cap bank <=c_data Pi_I,data+sum_j q_j log2(1/q_j)V_drop,j<=c_data I_data+2 I_ctrl<=c_data I(F), c_data=(1+2/e)/ln2. Adding J only spends 2 Pi_I,data/ln2 once, so cap-plus-J bank <=[(3+2/e)/ln2]I_data+2I_ctrl<=[(3+2/e)/ln2]I(F). Elementary e in (2,3), ln2 in (1/2,3/4) imply 2<c_data<4, enough for the last comparisons. These bounds pay the positive outer sector and its concavity gap only. The controller may have any dimension or finite number of states, and the proof supplies the finite-to-all-dimensional bridge; the census supplies checks of normalization, not that bridge.

## Exact projected-sector mass and frontier witnesses

Put M=1-qv+qt and M_+=M+2qm beta, M_-=M-2qm beta. Let P_+,P_- be normalized projected child spectra when their masses are positive, rho_+=M_+/(2M), rho_-=M_-/(2M). Direct homogeneous-entropy algebra gives J=M JS_rho(P_+,P_-)+M[1-h2(rho_+)]. Zero masses are treated by continuity and omitted; if M=0, J=0. The second summand is a projected-sector mass correction. The spectral weights rho are not physical branch probabilities, which stay 1/2. This concerns a projected empty-core sector, not the full normalized physical child spectra in the coherent transcript lift.

## Exact remaining signed inner ledger, without a reset

Define E_ne(u)=H(u)-phi((Eu)^2), the raw entropy of nonempty core frequencies. For every actual query, d(m,beta)=phi(m^2)+phi(beta^2)-E phi(m_child^2). Substitution gives D_i(u)-d(m,beta)=E_ne(u)-E E_ne(u_child)-phi(beta^2). Thus on any finite interleaved data-only prefix, including history-dependent partial stopping, the sum of the signed inner terms for core j is exactly E_ne(u_j)-E E_ne(u_j,leaf)-sum_{nodes querying j} w phi(beta_node^2). The expectation uses the actual joint stopping frontier. Queries of other independent cores have no change in this conditional core entropy and mean-square potential.

Consequently, with fixed selector probabilities q_j, the full exact root entropy is

H(F)=E H(F_leaf)+sum_j q_j[E_ne(u_j)-E E_ne(u_j,leaf)]-sum_data_nodes w q_j phi(beta_node^2)+sum_data_nodes w[M_node+J_node].

Every term is in bits. There is no control-first frontier substitution, history reset, or second influence account. For complete constant-core leaves, E_ne(u_j,leaf)=0. The new selector theorem pays the last positive bank using cap>=M and its J bound. The exact first remaining payment line is therefore a bound on the actual terminal entropy plus the signed nonempty-core entropy drop minus the already-declared singleton-entropy bank, compatible with the same exclusively allocated influence. Its dependence on arbitrary core nonempty entropy explains why this is still FEI-strength.
