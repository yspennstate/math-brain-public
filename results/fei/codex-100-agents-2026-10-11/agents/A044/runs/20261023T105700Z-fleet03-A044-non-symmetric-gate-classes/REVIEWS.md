Reconstruction: expand F=1-X+Xg and compute all three types of mixed
coefficients directly. A042's dyadic shifts are exact with the stated
upper-boundary convention. Dividing its gate scale by its actual outer
deficit gives ln4[1+a*j(a)/(1-a)+k/(2^k-1)]<=3ln4.
For its reference, partition nonempty physical labels into X and seed
labels {T,XT}; both physical and reference conditional placement laws
are (epsilon,1-epsilon), so they cancel before taking logarithms. This
gives the stated ordinary KL with epsilon K_g retained. Subtract the two
complete entropy identities to obtain the signed conversion; use
ln x<=x-1 and Z_outer>=w to bound its positive normalizer component.
The endpoint limits are finite-law continuity with a fixed seed and
interior Bernoulli arguments. Constants and reflected selectors are
handled separately as the source does. The optional imported A044
normalizer bound is not needed for those identities.

The present independent helper checks the dyadic specializations on
1,016 nonconstant seed/selector laws, within the frozen 2,048 activation
cases, by physical coefficients and complete atom likelihoods. Its
finite counts do not replace the arbitrary-arity analytic proof, and
the source's own test totals were not re-run or adopted as our counts.

The general-selector extension in this run makes clear why the dyadic
hypothesis cannot simply be deleted from the zero-carry/reference
identities. This is an extension and obstruction to a tempting stronger
statement, not a repair or objection to the actual A042 theorem.
