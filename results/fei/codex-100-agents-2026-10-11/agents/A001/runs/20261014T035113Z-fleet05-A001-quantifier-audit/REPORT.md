## 3. Exact binary loss and its single-owner obstruction

    w=Pr_mu(E)=4^(1-r)=4alpha^2,

    D_y=sum_sigma N_sigma delta log2(A/R_sigma).

    D0=D((w,1-w)||(z,1-z))+w D_y,
