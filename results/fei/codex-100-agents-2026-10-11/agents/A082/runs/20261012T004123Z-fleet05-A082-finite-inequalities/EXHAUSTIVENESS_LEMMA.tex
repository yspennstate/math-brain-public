\title{The five-coordinate certificate: covering and physical exhaustiveness}
\author{A082, independent review of A071}\maketitle
All entropies are in bits. Put $P_f(S)=\widehat f(S)^2$,
$B_i=H(S_i\mid S_{-i})$, $I_i=\Pr(f(x)\ne f(x+e_i))$,
and $K_5=16/3-(3/8)\log_2 3-(169/24)\log_2 13+(89/24)\log_2 89$.

\begin{theorem}[Finite covering]
The following covering filters on odd five-bit negative masks exhaust all
nonconstant Boolean functions up to output negation, input translation and
physical coordinate permutation: minority size at most $16$, nondecreasing
physical boundary counts, and minimality among all translates containing zero.
They need not give unique orbits.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Negate the output if necessary to make the negative class a nonempty set $A$
of size at most $16$. Translate any member of $A$ to zero. Permute the five
physical coordinates to put their influences in nondecreasing order. This
permutation fixes zero. Next select the smallest encoded translate of $A$
among translates containing zero. Translation leaves the physical influence
vector unchanged. Thus all filters can hold simultaneously. Output negation
preserves the squared spectrum; input translation multiplies Walsh
coefficients by characters and also preserves the squared spectrum.
Physical permutations merely reorder the five $B_i/I_i$ ratios.
Consequently the covering preserves their minimum. No general linear mixing
of input coordinates is admitted.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Exact finite arithmetic.}
For a negative class $A$, the Walsh half-coefficients are
\[
 c_s=16\mathbf1_{s=0}-|A|+2|\{x\in A:s\cdot x=1\}|,
 \qquad P(s)=c_s^2/256.
\]
Our reproduction computes these directly by population counts of parity masks,
without the author's butterfly transform. For a spectral pair put $a=c_T^2$,
$b=c_{T+i}^2$ and $G(a,b)=(a+b)\log_2(a+b)-a\log_2 a-b\log_2 b$.
Then $B_i=\sum G/256$ and $I_i=D_i/256$, where
$D_i=\sum_{T\not\ni i}c_{T+i}^2$. Physical boundary counts give a separate
check $D_i=16 e_i$, with $e_i$ the number of undirected crossing edges.

To enclose logarithms reduce an integer to $2^k r$ with $1\le r<2$.
For $z=(r-1)/(r+1)$, the rational $50$-term sum encloses $\ln r$ with
positive remainder at most $2z^{101}/[101(1-z^2)]$.
Divide numerator endpoints by opposite positive $\ln2$ endpoints, add the
exact integer $k$, and round outward at $2^{-110}$. Combine positive and
negative coefficients of $G$ with opposite endpoints and round its upper
bound outward at $2^{-40}$. Thus every packet and every sum gives a rigorous
upper bound. There is no floating-point sign decision.

The target lower bound is $2964159675584/2^{40}<K_5$.
For each table a relevant coordinate with
$\sum G_{\rm upper}\le2964159675584 D_i$ proves a strict upper pass.
The remaining mask is $A=\{0,1,2\}$: exact prime-log vectors show that three
coordinate ratios equal $K_5$ and the other two exceed it. Rational vector
differences are cleared of denominators and checked by integer products.
The integer intermediate bounds are at most
$16(512\cdot2^{40})+256<2^{63}$ for numerator sums, and
$256\cdot2964159675584<2^{63}$ for target products, so the signed int64
computation cannot overflow.

The finite certificate concludes that every nonconstant five-bit table has
a relevant $i$ with $B_i\le K_5 I_i$. All extremizers belong to the orbit of
the seed, since every other retained representative passes strictly.
There are $32\binom52=320$ seed translates/direction choices and $320$
distinct output complements: the center is the unique degree-two vertex of
the three-vertex induced path. Thus precisely $640$ truth tables attain the bound.

\begin{theorem}[Separate all-dimensional application]
Every nonconstant Boolean sign whose Fourier support is contained in an affine
space $c+V\subseteq\mathbb F_2^n$ with $\dim V\le5$ has a relevant physical
coordinate with $B_i\le K_5 I_i$. This holds hereditarily under all physical
restrictions and for disjoint products of such factors. In this class,
$H(f)\le(K_5+2/\ln2)I(f)$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
If a relevant $e_i$ lies outside $V$, no two supported Fourier sets differ by
$e_i$, so $B_i=0$. Both physical child squared spectra coincide under projection,
hence their entropy equals the parent entropy and the JS decrement is zero.
If every relevant $e_i$ lies in $V$, their independence shows that at most five
physical variables are relevant. Delete irrelevant variables and apply the
finite certificate. Every physical restriction projects the affine support
and cannot increase its dimension, so the argument applies at every descendant.
For disjoint products, squared spectra are independent, preserving each factor's
coordinate influence and conditional erasure entropy; restrictions preserve
the factorization. Finally use the exact identities
\[
 H(f)-\tfrac12[H(f_+)+H(f_-)]=B_i+\operatorname{JS}_i,
 \quad I(f)=I_i+\tfrac12[I(f_+)+I(f_-)],
\]
and $\operatorname{JS}_i\le(2/\ln2)I_i$. Induction on physical dimension
pays every entropy decrement and leaves only constant terminals of zero entropy.
The scalar JS bound is the previously reconstructed B17/C005 interface.
The lower seed makes $K_5$ optimal for selectable erasure within this whole
structural class; no optimality is claimed for the resulting $H/I$ bound.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{First unsupported universal line.}
No bound here controls the dimension of $V$ for arbitrary Boolean residuals.
Reaching these leaves by a stopping tree with total nonreused entropy cost
bounded by original influence remains open. The known six-bit matching witness
already exceeds $K_5$, so this constant cannot be universal. Aggregate erasure
$\sum_iB_i\le K_5 I$ is not asserted. Parity-coded zero erasure alone is not
a terminal-class certificate. Overlapping product factors are excluded.
