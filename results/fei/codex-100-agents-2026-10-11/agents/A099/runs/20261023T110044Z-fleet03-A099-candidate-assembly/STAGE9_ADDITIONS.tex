\subsection{The common payment identity}
\label{sec:centroid9}
For a physical query let $P$ be its parent spectrum, $P_+,P_-$ the
two actual child squared spectra, and $Q$ a normalized finite-CE
complete reference. Let $\bar Q=M_jQ$, $\bar P=M_jP$.
Its exact occupancy refund is
\[
r_{\mathrm{occ}}=\KL(P\Vert Q)-\KL(\bar P\Vert\bar Q)\ge0,
\quad
B_j+\tfrac12\sum_\tau\CE(P_\tau,\bar Q)-\CE(P,Q)
=-r_{\mathrm{occ}}.
\]
This is the earlier complete-reference chain; neither a label-channel
cost nor a new reference has been smuggled into it.

Choose a partition $\Pi$ of child frequency labels BEFORE observing
the physical outcome. Write $p_\tau=\Pi P_\tau$, $q=\Pi\bar Q$ and
$\bar p=(p_++p_-)/2$. Calibrate each inherited reference to the
actual masses on these blocks, retaining its conditional law within
each block. Its exact CE decrease is
$\rho_\tau=\KL(p_\tau\Vert q)$: within a block the factor is
$p_\tau(B)/q(B)$, and expansion of the CE proves the identity.
Finite CE ensures $q(B)>0$ on every actually occupied block;
zero actual blocks are assigned zero mass explicitly.
For $\rho=(\rho_++\rho_-)/2$ one has the exact centroid identity
\[
\rho=\operatorname{JS}(p_+,p_-)+\KL(\bar p\Vert q),\qquad
\operatorname{JS}(p_+,p_-)=H(\bar p)-\tfrac12\sum_\tau H(p_\tau).
\tag{PC9}\label{eq:centroid9}
\]
Indeed adding and subtracting $\log_2\bar p$ in each KL sum
leaves the two child-to-midpoint divergences and one midpoint-to-$q$
divergence. Terms at an unoccupied zero midpoint are omitted.
No outcome-dependent comparison reference is used.

Pinsker and the Euclidean parallelogram identity give
\[
\rho\ge\frac{\|\Delta\|_2^2+\|\bar p-q\|_2^2}{2\ln2},
\qquad\Delta=(p_+-p_-)/2.
\tag{PP9}\label{eq:euclid9}
\]
For completeness, log-sum first groups positive probability
differences. Binary KL has second derivative
$1/[u(1-u)\ln2]\ge4/\ln2$ in its first argument, so integration
from equality gives $\KL(v\Vert q)\ge\|v-q\|_1^2/(2\ln2)
\ge\|v-q\|_2^2/(2\ln2)$. Averaging the two Euclidean squares
proves the displayed bound. Endpoints follow by finite support or limits.

After calibration, changing a tail of actual mass $t$ from current
conditional reference $M$ to $(1-\epsilon)M+\epsilon S$, for any
prescribed probability law $S$, costs at most
\[
t\log_2\frac1{1-\epsilon}\le x/\ln2,
\qquad\epsilon=x/(t+x),\quad t,x>0.
\tag{PM9}\label{eq:mixture9}
\]
The first bound follows atomwise from the retained $(1-\epsilon)M$;
the second is $\ln(1+x/t)\le x/t$. It remains valid for target zeros
or newly introduced ghost labels because $\epsilon<1$ preserves
occupied old support. At zero tail or zero energy make no mixture.
If the average actual calibration refund is at least $2x/\ln2$,
then both full child mixture bills, averaged with physical weights,
are at most $x/\ln2$. The full transaction therefore satisfies
\[
B_j+\tfrac12\sum_\tau\CE(P_\tau,Q^{\mathrm{new}}_\tau)
-\CE(P,Q)\le-r_{\mathrm{occ}}-x/\ln2.
\tag{PT9}\label{eq:payment9}
\]
This scalar interface is a reconstruction of the existing signed-reset
account and block-calibration arguments, not another broad candidate.
Equation~\eqref{eq:centroid9} also identifies the exact calibration
nullspace: $\rho=0$ iff $p_+=p_-=q$. Isospectral children alone do
not imply zero calibration credit. Occupancy credit is a separate term.

\subsection{Complete coherent Boolean wrapper and paid restoration}
\label{sec:coherent9}
Let $a:\{-1,1\}^k\to\{-1,0,1\}$, $b=1-a^2$, and let Boolean
$h$ use disjoint uniform seed bits. Set $G=a+\sigma bh$, $\sigma\in\{-1,1\}$.
Write $A_U=\widehat a(U)$, $B_U=\widehat b(U)$, $m=\E h$,
$\beta=\sum_U B_U^2$. Supply a normalized finite-cost seed law $R$
with $R(0)=m^2$. The complete coherent reference is
\[
Q(U,0)=(A_U+\sigma mB_U)^2,\qquad
Q(U,T)=B_U^2R(T)\quad(T\ne0).
\tag{CQ9}\label{eq:coherent9}
\]
Since $ab=0$, Parseval gives $\sum AB=0$ and
$\sum A^2=1-\beta$. Thus this law is normalized; its true tail
mass is $\beta(1-m^2)$. No influence price is obtained from normalization.

At a seed query $j$, write $u_T=\widehat h(T)$,
$v_T=\widehat h(T+j)$ for $j\notin T$, $d=v_0$.
Actual child seed amplitudes are $u_T\pm v_T$, with means $m\pm d$.
Booleanity gives $\sum_Tu_Tv_T=0$ by the Fourier coefficient of $h^2=1$.
The common inherited nonempty row is $B_U^2\bar R(T)$, where
$\bar R=M_jR$. Calibrating each controller/vacuum atom and each
controller/nonempty-bin block to actual child mass gives the coherent law
\[
R^{\mathrm{cal}}_\tau(0)=(m+\tau d)^2,\qquad
R^{\mathrm{cal}}_\tau(T)=P_{h_\tau}(W_\ell)
\frac{\bar R(T)}{\bar R(W_\ell)}\quad(T\in W_\ell).
\]
Positive true bins have positive inherited normalizers; zero true bins
are set to zero. Within-bin reference-only mass is retained. This
operation is a PAID block calibration of the current complete law,
not a free substitution of the actual fine spectrum.

Controller queries are paid too. If a controller coordinate is deleted,
write $A^\tau_U=A_U+\tau A_{U+j}$ and $B^\tau_U=B_U+\tau B_{U+j}$.
The common inherited nonempty row is
$(B_U^2+B_{U+j}^2)R(T)$. Calibration of each individual vacuum atom
and each whole nonempty row to actual child masses gives precisely
$(A^\tau_U+\sigma mB^\tau_U)^2$ and $(B^\tau_U)^2R(T)$.
The same CE/block-KL calculation pays it, with no nonempty-profile
replacement hidden in this restoration. Recompute all controller
constants at a restricted child. A zero row, $\beta=0$, or a constant
seed uses explicit zero-tail formulas. These derivations provide the
coherent fragment credited to A079/A032 without importing any of

\subsection{Physical phase and optimal two-bin energy}
\label{sec:bins9}
Put
\[
C=\sum A_U^2B_U^2,\quad D=\sum A_UB_U^3,\quad
E_4=\sum B_U^4,\quad
\Gamma=C-D^2/E_4\ge0,
\]
with $\Gamma=0$ when $E_4=0$. Cauchy--Schwarz proves nonnegativity.
Also $F_m=\sum B_U^2(\sigma A_U+mB_U)^2\ge\Gamma$ by completing
the square (when $E_4>0$).
For any nonempty seed partition define $c_\ell=\sum_{T\in W_\ell}u_Tv_T$.
The half-difference of the two actual coarse child probabilities is
\[
2dB_U(\sigma A_U+mB_U)\quad\hbox{at }(U,0),\qquad
2B_U^2c_\ell\quad\hbox{at }(U,W_\ell).
\]
Equation~\eqref{eq:euclid9}, even dropping its mean-offset term, gives
\[
\rho\ge\frac{2}{\ln2}
\left(d^2F_m+E_4\sum_\ell c_\ell^2\right)
\ge2x/\ln2,\qquad
x=\Gamma d^2+E_4\sum_\ell c_\ell^2.
\tag{PE9}\label{eq:phase9}
\]

Equation~\eqref{eq:mixture9} proves A097's stated complete transaction.

For fixed phases $u_Tv_T$, the two bins of nonnegative and negative

block to increase the square sum, then merge like-sign blocks, which
increases it again. At $d=0$, Booleanity implies
$\sum_{T\ne0}u_Tv_T=0$, hence
\[
x=\frac{E_4}{2}\left(\sum_{T\ne0}|u_Tv_T|\right)^2.

For $\beta>0$ this is positive exactly when the two child squared
