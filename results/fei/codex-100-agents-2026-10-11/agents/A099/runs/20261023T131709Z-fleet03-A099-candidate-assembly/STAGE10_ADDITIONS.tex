\section{Zero-credit histories: complete ancestor accounting and admission}
This section incorporates A085's arbitrary-core depth obstruction,
A096's geometric-account theorem, A097's declared-floor admission, and
A003's distinction between entropy payment and influence allocation.
All are supplied-source or explicitly finite-core statements. The four
universal conjectures in the preceding source remain conjectures. All
logarithms in this section have base two.

\begin{lemma}[Parity-block isometry and legal deferral]\label{lem:v10lift}
For any Boolean core $g$ on $m$ bits replace each input by the parity of a
disjoint block of $L\ge2$ physical bits. Then $h_L=g(y_1,\ldots,y_m)$ has
Fourier coefficient $\widehat g(J)$ at the union of blocks indexed by $J$,
and zero elsewhere. In particular $H(h_L)=H(g)$ and $I(h_L)=LI(g)$.
A normalized finite-cost core reference $S$ pushes to the same block
unions with its entire mass, including reference-only atoms, and
$\operatorname{CE}(P_{h_L},S_{\rm lift})=\operatorname{CE}(P_g,S)$.
After revealing any physical coordinates while leaving at least one in
each block, write the remaining sizes as $\ell_j$ and revealed parity
signs as $\theta_j$. The entropy remains $H(g)$, and the influence is
$\sum_j\ell_j\operatorname{Inf}_j(g)$. A query in a block of size at least
two has zero erasure, identical child squared spectra, and zero inherited
occupancy refund. With matched references every calibration refund is
zero; a mismatched reference can release already paid KL.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Expand $g=\sum_J\widehat g(J)\chi_J$ and substitute the independent block
characters. At a restriction the coefficient is multiplied by
$\prod_{j\in J}\theta_j$ and its label becomes the union of remaining
blocks. Distinct $J$ remain distinct; squares and every reference mass
are preserved. A physical bit in block $j$ has influence
$\operatorname{Inf}_j(g)$ by conditioning on the other parity inputs.
If its block has at least two bits, toggling that singleton cannot connect
two block-union labels. Thus each queried fiber has at most one occupied
endpoint, for both the actual and lifted reference laws. Deletion is
injective and preserves the true and reference probabilities. The two
child coefficients $u\pm v$ have the same square because $uv=0$.
The occupancy KL is zero since each positive true fiber and its reference
use the same endpoint. Exact matched laws have zero KL for every
partition; an arbitrary mismatched law need not. The coherent-wrapper
formula already proved in the preceding section gives retained mismatch
$\beta D(P_g\Vert S)$, not zero. For depth $D\ge1$, choosing $L=D+1$
ensures every query up to that depth occurs before its block becomes a
singleton. This proves the stated arbitrary-depth obstruction for all
adaptive choices, without a finite-search extrapolation.
\end{proof}

The smallest entropy-bearing all-coordinate phase-null seed is
$h=(1-x_1x_2-x_1x_3-x_2x_3)/2$, with law $1/4$ at the empty label and
three pairs, $H=2$ and $I=3/2$. In dimensions at most two an
adjacency-free occupied support has at most two opposite labels. If both
opposite coefficients were nonzero, the Boolean autocorrelation identity
at their difference would read twice their product equal to zero, a
contradiction. Hence those dimensions have one-atom spectra and entropy
zero. At any query of this three-bit seed, both children have uniform
four-atom spectra. With the fixed controller $\beta=3/8$, replacing each
conditional nonempty tail by its $63/64$ mixture with a singleton costs
on each full child
$\frac3{32}\log(131072/130977)>0$; the conditional probability ratios
are $32/33,64/63,64/63$. All current matched query refunds vanish.
This proves positive optional conversion cost, not that conversion is
intrinsically required. Width-two source pricing of all arbitrary cores
already implies FEI within factor two by the isometry.

\begin{theorem}[Full-target geometric ancestor account]\label{thm:v10geo}
Consider any finite complete adaptive Boolean query tree whose every
queried pair satisfies $uv=0$. Let $P$ be the root squared law with
occupied support $\Omega$, and $\pi_l$ the probability-preserving
deletion bijection to leaf $l$, of physical reach $w_l$. Supply any
normalized finite-cost root reference $Q_0$ and complete terminal
references $Q_l$. Put
\[
 q_l(s)=Q_l(\pi_l(s)),\qquad
 G(s)=\prod_lq_l(s)^{w_l},\quad Z=\sum_{s\in\Omega}G(s),\quad R_*=G/Z.
\]
Extend $R_*$ by zero off $\Omega$. Then $0<Z\le1$ and the entire signed
replacement debit is
\[
 \Delta=D(P\Vert R_*)-D(P\Vert Q_0)-\log Z,
 \qquad -\log Z=\sum_l w_lD(\pi_lR_*\Vert Q_l)\ge0.
\]
At global reach $\rho$, with original allowance $A$, all other prior
debits and complementary terminals $W$, and actual unused
$U=A-W-\rho\operatorname{CE}(P,Q_0)$, this handoff is paid exactly when
$\rho\Delta\le U$. Disjoint frontier debits add with their actual
global reaches. No allowance is renewed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The condition $uv=0$ makes deletion a bijection on occupied labels at
each query, preserving every probability and giving erasure zero.
Compose these maps along each leaf. Finite CE implies $q_l(s)>0$ on
$\Omega$, hence $Z>0$. Weighted AM--GM and complete normalization give
$Z\le\sum_lw_l\sum_{s\in\Omega}q_l(s)\le1$; mass outside the actual
support is retained in the last inequality. By the definition of $G$,
\[
 \sum_lw_l\operatorname{CE}(P_l,Q_l)
 =-\sum_sP(s)\log G(s)=\operatorname{CE}(P,R_*)-\log Z.
\]
Subtract the incoming full CE and use $\operatorname{CE}=H+D$.
Likewise, average $\log(R_*(s)/q_l(s))$ over leaves: pointwise the result
is $-\log Z$. Averaging over $R_*$ proves the second identity. Its zero
case is equality of every full $Q_l$ with its transported $R_*$,
including zero ghost mass. With $Q_0=P$, $\Delta\ge0$, and equality
requires every $Q_l=P_l$. Finally substituting the exact new terminal
bill into $A-W$ proves the same-account and frontier assertions.
Intermediate reference changes telescope; their signed bills are not
removed by the ancestor representation.
\end{proof}

For the three-bit seed just displayed, prescribe opposite targets
$(3,3,1,1)/8$ and $(1,1,3,3)/8$ on the two transported four-atom
supports. Then $R_*=P$ but $Z=\sqrt3/2$ and
$\Delta=\frac12\log(4/3)>0$. Thus effective ancestor KL alone misses
the full bill. These references test accounting; they are not asserted
necessary for a universal FEI proof.

\begin{lemma}[Sharp declared-floor admission]\label{lem:v10floor}
Let $p$ have fixed vacuum $r$, tail $t=1-r>0$, and a specified tail event
$A$ of mass $a\in(0,t)$. For a declared admissible class
$q(0)=r$, $q(A)\ge\ell$, where $a<\ell<t$, the minimum complete
conversion cost from the matched source is
\[
 \min_qD(p\Vert q)
 =a\log(a/\ell)+(t-a)\log((t-a)/(t-\ell))>0.
\]
It is attained by preserving the conditional actual laws in $A$ and
its tail complement and assigning masses $\ell,t-\ell$ respectively.
For coherent complementary wrappers the full minimum is multiplied
by $\beta$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Log-sum on the two tail events gives the lower bound at $z=q(A)$.
Its derivative is $t(z-a)/(z(t-z)\ln2)>0$ for $z>a$; its minimum
over $z\ge\ell$ occurs at $\ell$. The displayed conditional rescaling
achieves equality and keeps every occupied denominator positive.
Reference-only atoms have zero mass at this minimizer, as required by
their complete normalization. In the wrapper the true signed vacuum
is unchanged, and the nonempty probability and reference in each
controller row share factor $\widehat b(U)^2$. Summing its KL gives
$\beta D(p\Vert q)$ exactly.
\end{proof}

For the fixed four-bit controller from the preceding section and either

\]
The terminal reaches sum to one; they do not multiply the cost by

This is class-relative necessity paid by an identified finite-core

\begin{theorem}[Entropy deferral versus influence compression]\label{thm:v10rate}
For a parity-block lift of any Boolean core, every legal deferral
frontier $F$ has entropy $H(g)$ at all terminals. Write
$J_F=\sum_{v\in F}\pi_vI(v)$ and $J=I(g)$. At supplied original
allowance $K L J$ the entropy-payment residual is always $K L J-H(g)$.
If a continuation additionally earmarks $K\operatorname{Inf}_i$ at
every physical query, its different residual is $KJ_F-H(g)$. Requiring
the latter nonnegative at every completely unwrapped cheaply admitted
matched lift, for a fixed $K>0$, is equivalent to FEI with that exact
$K$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Lemma~\ref{lem:v10lift} gives zero erasure and unchanged full CE at
every legal query, while influence decreases by its core coordinate
influence. Summing this decrease with physical reaches gives total
earmarked influence $LJ-J_F$. This proves the two residual formulas.
When all blocks have size one, $J_F=J$. Every nonconstant $n$-bit core
has $J\ge2^{1-n}$ from a changing cube edge, and $H(g)\le n$.
Choose integer $L\ge\max(2,\lceil n2^{n-1}/K\rceil)$; then the exact
root source is cheaply admitted without FEI, since $H(g)\le KLJ$.
The asserted endpoint condition yields $H(g)\le KJ$ for every core.
Conversely FEI($K$) and $J_F\ge J$ give all such residuals
nonnegative. Constants have both quantities zero. In the concrete
majority-three guard, $H=2,J=3/2,L=2,K=1$, the original residual
stays $1$ while the influence-earmarked endpoint residual is $-1/2$.
No target reference improves exact CE below $H$.
\end{proof}
