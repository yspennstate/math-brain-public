\section{A supplied source bank pays a negative-cell floor}
\label{sec:floor}
This is A097's version-1 theorem, independently accepted at its full stated
scope by A098 and reconstructed here by A099~\cite{A097e,A098e}. All costs are in bits. It
does not supply hereditary references for an arbitrary physical tree.

Let $a\ge1,b\ge0,n=a+b,r\ge1$, $s=2^{-a},t=2^{-n},p=2^{-r}$.
Let $A_j$ indicate a fixed point of a $j$-cube, and put
\[
 g=1-2A_a,\qquad F=g+2A_rD_n=1-2A_a(1-A_rA_b).
\]
Here $D_n$ is any specified negative data point of $g$; cube translations
reduce it to the displayed point without changing squared spectra.
Write $\mu=1-2s$, $\rho=4pt$, $\lambda=2p2^{-b}=\rho/(2s)\le1$.
The coefficients of $g$ are $\mu$ at the vacuum, $-2s$ on nonempty
active subsets, and zero elsewhere. Direct expansion of the point edit
therefore gives the data spectral marginal
\[
 q=(1-\lambda)P_g+\lambda\mu\delta_\emptyset+\rho U_n.
\]
These are nonnegative weights summing to one. Take any normalized source
reference $R$ positive on the source support, with finite supplied cost
$c=\operatorname{CE}(P_g,R)$. Calibrate its vacuum mass to $\mu^2$,
preserving the conditional nonempty reference, obtaining $R_0$. The
binary chain rule gives
\[
 c_0=\operatorname{CE}(P_g,R_0)
 =c-D_{\rm Ber}(\mu^2\Vert R(\emptyset))\le c.
\]
Set $R'=(1-\lambda)R_0+\lambda\mu\delta_\emptyset+\rho U_n$,
and lift by the actual conditional control kernel:
$Q(U,T)=R'(T)P_F(U,T)/q(T)$. This is a complete normalized reference.
The uniform floor makes every data normalizer positive. References with
zeros off the original source support are allowed.

\begin{theorem}\label{thm:floor}
Let $C=2ptb$, $E=2pta$, $J=2rpt$. Then
\[
 I(F)=I(g)+C-E+J,\quad I(g)=2as,\quad
 \operatorname{CE}(P_F,Q)\le c+10(C+E+J).
\]
For $K\ge10$, the supplied bank $S_K=KI(g)-c$ satisfies
\[
 KI(F)-\operatorname{CE}(P_F,Q)
 \ge S_K+(K-10)C-(K+10)E+(K-10)J.
\]
In particular, $c\le C_0I(g)$ implies
$\operatorname{CE}(P_F,Q)\le(2C_0+10)I(F)$. The structural choice
$R=P_g$ gives $\operatorname{CE}(P_F,Q)\le18I(F)$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Physical edge counting gives each active influence $2s-2pt$, each dummy
influence $2pt$, and each control influence $2pt$, proving the influence
identity. Use the same three mixture labels for the true and reference
data laws. Their posterior divergence is
$\kappa=\sum_Tq(T)D(\pi_P(\cdot\mid T)\Vert\pi_R(\cdot\mid T))$.
The chain rule and the common actual conditional kernel give exactly
\[
 D(P_F\Vert Q)=(1-\lambda)D(P_g\Vert R_0)-\kappa,
\]
\[
 \operatorname{CE}(P_F,Q)=(1-\lambda)c_0+\rho n+
 H(\text{labels})-H(\text{labels}\mid T)-\kappa+
 H(S_{\rm controls}\mid S_{\rm data}).
\]
The labels are analytical mixture labels, not a fair physical history.

For $a\ge2$ let $v=1-\mu^2$, $q_0=\mu^2+\rho(\mu+t)$,
$M=1-q_0$, $A=(1-\lambda)v$, $B_0=\rho(1-t)$.
Then $R'(\emptyset)=q_0$, $M=A+B_0\le v$.
On nonempty data sets retain the source conditional reference and the
uniform nonempty reference. The complete-reference mixture inequality,
followed by dropping only $(A-v)$ times the nonnegative source conditional
cross entropy, yields, with $\eta(x)=-x\log_2x$,
\begin{align*}
 \operatorname{CE}(q,R')-c_0
 &\le\eta(q_0)-\eta(\mu^2)+\eta(M)-\eta(v)\\
 &\quad+B_0\log_2(2^n-1)+(A+B_0)h_2(B_0/(A+B_0)).
\end{align*}
The mixture inequality follows directly by adjoining its label: joint
cross entropy is the weighted component cost plus label entropy; its
marginal cross entropy cannot be larger. Thus no source debt was erased.
Since $\mu^2\ge1/4$, $\eta'\le1$ on the vacuum interval and
$q_0-\mu^2\le\rho$. Since $\eta'\ge-1/\ln2$ everywhere,
$\eta(M)-\eta(v)\le\rho/\ln2$. Their sum is at most $4\rho$.
Also $v\le4s$, $B_0\ge3\rho/4$, and
$M/B_0\le(4/3)2^{b+r}$. The inequality
$h_2(x)\le x\log_2(e/x)$ gives the label bill at most
$\rho(b+r+2)$; the subset bill is at most $\rho n$.
Hence $\operatorname{CE}(q,R')\le c+\rho(n+b+r+6)$.
Each nonempty control set has marginal mass $4p^2t$, so their total mass
is $V=\rho(1-p)$. Consequently
\[
 H(S_{\rm controls})=h_2(V)+V\log_2(2^r-1)
 \le\rho(n+2r).
\]
Indeed the preceding binary bound gives
$\rho(1-p)(n+2r-2+1/\ln2)$, which is no larger than the displayed
bound. Combining costs gives $c+\rho(2n+b+3r+6)$.
Because $b\le n$ and $n+r\ge3$, this is at most
$c+5\rho(n+r)=c+10(C+E+J)$.

For $a=1$, $\mu=0$ and $\lambda=\rho$. The data marginal is simply
$(1-\rho)P_g+\rho U_n$. The joint-label bound gives
$\operatorname{CE}(q,R')\le c+\rho n+h_2(\rho)
\le c+\rho(2n+r)$, since $\log_2(1/\rho)=n+r-2$ and
$1/\ln2<2$. Adding the control bill gives
$c+3\rho(n+r)=c+6(C+E+J)$, including $n=r=1$.

Subtract the cost bound from the physical influence identity to obtain
the signed bank inequality. Since $E/I(g)=p2^{-b}\le1/2$, setting
$K=2C_0+10$ leaves
$S_K-(K+10)E\ge[K-C_0-(K+10)/2]I(g)=0$;
the created-edge and control terms are nonnegative. The source bank is
spent once for both the changed reference and the erased edges.
For $R=P_g$, at $a\ge2$ the source entropy is
$\eta(\mu^2)+4s(1-s)(2a-2)<8as=4I(g)$, using
$\eta(\mu^2)\le4s(1-s)/\ln2$; at $a=1$ it is zero.
This proves the structural constant $18$.
\end{proof}

For any independent Boolean terminal $h$ with supplied normalized finite
reference $R_h$, use its product with $Q$. Then exactly
\[
 \operatorname{CE}_{\rm total}=c_h+\operatorname{CE}(P_F,Q),\quad
 D_{\rm total}=D_h+(1-\lambda)D(P_g\Vert R_0)-\kappa,
 \quad I_{\rm total}=I(h)+I(F).
\]
Thus $c_h\le C_hI(h)$ gives constant $\max(C_h,2C_0+10)$;
the terminal is retained, rather than funding the floor. This algebraic
product clause is included in both full scoped reconstructions.

\paragraph{First unsupported hereditary line.}
After an actual active-bit query, construct the descendant reference
from the incoming $Q$, with its normalizer and reset paid by the same
remaining bank. The Boolean child's membership in the point-edit family
does not establish this reference statement. Positive-cell edits and
arbitrary cores are not admitted by the nonnegative mixture above.

\section{A root remainder allowance is not unused child surplus}
\label{sec:childbank}
The following complete derivation is A096 version 1 at author scope~\cite{A096e};
its two new claims have not acquired independent full scoped acceptance.
Let $b$ be any $d$-bit Boolean core, $d\ge1$, $b(0)=1$, and let $g$ be
any $r$-bit Boolean gate. Put $\delta=2^{-d}$, $c=b-2\mathbf1_0$ and
$F=b+\mathbf1_0(g-1)$. Write $\beta_T=\widehat b(T)$ and
$a_V=\widehat g(V)$. Then
\[
 \widehat F(T,V)=\beta_T\mathbf1_{V=\emptyset}
       +\delta(a_V-\mathbf1_{V=\emptyset}),\qquad
 e_i=\delta\operatorname{Inf}_i(g),
\]
and $I_X=\delta I(g)$,
$I_Y=((1+a_0)I(b)+(1-a_0)I(c))/2$, by conditioning the physical gate.
For a gate query $i$, retaining $(V,\beta_T)$ retains the complete KL:
its likelihood ratio is constant on each cell. This alphabet need not
have bounded size. Retaining only $V$ loses KL only in the vacuum gate
cell. Put
\[
 \rho_i=1-\delta+\delta(a_0^2+a_i^2),\qquad
 q_i(T)=\frac{(\beta_T+\delta(a_0-1))^2+\delta^2a_i^2}{\rho_i}.
\]
Parseval and $\sum_T\beta_T=b(0)=1$ prove normalization. If $a_i\ne0$
then $q_i$ is positive, and the exact weighted loss is
$L_i=\delta a_i^2D(U_d\Vert q_i)$; for $a_i=0$ it is zero.
All other gate cells have constant core weights and zero conditional
loss. Since $\rho_i\le1$ and $q_i\ge\delta^2a_i^2$,
\[
 \sum_iL_i\le\delta d W_1+\delta\Lambda_1,\quad
 W_1=\sum_i a_i^2,\quad
 \Lambda_1=\sum_i a_i^2\log_2(1/a_i^2).
\]
The derivative identity gives $|a_i|\le\operatorname{Inf}_i(g)$;
$u\ln(1/u)\le1/e$ therefore gives
$\Lambda_1\le[2/(e\ln2)]I(g)<(9/8)I(g)$.
Every nonconstant $d$-cube Boolean function has at least $d$ boundary
edges, hence influence at least $2d\delta$. To see the boundary bound,
split a nonempty proper subset into two halves. If both are nontrivial,
induction gives $2(d-1)\ge d$ for $d\ge2$. If one is trivial and the
other nontrivial, its internal boundary gives $d-1$ and at least one
cross edge gives the last edge. Opposite trivial halves have $2^{d-1}
\ge d$ cross edges; dimension one starts the induction.
If $b,c$ are nonconstant, $I_Y\ge2d\delta$ and $W_1\le1$, so
$\delta dW_1\le I_Y/2$. If $b\equiv1$ or $c\equiv-1$, then
$I_Y=d\delta(1\mp a_0)$ and $W_1\le1-a_0^2$ gives
$\delta dW_1\le2I_Y$. Consequently
\[
 \sum_iL_i\le2I_Y+\frac{2}{e\ln2}I_X,
\]
with $2I_Y$ replaced by $I_Y/2$ when both cores are nonconstant.
This spends fresh root influence only on the quotient remainder.

\paragraph{Sharp loss-only child-bank threshold.}
For $d\ge2$ take $b=-1+2\mathbf1_0$, $c=-1$, $g(x)=x$.
The output is a $(d+1)$-bit point sign, $e=\delta$, $I_Y=d\delta$,
$I_F=(d+1)\delta$, and all functions satisfy $H\le4I$.
The exact vacuum-cell loss is $L=\delta D_0$, where
\[
 D_0=(1-\delta)(d-1)+\delta\log_2(\delta/z),\quad
 z=(1-\delta)^2+\delta^2,\quad d-2\le D_0\le d.
\]
The lower bound follows from $z\le1$ and $(2d-1)2^{-d}\le1$;
the upper follows from $\delta/z\le1$. The actual averaged child bank
at a certified constant $C\ge4$ is
\[
 S_C=\tfrac12(CI(b)-H(b)),\quad
 H(b)=\eta((1-2\delta)^2)+(4\delta-4\delta^2)(2d-2).
\]
Thus $0\le S_4\le4\delta+4(d-1)\delta^2\le5\delta$ and
$S_C=S_4+(C-4)d\delta$. It follows that
\[
 (L-S_C)/e\ge(5-C)d-7.
\]
Every $4\le C<5$ therefore fails $L\le S_C+Ke$ for any fixed $K$,
although the actual Boolean family satisfies FEI with constant four.
At $C=5$, $S_5\ge d\delta\ge L$: five is sharp for this loss-only
bank. The full source entropy bound follows, for every point-sign
dimension $m\ge2$, from
$\eta((1-2^{1-m})^2)\le4\epsilon(1-\epsilon)/\ln2$
and the other mass $4\epsilon(1-\epsilon)(2m-2)$,
$\epsilon=2^{-m}$; dimension one is a character.

To make both query children nonconstant, tensor an independent point
sign on $2d$ bits. It has influence $4d\delta^2$ and entropy at most
four times that influence. Its common spectral factor cancels in both
KL laws, so $L/e$ is unchanged, while the actual bank gains exactly
$CI(h)-H(h)$. Hence the deficit is at least
$(5-C)d-7-4Cd\delta$, still unbounded for $C<5$. At $C=5$ it
still pays the loss. This is a tensor attachment, not a single edit on
the combined cube. Neither variant disproves FEI or the possibility of
a more generous certified terminal bank.

\section{Jensen sharpens the positive-erasure remainder}
\label{sec:jensen}
A100 independently accepted the original A073 family statement~\cite{A100e}; this
does not change its version or its original digest convention. Together
with A098 this supplies two full scoped acceptances of that statement.
For A100's separate refinement, put $w_\sigma=(1+\sigma\sqrt\delta)/2$
in the exact $D_0$ formula of Section~\ref{sec:binary}. Concavity gives
\[
 D_0\le\log_2R(\delta),\qquad
 R(\delta)=\frac{1+\delta-3\delta^2/4}{1+\delta^2/4}.
\]
Indeed summing $w_\sigma/(1-\sigma\sqrt\delta+\delta/2)$ and
multiplying by $1-\delta/2$ gives this rational function exactly.
For $0<\delta\le1/4$, the derivative numerator
$1-2\delta-\delta^2/4\ge31/64$ is positive, so
$R\le R(1/4)=77/65$. The integer certificate $77^4<2\cdot65^4$
implies $L_i/e=D_0/2<1/8$. Also
$(1+\delta)(1+\delta^2/4)-(1+\delta-3\delta^2/4)
=\delta^2+\delta^3/4>0$. Therefore
\[
 L_u+L_v=\delta D_0/2<\frac{\delta^2}{2\ln2},\qquad
 L_u+L_v<\delta/8\le\operatorname{Inf}_{y_1}(F)/20.
\]
An actually common independent spectator cancels from the full and
conditional KL ratios, so the improvement also holds for that tensor
extension with its entire terminal cost retained. The retained row cost
still exceeds $(d/2+3/4)e$. A099 independently reconstructs this separate
refinement at its full stated scope; its second independent review is
pending. The twentieth of core influence is a dedicated remainder
allowance, not a free entropy bank.
