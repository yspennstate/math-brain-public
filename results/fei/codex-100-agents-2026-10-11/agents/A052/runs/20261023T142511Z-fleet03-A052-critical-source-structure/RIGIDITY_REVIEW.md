# Independent scoped reconstruction and near-optimum rigidity

Uniform finite Boolean signs; entropy bits. Let a(f) be the minimum of I over invertible affine inputs and output characters, delta=Pr[f=-1], P=(-1)^p with GF2 degree p<=d, q=2^(1-d), gamma=q-4delta>=0, and r the affine dimension of Fourier support(P). For K>=0 put Daff=H-Ka.

1. Let Z be the directions along which P(x+v)/P(x) is constant. Fourier translation shows Z annihilates every difference between two support labels. Thus codim Z=r. After translating the support by a character it lies in a linear r-space U. Multiplication by P is an orthogonal convolution on each U-coset. Each coset squared mass is conserved. The chain rule for entropy, including zero-mass cosets, bounds its conditional entropy increase by r. Hence H(fP)-H(f)<=r. No support labels are thrown away.

A nonzero multilinear GF2 polynomial of degree b has relative weight at least 2^-b: split on a variable with nonzero derivative and induct, since at least one of the two fibres is one wherever that derivative is one. Apply also to its complement. A nonconstant derivative of p has degree<=d-1, so its centered disagreement is at least q. Every directional disagreement of f is at most 2delta, hence already centered (delta<=q/4<=1/4). Centering is distance to the two constant events; the XOR triangle inequality gives m_fP(v)>=m_P(v)-Inf_v(f). Off Z, m_fP(v)-m_f(v)>=q-2Inf_v(f)>=gamma. On Z these centered costs agree exactly.

Thus a(f) is precisely the minimum basis sum of centered directional costs. An optimizing basis for fP has at least r vectors outside Z. Its sum of f costs is at least a(f), and gamma>=0 permits replacing the active count by r. Therefore a(fP)>=a(f)+r gamma and Daff(fP)<=Daff(f)-r*(K gamma-1).
