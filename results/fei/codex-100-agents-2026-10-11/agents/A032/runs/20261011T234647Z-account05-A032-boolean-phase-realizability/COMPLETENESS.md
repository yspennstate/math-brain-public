# Pure product-slice finite-domain reduction

Let P=2^m and g:{0,1}^m->{0,+1,-1} be nonzero. Write its unnormalized Walsh integers W_s. Suppose W_s^2/sum W^2 is a product law. Remove deterministic spectral bits, leaving a coordinate face with r active bits. On that face every amplitude is nonzero. The primitive rational magnitude ratio in active coordinate j is B_j/A_j with coprime positive integers. Product independence gives

|W_s|=c product_j A_j^(1-s_j) B_j^s_j.

The gcd of the integer products is1: for any prime, choose in each pair a member not divisible by that prime. Therefore an integer linear combination of these products is1, and c is an integer. The gcd of all Walsh coefficients is c. Every physical inverse sum equals0 or+/-P, and some inverse sum is+/-P, hence c divides P. In particular c is a power of two. Parseval gives c^2 product_j(A_j^2+B_j^2)=P*k, where k is the number of occupied physical points and1<=k<=P. These are exactly the template bounds in pure_product.py; all coordinate faces, both orientations of every primitive ratio, every c dividing P, and every possible factor product <=P^2 are enumerated. No particular ratio is guessed or truncated.

For a fixed magnitude template, phases at the face origin and its r coordinate-basis labels can all be made positive by a physical translation on the active coordinates followed by a global sign. These r+1 phases are independent gauges; all other2^r-r-1 phases are enumerated. Every active translation is then restored in the physical support catalogue. Translations preserve the squared spectrum and data influence. Slice global signs are immaterial for disjoint pure configurations. Inactive physical translations change only a global sign because the spectral face is deterministic there. Consequently the slice catalogue is exhaustive for the objectives and every possible support relevant to the cover search.

For each physical support and squared-magnitude histogram, only a slice of minimum exact data influence is retained. This cannot discard a maximizer: slice support determines disjointness and norm, the histogram determines entropy, and replacing one slice by the retained realization leaves the other slices and full Booleanity intact while lowering influence. The catalogue may retain fewer sign functions than the full ternary family; it preserves the optimum exactly.

Every pure Boolean F(y,x)=sum_J chi_J(y)g_J(x) has pairwise disjoint occupied g_J supports with union the physical data cube. Conversely every such cover by at most four ternary slices is Boolean at every(y,x). Its full Fourier coefficients are the slice coefficients at the associated address label. Thus H(F)=sum_J[-sum_T ghat_J(T)^2 log2 ghat_J(T)^2] and I(F)=sum_J sum_T |T|ghat_J(T)^2+sum_J |J|w_J.

The four address Hamming weights are0,1,1,2. Every assignment of slices to these labels is valid in a pure configuration. The rearrangement inequality makes the lowest influence assignment descending masses to ascending weights. This is an optimization of actual physical influence, not an assertion that arbitrary address permutations preserve influence. Empty slices are padded with mass0. Cover recursion selects the slice containing the first uncovered point, so it visits every unordered cover once; the last complementary support is looked up exactly. It records every distinct objective signature (sum of integer prime-log coefficients divided by the integer influence numerator).

The n=6 result covers only pure configurations with four-variable data. General two-address Boolean functions also permit all four partial Walsh slices to have value+/-1/2 at the same physical point, with phase signs of odd parity. Those mixed configurations are deliberately outside this stage's search. This distinction is the first remaining admission gap, not a claim that full two-address realizability is settled.
