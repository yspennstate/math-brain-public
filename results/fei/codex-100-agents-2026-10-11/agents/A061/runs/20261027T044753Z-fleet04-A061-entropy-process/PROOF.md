# Deep selected resets have an exact square-root boundary

All entropies and divergences are in bits. All physical cubes and trees are finite. The new result concerns a selected data-query policy, not arbitrary Boolean initial funding. It awaits independent review.

1. Write p_s=1-2*1[x=0] on s bits, d_s=2^(1-s), H_s=H(p_s), and M_s=s*h2(d_s). Set H_1=M_1=0. Its actual Fourier law is P_s(empty)=(1-d_s)^2 and P_s(S)=d_s^2 for nonempty S. A physical query has one constant child and one translated p_(s-1) child, with physical probabilities 1/2. Translation changes signs but preserves the squared law. Thus H_s>=H_(s-1)/2: for any Boolean f, pair coefficients a_T,b_T across the queried coordinate, and put q_T=a_T^2+b_T^2. The two actual child laws are (a_T+b_T)^2 and (a_T-b_T)^2, whose average is q. Therefore H(P_f)=H(q)+H(S_i|S_-i)>=H(q)>=average H(P_child), by the entropy chain rule and concavity. Equivalently the difference is conditional-erasure entropy plus Jensen-Shannon divergence, both nonnegative. No claim about influence payment is used here.

2. Fix m>=2 and 1<=r<=m. In each live point_m block query any unqueried coordinate until the block dies or exactly r coordinates remain. A dead block is constant. At a surviving r-bit block actually replace the inherited exact spectral reference by its coordinate-marginal product and freeze that block. The other blocks may still be queried in any adaptive order. Stopping globally before or after any query or reset is admitted; it retains the current full reference. There is no query in a frozen block. Reference-only atoms introduced by a reset remain present. This is a declared policy, not permission to reset at a larger live dimension.

For a live s-bit block, s>=r, put

    V_s=max(H_s, 2^(r-s)*M_r).

For a dead block put V=0. Before its reset at s=r this is M_r, since M_r>=H_r by subadditivity. After reset keep V=M_r, equal to the full current H+KL. For s>r,

    E[V_child]=V_(s-1)/2
      =max(H_(s-1)/2, 2^(r-s)*M_r)<=V_s.

At a reset V does not change. At every stage V dominates actual full cross entropy: before reset this is H_s, and after reset it is exactly M_r. Deterministic spectral marginals use their exact point masses, so no division by a zero marginal is needed. In particular r=1 costs zero; r=m allows immediate reset and costs M_m.

3. The reference on every active block is its exact spectrum. The full reference is the tensor of these exact block spectra, the frozen product references, and point masses for dead blocks. When a physical coordinate in an active block is queried, its coherent prediction is exactly fair and its posterior is the exact spectrum of that physical child. The other factors are unchanged, including reference-only atoms in frozen blocks. Thus all actual query outcome-KL charges are zero. No inherited reference is silently replaced except at the declared r-boundary reset. Full cross entropy is additive across the disjoint physical blocks. Therefore the sum of their V values is a nonnegative supermartingale on the filtration generated by actual revealed fair bits and the declared deterministic reset actions. Decisions are measurable in this past. A reset can be represented by a separate time step with no new random bit. For every bounded stopping cut tau,

    E[H(F_tau)+KL(P_tau||Q_tau)+sum_(queries before tau) O_v]
      <=sum_blocks V_initial.

The sum of O is exactly zero here, not an omitted cost. Adding each predictable drop V_parent-E[V_child|past] to V gives a martingale. All old debits remain spent.

4. Now take N=2^(m-1)=1/d_m identical disjoint blocks, g_m=product_(j=1)^N p_m. Its influence is I(g_m)=m. Define

    K_r = r*h2(d_r)/d_r.

Since N*2^(r-m)=1/d_r, the initial sum in section 3 is

    N*V_m=max(H(g_m),K_r).

This is the EXACT least original full-cross-entropy capital for the declared policy and every global adapted cut. Necessity follows by stopping immediately (cost H(g_m)) or by completing all the declared queries and resets. A given block survives with probability 2^(-(m-r)), independently of the adaptive order in other blocks; finite cylinder probabilities give expected full terminal CE N*2^(-(m-r))*M_r=K_r. The displayed supermartingale gives sufficiency. No optimization over all reset policies is asserted. At a complete terminal, expected H and expected KL are separately N*2^(-(m-r))*H_r and N*2^(-(m-r))*(M_r-H_r); both are retained.

The same exact cost applies, with the root floor changed, to F_m=(-1)^(uv)*g_m if physical v is queried first. Start at the actual spectral reference P_F, paying its full CE H(g_m)+2.     log2(1/d)<=h2(d)/d<=log2(1/d)+log2(e),

    r(r-1)<=K_r<=r*(r-1+log2(e))<r*(r+1/2).

    max(H(g_m)+2,r(r-1))<=A_mr
      <=max(4m+2,r*(r+1/2)).
