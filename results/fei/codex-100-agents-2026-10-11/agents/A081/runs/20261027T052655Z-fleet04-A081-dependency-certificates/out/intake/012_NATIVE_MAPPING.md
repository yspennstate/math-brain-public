## Use and provenance rules

1. Continue the installed B assignment and its verified state. Select an F topic only when it contributes to that existing mathematical obligation.
2. A contribution may carry several F tags. Keep its actual B stream identity, author, source pins, and run provenance.
3. F-state files and F ownership columns in [CLAIMS.md](CLAIMS.md) are supplemental topical pointers. They do not establish native execution or exclusive ownership of a B stream.
4. [COUNTEREXAMPLES.md](COUNTEREXAMPLES.md), [SOURCES.json](SOURCES.json), and [SOURCES_README.md](SOURCES_README.md) remain usable mathematical evidence across all B streams.
5. Coordinate through the installed B workflow. B18 and B19 retain their review functions; B20 retains synthesis. F19 and F20 do not create parallel operational authorities.
6. Preserve existing native prompts except for separately authorized supplemental references, and preserve recurrence/count settings and the deadline. This file does not update any native task.

**Full FEI remains OPEN.** This crosswalk records research relevance. It is not evidence that a role was assigned, an agent executed, a result was saved, or a theorem was accepted.
