\section{The exact target and the starting assets}

For $f:\bits^n\to\bits$ under the uniform measure, set
\[
 P_f(S)=\wh f(S)^2,\qquad
 \HH(f)=\sum_S P_f(S)\log_2\frac1{P_f(S)},\qquad
 \I(f)=\sum_S |S|P_f(S).
\]
The campaign target is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:full}
 \exists C<\infty\quad\forall n\ge1\quad\forall f:\bits^n\to\bits,
 \qquad \HH(f)\le C\I(f).
\end{equation}
The constant must be independent of dimension, arity, depth, overlap, probability of the minority output, and any unbounded representation parameter. An explicit large constant would settle the conjecture. Optimizing that constant is secondary to proving its finiteness.

Throughout this report, entropy is in bits. Some historical Brain notes use natural logarithms and raw entropy of indicators. A coefficient in such a note cannot be copied into a sign-function statement without the corresponding conversion. Every imported result must state its measure, Fourier basis, normalization, log base, and treatment of the empty coefficient.

\subsection{Recovered manuscripts and verified scope}

The interrupted work supplied a 36-page version of \emph{Fourier entropy of threshold functions and their compositions}. The audited recovery and shared-data extension produce the 38-page public paper, released at repository commit \texttt{cd27aa96a5bb670a5b3ae5451095b7020db8e9d4} \cite{Threshold}. Its principal constant is
\[
 \Creg=\log_2(4+e)+2\log_2(e\pi)
       =8.93647465787758\ldots.
\]
With $D(f)=\sum_i\min\{\I_i(f),1-\I_i(f)\}$, its Theorem A gives $\HH(f)\le\Creg D(f)$ for every regular Boolean function. Here regular means unate with a total desirability order, and therefore includes every deterministic linear threshold function with arbitrary real weights. It does not mean small or uniformly comparable weights.

The proof proceeds through pivotal antichains, a relative resampling estimate, harmonic control of spectral ranks, and an Euler-product reference on Fourier levels. The proof was reconstructed in an independent audit during recovery; the product-biased threshold and bounded-overlap arguments were also independently read. No gap was identified in those audits. These are mathematical checks of a preprint, not external peer review or proof-assistant certification.

The package also contains logarithmic FEI bounds for Boolean combinations of regular functions and for read-$k$ unions of structured gates, and an arity-independent corrected FEI+ estimate for symmetric thresholds under every product measure. The latter composes through read-once threshold/parity formulas. The proof of a product-biased estimate for \emph{arbitrary weighted thresholds} is not supplied.

The public recovery revision includes the exact shared-data multiplexer identity from the separate reductions manuscript, with the improved regular-leaf constant. If adjacent branches are orthogonal and selectors are fresh at each node, recursive multiplexers preserve $\Creg$ even when the branch data variables overlap. This is a concrete further class theorem, with explicit hypotheses.

The second recovered manuscript, \emph{Restrictions and witness coding for the Fourier entropy--influence problem} \cite{Reductions}, contains conditioned posterior forests, exact physical-restriction identities, and simultaneous hardness-preserving transformations. It supplies research targets and counterexamples for this plan. It is not silently substituted for the threshold paper or published as part of the report.

\subsection{Why the literature check matters}

The corrected Chakraborty--Kulkarni--Lokam--Saurabh work bounds LTF entropy by $O(\sqrt n)$; an earlier all-LTF claim was removed. The random-LTF result treats specified random models \cite{CKLS,RandomLTF}. This makes verification of the recovered deterministic theorem particularly important. The current argument must stand on its complete proof, independently of earlier abstracts or claims.

Existing coding and composition theorems are essential prerequisites, especially Wan--Wright--Wu and O'Donnell--Tan \cite{WWW,ODT}. Shalev's low-influence reductions explain why rarity alone is not a structural simplification \cite{Shalev}. Recent restriction work supplies a directly relevant stopping-tree framework \cite{GMP}; the plateaued-function theorem supplies a useful structured benchmark \cite{Potapov}. The author's percolation result supplies a geometric encoding example, with spatial hypotheses that must remain explicit \cite{Percolation}.

Do not spend the campaign rediscovering the generic coordinate-marginal entropy estimate as a Boolean breakthrough. Keller--Mossel--Schlank already give the relevant general estimate \cite{KMS}. A later preprint claiming an information-theoretic sharpening was withdrawn because the result already appeared there \cite{Withdrawn}. A new universal argument must use additional structure of actual Boolean spectra or a genuinely stronger global accounting mechanism.

\section{The two highest-priority mathematical endpoints}

\subsection{Endpoint J: bound a complete witness, including its posterior entropy}

For a nonconstant Boolean function represented as a finite union of nonconstant gates with finite ordered covers of their relevant supports, the recovered queue construction generates a subprobability law on Fourier sets through finite successful runs. A partition forest assigns every coordinate of a nonempty set $A$ to a gate, visits each used gate once, and describes gate subsets, roots, attachments, and child labels. Its probability is an actual probability of the fixed random generator; failed or infinite runs contribute no mass. A Fourier-set-dependent choice of a \emph{witness} is legitimate because the reference generator itself remains fixed.

The conditioned version chooses global positive weights $\lambda_l$ satisfying
\[
 \sum_{l:j\in R_l}\lambda_l\le1\quad\text{for every coordinate }j.
\]
With $\pi_l=\min(\Pp(h_l=1),1/2)$, put
\[
 \Lambda=\sum_l\log_2(1+\lambda_l\pi_l),\qquad
 Z=1-2^{-\Lambda},\qquad v=\Var(f).
\]
For each $A$, let $\mathcal P_A$ be a law on \emph{distinct actual finite partition-forest runs} producing $A$. The recovered proof gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:forest}
 \HH(f)\le h_2(v)+K_{\rm ref}\I(f)+\Rcost,
 \qquad K_{\rm ref}=3+2\log_2 3+\log_2(6e),
\end{equation}
where
\begin{equation}\label{eq:residual}
 \Rcost=v(\Lambda+\log_2 Z)+
 \sum_{A\ne\varnothing}P_f(A)
 \left[\E_{r\sim\mathcal P_A}B_\lambda(A;r)-\HH(\mathcal P_A)\right].
\end{equation}
The cost $B_\lambda$ retains gate names, gate-order multiplicity, subset congestion, rank discrepancies, and root rarity. For reference, with $A_l$ the coordinates assigned to gate $l$, $s_l=|A_l|$, $\kappa_l=|A\cap R_l|$, roots $\mathcal R$, and $\gamma=6/5$, it has the form
\begin{align*}
 B_\lambda(A;r)={}&\sum_{l\text{ used}}\left[
 \log_2\frac1{\lambda_l}+s_l\log_2 M_l
 +\gamma s_l\log_2\frac{\kappa_l}{s_l}\right]\\
 &+\sum_{l\in\mathcal R}\log_2\frac1{\pi_l}
 +\frac{\gamma}{\ln2}\sum_{j\in A}F_{\tau(j)}^j(A).
\end{align*}
Here $M_l$ counts the ordered subsets covering the gate support, and $F_l^j(A)$ is the minimum logarithmic predecessor-rank discrepancy in those orders. The full definitions, including source strongly connected components, are in the recovered forest proof \cite{Reductions}.

\textbf{The missing statement is precise:} for every Boolean $f$, construct admissible global representations and weights and admissible conditional witness laws such that $\Rcost\le K\I(f)$ for one universal $K$. Since $h_2(v)\le2\I(f)$, this would prove FEI with constant $2+K_{\rm ref}+K$. Equation~\eqref{eq:residual} is a sufficient target, not an established universal estimate or an asserted equivalence of optimal constants.

The highest-value work is to find a quantitative mechanism for this bound. Merely minimizing over witnesses or invoking a logarithmic partition-sum identity leaves the same cross-entropy unpaid. A reference law may depend on the fixed function $f$, including
its spectrum, while remaining fixed across the sampled output
set $A$. The mathematical obligation is a proved bound on its
expected negative log probability. Choosing $Q=P_f$ is valid
but merely returns $\HH(f)$ as the same unpaid cost. If a
model or codebook is instead selected separately for a sampled
$A$, its selection must be included in the actual reference
experiment and cost ledger.

Addressing identifies one repair that is already real. With $a$ control bits,
\[
 \HH(\operatorname{Addr}_a)=2a,\qquad
 \I(\operatorname{Addr}_a)=1+a/2.
\]
Coordinatewise gate labels can charge order $a^2$, while one complete Fourier set needs one gate label and a control subset. The repaired queue accounting is order $a$, and the exact selector identity gives the sharp family relation $\HH=4(\I-1)$. Future candidates must match this scale and then extend to a family whose relevant complexity is unbounded.

\subsection{Endpoint R: choose coordinates or blocks with globally summable cost}

For a physical restriction at coordinate $i$, write
\[
 \Delta_i\HH(f)=\HH(f)-\tfrac12\bigl[\HH(f_i^+)+\HH(f_i^-)\bigr].
\]
If $\mathcal S$ has law $P_f$, let
\[
 B_i(f)=H\bigl(1_{\{i\in\mathcal S\}}\mid\mathcal S\setminus\{i\}\bigr).
\]
The recovered exact calculation yields
\begin{equation}\label{eq:decrement}
 \Delta_i\HH(f)=B_i(f)+M_i(f),\qquad
 0\le M_i(f)\le\frac2{\ln2}\I_i(f).
\end{equation}
Influence obeys the exact telescope
\[
 \I(f)=\I_i(f)+\tfrac12\bigl[\I(f_i^+)+\I(f_i^-)\bigr].
\]
Thus a theorem asserting that every nonconstant Boolean $f$ has a relevant coordinate with $B_i(f)\le K\I_i(f)$ would settle FEI with constant $K+2/\ln2$. Conversely, a global FEI constant $C$ implies existence of such a coordinate with $K=C$, because $\sum_i B_i(f)\le\HH(f)$. This is an exact existential route to finiteness, applied at every reached restricted function; it does not assert the inequality for every coordinate. A restricted-class version requires the corresponding hereditary hypothesis or an explicitly paid stopping rule.

The scalar decrement bound behaves as
\[
 \kappa(u)=u\log_2(1/u)+\frac{3u}{\ln2}+O(u^2)
 \quad(u\downarrow0).
\]
Consequently a local influence budget can lose a logarithm. The task is to select a good coordinate, use a whole block, or amortize difficult splits against later credit. A finite restriction tree satisfies
\begin{align*}
 \HH(f)&=\E\sum_{v\text{ visited}}\Delta_{i(v)}\HH(f_v)
                +\E\HH(f_{\rm leaf}),\\
 \I(f)&=\E\sum_{v\text{ visited}}\I_{i(v)}(f_v)
                +\E\I(f_{\rm leaf}).
\end{align*}
A successful stopping-tree theorem must bound the \emph{entire expected path cost} and the terminal entropy. The recovered threshold and formula classes provide useful terminal classes. The structural theorem guaranteeing cheap access to those terminals remains to be proved.


For a fixed block $W$, the recovered block identity is
\begin{align}
 \Delta_W\HH(f)
 &:=
 \HH(f)-\E_y\HH(f|_{W=y})\notag\\
 &=H(\mathcal S\cap W\mid\mathcal S\cap W^c)+M_W(f),
 \label{eq:block-erasure}\\
 0&\le M_W(f)\le\frac2{\ln2}\sum_{i\in W}\I_i(f),\notag\\
 \I(f)-\E_y\I(f|_{W=y})&=\sum_{i\in W}\I_i(f).\label{eq:block-influence}
\end{align}
The exposure is uniform physical restriction. An adaptive block rule
must be measurable from the already revealed physical history.
Taking $W$ to be every input makes the conditional erasure term
$\HH(f)$ itself; it is not a reduction in difficulty.

\paragraph{A sharper existing mismatch/KL bound.}
The recovered mismatch/KL estimate is a sharper starting point for
restriction work than the scalar profile alone \cite{BrainMismatch}.
For the actual Boolean children $g=f_i^+$ and $h=f_i^-$, put
\[
 a_T=\wh g(T),\quad b_T=\wh h(T),\quad
 q_T=\frac{a_T^2+b_T^2}{2},\quad
 m_T=\frac{(|a_T|-|b_T|)^2}{4},\quad
 \mu_i^{\rm mis}=\sum_Tm_T.
\]
Here $q$ is a probability law. If $\mu_i^{\rm mis}>0$, let
$\nu_i(T)=m_T/\mu_i^{\rm mis}$; zero-mass terms are omitted.
Then
\[
 \mu_i^{\rm mis}\le
 \min\{\I_i(f),1-\I_i(f)\}
\]
and, with $D_2$ denoting KL divergence in bits,
\[
 \Delta_i\HH(f)\le
 \mu_i^{\rm mis}\left[
 \log_2\frac1{\mu_i^{\rm mis}}
 -D_2(\nu_i\Vert q)+\frac3{\ln2}\right].
\]
If $\mu_i^{\rm mis}=0$, the decrement is exactly zero.
Along a fair physical restriction tree, the terms
$3\mu_i^{\rm mis}/\ln2$ already fit the queried-influence budget.
The remaining proposed tree payment is
\[
 \E\sum_{v\ {\rm visited}}\mu_v^{\rm mis}
 \left[\log_2\frac1{\mu_v^{\rm mis}}
       -D_2(\nu_v\Vert q_v)\right],
\]
together with terminal entropy. Bounding this complete quantity
by a universal multiple of root influence remains open.
The mismatch mass $\mu_i^{\rm mis}$ is different from the
influence-variation refund denoted $M$ in the B05 report.

\paragraph{A newly verified local refund obstruction.}
\label{par:b05-refund}
For the B05 packet statistics, let $G,H$ be the actual Boolean
children, $a_T=\wh G(T)$, $b_T=\wh H(T)$,
$q_T=(a_T^2+b_T^2)/2$, $e_T=(a_T-b_T)^2/4$ and
$\theta_T=e_T/q_T$, omitting $q_T=0$. Define
\[
 d=\sum_T e_T=\Pp(G\ne H),\quad
 L=\sum_T q_T h_2(\theta_T)=B_y,
\]
\[
 V_{\rm post}=\sum_Tq_T(\theta_T-d)^2,\qquad
 M_{\rm inf}=\sum_j\bigl(\I_j(G)-\I_j(H)\bigr)^2.
\]
These two refunds differ from the mixing term $M_i$ and the
mismatch mass $\mu_i^{\rm mis}$ above.
The first natural B05 run supplies a further actual Boolean
control. For even $k\ge4$, use the quadratic bent sign on $k$
bits, its one-point flip, and activate both on one point of
$2k$ control bits. The selector disagreement is $d=2^{-3k}$.
For the selected physical split, its erasure cost $L=B_y$ and
the report's two quadratic refunds satisfy
\[
 \frac{L}{d}=k+\log_2e+o(1),\qquad
 \frac{V_{\rm post}}d\le4\,2^{-k},\qquad
 \frac{M_{\rm inf}}d=12k\,2^{-3k}.
\]
Therefore no fixed finite nonnegative $K,A,B$ can make
$L\le Kd+A V_{\rm post}+B M_{\rm inf}$ hold at every Boolean
split. This retires that local payment proposal. It does not
retire existential coordinate selection or a suitably chosen
stopping tree. The next useful control is a full analysis of
control-first and selector-first trees on this family, including
their terminal entropy and the source of any logarithmic credit.
The complete B05 original and its proof are recorded in
\cite{B05Rare}; the displayed no-go has been independently
checked analytically. Its floating-point self-check is
supporting computation, not the proof of the asymptotic theorem.

\section{Other routes that deserve sustained effort}

\subsection{Boolean phase and conditional entropy}

For an actual Boolean sign, $f^2=1$ yields the signed convolution identities
\[
 \sum_S\wh f(S)\wh f(S\mathbin\triangle U)=1_{\{U=\varnothing\}}.
\]
An arbitrary probability law on subsets need not admit signs satisfying these identities. The Brain's cyclic-label construction disproves a proposed dimension-free signed-lifetime bound for an auxiliary subset law, but that divergent law is provably non-Boolean \cite{Brain51}. It is a powerful warning against overbroad relaxations, not an FEI counterexample.

Let $X,Y$ be disjoint groups of independent uniform physical input
bits. Suppose $f(X,Y)\in\{-1,1\}$ and every nonzero full Fourier
coefficient has $X$-degree at most an integer $d\ge1$. Define the
$X$-marginal of its squared Fourier law by
\[
 p_X(S)=\sum_{T\subseteq Y}\wh f(S,T)^2.
\]
The reviewed estimate, converted from nats to bits, is
\[
 H(p_X)\le\frac2{\ln2}\I_Y(f)+2(d-1).
\]
The auxiliary \emph{spectral} coordinates may be correlated; the
physical input law here remains the uniform product law.

The exact chain rule is
\[
 H(P_f)=H(p_X)+H(\mathcal S_Y\mid\mathcal S_X).
\]
The conditional term remains unpaid. The Brain also records an
embedding showing that an unrestricted conditional-completion
theorem even for one fixed cyclic marginal is FEI-equivalent
\cite{Brain54}. A useful restricted theorem must prove its
structural admission or stability hypothesis; the arbitrary-tensor
embedding remains a mandatory scope test.

Fixed prefixes are already known to be insufficient in two
distinct relaxations. The archived signed-convolution-order
family is normalized and dyadic and passes every prescribed
fixed cancellation order while its erasure/influence ratio
diverges \cite{BrainBooleanOrder}. A separate cyclic construction
passes every fixed atom-cap prefix while retaining an unbounded
signed-lifetime ratio \cite{Brain51}. Neither is an actual Boolean
FEI counterexample. B07 must state the exact hierarchy and
identify an additional consequence of full Booleanity absent
from the relevant obstruction.


\subsection{Arbitrary biased weighted thresholds}\label{sec:weighted-guards}

The public paper supplies the correct product-measure strong entropy normalization and proves it for symmetric thresholds. Extending this to arbitrary weighted thresholds with a constant uniform in weights, biases, and arity would immediately enlarge the depth-independent formula class through the established composition principle \cite{ODT,Threshold}. This is a substantial secondary target even if full FEI remains open.

For clarity, in the orthonormal product Fourier basis with
$v_i=4q_i(1-q_i)$ the target normalization is
\[
 \HH_\mu^+(f)=\sum_{S\ne\varnothing}\wh f_\mu(S)^2
       \log_2\frac{\prod_{i\in S}v_i}{\wh f_\mu(S)^2},
 \qquad
 \Delta_\mu(f)=\I_\mu(f)-\Var_\mu(f).
\]
The symmetric proof uses integer-valued sums, exponential
tilting, a mode estimate and a near-dictator regime. A weighted
extension needs a replacement that survives the already proved
obstructions below; a grid of biases cannot certify the
continuum or endpoint statement.

The separate reductions manuscript already supplies three
normalization obstructions that every proposed extension must
respect \cite{Reductions}. One is the weighted threshold
\[
 A_m=\left\{mX_0+\sum_{j=1}^mX_j\ge m\right\},\qquad
 q=\Pp(X_j=1)=m^{-2},\qquad
 q_0=\Pp(X_0=1)=\frac{(m-1)q^m}{1+(m-1)q^m}.
\]
For $f_m=2\mathbf1_{A_m}-1$, let
$\alpha=\Pp(A_m)$, $Q=\mu(\cdot\mid A_m)$,
$V=\Var_\mu(f_m)$, and let $\rho_i$ be the physical pivotal
probabilities. With total correlation and the displayed
logarithms measured in nats,
\[
 \frac{\alpha\operatorname{TC}(Q)}{\I_\mu(f_m)}
 =\frac{\ln m+1}{8}+O\!\left(\frac{\ln m}{m}\right),
\]
\[
 \frac{V\ln(1/V)-\sum_i\I_i^\mu(f_m)
                    \ln^+(4\rho_i/V)}{\I_\mu(f_m)}
 =\frac12\ln m-\ln2+O\!\left(\frac{\ln m}{m}\right).
\]
Nevertheless, its corrected entropy in bits satisfies
$\HH_\mu^+(f_m)/(\I_\mu(f_m)-V)\to2$.
Thus conditional total correlation is not universally paid by
influence, and the displayed pivotal-credit estimate fails with
coefficient one. A larger multiplier on those credits is not
refuted by this example.

The same two-branch threshold with
$q_0=q^m/(1+q^m)$ and $q\downarrow0$ at fixed $m$ has
\[
 \frac{\alpha\sum_{k\ne j}I_Q(X_j;X_k)}
      {\I_j^\mu(f_m)}\longrightarrow\frac{m\ln2}{2}
 \quad\hbox{for every }j.
\]
Letting $m$ increase rules out even an existential-coordinate
constant for this \emph{conditional physical total-correlation}
payment. It does not refute the spectral good-coordinate
erasure target. B09 should begin from these proved failures
and formulate a replacement that survives them.

\subsection{Shared-data composition}

For uniform physical selector bits $x\in\bits^a$ disjoint from the data cube and arbitrary Boolean branches $g_b$ on that common data cube, let $F(x,y)=g_x(y)$. With $N=2^a$, set
\[
 R_T=N^{-1}\sum_b\wh g_b(T)^2,\qquad
 r_T(b)=\frac{\wh g_b(T)^2}{NR_T}.
\]
For $R_T>0$, let $\nu_T$ be the squared normalized Walsh transform of the signed vector $\wh g_b(T)/\sqrt{NR_T}$. Omit zero-mass terms. Then
\[
 H(F)=a+N^{-1}\sum_bH(g_b)
       +\sum_T R_T[H(\nu_T)-H(r_T)].
\]
This preserves interference exactly. If every branch satisfies
$H(g_b)\le C\I(g_b)$, the already proved estimate
$H(F)\le2a+N^{-1}\sum_bH(g_b)$ gives closure with constant
$\max\{2/c,C\}$ whenever $\I_{\rm sel}(F)\ge ca$.
Neighboring branch orthogonality yields $c=1/2$ and constant
$\max\{4,C\}$. This quantitative corollary is completed baseline
work. A new result must establish its hypothesis for a new
unbounded family or control the full collision ledger when
$\I_{\rm sel}/a$ tends to zero.

For identical branches the exact formula cancels all selector
cost. Conversely, the recovered bent-splice example has a
one-bit selector with $\I_{\rm sel}=2^{-m}$ and
$\Delta_{\rm sel}H/\I_{\rm sel}\ge m-1$ for even $m$.
Thus a universal selector-only increment estimate is false;
total-influence closure and existential coordinate selection
are different assertions \cite{Reductions}.

There is an exact interface with block restriction accounting.
For the selector block $W$ of size $a$, the conditional erasure
and physical mixing terms are respectively
\[
 B_W=\sum_T R_T H(\nu_T),\qquad
 M_W=a-\sum_T R_T H(r_T).
\]
Consequently
\[
 \HH(F)-2^{-a}\sum_b\HH(g_b)=B_W+M_W,\qquad
 0\le M_W\le\frac2{\ln2}\I_W(F).
\]
The conditional law $\nu_T$ is the selector spectral law given
the data spectral set; $r_T$ is a different law, on physical
branch labels. These identities identify the shared unpaid term
for B04, B06 and B12, rather than supply three independent
payments. Taking $W$ to be every input gives $B_W=\HH(F)$ and is
not a reduction in difficulty.

\section{What would count as a significant result}

\subsection{Primary outcome: the unrestricted theorem or a rigorous disproof}

A full proof must close \eqref{eq:full} for every Boolean function and every dimension with one finite constant. It must contain its representation, selection, coding, or structural lemmas as proved statements, including any existence argument. A disproof must construct actual Boolean functions $f_m$ with $H(f_m)/I(f_m)\to\infty$. Exceeding one proposed constant or falsifying an auxiliary spectral inequality does not meet that standard.

A universal good-coordinate erasure inequality, a universal
whole-witness residual bound, or a globally summable stopping
tree with uniformly paid terminals would close a precise route
to \eqref{eq:full}. These are full-solution outcomes. Their
restricted-class variants can be substantial partial results.

\subsection{Major partial outcomes worth completing}

\begin{itemize}
\item A good-coordinate or fully paid stopping-tree theorem for a new explicit class, with its hereditary or admission hypothesis proved and the remaining universal obstruction identified.
\item A bound on the whole-witness residual for a class with genuinely unbounded overlap that lies outside the existing bounded-read or orthogonal-multiplexer hypotheses. State exactly how the new parameter improves the old logarithmic loss.
\item An arity-uniform product-biased corrected FEI+ theorem for arbitrary weighted thresholds, with its resulting depth-independent read-once formula consequence.
\item A Boolean-specific conditional entropy or phase-energy theorem under a proved stable structural condition, together with a clear account of the remaining class outside that condition.
\item A full FEI theorem for another broad, currently unresolved family, supported by a primary-source novelty check. Do not assert that a family is new merely because it is absent from one local manuscript.
\item A rigorous scalable counterfamily that rules out a major live universal helper, or a corrected sharp auxiliary theorem replacing it. Such a result can save substantial research effort without being a disproof of FEI.
\end{itemize}

Improving $8.937$ to a slightly smaller number, running more random examples of an already proved identity, or producing longer exposition without a new mathematical implication is a lower priority during this campaign. These can be useful after a major theorem is secure, but they should not displace work on the unpaid global terms.
