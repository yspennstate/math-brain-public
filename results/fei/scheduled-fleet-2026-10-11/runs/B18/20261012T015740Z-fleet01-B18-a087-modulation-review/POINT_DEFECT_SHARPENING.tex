\section*{Sharp auxiliary constant for point-defect modulation}

Let \(q_m:\{-1,1\}^m\to\{-1,1\}\) be negative at one cube point
and positive elsewhere. Put \(N=2^m\). Its squared Fourier law has mass
\((N-2)^2/N^2\) at the empty set and mass \(4/N^2\) at every nonempty set.
Consequently
\[
 I(q_m)=\frac{2m}{N},
 \qquad
 H(q_m)=h_2(\delta)+\delta\log_2(N-1),
 \qquad
 \delta=\frac{4(N-1)}{N^2}.
\]

For \(0<u<1\),
\[
 h_2(u)\le u\log_2(e/u).
\]
Indeed, in natural logarithms,
\(-(1-u)\log(1-u)\le u\), which follows from
\(-\log(1-u)\le u/(1-u)\).
Using the exact value of \(\delta\),
\[
\begin{aligned}
 H(q_m)
 &\le \delta\log_2\frac{e(N-1)}{\delta}\\
 &=\delta\log_2\frac{eN^2}{4}\\
 &=\delta\bigl(2m+\log_2e-2\bigr).
\end{aligned}
\]
For \(m\ge2\), the bracket is positive and \(\delta<4/N\). Hence
\[
 \frac{H(q_m)}{I(q_m)}
 <
 \frac{2}{m}\bigl(2m+\log_2e-2\bigr)
 <4.
\]
For \(m=1\), \(q_1\) is a character and \(H(q_1)=0\).
The exact formulas also give
\[
 \lim_{m\to\infty}\frac{H(q_m)}{I(q_m)}=4.
\]
Thus constant \(4\), rather than \(9/2\), is valid for every point-defect
auxiliary product and is asymptotically sharp within this family.

In the B06 modulation hierarchy where every modulator at a level is \(q_m\),
the occupied auxiliary products are either \(1\) or \(q_m\). The accepted
one-block identities therefore give
\[
 H(f_L)\le\max(C_0,4)\,I(f_L)
\]
at every finite depth, with the base entropy retained as terminal debt and
each fresh-block influence charged once.

This sharpening is contributor-proved and requires a fresh reviewer. It is
not a universal FEI bound and does not cover blocks whose physical branches
have unequal squared spectra.
