\section*{All-arity structural reduction for the block half-tax}

All entropies are in bits.  For $0\le p\le1$ put
\[
 h_2(p)=-p\log_2p-(1-p)\log_2(1-p),\qquad
 \Delta(p)=h_2(p)+\log_2\max\{p,1-p\},
\]
with $0\log 0=0$.  Let
\[
 U_p=\begin{pmatrix}\sqrt{1-p}&\sqrt p\\
                     \sqrt p&-\sqrt{1-p}\end{pmatrix}.
\]

\paragraph{Frozen input.}
The scalar input is the one-coordinate uncertainty bound
\[
 H(|z|^2)+H(|U_pz|^2)\ge r(p):=h_2(p)-\frac12\Delta(p)       \tag{1}
\]
for every unit $z\in\mathbb C^2$ and every $p\in[0,1]$.
At interior biases this is the source-pinned claim
\texttt{B10L-ONEBIT-HALF-TAX}; the repaired B17/A084--A085
certificates cover its scalar continuum realization.  Deterministic
endpoints follow directly by deleting the one-dimensional factor.

\paragraph{Theorem (no arity tail).}
For every finite set $A$, every biases $(p_i)_{i\in A}\in[0,1]^A$, and
every unit vector $w\in\mathbb C^{2^{|A|}}$, if
$U_A=\bigotimes_{i\in A}U_{p_i}$, then
\[
 H(|w|^2)+H(|U_Aw|^2)\ge\sum_{i\in A}r(p_i).                \tag{2}
\]
The constant and coefficient $1/2$ are independent of $|A|$.

\paragraph{Proof.}
Use the computational basis and the $U_{p_i}$-rotated basis as the two
nondegenerate projective measurements on the $i$th qubit.  Equation (1)
is their local state-independent linear Shannon-entropy uncertainty
relation with weights $(1,1)$.  Proposition 1 and Corollary 1 of
R. Schwonnek, \emph{Additivity of entropic uncertainty relations},
Quantum 2 (2018), 59, state that the optimal constant for two global
product projective measurements is the sum of the local optimal constants,
even over arbitrary (possibly entangled and complex) states.  Iterating the
proposition gives (2).  Pure states suffice because the measurement
entropies are concave in the density matrix, and the displayed coefficient
vector $w$ is already pure.  If $p_i\in\{0,1\}$, $U_{p_i}$ is a signed
permutation, $r(p_i)=0$, and the factor contributes zero to both sides;
thus deletion or continuity covers all rare endpoints.  This proves every
finite arity at once, with no compactness limit in dimension.

\paragraph{Exact block consequence.}
For a product-biased physical block $A$, the exact child/spectral reduction
from \texttt{B10L-BLOCK-HALF-TAX-PRODUCT} gives
\[
 J_A-M_A\le H(Z_A)-
 \inf_{\|w\|_2=1}\{H(|w|^2)+H(|U_Aw|^2)\},                 \tag{3}
\]
where $H(Z_A)=\sum_i h_2(p_i)$.  Combining (2)--(3),
\[
 \boxed{J_A\le M_A+\frac12\sum_{i\in A}\Delta(p_i)}.      \tag{4}
\]
This holds for arbitrary normalized coefficient vectors, hence in
particular for every genuine Boolean child family; Booleanity is not
relaxed in its application.

\paragraph{GU scope.}
Equation (4) closes the all-arity uncertainty sublemma only.  It does not
cancel or pay the $M_A$ terms along a hierarchy.  Indeed the accepted
growing-rank OR-defect obstruction rules out the naive cancellation that
would yield $G\le I/2$.  Universal GU still requires a signed telescoping
potential that retains the nonzero terminal $M$ reserve and charges each
physical influence once.  Thus (4) is a strict reduction of the unpaid
line, not a proof of GU or FEI.
