\section*{Terminal compensation for the one-point-defect restriction chain}

Let $q_n$ be the genuine Boolean gate that is $+1$ at one cube point
and $-1$ elsewhere.  For the complete physical selector/data packet of
the B05--B19 ledger, write
\[
 D_n=L_n+Q_n
\]
and let restriction of one defect coordinate leave the constant child
and the exceptional child $q_{n-1}$.  By the definition of the complete
order-swap credit,
\[
 \Gamma_n^D=D_n-\frac12D_{n-1}.
\]
The B19 repair proves $\Gamma_n^D<0$ for every $n\ge3$; its new phase
part is pending a fresh independent review, while the $L$ part has been
independently accepted at explicit $L$ scope.

\paragraph{Lemma (exact terminal-compensation identity).}
For every $N\ge3$,
\[
 \boxed{
 D_N=2^{-(N-2)}D_2+
 \sum_{n=3}^{N}2^{-(N-n)}\Gamma_n^D.}
\]
Consequently, conditional on acceptance of the B19 full-credit repair,
\[
 \sum_{n=3}^{N}2^{-(N-n)}\Gamma_n^D<0,
 \qquad
 0\le D_N<2^{-(N-2)}D_2.
\]

\paragraph{Proof.}
Multiply the recurrence
$D_n=\frac12D_{n-1}+\Gamma_n^D$ by $2^{-(N-n)}$ and sum from
$n=3$ to $N$.  Every intermediate $D_n$ cancels, leaving the displayed
identity.  Both $L_n$ and $Q_n$ are entropy packets, hence $D_n\ge0$.
Strict negativity follows from the B19 sign theorem for every summand.

\paragraph{Interpretation.}
The complete physical first credit is negative at every nonterminal
node on the canonical exceptional-child chain.  Nevertheless, this does
not refute a globally chosen restriction tree: the weighted terminal
packet $2^{-(N-2)}D_2$ is an unavoidable positive boundary term.  Any
induction that discards terminal costs converts the exact identity into
a false nonnegative-credit assertion.  Conversely, merely retaining the
terminal term gives no universal FEI proof, because a general tree still
needs a dimension-independent bound on the sum of all terminal packets
without reusing influence.

This is the precise cross-route substitution into the nonlocal-induction
program: replace the refuted nodewise refund by a signed telescoping
ledger plus an explicit terminal budget.  The next unpaid inequality is
not $\Gamma_v^D\ge0$; it is
\[
 \sum_{\ell\ \mathrm{terminal}}2^{-\mathrm{depth}(\ell)}D(f_\ell)
 \le C I(f)
\]
for a physically generated, globally chosen tree, with every coordinate
and endpoint charged once.  The displayed $q_N$ chain shows why the
terminal term cannot be omitted, but does not prove this universal bound.
