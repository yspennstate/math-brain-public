The target is

\[
\exists C\in[0,\infty)\ \forall n\in\mathbb N_0\ \forall f:\{-1,1\}^n\to\{-1,1\},\qquad H_2(f)\le C I(f).
\]

Here \(\widehat f(S)=2^{-n}\sum_x f(x)\prod_{i\in S}x_i\), \(P_f(S)=\widehat f(S)^2\), \(H_2(f)=\sum_SP_f(S)\log_2(1/P_f(S))\), and \(I(f)=\sum_S|S|P_f(S)=\sum_i\Pr[f(X)\ne f(X^{\oplus i})]\). Set \(0\log(1/0)=0\). Constants, including the zero-dimensional cube, have H=I=0. Ratios are taken only for nonconstant functions, equivalently I>0. The assertion \(\forall n\exists C_n\) is weaker and elementary; it is not FEI.

Equivalently, \(C_{\rm FEI}=\sup_{n,f:I(f)>0}H_2(f)/I(f)<\infty\). A disproof requires actual Boolean signs \(f_j\) with \(H_2(f_j)/I(f_j)\to\infty\). Refuting a proposed constant, a local payment, or a non-Boolean relaxation does not satisfy this target. Natural-log entropy is \(H_{\rm nat}=(\ln2)H_2\).

Use \(D_{\rm char}=\sum_i\min(I_i,1-I_i)\), \(v=\operatorname{Var}(f)\), and \(\delta=I-v\). These are distinct from dual total correlation, physical pivotal influence under biased measures, and the influence drop of a restriction. Uniform H+ is the nonempty-atom contribution; under biases it includes the product-basis correction and can be negative.

### 1. Optimization and computable truncation certificates

For a finite set Omega of actual runs and any finite cost b(r), put S=sum_{r in Omega} 2^(-b(r)). If S>0, then

    inf_p [sum_r p(r)b(r)-H(p)] = -log S,
    p_*(r)=2^(-b(r))/S.

Indeed, the expression equals -log S + KL(p || p_*). Infinite-cost/zero-weight runs must have p=0 at any finite optimum; if all weights vanish the value is +infinity. Unsuccessful or infinite queue paths are not in Omega. The actual partition-forest set is finite. For a countable successful-run collection and b=-log q, increasing finite collections converges to -log sum q whenever this sum is positive; finite-support posteriors suffice to establish the infimum even if the full conditional entropy is infinite.

This identity is baseline bookkeeping. Its computational use is as follows. For a deduplicated enumerated subcollection E, S_E gives an upper bound -log S_E on the full infimum. If every omitted run has cost >=b0 and their number is <=N, then

    -log(S_E+N*2^(-b0)) <= full infimum <= -log S_E.

More generally an upper bound U on the omitted weight replaces N*2^(-b0). Interval bounds on S_E and U yield rigorous logarithm intervals with their directions preserved. In the forest residual b=B; for the exact complete run cost b=-log q, S is actual conditioned output mass of the selected runs. These are different optimizers: p_B need not equal the true posterior p_q.

If a description map d->r has multiplicities n_r, summing descriptions would wrongly insert n_r in S. Either canonicalize to the actual queue key (initial root set, chronological visited-gate/subset/ordered-child trace), or use H(description)-H(description|run)=H(run). The correction follows directly from the entropy chain rule since run is a function of description. Child-label draws in different occurrence slots at the same attachment variable really are different random events; permutations of the written edge list which leave those draws unchanged are not.

For rational incidence weights and gamma=6/5, each 2^(-B) is algebraic: rank and congestion factors are rational quantities raised to rational powers. Rational bisection for fifth roots, products and finite sums gives explicit rational intervals for S; logarithms can then be bounded by a convergent rational series. No assertion of efficient enumeration in arbitrary dimension is made.

### 2. Exact Boolean law and influence

    f_m=x0-(1+x0)b.

### 3. Exhaustion of actual partition forests

For A={0}, exactly m runs exist: choose root r, subset {0}, and zero children. Each has B=log m+2. They differ in the activated gate, so posterior H=log m is justified.

For A=T nonempty private, every coordinate is forced to its own gate, no incidence edges exist, all t=|T| gates are roots, and there is exactly one run. Its cost is

    B_private=t(log m+16/5).

Here F_i^i=ln 2 since the common predecessor is absent.

For A={0} union T, assignment of coordinate 0 determines a unique source gate. All other used gates own only their private variable, and have no outgoing edge. Thus the forest is forced to be a star. There are two possibilities:

* Inside root: r in T owns {0,r}, other gates own their private singleton. There are t choices of r and (t-1)! distinct ordered child-label sequences, for t! runs. Every such run has B_in=t log m+2+(6/5)(t-1).
* Outside root: r outside T owns {0}, with t private leaves. There are m-t root choices and t! ordered child-label sequences, for (m-t)t! runs. Each has B_out=(t+1)log m+2+(6/5)t.

In both cases every child attaches at coordinate 0. The chronological child draws are part of the run; selected subsets are disjoint and every used gate is visited once. This exhausts all assignments, root choices, parents, attachments and permitted child orders, with no duplicated descriptions.

The complete residual partition weights are therefore

    S_B({0})=1/4,
    S_B(T)=m^(-t)*(a/4)^t,
    S_B({0} union T)= t! * a^(t-1)/(4*m^t) * [1+(1-t/m)*a].

Together with p_B(r) proportional to 2^(-B(r)), these determine the exact finite optimizer for every spectral set. In the last display, individual runs inside each root type have equal weight, but the two root types have different weights.

### 4. Dimension-independent residual payment

Using the preceding exact optima,

    R_m = v log D + 2(1-2^(-m))^2
      + 4^(-m) sum_{t=1}^m binom(m,t) *
        [2t log m +(22/5)t +4/5 -log(t!)
         -log(1+(1-t/m)*a)].

The root normalizer is well controlled in both directions:

    1/4 <= D < e^(1/4)-1 < 1/3.

The lower bound is the linear binomial term; (1+u)^m<=e^(mu) proves the middle bound. Since exp(x)<1/(1-x) for 0<x<1 by termwise comparison of its series with the geometric series, exp(1/4)<4/3. In particular log D<0, but the exact formula retains it before this bound.

    2^(-m) [m log m +(11/5)m +(4/5)(1-2^(-m))]

### 1. Exact Boolean spectrum, including a=0

The selector indicator expands as

    1{x=u} = N^{-1} product_j (1+u_j x_j).

Therefore

    f = N^{-1} sum_u sum_{T subset [a]} chi_T(u) chi_T(x) y_u.

The Fourier sets T union {y_u} are distinct because the data coordinates are distinct and disjoint from the selector block. There are N^2 such sets, each coefficient has magnitude 1/N, and all other coefficients vanish. Thus the spectral law is the independent pair U uniform on N labels and T uniform on 2^a selector subsets. Consequently

    H=2a,  |S|=1+Bin(a,1/2),  I=1+a/2,  H=4(I-1).

Each selector has influence 1/2: flipping it selects two independent fair data bits, which disagree with probability 1/2. Each data coordinate has influence 1/N because it matters exactly at its own address. At a=0, f=y_0, H=0, I=1. The ratio H/I=4a/(a+2) increases to 4.

### 2. Actual elementary codes: label sharing is explicit

The codebook, family, dimension and coordinate order are fixed before sampling S. No spectral-law-dependent selection is transmitted. All codewords for a fixed a have known parsing rules.

* Repeated-label complete code: first transmit the a-bit selector-subset mask T, then transmit the a-bit address u once for each coordinate in S, in selector order followed by the data coordinate. The decoder knows s=1+|T| after the mask and checks the labels agree. Its length is a+a*s. The code is prefix-free: different masks have different fixed-length prefixes, and for a fixed mask the remaining length is fixed. The exact expected cost is a(2+a/2), so cost/I = a + 2a/(a+2) = a+2+o(1). Its repeated-label component alone is a*I, with ratio a. Omitting the mask describes only a label charge, not a complete code.
* Complete-set one-root code: transmit u once in a bits and then the a-bit mask T. Length is exactly 2a; its ratio is 4a/(a+2), tending to 4. The uniform reference Q(T union {y_u})=1/N^2 has total mass one. This code achieves H exactly. For a=0 the empty word decodes the unique set {y_0}.
* Posterior version of this elementary code: the latent witness is (u,T). Its posterior given S is a point mass, so H(witness|S)=0. The variational expression E[-log q(witness)]-H(posterior) equals 2a exactly. There is no extra label credit: S itself identifies u.

These definitions show the quadratic-to-linear repair without claiming that an inefficient code is necessary.

### 3. The source's coordinatewise fractional pressure

The canonical DNF has h_b=[x=b and y_b=1], pi_b=1/(2N), and R_b=[a] union {y_b}. For a>=1 the selector incidence constraints are sum_b lambda_b<=1. At a selector j, the unique true endpoint of a pivotal edge forces the witness gate; symmetry gives q_{j,b}=1/N. At data coordinate y_b, its witness is deterministic. In the source's outerfractional bound the nonnegative pressure is

    P(lambda) = (48/5) sum_j Inf_j log2(sum_b sqrt(q_{j,b}) lambda_b^{-11/48}).

Convexity of t^{-11/48} minimizes every selector sum at lambda_b=1/N, and convexity of -log minimizes the sum of data costs at the same weights. Saturation is optimal because increasing a weight decreases these costs. Hence

    P_min=(7/2)a^2+(11/5)a,
    P_min/I=7a-48/5+96/[5(a+2)] ~ 7a.

For a=0 there is one data gate, lambda_0=1, and pressure zero, agreeing with this polynomial. P_min is a term of a complete upper bound, not a Kraft-normalized code length by itself. ### 4. Exact single-run queue cost and its conditioned posterior

Use one common regular-gate order: selectors first, data last. Put d=a+1, gamma=6/5, theta_s(r)=min(1,(s/r)^gamma),

    E_{d,s}=e_s(theta_s(1),...,theta_s(d)),

    q_0 = 2^{-Lambda_0}/(2N) * W_s(B)/[2 s(s+1) E_{d,s}].

Thus its complete expected negative logarithm is

    C_queue=Lambda_0+a+2+L_a.

For the conditioned forest generator, fix incidence weights lambda_b=1/N and put

    Lambda_f=N log2(1+1/(2N^2)),
    Z_f=1-(1+1/(2N^2))^{-N}.

Its same one-root run, after nonempty-root conditioning, has probability

    q_* = 2^{-Lambda_f}/Z_f * 1/(2N^2) * W_s(B)/[2s(s+1) E_{d,s}],

so

    C_posterior,one-root=Lambda_f+log2 Z_f+2a+2+L_a.

The posterior here is over the specified family of one-root partition runs; exactly one such run outputs each spectral S, so its entropy subtraction is zero. ### 5. Exact asymptotic constant for these literal queue costs

Define w(t)=1 for 0<=t<=1/2, and w(t)=(2t)^{-gamma} for 1/2<t<=1. There is a unique z>0 satisfying

    integral_0^1 z*w(t)/(1+z*w(t)) dt = 1/2.

Set

    G=integral_0^1 log2(1+z*w(t)) dt - (1/2)log2 z
      - (1/2)integral_0^1 log2 w(t) dt.

Then L_a/a -> G, and both C_queue/I and C_posterior,one-root/I tend to 2+2G. In particular,

    4 <= 2+2G <= 4+gamma*(1-1/(2 ln 2)).

This is an exact integral-defined constant, between 4 and approximately 4.3344; the decimal is illustrative.

Proof of the coefficient limit: on any sequence s/d -> 1/2, choose z_d so sum_r z_d theta_s(r)/(1+z_d theta_s(r))=s. The weights stay in a fixed compact subinterval of (0,1], so z_d stays bounded above and away from zero. Riemann sums converge, giving z_d -> z and

    log2 E_{d,s} = sum_r log2(1+z_d theta_s(r))-s log2 z_d + log2 Pr(K=s),

where K is the sum of independent Bernoulli variables with odds z_d theta_s(r) and mean s. The last term is o(d): Chebyshev gives an atom of probability at least c/sqrt(d) within O(sqrt(d)) of s; adjacent atom ratios are bounded above and below by constants in that central window, because

    min(odds)*(d-k)/(k+1) <= Pr(K=k+1)/Pr(K=k)
                                    <= max(odds)*(d-k)/(k+1).

This inequality follows by counting each (k+1)-subset k+1 times in elementary symmetric sums. Moving from that atom to s costs O(sqrt(d)) bits. This proves the coefficient limit without assuming a local central limit theorem.

Under the uniform selector-subset law, s/d ->1/2 exponentially in probability. Replacing theta_s by theta_{floor(d/2)} changes each log weight by at most gamma*|log2(s/floor(d/2))|, uniformly in r. Thus the expected selected log-weight sum, divided by d, tends to (1/2)integral log2 w; the always-present data coordinate contributes only O(1). The exceptional binomial tails contribute o(d), since every relevant cost is O(d log d). The size-choice logarithm is O(log d). Therefore L_a/a -> G. Finally Lambda_0=O(1), Lambda_f=o(1), and log2 Z_f=-a-1+o(1), proving the stated limits.

For the constant bounds, the uniform distribution on (s-1)-subsets of the a selectors has entropy log2 binom(a,s-1), and Gibbs applied to the subprobability reference B -> W_s(B)/E_{d,s} gives average subset cost at least that entropy. Averaging over s gives a-H(Bin(a,1/2)), hence G>=1. Conversely E_{d,s}<=binom(d,s) since theta<=1, and the preceding selected-weight calculation gives G<=1-(1/2)integral log2 w. Direct integration gives integral log2 w=-gamma*(1-1/(2ln2)).

### 1. Exact spectrum and all entropy terms

Tensor independence gives H(g_t)=th, I(g_t)=ti, and E g_t=0. For a data set T and control set U, the indicator coefficient is q times a sign epsilon_U. Consequently:

- empty T,U: coefficient 1-q, probability (1-q)^2;
- empty T, nonempty U: coefficient -q epsilon_U, probability q^2, multiplicity 2^k-1, total mass q-q^2;
- nonempty T and arbitrary U: coefficient q epsilon_U ghat_t(T), probability q^2 ghat_t(T)^2, total mass q.

These masses sum to one. No incoherent approximation or Boolean realizability assumption enters. Summing -p log p over the three classes gives

    H(F)=qth+B_k(q),
    B_k(q)=(4q-2q^2)k+b(q),
    b(q)=-2(1-q)^2 log(1-q).

In detail, the data-class entropy is qth+2kq, pure-control entropy is 2k(q-q^2), and constant-atom entropy is b(q). Every data influence is q times its core influence. A control bit is pivotal if the other k-1 controls match and g_t=-1, probability 2^{-(k-1)}/2=q. Hence

    I(F)=q(ti+k), E F=1-q, Pr(F=-1)=q/2,
    V=Var(F)=2q-q^2.

The spectral empty/nonempty split can also be retained exactly:

    H(F)=h_2(V)+V H(S | S nonempty),
    H(S | S nonempty)=[qth+B_k(q)-h_2(V)]/V.

This prevents losing normalization or the constant atom by replacing full entropy with a conditional spectrum. For any 0<v<1,

    h_2(v)=v log(1/v)+cv-cv^2/2+R_v,
    -cv^3/[6(1-v)] <= R_v <= 0.

Proof: -(1-v)ln(1-v)=v-sum_{j>=2}v^j/[j(j-1)], and the tail coefficients are at most 1/6. Substitution v=V is uniform in t; here V<=3/4.

### 2. Uniform expansion, with no restriction on tensor growth

Expanding the convergent logarithmic series for q<=1/2 gives

    b(q)=2cq-3cq^2+R(q),
    R(q)=-4c sum_{j>=3} q^j/[j(j-1)(j-2)],
    -2cq^3/[3(1-q)] <= R(q) <= 0.

The coefficients follow by multiplying sum q^j/j by 2(1-2q+q^2). All terms in R are negative, and their absolute coefficients are at most 2/3. Therefore, for every t,k,

    H(F)=q[th+4k+2c]-q^2[2k+3c]+R(q),
    I(F)=q(ti+k).

This is an absolute error bound independent of t, h, i, and seed dimension. In particular it does not require qt to be small. Let

    R0=(th+4k+2c)/(ti+k).

Then the exact ratio satisfies

    R0 - q(2k+3c)/(ti+k) - 2cq^2/[3(1-q)(ti+k)]
       <= H(F)/I(F) <= R0 - q(2k+3c)/(ti+k).

Since a balanced Boolean seed has i>=1 (Parseval on nonempty sets), these are uniform also over all balanced seeds. The correction is bounded independently of t by q(2+3c/k)+2cq^2/[3(1-q)k].

For a fixed seed, k->infinity and t/k->lambda in [0,infinity) imply

    H(F)/I(F) -> (lambda h+4)/(lambda i+1).

For t/k->infinity the limit is h/i. For t/k->0 it is 4. No convergence of t/k is needed for the uniform displayed interval. In the core-dominant small-influence regime k=o(t), qt->0, one has I(F)->0, minority probability ->0, and H(F)/I(F)->h/i. Example: t=k^2, q=2^{-k}.

For arbitrary nonconstant, possibly unbalanced f, use g_t=z product_{j=1}^t f_j instead. Then the same proof substitutes H(g_t)=tH(f), I(g_t)=tI(f)+1. Taking t=k^2 preserves H(f)/I(f), while influence and minority probability vanish. This reproves the baseline equivalence along a different explicit sequence; no new restricted endpoint is claimed.

### 3. Exact magnitude-mismatch/KL debt on a control split

At an active node with s>=1 controls remaining, set u=2^{-s}. Its children are constant 1 and an activation with s-1 controls and mask probability p=2u. The balanced core is unchanged. Denote the child Fourier coefficients a_W,b_W, packet law Q_W=(a_W^2+b_W^2)/2, magnitude mismatch m_W=(|a_W|-|b_W|)^2/4, mu=sum m_W, and nu_W=m_W/mu.

The all-empty packet has

    Q0=1-2u+2u^2, m0=u^2.

Every other nonzero packet has a_W=0, so m_W=Q_W/2. Thus mu=u and the remaining mismatch mass is u-u^2. This includes s=1, where the active child is the balanced core and Q0=1/2. Magnitude mismatch coincides with signed mismatch here because the empty child coefficient 1-2u is nonnegative and all other constant-child coefficients vanish. That coincidence is family-specific; magnitude mismatch and disagreement cannot be interchanged for general splits.

The full residual from the source theorem is therefore exactly

    J_s=mu[log(1/mu)-D_2(nu||Q)]
       =sum_W m_W log(Q_W/m_W)
       =u-u^2+u^2 log(Q0/u^2).

The KL credit is, explicitly,

    D_2(nu||Q)=s-1-u[2s-1+log Q0].

It is independent of t and of the balanced seed. The conditional packet entropy is exactly

    L_s=2(u-u^2)+Q0 h_2(u^2/Q0).

For s>=2, Q0>=1-2u and |log Q0|<=2cu/(1-2u)<=4cu. This yields uniform expansions

    J_s=u+(2s-1)u^2+E_s,       |E_s|<=4cu^3,
    L_s=2u+(2s-2+c)u^2+F_s,   |F_s|<=5cu^3.

For the second bound, write L_s=2(u-u^2)+u^2 log(Q0/u^2)+c u^2 minus a nonnegative tail bounded by c u^4/[2(Q0-u^2)]<=c u^4. Both errors in fact are nonpositive. The s=1 formulas are kept exact: J_1=1/2, L_1=1. They are not forced into a small-u expansion.

The physical entropy decrement is also exact and independent of t:

    Delta_s=B_s(u)-B_{s-1}(2u)/2, with B_0(1)=0.

At s=1 the active leaf's core entropy cancels separately and Delta_1=B_1(1/2)=2. This identity comes directly from Section 1 and needs no imported inequality. The source theorem's additive 3c mu term is a distinct, already linearly paid allowance; it is not part of J_s, and J_s is not the blind scalar mu log(1/mu).

For completeness its uniform small-u expansion, s>=2, is

    Delta_s=4u+(2s-4+3c)u^2+G_s,
    0<=G_s<=8cu^3/[3(1-2u)]<=16cu^3/3.

Indeed G_s=R(u)-R(2u)/2=4c sum_{j>=3}(2^{j-1}-1)u^j/[j(j-1)(j-2)] is nonnegative and at most |R(2u)|/2. Thus the entropy decrement, packet entropy and mismatch debt are each expanded uniformly in both parameters, with the final balanced-core split kept exact.

### 4. Exact control-first tree, nonreuse, and uniform tree asymptotics

Query each control, stopping immediately on the first mismatch. The active node with s controls remaining has physical reach probability w_s=2^{-(k-s)}=q/u. Inactive leaves are constant; the single fully active leaf is g_t and has reach q. The queried influence budget is exactly

    sum_s w_s mu_s=kq.

The unqueried leaf influence is qti, so I(F)=kq+qti: no unit is reused. Entropy telescopes exactly as

    H(F)=sum_s w_s Delta_s+qth=B_k(q)+qth.

Its complete mismatch debt is

    J_tree=sum_s w_s J_s=q[k+A_k],
    A_k=sum_{s=1}^k 2^{-s}[2s-1+log(1-2^{1-s}+2^{1-2s})].

For s=1 the bracket is zero. For s>=2, Q0>=1/2, so 2s-1+log Q0>=2s-2>=0; also log Q0<=0. Thus 0<=A_k<=sum_{s>=1}2^{-s}(2s-1)=3. The increasing sequence has a limit A in [0,3], and its tail obeys

    0<=A-A_k<=sum_{s>k}2^{-s}(2s-1)=(2k+3)2^{-k}.

Hence, uniformly in t,

    q(k+A)-q^2(2k+3) <= J_tree <= q(k+A),
    J_tree <= q(k+3) <= 4kq.

This closes the control-only mismatch payment for this family. If the source inequality Delta<=J+3c mu is used, the full upper ledger includes both J_tree+3ckq AND qth. The linear term is paid from queried control influence; terminal entropy requires a separate core payment from qti.

In contrast, the blind rarity charge sums to

    sum_s w_s mu_s log(1/mu_s)=q k(k+1)/2.

The exact weighted KL credit is the difference between this quantity and J_tree. Dropping it creates a factor of order k even on this ordinary balanced-core activation. This is not the sparse-bent surgery no-go and does not decide what tree is optimal for that different family.

### 5. Necessary potential tests, stated with their quantifiers

For any C and any functional P satisfying H(f)<=P(f)<=C I(f) on all these activations, necessarily

    P(F)/q >= th+4k+2c-q(2k+3c)+R(q)/q,
    C(ti+k) >= th+4k+2c-q(2k+3c)+R(q)/q.

Thus for every admitted seed, C>=h/i in the core-dominant regime and C>=4 in the control-dominant regime. A functional vanishing faster than qth when k=o(t) and h>0 cannot dominate entropy, even though I and minority probability vanish. The exact induction slack is

    C I(F)-H(F)
      =q[t(Ci-h)+(C-4)k-2c+q(2k+3c)]-R(q).

For C=h/i<4 and core-dominant t, the ratio still approaches C but the displayed finite-family slack is negative for all sufficiently large k. Limit ratio preservation must not be mistaken for inheritance of an exact finite constant.

For the specific tree potential T=J_tree+terminal_entropy, the terminal term is exactly qth. Its asymptotic coefficient is (lambda h+1)/(lambda i+1) for finite lambda=t/k, not the coefficient 4 of the actual entropy. If a proposed bound T<=K I is asserted for all these cores, it necessarily has K>=max(1,h/i). This is a conditional test of this explicit potential, not an assertion that every imaginable debt has that coefficient. Adding the separately paid 3c control allowance changes the selector coefficient to 1+3c and leaves the hidden-core obstruction unchanged.

Uniform FEI for this fixed-seed activation class follows, without any mismatch theorem, from b(q)<=2cq:

    H(F)/I(F) <= max(h/i,4+2c/k) <= max(h/i,4+2c).

The hidden seed ratio is fully retained. With arbitrary cores this is not an absolute bound, since a uniform h/i bound is exactly the missing FEI obligation.

### Balanced coefficient identity



Write g_i=sum_{U subset B_i} c_i(U) chi_U and h=sum_{T subset [k]} a(T) chi_T. Balance means c_i(empty)=0. For a physical Fourier set S, put S_i=S intersect B_i and T(S)={i:S_i is nonempty}. Expanding h after substitution, a term indexed by T can contribute to S only if T=T(S): unused blocks contribute the constant 1, and every used block has a nonempty set. Disjoint blocks make the union unique. Hence, including S=empty,



    Fhat(S) = a(T(S)) product_{i in T(S)} c_i(S_i).                  (1)



Parseval gives sum_{U nonempty} c_i(U)^2=1. Thus the spectral law is exactly the following experiment: sample T with probability a(T)^2; independently for each i in T sample a nonempty U_i with probability c_i(U_i)^2; output S=union U_i. Conversely S recovers T and every U_i. The experiment is bijective on positive-probability outcomes, so its entropy is H(h)+sum_i P(i in T)H(g_i). Its squared entries have entropy `H(S_A | S_B=T)`.  Define the normalized physical posterior vector



\[

b_z=\frac{\sqrt{\mu_{p_A}(z)}\,\widehat{f_z}_{p_B}(T)}{\sqrt{q_T}},

\qquad z\in\{0,1\}^A.

\]



Its squared entries are the conditional law `Pr[Z=z | T]`.



The two vectors are related by the tensor orthogonal matrix



\[

b=U_Aa,

\qquad

U_A=\bigotimes_{i\in A}

\begin{pmatrix}

\sqrt{1-p_i}&-\sqrt{p_i}\\

\sqrt{p_i}&\sqrt{1-p_i}

\end{pmatrix}.

\tag{3.1}

\]



The largest absolute matrix entry is



\[

c_A=\prod_{i\in A}\sqrt{\max\{p_i,1-p_i\}}.

\]



Maassen--Uffink therefore gives, fiber by fiber,



\[

H(a^2)+H(b^2)

\ge-2\log_2c_A

=-\sum_{i\in A}\log_2\max\{p_i,1-p_i\}.

\tag{3.2}

\]



Average (3.2) with weights `q_T`.  The first average is `M_A`; the second is `H(Z|T)`.  Since



\[

J_A=H(Z)-H(Z\mid T),

\qquad

H(Z)=\sum_{i\in A}h_2(p_i),

\]



we obtain



\[

J_A\le M_A+\Delta_A(p).

\]



Together with `L_A=M_A+J_A` and `J_A>=0`, this proves (0.1).  The endpoint expansion (0.3) follows by expanding `h_2(p)` and `log_2(1-p)`; the linear `p/ln 2` terms cancel.  QED.

This is a product-biased restriction lemma.  It is not a strong FEI inequality for arbitrary weighted thresholds, and it does not bound `M_A` by influence.  Its value is to identify exactly how the uniform Walsh uncertainty payment degrades at biased gate inputs.
