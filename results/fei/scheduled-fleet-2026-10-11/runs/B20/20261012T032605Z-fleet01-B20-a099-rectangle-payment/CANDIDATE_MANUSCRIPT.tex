\section*{A099 candidate assembly: rectangle payment and the surviving leaf}

All functions below are genuine Boolean functions on a uniform discrete cube,
all logarithms in entropy are base two, and all influences are physical
coordinate influences.  This note assembles only source-supported statements.
It does not assert the Fourier Entropy--Influence conjecture.

\subsection*{1. Definitions}

Write the variables as $x\in\{-1,1\}^A$ and
$y\in\{-1,1\}^B$, where $A\sqcup B=[n]$.  Define the multiplicative rectangle
defect
\[
 \rho_A(f)=\Pr_{x,x',y,y'}\!left[
 f(x,y)f(x',y)f(x,y')f(x',y')=-1\right].
\]
For a coordinate $i$, put $p_i=I_i(f)$.  Let
\[
 \Delta_iH(f)=H(f)-\frac{H(f_{i=1})+H(f_{i=-1})}{2}.
\]

\subsection*{2. Universal physical payment for the rectangle defect}

\paragraph{Theorem 1 (proved).}
For every Boolean $f$ and every nontrivial physical cut $A\mid B$,
\[
 \boxed{\rho_A(f)\le \min\{I_A(f),I_B(f)\}.}
\]

\paragraph{Proof.}
Fix $y,y'$ and set
\[
 r_{y,y'}(x)=f(x,y)f(x,y').
\]
For a $\{-1,1\}$-valued random variable $r(X)$, two independent copies
differ with probability $\operatorname{Var}(r)/2$.  Consequently
\[
 \rho_A(f)=\frac12\,\mathbb E_{y,y'}\operatorname{Var}_x(r_{y,y'}).
\]
The discrete-cube Poincar\'e inequality gives
\[
 \operatorname{Var}_x(r_{y,y'})\le \sum_{i\in A}I_i(r_{y,y'}).
\]
For fixed $x,i,y,y'$, the product $r_{y,y'}$ changes across the $i$-edge
exactly when precisely one of the two Boolean edges
$f(\cdot,y)$ and $f(\cdot,y')$ changes.  Therefore, by the union bound,
\[
 \mathbb E_{y,y'} I_i(r_{y,y'})
 \le 2 I_i(f).
\]
After summing over $i\in A$ and multiplying by $1/2$, this proves
$\rho_A(f)\le I_A(f)$.  Interchanging $A$ and $B$ proves the other bound.
\hfill$\square$

This theorem removes every dependence on dimension, block size, bias, depth,
address count, overlap, and correlation gap from the payment of the
\emph{geometric} rectangle defect itself.

\paragraph{Theorem 2 (proved exact singleton identity).}
For every Boolean $f$ and every coordinate $i$,
\[
 \boxed{\rho_{\{i\}}(f)=p_i(1-p_i).}
\]

\paragraph{Proof.}
For a fixed pair $y,y'$ of assignments to the other coordinates, the
rectangle product is negative only if the two sampled values of $x_i$ differ
and the pivotality indicators of the two $i$-edges disagree.  The first event
has probability $1/2$.  Two independent Bernoulli variables of mean $p_i$
disagree with probability $2p_i(1-p_i)$.  Multiplication proves the identity.
\hfill$\square$

In particular,
\[
 \sum_i\rho_{\{i\}}(f)=I(f)-\sum_i I_i(f)^2\le I(f).
\]

\paragraph{Corollary 3 (proved nonreusing stopping-tree budget).}
Let $T$ be any physical restriction tree which never queries a coordinate
twice on one root--leaf path.  If $w_v$ and $w_\ell$ are the uniform
probabilities of reaching an internal node $v$ and leaf $\ell$, respectively,
then
\[
 \sum_{v\in T}w_v\rho_{\{i(v)\}}(f_v)
 \le \sum_{v\in T}w_v I_{i(v)}(f_v)
 =I(f)-\sum_{\ell}w_\ell I(f_\ell)
 \le I(f).
\]

\paragraph{Proof.}
The first inequality is Theorem 2.  At every node,
\[
 I(f_v)=I_{i(v)}(f_v)+\frac{I((f_v)_{i=1})+I((f_v)_{i=-1})}{2}.
\]
Multiplying by $w_v$ and telescoping proves the equality and final inequality.
\hfill$\square$

Thus the rectangle defect is a globally nonreused physical-influence budget,
not merely a local diagnostic.

\subsection*{3. Substitution into the audited near-product route}

The A054 rectangle-interface theorem starts from the same $\rho_A(f)$ and,
for descendant-critical $f$ of ratio $R$, extracts a genuine Boolean
restriction $u(x)=f(x,y_0)$ satisfying
\[
 R I(u)-H(u)\le E_{n,a}(\rho),
\]
where $a=|A|$ and
\[
 E_{n,a}(\rho)=2h_2(2\sqrt\rho)+(2n-a)2\sqrt\rho+6an\rho.
\]
That statement is proved conditional on the still-unreviewed A053 input.
Theorem 1 now proves that its input interface is always physically paid:
\[
 \rho_A(f)\le I_A(f).
\]
This closes the geometric-payment half of the A054 admission gap.

It does \emph{not} close the analytic half.  Substitution only yields
\[
 E_{n,a}(\rho_A(f))
 \le 2h_2(2\sqrt{I_A(f)})+(2n-a)2\sqrt{I_A(f)}+6anI_A(f)
\]
when the displayed entropy term is in range.  The square-root and ambient
dimension losses are not bounded by a dimension-free constant times
$I_A(f)$.  For singleton cuts with $p_i\downarrow0$,
$\rho_i=p_i(1-p_i)\sim p_i$ while $\sqrt{\rho_i}/p_i\to\infty$.
Hence a generic Fannes/transport continuity estimate cannot finish the proof.

\subsection*{4. The surviving universal leaf}

The A096 critical-pruning identity proves that every descendant-critical
source of ratio $R$ obeys
\[
 \Delta_iH(f)\ge R I_i(f)
\]
at every essential coordinate.  Exact tensor purity and the A097 rank-one
classification remove nontrivial exact products and exact rank-one selectors
from a strict critical endpoint.  Combining those reductions with Theorem 2
isolates the following strictly narrower statement than full FEI.

\paragraph{Critical rectangle decrement inequality (open, version 1).}
There exists an absolute constant $C_{\rm rect}<\infty$ such that every
nonconstant, descendant-critical, tensor-prime Boolean function has an
essential coordinate $i$ satisfying
\[
 \boxed{\Delta_iH(f)\le C_{\rm rect}\rho_{\{i\}}(f)
 =C_{\rm rect}I_i(f)(1-I_i(f)).}
\tag{CRD}
\]

If (CRD) holds, then the critical inequality at that coordinate gives
\[
 R I_i(f)\le\Delta_iH(f)
 \le C_{\rm rect}I_i(f)(1-I_i(f))\le C_{\rm rect}I_i(f),
\]
and hence $R\le C_{\rm rect}$.  A minimum-dimensional counterexample to a
proposed constant is descendant-critical and tensor-prime by A096/A052, so
(CRD), together with those reviewed reductions, would prove full FEI with
$C=C_{\rm rect}$.

This is the first unpaid line in the present assembly.  It is narrower than
FEI in both its source class and its conclusion: it asks for one coordinate of
a critical prime source, rather than an entropy bound for every Boolean
function.  It also preserves the exact terminal and normalization terms.

\subsection*{5. Adversarial checks and finite evidence}

Theorems 1--3 are compatible with addressing and multiplexers, rare
restrictions, near tensors, shared variables, and prime gates because they use
the actual physical cut and charge its rectangle defect only once.  They do
not assert selector orthogonality, component entropy subadditivity, or a local
rare-event refund.  Parity-zero erasure is also preserved: when $p_i=1$,
$\rho_i=0$, and the pure parity split has $\Delta_iH=0$.

An exhaustive numerical probe of all Boolean functions through four variables
found 1,146 descendant-critical tensor-prime tables (under floating
classification).  The largest observed
$\min_i\Delta_iH/\rho_i$ was $8$, attained by the two-bit one-point gate.
The $m$-bit one-point/AND family has the ratio decreasing from $8$ at $m=2$
toward $4$ in the tested range $m\le12$.  This evidence is NUMERICAL only and
does not certify (CRD), any arbitrary dimension, or the candidate constant
$8$.

The exact checker independently verified Theorems 1--3 for every Boolean
truth table through four variables, all nontrivial cuts up to complement, and
the complete fixed-order restriction tree, using 1,313,644 integer
assertions.  The analytic proofs above establish the all-dimensional claims;
the finite audit is reproduction evidence only.
