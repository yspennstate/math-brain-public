# Cross-route scope

The proved transfer is an accounting identity, not an inequality.

- B11 supplies the local fixed-root deficit interpretation at a ratio-plateau
  node. This run independently rederives the edge identity and telescopes it
  over arbitrary finite physical trees.
- B19 independently accepted the algebraic signed telescope for the B05
  one-point-defect packet. This run transfers only that accounting pattern.
- The B05/B19 `L+Q` credit and the present `s_R` are different quantities.
  No sign theorem is transported between them.
- Descendant criticality makes terminal fixed-root deficits nonnegative, but
  it does not make every internal slack nonnegative. A complete tree has
  total signed slack exactly zero.
- The identity contains the unknown root ratio `R=H/I`; it cannot be used as
  a dimension-independent FEI constant without a separate compensator or
  stopping theorem.

The first concrete next test is a compensator of the form
`P_R(g)>=0` for which

`s_R(g,i)+E[P_R(child)]-P_R(g)`

has a paid sign along a globally selected tree, while `P_R` at the root and
leaves is bounded without assuming an a priori bound on `R`. Any candidate
must be evaluated first on the exact `q_n` signed chain and on equal-ratio
tensors, where local positivity and qualitative primitivity are known to be
misleading.
