\section{A paid reference survives every finite no-reset history}
\label{sec:conservative}
The following identities use complete normalized references. They refine
the continuation boundary of the supplied-source floor above; they do not
construct an inexpensive reference for an arbitrary Boolean function.
The source is A096, \emph{A paid reference can be carried without resetting it},
run \path{20261014T094929Z-fleet05-A096-live-gap-ledger},
\path{DERIVATION.md}, Sections 1--4, original version 1.

\begin{lemma}[Conservative complete-reference transport]
For every finite Boolean sign $f$, let $P(S)=\widehat f(S)^2$ and
let $Q$ be a probability law on all subsets with $Q(S)>0$ whenever
$P(S)>0$. For a physical query $j$, set
\[
 m_T=P(T)+P(T+j),\qquad v_T=Q(T)+Q(T+j).
\]
Give both actual children the same reference $v$. Then
\[
 \CE(P,Q)=B_j(f)+\tfrac12\sum_{\epsilon=\pm1}
                  \CE(P_{f_\epsilon},v)+R_j,
 \qquad R_j=D_2(P\Vert Q)-D_2(m\Vert v)\ge0.
 \tag{NR1}
\]
Every finite outcome-adaptive tree, using only these inherited
references, therefore satisfies
\[
 \CE(P_f,Q)=\sum_{u\text{ internal}}w_u(B_{j_u}(f_u)+R_u)
                  +\sum_{\ell\text{ leaf}}w_\ell\CE(P_{f_\ell},Q_\ell),
 \qquad w_u=2^{-\operatorname{depth}(u)}.
 \tag{NR2}
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Write $a_T=\widehat f(T)$ and $b_T=\widehat f(T+j)$.
The actual child coefficients are $a_T+\epsilon b_T$, hence their
mean squared masses are $a_T^2+b_T^2=m_T$. Parseval for the actual
Boolean children proves their normalization. If $v_T=0$, both
parent coefficients and both child coefficients vanish, so every
displayed cost is finite. The reference $v$ has total mass one,
including all reference-only atoms.

For $m_T>0$, disintegrate $P(T,b)$ and $Q(T,b)$ into their
marginal and conditional laws. The conditional cross entropy is
$H(P(b\mid T))+D_2(P(b\mid T)\Vert Q(b\mid T))$.
Multiplication by $m_T$ and summation give (NR1), because the first
conditional entropy sum is $B_j$ and cross entropy is linear in
its true-law argument. Unoccupied fibers contribute zero.
The conditional divergence is nonnegative by
$\ln x\le x-1$ applied to $x=q_b/p_b$ on occupied atoms:
$\sum p_b\ln(p_b/q_b)\ge\sum_{p_b>0}(p_b-q_b)\ge0$.
This also covers reference mass at unoccupied conditional atoms.

Multiply (NR1) at each internal node by its physical reach
probability. Every child's weighted cost cancels once against
its parent's child term. Finite depth makes the cancellation exact,
without any limit exchange. This proves (NR2). Signed coefficients
are required to determine each child law, even though marginalizing
the reference itself needs no phase information.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[One original paid source account]
For the supplied negative-cell floor above, assume $c\le C_0 I(g)$.
For every finite no-reset physical history on the edited block,
\[
 \sum_u w_uB_{j_u}(F_u)+
 \sum_\ell w_\ell\bigl[H(F_\ell)+D_2(P_{F_\ell}\Vert Q_\ell)\bigr]
 \le (2C_0+10)I(F).
 \tag{NR3}
\]
The structural source gives rate $18$. An independent untouched
terminal with prescribed reference cost $c_h$ adds exactly $c_h$
to both root and terminal costs and supplies no edit budget.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Use the supplied-source floor price already proved above in (NR2)
and discard only the nonnegative $R_u$ terms. In a tensor product,
the inherited reference is the edited-block marginal times the
untouched reference. Cross entropy adds, and leaf reach weights
sum to one. Thus the untouched cost is retained with coefficient one.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[A matching root does not give local leaf prices]
On the three-bit truth table $(1,1,1,1,-1,1,1,1)$, with coordinate
0 the least significant input bit, let $Q=P_f$. Query coordinate 0
and take outcome 1. The child is constant $+1$, but its inherited
reference is $(5/8,1/8,1/8,1/8)$. Its complete cost is
$\log_2(8/5)>0$, although its influence is zero.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The root has mean $3/4$; all seven nonempty Fourier coefficients
have magnitude $1/4$. Thus $Q(\varnothing)=9/16$ and every
other mass is $1/16$. Marginalizing one membership bit gives
the displayed reference. All negative truth-table entries have
coordinate 0 equal to zero, so the stated child is constant.
The complete cost of a point law is minus the logarithm of its
reference mass. Here $5/8<1$, proving strict positivity.
\end{proof}

\section{The signed reset bill is the remaining account test}
\label{sec:resetbill}
Fix a stopped frontier whose complete expected reference cost is
$T$, an ancestor quota $A$, and an already executed debit $d$.
Define the genuinely unused balance $U=A-d-T$.
After further no-reset queries, write $b$ for their expected erasure
bill, $r$ for their expected conditional refunds, and $T'$ for
their full expected terminal cost. Equation (NR2) gives
$T=b+r+T'$.

Suppose selected final references $Q'_\ell$ are now replaced by
specified normalized references $\widetilde Q_\ell$ which also
support their actual terminal laws. Define the signed full reset
bill
\[
 Z=\sum_\ell w_\ell\sum_S P_\ell(S)
                \log_2\frac{Q'_\ell(S)}{\widetilde Q_\ell(S)}.
 \tag{RS1}
\]
Unchanged terminals are included by taking
$\widetilde Q_\ell=Q'_\ell$. Then exactly
\[
 A-(d+b)-\sum_\ell w_\ell\CE(P_\ell,\widetilde Q_\ell)
       =U+r-Z.
 \tag{RS2}
\]
Indeed the new complete terminal cost is $T'+Z$, and substitution
of $T=b+r+T'$ proves the identity. A negative reset bill releases
capacity; a positive bill consumes it. This is bookkeeping, with
no assertion that the sign or magnitude is favorable. It keeps
every complementary terminal and every executed debit.

For this specified erasure-and-terminal account, the precise
necessary and sufficient admission is $Z\le U+r$. No-reset
continuation has $Z=0$ and is therefore automatic once the root
account is paid. A construction that additionally charges the
channel term $J_j$ or a code for a reference label must include
that extra debit as well; (RS2) does not pay it implicitly.

The universal missing statement is the existence of useful,
noncircularly chosen references and physical histories for arbitrary
Boolean sources, with one dimension-independent initial price and
all required resets, channel terms and complete terminals paid by
the same influence account. The four universal conjectures above
remain open. Neither marginalization nor (RS2) proves any of them.
The integrated dependency chain still requires two independent
audits. A099's reconstruction of A096 is a scoped review of (NR1)--(NR3)
and the constant-child boundary, not an acceptance of full FEI.
