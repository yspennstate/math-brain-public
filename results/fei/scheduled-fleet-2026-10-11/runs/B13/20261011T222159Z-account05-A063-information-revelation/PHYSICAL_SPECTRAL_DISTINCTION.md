# Physical branches, erased labels, and actual information

The joint law must be stated before claiming that a physical query reveals information about a spectral sample. The physical experiment first selects a fair actual Boolean child and samples its residual squared spectrum. It determines the joint (branch, residual label) law, but does not by itself determine a joint law including the deleted parent spectral label.

For a singleton write parent coefficients a_T,b_T and q_T=a_T^2+b_T^2. The erased spectral bit has conditional probability beta_T=b_T^2/q_T; the physical + branch given T has probability rho_T=(a_T+b_T)^2/(2q_T). These probabilities differ. The charges are B=sum q h2(beta) and J=sum q[1-h2(rho)], with decrement B+J. For a block, the campaign collision notation is B_W=B_A and M_W=J_A=a-sum q H(r_T). The latter is genuine mixture information, with its posterior label entropy retained.

The canonical A037 lift makes erased bit and branch independent given the residual label. It gives I(parent label; branch)=J. Other couplings consistent with the same physical/residual experiment can add I(erased bit; branch | residual). Therefore the phrase 'information actually revealed' means information under a specified kernel. It is not a unique coupling-free observable of the squared parent and child laws.

An exact nonuniqueness control is the three-bit point defect f(x)=1-2*1{x=0}, querying one coordinate. Its dominant fiber has q=5/8, erased-bit probability beta=1/10, and probability rho=1/5 of the sign + branch. In the four cells (B=0,Z=-),(B=0,Z=+),(B=1,Z=-),(B=1,Z=+), canonical conditional probabilities are (18/25,9/50,2/25,1/50). The alternative is (4/5,1/10,0,1/10). Both have the same B and Z marginals. The alternative adds (5/8)I(B;Z|this fiber)>0; strict positivity follows from nonindependence and Gibbs inequality. All other fibers may retain the canonical coupling. The physical branch/residual law is unchanged.

If two actual children are isospectral, then their squared residual laws P_+,P_- agree and J=0. Their equal magnitudes also imply a_T*b_T=0 in every parent fiber, so B=0. The fair branch outcome still has one bit of entropy. This holds even when the physical child functions differ, for example g versus -g. Branch entropy is not released spectral entropy.

For nearly identical squared child laws let alpha=sum_T sqrt(P_+(T)P_-(T)) and m=(1-alpha)/2. The minority parent mass in each fiber is min(a_T^2,b_T^2)=(sqrt(P_+)-sqrt(P_-))^2/4. Summing gives m. Hence Jensen's inequality gives B<=h2(m), and the scalar Hellinger/JS estimate gives J<=2m/ln2. Both charges vanish with m; no fixed one-bit fee is appropriate. These are existing baseline estimates, not universal linear erasure payment. The checker records exact expressions for one-point defects r=2..8; their children are a constant and a one-point defect on r-1 bits and are asymptotically identical.

Sequential queries of a fixed block create another precise distinction. Under the canonical singleton lift, the cumulative information equals J_A+R_A with R_A=I(S_A;X_A|S_Ac), whereas cumulative erasure equals B_A-R_A=H(S_A|S_Ac,X_A). Direct-block coupling makes this refund zero; the singleton lift can have a positive, order-dependent refund. These lifts have the same actual final physical assignment/residual-spectrum law. Their root-label/history correlation can differ.

There is a sharp ceiling on this mechanism. The elementary inequality h2(t)>=4t(1-t) gives J_h<=B_h in every singleton fiber, because the two relevant Bernoulli variances sum to 1/4. Consequently R_A<=(B_A-J_A)/2 and any canonical singleton transcript reveals at most [H(f)-E H(terminal)]/2 bits of the root spectrum. At least half the initial spectral entropy remains in the root posterior even after all physical coordinates have been exposed. AND2 attains equality: direct full-block charges are (B,J)=(2,0), sequential charges are (1,1), and refund R=1. Disjoint AND2 products make this half-entropy barrier sharp in unbounded dimension. This is a limit on this coupling-based information mechanism, not a counterexample to FEI.

For reference laws Q_x, the reference posterior is t_T(x)=2^-a Q_x(T)/Q(T). Its posterior divergence equals true J_A plus E log2(2^-a/t_T(X)). Omitting this signed correction confuses reference mismatch with actual released information. A037's channel/reset ledger is the appropriate generalization when references or reveal kernels change across nodes.
