All entropies and KL divergences are in bits. This is a delayed final conversion theorem, not an exercised-reset theorem.

In each branch query at least one private z coordinate, and optionally continue with distinct private coordinates in any adaptive order and stop at any subsequent cut. Do not query x or b. Propagate the SAME reference with the actual common kernel, including the fair extension on source-zero rows. Replace the reference by the actual product law only once, at the final cut.

Write A=KL(P||M), E for accumulated conditional erasure KL, O for accumulated outcome KL, and K for expected terminal inherited-reference KL. The established common-kernel identity gives A=E+O+K, including all terminal laws. If TC denotes expected final total correlation, the extra final-conversion payment beyond E is D=TC-K-E=O+TC-A. Since E,K>=0, O<=A, hence D<=TC. This inequality is independent of which supported original reference was used. It does not identify a new reserve and does not authorize intermediate resets.

After any private history the active child is a point defect or its x twist on m>=2 variables; inactive children are constants or the x character. Once each branch has made its first private query, the total reach of active children is at most 1/2 (later splitting preserves or decreases it).
