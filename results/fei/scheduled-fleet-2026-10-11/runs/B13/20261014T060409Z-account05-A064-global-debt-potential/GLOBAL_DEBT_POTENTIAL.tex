\begin{theorem}[Finite certified reference continuation]
Let $v$ be any Boolean function on three uniform data bits and let
$F=-1$ on $x=0,a=1$, $F=v$ on $x=1,b=1$, and $F=+1$ otherwise.
All original control and data blocks are independent. Query $a$, followed
by the same chosen physical data coordinate in both actual children.
Let $M$ be the completed original matched-marginal nonempty occupancy
reference, $A=D(P_F\Vert M)$, and $R_b,R_{bt}$ the actual child and
grandchild product projections. Use the full physical coherent channel,
extended fairly on source-zero fibers, for every inherited reference.
Write $T_b=D(P_b\Vert R_b)$, $T_{bt}=D(P_{bt}\Vert R_{bt})$ and $O$
for reference-outcome divergence from physical fairness. Define
\[
D_a=O_a+\mathbb E_b T_b-A,\quad
D_b=O_b+\mathbb E_t T_{bt}-T_b,\quad
W=[D_a]_++\mathbb E_b[D_b]_+.
\]
The exhaustive rational interval certificate for all $256\cdot3$ cases
proves $W<\overline S/2$, where
$S_b=4I(F_b)-H(F_b)\ge0$ and $\overline S=\mathbb E_bS_b>0$.
Assume this particular child surplus has not already been spent.
The nonnegative potential
\[
P_0=A+W,\qquad P_b=T_b+[D_b]_+,\qquad P_{bt}=T_{bt}
\]
satisfies $P-\mathbb E P_{\rm child}\ge O$ and
\[
\mathbb E P_\tau+\mathbb E\sum_{q<\tau}O_q\le P_0<A+\overline S/2
\]
on every stopping cut of the declared finite tree. Terminal KL is
retained. In particular
\[
O_a+\mathbb E_bO_b+\mathbb E_{b,t}{\rm CE}(P_{bt},R_{bt})
\le A+W+\mathbb E_{b,t}H(F_{bt})
< A+4\mathbb E_b I(F_b).
\]
All intermediate and terminal entropy and reference laws are actual
physical restrictions. No conditional reference KL, reference-only row,
or second copy of the original bank is discarded. No arbitrary-dimensional
core certificate or universal FEI claim is made.
\end{theorem}
\paragraph{Proof.} The exact normalized chain rule, signed conversion
identity, expected-terminal induction, complete terminal bill and finite
coverage/error certificate are in PROOF.md Sections 1--5 and code/check.py.
The root certificate and all margin intervals are in out/VALIDATION.json.
