## 1. Supplied carrier/reference and fair filtration

Let g be any Boolean sign on m ordinary physical coordinates, R a normalized spectral reference containing its squared Fourier support, and u,v fresh carrier bits. Let L be a linear parity of ordinary bits and c an affine bit. The actual function is

    F(u,v,y)=(-1)^((u+v+L(y)+c)*v) g(y).

Its actual spectral law and supplied reference are the lifts

    P(su,sv,T)=p_g(T xor su*L)/4,
    Q(su,sv,T)=R(T xor su*L)/4.

Both include all ambient spectral atoms. Source-zero/reference-positive rows use the prescribed fair physical kernel1/2. The underlying probability space is the original independent physical cube. The filtration is generated by revealed physical coordinates and outcomes; each next coordinate is measurable with respect to the current history. Remaining coordinates are uniform at every reached history. All stopping depths are bounded by m+1 after entering this carrier, so no infinite-stopping or limit exchange is used.

An ordinary query j erases its spectral indicator in both source and reference. If L_j=1, the erased terms merely exchange when su changes. The actual physical children have the same carrier, updated c+bL_j, and core g_b. Thus the full kernel and normalized transport reduce EXACTLY to the core-only kernel

    k_b(T)=p_(g_b)(T)/(2 p_g^(-j)(T)) on positive source rows,
           1/2 on zero source rows.

The fair extension agrees in the full lift and core transport, including every reference-only row. The new reference is the lift of the actual normalized R_b. This proves the invariant by induction for adaptive ordinary histories; it does not require independent query choices. The physical outcome remains fair while the reference prediction r_b need not be fair. Source support and normalization imply r_b>0 for each actual Boolean child. The KL chain reads

    K_rho=E_rho+O_rho+(K_(rho0)+K_(rho1))/2,

where K=KL(p_g||R), E is nonnegative erasure conditional KL and O=KL(fair||r). Every denominator is canceled only on a positive-source row, where R has support. Gibbs gives K>=0. Ordinary queries do not break the carrier pairing: a later v query has O_v=0, including reference-only pairs. Its actual children are g and a character translate of g, and both terminal entropies equal H(g); both terminal product KLs equal TC(g). The unused u-coordinate is deterministic in their spectra. These are exact conditional transfer facts, not a free terminal FEI bound.

## 2. A single generic original reserve for ALL ordinary histories

    M(g)=sum_j h2(Inf_j(g)),
    TC(g)=M(g)-H(g),
    V_rho=K_rho+M(g_rho).

For every surviving coordinate j, its parent influence is the arithmetic mean of its two child influences under a different physical query i. This follows by expanding the two actual Fourier coefficient pairs, or counting its physical edges and averaging the fixed i bit. Binary entropy concavity therefore gives

    M(g)-mean_b M(g_b)
       =h2(Inf_i(g))+sum_(j!=i)[h2(Inf_j(g))-mean_b h2(Inf_j(g_b))]>=0.

Boundary influences0 and1 are included by continuity with h2(0)=h2(1)=0; there is no canceled influence denominator. Consequently

    V_rho-mean_b V_(rho b)
