\title{A063: physical query information and spectral erasure}
\maketitle
All entropies are in bits. Let $f:\{-1,1\}^n\to\{-1,1\}$ be Boolean.
The root spectral set has probability $\Pr(S=s)=\widehat f(s)^2$.
For a physical block $A$ and complement $C$, write
\[
 a_{u,w}=\widehat f(u\cup w),\quad
 c_{x,w}=\sum_{u\subseteq A}a_{u,w}\chi_u(x),\quad
 q_w=\sum_u a_{u,w}^2=2^{-|A|}\sum_x c_{x,w}^2.
\]
The actual physical/spectral joint experiment is
\[
 \Pr(X_A=x,W=w)=2^{-|A|}c_{x,w}^2.
\]
The physical assignment $X_A$ is uniform, by Boolean Parseval at each child.
Define $\nu_w(u)=a_{u,w}^2/q_w$ and
$r_w(x)=2^{-|A|}c_{x,w}^2/q_w$. Omit zero fibers. Then
\[
 B_A=H(S_A\mid S_C)=\sum_w q_w H(\nu_w),\qquad
 J_A=I(X_A;W)=|A|-\sum_wq_wH(r_w),
\]
and the exact decrement is
\[
 H(f)-\mathbb E_x H(f_x)=B_A+J_A.
\]
Thus $B_W$ in the collision interface is $B_A$ and $M_W$ is $J_A$.
The physical branch entropy $H(X_A)=|A|$ is neither of these charges.

\paragraph{Local classical lifts are not uniquely specified by child spectra.}
Any joint extension $\Pr(U,X_A,W)$ with marginals
$\Pr(U=u,W=w)=a_{u,w}^2$ and
$\Pr(X_A=x,W=w)=2^{-|A|}c_{x,w}^2$ obeys
\[
 I(U,W;X_A)=J_A+I(U;X_A\mid W).
\]
The additional term is nonnegative and at most $B_A$.
The canonical lift chooses $U$ and $X_A$ conditionally independent given $W$:
\[
 \Pr(U=u,W=w,X_A=x)=a_{u,w}^2r_w(x).
\]
It attains the minimal full-label information $J_A$.
Alternative lifts are mathematical couplings, not changes to the physical child law.

\paragraph{Finite adaptive tree chain rule (A037 reconstruction).}
For any finite tree with no repeated physical coordinates, at node $h$ let
$R_h$ be the remaining coordinates and $g_h$ the actual restricted function.
Maintain $S_h=S\cap R_h$ and draw the next physical block outcome using
\[
 k_h(x\mid S)=r_{h,w}(x),\qquad w=S\cap (R_h\setminus A_h).
\]
The policy depends only on physical history. At every node the outcome has
the actual uniform conditional law and the next spectral set has the actual
child spectrum. This follows by summing the joint formula over the erased
subset, then over $w$. Inductively it constructs a single joint root-label/
physical-transcript law. Since the kernel depends on $S$ only through $w$,
\[
 I(S;X_{A_h}\mid h)=I(w;X_{A_h}\mid h)=J_h.
\]
For the full stopped transcript $L$, with node probability $p_h$, Shannon's
chain rule and the retained local decrement give
\[
 \sum_h p_hJ_h=I(S;L),\qquad
 H(S\mid L)=\sum_h p_hB_h+\sum_{\ell}p_\ell H(g_\ell).
\]
No full-label posterior is identified with a leaf spectrum. The latter is
the law of $S\cap R_\ell$, and previously erased coordinates remain uncertain.

\paragraph{Fixed-block coarsening refund.}
Fix a block $A$. Query all and only its coordinates in an arbitrary finite
order adaptive to the values already observed, using the canonical singleton
kernel at each step. Let $Z=X_A$ be the final assignment, $U=S_A$ and $W=S_C$.
The complete transcript is in bijection with $Z$ because the policy is
deterministic. The induced $(Z,W)$ law is the physical-block joint law above:
each leaf has probability $2^{-|A|}$ and conditional $W$ law $\widehat f_Z^2$.
Let
\[
 \mathcal R_A=I(U;Z\mid W).
\]
Then the cumulative query charges obey the exact identities
\[
 \boxed{\sum_h p_hJ_h=J_A+\mathcal R_A,\qquad
 \sum_h p_hB_h=B_A-\mathcal R_A.}
\]
Indeed $I(S;Z)=I(W;Z)+I(U;Z\mid W)$ proves the first. The local decrements
sum to $H(f)-\mathbb E_ZH(f_Z)=B_A+J_A$, proving the second by subtraction.
Equivalently $\sum_h p_h B_h=H(U\mid W,Z)$.
Thus $0\le\mathcal R_A\le B_A$; the direct block lift has $\mathcal R_A=0$,
whereas the singleton-refined lift need not. The refund may depend on order.
Independent policy seeds can be conditioned on throughout and averaged;
the theorem does not replace conditional information by information with an
unrecorded seed or unrecorded stopping outcome.

\paragraph{Sharp refund ceiling and retained-information barrier.}
For $t\in[0,1]$, the elementary binary entropy bound is
$h_2(t)\ge4t(1-t)$. To prove it, write $v=|1-2t|$ and expand
\[
 1-h_2(t)=\frac1{\ln2}\sum_{k\ge1}
       \frac{v^{2k}}{2k(2k-1)}\le v^2,
\]
since the positive coefficients sum to $\ln2$ at $v=1$.
For a singleton fiber put $\beta=b^2/(a^2+b^2)$ and
$\rho=1/2+ab/(a^2+b^2)$. Direct algebra gives
$4\beta(1-\beta)+4\rho(1-\rho)=1$. Thus
$h_2(\beta)+h_2(\rho)\ge1$, so $J_h\le B_h$ at every node.
The coarsening identities consequently sharpen to
\[
 \boxed{0\le\mathcal R_A\le\tfrac12(B_A-J_A).}
\]
For any stopped singleton tree (with $L$ retaining its history),
\[
 \boxed{I(S;L)\le\tfrac12[H(f)-\mathbb EH(g_L)],\qquad
 H(S\mid L)\ge\tfrac12[H(f)+\mathbb EH(g_L)].}
\]
Hence even complete revelation of the physical input cannot extract more
than half the root spectral entropy under this canonical coupling.
This is sharp: the two-bit sign $f(x_1,x_2)=1-2\mathbf1_{x_1=x_2=+1}$
has four squared Fourier masses $1/4$, so $H(f)=2$.
Its first query has $B=J=1$ (constant and character children), and its
second query has $B=J=0$. For $A=\{1,2\}$ the direct block charges are
$B_A=2,J_A=0$, whereas sequential charges are $1,1$ and
$\mathcal R_A=1$. Thus the refund ceiling is attained.
Disjoint products of this two-bit sign, queried one pair at a time,
give equality $I(S;L)=H(f)/2$ in arbitrarily large dimensions.

\paragraph{Reference correction.}
Let $P_x(w)=c_{x,w}^2$ and let $Q_x$ be normalized supported reference laws.
Set $Q=2^{-|A|}\sum_xQ_x$ and
$t_w(x)=2^{-|A|}Q_x(w)/Q(w)$. Expanding the joint divergence in two orders gives
\[
 2^{-|A|}\sum_xD_2(P_x\Vert Q_x)
 =D_2(q\Vert Q)+\sum_wq_wD_2(r_w\Vert t_w).
\]
Consequently
\[
 \sum_wq_wD_2(r_w\Vert t_w)
 =J_A+\sum_{w,x}2^{-|A|}P_x(w)
       \log_2\frac{2^{-|A|}}{t_w(x)}.
\]
The second term is the signed reference correction, not automatically zero
or free credit. All laws here are normalized and divergences finite; support
failure gives an infinite term and cannot be subtracted.

\paragraph{Open bridge.}
The finite-tree identities already imply
$H(f)=I(S;L)+H(S\mid L)$. The mixture information has the standard bound
$\sum_hp_hJ_h\le (2/\ln2)I(f)$, but no universal influence bound for the
remaining $H(S\mid L)$ is proved here. Maximizing the coarsening refund does
not establish such a bound or eliminate the retained terminal term.
