\section*{A038 exact occupancy-account audit}
All logarithms are base two. Write $v=P(S\ne\varnothing)>0$, $P^+=P(\cdot\mid S\ne\varnothing)$, $r_i=P^+(i\in S)$, $d_i=vr_i$, and let $Q_r$ be the maximum-entropy nonempty law with marginals $r$. Put $Q_d=\bigotimes_i\mathrm{Bern}(d_i)$ and $M=(1-v)\delta_\varnothing+vQ_r$. The affine log-likelihood calculation in REPORT.md proves
\[
D(P\Vert Q_d)=vD(P^+\Vert Q_r)+\Delta,\qquad
\Delta=D(M\Vert Q_d)=\sum_i h_2(d_i)-h_2(v)-vH(Q_r)\ge0.
\]
If $c_d=Q_d(S\ne\varnothing)$ then
\[
\Delta=D(\mathrm{Bern}(v)\Vert\mathrm{Bern}(c_d))+vD(Q_r\Vert Q_d^+).
\]
For $f_m=1-2\mathbf1_{\{x=0\}}$, $m\ge2$, set $d=2^{1-m}$. Its nonempty spectrum is uniform on $2^m-1$ sets, $v=2d-d^2$, $r_i=(2-d)^{-1}$, and the projection parameter is $c=1-d/2$, with $cr_i=1/2$. Thus $K_{\rm occ}=0$, $I=md$, and $\Delta=K_{\rm full}\ge(m-5)I$.
The actual signed unconditioned product reference has squared collision masses, for $k\ge1$,
\[
w_k=[4d(1-d)]^k\bigl[1-2^{2-k}(1-d)^{m-k}\bigr]^2,\quad w_0=1.
\]
Let $Z=\sum_{k=0}^m\binom mk w_k$ and $T=Z^{-1}\sum_{k=1}^m\binom mk w_k\log(1/w_k)$. The singleton bound proved in REPORT.md gives $T/(4I)>m-7$ for all $m\ge8$. Hence for every finite $\kappa$ the explicitly changed candidate $K_{\rm occ}\ge T/4-\kappa I$ fails at any integer $m\ge8$ with $m>\kappa+7$. The same holds for $vK_{\rm occ}$. This refutes only the cross-law substitution, not A036's $K_{\rm full}$ estimate or FEI.
