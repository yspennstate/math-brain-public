\section*{Affine-cell Walsh compression}
Let $A=y_0+H\subseteq\mathbb F_2^c$ have codimension $d$,
$\alpha=2^{-d}$, and $W=H^\perp$.  If
$t=|\operatorname{supp}W|$ and $B\ne\varnothing$ has size $\ell$, define
\[
F(y,z,x)=1_A(y)\chi_B(z)h(x)+(1-1_A(y))g(x).
\]
Then
\[
H(F)=(1-\alpha)H(g)+\alpha H(h)
+L((1-\alpha)^2)+2d\alpha(2-\alpha),
\]
and
\[
I(F)=(1-\alpha)I(g)+\alpha I(h)+\alpha(t+\ell).
\]
Indeed, $\widehat{1_A}(T)=\alpha\chi_T(y_0)$ on $W$ and vanishes
off $W$.  The two displayed summands of $F$ have disjoint $z$-frequency
support.  This gives the entropy identity.  For influence, a selector
coordinate is free precisely when $e_i\in H$; otherwise $A$ and
$A+e_i$ are disjoint, and the fresh nontrivial character makes the
branch disagreement probability $1/2$, giving influence $\alpha$.
Finally $t\ge d$ and
\[
L((1-\alpha)^2)<3\alpha,qquad
2d\alpha(2-\alpha)<4d\alpha,
\]
so the surcharge is less than $4\alpha(d+\ell)\le
4\alpha(t+\ell)$.  Hence the class is closed under $H\le4I$.
