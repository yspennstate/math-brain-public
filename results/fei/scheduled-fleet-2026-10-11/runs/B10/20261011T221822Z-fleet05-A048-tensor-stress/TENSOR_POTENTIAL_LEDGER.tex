\title{A048: tensor scope of the existing B09 ledger}
\author{A048, stream B10}\date{11 October 2026}\maketitle
All entropies are Shannon bits. Write $e(x)=-x\log_2x$, with $e(0)=0$.
For an actual Boolean restriction channel, let
\[
 M_A=H(S_A\mid S_B),\quad J_A=I(Z;T),\quad L_A=M_A+J_A,
 \quad P_A=M_A+\Delta_A,
\]
where $\Delta_A=\sum_{i\in A}(h_2(p_i)+\log_2\max(p_i,1-p_i))$.
The existing candidate is $J_A\le P_A$. Its full uncertainty proof remains under review.

\paragraph{Aligned tensors.}
For disjoint independent Boolean factors, squared Fourier laws and actual child channels factor.
Hence $M,J,L,\Delta,P$ add for aligned reveals. Their $t$-fold values are exactly
$t$ times the one-factor values. No $t\log t$ term occurs.
An arbitrary wholly unrevealed common factor preserves these local ledgers and adds its
entropy to both parent entropy and expected terminal entropy.

\paragraph{Irrelevant coordinates.}
A revealed independent coordinate ignored by $f$ has deterministic empty spectral label
and is independent of $T$. It adds zero to $M,J,L,I_A$, but adds $\delta(p)$ to $P$.
At $p=1/8$, $\delta(p)=\log_2(7)/8>0$. Thus payment of the unreduced displayed
$P$ by revealed influence, or by total influence after arbitrarily many dummies, is false.
Contracting such coordinates repairs this specific scope defect, not universal payment.

\paragraph{One shared selector.}
Let $F=1+X(g-1)$, $\Pr(X=1)=p$, $m=\mathbb Eg$, $v=1-m^2$.
Put $a_0=(1-p+pm)^2$, $b_0=p(1-p)(1-m)^2$, $q_0=1-pv$.
Each nonempty data coefficient contributes selector masses $p^2G_T^2$ and
$p(1-p)G_T^2$, giving
\[
 M=e(a_0)+e(b_0)-e(q_0)+pv h_2(p),\qquad
 J=h_2(pv)-p h_2(v),\qquad H(F)=pH(g)+M+J.
\]
For $g_t=\prod_{j=1}^t g_1^{(j)}$ replace $m$ by $(\mathbb Eg_1)^t$ and
$H(g)$ by $tH(g_1)$. This is a shared selector, not $t$ aligned selectors.
For $m=0$, $M=J=h_2(p)$, independently of $H(g)$.

\paragraph{Fair point activation.}
For a point indicator on $k$ fair controls let $p=2^{-k}$, $d=1-m$, $v=1-m^2$,
$q_0=1-pv$ and $r=p(1-p)d^2$. Exact coefficient summation gives
\begin{align*}
 M&=q_0h_2(r/q_0)+r\log_2(2^k-1)+pvk,\\
 J&=h_2(pv)-p h_2(v),\\
 L&=e((1-pd)^2)+(p-p^2)d^2(2k-\log_2d^2)+2kpv-p e(m^2),\\
 I_A&=kpd,\quad I(F)=pI(g)+kpd,\quad \mathbb EH(F_Z)=pH(g).
\end{align*}
Terms with $d=0$ are interpreted by continuity.
The spectrum has mass $(1-pd)^2$ at the all-empty label, $p^2d^2$ at each
of $2^k-1$ nonempty pure-control labels, and $p^2G_T^2$ for every control
label paired with $T\ne\varnothing$. A control is pivotal with probability $pd$.

Since $e((1-y)^2)\le2y/\ln2$ and
$d^2\log_2(1/d^2)\le2d/(e\ln2)$ when $d\le1$ (it is nonpositive when $d\ge1$),
using $d^2+v=2d$ gives
\[
 0\le L\le\left(4+\frac{2(1+1/e)}{k\ln2}\right)I_A\le8I_A.
\]
The final inequality is strict for $I_A>0$. The strict constant follows from $e>8/3$ and
$\ln2>2(1/3+1/81)=56/81>11/16$. For $d=0$ everything vanishes.
Uniformly over $m\in[-1,1]$,
\begin{align*}
 |L-4pk(1-m)|&\le17p+8kp^2,\\
 |J-pkv|&<3p,\\
 |M-pk(3-4m+m^2)|&\le20p+8kp^2.
\end{align*}
These follow by bounding the exact empty atom by $8p$,
$|d^2\log_2d^2|$ by $8$, $e(m^2)$ by $1$, and the quadratic control
term by $8kp^2$. Also
$J=pvk+e(1-pv)-p e(1-v)$.
Thus the logarithm is $k=\log_2(1/p)$, paid locally by control influence;
arbitrary terminal entropy $pH(g)$ remains unpaid.

\paragraph{Collision control.}
For $Z=\sum_U v_U>0$, $L_{\rm coll}=\sum_U e(v_U)$ and
$T=L_{\rm coll}/Z$, direct expansion gives
\[
 T=H(v/Z)-\log_2Z,\quad Z_t=Z^t,\quad
 L_t=tZ^{t-1}L_{\rm coll},\quad T_t=tT.
\]
These are product-compatible normalization identities, not a new Boolean payment theorem.
Rescaling $v$ by $c$ preserves its normalized law and changes $T$ to $T-\log_2c$.

\paragraph{Open bridge.}
Essential-coordinate contraction and paid activation decrements do not pay arbitrary terminal
entropy or a universal stopping tree without influence reuse. Full FEI is open.
