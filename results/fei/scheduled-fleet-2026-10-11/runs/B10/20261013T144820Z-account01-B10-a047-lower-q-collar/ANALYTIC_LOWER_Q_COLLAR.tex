\section*{Analytic lower-$q$ collar}
Let $h=h_2$, $s=4q(1-q)$, $c=4q^2$, and
$z=cp(1-p)/(1-sp)$.  For
$p\in[1/64,49/100]$ and $q\in[0,1/100]$, set
\[
D=(1-sp)h(z)+sp h(p)+\frac p2\log_2\frac{1-p}{p}
-h(sp)+p h(s).
\]
Then
\[
D>\frac{5\,027\,251}{19\,592\,160\,000}.
\]
Indeed, with $\Phi_p(t)=h(pt)-ph(t)$,
\[
\Phi_p'(t)=p\log_2\frac{1-pt}{p(1-t)}.
\]
The ratio $(1-pt)/(1-t)$ is increasing, so
\[
\Phi_p(s)\le ps\log_2\frac{1-ps}{p(1-s)}.
\]
Discarding only the nonnegative term $(1-sp)h(z)$ and writing
$L_p=\log_2((1-p)/p)$ gives
\[
D\ge p\left[(1/2-s(1-p))L_p
-s\log_2\frac{1-ps}{1-s}\right].
\]
Since $s\le99/2500$, $L_p\ge2/(51\ln2)$, and
$\log_2(1+x)\le x/\ln2$,
\[
\frac Dp\ge\frac1{\ln2}
\left[(1/2-s)\frac2{51}-\frac{s^2}{1-s}\right].
\]
The bracket decreases in $s$ and at $99/2500$ is
$5\,027\,251/306\,127\,500$.  Finally $p\ge1/64$ and
$1/\ln2>1$.  The endpoint $q=0$ follows with $h(0)=0$.
