\section*{A rigorous compact-core certificate for the two-bit half-tax candidate}

For the AND representative, let
\[
 D(p,q)=M_A+\frac12\Delta(p)-J_A,qquad
 s=4q(1-q),\quad c=4q^2,
\]
with
\[
 J_A=h_2(sp)-p h_2(s),
\]
\[
 M_A=(1-sp)h_2\!\left(\frac{cp(1-p)}{1-sp}\right)+sp h_2(p),
 \qquad
 \Delta(p)=p\log_2\frac{1-p}{p}.
\]

The accompanying directed-rounding certificate proves
\[
 \boxed{D(p,q)>0}
\]
throughout the compact rectangle
\[
 \frac1{64}\le p\le\frac{49}{100},
 \qquad
 \frac1{100}\le q\le\frac{99}{100}.
\]

The program uses 70-decimal `Decimal` arithmetic with `ROUND_FLOOR` for
every lower endpoint and `ROUND_CEILING` for every upper endpoint.  Binary
entropy ranges are enclosed using its concavity: the minimum on an interval
is at an endpoint, and the maximum is at the endpoint nearest $1/2$ (or is
exactly one if the interval contains $1/2$).  The functions
$4q(1-q)$, $4q^2$, $p(1-p)$ and
$\log_2((1-p)/p)$ are enclosed with their exact monotonicity on the stated
domain.  All products, quotients, additions and subtractions then use
outward interval operations.

Adaptive bisection accepts a box only when the resulting lower endpoint for
$D$ is strictly positive.  `CHECK.out` records the number of boxes, maximum
depth, smallest certified positive endpoint and a SHA-256 digest of the
ordered accepted leaves.  Re-running the program reconstructs the entire
certificate; no floating search value is treated as proof.

This bridges the analytic rare-bias theorem to a large interior region, but
does not complete the two-bit continuum.  The still-unpaid collars are
\[
 0<p<1/64,
 \quad 49/100<p\le1/2,
 \quad q<1/100,
 \quad q>99/100,
\]
including the exact equality corners at $p=1/2$ and $q\in\{0,1/2,1\}$.
The existing uniform asymptotic theorem proves positivity for sufficiently
small $p$ without giving an explicit certified cutoff.  Boundary-specific
factorizations or a second interval chart are required to close these
collars.  Higher arity and global nonreuse remain separate; full FEI is not
proved.
