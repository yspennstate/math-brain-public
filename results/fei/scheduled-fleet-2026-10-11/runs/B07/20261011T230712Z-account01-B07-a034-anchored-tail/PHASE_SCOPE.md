# A034 anchored-tail scope

- Physical variables remain disjoint uniform Boolean cubes `X,Y`.
- Booleanity enters through the previously proved degree-`d` granularity phase bound.
- Conditional entropy is paid fiberwise by the minimum of the full Rényi-half cost and an anchored rare-tail cost.
- The anchored cost retains the rare mass `epsilon`; for one-point defects it equals Shannon entropy exactly and is at most `4 I`.
- Since the cost is always at least conditional Shannon entropy, a fixed cap excludes repeated fresh tensor factors of positive spectral entropy.
- No universal bound on the anchored-tail complexity is proved. Full FEI remains open.
