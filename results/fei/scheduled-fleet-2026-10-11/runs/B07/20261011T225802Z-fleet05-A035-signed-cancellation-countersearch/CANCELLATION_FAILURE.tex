\section*{A035: exact cancellation and scope audit}
All entropy is in bits. Put $p_S=\widehat f(S)^2$ and
$H_\infty=-\log_2\max_Sp_S$.

For the quadratic bent sign on even $n$ bits,
$b(x)=(-1)^{\sum_{j=1}^{n/2}x_{2j-1}x_{2j}}$ (binary inputs),
every Fourier amplitude has magnitude $2^{-n/2}$.
Consequently, for every $U\ne\varnothing$,
\[
 \sum_S\widehat b(S)\widehat b(S\triangle U)=0,
 \qquad\sum_S|\widehat b(S)\widehat b(S\triangle U)|=1.
\]
Thus the absolute autocorrelation sum over nonzero shifts is
$2^n-1$, whereas $I(b)=n/2$. There is no finite universal $K$
paying that unsigned sum by $K I$. The valid triangle inequality
is $|\sum a_Sa_{S\triangle U}|\le\sum|a_Sa_{S\triangle U}|$;
it supplies no upper bound on the right side from the zero left
side. Equality, or an inequality in the reverse direction, is the
first false proof step. This does not invalidate a signed target.

\section*{The min-entropy gap retains the full conjecture}
For any Boolean $h$ and positive integer $t$, define on fresh inputs
\[
 g=y\prod_{j=1}^t h(x^{(j)}),\qquad
 F(z,y,x)=\frac{1+z}{2}+\frac{1-z}{2}g(y,x).
\]
The sign $g$ is balanced. The constant and $z$ coefficients of $F$
are both $1/2$; each nonzero coefficient of $g$ supplies two
coefficients of magnitudes $|\widehat g(S)|/2$. Hence
\[
 H(F)=2+\frac{tH(h)}2,\quad H_\infty(F)=2,\quad
 I(F)=1+\frac{tI(h)}2.
\]
A uniform bound $H-H_\infty\le K I$ applied to $F$, divided by
$t/2$, and followed by $t\to\infty$ gives $H(h)\le K I(h)$.
The converse follows from $H-H_\infty\le H$.
Therefore the two optimal universal constants are equal, including
the possibility of infinity. This is an application of the exact
activation/selector identities, not a new entropy payment.

\section*{Two actual Boolean refutations}
Let $h_{w,m}$ be OR of $m$ disjoint width-$w$ AND gates, identified
with a sign. Set $q=2^{-w}$, $r=1-q$. The constant coefficient is
$1-2r^m$. Every Fourier set touching exactly $j$ tribes has squared
coefficient $4q^{2j}r^{2(m-j)}$ and multiplicity
$\binom mj(2^w-1)^j$. Moreover $I(h_{w,m})=2wmq r^{m-1}$.
For $w=2,m=3$, the squared-law histogram is
\[
 \{25/1024:1,\;81/1024:9,\;9/1024:27,\;1/1024:27\}.
\]
Thus $I=27/16$ and $H_\infty=10-4\log_2 3>27/8=2I$,
certified by $3^{32}<2^{53}$.

For $h=h_{2,8}$, $I(h)=2187/2048$ and independent rational
logarithm bounds certify
$4.623452413789<H(h)<4.623452413790$.
Taking $t=32$ in the preceding selector gives an actual sign on
$32\cdot16+2=514$ bits, $I(F)=2315/128$, $H_\infty(F)=2$, and
\[
 H(F)-H_\infty(F)-4I(F)
 \in[1.631488620635,1.631488620636].
\]
Only the constants $2$ and $4$ in the respective proposed helpers
are refuted. No unbounded Boolean entropy/influence ratio results.

\section*{Non-Boolean controls, separately}
The normalized real sign sum $\psi_n=n^{-1/2}\sum_i x_i$ has
$I=1$ and $H=H_\infty=\log_2n$. It defeats constant-2 FMEI for
$n>4$, but its gap is zero. To test the gap instead, let
\[
 \phi_t=\frac34+\frac1{4\,2^t}\sum_{i=1}^{7\,4^t}x_i\quad(t\ge1).
\]
This is a normalized real function with dyadic amplitudes,
$p_\varnothing=9/16$ and $I=7/16$.
Its gap satisfies $(H-H_\infty)/I=2t+\log_2 9\to\infty$.
It violates full Booleanity already on singleton shifts:
$\sum_Sa_Sa_{S\triangle\{i\}}=3/(8\,2^t)>0$.
These are missing-hypothesis diagnostics, not Boolean witnesses.
