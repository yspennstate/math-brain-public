# Full two-address pointwise admission

Fix four data variables x and two addresses y. Partial address Fourier coefficients g_J(x) are one quarter of a sum of four Boolean signs, hence lie in {0,+/-1/2,+/-1}. For a fixed x, the four-address truth table either has zero/two/four negative signs and is a signed character (one g_J=+/-1 and the others0), or has one/three negative signs and is bent (all four g_J=+/-1/2, product of their signs-1). This follows by direct enumeration of the16 four-sign tables. With h_J=2g_J the two allowed pointwise forms are one occupied+/-2, or four occupied+/-1 with negative product.

Every row h_J is integer-valued in {0,+/-1,+/-2}. Its unnormalized Walsh transform is integer. Product spectral magnitudes factor with primitive coprime positive ratios as in COMPLETENESS.md. Their common gcd c divides2P because at least one inverse Walsh sum is+/-P or+/-2P. The squared Walsh norm is P*sum_x h_J(x)^2 <=4P^2, and max |W_J(T)|<=2P. These facts give the finite complete template domain in mixed_product.py: every face, every primitive ratio with members<=32, every c dividing32, and every factor product satisfying norm<=1024 and norm divisible16. All integer Walsh entries have the same parity (each character is1 modulo2); consequently a face with zeros outside it must have even nonzero magnitudes, and on a full face all magnitudes must have common parity. These necessary filters are applied in v2, along with max magnitude<=32. All remaining gauge phases and every active translation are restored. Truth vectors are stored modulo global sign, without dropping sign patterns that can affect joint admission. Exact inverse transforms check the permitted five-valued range. Batched16-column transforms use int64; all intermediate sums have absolute value<=512, so no overflow or rounding is possible.

In any realizing four-row tuple, the odd-value mask M={x:|h_J(x)|=1} must be the same in every row. Outside M, occupied +/-2 masks E_J must be disjoint and cover its complement. On M the product of the four signs must be-1. If rows are stored modulo global sign, this condition is satisfiable iff the XOR of their negative-sign masks is either0 or M (a constant product); one global slice sign then realizes the required-1. This retains the common phase constraint rather than checking equations separately. For each row, the data influence, entropy histogram and squared norm are exact.

M=0 is exactly the pure class already exhaustively certified. M=all16 points can be disposed of analytically: every h_J is Boolean with product spectrum, w_J=1/4, and H(h_J)<=2 I(h_J) by product spectral rigidity (each nondeterministic spectral bit is fair). For completeness, the template norm identity for a Boolean product-spectrum h is c^2 product_j(A_j^2+B_j^2)=P^2, a power of two. Each primitive sum of squares is therefore a power of two. Coprimality and parity force A_j=B_j=1: opposite parity would give an odd factor>1; both odd give the squared sum2 modulo8, hence the only power of two is2. The law on its spectral face is uniform, so its entropy r is at most twice its expected degree. Thus H(F)=2+(1/4)sum_J H(h_J)<=2[1+(1/4)sum_J I(h_J)]=2I(F). This all-mixed argument is independent of the data dimension.

For every other M, enumerate unordered row pairs allowing repeats only when their E masks are empty. A pair has disjoint E masks, union U and phase XOR R on M. A complementary pair must have pure union M^c minus U and phase XOR R or R xor M. Hashing these exact keys covers every globally admitted four-row tuple. Address label optimization again assigns descending slice masses to0,1,1,2, and a realizing full truth table is constructed for any selected tuple. Pair search may quotient unordered pairs; it never asserts independent row phases are sufficient.

The first unsupported line, unless every group is completed, is a bound for all remaining complementary-pair groups. Even complete admission at n=6 would not prove the all-dimensional two-address conjecture or FEI.
