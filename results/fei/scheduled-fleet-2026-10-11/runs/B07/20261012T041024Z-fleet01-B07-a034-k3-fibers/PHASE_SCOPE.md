# Scope and unpaid line

- Physical inputs are the disjoint uniform cubes on address variables `A` and data variables `C`.
- The classification is pointwise in the physical data input and uses the full signed Boolean inverse Walsh transform; it does not apply to an arbitrary spectral probability vector.
- The row consequence additionally assumes the normalized squared Fourier law of the named row is product, exactly as in `A2-L3k`.
- Correlations among the eight address rows are retained.  In particular, the point-defect set is common to every row and yields the exact joint reserve (2)--(4).
- The theorem covers every three-bit Boolean fiber, not just AND/point-defect fibers.  Semibent supports are affine two-flats and cost at least one unit of address influence pointwise.
- The cyclic-marginal arbitrary-tensor obstruction is not admitted as a proof of full FEI: the present result is a three-address local structure theorem and makes no universal claim about arbitrary tensor factors.
- First unpaid line: even product rows with `nu'=2` can receive contributions from both semibent fibers and pairs of point defects.  Their Walsh numerators are even, but the mod-4 signs need not define a common event across all rows.  A K3 bound still needs a joint mod-4 classification/envelope.
- Full FEI remains open.  A fixed-K3 theorem would still leave the dependence on `k`; equation (5) shows why a local point-defect reserve alone would give exponential loss.
