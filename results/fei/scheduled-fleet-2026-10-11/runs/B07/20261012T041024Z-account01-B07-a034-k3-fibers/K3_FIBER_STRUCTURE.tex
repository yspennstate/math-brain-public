\section*{Three-address Boolean fibers and the first joint 2-adic reserve}

All cubes below are uniform.  Let
\[
 f:\{-1,1\}^{A}\times\{-1,1\}^{C}\longrightarrow\{-1,1\},
 \qquad |A|=3,
\]
and, for $T\subseteq A$, put
\[
 g_T(x)=\mathbb E_y[f(y,x)\chi_T(y)],\qquad
 w_T=\mathbb E_x g_T(x)^2.
\]
Thus $\widehat f(T\cup U)=\widehat g_T(U)$ and
$I_A(f)=\sum_T |T|w_T$.

\paragraph{Theorem (exact $K_3$ fiber trichotomy).}
For each fixed $x$, exactly one of the following occurs.
\begin{enumerate}
\item Character: one $g_D(x)$ has modulus $1$ and the other seven vanish.
\item Point defect: one $g_D(x)$ has modulus $3/4$ and the other seven
have modulus $1/4$.  Equivalently, $f(\cdot,x)$ differs at exactly one
point from a signed character.
\item Semibent: four coefficients have modulus $1/2$ and four vanish.
The support is an affine two-flat in $\mathbb F_2^3$; after translating
that flat, the four signs form a two-bit bent phase.
\end{enumerate}
Across the $256$ Boolean fibers the three types occur respectively
$16,128,112$ times.

If
\[
 J_A(x)=\sum_{T\subseteq A}|T|g_T(x)^2,
\]
then the exact values are
\[
 J_A(x)=
 \begin{cases}
 |D|,&\text{character with support $D$},\\
 \frac34+\frac12|D|,&\text{point defect with major coefficient $D$},\\
 \frac14\sum_{T\in F}|T|\in\{1,\frac32,2\},
   &\text{semibent with support flat $F$}.
 \end{cases}
\]
Consequently, if $N$ is the set of non-character fibers, then
\[
 \mu(N)\le \frac43 I_A(f).
\tag{1}
\]

\paragraph{Proof.}
Write
\[
 a_T(x)=4g_T(x)=\frac12\sum_y f(y,x)\chi_T(y)\in\mathbb Z.
\]
Parseval gives $\sum_T a_T^2=16$.  Moreover all eight $a_T$ have the
same parity: for $T,U$, the difference $a_T-a_U$ is a sum of four
integers congruent modulo two.  If the common parity is odd, the only
way eight odd squares sum to $16$ is $(9,1,\ldots,1)$.  If it is even,
division by four leaves a sum of four squares, hence either $(16,0^7)$
or $(4,4,4,4,0^4)$.  This proves the three magnitude patterns.

In the first case Fourier inversion gives a signed character.  In the
second, the coefficient of modulus $3/4$ has correlation $3/4$ with a
unique signed character, hence Hamming distance $(1-3/4)/2=1/8$, one
point.  In the third case let $K$ be the four-point Fourier support.
The Boolean convolution identities
\[
 \sum_T g_Tg_{T\triangle U}=0\qquad(U\ne\varnothing)
\]
exclude $|K\cap(K+U)|=2$: the two ordered summands are equal and cannot
cancel.  Therefore every difference of two points of $K$ stabilizes
$K$, so $K$ is a coset of a two-dimensional subspace.  The same
identities reduce the phase to the two-bit bent condition.  There are
$16$ signed characters, $16\cdot8=128$ one-point defects, and
$14\cdot8=112$ affine-flat bent phases.  The formulas for $J_A$ follow
by summing the displayed squared magnitudes.  Every non-character
fiber has $J_A\ge3/4$ and $I_A=\mathbb E_xJ_A(x)$, proving (1). \qed

\paragraph{Joint odd-granularity reserve for product rows.}
Assume the conditional squared Fourier law of a nonzero row $g_T$ is
product.  Factor its frozen coordinates as in A2-L3k, let $R_T$ be the
active coordinates, $r_T=|R_T|$, and write the least active Walsh
coefficient as
\[
 \kappa_T=\nu_T2^{-r_T-2},\qquad
 \nu'_T=\nu_T\Big/\prod_{i\in R_T}q_{T,i}\in\mathbb Z_{>0},
\]
where the active magnitude ratios are reduced $p_{T,i}/q_{T,i}$.
Let $P$ be the common point-defect set and let $D(x)$ be its unique
major address.  Then
\[
 w_U(P):=\mathbb E[1_Pg_U^2]
   =\frac{\mu(P)}{16}+\frac12\mu\{x\in P:D(x)=U\},
\tag{2}
\]
and hence
\[
 \sum_U|U|w_U(P)=\frac34\mu(P)
   +\frac12\mathbb E[1_P|D(x)|]\ge\frac34\mu(P).
\tag{3}
\]
Furthermore, $\nu'_T$ is odd if and only if $P$ is a cylinder over
$R_T$ whose trace has odd cardinality.  In that case
\[
 \mu(P)\ge2^{-r_T},\qquad
 w_U\ge2^{-r_T-4}\quad\hbox{for every }U\subseteq A,
\qquad I_A(f)\ge\frac34,2^{-r_T}.
\tag{4}
\]

Indeed $4g_T(x)$ is odd exactly on $P$, independently of $T$.  After
the frozen coordinates are removed, the parity of every Walsh
numerator $2^{r_T+2}\widehat g_T(V)$ equals the parity of the number of
points of the trace of $P$.  The divisibility argument in A2-L3k then
says that this common parity is odd exactly when $\nu'_T$ is odd.
The row factorization makes $P$ a cylinder.  Equations (2)--(4) now
follow from the point-defect squared magnitudes.

\paragraph{Why this does not yet prove $C_3<\infty$ sharply.}
The theorem pays every non-character fiber by physical address
influence and completely identifies the first (odd) 2-adic layer.
It does not yet control even product rows with $\nu'_T=2$ whose
semibent and point-defect contributions can cancel modulo four.  That
is the first unpaid line in the $K_3$ analogue of the $K_2$ proof.

The natural general-$k$ warning is already exact for $k\ge2$.  A one-point defect
from a signed character with major address $D$ has
\[
 J_A= k2^{1-k}+(1-2^{2-k})|D|.
\tag{5}
\]
For $D=\varnothing$ the available local address charge is only
$k/2^{k-1}$.  Thus a recurrence which pays a fixed per-defect entropy
cost only from this local reserve necessarily loses order $2^k/k$;
additional multi-row or conditional entropy credit is required for a
constant uniform in $k$.  This is an obstruction to that payment
mechanism, not a counterexample to FEI.
