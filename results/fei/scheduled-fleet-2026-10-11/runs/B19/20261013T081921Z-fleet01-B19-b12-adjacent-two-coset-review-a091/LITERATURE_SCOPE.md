# Primary-source scope comparison

- O'Donnell and Tan, *A composition theorem for the Fourier
  Entropy-Influence conjecture*, arXiv:1304.1347: disjoint child input blocks
  and a product-biased outer `FEI+` hypothesis.  It does not prove the present
  shared-variable signed associator or the rejected full-influence telescope.
- Wan, Wright, and Wu, *Decision Trees, Protocols, and the Fourier
  Entropy-Influence Conjecture*, arXiv:1312.3003: decision-tree/read-k bounds
  retaining a read parameter.  Pathwise query nonreuse does not license
  repeated charging of complete reached-source influence.
- Potapov, *On the Fourier Entropy-Influence Conjecture for Boolean Plateaued
  Functions*, arXiv:2609.21563: FEI for plateaued functions and partially bent
  functions.  A generic non-affine union of two affine cosets has multiple
  nonzero spectral magnitudes and is not automatically in those classes.

Therefore the accepted local theorem has a distinct proper-class scope.  None
of these sources repairs its hierarchy corollary.
