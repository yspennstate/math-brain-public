# Primary-source scope check

Retrieved 13 October 2026.

## Potapov, plateaued and partially bent functions

V. N. Potapov, *On the Fourier Entropy--Influence Conjecture for Boolean
Plateaued Functions*, arXiv:2609.21563v1, 18 September 2026:
https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21563

Theorem 1 proves (H\le2I) for partially bent functions.  Theorem 2 proves
(H\le4I) for plateaued functions, with sharp class constant four.  This
overlaps the flat-spectrum endpoint of the earlier full-rank/nullity-one
shield results.  A general aligned shield in the reviewed theorem is not
plateaued: the arbitrary Boolean fiber spectra can have unequal masses, and
different (u)-fibers can have different profiles.  Potapov does not state
the upper-set functional (U), the complete signed restriction-tree
telescope, or the exact finite Boolean constants (C_3,C_4).

No novelty claim is made here for flatness or plateaued FEI.

## O'Donnell--Tan composition

R. O'Donnell and L.-Y. Tan, *A Composition Theorem for the Fourier
Entropy--Influence Conjecture*, arXiv:1304.1347, 4 April 2013:
https://arxiv.org/abs/1304.1347

Their theorem composes inner functions on disjoint input blocks, with the
outer function controlled in the corresponding biased Fourier basis.  In
the reviewed shield, (D(u,z)) shares the (u)-variables with the bilinear
phase (x^TM_0u); it is not a disjoint-inner-block composition to which the
theorem applies directly.  The rank-one hardness test (qg^{\otimes N})
does use ordinary disjoint tensor additivity, a known identity rather than a
new composition theorem.

## Priority conclusion

The reviewed package makes no first/best/latest priority claim.  At its
declared scope, the exact aligned-fiber decomposition plus retained-(U)
endpoint is not supplied by these two primary sources.  Exhaustive priority
coverage for the finite (C_3,C_4) computations was not attempted; they
should be presented as source-bound exact computations, not as a literature
priority claim.
