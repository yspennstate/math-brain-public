## Scope and literature comparison

This is a genuine shared-variable restricted-class theorem: the quadratic
star and the point defect overlap on (R).  O'Donnell--Tan's composition
theorem assumes disjoint input blocks, so it does not imply this overlap
payment.  Potapov's plateaued-function theorem covers plateaued and partially
bent endpoint classes, whereas (F_{n,R}) has four spectral mass levels in
general and the proof here concerns the upper-set potential (U), not only
spectral entropy.  No broad priority claim is made.

The theorem does **not** cover two overlapping quadratic centres.  In that
case one dust fiber may inherit incompatible upper-set caps, and the present
single pairing supplies no nonreuse theorem.  It also does not prove a
universal (G\le aI+bU), (U\le KI), or full FEI.
