# Primary-source comparison

1. Ryan O'Donnell and Li-Yang Tan, *A composition theorem for the Fourier Entropy-Influence conjecture*, arXiv:1304.1347. The theorem explicitly assumes inner functions over disjoint variable sets. It does not cover the shared coordinate in the reviewed factorization. <https://arxiv.org/abs/1304.1347>
2. Ryan O'Donnell, John Wright, and Yuan Zhou, *The Fourier Entropy-Influence Conjecture for certain classes of Boolean functions*. Its symmetric-function result covers the majority core's ordinary FEI endpoint, not the campaign-specific `Phi` payment. <https://www.math.ias.edu/files/ODonnell_Fourier.pdf>

No novelty or priority claim is made. These sources delimit the scope; they do not supply the missing shared-coordinate upper-set payment.
