# Primary-source scope comparison

Retrieved 2026-10-13 UTC.

## O'Donnell--Tan

Ryan O'Donnell and Li-Yang Tan, *A composition theorem for the Fourier
Entropy-Influence conjecture*, arXiv:1304.1347:

- https://arxiv.org/abs/1304.1347

The theorem assumes inner functions on disjoint variable blocks and a
product-biased FEI hypothesis for the outer function.  It supports the
fresh-block composition setting but does not state the upper-set potential
`U`, the one-sided scalar `R`, or the adaptive common-core retained endpoint.

## Potapov

Vladimir N. Potapov, *On the Fourier Entropy-Influence Conjecture for Boolean
Plateaued Functions*, arXiv:2609.21563, version available 2026-09-18:

- https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21563

The preprint proves FEI for plateaued and partially bent functions and gives
biased-cube estimates.  It does not contain the present arbitrary-rank
negative point-defect activation recurrence or a retained upper-set tree
telescope.

No first/best/novelty claim is made for the reviewed theorem or its `5/3`
strengthening.
