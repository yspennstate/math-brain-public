# Primary-source overlap and novelty scope

Checked on 13 October 2026.

- Pasalic--Polujan--Kudin--Zhang, *Design and analysis of bent functions using
  M-subspaces*, arXiv:2304.13432, explicitly treats the classical
  Maiorana--McFarland form \(x\cdot\pi(y)+h(y)\).  The square invertible
  spectral flatness in the reviewed claim is an instance of this established
  bent-function mechanism.
- Potapov, *On the Fourier Entropy-Influence Conjecture for Boolean Plateaued
  Functions*, arXiv:2609.21563 (submitted 18 September 2026), proves FEI for
  plateaued functions, with sharp class constant 4 according to the current
  abstract.  This covers the entropy/influence endpoint of the flat or
  plateaued root family.
- Neither source supplies the reviewed complete-tree retained-\(U\) endpoint
  telescope with a common disjoint terminal factor.  That distinction is a
  statement of scope, not a claim of publication priority.

No first/best/novelty claim is approved.  The reviewer-derived rectangular
constant-2 improvement requires its own literature comparison and fresh
independent proof review before adoption.
