# Primary-source overlap check

Retrieved 2026-10-13.

1. Ryan O'Donnell and Li-Yang Tan, *A composition theorem for the Fourier Entropy-Influence conjecture*, arXiv:1304.1347. The theorem concerns disjoint-block composition under biased FEI+ hypotheses and records ordinary entropy/influence tensorization. It does not state an upper-set-potential restriction telescope.

2. Andrew Wan, John Wright, and Chenggang Wu, *Decision Trees, Protocols, and the Fourier Entropy-Influence Conjecture*, arXiv:1312.3003. Their read-\(k\) theorem bounds spectral entropy for a function computed by a decision tree. The reviewed object is a partial restriction ledger with a retained arbitrary terminal factor; it is not derived from the read-\(k\) theorem.

3. Vladimir N. Potapov, *On the Fourier Entropy-Influence Conjecture for Boolean Plateaued Functions*, arXiv:2609.21563. This establishes FEI for plateaued functions, covering quadratic phases as an endpoint class. It does not provide the coordinatewise \(U\)-increment inequality, adaptive tree telescope, or physical-cylinder ownership lemma.

Conclusion: the reviewed theorem is compatible with all three sources. Existing results prevent an overstatement that quadratic FEI itself is new. No external novelty or priority claim is certified.
