# Primary-source scope check

Retrieved 2026-10-13.

## O'Donnell--Tan

Ryan O'Donnell and Li-Yang Tan, *A composition theorem for the Fourier
Entropy-Influence conjecture*, arXiv:1304.1347.

The theorem composes an outer function with inner functions on disjoint
variable sets, under a product-biased Fourier/FEI hypothesis for the outer
gate.  This supports disjoint-block composition but does not contain the
upper-set potential `U`, the half-activation scalar `R`, or an adaptive
retained-`U` tree theorem.

Primary source: https://arxiv.org/abs/1304.1347

## Potapov

Vladimir N. Potapov, *On the Fourier Entropy-Influence Conjecture for Boolean
Plateaued Functions*, arXiv:2609.21563 (submitted 2026-09-18).

The paper proves FEI for plateaued and partially bent Boolean functions and
records sharp constants for those root classes.  It does not prove the
upper-set activation recurrence or the signed adaptive-tree endpoint used in
the B05 claim.

Primary source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21563

No first/best/novelty claim is supported or needed.  The reviewed result is a
