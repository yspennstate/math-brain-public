# Primary-source overlap check

- Ryan O'Donnell and Li-Yang Tan, *A composition theorem for the Fourier
  Entropy-Influence conjecture*, arXiv:1304.1347 (4 April 2013),
  https://arxiv.org/abs/1304.1347. Scope: FEI composition for disjoint
  child blocks under biased-FEI hypotheses. It does not provide a bound
  for the custom upper-set potential $U$ or the child-only terminal
  reserve refuted here.
- Vladimir N. Potapov, *On the Fourier Entropy-Influence Conjecture for
  Boolean Plateaued Functions*, arXiv:2609.21563 (18 September 2026),
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21563. Scope: endpoint FEI for plateaued and
  partially bent functions. It does not state the biased-child cocycle or
  either tensor reserve no-go.

Conclusion: neither primary source supplies the missing universal joint
payment, and neither duplicates the exact A056 no-go at the inspected
scope. No broader novelty claim is made.
