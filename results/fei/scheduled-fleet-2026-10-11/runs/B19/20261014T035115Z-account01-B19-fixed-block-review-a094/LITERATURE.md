# Primary-source scope comparison

Retrieved 14 October 2026.

1. Ryan O'Donnell and Li-Yang Tan, *A composition theorem for the Fourier
   Entropy-Influence conjecture*, arXiv:1304.1347.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/1304.1347

   Theorem 1 composes an outer function with inner functions on disjoint
   variable blocks under product-biased FEI+ hypotheses.  It does not cover
   multiplication of two factors sharing the same physical block `X` and does
   not state the orbit-potential inequalities reviewed here.

2. Vladimir N. Potapov, *On the Fourier Entropy-Influence Conjecture for
   Boolean Plateaued Functions*, arXiv:2609.21563v1, 18 September 2026.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21563

   The paper proves root FEI bounds for plateaued and partially bent classes.
   It does not address the singleton potential, reused-coordinate product
   transport, or the exact upper-set restriction charge.

3. Andrew Wan, John Wright and Chenggang Wu, *Decision Trees, Protocols, and
   the Fourier Entropy-Influence Conjecture*, arXiv:1312.3003.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/1312.3003

   The read-k decision-tree result has a constant depending linearly on k.
   This is context for parameter dependence, not a source for the logarithmic
   orbit theorem.

No priority or novelty claim is made for the reviewer-derived result.
