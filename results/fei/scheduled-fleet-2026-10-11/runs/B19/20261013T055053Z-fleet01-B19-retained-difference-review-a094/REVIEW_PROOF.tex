\section{Verdict}

The claims \texttt{B05\_A023\_RETAINED\_DIFFERENCE\_NODE\_v1} and
\texttt{B05\_A023\_RETAINED\_DIFFERENCE\_TREE\_v1} are accepted at their
declared scope.  No repair was needed.  This reviewer did not contribute to
the source proof.

The word ``single-use'' must retain its narrow meaning: one displayed packet
per tree node.  The theorem does not prove that the packets have disjoint
ownership of their underlying physical data coordinates.  That stronger
payment is precisely the open claim
\texttt{B05\_A023\_RAW\_DIFFERENCE\_OWNERSHIP\_v1}.

\section{Independent one-node reconstruction}

Let $F$ use a fresh fair bit to choose Boolean $g$ or $h$.  Put
$a_S=\widehat g(S)$, $b_S=\widehat h(S)$,
$u_S=(a_S+b_S)/2$, $v_S=(a_S-b_S)/2$, and
$M_S=u_S^2+v_S^2=(a_S^2+b_S^2)/2$.  Parseval gives
\[
 q:=\Pr[g\ne h]=\sum_Sv_S^2.
\]
The Fourier atoms of $F$ not containing the selector have coefficients $u_S$;
those containing it have coefficients $v_S$.  Therefore, without using an
upper-set identity,
\[
 \cH(F)-\frac{\cH(g)+\cH(h)}2
 =\operatorname{JS}(a^2,b^2)
  +\sum_SM_Sh_2(v_S^2/M_S).                              \tag{1}
\]

For real $x,y$, the scalar Jensen--Shannon contribution satisfies
\[
 \frac12\left[x^2\log_2\frac{2x^2}{x^2+y^2}
 +y^2\log_2\frac{2y^2}{x^2+y^2}\right]
 \leq \frac{(x-y)^2}{2\ln2}.                            \tag{2}
\]
Indeed, signs only enlarge the right side.  For $x,y\geq0$, homogeneity
reduces to $y=1$, $r=x$.  The difference after multiplication by $2\ln2$ is
\[
 G(r)=(r-1)^2-r^2\ln\frac{2r^2}{r^2+1}
             -\ln\frac2{r^2+1}.
\]
Writing
\[
 A(r)=1-r^{-1}-\ln\frac{2r^2}{r^2+1},
 \qquad A'(r)=\frac{(r-1)^2}{r^2(r^2+1)},
\]
we have $G'(r)=2rA(r)$ and $A(1)=0$.  Thus $G$ decreases up to one and
increases afterwards, proving (2), including endpoints by continuity.
Summing (2) and using $\sum_S(a_S-b_S)^2=4q$ gives
\[
 \operatorname{JS}(a^2,b^2)\leq\frac{2q}{\ln2}.         \tag{3}
\]

Let $d=(g-h)/2$, so $\widehat d=v$.  Since $M$ is a probability law,
$M_S\leq1$, and $\ln(1+t)\leq t$ gives
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 M_Sh_2(v_S^2/M_S)
 &=v_S^2\log_2(M_S/v_S^2)
   +u_S^2\log_2(1+v_S^2/u_S^2)\\
 &\leq v_S^2\log_2(1/v_S^2)+v_S^2/\ln2.
 \end{aligned}                                          \tag{4}
\]
Zero atoms follow by continuity.  Combining (1)--(4),
\[
 \cH(F)-\frac{\cH(g)+\cH(h)}2
 \leq \mathcal L(d)+\frac{3q}{\ln2}.                   \tag{5}
\]

For $V=\cH-\cU$, equation (5) is exactly
\[
 \cU(F)-\frac{\cU(g)+\cU(h)}2
 \leq -V(F)+\frac{V(g)+V(h)}2
       +\mathcal L(d)+\frac{3q}{\ln2}.                 \tag{6}
\]
Also $V\geq0$ termwise because
$\widehat f(S)^2\leq\pi_f(S)\leq1$.  This proves the node claim.

\section{Tree reconstruction and physical accounting}

At an internal node $v$ at depth $j$, let $w_v=2^{-j}$.  Multiplying (6) by
$w_v$ makes each child coefficient $w_v/2$, equal to its reach weight.
Summation over a finite tree cancels every internal $\cU$ and $V$ term and
leaves
\begin{equation}
\begin{split}
 \cU(F_{\rm root})-\sum_\ell w_\ell\cU(g_\ell)
 \leq{}&\sum_\ell w_\ell V(g_\ell)-V(F_{\rm root})\\
 &+\sum_vw_v\mathcal L(d_v)+\frac3{\ln2}\sum_vw_vq_v.
\end{split}
\tag{7}
\end{equation}

If $z_v$ occurs at exactly this node and nowhere in a terminal core, the
ancestors determine reach without consulting $z_v$.  Off the reached cylinder
the output ignores $z_v$; on it, flipping $z_v$ changes the output with
probability $q_v$.  Hence
\[
 \operatorname{Inf}_{z_v}(F_{\rm root})=w_vq_v,
 \qquad I_{\rm sel}=\sum_vw_vq_v.                       \tag{8}
\]
Equations (7)--(8) prove the tree claim for arbitrary shared data variables
and arbitrary Boolean terminal cores.

Freshness is essential to (8).  If a root and a reached child formally query
the same bit, consistent assignments can make the global function constant
while the formal child ledger still charges $1/2$.  The supplied independent
checker records this explicit control.  Thus reused selectors are correctly
excluded.

\section{Scope and remaining gap}

The proof has no factorization, flatness, nonzero-atom, or disjoint-data
hypothesis.  It survives coherent shared cores, zero Fourier atoms, and
arbitrarily small dyadic reach weights.  The constants $1$ and $3/\ln2$ are
independent of depth and dimension.

However, a data coordinate may influence several node differences $d_v$.
Thus $\sum_vw_v\mathcal L(d_v)$ is an explicit retained ledger, not yet a
nonreused physical payment.  The sparse-surgery counterfamily already rules
out replacing it by a constant times selector influence.  No implication
$\cU\leq K I$, no new FEI class bound, and no full FEI proof follows.

The source makes no novelty claim, and this review accepts none.  The result
is self-contained and does not require a composition or plateaued-function
theorem as a premise.
