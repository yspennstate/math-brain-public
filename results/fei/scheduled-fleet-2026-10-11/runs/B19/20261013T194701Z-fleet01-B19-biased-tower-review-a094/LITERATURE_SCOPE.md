# Primary-source scope comparison

## O'Donnell--Tan composition theorem

O'Donnell and Tan, *A composition theorem for the Fourier
Entropy-Influence conjecture*, arXiv:1304.1347 (submitted 4 April 2013), proves
an FEI composition theorem for inner functions on disjoint variable blocks,
under an FEI hypothesis for the outer function in the corresponding
product-biased basis.

That theorem is compatible with the fresh-block setup reviewed here, but it
does not itself provide the `U` activation identity, the one-sided `R(t)<=1`
bound, or the retained restriction-tree endpoint payment. It also does not
cover reused variables or changing coherent cores. The B05 proof does not need
to import this composition theorem.

Primary source: https://arxiv.org/abs/1304.1347

## Potapov plateaued theorem

Potapov, *On the Fourier Entropy-Influence Conjecture for Boolean Plateaued
Functions*, arXiv:2609.21563 (submitted 18 September 2026), proves sharp FEI
constant 4 for plateaued functions and 2 for partially bent functions, with
additional biased-cube estimates.

Those results can overlap some special base functions, but they do not state
the upper-set potential `U`, the negative-half biased activation recurrence,
or the common-core restriction-tree payment. Conversely, the reviewed B05
result does not improve Potapov's sharp FEI constants for those classes.

Primary source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21563

## Novelty-safe conclusion

No checked primary source supplies the exact depth-uniform retained-credit
theorem reviewed here. This is a scope comparison, not an exhaustive priority
claim. The result remains a proper fresh-block class and cannot be cited as a
universal composition or full-FEI theorem.
