# Primary-source scope check

Checked on 12 October 2026:

- Ryan O'Donnell and Li-Yang Tan, *A composition theorem for the Fourier
  Entropy-Influence conjecture*, arXiv:1304.1347.  Its composition theorem
  assumes inner functions on disjoint variable sets.  It does not supply
  the shared-core upper-set identity or the odd-surgery refund reviewed here.
- Ryan O'Donnell, John Wright, and Yuan Zhou, *The Fourier
  Entropy–Influence Conjecture for certain classes of Boolean functions*,
  ICALP 2011.  Its read-once/decision-tree results do not imply the present
  retained-child upper-set payment for two children sharing all core
  variables.
- Vladimir N. Potapov, *On the Fourier Entropy-Influence Conjecture for
  Boolean Plateaued Functions*, arXiv:2609.21563.  Its plateaued and
  partially bent FEI theorems do not show that an arbitrary triple surgery
  of a bent function is plateaued, nor do they establish the present
  upper-set potential identity.

URLs:

- https://arxiv.org/abs/1304.1347
- https://www.math.ias.edu/files/ODonnell_Fourier.pdf
- https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21563

No novelty claim is made.  This comparison only confirms that the accepted
proof is self-contained and that none of these sources may be used to widen
its scope to shared-variable hierarchies.
