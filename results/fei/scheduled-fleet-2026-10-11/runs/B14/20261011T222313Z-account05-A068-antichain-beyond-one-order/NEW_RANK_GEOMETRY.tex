\title{Context-oriented pivotal cubes with quadratic spectral fibers}
\author{A068, stream B14}
\maketitle
All entropies are in bits; all physical variables are independent uniform signs.
Write $C_{\rm reg}=\log_2(4+e)+2\log_2(e\pi)<8.937$.
\begin{theorem}
Let $h$ be a regular Boolean function on $q$ data coordinates and let
$Q_i:\mathbb F_2^m\to\mathbb F_2$ have degree at most two. Put
$a_i(z)=(-1)^{Q_i(u)}$, where $z_j=(-1)^{u_j}$, and
$F(x,z)=h(x_i a_i(z):i\in[q])$. Then
\[
 H(F)\le C_{\rm reg} I_x(F)+2I_z(F)\le C_{\rm reg}I(F).
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For fixed $z$, the orientation $y_i=x_i a_i(z)$ carries data pivotal
fibers to the antichains of $h$. Resampling predecessor data coordinates
keeps this exterior-dependent orientation fixed. Thus the baseline signed
noise and harmonic rank proof gives derivative rank mean at most $1+\ln2$.
For $a_S=\prod_{i\in S}a_i$, exact Fourier expansion gives
\[
 \widehat F(S,T)=\widehat h(S)\widehat a_S(T),\quad
 I_x(F)=I(h),\quad I_z(F)=\sum_S\widehat h(S)^2 I(a_S).
\]
If $B_S$ is the alternating polar matrix of $\sum_{i\in S}Q_i$ and
$r_S=\operatorname{rank}B_S$, quadratic autocorrelation and Walsh inversion
show that $\widehat a_S^2$ is uniform on an affine space $L_S$ of dimension
$r_S$. Its coordinate support has size at least $r_S$, so
$r_S=H(a_S)\le2I(a_S)$.

After ranking data coordinates by $h$'s order, define
\[
 Z_k=\sum_{|S|=k}\prod_{i\in S}i^{-2},\qquad
 R_k^F(S,T)=Z_k^{-1}\prod_{i\in S}i^{-2}\,2^{-r_S}
                 \mathbf1_{T\in L_S}.
\]
This is normalized on each data level $k$. Euler's product bounds
$Z_k\le\pi^{2k}/(2k+1)!$. For $S=\{i_1<\cdots<i_k\}$ its full cost is
\[
 -\log_2 R_k^F(S,T)
 \le\frac2{\ln2}\sum_{j=1}^k\ln(i_j/j)
                  +k\log_2(\pi^2/4)+r_S.
\]
Mix levels with weights $w_k=\Pr_h(|S|=k)$; this is a normalized complete
reference, charging $\log_2(1/w_k)$ as well. The Boolean level bound
$H(|S|)\le\log_2(4+e)I(h)$, derivative rank mean, and
$\sum_S\widehat h(S)^2r_S\le2I_z(F)$ give
\[
 H(F)\le\left[\log_2(4+e)+
       \frac{2(1+\ln2)+\ln(\pi^2/4)}{\ln2}\right]I_x(F)+2I_z(F),
\]
as required. The private data coordinates prevent coherent collisions;
no arbitrary shared-variable entropy subadditivity is used.
\end{proof}
