\section*{Parity lifts do not repair semigroup monotonicity}

Let $g:\{-1,1\}^n\to\{-1,1\}$ and define
\[
 F(x,z)=g(x_1z,\ldots,x_nz).
\]
If $g=\sum_S\widehat g(S)\chi_S$, then
\[
 F=\sum_{|S|\text{ even}}\widehat g(S)\chi_S(x)
 +\sum_{|S|\text{ odd}}\widehat g(S)\chi_S(x)z.
\]
Thus the map
\[
 \lambda(S)=\begin{cases}S,&|S|\text{ even},\\
 S\cup\{z\},&|S|\text{ odd}
 \end{cases}
\]
is injective and $\widehat F(\lambda(S))=\widehat g(S)$.  In
particular every occupied Fourier set of $F$ has even cardinality and
\[
 H(F)=H(g),\qquad
 I(F)=I(g)+\Pr_{S\sim\widehat g^2}(|S|\text{ odd})\le 2I(g).
\tag{1}
\]

For a squared Fourier law $p_S$ and energy $e_S$, put
\[
 p_t(S)=\frac{p_Se^{-2te_S}}{\sum_Tp_Te^{-2te_T}},\qquad
 \mathcal V_-(p,e)=H(p_\infty)+\int_0^\infty[-H'(p_t)]_+\,dt.
\]
For the lift, $e_S=|S|+\mathbf1_{\{|S|\text{ odd}\}}$.  Since
$\mathcal V_-(F)\ge H(F)$, a universal inequality
$\mathcal V_-(F)\le C I(F)$ even only on this parity-lift class implies
full FEI with constant $2C$ by (1).  Hence the even-support restriction is
not a simpler terminal class for the semigroup target.

It also does not restore pointwise entropy dissipation.  Let $N=2^r$,
\[
 g_r(x)=1-2\mathbf1_{\{x=\mathbf1\}},\qquad
 h_r(x)=\chi_{[r]}(x)g_r(x),
\]
where the displayed point may equivalently be moved to the origin in
$\mathbb F_2^r$.  The squared spectrum of $h_r$ consists of one mass
\[
 a=(1-2/N)^2
\]
at $A=[r]$ and $N-1$ masses $b=4/N^2$.  Under the parity lift the heavy
atom has energy
\[
 e_A=r+\mathbf1_{\{r\text{ odd}\}}.
\]
The mean lifted energy of the other atoms is $\bar e$, and direct counting
gives
\[
 e_A-\bar e=
 \begin{cases}
 \dfrac{N(r-1)}{2(N-1)},&r\text{ even},\\[6pt]
 \dfrac{N(r+1)}{2(N-1)},&r\text{ odd}.
 \end{cases}
\tag{2}
\]
Writing $c=1-a=(N-1)b$, the two-mass covariance identity gives, in nats,
\[
 H_F'(0)=2ac(e_A-\bar e)\log(a/b)>0\qquad(r\ge3).
\tag{3}
\]
Thus normalized Fourier-degree damping can initially create entropy even
for genuine Boolean functions whose occupied Fourier sets all have even
size.

Two exact positive controls remain useful.  If the source squared spectrum
is uniform on its support, then $\log p$ is constant and
\[
 H'(t)=-4t\operatorname{Var}_t(e)\le0.
\tag{4}
\]
Consequently the parity lift of ordinary addressing has
\[
 \mathcal V_-=2a,\qquad I=\frac{a+3}{2},\qquad
 \frac{\mathcal V_-}{I}=\frac{4a}{a+3}<4.
\tag{5}
\]
For the unshifted point defect, the unique heavy atom is at energy zero
and all excited atoms have equal mass $b$.  At every time,
$\operatorname{Cov}_t(e,\log p)\le0$, so again $H'(t)\le0$.  Its parity
lift satisfies
\[
 \mathcal V_-=H(g_r),\qquad I=\frac{2(r+1)}{2^r},\qquad
 \frac{\mathcal V_-}{I}<4.
\tag{6}
\]
For (6), put $\delta=2^{1-r}$.  The excited contribution to entropy is at
most $4\delta(r-1)$ and the heavy-atom contribution is at most
$2\delta/\ln2$, while $4I=4\delta(r+1)$.

Equations (1)--(6) do not prove the universal variation inequality.  They
show that parity erasure is simultaneously a full-FEI-hard restriction and
a counterexample to restoring monotonicity.
