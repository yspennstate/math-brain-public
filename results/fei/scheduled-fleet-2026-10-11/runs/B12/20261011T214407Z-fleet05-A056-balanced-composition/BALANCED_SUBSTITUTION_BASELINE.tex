\title{A056: balanced disjoint substitution baseline}
\author{A056, stream B12}
\maketitle
All logarithms are base two. For a Boolean sign $u$ on the uniform cube,
$H(u)=\sum_S\widehat u(S)^2\log_2(1/\widehat u(S)^2)$ and
$I(u)=\sum_S|S|\widehat u(S)^2$, including the empty entropy atom.
\begin{theorem}
Let $B_1,\ldots,B_k$ be mutually disjoint finite blocks of independent
uniform signs. Let $g_i:\{-1,1\}^{B_i}\to\{-1,1\}$ satisfy
$\mathbb E g_i=0$, and let $h:\{-1,1\}^k\to\{-1,1\}$ be arbitrary.
For $F=h(g_1,\ldots,g_k)$,
\begin{align*}
\mathbb EF&=\mathbb Eh,&\operatorname{Var}F&=\operatorname{Var}h,\\
H(F)&=H(h)+\sum_i\operatorname{Inf}_i(h)H(g_i),\\
I(F)&=\sum_i\operatorname{Inf}_i(h)I(g_i),\\
\operatorname{Inf}_x(F)&=\operatorname{Inf}_i(h)\operatorname{Inf}_x(g_i)
\quad(x\in B_i).
\end{align*}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For $S\subseteq\bigcup_i B_i$, set $S_i=S\cap B_i$ and
$T(S)=\{i:S_i\ne\varnothing\}$. Balance eliminates empty coefficients
in every used child, so expansion gives uniquely
\[
\widehat F(S)=\widehat h(T(S))\prod_{i\in T(S)}\widehat g_i(S_i).
\]
For the empty $S$ this also proves the mean and variance assertions.
The squared law is generated by first sampling $T$ with probability
$\widehat h(T)^2$, then independently sampling nonempty $S_i$ with
probability $\widehat g_i(S_i)^2$ for each $i\in T$, and taking their
union. Parseval makes these child laws probability measures. The union
recovers $T$ and all $S_i$, so the entropy chain rule proves the claimed
formula. Expected cardinality and coordinate inclusion prove influence.
\end{proof}
\paragraph{Arbitrary child means and physical restrictions.}
Fix constant children into $h$ first. For each remaining child put
$m_i=\mathbb E g_i$, $v_i=1-m_i^2>0$, and expand $h$ in the independent
output product basis $\psi_i(y_i)=(y_i-m_i)/\sqrt{v_i}$, with coefficients
$a_\mu(T)$. Put $w_i=\sum_{T\ni i}a_\mu(T)^2$ and
$H_\mu(h)=H((a_\mu(T)^2)_T)$. The same proof yields
\begin{align*}
\widehat F(S)&=a_\mu(T(S))\prod_{i\in T(S)}
\frac{\widehat g_i(S_i)}{\sqrt{v_i}},\\
H(F)&=H_\mu(h)+\sum_i\frac{w_i}{v_i}\bigl(H(g_i)-h_2(v_i)\bigr),\\
I(F)&=\sum_i\frac{w_i}{v_i}I(g_i),&
\mathbb EF&=a_\mu(\varnothing).
\end{align*}
Here $\widehat g_i(S_i)^2/v_i$ is the conditional nonempty child law,
whose entropy is $(H(g_i)-h_2(v_i))/v_i$.
After any fixed physical restriction, remaining coordinates are still
independent uniform and blocks remain disjoint. Recompute every child
mean, absorb newly constant children, and recompute $a_\mu$ and $w_i$
under the new output law. The balanced formulas apply if all remaining
children are balanced. Balance of a child before restriction does not
imply balance after restriction.

For one initially balanced child $j$ changed to mean $m$, write
$h=A+y_jB$. The new outer coefficients are
$\widehat A(T)+m\widehat B(T)$ and
$\sqrt{1-m^2}\widehat B(T)$; their squared weights retain the signed
cross term $2m\widehat A(T)\widehat B(T)$. At $|m|=1$, fix $y_j=m$
instead of dividing by zero.

\paragraph{Exact restriction witness.}
Take $h=\mathrm{AND}_2$, children majority on $(x,a,b)$ and a fresh
dictator $z$, and physically fix $x=+1$. The changed child is
$\mathrm{OR}_2(a,b)$, with mean $1/2$, entropy $2$, and influence $1$.
The resulting function has seven spectral atoms $1/16$ and one $9/16$:
\[
\mathbb EF=-1/4,\qquad I(F)=5/4,\qquad
H(F)=4-\tfrac98\log_2 3.
\]
Reusing the old balanced gate law incorrectly gives mean $-1/2$,
influence $1$, and entropy $3$. This is a scope counterexample, not a
counterexample to FEI. See REPORT.md and out/checks.json for the complete
proof, exact finite verification, source pins, and pending review.
