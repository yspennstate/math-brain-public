# Grouped-root collision companion for the GU composition ledger

## 1. Signed collision and all-coordinate charge

For genuine Boolean functions `g,h` on the same finite uniform cube, put

\[
C(g,h)=U(gh)-U(g)-U(h).
\]

For a coordinate `i`, let

\[
\delta_i C=C(g,h)-\frac{C(g_{i,+},h_{i,+})+
C(g_{i,-},h_{i,-})}{2}.
\]

The preceding run proved the exact restriction cocycle

\[
\delta_i C=\Delta_iU(gh)-\Delta_iU(g)-\Delta_iU(h).
\]

Consequently the simultaneous collision charge in `G` is

\[
LC(g,h)=\sum_i\delta_i C.
\tag{1.1}
\]

The problem is that one root collision can occur in several summands of
(1.1).  A single restriction path pays `C` once, not once per coordinate.

## 2. Closed-form symmetric face companion

Suppose the ambient cube has `n` coordinates.  Let `A_k(g,h)` be the uniform
average of `C` over all codimension-`k` faces: choose a `k`-set of coordinates
uniformly, choose its signs uniformly, and restrict both functions.  Thus

\[
A_0=C(g,h),\qquad A_n=0,
\]

because both terminal functions are constants.  Define

\[
P_n^{\rm face}(g,h)=(n-1)A_0-2\sum_{k=1}^{n-1}A_k.
\tag{2.1}
\]

Let `R` choose one coordinate and one sign uniformly and apply the same
potential to the resulting `(n-1)`-cube pair.  Averaging a codimension-`k`
face after this first step gives `A_{k+1}`.  Hence

\[
RP_{n-1}^{\rm face}=(n-2)A_1-2\sum_{k=2}^{n-1}A_k.
\]

Subtracting from (2.1),

\[
P_n^{\rm face}-RP_{n-1}^{\rm face}=(n-1)A_0-nA_1.
\]

But `LC=n(A_0-A_1)`, so

\[
\boxed{LC=C+P_n^{\rm face}-RP_{n-1}^{\rm face}.}
\tag{2.2}
\]

Equivalently, if `d_k=A_k-A_{k+1}` are the signed collision decrements along
a random coordinate permutation, then

\[
P_n^{\rm face}=\sum_{k=0}^{n-1}(n-1-2k)d_k.
\tag{2.3}
\]

The coefficients in (2.1) are forced among symmetric linear face potentials:
matching the `A_0` coefficient gives `n-1`; matching `A_1` then gives `-2`,
and the remaining coefficients propagate recursively as `-2`.

### Dummy-coordinate defect

The fixed-ambient potential is not an acceptable standalone universal
reserve.  If an irrelevant coordinate is appended, then

\[
A'_k=\frac{n+1-k}{n+1}A_k+\frac{k}{n+1}A_{k-1}.
\]

Substitution in (2.1) gives the exact inflation law

\[
\boxed{P_{n+1}^{\rm face}=\frac{n+2}{n+1}P_n^{\rm face}.}
\tag{2.4}
\]

Thus adding `d` dummy coordinates multiplies the potential by
`(n+d+1)/(n+1)`.  This refutes use of (2.1) as a dimension-free reserve,
although identity (2.2) remains correct.

## 3. Active policy companion

The correction is to follow an actual adaptive physical hierarchy and never
activate an irrelevant coordinate.  At a restricted pair `v`, let
`A(v)` be the coordinates on which at least one of `g_v,h_v` is nonconstant.
Fix any policy `q_v` supported on `A(v)`; deterministic branch-dependent
coordinate choices are allowed.  After choosing a coordinate, its physical
sign remains uniform.  Write `R_q` for the resulting child expectation.

Define recursively

\[
P^q(v)=LC(v)-C(v)+R_qP^q(v_{\rm child}),
\tag{3.1}
\]

with `P^q=0` when no active coordinate remains.  Rearranging gives the exact
grouped-root identity

\[
\boxed{LC(v)=C(v)+P^q(v)-R_qP^q(v_{\rm child}).}
\tag{3.2}
\]

Although (3.2) is elementary, it has the required ownership semantics:
`P^q` is the signed cumulative surplus `LC-C` along one actual active
hierarchy, not a separately recharged unsigned bound.

Let `w_v` be the actual reach probabilities generated by the policy and
uniform physical signs.  Summing (3.2) over any finite adaptive tree yields

\[
\boxed{
\sum_{v\ {m internal}}w_vLC(v)
=\sum_{v\ {m internal}}w_vC(v)+P^q({\rm root})
-\sum_{\ell\ {m leaf}}w_\ell P^q(\ell).}
\tag{3.3}
\]

The terminal companion is explicit.  It is zero only when the hierarchy
continues until no active coordinate remains.  The construction is invariant
under adjoining any number of dummy coordinates, provided the policy depends
only on the active state.  Branches can lose different coordinates and stop
at different depths; no activation lower bound is used.

## 4. Exact GU implication

For fixed `a>=0`, `0<=b<1`, define

\[
D_{a,b}(f)=G(f)-aI(f)-bU(f)
\]

and let `J(g,h)` be the sum of the probabilities that both factors flip on
the same coordinate edge.  The exact Boolean edge identity gives

\[
I(gh)=I(g)+I(h)-2J(g,h).
\]

Therefore

\[
D_{a,b}(gh)-D_{a,b}(g)-D_{a,b}(h)
=LC+2aJ-bC.
\]

Using (3.2), the remainder becomes

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal R_{a,b}
=(1-b)C+2aJ+P^q-R_qP^q_{\rm child}.}
\tag{4.1}
\]

Over a weighted adaptive composition hierarchy,

\[
\sum_vw_v\mathcal R_{a,b}(v)
=P^q({\rm root})-\sum_\ell w_\ell P^q(\ell)
+\sum_vw_v\bigl((1-b)C(v)+2aJ(v)\bigr).
\tag{4.2}
\]

Thus one fixed pair `(a,b)` proves universal GU if every Boolean function has
such a composition hierarchy whose leaf defects are nonpositive and whose
right side in (4.2), together with any noncollision posterior/terminal terms
of the exact composition ledger, is nonpositive.  Once this is proved for a
restriction-closed universal class, the reviewed reduction gives

\[
U(f)\le\frac{a}{1-b}I(f),\qquad
H(f)\le\left(2+\frac{2a}{1-b}\right)I(f).
\]

## 5. Tests against the prior obstruction

For

\[
g=(-1,-1,-1,-1,-1,1,1,1),\qquad
h=(-1,1,1,-1,-1,1,1,-1),
\]

the prior certificate gave

\[
C=\frac1{16}\log_2\frac{120^3 3^9}{2^{25}}>0,
\qquad LC=3C,\qquad J=\frac12.
\]

All immediate child collisions and companions vanish, so the corrected
active companion is exactly

\[
P^q=LC-C=2C.
\]

This shows that (3.2) accounts for the multiplicity exactly.  It also shows
that the companion alone does not prove GU: (4.1) remains
`(3-b)C+a>0`.  A further joint companion-sign or posterior reserve is still
necessary.

The active companion is sign-indefinite.  The positive example above has
`P^q=2C>0`.  For the two-bit function
`g=h=(-1,-1,-1,1)`, one has `C=-2`, `LC=-4`, all child companions zero,
and hence `P^q=-2`.  Absolute-value replacement would destroy the exact
cancellation.

## 6. Remaining universal gap

The all-coordinate multiplicity is now isolated into a dummy-invariant,
single-use telescoping companion.  What remains is a genuinely signed scalar
payment for

\[
P^q({\rm root})-\sum_\ell w_\ell P^q(\ell)
+\sum_vw_v\bigl((1-b)C(v)+2aJ(v)\bigr)
\]

together with the exact posterior/terminal reserves of the chosen Boolean
composition.  The three-bit witness proves that this payment cannot be made
from `C` and joint pivotality alone.  The next candidate must couple the
actual signing to its companion signing before (4.2) is summed.
