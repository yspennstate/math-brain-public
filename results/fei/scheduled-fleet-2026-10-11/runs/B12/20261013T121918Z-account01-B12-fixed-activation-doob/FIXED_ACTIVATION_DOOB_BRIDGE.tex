\section*{Exact Doob bridge for the complete biased coherent remainder}

All logarithms are natural and
\[
 \psi(t)=t^2\log(1/t^2),\qquad
 \mathcal E_{\rm raw}(c)=\sum_T\psi(c_T).
\]
Let $a,b$ be functions on a block $W$, let $g$ be a Boolean sign on a
disjoint block $X$, assume $a+b$ and $a-b$ are Boolean signs, and put
\[
 F_g=a+gb,\qquad q=\|b\|_2^2,
 \qquad \mathcal J_{a,b}(g)=\mathcal E_{\rm raw}(F_g)
                         -q\mathcal E_{\rm raw}(g).                 \tag{1}
\]

\subsection*{Theorem 1: the biased remainder is already event-local}

At a restriction node write $g=u+x_i v$, let
$\mu=\mathbb Eg$ and $\alpha=\widehat g(\{i\})$, and let $g_+,g_-$ be
the two physical restrictions.  The exact remainder $R_\mu(\alpha)$ of
the biased-child kernel identity satisfies
\[
 \boxed{R_\mu(\alpha)=\mathcal J_{a,b}(g)
 -\frac{\mathcal J_{a,b}(g_+)+\mathcal J_{a,b}(g_-)}2.}             \tag{2}
\]
Consequently, for every complete adaptive restriction tree on $X$, with
node reach probabilities $w_v$,
\begin{align*}
 \sum_{v\ {m internal}}w_vR_{\mu_v}(\alpha_v)
  ={}&\mathcal J_{a,b}(g)
    -\Pr[g=1]\,\mathcal E_{\rm raw}(a+b)\\
   &-\Pr[g=-1]\,\mathcal E_{\rm raw}(a-b).                         \tag{3}
\end{align*}
Both identities are uniform in $\mu/\alpha$, in every coherent collision
ratio, in the dimension, and in the adaptive query order.

\paragraph{Proof.}
The physical split decrement is
\[
 D_i(f)=\mathcal E_{\rm raw}(f)
       -\frac{\mathcal E_{\rm raw}(f_+)+
              \mathcal E_{\rm raw}(f_-)}2.
\]
The exact biased identity is
$D_i(F_g)=qD_i(g)+R_\mu(\alpha)$.  Subtracting the two decrements is
exactly (2).  Multiplying (2) by $w_v$ and summing cancels every internal
state.  At a complete leaf $g=\pm1$, whose raw entropy is zero, so
$\mathcal J_{a,b}(\pm1)=\mathcal E_{\rm raw}(a\pm b)$.  This proves
(3).  Notice in particular that (2) is zero on every unchanged dummy
cylinder. \(\square\)

Equation (2) gives the precise relation to the event-local compensator:
the complete $R_\mu(\alpha)$, not merely its alpha-log envelope, is the
Doob decrement of the composition defect (1) whenever the activation pair
$(a,b)$ is fixed.  Thus a near/separated coefficient split is unnecessary
in this fixed-activation regime.

\subsection*{Theorem 2: arbitrary Boolean source at a selector interface}

Let $x$ be one fresh selector bit, let $s:\{-1,1\}^n\to\{0,1\}$ be
arbitrary, put $h=2s-1$ and $p=\mathbb Es$, and choose
\[
 a(x)=\frac{1+x}{2},\qquad b(x)=\frac{1-x}{2},\qquad
 F=a+hb.
\]
Then $q=1/2$, $a+b=1$, $a-b=x$, and every complete adaptive physical
restriction tree for $s$ satisfies the exact scalar law
\[
 \boxed{\sum_vw_vR_{\mu_v}(\alpha_v)=Q(p)}                         \tag{4}
\]
where
\[
 Q(p)=\psi(p)+\psi(1-p)-\frac12\psi(2p-1)
       +4p(1-p)\log2.                                             \tag{5}
\]
Moreover
\[
 Q(p)\le8p\log2.                                                  \tag{6}
\]
If $d=bs$ is the activated source, then
\[
 I(d)=\frac12I(s)+\frac p4,
\]
and hence
\[
 \boxed{\sum_vw_vR_{\mu_v}(\alpha_v)
       \le32\log2\,I(d).}                                        \tag{7}
\]
In bits, the constant in (7) is $32$.

\paragraph{Proof.}
For nonempty source Fourier indices $T$, the two selector copies of the
$F$ coefficient are $s_T$ and $-s_T$; its two constant coefficients are
$p$ and $1-p$.  Also $\widehat h(\varnothing)=2p-1$ and
$\widehat h(T)=2s_T$ for $T\ne\varnothing$.  Parseval gives
$\sum_{T\ne\varnothing}s_T^2=p-p^2$.  Therefore
\begin{align*}
 \mathcal E_{\rm raw}(F)-\frac12\mathcal E_{\rm raw}(h)
  =\psi(p)+\psi(1-p)-\frac12\psi(2p-1)
   +4p(1-p)\log2,
\end{align*}
which, together with (3) and the zero entropy of $1$ and $x$, proves
(4)--(5).

The function $Q$ is symmetric under $p\leftrightarrow1-p$.  For
$0<p\le1/2$, discard the nonpositive term $-\psi(1-2p)/2$ and use
\[
 \psi(p)=2p^2\log(1/p)\le\frac{2p}{e},\qquad
 \psi(1-p)\le2p(1-p)\le2p.
\]
Thus
\[
 Q(p)\le p\left(\frac2e+2+4\log2\right)\le8p\log2;
\]
the last elementary inequality follows, for example, from
$e>8/3$ and $\log2>11/16$.  Symmetry handles $p\ge1/2$, and the endpoints
are immediate.  Finally, tensor/edge counting for $d=bs$ gives
$I(d)=I(s)/2+p/4$, proving (7). \(\square\)

\subsection*{Exact scope}

The result pays the entire changing biased coherent remainder, uniformly
through balanced, near-balanced, separated, and exact-collision nodes, for
one fixed selector activation and an arbitrary Boolean source.  It is
cylinder-safe and uses only one terminal activation reserve.  It does not
cover a hierarchy in which the pair $(a_v,b_v)$ itself changes coherently
with restricted physical variables, nor an overlapping-input interface.
For those regimes, (2) identifies the exact new term: the inter-node drift
of the potential $\mathcal J_{a_v,b_v}(g_v)$ caused by changing activation.
