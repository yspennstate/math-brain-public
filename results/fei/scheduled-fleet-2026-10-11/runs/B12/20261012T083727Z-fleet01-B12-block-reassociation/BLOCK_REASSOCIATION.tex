\section*{Block channelization and exact reassociation transfer}

All logarithms are natural.  For a real function $c$ on a finite cube put
\[
 \mathcal E(c)=\sum_U \widehat c(U)^2
          \log\frac1{\widehat c(U)^2},
\]
where zero summands are omitted.  Let $B$ be a block of $m$ physical
coordinates.  Every $f$ has the unique expansion
\[
 f=\sum_{\gamma\subseteq B}\chi_\gamma f_\gamma,
\tag{1}
\]
with the $f_\gamma$ functions on the complementary cube.  Define the block
entropy decrement
\[
 D_B(f)=\sum_{\gamma\subseteq B}\mathcal E(f_\gamma)
 -2^{-m}\sum_{\varepsilon\in\{-1,1\}^{B}}
       \mathcal E\!\left(\sum_{\gamma\subseteq B}
                    \varepsilon^\gamma f_\gamma\right).
\tag{2}
\]
Thus $D_B(f)=\mathcal E(f)-\mathbb E_\varepsilon
\mathcal E(f|_{x_B=\varepsilon})$.

Let $g_+,g_-,h$ be Boolean signs on the same cube and set
\[
 a=(g_++g_-)/2,\qquad b=(g_+-g_-)/2,\qquad F=a+hb.
\tag{3}
\]
Expand $a,b,h$ as in (1).  Physical multiplication gives the exact block
convolution
\[
 F_\gamma=a_\gamma+\sum_{\alpha\subseteq B}
                 h_\alpha b_{\gamma\triangle\alpha}.
\tag{4}
\]
All products in (4) are pointwise products on the complementary cube.
Separate the zero selector frequency by putting
\[
 A_\gamma=a_\gamma+h_\varnothing b_\gamma,
 \qquad C^\alpha_\gamma=h_\alpha b_{\gamma\triangle\alpha}
       \quad(\alpha\ne\varnothing).
\tag{5}
\]
Let $A$ and $C^\alpha$ denote the physical functions with these block
coefficients, and let $q=\lVert b\rVert_2^2$.  Define
\[
 S_B=D_B(A),\qquad X_{B,\alpha}=D_B(C^\alpha),
\tag{6}
\]
\[
 \Omega_B=D_B(F)-S_B-\sum_{\alpha\ne\varnothing}X_{B,\alpha},
 \qquad R_B^{\rm sh}=\Omega_B-qD_B(h).
\tag{7}
\]

\subsection*{Theorem 1 (exact block-channel identity; proved)}

For every finite cube, every Boolean triple $(g_+,g_-,h)$, and every
physical block $B$,
\[
 \boxed{D_B(F)=S_B+\sum_{\alpha\ne\varnothing}X_{B,\alpha}
                 +qD_B(h)+R_B^{\rm sh}.}
\tag{8}
\]
No disjointness, orthogonality, bias floor, or collision-separation
hypothesis is present.

\paragraph{Proof.}
Equation (4) and (5) give
$F=A+\sum_{\alpha\ne\varnothing}C^\alpha$.  Substitute the definitions
(6)--(7). \(\square\)

For $|B|=1$, (5) gives exactly the source and crossed functions
$(A,B)$ and $(C,D)$ in the preceding four-channel theorem.  Hence (8)
strictly extends that result.  If the selector is independent of $B$, all
$C^\alpha$ vanish and $R_B^{\rm sh}=0$.  If the source is independent of
$B$, the source block decrement vanishes but the nonzero selector channels
remain, retaining all coherent collisions.

\subsection*{Theorem 2 (sequential/block reassociation; proved)}

Fix an ordering $\pi=(i_1,\dots,i_m)$ of $B$.  At every node of the induced
restriction tree, apply the one-coordinate four-channel identity and weight
the node at depth $t-1$ by $2^{-(t-1)}$.  Let $L_\pi$ be the weighted sum of
the local source-plus-crossed channels, and let $M_\pi$ be the weighted sum
of the local $qD_i(h)+R_i^{\rm sh}$ channels.  Put
\[
 L_B=S_B+\sum_{\alpha\ne\varnothing}X_{B,\alpha},\qquad
 M_B=qD_B(h)+R_B^{\rm sh},
\tag{9}
\]
and define the reassociation transfer
\[
 T_\pi=L_\pi-L_B.
\tag{10}
\]
Then
\[
 D_B(F)=L_\pi+M_\pi=L_B+M_B,
 \qquad M_\pi=M_B-T_\pi.
\tag{11}
\]

\paragraph{Proof.}
For any real $f$, iterating the definition of the one-coordinate decrement
and cancelling intermediate restricted entropies gives
\[
 D_B(f)=\sum_{t=1}^m2^{-(t-1)}
   \sum_{\varepsilon\in\{-1,1\}^{t-1}}
   D_{i_t}(f|_{x_{i_s}=\varepsilon_s,\,s<t}).
\tag{12}
\]
Apply the exact one-coordinate four-channel identity at every term in (12).
This proves the first equality in (11).  Theorem 1 proves the second.
Subtracting the two and using (10) proves the last equality. \(\square\)

The transfer $T_\pi$ is therefore not an error term: it is the exact amount
by which sequential restriction assigns coherent collision entropy to a
different channel family than the simultaneous block expansion.  It need
not vanish and need not be invariant under the order $\pi$.

\subsection*{A two-bit activation-irreducible witness}

In zero-based lexicographic order take
\[
 g_+=(-1,-1,-1,-1),\qquad
 g_-=(-1,-1,1,1),\qquad
 h=(-1,-1,-1,1).
\tag{13}
\]
The disagreement support is $\{2,3\}$ and the selector signs there are
$-1,+1$, so both branches are physically selected on disagreement points.
The output is $F=(-1,-1,1,-1)$.

For $B=\{0,1\}$ exact Walsh arithmetic gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
 S_B&=\frac94\log2-\frac98\log3,\\
 X_{B,\alpha}&=\frac14\log2\quad
       (\alpha\in\{\{0\},\{1\},\{0,1\}\}),\\
 D_B(h)&=2\log2,\qquad q=\frac12,\\
 R_B^{\rm sh}&=\tau:=-2\log2+\frac98\log3,\\
 D_B(F)&=2\log2.
\end{aligned}
\tag{14}
\]
The sign is rigorous:
\[
 \tau=\frac18\log\frac{3^9}{2^{16}}<0
 \quad\text{because}\quad 3^9=19683<65536=2^{16}.
\tag{15}
\]

For the order $(0,1)$ the sequential ledger is
\[
 L_{(0,1)}=\log2,\qquad
 \sum q_vD_{i_v}(h_v)=\log2,\qquad
 \sum R_{i_v}^{\rm sh}=0,
\tag{16}
\]
so $T_{(0,1)}=\tau$.  For the reversed order,
\[
 L_{(1,0)}=L_B=3\log2-\frac98\log3,\qquad
 \sum q_vD_{i_v}(h_v)=\log2,\qquad
 \sum R_{i_v}^{\rm sh}=\tau,
\tag{17}
\]
so $T_{(1,0)}=0$.  The total decrement is $2\log2$ in both orders.  Thus
the same signed coherent term is classified as block remainder, sequential
remainder, or source/crossed transfer depending on grouping and query order.

\subsection*{Consequence for the open hierarchy payment}

The two-query experiment does not produce the hoped-for termwise
cancellation of internal $S/X$ channels.  It proves the correct replacement:
the complete decrement telescopes, while its channel allocation changes by
the signed transfer $T_\pi$.  Any valid adaptive hierarchy proof must either
carry $T_\pi$ explicitly or use an order-independent aggregate gauge.

The exact block expansion has $2^m-1$ nonzero selector-frequency channels.
This is an algebraic identity, not a dimension-free estimate: separately
enveloping those channels would risk an exponential block-size loss.  The
remaining universal problem is to compress or pay their aggregate, together
with $R_B^{\rm sh}$ and activation weights, from one source-influence or
terminal-reserve budget.  Nothing here proves full FEI.
