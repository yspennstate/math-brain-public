\section*{Aggregate selector-channel influence and the exact collision credit}

Let $B$ be a finite block of physical coordinates and let $Y$ denote the
remaining coordinates.  Write the partial Fourier expansions
\[
 h(x,y)=\sum_{\alpha\subseteq B}\chi_\alpha(x)h_\alpha(y),
 \qquad
 b(x,y)=\sum_{\beta\subseteq B}\chi_\beta(x)b_\beta(y).
\tag{1}
\]
As in the preceding block-channel identity, for every nonzero selector
frequency $\alpha$ define
\[
 C^\alpha_\gamma(y)=h_\alpha(y)b_{\gamma\triangle\alpha}(y).
\tag{2}
\]
Equivalently, in physical coordinates,
\[
 C^\alpha(x,y)=\chi_\alpha(x)h_\alpha(y)b(x,y).
\tag{3}
\]

For a real function $u=\sum_{\gamma\subseteq B}\chi_\gamma u_\gamma$
put
\[
 \mathbf I_B(u)=\sum_{\gamma\subseteq B}|\gamma|
                         \lVert u_\gamma\rVert_2^2.
\tag{4}
\]
For each fixed $y$ introduce
\[
\begin{aligned}
 V_h(y)&=\sum_{\alpha\ne\varnothing}h_\alpha(y)^2,\\
 J_h(y)&=\sum_{\alpha\ne\varnothing}|\alpha|h_\alpha(y)^2,\\
 p_b(y)&=\sum_\beta b_\beta(y)^2,\\
 J_b(y)&=\sum_\beta|\beta|b_\beta(y)^2,\\
 \mathsf O_B(y)&=\sum_{\alpha\ne\varnothing,\beta}
       |\alpha\cap\beta|h_\alpha(y)^2b_\beta(y)^2.
\end{aligned}
\tag{5}
\]

\subsection*{Theorem 1 (exact aggregate collision credit; proved)}

For every Boolean selector $h$, every Boolean source pair $g_+,g_-$, and
$b=(g_+-g_-)/2$, the entire family of nonzero selector-frequency channels
satisfies
\[
 \sum_{\alpha\ne\varnothing}\lVert C^\alpha\rVert_2^2
   =\mathbb E_y[V_h(y)p_b(y)]\le \lVert b\rVert_2^2,
\tag{6}
\]
and
\[
 \boxed{
 \sum_{\alpha\ne\varnothing}\mathbf I_B(C^\alpha)
 =\mathbb E_y\!left[
      V_hJ_b+p_bJ_h-2\mathsf O_B\right].}
\tag{7}
\]
Consequently,
\[
 \sum_{\alpha\ne\varnothing}\mathbf I_B(C^\alpha)
 \le \mathbf I_B(b)+\mathbf I_B(h).
\tag{8}
\]
The last term of (7) is an exact nonnegative overlap refund; discarding it
can lose the entire gross budget.

\paragraph{Proof.}
Equation (3) and Parseval in the $B$ variables give
\[
 \lVert C^\alpha\rVert_2^2
 =\mathbb E_y\left[h_\alpha(y)^2p_b(y)\right],
\]
and summing proves the equality in (6).  Since $h$ is Boolean,
$V_h=1-h_\varnothing^2\le1$; since $b\in\{0,\pm1\}$, $p_b\le1$.  This proves
the inequality in (6).

From (2),
\[
 \mathbf I_B(C^\alpha)
 =\mathbb E_y h_\alpha(y)^2
       \sum_\beta|\beta\triangle\alpha|b_\beta(y)^2.
\]
Use
$|\beta\triangle\alpha|=|\beta|+|\alpha|-2|\alpha\cap\beta|$
and sum over $\alpha\ne\varnothing$ to obtain (7).  Finally
$V_h\le1$, $p_b\le1$, and $\mathsf O_B\ge0$, while
$\mathbb E_yJ_b=\mathbf I_B(b)$ and
$\mathbb E_yJ_h=\mathbf I_B(h)$.  This proves (8). \(\square\)

\subsection*{Theorem 2 (adaptive nonreusing hierarchy budget; proved)}

Let an adaptive physical restriction hierarchy query a block $B_v$ at node
$v$, with reach probability $w_v$, and assume no physical coordinate is
queried twice on a root-to-leaf path.  Let $b_v,h_v$ be the actual
restrictions at $v$ and let $C_v^\alpha$ be their selector-frequency
channels.  Then
\[
 \boxed{
 \sum_vw_v\sum_{\alpha\ne\varnothing}
        \mathbf I_{B_v}(C_v^\alpha)
 \le \mathbf I(b)+\mathbf I(h).}
\tag{9}
\]
More precisely, the left side is the weighted sum of the exact activated
gross terms and overlap refunds in (7).

\paragraph{Proof.}
Apply (8) at each node.  For any real function $f$, the physical derivative
form of influence gives
\[
 \sum_vw_v\mathbf I_{B_v}(f_v)\le\mathbf I(f).
\tag{10}
\]
Indeed, for a fixed coordinate $i$, the events that the hierarchy reaches a
node which queries $i$ are disjoint along a full input path, depend only on
previously revealed coordinates, and therefore select disjoint portions of
the squared physical derivative in direction $i$.  Their integral is at
most $\operatorname{Inf}_i(f)$.  Sum over $i$, first for $b$ and then for
$h$, to prove (9). \(\square\)

Thus the $2^{|B|}-1$ channel count from the block expansion causes no loss at
the influence level and no depth loss in an actual adaptive hierarchy.

\subsection*{Parity-zero erasure is the overlap refund}

Take on two bits
\[
 g_+=(-1,-1,1,1),\quad g_-=(1,1,-1,-1),\quad
 h=(-1,-1,1,1).
\tag{11}
\]
Then $a=0$, $b=h$ is one character, and the composed output is constant.
For the full block,
\[
 V_h=J_h=p_b=J_b=\mathsf O_B=1.
\]
The gross influence in (7) is $2$, the collision refund is $2$, and the
unique selector channel is constant, hence has block influence zero.  This
is exactly the mandatory parity-zero-erasure control, not an exceptional
convention.

\subsection*{The channel-entropy payment is already full FEI}

The influence aggregation (9) does not by itself pay the channel entropy
decrements $X_{B,\alpha}=D_B(C^\alpha)$.  In fact the tempting standalone
estimate
\[
 \sum_{\alpha\ne\varnothing}D_B(C^\alpha)
 \le C\sum_{\alpha\ne\varnothing}\mathbf I_B(C^\alpha)
\tag{12}
\]
for one universal $C$ is not a smaller auxiliary lemma: it contains full FEI
exactly.

To see this, let $k$ be an arbitrary Boolean function on a cube $Z$ and add
one fresh coordinate $x$.  Set
\[
 h(x,z)=x,\qquad g_+(x,z)=xk(z),\qquad
 g_-(x,z)=-xk(z).
\tag{13}
\]
Then $a=0$, $b=xk$, and $F=a+hb=k$.  For the full block
$B=\{x\}\cup Z$, the selector has exactly one nonzero frequency
$\alpha=\{x\}$ and
\[
 C^\alpha=\chi_\alpha b=x^2k=k.
\tag{14}
\]
All other channels and the block remainder vanish.  Hence
\[
 D_B(C^\alpha)=\mathbf H_{\rm nat}(k),\qquad
 \mathbf I_B(C^\alpha)=\mathbf I(k).
\tag{15}
\]
Applied to (13), (12) is precisely FEI in natural-log units.  Conversely,
full FEI applied to Boolean channels would give this special case.  The
fresh selector coordinate contributes two units to the gross right side of
(7), and the overlap refund removes both, leaving exactly $\mathbf I(k)$.

Therefore the new collision credit eliminates exponential bookkeeping but
does not license a separate channel-by-channel entropy bound.  Progress must
come from coupling the channel entropies to the signed block remainder,
source channel, or terminal reserve before applying FEI-type control.
