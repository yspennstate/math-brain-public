# Commands and deterministic scope

Primary command, run from this directory:

`python3 CHECK.py`

The exact output is preserved in `CHECK.out`.  There is no randomness and no
seed.  Walsh coefficients, squared masses, upper masses, and influences use
exact `Fraction` arithmetic; only base-two logarithms are floating point.
The all-dimensional statements are analytic in `PROOF.md`.

The exhaustive portion covers every parity mask and unordered point pair
through dimension five.  Higher-dimensional cases are structured
reconstructions, not evidence substituted for the proof.
