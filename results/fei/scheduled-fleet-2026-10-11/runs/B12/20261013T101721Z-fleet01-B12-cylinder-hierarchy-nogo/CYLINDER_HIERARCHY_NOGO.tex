\section*{An unbounded-depth cylinder obstruction to the full-influence telescope}

All logarithms are natural.  Let $s_0$ be the indicator of
$\{000,001,010\}\subset\mathbb F_2^3$.  These are three distinct parallel
affine cosets of the zero subspace.  With an independent selector
$x\in\{-1,1\}$ put
\[
 d_0(x,z)=\mathbf1_{\{x=-1\}}s_0(z),\quad
 a_0=x+d_0,\quad F_0=x+2d_0,
\]
and
\[
 \Omega_0=\mathcal E_{\rm raw}(F_0)
 -\mathcal E_{\rm raw}(a_0)-\mathcal E_{\rm raw}(d_0).
\]

The exact parallel-triple spectrum and selector identity give
\[
 \Omega_0=
 \frac{169}{128}\log13-\frac{45}{128}\log3
 -\frac{25}{32}\log5-2\log2
 =0.356636085579521\ldots>0.                         \tag{1}
\]
Positivity has the integer certificate
\[
 13^{169}>3^{45}5^{100}2^{256}.                        \tag{2}
\]
The exact physical influence is
\[
 I(d_0)=\frac14.                                       \tag{3}
\]

For $L\ge1$, adjoin free physical variables $t_1,\dots,t_L$ and define
$d_L(x,z,t)=d_0(x,z)$.  Query the free variables successively, at every node
of the complete binary restriction tree.  This hierarchy is pathwise
coordinate-nonreusing.  Every reached restriction is the same local source
$d_0$ tensored with the remaining free coordinates, so every internal node
has associator $\Omega_0$.  The activation weights on every depth sum to one.
Consequently
\[
 \sum_{v\text{ internal}}w_v\Omega_v=L\Omega_0,
 \qquad I(d_L)=I(d_0)=\frac14.                        \tag{4}
\]
It follows that
\[
 \frac{\sum_vw_v\Omega_v}{I(d_L)}=4L\Omega_0
 \longrightarrow\infty.                              \tag{5}
\]

\paragraph{No-go theorem.}
There is no finite constant $C$ such that
\[
 \sum_vw_v\Omega_v\le C I(d)
\]
for every adaptive pathwise-coordinate-nonreusing hierarchy of restrictions
of one source, even when every nonempty local source is a union of exactly
three parallel affine cosets.

The same family also refutes a directional repair that charges the newly
queried/owned coordinate block: every queried dummy direction has zero
influence, while $\Omega_v=\Omega_0>0$.  A valid hierarchy statement must
therefore change the event accounting.  It may charge only structural
composition/activation events, require disjoint ownership of the full active
derivative support, or retain a terminal reserve whose value is not charged
again at unchanged cylinder nodes.

This is an obstruction to a helper telescope, not to FEI.  The composed
outputs are genuine Boolean functions, and adjoining dummy variables changes
neither their Fourier entropy nor their influence.
