\section*{Uniform coherent-kernel envelope and a depth-free fixed-source theorem}

All logarithms are natural.  Put
\[
 \psi(x)=x^2\log\frac1{x^2},\qquad
 K(x,y)=\psi(x)+\psi(y)-\frac{\psi(x+y)+\psi(x-y)}2,
\]
with \(\psi(0)=0\).  We use the exact coefficientwise identity proved in
the preceding B12 run.  If \(F=a+h b\), \(\mu=\mathbb Eh\),
\(\alpha=\widehat h(\{i\})\), and \(q=\sum_Tb_T^2\), then
\[
 R_\mu(\alpha)=\sum_{T:b_T\ne0}b_T^2
 \left[K\left(\frac{a_T}{b_T}+\mu,\alpha\right)-K(\mu,\alpha)\right].       \tag{1}
\]
Here \(a+b\) and \(a-b\) are Boolean signs, so in particular
\(|a_T|,|b_T|\le1\).

\subsection*{1. The scalar kernel}

The quadratic logarithmic scale terms cancel in \(K\), and hence
\[
 K(\lambda x,\lambda y)=\lambda^2K(x,y).                              \tag{2}
\]
Define the even function \(k(r)=K(r,1)\).  For \(r\ge0\), with
\(G(x)=x^2\log x\) and \(G(0)=0\),
\[
 k(r)=G(r+1)+G(|r-1|)-2G(r).                                          \tag{3}
\]

\paragraph{Lemma 1 (proved).}
The function \(k\) is nonnegative and increasing on \([0,\infty)\), and
for every real \(r\),
\[
 2\log(1+|r|)-1\ \le\ k(r)\ \le\ 2\log(1+|r|)+3.                    \tag{4}
\]
Consequently, for all real \(r,s\),
\[
 k(r)-k(s)\le4+2\log(1+|r-s|).                                      \tag{5}
\]

\paragraph{Proof.}
Evenness is immediate.  For \(0<r<1\), differentiation of (3) gives
\[
 \frac{k'(r)}2=(1+r)\log(1+r)-(1-r)\log(1-r)-2r\log r=:u(r).
\]
Now \(u(0)=0\), \(u(1)=2\log2\), and
\[
 u'(r)=\log\frac{1-r^2}{r^2}.
\]
Thus \(u\) increases and then decreases, and is nonnegative.  For
\(r>1\), \(G'''(r)=2/r>0\), so the centered second difference (3) is
increasing.  Since \(k(0)=0\), this proves monotonicity and
nonnegativity.

For \(r>1\), the second-difference integral is
\[
 k(r)=\int_{-1}^1(1-|t|)\,[2\log(r+t)+3],dt.                         \tag{6}
\]
The triangular density has mass one and mean zero.  Jensen's inequality
therefore yields \(k(r)\le2\log r+3\), which proves the upper bound for
\(r\ge1\); on \([0,1]\), monotonicity gives
\(k(r)\le k(1)=4\log2<3\).

For the lower bound, on \([0,1/2]\) use \(k\ge0\) and
\(2\log(3/2)<1\).  On \([1/2,1]\), use monotonicity at \(1/2\);
direct substitution in (3) gives
\(k(1/2)=\tfrac94\log(3/2)+\tfrac14\log2>2\log2-1\).
On \([1,2]\), use \(k(r)\ge k(1)=4\log2>2\log3-1\).  Finally, for
\(r\ge2\), (6) gives
\[
 k(r)\ge2\log(r-1)+3\ge2\log(r+1)-1,
\]
because \(4+2\log((r-1)/(r+1))\ge4-2\log3>0\).
This proves (4).  Applying its upper bound to \(r\) and lower bound to
\(s\), then using
\[
 \frac{1+|r|}{1+|s|}\le1+|r-s|,
\]
proves (5). \(\square\)

\subsection*{2. The requested near/separated split}

Let \(\beta=|\alpha|>0\),
\[
 r_T=\frac{a_T+\mu b_T}{\beta b_T},\qquad s=\frac{\mu}{\beta}.
\]
By (2), the \(T\)-summand in (1) is
\(\beta^2b_T^2[k(r_T)-k(s)]\).  Fix any \(c>0\) and define
\[
 \mathcal N_c=\{T:0<|b_T|,\ |a_T+\mu b_T|\le c\beta|b_T|\},
 \qquad \mathcal S_c=\{T:b_T\ne0\}\setminus\mathcal N_c.
\]

\paragraph{Lemma 2 (proved: signed near region).}
\[
 R_{\mathcal N_c}\le
 \beta^2\sum_{T\in\mathcal N_c}b_T^2\,[k(c)-k(|\mu|/\beta)].          \tag{7}
\]
In particular, the entire near region is a refund whenever
\(|\mu|\ge c\beta\).

\paragraph{Proof.}
On \(\mathcal N_c\), \(|r_T|\le c\).  Evenness and monotonicity give
\(k(r_T)\le k(c)\), while \(k(s)=k(|\mu|/\beta)\).  Sum the exact
coefficient contributions. \(\square\)

\paragraph{Lemma 3 (proved: uniform separated region).}
For every \(T\in\mathcal S_c\), and in fact for every \(T\) with
\(b_T\ne0\),
\[
 k(r_T)-k(s)\le4+2\log\left(1+\frac{|a_T|}{\beta|b_T|}\right).         \tag{8}
\]
The bound is uniform in \(\mu/\alpha\), the collision gap, and \(c\).

\paragraph{Proof.}
Apply (5) and observe that
\(|r_T-s|=|a_T|/(\beta|b_T|)\). \(\square\)

Thus (7) retains the exact fixed-bias collision refund, while (8) has no
singularity in either live transition parameter.

\subsection*{3. One-node envelope and the fixed-source hierarchy}

Define the outer collision reserve
\[
 M(a,b)=2\sum_{T:b_T\ne0}b_T^2\log_+\frac{|a_T|}{|b_T|}.              \tag{9}
\]
Since \(|a_T|\le1\),
\[
 0\le M(a,b)\le\mathcal E_{\rm raw}(b)
 :=\sum_Tb_T^2\log\frac1{b_T^2}.                                    \tag{10}
\]

\paragraph{Theorem 4 (proved: uniform complete one-node envelope).}
For every genuine Boolean disjoint splice and every physical child
coordinate,
\[
 \boxed{
 R_\mu(\alpha)\le q\psi(\alpha)
 +(4+2\log2)q\alpha^2+M(a,b)\alpha^2.}                              \tag{11}
\]
This includes \(\alpha=0\), where both sides vanish.

\paragraph{Proof.}
For \(0<\beta\le1\), sum (8) and use
\[
 2\log(1+u)\le2\log2+2\log_+u,
\]
and
\[
 2\log_+\frac{|a_T|}{\beta|b_T|}
 \le\log\frac1{\beta^2}+2\log_+\frac{|a_T|}{|b_T|}.
\]
After multiplication by \(\beta^2b_T^2\), the first term is
\(b_T^2\psi(\beta)\), and summing the final term gives
\(M(a,b)\beta^2\).  Evenness of \(\psi\) completes the proof. \(\square\)

Now hold the outer Boolean pair \(a\pm b\) fixed and run any deterministic
physical restriction tree inside the disjoint Boolean child \(h\), with
exact reach weights \(w_v\), node means \(\mu_v\), and queried singleton
coefficients \(\alpha_v\).  The preceding B12 theorem proved
\[
 \sum_vw_v\psi(\alpha_v)\le\frac2e\mathbf I(h).
\]
The same pivotal-event argument, using \(\alpha_v^2\le|\alpha_v|\), gives
\[
 \sum_vw_v\alpha_v^2\le\mathbf I(h).                                \tag{12}
\]

\paragraph{Theorem 5 (proved: depth-free fixed-source payment).}
For every such tree,
\[
 \boxed{
 \sum_vw_vR_{\mu_v}(\alpha_v)
 \le\left[M(a,b)+\left(4+2\log2+\frac2e\right)q\right]\mathbf I(h).} \tag{13}
\]
No bias floor, collision separation, depth bound, or nonadaptive query
order is assumed.

In particular, if a family of outer Boolean pairs satisfies
\(M(a,b)\le Lq\) for one absolute \(L\), then its complete coherent
remainder is paid by
\[
 \left(L+4+2\log2+\frac2e\right)q\mathbf I(h),                       \tag{14}
\]
uniformly over every Boolean child and every adaptive nonreusing
physical hierarchy.  This is a genuine all-parameter class extension:
the previous result paid only the explicit \(\alpha\)-log envelope,
whereas (13) pays the entire biased coherent remainder.

\subsection*{4. Exact remaining universal gap}

The price \(M(a,b)/q\) need not be uniformly bounded by this proof.
It is a reverse spectral collision reserve, and (10) shows that the
normalized derivative entropy is a sufficient payment.  Removing this
reserve without a new mechanism would subsume activation embeddings in
which the outer derivative carries an arbitrary Boolean spectrum.
Therefore (13) is not full FEI and does not close A057 universally.

For a hierarchy in which the outer witness \((a_v,b_v)\) itself changes
coherently, the remaining precise obligation is to prove one nonreused
source/terminal budget for
\[
 \sum_vw_v\alpha_v^2M(a_v,b_v),                                     \tag{15}
\]
or to replace (8) by a sharper signed estimate whose retained near-region
refund pays (15).  Equation (7) identifies the refund that must not be
dropped in that next step.
