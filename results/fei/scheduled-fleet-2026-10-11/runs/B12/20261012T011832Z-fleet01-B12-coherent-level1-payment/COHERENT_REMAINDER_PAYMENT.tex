\section*{A first-layer payment for the logarithmic coherent remainder}

All logarithms in this note are natural.  Let
\[
  \mathcal E_{\rm raw}(v)=\sum_T v_T^2\log\frac1{v_T^2},
  \qquad 0\log(1/0)=0.
\]
The starting point is the reviewed balanced-composition identity.  If
\(F=g(h_1,\ldots,h_m)\), the children use pairwise disjoint physical
blocks and are balanced Boolean signs, write, for a fixed outer coordinate
\(j\),
\[
   g(y)=a(y_{\ne j})+y_jb(y_{\ne j}),\qquad
   q_j=\operatorname{Inf}_j(g)=\sum_T b_T^2,
\]
and put \(\alpha_{j,i}=\widehat h_j(\{i\})\).  Then the source identity is
\begin{align*}
 \operatorname{Inf}_i(F)&=q_j\operatorname{Inf}_i(h_j),\\
 D_i(F)&=q_jD_i(h_j)+R_j(\alpha_{j,i}),\\
 R_j(\alpha)&={}&q_j\alpha^2\log\frac1{\alpha^2}
      +\mathcal E_{\rm raw}(a)+\alpha^2\mathcal E_{\rm raw}(b)\\
 &\quad-\frac12\left(\mathcal E_{\rm raw}(a+\alpha b)
                 +\mathcal E_{\rm raw}(a-\alpha b)\right).
\end{align*}
In particular, the two coherent spectra \(a+\alpha b\) and
\(a-\alpha b\) are both retained; no equality of restricted-child
spectra is assumed.

\subsection*{Proposition (proved): nonreusing first-layer log payment}

For every balanced Boolean sign \(h:\{-1,1\}^n\to\{-1,1\}\),
\[
 \sum_{i=1}^n \widehat h(\{i\})^2
       \log\frac1{\widehat h(\{i\})^2}
 \le \frac2e\,\mathbf I(h).
 \tag{1}
\]
Consequently, for every balanced disjoint substitution as above, the sum
of the explicit logarithmic pieces of all coherent remainders at this
physical layer satisfies
\[
 \sum_{j=1}^m q_j\sum_{i\in B_j}\alpha_{j,i}^2
       \log\frac1{\alpha_{j,i}^2}
 \le \frac2e\sum_{j=1}^m q_j\mathbf I(h_j)
 =\frac2e\,\mathbf I(F).
 \tag{2}
\]
In base-two entropy the coefficient is \(2/(e\log 2)<1.062\).

\paragraph{Proof.}
Fix a coordinate \(i\), and for \(x_{\ne i}\) set
\[
 \delta_i(x_{\ne i})=
 \frac{h(x_{\ne i},+1)-h(x_{\ne i},-1)}2\in\{-1,0,1\}.
\]
Pairing the two points on every \(i\)-edge gives
\[
 \widehat h(\{i\})=\mathbb E_{x_{\ne i}}\delta_i(x_{\ne i}),
 \qquad
 \operatorname{Inf}_i(h)=\mathbb E_{x_{\ne i}}|\delta_i(x_{\ne i})|.
\]
Thus \(|\widehat h(\{i\})|\le\operatorname{Inf}_i(h)\).  For
\(0\le u\le1\), with the endpoint interpreted continuously,
\[
 u^2\log\frac1{u^2}=2u\left(u\log\frac1u\right)\le\frac2e u,
\]
because \(\max_{0\le u\le1}u\log(1/u)=1/e\).  Apply this with
\(u=|\widehat h(\{i\})|\), then sum over \(i\), proving (1).  Multiplying
the inequality for child \(h_j\) by \(q_j\), summing over disjoint blocks,
and using the exact influence identity proves (2). \(\square\)

\subsection*{Exact cancellation form and scope}

Writing \(\psi(t)=t^2\log(1/t^2)\), the complete remainder can also be
written coefficientwise as
\[
 R_j(\alpha)=\sum_T\left[
   \psi(a_T)+\psi(\alpha b_T)
   -\frac{\psi(a_T+\alpha b_T)+\psi(a_T-\alpha b_T)}2
 \right].
 \tag{3}
\]
Indeed
\(\mathcal E_{\rm raw}(\alpha b)=
q_j\alpha^2\log(1/\alpha^2)+\alpha^2\mathcal E_{\rm raw}(b)\).
This form proves immediately that \(R_j(0)=0\), and also that
\(R_j(\alpha)=0\) whenever \(a_Tb_T=0\) for every \(T\).  The latter is
coefficientwise neighboring-support orthogonality, stronger than the
automatic Hilbert-space orthogonality \(\langle a,b\rangle=0\).

Equation (2) pays exactly the term whose pointwise replacement by
\(O(q_j\alpha^2)\) is false.  It does not yet pay the remaining coherent
term
\[
 C_j(\alpha)=\mathcal E_{\rm raw}(a)+\alpha^2\mathcal E_{\rm raw}(b)
 -\frac{\mathcal E_{\rm raw}(a+\alpha b)
              +\mathcal E_{\rm raw}(a-\alpha b)}2.
\]
Nor does it iterate after physical restrictions: a restricted child need
not remain balanced, so the balanced-composition interface is not closed.
Thus this is a complete scoped theorem for a single nonreusing balanced
physical layer, not completion of packet A057 and not a proof of FEI.

\subsection*{Mandatory logarithmic control}

The reviewed AND$_2$ outer control has
\[
 R(\alpha)=\frac{\alpha^2}{2}\log\frac1{\alpha^2}
             +\frac32\alpha^2+O(\alpha^4),
\]
and genuine balanced children with arbitrarily small nonzero \(\alpha\)
and leaf influence one.  Therefore no estimate
\(R(\alpha)\le Cq\alpha^2\) can replace the logarithm.  Proposition (1)
does not make that replacement: it sums the exact logarithmic quantities
against the full physical influence budget.  On the cited control family,
each summand is at most \((2/e)\operatorname{Inf}_i(h)\), while the
selected-coordinate entropy/influence ratio still tends to zero as in the
reviewed source.
