# Handoff

The exact next object is a three-piece collision graph. For disjoint affine cosets `A_j=a_j+V_j`, label a physical coordinate by the pair of pieces it joins. The two-piece proof works because all joining columns have one fixed nonzero image in `G/(V_1+V_2)`, forcing rank slack, while the common annihilator has one balanced relative character, forcing a Hadamard entropy refund.

For three pieces, determine whether the sum of pairwise edge cancellations is bounded by:

1. unused ranks `sum_j p_j(m_j-k_j)`;
2. one terminal density `sum_j p_j`; and
3. the exact multiway entropy deficit on common annihilator intersections.

Freeze and test the candidate before proving it. Include triples whose pairwise direction sums differ, triples with a mergeable pair but non-affine total union, rare point/flat mixtures, and whole-cube partitions. A failure of the multiway inequality is a terminal-boundary-reserve obstruction, not an FEI counterexample.
