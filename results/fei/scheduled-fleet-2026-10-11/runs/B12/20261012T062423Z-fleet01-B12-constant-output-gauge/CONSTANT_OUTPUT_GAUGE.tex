\section*{Activation quotient collapse and a constant-output gauge obstruction}

All logarithms are natural.  For Boolean branches $g_+,g_-$ and a Boolean
selector $h$ on the same cube, put
\[
 a=(g_++g_-)/2,\qquad b=(g_+-g_-)/2,\qquad F=a+hb.
\]
Write
\[
 q=\|b\|_2^2,\quad Z={\cal E}_{\rm raw}(b),\quad
 V=\operatorname{Var}(h),\quad J=\mathbf I(h).
\]

\subsection*{The activation quotient is identically zero}

Fix Boolean $F,h$.  Consider all Boolean branch-pair representations of the
same selected output,
\[
 {\cal R}(F,h)=\{(g_+,g_-):g_{h(z)}(z)=F(z)\text{ for every }z\}.
\]
The degenerate pair $g_+=g_-=F$ belongs to ${\cal R}(F,h)$.  For it $b=0$,
and hence $q=Z=0$.  Therefore, for every $A,\lambda\ge0$,
\[
 \inf_{{\cal R}(F,h)}\{AqJ+\lambda VZ\}=0.                 \tag{1}
\]
Thus unrestricted minimization over activation-equivalent representations
deletes all source-disagreement information.  It cannot serve as the reserve
for the already proved coherent-remainder inequality unless the charged
remainder is simultaneously replaced by a representation-invariant quantity.

\subsection*{An activation-irreducible constant-output witness}

On three bits, in zero-based lexicographic truth-table order, take
\[
\begin{split}
 g_+&=(1,1,-1,1,-1,1,-1,-1),\\
 g_-&=(-1,1,1,1,1,1,1,1),\\
 h&=(1,-1,-1,-1,-1,-1,-1,-1).
\end{split}                                                   \tag{2}
\]
The selected output is $F\equiv1$.  The branches disagree on
$\{0,2,4,6,7\}$, and $h$ takes both signs on that set.  Thus both branches
are genuinely selected on disagreement points; this is not an unused-branch
example.

Query coordinate one (coordinate zero in zero-based notation).  Exact Walsh
calculation gives
\[
 (q,Z,V,J)=\left(\frac58,\frac{15}{4}\log2-\frac98\log3,
                  \frac7{16},\frac34\right).
\]
The two children have data
\[
 (q_0,Z_0,V_0,J_0)=\left(1,2\log2,\frac34,1\right),
\]
\[
 (q_1,Z_1,V_1,J_1)=\left(\frac14,\log2,0,0\right).
\]
Consequently
\[
 \Delta(qJ)=-\frac1{32},                                      \tag{3}
\]
and
\[
 \Delta(VZ)=\frac{57}{64}\log2-\frac{63}{128}\log3.          \tag{4}
\]
Moreover
\[
 \Delta(VZ)<\frac18\log2<\frac18,                            \tag{5}
\]
because $2^{14}<3^9$ implies $2^{98}<3^{63}$, which is exactly
$98\log2<63\log3$.

It follows from (3)--(5) that for every $A,\lambda\ge0$, not both zero,
satisfying $A\ge4\lambda$,
\[
 \Delta\{AqJ+\lambda VZ\}<0.                                 \tag{6}
\]
In particular (6) applies to the preceding B12 coefficient
$A=4+2\log2+2/e$, $\lambda=1$.

\subsection*{Why output-only local repairs fail}

For the witness (2), the output and both of its restricted children are the
same constant Boolean function.  Hence all of the following local drops are
zero:
\[
 \Delta H(F)=0,\qquad \Delta\mathbf I(F)=0,
\]
\[
 \operatorname{Inf}_i(F)=0,\qquad
 {\cal E}_{\rm raw}\left((F_+-F_-)/2\right)=0.                \tag{7}
\]
Thus no finite multiple of output entropy, output influence, output-split
mass, or output-split raw entropy can repair (6).  More generally, any
normalized restriction-consistent functional of the actual output alone has
zero drop on this constant-output step.

The conclusion is scoped.  It does not refute FEI: the actual output is
constant and satisfies $H(F)=\mathbf I(F)=0$.  It refutes a proof mechanism
that attempts to repair a representation-dependent coherent potential using
only a local functional of the represented output.

\subsection*{Repaired obligation}

Any surviving overlap proof must retain information beyond the selected
output while remaining invariant under harmless changes of unselected
branches.  Concretely, it must either
\begin{enumerate}
\item choose a canonical physical representation before restriction and use
      a global one-use gauge budget;
\item charge a joint witness supported only where a branch can become active
      along the future physical hierarchy; or
\item prove a cancellation only after summing several shared-coordinate
      steps, rather than by a one-node Bellman inequality.
\end{enumerate}
The old nonnegative two-term potential, its unrestricted activation quotient,
and every output-only local correction are now all excluded.
