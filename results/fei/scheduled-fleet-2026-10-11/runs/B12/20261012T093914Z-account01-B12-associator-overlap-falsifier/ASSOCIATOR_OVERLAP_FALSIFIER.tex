\section*{A character--point-defect obstruction to overlap-only associator payment}

All logarithms are natural.  Put
\[
 \Phi(t)=t^2\log(1/t^2),\qquad \Phi(0)=0,
\]
and for a real function $u$ write
\(\mathcal E(u)=\sum_S\Phi(\widehat u(S))\).

Fix $n\geq1$, let $N=2^n$, fix a coordinate character $\chi$, and choose
$y$ with $\chi(y)=-1$.  Define genuine Boolean functions
\[
 g_- =\chi,\qquad g_+=\chi+2\mathbf1_{\{y\}},\qquad h=-\chi.
\tag{1}
\]
The pointwise formula is Boolean because the value of $\chi$ at $y$ is
$-1$.  With
\[
 a=(g_++g_-)/2=\chi+\mathbf1_{\{y\}},\qquad
 b=(g_+-g_-)/2=\mathbf1_{\{y\}},
\]
the composition is
\[
 F=a+hb=g_+.
\tag{2}
\]

Take the physical block to be the full $n$-cube.  Since $h$ is the single
Fourier character $-\chi$, its zero-frequency part vanishes.  Thus the
block decomposition has source channel $A=a$ and exactly one nonzero
selector channel $C=hb=\mathbf1_{\{y\}}$.  In particular its signed
interference and shared remainder coincide:
\[
 \Omega_N=R_B^{\rm sh}=\mathcal E(F)-\mathcal E(A)-\mathcal E(C).
\tag{3}
\]

\subsection*{Exact formula}

The Fourier coefficients of a point mass have magnitude $1/N$.  At the
character frequency the point-mass sign is negative.  Therefore
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \mathcal E(F)&=\Phi(1-2/N)+(N-1)\Phi(2/N),\\
 \mathcal E(A)&=\Phi(1-1/N)+(N-1)\Phi(1/N),\\
 \mathcal E(C)&=N\Phi(1/N),
\end{aligned}
\]
and hence
\[
 \boxed{\Omega_N=
 \Phi(1-2/N)+(N-1)\Phi(2/N)
 -\Phi(1-1/N)-(2N-1)\Phi(1/N).}
\tag{4}
\]

The selector has one coefficient of squared magnitude one, so its complete
$\alpha$-log envelope is exactly zero:
\[
 \sum_{\alpha\ne0}\widehat h(\alpha)^2
        \log\frac1{\widehat h(\alpha)^2}=0.
\tag{5}
\]
For the collision credit from the preceding B12 theorem, only source
frequencies containing the selected coordinate contribute.  Exactly $N/2$
of the $N$ point-mass frequencies do so, each with squared mass $1/N^2$.
Consequently
\[
 \mathsf O_B={1\over2N},\qquad 2\mathsf O_B={1\over N}.
\tag{6}
\]

\subsection*{Asymptotic separation}

For fixed $c$,
\[
 \Phi(1-c/N)={2c\over N}+O(N^{-2}).
\tag{7}
\]
The remaining terms in (4) are explicit.  Substitution gives
\[
 \boxed{N\Omega_N=4\log N+2-8\log2
       +O\!\left({\log N\over N}\right).}
\tag{8}
\]
Combining (5), (6), and (8),
\[
 {\Omega_N\over
  2\mathsf O_B+
  \sum_{\alpha\ne0}\widehat h(\alpha)^2
       \log(1/\widehat h(\alpha)^2)}
 =N\Omega_N\longrightarrow\infty.
\tag{9}
\]
Thus no finite universal coefficient can pay the positive signed block
associator by the exact overlap refund plus the already-paid selector
$\alpha$-log envelope.  This is a genuine Boolean counterfamily to that
helper inequality, not to FEI.

\subsection*{The missing source budget has the correct scale}

For $b=\mathbf1_{\{y\}}$, every squared Fourier coefficient is $1/N^2$.
Therefore
\[
 \mathbf I(b)={1\over N^2}\sum_{S\subseteq[n]}|S|
              ={n\over2N}.
\tag{10}
\]
Since $\log N=n\log2$, (8) and (10) yield the finite limit
\[
 \boxed{\lim_{n\to\infty}{\Omega_N\over\mathbf I(b)}=8\log2.}
\tag{11}
\]
The family therefore identifies the missing order of payment: the overlap
refund is smaller by a logarithmic factor, while one-use source influence
has exactly the required scale.  Any next universal B12 estimate must retain
source influence or an equivalent terminal derivative-entropy reserve; an
overlap-only repair of the scalar associator cannot work.
