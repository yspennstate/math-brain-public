# Handoff

The next exact target is the sharp triple inequality. For three disjoint affine cosets, stratify Fourier space by the intersections of their three annihilators. On each stratum the coefficient vector is a three-character row, not an arbitrary coherent triple. Retain the exact entropy deficit relative to `sum_j E(s_j)` and pair it with the internal physical-edge cancellation between each pair.

Candidate to prove or falsify:

`Omega <= 8 ln(2) I(d)`

for every triple of pairwise disjoint affine cosets. It has zero violations on 629,590 decompositions through four variables, but the finite census is not proof. A successful character-table lemma should identify where the worst-case scalar factor `3` is forbidden. A falsifier must be an all-parameter genuine Boolean family and must retain the terminal selector density.
