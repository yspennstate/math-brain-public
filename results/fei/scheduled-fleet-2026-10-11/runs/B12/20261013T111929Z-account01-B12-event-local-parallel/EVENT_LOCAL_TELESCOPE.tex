\section*{A cylinder-safe event-local telescope for parallel triples}

All logarithms are natural.  For a Boolean source indicator $s$ define the
selector splice
\[
 d(x,z)=\mathbf1_{\{x=-1\}}s(z),\quad a_0=x+d,\quad F=x+2d,
\]
and its local associator
\[
 \Omega(s)=\mathcal E_{\rm raw}(F)
 -\mathcal E_{\rm raw}(a_0)-\mathcal E_{\rm raw}(d).
\]

Let a restriction tree query physical source coordinates, adaptively if
desired, and let $s_v$ be the reached source at node $v$.  For an internal
node with children $v0,v1$, define the event-local compensated charge
\[
 \Gamma_v=\Omega(s_v)-\frac{\Omega(s_{v0})+\Omega(s_{v1})}{2}. \tag{1}
\]
If $w_v$ is the reach probability, the martingale identity gives
\[
 \sum_{v\ {m internal}}w_v\Gamma_v
 =\Omega(s_{\rm root})-\sum_{\ell\ {m leaf}}w_\ell\Omega(s_\ell).
                                                                    \tag{2}
\]
In particular, an unchanged cylinder restriction has $\Gamma_v=0$.

Assume the tree is complete on the source variables.  At a leaf,
$s_\ell\in\{0,1\}$.  Direct selector calculation gives
\[
 \Omega(0)=0,\qquad \Omega(1)=-2\log2.
\]
If $p=\mathbb E s$, then $\sum_{\ell:s_\ell=1}w_\ell=p$, and therefore
\[
 \sum_vw_v\Gamma_v=\Omega(s)+2p\log2.                \tag{3}
\]

\paragraph{Theorem.}
If $s$ is the union of three distinct parallel affine cosets, then every
complete adaptive restriction tree satisfies
\[
 \boxed{\sum_vw_v\Gamma_v\le8\log2\,I(d).}          \tag{4}
\]
The constant is independent of dimension, codimension, physical orientation,
tree depth, and query order, and is the sharp supremum.

\paragraph{Proof of the root estimate.}
Let the common direction be $V$ of codimension $k\ge2$, and put
$y=2^{-k}$, so $p=3y$.  The three quotient points form an affine two-plane;
if $U$ is its lifted direction, the exact two-shell spectrum gives
\[
 \mathcal E_{\rm raw}(s)=6ky\log2-\frac92y\log3.     \tag{5}
\]
If $m_V=|\{i:e_i\notin V\}|$ and
$m_U=|\{i:e_i\notin U\}|$, exact edge counting gives
\[
 I(s)=\frac y2(m_V+2m_U)\ge\frac y2(3k-4).             \tag{6}
\]
The selector identity is
\[
 \Omega(s)=\mathcal E_{\rm raw}(s)+C(p),               \tag{7}
\]
where
\[
 C(p)=\Phi((1-p)^2)-3\Phi(p^2/4)-\Phi((1-p/2)^2)
      -p(1-p)\log4.
\]
We claim
\[
 C(3y)\le y\left(\frac92\log3-8\log2\right).        \tag{8}
\]
For $k\ge5$, write $f(t)=\Phi((1-t)^2)$.  Since $f'(t)\le2$,
\[
 C(p)\le p-p(1-p)\log4.
\]
Here $p\le3/32$, and
\[
 1-\frac{29}{32}\log4
 <\frac32\log3-\frac83\log2.
\]
The last inequality is elementary: the atanh series gives
$\log3>549/500$ and $\log2<347/500$, so its right-minus-left side exceeds
$1301/24000$.  This proves (8) for $k\ge5$.

For $k=2,3,4$, after multiplying the positive gap
$4\log2\,I(s)-\Omega(s)$ by $32,128,512$, respectively, positivity is
equivalent to the exact integer comparisons
\[
 2^{96}3^9>5^{25},\qquad
 2^{416}3^{45}5^{100}>13^{169},\qquad
 2^{1536}3^{117}13^{676}>29^{841}.
\]
Thus (8) holds for every $k\ge2$.  Combining (5)--(8) yields
\[
 \Omega(s)\le4\log2\,I(s).                            \tag{9}
\]
Finally,
\[
 I(d)=\frac12I(s)+\frac p4,
\]
so (3) and (9) give
\[
 \sum_vw_v\Gamma_v
 \le4\log2\,I(s)+2p\log2
 =8\log2\,I(d).
\]

For coordinate-minimal point triples with $k\to\infty$, the ratio in (4)
tends to $8\log2$, proving sharpness.

\paragraph{Scope.}
Equation (4) repairs the dummy-cylinder repetition only for the compensated
charge (1).  It does not make the false unrenormalized sum
$\sum_vw_v\Omega(s_v)$ finite.  To use (4) in full FEI, the global entropy
decomposition must identify its coherent remainder with $\Gamma_v$ (plus
already paid endpoint terms), or bound the difference by a separate
single-use reserve.
