\section*{Affine-coset character flips admit a sharp source-influence payment}

All logarithms are natural.  For a real function $u$ on a Boolean cube put
\[
  {\cal E}(u)=\sum_S \Phi(\widehat u(S)^2),\qquad
  \Phi(t)=t\log(1/t),\quad \Phi(0)=0,
\]
and $I(u)=\sum_S |S|\widehat u(S)^2$.

\subsection*{Local theorem}
Let $D$ be a nonempty affine subspace of $\mathbb F_2^n$ of codimension
$k\geq 1$, let $\chi_r$ be a nonconstant character, and define the genuine
Boolean branches
\[
 g_- = \chi_r,\qquad g_+=\chi_r(1-2\mathbf 1_D).
\]
Thus
\[
 a={g_++g_-\over2}=\chi_r(1-\mathbf 1_D),\qquad
 b={g_+-g_-\over2}=-\chi_r\mathbf 1_D.
\]
Use the shared physical selector $h=-\chi_r$ and put $F=a+hb$.  Define the
signed full-block associator
\[
 \Omega(D,r)={\cal E}(F)-{\cal E}(a)-{\cal E}(hb).
\]
Then
\[
 \boxed{\ \Omega(D,r)\leq 8\log 2\,I(b)\ } .                    \tag{1}
\]
More precisely, exactly one of the following holds.

\begin{enumerate}
\item If $\chi_r$ is nonconstant on $D$, then $\Omega(D,r)=0$.
\item If $\chi_r=+1$ on $D$, then $F=\chi_r$ and $\Omega(D,r)\leq0$.
\item If $\chi_r=-1$ on $D$, write $p=2^{-k}$ and $L=\log(1/p)=k\log2$.
Then
\[
 \Omega_k=\Phi((1-2p)^2)-\Phi((1-p)^2)
 +(p^{-1}-1)\Phi(4p^2)-(2p^{-1}-1)\Phi(p^2),                 \tag{2}
\]
and
\[
 \Omega_k\leq 4pL\leq8\log2\,I(b).                          \tag{3}
\]
\end{enumerate}

The constant in (1) is sharp for this class: for coordinate subcubes with
codimension $k$ and $\chi_r=-1$ on $D$,
\[
 {\Omega_k\over I(b)}\longrightarrow 8\log2.                \tag{4}
\]

\subsection*{Proof}
Let $W$ be the annihilator of the direction space of $D$.  It is a
$k$-dimensional linear space.  The spectrum of $\mathbf1_D$ is supported on
$W$, is flat there, and every nonzero coefficient has magnitude $p$.
Multiplication by $\chi_r$ translates this support to $r+W$.

If $r\notin W$, the character varies on $D$.  The set
$D_-=D\cap\{\chi_r=-1\}$ has codimension $k+1$ and annihilator
$W'=\langle W,r\rangle$.  Now $a=\chi_r(1-\mathbf1_D)$ has one Fourier
coefficient of magnitude $1-p$ and $2^k-1$ coefficients of magnitude $p$.
Also
\[
 F=\chi_r+2\mathbf1_{D_-}
\]
has one coefficient of magnitude $1-p$ and $2^{k+1}-1$ coefficients of
magnitude $p$.  Finally $hb=\mathbf1_D$ has $2^k$ coefficients of magnitude
$p$.  Hence the entropy contributions cancel coefficient-for-coefficient:
$\Omega(D,r)=0$.

If $r\in W$, the character is constant on $D$.  In the $+1$ case,
$F=\chi_r$, so nonnegativity of ${\cal E}(a)$ and ${\cal E}(hb)$ gives
$\Omega\leq0$.  In the $-1$ case the flat spectra give (2) directly.
Writing
\[
 A(p)=\Phi((1-2p)^2)-\Phi((1-p)^2),
\]
elementary simplification of (2) yields
\[
 \Omega_k=A(p)+(4p-6p^2)L-8p(1-p)\log2.                     \tag{5}
\]
For $f(t)=\Phi((1-t)^2)$,
\[
 f'(t)=2(1-t)(1+2\log(1-t))\leq2,
\]
and therefore $A(p)=f(2p)-f(p)\leq2p$.  Since $p\leq1/2$,
$2p\leq8p(1-p)\log2$.  Equation (5) now gives
$\Omega_k\leq(4p-6p^2)L\leq4pL$.

Let $m$ be the number of physical coordinates used by $W$.  Each used
coordinate occurs in exactly half of the elements of $W$, so
\[
 I(b)=p^2\sum_{S\in W}|S|={pm\over2}\geq{pk\over2}.
\]
This proves (3).  Finally $A(p)=2p+O(p^2)$ in (5), while
$I(b)=pk/2$ for a coordinate $k$-subspace.  Division proves (4).

\subsection*{Adaptive hierarchy corollary}
Consider an adaptive nonreusing physical hierarchy with actual reach weights
$w_v$.  Suppose the restricted local branch pair at every queried node is an
affine-coset character flip of the form above, and the local branch-difference
sources $b_v$ are restrictions of one physical source $b$.  Then
\[
 \sum_v w_v\Omega_v
 \leq 8\log2\sum_v w_v I_{B_v}(b_v)
 \leq 8\log2\,I(b).                                         \tag{6}
\]
The second inequality is the exact nonreuse derivative-energy ledger: for
each physical coordinate, reached nodes that query it select disjoint pieces
of the source derivative energy.  Thus (6) keeps changing activation weights
and pays every positive associator once, with no depth, address-count,
codimension, or correlation-gap factor.

This is a proper-class theorem, not full FEI.  Outside affine-coset character
flips the complete signed coherent remainder is still unpaid.
