\section*{Exact shared-data collision interface and a packetized addressing control}

All entropies are in bits.  Let $x\in\{-1,1\}^a$ be a fresh uniform
selector, let $N=2^a$, and let $g_b$ be Boolean functions on a common data
cube.  Define $F(x,y)=g_x(y)$.  For each data Fourier set $T$, put
\[
R_T=\frac1N\sum_b\widehat g_b(T)^2.
\]
When $R_T>0$, define
\[
r_T(b)=\frac{\widehat g_b(T)^2}{NR_T},\qquad
u_T(b)=\frac{\widehat g_b(T)}{\sqrt{NR_T}},
\]
and let $\nu_T$ be the squared-coordinate law of the normalized Walsh
transform of $u_T$:
\[
\nu_T(S)=\left(\frac1{\sqrt N}\sum_b\chi_S(b)u_T(b)\right)^2.
\]
Zero-mass $T$ are omitted.  Parseval shows that $r_T$ and $\nu_T$ are
probability laws and that
\[
\widehat F(S,T)^2=R_T\nu_T(S).
\]
The physical joint law
\[
\Pr(B=b,T)=N^{-1}\widehat g_b(T)^2
\]
has entropy $a+N^{-1}\sum_bH(g_b)$, marginal $R$, and conditional law
$r_T$.  Applying the chain rule once to this law and once to the spectral
law of $F$ gives the exact collision identity
\[
H(F)=a+\frac1N\sum_bH(g_b)
 +\sum_{T:R_T>0}R_T\bigl[H(\nu_T)-H(r_T)\bigr].
\]
Equivalently, if
\[
B_W=\sum_T R_TH(\nu_T),\qquad
M_W=a-\sum_T R_TH(r_T),
\]
then
\[
H(F)-\frac1N\sum_bH(g_b)=B_W+M_W.
\]
The source proof also gives
\[
0\le M_W\le\frac{2}{\ln2}I_{\rm sel}(F).
\]
This is the frozen B04--B06 interface.  It includes $T=\varnothing$ and
does not replace a coherent sum by an incoherent mixture.

\subsection*{Shared-address packet family}

Let $t,k\ge1$, $N=2^t$, and introduce mutually distinct fair data signs
$y_{r,b}$ for $1\le r\le k$ and $b\in\{-1,1\}^t$.  Define
\[
F_{t,k}(x,y)=\prod_{r=1}^k y_{r,x}.
\]
The branch $g_b=\prod_r y_{r,b}$ has one Fourier atom, at the data set
$D_b=\{(r,b):1\le r\le k\}$.  For $T=D_b$,
\[
R_T=N^{-1},\qquad r_T=\delta_b,\qquad
\nu_T=\operatorname{Unif}(2^{[t]}).
\]
Therefore
\[
B_W=t,\qquad M_W=t,
\]
and the exact squared Fourier law is
\[
\Pr\{(S,T)=(S,D_b)\}=N^{-2}
\quad(b\in\{-1,1\}^t,\ S\subseteq[t]).
\]
It follows that
\[
H(F_{t,k})=2t,qquad I(F_{t,k})=\frac t2+k.
\]

This law supplies a lossless joint packet code.  First encode the packet
label $B=b$ (cost $t$ bits), then the selector Fourier subset $S$ given the
packet (cost $t$ bits); the data set $D_b$ is deterministic within the
packet.  The corresponding expected rank is
\[
\mathbb E|S|+|D_B|=t/2+k.
\]
Every selector coordinate occurs in all $N$ packet descriptions, so the
unweighted worst-case read is $N$.  With the actual packet weights $1/N$,
its spectral congestion is only
\[
\sum_b\frac1N\Pr(i\in S\mid B=b)=\frac12.
\]
Every data coordinate has congestion $1/N$.  Thus the cover has unbounded
worst-case read but uniformly bounded fractional spectral congestion, with
all label and rank costs included.

By contrast, coordinatewise marginal-entropy accounting gives
\[
\sum_i h_2(I_i)=t+kN h_2(N^{-1}),
\]
because selector influences are $1/2$ and data-coordinate influences are
$1/N$.  Taking $k=t$ yields
\[
\frac{\sum_i h_2(I_i)}{I(F_{t,t})}\longrightarrow\infty,
\qquad
\frac{H(F_{t,t})}{I(F_{t,t})}=\frac43.
\]
The divergence is therefore repeated address-label payment, not FEI
growth.  The joint packet code charges the label exactly once.

\subsection*{Boundary controls}

If every branch is identical, $r_T$ is uniform while $\nu_T$ is a point
mass, so $B_W=M_W=0$ and the complete selector cost cancels.  Multiplying
the common branch by a selector character merely moves that point mass and
has the same entropy cancellation.  At the opposite boundary, the accepted
bent-splice source has one selector bit with
\[
I_{\rm sel}=2^{-m},\qquad
\Delta_{\rm sel}H/I_{\rm sel}\ge m-1.
\]
Thus no selector-only increment bound follows from this packetization.
The unrestricted shared signed-dictator choice $g_b(y)=h(b)y$ makes the
remaining packet entropy exactly $H(h)$ and selector influence exactly
$I(h)$; controlling it universally is FEI itself.  The packet theorem is a
constructive repair for the displayed family, not a universal existence
theorem.
