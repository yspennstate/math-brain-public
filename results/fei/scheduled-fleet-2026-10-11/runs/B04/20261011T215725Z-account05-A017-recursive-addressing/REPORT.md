## complete derivation or exact first unsupported line

The full proof is in `RECURSIVE_ADDRESSING.tex`. Its essential reconstruction is as follows. On each affine fiber x_z+K, the phase is a quadratic sign with the same polar rank k. A quadratic sign on a D-dimensional binary space has Fourier squared magnitudes either zero or 2^(-k): its autocorrelation vanishes off the radical of its polar form, and on the radical the phase difference is a linear character. Squared Fourier coefficients are the Walsh transform of that autocorrelation. The rank of an alternating form is even, by successively splitting nondegenerate two-dimensional pairs.

For a reachable label z, the signed indicator a_z(x)=1_(Mx=z)(-1)^q(x) has Fourier coefficients 2^(-r) times those fiber coefficients, up to signs. Ambient frequency restriction to K has 2^r lifts. Thus a_z has 2^(r+k) nonzero coefficients of squared magnitude 2^(-2r-k). There are 2^r reachable labels, and their data frequencies are distinct singleton coordinates y_z. This proves the exact flat spectrum and H=2r+k. History collisions are resolved in this signed indicator before squaring; there are no unaccounted cross-label collisions.

Flipping an active-address control changes the terminal data label, so its output disagreement is 1/2 even when the phase changes simultaneously. Flipping an inactive-address control changes only the phase, whose derivative is l_i+sum_j Q_ij x_j: influence 1/2 if nonconstant, otherwise l_i. Flipping a reachable y_z is pivotal with probability 2^(-r); the total data influence is one. These give the exact physical influence ledger.

Let V span the c active address directions and the b remaining directions with nonzero polar row. Both address map and polar form vanish on all other coordinate directions; M restricted to V retains rank r. The polar rank on ker M is therefore at most c+b-r. Hence 2r+k<=r+c+b<=2(c+b)=4(I-1-d). This uses no universal FEI premise and includes n=0, a=0, rank zero, and linear/constant phases.

At stage t, recompute these five statistics from M_t and q_t, including the changed kernel. Exact differences are Delta H=2 Delta r+Delta k and Delta I=(Delta c+Delta b)/2+Delta d. For n=a=m, cyclically adding x_j to state coordinate j makes M return to zero after every two cycles. With q=0, H=2t and I=1+t/2 on the first cycle, then H=2(2m-t) and I=1+(2m-t)/2 on the second. The tower may have arbitrarily many levels while the final function is y_0 at every even cycle. Fresh-stage accounting is false for this program.
