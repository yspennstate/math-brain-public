\title{A055 stage 2: rare-activation block limits}\maketitle
On a uniform cube let $g$ be a nonconstant sign, $h=(1-g)/2$,
$c=1-\mathbb Eg>0$, $p=2^{-r}$, and $F_r=1+\mathbf1_{y=0}(g-1)$.
All entropies are in bits. Write $T(g)=\mathcal F(g-1)$ for raw Fourier
entropy. For $a(d)=-(1-d)^2\log_2(1-d)^2+d^2\log_2d^2$,
\[
 H(F_r)=pT(g)+4rpc+a(pc),\qquad I(F_r)=p[I(g)+rc].
\]
Every physical block consists of a data subset $B$ and $k$ controls, and
\[
 L_{B,k}=H(F_r)-2^{-k}\mathbb E_bH(F_{r-k}(g_b)),\quad
 I_{B,k}=p[I_B(g)+kc].
\]
The full block is excluded. Let $\Lambda_B(h)$ be the raw-indicator
entropy decrement and $\mathcal E_B(h)=I_B(g)/4$. Then
\[
 \lim_{r\to\infty}\phi(F_r)=
 \min\left(4,\min_{B:\,I_B(g)>0}
 \frac{\Lambda_B(h)}{\mathcal E_B(h)}\right).
\]
The inner $B$ may be all data coordinates. A uniform error is
$2\delta_{\lfloor r/2\rfloor}/d_0+(30+2\delta_r)/(rc)$,
where $\delta_t=(8t+19)2^{-t}$ and
$d_0=\min(c,\min_{i:\operatorname{Inf}_i(g)>0}\operatorname{Inf}_i(g))$.

For any control singleton, $d=pc$ and
\[
 B_i=2d-2d^2+\Phi((1-d)^2,d^2),\quad
 \beta_i=\min(d,1-d),\quad
 L_i=4d+a(d)-\tfrac12a(2d).
\]
Thus actual singleton price tends to $4$, while the R2 upper price tends
to $2+2/\ln2$.

For the C013 family $g_m=z f^{\otimes m}$,
$r_m=\lceil2\log_2(m+1)\rceil$, let $v_m=(\mathbb Ef)^{2m}$.
The balancing singleton has $\beta_z=p_mv_m$ and
\[
 B_z=(p_m-p_m^2)\Phi(1,v_m)+\Phi((1-p_m)^2,p_m^2v_m).
\]
It follows that $\phi(F_m)\to0$ although
$H(F_m)/I(F_m)\to H(f)/I(f)$. If $H(f)>CI(f)$ for fixed $C>0$,
the R2 necessary criticality inequality eventually fails at $z$.
This is a scoped obstruction to criticality-preserving embeddings,
not a universal paid selection theorem. Complete proofs and conventions
are in DERIVATION.md.
