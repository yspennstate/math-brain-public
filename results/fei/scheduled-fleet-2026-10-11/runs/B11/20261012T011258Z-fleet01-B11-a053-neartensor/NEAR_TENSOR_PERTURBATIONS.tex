\section*{Critical $H/I$ sources are separated from low-ratio tensor factors}

All entropies use base two.  Let
$f,T:\{-1,1\}^N\to\{-1,1\}$ disagree on a set $E$ of density
$\varepsilon$, and put $t=2\sqrt\varepsilon$.  Write
$a_S=\widehat T(S)$ and $d_S=\widehat{(f-T)}(S)$.  The signed Fourier
perturbation is
\[
 \widehat f(S)=a_S+d_S,
 \qquad
 \widehat f(S)^2-\widehat T(S)^2
 =2a_Sd_S+d_S^2.                                      \tag{1}
\]
Thus the cross term is retained before absolute values are taken.  Parseval
gives $\sum_Sd_S^2=4\varepsilon$, and hence
\[
 \sum_S\left|\widehat f(S)^2-\widehat T(S)^2\right|
 \le \|d\|_2\|2a+d\|_2\le4\sqrt\varepsilon.          \tag{2}
\]
The total variation between the two spectral laws is therefore at most $t$.

For a physical block $A$ of size $a$, define
\[
 \Delta_AH(f)=H(f)-\mathbb E_zH(f_{A=z}),\qquad
 I_A(f)=\sum_{i\in A}I_i(f).
\]
For an alphabet of size $M$, use the sharp continuity modulus
\[
 \omega_M(s)=
 \begin{cases}
 h_2(s)+s\log_2(M-1),&0\le s\le1-M^{-1},\\
 \log_2M,&1-M^{-1}\le s\le1.
 \end{cases}
\]
It is concave and nondecreasing.  The parent bound and Jensen over the
restriction branches therefore yield
\[
 |\Delta_AH(f)-\Delta_AH(T)|
 \le 2h_2(t)+(2N-a)t.                                  \tag{3}
\]
Indeed the branch disagreement densities $\varepsilon_z$ average to
$\varepsilon$, their spectral total variations are at most
$\min\{2\sqrt{\varepsilon_z},1\}$, and
$\mathbb E_z\min\{2\sqrt{\varepsilon_z},1\}\le t$.
Apply Jensen to $\omega_{2^{N-a}}$ (with the zero-dimensional child case
interpreted as zero entropy) and use $\log_2(2^d-1)\le d$.

For each coordinate, the two pivotality indicators can differ only when one
endpoint of the corresponding edge lies in $E$.  Consequently
\[
 |I_i(f)-I_i(T)|\le2\varepsilon,
 \qquad |I_A(f)-I_A(T)|\le2a\varepsilon.               \tag{4}
\]

\begin{theorem}[critical near-tensor separation]
Let $f$ be descendant-critical: every nonconstant physical descendant $g$
satisfies $H(g)/I(g)\le R:=H(f)/I(f)$.  Suppose
\[
 T(x_A,x_{A^c})=u(x_A)h(x_{A^c})
\]
on disjoint physical blocks, where $u$ is nonconstant.  If
$\Pr[f\ne T]=\varepsilon\le1/16$, then
\[
 \boxed{
 R\bigl(I(u)-2a\varepsilon\bigr)
 \le H(u)+2h_2(t)+(2N-a)t.}                            \tag{5}
\]
In particular, when $2a\varepsilon<I(u)$,
\[
 R\le
 \frac{H(u)+2h_2(2\sqrt\varepsilon)
 +(2N-a)2\sqrt\varepsilon}
 {I(u)-2a\varepsilon}.                                 \tag{6}
\]
Thus a high-ratio critical source cannot be arbitrarily close to a tensor
carrying a fixed lower-ratio factor.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Fixing all coordinates of $A$ produces physical descendants.  Criticality and
the exact block influence identity give
\[
 \mathbb E_zH(f_{A=z})\le R\mathbb E_zI(f_{A=z}),
 \qquad
 \Delta_AH(f)\ge RI_A(f).                              \tag{7}
\]
For the tensor, entropy and influence add exactly, so
$\Delta_AH(T)=H(u)$ and $I_A(T)=I(u)$.  Insert (3)--(4) into (7) to obtain
(5), and rearrange when the denominator is positive.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{A concrete uniform-cube obstruction.}
Let $\phi$ be the six-bit one-point defect, equal to $-1$ at one input and
$+1$ elsewhere.  Its Fourier coefficients have magnitudes $31/32$ at the
empty set and $1/32$ at every nonempty set.  Hence
\[
 I(\phi)=\frac3{16},\qquad
 H(\phi)=\frac{961}{1024}\log_2\frac{1024}{961}
          +\frac{630}{1024}.                            \tag{8}
\]
Using $\log(1+x)\le x$ and $\ln2>2/3$,
\[
 H(\phi)<\frac{1449}{2048},
 \qquad 4I(\phi)-H(\phi)>\frac{87}{2048}.             \tag{9}
\]

If $R(f)>4$ and $f$ is descendant-critical, then applying (7) with the fixed
constant $4$ and comparing with $T=\phi h$ shows that
\[
 2h_2(t)+(2N-6)t+48\varepsilon>\frac{87}{2048}.        \tag{10}
\]
For every $N\ge6$, (10) is violated whenever
\[
 \varepsilon\le\frac1{2^{18}N^2}.                     \tag{11}
\]
To see this, use
$h_2(s)\le s(\log_2(1/s)+1/\ln2)$,
$\log_2(256N)+1/\ln2\le3N$, and $t\le1/(256N)$.
The three terms in (10) are then strictly below
$3/128$, $1/128$, and $1/1024$, whose sum is
$66/2048<87/2048$.

Therefore every descendant-critical Boolean source with $H/I>4$ is at
Hamming distance more than $1/(2^{18}N^2)$ from every tensor
$\phi(x_A)h(x_{A^c})$ carrying the six-bit point-defect factor.

\paragraph{One-point surgery and quantitative primitivity.}
Let $T_m=\phi^{\otimes m}$ on $N=6m$ bits and flip one truth-table value to
obtain $G_m$.  The standard multiplicative-rectangle certificate shows that
$G_m$ is tensor-prime for every $m\ge3$.  Across a partition separating one
whole six-bit factor, however, the normalized rectangle-defect probability is
positive but at most $4\cdot2^{-N}$: a tensor rectangle can change sign only
if one of its four corners is the surgically changed input.  Thus qualitative
primitivity can be created by an exponentially small interface.

For $m\ge5$, $2^{-6m}\le1/(2^{18}(6m)^2)$.  The separation theorem therefore
proves that $G_m$ is not descendant-critical with ordinary Fourier
$H/I>4$.  This is not a counterexample to FEI; it is a quantitative exclusion
of a near-tensor obstruction class.
