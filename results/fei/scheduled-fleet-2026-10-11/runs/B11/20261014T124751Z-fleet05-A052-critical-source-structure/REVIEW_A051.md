Reconstruction. For each fixed dimension there are finitely many Boolean truth tables, including constants. Thus b_K(n)=max(H-KI) exists for every real K. If a positive deficit exists, well-order its essential dimension and take a full-cube maximal-deficit table at the least such dimension. An inessential maximizer would compress to a smaller positive-deficit table, impossible. Every proper physical child and smaller Boolean phase has deficit at most zero; all same-dimensional vertex edits have nonpositive deficit increment. Constants show each lower-dimensional cap is exactly zero. At an epsilon maximizer the edit increment is at most epsilon. The exact physical telescope gives L_A-KI_A >= D_K(f)-b_K(n-|A|). Tensor additivity gives superadditivity of b; none of this needs an optimizer over all dimensions or preservation of extremality by a wrapper.

Independently differentiate k(t)=-t^2 log2(t^2)+3t^2/ln2: k''(t)=-2 ln(t^2)/ln2 >=0 on [-1,1] away from zero. Its derivative tends to zero from both sides at zero. The supporting tangent inequality therefore holds even for zero Fourier coefficients. For the Fourier edit increment u, Parseval gives sum a u=-2 delta and sum u^2=4 delta. Expanding k's tangent and subtracting its quadratic correction gives Delta H >=4 E[1_E fG]-(8/ln2)delta. This is universal, not a criticality assumption. Directly counting boundary edges gives Delta I=(2/N)sum_boundary f(x)f(y). Combining the two bounds with Delta D<=epsilon yields A051 V3 with kappa=2/ln2 and error N epsilon/4; the singleton boundary sum n-2p yields V4. Thus zero-error cuts require exact finite maximization. Three-bit parity at K=3 fails V4 and is not a maximizer, as required.

Independent finite implementation: code/check_flows.py computes integer Walsh transforms and separately character sums, direct physical edge counts, exact prime-log deficits, and outward rational logarithm intervals. It checks the stronger 8/ln2 entropy-edit bound, rather than merely the source's relaxed constant 12, for all 65,808 ordered edits of all 276 tables of dimensions 1--3. It checks exact influence edits, finds every maximal-deficit table at K=1,2,3,4, and tests strong V3 there. The source implementation and its assertion totals are not reused. Main output reports 148,933 assertions and PASS. Our code/phase_library.py and code/exact_library.py are reused from our own earlier stage; the signed-cut, entropy-edit and flow checks are new. Their independence is representation and implementation independence, not a claim that every logarithm helper was newly written.

The accepted theorem supplies simultaneous signed-cut inequalities and lower-dimensional physical reserves. It does not give a pivotal payment for agreeing-edge flow, does not say these necessary inequalities characterize maxima, and does not prove FEI. Our majority guard demonstrates the last distinction without contradicting A051. The Bregman refinement in MONOCHROMATIC_FLOW_PROOF.md is a downstream application, not a repair to the reviewed source.
