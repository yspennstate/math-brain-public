Fix 0<eta<=1 and 0<=c<infinity. Let e_N be real numbers with |e_N|/N tending to zero. If every balanced genuine Boolean h on every N-dimensional finite uniform cube satisfies M(h)+(1+eta)U(h)<=c I(h)+e_N, then every genuine Boolean f satisfies M(f)+(1+eta)U(f)<=c I(f), hence G(f)<=(c+2)I(f)+(1-eta)U(f) and H(f)<=(2+2(c+2)/eta)I(f). Conversely, universal exact SMR implies the balanced statement with e_N=0.
