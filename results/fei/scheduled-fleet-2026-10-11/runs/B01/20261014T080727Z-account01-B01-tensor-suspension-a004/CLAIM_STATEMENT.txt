For L_{c,eta}(f)=M(f)+(1+eta)U(f)-cI(f), let q be the four-bit Boolean sign with LSB-first +1 mask 56242, let F=A(q), let Q=F^{tensor 4} on disjoint physical blocks, and let W=A(Q) with one fresh fair selector.  For every eta>=0, the threshold c_W(eta) for which L_{c,eta}(W)<=0 is c_W(eta)=alpha_4+beta_4 eta, with alpha_4=1.770873264041823... and beta_4=1.451867015896827....  Exact rational-log interval certificates prove c_W(eta)>c_F(eta) for every eta>=0.  At eta=1/4, c_W=2.133840018016030...>2.1338.  Hence any universal SMR(c,eta) theorem must have c>=c_W(eta); at eta=1/4 it must have c>2.1338.  This is a lower bound on the signed-mismatch sufficient route, not a refutation of GU or FEI.
