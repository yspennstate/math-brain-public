B01-A002-FIXED-BIAS-GU-REDUCTION-v1

Fix finite a>=0 and 0<=b<1. Universal GU, G(f)<=aI(f)+bU(f) for every genuine Boolean f on every finite uniform cube, is equivalent to the following fixed-bias statement: there exists a real sequence e_N with |e_N|/N->0 such that every Boolean F on every N-cube with E F=1/2 satisfies G(F)<=aI(F)+bU(F)+e_N. The forward direction takes e_N=0. For the converse, apply the fixed-bias statement to T(q^{tensor t}), where T(q)(z,y,x)=1 for z=+1 and yq(x) for z=-1. Then E T=1/2, U(T(q))=1+U(q)/2, I(T(q))=1+I(q)/2, and G(T(q))=G(q)/2+kappa(Eq) with bounded kappa. Divide by t and let t tend to infinity. This is a hardness-preserving reduction, not a proof of an admissible pair.
