B01-A004-REUSED-CONE-WEDGE-v1

For the genuine four-bit Boolean sign q with LSB-first truth-table mask
10049, coordinate x=3, and F=A(chi_x q), define
L_{c,eta}(h)=M(h)+(1+eta)U(h)-cI(h). Then for every eta>=0,
L_{c,eta}(q)<=0 exactly when c>=23/16+eta, whereas
L_{c,eta}(F)<=0 exactly when
c>=1.505684707963186...+1.336806053967517... eta. The latter threshold
strictly exceeds the former for every eta>=0, with the exact positive
coefficient certificates given in PROOF.md. Thus reused-coordinate
zero-cone preservation fails for every eta>=0 on the nonempty interval
c_q(eta)<=c<c_F(eta), and universal SMR(c,eta) requires c>=c_F(eta).
This does not refute GU or FEI.
