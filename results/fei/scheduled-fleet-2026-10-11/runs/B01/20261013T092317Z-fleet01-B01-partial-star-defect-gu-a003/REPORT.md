## Result

I proved a strict extension of the full-star point-defect theorem. For every
\(R\subseteq[n]\), \(1\le |R|\le n\), let
\[
F_{n,R}(z,x)=(-1)^{z\sum_{j\in R}x_j}(1-2\mathbf1_{\{x=0\}}).
\]
On the restriction-closed class generated by every \(F_{n,R}\), every
parity-shifted defect \(\chi_Rd_n\), affine-quadratic phases, and disjoint
products of these blocks,
\[
\boxed{G(f)\le14I(f),\qquad U(f)\le14I(f),\qquad H(f)\le30I(f).}
\]
Equivalently GU' holds with \((a,b)=(14,0)\), uniformly in dimension, support
size \(|R|\), number \(n-|R|\) of inactive defect coordinates, quadratic
rank, and total dimension.

## New mechanism

The prior full-star proof used \(U=O(1)\) and influence \(1/2\) on every
coordinate. That fails here: an inactive coordinate has influence
\(2^{1-n}\), while the parent still has \(U=\Theta(1)\).

For an inactive coordinate, I paired the parent Fourier sets \(C\) and
\(C\cup\{y\}\) above each child set. Generic pairs cost exactly one dust mass
\(4^{1-n}\). Subsets of the active support have an explicit signed charge
\[
4^{1-n}\left[h+4+\log_2\frac{P4^{1-n}}{(P')^2}\right],
\qquad P'=2P-\tfrac12,
\]
whose bracket is at most 4. The two exceptional atoms are controlled by
uniform derivative bounds. Altogether the inactive-coordinate loss is less
than \(27\cdot2^{-n}<14\operatorname{Inf}_y\). The same pairing treats every
parity-shifted defect boundary. This is an exact retained rare-logarithm
refund, rather than a parent-\(U\)-per-coordinate charge.

The complete proof is in `PARTIAL_STAR_DEFECT_GU.md`.

## Exact movement

**Previous:** the class covered only the full star \(R=[n]\), where all data
coordinates had influence \(1/2\), with \(G\le8I\), \(U\le8I\), and
\(H\le18I\).

**New:** the class permits arbitrary active support \(R\), including an
unbounded number of zero columns/inactive variables with exponentially small
influence. The constant remains dimension independent, at the explicit
values 14 and 30. This removes the full-star/all-coordinates-active hypothesis
and replaces its crude payment by a fiberwise signed payment.

**Still unpaid:** one quadratic centre is essential. With two or more
overlapping centres, the same dust fiber can receive incompatible upper-set
caps, and the present pairing no longer proves single-use payment. No
dimension-independent GU' inequality is proved for arbitrary Boolean
functions, so full FEI remains OPEN.
