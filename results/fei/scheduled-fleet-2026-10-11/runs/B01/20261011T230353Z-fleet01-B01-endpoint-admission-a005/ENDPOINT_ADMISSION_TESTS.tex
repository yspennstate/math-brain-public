\section*{A005 endpoint-admission tests}

Let
\[
\mathcal C_\eta=\left\{F:
I(F)\le\eta,\quad \max_i I_i(F)/I(F)\le\eta,\quad
\Pr(F=-1)\le\eta,\quad \widehat F(S)\ne0\ \forall S\right\}.
\]
For a nonconstant seed $g$ on $n$ variables, let $u_m$ differ at at
most one truth-table point from $g^{\otimes m}$ and have odd negative
count.  Put
\[
r_m=\lceil\sqrt m\rceil,\quad p_m=2^{-r_m},\quad
F_m=1+A_{r_m}(u_m-1).
\]
Then $F_m\in\mathcal C_\eta$ for all sufficiently large $m$, and
\[
\frac{H(F_m)}{I(F_m)}\to\frac{H(g)}{I(g)}.
\]

For the downward functional
\[
L(f)=\sum_Ap_A\sum_{i\in A}
\log_2\left(1+\frac{p_{A\setminus i}}{p_A}\right),
\qquad p_A=\widehat f(A)^2,
\]
write $\ell(x,y)=x\log_2(1+y/x)$.  If two $N$-variable Boolean
functions differ at one truth-table point, their squared Fourier laws
have total variation at most $\tau_N=2^{1-N/2}$.  Every nonzero Boolean
Fourier mass is at least $2^{2-2N}$, whence
\[
|L(f)-L(u)|\le4N^2\tau_N=8N^2 2^{-N/2}.
\]
Since $L$ is additive under disjoint products,
\[
L(u_m)=mL(g)+o(1).
\]

For a full-support core with mean $\mu$, put $q=1-\mu$, $p=2^{-r}$,
and $c=1-pq$.  Exact Fourier partition gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
L(1+A_r(g-1))={}&p\sum_{|S|\ge2}a_S^2
\sum_{i\in S}\log_2\left(1+\frac{a_{S\setminus i}^2}{a_S^2}\right)\\
&+\sum_{|S|=1}\left[\ell(p^2a_S^2,c^2)
+(2^r-1)\ell(p^2a_S^2,p^2q^2)\right]\\
&+\frac{rp}{2}(1-\mu^2)
+\left(\frac{rp}{2}-rp^2\right)q^2+r\ell(p^2q^2,c^2).
\end{aligned}
\]
The singleton mass of $u_m$ is $o(1)$, and $r_m/m\to0$, so
\[
L(F_m)=p_m mL(g)+o(p_m m),\qquad
\frac{L(F_m)}{I(F_m)}\to\frac{L(g)}{I(g)}.
\]
Thus, for every $\eta>0$,
\[
\sup_{F\in\mathcal C_\eta}\frac{L(F)}{I(F)}=C_L.
\]

In contrast, an activation control $z$ satisfies
\[
\begin{aligned}
\Delta_zH={}&2p(1-\mu^2)+\phi(p^2q^2)+2pq^2-4p^2q^2\\
&+\phi((1-pq)^2)-\frac12\phi((1-2pq)^2),
\end{aligned}
\]
while $I_z=pq$.  Along the embedding, $\mu(u_m)\to0$ and $p_m\to0$,
so
\[
\frac{\Delta_zH(F_m)}{I_z(F_m)}\to4.
\]
Hence this construction does not preserve the seed's existential
restriction-step statistic.  No conclusion about a different embedding
or about the truth of the universal STEP endpoint follows.
