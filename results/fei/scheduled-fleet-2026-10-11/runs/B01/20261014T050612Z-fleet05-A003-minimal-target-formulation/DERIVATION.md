    g_d(s,y)=-1+1_(y=0)(1+s).

Its actual s children are b_d and c_d. Let Q_d=B_s(g_d), and retain the ACTUAL price-four terminal surplus S4=[4I(b_d)-H(b_d)]/2. With eta(t)=-t log2(t), zero convention, direct Fourier expansion gives

## 2. A sharp dimension-independent query allowance

For a,b>=0, Phi(a,b)<=b log2(e(a+b)/b), by ln(1+t)<=t. Since (1-delta)^2+delta^2<=1,

 Phi((1-delta)^2,delta^2)<=delta^2(2d+ell).

Also -ln(1-2delta)<=2delta/(1-2delta) for delta<1/2 implies eta((1-2delta)^2)/2<=2ell delta(1-2delta). The endpoint delta=1/2 satisfies this by continuity. Subtracting (1) therefore gives

Thus EVERY d>=1 satisfies Q_d<=S4+A0 delta at A0=2/ln2-2. This constant is optimal as an all-dimensional additive allowance: from (1), Phi((1-delta)^2,delta^2)/delta->0, eta((1-2delta)^2)/(2delta)->2ell and d delta->0, so

    (Q_d-S4)/delta -> 2ell-2=A0.                   (3)

All limits are elementary; they do not extrapolate finite checks. In particular, unlike A096's discarded quotient loss, this actual retained shadow bill is paid by the SAME legitimate price-four terminal bank with a finite absolute query allowance.
