\section*{A displacement-owned refund for two coherent surgeries}

All logarithms are base two.  Let $g:\{0,1\}^k\to\{-1,1\}$ be bent,
put $\delta=2^{-k}$, and let $x\ne y$.  Write
$R=\operatorname{supp}(x\oplus y)$, $r=|R|\ge1$, and
$\tau=g(x)g(y)$.  Flipping $g$ at $x$ and then at $y$ changes the Walsh
coefficient indexed by $T$ by
\[
 e_x(T)=-2g(x)\delta\chi_T(x),\qquad
 e_y(T)=-2g(y)\delta\chi_T(y).
\tag{1}
\]
For an arbitrary real spectral vector $e$, define
\[
 D_e(S)=\sum_{T\supseteq S}e(T)^2,
 \qquad
 \mathcal U(e)=\sum_T e(T)^2\log\frac{D_e(T)}{e(T)^2},
\tag{2}
\]
with the continuous zero convention.  This is precisely the
difference-spectrum packet in the exact shared-selector identity.

\subsection*{1. Exact cross-cap localization}

Since
\[
 \sum_{T\supseteq S}\chi_T(x\oplus y)
 =\begin{cases}
  2^{k-|S|}(-1)^r,&R\subseteq S,\\
  0,&R\not\subseteq S,
 \end{cases}
\tag{3}
\]
direct expansion of $(e_x+e_y)^2$ gives, with
$\sigma=\tau(-1)^r$,
\[
 \boxed{D_{e_x+e_y}(S)
 =8\delta,2^{-|S|}\bigl(1+\sigma\mathbf1_{R\subseteq S}\bigr).}
\tag{4}
\]
For either individual surgery,
$D_{e_x}(S)=D_{e_y}(S)=4\delta2^{-|S|}$.  Therefore the entire quadratic
cross-cap is
\[
 \boxed{C_R(S)=D_{e_x+e_y}(S)-D_{e_x}(S)-D_{e_y}(S)
 =8\sigma\delta2^{-|S|}\mathbf1_{R\subseteq S}.}
\tag{5}
\]
It has one canonical owner: the minimal filter generator $R$.  It is zero
off the principal upper filter of the physical displacement.

Under the flat bent atom law $p(S)=\delta$, its complete relative first
variation is exactly
\[
 \sum_Sp(S)\frac{|C_R(S)|}{2^{-|S|}}
 =8\delta2^{-r}\le4\delta.
\tag{6}
\]

\subsection*{2. Exact entropy refund}

The atom law is
\[
 (e_x(T)+e_y(T))^2
 =8\delta^2\bigl(1+\tau\chi_T(x\oplus y)\bigr).
\tag{7}
\]
Thus exactly half the atoms are nonzero, each with mass $16\delta^2$.
If $R\not\subseteq T$, (4) divided by (7) is
$2^{k-|T|-1}$.  If $R\subseteq T$, a nonzero atom exists exactly when
$\sigma=1$, and its ratio is $2^{k-|T|}$.

For $r\ge2$, conditioning on the parity of $|T\cap R|$ leaves every
coordinate marginal equal to $1/2$.  For $r=1$, the unique coordinate in
$R$ is fixed.  Summing the integer logarithms gives
\[
 \mathcal U(e_x)=\mathcal U(e_y)=2\delta k,
\tag{8}
\]
and
\[
 \boxed{
 \mathcal U(e_x+e_y)=\mathcal U(e_x)+\mathcal U(e_y)-\rho_{r,\sigma},}
\tag{9}
\]
where
\[
 \rho_{r,\sigma}=
 \begin{cases}
 4\delta,&r=1,\\
 8\delta-2^{4-r}\delta\,\mathbf1_{\sigma=1},&r\ge2.
 \end{cases}
\tag{10}
\]
Consequently
\[
 4\delta\le\rho_{r,\sigma}\le8\delta
 \quad\text{and}\quad
 \sum_Sp(S)\frac{|C_R(S)|}{2^{-|S|}}
 \le\rho_{r,\sigma}.
\tag{11}
\]
The two-surgery interaction therefore creates no unpaid logarithmic term
inside the difference-spectrum packet.  It creates a dimension-free refund
that already dominates the entire flat-law first variation of the
cross-cap.

\subsection*{3. Scope}

Equations (4)--(11) are all-parameter and require only the flat atom law
when (6) is invoked.  They cover every even $k$, every bent phase pattern,
and every distinct pair $x,y$.  They identify a single physical owner for
the cross term and remove the difference-spectrum packet from the list of
possible positive two-surgery debts.

This does not prove the complete two-selector node estimate.  The coherent
empty-control packet and the retained child packets also contain the
one-surgery linear cap perturbations; a nonlinear comparison must show that
their residual is absorbed by (10) plus physical selector influence.  Nor
does the two-point formula control an arbitrary number of coherent core
changes.  Universal GU$'$ and full FEI remain open.
