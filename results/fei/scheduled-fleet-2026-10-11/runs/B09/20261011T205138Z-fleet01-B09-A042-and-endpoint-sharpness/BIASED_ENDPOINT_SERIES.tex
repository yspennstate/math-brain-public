\section*{A genuine Boolean endpoint lower-bound family}
Let $g(x,y)=(-1)^{xy}$ under independent Bernoulli biases $p,q$, and reveal
$A=\{X\}$.  Put $s=4q(1-q)$ and $c=4q^2$.  The exact squared product-biased
Fourier masses are
\[
 (1-2pq)^2,\quad cp(1-p),\quad sp^2,\quad sp(1-p).
\]
Consequently, with $u=cp(1-p)/(1-sp)$,
\[
 M_A=(1-sp)h_2(u)+sp h_2(p),\qquad
 J_A=h_2(sp)-p h_2(s).
\]
For fixed $q\in(0,1/2)$, the elementary expansion
\[
 h_2(ap+O(p^2))=ap\log_2(1/p)
 +p\left(a\log_2(1/a)+\frac a{\ln2}\right)
 +O_a(p^2\log(1/p))
\]
gives
\[
 J_A-M_A=(s-c)p\log_2(1/p)+\kappa(q)p
 +O_q(p^2\log(1/p)),
\]
where
\[
 \kappa(q)=(1-s)\log_2(1-s)+c\log_2c+\frac{s-c}{\ln2}.
\]
At $q=1/4$, this becomes
\[
 J_A-M_A=\frac12p\log_2(1/p)
 +\left(-1+\frac1{2\ln2}\right)p
 +O(p^2\log(1/p)).
\]
Thus any correction $\epsilon(p)$ valid for every Boolean gate and all other
product biases in $J_A\le M_A+\epsilon(p)$ satisfies
\[
 \liminf_{p\downarrow0}\frac{\epsilon(p)}{p\log_2(1/p)}\ge\frac12.
\]
The statement is fixed-$q$, not uniform as $q\to0$ or $q\to1/2$.  At $p=0$
the coordinate contracts and both $M_A,J_A$ are zero.
