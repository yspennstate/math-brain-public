The lead's target is `E_ge2(F) <= K Delta(F)` for arbitrary two-weight groups and all product biases. The theorem proved here covers exactly **one exceptional physical input**, with every other input having a common weight. It is uniform in the number of those other inputs, the exceptional real weight, the threshold, and all biases. It does not assert the result for arbitrary numbers of exceptional inputs.

The full two-group identities in `SLICE_DEFICIT_PAYMENT.tex` apply without that restriction, but they control covariance and mixed spectral energy, not corrected entropy.
