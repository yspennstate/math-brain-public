\section*{A signed single-use rarity payment for the Boolean point-defect chain}

All logarithms are base two.  Let $q_n:\{0,1\}^n\to\{-1,1\}$ be $+1$
only at the origin and $-1$ elsewhere.  Put $\delta_n=2^{-n}$ and, for a
midpoint/half-difference Fourier pair $(a,v)$, define
\[
 \phi(u)=u\log_2(1/u),\qquad
 L(a,v)=\phi(a^2)+\phi(v^2)-\phi(a^2+v^2),
\]
and
\[
 Q(a,v)=(a^2+v^2)\left[1-h_2\left(
 \frac12+\frac{|av|}{a^2+v^2}\right)\right],\qquad D=L+Q,
\]
with the zero cell interpreted continuously.  The complete physical
selector packet of $q_n$ is
\[
 D_n=D(-1+\delta_n,\delta_n)
 +(2^n-1)D(\delta_n,\delta_n).
\]
Restricting any data coordinate has one constant child, whose packet is
zero, and one exceptional child equal to $q_{n-1}$.  Hence the exact
data/selector swap credit is
\[
 \Gamma_n^D=D_n-\frac12D_{n-1}.
\]
The preceding B05/B19 calculations prove $\Gamma_n^D<0$ for every $n\ge3$.

\subsection*{Renormalized telescope}

Define $C_n=2^nD_n$.  Then, identically,
\[
 2^n\Gamma_n^D=C_n-C_{n-1}.                                      \tag{1}
\]
Fix integers $2\le t<n$.  Reveal any predetermined $n-t$ data coordinates
and stop on the unique exceptional branch once $q_t$ is reached.  The reach
probability of the node carrying $q_m$ is $2^{m-n}$.  The total expected
negative swap debt is therefore
\[
 \mathfrak D_{n,t}
 =\sum_{m=t+1}^n2^{m-n}(-\Gamma_m^D)
 =2^{-n}\sum_{m=t+1}^n(C_{m-1}-C_m)
 =2^{-n}(C_t-C_n).                                                \tag{2}
\]
The expected terminal packet is $2^{t-n}D_t=2^{-n}C_t$.  Thus
\[
 \boxed{D_n+\mathfrak D_{n,t}=2^{t-n}D_t=2^{-n}C_t.}              \tag{3}
\]
Every debt increment appears exactly once, at its first physical revelation,
and the sole terminal packet is spent once.  The stopping rule is physical,
nonadaptive, and finite.

\subsection*{Uniform bound and retained rare logarithms}

The all-$n$ negativity makes $C_n$ strictly decreasing for $n\ge3$.
Furthermore $D(\delta_n,\delta_n)=4\delta_n^2$, while
$D(-1+\delta_n,\delta_n)=O(n\delta_n^2)$, so $C_n\to4$.  Consequently
\[
 0\le\mathfrak D_{n,t}
 \le 2^{-n}(C_t-4)
 \le 2^{-n}(C_2-4),                                              \tag{4}
\]
and
\[
 4\,2^{-n}<2^{-n}C_t\le C_2,2^{-n}.                             \tag{5}
\]
Here
\[
 D_2=\frac34+\phi(9/16)+\phi(1/16)-\phi(5/8)
 +\frac58\bigl(1-h_2(4/5)\bigr),
\]
so
\[
 C_2=4D_2=4.867668746754\ldots,
 \qquad C_2-4=0.867668746754\ldots .                             \tag{6}
\]
In particular, even at infinite depth the debt consumes at most
\[
 1-\frac4{C_2}=0.17825139545\ldots
\]
of the single-use terminal packet.

The rare logarithm is not replaced by a pointwise $O(I)$ charge.  The
previous full-credit expansion
\[
 \Gamma_m^D=4^{-m}\left(-2m+8-\frac3{\ln2}\right)+O(8^{-m})
\]
gives, after multiplying by the reach probability,
\[
 2^{m-n}(-\Gamma_m^D)
 =2^{-n}\left(2m+O(1)\right)2^{-m}+O(2^{-n}4^{-m}).              \tag{7}
\]
Thus the logarithmic factor $m=\log_2(1/\delta_m)$ remains visible at
every level and is amortized geometrically.  More sharply, (2) telescopes it
without loss.  Summing the full-credit expansion and using $C_n\to4$ yields
\[
 C_n=4+2^{-n}\left(2n-4+\frac3{\ln2}\right)+O(n4^{-n}).          \tag{8}
\]

\subsection*{Scope}

Equations (1)--(8) prove a complete signed first-revelation payment for the
unbounded genuine-Boolean family $\{q_n\}$, and the same ledger scales by an
independent activation probability.  They do not prove that an arbitrary
Boolean function has such a one-branch restriction chain, that a near tensor
has a factorwise terminal refund, or that the terminal packet is universally
bounded by total influence.  Addressing, reused selectors, and shared-variable
products therefore remain outside this theorem.  In particular, this is not
a proof or counterexample for full Fourier Entropy--Influence.
