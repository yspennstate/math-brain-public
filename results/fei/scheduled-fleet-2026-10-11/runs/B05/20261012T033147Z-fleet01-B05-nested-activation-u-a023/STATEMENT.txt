For every finite n>=0, every genuine Boolean g:{-1,1}^n->{-1,1}, every d>=1, every active point z_* in {-1,1}^d, and every deterministic order of the d fresh control coordinates, let p=2^-d, r=Pr[g=-1], and F(z,x)=g(x) if z=z_* and F(z,x)=1 otherwise. For the physical control-first restriction tree that stops at the inactive constant child and at g after all controls activate, the complete signed upper-set charge satisfies sum_v w_v Delta_{i_v}U(F_v)=U(F)-pU(g)<=C_* I_ctl(F), where I_ctl(F)=2rpd and C_*=1+2(1+e^-1)/ln(2)<4.947. The full terminal packet pU(g) is retained once; no bound on U(g)/I(g) is assumed.
