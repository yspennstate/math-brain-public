For every genuine Boolean q, every 0<eta<=1, and every c>=3/2+eta, let T(q)(z,y,x)=1 for z=+1 and T(q)(z,y,x)=yq(x) for z=-1, where y,z are fresh uniform signs. With L_{c,eta}=M+(1+eta)U-cI and M=G-B=sum_i(K_i-Gamma_i), one has L_{c,eta}(T(q))<=L_{c,eta}(q)/2. Therefore every base class satisfying L<=0 remains so under output sign, disjoint products, and arbitrary repeated changing-core applications of T. At eta=1/4 and c=7/4 this gives G<=(15/4)I+(3/4)U on the generated endpoint class.
