## Bounded objective

Extend the accepted one-node quadratic inequality to a complete finite adaptive restriction tree, prove that adaptive branch reuse of the same physical coordinate cannot duplicate pivotal credit, and retain an arbitrary disjoint Boolean terminal core exactly. Constants must be independent of control dimension, quadratic rank/overlap, stopping depth, tree policy, and dyadic activation probability.

## Result

Let

\[
q(x)=(-1)^{Q(x)},\qquad x\in\{0,1\}^m,
\]

where \(Q\) has degree at most two over \(\mathbb F_2\). Let \(g:\{0,1\}^r\to\{-1,1\}\) be arbitrary on variables disjoint from the controls, and put \(F(x,y)=q(x)g(y)\).

Let \(T\) be any finite deterministic adaptive restriction tree which queries only physical control coordinates. A coordinate may occur at different incomparable nodes, but is not queried twice on one root-to-leaf path. For a node \(v\), let \(w_v\) be its uniform-cube reach probability, \(F_v\) its restricted function, and \(i_v\) its queried physical coordinate. For a leaf \(\ell\), let \(w_\ell\) and \(F_\ell\) have the analogous meanings. Then

\[
\boxed{
U(F)-\sum_{\ell}w_\ell U(F_\ell)
=\sum_{v\in T^\circ}w_v\Delta_{i_v}U(F_v)
\le 3\sum_{v\in T^\circ}w_v\operatorname{Inf}_{i_v}(F_v)
\le 3 I_X(F)=3I(q).
}
\]

The result holds for arbitrary finite pruning/stopping and hence all dyadic rare activation probabilities generated by physical uniform restrictions. It is restriction closed in the control variables. The coefficient 3 is optimal for this local-to-tree argument: a one-node tree on the even clique quadratic has ratio \(3-2^{2-m}\), tending to 3.

## Proof

### 1. Exact tree telescope

At every internal node,

\[
\Delta_{i_v}U(F_v)
=U(F_v)-\tfrac12U(F_{v,+})-\tfrac12U(F_{v,-}).
\]

Multiplication by \(w_v\) and summation cancels every nonroot internal term once with its parent contribution, giving

\[
\sum_vw_v\Delta_{i_v}U(F_v)
=U(F)-\sum_\ell w_\ell U(F_\ell).
\]

No sign assumption is used.

### 2. Arbitrary disjoint core is retained exactly

For functions on disjoint blocks the squared Fourier law is a product and upper-set masses multiply, so

\[
U(h_1h_2)=U(h_1)+U(h_2).
\]

Every control restriction has \(F_v=q_vg\), hence

\[
\Delta_{i_v}U(F_v)=\Delta_{i_v}U(q_v),
\qquad
\operatorname{Inf}_{i_v}(F_v)=\operatorname{Inf}_{i_v}(q_v).
\]

Equivalently, \(U(g)\) appears once at the root and with total leaf weight one at the boundary, so it cancels without being charged.

### 3. Local quadratic payment

A restriction of a quadratic phase is again a quadratic phase. The source theorem `B01_ALL_QUADRATIC_GU_v1` proves, for every remaining coordinate \(i\),

\[
\Delta_iU(q_v)\le 3\operatorname{Inf}_i(q_v).
\]

Its proof is the exact affine-coset merge argument: quadratic spectra are uniform on affine supports, the two restriction children form affine translates, cross upper fibers are empty and within-fiber multiplicities agree; every merge costs at most one bit. Thus

\[
\sum_vw_v\Delta_{i_v}U(F_v)
\le3\sum_vw_v\operatorname{Inf}_{i_v}(F_v).
\]

### 4. Adaptive physical-coordinate ownership

Fix a physical coordinate \(i\). For each node \(v\) at which \(i_v=i\), let \(E_v\) be the cylinder of root inputs reaching \(v\). Because \(i\) is not queried earlier on that path, \(E_v\) is invariant under flipping \(i\). Moreover nodes querying \(i\) are pairwise incomparable, so their cylinders are disjoint.

Let \(\mathrm{Piv}_i(F)=\{z:F(z)\ne F(z^{\oplus i})\}\). Conditional uniformity on \(E_v\) yields the exact identity

\[
w_v\operatorname{Inf}_i(F_v)
=\Pr(E_v\cap\mathrm{Piv}_i(F)).
\]

Consequently,

\[
\sum_{v:i_v=i}w_v\operatorname{Inf}_i(F_v)
\le\Pr(\mathrm{Piv}_i(F))=\operatorname{Inf}_i(F).
\]

Summing over control coordinates gives the single-use credit inequality

\[
I_T:=\sum_vw_v\operatorname{Inf}_{i_v}(F_v)
\le I_X(F)=I(q).
\]

This permits the same physical coordinate at different branches; the necessary no-reuse condition is pathwise, not global across the tree.

## Class extension and why it matters

Previous B05 tree results handled separated flat/nonoverlapping hierarchies or retained a terminal deficit but left a raw packet. The new class allows:

1. arbitrary overlapping quadratic control graphs and affine shifts;
2. arbitrary adaptive and pruned finite control trees;
3. reuse of a physical control coordinate on incomparable branches;
4. an arbitrary disjoint Boolean core of any dimension, bias, spectrum, entropy, and influence, retained without charge.

The constant is 3 and has no dependence on dimension, polar rank, quadratic overlap, address count, depth, tree policy, dyadic rarity, or core complexity. This is a genuine expanding restriction-closed class, not a finite maximum.

## Scope boundary and mandatory falsifiers

The theorem does **not** cover a queried coordinate coupled to arbitrary shared core variables. In that setting the two child spectra are arbitrary translates/convolutions rather than equal-density affine cosets, and the one-bit merge estimate has no established replacement. This is exactly where sparse defects, coherent changing cores, and rare logarithmic charges can reappear.

It also does not prove a universal biased-activation statement beyond dyadic activation produced by uniform physical restrictions, and it does not turn the retained arbitrary factor \(g\) into a paid FEI term. Therefore it does not prove full FEI.

The result survives the relevant controls at its stated scope:

- rare/pruned activation: included exactly;
- near tensors and arbitrary terminal spectra: included through exact disjoint tensor additivity;
- quadratic overlap: arbitrary;
- shared-variable interference: explicitly excluded and retained as the next gap;
- whole-set addressing and higher-degree selectors: not claimed.
