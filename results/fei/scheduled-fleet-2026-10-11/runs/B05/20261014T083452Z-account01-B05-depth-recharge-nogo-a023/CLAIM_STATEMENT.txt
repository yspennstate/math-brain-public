Let q(t)=t log_2(1/t)+2t/ln2, psi_k(t)=q(t)+t log_2 k, and let tildeLambda_X be the reviewed fixed-block orbit potential. There is no finite C such that for every Boolean f and every finite sequence of two-coordinate blocks X_0,...,X_d with consecutive one-coordinate overlap, the positive variation sum_t[tildeLambda_{X_{t+1}}(f)-tildeLambda_{X_t}(f)]_+ is at most C I(f), or even C times the sum of influences of all coordinates appearing in the blocks. The fixed four-variable sign f(y,x,z,u)=x for y=1 and zu for y=-1, with the alternating path X={y,x},Y={y,z},X,Y,..., has I(f)=2 and positive variation r/ln2 after 2r-1 swaps. The signed sum retains r-1 refunds and equals 1/ln2 independently of r. Thus any depth-independent changing-overlap assembly must preserve signed refunds or persistent packet ownership; a memoryless nonnegative per-swap ledger cannot suffice. This does not refute GU or FEI.
