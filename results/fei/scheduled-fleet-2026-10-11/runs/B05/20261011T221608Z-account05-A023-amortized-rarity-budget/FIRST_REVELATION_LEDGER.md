# Single-use account certificate

Event ID: one specified physical control point on a fresh r-bit block. Open once, when its first control is queried; close at first mismatch or after all r matches. Descendants contribute conditional subbills to this account and cannot reopen it.

Exclusive resources: control account r2^-r(1-Eg); terminal account 2^-r I(g). Actual root influence is their sum. The stopping rule is a finite physical tree of maximum depth r, stopping at a constant or the certified core.

Cost owned: the COMPLETE expected minority allocation sum mu[log2(1/mu)-D2(nu||Q)], <=(1+c+8c/e) times the control account (<7 times). Additive recombination/remainder allowance 3c sum mu has its own coefficient and is included in the <12 control-account total. Terminal entropy is an additional 2^-r H(g), paid only from the terminal account under an actual H(g)/I(g) certificate.

Generated credit: at each actual conditional node, normalize its own minority measure and subtract exactly mu D2(nu||Q) once. No cross-reference-law credit transfer is assumed. No blind scalar rarity bill is reposted in descendants. For positive-mean cores the blind total is p a[r(r+1)/2+r log2(1/a)] and the consumed credit is this minus the exact signed J_tree. For negative-mean cores use the corrected final-node minority, never rename signed disagreement mu.

Unconditional terminals: a selector between any k-bit bent function and its arbitrary one-point flip, k>=4 even, has H/I<=20/7<3 by a proved influence lower bound and finite-support entropy bound. Disjoint products of these terminals add their entropy and influence. Both tree orders and every terminal bill are specified in REPORT.md.

Do not reuse this certificate for overlapping physical control blocks: sharing one coordinate requires a new account correction. It also does not certify an arbitrary terminal core. These are exactly the remaining universal obligations.
