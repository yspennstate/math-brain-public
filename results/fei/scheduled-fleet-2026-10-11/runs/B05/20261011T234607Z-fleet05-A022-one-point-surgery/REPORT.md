ODDFACE_GAP.tex gives the proof. For an actual sign table g on m bits and h=g(1-2 1_D), let s=|D|, delta=2^-m, and E(D) count internal unordered edges. Then d=s delta and c_D=delta(ms-2E(D)). With signed half-difference t_T=delta sum_D g(v)chi_T(v), midpoint u_T=(ghat(T)-t_T)^2 and selector mass e_T=t_T^2, q_T=u_T+e_T. The parent erasure is B=sum q_T h2(e_T/q_T), and h2(d)-B is the selector/residual spectral mutual information M. The exact slack decomposes as

c_D+log2(e)d-B = delta[s log2(s)-2E(D)] + [d+(1-d)ln(1-d)]/ln2 + M.

All three terms are nonnegative. An elementary coordinate-splitting induction proves the edge-isoperimetric term without an imported theorem. The scalar term has positive series sum_(j>=2) d^j/[j(j-1)ln2], between d^2/(2ln2) and d^2/[2(1-d)ln2]. One point and an adjacent pair have zero geometric excess; separated points have geometric excess d. These facts do not remove spectral mutual information. Adjacent interference and physical/minority normalization are preserved.
