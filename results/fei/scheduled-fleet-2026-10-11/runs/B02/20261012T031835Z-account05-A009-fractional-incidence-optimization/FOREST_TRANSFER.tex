\title{A009: a full prime-implicant forest certificate on affine planes}
\author{A009, B02}\maketitle
All $\log$ are base two, $\gamma=6/5$. Full FEI remains open.
Complete source locators, actual-run construction, analytic derivation,
exact checks and the first universal gap are in \texttt{REPORT.md}.

\begin{theorem}
For every prime power $q\ge16$, let $f_q$ be the Boolean union of the
positive conjunctions on all affine lines of $\mathbb F_q^2$.
These are all its prime implicants. With $N=q(q+1)$, $k=q+1$,
$\rho=2^{-q}$, $\lambda_l=1/k$, $\pi_l=\rho$, and one fixed full order
per gate, there exist actual deterministic partition-forest laws with
complete conditioned residual
\[
 R\le\frac{186791}{13540}I(f_q)<14I(f_q).
\]
The bound includes $v(\Lambda+\log Z)$, labels, root rarity, all subset
congestion and ranks. No posterior entropy saving is required.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
When a spectral set is contained in one line, use that gate alone;
otherwise use first-incidence ownership and a deterministic forest
rooted at one gate of every source SCC. The queue orders roots and
attachment occurrences globally, visits each owner once and selects
disjoint subsets. Every draw has positive probability at the fixed
packing vector $\lambda$.

Write $K=\sum_l b_l$, $G=2K-1$ and $e=f_q-G$. The elementary bound
$(K-1)_+^2\le K(K-1)$ and pairwise line intersections give
\[
 E_2=\|e\|_2^2\le8N^2\rho^2.
\]
For a nonempty Fourier set $A$, $|\widehat G(A)|=2\rho$ times its
number of containing lines. Sets of size at least two have at most
one containing line; singletons have multiplicity $k$. Thus the ideal
mass off the line-supported sets is zero. Let
\[
 D=(1+\rho/k)^N-1,\qquad
 c=q+\log D+\log k=\log N+\delta,\quad
 0\le\delta\le q\rho\log e.
\]
The complete conditioned cost on a good set is $C_A=c+F_l(A)$.
The uniform-subset binomial predecessor identity gives mean rank
cost at most $\gamma q/2$ on one line. Summing over line amplitudes
and paying coherent singleton amplitudes separately gives
\[
 \sum_{\rm good}\widehat G(A)^2\le4N\rho+4q^2k^2\rho^2,
\]
\[
 \sum_{\rm good}\widehat G(A)^2F_l(A)
 \le2\gamma Nq\rho+4\gamma q^2k^2\rho^2\log q.
\]
Since the good cost is nonnegative,
$\widehat f^2\le2\widehat G^2+2\widehat e^2$ transfers this cost.
Off the good sets $\widehat f=\widehat e$. Every generic forest has
$B\le q^2(q+\log k+2\gamma\log q)$.
For $q\ge16$, $\log D<0$, and
\[
 c\le5q/8,\quad \max C_A\le q^2/2,\quad \max B<2q^3.
\]
These follow from $\log q\le q/4$, $\log k\le5q/16$,
$\log N\le9q/16$, and $\delta\le q/16$.
Consequently
\[
 R\le\frac{49}{5}Nq\rho+\frac{37}{5}q^3k^2\rho^2
       +8N^2(q^2+2q^3)\rho^2.
\]
Clean pivotal events give
$I\ge2Nq\rho(1-2N\rho)>0$. Dividing yields
\[
 \frac RI\le
 \frac{49/5+(37/5)N\rho+8N(q+2q^2)\rho}{2(1-2N\rho)}.
\]
Every positive-polynomial term divided by $2^q$ decreases after
$q=16$: successive ratios are at most $(17/16)^4/2<1$.
The denominator increases. At $q=16$ the fraction is exactly
$186791/13540$, leaving margin $2769/13540$ below $14$.
The proof uses only finite-field incidence and applies to all prime
powers, independently of the finite checks at $q=2,3$.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Full scalar transfer for equal-width linear hypergraphs]
For $N$ distinct positive width-$w$ conjunctions on $n$ covered
variables with pairwise support intersection at most one, let $k$ be
maximum read, $\rho=2^{-w}$, $\lambda_l=1/k$,
$D=(1+\rho/k)^N-1$, $c=w+\log D+\log k\ge0$.
The same fixed deterministic forest rule obeys
\begin{align*}
 R\le{}&2\{c(4N\rho+4nk^2\rho^2)+2\gamma Nw\rho
              +4\gamma nk^2\rho^2\log w\}\\
 &+8N^2\rho^2\{2(c+\gamma w\log w)
       +n(w+\log k+2\gamma\log w)+\max(0,\log D)\}.
\end{align*}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Repeat the preceding spectral-error transfer with at most $k$
singleton overlaps. There are $n$ possible singletons; all sets of
size at least two have at most one owner support. A good-set maximum
is $c+\gamma w\log w$ and the generic forest maximum is
$n(w+\log k+2\gamma\log w)$. If $\log D$ is positive, retain it also
in the off-support cost. The same $E_2\le8N^2\rho^2$ bound applies.
The displayed formula follows by substitution, without claiming it
is uniformly $O(I)$ for arbitrary density.
\end{proof}
The stage-1 coordinatewise pressure lower bound, even with A007's
retained-normalizer refinement, therefore obstructs that coarsening
only. It does not obstruct PI-J on affine planes. The universal
missing inequality is an influence-paid full-cost error budget where
the raw indicator-sum approximation is no longer close to the union.
