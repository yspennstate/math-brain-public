\title{A076 stage 2: majority-of-tribes tree controls}\author{A076}\maketitle
The complete proof and exact certificate interpretation are in
\texttt{MIXED\_TREE\_PROOF.md}. All physical inputs below are independent
uniform signs and all entropies are in bits.

For even $m\ge2$, put $\alpha=2^{-m}\binom{m}{m/2}$,
$g(x)=\operatorname{sign}(\sum x)$ with $g=0$ on ties,
$c(x)=\mathbf1_{\sum x=0}$, and
$F=\operatorname{Maj}_{m+1}(x_1,\ldots,x_m,G(y))=g+cG$.
The $x$ supports of $g$ and $c$ have odd and even cardinality,
respectively. Consequently
\[
\widehat F(X,Y)=\widehat g(X)\mathbf1_{Y=\varnothing}
                  +\widehat c(X)\widehat G(Y),\qquad
H(F)=H(\operatorname{Maj}_{m+1})+\alpha H(G),
\]
\[
I(F)=\alpha(m+I(G)),\quad B_i(F)=\alpha B_i(G)\ (i\in y).
\]
The same scaling holds for minority mass, retained packet logarithm,
and actual Jensen--Shannon decrement at a data split. If $G$ is
balanced, every $x$ erasure is zero and $B(F)=\alpha B(G)$.
For $m=2$ and $\mu=\mathbb EG$,
\[
H(F)=2+\tfrac12H(G),\quad I(F)=1+\tfrac12I(G),\quad
B(F)=\tfrac12B(G)+(1+\mu^2)h_2\!\left(\frac{\mu^2}{1+\mu^2}\right).
\]

Define the actual scalar charge
$\Lambda_v=j_v\log_2(1+\kappa_{\min,v}/(2j_v))$,
$j_v=\min(I_{i_v},1-I_{i_v})$,
$\kappa(h)=1-\max_S\widehat h(S)^2$.
At any data split while both majority3 inputs remain, both children
have $\kappa=3/4$, independently of the core.
For tribes $G=G_{w,t}$, put $q=2^{-w}$, $b=1-q$,
$\beta=2qb^{t-1}$. The exact y-first charge is
\[
\Lambda_{Y}=\frac\beta2\sum_{j=0}^{t-1}\sum_{u=1}^{w}
\log_2\left(1+\frac34 2^{u-1}b^{-(t-j-1)}\right).
\]
Stopping at symmetric thresholds subtracts only
$\frac\beta2\log_2(7/4)$; its exact terminal values are
$\mathbb EI(\mathrm{leaf})=1+\beta/2$ and
$\mathbb EH(\mathrm{leaf})=2$.

For even $w\ge16$ and $t=2^w$, every fixed interleaving of the two
majority inputs with the contiguous block-by-block y order satisfies
\[
\boxed{\frac{\Lambda_{\mathcal T}}{I(F)}\ge\frac{w}{64}.}
\]
Indeed, at the first $w/2$ positions of the first $t/4$ blocks the
weighted queried influence is exactly $\beta/2$ per position.
Both core children have mean magnitude at most $1/2$ and
$\kappa\ge3/4$. A branch with one remaining majority input has
$\kappa\ge7/16$. Thus each logarithm is at least $w/4$.
These nodes are nonsymmetric and have strictly positive erasure,
so neither symmetric stopping nor exact-isospectral skipping removes
them. Summing gives $\Lambda\ge I(G)w/64$; since
$I(G)\ge2w/3>2$ and $I(F)=(2+I(G))/2$, the displayed bound follows.
For y-first the exact sum improves this to
$\Lambda_Y/I(F)=w/2+O(1)$.

On the same family the exact tribes spectrum gives $H(G)\le5I(G)$,
and hence $H(F)\le5I(F)$. Nonnegative JS and tree telescoping imply
\[
B_{\mathcal T}+\mathbb EH(\mathrm{leaf})\le H(F).
\]
Using only the symmetric-threshold leaf certificate
$H(\mathrm{leaf})<9I(\mathrm{leaf})$ therefore gives
\[
\boxed{B_{\mathcal T}+9\mathbb EI(\mathrm{leaf})\le14I(F).}
\]
The same leaves thus have a bounded true bill and a divergent
scalar child-defect bill. This refutes the proposed scalar payment
for these schedules, not its valid local entropy upper bound,
arbitrary adaptive schedules, or FEI.

For any seed $h$, the balanced core $G=z\prod_{a=1}^k h_a$ on
disjoint blocks has $B(G)=kB(h)$ and $I(G)=1+kI(h)$.
Thus $B(F)/I(F)\to B(h)/I(h)$ at fixed even $m$.
The central-majority/arbitrary-balanced-core endpoint retains the
optimal universal erasure ratio; omitting the core entropy is
therefore not a route to full FEI.
