\section*{A sharp correlated-encoder barrier for the B09 rare gate (A079)}

All physical cubes are uniform and all logarithms are base two.  Write
$h_2$ for binary entropy.  This note treats the live question left by the
A077 compensated tower: can shared or correlated fair-bit auxiliaries realize
the same rare biased gate while avoiding its $r k$ physical cost?

\paragraph{A genuinely shared-variable realization.}
Let $a,b\in\mathbb F_2^k$ be independent uniform vectors, put
\[
 U=\mathbf 1_{\{a=0\}},\qquad V=\mathbf 1_{\{a+b=0\}},\qquad
 f_k(a,b)=(-1)^{UV}.
\]
Both encoders depend on the physical block $a$, so this is not independent
block padding.  Nevertheless $(a,a+b)$ is uniform, whence $U,V$ are
independent Bernoulli variables of bias $\epsilon=2^{-k}$.  Their joint
activation is the singleton $\{a=b=0\}$, and therefore $f_k$ is the ordinary
point defect on $2k$ variables.

The normalized centered Fourier label of $U$ is uniform on the $2^k-1$
nonempty subsets $S$ of the $a$ coordinates.  For $V$ it is uniform on the
diagonal labels $(T,T)$, $T\ne\varnothing$.  In the product term the physical
Fourier label is
\[
 (R,T)=(S\triangle T,T).
\]
Thus the two physical label blocks are correlated: $T\ne\varnothing$ and
$R\ne T$ deterministically.  The map $(S,T)\mapsto(S\triangle T,T)$ is
injective, so
\[
 H(R\mid T)=\log_2(2^k-1).
\]
Writing $q=2^{k-1}/(2^k-1)$, the exact mean physical degree of this product
label is
\[
 \mathbb E(|R|+|T|)=2kq(1-q)+kq.
\]
The shared variables therefore create spectral dependence but no hidden
label compression.

\paragraph{A universal energy lower bound.}
The preceding failure is not peculiar to the linear realization.  Let
$U,V:\{0,1\}^n\to\{0,1\}$ be arbitrary deterministic encoders on one uniform
physical cube.  Suppose their output law is the same independent biased law
as the B09 gate:
\[
 \Pr(U=1)=\Pr(V=1)=\epsilon=2^{-k},\qquad
 \Pr(U=V=1)=\epsilon^2.
\]
No disjointness, linearity, or product structure is assumed of the encoders.
Set $f=(-1)^{UV}$ and $A=\{U=V=1\}$.  The edge-isoperimetric inequality on
the cube gives, for $\delta=|A|/2^n\le1/2$,
\[
 I(f)=\frac{2|\partial A|}{2^n}
 \ge 2\delta\log_2(1/\delta).
\]
Since $\delta=\epsilon^2=2^{-2k}$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:energy}
 \boxed{I(f)\ge4k\epsilon^2.}
\end{equation}
This includes all shared-variable and spectrally correlated realizations of
the same gate law.  Equality holds when $A$ is a codimension-$2k$ subcube,
so the bound is sharp.

To compare with the exact A077/B09 restriction gain, put
\[
 r=4\epsilon^2(1-\epsilon),\qquad
 s=4\epsilon(1-\epsilon),\qquad
 J_k=h_2(r)-\epsilon h_2(s).
\]
Using, for $0<u<1$,
\[
 u\log_2(1/u)\le h_2(u)
 \le u\log_2(1/u)+u\log_2 e
\]
and $r=\epsilon s$ yields
\[
 J_k\le r\bigl(k+\log_2e\bigr)
 \le4\epsilon^2\bigl(k+\log_2e\bigr).
\]
Combining this with \eqref{eq:energy},
\[
 \boxed{\frac{J_k}{I(f)}\le1+\frac{\log_2e}{k}.}
\]
Adding any nonnegative conditional-entropy charge only decreases this ratio.
Consequently the divergent tax-free biased helper from A077 cannot be turned
into a uniform FEI counterfamily by a correlated realization that preserves
the B09 independent output law.  The missing cost may move between spectral
labels, but the boundary of the rare joint cell already pays it physically.

\paragraph{Exact FEI ledger for the shared-variable example.}
Let $\delta=2^{-2k}$ and $W=4\delta(1-\delta)$.  Since the negative set is a
singleton,
\[
 H(f_k)=h_2(W)+W\log_2(2^{2k}-1),\qquad
 I(f_k)=4k\delta,
\]
and hence $H(f_k)/I(f_k)\to4$.  This is a control, not a disproof of FEI.

\paragraph{Scope.}
The cube edge-isoperimetric inequality is a known theorem; the application
above is the new source-bound obstruction.  It excludes correlated encoders
of the same two-bit independent biased gate and, more generally, every
realization for which the final negative set has prescribed density
$\delta$: its influence is at least $2\delta\log_2(1/\delta)$.  It does not
exclude a different gate whose negative set is not rare, a construction
whose claimed gain is not tied to the joint activation cell, or a genuinely
nonlocal mechanism that pays its entropy elsewhere.  Full FEI remains open.
