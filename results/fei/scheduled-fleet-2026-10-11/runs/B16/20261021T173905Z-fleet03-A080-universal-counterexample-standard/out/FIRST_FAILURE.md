The initial full verifier completed its 168 actual Boolean fixtures and then failed the closed parity upper-bound check at large selector width. The cause is the verifier's fixed 80-bit absolute logarithm rounding: the constant-atom entropy is O(2^-k), so its rounded interval width divided by a=2^-k is useless when k>80. This is a failed certificate, not a negative mathematical margin or a FEI counterexample. The corrected helper uses the direct positive series ln(1/x)=2 atanh((1-x)/(1+x)) for x>1/2, retaining an error proportional to 1-x. All parameters and mathematical targets remain frozen. FROZEN_SPEC_AMENDMENT.json records the helper change before the rerun.
