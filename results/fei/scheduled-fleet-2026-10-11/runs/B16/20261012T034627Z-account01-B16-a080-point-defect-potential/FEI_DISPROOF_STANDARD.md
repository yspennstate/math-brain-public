# A080 universal-counterexample standard and verdict

## Acceptance standard

A claimed disproof of uniform-cube FEI must supply genuine Boolean functions
`f_m` in unbounded dimensions and prove

`H(f_m)/I(f_m) -> infinity`,

with every physical coordinate, sign, logarithm, normalization, auxiliary
label, and limiting regime included. Divergence of a biased local gain,
selector tax, derivative potential, or another surrogate is not enough.

For the campaign's upper-set potential

`U(f)=sum_S p_S log2(pi(S)/p_S)`,

where `pi(S)=sum_{T superset S}p_T`, a candidate must use the exact
empty-set convention and prove the implication it needs. A finite sweep or a
single ratio above a proposed constant establishes neither divergence nor a
universal theorem.

## Candidate audited

The audited sequence is the A077 compensated sparse-defect tower and its A079
shared-variable realization. Each uniform component is a Boolean point
defect, and all tower copies use disjoint coordinates. This is an actual
all-parameter Boolean family, not an abstract spectral law.

## Verdict

**REFUTED AS AN FEI OR U/I COUNTERFAMILY.** For every tensor tower of point
defects, with arbitrary dimensions and multiplicities,

`H < 4 I` and `U < 3 I`.

For the exact A077 multiplicities,

`H/I -> 4` and `U/I -> 1`.

The challenged biased helper diverges only after omitting encoder-label and
physical-boundary costs. Restoring the actual uniform Boolean realization
makes its aggregate charge comparable to physical influence.

## Surviving gap

The next candidate must leave disjoint point-defect tensor closure. The first
unpaid mechanism is an overlapping nonlinear Boolean surgery or a different
gate with a non-singleton output cell, for which `H`, `I`, and `U` are all
computed anew and no auxiliary cost is discarded. This audit does not prove
FEI and does not rule out such a family.
