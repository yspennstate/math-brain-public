\section*{A compensated sparse-defect tower (A077)}

All logarithms and entropies below are in bits.  This construction is a
counterexample to a tax-free \emph{biased local helper}; it is not a
counterexample to the uniform Fourier Entropy--Influence conjecture.

\paragraph{The biased core.}
Let $X,Y$ be independent Bernoulli variables of common bias
$\epsilon=2^{-k}$, $k\ge3$, and let
\[
 g_k(X,Y)=(-1)^{XY}.
\]
In the product-biased orthonormal Fourier basis the squared coefficients,
indexed by $(S_X,S_Y)\in\{0,1\}^2$, are
\[
 m_0=(1-2\epsilon^2)^2,
 \quad m_X=m_Y=4\epsilon^3(1-\epsilon),
 \quad m_{XY}=4\epsilon^2(1-\epsilon)^2.
\]
Put
\[
 r=m_Y+m_{XY}=4\epsilon^2(1-\epsilon),\qquad
 s=4\epsilon(1-\epsilon).
\]
For the physical restriction of the $X$ coordinate, the B09 quantities are
\[
 J_k=h_2(r)-\epsilon h_2(s),
\]
and
\[
 M_k=H(S_X\mid S_Y)
 =(1-r)h_2\!\left(\frac{r\epsilon}{1-r}\right)+r h_2(\epsilon).
\]
The complete biased spectral influence of the two-coordinate gate is
$I_k^{\rm core}=2r$.

For $0<u<1$ we use
\[
 u\log_2(1/u)\le h_2(u)
 \le u\log_2(1/u)+u\log_2 e.
\]
Because $r=\epsilon s$, these bounds give the cancellation
\[
 J_k\ge r(k-\log_2e).
\]
Writing $\alpha=r\epsilon/(1-r)$ and applying the upper bound to the two
conditional fibers gives
\[
 M_k\le r\epsilon(4k+2\log_2e).
\]
Indeed
$\log_2((1-r)/(r\epsilon))\le\log_2(1/(r\epsilon))\le3k$
for $k\ge3$.  Consequently
\[
 \frac{J_k}{I_k^{\rm core}+M_k}
 \ge
 \frac{k-\log_2e}{2+2^{-k}(4k+2\log_2e)}\longrightarrow\infty.
\]
This is the exact local-surcharge obstruction.  It lives under a biased
physical law and therefore is not an FEI counterfamily.

\paragraph{Uniform Boolean realization and the missing cost.}
Let $U=(U_1,\ldots,U_k)$ and $V=(V_1,\ldots,V_k)$ be independent fair
bits, set $A_k(U)=\prod_i U_i$ in $\{0,1\}$ notation, and define
\[
 f_k(U,V)=(-1)^{A_k(U)A_k(V)}.
\]
Thus $f_k$ is an actual Boolean function on the uniform $2k$-cube and is
$-1$ at exactly one input.  The normalized Fourier law of the centered AND
bit is uniform on the $2^k-1$ nonempty subsets.  Its label entropy and mean
degree are
\[
 L_k=\log_2(2^k-1),\qquad
 D_k=\frac{k2^{k-1}}{2^k-1}.
\]
Expanding the two biased coordinates into their fair-bit encoders proves
the exact ledger
\[
 J_k^{\rm code}=J_k,\qquad
 M_k^{\rm code}=M_k+rL_k,\qquad
 I(f_k)=2rD_k=k2^{2-2k}.
\]
The equality for $J$ holds because the conditional encoder-label entropy
cancels: $r=\epsilon s$.  The new term $rL_k$ in $M$ and the physical
influence $2rD_k$ are both of order $rk$.  In particular
\[
 \frac{J_k}{M_k^{\rm code}}\to1,
 \qquad \frac{J_k}{rD_k}\to2,
 \qquad \frac{J_k}{I(f_k)+M_k^{\rm code}}\to\frac12.
\]
(Here $I(f_k)=2rD_k$ is the total influence; using only the $U$ block gives
the displayed limit $2$.)

For completeness, if $\delta=2^{-2k}$ and
$W_k=4\delta(1-\delta)$, the exact uniform FEI ledger is
\[
 H(f_k)=h_2(W_k)+W_k\log_2(2^{2k}-1),\qquad
 I(f_k)=4k\delta.
\]
Hence $H(f_k)/I(f_k)\to4$, agreeing with the known Shannon point-defect
control rather than contradicting FEI.

\paragraph{A genuine multiscale tower.}
For $K\ge3$, take $N_k=2^{2k}$ disjoint copies of $f_k$ at every scale
$3\le k\le K$ and tensor them:
\[
 F_K=\prod_{k=3}^K\prod_{j=1}^{N_k}f_{k,j}.
\]
Every physical coordinate is distinct, so there is no overlap or hidden
cross-scale interference; Fourier entropy and influence are exactly
additive.  Since $N_kr=4(1-2^{-k})$, the collapsed biased-core ledger has
\[
 \sum N_kJ_k=\Theta(K^2),\quad
 \sum N_kI_k^{\rm core}=\Theta(K),\quad
 \sum N_kM_k=O(1),
\]
and therefore its tax-free local-payment ratio diverges linearly.

The uniform realization restores the omitted costs.  At scale $k$ its
total physical influence is exactly
\[
 N_k I(f_k)=4k,
\]
and its label charge is
$N_krL_k=4(1-2^{-k})L_k=4k+O(k2^{-k})$.
Moreover
\[
 N_kH(f_k)=16k+O(1).
\]
Thus, with $S_K=\sum_{k=3}^Kk$,
\[
 I(F_K)=4S_K,\qquad
 H(F_K)=16S_K+O(K),\qquad
 \frac{H(F_K)}{I(F_K)}\to4.
\]
The tower therefore certifies repeated use of an omitted label/influence
budget by the biased helper while supplying a complete compensation ledger
for the actual uniform Boolean family.
