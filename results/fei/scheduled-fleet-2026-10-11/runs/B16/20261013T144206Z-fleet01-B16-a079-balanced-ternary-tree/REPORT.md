## Why this is not independent padding

For majority, the outer spectral support is uniformly distributed over
[
{{1},{2},{3},{1,2,3}}.
]
The three child-activation indicators are therefore strongly correlated: either exactly one child is active or all three are active. The exact (H(G)=2) term pays this conditional auxiliary label at every node. The result is consequently a genuine correlated spectral-auxiliary construction, not a product-padding calculation.

## Scope and obstruction tests

- **Actual Booleanity:** every node is an explicit Boolean truth table.
- **Uniform physical cube:** all leaves are distinct unbiased physical bits.

- **Tensor warning:** this is nonlinear recursive composition, not tensor multiplication.
- **FEI scope:** the theorem proves FEI with sharp class constant (4) only for balanced disjoint ternary trees. It does not prove full FEI.
