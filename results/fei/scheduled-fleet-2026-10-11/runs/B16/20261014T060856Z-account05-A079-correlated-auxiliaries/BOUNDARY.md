# Nonproduct two-branch frontier: an exact paid endpoint and remaining gap

The accepted all-product theorem does not cover a spectrum that is product
only after conditioning on a physical frequency bit. Conditioning on a
Fourier label produces partial Fourier rows, rather than Boolean physical
restrictions. One cannot substitute the product theorem at these branches
without checking their physical height and carrying their actual mass.

There is nevertheless one exact all-dimensional endpoint which can be closed.
Let g(z,y) be actual Boolean on the independent uniform physical cube. Suppose
both positive laws of S_Y given S_z=b are coordinate products with only fair
varying marginals and deterministic frequency coordinates; arbitrary Fourier
signs are allowed. Then

    H(g)<=2 I(g).

When both branches are positive, their probabilities are exactly1/2.
Writing m_b for their fair counts and a_b for their mean physical Y-frequency
degrees, the FULL entropy and influence are

    H(g)=1+(m_0+m_1)/2,
    I(g)=1/2+(a_0+a_1)/2,
    a_b>=m_b/2.

This gives the claimed bound without discarding either branch's entropy.
Both branch spectra and the single physical z account are retained. This is
an entropy identity, not a stopping-tree payment and not a statement that the
partial rows are Boolean.

Proof of the probability assertion: the two partial Fourier rows
u_b(y)=[g(+1,y)+(-1)^b g(-1,y)]/2 take values0,+/-1 and have disjoint
physical supports partitioning Y. Let their probabilities be q_b=E u_b^2.
Each fair-product spectrum is constant-magnitude on a coordinate frequency
face of size2^m_b. Its raw Walsh magnitude c_b is an integer. An odd divisor
of c_b would divide every physical value of u_b after power-of-two inverse
Walsh division, impossible since a positive row has a value+/-1. Therefore
c_b is a power of two and q_b=2^m_b c_b^2/2^(2 dimY) is a power of two.
Two positive powers of two summing to1 must each be1/2. If one branch is
zero, the remaining whole spectrum is fair product and H<=2I directly.
The proof includes arbitrary fixed-one frequency characters, which add energy.

## First unsupported line

If either conditional frequency branch has a nonfair product marginal,
its norm can have an odd factor. The dyadic norm argument no longer forces
q_0=q_1=1/2. The missing payment is the FULL branching term
h2(q_1), together with both complete conditional spectra, from the actual
physical branch influence q_1 and genuinely unused conditional-row energy,
while retaining the signed address/Renyi gap and any inherited terminal debts.
An unconditional Renyi/I estimate is not substituted for this line. For a
general height-two address row the frequency branches may be half-integral,
even before this nonfair extension; its scaled physical height must also be
carried. NEXT targets precisely that unequal-mass partial-row interface,
rather than another flat spectrum, fixed dimension or quadratic phase family.
