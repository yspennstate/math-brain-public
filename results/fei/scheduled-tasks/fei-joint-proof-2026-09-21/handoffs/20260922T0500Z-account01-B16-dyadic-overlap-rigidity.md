# FEI handoff addendum — dyadic Boolean rigidity of the balanced overlap envelope

For a genuine Boolean restriction pair on a **uniform** remaining cube, equality in the balanced-pivot scalar joint-credit relaxation
\[
\mathbb E_T\Phi(X)\ge\Phi(|1-2\delta|)
\]
can occur at a nontrivial disagreement density only when
\[
\boxed{\delta=1/2.}
\]
Proof: equality forces \(c_S=1-2\delta\) on every active spectral atom. With \(g=(f_0+f_1)/2\), \(d=(f_0-f_1)/2\), \(N=2^m\), \(k=N\delta\), integer Walsh sums give
\[
kA_S^2=(N-k)B_S^2.
\]
Thus \((N-k)/k\) is a rational square. Writing the ratio in lowest squares makes \(N=s(a^2+b^2)\); because \(N\) is a power of two and \(a,b\) are coprime positive integers, this forces \(a=b=1\), hence \(k=N/2\).

So every sparse dyadic defect \(0<k<N/2\) earns **strictly positive Booleanity credit** beyond the sharp non-Boolean scalar envelope. This is exact but not yet quantitative dimension-independently; the next task is to turn this dyadic rigidity into a lower bound strong enough for the live \(C_*=6.5218512\) local margin, or construct a Boolean near-isoclinic sequence showing such a bound is false.
