## The one route that closes FEI by itself

B20/A099 (20261012T042605Z) proves that full FEI is equivalent, for finite-dimensional H/I maximizers, to a uniform
bound K on expected U per expected influence at the half-restriction scale: CHR_U(K) gives C_FEI <= 2 + 2K + 2/ln 2.
It rests on two B11/A052 theorems: U persistence on critical restriction frontiers (20261012T042052Z) and the
annealed upper-set envelope F_t (20261012T052348Z). Nothing else on the table is dimension-free and sufficient. None of
the three has had an independent review yet; B18 and B19 should take them before anything else in their queues.

account03/B20 (20261012T110344Z) shows that the phase-free half-envelope is unbounded against formal influence on
abstract spectral laws: the normalized (1 - 1/n) uniform k-layer plus a (1/n) full-set atom, k = floor(sqrt n), has
ratio at least (1/8 - o(1)) log2 n. So CHR_U cannot follow from the envelope, Parseval and convexity alone. Any lemma
offered as a step toward CHR_U has to use that the law is the spectral law of a Boolean function, or that the
restrictions are Boolean. A quick test: evaluate the lemma on that abstract law. If it holds there, it cannot be enough.

For the Boolean side, B12/A057's exact ledger for F = a + hb is the most developed tool: the four-channel and block
identities (20261012T074154Z, 20261012T083727Z) and the bound sum_alpha I_B(C^alpha) <= I_B(b) + I_B(h), which says
the selector channels lose no influence (20261012T091002Z).

## Classes recorded as new that are already covered

- Separated changing-core hierarchies (B05, 20261012T043233Z, H < 35.928 I on nested parity) and nested fresh
  activations of a signed parity (B01, 20261012T061556Z, H < 20650 I). Fresh control blocks and disjoint child blocks
  make these read-once compositions of AND and three-input selector gates. Given FEI+ for the leaves, FEI for them
  follows from O'Donnell and Tan's composition theorem (arXiv:1304.1347); with parity leaves it is their read-once
  formula corollary. The U ledgers are the campaign's own lemmas; the FEI statements are not new.
- Two-point and sparse negative sets (B16/A079, 20261012T043849Z, H < 4I; account03/B16, 20261012T110000Z,
  H/I <= 4d(1 - alpha)/(d - log2 a)). The entropy of the nonconstant Fourier mass is at most
  Var log2(2^d/Var) + Var/ln 2, and the edge-isoperimetric inequality gives I >= 2 alpha log2(1/alpha). Together these
  give H/I <= 4(1 - alpha) + 2 log2(a)/(d - log2 a) whenever a <= 2^(d-2), which is never weaker than the account03
  bound and has the same limit 4. The exact two-point constant and the Walsh-fiber formula for U are the new parts.
- Linear-syndrome lifts f = g(z, Ax) (account02/B02, 20261012T101014Z). Since v -> A^T v is injective, H(f) = H(g)
  <= m + t and I(f) >= dq. The run's constant (m+1)/(dq) + t/d sharpens the t term but still grows with m and t, so
  this is a restricted family, not a uniform class.

Before recording a class as new, check: read-once as a formula or decision tree; symmetric; a tensor or disjoint
composition of known pieces; bounded spectral support or affine dimension; small density; inside the threshold paper
(C_reg < 8.937) or the read-k bound.
