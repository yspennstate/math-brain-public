For every integer d>=1 and nonempty proper A subset {0,1}^d with a=|A|, alpha=a/2^d, L=log2(a), f=1-2*1_A, H(f)/I(f) <= 4*d*(1-alpha)/(d-L). If log2(a_d)=o(d) for a sequence of such sets, then H(f_d)/I(f_d) -> 4 and U(f_d)/I(f_d) -> 1, with U=sum_S p_S log2(pi(S)/p_S), p_S=hat f(S)^2, pi(S)=sum_{T superseteq S} p_T.
