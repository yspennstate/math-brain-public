## Takeaways

1. **Do not use a uniform one-variable pointwise stationary-phase bound for arbitrary bounded densities.**  For every fixed nonzero `s`, the supremum of `|int e^{isx^2}f(x)dx|` over probability densities `0<=f<=1` is exactly `1`.  Phase-matched disconnected supports give the obstruction.

2. **Exact replacement in the aligned case.**  If `Y=lambda X^2`, then
   `||p_Y||_{3/2} <= 2 |lambda|^{-1/3}`.
   Hence three independent aligned square terms with `|lambda_j|>=1` have convolution density bounded by `8`, for every sign pattern.  Remaining independent diagonal terms cannot increase `L^infinity`.

3. **Do not rotate and retain only a joint-density `L^infinity` bound.**  Arbitrary bounded joint densities on thin remote spherical shells make the `|y|^2` pushforward density arbitrarily large.  The original product structure must remain active.
