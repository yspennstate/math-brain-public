# Interactive QFORM handoff: arbitrary self-interaction in two physical hubs

Let \(H\in\mathbb R^2\) be independent of \(Z=(Y_1,Y_2,V)\); assume their **joint** densities satisfy \(f_H\le1\) and \(g_Z\le M\). In particular, two untouched original independent cap-one hubs qualify. For any finite measurable hub self-function \(R(H)\), arbitrary finite measurable light functions \(F(Y),G(Y)\), and any nonzero real \(\lambda\), the form
\[
 Q=R(H)+2H\cdot Y+\lambda V^2+2V G(Y)+F(Y)
\]
has a continuous density vanishing at infinity and
\[
 \|p_Q\|_\infty\le14.6818\ldots M^{2/5}|\lambda|^{-1/5}
 <16M^{2/5}|\lambda|^{-1/5}.
\]
Thus an arbitrary \(H^{\mathsf T}BH\), for every symmetric \(B\) and all affine hub terms, costs **nothing** in this analytic receiver. It is uniform in fragmented/unbounded laws and either sign of \(\lambda\). A full-rank product light projection \(Z=TX'\) has \(M\le2^{3/2}/\sqrt{\det(TT^{\mathsf T})}\) by the cited product-marginal theorem.

**Proof currency:** Complete the square in \(V\) via a measurable Jacobian-one shear. With \(G_t(y)=e^{itF(y)}\int g(y,v)e^{it\lambda v^2}dv\), two-dimensional Plancherel and \(\|f_H\|_2\le1\) give \(|\varphi_Q(t)|\le\pi|t|^{-1}\|G_t\|_2\). The hub self-term is merely modulus-one phase. For fixed \(y\), the pushforward of \(g(y,v)dv\) under \(v\mapsto\lambda v^2\) places at most \(2M\sqrt{r/|\lambda|}\) mass in any interval of length \(r\). A Gaussian Fourier majorant and interval partition prove
\(\int_{-T}^T\|G_t\|_2^2dt\le30M\sqrt{T/|\lambda|}\).
On the dyadic shell \(T\le|t|\le2T\), Cauchy--Schwarz yields
\(\int|\varphi_Q|\le\pi\sqrt{30}\,2^{1/4}\sqrt M|\lambda|^{-1/4}T^{-1/4}\).
The shells sum; optimizing the low-frequency cutoff gives the constant above.

**Higher hubs:** The same argument for \(k\ge2\) joint cap-\(L\) hubs and a cap-\(M\) light \((Y\in\mathbb R^k,V)\) proves a density bound \(C_k(LM)^{2/(2k+1)}|\lambda|^{-1/(2k+1)}\), with arbitrary hub self-function, light \(F(Y)\), and \(2VG(Y)\).

**What is still unpaid:** The theorem needs an actual split into independent physical hubs and a three-statistic light bank with full-rank projection, a transverse square, and all other light terms confined to \(F(Y)+2VG(Y)\). The bare condition \(s_3(A)\ge1\) does not establish such a split, and extra dense light quadratic terms can violate the receiver's normal form. Do not state this as the full conjecture. It directly removes the prior analytic obstacle that a nonzero quadratic hub block invalidated the two-linear-hub conditional-density argument.
