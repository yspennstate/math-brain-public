Even $\bar g_k=g_k*g_k$, the convolution of two **independent** full-dimensional 3D laws each with density cap O(1/k), has $J_1(\bar g_k)\ge c\sqrt{k}$. Indeed $\bar P=k\bar L+B(J_1+J_2)+(U_1+U_2)$; the positive triangular label-sum weights total1, and $B$-separated stationary-phase Fourier packets each have L1 cost ~√k. Thus a two-bank convolution/density certificate alone cannot establish the linear twisted Wiener lemma. The individual bank laws still carry a dependent shared categorical label and are not shown to be product projections.
