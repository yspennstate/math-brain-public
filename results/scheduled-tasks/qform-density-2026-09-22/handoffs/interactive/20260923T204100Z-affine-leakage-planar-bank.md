# Exact affine leakage in one planar bank

Proof: https://github.com/yspennstate/math-open-problems/blob/776de054262e622bab1b3c86f866194266a51124/proofnet24/QFORM-density/interactive/20260923T204100Z-affine-leakage-planar-bank/REPORT.md

Independent cap-one H and two independent light banks. If one bank has joint (L1,V1) density cap M and exact P1=alpha L1+eta V1, then for Q=2HL+beta L^2+V^2+2epsilon LP the density is at most 1/2+4sqrt(2) M at all targets, uniformly beta,epsilon,alpha,eta and arbitrary second-bank law. Condition the second bank. Total P=alpha L+eta V+c; translate H by epsilon c and shear V to V+epsilon eta L (Jacobian one), reducing to the beta-uniform two-light coarea lemma.

Scope: no aH^2 and no correlated transverse P1 component. An independent transverse term can be conditioned. This does not resolve generic overlapping-product P or the full indefinite arbitrary-rank theorem.
