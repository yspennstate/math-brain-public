# Curved dyadic product tube benchmark (2026-09-23)

**Axis case proved; rotated diffuse case conjectural; full QFORM remains open.** [Exact report](https://github.com/yspennstate/math-open-problems/blob/728e325d9c432026bd364c676c6b293f13b41256/proofnet24/QFORM-density/interactive/20260923T202000Z-curved-dyadic-product-tube/REPORT.md).

For mutually independent cap-one L,V,P, any |γ|≥1, r,a>0, arbitrary c and v interval B, the curved tube T={r≤|L|≤2r, V∈B, |P+c+(γ+V²)/(2L)|≤a} has P(T)<87 a r². Split |γ+v²|≥1/2: conditioned on v,p, l↦(γ+v²)/(2l) has derivative ≥1/(16r²), so l total permitted length ≤64ar². On complement γ≤−1 and |v|≥1/√2; conditioned on l,p, v derivative≥1/(2√2r), v permitted length≤8√2ar, and l shell length2r. No support/moment assumptions.

A *potential* dimension-uniform route asks for P(T)≤C_κ a r^{1+α}, α>0, under a 3D Parseval light frame U and product original coordinates, with a two-bank Gram split U_IᵀU_I,U_JᵀU_J≽κI. This would suppress generic cap O(ar) dyadic log accumulation, but neither the bound nor the asserted bank split from small row leverage is established. Even/odd rows of the m≥8 divisible-by-4 circle frame split exactly at κ=1/2. Remote comb laws and dependence under rotation are critical stress tests.

Added a proved product-preserving physical-triple extension: if some three original coordinate rows of the light frame form a 3×3 matrix M with σmin(M)≥κ, then P(T)≤[192√3/κ+48√6/κ²] ar² after conditioning all other originals, uniformly in translations. On |γ+v²|≥1/2, some original derivative of F=p+c+(γ+v²)/(2l) is ≥κ/(16√3r²); one-variable area formula (quadratic/linear multiplicity≤2) yields the first term. On its complement |v|≥1/√2, the exterior Jacobian of (l,F) along some physical pair is ≥κ²/(2√6r); two-variable area formula with quadratic multiplicity≤2 yields the second. This scales like κ^-2 and remains insufficient for diffuse frames whose every physical triple is ill-conditioned.

Crucial affine-shear caveat: βL²+2εLP induces dL with d=β/(2ε) when solving for P. The ar² benchmarks omit d and are false uniformly over d. Explicit independent cap-one L,V,P with l0=3r/2, γ=−1, d=−1/(2l0²), c=1/l0 has F_d=[v²−(1−l/l0)²]/(2l), a saddle with exact crossing lines. A region l=l0+s, 4l0^{3/2}≤s≤l0^{5/4}, |v−s/l0|≤l0²/(16s), contributes P(tube)≥(a l0²/4)[(1/4)log(1/l0)−log4]≈a r²log(1/r) for 0<a≤1/10. Thus α=1 strict uniform tube exponent fails with arbitrary d; α<1 remains plausible and sufficient to sum dyads. This is a dyadic-lemma falsifier only, not QFORM counterexample.
