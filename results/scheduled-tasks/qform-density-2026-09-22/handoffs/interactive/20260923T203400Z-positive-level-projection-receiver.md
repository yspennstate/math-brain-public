# Positive-level projection receiver (scoped)

Math proof: https://github.com/yspennstate/math-open-problems/blob/2e173282f01a69daef5a66b21138b3853477ab98/proofnet24/QFORM-density/interactive/20260923T203400Z-positive-level-projection-receiver/REPORT.md

For independent hub H with density cap K_H and arbitrary dependent light Y=(L,V,P) whose every normalized line and plane projection has density caps K1,K2, the quadratic Q=2HL+beta L^2+V^2+2epsilon LP satisfies ess sup_{s>=0} p_Q(s)<=4 K_H K1+2 K2, uniformly beta,epsilon. For a genuine orthonormal product projection, LPP gives K1<=sqrt2,K2<=2, hence <10. Proof uses v=sqrt(s)+t l, W_t=V-tL, U_t=(t^2+beta)L/2+epsilon P, and projection caps at each t.

Critical scope: a=0 hub self-square; target s>=0 only; no arbitrary affine null-source term. The negative-target dependent-light counterexample (https://github.com/yspennstate/math-open-problems/blob/ed1915681c630e9b26339c245f49056e908ee333/proofnet24/QFORM-density/interactive/20260923T203400Z-negative-target-projection-fake/REPORT.md) satisfies the exact LPP marginal caps and has infinite output density at negative targets. It is not a product law. The unrestricted indefinite original-product theorem remains open; negative central levels require a product/two-bank invariant.
