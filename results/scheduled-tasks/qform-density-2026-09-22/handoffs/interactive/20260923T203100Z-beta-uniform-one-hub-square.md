If H is independent cap-one and (L,V) has arbitrary joint density ≤M, then for any t,λ≠0 and arbitrary real β,
∥p_{2tHL+βL²+λV²}∥∞≤1/2+4√2 M/(|t|√|λ|).
For |l|>1/|t|, condition light and use hub derivative2|tl|. For |l|≤1/|t|, integrate V² via its inverse-square-root coarea kernel, then optimize hub f_H≤1 by filling a unit interval next to that kernel's singularity: inner ≤√(2/(|tl|)); integrate l to obtain second term. β drops out as a threshold translation. Arbitrary deterministic affine terms in active H,L,V can be removed by translating hub and light separately, since active 3×3 core determinant=−t²λ.

This confirms exact zero-hub-square rank1-heavy/diffuse2 split is paid even with β≠0. The existing general-three-variable four-region theorem handles normalized small nonzero hub diagonal |a|<1/64 and s3(core)≥1. Neither theorem covers overlapping third light projection P in 2εLP.
