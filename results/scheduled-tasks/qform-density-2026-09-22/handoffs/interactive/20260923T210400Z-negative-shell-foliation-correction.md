**Correction.** The negative-target dependent-light shell summary formerly reversed the direction used to foliate an affine plane. The numerical all-plane bound itself is valid. The corrected source is [the read-back report at commit d1edac5](https://github.com/yspennstate/math-open-problems/blob/d1edac5d325b9135bad569e769500db52dab5167/proofnet24/QFORM-density/interactive/20260923T203400Z-negative-target-projection-fake/REPORT.md). The earlier [detailed line-section report](https://github.com/yspennstate/math-open-problems/blob/master/proofnet24/QFORM-density/interactive/20260923T192452Z-independent-hub-all-projections-fake/REPORT.md) already gave the correct foliation.

Given a plane \(\Pi\), choose a horizontal in-plane unit vector \(w=(w_l,w_v,0)\), and an in-plane unit vector \(t\perp w\). Foliate \(\Pi\) by lines parallel to \(t\), indexed by the transverse coordinate \(x\cdot w\). The independently audited whole-union line-section estimate gives length at most \(160\) on each fiber. As the entire shell union lies in \(0\le l,v\le1\), the transverse coordinate spans an interval of length at most \(|w_l|+|w_v|\le\sqrt2\). Hence \({\cal H}^2(E\cap\Pi)\le160\sqrt2\), uniformly even for nearly vertical planes. Both the 2D and 1D projection caps therefore survive this wording correction.

**Scope.** The shell is a counterexample only to a projection-cap-based sufficient lemma. Its light triple is dependent and is not represented as an orthonormal projection of independent cap-one physical coordinates. It is **not** a counterexample to the original product-law conjecture. The original conjecture remains open.

It does not control sparse matched bank pairs and does not prove the full density conjecture.
