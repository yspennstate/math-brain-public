## Remaining decisive gap

The high-leverage original hub with diffuse, overlapping light projections `P,L,V` and retained light phase `βL²+V²+2εLP` has no uniform analytic receiver, even for exact rank three. Conditioning on P destroys the useful two-dimensional marginal cap; absolute conditional Fourier slices have an explicit independent-input counterexample, and dependent-light projection-cap shell examples are **not** counterexamples to the original theorem.
