- G_H≽I/12: volume sample a physical triple inside H; three-dimensional core gives ≤263160/σ.

The remaining branch has λmin(G_H)<1/12 and λmin(G_R)<1/12, so a heavy-dominated spectral direction coexists with a diffuse-dominated spectral direction.
