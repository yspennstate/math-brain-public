# Uniform shift-stable product-hub L² invariant (2026-09-23)

**Proved supporting result; full bounded-density target remains open.** [Rigorous report](https://github.com/yspennstate/math-open-problems/blob/5f6c29192d4f6ca5376d24435686029bbbdc17b5/proofnet24/QFORM-density/interactive/20260923T200500Z-shift-stable-product-l2/REPORT.md).

If H is independent with cap-one density, (L,V,P) is any dependent light triple with (L,V) joint density g≤M, and u(L,P) is any measurable hub shift, then Z=L(H+u(L,P)) has joint density k(z,v) with V satisfying ∥k∥₂²≤4M. The sharp constant follows by expanding ∫k² and bounding overlap of two scaled and independently shifted hub densities by min(|l|,|l′|); the remaining radius kernel 1/max(|l|,|l′|) is at most 4M after integrating each v section. Taking u=βL/2+εP preserves any β and ε with no P independence assumption. Equality for M=1 occurs with independent H,L,V each uniform[-1/2,1/2] and u=0; p_HL(z)=−2log(4|z|) for |z|<1/4.

It does not control p_{2Z+V²}(s)=(1/2)∫k((s−v²)/2,v)dv: restriction of an L² planar density to a parabola can be arbitrarily large. The dependent-light fake already shows caps alone fail; an additional product-provenance, oscillatory, or curvature argument is still required.

A later addendum proves for every 1≤p<∞ the uniform shift-stable joint bound ∥k∥_p≤p(2M)^{1−1/p} by Minkowski and rearrangement in each v section. For independent H,L,V uniform[-1/2,1/2], ∥k∥_p^p=2^{p−1}Γ(p+1), so the linear p growth is sharp in order. The sharp p=2 constant remains 2√M. Even all finite-p bounds do not provide a curved trace estimate.
