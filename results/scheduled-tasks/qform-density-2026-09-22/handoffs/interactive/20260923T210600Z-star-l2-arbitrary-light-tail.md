# Arbitrary light-tail star L² receiver

Published/read-back theorem: https://github.com/yspennstate/math-open-problems/blob/553dfd3bdaec63090ed462311eb01b97dc2a68bf/proofnet24/QFORM-density/interactive/20260923T210600Z-star-l2-arbitrary-light-tail/REPORT.md

If H is independent of Y with density cap K, L(Y) has marginal cap M, and R(Y) is any measurable light-only term, then Z=cH L+uH+R(Y), c≠0, has ||p_Z||₂²≤4KM/|c|. The conditional hub densities are shifted by R(Y), but the overlap in the L² calculation is at most K min(|cL|,|cL'|), independent of those shifts. Layer cake gives E[1/max(|L|,|L'|)]≤4M. No moment/support assumptions.

Two independent such stars convolve to density cap ≤4√(K1M1K2M2/(|c1c2|)); any further independent term is harmless. For prior two-star family c1=4,c2=2,M1=M2=√2,Kj=1, this is exactly2, now allowing arbitrary within-star light quadratic tails and original affine terms (light terms in R, hub affine uH). Hub self-squares and cross-block residual terms remain outside. The full QFORM theorem is open.
