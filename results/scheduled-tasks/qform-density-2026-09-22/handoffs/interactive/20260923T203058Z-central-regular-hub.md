If the independent cap-one hub has N=∫|φ_H(t)|/√|t|dt<∞ and two independent light banks have planar (L,V) density caps M2A,M2B, then Q=2HL+βL²+V²+2εLP has full density ≤1/2+C√(M2A M2B)N, uniformly ε, β, target s, and support locations. Dyadic r≤1 contribution is ≤C√r N. Fourier in P plus unrestricted-V Fresnel, bank fixed-frequency planar Plancherel, and ζ=εt gives exact ε cancellation. The ε=0 case has unconditional physical coarea bound C√r.

Rademacher trigonometric-polynomial construction in the report proves N is unbounded among cap-one hubs, so absolute Fourier endpoint cannot finish the general case. Exact gap: central |s|<1, r≫|ε|, overlapping P, rough hub. Small |V|≤R√r already costs at most C M2 R√r; large-V compensation is the issue. No admissible product counterexample found.
