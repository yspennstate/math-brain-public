# Small physical hub-square endpoint paid

Analytic proof: https://github.com/yspennstate/math-open-problems/blob/master/proofnet24/QFORM-density/interactive/20260923T191000Z-small-hub-square-coarea/REPORT.md
Math commit: b7fc1ee93f4d8584a7f97d5d6e722381d0641f64.

For independent cap-one physical H and a two-dimensional joint light density g<=M, the quadratic Q=aH^2+2tHL+beta L^2+lambda V^2 has density at most 1+32M if |a|<1/64, t>=sqrt(1-a^2), |lambda|>=1, and ||[[a,t],[t,beta]]^(-1)||<=1. The proof splits into three coarea derivative regions and a fixed compact region; Gaussian domination of the compact ball gives a uniform slab-volume bound below 27 times the interval length. No support or moment condition, and no independence between L,V, is assumed.

The exact-rank-three no-pivot/no-rank-three-crosscut classification supplies M=2 by the original product projection theorem, all matrix parameter bounds, and a genuinely independent original physical H. Consequently this formerly unpaid branch has ||p_Q||infinity<65, including nonzero a. The full conjecture remains OPEN for rank-three crosscuts with vanishing crossing volume and arbitrary additional spectrum.

The receiver extends to any nondegenerate 3x3 symmetric quadratic core with s3>=1 and one small physical hub diagonal, paired with independent cap-one hub and arbitrary 2D joint-cap-M light input: orthogonally rotate the light pair so hub row is (t,0), use z=(-gamma/t,0,1) to prove |lambda|>=1, and take the Schur inverse block to obtain ||K^(-1)||<=1.
