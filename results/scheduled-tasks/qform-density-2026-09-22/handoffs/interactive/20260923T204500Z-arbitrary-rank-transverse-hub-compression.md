# Arbitrary-rank one-hub transverse compression

Published proof (sharpened): https://github.com/yspennstate/math-open-problems/blob/f442cbc6315eeb17b8c7e8c90278ac7ee7127ac4/proofnet24/QFORM-density/interactive/20260923T204500Z-arbitrary-rank-transverse-hub-compression/REPORT.md

For a literal original-coordinate hub/light split A=[[a,w^T],[w,D]], w≠0, set e=w/||w|| and P=I−ee^T. Then **s3(A)≤max(|a|,||PDP||op)**. Delete a e_h e_h^T and PDP; the remaining matrix has rank at most two, while the deleted pieces form orthogonal blocks, so the error norm is exactly the maximum. Consequently, s3(A)≥σ and |a|<σ force ||PDP||op≥σ without quantitative loss. Arbitrary rank, inertia, other spectrum, and dimension allowed.

This is a spectral reduction only. It does not itself provide a product-compatible physical square bank. The full bounded-density theorem remains open, particularly for overlapping P leakage at negative targets. Affine terms are unrestricted in the spectral lemma, but an analytic density receiver must handle them separately.
