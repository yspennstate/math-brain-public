## New theorem

Let `A=R^T Lambda R` have rank `r>=3`, let

`D=(prod_{k=1}^r |lambda_k|)^(1/r)`,

and assume `max_j ||P e_j||_2<=1/2`. Then the law of `Q=X^TAX` has a continuous density and

`||p_Q||_inf <= [B^2/(4 pi^2 D)] * r/(r-2)`.

Proof: pair the projected-law Wiener bound with the exact regularized Fresnel transform. For `t!=0`,

`|phi_Q(t)| <= B^r (4 pi |t|)^(-r/2) |det Lambda|^(-1/2)
              = [B/sqrt(4 pi D |t|)]^r`.

Integrating `min(1,(alpha/|t|)^(r/2))`, `alpha=B^2/(4 pi D)`, gives

`int |phi_Q| <= 2 alpha r/(r-2)`,

hence Fourier inversion yields the displayed density bound. If `D>=1`, the universal bound is `3B^2/(4pi^2)`.
