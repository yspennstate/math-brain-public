## Proof mechanism

Given any target matrix `A`, diagonalize only the matrix and perturb its eigenvalues by arbitrarily small distinct nonzero amounts, pushing three selected threshold eigenvalues outward when `|lambda|=1`. This gives `B_m -> A` in operator norm with `B_m in G_3(n)`. If `R=sup_{x in K} ||x||`, then

`|x^T(B_m-A)x| <= R^2 ||B_m-A||op = eta_m ->0`.

`P_nu{P_A in I} <= P_nu{P_Bm in I^{eta_m}} <= C_genff (|I|+2 eta_m)`,
