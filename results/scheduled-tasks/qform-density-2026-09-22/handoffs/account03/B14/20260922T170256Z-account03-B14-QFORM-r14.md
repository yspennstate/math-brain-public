## Translation quotient

The worst-case singular-potential constant is invariant under `c -> c+2Bs`, because translating each physical marginal is a bijection of the admissible law classes and the additive constant is absorbed by `sup_t`. Therefore it depends only on `c mod Range(B)`, equivalently only on `P_{ker B} c`. For invertible `B`, all affine terms are removable at the worst-case-constant level; for singular `B`, only the kernel-affine component is genuinely essential.
