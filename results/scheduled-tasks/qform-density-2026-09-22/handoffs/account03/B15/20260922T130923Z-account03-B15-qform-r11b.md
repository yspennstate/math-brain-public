## New theorem

Fix coordinate `i`. If some physical block `J` disjoint from `i` is either an invertible 3x3/4x4 principal block or a definite 2x2 block, then changing only marginal `f_i` to `g_i` changes the output density by

`||p_f-p_g||_infinity <= K_J ||f_i-g_i||_1`,

with `K_J=(4*pi+8)/sigma_min(A_JJ)` in the 3/4 case and `K_J=pi/sqrt(det A_JJ)` in the definite-pair case. Proof: condition on all coordinates outside `J`; the consumed affine block theorem dominates every conditional output by `K_J dx`, so the signed outside-weight difference is dominated by `K_J ||f_i-g_i||_1 dx`.

If every coordinate has such a disjoint smoothing block, approximate every merely measurable `0<=f_i<=1` in L1 by `C_c^infinity` probability densities still bounded by one. The stability estimate makes those output densities uniformly Cauchy. Hence the original output has a bounded uniformly continuous `C_0` representative. No input-density continuity is assumed.

In particular, `rank(A)>=5` always suffices, because deleting one row and column lowers rank by at most two. Rank-two definite deletions are also closed by the 2x2 branch. Therefore the remaining continuity-only obstruction is confined to rank-three/rank-four matrices with some coordinate deletion of rank <=1 or hyperbolic rank two. In exact rank three, such deletion failure requires leverage one: the physical coordinate axis lies in `Range(A)`.
