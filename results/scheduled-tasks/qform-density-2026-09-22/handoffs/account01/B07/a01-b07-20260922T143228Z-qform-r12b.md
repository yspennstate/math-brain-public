## New arbitrary-residual cross-block theorem

Let `I,J` be disjoint ORIGINAL coordinate sets and let `B=A[I,J]` have rank exactly three, with thin SVD

`B=L Sigma R^T`.

For the left/right singular frames define Pluecker laws

`w^L_K=det(L_K)^2`, `w^R_H=det(R_H)^2`.

If there are basis laws `pi_L,pi_R` with coordinate inclusion marginals at most `1/2`, and relative entropies `D_L,D_R` with respect to those Pluecker laws, then

`||p_Q||_infinity <= 3e |det Sigma|^(-1/3) exp((D_L+D_R)/6).`

The density is continuous and vanishes at infinity. Importantly, **all self blocks, all outside couplings, all additional eigenvalues and both signs are arbitrary**.

Proof mechanism: condition outside `I union J`; one Cauchy--Schwarz removes the arbitrary self phases and gives

`|phi(t)|^2 <= E_D prod_{i in I}|hat f_i(2t[BD]_i)|`,

where `D=X_J-X'_J` has independent cap-one difference densities. Apply the partner entropy-BL theorem to the right singular frame to bound the density of `R^T D`, and again to the left singular frame to bound the remaining Fourier `L^1` integral. This yields

`|phi(t)| <= e^(3/2) pi^(3/2) |det Sigma|^(-1/2)
             exp((D_L+D_R)/4) |t|^(-3/2)`.

Integrating `min(1,K|t|^-3/2)` gives the displayed constant exactly.

For the canonical disjoint-basis laws, put

`delta_L=sum_{K cap K'=empty}w^L_Kw^L_K'`,
`delta_R=sum_{H cap H'=empty}w^R_Hw^R_H'`.

The partner theorem gives `D_L<=log(1/delta_L)`, `D_R<=log(1/delta_R)`, hence

`boxed(||p_Q||_infinity <=3e |det Sigma|^(-1/3)(delta_L delta_R)^(-1/6)).`

Since for rank three

`P_3(B)=e_3(BB^T)=|det Sigma|^2=sum det(B[K,H])^2`,

this is equivalently

`||p_Q||_infinity <=3e [P_3(B) delta_L delta_R]^(-1/6)`.

This is a genuine determinant-weighted selection result; it does not rely on one large minor or on a coordinate-leverage cap.
