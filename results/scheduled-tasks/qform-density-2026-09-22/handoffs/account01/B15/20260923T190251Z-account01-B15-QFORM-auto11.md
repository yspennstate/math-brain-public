`D_infty(mu)=inf{C: mu(E)<=C|E| for every Borel E}`,

`D_infty(mu)=sup_I mu(I)/|I|`

`mu_m => mu  ==>  D_infty(mu) <= liminf_m D_infty(mu_m)`.
