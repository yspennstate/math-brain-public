## New theorem

If, conditional on arbitrary `W`, two real packets are independent and have uniform characteristic-function collision budgets

`(2pi)^(-1) int |psi_{j,w}(t)|^2 dt <= M_j^2`,

then their sum plus an arbitrary `W`-measurable shift has an unconditional continuous `C_0` density with

`||p||_infinity <= M_1 M_2`.

Proof: integrate the unconditional characteristic function and apply Cauchy--Schwarz in the time variable before Fourier inversion.
