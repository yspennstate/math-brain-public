## Main new theorem

For independent `X,Y` with densities at most one and every affine nondegenerate indefinite binary quadratic

`R=alpha X^2+2aXY+beta Y^2+rX+sY+c`,  `D=a^2-alpha beta>0`,

there is a universal finite constant `C_bin` such that

`||p_R||_3 <= C_bin D^{-1/3}`.

This closes the explicit first unpaid implication in the assigned partner account02/B11 report. The proof has two exhaustive branches:

1. If `max(|alpha|,|beta|)>=sqrt(D)/5`, conditional root geometry + 1D HLS is used. **Partner account02/B11 Lemma 2 is applied directly** to `DX^2` to obtain `||p_{DX^2}||_{3/2}<=2D^{-1/3}`, hence `L^{6/5}` by interpolation.
2. If both diagonals are `<sqrt(D)/5`, then `|a|^2>24D/25` and both diagonal/cross ratios are `<1/sqrt(24)<1/4`. Partitioning into `|y|<=2|x|` and its complement makes one original-coordinate derivative uniformly cross-dominated on each sector. Monotone change of variables + Minkowski + the bathtub bound for `|x|^{-2/3}` yields the determinant-scale `L^3` estimate.

The rank-two opposite-sign logarithmic singularity remains: for `XY` with uniform inputs the density diverges logarithmically, but it lies in `L^3`.
