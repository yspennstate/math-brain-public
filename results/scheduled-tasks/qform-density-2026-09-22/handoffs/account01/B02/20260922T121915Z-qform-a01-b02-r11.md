## New theorem

For rank exactly three `A=U Lambda U^T`, let rows be `u_i`, and for a physical-coordinate subset `S` put `M_S=sum_{i in S} u_i u_i^T`. If `M_S>0` and every normalized leverage obeys

`u_i^T M_S^{-1} u_i <= 1/4`,

then, for arbitrary independent input densities <=1,

`|phi_Q(t)| <= min(1, K_RV (|det Lambda| det M_S)^(-1/2)|t|^(-3/2))`,

`||phi_Q||_1 <= 6 K_RV^(2/3)(|det Lambda|det M_S)^(-1/3)`,

and `Q` has a continuous density with

`||p_Q||inf <= (3/pi)K_RV^(2/3)(|det Lambda|det M_S)^(-1/3)`.

Here `K_RV=B_RV^3/(8 pi^(3/2))`, with `B_RV` the proof-level small-coordinate Fourier-algebra constant from Rudelson--Vershynin used in the completed partner result account02/B02 `QFORM-DELOCALIZED-RANK3-INTEGRATED-FOURIER-v1` (`math-open-problems@2d79dea715c2baa9e8ef6c0ac174eee1712d2888`). The proof conditions on `S^c`, normalizes the remaining frame by `M_S^{-1/2}`, completes the resulting deterministic linear term, and applies the partner Fresnel transfer uniformly over the conditioning.

## Finite-heavy reduction

For any `c in (0,1)`, define `L_c={i:||u_i||<=sqrt(c)/2}`, `H_c=L_c^c`, and `M_c=sum_{L_c}u_i u_i^T`. Then `|H_c|<12/c`. Either `lambda_min(M_c)>=c`, in which case the theorem gives a universal integrated-Fourier bound of size `6K_RV^(2/3)c^{-1}`, or there exists a unit spectral direction `v` with

`sum_{i in H_c}<u_i,v>^2>1-c`.

At `c=1/2`, failure of the diffuse certificate forces fewer than 24 physical coordinates to carry more than half of one spectral direction's squared mass.
