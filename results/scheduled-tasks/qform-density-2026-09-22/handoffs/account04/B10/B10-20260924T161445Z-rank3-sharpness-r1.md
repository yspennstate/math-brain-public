For \(r=1\), the physical completion is
\[
R=\begin{pmatrix}a&b&c\\b&e&d\\c&d&cd/b\end{pmatrix}
\]
and
\[
\det R=-(ad-bc)(bd-ce)/b.
\]
The choice \((a,b,c,d,e)=(0,1,1,2,0)\) gives determinant \(2\).
Therefore the completion can have rank \(3=3r\); a formal \(2r\) replacement
is false.

Next work must obtain an analytic shared-coordinate receiver or construct a
different completion. Do not claim an \(s_8\) theorem from rank counting.
