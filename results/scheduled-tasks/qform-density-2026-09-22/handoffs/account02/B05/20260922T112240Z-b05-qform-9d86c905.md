## Main B05 geometry result

For even `n`, take the cube product density `F=1_[0,1]^n` and the orthonormal plane `E=span(r1,r2)` where `r1=(1,...,1)/sqrt(n)` and `r2` is `+1/sqrt(n)` on the first half and `-1/sqrt(n)` on the second half. Then

`int_{E^perp} sup_{z in E} F(w+z) dw = Vol_{n-2}(P_{E^perp}[0,1]^n) = n/2`.

The zonotope/Hodge formula is

`Vol = sum_{i<j}|r1_i r2_j-r1_j r2_i|`;

only cross-half pairs contribute, each by `2/n`, and there are `n^2/4` of them. Therefore the tempting same-sign spectral-plane coarea completion

`p_Q(y) <= pi int_{E^perp} sup_{z in E} F(w+z)dw`

has an unavoidable `Theta(n)` loss. This is a proof-mechanism counterexample, not a target counterexample. It identifies the missing factor precisely: fiberwise supremum discards the third large direction and turns product normalization into projected-cube volume.

## Product-preserving replacement resource

For `phi_A(t)=E exp(itX^TAX)` and an independent copy `X'`, define

`K_i(u,eta)=(1/2) int e^{i eta v} f_i((v+u)/2)f_i((v-u)/2)dv`.

Then exactly

`|phi_A(t)|^2 = int prod_i K_i(u_i,t(Au)_i)du`.

If `h_i(u)=int f_i(x)f_i(x-u)dx`, then `|K_i|<=h_i`, `int h_i=1`, `h_i<=1`, and

`int int |K_i(u,eta)|^2 du deta = pi (int f_i^2)^2 <= pi`.

Bare absolute-value `L^2` Brascamp--Lieb after one squaring still loses half the exponent: in the ideal three-transverse-coordinate scaling it yields at best `|phi|^2=O(|t|^-3/2)`, hence `|phi|=O(|t|^-3/4)`, not `L^1`. So the next inequality must couple the unit `u`-marginal masses with these phase-space budgets, or otherwise spend the third large direction without a square-root loss.

## Supporting lemma

For `0<=f<=K`, `Z=lambda X^2+bX+c`, `lambda!=0`, and `1<p<2`,

`||p_Z||_p <= [2^{p-1}/(2-p)]^{1/p} K^{2-2/p}|lambda|^{1/p-1}`.

At `p=3/2` this is `2 K^{2/3}|lambda|^-1/3`.
