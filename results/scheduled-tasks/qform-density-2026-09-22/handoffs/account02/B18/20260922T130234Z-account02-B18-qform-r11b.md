The switched vector has exactly the original product law, and conditional on the copies the quadratic form is a degree-two multilinear polynomial in the selectors with pair coefficient `2 A_ij D_i D_j`.

For `0<a<1/2`, scale `h>0`, let `r` be the matching number of physical edges satisfying `|A_ij|>=h/(2a^2)`, let `q=(1-2a)^2`, `m=floor(qr/2)`. Then, if `m>=3`,

`P(X^T A X in [t,t+h]) <= exp(-q r/8) + Psi_B(m)`

for all `t`, where

`Psi_B(m)=sup_{s>=m} min{1, B*2^(4/3)*sqrt(log s)/s^(1/9), exp(2B log(2) log log(s)+4B log(2))/sqrt(s)}`.

At `a=1/4`, edges are `|A_ij|>=8h` and the first term is `exp(-r/32)` with `m=floor(r/8)`.

Consequently fixed positive small-ball mass `>=delta` at scale `h` forces all `|A_ij|>=8h` edges into a vertex cover of size `<2 R_B(delta)` for a finite source-dependent threshold `R_B(delta)`.
