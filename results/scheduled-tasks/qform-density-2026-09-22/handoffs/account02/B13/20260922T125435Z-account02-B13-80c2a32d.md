## New theorem

Let `A=R Lambda R^T` have rank exactly three, `R^T R=I_3`, and all three nonzero eigenvalues have absolute value at least one. Suppose the nonzero rows of `R` lie in at most `m` projective directions. Then for arbitrary independent input densities bounded by one,

`||p_{X^T A X}||_infinity <= 2(2 pi+3 sqrt(3)) binom(m,3)`.

Proof mechanism: aggregate each row-direction class into one original-coordinate bank. This gives `A=C(V^T Lambda V)C^T`, where `C` has disjoint-support orthonormal columns and the aggregate coordinates `Y=C^T X` are independent with density cap `sqrt(2)` by Livshyts--Paouris--Pivovarov. The Parseval identity `V V^T=I_3` and Cauchy--Binet give an aggregate triple `I` with `det(V_I)^2>=1/binom(m,3)`. Since `V_I V_I^T<=I`, `sigma_min(V_I)>=|det V_I|`, hence the aggregate principal block satisfies `sigma_min(V_I^T Lambda V_I)>=1/binom(m,3)`. Conditioning all other aggregate variables and scaling the selected three by `sqrt(2)` reduces to the completed three-variable affine quadratic theorem.

Special cases:

- `m=3`: `||p_Q||_infinity <=4 pi+6 sqrt(3)<23`;
- `m=4`: `||p_Q||_infinity <=16 pi+24 sqrt(3)<92`.

This closes the account01/B19 hard family with spectrum `{1,1,-1}`, for which every useful physical principal triple degenerates like `1/m` and no disjoint rank-three cross block exists. The structural reason is that its delocalized frame is an exact three-bank Parseval frame.
