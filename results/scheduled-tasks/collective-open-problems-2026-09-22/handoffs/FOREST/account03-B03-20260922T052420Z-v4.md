## New exact result

For a host graphic matroid `M`, marked edges `e,f`, and unmarked edge `g`, replace `g` via a 2-sum by `Q_{k,a}`: distinguished edge `g=uv` plus `k` internally-disjoint `uv` paths of length `a>=2`. After the 2-sum the host edge is replaced by the `k` paths and a simple host remains simple. Put

`q_g = k/(2^a-1)`,  `D_g=(2^a-1)^k`.

For any collection `X` of unmarked edges,

`I(M[Q^X];1) = (prod_{g in X} D_g) I(M;q)`

and therefore

`Delta_{M[Q^X]}(1;e,f) = (prod D_g)^2 Delta_M(q;e,f)`.

The values `k/(2^a-1)` with `a>=2` are dense in `(0,infinity)`. Thus a weighted failure on a fixed host compiles to a unit failure on a larger **simple** graph, while weighted positivity of the host follows if all these expansions are unit-pNC.

This is the exact expansion mechanism in Erickson's 2008 thesis Proposition 5.2.2 / Corollary 5.2.3, described there as independently published by Cocks. The inspectable proof is **class-global / expanded-family**: it requires unit pNC of `M[Q^{E-{e,f}}]`; it does not by itself infer weighted pNC from the unit inequality on the original fixed `M`.
