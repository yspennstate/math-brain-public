## q-deformation

For a graph define

`W_q(w)=sum_{S subset E} q^{nu(S)} prod_{e in S} w_e`, `nu(S)=|S|-r(S)`.

For a graph on n vertices this is `q^{-n}` times the random-cluster partition function at cluster parameter q and activities `v_e=q w_e`. Thus q=0 is exactly the all-forest polynomial and q=1 is product measure. For `0<q<=1`, Branden--Huh's multivariate-Tutte Hodge--Riemann theorem applies after this positive diagonal variable scaling and gives log-concavity; at q=0 the ALOV homogenized-independent-set theorem applies directly to the graphic-matroid forest polynomial. None of these spectral inputs alone is asserted to imply entrywise Rayleigh signs.

Let v be cubic with neighbours u1,u2,u3, H=G-v, and let A,B12,B13,B23,C be the nonnegative q-weighted masses of subsets of H according to the terminal connectivity partitions `1|2|3, 12|3, 13|2, 23|1, 123`. For a terminal block B, the exact local factor is

`Psi_B = 1 + sum_{nonempty J subset B} q^(|J|-1) prod_{j in J} x_j`.

Hence

`Phi_q = A Psi_1 Psi_2 Psi_3 + B12 Psi_12 Psi_3 + B13 Psi_13 Psi_2 + B23 Psi_23 Psi_1 + C Psi_123`.

For marked star edges e1,e2 and third-star activity g>0, direct differentiation and collection gives

`Delta_q(e1,e2)=(1-q) R_{q,g}`

with

`R_{q,g} = (g+1)^2 B12(A+B12+B13+B23)`
`          +(g+1)(qg+2)B12 C`
`          +(g+1)(1-(1-q)g) A C`
`          +(1-q)g^2 B13 B23`
`          +(1+qg) C(B13+B23+C)`.

Therefore `Delta_q>=0` for every `0<=q<1` whenever `0<g<=1/(1-q)`; at q=1, `Delta_q=0` for arbitrary g. Outer-edge weights are arbitrary positive, and by multiaffinity the Rayleigh difference is independent of the two marked star activities, so those may also be arbitrary positive.

At q=0,g=1 this reduces exactly to the account03/B03 theorem:

`R_{0,1}=4 B12 T + (B13+C)(B23+C)`.

This supplies a source-valid local segment of the broader q<1 random-cluster negative-correlation problem without assuming the still-open general pairwise conjecture.

## Sources

- Petter Branden and June Huh, *Hodge-Riemann relations for Potts model partition functions*, arXiv:1811.01696. Their Theorem 1 gives the one-positive-eigenvalue/log-concavity statement for the homogeneous multivariate Tutte polynomial for `0<q<=1`.
- Nima Anari, Kuikui Liu, Shayan Oveis Gharan and Cynthia Vinzant, *Log-Concave Polynomials III: Mason's Ultra-Log-Concavity Conjecture for Independent Sets of Matroids*, arXiv:1811.01600. The homogenization of every matroid independent-set generating polynomial is completely log-concave; graphic independent sets are precisely forests.
- Pengfei Tang and Zibo Zhang, *Negative correlation for the random-cluster model below one on high-degree regular graphs*, arXiv:2609.11578 (2026-09-10). Their abstract explicitly states that even pairwise negative correlation remains open on arbitrary finite graphs for `0<Q<1`, so no global q-Rayleigh theorem is imported here.
