`support inequality <=> A(U)+S(U) >= Delta`,

assume `K_rho` has a unique parity-bad maximizer `rho0`. Define

- `m = min_i |G_{i,rho0(i)}|`,
- `q(tau) = # {i: tau(i) != rho0(i)}`,
- `kappa = min_{tau != rho0} (K* - K_tau)/q(tau)`.

Then for every unitary `U`,

`A(U)+S(U) >= 2 min(m,kappa)`.

Proof skeleton: if `E=sum_i(1-r_{i,rho0(i)})`, Birkhoff assignment slack gives `A>=kappa E`, while the exact odd-parity Hadamard-block inequality gives `S>=m(2-E)_+`; minimize their sum over `E in [0,3]`.
