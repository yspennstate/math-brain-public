## New exact lemma

For any width-<=3 poset and incomparable strong pair `p prec_* q`, put `E=e(P)`, `M=#{L:p<q}`, `N=E-M`, with `M>2E/3`. Swap the positions of `p,q` in each full extension. If `D_M,D_N` are the illegal-swap sets on the two orientations, the legal swaps are an involution, so

`|D_M|-|D_N| = M-N = 2M-E`, hence `|D_M|>E/3`.

Every outgoing defect has a Hasse witness in

`U={r:p lessdot r, r||q}` or `D={r:r lessdot q, r||p}`.

Width <=3 gives `|U|,|D|<=2`, so deterministic witness assignment has at most four channels. Therefore one channel carries `>E/12` marks. A `U` channel is contained in the genuine incomparable-pair event `{r<q}`; a `D` channel is contained in `{p<r}`. Under no-balancedness, the selected marked orientation either is strong-majority, giving the campaign's strict span-descent exit, or is minority and supplies an outward Hasse-witnessed pair with minority mass `>1/12`. This is entirely on the original uniform full-extension law; no deletion distribution appears.
