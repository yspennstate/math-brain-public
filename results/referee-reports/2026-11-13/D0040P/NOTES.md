## Final statement

For every beta > 0 and a > -1, choose independent d_i = chi_{beta(a+i)}/sqrt(beta) and ell_i = chi_{beta i}/sqrt(beta). The entrywise Jacobi matrix L = B B^T, for lower bidiagonal B with these entries, has actual leading blocks L_n = B_n B_n^T. Their ordered eigenvalue density is proportional to

Delta(x)^beta product_j x_j^{beta(a+1)/2-1} exp(-beta x_j/2), 0 < x_1 < ... < x_n.

All primitive laws and retained entries are independent of terminal size. In the original Dumitriu–Edelman theorem the shape is A_n = beta(n+a)/2, its auxiliary parameter is p_n = 1+beta(n-1)/2, and the effective column count is m_n = n+a. These three quantities depend on n. The random Jacobi matrix is essentially self-adjoint with nonnegative closure, and its spectral measure at e_1 gives one orthogonal-polynomial sequence with these zero laws.

The zero-diagonal Jacobi matrix with independent r_i^2 ~ Gamma(i beta/4, rate 1) likewise gives the antisymmetric Gaussian beta laws at every size. The positive eigenvalue powers are beta/2-1 at even sizes and 3 beta/2-1 at odd sizes. This answers the existential question positively for the stated families. The paper does not classify nesting potentials.

## Derivations and changes from the claim report

- Re-derived the finite identity before invoking a density: L_n = B_n B_n^T; the leading block of B^T B instead includes ell_n^2 e_n e_n^T. For C_n = R_n B_n^T R_n, C_n^T C_n = R_n L_n R_n; the raw DE eigenvalues are beta times the desired eigenvalues. The scale-change Jacobian is beta^n. The hard-edge exponent is A_n-p_n, not A_n itself.
- Added the full joint density of the leading Jacobi block in Cholesky coordinates. In interleaved coordinates the derivative matrix is lower triangular and the Jacobian is 2^n d_n product_{i<n} d_i^2. Dividing the primitive chi density gives powers d_i^{beta a-2} for i<n and d_n^{beta(a+n)-2}, together with product c_i^{beta i-1}. This density is not the canonical independent-Dirichlet endpoint model.
- Kept and re-derived the chi exponential tail bound and deficiency-vector proof of essential self-adjointness. Positivity is established on finite support, then passed to the closure. Thus the infinite product notation is entrywise and does not silently assert an unproved equality of unbounded product domains.
- Replaced the antisymmetric corollary's reliance solely on the published density by an explicit odd/even-coordinate proof. For even N, the squared positive eigenvalues come from the a=-1/2 Laguerre factor at rate 1. For odd N, the rectangular Gram matrix has an independent Cholesky law with a=1/2, derived by beta–gamma splitting. The change s_j=lambda_j^2 has Jacobian 2^m product lambda_j. This gives the stated parity powers without a hidden sqrt(2) scale.
- Retained the generalized Dirichlet-residue Jacobian Delta(y)/Delta(x) and all parameters s_i>0. Re-derived the ordinary Jacobi shifts using poles (0,y,1). For the Laguerre limit, used convergence of normalized top densities in total variation and contraction by the fixed Markov kernel. This avoids needing an additional domination argument across moving interlacing singularities.
- Extended the failed reversed Killip–Nenciu candidate to symmetric powers using the referee's variance calculation, independently re-derived from the beta second moments. For p != q, the wrong mean is 1/2+(p-q)/(2(S+beta)), with S=p+q+2. For p=q, the variance is 1/(4(S+beta+1)), not 1/(4(S+1)). The conclusion remains about this one candidate, not every possible beta-Jacobi construction.

The first requires the canonical Dirichlet endpoint vector independent of eigenvalues, as well as independence of the three entries at size two and V in C^2(R). Its spectral Jacobian is gap^2/(2b). The second is beta=2, on the entire real line, for a positive C^1 fixed weight with the stated moment and partition-function conditions, and requires the ordinary Dixon–Anderson link.
- Kept the dimension bound for absolutely continuous full arrays, with no entry-independence assumption, and the unitary conclusion only for actual unitary principal blocks. The paper does not deduce that every eigenvalue-only circular coupling is excluded.
- Added the three requested later operator references and distinguished their precise statements from all-size leading blocks. In particular, the attribution inside Theorem 21 of the Sine_beta paper is retained.
