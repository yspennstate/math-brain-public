E0022 proof audit, 13 November 2026

Status: PARTIAL; no general theorem or counterexample obtained.

1. Recomputed the exponent sign from E exp(i z.X_t)=exp(-t psi).
   Physical drift b contributes -i b.z. Removing a finite jump measure changes
   canonical a by +integral of its small jumps, yielding the d_S used in the proof.
2. Re-derived the covariance form from the full matrix, then checked 196
   rational cases. Its eigenvalues are 4 and 4 epsilon. The exceptional line
   has zero real exponent and nonzero linear imaginary exponent.
3. Independently exhibited the Gaussian example's thin nonpolar hyperplane:
   difference coordinate evolves deterministically, crossing it once.
4. Differentiated the ray sector ratio to obtain its maximum. Re-derived the
   angular singularity using half cot(delta), and verified the stable radial
   maximum and alpha=1 boundary separately.
5. Checked both energy-kernel denominator identities symbolically by expansion,
   then in 80 exact-rational cases. Dominated convergence is applied only with
   the ambient finite-energy majorant and the stated resolvent-density hypothesis.
6. Audited the compound-Poisson argument at jump endpoints as well as on holding
   intervals. Condition on J only; Y remains an independent Levy process.
   Reverse transfer uses a no-jump event of positive probability.
7. Audited the finite-off-subspace proof for Gaussian containment, drift removal,
   correct state space on S, and the absence of any independence assumption on
   the two observed projections. The independence used is justified by the
   Levy-Ito split, not by the projection hypothesis.
8. Matched Question 1 and Theorems 3.6, 3.7 and Remark 3.8 to the page-marked
   source and an independent pdftotext extraction. The 2018 lemma omits both a
   drift/complement condition and Gaussian containment; its literal repair by
   only an extra projection hypothesis is contradicted by Example 3.5.
9. Kept product-measure energy statements separate from the all-measures (H)
   criterion. Did not assume general sum/product closure or closure under weak
   convergence. Did not infer non-(H) from sector failure.
10. All source artifacts are UTF-8 plain text; source PDF bytes existed only in
    memory. Historical originals and the publisher's 2021 full survey were not
    all accessible; limitations are explicit in REPORT.md.
