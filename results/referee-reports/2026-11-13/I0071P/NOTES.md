## Proof reconstruction and changes from the claim report

Both reports were read in full before writing. The claim's proof survives the reconstruction. The paper includes the following details explicitly rather than appealing to an arbitrary convex mixed energy.

- **Polynomial and cone.** Differentiate the original multinomial coefficients: alpha_i binom(m,alpha) = m binom(m-1,alpha-e_i), and differentiate once more for the factor m(m-1). At degree m-1 the common coefficient factor is theta^{sum beta_i^2+1} t^beta. The backward recursion gives v_i >= sum_{j>i}(R_ji/R_jj)v_j for every such beta, including its extreme indices. Back substitution in R^T eta=v proves both eta_i >= 0 and eta_i <= v_i/R_ii. No positivity of R^{-1} is assumed. This proves reaction compatibility. Replacing r by r^+ is legitimate because lambda >= 0.
- **Hessian.** The quadratic exponent difference at beta+e_i+e_j and the two pure endpoints is exactly -2. Linear weights cancel in the ratio. Each coefficient matrix is a positive diagonal congruence of (1-rho)I+rho 11^T. Its normalized eigenvalues give the two form bounds, and finite-sum Cauchy–Schwarz gives H_ij <= rho sqrt(H_ii H_jj). This proof includes faces and the degenerate origin.
- **Coercivity and stochastic covariance.** Put V=max nu_i, B=max_i sup_x(sum_n |b_ni|^2)^{1/2}, K=V+B^2/2, and choose rho K(ell-1) <= mu/4. The diagonal leading contribution is at least mu D/2. The entire ordered off-diagonal sum is at most rho K(ell-1)D in absolute value. The remaining margin is mu D/4. This proves the claim's estimate (11), appearing as (4.4) in the paper, for this polynomial only. Setting B=0 separately identifies ordinary mixed-diffusion coercivity.
- **Lower noise constants.** Let Lambda=1+(ell-1)rho, L=max L_i, and N_m=m(m-1). Face vanishing gives ||g_i(y)|| <= L_i y_i. Euler's Hessian identity yields sum H_ii y_i^2 <= N_m Phi_m. The zero-order trace is at most Lambda L^2 N_m Phi_m; the cross term is at most Lambda B L sqrt(N_m D Phi_m). Young's inequality consumes mu D/8 and contributes exactly 2 Lambda^2 B^2 L^2 N_m Phi_m / mu. Correlations are retained throughout.
- **Itô formula.** The diffusion form is symmetrized with (nu_i+nu_j)/2; the transport/zero-order cross covariance has coefficient one. The local variational equation is justified on positive-time intervals stopped by spatial supremum and integral H^1 cutoffs. For spatial convolution, apply finite-dimensional Itô at each spatial point and stochastic Fubini, then pass through strong H^1 and square-summable L^2 noise convergence. This avoids assuming that the integral Nemytskii functional is twice continuously Fréchet differentiable on all of L^2. The convolved noise is J_epsilon(b grad u), without a commutation through b. No derivative of a Hölder field is taken.
- **Martingale and stopping.** The paper states the F_s starting event, deals with cutoffs already passed at s by making the stopped interval empty, and explains measurability of the range and accumulated H^1 cutoffs. Bounded polynomial derivatives and square-integrable stopped noise give a deterministic bracket bound C_N(T+N). Optional stopping is therefore applied to an actual stopped martingale.
- **Tails and dissipation.** The integrating factor is applied to 1+E_m and also retains the nonnegative weighted dissipation. Strict energy thresholds on the half-open lifetime avoid an unattained-supremum issue. The resulting tail is e^{C(T-s)} E[1_Gamma(1+E_m(s))]/(1+A). The dissipation expectation is at most (8/mu)e^{C(T-s)} times the same initial expectation. Exhaust Gamma_k={s<sigma,E_m(s)<=k}. This proves pathwise boundedness, without claiming an L^1 bound on the time supremum or using a BDG absorption.
- **Positivity and local theory.** The precise face conditions are checked against AV local Theorem 2.13 and survey Proposition 8.10. A negative-part drift calculation is included for bounded variational solutions. The cited positivity theorem supplies the extension to the stated rough traces. Polynomial Lipschitz extensions by y -> y^+ are specified. Hölder-to-Sobolev coefficient membership is checked by choosing delta-1<alpha<min(gamma,1) and finite varrho with d/varrho<alpha.
- **Continuation.** Choose h_0>max(h,1+4/d), q_0=d(h_0-1)/2>2, and 1<delta_0<min(1+gamma,(h_0+1)/h_0,d-2/(h_0-1)). Then p_0 large and kappa_0=p_0(1-delta_0/2)-1 give all strict local inequalities. The dimension-lifting corollary checks parabolicity in the dummy direction, Besov pullback along the Fourier axis, and rational translation invariance followed by positive-time continuity.
