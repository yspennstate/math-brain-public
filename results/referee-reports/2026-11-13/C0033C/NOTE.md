C0033 (concavity of determinantal-process entropy in the kernel), runs B and C on Mac mini 1, 12-13 Nov 2026.

C0033B (PARTIAL): joint concavity along one row and column in every dimension; concavity along indefinite rank-two
directions on three points; reduction of three-point concavity to the scalar inequality (17); interval certificate.

C0033C (PARTIAL): Theorem A, the singular stratum of R_Q is exactly the disconnected kernels, and there the Hessian is
negative semidefinite in every direction; Theorem B, H(Q0 + s e_i e_i^* + t b b^*) is jointly concave in every
dimension, so entropy is concave along every rank-two direction whose range contains a coordinate vector, with
D^2H[A,A] <= -4 alpha^2 for A = alpha e_i e_i^* + b b^*.

Open: inequality (17) on the connected strict three-point domain (general PSD rank-two directions on three points), and
general rank-two directions in higher dimensions. No counterexample. Neither run claims the conjecture, so neither was
sent to a referee; the proofs are unreviewed.
