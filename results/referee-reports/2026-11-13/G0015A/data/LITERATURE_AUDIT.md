# Literature audit, 2026-11-13

Primary source is Kumar and Duddala, arXiv:2608.21125v1, not Kumar and Dudeja. Theorem 6.1 is on PDF pp. 6–7; Conjecture 7.2 and Programme 7.3 on PDF p. 8. `sources/exact_requested_statements.tex.txt` contains unaltered downloaded TeX environments, including Theorem 5.1, to preserve exact hypotheses and formulas without PDF extraction errors. Full PDF-derived text is also archived. The source states no numerical formula for Sigma_c.

No retrieved printed theorem has been verified to imply the conjecture under exactly the source hypotheses. This is a bounded literature search, not an assertion that none exists.

- Miermont (2008), AIHP 44, 1128–1161: hypothesis (H), printed p. 1130, is finite-type irreducible nondegenerate critical reproduction with finite second moments. Theorem 2, printed pp. 1133–1134 (PDF pp. 6–7), adds small exponential moments and conditions on the number of vertices of one specified type. It gives height convergence to 2e/(sigma sqrt(a_j)). It does not state a Polish-type theorem. Its variance is a-weighted factorial offspring moments evaluated on the right Perron vector, not generally the variance of an ordinary count averaged along a spine.
- de Raphelis (2017), arXiv:1405.3916v3, Theorem 2, PDF p. 8: type set X is explicitly countable (p. 6). Conditions (HM), pp. 7–8: irreducible finite iterate coefficients and positive left/right eigenvectors a,b normalized by sum a=sum ab=1. (HQ), p. 8: finite factorial second moments and eta^2=sum a_x b_y Q^x_yz b_z finite. (HR_x0), p. 8: y^2 times the probability of an excursion avoiding x0 of height >y tends to zero, and y^2 times the expected number of first returns beyond height y tends to zero. Conclusion: UNCONDITIONED forest height ->2|B|/eta; also tree-index and survival/height-tail limits. Continuous densities have zero probability of returning to a singleton. Source gives neither these return-tail estimates nor a theorem upgrading this forest result to exact total-size conditioning.
- Addario-Berry, Beltran, Stufler and Thevenin, arXiv:2507.23241v2, revised 12 June 2026: Theorem 1.3, PDF pp. 5–6, proves rooted GHP CRT convergence for FINITELY many types, under criticality, irreducibility, nondegeneracy, extinction possibility and finite exponential offspring moments, conditioned on a nonnegative integer linear combination of type populations. For total size lambda=1, c_scal=sigma/2, with sigma^2=sum a_i b_j b_k Q^(i)_jk (equation (1.3), p. 6). Theorem 1.4, p. 6, supplies height tails. This will justify the finite-mixture partial theorem, not arbitrary continuous kernels.
- Haas and Miermont (2012), arXiv:1003.3632 published-version text: Theorems 5–6, PDF p. 13, require Markov branching with size-only splitting laws and convergence of suitably rescaled splitting measures. Theorem 8, PDF p. 15, gives single-type finite-variance conditioned GW GHP convergence. A type-dependent tree with hidden correlated types is not automatically size-only Markov branching.
- Janson (2012), arXiv:1112.0510: Theorem 7.1, PDF pp. 20–21, and Theorem 7.11, p. 22, concern simply generated single-type trees and offspring/degree frequencies, not Polish-type conditioned CRTs. Cyclic allocation representations are useful for exact simulation only after genuine single-type reduction.
- Stephenson (2018), arXiv:1412.6911: finite K types and local convergence to a Kesten tree under population conditioning, rather than global rescaled compact-tree convergence. Main Theorem 3.1 is located in PDF pp. 12–13 (precise statement will be checked in final report).
- Abraham, Delmas and Hoscheit (2013), arXiv:1202.5464: Theorems 2.3, 2.7, 2.9, PDF pp. 4–5, construct GHP Polish spaces and compactness criteria. These supply topology, not a branching invariance principle for Polish *marks*.
- Biggins and Kyprianou (2004), Adv. Appl. Probab. 36, 544–581: Theorem 2.1, printed p. 548 (PDF p. 5), gives mean-martingale convergence/degeneration via spine-series conditions on a GENERAL type space. It does not give a critical size-conditioned CRT theorem. A spectral gap alone is not its stated criterion.
- Berzunza, arXiv:1605.04810v2 (ALEA 2018): abstract explicitly restricts to finitely many types. Stable height limits do not remove that restriction.
- Marzouk, arXiv:1903.06138: Theorem 4, PDF p. 5, gives convergence of prescribed-degree trees in a Gromov–Prokhorov sense subject to no dominant degree. The surrounding paragraph explicitly distinguishes the missing general height tightness. It does not manufacture the conditional degree law of a continuous-type cascade.
- Angtuncio Hernandez and Clancy, arXiv:2502.04238: multitype Levy-tree construction with finitely many types and a different conditioning (root monochromatic component), not a continuous-type version of the requested theorem.
- Addario-Berry, Devroye and Janson, arXiv:1011.4121, PDF pp. 2–3: equation (1.3) gives width/height limits for finite-variance single-type conditioned GW; Theorems 1.1 and 1.2 give sub-Gaussian tails. W/(sigma sqrt(n)) and sigma H/(2 sqrt(n)) have the same limiting marginal law, max e. They need not agree jointly. This gives independent simulation targets.
- Athreya–Ney, Branching Processes (1972; Dover reprint 2004): publisher/catalog contents locate finite multitype theory at Chapter V, pp. 181–228, with critical limits p. 189 and supercritical limits p. 192. Full book theorem text not obtained; no exact theorem application is claimed. General type-space martingale theory is checked instead from the accessible Biggins–Kyprianou primary paper.

Search phrases and retrieval hashes are recorded in `search_queries.txt` and `source_manifest.json`. PDFs were handled in memory and never saved.

## Additional general-state results checked after broadening the search

- Foutel-Rodier, arXiv:2412.16035: Assumption 1, PDF pp. 3–4, asks for a positive continuous bounded h, left probability eigenmeasure pi with pi(h)=1, UNIFORM convergence of mean type measures against every bounded continuous test function, uniform moments of ALL orders, and continuity of the offspring second moment. Its variance on p. 4 is exactly integral pi(dx) E_x sum_{i != j} h(Y_i)h(Y_j). Theorem 1.1, p. 6, gives marked Gromov-vague excursion-measure convergence. Assumption 2 and Theorem 1.2, p. 7, require a UNIFORM Kolmogorov survival asymptotic to upgrade to marked GHP. Corollary 1.3, p. 8, gives SURVIVAL conditioning and forest limits. No theorem there conditions on a single exact value of the total mass. Source's L2 spectral claim does not check the uniform assumptions; its exponential moment would give the required all-order moments only if uniform in type.
- Horton–Powell, arXiv:2601.05906v2 (2026): Assumption 1, PDF pp. 2–4, includes (VAR), (CAD), (PF) with bounded harmonic function and UNIFORM exponential mean-semigroup convergence, (EXT), a truncated pair-offspring lower bound (ERG), and generator condition (CC). Theorem 2, PDF p. 5, is a CONTINUOUS-TIME genealogical limit in Gromov–Hausdorff–weak topology, CONDITIONED ON SURVIVAL to time n. The paragraph immediately following the theorem explicitly says the discrete-time extension is expected; it is not stated as proved there. The mass measure is occupation/lifetime mass. This is not the requested discrete-generation, vertex-count, exact-size theorem.
- Powell, arXiv:1512.00031 / PTRF 2019: bounded-domain branching diffusion, survival-conditioned genealogical limit; not exact total vertex-count conditioning for the OU strength kernel on R.

These papers materially narrow the difficulty: Polish types alone are no longer an obstacle to general genealogical limits. The exact-size conditioning and the quantitative uniform hypotheses remain real checks. They do not justify RESOLVED_IN_LITERATURE.
