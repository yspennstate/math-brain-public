    <title>The expected degree of minimal spanning forests</title>
    <updated>2013-06-03T06:33:59Z</updated>
    <link href="https://arxiv.org/abs/1306.0303v1" rel="alternate" type="text/html"/>
    <link href="https://arxiv.org/pdf/1306.0303v1" rel="related" type="application/pdf" title="pdf"/>
    <summary>We give a lower bound on the expected degree of the free minimal spanning forest of a vertex transitive graph in terms of its spectral radius. This result answers a question of Lyons-Peres-Schramm and simplifies the Gaboriau-Lyons proof of the measurable-group-theoretic solution to von Neumann's problem. In the second part we study a relative version of the free minimal spanning forest. As a consequence of this study we can show that non-torsion unitarizable groups have fixed price one.</summary>
