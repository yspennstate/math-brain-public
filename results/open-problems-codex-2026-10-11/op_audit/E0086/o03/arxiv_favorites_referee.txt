    <title>Favorite sites of one-dimensional transient random walk</title>
    <updated>2026-10-02T03:02:56Z</updated>
    <link href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02727v1" rel="alternate" type="text/html"/>
    <link href="https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.02727v1" rel="related" type="application/pdf" title="pdf"/>
    <summary>In this paper, we study favorite sites of one-dimensional transient random walk on $\mathbb{Z}$ beyond the simple case. Denote by $\mathcal{K}(n)$ the set of favorite sites at time $n$. The goal of this paper is twofold. First, we study $\# \mathcal{K}(n)$, the cardinality of $\mathcal{K}(n)$, and show that, under some mild conditions, for any positive integer $k$, $\mathbf{P}(\#\mathcal{K}(n)=k, {\rm i.o.})=1$, and $\limsup_{n\to\infty}\#\mathcal{K}(n)/\log\log n=-1/\log γ$, where $γ$ is the probability that the random walk never returns to the starting point. Second, we study the escape rate of favorite sites, and give the limsup and liminf behaviors, where the liminf behavior is characterized via an integral test. To the best of our knowledge, this integral test is the first result of this nature for random walks (in non-random environments).</summary>

    <title>Favorite sites for simple random walk in two and more dimensions</title>
    <updated>2025-11-12T10:05:50Z</updated>
    <link href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.00995v2" rel="alternate" type="text/html"/>
    <link href="https://arxiv.org/pdf/2409.00995v2" rel="related" type="application/pdf" title="pdf"/>
    <summary>On the trace of a discrete-time simple random walk on $\mathbb{Z}^d$ for $d\geq 2$, we consider the evolution of favorite sites, i.e., sites that achieve the maximal local time at a certain time. For $d=2$, we show that almost surely three favorite sites occur simultaneously infinitely often and eventually there is no simultaneous occurrence of four favorite sites. For $d\geq 3$, we derive sharp asymptotics of the number of favorite sites. This answers an open question of Erdős and Révész (1987), which was brought up again in Dembo (2005).</summary>

    <title>Favorite sites of randomly biased walks on a supercritical Galton-Watson tree</title>
    <updated>2016-11-14T17:53:06Z</updated>
    <link href="https://arxiv.org/abs/1611.04497v1" rel="alternate" type="text/html"/>
    <link href="https://arxiv.org/pdf/1611.04497v1" rel="related" type="application/pdf" title="pdf"/>
    <summary>Erdős and Révész initiated the study of favorite sites by considering the one-dimensional simple random walk. We investigate in this paper the same problem for a class of null-recurrent randomly biased walks on a supercritical Gaton-Watson tree. We prove that there is some parameter $κ\in (1, \infty]$ such that the set of the favorite sites of the biased walk is almost surely bounded in the case $κ\in (2, \infty]$, tight in the case $κ=2$, and oscillates between a neighborhood of the root and the boundary of the range in the case $κ\in (1, 2)$. Moreover, our results yield a complete answer to the cardinality of the set of favorite sites in the case $κ\in (2, \infty]$. The proof relies on the exploration of the Markov property of the local times process with respect to the space variable and on a precise tail estimate on the maximum of local times, using a change of measure for multi-type Galton-Watson trees.</summary>
