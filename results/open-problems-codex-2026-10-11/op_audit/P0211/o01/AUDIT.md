## Claim and match to the question

ATTEMPT.md lines 7–16 claim that, for every integer n >= 2 and every beta > 0, the continuous-time integer-partition chain with merger rate 2/n for each unordered pair of distinct cluster instances and fragmentation rate beta per internal polymer link has spectral gap gamma satisfying

    (beta + sqrt(beta) + 1/n)/6 <= gamma <= 4(beta + sqrt(beta) + 1/n).

Thus its relaxation time, the inverse spectral gap, is uniformly of order `(beta + sqrt(beta) + 1/n)^(-1)`. In particular, whenever `0 < b_- <= n beta_n <= b_+ < infinity` eventually, relaxation is Theta(sqrt(n)), with constants allowed to depend on b_- and b_+.

I fetched Aldous's page and its linked notes directly. PDF page 1 specifies precisely these transition rates and this stationary law. PDF page 4 explicitly discusses beta_n ~ b/n and the unresolved bounds of orders sqrt(n) and n. The claim therefore answers the stated linear-polymer relaxation-order question, including the joint small-beta limit; it does not merely pass off a fixed-beta result as uniform. Excluding n=1 is appropriate because that chain has no nonconstant observable or positive eigenvalue. No order theorem for every possible coagulation-fragmentation model is asserted or established.

## Mathematical issues found

No failing inequality, identity, unproved essential step, hypothesis mismatch or missing parameter regime was found in Sections 1–5 or Appendix A. No correction is needed for the claimed order theorem. The following records the potentially fatal points examined and why they survive independent checking.

1. **Projection is only stationary, not strong lumpability (lines 20–46).** Detailed balance on a nonempty cut set and a one-cut enlargement gives the ratio beta*n/k. Counting cut sets with a distinguished cut gives `k N(m) = n k! / product m_j!`, including k=1 and k=n. Conditional on a partition, selecting a distinguished cut produces a uniform ordered segment multiset. A boundary consequently joins unlike sizes with expected count `2 m_i m_j/(k-1)`, and equal sizes with expected count `m_i(m_i-1)/(k-1)`. Multiplication by `(k-1)/n` gives exactly the merger rates, even at k=2, where two boundaries join the same two clusters. Fragmentation is already correct configuration by configuration. This proves the conditional-generator identity and hence the Dirichlet-form identity. The lower-gap comparison has the right direction: polymer test functions form a subspace of edge test functions. Strong lumpability is neither true in general nor used. Exact rational enumeration independently checked all subset counts, all conditional rates and polymer detailed balance for n=2,...,9 at beta=3/7.

2. **Count-chain comparison and upper certificate (lines 50–92).** The finite positive-vector comparison follows by evaluating the positive principal eigenvector at the minimum and maximum of v/h; both inequality directions check. The total split and merge rates are beta(n-k) and k(k-1)/n. Computing the discrete difference of Qg gives a birth rate beta(n-k-1), a death rate k(k-1)/n, and killing beta+2k/n on k=1,...,n-1. The difference map has kernel exactly the constants and is onto, so the positive count spectrum is exactly the killed spectrum. The double-reciprocal identity in line 87 was independently simplified to zero symbolically. With a=max(1,n sqrt(beta)), its maximum is at most beta+4 max(sqrt(beta),1/n). The variational upper comparison gamma_M <= gamma_Q is correct. For n=2, gamma_M=beta+1 provides an additional exact boundary check.

3. **Completeness and possible missing slow modes (lines 100–167).** Re-deriving the up/down commutator gives `(n-2j)I`: extension and deletion diagonal counts differ by n-2j and exchange terms cancel. The norm identity implies injectivity below the middle layer and the required surjectivity. Commuting D through successive U's gives the coefficient `j(n-2l-j+1)`, establishing nonvanishing lifts up to layer n-l and vanishing beyond it. Taking adjoints proves orthogonality between distinct harmonic degrees; the dimension sum telescopes to the entire layer dimension. Thus the decomposition loses no spectral sector. Counting the removals and using harmonicity for additions gives precisely the birth, death and killing terms in (H). These sectors are orthogonal under the actual stationary inner product and invariant under the reversible generator. Their birth-death spectra are real, including the one-state sector at n even and l=n/2.

   The crucial degree-1 exclusion is sound: the lift from zero-sum coefficient vectors is injective on each layer 1 <= k <= n-1, sector projection commutes with cyclic rotation, and a zero-sum coefficient vector fixed by an n-cycle must vanish. 4. **Uniform square-root lower bound (lines 173–203).** Both single-reciprocal certificate identities were re-derived and checked symbolically. If k<=a, the available birth numerator is at least n/2 and the denominator at most 5a/2, giving sqrt(beta)/5. If k>=a, the sector contribution is at least a/(3n), since `(k-1)/(k+a-1)>=1/3`; the count contribution is at least a/n.
