Create New Folder SAVE > ## Abstract We prove that random walks in i.i.d. random environments which oscillate in a given direction have velocity zero with respect to that direction. This complements existing results thus giving a general law of large numbers under the only assumption of a certain zero-one law, which is known to hold if the dimension is two. ## Citation [Download Citation](https://projecteuclid.org/#) Martin Zerner."A Non-Ballistic Law of Large Numbers for Random Walks in I.I.D.
