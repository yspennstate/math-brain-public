Primary 60J10; Secondary 05C81, 60J45, 60J46. 1 Main result and setup The lazy random walk on { 0 , 1 } n \{0,1\}^{n} mixes in time of order n ​ log ⁡ n n\log n . If moves leaving a subset A A are rejected, the resulting walk converges to the uniform measure on A A whenever the induced graph is connected, but its mixing time may be exponentially larger [ 7 , Section 1] . We show that the O ⁡ ( n ​ log ⁡ n ) O(n\log n) bound continues to hold whenever A A is increasing and its density is bounded away from zero.
