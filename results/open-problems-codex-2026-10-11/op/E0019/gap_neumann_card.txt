[card] Von Neumann Series Lemma (Neumann Series for Bounded Operators)  (id: claim_d42919cf2ac24118a84a6be3f315f5c9; claim)
    Let X be a Banach space and T: X -> X a bounded linear operator with operator norm ||T|| < 1. Then the operator I - T is invertible, with bounded inverse given by the norm-convergent series (I-T)^{-1} = sum_{n=0}^infinity T^n, and ||(I-T)^{-1}|| <= 1/(1 - ||T||). [standard, e.g. Conway, A Course in Functional Analysis]
