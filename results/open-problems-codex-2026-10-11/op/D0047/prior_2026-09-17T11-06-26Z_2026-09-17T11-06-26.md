Consider the most economical superpotential compatible with the singularities and exponential terms,

\[
A=\partial_z+a,\qquad
a=A_c\tanh z+B_c\coth z+C e^{2t}\sinh2z.

to hold, coefficient comparison gives the two standard intertwiner identities

\[

\[

\[
A_c(A_c+1)=4\zeta^2-\frac14,
\]

\[
B_c(B_c+1)=4\eta^2-\frac14,

\[
4C^2=1,

2C(A_c+B_c)+2\sigma-1=0.

\[

B_c=-\frac12+2s_\eta\eta,\qquad
C=\frac{\epsilon}{2},

\[
\boxed{\epsilon(A_c+B_c)+2\sigma-1=0.}

\begin{cases}
1-\sigma,&\epsilon=+1,\\
\sigma,&\epsilon=-1.
