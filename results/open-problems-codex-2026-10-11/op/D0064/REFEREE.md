# Hostile-referee checks, 11 October 2026

1. **Hypotheses accidentally strengthened?** Definition 2.17 uses M(A/epsilon) with no prefactor. The proof above follows that convention. Allez--Rhodes--Vargas use a different normalization; their formulas must not be copied without the epsilon^d shift. Section 1.2 explicitly allows gamma=0. Section 6.3's extra dependence assumption is unspecified, and is not claimed checked.
2. **Gaussian must be nondegenerate?** Definition 2.17 does not say this. A constant process is Gaussian in the usual definition allowing variance zero. The result is restricted to that interpretation and does not address a formulation imposing gamma>0.
3. **Local finiteness despite infinite intensity?** Small jumps have finite total expected mass; large jumps hitting each compact have finite count. Both are necessary; finite expectation of the full mass would be false. Countable exhaustion makes the Radon assertion simultaneous.
4. **A pair far away could invalidate finiteness?** The displaced-endpoint map is a translation for fixed z. Its hitting intensity is exactly |K| for each z, just like the first endpoint. It is already included in the large-jump count.
5. **Only one-set scaling shown?** The Poisson mapping constructs the whole transformed measure, so scaling holds jointly for all Borel sets, not just one-dimensional mass distributions.
6. **Mixing confused with independent increments?** The coupling bounds the total variation distance for whole bounded-window restrictions. It proves mixing at large separation, while the strict Laplace cross term proves dependence at each fixed separation.
7. **Conditioning on no shared cluster gives a mistaken independence claim?** The proof uses an explicit independent copy of the shared Poisson restriction. It does not infer unconditional independence from an event.
8. **Ergodicity merely asserted?** The bounded-window approximation argument extends cylinder mixing to all events; an invariant event then has probability equal to its square.
9. **Infinite mean makes Laplace transforms divergent?** False. Local finiteness makes exp(-q M(A)) well defined. Direct bounds make H(f) finite for every bounded compactly supported f.
10. **Could deterministic multiplication remove the dependence?** No. Replace q,r by cq,cr in the strictly positive cross term for any c>0. Zero multiplication would be the excluded trivial solution.
11. **Could the claimed dual chaos have correlated base measure at gamma=0?** The specific candidate in Section 6.2 has standard zero-gamma GMC as its control, hence deterministic Lebesgue measure. Global random scaling is excluded by the source's ergodicity requirement; the candidate up to a deterministic factor still has independent disjoint masses.
12. **Could a stronger decay condition invalidate this example?** Yes. The proof gives bounded-window O(R^-2) total-variation decay, not finite-range independence or uniform half-line mixing. This is the reason for PARTIAL as the overall verdict.
13. **Numerics mistaken for a proof?** All measure-theoretic claims have analytic proofs in ATTEMPT.md. numerical_checks.py is an independent deterministic quadrature/algebra sanity check, with constant memory and no Monte Carlo inference.
14. **Lean coverage overstated?** BoundaryChecks.lean only checks four algebraic identities and positivity of the cross expression. It does not formalize Poisson construction, Gaussian processes, ergodicity, or the classification. A passing compiler log is required before calling those identities checked.
15. **Stable transformation assumed invertible without proof?** REDUCTIONS.md recovers every joint Laplace transform of the directing masses, then proves uniqueness using polynomial moments on [0,1]^n. This requires conditional independent scattering; it is explicitly not inferred for an arbitrary solution.
16. **Conditional expectations on different spaces asserted uniformly integrable?** The proof uses E[Y1_{Y>K}]<=2 E[U1_{U>K/2}], and U has the same integrable law at every scale. A common probability-space coupling is unnecessary.
17. **Known degeneracy invoked beyond its assumptions?** The finite-mean exclusion is restricted to kernels for which ordinary critical/supercritical GMC vanishing is established, with the regular compact-support setting given as an example. It is not labeled an unrestricted result for arbitrary continuous k.
18. **Checks actually completed?** lean_check.txt records EXIT_CODE 0 and only the usual logical axioms. numerical_results.json records all three checks true. The compiler's two warnings concern an unused, unreachable trailing ring tactic, not an unproved goal.
