    SGD minimizes an expected or finite-sum objective by stepping along an unbiased noisy estimate of the gradient: x_{k+1} = x_k - eta_k g_k with E[g_k | x_k] = grad f(x_k). For convex L-smooth objectives with bounded gradient noise, diminishing steps (e.g. eta_k of order 1/sqrt(k)) give expected suboptimality of order 1/sqrt(k); with mu-strong convexity, order 1/k. Constant steps converge to a noise-dominated neighborhood.
