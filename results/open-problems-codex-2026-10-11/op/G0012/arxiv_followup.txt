    <title>The Birthday Paradox for non-backtracking walks on regular graphs</title>
    <updated>2026-08-26T18:53:44Z</updated>
    <link href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26321v1" rel="alternate" type="text/html"/>
    <link href="https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.26321v1" rel="related" type="application/pdf" title="pdf"/>
    <summary>We show a birthday paradox for random non-backtracking walk on regular graphs of degree at least $3$: such a walk of length $k$ has high probability of self-intersecting when $k$ is significantly greater than $\sqrt n$, where $n$ is the number of vertices of the graph. This resolves a conjecture of Noga Alon and Yuval Peres for the fixed degree case.</summary>

    <id>http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09729v1</id>
    <title>On the self-intersection time of non-backtracking random walks</title>
    <updated>2026-08-10T15:30:31Z</updated>
    <link href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09729v1" rel="alternate" type="text/html"/>
    <link href="https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.09729v1" rel="related" type="application/pdf" title="pdf"/>
    <summary>We study the self-intersection time of the non-backtracking random walk on connected undirected graphs. For every fixed $Δ\geq 3$ we show that the expected self-intersection time is $O(\sqrt{n} \log n)$ on $n$-vertex graphs with minimum degree at least $3$ and maximum degree at most $Δ$. For regular graphs with a uniform spectral gap, we improve this to $O(\sqrt{n})$. We also show an $Ω(\sqrt{n})$ lower bound on a class of regular expanders. Our upper bound on the expected self-intersection time implies an improved mixing time bound on Glauber dynamics for the Ising model on $Δ$-regular graphs at the tree uniqueness threshold.</summary>

    <id>http://arxiv.org/abs/2209.11218v1</id>
    <title>Simple vs non-simple loops on random regular graphs</title>
    <updated>2022-09-22T17:56:59Z</updated>
    <link href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.11218v1" rel="alternate" type="text/html"/>
    <link href="https://arxiv.org/pdf/2209.11218v1" rel="related" type="application/pdf" title="pdf"/>
    <summary>In this note we solve the ``birthday problem'' for loops on random regular graphs. Namely, for fixed $d\ge 3$, we prove that on a random $d$-regular graph with $n$ vertices, as $n$ approaches infinity, with high probability:
  (i) almost all primitive non-backtracking loops of length $k \prec \sqrt{n}$ are simple, i.e. do not self-intersect,
  (ii) almost all primitive non-backtracking loops of length $k \succ \sqrt{n}$ self-intersect.</summary>
