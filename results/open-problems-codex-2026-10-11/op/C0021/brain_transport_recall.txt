    Let X and Y be real-valued random variables. Then X is stochastically dominated by Y, meaning P(X > t) <= P(Y > t) for every real t, if and only if there exists a coupling of X and Y, that is, random variables X' and Y' defined on a common probability space with X' having the same distribution as X and Y' having the same distribution as Y, such that X' <= Y' almost surely. [Strassen (1965); Lindvall, Lectures on the Coupling Method]

Average its translates over a Folner sequence. The product space of two edge configurations is compact, the prescribed marginals are unchanged, and the order relation is closed.
