# D0097 working mathematics

Date: 2026-10-12. No new ARW theorem proved yet.

## Exact target

For each fixed translation-invariant nearest-neighbour, zero-drift kernel on Z² whose support spans Z², at total jump rate 1, prove that the infinite-volume ARW critical density satisfies ζ_c(p,λ)<1 for every 0<λ<∞ and lim_{λ↓0} ζ_c(p,λ)=0. The symmetric kernel p(±e_1)=p(±e_2)=1/4 is included; whether later printed results cover unequal coordinate weights must be checked.

## First referee checks

The critical density in the target is the conservative infinite-volume threshold, not a driven finite-volume stationary density. A finite-box computation is insufficient without a justified infinite-volume criterion. A replacement of the infinite Green function by a killed or truncated Green function in the transient argument is not automatically valid: the requisite stabilization and mass-balance statements must be reproved.

The prior ledger's suggested scale sqrt(λ) log(1/λ) is a conjectural heuristic, not a theorem or an established asymptotic. No claim here relies on it.
