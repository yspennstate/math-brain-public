> For the partially oriented walk, Guillotin-Plantard and Le Ny [2008b, Section 4] ask a related question when an irrational rotation determines the horizontal orientations. Fix an irrational α. Direct the line at height y ∈ ℤ to the right if the fractional part of yα is less than 1/2, and to the left otherwise; vertical edges can be crossed in either direction. Is this walk recurrent or transient?

The intended chain is simple random walk on these three outgoing edges (probability 1/3 each); this is the Campanino–Petritis specialization explicitly defined on PDF p. 2 of Brémont below. The source question itself does not repeat the transition probabilities. We retain the exact phase zero and do not substitute an almost-everywhere phase.
