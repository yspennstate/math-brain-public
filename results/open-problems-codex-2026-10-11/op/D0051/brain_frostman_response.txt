[card] Hausdorff dimension  (id: claim_e8f3827645b13dbf1ff1701188ae2135; claim)
    $\dim_H A=\inf\{s\ge0:\mathcal H^s(A)=0\}$, where $\mathcal H^s$ is the $s$-dimensional Hausdorff measure; for random fractals (Brownian paths, SLE traces, percolation interfaces) it is usually computed from first and second moment (two-point) estimates via Frostman's lemma.
