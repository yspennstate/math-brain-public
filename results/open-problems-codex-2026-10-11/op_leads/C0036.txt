Let Gamma be a finite group and Q a Gamma-equivariant positive contraction on l^2(Gamma) with 0 < Q < I, and P_Q the determinantal measure with kernel Q. Let HM(Q) be the harmonic mean of the eigenvalues of Q. Then P_Q stochastically dominates the product Bernoulli measure of density HM(Q), and P_Q is dominated by the product measure of density 1 - HM(I - Q).
