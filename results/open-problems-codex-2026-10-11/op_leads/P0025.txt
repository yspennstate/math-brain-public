[4] Attempt on Failure of synchronous contraction in a bounded planar domain  (id: claim_0eb828114a067a3e40a0be5fbb76bfa8b5b7975318e1e3decaca15ad13d02463; claim; score 3.43)
    ATTEMPT RECORD from the Codex campaign (author-checked by that worker only). Method: Synchronous contraction on convex domains Result: For two reflected motions driven by the same Brownian path, the noise cancels in their difference. With inward normals, d|X-Y|^2=2<X-Y,n(X)>dL_X-2<X-Y,n(Y)>dL_Y. Convexity makes both contributions nonpositive, so the distance is nonincreasing. Check: Each sign follows from the supporting half-space inequality at the boundary point. The calculation uses finite-variation reflection terms and has no missing quadratic-variation term. Obstruction: Nonincrease alone does not prove convergence to zero. Nonconvex domains can have both signs and require control of the long-run reflection geometry.
