[4] Orthonormal basis of a Hilbert space  (id: claim_8e61bd090f62d43a44ea41fdfc55f56cf5875d06c224e218013ae47a821947fe; claim; score 7.37)
    A family {e_i : i in I} in a Hilbert space H is orthonormal if <e_i, e_j> = 0 for i distinct from j and ||e_i|| = 1 for every i. An orthonormal basis (complete orthonormal system) of H is an orthonormal family whose closed linear span is all of H. Equivalent characterizations: the family is a maximal orthonormal set; the only vector orthogonal to every e_i is 0; every x in H has the expansion x = sum over i of <x, e_i> e_i, where at most countably many coefficients <x, e_i> are nonzero and the series converges in norm regardless of ordering. Every Hilbert space has an orthonormal basis (by Zorn's lemma), any two orthonormal bases of H have the same cardinality, and H is separable exactly when it has a countable orthonormal basis. An orthonormal basis of an infinite-dimensional Hilbert space is not a vector-space (Hamel) basis.
