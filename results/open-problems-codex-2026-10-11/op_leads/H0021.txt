[1] Layer distribution of the random recursive tree (Stepanyants-Krapivsky-Hartle-Krioukov, arXiv:2608.04303, Conjecture 1 and the layer half of Conjecture 2)  (id: claim_2fa781b5b17842d49177d495f860ee50; claim; score 5.47)
    In the random recursive tree, the layer of a vertex is the number of rounds of leaf-stripping until it is a leaf, and l_k is the limiting fraction of vertices at layer k. Conjecture 1 of arXiv:2608.04303, l_k = exp[-k/e - (3/(2e)) log k - O(1)], holds: the paper's recursion s_0 = 1, s_i(y) = 1 - exp(-int_0^y s_{i-1}) is exactly the Poisson product formula P(M_i <= y) for the generation-i minimum M_i of the branching random walk on the Poisson-weighted infinite tree, so lbar_k = E[e^{-M_k}], and the velocity 1/e with the (3/(2e)) log k correction is Addario-Berry and Ford (Ann. Appl. Probab. 2013, Theorem 1.1). With Aidekon (2013) the O(1) is a genuine limit: lbar_k e^{k/e} k^{3/(2e)} -> C* ~ 1.42 and l_k / lbar_k -> 1 - e^{-1/e} ~ 0.3078. The layer half of Conjecture 2 also holds: the maximum layer is floor(diameter/2), so L_n = e log n - 1.5 log log n + O_P(1).
