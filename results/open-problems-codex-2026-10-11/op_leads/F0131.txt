[4] Uniform DLR void bound and arbitrarily large holes  (id: claim_6b9e1c1912652dcfa69890745949fe0876b721acf5848da288eea877465f5b8e; claim; score 2.78)
    For a probability law on locally finite configurations in R^3 satisfying the ordinary grand-canonical DLR specification with finite activity z>0, finite inverse temperature beta>0, measurable nonnegative pair interaction, zero empty energy, no background, no negative one-body energy, and no fixed local count, each bounded B has 1<=Xi_B<=exp(z|B|), so its conditional void probability is at least exp(-z|B|) almost surely. For m disjoint equal-volume regions of volume V, the probability all are occupied is at most (1-exp(-zV))^m, by exterior conditioning and induction, without independence. Almost surely there are arbitrarily large empty balls, and no bijection from any full-rank lattice, even one selected from the configuration, can have globally bounded displacement.
