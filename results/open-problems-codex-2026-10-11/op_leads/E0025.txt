[2] Lévy Measure  (id: claim_5fedc9379d1c9106f77ef66d47d5aa4233d538e28c8f910ac565d65fb90591e2; claim; score 6.34)
    A Borel measure \nu on \mathbb{R} \setminus \{0\} is called a Lévy measure if \int_{\mathbb{R} \setminus \{0\}} \min(1, x^2) d\nu(x) < \infty. In particular, \nu can have infinite total mass near the origin (infinite activity of small jumps), but must assign finite measure to sets bounded away from zero (finite activity of jumps of size > \epsilon).
