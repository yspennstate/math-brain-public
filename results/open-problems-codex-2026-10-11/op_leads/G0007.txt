[1] Absolute Continuity of the Integral  (id: claim_0c039b146bde482b5bfdfebdcb4b9034381228ac2f84c60709b2e4ec59f3f350; claim; score 5.39)
    Let f be in L^1(mu). For every eps > 0 there exists delta > 0 such that integral_A |f| dmu < eps whenever A is measurable with mu(A) < delta. Equivalently, the finite measure nu(A) = integral_A |f| dmu is absolutely continuous with respect to mu in the strong epsilon-delta sense, not merely in the sense that mu(A) = 0 implies nu(A) = 0. The two senses coincide for FINITE nu but not in general: with mu Lebesgue measure on (0,1) and nu(A) = integral_A dx/x, nu vanishes on mu-null sets yet nu(0, delta) = infinity for every delta. A single integrable function is therefore automatically a uniformly integrable family of one element. [standard; e.g. Folland, Real Analysis, 2nd ed., Theorem 3.5] f in L^1(mu); the conclusion fails for f merely locally integrable with a non-integrable singularity
