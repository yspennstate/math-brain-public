Then N^1 and N^2 are independent if and only if almost surely they have no common jump time in [0, T]. For the 'if' direction it suffices that each N^i be a cadlag adapted counting process with N^i(0) = 0 and jumps of size 1 such that N^i(t) - lambda_i t is an (F_t)-martingale.
