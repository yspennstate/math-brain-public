[1] Spectral radius  (id: claim_a856b6832eb14a549e1823bdee33fbdf; claim; score 6.53)
    In mathematics, the spectral radius of a square matrix is the maximum of the absolute values of its eigenvalues. More generally, the spectral radius of a bounded linear operator is the supremum of the absolute values of the elements of its spectrum. The spectral radius is often denoted by $\rho (\cdot)$.
