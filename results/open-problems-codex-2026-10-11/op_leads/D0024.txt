[2] Weak Convergence of a Sequence in a Banach Space  (id: claim_d80576e85e80446fbbf1e8d83b264e90; claim; score 5.32)
    Let X be a Banach space with continuous dual X*. A sequence (x_n) in X converges weakly to x in X, written x_n converges to x weakly, if f(x_n) converges to f(x) for every f in X*. Norm convergence implies weak convergence, but not conversely in infinite dimensions; a weakly convergent sequence is norm-bounded (by the uniform boundedness principle) and its weak limit, if it exists, is unique (by Hahn-Banach). [standard, e.g. Conway, A Course in Functional Analysis]
