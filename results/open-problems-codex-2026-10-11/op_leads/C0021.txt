    PRIVATE AUTHOR-CHECKED DRAFT, no independent review. Let f_k be the iterated majority-of-three function on n = 3^k bits. The greedy recursive algorithm (evaluate two random children, the third only if they disagree) has revealment (5/6)^k = n^{-log(6/5)/log 3} = n^{-0.1660}, so the optimal revealment delta(f_k) is at most that. So the exponent sigma asked for in Exercise VIII.5 of the Garban-Steif notes lies in [0.1660, 0.3691]; the exact value is open.
