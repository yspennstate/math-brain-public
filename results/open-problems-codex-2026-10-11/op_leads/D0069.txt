[3] Erdos 1947 probabilistic lower bound for the Ramsey number R(k,k)  (id: claim_af7faf3b27b053c03395f6d63edc5d5d5ce31c728526823043a89ac793dcdeb1; claim; score 7.45)
    Erdos (1947): R(k,k) > 2^{k/2} for every k >= 3. Colour the edges of the complete graph on n vertices red or blue independently and uniformly at random. The expected number of monochromatic k-cliques is C(n,k) 2^{1 - C(k,2)}; when n = floor(2^{k/2}) this expectation is below 1 for k >= 3, so some colouring has no monochromatic k-clique, and R(k,k) > n. This is the first-moment method (a union bound over the C(n,k) cliques); only linearity of expectation is used, and no clique needs to be independent of another. With Stirling's formula the same count gives R(k,k) >= (1 + o(1)) (k / (e sqrt 2)) 2^{k/2}; the Lovasz local lemma (Spencer 1975) improves the constant to (1 + o(1)) (sqrt 2 / e) k 2^{k/2} and nothing better than the exponent k/2 is known. The upper bound side is separate: R(k,k) <= 4^k from Erdos-Szekeres, improved to (4 - epsilon)^k by Campos, Griffiths, Morris and Sahasrabudhe  [...]
