[3] Scale-free percolation: an effective long-range exponent and the predicted mean-field region  (id: claim_488351251b7e093cde560039f790c589d23aede0a390bffd578b463c89458696; claim; score 5.95)
    PRIVATE HEURISTIC, author-recorded. In scale-free percolation with weight tail exponent tau and long-range parameter alpha (connection probability 1 - exp(-lambda W_x W_y |x-y|^{-alpha d})), the annealed two-point connection decays like |x-y|^{-alpha d (tau-1)}, i.e. like long-range percolation with P(x <-> y) ~ |x-y|^{-d-s} for s = d(alpha(tau-1) - 1). Long-range percolation is mean-field for d > 3 min(s, 2) (Heydenreich-van der Hofstad-Sakai), which predicts mean-field critical behaviour for scale-free percolation iff alpha(tau - 1) < 4/3 or d > 6, with tau > 3 (for tau in (2,3) the model percolates instantly). This is a prediction for Heydenreich-van der Hofstad Open Problem 16.8, not a theorem: the quenched weights induce correlations that the annealed long-range comparison ignores.
