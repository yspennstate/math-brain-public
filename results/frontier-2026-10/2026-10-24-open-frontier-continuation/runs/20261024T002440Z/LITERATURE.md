# Literature and priority boundary

Primary sources re-opened on 2026-10-24 UTC; targeted searches used both available search engines. This was a bounded verification, not an exhaustive priority review. External novelty and publication readiness remain UNVERIFIED.

1. Favaro, Fortini and Peluchetti, *Large-width asymptotics and training dynamics of alpha-Stable ReLU neural networks*, TMLR, November 2024. [Published manuscript](https://iris.unibocconi.it/retrieve/1d4101f5-ffcd-480d-8966-6c0f01de8f2b/2651_Large_width_asymptotics_a.pdf); [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.08065). Primary background for stable ReLU scaling and random-kernel training. The logarithmic normalization and surviving random initialization are not discoveries of this run. The current proofs use the private continuation's precise initialization/compactness lemmas rather than treating this abstract as a proof of our selected theorem.

2. Min, Mallada and Vidal, *Early Neuron Alignment in Two-layer ReLU Networks with Small Initialization*, ICLR 2024, [arXiv v2, 25 March 2024](https://arxiv.org/html/2307.12851v2). Sections 3 and Appendix E explicitly study activation/dead cones and regular gradient-flow solutions for separated-label data; this is relevant prior art for terminal-sector geometry. Their small-initialization classification framework differs from the iid stable critical-grid squared-loss limit here. Neither the general dead-cone mechanism nor regularity issues are claimed as novel. The current contribution within this project is the exact gamma-dependent Poisson selector and its macroscopic law, with external priority unverified.

3. Boursier, Pillaud-Vivien and Flammarion, *Gradient Flow Dynamics of Shallow ReLU Networks for Square Loss and Orthogonal Inputs*, NeurIPS 2022. [Primary paper](https://papers.nips.cc/paper/2022/file/7eeb9af3eb1f48e29c05e8dd3342b286-Paper-Conference.pdf). Verified in the preceding package and cited as orthogonal-input background; not re-opened this run. Its orthogonality assumptions must not be silently transferred to nonorthogonal inputs.

Searches for stable ReLU training and Euler/boundary selection also returned broader step-size/generalization and phase-diagram work. Those search results are not evidence that our exact theorem is absent from the literature. No negative search result is used as a novelty proof. The proof package establishes internal mathematical claims with precise assumptions; it does not settle catalog-level external priority.
