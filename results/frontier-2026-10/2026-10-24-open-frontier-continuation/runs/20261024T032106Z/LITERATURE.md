1. Hancheng Min, Enrique Mallada and Rene Vidal, *Early Neuron Alignment in Two-layer ReLU Networks with Small Initialization*, arXiv:2307.12851v2 (25 March 2024), https://arxiv.org/html/2307.12851v2 . The primary text studies small-initialization ReLU gradient flow and activation alignment; Appendix E explicitly treats Caratheodory existence/nonuniqueness issues.
