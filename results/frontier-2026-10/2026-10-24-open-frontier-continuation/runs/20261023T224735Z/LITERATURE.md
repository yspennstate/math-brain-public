The original eight-page paper was read at https://webhomes.maths.ed.ac.uk/~v1ranick/papers/sard.pdf . Feature-learning scope: the results describe order-one changes in a training tangent kernel and a label-dependent switching law. They do not establish a full test-input prediction law, a representation-learning generalization advantage, or an arbitrary-depth theorem. Those should not be inferred solely from kernel nonfreezing.
