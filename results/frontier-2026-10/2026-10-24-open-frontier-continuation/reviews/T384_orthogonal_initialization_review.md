# Independent review: T384 standard-coordinate joint initialization

**Disposition: pass under iid standard-coordinate initialization.** I reviewed `research/T384_orthogonal_initialization.md` for fixed finite `k`, independent symmetric standard `α`-stable variables `W_i,U_{ij}`, `0<α<2`, and inputs `x_j=e_j` in that coordinate basis. The marked endpoint law, separation of product/square/endpoint jumps, removal of small jumps, and the conditional effective-rate transform are consistent. This is an internal proof review, not a literature-priority assessment.

The marked Poisson intensity is `αc_α|z|^(−1−α) dz P_U^{⊗k}(du)` on **each** signed half-axis. The scalar positive product tail `P(WU_{j,+}>t)~αc_α²t^(−α)log t` and `b_m=(m log m)^(1/α)` imply one-sided normalized jump tail `c_α²x^(−α)`. Its symmetric stable exponent is `c_α|t|^α`, as claimed; the U-square exponent is `c_αΓ(1−α/2)λ^(α/2)`. A shared extreme W of order `m^(1/α)` carries ordinary U marks and does not cause an order-one product jump, whose scale is larger by `(log m)^(1/α)`.

For fixed positive cutoffs, every two distinct retained jump categories have vanishing one-row overlap after multiplication by m. The only nontrivial added cross-coordinate case is two products sharing W: conditional independence of `U_j,U_ℓ` and `P(U>x)≤C x^(−α)` bound the overlap by `Cm E[min(1,(|W|/b_m)^α)^2]=O(m/b_m^α)=O(1/log m)`. All other categories are separated by the W- or U-endpoint split or independent U coordinates. Thus the one-row mixed transform has only the separate axis contributions at order `1/m`, giving independent limiting `N,F_1,…,F_k,C_1,…,C_k`. Symmetry centers discarded small product jumps even when `α≥1`; the variance `O(ε^(2−α))` and square mean `O(δ^(1−α/2))` justify removing cutoffs. Mark tails beyond a bounded set are negligible for any fixed `|z|>η`.

The effective rates are Poisson integrals of `z²1_{u_j>0,sgn(z)=σ_j}` plus `C_j`. The small-z mass is uniformly `O(ε^(2−α))`, large-z atoms have probability `O(R^(−α))`, and atomless marks and outputs avoid indicator boundaries. Therefore joint convergence of `(F_m,Q_m)` follows from the point-process limit and truncation. On either z half-axis, `x=z²` has Lévy measure `βc_αx^(−1−β)dx`, `β=α/2`; independent Bernoulli(1/2) marks yield equation (8). This fixes the coefficients and shows that each marginal Q has Laplace exponent `(3c_α/2)Γ(1−β)λ^β`.

**Dependence qualification is essential and correctly stated.** The primitive F vector is independent of N and C, but the *joint* selected Q law depends on the pattern of `σ_j=sgn(y_j−F_j)`. For two indices in the same sign group, an extreme W can contribute to both rates; opposite signs use disjoint PPP half-axes. Equation (8) consequently does not factor into an F-independent Q-vector law for `k≥2`, even though each individual `(F_j,Q_j)` has the scalar independent-rate law. The claimed coordinate-input training limit should retain this conditional joint law and should not infer arbitrary-rotation invariance from the word “orthogonal.”

No mathematical correction is needed for the stated initialization theorem.
