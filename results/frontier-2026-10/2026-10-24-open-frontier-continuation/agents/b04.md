# B04 — raw-RBF finite hierarchical GP and unconditional mixture-NLL ERM

**Status (2026-10-23 UTC): a quantified restricted theorem.** This composes the already audited finite-feature mixture circuit (`agents/a30.md`), explicit RBF encoder (`agents/a28.md`), encoder-grid argument (`research/T336_ENCODER_GRID.md`), and the unconditional-loss strategy (`research/T336_UNCONDITIONAL_ERM.md`). The genuinely additional calculations here are the RBF **log-determinant/evidence perturbation**, a global finite-class **mixture** NLL envelope, and posterior-weighted Gaussian-tail transfer. It treats a finite hyperparameter prior, one noisy query, unit-amplitude RBF components, finite maximum context size, known positive noise/length scales and prior masses, independent exogenous input design, exact ERM, and the masked unnormalized signed-ReLU architecture of A30. It does not prove optimization by SGD or a theorem for standard softmax/layer-normalized attention. The internal Brain T336 row already closed the supplied finite-rank scalar target; do not present that closure as new.

## Model and explicit approximation class

Fix integers `H,d,N≥1`, radius `R>0`, length scales `ell_h>0`, noise variances `s_h∈[a,s_1]` with `a>0`, and masses `π_h>0`, `Σ_h π_h=1`. Assume `n≤N` context locations and one query in the ball `||x||≤R`. First draw latent component `Z∼π`; independently of `Z` and all GP values, draw exogenous locations from any prescribed distribution; then draw a zero-mean GP with kernel

`k_h(x,z)=exp(−||x−z||²/(2 ell_h²))`

and independent context/query noise `N(0,s_h)`. The conditional noisy-query law given the context `D=(n,X,Y_c,x_*)` is `P_D=Σ_h w_h(D)G_h(D)`, where

`A_h=K_h+s_h I_n`, `v_h=k_h(X,x_*)`, `j_h=1`,

`l_h=logπ_h−[logdet A_h+Y_cᵀ A_h^{-1}Y_c]/2`, `w=softmax(l)`,

`G_h=N(μ_h,C_h)`, `μ_h=v_hᵀ A_h^{-1}Y_c`, `C_h=1+s_h−v_hᵀ A_h^{-1}v_h≥s_h≥a`.

The common Gaussian `n log(2π)/2` term cancels from the evidence weights. Empty contexts may be handled with determinant 1 and the A30 active-slot mask.

Choose a bound `B>0` and an accuracy `h∈(0,1]` for the constructive comparator. Put `α=max_h R²/ell_h²`,

`ν=ceil(max{1,2e α,log₂(2/h)})`, `k=ν−1`, `p=binom(d+k,k)`.

For each component use A28's explicit degree-`k` RBF ReLU encoder with target feature error `h/16`, subsequently grid its affine parameters and clip **each coordinate** to `[-2,2]`. More precisely, for `α_h=R²/ell_h²`, let

`C_h^enc=(Σ_{j=0}^k(dα_h)^j/j!)^{1/2}`, `G_h^enc=α_h d/2+α_h²/32+2 max(1,k)`,

`n_h=max{2,ceil(sqrt(16 C_h^enc G_h^enc/h))}`, `H_enc=5+2 max(0,k−1)`,

`W_h=2(n_h+3)(p+d+1)`, `Q_h=64[1+R^{−1}+α_h(d+1)+max(1,α_h^{k/2})]`,

`P_h=dW_h+W_h+(H_enc−1)(W_h²+W_h)+p(W_h+1)`.

These are deliberately loose fully connected shape/weight bounds, including padded zeros. Define `T_enc=H_enc+1`, `D_{h,0}=max{R,1}`, `D_{h,j}=W_h Q_h D_{h,j−1}+Q_h`, and

`E_h=Σ_{j=1}^{T_enc}(W_h D_{h,j−1}+1)(W_hQ_h)^{T_enc−j}`.

Choose `0<δ_w≤min_h Q_h` and `sqrt(p) δ_w max_h E_h≤h/16`; grid each scalar weight/bias on the evenly spaced endpoint grid in `[-Q_h,Q_h]` of mesh at most `δ_w`. Every rounded target encoder differs in feature norm by at most `h/16`, even after fixed coordinate clipping. Consequently the **actual gridded comparator** has uniform feature error at most `ζ=h/8` relative to the truncated exact map, feature norm at most `1+ζ≤2`, and uniform true-versus-feature kernel entry error

`δ_k≤α^ν/ν!+2ζ+ζ²≤h/2+h/4+h²/64≤49h/64<h`.                                      (1)

Every grid member, including a poor one, has `||ψ_{f,h}(x)||≤2sqrt(p)`. The product grid has size

`J≤∏_{h=1}^H(4Q_h/δ_w)^{P_h}`.                                                    (2)

The fixed A30 attention core uses the common spectral interval `[a,b]`, `b=s_1+4Np`, for **all** these grid encoders and all components. Put `q=(sqrt(b/a)−1)/(sqrt(b/a)+1)∈(0,1)`, `e_L=4q^L/(1+q^L)²`, `D_L=e_L/a`, `η_r=2q^{r+1}/((r+1)(1−q))`. The A30 polynomials `P_L` (one-sided underinverse) and `L_r` (logarithm) use fixed exact coefficients; `0≼P_L(A_{f,h})≼A_{f,h}^{−1}`. Choose integer `L≥1,r≥0` such that `e_L≤h` and `η_r≤h`. An explicit sufficient choice is

`L≥max{1,ceil(log(4/h)/log(1/q))}`,

`r+1≥max{1/(1−q),log(4/h)/log(1/q)}`.                                      (3)

Clip context labels to `[-B,B]` before evidence and prediction. A30 computes the padded-mask log trace and likelihood quadratic with its signed attention recurrence, then computes the component Gaussian means/variances from the same underinverse. Its piecewise-affine ReLU simplex readout `S` is fixed once for the class. On the logit cube specified below, triangulate with cell side `z≤u_* h`, interpolate the **exact** softmax at its vertices, and realize this continuous piecewise-affine interpolation by a finite ReLU network. Every output of `S` is a genuine positive probability vector. A conservative simplex count is `S_count≤H!(ceil(2M_*/z)+1)^H`; A29 notes an extremely loose `O(H S_count 2^{S_count})` ReLU-gate realization through a max/min-of-affine representation. This fixed exact-real readout is not counted in the learned encoder grid.

## Evidence perturbation includes the log determinant

The following calculation applies to *any* two PSD kernels whose train/train-query/query entries differ by at most `δ`, with noise `s_h≥a`, and `|Y_{c,i}|≤B`. Their training matrices `A,A'` both have eigenvalues at least `a`. Writing `E=A'−A`, `||E||op≤Nδ`, and using `A_t=A+tE≽aI`,

`|logdet A'−logdet A|=|∫₀¹ tr(A_t^{-1}E)dt|≤N²δ/a`,

`|Y_cᵀ(A'^{-1}−A^{-1})Y_c|≤||Y_c||² ||E||op/a²≤N²B²δ/a²`.

Hence the exact feature-GP evidence logit `l'_h` satisfies

`|l'_h−l_h|≤c_K δ`, `c_K=N²(1+B²/a)/(2a)`.                                (4)

This term cannot be obtained merely from a posterior mean/covariance perturbation estimate: it explicitly controls **both** terms of the marginal likelihood. The approximate trace/inverse circuit adds at most `Nη_r/2+NB²D_L/2`. Thus for the rounded comparator,

`max_h |lhat_h−l_h|≤E:=c_K δ_k+Nη_r/2+NB²D_L/2`.                         (5)

For component predictions, both true and rounded feature kernels have entries bounded by 4 in absolute value, so `||v||,||v'||≤4sqrt(N)`, `||v−v'||≤sqrt(N)δ`, and `|j−j'|≤δ`. The resolvent identity yields

`|μ_h−μ'_h|≤c_μ δ`, `c_μ=NB(1+4N/a)/a`,

`|C_h−C'_h|≤c_C δ`, `c_C=(1+4N/a)²`.                                      (6)

For the feature-kernel circuit, the underinverse adds mean error at most `4NB D_L` and covariance error at most `16N D_L`; both the exact true covariance and emitted candidate covariance remain at least `a` (the latter is conservative relative to its **own** feature GP, with no cross-kernel Loewner claim). A direct scalar forward-Gaussian KL calculation on the positive covariance segment gives

`KL(G_h || Ghat_h)≤Ξ:= [c_μ δ_k+4NB D_L]²/(2a)+[c_C δ_k+16N D_L]²/(4a²)`. (7)

For all grid members, their clipped-label feature-GP exact logits are in a fixed cube. Put

`c_sol=N/2+NB²/(2a)`, assume `c_sol h≤1`,

`M_*=|logπ_min|+(N/2) max{|log a|,|log b|}+NB²/(2a)+1`,

`u_*=e^(−2M_*)/H`, `ω=u_*`, `V=s_1+4p`, `M=4pNB/a`.                    (8)

The exact feature-GP logit magnitude is at most `M_*−1`; its polynomial evaluation differs by at most `c_sol h≤1`, so even poor grid members feed `S` only this cube. Barycentric softmax interpolation gives `S_h≥ω` and `Σ_h S_h=1` **exactly**. For every actual logit vector, its coordinatewise log ratio from exact softmax is at most `τ=z/u_*≤h` (the bound in A30). Regard this ratio only analytically as an effective logit perturbation. Hoeffding's bounded-variable lemma, the KL chain rule for the joint latent-label law, then data processing to forget the latent index give the comparator's bounded-context mixture guarantee

`sup_{|Y_{c,i}|≤B} KL(P_D || Q_{f_B,D})≤ ε_app:= (E+τ)²/2+Ξ`.             (9)

This is forward KL of the *marginal mixture density*, without a spurious KL triangle inequality. The sufficient, fully specified target choice

`c_E=c_K+c_sol`, `c_M=c_μ+4NB/a`, `c_V=c_C+16N/a`,

`C_mix=(c_E+1)²/2+c_M²/(2a)+c_V²/(4a²)`,

`h=min{1,(1+c_sol)^{-1},sqrt(ε/C_mix)}`, `z≤u_*h`,                     (10)

combined with (1)–(3) gives `ε_app≤C_mix h²≤ε` for any `ε>0`. All parameters in (8), including the simplex floor, are public functions of this construction. The final circuit has A30's `max(r,L−1)+2` attention layers with no more than `5H` heads in its readout, plus the parallel finite raw encoders and a finite ReLU simplex readout. The learned class varies only the encoder grid parameters; all evidence/solver/readout constants are held exact and fixed.

## Unconditional mixture-NLL finite-class ERM

Now draw `T` independent **whole** hierarchical-GP tasks from the original, unconditioned prior. For each, observe `D_t` and a noisy query label `Y_{*,t}` from `P_{D_t}`. Every grid predictor has weights at least `ω`, component means `|μhat_h|≤M`, and variances `a≤Ĉ_h≤V` for **all** original context labels, because labels are clipped first. The class size obeys (2). For a generic member write `q_f(y|D)=Σ_h wh_f(D) φ(y; μhat_{f,h},Ĉ_{f,h})` and `ℓ_f=−log q_f(Y_*|D)`. Its pathwise envelope is

`0≤Z_f:=ℓ_f−(1/2)log(2πa)≤d_M+Y_*²/a`,

`d_M=log H+(1/2)log(V/a)+M²/a`.                                      (11)

Indeed the mixture density is at most `(2πa)^{-1/2}`; at least one of its weights is at least `1/H`, and its component Gaussian lower-bounds the density by `H^{-1}(2πV)^{-1/2} exp[−(|Y_*|+M)²/(2a)]`. This bound is **pathwise** despite arbitrary dependence of the candidate on correlated context labels.

Conditional on exogenous locations and the latent component, the *unconditioned* query marginal is `N(0,1+s_h)`. Set `V_Y=1+s_1` and

`Λ=4(d_M+2V_Y/a)`, `u=log(2J/δ_conf)`.                                    (12)

For `t<1/(2V_Y)`, `E exp(tY_*²)≤(1−2tV_Y)^{-1/2}` by conditioning on the latent component. At `t=1/(aΛ)`, (11) implies `E exp(Z_f/Λ)≤e^{1/4}(3/4)^{-1/2}<2`, uniformly in `f`. The centered-moment and Bernstein calculation of the unconditional-ERM addendum therefore applies verbatim, including two-sided deviations and a union bound. An exact empirical mixture-NLL minimizer `fhat` satisfies, with probability at least `1−δ_conf`,

`K(fhat)−inf_{f∈F}K(f)≤8sqrt(2)Λ sqrt(u/T)+4Λu/T`,                    (13)

where `K(f)=E_D KL(P_D||Q_{f,D})` is expected forward **mixture** KL under the unconditioned prior. An empirical `η_opt`-minimizer adds `η_opt`. For a desired excess `ξ`, `T≥max{512Λ²u/ξ²,8Λu/ξ}` suffices. The identity `E ℓ_f=E_D H(P_D)+K(f)` justifies minimizing mixture NLL; using Gaussian NLL for one component would be a different loss.

To transfer the bounded-context comparator, let `E_B={max_i |Y_{c,i}|≤B}`. Mixture-Gaussian tails give

`Pr(E_B^c)≤ρ_B=min{1,2N exp[−B²/(2V_Y)]}`.                          (14)

For every context, latent-label KL and `wh_f≥ω` imply `KL(P_D||Q_{f,D})≤log(1/ω)+Σ_h w_h KL(G_h||Ghat_{f,h})`. The exact component variance is in `[a,V_Y]`; a direct Gaussian formula yields

`KL(G_h||Ghat_{f,h})≤V_Y/(2a)+(1/2)log^+(V/a)+(μ_h²+M²)/a`.

Under the *true* hierarchical prior, the posterior-weighted mean moment has a useful latent-variable identity: for any context-measurable event `E`,

`E_D[1_E Σ_h w_h(D) μ_h(D)^2]=E_{Z,D}[1_E μ_Z(D)^2]`.

Conditional on `Z=h` and locations, `μ_h` is centered Gaussian with variance `v_hᵀ A_h^{-1}v_h≤1` by the joint-kernel Schur complement; its fourth moment is at most 3. Cauchy–Schwarz and (14) therefore give `E[1_{E_B^c}Σ_h w_h μ_h²]≤sqrt(3ρ_B)`. Defining

`C_out=log(1/ω)+V_Y/(2a)+(1/2)log^+(V/a)+M²/a`,

the actual rounded comparator satisfies

`K(f_B)≤ε_app+C_out ρ_B+(sqrt(3)/a)sqrt(ρ_B)`.                    (15)

Combining (13) and (15) proves the unconditional finite-class theorem

`K(fhat)≤ε_app+C_outρ_B+[sqrt(3)/a]sqrt(ρ_B)
         +8sqrt(2)Λ sqrt(log(2J/δ_conf)/T)+4Λ log(2J/δ_conf)/T`.    (16)

All its constants, class cardinality, approximation degrees, encoder sizes, and weight floor are given above. There is no conditioning of the sampled tasks on `E_B`. A consistent existence sequence is `B_j=j`, `h_j` chosen from (10) with `ε_j=1/j`, `δ_conf,j=j^{-2}`, and `T_j≥j²Λ_j² log(2J_j/δ_conf,j)` (increase `T_j` if needed for small `j`). Since `p_j=O((log j)^d)`, `M_j=O(j(log j)^d)`, `log(1/ω_j)=O(j²+log p_j)` and `ρ_{B_j}=O(exp(−c j²))`, the tail and comparator terms vanish, while both concentration terms vanish. Borel–Cantelli gives almost-sure convergence of this sequence of **population expected-KL risks** for exact ERMs. This is neither a pointwise-in-context trained guarantee nor a polynomial-time training result.
