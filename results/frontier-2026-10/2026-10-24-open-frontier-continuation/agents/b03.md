# T364: exact finite random-sphere spurious maximum certificate

The [T364 dossier](https://github.com/<private-repo>/blob/main/06_math_research/frontier/rows/T364/DOSSIER.md), [row moves](https://github.com/<private-repo>/blob/main/06_math_research/frontier/rows/T364/moves.jsonl), and [23 October STEP3](https://github.com/<private-repo>/blob/main/06_math_research/frontier/rows/T364/claude_b1e26540/STEP3.md) were inspected before this work. STEP3 has extensive finite-dimensional experiments and an unreviewed Gaussian-proxy comparison, but leaves asymptotic bulk-maxima existence open. The earlier A05 report proves component-height statements and an orthogonal-diagonal perturbation criterion; the off-component certificate below is separate.

Ge and Ma, [On the Optimization Landscape of Tensor Decompositions](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2017/file/a48564053b3c7b54800246348c7fa4a0-Paper.pdf), use **Gaussian** $a_i\sim N(0,I_d)$, unlike T364's sphere-uniform sampling. Their Theorem 1.1 assumes $d^{1+\epsilon}<n<d^{2-\epsilon}$ and controls a high superlevel only. Their conclusion asks about spurious maxima outside that set.

## Exact configuration

Let $d=2$, $n=4$, $p=12/13$, $q=5/13$, and

$$
b_1=(p,q),\qquad b_2=(p,-q),\qquad b_3=b_4=e_2.
$$

Define $F_0(x)=\sum_{i=1}^4\langle b_i,x\rangle^4$ on $S^1$, and write $x(\theta)=(\cos\theta,\sin\theta)$. The two first-term tangential gradients cancel at $e_1=x(0)$, and the last two vanish to fourth order there. Its **entire spherical tangent Hessian**, a one-by-one matrix, is

$$
F_0''(0)=8p^2(3q^2-p^2)=-\frac{79488}{28561}<-\frac{11}{4}.
$$

Thus $e_1$ is a strict local maximum away from every component: $\max_i|\langle b_i,e_1\rangle|=12/13<0.95$. The exact heights are

$$
F_0(e_1)=\frac{41472}{28561}<\frac32,\qquad
F_0(e_2)=\frac{58372}{28561},\qquad
F_0(e_2)-F_0(e_1)=\frac{100}{169}>0.
$$

The sphere-uniform expectation at every fixed direction is $3n/[d(d+2)]=3/2$. The strict off-component local maximum is below this expectation and nonglobal.

## Positive-probability event with robust certificate

Sample $a_1,\dots,a_4$ iid uniform on $S^1$. Let $E$ require each oriented angle to be within $\delta=1/1000$ radians of its corresponding $b_i$ angle. Each arc has probability $\delta/\pi$, so

$$
\mathbb P(E)=\left(\frac{1}{1000\pi}\right)^4>0.
$$

Put $F(\theta)=\sum_i\langle a_i,x(\theta)\rangle^4$ and $h(t)=\cos^4t=(3+4\cos2t+\cos4t)/8$. Since $|h'|\le4$, $|h''|\le4$, and $|h'''|\le12$, on $E$ we have uniform bounds

$$
|F-F_0|\le16\delta,\qquad
|F'-F_0'|\le16\delta,\qquad
|F''-F_0''|\le48\delta.
$$

Set $r=1/40$. The third-derivative bound also makes $F_0''$ $48$-Lipschitz. For $|\theta|\le r$,

$$
F''(\theta)\le-\frac{79488}{28561}+48(r+\delta)<-\frac32,
$$

because $79488/28561>11/4$ and $48(r+\delta)=1.248<5/4$. Also $|F'(0)|\le16\delta=0.016$. Integrating the Hessian inequality yields $F'(-r)>-0.016+3r/2>0$ and $F'(r)<0.016-3r/2<0$. There is a unique zero $\theta_*\in(-r,r)$, with **strict local maximum** $x_*=x(\theta_*)$ and full tangent Hessian $F''(\theta_*)<-3/2$.

The chordal distances from $x_*$ to $e_1$, and from each $a_i$ to $b_i$, are at most $r$ and $\delta$. Thus

$$
\max_i|\langle a_i,x_*\rangle|
\le\frac{12}{13}+r+\delta
=\frac{6169}{6500}<\frac{19}{20}.
$$

Since $F_0$ is strictly concave in this interval, $F_0(x_*)\le F_0(e_1)$. The uniform value perturbation gives

$$
F(x_*)\le\frac{41472}{28561}+\frac{16}{1000}<\frac32,\qquad
F(e_2)-F(x_*)\ge\frac{100}{169}-\frac{32}{1000}
=\frac{11824}{21125}>0.
$$

Therefore

$$
\mathbb P\left(\exists\text{ strict local maximum }x_*:
\max_i|\langle a_i,x_*\rangle|<0.95,\ 
F(x_*)<3/2,\ F(x_*)<\max_xF(x)\right)
\ge(1000\pi)^{-4}.
$$

This is $n=4=d^2>d$ in the actual sphere-uniform model. All Hessian and height comparisons are exact rational inequalities.

## Extension and scope limit

For each **fixed** $d\ge2,n\ge4$, embed $b_1,b_2$ into the first two coordinates and put $b_3=\cdots=b_n=e_2$. At $e_1$, the tangent Hessian is diagonal with eigenvalue $-79488/28561$ in the $e_2$ direction and, for $d>2$, eigenvalue $-4F_0(e_1)=-165888/28561$ in all other tangent directions. The component overlap bound remains $12/13$, and $F_0(e_2)-F_0(e_1)=n-4+100/169>0$. A nondegenerate critical point persists under sufficiently small perturbations of all components by the implicit-function theorem, so a product of spherical caps gives **positive probability for every fixed $(d,n)$**. In particular one can take $n=\lceil d^{1+\eta}\rceil\le c d^2$ for any fixed $c>0$, $\eta\in(0,1)$, and sufficiently large $d$.

This says the sampling law's support includes bad landscapes and refutes deterministic or almost-sure absence of off-component nonglobal maxima. The cap probability can vanish rapidly with $d$; **no high-probability asymptotic T364 result follows**. The near-colliding target vectors are deliberately atypical. Proving typical bulk-maxima existence remains the unresolved step in the existing work.
