# A30 — finite-feature hierarchical GP: audited evidence circuit and mixture KL

**Scope.** This is a constructive finite-mixture, finite-feature, exact-real-arithmetic extension of the scalar GP attention result, rather than a solution of the full T336 pretraining/sample-complexity problem. Kang–Lee–Cheng, *Transformers Can Learn Posterior Predictive Distributions In-Context* ([arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.26713v1), Remark 3.2; [PMLR](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/kang26h.html)), expressly leave their hierarchical-GP extension for future work. The construction below builds on the signed, **unnormalized ReLU** finite-feature matrix product and Chebyshev underinverse in `agents/a21.md`, and independently audits the log-determinant and weight analysis in `agents/a26.md`. It does not apply as written to normalized/softmax attention, RBF kernels supplied only through raw inputs, or learned rather than constructed weights.

## A restricted theorem with an explicit computational route

Fix finite `H,N,p_h`, positive prior masses `π_h` summing to one, noise variances `s_h∈[s_0,s_1]` with `s_0>0`, and *supplied* component feature coordinates `φ_h(x)∈R^{p_h}` satisfying `||φ_h(x)||≤R` at every train/query token. Each prompt has `n≤N` active training slots, with `|y_i|≤B` (the weaker `||Y||≤B√N` suffices for the estimates); inactive slots have zero features and zero labels. Every active train slot carries its distinct positional one-hot `e_i∈R^N`, while an inactive slot carries `e_i=0`. One query token carries all `φ_h(x_*)` and a scalar query marker equal to one. The active-slot marker is the local sum of its ID coordinates. Assume a fixed source mask that can select either this query or all `N` training slots, positionwise linear/ReLU mixing, and exact real arithmetic. Write

`K_h=Φ_h Φ_hᵀ`, `v_h=Φ_h φ_h(x_*)`, `j_h=||φ_h(x_*)||²`, `A_h=K_h+s_h I_n`, and `V=√N R²` (`||v_h||≤V`). Set `a=s_0`, `b=s_1+NR²`, and suppose `a<b`. The exact training evidence and noisy-query component law are

`ℓ_h=logπ_h−(tr log A_h+YᵀA_h^{-1}Y)/2`, `w=softmax(ℓ)`,

`G_h=N(μ_h,C_h)`, with `μ_h=v_hᵀ A_h^{-1}Y` and `C_h=j_h+s_h−v_hᵀ A_h^{-1}v_h≥s_0`.

Put `κ=b/a`, `q=(√κ−1)/(√κ+1)`, `c=(a+b)/2`, `d=(b−a)/2`, `γ=(√b+√a)²/4`, and `D(x)=(x−c)/d`. For integers `r≥0,L≥1` define

`L_r(x)=logγ+2 Σ_{j=1}^r [(-1)^{j+1}q^j/j] T_j(D(x))`,

`ρ_L(x)=[T_L((c−x)/d)+1]/[T_L(c/d)+1]`, `P_L(x)=(1−ρ_L(x))/x`.

The quotient is a genuine degree-`L−1` polynomial, since its numerator vanishes at zero. On `[a,b]`, `0≤ρ_L≤ε_L=4q^L/(1+q^L)²≤4q^L`, so `0⪯P_L(A_h)⪯A_h^{-1}`. Use the same `P_L` for the likelihood quadratic and prediction:

`ℓ̂_h=logπ_h−[tr L_r(A_h)+YᵀP_L(A_h)Y]/2`,

`μ̂_h=v_hᵀP_L(A_h)Y`, `Ĉ_h=j_h+s_h−v_hᵀP_L(A_h)v_h≥C_h`, `Ĝ_h=N(μ̂_h,Ĉ_h)`.

The following completely explicit errors hold uniformly in prompts and components:

`η_r=2q^{r+1}/((r+1)(1−q))`, `D_L=ε_L/a`,

`E_{L,r}=Nη_r/2+D_L B²N/2`,

`max_h |ℓ̂_h−ℓ_h|≤E_{L,r}`, and

`max_h KL(G_h || Ĝ_h)≤Ξ_L:=D_L²[V²B²N/(2s_0)+V⁴/(4s_0²)]`.

If the output layer has an **exact softmax**, then, with `ŵ=softmax(ℓ̂)` and `P=Σ_h w_hG_h`, `P̂=Σ_h ŵ_hĜ_h`,

`KL(P||P̂)≤E_{L,r}²/2+Ξ_L`, `TV(P,P̂)≤√(E_{L,r}²/4+Ξ_L/2)`.

One may replace exact softmax with a finite ReLU readout `S_h` with a tunable error `τ>0`, **whose outputs are exactly nonnegative and sum to one**, obtaining the same bound with `E_{L,r}` replaced by `E_{L,r}+τ`. This readout and its size are specified below. For an explicit target `0<δ<1`, put `C_*=V²B²N/(2s_0)+V⁴/(4s_0²)`. Sufficient choices (omitting a logarithmic constraint when its numerator is zero) are

`r+1≥max{1/(1−q), log(4N/δ)/log(1/q)}`,

`L≥max{1, log_+(8B²N/(aδ))/log(1/q), log_+(4√C_*/(aδ))/log(1/q)}`,

and `τ≤δ/2`. They ensure `E_{L,r}≤δ/2`, `Ξ_L≤δ²`, and `TV(P,P̂)≤√3 δ/2<δ`. Thus polynomial degrees are `O(√κ[1+log(N(1+B²/a+√C_*/a)/δ)])`; the exact constraints give the useful constants. Here `log_+(x)=max(0,log x)` for `x>0`.

**Numerical proof.** The log Chebyshev identity is `x=γ(1+2q D(x)+q²)` and

`log x=logγ+2 Σ_{j≥1}(-1)^{j+1}q^j T_j(D(x))/j`.

Its tail is at most `η_r`, so the trace error is at most `nη_r≤Nη_r`. For the inverse, `A_h^{-1}−P_L(A_h)=A_h^{-1}ρ_L(A_h)`, a positive matrix bounded by `D_L I`, hence the quadratic error is nonnegative and at most `D_L||Y||²`. These imply `E_{L,r}`. The mean and covariance errors obey `|μ̂_h−μ_h|≤D_LVB√N` and `0≤Ĉ_h−C_h≤D_LV²`. In the scalar Gaussian KL formula, put `t=(Ĉ_h−C_h)/C_h≥0`; the covariance contribution is `[log(1+t)−t/(1+t)]/2≤t²/4`, while the mean contribution is at most `(μ̂_h−μ_h)²/(2s_0)`. This gives `Ξ_L`. Positivity `C_h≥s_0` is the Schur complement of the joint finite-feature prior covariance, plus independent query noise.

For weights, if `e_h=ℓ̂_h−ℓ_h∈[−E,E]`, then `KL(w||softmax(ℓ̂))=log E_w e^e−E_w e≤E²/2` by the bounded-variable exponential/Hoeffding inequality; no posterior-weight lower bound or factor `H` is needed for this *exact-softmax* step. For joint laws on `(h,y_*)`, the KL chain rule is `KL(w||ŵ)+Σ_h w_h KL(G_h||Ĝ_h)`. Forgetting `h` only decreases KL, and Pinsker gives the claimed mixture TV. These are classical polynomial approximation, Gaussian, and data-processing facts, not novelty claims.

## Actual signed-ReLU circuit, including the trace

The shared `N`-slot padded matrix is `A_h^{ext}=K_h^{ext}+s_hI_N`; inactive features vanish, so it is block diagonal with `A_h` and `s_h I_{N−n}`. Its spectrum remains in `[a,b]`. Let `M=diag(1_{slot i active})`, and initialize the *row-vector state* `U_{h,0}[i,:]=e_iᵀ`, so `U_{h,0}=M`, **not** the full padded identity. Initialize its past state as zero, a scalar trace accumulator `t_h(i)=1_{active(i)} logγ`, and the solver states `X_{h,0}=0`, `X_{h,1}=[Y,v_h]/c` (two right-hand sides; padded rows zero). The query-source read-in pair produces `v_h(i)=φ_h(x_i)·φ_h(x_*)` at train slots and `j_h` at the query, using the signed identity `ReLU(z)−ReLU(−z)=z` from `a21`.

The exact determinant recurrence is `U_{h,j}=T_j((A_h^{ext}−cI)/d)M`: first `U_{h,1}=((A_h^{ext}−cI)/d)M`, then `U_{h,j+1}=2((A_h^{ext}−cI)/d)U_{h,j}−U_{h,j−1}`. Each step needs one `K_h U` matrix product from two signed ReLU heads with `Q,K` selecting `φ_h`; the `s_hI`, `cI`, and recurrence coefficients are local linear mixing. Since `M` removes padded columns and inactive rows form a separate zero-feature block, the active block is exactly `T_j((A_h−cI)/d)` and the rest is zero. Therefore `tr U_{h,j}=tr T_j((A_h−cI)/d)`, including when `n=0`, **without** adding or subtracting a padded-noise log-determinant.

At each active row, the diagonal can be extracted by an **exact bounded binary ReLU gate**, not by a variable-variable multiplication. For `e∈{0,1}`, `|z|≤1`, define `g(e,z)=ReLU(z)−ReLU(−z)−ReLU(z−e)+ReLU(−z−e)`. It is `ez`. Spectral calculus gives `||U_{h,j}||_op≤1`, so every entry lies in `[-1,1]`, including at every intermediate depth. Thus `d_{h,j}(i)=Σ_{k=1}^N g(e_i[k],U_{h,j}[i,k])` is its diagonal entry for an active slot and zero for padding. After computing each `U_{h,j}`, a positionwise ReLU network updates

`t_h(i) ← t_h(i)+2(-1)^{j+1}q^j d_{h,j}(i)/j`.

Consequently `Σ_i t_h(i)=tr L_r(A_h)` exactly. There are `O(HN)` recurrent coordinates per token (`U_current,U_past` across components) and `O(HN)` local ReLU gate units; this deterministic trace uses `O(HN²)` scalar token state. No random trace estimator or determinant oracle is hidden here.

In the same matrix-product layers, carry the `a21` solver recurrence for two columns `[Y,v_h]`: for `j=1,...,L−1`, with `β_j=T_{j−1}(c/d)/T_{j+1}(c/d)` and `α_j=2T_j(c/d)/(dT_{j+1}(c/d))`,

`X_{h,j+1}=(1+β_j−α_js_h)X_{h,j}−β_jX_{h,j−1}+α_j[Y,v_h]−α_jK_h^{ext}X_{h,j}`.

As proved in `a21`, `X_{h,L}=F_L(A_h^{ext})[Y,v_h]` with `P_L=F_L/(1+1/T_L(c/d))`. A pair of signed heads per component can matrix-multiply the **bundled** value `[U_{current},X_{current}]∈R^{N+2}` in each layer. The first such layer computes `U_1` and, if `L≥2`, `X_2`; later layers advance whichever recurrence still needs work. The number of matrix-product layers is `max(r,L−1)`.

In a final aggregation/readout attention layer at the query token, for each component: (i) one head with query scalar key `1` (the query marker) and source scalar key `1_{active}` (the sum of the source ID coordinates) sums `t_h(i)` with positive score; (ii) two signed heads with query key selector `1`, source scalar key `y_i`, and source value `X_{h,L}[i,1]` sum `Σ_i y_i X_{h,L}[i,1]`, obtaining `YᵀP_L(A_h)Y` after the fixed scaling; and (iii) two signed heads with query `φ_h(x_*)`, training keys `φ_h(x_i)`, and vector values `X_{h,L}[i,:]` give the mean and variance-reduction readout, likewise scaled. The first read-in layer already saved `j_h`; local linear mixing adds `s_h`. In particular, **the quadratic form needs no local ReLU multiplication of two data-dependent numbers**. The output also has fixed `logπ_h` and prior/noise scalars. The attention-layer count is `max(r,L−1)+2`: one read-in, the matrix layers, and one readout. It uses `2H` heads per read-in/matrix layer and at most `5H` in the final layer. With independently sized head keys/values, the per-token width is `O(Σ_h p_h+HN+H)`; a conventional equal-dimension multihead layer can be padded to `O(H·max{N+2,max_h p_h})` coordinates. It requires fixed train/query masks, identity position coordinates, vector-valued head values, and positionwise ReLU/linear updates. It does **not** claim a two-head, constant-width network independent of `H,N`.

## ReLU-only simplex readout

The approximation `softmax(ℓ̂)` in the analytic theorem should not be called an exact ReLU map. Fix `π_min=min_h π_h>0` and choose `r,L` with `E_{L,r}≤1`. All logits `ℓ̂_h` lie in `[-M_*,M_*]`, where

`M_*=|logπ_min|+(N/2) max(|log a|,|log b|)+B²N/(2a)+1`.

On this compact cube, every softmax coordinate is at least `u_*=e^{−2M_*}/H`. Triangulate a coordinate grid of side at most `z≤u_*`; the cube has at most `H!⌈2M_*/z⌉^H` simplices (boundary bookkeeping can add one grid interval per axis). Interpolate the **exact** softmax values at the vertices affinely on each simplex. The resulting continuous piecewise-affine map `S` is a finite ReLU-MLP map ([Ovchinnikov's max/min-of-affine representation theorem](https://emis.de/ft/11499), Theorem 2.1, applies on this convex cube; `max(u,v)=ReLU(u−v)+v`, `min(u,v)=u−ReLU(u−v)`). Barycentric interpolation ensures `S_h>0` and `Σ_h S_h=1` **exactly**, unlike an arbitrary coordinatewise approximator. Since a softmax coordinate is `1/2`-Lipschitz for `||·||_∞`, `|S_h(z')−softmax_h(z')|≤z/2` for every input `z'` in the cube, and `|log[S_h(z')/softmax_h(z')]|≤z/u_*`.

For the actual readout `\widetilde w=S(ℓ̂)`, define effective logits `\widetildeℓ_h=ℓ̂_h+log(\widetilde w_h/softmax_h(ℓ̂))`; this logarithm is **only in the proof**, not computed by the network. As `Σ_h\widetilde w_h=1`, `softmax(\widetildeℓ)=\widetilde w`. Thus `|\widetildeℓ_h−ℓ_h|≤E_{L,r}+z/u_*`, so the same Hoeffding/joint-label KL proof works with `τ=z/u_*`. Choosing `z≤u_*τ` gives an explicit finite (potentially enormous in `H,M_*`) ReLU readout. The claim is existence and a mesh complexity route, not an efficient tight bound on its ReLU parameter count or finite-precision stability.

## Audit and remaining boundary

1. The algebraic log polynomial and one-sided inverse bounds in `a26` are correct. Its padding discussion needed care: `tr log(A_h^{ext})` alone has an extra `(N−n)log s_h`, which is component-dependent; starting the trace recurrence from `M` as above fixes this exactly.
2. `a21`'s signed-head identity makes every finite-feature `K_h` matrix-vector product exact. The positional diagonal gate is valid only because Chebyshev spectral bounds keep the trace-state entries within `[-1,1]`; without that bound an unqualified one-hot times variable assertion would be wrong.
3. A finite ReLU network does not calculate softmax exactly on an open domain. Exact softmax may be included as an output operation; the simplex-preserving piecewise-affine readout above handles a strict ReLU-only output on the bounded domain.
4. This theorem supplies explicit **weights for expressivity** under feature-coordinate, masking, and unnormalized-attention assumptions. It gives no pretraining dataset count or proof SGD/ERM finds the circuit, no RBF/raw-input feature-map approximation, no guarantee for normalized or standard softmax attention, and no finite-precision error analysis. Those are separate missing steps for the literal T336 formulation. A next theorem could replace exact supplied features by a uniformly controlled feature approximation and propagate its perturbation through `A`, the determinant, and the Gaussian mixture; another would need a learning-theoretic sample and optimization analysis rather than only an existence circuit.
