# A17 — T228 novelty and overlap audit (23 October 2026 UTC)

## Verdict

**Do not present the proposed `d≥2` Gaussian SGD model with `σ(z)=z+ε tanh z`, exact radial multiplier `M²=(1−ηZ²)²+η²Z²χ²_{d−1}`, and positive Pareto tail as a new T228 solution relative to the current Math Brain.** Its existing [T228 dossier](https://github.com/<private-repo>/blob/main/06_math_research/frontier/rows/T228/DOSSIER.md) expressly proposes `σ=z+c tanh z`; its [target-3 claim](https://github.com/<private-repo>/blob/main/06_math_research/frontier/rows/T228/claude_b1e26540/T3_CLAIM.md), dated 22 October, states the same exact positive radial tail for **every `d≥1`, batch `b≥1`, nonzero Gaussian label noise, and even a nonzero teacher**, provided `E log ξ<0` and its class-(A) activation conditions. For `b=1`, `ξ=M` is precisely the proposed multiplier. Its exponent is independent of the bounded activation perturbation, and `α<2` on a nonempty step interval. The claim records two internal independent reviews; I fetched and read the first [review table](https://github.com/<private-repo>/blob/main/06_math_research/frontier/rows/T228/claude_b1e26540/REVIEW.tsv) and the proof steps, but did not retrieve the second reviewer file referenced there. This is internal Brain priority/overlap evidence, not external peer review.

The literal existing class-(A) condition `sup|φ'|<1` with `σ=z+φ` covers `φ=ε tanh` for `0<ε<1`, **not all `ε>0`**. The proposed all-positive-`ε` statement is a formal parameter extension for `ε≥1`. In the existing [step-1 proof](https://github.com/<private-repo>/blob/main/06_math_research/frontier/rows/T228/claude_b1e26540/T3_STEP1.md), the condition gives lower/upper derivative bounds `0<m≤σ'≤L` for Gaussian-label minorization; for this particular positive perturbation `σ'=1+ε sech²z`, one can instead take `m=1,L=1+ε` for every finite `ε>0`. Bounded `φ` and `zφ'` and all Gaussian moments persist. **Inference:** the existing drift/Harris/renewal/positivity argument likely extends directly to all finite positive `ε`, but that wider statement is not literally in the posted theorem and merits a targeted proof check. The local A10/A12 small-`ε` global radial average-Lipschitz argument is an additional *contraction/coupling certificate* for `d≥2`; the older target-3 proof uses Foster/Harris stability from `ElogM<0` and already obtains the stationary tail. This is a refinement of mechanism, not a new tail law.

There is also a fresh same-day [Brain checkpoint](https://github.com/yspennstate/math-research-brain/blob/main/research_pushes/2026-10-23-t228-closure-30-agents/campaign/admin/CHECKPOINT_NOTICE.md): [explicit scalar closure](https://github.com/yspennstate/math-research-brain/blob/main/research_pushes/2026-10-23-t228-closure-30-agents/campaign/proofs/T228_EXPLICIT_CLOSURE.md) takes `d=1, ε=10^{-3}, η∈[.99,1.01]` and proves `E Lip(Ψ)<1`, exact positive tail and `1<α<2`; its [nonzero-teacher extension](https://github.com/yspennstate/math-research-brain/blob/main/research_pushes/2026-10-23-t228-closure-30-agents/campaign/proofs/T228_NONZERO_TEACHER.md) has an internally reviewed analogous result. These scalar results are parallel to the local A10/A12 strategy but do not preempt the *specific d-dimensional radial average-contraction calculation*. The same checkpoint acknowledges that `z+ε tanh z` is an **unbounded activation with a bounded perturbation**, so do not describe it as a bounded-activation network.

The exponent's independence from `ε` is a consequence of the unchanged leading homogeneous map and is already stated in that proof.
