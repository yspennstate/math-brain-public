# T336: prior-averaged Gaussian-predictive pretraining bound

Let a task $D=(n,X_c,Y_c,X_q)$ include $n\leq N$ context examples and $m\geq1$ query locations. Draw each task from the stated GP prior and design distribution **conditioned on**

\[
E=\{\max_{1\leq i\leq n}|Y_{c,i}|\leq B\},\qquad \Pr(E)>0.
\]

Write $D\sim\mathcal P_E$ for this conditional task distribution. Given $D$, independently draw its query-label vector $Y\sim p_D=\mathcal N(\mu_0(D),\Sigma_0(D))$. The $T$ training pairs $(D_t,Y_t)$ are independent, identically distributed copies of this **whole** experiment (e.g. independent GP task draws followed by rejection on $E$); they are not multiple observations coupled through a single latent GP draw. Since $E$ is determined by the observed context, conditioning on $E$ does not truncate $Y\mid D$. Suppose throughout the support of $\mathcal P_E$ that

\[
\|\mu_0(D)\|_2\leq M_0,\qquad s I_m\preceq\Sigma_0(D)\preceq V_0 I_m,\quad s>0.
\]

Let a predictor $f$ output $q_f(\cdot\mid D)=\mathcal N(\mu_f(D),\Sigma_f(D))$, with

\[
\|\mu_f(D)\|_2\leq M,\qquad (s/2)I_m\preceq\Sigma_f(D)\preceq V I_m.
\]

These output constraints can be enforced by projection onto the Euclidean mean ball and spectral clipping of a symmetric covariance matrix. Define $R=M_0+M$ and the Gaussian NLL

\[
\ell_f(D,Y)=\tfrac12\bigl[m\log(2\pi)+\log\det\Sigma_f(D)
 +(Y-\mu_f(D))^\top\Sigma_f(D)^{-1}(Y-\mu_f(D))\bigr],
\]

and $K(f)=\mathbb E_{D\sim\mathcal P_E}\operatorname{KL}(p_D\|q_f(\cdot\mid D))$. Since the true conditional entropy is independent of $f$, $\mathbb E\ell_f=\mathbb E_D H(p_D)+K(f)$. The assertion below is about this **prior-averaged** $K(f)$, not a supremum over tasks.

**Finite-class theorem.** Let \(\mathcal F\) be fixed before seeing the training samples, finite with cardinality \(J\), and let \(\hat f\) be an exact empirical NLL minimizer over \(\mathcal F\). Put

\[
D_* =\tfrac m2\log(2V/s)+\frac{mV_0}{s}+\frac{R^2}{s},\quad
v_*^2=\frac{4mV_0^2}{s^2}+\frac{8V_0R^2}{s^2}+\frac{D_*^2}{4},\quad
c_*=\frac{4V_0}{s},\quad u=\log\frac{2J}{\delta}.
\]

With probability at least $1-\delta$ over the $T$ pretraining pairs,

\[
K(\hat f)-\inf_{f\in\mathcal F}K(f)
\leq 2\sqrt{\frac{2v_*^2u}{T}}+\frac{2c_*u}{T}. \tag{1}
\]

In particular, if $\mathcal F$ contains $f_*$ with the stronger uniform approximation property $\sup_{D\in\operatorname{supp}\mathcal P_E}\operatorname{KL}(p_D\|q_{f_*})\leq\varepsilon_{\rm approx}$, then the right side of (1) plus $\varepsilon_{\rm approx}$ bounds $K(\hat f)$. An empirical $\eta$-minimizer adds $\eta$ to (1). Nothing here proves that gradient descent finds such a minimizer.

**NLL concentration despite unbounded labels.** For fixed $D,f$, set $A=\Sigma_f^{-1}$, $a=\mu_0-\mu_f$, write $Y=\mu_0+\Sigma_0^{1/2}g$ with $g\sim\mathcal N(0,I_m)$, and put $H=\Sigma_0^{1/2}A\Sigma_0^{1/2}$, $b=\Sigma_0^{1/2}Aa$. Then

\[
\ell_f-\mathbb E[\ell_f\mid D]
=\tfrac12(g^\top Hg-\operatorname{tr}H)+b^\top g,
\quad \|H\|_{\rm op}\leq2V_0/s,\quad
\|H\|_F^2\leq4mV_0^2/s^2,\quad\|b\|^2\leq4V_0R^2/s^2.
\]

The exact Gaussian quadratic-form MGF gives, for \(|\lambda|\leq s/(4V_0)\),

\[
\log\mathbb E\bigl[e^{\lambda(\ell_f-\mathbb E[\ell_f\mid D])}\mid D\bigr]
\leq \lambda^2\left(\frac{2mV_0^2}{s^2}+\frac{4V_0R^2}{s^2}\right). \tag{2}
\]

The conditional expected loss ranges over an interval of width at most $D_*$: use $\log\det\Sigma_f\in[m\log(s/2),m\log V]$, $\operatorname{tr}(A\Sigma_0)\in[0,2mV_0/s]$, and $a^\top Aa\in[0,2R^2/s]$. Hoeffding's lemma for this bounded conditional mean adds at most $\lambda^2D_*^2/8$ to (2). Hence each *unconditionally centered* loss is sub-gamma with MGF $\leq\exp(\lambda^2 v_*^2/2)$ for $|\lambda|\leq1/c_*$. The usual two-sided Bernstein bound and a union bound over $J$ give

\[
\sup_{f\in\mathcal F}|\widehat{\mathbb E\ell_f}-\mathbb E\ell_f|
\leq \sqrt{2v_*^2u/T}+c_*u/T,
\]

then the ERM comparison gives (1). A coarser transparent scale is $v_*+c_*=O(m\log(2V/s)+mV_0/s+R^2/s)$, because $m\geq1$ and $V_0\geq s$. Thus the generalization remainder is

\[
O\!\left(\mathsf S\sqrt{\frac{\log J+\log(1/\delta)}T}
+\mathsf S\frac{\log J+\log(1/\delta)}T\right),\quad
\mathsf S=m\log(2V/s)+mV_0/s+R^2/s.
\]

**Parameter quantization and approximation accounting.** If $P$ parameters lie in $[-W,W]$, $W>0$, a per-coordinate grid of spacing at most $\delta_w\leq W$ has $J\leq(4W/\delta_w)^P$. Consequently $u\leq P\log(4W/\delta_w)+\log(2/\delta)$. A finite class by itself suffices for (1); the approximation hypothesis must separately hold *in the grid class*.

For a continuous approximant that needs rounding, a fixed finite-depth, finite-width architecture with bounded input tokens (context labels as well as coordinates, positional encodings, and other features), bounded parameters, bounded token count $N+m$, and affine/multiplicative, ReLU and standard softmax-attention operations admits finite layerwise constants $L_j,A_j$ on the compact forward-reachable sets such that

\[
\|\Phi_j(h;\theta_j)-\Phi_j(h';\theta'_j)\|
\leq L_j\|h-h'\|+A_j\|\theta_j-\theta'_j\|_\infty.
\]

For padded sequences or finitely many masks, take the largest constants over masks. Rounding each parameter by at most \(\delta_w\) gives the explicit recurrence \(e_0=0\), \(e_j\leq L_j e_{j-1}+A_j\delta_w\), hence

\[
e_L\leq\delta_w\sum_{j=1}^L A_j\prod_{k=j+1}^L L_k. \tag{3}
\]

For clarity, the softmax-attention ingredient is globally Lipschitz on this bounded set: row softmax satisfies \(\|\operatorname{softmax}(z)-\operatorname{softmax}(z')\|_1\leq2\|z-z'\|_\infty\); the query-key logits are bilinear on bounded inputs, and the attention-weighted value map is Lipschitz by the triangle inequality. ReLU is 1-Lipschitz. Euclidean-ball projection and spectral clipping of symmetric matrices in Frobenius norm are nonexpansive. Thus (3) also yields uniform output moduli \(\|\Delta\mu\|_2\leq L_{\theta,\mu}\delta_w\), \(\|\Delta\Sigma\|_{\rm op}\leq L_{\theta,\Sigma}\delta_w\), with constants calculated from the actual architecture. A normalization with a potentially zero denominator would need an additional lower-bound assumption.

The Gaussian forward KL is Lipschitz on the stated bounded output set. For two candidate outputs at the same task,

\[
|\operatorname{KL}(p_D\|q_f)-\operatorname{KL}(p_D\|q_g)|
\leq \frac{2R}{s}\|\mu_f-\mu_g\|_2
+\left(\frac{2mV_0+2R^2}{s^2}+\frac m s\right)
\|\Sigma_f-\Sigma_g\|_{\rm op}. \tag{4}
\]

This follows from \(\|\Sigma_f^{-1}-\Sigma_g^{-1}\|_{\rm op}\leq4\|\Sigma_f-\Sigma_g\|_{\rm op}/s^2\), the trace bound, and integration of the log determinant along the SPD segment. If the continuous predictor, including the same output projections, has uniform forward KL at most \(\varepsilon_{\rm cont}\), its rounded grid predictor therefore has

\[
\varepsilon_{\rm approx}\leq\varepsilon_{\rm cont}
+\delta_w\left[\frac{2R L_{\theta,\mu}}s
+\left(\frac{2mV_0+2R^2}{s^2}+\frac m s\right)L_{\theta,\Sigma}\right]. \tag{5}
\]

Substitute $J\leq(4W/\delta_w)^P$ into (1), and use either the post-quantization $\varepsilon_{\rm approx}$ or (5). This is a sample-complexity/generalization statement, not a uniform-in-context guarantee for the learned predictor.

**Where $B$ can enter.** In a zero-mean GP with $|k(x,x')|\leq\kappa^2$, independent Gaussian observation noise of variance $\tau^2>0$, and noisy query labels, conditioning on $E$ gives $\|\mu_0\|\leq\kappa^2 BN\sqrt m/\tau^2$, $\Sigma_0\succeq\tau^2I$, $\Sigma_0\preceq(\tau^2+m\kappa^2)I$. Thus one may take $M_0=\kappa^2BN\sqrt m/\tau^2$, $s=\tau^2$, $V_0=\tau^2+m\kappa^2$ in this example. This does not assert the same constants for every GP/kernel or a noiseless query process.
