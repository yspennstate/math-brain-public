- The signed pair uses `ReLU(u·v)−ReLU(−u·v)=u·v` exactly, including negative finite-feature inner products. Initial query-only source masking forms `h(x_i)=H[i,:]`, `h(q_b)=J[b,:]`; train-only source masking forms `KX_j` and final `H[:,b]^T X_L`. Local linear mixing supplies `sX_j`, the recurrence coefficients, and `s e_b`. No intra-token product is hidden in this part. With feature `p`, label `1`, ID `m`, read-in row `m`, and two solver vectors `2(m+1)`, `D_0=p+4m+3`; equal-head padding `2 max{p,m+1,ceil(D_0/2)}` suffices. The query readout's first RHS column is the mean, and the next `m` RHS columns yield the entire covariance row including off-diagonals; the ID adds only the diagonal noise.
- The readout in the initial a21 draft used `u_b` for both raw and divided values, appearing to divide its covariance reduction twice. The author corrected it: define `u_b=H[:,b]^T X_L`, `v_b=u_b/(1+δ_L)`, then emit `(v_b[0],J[b,:]+s e_b−v_b[1:m])`.
- `r_D=(1+T_D((c−λ)/d))/(1+T_D(c/d))` is in `[0,ε_D]` with `ε_D=4ρ^D/(1+ρ^D)^2<1`; `P_{D−1}=(1−r_D)/λ` is a polynomial and `0≺P_{D−1}(A)≼A⁻¹`. The mean estimate `||e||²≤ε²||Y||² tr(J)/s` and covariance estimate `||C^{-1/2}ΔC^{-1/2}||_F²≤ε²(tr J)²/s²` give the a22 forward-KL constant `ε²[2||Y||²mR²+m²R⁴]/(4s²)` directly. ## PASS: RBF feature tail, raw encoder, composition (`a23`, `a28`)

- In a28, the square interpolation error is `M²/n²`; polarization gives a multiplication error `2/n²`, and the exponential interpolant error is `U²/(8n²)`. Carrying signed rails and clipping each product yields monomial error `≤2(j−1)/n²`. After the last approximate exponential multiplication, each coordinate error is at most `c_α[ad/2+a²/32+2max(1,k)]/n²`. Crucially, `Σ_{|α|≤k}c_α²=Σ_{j≤k}(da)^j/j!`; the displayed `n`, depth `H=5+2max(0,k−1)`, loose width `W=2(n+3)(D+d+1)`, and encoder parameter-magnitude bound `Q` are sufficient. ## PASS/GAP: conditional Gaussian-NLL ERM (`a24`)

The conditional on `E_B={max_i |Y_{c,i}|≤B}` theorem is correct for iid *whole* GP tasks. Given context, the query Gaussian stays untruncated. For `g∼N(0,I)`, centered NLL equals `(g^THg−tr H)/2+b^Tg`; `||H||≤2V_0/s`, `||H||_F²≤4mV_0²/s²`, `||b||²≤4V_0(M_0+M)²/s²`. The exact quadratic Gaussian MGF for `|λ|≤s/(4V_0)` gives the displayed conditional coefficient; Hoeffding on the conditional mean's interval width `D_*` adds `D_*²/8`, resulting in `v_*²=4mV_0²/s²+8V_0(M_0+M)²/s²+D_*²/4` and `c_*=4V_0/s`. Two-sided union bound with `u=log(2J/δ)` and ERM comparison give (1). The parameter grid bound `(4W/δ_w)^P`, the layerwise rounding recurrence, and candidate-output KL Lipschitz coefficients in (4) check. This proves prior-averaged forward KL for a fixed pre-sample finite class containing an approximant; it does not imply uniform-in-context accuracy of the ERM or that SGD locates it. The global mean/covariance projections must be included in the continuous approximant before invoking its uniform approximation property.

## PASS with complexity qualification: restricted hierarchical finite-feature mixture (`a30`)

The common padded matrix `A_h^{ext}` has extra `(N−n)log s_h` if naively traced; a30 correctly initializes its Chebyshev trace state from the active-slot diagonal mask `M`, so `U_j=T_j((A_h^{ext}−cI)/d)M` has active block exactly `T_j((A_h−cI)/d)` and zero padded columns/rows. `||U_j||op≤1` makes its entries `[-1,1]`; the four-ReLU gate `g(e,z)` equals `ez` for binary position ID `e` on that bounded interval and extracts each diagonal entry. Bundling `U_current` with the two RHS solver vector yields one `K_h` matrix product per component per layer using a signed head pair. Final query aggregation uses one constant-score head for the log trace, two signed heads with source key `±y_i` and value `X_{h,L}[i,1]` for `YᵀP_LY`, and two signed heads for `v_hᵀP_L[Y,v_h]`: at most `5H` readout heads and `max(r,L−1)+2` attention layers. The quadratic term therefore does **not** require a hidden local product of data-dependent channels. The log-Cheb tail, one-sided inverse, component Gaussian KL and mixture weight KL/data processing constants check.

For a strictly ReLU-only weight readout, interpolating the exact softmax at vertices of a triangulated compact logit cube yields a continuous piecewise-affine simplex map: barycentric interpolation keeps every coordinate positive and the sum exactly one. A softmax coordinate is `1/2`-Lipschitz in `||·||∞`; each interpolated coordinate differs by at most `z/2`, and all exact/interpolated probabilities are at least `u_*=e^{-2M_*}/H`, so the log-ratio bound `z/u_*` is safe. Ovchinnikov's max/min-of-affine representation on a convex cube gives an exact finite ReLU circuit. The note counts simplices but not a tight resulting ReLU parameter count; a crude bound is available by computing at most `2^S` minima of at most `S` affine pieces per output (`S` the simplex count), costing `O(H S2^S)` ReLU gates. The restricted theorem assumes **supplied** finite component features, one query, bounded labels, fixed masks and exact weights; it does not yet compose raw-RBF approximation and unconditional pretraining with a mixture evidence perturbation.

**Architecture and originality boundary.** The exact attention implementation assumes an unnormalized sum of `ReLU(Qz·Kz_i)Vz_i`, fixed source-role masks, local linear mixing, exact real fixed-core weights and no layer normalization. A softmax/row-normalized ReLU head changes the matrix-vector product and is not covered by the signed-head identity. The primary Kang–Lee–Cheng Theorem 3.1 also uses unnormalized *PSD-kernel attention* and one query; its separate normalized construction is for context-size extrapolation. The current construction fixes a maximum `N` and weights accordingly. The strongest defensible combined claim is explicit fixed-`m` joint noisy-RBF posterior approximation from compact raw inputs and bounded context labels with a quantifiable shared ReLU encoder and two-head unnormalized masked attention, conservative relative to its finite-feature GP covariance, uniform forward KL at quantified feature width and Chebyshev depth, plus an encoder-grid finite-class Gaussian-NLL ERM bound for either conditioned tasks or the original unconditioned GP prior (with clipping and tail term). Do not label this a solution of the literal closed scalar T336 target or a theorem for an ordinary softmax/layer-normalized transformer.
