# Target 1: accelerated finite-feature GP prediction

Claim T336-w727-C002 v1. Rung: proof (unaudited), subject to recorded checks. The full theorem is not Lean-checked and no independent audit or novelty certification is claimed.

The hardest step is realizing both Chebyshev recurrences in normalized ReLU attention, including the previous-iterate registers and variance readout. Section 4 specifies every head.

## 1. Statement

Fix integers p>=1 and n>=0, reals s>0, B>=0, R>0, eps>0, and a feature map z:X->R^p with ||z(x)||<=R. The zero-mean GP prior has kernel k(x,x')=z(x)^T z(x'). Observations have independent N(0,s) noise. A prior scale tau^2 is absorbed into z. The prediction concerns a new **noisy observation**, not the latent function value.

Input tokens supply any t<=n context pairs (z_i,y_i), |y_i|<=B, and one query q=z(x_*). Zero padding fixes N=n+1 tokens. The supplied feature encoding is specified in Section 4. Its computation from arbitrary raw inputs is not included in the size bound.

Use the bidirectional residual normalized ReLU-attention architecture of Bai, Chen, Wang, Xiong and Mei, arXiv:2306.04637v2, Definition 1, PDF page 5, Eq. (1):

    h_i^+ = h_i + (1/N) sum_heads sum_j ReLU(<Q h_i,K h_j>) V h_j.

Q,K,V are real D-by-D matrices. There is no softmax, layer normalization, or causal mask. Feed-forward blocks are identities. Define

    n_+=max(1,n), L=s+n_+R^2, kappa=L/s, M=nR^2B/s,
    delta=min(1/2, s/(2R^2), sqrt(s eps)/(1+M), s sqrt(eps)/R^2),
    T=ceil((sqrt(kappa)/2) log(2/delta)).

An attention-only transformer of width D=7p+4, depth T+2, and at most five heads per layer outputs (mhat,vhat) with vhat>=s and

    KL(N(m,v) || N(mhat,vhat)) <= eps

uniformly over all these contexts and queries. Here (m,v) are the exact posterior noisy predictive mean and variance. A dense parameter bound is 15(T+2)D^2+2D, including a linear readout. All weights depend only on the fixed bounds, never the observed inputs or labels. They are explicit in Section 4.

If R=0, (mhat,vhat)=(0,s) is exact and the formulas dividing by R are unnecessary. No training, ERM generalization, arbitrary raw-feature complexity, softmax approximation, or infinite-rank truncation theorem is asserted.

## 2. Posterior and spectral interval

Set A=s I+sum_i z_i z_i^T and b=sum_i z_i y_i. Completing the square in the standard-normal weight prior and Gaussian likelihood gives posterior weight mean A^{-1}b and covariance s A^{-1}, hence

    m=q^T A^{-1}b, v=s+s q^T A^{-1}q.

For every r, s||r||^2<=r^T A r<=(s+nR^2)||r||^2<=L||r||^2. Thus A is symmetric positive definite, spec(A) is contained in [s,L], v>=s, and

    ||A^{-1}b||<=nRB/s, ||A^{-1}q||<=R/s.

These are deterministic bounds and use no independence among context features.

## 3. Accelerated iteration and its error

Put c=(L+s)/2, d=(L-s)/2, a=c/d. Since n_+>=1 and R>0, d>0,a>1. Define Chebyshev polynomials C_0(x)=1,C_1(x)=x,C_{j+1}(x)=2xC_j(x)-C_{j-1}(x).

For a right-hand side f start x_0=x_{-1}=0. Let eta_0=1/c,beta_0=0 and, for j>=1,

    eta_j=2C_j(a)/(d C_{j+1}(a)), beta_j=C_{j-1}(a)/C_{j+1}(a).

Take T updates

    x_{j+1}=x_j+beta_j(x_j-x_{j-1})+eta_j(f-Ax_j).

Every denominator is positive: for zeta=a+sqrt(a^2-1)>1 the polynomial recurrence gives C_j(a)=(zeta^j+zeta^{-j})/2>0.

For x*=A^{-1}f, induction gives

    x*-x_j=r_j(A)x*,
    r_j(lambda)=C_j((c-lambda)/d)/C_j(a).

At j=0 this is the identity; at j=1 it is 1-lambda/c. For the induction, C_{j+1}(a)+C_{j-1}(a)=2a C_j(a) gives 1+beta_j=eta_j c. Substitution in the error recurrence then gives exactly the recurrence for C_{j+1}. All coefficients are fixed independently of A and f.

For lambda in [s,L], (c-lambda)/d is in [-1,1]. By the cosine addition formula and induction, C_j(cos theta)=cos(j theta); thus |C_j(x)|<=1 there. Also

    zeta=(sqrt(kappa)+1)/(sqrt(kappa)-1), rho=zeta^{-1} in (0,1).

Orthogonal spectral decomposition gives ||r_T(A)||_op<=1/C_T(a)<=2rho^T. With u=1/sqrt(kappa) in (0,1),

    log((1+u)/(1-u))=integral_0^u 2/(1-x^2) dx>=2u.

The denominator is positive throughout the integration interval. Hence 2rho^T<=2exp(-2T/sqrt(kappa))<=delta by the stated T.

Apply the iteration simultaneously to f=b and f=q, with final iterates w_T,u_T. It follows that

    ||w_T-A^{-1}b||<=delta nRB/s,
    ||u_T-A^{-1}q||<=delta R/s.

Furthermore u_T=g_T(A)q, where g_T(lambda)=(1-r_T(lambda))/lambda. Since |r_T(lambda)|<=delta<=1/2 and lambda>=s>0, g_T(lambda)>0, so q^T u_T>=0.

## 4. Explicit attention heads

Each token contains seven p-dimensional registers

    (z_c,z_q,qbar,w,u,w_old,u_old)

and four scalars (y,one,mout,vout), for D=7p+4. Every token has one=1. Context i has z_c=z_i,z_q=0,y=y_i. The query has z_c=0,z_q=q,y=0. Padding has z_c=z_q=0,y=0. All working registers and mout start at zero; vout=s. This is a fixed role-dependent feature encoding with no query label.

Specifying linear Q and K coordinate lists of equal length and a linear V output defines ordinary D-by-D matrices, with all unspecified coordinates zero. Constant scores use the register one.

**Broadcast layer, one head:** Q=one,K=one, and V writes N z_q into qbar. Exactly one token supplies z_q, so after the normalized sum qbar=q on every token.

The four iterate registers are identical on all tokens at initialization. The following layers preserve that invariant.

**Solver layer j, five heads:** All expressions below use pre-layer registers; eta=eta_j,beta=beta_j.

1. Q=(w,one), K=(-z_c,y). V writes N eta z_c into w. Its score is y_j-z_{c,j}^T w_i.
2. Q=-(w,one), K=(-z_c,y). V writes -N eta z_c into w.

Together these write eta sum_i z_i(y_i-z_i^T w), by ReLU(r)-ReLU(-r)=r.

3. Q=u,K=z_c. V writes -N eta z_c into u.
4. Q=-u,K=z_c. V writes N eta z_c into u.

Together these write -eta sum_i z_i z_i^T u.

5. Q=one,K=one. V writes the following, with **no N factor**:

       beta(w-w_old)-eta s w        into w,
       beta(u-u_old)+eta(qbar-s u)  into u,
       w-w_old                     into w_old,
       u-u_old                     into u_old.

The normalized sum in head 5 is the average of identical registers and equals the displayed expression. The residual connection makes the old registers equal to the pre-layer w,u. Summing all five heads produces exactly the recurrences of Section 3 for f=b,q. Context, feature, label, constant, and output registers are unchanged. Query and padding contribute zero to the data sums because z_c=y=0. This proves the invariant and exact implementation by induction.

**Readout layer, four heads:** Q=qbar,K=w,V writing one into mout, and Q=-qbar,K=w,V writing -one into mout, together produce q^T w_T. The factors 1/N cancel the sum over N identical w registers. Likewise Q=qbar,K=u,V writing s one into vout, paired with Q=-qbar,K=u,V writing -s one into vout, produces s q^T u_T. The preexisting vout=s supplies the noise floor. Thus the query token outputs are exactly

    mhat=q^T w_T, vhat=s+s q^T u_T>=s.

This is T+2 layers and at most five heads. Counting three D-by-D matrices per head gives the parameter bound. There is no nonlinear readout computing products or inverses outside the transformer.

## 5. Uniform forward KL

The errors obey

    |m-mhat|<=M delta<=sqrt(s eps),
    |v-vhat|<=R^2 delta<=min(s/2,s sqrt(eps)).

For the mean inequality use M/(1+M)<=1, also when M=0. Both variances are >=s. Put h=(v-vhat)/vhat, so |h|<=1/2 and 1+h>0. The exact Gaussian formula is

    KL=(h-log(1+h))/2+(m-mhat)^2/(2vhat).

For h in [-1/2,1/2], h-log(1+h)=integral_0^h x/(1+x) dx<=h^2. If h>=0, use x/(1+x)<=x. If h<=0, reverse the integral and use |x/(1+x)|<=2|x| on [h,0]. All denominators remain >=1/2. Consequently

    KL<=|v-vhat|^2/(2s^2)+|m-mhat|^2/(2s)<=eps.

All estimates hold for every admissible context and query. There is no conversion from a prior-averaged loss to uniform KL.

## 6. Prior work and boundaries

Bai et al., arXiv:2306.04637v2, Definition 1 (PDF page 5), supplies the architecture. Theorem 4 (PDF page 7) and Appendix D.1 supply known GD ridge point prediction with three heads and depth proportional to the condition number. Those facts are settled and are not claimed anew. The construction here combines classical Chebyshev iteration with exact attention operations to obtain simultaneous mean/variance and an explicit uniform KL budget. Novelty remains subject to literature and independent review.

The feature-input qualification is essential. A bounded finite-rank kernel on a compact set alone imposes no continuous or efficiently computable feature map on the raw inputs. A raw-input theorem needs additional regularity and an explicit feature-computation cost. The dossier's feature-map formulation motivates the feature-input reading used here; the reviewer must decide whether it closes Target 1 as intended.
