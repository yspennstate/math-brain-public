## Proven result

For \(A\sim N(0,1)\), define \(h(\eta,p)=\mathbb E|1-\eta A^2|^p\). The positive root \(\alpha(\eta)\in(1,2)\) exists uniquely throughout the open interval \((49/50,51/50)\), is \(C^\infty\) there, and satisfies

\[
\alpha'(\eta)
=-\frac{N_\eta}{m_\eta}<0,
\qquad
N_\eta=-\alpha\,\mathbb E[A^2\operatorname{sgn}(1-\eta A^2)|1-\eta A^2|^{\alpha-1}],
\qquad
m_\eta=\mathbb E[|1-\eta A^2|^\alpha\log|1-\eta A^2|].
\]

Both \(N_\eta\) and \(m_\eta\) are strictly positive. On the requested closed interval \([99/100,101/100]\), the supplement proves the exact rational estimates

\[
1/50<\partial_p h(\eta,\alpha)<16,
\qquad
25/101<\partial_\eta h(\eta,\alpha)<303/50,
\qquad
25/1616<-\alpha'(\eta)<303.
\]

These bounds are intentionally coarse. No quadrature or floating-point estimate is used as proof.

## Checks that matter

1. **Logarithmic singularities are included.** Rescaling \(y=\eta A^2\) gives the fixed-singularity integral

   \[
   h(\eta,p)=\frac1{\sqrt{2\pi\eta}}
   \int_0^\infty |1-y|^p y^{-1/2}e^{-y/(2\eta)}\,dy.
   \]

   Compact-parameter envelopes at 0, 1, and infinity justify every mixed derivative on \(\eta>0,p>-1\). The local identity \(\int_0^\varepsilon u^q|\log u|^sdu<\infty\) for \(q>-1\) proves both the weighted logarithmic expectations and \(\mathbb E|\log|1-\eta A^2||<\infty\).

2. **The all-orders claim uses the appropriate integral.** Higher direct \(\eta\)-derivatives of the original pointwise power may fail to be integrable when \(1<p<2\). The proof differentiates the smooth scale density after rescaling. A separate Gaussian-polynomial bound validates the useful direct first derivative formula.

3. **Both signs in the implicit derivative are proved.** Strict convexity of \(p\mapsto h(\eta,p)\), together with \(h(\eta,1)<1=h(\eta,\alpha)\), makes \(h_p>0\). Strict convexity of \(u\mapsto h(u,\alpha)\), together with \(h(0,\alpha)=h(\eta,\alpha)=1\), makes \(h_\eta>0\). The proof does not assume pointwise monotonicity of \(|1-\eta A^2|\).

4. **Endpoints are handled by a genuine extension.** The interval \((49/50,51/50)\) has \(h(\eta,1)<99/100<1\) and \(h(\eta,2)>1\), so local smooth implicit roots patch on an open neighborhood of the requested closed interval.

5. **The stationary consequence is the correct one.** Using only the base source's positive exact constants, for every fixed \(\eta_1<\eta_2\),

   \[
   \frac{\pi_{\eta_2}(|x|>t)}{\pi_{\eta_1}(|x|>t)}
   \sim\frac{C_{\eta_2}}{C_{\eta_1}}
   t^{\alpha(\eta_1)-\alpha(\eta_2)}\to\infty.
   \]

   The proof additionally identifies the interval of moments that become infinite as the stepsize increases. It does not infer an ordering of the constants, a full stochastic order, uniformity over pairs of stepsizes, or the derivative of a tail asymptotic.
