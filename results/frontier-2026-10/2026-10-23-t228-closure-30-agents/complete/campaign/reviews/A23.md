# A23 review: nonzero-teacher signed-tail strengthening

Date: 23 October 2026.

**Status: proved as an optional addition.** The existing T228 parameter
rectangle admits equal finite, strictly positive one-sided constants even
when the fixed teacher is nonzero and the stationary law is not symmetric.

Deliverable: `campaign/proofs/T228_SIGNED_TAILS.md`.

## Primary-source audit

Checked the primary PDF of Alsmeyer–Brofferio–Buraczewski,
[arXiv:2102.02299v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2102.02299v2), dated
6 October 2021, including visual inspection of printed pp. 7 and 36.
The proof file contains the full call to Theorem 2.1, equations (12)–(16),
and Proposition 8.1, together with the standing continuity and bounded
remainder conditions. Section 4.3 was checked for the Markov additive
interpretation of nonarithmeticity.

## Decisive checks

| Issue | Resolution |
|---|---|
| Dependence of labels and inputs | The iid object is the complete step; independence of `M` and `Bθ` is not needed. |
| The paper's `B≥1` convention | Take `B=1∨Bθ`; all positive moments remain finite. |
| Slopes on both half-lines | Both are `M=1−ηA²`. |
| Irreducibility | Both off-diagonal probabilities equal `P(M<0)>0`. Positive self-loops also occur. |
| Spectral root and eigenvector normalization | `P(s)=[[d_s,o_s],[o_s,d_s]]`, `ρ(s)=E abs(M)^s`; `u=(1/2,1/2)`, `v=(1,1)`. |
| Logarithmic moments | The explicit multiplier density gives `E[abs(M)^α abs(log(abs(M)))]<∞`. |
| Markov arithmetic with arbitrary state offsets | On `M>0`, the offsets cancel and `log M` has a conditional density. The lattice event has probability at most `P(M<0)<1`. |
| Tilted formulation | One-step tilt density is `abs(M)^α`; the same bound becomes `o_α<1`. |
| Positivity support assumption | Stationarity plus a nondegenerate conditional Gaussian transition gives `supp π=R`. |
| Equality of constants | The theorem's Perron relation and equal components of `u` yield `C_+=C_-`; adding tails identifies each as `C_abs/2`. |
| Exact symmetry | Neither assumed nor implied; the source model's obstruction for `θ≠0` is preserved. |

## Dependencies and scope

The source model was read at SHA256
`f95fa6fade82ba0dca4dab8e311fc61bb57b3267f3953b465c94228cb1315933`.
The new proof relies on its actual nonlinear remainder bound, established
stationary existence and exponent calculations. It rechecks the primary
theorem's moments, sign transition structure, nonarithmeticity and support.
The existing absolute-tail formula identifies the constant after the
signed limits have been obtained. No simulation is used.

The result is pointwise in `(η,θ)` on the existing rectangle. It does not
give a uniform lower bound, exact finite-threshold tail equality, uniform
stationary sign probabilities, or a reflection-invariant law for nonzero
teacher. It does not claim a result outside this model or parameter range.

Only the two named deliverables were written. The baseline proof and all
other files were left unchanged; no remote write was made. The natural
integration is to replace the baseline's one-sided-tail disclaimer with
the new limit while retaining its proof of nonsymmetry.
