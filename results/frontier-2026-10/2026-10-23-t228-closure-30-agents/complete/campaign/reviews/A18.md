## Mathematical checks and theorem boundary

I rederived the derivative bound, mixed input-label moment, moment equation, and exact arithmetic. The contraction is a bound on the global Lipschitz constant of the actual nonlinear update. A negative `E log|M|`, or a Foster drift estimate, would not by itself prove that obligation. The old T1 proof explicitly disclaims global Lipschitz contraction; the current explicit bound supplies the additional certificate.

For a nonzero teacher, one must retain dependence between `A` and `Y`. The needed estimate is `E[A^2 |Y|] <= (1+delta)|theta| E|A|^3 + E[A^2 |E|] < 3+2delta`; only `A` and the additive noise `E` are independent. Substituting this into the actual derivative envelope gives `99010707/100000000<1`. Calling the nonzero-teacher labels themselves independent Gaussian variables would be incorrect. Their additive noise is Gaussian and independent; their conditional mean depends on the input.

I independently opened the [primary Jelenkovic–Olvera-Cravioto PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1006.3295v5), visually checked Theorem 3.1 on printed page 5, and compared the [HTML statement](https://arxiv.org/html/1006.3295v5), numbered Theorem 2. It permits arbitrary independent `R` and coefficients subject to its discrepancy and moment hypotheses. The scalar specialization therefore applies to `R=|X|` and `C=|M|`; an affine fixed-point identity is not required. The theorem permits a zero limiting constant, so it does not replace the separate positivity argument.

I also checked the [GSZ primary source](https://arxiv.org/html/2006.04740v5), Section 3. Its mean-contraction setup uses the global Lipschitz constant; its displayed Theorem 1 lists the bounded remainder, nonarithmetic, and moment requirements. The current proofs verify those displayed inputs and establish the desired conclusion with a precise renewal theorem plus positivity. They should not claim to certify every hypothesis of Mirek's original theorem or rely on an automatic positive-constant conclusion from the abbreviated GSZ statement.

The positivity reasoning was checked directly: tilting the **whole** step preserves allowed within-step dependence; the stopped event uses `V_tau`, not an unstopped future-measurable event; overshoots are uniformly controlled by translated occupation bounds; and the last-exit inequality is in the direction needed for a stationary lower bound. Nothing requires a finite stationary alpha moment. The numerator in the constant formula is an integrable coupled difference, not a difference of two separately finite expectations.

For common globally bounded activations with bounded derivative, such as tanh or sigmoid, this same unregularized scalar construction instead has `Psi(x)=x+O_omega(1)`. Its asymptotic slope is one and its global Lipschitz constant is at least one. That observation explains why replacing a bounded perturbation by a bounded activation would change the proof materially. It is not a blanket impossibility theorem for every smooth bounded activation or every network architecture.
