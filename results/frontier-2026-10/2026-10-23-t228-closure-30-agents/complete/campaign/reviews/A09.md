## Symmetry warning, with a direct check

Although the marginal label distribution remains symmetric, this does not
make the invariant state law symmetric for a fixed nonzero teacher. The
valid identity is

```text
Ψ_(−θ,A,−E)(−x)=−Ψ_(θ,A,E)(x),
```

so it relates two different teacher models. The proof additionally shows
that `π_(η,θ)` is not symmetric about zero when `θ≠0`: stationarity requires

```text
E[Aσ'(AX)σ(AX)] = E[Aσ'(AX)σ(Aθ)].
```

The left side would be zero under a symmetric `X`, whereas the right side
has the strict sign of `θ`. The integrability needed for this argument
follows from the already established first moment. This does not rule out
asymptotic equality of one-sided constants through a separate signed
renewal theorem; no such conclusion is used by the extension.
