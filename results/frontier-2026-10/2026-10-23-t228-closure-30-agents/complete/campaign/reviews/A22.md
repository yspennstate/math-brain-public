The new regime is

```text
σ(z)=z+0.001 tanh(z),  |θ|≤1,  b=2,  11/10≤η≤6/5.
```

Every step averages two iid squared sample losses. Each input and each
additive noise remains standard normal, with `Yᵢ=σ(Aᵢθ)+Eᵢ`. All
four Gaussian variables in a batch are independent, and entire batches
are iid across steps. The update is

```text
Ψθ,2(x)=x−(η/2)Σ_(i=1)^2 Aᵢσ'(Aᵢx)[σ(Aᵢx)−Yᵢ].
```

In particular the normalization is the average loss, not its sum and
not two sequential updates. The theorem includes the genuine nonzero
teacher `θ=1` at `η=6/5`.

## Concrete mathematical addition

The asymptotic multiplier is

```text
M=1−(η/2)(A₁²+A₂²)=1−ηT,   T∼Exp(1).
```

Its first and second absolute moments have the exact formulas

```text
h_(η,2)(1)=1−η+2ηe^(−1/η),
h_(η,2)(2)=1−2η+2η².
```

For the first moment, differentiation and `e<3` give

```text
d/dη h_(η,2)(1)>−1+2(1/3)(11/6)=2/9>0.
```

The exact positive Taylor sum

```text
Σ_(k=0)^4(5/6)^k/k!=71449/31104,
71449/31104−25/11=8339/342144>0
```

proves `e^(−5/6)<11/25`. Hence `h_(η,2)(1)<107/125` uniformly.
The derivative perturbation remains bounded in expectation by
`η(10007/10^6)` because the two sample perturbations are averaged.
The resulting actual global Lipschitz certificate is

```text
E Lip(Ψθ,2)
<107/125+(6/5)(10007/10^6)
=2170021/2500000=0.8680084<0.87<1.
```

Its exact margin is `329979/2500000=0.1319916`. Meanwhile
`h_(η,2)(2)≥61/50>1`. The unique positive moment root therefore lies
strictly between 1 and 2 throughout the new interval.
