## Main conclusions

- Every positive absolute moment is finite at every finite time.
- For `p<α`, the moments converge to their finite stationary values.
- For `p≥α`, the moments tend to infinity along the entire sequence,
  by weak convergence and bounded truncations; mere subsequential
  unboundedness is not the conclusion.
- The critical moment has the exact linear asymptotic
  `E|X_n|^α/n→α E[|M|^α log|M|] C_η>0`.
- For every `p>α`,
  `E|X_n|^p/h(p)^n→K_p(x_0,η)∈(0,∞)`.
- In particular, both `E X_n²` and `Var(X_n)` are asymptotic to
  `K_2(x_0,η)(1−2η+3η²)^n`.

The crucial elementary estimate is the two-sided norm inequality

```text
| ||X_{n+1}||_p − h(p)^{1/p}||X_n||_p | ≤ (E B^p)^{1/p}.
```

It requires only fresh-step independence from the state and the
uniform nonlinear remainder bound. It does not require independence
between the multiplier and remainder. After normalization, its errors
are summable whenever `h(p)>1`. If the moments are unbounded, the
normalized norm limit must be strictly positive, because its
unnormalized error is bounded by
`(E B^p)^{1/p}/(h(p)^{1/p}−1)`.

## Explicit second-moment bound independent of stationary tail theory

With `q=0.98606303`, let

```text
N = min{n≥0:q^n|x_0|≤50},
h_2 = 1−2η+3η².
```

Separating the independent Gaussian label term from the deterministic
nonlinear perturbation gives, for every `n≥N`,

```text
E X_{n+1}² ≥ h_2 E X_n²+39/100.
```

Consequently, for every integer `k≥0`,

```text
E X_{N+k}² ≥ h_2^k E X_N²
              +(39/100)(h_2^k−1)/(h_2−1)
            ≥ (39/100)((1.9603)^k−1)/0.9603.
```

The second inequality and the starting index are uniform throughout
the learning-rate interval. The exact normalized second-moment limit
therefore satisfies the explicit positive bound

```text
K_2 ≥ (39/100)h_2^(−N)/(h_2−1).
```

This proves the full exponential factor `h_2`, stronger than an
eventual lower bound with an arbitrary factor `ρ<h_2`. Its proof uses
only the actual update and average contraction; it does not depend on
the existence, value, or positivity of the stationary power-tail
constant.

The same drift independently proves infinite stationary variance:
`E_π|X|<47`, and a hypothetical finite `v_π=E_πX²` would satisfy
`v_π>h_2 v_π+0.6981`, a contradiction. Thus both infinite stationary
second moment and the finite-time exponential growth are verified
without invoking the stationary tail theorem.
