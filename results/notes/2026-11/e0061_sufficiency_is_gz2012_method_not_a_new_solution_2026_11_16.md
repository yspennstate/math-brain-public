status: verified (the Ganikhodjaev-Zanin text read on 16 Nov).

E0061's 8 Sep sufficiency proof (top species of a transitive tournament has a nondecreasing coordinate w_{n+1}=w_n(1+p u_n+q v_n), so the other two go to 0) is correct, but it is the argument Ganikhodjaev and Zanin print in sec 2.1 of arXiv:1205.3841 (rows of the skew matrix all nonnegative => monotone coordinate => convergence to a vertex), written there for m=4, and their Theorem 1 states the S^2 iff (attributed to their 2004 note, which proves only necessity). So the result and the method belong to them; a paper from us would at most be a written-out S^2 corollary, not an open problem solved. Not posted; the internal list that counted E0061 as 'solved, no repo' was corrected.

How to apply: before posting any 'solved' row, read the cited source itself AND the same authors' later papers for the method, not only for the statement.
