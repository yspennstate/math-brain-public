Is the oriented Kesten-McKay law for random directed d-regular graphs (top-200 T141) still open? Claimed solved, unrefereed, as of 14 Nov 2026.

- Yukun He and Jiaoyang Huang, arXiv:2609.05297 (4 Sep 2026, 44 pp): for every fixed d >= 2 the empirical eigenvalue distribution of a uniform random d-regular digraph converges in probability to the oriented Kesten-McKay law; the proof goes through a small-ball bound for the smallest singular value of the shifted adjacency matrix.
