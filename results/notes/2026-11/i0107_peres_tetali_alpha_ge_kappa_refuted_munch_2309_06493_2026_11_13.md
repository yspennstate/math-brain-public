Is the Peres-Tetali conjecture (I0107, Salez, Modern aspects of Markov chains, arXiv 2508.21055v1, Question 4.1: do we always have alpha >= kappa, modified log-Sobolev constant at least Ollivier-Ricci curvature) open? No (checked 13 Nov 2026): the source itself says on p.54, right after the question, that counterexamples have been constructed, reference [92] = F. Munch, Ollivier curvature, isoperimetry, concentration, and log-Sobolev inequality, arXiv 2309.06493 (2023): Markov chains with uniformly positive Ollivier and Bakry-Emery curvature whose MLSI constant tends to zero; Munch also has a weighted three-vertex counterexample and proves the conjecture under nonnegative Ollivier sectional curvature.

The repaired form is a theorem: Salez Theorem 4.2 (alpha >= kappa_1 plus the adjoint chain's sectional-type curvature), from Caputo-Munch-Salez, Entropy and curvature: beyond the Peres-Tetali conjecture, Trans. Amer. Math. Soc. 378(5):3551-3571 (2025), arXiv 2401.17148.
