Is the Parisi (Mezard-Parisi) formula for diluted spin glasses still open (top-200 T137)? Claimed solved, unrefereed, as of 14 Nov 2026.

- D. Panchenko, "The Mezard-Parisi formula", arXiv:2610.39545 (29 Oct 2026, 66 pp). Theorem 2.1 (read in the PDF, pp. 3-5): for the diluted K-spin model with even K >= 2 and J standard Gaussian or symmetric sign, and for random K-SAT with K >= 2, with Poisson(lambda N) clauses, lim F_N = inf over (r, zeta, h0) of the Mezard-Parisi functional, at every beta > 0. The lower bound (2.11) holds for every symmetric clause law with exponential moments; the upper bound is Franz-Leone. Viana-Bray is the K = 2 case. The paper says the proof was found by ChatGPT Astra and checked and rewritten by Panchenko.
