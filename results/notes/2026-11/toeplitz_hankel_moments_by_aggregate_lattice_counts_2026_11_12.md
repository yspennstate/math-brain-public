Paper 8 (toeplitz-hankel-section-profiles) quoted Toeplitz m16-m22 and Hankel m18 with no script in its repo producing them and no sentence saying how. Method that does it: L_k(N) = sum over pairings of lattice points of N*P(pi), computed as a slot scan whose state is (height, multiset of open pair values); Hankel uses the walk x -> s-x with the opening slot's parity in the state (parity-mixing pairs only). Every moment polytope is Gorenstein of index two (paper 7 Thm 5.1, both ensembles), so the aggregate h* is palindromic of degree k and L_k(0..floor(k/2)) fixes it; m_2k = h*(1)/(k+1)!. Checked: Toeplitz m2..m22 exact, order-22 counts equal check_extension A11; Hankel m2..m18 exact; palindromy tested directly for k<=6. Toeplitz k=11 about a minute, Hankel k=9 26 s. Repo file verify/aggregate_lattice_counts.py (commit 8a3c008, bundle in fei_papers_agent/repo_bundles); scratch copies in fei_papers_agent/checks/. Lesson for paper reads: a number the paper says it computed needs its generator in the repo and one sentence of method in the text. Status verified; would change if the Gorenstein theorem's Hankel case failed in these coordinates (palindromy was only tested directly to k=6).
