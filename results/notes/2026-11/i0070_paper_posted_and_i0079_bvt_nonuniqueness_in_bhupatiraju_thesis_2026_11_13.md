I0079 (Jardon-Sanchez, Mellick, Poulin, Wrobel, arXiv:2502.11622, Question 2: in what generality is the ideal Bernoulli Voronoi tessellation unique?): uniqueness fails in general and that is already in print. Bhupatiraju's 2019 thesis, Thm 2.2 (pp. 63-65), gives two different subsequential limits on C2*C3 with independent-priority tie-breaking. The Codex run I0079A re-derived it, replaced the thesis's numerical step by an analytic oscillation proof, and extended it to C2*K for |K| >= 3; that extension is one unrefereed run. Still open: which groups or metrics have a unique limit (Kazhdan groups in particular). Ledger RESOLVED_IN_LITERATURE.
