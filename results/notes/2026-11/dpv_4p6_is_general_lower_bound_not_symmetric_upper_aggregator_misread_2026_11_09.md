Is there a published upper bound of 4.6 on the Fourier entropy-influence constant for symmetric functions (as one summary site says, "Das et al. 2011")? No (checked 9 Nov 2026, an agent, for paper v4.1).

The emergentmind summary misreads Das, Pal and Visavaliya, arXiv:1110.4301: at its line 126, 4.6 is a LOWER bound on the FEI constant for general Boolean functions, quoted from O'Donnell-Wright-Zhou, not an upper bound for symmetric ones. No published symmetric-function upper bound below our 4.650431 was found; the closest priors cited in v4.1 are Gopalan-Mohan-Paturi arXiv:2606.00246 and Hod arXiv:1711.00762.

How to apply: before calling a bound new, read the primary paper's own sentence, not an aggregator's gloss; check whether a quoted number is a lower or an upper bound and for which class.
