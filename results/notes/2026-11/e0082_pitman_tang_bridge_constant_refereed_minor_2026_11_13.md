Open problem E0082 (Pitman-Tang, Patterns in random walks and Brownian motion, arXiv:1411.0041v3, Open problem 2.3), 13 Nov 2026. Claim E0082A PARTIAL, referee E0082AR MINOR: no math gap. Proved: for every fixed real alpha the bridge-pattern waiting time satisfies E T(BR_alpha,n) ~ n c_BR(alpha), c_BR an explicit convergent series of finite-dimensional integrals, via weighted matching-matrix column sums, a lattice local limit theorem and uniform integrability. For alpha<0 the first-passage family has E T of order n log n (the Peres order conjecture in the published chapter), which is the E0081 result already public as github.com/yspennstate/first-passage-pattern-waiting-time; its leading constant is open.
