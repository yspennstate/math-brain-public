P0383 (200 list; Levine-Silvestri, "Universality conjectures for activated random walk", Probab. Surveys 21 (2024), arXiv
2306.01698, Conjecture 20): for zeta > zeta_s, the number of sleepers above density zeta in an L-cube under the infinite-volume
wired stationary state pi has probability exp(-c L^d).

Status 13 Nov 2026: OPEN, and conditional on something open. The conjecture presupposes pi (the V -> Z^d limit of the
finite wired chains' stationary laws, Conjecture 13), and its existence is not proved in any dimension: Hoffman-Richey-Son,
"Local density of ARW on Z", arXiv 2601.07816 (Jan 2026), call their point-source local density result "a first step towards
understanding the local structure of the critical stationary measure". What is proved: the density conjecture zeta_s = zeta_c
on Z (Hoffman-Johnson-Junge arXiv 2406.01731; new proof Forien arXiv 2502.02579); mean-field limits (Junge-Kaufman-Meisel
arXiv 2509.10616) and the complete graph (Junge-Kaufman arXiv 2604.04747). I recall HJJ also bound the finite-volume
stationary density's deviations on an interval; NOT checked against the paper, check before citing.

The route, if anyone tries: in d = 1, upgrade a finite-interval exponential upper tail (if HJJ have it) to a statement uniform
in the window position, then pass to the limit along the HRS local-density estimates. Existence of pi in d = 1 is the first
gap; Conjecture 20 in d >= 2 is out of reach.

How to apply: not a solve target now. Cite as conditional on Conjecture 13. One extended search on 13 Nov; abstracts of
2601.07816 and 2406.01731 read.
