Accuracy fell from 5.00 to 3.83 in both plain and writing-brain arms; news worst (3.08). Errors: writer computed a slightly different estimate (other exclusions, 1-5 pct off) or led with a competing estimate. Writing brain cards are about voice and do not check numbers. Detection unchanged (P(machine) ~95.5, AUC 1.0 vs matched humans). This is the first arm with headroom on accuracy; use it to test anything claimed to help science writing, e.g. a check-every-figure step.
