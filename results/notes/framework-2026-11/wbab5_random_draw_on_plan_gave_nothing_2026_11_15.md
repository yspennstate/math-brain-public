The plan brief of [[wbab4_plan_ranks_far_better_not_a_win_by_rule_2026_11_15]] plus one step: run `turn.py draw`, a
random leaf of a tree of scene shapes with a pinned five-step walk to the turn, and write inside the drawn shape. Rule
registered first; six writers, the same three tasks, judged against the same eighteen control pages.

- Mean rank 6.32 against 6.68, one-sided p 0.36; 8 misfit flags on three pages against none. Not a win.
- On the same control pages the plan alone stood at 4.78 against 8.22, so the draw took away most of what the plan
  gained and brought misfit words. The pages were not less alike than plan pages (word overlap 0.158 against 0.152).

This is the third random mechanism tested blind (a word walk, a paragraph list, and this scene-shape draw), and the
third that did not help. How to apply: do not hand a writer a random draw of words, paragraphs or scene shape and
expect a better page; keep `turn.py draw` only as a way to break a habit by hand.

Limits: the judges are one model family, the one that wrote the pages; no person ranked anything.
