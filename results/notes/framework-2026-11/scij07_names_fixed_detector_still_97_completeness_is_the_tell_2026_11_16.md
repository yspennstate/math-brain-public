The fix proposed in [[scij06_word_budget_detector_names_coded_labels_2026_11_16]], run with the same twelve writers
and tasks: a deterministic map rewrote every code (site S07, cohort C3) as a natural name in the briefs, the data and
the grading facts alike.

- Codes disappeared from the detector's stated reasons (43 of 72 to 0).
- Detection did not move: 97.42 percent of plain pieces and 97.33 percent of writing-brain pieces called
  machine-written, human pieces 4 to 14 percent.
- Print-ready ratings: plain 3.61, writing brain 3.39 (3.92 and 3.64 the round before, 4.17 and 4.06 the round before
  that). The writing brain rates lower three rounds running.

What is left is completeness against the brief: every fact asked for, in the order asked, polished. A person writing
from the same material chooses. The next arms: a brief that names a reader and a purpose but gives no fact list, and a
writer told to choose one finding and drop the rest.

The general lesson: when a judge names a feature, removing the feature is the test of whether it was causal. A named
reason is a lead, not a cause.
