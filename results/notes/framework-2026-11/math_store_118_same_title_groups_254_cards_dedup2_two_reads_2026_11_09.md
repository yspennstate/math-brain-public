How many duplicate cards does the math store have now, and is anyone merging them?

118 groups of claim cards share a title once case, accents, punctuation and a leading "Technique:" or "Attempt on" are folded (numbers and signs kept, the 6 Oct rule). That is 254 cards with no supersedes edge on any of them, out of 9,073 claim cards not already in a supersedes edge (11,107 claim nodes in all). Counted read-only on the live store of the second machine ($MATHBRAIN_STORE) on 9 Nov 00:30 with dup_census.py. Group sizes: 102 pairs, 14 triples, 2 of four (total variation distance, sufficient statistic). 18 groups have byte-identical bodies after trimming. Title matching misses near-duplicates under different names (the Hoeffding family), so 118 is a floor.

Both W96 probes saw the same thing. 7 Nov: Glivenko-Cantelli x2, Talagrand x4. 9 Nov: Paley-Zygmund x3, Skorokhod x2, characteristic function x2. Neither probe found a miss (10 of 10 at rank 1 or 2), so duplicates, not gaps, are what the store visibly gets wrong. A duplicate splits a theorem's links across two cards and pushes a neighbour out of the top 3.

The merge lane: inputs in dedup2 (GROUPS.md has whole bodies, ids and link counts; groups.jsonl holds the same data). Two Codex briefs, DEDUP2A and DEDUP2B, read every group independently and write MERGE_PLAN.jsonl with a verdict of same, partial, different or wrong and a keeper. an agent lands only the groups where both reads agree, as one keeper -[supersedes]-> retired edge each (the Kakutani way, [[math_store_dup_card_race_check_before_claim_head_guard_2026_11_08]]), under a store claim, then queries each old title to check the keeper comes first. The Windows campaign tools (land_merge_plans.py, SPEC_DEDUP.md) are not on this machine; [[the_math_store_dedup_pipeline_and_its_first_day_2026_10_06]] is the procedure they followed.
