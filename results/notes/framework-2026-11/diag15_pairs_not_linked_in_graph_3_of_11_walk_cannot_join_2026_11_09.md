Would following the graph from one cause reach the second cause in a DIAG15 join item? Mostly no: for 3 of the 11 pairs the second memory is named in the first one's node text or sits in its top-20 neighbours (either direction); 1 of 11 by node text alone. Checked 09 Nov 00:15 by an agent with brain.py node and neighbors -n 20 on the pair slugs from keys_private/diag15/key.json (script: diag15_links.py).

DIAG15's pairs were built by pairing freshly drawn, unrelated scars into one two-cause symptom, so the graph has no reason to link them. A graph walk or spreading step cannot be the fix for joins of this kind; recall has to be asked two separate questions (decomposition, as recall --each does), or the second question has to come after the first cause is named, which is the staged design DIAG15's own Next proposed.

**Why:** DIAG15 found plain recall held both memories of a pair in its top 40 for 0 of 14, and the obvious next idea is to follow links from the first hit. This rules that out before anyone builds it.

**How to apply:** for multi-cause faults, split the symptom into one question per suspected cause; do not expect neighbours of the first hit to supply the second. Same test as [[diag15_two_memory_joins_brain_6_9_of_66_bare_0_below_bar_2026_11_08]]; reading nodes in the same call is [[recall_read_k_one_call_finds_and_reads_sc3b_gold_4_to_23_of_67_2026_11_09]].
