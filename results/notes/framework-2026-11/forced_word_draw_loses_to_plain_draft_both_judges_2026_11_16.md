Blind_draw test, 16 Nov 2026 (an agent), plan fixed first (12_cognitive_architecture/writing_brain/dictionary/blind_draw/PLAN.md, RESULT.md). Writer Codex gpt-6.1-sol high on a second machine wrote 40 plain stories (P); a fresh session marked six default content words per story with six fitting alternatives each; draw.py picked one per slot, arm D weighted band/habit from word_walk, arm U uniform; a fresh session placed them (D 240/240, U 239/240). Judges, pairs in both orders, flip = tie: Opus 5.5 P beat D 39-0 (1 tie), P beat U 39-0, D vs U 12-19 (9 ties); Codex P beat D 34-0 (6 ties), P beat U 32-0 (8), D vs U 5-11 (24). Human-likeness 1-5 dropped 0.8-1.6 against P (p<0.001 all four); Opus also saw sense drop 0.5, Codex did not. Verified: both families agree, decision rule met for P. Why D did not beat U: drawn words were rarer than the originals in both arms (median Zipf -0.71 vs -0.55), so the contrast was small. Meaning: with [[dictionary_walk_offers_taken_0_of_40_thesaurus_drift_2026_11_16]] this closes word substitution into a finished-shape draft; still open is offering words before the sentence is written. What would change it: a marking rule restricted to agent-overused words (49 of 240 marked here), fewer slots, or pre-draft offers, tested the same way. Limits: one writer, one run, forty stories.
