Same design as [[wbh1_author_found_32_of_32_plan_pages_last_2026_11_15]], rule registered the day before any page.
Eight passages; a writer continues for about 350 words. The plain arm gets the task; the outline arm also gets the
page's paragraphs in order (speech or narration, rough word counts), drawn from a Markov chain fitted on 121 novels.
Four blind judges ranked seven pages per passage by nearness to the author.

- Outline arm mean rank 5.20, plain arm 3.80 (1 is nearest). On the registered side (outline nearer) p = 0.9994; the
  difference runs the other way at about p = 0.001, and every outline writer is behind every plain writer.
- The outlines were followed (24 of 24 pages), and those pages have a published page's shape (nine paragraphs, a long
  speech on 16 of 24), so the idea was tested and failed, not the prompt.
- 92 of the 130 tells the judges quoted are from the outline arm: a meaning stated or explained (26 against 5), a
  speech used to recap (11 against 3).

How to apply: do not hand a writer a paragraph outline; paragraph shape is measurable on a finished page, and it is not
what these judges go by. With [[wbab5_random_draw_on_plan_gave_nothing_2026_11_15]] this is one of three random or
structural aids tested blind that did not help.

Doubt to carry: one model family wrote and judged; the outlines drew half the speech the authors wrote (0.21 against
0.41 of words), which may be part of the loss; judges probably recognised the book in 22 of 32 cells, which bears on
the author-first count but not on the comparison of the two arms.
