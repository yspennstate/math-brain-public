THE MOVE: judge a SUPPRESSED reflex fire on a window that includes the next two or three actions, never on
the moment it fired - the surface at the fire point is not the territory the fire was about.

WHERE IT WORKED, with the number: the four suppressed cases in the 12 Nov nap (J3, case ids
s1112103125_000..003). Judged at the fire point alone, three of the four look off-domain: the fires landed
on a loop-state append, a notes-markdown write, and a brief being drafted. Judged over what followed, all
four were on-domain - the long multi-section rebuild, the script that copies a tested builder over the live
one, the launch onto a shared Mac - and one was a fire the gate should have let through:
a_pipe_reports_the_filters_verdict, eaten by the read-only MENTION filter because the moment was an
assistant sentence ("Now checking whether the audit tool can run again"), while two tool calls later the
agent read `paper_audit.py status 2>&1 | tail -15`, concluded from a run that had not yet failed that a
usage limit had reset, and launched a gated job on that. One useful miss in four is the number the gates
are now carrying; without the forward window it would have scored zero and the mention filter would have
looked free.

THE SAME ERROR ONE LEVEL UP: three of the four gates here decide by the SURFACE of the moment - prose
versus act, read-only mention, a precision score attached to the instinct rather than the situation. That
is the shape in
[[read_time_hook_finds_a_lesson_by_the_sources_name_not_its_meaning_2026_11_11]] (a hook matching the
archive's names instead of meaning) and in
[[paper_hooks_audit_pattern_lists_measure_their_own_sentences_2026_11_12]] (a checker's patterns matching
its own prose). A filter keyed on the surface is cheap and will be wrong in the same direction every time:
it silences the fire whose territory arrives late.

WHERE IT WOULD NOT WORK: the firehose and nightly strata, where the fire WAS shown. There the following
actions may have been chosen because of the injection, so a forward window is contaminated and scores the
injection's effect, not the fire's domain - `followed` is the right instrument there and `useful` has to be
judged at the moment. The forward window is sound only for fires the agent never saw.

THE CHECK THAT WOULD BREAK IT: take ten suppressed cases and judge each twice - once on at_fire alone,
once with the next three actions - and count the domain_at_play and useful disagreements. If the two
passes agree, the window buys nothing and the extra reading is waste. It also fails if the harvester's
`after` field is ever populated from a different session or a later prompt: confirm the actions in `after`
are the same session's next actions before trusting them.
