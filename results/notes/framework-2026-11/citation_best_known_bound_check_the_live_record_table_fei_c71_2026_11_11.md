Five posted FEI papers (P2, P7, P8, P9, P10 of fei_papers_agent) named Hod's 6.4548 (2017) as the best known lower bound on the FEI constant. The record since July 2026 is Numaro 6.521845710923 (Zenodo 21497769), after Mosaic Intelligence 6.4901 (20794146) and 6.5143 (21113935); all are on Tao's C71 page (teorth/optimizationproblems constants/71a.md), with an independent replay (Toledo, zenodo 23171167). The brain had it since 22 Sep ([[fei_public_lower_bound_record_is_numaro_6_5218_not_hod_2026_09_22]]) but the papers were written from the classic literature. Fixed 11 Nov 17:1x (GitHub P2 090df69, P7 20dee69, P8 e23c483, P9 ef15e1b, P10 19853ca); new Zenodo versions asked of an agent.

**Why:** a 'best known' sentence goes stale fastest; recent records often live in certificate archives and crowd-sourced tables, not in journals, so a literature search by paper title misses them.

**How to apply:** before posting, grep the .tex for 'best known', 'largest known', 'record', 'current bound'; for each, check the live table for that constant (Tao's optimization-constants repo for named constants, OEIS, the problem's own record page) and recall the brain for '<constant> lower bound record'.
