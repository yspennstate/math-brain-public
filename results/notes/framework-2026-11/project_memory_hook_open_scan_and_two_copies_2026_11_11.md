The project-memory prompt hook (the top hook in hook_profile on 10-11 Nov, median 1.67 s, p90 4.3 s) spent its time opening the store, not writing to it. ProjectMemoryStore._install_schema ran on every open and ended with an integrity check, `SELECT COUNT(*) FROM prompt_events WHERE <source binding broken>`, a full scan of 365,691 rows (0.44-2.7 s measured 11 Nov, 0 bad rows every time), then wrote schema_version back unchanged under the write lock. Per-phase times logged from the live hook (state/project_memory/hook_phase_times.jsonl): open 0.46-0.71 s, ingest 0.03-0.11 s, project lookup and offers about 0.

Fix (11 Nov 00:42, an agent-loop): `ProjectMemoryStore(..., verify_bindings=True)`. The hook passes False. On a current-schema store it skips the scan, and every open skips the no-op version write. The hourly pass and the CLI keep the scan. This is safe because the hook inserts only source_id='hook' rows with source_content_hmac NULL (store.py insert), which pass the check by construction. Live after the change: open 0.004-0.027 s, no new rows in contract-failures.jsonl. Backups: hook.py.bak / store.py.bak in the session scratch area. The change lives only on this workstation: the brain repo holds no mirror of ~/.brain-framework/runtime (only Mac fleet fixtures), and the sanitized brain-framework repo has not been updated.
