Second writing-brain A/B (WBAB2, 14 Nov 2026, an agent). Eight Codex writers (gpt-6.1-sol high, normal speed), four with the writing brain (practice warm-up, writing_recall, after_page, word_walk) and four without, three harder fiction tasks (death telegram at a dinner, sisters dividing the kitchen, a night nurse's last hour). Two blind Codex judges ranked all eight pages per task, letters reshuffled per judge per task, BOM stripped (C2's files carried one, a fingerprint), key kept in the session scratchpad only.

Result, against the rule written before scoring (brain wins if one-sided Mann-Whitney p < 0.10 and no more misfit flags):
- mean rank brain 5.08, control 3.92 (1 = best of 8); U = 96, p = 0.92 for "brain better". Brain does not win; the direction favours the controls.
- per judge: J1 brain 5.67 vs 3.33; J2 4.50 vs 4.50.
- misfit flags: brain 2, control 3.
- judges barely agree: Spearman 0.12, 0.05, 0.48 by task. W1 was 6,8,7 for J1 and 1,3,2 for J2.
- tokens: brain arm 690,183 (W3 includes a 24,429-token first run that did nothing), control arm 259,987, so 2.65x. J1 48,571.

Room to fail: this test could have shown a brain win. A 12-vs-12 rank test reaches p < 0.10 at roughly a 1.2-place mean-rank gap, and the gap came out 1.17 places the other way. So it is a real null-or-worse at this size, not a ceiling artefact like WBAB1 (8.83 vs 8.83 on 1-10 scores).

What the judges said about the brain pages placed last: physical business (serving, slippers, cups) repeated without a new consequence, an ending that reuses an earlier gesture. One untested reading is that the word walk and recall push writers toward busy concrete detail, which reads as repetition.

How to use this: do not claim the writing brain improves prose. Before a third test, change something about the method (for example drop the word walk, or have recall serve one specific scar per page), use three or more judges or pairwise comparisons since two judges agree at about 0.2, and keep the rule written before scoring.
