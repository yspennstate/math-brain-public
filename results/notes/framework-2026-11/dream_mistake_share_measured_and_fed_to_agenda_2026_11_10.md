Is the dream giving the corrections the 30% the author ordered? Until 10 Nov 2026 nothing measured it. Now `python ~/.claude/dreaming/dream_mistakes.py` does.

**The author's order.** A rule that about 30 percent of dreaming goes to mistakes (8 Jul 2026, repeated in the author's dream prompts). The agenda's section M and all three dream prompts asked for it; no step checked what the journals did with it.

**The measure.** `dream_mistakes.py` reads the journals in memory\dreams, newest first, and for each one takes the share of its characters that sit under a heading naming mistakes, corrections, scars or section M (subsections included), and whether it carries a line starting `MISTAKES:`. A stub, a partial or a journal under 1,500 bytes is not judged as a dream; a nap is read but left out of the 30% (the nap prompt only keeps section M warm). `--summary` one line, `--agenda` the lines for the agenda, `--selftest` 19 checks on fixture journals.

**First read, 10 Nov 08:49, last ten journals.** Five were stubs or died early (REM cut by a usage limit or the watchdog). Of the three finished full dreams: 7 Nov REM 33%, 5 Nov REM 26%, 10 Nov SWS 0%. The two naps 19% and 17%. No journal has a `MISTAKES:` line. So when a REM finishes it does about what the author asked; the loss is the nights with no journal and the SWS pass, which on 10 Nov filed for seventeen minutes and never reached the corrections (section M sat at the bottom of its prompt).

**What changed.**
- dream.py: agenda section M opens with the last three full dreams' shares; three in a row under 20% turns that line into a SHORTFALL that tells the dream to work M first. It always asks for a `## M. Mistakes` heading and a closing `MISTAKES:` line (corrections worked, reflexes written or extended, which recur).
- dream_prompt_sws.md and dream_prompt_rem.md: M comes right after the safety gate in the SWS pass, before the filing; both ask for the heading and the line.
- brain_status.py prints the share under the dream health lines.

**Not yet seen.** No dream has run on these prompts; the first is 10 Nov 10:30. The share counts characters under a heading, so a dream can reach 30% with padding: read the `MISTAKES:` line and the reflexes it names, not only the number.
