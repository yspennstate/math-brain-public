Recall on the 11 real wordings that name these objects: the new card is first for 10. "Kakutani's dichotomy" and "Campbell's theorem" still print WEAK, with the right card first. The title lane matched a whole title or a title with its bracket removed, never the name before a colon, and every add_classical card is titled 'Name: what it says'. A short exact name of a long-titled card loses the word ranking to shorter cards.

Fixed in math_recall.py the same morning: _title_keys() adds the name before ': ' (with brackets removed) as a third title key. MATH_RECALL_TITLE_COLON=0 turns it off; the backup is math_recall.py.pre_titlecolon_20261108. A/B on a trial copy with the server off, 178 real wordings (math_recall_title_colon_ab_2026_11_08.py):
- switch off vs live: 0 differences.

- other changes: 40, all in the 'found by' provenance lines. The title line now reads "is this card's title or the name it opens with".
After promotion the server and cold paths printed the same on 6 of 6 wordings, and the GCI card is first. Pushed 8aae39e379, read back SAME.

My own wordings, written knowing the titles, went from 0 of 11 first to 9 of 11. That is the soft test; the 10 of 11 above is the real one.

**Why:** cards titled 'Name: statement' are invisible to a lookup by the name alone when the name's words are common, so the store looks like it lacks a card it has. The second-reader checks of the numbers caught nothing wrong, but the 0.00000 shows how a degenerate sample can look like a perfect pass.

**How to apply:** before filing a WEAK MATCH as a missing card, search titles and payloads. After adding a card, run the real wordings again, not only your own. Distrust any check whose result is exactly zero until a narrower sample gives a spread.
